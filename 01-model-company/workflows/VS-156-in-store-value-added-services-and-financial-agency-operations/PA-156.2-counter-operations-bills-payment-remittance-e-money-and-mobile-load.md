## W4609. Bills-Payment Counter Transaction Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer bills-payment request |
| **Frequency** | Per transaction (high frequency at target stores) |
| **Volume** | Bills-payment transactions across counters |
| **Owner** | Counter Staff / Store Ops (VS-07) |
| **Participants** | Cash Mgmt (VS-08.2), Aggregator (W4602), Compliance (VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Counter Staff process the bills-payment (select biller, enter account/reference/amount, validate per aggregator W4602), collect cash, and confirm posting | Counter Staff | Store Manager / Counter Lead | Per transaction |
| 2 | Counter Staff issue the official receipt/transaction record (BIR-compliant cash receipt VS-79/W4620), and capture the transaction to the VAS system (W4608) | Counter Staff / Cash Mgmt | Store Manager | Per transaction |
| 3 | Counter Staff handle any posting exception/error (W4615), and reconcile cash to transactions at end-of-day (W4613) | Counter Staff / Cash Mgmt | Store Manager | Per transaction/daily |

### System Touchpoints
- VAS terminal/aggregator (W4602/W4608); cash mgmt (VS-08.2); BIR receipt (VS-79/W4620); GL (VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Posting-error risk**: Wrong biller/amount posted → customer loss; mitigated by validation & confirmation
- **Cash-shortage risk**: Cash ≠ transactions (VS-142/VS-23); mitigated by end-of-day reconciliation & SoD

### Time Estimate
- ~2–5 min per transaction

### Automation Opportunity
- Aggregator-integrated validation; BIR-receipt automation; cash-transaction reconciliation

### Controls
- CTL-699 (ensure controlled execution — Counter Operations — Bills Payment, Remittance, E-Money & Mobile Load (PA-156.2).)
- CTL: transaction validated before posting; BIR-receipt issued (VS-79); cash reconciled daily (SoD)

### Cross-references
- links to W4602 (aggregator), W4608 (systems), VS-08.2 (cash), VS-79/W4620 (BIR), VS-142 (cash handling), W4613 (reconciliation), W4615 (exceptions)

## W4610. Domestic Remittance Send/Receive Transaction Processing

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Counter Staff process the remittance (sender/receiver KYC W4614, amount, destination), apply AML/threshold checks as agent (VS-86/W4619), and confirm via principal (W4603) | Counter Staff / AML | Store Manager / Counter Lead | Per transaction |
| 2 | Counter Staff collect/disperse cash (send = collect; receive = disperse), issue receipt/reference, and capture to VAS system (W4608) | Counter Staff / Cash Mgmt | Store Manager | Per transaction |

- **Mis-disbursement risk**: Paying wrong receiver → loss; mitigated by receiver KYC/ID verification

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 min per transaction (KYC-driven)

### Automation Opportunity
- Principal-integrated remittance; KYC/threshold automation; AML-suspicious-transaction flagging

### Controls
- CTL-699 (ensure controlled execution — Counter Operations — Bills Payment, Remittance, E-Money & Mobile Load (PA-156.2).)
- CTL: sender/receiver KYC (W4614); AML thresholds/reporting as agent (VS-86/W4619); cash reconciled (SoD)

### Cross-references

## W4613. VAS Cash Handling, Custody & End-of-Day Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily close; cash cycle |
| **Frequency** | Daily per store |
| **Volume** | VAS cash handling across counters |
| **Owner** | Cash Mgmt (VS-08.2) / Store Ops (VS-07) |
| **Participants** | Treasury (VS-18), Finance (VS-17), Audit (VS-21), CIT (VS-81) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Counter Staff/Cash Mgmt handle VAS cash with SoD (collect ≠ reconcile ≠ post ≠ bank), tamper-evident custody, and daily remittance limit, per VS-142/W4607 standards | Counter Staff / Cash Mgmt | Store Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Cash Mgmt reconcile VAS cash to transactions (bills/remittance/e-money/load) end-of-day, investigate variances, and segregate VAS cash from retail cash (VS-08.2) | Cash Mgmt / Store Manager | Store Manager / Finance | Daily |
| 3 | Treasury/CIT bank the VAS cash (VS-18/VS-81), settle to principals (W4617), and report cash integrity to finance/audit (VS-17/VS-21) | Treasury / CIT / Finance | CFO | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Cash mgmt (VS-08.2); treasury (VS-18); CIT (VS-81); finance/GL (VS-17); audit (VS-21); COD/cash standard (VS-142)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cash-shrinkage risk**: VAS cash skim/shortage (VS-23/VS-142); mitigated by SoD, reconciliation, tamper-evident custody
- **Commingling risk**: VAS cash mixed with retail cash → reconciliation confusion; mitigated by segregation

### Time Estimate
- ~30 min/day per store reconciliation

### Automation Opportunity
- Cash-transaction reconciliation app; variance-alerting; segregated-cash tracking

### Controls
- CTL-93 (ensure VAS agency cash & BSP compliance.)
- CTL: SoD (collect/reconcile/post/bank segregated); daily reconciliation; VAS cash segregated; tamper-evident custody (VS-142)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08.2 (cash), VS-18/VS-81 (treasury/CIT), VS-17/VS-21 (finance/audit), VS-142/VS-23 (cash/shrink standard), W4617 (settlement)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Counter Staff collect & verify customer ID per principal/BSP KYC requirements (valid ID, name, address) for remittance/e-money/threshold transactions, with consent (VS-91) | Counter Staff / AML | Store Manager / Counter Lead | Per transaction |

### System Touchpoints
- AML/KYC (VS-86/W4619); privacy (VS-91); records/retention (VS-88); principal integration (W4603); audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inadequate-KYC risk**: Weak ID verification → AML/fraud exposure (VS-86/VS-125); mitigated by mandatory valid-ID & verification
- **Privacy-misuse risk**: KYC data over-collected/misused; mitigated by purpose-limit & secure handling (VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- ~3–8 min per KYC transaction

### Automation Opportunity
- ID-verification tool; suspicious-pattern screening; secure KYC-data capture

### Controls
- CTL-93 (ensure VAS agency cash & BSP compliance.)
