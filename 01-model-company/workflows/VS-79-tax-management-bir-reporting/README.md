## Overview

Tax Management & BIR Statutory Reporting workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — covering the
full Philippine indirect tax (VAT/percentage tax), withholding tax (EWT/CWT), corporate income
tax, local business tax, documentary stamp tax, BIR eFPS/e-Invoicing/CAS compliance, and audit
defense lifecycle across all 5 legal entities and 205 locations.

While transactional tax entries are posted by Record-to-Report (VS-17) and tax master data is
governed by Master Data (VS-29), this value stream owns the **tax compliance program**:
return preparation, eFPS filing, e-Invoicing/EIS transmission, withholding agent obligations,
tax credit reconciliation, BIR assessment response, and tax risk governance.
