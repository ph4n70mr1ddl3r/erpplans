## W2761. Expanded Withholding Tax (EWT) Determination & Monthly Form 1601E/1601EQ

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close; vendor payment run |
| **Frequency** | Monthly per entity |
| **Volume** | ~8,500 AP invoices/month; ~PHP 42–45B annual COGS subject to EWT |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, AP, Tax Accountant |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AP applies EWT rate per nature-of-payment (rent 5%, professional 10–15%, supplies 1%, contractors 2% etc.) at invoice entry via ERP tax code; withholds at payment | AP | Tax Manager | Per payment |
| 2 | Tax Accountant reconciles monthly EWT withheld ledger to GL withholding liability; investigates un-withheld payments; books accruals for late-detected under-withholding | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 1–2 days/entity |
| 3 | Tax Manager prepares and files BIR Form 1601E (monthly) / 1601EQ (quarterly) via eFPS; remits withheld amounts; archives schedules | Tax Manager | CFO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP AP EWT engine; withholding liability GL account; BIR eFPS

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Under-withholding disallows vendor deduction and imposes 25% surcharge + interest; mitigated by automated tax-code mapping and payment-run validation


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-count (tax accountant reconciles monthly ewt)
- auto-generate (tax manager prepares and files bir)

### Controls
operational: automated tax-code mapping and payment-run validation

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 days per entity per month

## W2762. Creditable Withholding Tax (CWT) at Source & Vendor 2307 Issuance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Each taxable vendor/contractor payment subject to CWT |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~3,000–4,000 payments/month |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 active vendors |
| **Owner** | AP Manager |
| **Participants** | AP, Tax Accountant, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AP withholds CWT at payment per vendor's ATC (Alpha list Tax Code); generates BIR Form 2307 (Certificate of Tax Withheld) from ERP per payee/quarter | AP | AP Manager | Per payment |
| 2 | AP submits 2307 to vendor via portal/email; tracks acknowledgment; handles vendor disputes over amount/tax base | AP | AP Manager | Per payment |
| 3 | Tax Accountant rolls up 2307 totals into quarterly 1604E; reconciles sum of issued 2307 to GL withholding; archives certificates | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP AP 2307 generation; vendor portal; withholding GL; 1604E rollup

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Operational risk**: Manual 2307 issuance errors trigger vendor complaints and delayed input VAT recovery; mitigated by automated generation and portal delivery


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-generate (tax code); generates bir form 2307)
- auto-submit (ap submits 2307 to vendor)
- auto-count (tax accountant rolls up 2307)

### Controls
operational: automated generation and portal delivery

### Time Estimate
- 5–10 min per 2307; quarterly rollup 2–3 days

## W2763. Customer CWT Collection (2307) & AR Application

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Government/corporate customer withholds tax on payment |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~1,500–2,000 AR receipts/month |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 AR trade/corporate accounts |
| **Owner** | AR Manager |
| **Participants** | AR, Tax Accountant, Customer, Collector |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collector obtains customer-issued BIR Form 2307 at collection; AR matches withholding to open invoice(s); applies as credit to customer's withholding tax receivable | AR / Collector | AR Manager | Per receipt |
| 2 | Tax Accountant validates customer 2307 authenticity and ATC; reconciles to customer's withholding against books; flags missing certificates for follow-up | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | Tax Accountant uses accumulated customer CWT certificates to reduce income tax payable on 1702Q/1702RT; files refund/TCC claim for excess creditable withholding | Tax Accountant / Tax Manager | CFO | Quarterly/Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP AR withholding application; withholding tax receivable GL; BIR refund/TCC module

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cash-flow risk**: Unrecovered CWT sits as receivable; mitigated by aggressive 2307 collection and TCC claim-back


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-match (collection; ar matches withholding to open)
- auto-validate (tax accountant validates customer 2307 authenticity)
- auto-count (tax accountant uses accumulated customer)

### Controls
operational: aggressive 2307 collection and TCC claim-back

### Time Estimate
- 10–15 min per customer receipt; quarterly claim 3–5 days

## W2764. Compensation Withholding Tax (1601C) & TRAIN Tax Table Maintenance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Semi-monthly payroll run; monthly close |
| **Frequency** | Semi-monthly withholding; monthly filing |
| **Volume** | 6,757 employees across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Payroll Manager |
| **Participants** | Payroll Mgr, Tax Accountant, HR |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payroll Manager maintains TRAIN-law graduated withholding tax table in payroll engine per latest BIR revenue regulations; validates per-pay-period computation against manual sample | Payroll Manager | Tax Manager | Per payroll cycle |
| 2 | Payroll withholds compensation tax semi-monthly; remits via 1601C monthly; reconciles withholding register to payroll register and GL liability | Payroll / Tax Accountant | Payroll Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | Tax Accountant files 1601C via eFPS; supports BIR 2316 issuance to each employee by January 31; archives employer copy | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Monthly/Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Payroll tax engine; withholding GL; BIR eFPS; 2316 generation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Stale tax table causes systematic over/under-withholding for 6,757 employees; mitigated by version-controlled table updates and sample validation


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-validate (revenue regulations; validates per-pay-period computation against)
- auto-reconcile (1601c monthly; reconciles withholding register to)
- auto-count (tax accountant files 1601c via)

### Controls
operational: version-controlled table updates and sample validation

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 days per entity per month

## W2765. Annual Information Return (1604E/1604C) & Alphabetical Employee List

| **Frequency** | Annual per entity |
| **Volume** | 5 entities; ~800 vendors + 6,757 employees |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, Tax Accountant, Payroll, AP |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Accountant compiles annual Alpha List of Payees (1604E — income/payments and 1604C — compensation); validates vendor TINs against BIR TIN Validation System | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 3–5 days/entity |
| 2 | Payroll compiles Alphabetical List of Employees ( alphalist.dat); cross-checks 2316 totals to payroll register; resolves duplicate/invalid TIN records | Payroll | Tax Manager | 2–3 days |
| 3 | Tax Manager files 1604E/1604C with Alpha List attachments via eFPS BIR system before January 31; archives annual withholding certificates | Tax Manager | CFO | 1–2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP annual rollup; BIR alphalist.dat generator; eFPS attachment portal

### Automation Opportunity
- auto-validate (— compensation); validates vendor tins against)
- auto-record (duplicate/invalid tin records)
- auto-file (tax manager files 1604e/1604c with alpha)

### Controls
operational: TIN validation at onboarding and pre-filing scrub

### Time Estimate

## W2767. EWT Rate Master Governance & Tax Code Mapping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | BIR revenue regulation update or new payment type |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly review; event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~60 ATC codes; ~500 item/service categories mapped |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, IT, Master Data, AP |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Manager monitors BIR Revenue Regulations/Revenue Memoranda for rate changes (e.g., CREATE adjustments); maintains EWT/FWT rate master per ATC | Tax Manager | CFO | Quarterly |
| 2 | Master Data maps service/purchase categories and vendor types to ATC codes; configures ERP tax determination rules; versions and tests changes in sandbox | Master Data / IT | Tax Manager | Per change |
| 3 | Tax Manager communicates changes to AP/Procurement; runs retro-impact analysis on recent payments; documents control evidence for audit | Tax Manager | CFO | Per change |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP tax-code/ATC master; vendor type master; change-control workflow

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Outdated rates cause systematic mis-withholding; mitigated by quarterly regulatory scan and versioned tax master


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-create (changes (e.g., create adjustments); maintains ewt/fwt)
- auto-test (versions and tests changes in sandbox)
- auto-document (recent payments; documents control evidence for)

### Controls
operational: quarterly regulatory scan and versioned tax master

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 days per regulatory change
