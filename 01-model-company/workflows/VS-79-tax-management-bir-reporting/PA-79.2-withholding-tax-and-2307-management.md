## W2761. Expanded Withholding Tax (EWT) Determination & Quarterly Form 1601EQ

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close; vendor payment run |
| **Frequency** | Monthly per entity |
| **Volume** | ~8,500 AP invoices/month; ~PHP 42–45B annual COGS from which EWT-applicable payments (services, rent, contractors) are screened at payment |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, AP, Tax Accountant |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AP applies EWT rate per nature-of-payment (rent 5%, professional 10–15%, supplies 1%, contractors 2% etc.) at invoice entry via ERP tax code; withholds at payment | AP | Tax Manager | Per payment |
| 2 | Tax Accountant reconciles monthly EWT withheld ledger to GL withholding liability; investigates un-withheld payments; books accruals for late-detected under-withholding | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 1–2 days/entity |
| 3 | Tax Manager prepares and files BIR Form 1601-EQ (quarterly, due the last day of the month following quarter close) via eFPS; remits withheld amounts; archives schedules | Tax Manager | CFO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP AP EWT engine; withholding liability GL account; BIR eFPS

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Under-withholding disallows vendor deduction and imposes 25% surcharge + interest; mitigated by automated tax-code mapping and payment-run validation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Accountant reconciles monthly EWT withheld ledger to GL withholding liability" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-generate of "prepares and files BIR Form 1601-EQ" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-214 (ensure BIR tax compliance & filing integrity.)
- operational: automated tax-code mapping and payment-run validation

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 days per entity per month

## W5508. Fringe Benefits Tax (FBT) Determination, Valuation & Quarterly BIR Form 1605 Filing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly FBT computation and filing cycle (15 days after quarter close); benefit-plan or de-minimis-threshold changes at C&B; new fringe-benefit source activation (housing allocation, vehicle assignment, loan issuance) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous benefit-event accrual; quarterly filing per entity |
| **Volume** | 5 entities × 4 quarters = 20 BIR 1605 filings/year; ~40–120 FBT-liable benefit instances per quarter chain-wide (managerial/supervisory recipients only — housing allocations, executive vehicle personal-use, club memberships, below-market loans, officer-only medical) |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | Tax Accountant, C&B Manager, Payroll Manager, HRIS Analyst, Controller (computation review), CFO (filing approval), external tax advisor (interpretation) |

### Background

Benefits in kind furnished to managerial and supervisory employees carry a **final tax on the employer** under NIRC §33 (as amended by RA 10963 — TRAIN — to a 35% rate on the grossed-up monetary value), filed quarterly on **BIR Form 1605**. The capability had no owner: 'fringe benefit'/'FBT'/'1605' appeared in zero PA files as dedicated headers (only PA-144.1 flags 'BIR (fringe benefit)' as a dormitory-housing compliance obligation with no downstream workflow), while the adjacent workflows each own a different tax species — W2764 (compensation withholding on cash, 1601C), W2766 (final withholding on passive income), W3314 (SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG statutory contributions), W3316 (allowance/flex-benefit policy administration, no tax filing) — and VS-144 dormitory housing is one of the largest recurring fringe-benefit *sources* for the store-workforce population. This workflow owns the rank test, the exclusion classification (de minimis and otherwise-exempt benefits), the NIRC valuation rules, the gross-up computation, monthly accrual, and the quarterly 1605 filing across all 5 legal entities.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Benefit-source identification**: Tax Accountant and C&B Manager maintain the fringe-benefit source register fed from HRIS and the C&B benefit master (W3313/W3316): employer-provided housing incl. dormitory allocations to managerial residents (VS-144), vehicle personal-use share from fleet assignment logs and telematics (VS-06), officer-only HMO/medical beyond the universal plan (W3313), educational assistance, club memberships, below-market employee loans (imputed interest on W76 loan issuances), and employee-discount patterns beyond policy (W5B.12 limits); each source carries a valuation method, rank-eligibility flag, and entity attribution | Tax Accountant / C&B Manager | Tax Manager | Quarterly refresh, 2–3 hours |
| 2 | **Rank and exclusion test**: system classifies each benefit event: (a) recipient rank — FBT applies only to managerial/supervisory employees; benefits furnished to the general rank-and-file population are instead taxable compensation routed to W2764; (b) exclusion screening — de minimis benefits within the BIR threshold schedule (RR 3-98 as updated by RR 5-2018: monetized unused leave within limits, rice subsidy, uniforms, rank-and-file medical assistance within caps), mandatory contributions, and benefits required for the employer's convenience / furnished on the necessity of the business (e.g., worksite housing where residency is required) are excluded with documented rationale | System / Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Automated + 1–2 hours/quarter review |
| 3 | **Valuation per NIRC §33 rules**: housing at fair rental value; vehicles at depreciation plus operating costs × personal-use share (telematics/logbook evidence); loans at imputed interest using the prescribed rate × forgone spread; other benefits at acquisition cost; Tax Accountant overrides system values where evidence dictates (e.g., co-occupied housing pro-ration) | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 1–2 hours/quarter |
| 4 | **Gross-up computation and aggregation**: system computes the grossed-up monetary value (monetary value ÷ (1 − 35%)) and FBT thereon at 35%, aggregated per employee, per benefit type, per entity; FBT is a final tax — the benefit is excluded from the employee's taxable compensation so no W2764 double-count (BIR 2316 coordination at year-end via W2765) | System | Tax Manager | Automated |
| 5 | **Monthly accrual and reconciliation**: month-end close posts the FBT accrual (Dr. Fringe Benefits Tax Expense / Cr. FBT Payable) per the W9A tax-provision step; Tax Accountant reconciles the benefit-event register to the GL accrual and to payroll/payslip exclusion trails; variances investigated before filing | Tax Accountant / Chief Accountant | Controller | 30–45 min/month |
| 6 | **Quarterly BIR 1605 filing**: Tax Accountant generates BIR Form 1605 per entity from the FBT register, reviews against the accrual, and files via eFPS within 15 days following quarter close (due-date governance per the W2776 tax calendar); payment via e-Payment rails per W2755; BIR confirmation and PRN archived in the tax evidence repository | Tax Accountant | CFO | 2–3 hours/entity/quarter |
| 7 | **Evidence, audit defense and employee-facing consistency**: FBT register, valuation evidence (telematics extracts, rental appraisals, loan amortization schedules), exclusion rationale memos, and filed 1605s retained 10 years per BIR rules; register excerpt feeds BIR audit response (W2773) and the annual information-return ecosystem (W2765); employee-side: FBT-covered benefits flagged in the HRIS so they neither appear as taxable compensation on 2316s nor are double-reported | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 30 min/quarter |
| 8 | **Annual benefit-design review**: C&B Manager and Tax Accountant review benefit-plan changes for FBT efficiency (structure vs cash-equivalent, de-minimis headroom under updated BIR issuances, rank-population design that keeps benefits universal where intended), feeding the W3320 benefits cost model, the W2776 tax-risk register, and next year's plan design (W3313 renewal) | C&B Manager / Tax Accountant | CHRO / CFO | Annual, 2–4 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS-to-tax-engine benefit-event feed with rank flag, benefit type, valuation method, and entity attribution
- Fleet telematics personal-use allocation export feeding vehicle valuation (VS-06 integration)
- Loan module imputed-interest computation on below-market issuances (W76)
- Tax engine FBT gross-up (MV ÷ (1 − 35%) × 35%) with per-employee/per-entity aggregation and exclusion-rule flags
- FBT Payable GL account and month-end accrual posting (W9A tax-provision step); reconciliation report register ↔ GL ↔ payroll exclusion trail
- BIR eFPS 1605 quarterly filing with e-Payment PRN linkage (W2755) and archive in the tax evidence repository (10-year retention, feeds W2773)
- De-minimis threshold parameter table versioned per BIR issuance

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Misclassification risk**: applying FBT to rank-and-file benefits (or missing the managerial population) either over-taxes or under-reports — the rank test and exclusion screen are the control points; mitigated by HRIS rank flags plus documented exclusion rationale memos (steps 2, 7)
- **Valuation dispute risk**: vehicle personal-use and co-occupied housing valuations are BIR audit hotspots when unsupported; mitigated by telematics/logbook and appraisal evidence attached to the register (step 3)
- **Late-filing exposure**: 20 statutory deadlines/year across 5 entities; mitigated by W2776 tax-calendar governance with pre-deadline escalations (step 6)
- **Double-counting risk**: FBT-covered benefits re-appearing as taxable compensation on 2316s (or vice versa); mitigated by the HRIS exclusion flag and W2764/W2765 reconciliation (steps 4, 7)
- **Plan-design blindness**: C&B rolling out a new perk without FBT costing understates true cost by ~54% of monetary value at the 35% grossed-up rate; mitigated by the annual design review gate (step 8)

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-feed of benefit events from HRIS/telematics/loan modules into the FBT register with rank and valuation metadata (supports Step 1).
- Exclusion-rule engine applying the versioned de-minimis threshold table with rationale capture (replaces manual Step 2 screening).
- Auto gross-up computation and per-entity aggregation with register-to-GL-to-payroll three-way reconciliation report (supports Steps 4–5).
- eFPS 1605 prefill and PRN-linked payment posting (supports Step 6).

### Controls
- CTL-467 (ensure controlled execution — Withholding Tax (EWT/CWT) & Form 2307 Management (PA-79.2).)
- operational: exclusion rationale memos per benefit type; telematics/appraisal valuation evidence attached before computation; three-way register/GL/payroll reconciliation before each quarterly filing; 10-year evidence retention feeding W2773 audit defense

### Time Estimate
- Steady state ~30–45 min/month accrual + 2–3 hours per entity per quarter for computation review and 1605 filing (≈ 45–65 hours/year chain-wide) + annual design review 2–4 hours; absorbed by the Tax team and C&B; no incremental headcount
