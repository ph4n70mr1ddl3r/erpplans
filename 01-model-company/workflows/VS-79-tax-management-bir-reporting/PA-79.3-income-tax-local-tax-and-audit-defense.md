## W2769. Quarterly Corporate Income Tax (1702Q) & Annual Return (1702RT) Preparation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarter-end / year-end close |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + annual per entity |
| **Volume** | 5 entities; ~PHP 62.3B consolidated revenue |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, GL, Tax Accountant, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GL closes books per entity; Tax Accountant derives taxable income from financial income using TAFI schedule; nets prior-quarter over/under payments | GL / Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 5–7 days/entity |
| 2 | Tax Manager applies corporate income tax rate (25% regular; 20% under CREATE for net taxable income ≤ PHP 5M and total assets ≤ PHP 100M); computes quarterly tax due | Tax Manager | CFO | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Tax Manager files 1702Q (quarterly) and 1702RT (annual) via eFPS with AFS attachments; coordinates external auditor sign-off on annual | Tax Manager / External Auditor | CFO | 1–2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP GL/financial close; TAFI worksheet; BIR eFPS; AFS generation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: CREATE rate misapplication; mitigated by entity-level eligibility testing each quarter


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Accountant derives taxable income from financial income using TAFI schedule" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-create of "CREATE for net taxable ≤ PHP 5M and total assets ≤ PHP 100M" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-file of "files 1702Q" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-468 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Income Tax, Local Tax, DST & BIR Audit Defense (PA-79.3).)
- operational: entity-level eligibility testing each quarter

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per entity per year

## W2773. BIR Tax Audit / LOAN Response & Assessment Contestation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~2–4 active audits/year |
| **Volume** | Multi-year audit scope (3-year + 5-year) |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, CFO, Legal, External Tax Counsel, Entity Controllers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Manager validates LOA jurisdiction/scope and signatory authority; assembles audit team; gathers documents (books, schedules, 2307s, subledger) in BIR-defined format | Tax Manager | CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | External Tax Counsel responds to BIR findings (PAN/LA/FDDA) with legal and factual defenses; negotiates settlement or files protest within 15/30-day windows | External Tax Counsel / Tax Manager | CFO | Weeks–months |

- System auto-update of "updates tax risk register" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-468 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Income Tax, Local Tax, DST & BIR Audit Defense (PA-79.3).)
