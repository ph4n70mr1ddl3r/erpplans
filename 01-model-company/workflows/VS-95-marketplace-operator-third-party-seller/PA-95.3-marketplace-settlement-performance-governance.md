## W3153. Marketplace Commission, Fee Deduction & Seller Payout

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Payout cycle (e.g., bi-weekly/monthly); order completion |
| **Frequency** | Per payout cycle |
| **Volume** | All sellers with completed orders |
| **Owner** | Marketplace Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketplace Mgr, Finance, AP/Treasury (links to VS-15/VS-18), Seller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System computes per-seller settlement: gross GMV (links to VS-10) − commission % (W3141) − fees (listing, fulfillment, advertising — W3141/W3152) − returns/refunds (W3151) − withholding tax (W3157) − holdback reserve; produce seller statement | System / Finance | Finance Manager | Per cycle |
| 2 | Finance/AP (links to VS-15) review settlement; reconcile to order/returns records; resolve disputes; Treasury (links to VS-18) disburse net payout to seller's verified bank account | Finance Analyst / Treasury | CFO | Per cycle |
| 3 | System posts settlement entries: marketplace revenue (commission/fees), VAT output (W3157), AP to seller, withholdings remitted to BIR (links to VS-79); archive statement per records (links to VS-88) | System / Finance | Finance Manager | Per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Settlement engine; AP/payout (links to VS-15/VS-18); tax (links to VS-79); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Payout-error risk**: Incorrect settlement damages seller trust; mitigated by automated reconciliation and dispute window


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-record (to order/returns records; resolve disputes; treasury)
- auto-post (system posts settlement entries: marketplace)

### Controls
operational: automated reconciliation and dispute window

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 days per payout cycle

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
