# VS-95: Marketplace Operator & Third-Party Seller Management

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Marketplace Operator & Third-Party Seller Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing BuildRight as **operator of its own third-party (3P) marketplace** on
buildright.com.ph and the BuildRight app, where vetted third-party sellers list, sell, and
fulfill specialty SKUs (niche tools, regional brands, hard-to-stock items, refurbished/certified
pre-owned, vendor direct-ship) alongside BuildRight's own first-party (1P) catalog — expanding
assortment beyond the 35,000 active SKUs without proportional inventory investment.

This is distinct from **VS-48 (Retail Media Network)** which sells advertising, from **VS-65
(E-Commerce Marketplace Integration)** which lists BuildRight as a seller on external
marketplaces (Lazada/Shopee), and from **VS-10 PA-10.2 (Order Fulfillment)** which fulfills
BuildRight-owned inventory. The marketplace operator owns the **3P marketplace operating model**:
platform business model and seller recruitment, seller onboarding/KYB and contracting, unified
catalog and offer/buy-box logic, marketplace order capture and routing to sellers, seller
fulfillment SLA and POD, multi-seller basket orchestration, marketplace settlement and
commission, seller performance governance and policy enforcement, marketplace consumer
protection and tax compliance (VAT per seller / BIR), and marketplace P&L/take-rate analytics.
