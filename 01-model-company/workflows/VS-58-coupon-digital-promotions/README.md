# VS-58: Coupon & Digital Promotions Management

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

**Event custody:** owns coupon/voucher campaign issuance and redemption-fraud programs; price-file execution rides the VS-01 W13 promo-execution canon and POS redemption mechanics ride VS-08 W539 — boundaries per the [Event Custody & Precedence Register](../event-custody-and-precedence-register.md) (E-15).

## Overview

Manages BuildRight's coupon and digital promotions program covering paper coupons, digital vouchers, loyalty point multipliers, and promotional code campaigns. Coordinates with marketing campaigns (VS-14), POS (VS-08), loyalty (VS-13), and ecommerce (VS-10). Targets ~15% of monthly transactions involving a promotional offer.
