## W2254. Campaign ROI Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-campaign |
| **Frequency** | Per campaign (~18/year) |
| **Volume** | 18 analyses/year |
| **Owner** | Marketing Analyst |
| **Participants** | VP Marketing |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing Analyst computes the campaign ROI: incremental sales vs. baseline, coupon cost, marketing cost, margin impact, new-customer acquisition; recommends next-campaign adjustments | Marketing Analyst | VP Marketing | 4–6 hours/campaign |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113, VS-57 / W2234 promo-pricing ROI) — the ROI dataset
- Coupon platform (links to VS-58 / W2245 dashboard) — the redemption and cost data
- CRM (links to VS-13 / W156) — the new-customer acquisition

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Baseline-confound risk**: concurrent factors (season, competitor) inflate the baseline; mitigated by the controlled-baseline (comparable non-promo period/store)
- **Incremental-vs-pulled-forward risk**: the "incremental" sales are pulled-forward (cannibalized from future periods), not true increment; mitigated by the post-campaign-dip analysis

### Automation Opportunity
- ROI auto-computed; baseline auto-selected; pull-forward auto-detected

### Controls
- CTL (ROI-accuracy — links to VS-17); operational: VP Marketing sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/campaign × 18 = **~90 hours/year** Marketing Analyst.

## W2257. Promotion Halo Effect Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-major campaign |
| **Frequency** | 6/year |
| **Volume** | 6/year |
| **Owner** | Analytics Manager |
| **Participants** | VP Marketing |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analytics Manager measures the halo effect: did the promo on Category A lift sales of non-promoted Category B (the halo)? Uses basket-analysis on the promo-period transactions | Analytics Manager | VP Marketing | 2–3 days/campaign |

### System Touchpoints
- Basket analytics (links to VS-28 / W113, VS-13 / W156) — the basket-level transaction data
- Coupon platform (links to VS-58 / W2246) — the promo-transaction identification
- Analytics (links to VS-28 / W208 retail analytics) — the halo computation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Halo-overattribution risk**: the "halo" is attributed without a clean causal link (the Category B lift may be from a different driver); mitigated by the controlled-comparison (promo-period basket vs. non-promo-period basket)
- **Negative-halo-blindness risk**: the promo cannibalized Category B (customers bought the promo instead of B), and the negative halo is missed; mitigated by the two-directional analysis (positive and negative halo)

### Automation Opportunity
- Halo auto-computed from basket data; negative-halo auto-flagged

### Controls
- Operational: Analytics Manager methodology sign-off; VP Marketing review

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/campaign × 6 = **~120 hours/year** Analytics Manager.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual comprehensive |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | Full year review |
| **Owner** | VP Marketing |
| **Participants** | COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Marketing reviews the full-year promotion performance: campaign count, total spend, total incremental sales, average ROI, trend vs. prior year; identifies improving/declining patterns; recommends next-year promotion strategy | VP Marketing | COO | 3–5 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113, VS-58 / W2254 ROI) — the full-year dataset
- Strategy (links to VS-14 / W83 campaign, VS-33 / strategic planning) — the promotion strategy
- Budget (links to VS-17 / W26) — the promotion-spend trend

### Pain Points / Risks
- **ROI-decline-blindness risk**: the promotion count grew but the average ROI declined (more promos, less effective each); mitigated by the average-ROI trend, not just the total-spend trend
- **Promo-dependency risk**: BuildRight becomes dependent on promos (customers only buy when there's a coupon), eroding the full-price business; mitigated by the full-price-vs-promo sales mix monitored alongside the promo performance

### Automation Opportunity
- Trend auto-assembled from the year's campaign analytics; ROI-decline auto-flagged

### Controls
- Operational: COO sign-off on the next-year strategy

### Time Estimate
~4 days/year = **~30 hours/year** VP Marketing.

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual planning |

| **Volume** | ~18+ events/year |
| **Owner** | VP Marketing |
| **Participants** | COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Marketing optimizes the promotion calendar: event timing (align with seasonal calendar, avoid promo-fatigue clusters), event depth (calibrate to the ROI history), event mix (coupon vs. loyalty-multiplier vs. markdown); balances promo frequency against full-price sales | VP Marketing | COO | 3–5 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Promotion calendar (links to VS-29 / W306 seasonal calendar, VS-14 / W13) — the event scheduling
- ROI history (links to VS-58 / W2254, W2258 YoY trend) — the depth-calibration
- Strategy (links to VS-33 / strategic planning, VS-14) — the promotion strategy

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Promo-fatigue risk**: too many promos in a period desensitize customers and erode full-price sales; mitigated by the frequency-cap and the full-price-vs-promo mix monitored
- **Calendar-collision risk**: two promos overlap (a category promo and a chain-wide promo), creating stacking chaos; mitigated by the calendar-conflict check

### Automation Opportunity
- Calendar auto-optimized from the ROI history; conflict auto-detected; frequency-cap auto-enforced

### Controls
- CTL (promo-calendar authorization); operational: COO sign-off

### Time Estimate
~4 days/year = **~30–40 hours/year** VP Marketing.
