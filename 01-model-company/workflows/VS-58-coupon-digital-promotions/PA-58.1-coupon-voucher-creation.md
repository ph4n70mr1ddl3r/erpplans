## W2238. Coupon Design & Configuration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Marketing campaign launch |
| **Frequency** | ~18 campaigns/year |
| **Volume** | ~18 designs/year |
| **Owner** | Marketing Promotions Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Promotions Mgr, VP Marketing |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing Promotions Manager designs the coupon: discount type (PHP-off, %-off, BOGO), eligible SKUs/categories, min-spend, validity period, stacking rules, channel (POS/ecom/app), redemption limit per customer; configures in the promo-rule engine | Marketing Promotions Mgr | VP Marketing | 1–2 days/campaign |

### System Touchpoints
- Promo-rule engine (links to VS-29 / W300 promo-rule master, VS-14 / W13 promotions, VS-57 / W2223 promo pricing) — the coupon configuration
- DTI permit (links to VS-22 / W868) — the "Per DTI Permit No." requirement
- Item/category master (links to VS-29 / W252, W290 category) — the eligible-SKU scope

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-discount risk**: the coupon depth + stacking creates a net-loss transaction (customer pays less than COGS); mitigated by the margin-floor guard in the config and the stacking-limit
- **DTI-permit-lapse risk**: the coupon launches without the DTI permit; mitigated by the permit-gate in the design workflow

### Automation Opportunity
- Coupon auto-configured from the design template; margin-impact auto-checked; DTI-permit-gate auto-enforced

### Controls
- CTL-196 (prevent coupon & promo-code abuse.)
- CTL (promo-rule authorization — links to VS-14); CTL (DTI-permit compliance — links to W868); operational: VP Marketing sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1.5 days/campaign × 18 = **~200 hours/year** Marketing Promotions Manager.

## W2241. Coupon Budget & Liability Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Campaign launch |
| **Frequency** | ~18/year |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 50–100M annual promo budget |
| **Owner** | Finance Manager |
| **Participants** | CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Manager sets the coupon budget per campaign; tracks the coupon liability (unredeemed coupons as a PFRS 15 liability); monitors the redemption rate vs. budget; flags over-budget campaigns | Finance Manager | CFO | Per campaign + monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Coupon liability (links to VS-17 / W28, VS-54 / W2156 breakage pattern, VS-157 / PFRS 15) — the unredeemed-coupon liability
- Budget (links to VS-17 / W26) — the promo-spend budget
- Coupon platform (links to VS-58 / W2238) — the issued-vs-redeemed count

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Liability-overstatement risk**: the coupon liability is overstated (assumes 100% redemption when the actual is 15%), tying up capital; mitigated by the expected-redemption-rate estimation (the PFRS 15 breakage approach)
- **Budget-overrun risk**: a viral campaign exceeds the budget; mitigated by the redemption-cap and the kill-switch

### Automation Opportunity
- Liability auto-computed from issued-vs-redeemed; budget-burn auto-tracked

### Controls
- CTL (coupon-liability accuracy — links to VS-157); CTL-44 (issued-count integrity); operational: CFO monthly sign-off

### Time Estimate
~3 hours/campaign + ~3 hours/month = **~90 hours/year** Finance Manager.

- Multiplier auto-configured; liability-impact auto-computed; incremental-basket auto-measured

- CTL-403 (ensure controlled execution — Coupon Voucher Creation (PA-58.1).)

## W2244. Coupon Fraud Prevention Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Every coupon design before launch |
| **Frequency** | ~18/year |
| **Volume** | ~18 designs reviewed |
| **Owner** | Loss Prevention Analyst |
| **Participants** | LP Analyst, Loss Prevention Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LP Analyst reviews each coupon design for fraud vectors: unique-code (not generic), per-customer-limit, expiry, channel-binding, stacking-cap; recommends design changes before launch | LP Analyst | Loss Prevention Mgr | 1–2 hours/design |

### System Touchpoints
- Coupon platform (links to VS-58 / W2238, VS-125 / W266 fraud) — the fraud-prevention config
- Fraud monitoring (links to VS-58 / W2249 redemption fraud) — the post-launch detection
- LP (links to VS-23 / W248) — the LP oversight

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Generic-code-fraud risk**: a generic coupon code is shared virally beyond the intended audience; mitigated by the unique-code-per-customer design
- **Stacking-abuse risk**: multiple coupons stacked to near-free transactions; mitigated by the stacking-cap and the margin-floor guard

### Automation Opportunity
- Fraud-design checklist auto-applied per coupon; design-issues auto-flagged before launch

### Controls
- CTL-196 (prevent coupon & promo-code abuse.)
- CTL (coupon-fraud-prevention — links to VS-125); operational: Loss Prevention Manager pre-launch sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1.5 hours/design × 18 = **~27 hours/year** LP Analyst.

| 1 | Dashboard: distribution count, claim rate, redemption rate, ROI (incremental sales vs. coupon cost), channel split, segment response; daily during active period | System / Marketing Analyst | VP Marketing | 2–3 hours/campaign |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113, VS-58 / W2254 campaign ROI) — the performance dashboard
- Coupon platform (links to VS-58 / W2238) — the issued/claimed/redeemed data
- CRM (links to VS-13) — the segment response

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vanity-metric risk**: the dashboard shows distribution count (vanity) not redemption-derived ROI (substance); mitigated by the ROI-primary structure

### Automation Opportunity
- Dashboard auto-refreshed daily during the active period; underperformance auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-403 (ensure controlled execution — Coupon Voucher Creation (PA-58.1).)
- Operational: VP Marketing daily review during active campaigns

### Time Estimate
~2.5 hours/campaign × 18 = **~45 hours/year** Marketing Analyst.
