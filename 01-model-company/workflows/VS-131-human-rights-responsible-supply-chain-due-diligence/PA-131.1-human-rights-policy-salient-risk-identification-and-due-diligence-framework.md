## W4001. Human Rights & Responsible Sourcing Policy, Commitments & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program stand-up; annual review; regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise program (5 entities, ~800–1,000 vendors) |
| **Owner** | Chief Sustainability Officer / VP Legal |
| **Participants** | Sustainability (links to VS-25), Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34/VS-122), Legal (links to VS-100), HR (links to VS-19/VS-103), Compliance (links to VS-119), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Board (links to VS-36), Global Sourcing (links to VS-122) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CSO/VP-Legal define the human-rights & responsible-sourcing policy aligned to the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights (UNGPs), ILO core conventions, the UN Global Compact, and Philippine labor law (links to VS-84/VS-19); cover own operations and the full supply chain; set commitments (zero forced/child labor, living-wage direction, freedom of association, responsible recruitment, no land-rights harm) | CSO / VP Legal / Sustainability | CEO / Board | Annual |
| 2 | CSO establish governance: a cross-functional responsible-sourcing steering (Procurement, Merchandising, Legal, HR, Compliance, Global Sourcing), decision rights, the integration with vendor management (VS-03), ESG (VS-25), ethics/speak-up (VS-119), and audit (VS-21), and KPIs | CSO / Procurement / Legal | CEO | Annual |
| 3 | CEO/Board ratify the policy, approve the budget/resources, and cascade as a board-level enterprise risk (links to VS-21.2 ERM and VS-36 governance); publish externally and to suppliers | CEO / Board | Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ESG (links to VS-25); procurement/vendor (links to VS-03/VS-34); legal (links to VS-100); HR (links to VS-19/VS-103); governance (links to VS-36); global sourcing (links to VS-122)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-owner risk**: Human rights unowned beyond ESG-reporting mention; mitigated by dedicated program
- **Policy-without-execution risk**: Commitments not operationalized; mitigated by HRDD framework (W4003)


### Automation Opportunity
- System continuous audit of "audit" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-capture of "enterprise risk" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-73 (ensure human-rights supply-chain due diligence.)
- operational: dedicated program; operational: HRDD framework (W4003)

### Time Estimate
- 3–4 weeks annual

## W4002. Salient Human Rights Risk Identification & Materiality Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual; new category/geography; major event |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | ~13 categories × sourcing geographies |
| **Owner** | CSO / Sustainability |
| **Participants** | Sustainability, Procurement/Global Sourcing (links to VS-122/VS-03), Insights (links to VS-44), Legal, HR, external human-rights advisors, stakeholders |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability/Procurement identify salient human-rights risks across BuildRight's operations and supply chain using the UNGP saliency lens (scale, scope, irremediability): forced/modern-slavery labor, child labor, unsafe conditions, wage/hour violations, discrimination, freedom-of-association limits, migrant-labor abuse, and community/land-rights impacts — mapped by category (tools/electronics, paint/chemicals, lumber, tiles, PPE, garden/agro) and sourcing geography | Sustainability / Procurement / Global Sourcing | CSO | Annual |
| 2 | Sustainability prioritize salient risks and the supply-chain tiers/geographies/categories where they concentrate (tier-1 vendors, tier-2 component/material suppliers, raw-material origin), and integrate findings into ESG materiality (links to VS-25.2) and the ERM risk register (links to VS-21.2) | Sustainability / Insights / Legal | CSO | Annual |
| 3 | Sustainability document the saliency assessment, retain evidence, and feed it into the HRDD framework (W4003), risk modeling (W4006), and reporting (links to PA-131.3) | Sustainability | CSO | Annual + on change |

### System Touchpoints
- ESG (links to VS-25.2); procurement/global sourcing (links to VS-03/VS-122); insights (links to VS-44); ERM (links to VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-scoping risk**: Saliency missing key risks (e.g., deeper-tier minerals); mitigated by UNGP lens and geography/category mapping
- **Static-assessment risk**: Saliency not refreshed; mitigated by annual cycle and event triggers


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-identify of "identify salient human-rights risks across BuildRight's operations and supply…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-register of "register" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document the saliency assessment, retain evidence, and feed it into the HRDD…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-73 (ensure human-rights supply-chain due diligence.)
- operational: UNGP lens and geography/category mapping; operational: annual cycle and event triggers

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual

## W4003. Human Rights Due Diligence (HRDD) Framework & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Policy (W4001); saliency (W4002) |
| **Frequency** | Maintained + continuous |
| **Volume** | Enterprise HRDD framework |
| **Owner** | CSO / VP Supply Chain |
| **Participants** | Sustainability, Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34/VS-122), Legal, Compliance (links to VS-119), Internal Audit (links to VS-21), HR, affected business units |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CSO/Procurement establish the HRDD framework per UNGP: identify and assess (W4009), act on impacts (prevent/mitigate/remediate — W4010–W4014), track effectiveness (W4019), and communicate (W4020/W4021); define risk-based intensity (enhanced DD for high-risk categories/geographies) | CSO / Procurement / Sustainability | CSO / VP Supply Chain | Maintained |
| 2 | CSO/Procurement embed HRDD into the procurement lifecycle: sourcing/onboarding gates (links to VS-03.1/VS-122.2), contract safeguards (W4005), PO and order allocation (links to VS-03.2), and supplier management (links to VS-67) — making human-rights DD a prerequisite to doing business | CSO / Procurement | VP Supply Chain | Maintained |
| 3 | CSO/Sustainability operate continuous HRDD: monitor risk intelligence, refresh saliency (W4002), and coordinate remediation/grievance (W4012/W4013) and responsible disengagement (W4014) | CSO / Sustainability | CSO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34/VS-122); vendor mgmt (links to VS-67); audit (links to VS-21); ESG (links to VS-25); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Bolt-on risk**: HRDD not embedded in procurement decisions; mitigated by gates and contract safeguards
- **Inconsistent-intensity risk**: Same DD for low- and high-risk suppliers; mitigated by risk-based tiering


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-track of "track effectiveness" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-monitor of "monitor risk intelligence, refresh saliency" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-623 (ensure controlled execution — Human Rights Policy, Salient-Risk Identification & Due Diligence Framework (PA-131.1).)
- operational: gates and contract safeguards; operational: risk-based tiering

### Time Estimate
- Maintained + continuous
