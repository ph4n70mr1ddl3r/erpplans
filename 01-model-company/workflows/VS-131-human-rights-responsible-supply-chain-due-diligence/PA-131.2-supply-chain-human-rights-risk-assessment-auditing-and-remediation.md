## W4009. Supplier Human Rights Risk Assessment & Tiering

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Onboarding; periodic; risk-model update (W4006) |
| **Frequency** | Per supplier + periodic refresh |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 vendors |
| **Owner** | VP Procurement / CSO |
| **Participants** | Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-122), Sustainability, Global Sourcing (links to VS-122), Legal, third-party risk-data providers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement/Sustainability conduct the supplier human-rights risk assessment using the model (W4006), geography/category risk, questionnaire (self-assessment), certifications, media/NGO/sanctions screening (links to VS-86), and prior-audit history | Procurement / Sustainability | VP Procurement | Per supplier |
| 2 | Procurement/Sustainability tier each supplier (low / enhanced / high) and define the DD intensity: low = self-assessment + code; enhanced = audit/capability; high = enhanced audit + traceability + remediation tracking | Procurement / Sustainability | VP Procurement | Per supplier |
| 3 | Procurement integrate tiering into vendor master (links to VS-29) and scorecard (links to VS-67), trigger audits (W4010), and refresh on cycle or event | Procurement / Sustainability | VP Procurement | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/vendor (links to VS-03/VS-122/VS-67); MDM (links to VS-29); ABC/sanctions (links to VS-86); ESG (links to VS-25); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Tiering-error risk**: High-risk supplier mis-tiered low; mitigated by multi-input assessment and refresh
- **Questionnaire-only risk**: Self-assessment gaming; mitigated by audit of high-risk suppliers


### Automation Opportunity
- System continuous audit of "audit history" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-track of "tracking" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
operational: multi-input assessment and refresh; operational: audit of high-risk suppliers

### Time Estimate
- Per supplier + periodic

## W4011. On-Site Assessment, Worker Voice & Grievance Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | High-risk audit; worker-grievance signal; direct assessment need |
| **Frequency** | Per high-risk supplier + on signal |
| **Volume** | High-risk vendors/factories |
| **Owner** | CSO / Sustainability |
| **Participants** | Sustainability, Procurement, external assessors, worker-voice technology providers, local stakeholders |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability/external-assessors conduct on-site assessment beyond paperwork: worker interviews (confidential, multiple shifts), document verification (payroll, age/ID, hours), and physical inspection (safety, dormitories where relevant) | Sustainability / assessors | CSO | Per high-risk supplier |
| 2 | Sustainability deploy/leverage worker-voice channels (anonymous surveys, hotlines, technology tools) to verify audit findings and surface hidden issues (forced-labor indicators, harassment, wage/hour violations) | Sustainability / worker-voice provider | CSO | Per supplier + ongoing |
| 3 | Sustainability cross-verify audit vs worker-voice vs documents, investigate discrepancies, and feed verified findings into corrective action (W4012) and grievance remediation (W4013) | Sustainability | CSO | Per assessment |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-122); ESG (links to VS-25); ethics/speak-up (links to VS-119); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Document-only risk**: Audits verifying documents not reality; mitigated by worker voice and on-site verification
- **Retaliation risk**: Workers punished for speaking up; mitigated by confidentiality and retaliation monitoring


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "document verification" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "logy tools" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "verify audit vs worker-voice vs documents, investigate discrepancies, and feed…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: worker voice and on-site verification; operational: confidentiality and retaliation monitoring

### Time Estimate
- Per high-risk supplier

## W4012. Non-Conformance, Corrective Action & Remediation Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Audit/assessment finding; grievance (W4013) |
| **Frequency** | Per finding |
| **Volume** | Findings from audits/assessments |
| **Owner** | CSO / VP Procurement |
| **Participants** | Sustainability, Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-67/VS-122), affected vendor, Compliance, Legal (links to VS-100), affected workers/community |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability issue corrective-action plans (CAPs) per finding with root cause, required remediation (e.g., back-pay, reimbursement of recruitment fees, safety fixes, age-verification systems), owner, and deadline; require supplier commitment | Sustainability / Procurement | CSO | Per finding |
| 2 | Sustainability/Procurement track CAP closure, verify remediation (re-payment evidence, re-inspection, worker confirmation), and address systemic causes (management systems); escalate critical/major findings | Sustainability / Procurement | CSO / VP Procurement | Per finding |
| 3 | Sustainability provide/leverage remedy for affected workers/community, document remediation outcomes, and feed into sourcing decisions (W4014), reporting (W4020), and continuous improvement (W4019) | Sustainability / Procurement / Legal | CSO | Per finding |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/vendor (links to VS-03/VS-67/VS-122); legal (links to VS-100); ESG (links to VS-25); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Non-closure risk**: CAPs open indefinitely; mitigated by tracking and sourcing consequences (W4014)
- **Incomplete-remedy risk**: Workers not made whole; mitigated by verification and worker confirmation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-issue of "issue corrective-action plans" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "verify remediation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document remediation outcomes, and feed into sourcing decisions" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: tracking and sourcing consequences (W4014); operational: verification and worker confirmation

### Time Estimate
- Per finding (weeks to months)

## W4014. Sourcing Decision, Pause & Exit (Responsible Disengagement) Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Uncorrected critical finding; unremediated abuse; sourcing decision |
| **Frequency** | On event |
| **Volume** | High-risk suppliers as needed |
| **Owner** | VP Procurement / CSO |
| **Participants** | Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-122/VS-67), Sustainability, Merchandising (links to VS-01), Legal (links to VS-100), Supply Planning (links to VS-02), Compliance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement/Sustainability integrate human-rights performance into sourcing decisions: prefer compliant/improving suppliers; pause new orders for unremediated critical findings; and weigh responsible disengagement as a last resort per UNGP (leverage, not reflexive exit) | Procurement / Sustainability / Merchandising | VP Procurement | Per decision |
| 2 | Procurement/Sustainability manage responsible exit where required: phased disengagement, mitigate harm to workers (avoid sudden mass job loss), source alternatives (links to VS-122/VS-03), and protect supply continuity (links to VS-02) | Procurement / Supply Planning / Sustainability | VP Procurement | Per event |
| 3 | Procurement/Sustainability document the decision basis (links to VS-88), report to governance/Board (links to VS-36/W4017), and apply lessons to risk model (W4006) and sourcing strategy (links to VS-122) | Procurement / Sustainability / Legal | VP Procurement | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/supply planning (links to VS-03/VS-02/VS-122/VS-67); merchandising (links to VS-01); legal (links to VS-100); governance (links to VS-36); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reflexive-exit risk**: Disengagement harming workers more than helping; mitigated by UNGP leverage-first principle
- **Supply-shock risk**: Exit disrupting supply; mitigated by phased approach and alternatives


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "document the decision basis" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: UNGP leverage-first principle; operational: phased approach and alternatives

### Time Estimate
- Per event
