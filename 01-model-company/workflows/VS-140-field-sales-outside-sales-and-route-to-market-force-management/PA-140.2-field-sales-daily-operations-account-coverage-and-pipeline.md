## W4225. Daily Route & Call Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily sales cycle; coverage plan (W4220) |
| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | Field reps × daily routes (visits/day) |
| **Owner** | Field Sales Rep / Sales Operations |
| **Participants** | Regional Mgrs, Trade Sales (VS-11), Logistics (route — VS-06) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rep/Sales Ops plan the daily route from the coverage plan (W4220), prioritizing high-value/at-risk/active-opportunity accounts, and scheduling visits within travel/time capacity | Field Sales Rep / Sales Ops | Regional Mgr | Daily |
| 2 | Rep optimize the route for travel efficiency (geography, traffic, cluster), align with appointments, and prepare per-account briefs (history, open opps, last visit — from CRM VS-11/CDP VS-126) | Field Sales Rep | Regional Mgr | Daily |
| 3 | Rep execute visits, log activity/next steps in mobile CRM (W4224), and report daily activity to the manager | Field Sales Rep | Regional Mgr | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile CRM/route (VS-11/W4224); CDP/account brief (VS-126); coverage plan (W4220)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inefficient-route risk**: Poor routing wastes field time/travel cost; mitigated by route optimization
- **Coverage-skew risk**: Reps visit easy accounts, neglect strategic; mitigated by priority-based planning + adherence (W4232)

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min/day planning per rep; field time the rest

### Automation Opportunity
- Route-optimization app; priority-aware daily-plan suggestions; auto-activity logging

### Controls
- CTL-90 (ensure field-sales conduct & pipeline integrity.)
- CTL: daily route from coverage plan; activity logged in CRM; adherence monitored

### Cross-references
- links to VS-11/VS-126 (CRM/CDP), W4220 (coverage), W4224 (tooling), VS-06 (route optimization)

## W4226. Customer Visit, Needs Analysis & Relationship Management

|---|---|

| **Owner** | Field Sales Rep |
| **Participants** | Account contact (contractor/developer/architect/buyer), Trade-Pro (VS-43), Trade Sales (VS-11) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rep conduct the visit — relationship-building, needs discovery (upcoming projects, specifications, pain points), and consultative advice (links to VS-124 selling) | Field Sales Rep | Regional Mgr | Per visit |

### Time Estimate

### Controls
