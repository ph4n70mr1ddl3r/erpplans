## W4217. Field Sales Strategy & Go-to-Coverage Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | B2B-growth strategy; coverage review; competitive pressure |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on strategy change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise field-sales strategy (~40% B2B revenue; ~5,000 trade + ~200 corporate accounts) |
| **Owner** | Head of Field Sales / Sales Director / Commercial Leadership |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales (VS-11), Key Accounts (VS-107), Trade-Pro (VS-43), Strategy (VS-33), Merchandising (VS-01) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Field Sales define the go-to-coverage model — which B2B segments/projects are covered by field reps (high-value/strategic) vs digital/self-service (VS-10/VS-11 portal) vs store (VS-07) — aligned to B2B strategy (VS-33) | Head of Field Sales / Strategy | Commercial Director / COO | Annual |
| 2 | Field Sales define the field-sales value proposition and selling motion (consultative/spec selling, project pursuit, account farming vs new-logo hunting), and the segment focus (trade, corporate, B2G links to VS-46) | Head of Field Sales / Trade Sales / Key Accounts | Commercial Director | Annual |
| 3 | Field Sales document the strategy, obtain approval, resource it (W4218), and cascade to territory/account/coverage plans (W4219–W4221) | Head of Field Sales / Finance | Commercial Director / COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy (VS-33); B2B/portal (VS-11/VS-10); key accounts (VS-107); trade-pro (VS-43); B2G (VS-46); merchandising (VS-01)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Coverage-gap risk**: High-value accounts uncovered; mitigated by segment-based coverage model
- **Channel-conflict risk**: Field vs digital vs store overlap on the same account; mitigated by clear channel assignment (W4222)

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual strategy

### Automation Opportunity
- Coverage-model scenario planning; segment-value analytics

### Controls
- CTL: field-sales strategy approved; aligned to B2B strategy (VS-33); channel conflict managed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-11/VS-10 (B2B/digital), VS-107 (key accounts), VS-46 (B2G), W4222 (channel assignment)

## W4218. Force Sizing, Structure & Role Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4217); growth; cost review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Field-sales headcount sized to ~5,200 B2B accounts + prospects |
| **Owner** | Head of Field Sales / HR (VS-19) / Workforce Planning (VS-103.3) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19), Finance (VS-17.4), Workforce Planning (VS-103.3), Comp (VS-102) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Field Sales/HR size the force (workload-based: accounts × call-frequency × call-duration; capacity model), and structure roles (outside sales rep, trade-account exec, project/spec specialist, sales engineer) | Head of Field Sales / HR / Workforce Planning | Commercial Director / CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | Field Sales/HR design role profiles, spans of control, reporting lines, and specialist roles (e.g., solar/renewable spec links to VS-70, electrical/plumbing spec), aligned to coverage (W4220) | Head of Field Sales / HR | Commercial Director | Annual |
| 3 | Field Sales/Finance build the force business case (cost vs incremental B2B revenue), obtain approval, and sequence hiring with recruitment (VS-121/VS-19.1) | Head of Field Sales / Finance | Commercial Director / CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Workforce planning (VS-103.3); HR (VS-19); compensation (VS-102); recruitment (VS-121/VS-19.1); finance (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-sizing risk**: Too few reps → uncovered accounts/low call-frequency; mitigated by workload-based sizing
- **Over-sizing risk**: Too many reps → cost without return; mitigated by business-case discipline

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks annual sizing/structure

### Automation Opportunity
- Workload-based force-sizing models; span-of-control analytics

### Controls
- CTL: force sizing workload-justified; business case approved (Finance/COO)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103.3 (workforce planning), VS-19/VS-121 (HR/recruitment), VS-102 (comp), VS-17.4 (finance), VS-70 (specialist spec)

## W4219. Territory Design, Alignment & Balancing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Force structure (W4218); account growth; rebalance cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on major change |
| **Volume** | Territories across the Philippine archipelago (Mindanao/Visayas/Luzon/NCR) |
| **Owner** | Head of Field Sales / Sales Operations |
| **Participants** | Regional Sales Managers, Key Accounts (VS-107), Trade-Pro (VS-43), Analytics (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Ops/Analytics design territories balancing workload (account count, revenue potential, geography/travel, project density) via territory-modeling, aligned to store/DC regions (VS-07/VS-136) | Sales Ops / Analytics | Head of Field Sales | Annual |
| 2 | Sales Ops assign accounts to territories/reps, handle overlaps (multi-territory corporate accounts links to VS-107), and document territory boundaries | Sales Ops / Regional Mgrs | Head of Field Sales | Annual |
| 3 | Sales Ops rebalance on growth/reorg, manage territory-change communications/disputes, and monitor territory equity (W4232) | Sales Ops / Regional Mgrs | Head of Field Sales | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Territory-modeling tool; analytics (VS-28); CRM/account assignment (VS-11/VS-107); network regions (VS-136)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Imbalance risk**: Some territories overloaded, others light; mitigated by workload-balanced design + equity monitoring
- **Orphan-account risk**: Accounts unassigned; mitigated by complete assignment + audits

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks annual design; ongoing rebalance

### Automation Opportunity
- Territory-modeling/optimization; account-auto-assignment; equity dashboards

### Controls
- CTL: territories workload-balanced; assignments complete & audited; disputes managed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-28 (analytics), VS-11/VS-107 (CRM/accounts), VS-136 (regions), VS-07 (store regions), W4232 (analytics)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

### Time Estimate
- ~2 weeks annual + ongoing refresh

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL: segmentation potential-based; coverage plan documented; adherence monitored

### Cross-references

## W4221. Quota, Target Setting & Capacity Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual planning; territory/account changes |
| **Frequency** | Annual + quarterly adjustment |
| **Volume** | Quotas per field rep × territory × product line |
| **Owner** | Sales Operations / Finance (VS-17.4) / Comp (VS-102) |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17.4), Comp (VS-102), Regional Mgrs, Trade Sales (VS-11) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Ops/Finance set territory/rep quotas from the B2B revenue plan (VS-33/VS-101 MFP), account potential (W4220), and historical performance, with bottom-up validation by regional managers | Sales Ops / Finance / Regional Mgrs | Commercial Director / CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Sales Ops/Comp design the incentive link (quota attainment → comp, links to VS-102.3 variable pay), test for fairness/attainability, and align to capacity (W4218) | Sales Ops / Comp | Commercial Director | Annual |
| 3 | Sales Ops cascade quotas to reps, manage quarterly adjustments (market shift, territory change), and track attainment (W4233) | Sales Ops / Regional Mgrs | Head of Field Sales | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance/revenue plan (VS-17.4/VS-101); comp (VS-102.3); CRM attainment (VS-11); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Sandbagging/stretch risk**: Quotas too easy/impossible; mitigated by bottom-up validation + potential-based
- **Mis-incent risk**: Quota drives wrong behavior (e.g., discount to hit volume); mitigated by margin-aware incentive design

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks annual quota-setting

### Automation Opportunity
- Quota-modeling/scenario tools; attainment-tracking automation; fairness analytics

### Controls
- CTL: quotas validated bottom-up; incentive fairness-tested; margin-aware

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.4/VS-101 (revenue plan), VS-102.3 (comp/incentive), VS-33 (strategy), VS-11 (CRM), W4233 (attainment)

## W4222. Route-to-Market & Channel Assignment (Field vs Digital vs Store)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Coverage model (W4217); channel strategy; account need |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 B2B accounts × channel assignment |
| **Owner** | Head of Field Sales / Commercial Operations |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales (VS-11), Digital (VS-10), Store Ops (VS-07), Key Accounts (VS-107) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Field Sales/Commercial define the route-to-market per segment (field rep, digital/self-service portal VS-11, store VS-07, phone/inside-sales, partner), and the channel-assignment rules | Head of Field Sales / Commercial Ops | Commercial Director | Annual |
| 2 | Field Sales define channel-interaction rules (who leads on a multi-channel account, lead handoff field↔digital↔store), and prevent conflict (links to VS-126 single customer view) | Head of Field Sales / Trade Sales / Digital | Commercial Director | Annual |
| 3 | Field Sales assign accounts to RTM, monitor channel mix/effectiveness (W4232), and reallocate as behavior shifts (e.g., digital-ready accounts moved to portal) | Sales Ops / Commercial Ops | Head of Field Sales | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- B2B portal (VS-11); digital (VS-10); store (VS-07); CDP/single-customer-view (VS-126); CRM (VS-11)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Channel-conflict risk**: Field and digital/store compete for the same sale/credit; mitigated by interaction rules + single-customer-view
- **Mis-assignment risk**: Low-value accounts burning field time; mitigated by segment-based RTM

### Time Estimate
- ~2 weeks annual RTM design; ongoing reallocation

### Automation Opportunity
- Channel-mix analytics; digital-readiness scoring (VS-126); auto-reassignment rules

### Controls
- CTL: channel-interaction rules prevent conflict; accounts RTM-assigned by segment; single-customer-view (VS-126)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-11 (B2B/portal), VS-10 (digital), VS-07 (store), VS-126 (single customer view), W4220 (segmentation), W4232 (analytics)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

| 2 | Enablement/Merchandising run ongoing enablement (new products VS-01.1, spec/technical updates, competitive intel VS-33.3, selling skills VS-124), and certify reps on categories/specialties | Field Sales Enablement Lead / Merchandising | Head of Field Sales | Continuous |

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 weeks onboarding/hire; ongoing enablement ~0.5–1 FTE

### Controls
- CTL: onboarding structured; reps certified before independent coverage; ABC trained (VS-86.3)

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per lifecycle + continuous operation |
| **Volume** | Field-sales tooling for the whole force (mobile CRM, route, quoting, content) |
| **Owner** | Field Sales Ops / Sales Technology / IT (VS-27) / EA (VS-113) |
| **Participants** | IT (VS-27), EA (VS-113), InfoSec (VS-27.3), Digital (VS-10), TBM (VS-135) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Tech/EA/IT define the field-sales tooling stack (mobile CRM, route/call planning, estimating/quoting, product/content access VS-137, order entry VS-11), and the mobile/offline requirements | Sales Tech / EA / IT | CIO / Head of Field Sales | Per lifecycle |
| 2 | Sales Tech/IT deploy and integrate the stack (CRM↔ERP↔CDP↔PIM, mobile/offline, security VS-27.3), and train reps (W4223) | Sales Tech / IT / Enablement | Sales Tech | Per deployment |
| 3 | Sales Tech/IT govern the platform lifecycle (roadmap, adoption, cost via VS-135 TBM, data quality), and optimize based on rep feedback/analytics (W4232) | Sales Tech / IT / EA | CIO / Head of Field Sales | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile CRM (VS-11); CDP (VS-126); PIM/content (VS-137); ERP/order (VS-11/VS-16); security (VS-27.3); TBM (VS-135); EA (VS-113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Adoption risk**: Reps don't use CRM/tools → poor data; mitigated by usability, training, and workflow-embedded use
- **Offline/reliability risk**: Field reps lose connectivity; mitigated by mobile/offline capability

### Time Estimate
- Per deployment weeks; continuous operation/governance

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile CRM with offline sync; auto route/call planning; in-flow quoting/order entry

### Controls
- CTL: tooling security/privacy assessed (VS-27.3/VS-91); adoption monitored; cost governed (VS-135)

### Cross-references
