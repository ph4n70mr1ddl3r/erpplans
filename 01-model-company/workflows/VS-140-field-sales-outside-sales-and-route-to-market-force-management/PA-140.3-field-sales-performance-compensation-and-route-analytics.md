## W4233. Field Sales Performance & Quota Attainment Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly cycle; attainment review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Force-wide attainment vs quotas (W4221) |
| **Owner** | Sales Operations / Head of Field Sales |
| **Participants** | Regional Mgrs, Finance (VS-17.4), Comp (VS-102), Analytics (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Ops measure rep/territory/team attainment vs quota (W4221), and pipeline coverage vs target, with margin/revenue/mix views | Sales Ops / Analytics | Head of Field Sales | Monthly |
| 2 | Regional Mgr review attainment with reps, identify high/low performers, and intervene (coaching W4238, reallocation, PIP) | Regional Mgrs | Head of Field Sales | Monthly |
| 3 | Sales Ops report attainment/performance to leadership, feed comp (W4234) and workforce planning (W4239), and adjust forecasts (W4228) | Sales Ops / Finance | Head of Trade / Account Management | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM attainment (VS-11); analytics (VS-28); finance (VS-17.4); comp (VS-102); quota (W4221)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Gaming risk**: Reps manipulate timing/credits to hit quota; mitigated by clean-attainment rules + audit
- **Late-intervention risk**: Underperformance addressed too late; mitigated by monthly review + PIP

### Automation Opportunity
- Real-time attainment dashboards; performance-alert automation; PIP tracking

### Controls
- CTL-652 (ensure controlled execution — Field Sales Performance, Compensation & Route Analytics (PA-140.3).)
- CTL-652: attainment rules clean & audited; underperformance managed (PIP)

### Time Estimate
- Monthly ~1–2 days; quarterly deeper review

### Cross-references
- links to W4221 (quota), W4228 (pipeline), VS-102 (comp), VS-17.4 (finance), VS-28 (analytics), W4238 (coaching)

## W4234. Field Sales Compensation & Incentive Administration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Monthly/quarterly pay + annual plan |
| **Volume** | Force-wide variable comp/commission |
| **Owner** | Compensation (VS-102) / Sales Comp Admin / Payroll (VS-19.2) |
| **Participants** | Comp (VS-102.3), Payroll (VS-19.2), Finance (VS-17.4), Sales Ops, HR (VS-19) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Comp/Sales Ops compute commission/incentive from attainment (W4233) per the plan (W4221/VS-102.3), with splits/credits, caps, and margin adjustments | Sales Comp Admin / Comp | Head of Field Sales / Comp | Per cycle |

### Automation Opportunity
- Comp-calc automation from CRM attainment; rep self-service statements; plan-modeling tools

### Controls
- CTL-652 (ensure controlled execution — Field Sales Performance, Compensation & Route Analytics (PA-140.3).)
- CTL-652: comp validated & transparent; plan margin-aware & reviewed annually (VS-102)

### Time Estimate
- Per cycle ~2–4 days; annual plan review weeks

### Cross-references
- links to VS-102.3 (variable pay), VS-19.2 (payroll), VS-11 (attainment), VS-17.4 (finance), W4221 (quota), W4233 (performance)

- CTL-90 (ensure field-sales conduct & pipeline integrity.)
- CTL-652: coverage adherence monitored; selling-time optimized (admin-reduction VS-133)
