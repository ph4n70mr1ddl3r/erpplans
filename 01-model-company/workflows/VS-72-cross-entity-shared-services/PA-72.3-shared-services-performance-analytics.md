## W2590. Shared Services Efficiency & Cost-per-Transaction Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly analytics cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~15 shared service categories |
| **Owner** | Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Analyst, Finance Controller, COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Analyst calculates cost-per-transaction for each shared service: AP cost per invoice processed (target: PHP 150–200), AR cost per invoice, payroll cost per payslip, IT cost per user, procurement cost per PO; compares against industry benchmarks | Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Analyst identifies efficiency improvement opportunities: automation potential (AP OCR, payroll automation), volume consolidation across entities, shared service center staffing optimization, technology upgrade ROI | Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1 day |
| 3 | Finance Controller presents efficiency analytics to COO and CFO: cost trend by shared service, benchmark comparison, improvement recommendations with projected savings; proposes annual efficiency targets | Finance Controller | CFO | 1 day/quarter |
