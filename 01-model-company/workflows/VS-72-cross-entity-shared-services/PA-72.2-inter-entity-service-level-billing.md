## W2582. Intercompany Service Agreement Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual review or new service establishment |
| **Frequency** | Annual; event-driven |
| **Volume** | 5 entities; ~20 IC service agreements |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, Legal, Entity CFOs |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Controller maintains intercompany service agreement registry: warehousing services (Depot Inc. → Logistics Inc.), ecommerce platform services (Digital Commerce Inc. → Depot Inc.), property leasing (Property Inc. → Depot Inc.), management services (Holdings → all subsidiaries) | Finance Controller | CFO | Ongoing |
| 2 | Legal reviews each agreement annually: confirms scope and pricing reflect actual services, verifies compliance with transfer pricing regulations (BIR Revenue Regulations), updates for any service changes; CFO signs off on renewals | Legal / Finance Controller | CFO | 1–2 weeks/year |
| 3 | System stores IC service agreements with auto-renewal alerts; tracks billing milestones and payment terms; generates IC invoice templates per agreement; flags agreements approaching expiration for review | Finance / IT | Finance Controller | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract repository (links to VS-100 / W230, VS-88 / W255) — the IC agreement registry with expiry alerts
- IC billing (links to VS-17 / W14 intercompany, VS-72 / W2583) — the invoice templates per agreement
- Transfer-pricing documentation (links to VS-72 / W2594, VS-17 / W235) — the arm's-length basis

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Agreement-vs-actual-drift risk**: the agreement says one pricing methodology but the actual billing uses another (silent drift since the last renewal); mitigated by the annual Legal review comparing agreement to actual billing
- **Lapsed-agreement risk**: an agreement auto-renews past its intended term without the renegotiation that should have happened; mitigated by the expiry-alert in step 3 (not silent auto-renewal)

### Automation Opportunity
- Agreement registry with expiry-alert auto-triggering the renewal review; invoice-template auto-generated per agreement

### Controls
- CTL (transfer-pricing arm's-length — links to W235); operational: CFO sign-off on annual renewals

### Time Estimate
~Ongoing registry maintenance + ~1.5 weeks/year Legal review = **~80–120 hours/year** Finance Controller + Legal.

## W2583. Monthly IC Invoice Generation & Settlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly IC billing cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~20 IC invoices across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Analyst, Entity Accountants, Finance Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System auto-generates monthly IC invoices based on service agreements: warehouse fees (per-pallet + per-order), ecommerce platform fees (per-order), rent (fixed monthly per lease), management fees (allocated per methodology); Finance Analyst reviews and validates each invoice | System / Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1 day |
| 2 | Entity accountants verify IC invoices against internal records: Depot Inc. confirms warehouse usage, Digital Commerce confirms order count, Property Inc. confirms lease terms; disputes resolved within 5 business days | Entity Accountants | Entity CFOs | 2–3 days |
| 3 | System posts IC settlement: offsetting AP/AR entries between entities; net settlement for intercompany dividends and management fees; Finance Controller reviews net IC position; Treasury executes any required cash settlement between entity bank accounts | System / Treasury | Finance Controller | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- IC invoicing (links to VS-17 / W14, VS-72 / W2582) — the auto-generated invoices per agreement
- IC AP/AR netting (links to VS-17 / W14 IC settlement) — the offsetting entries
- Treasury (links to VS-18 / W30 cash management, VS-72 / W2586 cash settlement) — the net cash movement

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Volume-data-dispute risk**: the invoice is based on a volume (warehouse pallets, ecommerce orders) the receiving entity disputes; mitigated by the 5-business-day verification window and the operational-record cross-check in step 2
- **Unsettled-IC-balance risk**: IC balances accumulate without cash settlement, creating inter-entity debt that distorts entity financials; mitigated by the monthly net-settlement in step 3 and the W2586 aging monitor

### Automation Opportunity
- Invoices auto-generated from the agreement + operational volumes; net-settlement auto-computed; disputes auto-routed to the entity accountant worklist

### Controls
- CTL-44 (duplicate-IC-invoice guard); CTL (IC-settlement accuracy — links to VS-17 / W14); operational: Finance Controller net-position sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 days/month (generation + verification + settlement) = **~600 hours/year** Finance Analyst + Entity Accountants + Treasury.

## W2584. IC Pricing Dispute Resolution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Entity disputes IC service pricing |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; 3–5 per year |
| **Volume** | 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, Entity CFOs, CFO, External Advisor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receiving entity CFO disputes IC charge: documents basis for dispute (volume variance, service quality issue, pricing methodology concern); submits formal dispute to Finance Controller within 10 business days of invoice receipt | Entity CFO | CFO | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Finance Controller investigates: reviews allocation methodology, verifies data accuracy, benchmarks against external market rates; engages external transfer pricing advisor if methodology dispute; proposes resolution | Finance Controller / External Advisor | CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | CFO renders decision: adjusts pricing prospectively, issues credit note for disputed amount if warranted, updates service agreement if methodology change required; documents resolution for transfer pricing file | CFO | CEO | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Dispute/case management (links to VS-72 / W2585 audit findings pattern, VS-100 / W125 litigation pattern) — the dispute register
- TP documentation (links to VS-72 / W2594) — the dispute resolution added to the TP file
- Credit-note (links to VS-16 / W70 credit/debit note, VS-17 / W14) — the credit-note issuance

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Dispute-escalation risk**: an unresolved IC dispute festers and distorts the entity P&L for months; mitigated by the 10-business-day submission window and the CFO-decision timeline
- **TP-documentation-gap risk**: a dispute resolution changes the methodology but the TP file isn't updated, creating a BIR-audit exposure; mitigated by the documentation step in step 3

### Automation Opportunity
- Dispute auto-routed with the 10-day deadline; resolution auto-documented to the TP file

### Controls
- CTL-48 (dispute resolution — links to VS-39); CTL (TP arm's-length — links to W235); operational: CFO decision sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2.5 weeks/dispute × ~4/year = **~160–200 hours/year** Finance Controller + Entity CFOs.

## W2585. IC Billing Accuracy Audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly audit cycle |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~20 IC billing streams |
| **Owner** | Internal Auditor |
| **Participants** | Internal Auditor, Finance Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Internal Auditor samples IC invoices: verifies calculation accuracy (rate × volume = charge), confirms billing matches service agreement terms, tests volume data against operational records (warehouse pallets stored, ecommerce orders processed) | Internal Auditor | VP Internal Audit | 1 week |
| 2 | Auditor identifies discrepancies: over/under billing, incorrect rates applied, missing invoices for services rendered, late billing; quantifies financial impact; reports findings to Finance Controller and VP Internal Audit | Internal Auditor | VP Internal Audit | 3–5 days |
| 3 | Finance Controller implements corrections: issues adjusting entries, corrects billing templates, strengthens automated validation rules; prevents recurrence through system controls | Finance Controller | CFO | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Audit module (links to VS-21 / W120 audit planning, W348 e-commerce & omni-channel audit pattern applied IC-side) — the findings register
- IC billing (links to VS-72 / W2583) — the invoices audited
- Operational records (links to VS-04 WMS, VS-10 ecommerce) — the volume-data cross-check source

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Volume-data-unreconciled risk**: the billed volume (e.g., pallets) doesn't match the operational record (WMS pallet count), indicating a billing error; mitigated by the operational-record cross-check in step 1
- **Recurrence risk**: the same billing error recurs quarter after quarter because the root cause isn't fixed; mitigated by the system-control strengthening in step 3 (not just the one-time correction)

### Automation Opportunity
- Calculation auto-verified (rate × volume); volume-vs-operational-record auto-reconciled

### Controls
- CTL-338 (segregation of duties — auditor independent); CTL-44 (duplicate-invoice guard); operational: VP Internal Audit findings sign-off; Finance Controller remediation sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1.5 weeks/quarter audit + ~1 week remediation = **~180–240 hours/year** Internal Audit + Finance Controller.

## W2586. Intercompany Cash Settlement & Netting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly IC settlement cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | 5 entities; ~PHP 500M–1B in monthly IC flows |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury Mgr, Finance Controller, Entity CFOs |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury Manager compiles monthly IC net position per entity: aggregates all IC receivables and payables; calculates net settlement amount per entity pair; prepares netting statement | Treasury Manager | Finance Controller | 1 day |
| 2 | Finance Controller verifies netting statement against GL IC sub-ledgers; confirms elimination entries in consolidated GL; Treasury executes cash movements for net settlement positions | Finance Controller / Treasury | CFO | 1 day |
| 3 | Treasury ensures IC cash settlements are executed within 5 business days of month-end; monitors aging of IC balances; escalates overdue IC settlements; maintains IC cash settlement audit trail | Treasury Manager | Finance Controller | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- IC netting (links to VS-17 / W14 IC settlement, W234 consolidated profit elimination) — the net-position computation
- Treasury / cash (links to VS-18 / W30 cash management, W323 cash concentration & inter-entity pooling) — the cash movement and pooling
- GL reconciliation (links to VS-17 / W9A month-end close) — the netting-vs-GL verification

### Pain Points / Risks
- **IC-balance-aging risk**: IC balances age because cash settlement lags the billing, creating inter-entity debt and FX/interest distortion; mitigated by the 5-business-day settlement SLA and the aging escalation
- **Elimination-error risk**: the consolidated-GL elimination doesn't match the netting, leaving residual IC balances in consolidation; mitigated by the GL-vs-netting verification in step 2

### Automation Opportunity
- Net-position auto-computed from the IC sub-ledger; cash movement auto-initiated on Controller sign-off; aging auto-tracked

### Controls
- CTL (IC-settlement accuracy — links to VS-17 / W14); CTL (cash-movement authorization — links to VS-18 / W320 electronic-banking security); operational: Finance Controller netting sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2 days/month Treasury + Controller = **~240 hours/year** Treasury + Finance Controller.

## W2587. IC Service Level Performance Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly SLA review cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~20 IC service agreements |
| **Owner** | Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Analyst, Service Providers, Entity CFOs |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System collects SLA metrics: Logistics Inc. — warehouse throughput, order accuracy, delivery timeliness; Digital Commerce Inc. — platform uptime, order processing time; Property Inc. — facility response time, maintenance completion; generates SLA performance scorecards | System / Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Finance Analyst reviews SLA performance vs. contractual targets: identifies SLA breaches; calculates penalty credits per agreement terms (e.g., 2% credit for warehouse accuracy <99%); adjusts IC invoices for SLA penalties | Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1 day |
| 3 | Quarterly: Finance Controller convenes IC service review meeting with entity heads; discusses SLA trends, service improvement opportunities, pricing adjustments; documents decisions for transfer pricing file | Finance Controller / Entity Heads | CFO | Half day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- SLA monitoring (links to VS-04 / W586 DC KPI dashboard, VS-10 ecommerce, VS-138 facilities) — the SLA-metric source per service
- IC billing (links to VS-72 / W2583) — the penalty-credit adjustment
- TP documentation (links to VS-72 / W2594) — the SLA-driven pricing decisions documented

### Pain Points / Risks
- **SLA-without-consequence risk**: SLA breaches are measured but the penalty credit is never applied, eroding the SLA's teeth; mitigated by the auto-adjustment in step 2 and the quarterly review
- **Gaming-the-metric risk**: a service provider games the SLA metric (e.g., warehouse marks "complete" before actual completion) to avoid the penalty; mitigated by the operational-record cross-check (the W2585 audit pattern)

### Automation Opportunity
- SLA scorecard auto-generated from the operational systems; penalty-credit auto-computed on breach

### Controls
- CTL (SLA-penalty accuracy); operational: Finance Controller quarterly SLA-review sign-off

### Time Estimate
~3 days/month Analyst + ~half-day/quarter Controller review = **~280–340 hours/year**.

## W2588. New Entity IC Service Onboarding

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New entity establishment or entity restructuring |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; rare |
| **Volume** | 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, Legal, IT, Entity CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Controller establishes IC service framework for new/restructured entity: identifies required shared services, negotiates service scope and pricing with entity head, drafts intercompany service agreement with Legal | Finance Controller / Legal | CFO | 2–3 weeks |
| 2 | IT configures entity in ERP: creates entity GL, IC account pairs, cost center structure, billing templates; tests IC posting rules in sandbox; Finance Controller validates parallel run | IT / Finance Controller | CIO / CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Finance Controller activates IC billing for new entity; monitors first 3 months for accuracy; adjusts allocation methodology based on actual volumes; external advisor updates transfer pricing documentation | Finance Controller / External Advisor | CFO | 1–3 months |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP entity config (links to VS-29 / W288 financial master, VS-17 / W14) — the new entity GL and IC account pairs
- IC billing templates (links to VS-72 / W2582, W2583) — the new entity's billing setup
- TP documentation (links to VS-72 / W2594) — the new entity's IC flows added to the TP file

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Config-error risk**: the new entity's IC account pairs mis-map, causing cross-entity posting errors; mitigated by the sandbox test and parallel-run validation in step 2
- **TP-documentation-lag risk**: the new entity starts transacting before the TP documentation is updated, creating an undocumented-period exposure; mitigated by the documentation refresh concurrent with onboarding (not deferred)

### Automation Opportunity
- Entity-config template reused from existing entities; parallel-run auto-reconciled

### Controls
- CTL (transfer-pricing arm's-length — links to W235); operational: CFO + CIO sign-off on go-live after parallel-run validation

### Time Estimate
~5 weeks per onboarding (rare) = **~200 hours per event**, absorbed into the Finance Controller capacity when triggered.

## W2589. IC Service Agreement Renewal & Renegotiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual renewal cycle or service change trigger |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | ~20 agreements |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, Entity CFOs, Legal, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Controller prepares renewal analysis: actual vs. budgeted service volumes, cost trend per service, market rate benchmarking, SLA performance summary, transfer pricing compliance status; proposes renewal terms (maintain, adjust pricing, modify scope) | Finance Controller | CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | Entity CFOs review renewal proposals: negotiate pricing adjustments (typically ±5% annually based on cost changes), request scope modifications, raise service quality concerns; CFO mediates if agreement cannot be reached | Entity CFOs / Finance Controller | CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Legal updates executed agreements; Finance Controller updates ERP billing templates and allocation rules; communicates changes to entity accounting teams; documents in transfer pricing file for BIR audit readiness | Legal / Finance Controller / IT | CFO | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Agreement registry (links to VS-72 / W2582) — the renewal-cycle tracking
- Billing/allocation config (links to VS-72 / W2574, W2583) — the rate/methodology update on renewal
- TP documentation (links to VS-72 / W2594) — the renewal terms added to the TP file

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Renewal-without-negotiation risk**: agreements auto-renew without the ±5% renegotiation that should reflect cost changes; mitigated by the renewal-analysis in step 1 forcing an active decision
- **BIL-audit-readiness risk**: the renewal isn't documented in the TP file, leaving a gap for a BIR audit; mitigated by the documentation step in step 3

### Automation Opportunity
- Renewal-analysis auto-assembled from the volume/cost/SLA datasets; TP-file update auto-generated from the renewal

### Controls
- CTL (transfer-pricing arm's-length — links to W235); operational: CFO sign-off on renewal terms

### Time Estimate
~4 weeks annual renewal cycle = **~160–200 hours/year** Finance Controller + Legal + Entity CFOs.
