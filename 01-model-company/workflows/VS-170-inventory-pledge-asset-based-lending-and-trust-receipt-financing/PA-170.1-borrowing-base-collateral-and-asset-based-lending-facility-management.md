|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per origination/refinance (multi-year) + annual strategy review |
| **Volume** | 1–3 revolving/ABL facilities + import TR lines across the 5-entity group |
| **Owner** | Treasurer / ABL & Collateral Operations Manager (Treasury VS-18) |
| **Participants** | Treasury (VS-18), CFO/Finance (VS-17), Legal (VS-100), Procurement/Supply Chain (VS-02/VS-03), AR (VS-16), Inventory (VS-05) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the inventory/AR-pledge financing strategy: target facility size against ~PHP 37B inventory + ~PHP 5B+ trade/ecommerce AR, the own-vs-syndicated decision, the ABL revolver vs TR-import-line vs warehouse-receipt mix, the advance-rate/eligibility expectations, the cost/utilization target, and the bank-relationship concentration limits | Treasurer / ABL Manager / Finance | CFO | Per origination + annual |
| 2 | Structure the facility with the bank syndicate/Legal (VS-100.3): the borrowing-base definition, advance rates and reserves, eligibility/ineligibility criteria, reporting cadence, covenants (financial + reporting), events of default, cross-collateralization, the security documents (pledge/mortgage/trust-receipt framework under PD 115 + Civil Code), and the multi-entity (VS-72) guarantor structure | Treasurer / Legal / ABL Manager | CFO / Legal | Per origination |
| 3 | Govern the relationship post-close: the primary bank/agent relationship, annual review, the covenant calendar (W4944), RFP/refinance at term, and the integration with treasury operations (VS-18.1 W319 facility/covenant) and the SCF program (VS-105 supplier-side) | Treasurer / ABL Manager / Finance | CFO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/debt facility module (VS-18.1 W319); contract/legal repository (VS-100.3); inventory ledger (VS-05); AR ledger (VS-16); FP&A (VS-17.4); bank portal/agent platform

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Concentration risk**: Over-reliance on one lender/facility → refinancing/withdrawal exposure; mitigated by relationship-concentration limits and syndication
- **Structural-mismatch risk**: Advance rates/eligibility that don't match BuildRight's actual inventory/AR mix (e.g., slow-moving import pipeline excluded) → chronic under-availability; mitigated by data-driven structuring (W4938)
- **Document-defect risk**: Security/pledge documents defective → unenforceable collateral; mitigated by Legal (VS-100.3) review and perfection (W4939/W4954)

### Time Estimate
- ~10–16 weeks per origination/refinance; ~annual review 2–3 weeks; ~relationship governance continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Facility-term-to-ledger comparison; covenant-calendar auto-build; bank-portal integration for availability; borrowing-base-vs-actual variance alerts

### Controls
- CTL-116 (ensure borrowing-base & covenant compliance.)
- CTL: facility approved by board/CFO; security perfected; covenant calendar maintained; bank-relationship concentration tracked

## W4938. Borrowing-Base Model, Advance-Rate Matrix & Eligibility Criteria Definition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Facility close (W4937); covenant reset; inventory/AR profile shift (imports, private label, B2B) |
| **Frequency** | Per facility + on material profile change |
| **Volume** | 1 borrowing-base model per facility × advance-rate matrix (inventory category × AR bucket) |
| **Owner** | ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / Finance |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17.4), Treasury (VS-18), Inventory/Supply Chain (VS-05/VS-02), AR (VS-16), Data/Analytics (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the borrowing-base model per the credit agreement: eligible inventory ( Finished goods, raw/intermediate, import-in-transit treatment) × advance rate (e.g., 50–70% retail, lower for slow-moving/seasonal/SLOB); eligible AR (domestic trade, B2B, ecommerce) × advance rate (e.g., 80–90% current); less reserves (ineligibles, concentrations, floor); and the resulting availability formula | ABL Manager / Finance / Analytics | Treasurer / CFO | Per facility |
| 2 | Codify the eligibility/ineligibility criteria: eligible inventory excludes consignment/VMI (vendor-owned, VS-45), vendor-owned (W20), obsolete/SLOB (W220), damaged/quarantine (W91/W219), in-transit classification, and location eligibility (DC vs store vs in-transit); eligible AR excludes >90-day aged, related-party, COD-cash, disputed, foreign without LC/insurance, and concentration-over-limit | ABL Manager / Inventory / AR / Finance | Treasurer | Per facility |
| 3 | Configure the model in the treasury/ERP analytics (VS-28/VS-18): map inventory categories (VS-01 assortment), AR aging buckets (VS-16), location hierarchy (VS-29), and reserves; validate against historical availability; and document for the periodic certificate (W4939) | Analytics / ABL Manager / Finance | Treasurer | Per facility |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/borrowing-base module (VS-18/VS-28); inventory ledger & category master (VS-05/VS-01/VS-29); AR aging (VS-16); FP&A (VS-17.4); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-eligibility risk**: Ineligible inventory/AR included → over-advance and true-down (W4943); mitigated by precise criteria + automated testing (W4940/W4941)
- **Reserve-understatement risk**: Reserves too low → overstated availability → over-draw; mitigated by data-driven reserve modeling and bank alignment
- **Profile-drift risk**: BuildRight's shift to imports/private label/B2B changes eligibility but model isn't refreshed; mitigated by change-triggered refresh

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks initial modeling; ~1–2 weeks per refresh

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-eligibility testing from inventory/AR ledgers; advance-rate matrix engine; reserve auto-calc; availability-vs-history variance flagging

### Controls
- CTL-740 (ensure controlled execution — Borrowing-Base, Collateral & Asset-Based Lending Facility Management (PA-170.1).)
- CTL: model reconciles to credit agreement; eligibility criteria documented; advance rates bank-confirmed; reserve basis documented

### Cross-references
- links to VS-05/VS-29 (inventory/location), VS-16 (AR aging), VS-45 (consignment/VMI exclusion), W220 (SLOB), W91/W219 (quarantine), W4939 (certificate), W4940/W4941 (eligibility testing)

| **Owner** | ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / AR Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Test AR eligibility per the model (W4938): include domestic trade/corporate current AR, exclude >aging-limit (e.g., >90 days), related-party/intercompany (VS-72), COD-cash, disputed/credit-held (VS-16 W8), foreign without LC/insurance/credit-cover (VS-68), and government receivables beyond limit; net of allowances (VS-16 W81) and returns/reserves (VS-32) | AR Finance / Analytics / Credit | ABL Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | Apply concentration limits: per-account/concentration caps (e.g., top-account and single-industry limits), aggregate the eligible pool, and flag breaches for reserve/eligibility adjustment; reconcile to the AR aging (VS-16) and the trade-credit-risk view (VS-68) | AR Finance / Credit / Analytics | ABL Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | Reconcile to the AR subledger & remittance (VS-16): tie eligible AR to the GL, document ineligibles/reserves, integrate with collections (which reduce eligible AR — links W4942 availability), and feed the certificate (W4939) and field-exam pack (W4943) | AR Finance / Finance / Analytics | Controller / ABL Manager | Monthly |

- **Over-advance risk**: Aged/disputed/concentration AR included → overstated availability; mitigated by automated aging & concentration testing
- **Collection-cascade risk**: Collections reduce eligible AR faster than inventory rebuilds → availability whipsaw; mitigated by availability monitoring (W4942)
- **Credit-quality risk**: Eligible AR of deteriorated credit → true-down at field exam (W4943); mitigated by credit-cover (VS-68) and concentration limits

### Time Estimate

### Automation Opportunity

## W4942. Availability Calculation, Draws, Repayments & Excess-Availability Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily/weekly treasury cycle (VS-18); certificate (W4939); funding need; collection inflow |
| **Frequency** | Daily availability; draws/repayments event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~daily availability across 1–3 facilities; ~draws/repayments per working-capital cycle |
| **Owner** | Treasurer / Cash Manager (VS-18) / ABL Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury/Cash (VS-18), Finance (VS-17), Collections (VS-16), Procurement/AP (VS-15) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calculate availability daily from the latest certificate (W4939) + collection/AR roll-forward + inventory movements: borrowing base − outstanding loans/letters − reserves = available; track against utilization, excess-availability covenant thresholds, and the minimum-availability covenant | Cash Manager / ABL Manager / Treasury | Treasurer | Daily |
| 2 | Execute draws/repayments against availability: fund the cash need (vendor payments VS-15, import settlements W232/W239, payroll VS-19.2) via draw, and sweep excess cash (collections VS-16, COD VS-142) to repay; record against the facility and update availability | Cash Manager / Treasury / AP / Collections | Treasurer | Per event |
| 3 | Monitor & alert: alert on availability thresholds (excess-availability covenant, minimum-availability covenant, approaching cap), reconcile draws/repayments to the bank, and escalate constraint to the funding plan (VS-18.2/W233) and SCF (VS-105) | Treasury / ABL Manager / Finance | Treasurer | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/cash management (VS-18.2 W233 forecasting); bank portal/agent platform; AP (VS-15); AR/collections (VS-16); payroll (VS-19.2); COD (VS-142)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-draw risk**: Drawing above availability → immediate default and overdraft; mitigated by availability-first funding controls and automation
- **Covenant-breach risk**: Excess-availability or minimum-availability covenant breached; mitigated by threshold alerts and the funding plan (W233)
- **Float-inefficiency risk**: Idle excess cash not swept to repay → interest leakage; mitigated by auto-sweep

### Time Estimate
- ~daily availability 30–60 min (automated); ~draws/repayments per event 15–30 min

### Automation Opportunity
- Daily availability auto-calc; availability-first draw gate; auto-sweep of excess cash; threshold alerts; covenant-projection forecasting

### Controls
- CTL-116 (ensure borrowing-base & covenant compliance.)
- CTL: availability-first funding (no over-draw); daily availability reconciled to bank; covenant thresholds alerted; draws/repayments dual-approved

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18.2 (W233 cash forecast), VS-15 (AP), VS-16 (collections), VS-19.2 (payroll), W232/W239 (import settlement), VS-142 (COD), W4939 (certificate), W4944 (covenants)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Scheduled field exam (annual/semi-annual per agreement); bank-requested audit; availability event |
| **Frequency** | 1–2×/yr scheduled + event-driven |
| **Volume** | 1 field exam per facility per cycle × 1–3 facilities |
| **Owner** | ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / Finance / Internal Audit (VS-21) |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17), Treasury (VS-18), Inventory (VS-05), AR (VS-16), DC/Store Ops (VS-04/VS-07), Legal (VS-100), Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinate the field exam: scope with the bank's examiner (inventory test-count at sampled DCs/stores, AR confirmation, eligibility/ineligible testing, advance-rate application, reserves), prepare the data pack (W4940/W4941 reconciliations, certificates, GL ties), and schedule site visits (VS-04/VS-07) | ABL Manager / Finance / Inventory / AR | Treasurer / Controller | Per exam |
| 2 | Support the exam & respond: provide inventory/AR evidence, reconcile discrepancies, confirm AR (customer confirmations), and respond to findings; manage disputed ineligibles/true-downs with the bank | Finance / Inventory / AR / ABL Manager | Treasurer / Controller | Per exam |
| 3 | Manage true-downs & remediation: if the exam reduces the borrowing base (ineligibles, over-statement, shrink), record the true-down, manage availability impact (W4942), remediate root cause (data, process, controls), and document for audit (VS-21) and the certificate (W4939) | ABL Manager / Finance / Internal Audit | CFO / Treasurer | Per exam |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/borrowing-base module (VS-18); inventory (VS-05); AR (VS-16); GL (VS-17); document repository (VS-88); internal audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **True-down risk**: Exam reduces availability below operating need → liquidity crunch; mitigated by pre-exam self-testing and reserve buffer
- **Reconciliation-failure risk**: Inventory/AR cannot tie out → credibility loss and deeper exam; mitigated by ≥97% inventory accuracy and clean GL ties
- **Dispute risk**: Bank over-excludes eligible collateral; mitigated by documented eligibility and Legal (VS-100) engagement

### Time Estimate
- ~3–6 weeks per exam (prep + on-site + report + remediation)

### Automation Opportunity
- Pre-exam self-test (run the bank's methodology on own data); AR auto-confirmation; discrepancy auto-flag; true-down impact modeling

### Controls
- CTL-116 (ensure borrowing-base & covenant compliance.)
- CTL: pre-exam self-test passed; inventory ≥97% accurate; AR reconciled; true-downs remediated and audited (VS-21)
