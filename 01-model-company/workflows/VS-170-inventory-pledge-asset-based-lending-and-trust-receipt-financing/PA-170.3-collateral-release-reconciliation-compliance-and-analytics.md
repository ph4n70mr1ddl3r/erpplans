## W4953. Facility Paydown, Collateral Release & Exit/Refinance Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Facility maturity; refinance decision; excess-liquidity paydown; collateral release request |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven (maturity/refinance/paydown) |
| **Volume** | ~1–3 facilities × maturity/refinance events |
| **Owner** | Treasurer / ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / Legal |
| **Participants** | Treasury (VS-18), Finance (VS-17), Legal (VS-100), Inventory (VS-05), AR (VS-16) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role(A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manage the paydown/refinance/exit: at maturity or strategic refinance (W4937), plan the paydown (from cash/SCF/collections), the new facility transition, and the collateral release schedule; coordinate with the bank/agent and Legal (VS-100.3) on termination/release documentation | Treasurer / ABL Manager / Legal / Finance | CFO | Per event |
| 2 | Execute collateral release: on paydown/termination, release the inventory/AR pledge (W4951), the trust-receipt residual (W4948), and warehouse-receipt collateral (W4949); record the release of security interest, reclassify any residual goods-in-trust to owned, and update the encumbrance register/disclosure (W4955) | ABL Manager / Legal / Finance / Inventory | Treasurer / Legal | Per event |
| 3 | Close the facility & transition: settle final interest/charges (W4959), terminate covenants/reporting, archive the facility file, transition to the new facility (W4937) if refinancing, and report the exit to management/board (VS-36) | Treasury / Finance / Legal | CFO | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/debt facility (VS-18); security/pledge register (VS-100/VS-88); inventory status release (VS-05/VS-29); GL/notes (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transition-gap risk**: Collateral released before new facility in place → uncovered period; mitigated by sequenced transition planning
- **Residual-encumbrance risk**: Security interest not formally released → stale encumbrance/disclosure; mitigated by the release documentation and register update
- **Refinance-timing risk**: Maturity hit without refinancing in place → default; mitigated by the maturity calendar and early RFP

### Time Estimate
- ~4–8 weeks per refinance/exit

### Automation Opportunity
- Maturity calendar; release-of-security workflow; encumbrance-register auto-update; new-facility data carry-forward

### Controls
- CTL: paydown/refinance board-approved; security formally released; encumbrance register updated; transition sequenced

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury), VS-100 (legal/release), VS-05/VS-29 (inventory release), VS-17.4 (notes), W4937 (new facility), W4948/W4949/W4951 (collateral), W4955 (disclosure)

## W4954. Borrowing-Base to Inventory/AR Subledger Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Each borrowing-base cycle (W4939); close (VS-17.1); field exam (W4943) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly (+ event-driven) |
| **Volume** | ~reconciliations per facility per cycle × 1–3 facilities |
| **Owner** | ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / Finance / Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17), Inventory (VS-05), AR (VS-16), Treasury (VS-18), Data (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reconcile the borrowing base to the subledgers: tie eligible inventory (W4940) to the inventory subledger/GL, eligible AR (W4941) to the AR subledger, identify and explain variances (timing, ineligibles, reserves, shrink), and document the reconciliation | Finance / Inventory / AR / Analytics | Controller / ABL Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | Investigate & resolve variances: root-cause variances (data quality VS-29, posting timing, status errors), correct the data/process, and document for the certificate (W4939) and audit (W4943/W4958) | Finance / Inventory / AR / IT | Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Govern data quality for the borrowing base: the inventory accuracy target (≥97%), AR aging integrity, master-data quality (VS-29), and the controls that prevent recurrence; report to management and the bank | Finance / Data Governance / Analytics | Controller | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/borrowing-base (VS-18); inventory subledger/GL (VS-05/VS-17); AR subledger (VS-16); master data (VS-29); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Variance risk**: Borrowing base ≠ subledgers → certificate misstatement/over-advance; mitigated by monthly reconciliation and variance root-cause
- **Data-quality risk**: Poor inventory/AR data → chronic variances and exam true-downs; mitigated by the ≥97% accuracy target and master-data governance (VS-29)
- **Recurring-error risk**: Same variance recurs; mitigated by corrective controls

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 business days per monthly reconciliation (automated with exception handling)

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-reconciliation borrowing-base↔subledgers; variance auto-flag & categorize; master-data-quality feed (VS-29)

### Controls
- CTL: monthly reconciliation; variances investigated & resolved; ≥97% inventory accuracy; master-data governed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (GL), VS-05/VS-16 (inventory/AR subledgers), VS-29 (master data), W4939 (certificate), W4940/W4941 (eligibility), W4943/W4958 (audit)

## W4955. PFRS Encumbrance Disclosure, PAS 39 / Contingent-Liability Reporting & Note Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Period close (VS-17.1); external reporting; audit (VS-21) |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + annual |
| **Volume** | ~disclosure per facility × 1–3 facilities × 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Controller / ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / Finance |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17), Treasury (VS-18), Legal (VS-100), External Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare the PFRS encumbrance/borrowing disclosure: the carrying amount and classification of borrowings (current/non-current), the encumbered inventory/AR (carrying amount and nature of encumbrance — pledge/trust/warehouse-receipt), the facility terms/availability, and contingent liabilities (guarantees W325, cross-entity) per PFRS/PAS disclosure requirements | Finance / Controller / Treasury | CFO / Controller | Quarterly + annual |
| 2 | Reconcile to the ledger & facility: tie disclosure to the GL (VS-17), the borrowing base (W4939), the security register (W4951), and the contingent-liability register (W325); ensure the notes reconcile to the face of the statements | Finance / Treasury / Legal | Controller | Quarterly + annual |
| 3 | Support external audit (VS-21): provide the disclosure workpapers, facility agreements, security/perfection evidence (W4951), and reconciliations; manage audit findings; and submit the audited statements to the bank (covenant W4944) | Finance / External Audit / Legal | CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- GL/notes (VS-17.4); treasury/debt facility (VS-18); security register (VS-100); contingent-liability register (W325); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Disclosure-misstatement risk**: Encumbered assets/borrowings mis-stated → audit qualification; mitigated by reconciliation and workpapers
- **Note-reconciliation risk**: Notes ≠ face of statements; mitigated by the reconciliation control
- **Contingent-liability omission risk**: Guarantees/cross-entity commitments omitted; mitigated by the contingent-liability register (W325)

### Time Estimate
- ~quarterly disclosure 3–5 days; ~annual audit support absorbed into close

### Automation Opportunity
- Encumbrance auto-disclosure from security register; note-to-face auto-recon; contingent-liability auto-roll from W325

### Controls
- CTL: disclosure reconciles to GL/security register; notes reconcile to statements; contingent liabilities complete; audited (VS-21)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.4 (notes), VS-18 (W319/W325 facility/contingent), VS-100 (security), VS-21 (audit), W4939 (borrowing base), W4951 (perfection), W4944 (covenant submission)

## W4956. Financing-Cost, Effective-Rate & Capital-Structure Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Periodic review; refinance decision (W4937); strategic review (VS-33) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly deep-dive |
| **Volume** | ~1 analytics program across 1–3 facilities |
| **Owner** | Treasury / ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / FP&A |
| **Participants** | Treasury (VS-18), Finance/FP&A (VS-17.4/VS-33), Data (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compute financing cost & effective rate: the all-in cost (interest margin, commitment fee, utilization fee, field-exam fees, collateral-manager fees, bank charges W4959) against drawn/undrawn, the effective rate per facility and blended, and the cost-vs-WACC/benchmark comparison | Treasury / FP&A / Analytics | Treasurer / CFO | Monthly + quarterly |
| 2 | Analyze capital structure & cost-of-capital: the debt/equity mix, the collateralized vs uncollateralized split, the cost-of-debt impact on EBITDA/net-income, the working-capital-freed value, and the optimal facility/utilization profile (links VS-105 SCF, VS-18.2 cash) | FP&A / Treasury / Analytics | CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | Recommend optimization: refinance/resize/restructure recommendations, the SCF-vs-ABL-vs-TR trade-off, advance-rate/eligibility improvements, and feed strategy (W4937) and the board/finance committee (VS-36/VS-33) | Treasury / FP&A / Strategy | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/analytics (VS-18/VS-28); FP&A (VS-17.4/VS-33); bank portal (charges W4959); consolidation (VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cost-leakage risk**: Unused commitment fees on over-sized facility; mitigated by utilization analytics and resize
- **Sub-optimal-mix risk**: Over-reliance on expensive collateralized debt vs SCF (VS-105); mitigated by the cost-of-capital analysis
- **Decision-lag risk**: Slow optimization response to rate/market change; mitigated by quarterly deep-dive

### Time Estimate
- ~monthly analytics automated; ~quarterly deep-dive 1 week

### Automation Opportunity
- Effective-rate auto-calc; cost-of-debt analytics; facility-utilization dashboards; optimization scenario modeling

### Controls
- CTL: financing cost reconciled to bank charges (W4959); effective-rate documented; optimization recommendations reviewed by CFO

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury), VS-17.4/VS-33 (FP&A/strategy), VS-105 (SCF), VS-36 (board), W4937 (strategy), W4959 (charges)

## W4957. Over-Advance, Fraud & Proceeds-Diversion Detection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous monitoring; certificate (W4939); field exam (W4943); anomaly alert |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~monitoring across all facilities/cycles |
| **Owner** | ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / Internal Audit (VS-21) / Financial Crime (VS-86) |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17), Treasury (VS-18), Inventory (VS-05), AR (VS-16), Internal Audit (VS-21), Compliance (VS-86) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Detect over-advance: continuously test that availability (W4942) ≥ outstanding, that the certificate (W4939) excludes ineligibles (W4940/W4941), and that draws don't exceed availability; alert on over-advance, certificate misstatement, or eligibility erosion | Analytics / ABL Manager / Treasury | Treasurer | Continuous |
| 2 | Detect proceeds diversion (PD 115): monitor trust-receipt proceeds segregation (W4947) vs remittance (W4948), flag commingling/diversion patterns, reconcile trust-goods depletion to proceeds, and detect ghost sales/under-reporting (links VS-86 financial-crime) | Analytics / Finance / ABL Manager / Compliance | Treasurer / Compliance | Continuous |
| 3 | Investigate & escalate: investigate anomalies (data error vs fraud), escalate confirmed issues to Internal Audit (VS-21)/Legal (VS-100)/the bank per the agreement, manage remediation and potential self-disclosure, and document for the audit trail | Internal Audit / Legal / ABL Manager / Compliance | CFO / Audit Committee | Event-driven |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/borrowing-base (VS-18); analytics/exception monitoring (VS-28/VS-21.3); trust-receipt proceeds (VS-18); financial-crime (VS-86); inventory/AR (VS-05/VS-16)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-advance risk**: Drawing > availability → immediate default; mitigated by availability-first controls (W4942) and over-advance detection
- **Diversion risk**: Trust proceeds diverted → PD 115 criminal liability + default; mitigated by proceeds segregation and diversion detection
- **Detection-lag risk**: Fraud undetected until field exam; mitigated by continuous monitoring (not just periodic)

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous (automated); ~investigation per event

### Automation Opportunity
- Over-advance auto-alert; proceeds-diversion pattern detection; trust-depletion-vs-proceeds auto-recon; exception escalation workflow

### Controls
- CTL: availability-first funding; proceeds segregated & monitored; anomalies investigated & escalated; audited (VS-21/VS-86)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21 (audit), VS-86 (financial crime), VS-100 (legal), W4939 (certificate), W4942 (availability), W4947/W4948 (proceeds), W4943 (field exam)

| **Owner** | Internal Audit (VS-21) / ABL & Collateral Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Internal Audit (VS-21), Finance (VS-17), Treasury (VS-18), Legal (VS-100), External Audit |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audit the collateral-financing program: test certificate accuracy (W4939), eligibility testing (W4940/W4941), availability controls (W4942), proceeds segregation (W4947), release authorization (W4952), covenant compliance (W4944), and disclosure (W4955); coordinate with the bank's field-exam findings (W4943) and external audit (VS-21) | Internal Audit / ABL Manager | Chief Audit Executive | Annual |
| 2 | Manage issues & remediation: log audit/bank findings, root-cause, assign remediation owners/dates, track to closure, and verify effectiveness; manage any control deficiencies (VS-21 CAPA) | Internal Audit / ABL Manager / Process Owners | Chief Audit Executive | Continuous |

### Automation Opportunity

## W4959. Interest Accrual, Charge Settlement & Bank Fee Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close (VS-17.1); bank billing statement |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~1 settlement/reconciliation per facility per month × 1–3 facilities |
| **Owner** | Treasury / Finance / ABL & Collateral Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury (VS-18), Finance (VS-17), AP (VS-15), Data (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accrue interest & charges: accrue interest on drawn balances, commitment fee on undrawn, utilization/capitalized fees, field-exam/collateral-manager fees, and other facility charges; post to the GL (VS-17) by entity/facility | Treasury / Finance / AP | Controller | Monthly |
| 2 | Settle & reconcile bank charges: receive the bank billing statement, settle the charges, reconcile to the accrual, investigate variances (rate/fee/days), and correct; allocate cost across entities (VS-72) | Treasury / Finance / AP / Analytics | Treasurer | Monthly |
| 3 | Validate effective rate & feed analytics: confirm the settled charges reconcile to the effective-rate analytics (W4956), flag cost anomalies, and report to management | Treasury / FP&A / Analytics | Treasurer | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/debt facility (VS-18); GL (VS-17); AP (VS-15); intercompany allocation (VS-72); bank portal; analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-accrual risk**: Interest/charges mis-accrued → P&L distortion; mitigated by monthly accrual & reconciliation
- **Over-charge risk**: Bank over-charges (rate/fee error) undisputed → cost leakage; mitigated by statement reconciliation and dispute
- **Allocation-error risk**: Charges mis-allocated across entities; mitigated by the VS-72 allocation

### Time Estimate
- ~monthly 2–3 business days

### Automation Opportunity
- Interest auto-accrue from facility/draws; bank-statement auto-reconcile; variance auto-flag; entity auto-allocation

### Controls
- CTL: monthly accrual & reconciliation; bank charges disputed on variance; effective-rate validated; cost allocated (VS-72)

### Cross-references
