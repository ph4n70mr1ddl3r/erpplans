## W4945. Trust-Receipt Financing Program Setup & PD 115 Compliance Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Import-line close (W4937); TR program launch; legal/regulatory update (PD 115) |
| **Frequency** | Per setup + on legal/bank change |
| **Volume** | 1 TR program × import-line facilities × ~400–600 TEUs/month |
| **Owner** | ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / Treasury / Legal |
| **Participants** | Treasury (VS-18), Legal (VS-100), Import/Customs (VS-02.2/VS-87), AP (VS-15), Finance (VS-17), Compliance (VS-86) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish the TR-financing framework per PD 115 (Trust Receipts Law) and the credit agreement: the trust-receipt instrument, the goods covered (imported goods under LC/DA/DP — links VS-15.1 W232), the proceeds obligation (sell and remit, or hold and pay at term), segregation of trust goods from general inventory, recordkeeping, and the compliance/anti-diversion controls (PD 115 Sec. 13–14 criminal liability) | Legal / ABL Manager / Treasury | CFO / Legal | Per setup |
| 2 | Configure the TR operational model in ERP/treasury: TR subledger (per shipment/lc/BL), goods-in-trust inventory status (distinct from owned — links VS-05/VS-29), proceeds tracking, remittance scheduling, and the integration with import settlement (W232/W239) and inventory ownership transfer on remittance/release | ABL Manager / Treasury / IT (VS-27) / Finance | Treasurer | Per setup |
| 3 | Train & govern: brief Import/Customs, AP, Inventory, and DC/Store Ops on goods-in-trust segregation, the prohibition on diversion/sale-without-remittance, and the audit trail; establish the compliance review (links VS-21/VS-86) and reporting to the bank | ABL Manager / Legal / Compliance / Internal Audit | CFO / Treasurer | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/TR subledger (VS-18/VS-27); inventory status master — goods-in-trust (VS-05/VS-29); import/LC (VS-15.1 W232); customs (VS-15.1 W239/VS-87); document repository (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Diversion risk**: Trust goods sold/consumed without remittance → PD 115 criminal liability (officer personal) + bank default; mitigated by segregation, proceeds tracking, and anti-diversion controls (W4947)
- **Segregation risk**: Trust goods commingled with owned inventory → untraceable proceeds; mitigated by goods-in-trust inventory status
- **Compliance-ignorance risk**: Staff unaware of PD 115 obligations; mitigated by training and the compliance review

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks initial setup; ~continuous governance

### Automation Opportunity
- TR subledger auto-create on import release; goods-in-trust inventory status auto-flag; proceeds-auto-link to sale; remittance-due alerts

### Controls
- CTL: TR framework per PD 115; goods-in-trust segregated; anti-diversion controls; officer trained; audited (VS-21/VS-86)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-15.1 (W232 LC/W239 customs), VS-05/VS-29 (inventory status), VS-100 (PD 115 legal), VS-86 (anti-diversion/AML), W4937 (facility), W4946–W4948 (TR lifecycle)

## W4946. Import Document Release Against Trust Receipt & Goods-in-Trust Establishment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Import arrival & clearance (VS-02.2/VS-87); bank document release request |
| **Frequency** | Per import shipment (~400–600 TEUs/month; ~10–15 regulated shipments) |
| **Volume** | ~1 TR per shipment/lc × import volume |
| **Owner** | ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / Import Finance / Treasury |
| **Participants** | Import/Customs (VS-02.2/VS-87), AP (VS-15.1), Treasury (VS-18), DC Ops (VS-04), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On import arrival & customs clearance (VS-02.2 W144/W464, VS-87), request the bank release the shipping documents (BL, invoice, LC) against execution of a trust receipt: execute the TR instrument (W4945), acknowledge goods held in trust for the bank, and take physical/constructive possession for sale/manufacture per the TR terms | Import Finance / Treasury / Legal | Treasurer | Per shipment |
| 2 | Establish goods-in-trust: receive the documents, post the inventory as goods-in-trust status (distinct from owned — links VS-05/VS-29), record the TR obligation (principal + interest/charges), and stage at the DC (VS-04) under the segregated status | Import Finance / DC Ops / Treasury / IT | ABL Manager | Per shipment |
| 3 | Settle the import costs & integrate: settle customs duty (VS-15.1 W239), freight/demurrage (W249), and the LC (W232); record the trust-receipt receivable payable to the bank; and trigger proceeds tracking (W4947) and remittance scheduling (W4948) | Import Finance / AP / Treasury | Treasurer | Per shipment |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/TR subledger (VS-18); import/LC (VS-15.1 W232); customs (VS-15.1 W239/VS-87); inventory status — goods-in-trust (VS-05/VS-29); DC WMS (VS-04)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Document-execution risk**: TR improperly executed → goods released without enforceable bank security; mitigated by Legal-reviewed TR template and execution log
- **Status-error risk**: Goods-in-trust not flagged in inventory → sold as owned, proceeds lost; mitigated by mandatory goods-in-trust status on receipt
- **Cost-settlement risk**: Duty/freight/LC mis-settled against TR → reconciliation breaks; mitigated by the integrated settlement flow

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 days per shipment (document release + receipt + posting)

### Automation Opportunity
- TR auto-create on import clearance; goods-in-trust status auto-flag; TR-payable auto-post; duty/freight/LC auto-settle

### Controls
- CTL: TR executed per template; goods-in-trust status enforced; TR-payable recorded; cost-settlement reconciled

### Cross-references
- links to VS-02.2 (W144/W464 import), VS-87 (customs), VS-15.1 (W232 LC/W239 duty/W249 demurrage), VS-05/VS-29 (inventory status), VS-04 (DC), W4945 (framework), W4947 (proceeds), W4948 (remittance)

## W4947. Trust-Receipt Sell-Through Tracking & Proceeds Segregation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Sale/usage of trust goods (POS VS-08, B2B VS-11, ecommerce VS-10) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (per transaction against trust goods) |
| **Volume** | ~portion of 2.8M monthly POS + B2B + ecommerce transactions tied to trust goods |
| **Owner** | ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / Finance / Treasury |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17), Treasury (VS-18), POS/Ecommerce (VS-08/VS-10), B2B (VS-11), Data (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Track trust-goods sell-through: as goods-in-trust inventory sells (POS VS-08, B2B VS-11, ecommerce VS-10), compute the proceeds attributable to the trust shipment (FIFO/identified-lot per TR terms), and segregate the proceeds from general cash into the TR-proceeds bucket for the bank | Finance / Analytics / Treasury | ABL Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Segregate & hold proceeds: hold the segregated proceeds pending remittance (W4948), apply against the TR-payable (principal + charges), and prevent commingling/diversion to other uses (anti-diversion per PD 115 W4945); reconcile proceeds to sales and trust-goods depletion | Finance / Treasury / Analytics | Treasurer | Continuous |
| 3 | Report & monitor trust-goods position: report trust-goods-on-hand vs sold vs proceeds-segregated to the bank/management, flag shortfalls (proceeds < obligation as goods deplete), and feed remittance scheduling (W4948) and availability (W4942) | Finance / Treasury / ABL Manager | Treasurer | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/TR-proceeds subledger (VS-18); POS/ecommerce/B2B sales (VS-08/VS-10/VS-11); inventory depletion (VS-05); GL/cash (VS-17); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Diversion risk**: Proceeds used for other purposes before remittance → PD 115 violation + default; mitigated by proceeds segregation and anti-diversion controls (W4945)
- **Attribution-error risk**: Proceeds mis-attributed to the wrong TR/shipment → remittance errors; mitigated by FIFO/lot-identified tracking and reconciliation
- **Shortfall risk**: Proceeds lag trust-payable as goods deplete (markdown/discount below cost); mitigated by monitoring and reserve/cash cover

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous (automated); ~daily recon

### Automation Opportunity
- Proceeds auto-segregate on sale; FIFO/lot attribution engine; TR-payable auto-reduce; shortfall alerts; bank reporting auto-feed

### Controls
- CTL: proceeds segregated; anti-diversion enforced; attribution reconciled to sales/depletion; shortfalls alerted

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08/VS-10/VS-11 (sales), VS-05 (depletion), VS-17 (cash/GL), W4945 (anti-diversion), W4946 (TR establishment), W4948 (remittance), W4942 (availability)

## W4948. Trust-Receipt Proceeds Remittance & Release Lifecycle

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | TR remittance due date (per term); trust-goods fully sold; maturity |
| **Frequency** | Per TR (event-driven) |
| **Volume** | ~1 remittance/release per TR × import volume (~400–600 TEUs/month → hundreds of TRs/yr) |
| **Owner** | ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / Treasury / Finance |
| **Participants** | Treasury (VS-18), Finance (VS-17), AP (VS-15), Legal (VS-100), Data (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Remit proceeds to the bank per the TR term (sell-and-remit on sale, or hold-and-pay at maturity): draw from the segregated proceeds (W4947), settle the TR-payable (principal + interest/charges), reconcile to the bank, and record the release of the corresponding trust-goods from trust status | Treasury / Finance / AP | Treasurer | Per TR |
| 2 | Release the inventory from goods-in-trust to owned status: once the TR is settled, reclassify the remaining/sold trust goods to owned inventory (links VS-05/VS-29), update the inventory valuation/ownership, and release the bank's security interest in that shipment | Finance / Inventory / Treasury | ABL Manager | Per TR |
| 3 | Close the TR & document: archive the TR instrument, settlement evidence, and release; report to the bank; feed availability (W4942), the borrowing base (W4939), and audit (VS-21); manage exceptions (short-pay, unsold trust goods at maturity → rollover/cash-settle per agreement) | Treasury / Finance / Legal / Internal Audit | Treasurer | Per TR |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/TR subledger (VS-18); bank portal; AP (VS-15); inventory status release (VS-05/VS-29); GL (VS-17); document repository (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Late-remittance risk**: Proceeds not remitted on term → PD 115 default + charges; mitigated by due-date alerts and automated remittance
- **Release-error risk**: Inventory not released from trust after settlement → overstated encumbrance; mitigated by the release-on-settlement control
- **Maturity-shortfall risk**: Unsold trust goods at maturity (slow-moving import) → cash-settle or rollover; mitigated by monitoring and the rollover/cash-cover process

### Time Estimate
- ~per TR remittance/release 1–2 hours (automated); ~exception handling per event

### Automation Opportunity
- Remittance auto-trigger at term; TR-payable auto-settle; inventory auto-release on settlement; exception flagging (short-pay/unsold-at-maturity)

### Controls
- CTL: proceeds remitted on term; TR-payable settled & bank-reconciled; inventory released on settlement; exceptions managed; audited (VS-21)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury), VS-15 (AP), VS-05/VS-29 (inventory release), VS-17 (GL), VS-21 (audit), W4946/W4947 (TR lifecycle), W4939 (borrowing base), W4942 (availability)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish warehouse-receipt / field-warehousing collateral: where the bank requires a collateral manager/warehouse-receipt structure over designated DC inventory (distinct from trust-receipt import financing), set up the warehouse receipts, the designated/collateralized bin/zone, the collateral-manager or field-warehousing arrangement, and the access/segregation controls | ABL Manager / Legal / Treasury / DC Ops | Treasurer / Legal | Per arrangement |
| 2 | Operate collateralized-inventory controls: maintain the designated collateral pool, control releases (no release without authorization W4952), reconcile receipts/issues, and support periodic bank/collateral-manager counts (links VS-05.1 W6/W42) | DC Ops / Inventory / ABL Manager | ABL Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Verify & report: periodic verification of warehouse-receipt collateral vs the inventory ledger, report to the bank/collateral-manager, manage substitutions/releases (W4952), and feed the borrowing base (W4939) and availability (W4942) | Finance / Inventory / Treasury / Analytics | Treasurer | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- WMS/collateral zone (VS-04/VS-05); inventory ledger (VS-05); treasury/borrowing-base (VS-18); cycle count (VS-05.1); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unauthorized-release risk**: Collateral released without authorization → bank security breached; mitigated by release-authorization controls (W4952)
- **Count-discrepancy risk**: Collateral pool short vs receipts → over-advance; mitigated by periodic counts and ≥97% accuracy
- **Collateral-manager risk**: Third-party collateral-manager failure (TPRM VS-161); mitigated by TPRM and the bank's oversight

### Time Estimate
- ~per arrangement setup 2–4 weeks; ~continuous operations; ~periodic verification per cycle

### Automation Opportunity
- Collateral-zone WMS integration; release-authorization gate; receipt-vs-ledger auto-recon; bank reporting auto-feed

### Controls
- CTL: designated collateral pool; release-authorization enforced; periodic count; reconciled to ledger; TPRM on collateral manager (VS-161)

### Cross-references

## W4950. Import-Inventory-In-Transit Financing & Pre-Shipment / LC Settlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Import PO & LC issuance (VS-15.1 W232); pre-shipment financing need |
| **Frequency** | Per import shipment |
| **Volume** | ~400–600 TEUs/month × in-transit financing |
| **Owner** | ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / Import Finance / Treasury |
| **Participants** | Import/Customs (VS-02.2/VS-87), AP (VS-15.1), Treasury (VS-18), Finance (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance the in-transit/pre-shipment stage: from LC issuance (VS-15.1 W232) through ocean transit (45–90 days) to arrival, fund the LC/supplier payment via the import line, classify the in-transit inventory for borrowing-base eligibility (W4938), and track the in-transit position (links VS-02.3 control tower W250) | Import Finance / Treasury / Supply Chain | Treasurer | Per shipment |
| 2 | Settle the pre-shipment/LC: draw on the facility for the LC/supplier, settle freight forwarder (VS-02.2), and accumulate landed cost (links VS-158 landed-cost) pending the trust-receipt release (W4946) at arrival; track the financing cost (interest/charges) into the import cost | Import Finance / AP / Treasury / Costing | Treasurer | Per shipment |
| 3 | Transition to trust receipt at arrival: on arrival & clearance, transition the in-transit financing to the trust-receipt (W4946), reconcile the pre-shipment draws, and update availability (W4942) and the borrowing base (W4939) | Import Finance / Treasury / Finance | Treasurer | Per shipment |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/import-line (VS-18); LC (VS-15.1 W232); in-transit tracking (VS-02.3 W250); landed cost (VS-158); AP (VS-15); GL (VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **In-transit eligibility risk**: In-transit inventory excluded from the borrowing base during 45–90-day transit → availability gap; mitigated by structuring eligible-in-transit (W4938) where the agreement allows
- **Cost-accumulation risk**: Financing cost not captured in landed cost → understated COGS (VS-158); mitigated by integrated landed-cost capture
- **Transition risk**: In-transit → TR transition mis-handled → double-counting or gap in availability; mitigated by the controlled transition flow

### Time Estimate
- ~per shipment tracking continuous over transit; ~settlement per event

### Automation Opportunity
- In-transit eligibility flag; LC/landed-cost auto-accumulate; in-transit→TR auto-transition; availability impact auto-update

### Controls
- CTL: in-transit eligibility per agreement; financing cost in landed cost; transition reconciled; availability updated

### Cross-references
- links to VS-15.1 (W232 LC), VS-02.2/VS-02.3 (import/in-transit W250), VS-158 (landed cost), W4938 (eligibility), W4946 (TR establishment), W4939/W4942 (borrowing base/availability)

## W4952. Pledged-Inventory Movement, Location Control & Release Authorization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Inventory movement/transfer (VS-05.2); sale (W4947); release request; collateral substitution |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (per movement/release) |
| **Volume** | ~portion of ~134M annual POS line items + transfers tied to pledged/collateralized inventory |
| **Owner** | ABL & Collateral Operations Manager / DC Ops / Finance |
| **Participants** | DC/Store Ops (VS-04/VS-07), Inventory (VS-05), Treasury (VS-18), Finance (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Control pledged/collateralized-inventory movement: gate transfers (VS-05.2 W22) and inter-DC moves (W218) of pledged/collateralized stock against the facility/location rules, prevent movement to ineligible locations, and maintain the location/encumbrance register (W4951) | DC Ops / Inventory / ABL Manager | ABL Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Authorize releases & substitutions: release collateralized inventory only on authorized sale (W4947 — with proceeds segregation) or authorized substitution (replace with equivalent eligible collateral), maintain the dual-control release authorization, and reconcile releases to the borrowing base (W4939) | ABL Manager / Treasury / DC Ops | Treasurer | Per release |
| 3 | Reconcile & report: reconcile pledged/collateralized inventory movements to the ledger and the borrowing base, report location/movement exceptions, and support the field exam (W4943) and availability (W4942) | Finance / Inventory / Analytics / ABL Manager | Treasurer | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- WMS/inventory movement (VS-04/VS-05); transfer order (VS-05.2 W22); treasury/borrowing-base (VS-18); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unauthorized-movement risk**: Pledged inventory moved to ineligible location or sold without authorization → bank security breached; mitigated by movement gates and release authorization
- **Substitution-error risk**: Released collateral not replaced with equivalent → availability true-down; mitigated by the equivalent-substitution control
- **Reconciliation risk**: Movements not reconciled to the borrowing base → availability error; mitigated by automated reconciliation

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous (automated gates); ~release authorization per event 15–30 min

### Automation Opportunity
- Movement gate against eligible-location master; release-authorization workflow; auto-substitution validation; movement-to-borrowing-base auto-recon

### Controls
- CTL: movement gated by eligibility; release dual-authorized; substitution equivalent; reconciled to borrowing base; audited (W4943)

### Cross-references
