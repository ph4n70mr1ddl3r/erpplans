## W2422. Marketplace Financial Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly settlement cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; 1–2 platforms |
| **Volume** | ~1–2 reconciliations/month |
| **Owner** | Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Analyst, Ecommerce Dir, Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analyst reconciles: marketplace settlement report vs. BuildRight order records; verifies: gross sales, marketplace commission, shipping fee deductions, promotional subsidies, refunds, net settlement | Finance Analyst | Finance Manager | 4–6 hours/month |
| 2 | Disputes discrepancies with marketplace (missing orders, incorrect commissions, unauthorized refunds); resolves within marketplace dispute window (typically 30 days) | Finance Analyst | Ecommerce Director | 2–3 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Integration-failure risk**: marketplace API sync fails (catalog, inventory, or order), causing overselling or customer-facing errors; mitigated by real-time sync monitoring and the failover to manual processing
- **Reconciliation-error risk**: marketplace settlement doesn't match BuildRight's order records (commission discrepancy, refund timing), requiring manual investigation; mitigated by the auto-reconciliation and the discrepancy-flagging

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~1–2 reconciliations/month
- Frequency: Monthly; 1–2 platforms
- Absorbed into the Finance Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Automation Opportunity
- Process steps auto-sequenced with system controls; data-entry auto-populated from master data; exceptions auto-flagged for human review

### Controls
- CTL-51: master-data quality — links to VS-29 / W291; Operational: Finance Analyst sign-off on the workflow output
