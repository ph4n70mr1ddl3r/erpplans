## W2414. Marketplace Order Download & Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous order sync from marketplace |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~5,000–10,000 marketplace orders/month |
| **Volume** | ~5K–10K/month across platforms |
| **Owner** | Order Management System |
| **Participants** | OMS, Ecommerce Ops, DC Ops, Logistics |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System downloads marketplace orders via API; converts to BuildRight fulfillment orders; reserves inventory; routes to fulfillment source (DC, store, vendor) per VS-60 routing logic | System | Ecommerce Operations Manager | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Integration-failure risk**: marketplace API sync fails (catalog, inventory, or order), causing overselling or customer-facing errors; mitigated by real-time sync monitoring and the failover to manual processing
- **Data-quality risk**: incomplete or inaccurate master data (pricing, inventory, customer) corrupts the workflow output; mitigated by the master-data governance (VS-29 / W291) and the periodic audit

### Automation Opportunity
- Process steps auto-sequenced with system controls; data-entry auto-populated from master data; exceptions auto-flagged for human review

### Controls
- CTL-425 (ensure controlled execution — Marketplace Order & Inventory Sync (PA-65.2).)
- operational: Order Management System sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: ~5K–10K/month across platforms
- Frequency: Continuous; ~5,000–10,000 marketplace orders/month
- Absorbed into the Order Management System role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2415. Marketplace Inventory Synchronization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Real-time inventory sync |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; every 15 minutes |
| **Volume** | ~35,000 SKUs × 1–2 platforms |
| **Owner** | IT Integration Lead |
| **Participants** | IT Integration, Inventory Mgr, Ecommerce Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System pushes inventory availability to marketplace every 15 minutes: BuildRight available quantity minus marketplace allocation; prevents overselling; handles sync errors and reconciles discrepancies | System / IT Integration Lead | Inventory Manager | Automated + 2–3 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
- Inventory/ATP (links to VS-05 / W105 multi-channel allocation, VS-29 / W252 item master)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Integration-failure risk**: marketplace API sync fails (catalog, inventory, or order), causing overselling or customer-facing errors; mitigated by real-time sync monitoring and the failover to manual processing
- **Inventory-desync risk**: marketplace inventory sync lags, causing overselling (listing items already sold); mitigated by the real-time ATP sync and the buffer-stock reservation

### Automation Opportunity
- API-driven real-time sync (catalog, inventory, pricing, orders) eliminating manual data entry; auto-reconciliation of marketplace settlement vs. BuildRight records

### Controls
- CTL-44: inventory-count integrity; Operational: IT Integration Lead sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- System pushes inventory availability to marketplace every 15 minutes
- Volume: ~35,000 SKUs × 1–2 platforms
- Frequency: Continuous; every 15 minutes
- Absorbed into the IT Integration Lead role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
