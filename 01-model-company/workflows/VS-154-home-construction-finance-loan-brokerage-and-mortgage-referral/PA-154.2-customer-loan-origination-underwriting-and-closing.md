## W4561. Customer Identification, Finance-Need Discovery & Pre-Qualification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer inquiry (store/trade/field); project quote (VS-66) |
| **Frequency** | Per customer inquiry (high volume at 200 stores + trade) |
| **Volume** | Finance inquiries across B2C homeowners + B2B trade |
| **Owner** | Finance Desk / Sales Associate / Field Sales (VS-140) |
| **Participants** | Trade (VS-11), Project Design (VS-66), Insights (VS-44), Lenders |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Desk/Sales identify the finance need (home build/renovation, project size, timeline, affordability sense) during the sales/project conversation (VS-66/VS-11) | Finance Desk / Sales | Store Manager / Trade Manager | Per inquiry |
| 2 | Finance Desk conduct soft pre-qualification (income/credit profile, indicative eligibility per W4556) WITHOUT a hard credit pull (privacy VS-91), and present product options | Finance Desk | Finance Desk Lead | Per inquiry |
| 3 | Finance Desk route qualified interest to application (W4562) with customer consent, and capture the lead in CRM (VS-13/VS-107) for follow-up/analytics (W4575) | Finance Desk / CRM | Finance Desk Lead | Per inquiry |

### System Touchpoints
- Project design (VS-66); trade (VS-11); CRM (VS-13/VS-107); privacy (VS-91); pre-qualification (W4560)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-promise risk**: Frontline implies approval/rate before lender underwriting; mitigated by "indicative only" framing & training (W4559)
- **Privacy risk**: Premature hard credit pull; mitigated by soft pre-qualification & consent (VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min per inquiry

### Automation Opportunity
- Soft-pre-qualification scoring tool; CRM lead capture; indicative-eligibility calculator

### Controls
- CTL: pre-qualification indicative-only (no approval promise); soft (consented) pre-qual; lead captured (VS-13)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-66/VS-11 (project/trade), VS-13/VS-107 (CRM), VS-91 (privacy), W4556 (terms), W4559 (enablement), W4562 (application)

## W4562. Loan Application Intake & Documentation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per application |
| **Volume** | Loan applications across the partner network |
| **Owner** | Finance Desk / Lenders |
| **Participants** | Project Design (VS-66), Trade (VS-11), Compliance (VS-22), Privacy (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Desk collect the application & documentation (identity, income/financials, property/project docs, contractor/quote VS-66) per lender checklist (W4556) | Finance Desk / Customer | Finance Desk Lead | Per application |

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Consent-gap risk**: Processing without consent → privacy breach (VS-91); mitigated by mandatory consent capture

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 hours per application (customer-driven)

### Automation Opportunity
- Document-checklist/upload app; consent capture automation; lender-submission tracking

### Controls
- CTL: application complete per lender checklist; consent obtained (VS-91); submission tracked

### Cross-references
- links to W4560 (systems), VS-91 (privacy/consent), VS-66 (quote), W4558 (integration), W4561 (lead), W4565 (underwriting)

## W4563. Credit Assessment, Affordability & Bureau Scoring Handoff

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Application submitted (W4562) |
| **Frequency** | Per application |
| **Volume** | Credit assessments across applications |
| **Owner** | Lenders (underwriting) / Finance Desk (handoff) |
| **Participants** | Risk (VS-21.2), Compliance (VS-22), Bureau/Credit data providers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lenders perform credit assessment & affordability (bureau score, debt-to-income, income verification) — the lender's underwriting role, not BuildRight's (W4557) | Lenders | Lender UW Manager | Per application |
| 2 | Finance Desk/Lenders coordinate the credit-bureau pull & supplementary info (with consent VS-91), and respond to underwriter queries | Finance Desk / Lenders | Finance Desk Lead | Per application |
| 3 | Lenders/Risk capture the credit decision (approve/decline/conditions), and Finance Desk communicates to the customer fairly & without misleading reasons | Lenders / Finance Desk | Lender UW Manager / Finance Desk Lead | Per application |

### System Touchpoints
- Lender underwriting (W4560); credit bureau; risk (VS-21.2); privacy/consent (VS-91); compliance (VS-22)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Bias/discrimination risk**: Adverse decision based on prohibited factors; mitigated by lender's documented criteria (W4557) & BuildRight fair-communication standard
- **Slow-decision risk**: Slow underwriting kills the project sale; mitigated by SLA & lender prioritization

### Time Estimate
- Days–weeks (lender-dependent)

### Automation Opportunity
- Lender-integrated underwriting status; decision-tracking; fair-communication templates

### Controls
- CTL: underwriting is lender's role (W4557); decisions communicated fairly; consent for bureau pull (VS-91)

### Cross-references
- links to W4560 (systems), VS-91 (privacy), VS-21.2/VS-22 (risk/compliance), W4557 (credit policy), W4564 (valuation), W4565 (approval)

## W4565. Lender Underwriting, Approval & Conditions

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Credit (W4563) + collateral (W4564) complete |
| **Frequency** | Per application |
| **Volume** | Lender approvals/conditions across applications |
| **Owner** | Lenders (underwriting decision) |
| **Participants** | Finance Desk, Risk (VS-21.2), Compliance (VS-22), Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lenders make the underwriting decision (approve/decline/conditioned) based on credit (W4563), collateral (W4564), and product terms (W4556), and document the rationale | Lenders | Lender UW Manager / Credit Committee | Per application |
| 2 | Lenders/Finance Desk communicate conditions (additional docs, collateral, milestones) and work the customer/BuildRight to satisfy them | Lenders / Finance Desk | Lender UW Manager / Finance Desk Lead | Per application |
| 3 | Lenders issue the approval/offer (loan amount, rate, tenor, draw schedule W4567), Finance Desk reviews fit/suitability (W4557), and supports customer decision | Lenders / Finance Desk | Lender UW Manager / Finance Desk Lead | Per application |

### System Touchpoints
- Lender underwriting; credit (W4563); collateral (W4564); product terms (W4556); suitability (W4557)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unsuitable-approval risk**: Approved loan unsuitable for customer → harm/complaint; mitigated by BuildRight suitability check (W4557)
- **Condition-fatigue risk**: Excessive conditions lose the customer; mitigated by streamlined condition mgmt

### Time Estimate
- ~days–weeks per application

### Automation Opportunity
- Decision/condition tracking; suitability-check support; offer-comparison tooling

### Controls
- CTL: lender underwriting documented; suitability checked (W4557); offer fit-for-purpose before customer acceptance

### Cross-references
- links to W4563 (credit), W4564 (collateral), W4556 (terms), W4557 (suitability), W4566 (closing), W4567 (disbursement)

## W4566. Loan Closing, Documentation & Disclosure

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approval accepted (W4565) |
| **Frequency** | Per loan |
| **Volume** | Loan closings across approved applications |
| **Owner** | Lenders / Legal (VS-100.3) / Finance Desk |
| **Participants** | Customer, Trade/Contractor (VS-11), Compliance (VS-22), Notary/Registry of Deeds |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lenders/Legal prepare loan/collateral documents (promissory note, real-estate mortgage/REM, disclosure statement), per lender/legal & BSP disclosure rules | Lenders / Legal | Lender Legal / VP Legal | Per loan |
| 2 | Lenders/Finance Desk conduct closing with the customer (explain terms, fees, rate, draw schedule W4567, total cost), ensuring informed consent (RA 7394/BSP) | Lenders / Finance Desk | Lender UW Manager / Finance Desk Lead | Per loan |
| 3 | Legal/Notary execute & register (REM registration with Registry of Deeds, notary), and lodge documents in records (VS-88), triggering disbursement (W4567) | Legal / Notary | VP Legal | Per loan |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal/title (VS-100.3/VS-42); records (VS-88); compliance/disclosure (VS-22); lender closing (W4560)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Undisclosed-cost risk**: Customer surprised by fees/terms → complaint (VS-13)/regulatory; mitigated by clear closing disclosure
- **Registration-failure risk**: REM not registered → unsecured; mitigated by registration SLA (VS-100.3)

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 weeks per closing (registration-driven)

### Automation Opportunity
- Closing/disclosure document generation; e-signature; registration tracking

### Controls
- CTL: closing disclosure compliant (RA 7394/BSP VS-22); informed consent; REM registered (VS-100.3); documents lodged (VS-88)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100.3/VS-42 (legal/title), VS-88 (records), VS-22 (disclosure), VS-13 (complaints), W4565 (approval), W4567 (disbursement)

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Loan closed (W4566); project ready |
| **Frequency** | Per loan |
| **Volume** | Disbursement/draw-schedule setups across loans |
| **Owner** | Lenders / Treasury (VS-18) / Finance Strategy |
| **Participants** | Project Design (VS-66), Installation (VS-12), Trade (VS-11), Escrow/Trust |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lenders/Finance Strategy set up the draw schedule (milestone-based per W4558) and escrow/trust account (where lender uses one), aligning draws to VS-66/VS-12 milestones | Lenders / Finance Strategy / Treasury | Lender UW Manager / CFO | Per loan |
| 2 | Lenders/Escrow release the first draw (or initial disbursement), and set the milestone-confirmation process (BuildRight confirms completion → next draw) | Lenders / Escrow / Finance Desk | Lender UW Manager | Per milestone |

- Treasury (VS-18); finance/GL (VS-17); project design (VS-66); install (VS-12); draw integration (W4558); escrow/trust

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Draw-misalignment risk**: Draw timing ≠ BuildRight billing → cash strain (VS-18); mitigated by milestone-aligned billing (W4558)
- **Escrow-misuse risk**: Escrow funds misapplied; mitigated by escrow/trust controls & reconciliation

### Time Estimate
- Per setup ~days; per milestone ongoing

### Automation Opportunity
- Milestone→draw→invoice integration (W4558); escrow/settlement automation; reconciliation

### Controls
- CTL: draw-schedule milestone-aligned (VS-66/VS-12/W4558); billing timed to draws; escrow reconciled (VS-17/VS-18)

## W4568. Post-Closing Handoff, Project Activation & Material Release

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | First draw released (W4567); loan active |
| **Frequency** | Per loan |
| **Volume** | Project activations across financed projects |
| **Owner** | Project Design (VS-66) / Trade (VS-11) / Installation (VS-12) |
| **Participants** | Finance Desk, Stores (VS-07), Logistics (VS-06), CRM (VS-13) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Desk/Project Design hand off the financed project to execution (VS-66 design→VS-12 install/VS-11 supply), confirming funding is active (W4567) | Finance Desk / Project Design | Project Design Manager | Per project |
| 2 | Trade/Stores/Logistics release materials/services against the funded project (VS-11/VS-07/VS-06), billing per the draw schedule (W4567) | Trade / Stores / Logistics | Trade Manager / Store Manager | Per project |
| 3 | Finance Desk/CRM track project progress & loan draw (W4567), capture satisfaction/ basket (VS-44/W4575), and manage the relationship to completion | Finance Desk / CRM | Finance Desk Lead | Per project |

### System Touchpoints
- Project design (VS-66); install (VS-12); trade (VS-11); stores (VS-07); logistics (VS-06); CRM (VS-13); draw (W4567)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Funding-activation-gap risk**: Materials released before funding confirmed → AR risk (VS-16); mitigated by confirmed-funding gate
- **Project-loan-drift risk**: Project scope ≠ financed scope → draw disputes; mitigated by scope-control (VS-66)

### Time Estimate
- Per project activation ~days; relationship to completion ongoing

### Automation Opportunity
- Funding-confirmation gate; project↔draw tracking; satisfaction/basket analytics (W4575)

### Controls
- CTL: materials released only on confirmed funding (W4567); billing per draw schedule; project scope controlled (VS-66)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-66/VS-12/VS-11 (project/install/trade), VS-07/VS-06 (stores/logistics), VS-13 (CRM), VS-16 (AR), W4567 (draws), W4575 (analytics)
