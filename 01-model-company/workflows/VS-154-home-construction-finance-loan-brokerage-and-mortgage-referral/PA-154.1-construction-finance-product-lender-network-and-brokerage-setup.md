## W4553. Construction-Finance Strategy & Product Portfolio Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (VS-33); customer insight (VS-44); basket-lift goal |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | One construction-finance strategy/product portfolio |
| **Owner** | Finance Strategy / Commercial / CFO |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01), Trade (VS-11), Project Design (VS-66), Insights (VS-44), Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Strategy/Commercial set the construction-finance strategy (brokerage/referral, not lending), product portfolio (construction loan, home-equity/renovation, Pag-IBIG end-user, bank housing loan, B2B contractor financing), and target segments | Finance Strategy / Commercial | CFO / CMO | Annual |
| 2 | Finance Strategy/Insights size the opportunity (financing-constrained demand, basket-lift, conversion, referral revenue) using VS-44/VS-13 data, and define success metrics | Finance Strategy / Insights | CFO / CMO | Annual |
| 3 | Finance Strategy/Legal validate the strategy within BuildRight's retail charter (no deposit-taking/lending; brokerage/accreditation model) and obtain board approval (VS-36) | Finance Strategy / Legal | CFO / Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy (VS-33); insights (VS-44); trade/project (VS-11/VS-66); legal (VS-100.3); board (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Charter-overstep risk**: BuildRight inadvertently lending (regulatory); mitigated by strict brokerage model & legal validation
- **Mis-sized-product risk**: Products customers don't want; mitigated by insight-driven sizing

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual strategy

### Automation Opportunity
- Opportunity-sizing analytics; product-portfolio modeling; charter-compliance check

### Controls
- CTL-692 (ensure controlled execution — Construction Finance Product Lender Network and Brokerage Se.)
- CTL: strategy brokerage-only (legal-validated VS-100.3); insight-sized; board-approved (VS-36)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-44 (insights), VS-11/VS-66 (trade/project), VS-100.3 (legal), W4554 (lenders), W4555 (licensing)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-692 (ensure controlled execution — Construction Finance Product Lender Network and Brokerage Se.)
