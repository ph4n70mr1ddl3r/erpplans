## W4569. Loan Servicing Coordination, Payment & Customer Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Loan active (W4568); payment cycle; servicing query |
| **Frequency** | Per cycle + per query |
| **Volume** | Servicing coordination across active loans (lender services; BuildRight supports) |
| **Owner** | Lenders (servicing) / Finance Desk (support) |
| **Participants** | Customer Support (VS-13), Treasury (VS-18), Compliance (VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lenders service the loan (amortization, collections, statements, payoff) — the lender's role; BuildRight does not service, only coordinates customer queries | Lenders | Lender Servicing Manager | Per cycle |
| 2 | Finance Desk/Customer Support handle BuildRight-side queries (payment channel, statement, draw status W4567), routing lender-specific items to the lender (VS-13) | Finance Desk / Customer Support | Finance Desk Lead | Per query |
| 3 | Treasury/Finance Desk coordinate payment/draw timing where BuildRight billing interacts with loan draws (W4567/W4568), ensuring clean settlement | Treasury / Finance Desk | CFO | Per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Customer support (VS-13); treasury (VS-18); draw (W4567); lender servicing integration (W4560)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Support-boundary risk**: BuildRight incorrectly assumes servicing liability; mitigated by clear boundary (lender services)
- **Confused-customer risk**: Customer unsure whom to pay/contact; mitigated by clear handoff & support routing

### Time Estimate
- Per query minutes; per cycle ongoing

### Automation Opportunity
- Query-routing/support app; draw-billing status visibility; lender-servicing integration

### Controls
- CTL: lender services the loan (boundary clear); BuildRight coordinates queries (VS-13); draw-billing reconciled (VS-18)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-13 (support), VS-18 (treasury), W4567 (draws), W4568 (activation), W4560 (integration)

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lenders manage collection/foreclosure on default (the lender's loan); BuildRight/Risk assess BuildRight's exposure (delivered-but-unpaid materials/services if billing led draws) | Lenders / Risk / Finance | Lender / CRO / CFO | On default |
| 2 | Finance/Legal manage BuildRight's exposure (recover delivered materials/services, mechanics-lien/contractor rights VS-100.3, AR risk VS-16/VS-68), and coordinate with the lender | Finance / Legal / Trade | CFO / VP Legal | On default |

### Automation Opportunity
- Exposure-at-default monitoring; referral-default-correlation analytics; recovery-case management

### Controls
- CTL: BuildRight exposure monitored (materials/services paid before delivery W4568); fair recovery (VS-100.3); default feeds pre-qualification tuning (W4557)
