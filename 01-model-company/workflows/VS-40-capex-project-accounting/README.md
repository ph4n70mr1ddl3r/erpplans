# VS-40: Capex & Project Accounting

> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages capital expenditure workflow and project accounting across BuildRight Depot's capital program — including new store construction (~10–15/year × PHP 40–60M), DC expansion and automation, major IT infrastructure projects, store renovation/refresh programs, and fleet additions. Covers the full capex lifecycle from request and approval through commitment tracking, construction-in-progress (CIP) accounting, milestone-based cost capitalization, asset turnover upon completion, and post-implementation review. With annual capex of PHP 800M–1.2B, rigorous project accounting ensures capital is deployed efficiently, costs are controlled against budgets, and PFRS capitalization rules are consistently applied across 5 legal entities.
