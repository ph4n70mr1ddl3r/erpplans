## W1827. Construction-in-Progress (CIP) Account Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | First cost incurred on approved capex project |
| **Frequency** | Continuous for active projects |
| **Volume** | ~10–20 active CIP accounts at any time |
| **Owner** | Project Accountant |
| **Participants** | Project Accountant, Accounting Manager, FP&A |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CIP account structure in GL: (a) one CIP account per active capex project (e.g., CIP-BRD-001 Mindanao Store, CIP-IT-ERP-Upgrade), (b) sub-accounts by cost category: materials, labor, services, equipment, professional fees, capitalized interest, (c) entity-specific: each entity's CIP tracked separately, (d) group-level CIP visible for consolidation | System | Accounting Manager | Per project creation |
| 2 | Monthly CIP management: (a) all project costs accumulated in CIP (not depreciated — CIP assets are not yet in use), (b) monthly review per W1705 (CIP reconciliation), (c) CIP aging report: flag projects in CIP > 12 months (risk of stalled or abandoned projects), (d) CIP balance should increase as project progresses and decrease to zero when asset is turned over | Project Accountant | Accounting Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | CIP balance sheet presentation: (a) CIP classified as non-current asset (part of Property, Plant & Equipment) in balance sheet, (b) separate line item or combined with fixed assets (materiality-dependent), (c) CIP balance = total costs incurred but not yet capitalized as completed assets | Accounting Manager | CFO | At reporting date |
| 4 | Impairment of CIP: if project is stalled or abandoned, CIP must be assessed for impairment per PFRS 136 (same as W1700 for completed assets); abandoned projects written off per W2750 | Project Accountant | Accounting Manager | If triggered |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project Manager confirms practical completion: (a) all construction/installation work complete (punch list items may remain but are not critical to operation), (b) systems tested and operational (IT projects: UAT passed; stores: POS live), (c) safety inspection passed, (d) occupancy permit received (if required), (e) asset is **ready for its intended use** per PFRS — this is the trigger for depreciation start (not the grand opening date) | Project Manager | VP Real Estate / CIO | 1 day |

## W1829. CIP-to-Fixed Asset Conversion Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project completion certified (W1828) |
| **Frequency** | ~20–30 conversions/year |
| **Volume** | 1 conversion per completed project (may result in multiple fixed assets) |
| **Owner** | Fixed Asset Accountant |
| **Participants** | Fixed Asset Accountant, Project Accountant, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fixed Asset Accountant decomposes project cost into component assets per W1694: (a) **new store example** (total cost PHP 50M): (i) leasehold improvements: PHP 20M (depreciate over lease term 10–15 years), (ii) HVAC system: PHP 5M (10–15 years), (iii) electrical installation: PHP 4M (15–20 years), (iv) plumbing: PHP 3M (15–20 years), (v) racking/shelving: PHP 5M (10 years), (vi) POS system (3 terminals): PHP 1.5M (5 years), (vii) signage: PHP 2M (7–10 years), (viii) generator: PHP 1.5M (7 years), (ix) CCTV system: PHP 1M (5 years), (x) initial inventory: PHP 5M (not fixed asset — transferred to inventory), (xi) pre-opening expenses (training, marketing): PHP 2M (not fixed asset — expensed), (xii) contingency unused: returned to capex pool | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 1–2 days/project |
| 2 | System processes CIP-to-asset conversion: (a) CIP account debited to zero, (b) new fixed asset records created with component structures, (c) each component assigned useful life and depreciation method per W1691, (d) depreciation begins from the **ready-for-use date** (completion certificate date, not grand opening date), (e) pre-opening and non-capitalizable costs reclassified from CIP to expense | System | Fixed Asset Accountant | 30 min/project |
| 3 | Journal entries posted: (a) debit: fixed asset accounts (gross) per component, (b) credit: CIP account (cleared to zero), (c) debit: pre-opening expense (for non-capitalized costs), (d) debit: inventory (for initial stock), (e) credit: CIP (for above reclassifications) | System | Accounting Manager | Automated |
| 4 | Fixed Asset Accountant verifies new asset records: (a) total asset value = CIP balance before conversion (no unexplained difference), (b) depreciation start date correct, (c) useful life per component is appropriate, (d) asset tags ordered per W1692 | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 1 day |

## W2748. Post-Implementation Review & Actual vs. Projected ROI Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | 12 months after project completion |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 post-implementation reviews/year |
| **Volume** | 1 review per completed project |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, Project Sponsor, Department Head, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A compares actual project outcomes to original business case: (a) **cost**: actual total cost vs. approved budget (typically actual is 5–15% over budget for construction projects), (b) **timeline**: actual completion date vs. planned (typically 1–3 months delayed), (c) **revenue**: for new stores — actual Year 1 revenue vs. projected revenue per W1652, (d) **profitability**: store EBITDA margin vs. projected, (e) **payback**: actual payback trajectory vs. projected payback period, (f) **IRR/NPV**: recalculate with actual cash flows | FP&A | CFO | 1–2 days/project |
| 2 | For non-revenue projects (IT, equipment replacement): (a) cost savings achieved vs. projected, (b) operational improvements measured vs. baseline, (c) user satisfaction survey, (d) compliance achieved (if regulatory project) | FP&A | Department Head | 1 day/project |
| 3 | FP&A prepares post-implementation review report: (a) summary of actual vs. projected, (b) key learnings (what went well, what didn't), (c) calibration: how accurate were original projections? (d) recommendations for future similar projects, (e) presented to steering committee or CFO | FP&A | CFO | 1 day |
| 4 | Annual: FP&A compiles post-implementation results across all completed projects; identifies systematic biases in project estimation (e.g., consistently overestimating revenue, underestimating costs); adjusts estimation methodology for future business cases | FP&A | CFO | 1 day/year |

| **Owner** | Fixed Asset Accountant |
| **Participants** | Fixed Asset Accountant, Project Manager, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scenarios requiring partial turnover: (a) DC expansion: new wing operational while second wing still under construction, (b) multi-store build: first 3 stores of a 5-store batch open before remaining 2, (c) IT rollout: phase 1 modules live while phase 2 still in development | Project Manager | Department Head | Event-driven |
| 2 | Fixed Asset Accountant allocates CIP costs between completed and in-progress portions: (a) completed portion: identify specific costs attributable to the ready-for-use portion, (b) in-progress portion: remaining costs stay in CIP, (c) apportionment methodology: direct identification where possible; proportional allocation for shared costs (e.g., project management, permits) | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 1–2 days |

## W2750. Abandoned Project Write-Off & Cost Recovery

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Decision to abandon or indefinitely suspend a capex project |
| **Frequency** | ~1–3 abandoned projects/year (low frequency but high impact) |
| **Volume** | Low |
| **Owner** | CFO |
| **Participants** | CFO, FP&A, Project Manager, CEO, Board |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Triggers for project abandonment: (a) site issues discovered during construction (soil, legal, environmental), (b) market conditions change (trade area no longer viable — competitor opens nearby, economic downturn), (c) regulatory block (permit denied, zoning change), (d) strategic pivot (company changes expansion plan), (e) contractor failure (contractor abandons project) | Project Manager / VP Real Estate | CEO | Event-driven |
| 2 | CFO and FP&A assess financial impact: (a) total CIP costs incurred to date, (b) recovery potential: (i) materials salvageable and reusable (transfer to other projects), (ii) equipment already purchased (sell, return to vendor, or redeploy), (iii) contractor claims and counterclaims, (iv) insurance recovery (if applicable), (c) net write-off amount = CIP costs − recoveries | CFO / FP&A | CEO | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Write-off approval: (a) < PHP 5M: CFO approval, (b) PHP 5–50M: CEO approval, (c) > PHP 50M: Board approval, (d) write-off recognized as non-current asset disposal loss (not inventory write-off) | Approver per tier | Board (if material) | 1–5 days |
| 4 | Write-off processing: (a) CIP balance written off to P&L (debit: project abandonment loss, credit: CIP), (b) recoverable amounts recorded as receivable, (c) when recovery received: credit to project abandonment loss (partial offset), (d) Board notified at next meeting regardless of amount | Project Accountant / Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 1–2 days |
| 5 | Lessons learned documented per W2751; preventive measures implemented to reduce future abandonment risk | VP Real Estate / CIO | CEO | 1 week |

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

| 1 | FP&A prepares annual capex portfolio review: (a) total capex spent vs. budget (by category: new stores, renovations, DC, IT, equipment), (b) project delivery performance: % on time, % on budget, % achieving projected ROI, (c) cost estimation accuracy: average budget variance per project type, (d) timeline estimation accuracy: average schedule variance, (e) post-implementation ROI results: actual vs. projected across completed projects, (f) abandoned project cost and reasons, (g) capital productivity: revenue growth ÷ capex invested | FP&A | CFO | 1 week |
