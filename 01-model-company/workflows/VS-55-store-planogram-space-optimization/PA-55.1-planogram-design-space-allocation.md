## W2166. Seasonal Planogram Refresh

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Seasonal calendar trigger (6 rotations/year) |
| **Frequency** | Bi-monthly; 200 stores × 8 zones |
| **Volume** | ~1,600 planogram updates per rotation |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Planner |
| **Participants** | Merch Planner, Category Mgr, Store Ops Director |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Planner reviews category performance (sales/sqm, margin/sqm, turns); designs planogram with space planning software assigning linear meterage per category | Merchandising Planner | Category Manager | 2–3 days |
| 2 | Category Manager validates assortment alignment and cross-merchandising; Store Ops Director reviews format-specific adaptations for large/standard/compact stores | Category Mgr / Store Ops Dir | VP Merchandising | 4–6 hours |
| 3 | System generates store-specific planogram packages; distributes to stores via VS-63 communication system | System | Merchandising Planner | Automated |
