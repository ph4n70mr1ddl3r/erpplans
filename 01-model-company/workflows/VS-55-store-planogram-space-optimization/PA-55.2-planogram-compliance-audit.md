## W2174. Planogram Compliance Audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly per store |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~200 audits/month |
| **Owner** | Regional Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Regional Ops Mgr, Store Manager, Merch Planner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Regional Ops Manager walks store zone by zone comparing actual to planogram; scores compliance (SKU position, facing count, labels, displays) | Regional Ops Mgr | Store Ops Director | 2–3 hours/store |
| 2 | Photographs non-compliant zones; uploads to compliance system; Store Manager remediates within 48 hours; monthly scorecard (target ≥90%) | Regional Ops Mgr / Store Manager | Store Ops Director | 2–4 hours |
