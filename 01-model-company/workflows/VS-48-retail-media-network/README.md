# VS-48: Retail Media Network & Vendor Advertising

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages BuildRight's retail media network — the sale of advertising space and promotional opportunities to vendors and third-party brands across BuildRight's physical and digital channels. Covers in-store advertising (endcaps, shelf talkers, digital screens), ecommerce advertising (sponsored products, banner ads), and print advertising (catalog, flyers). Retail media is a growing revenue stream for big-box retailers, allowing vendors to purchase premium visibility where purchase decisions are made. Targets ~PHP 50–100M in annual media revenue from BuildRight's 800–1,000 vendor base.
