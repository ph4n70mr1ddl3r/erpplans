## W1998. Retail Media Inventory Audit & Availability Mapping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual media inventory audit; quarterly refresh |
| **Frequency** | Annual full audit; quarterly availability update |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × media placements per store |
| **Owner** | Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Manager, Visual Merchandiser, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing Manager catalogs all advertising inventory: (a) in-store: (i) endcap displays (4–8 per store), (ii) power aisle promotional bays (2–4 per store), (iii) shelf talkers and wobblers (unlimited, per SKU), (iv) checkout area posters (3 per store, near POS), (v) digital screens (if installed, 1–2 per store), (vi) floor graphics (2–4 per store), (vii) shopping cart ads (if applicable), (b) ecommerce: (i) sponsored product slots (top 3 search results per category), (ii) homepage banner (1–3 slots), (iii) category page banner (1 per category × 12 categories), (iv) product page recommendation carousel, (c) print: (i) catalog back cover, inside covers, full pages, half pages, (ii) flyer inserts | Marketing Manager | CMO | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | Quantify total media inventory: (a) in-store: 200 stores × average 15 placements = ~3,000 in-store placements, (b) ecommerce: ~50 digital ad positions, (c) print: ~20–30 catalog positions (6 catalogs/year) | Marketing Manager | CMO | 2–3 days |
| 3 | Quarterly: update availability based on store remodels, new digital signage installations, and ecommerce platform changes | Marketing Manager | CMO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Media inventory catalog in marketing module (W1998.1)
- Availability tracking per placement per store (W1998.2)
- Integration with W86 (planogram) for endcap and display placement mapping

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inventory accuracy**: Media placements may be physically removed or relocated by store staff without marketing knowledge; quarterly physical verification needed


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "print" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "catalog positions" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-update of "update availability based on store remodels, new digital signage installations,…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-193 (ensure retail-media billing & placement delivery.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks initial; 1 day/quarter update

## W1999. Retail Media Rate Card Development & Pricing Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual rate card review; new media type launch |
| **Frequency** | Annual review; per new media type |
| **Volume** | ~30–50 rate card line items |
| **Owner** | CMO |
| **Participants** | CMO, Pricing Analyst, CFO, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing Analyst develops rate card: (a) in-store: (i) endcap: PHP 50K–200K/month per store (varies by location tier), (ii) shelf talker: PHP 5K–15K/month per SKU per store, (iii) checkout poster: PHP 30K–80K/month per store, (iv) digital screen: PHP 20K–50K/month per store, (b) ecommerce: (i) sponsored product: CPC (cost per click) PHP 5–20 per click, (ii) homepage banner: PHP 100K–300K/month, (iii) category banner: PHP 30K–80K/month, (c) print: (i) catalog back cover: PHP 500K–1M per edition, (ii) full page: PHP 150K–300K per edition | Pricing Analyst | CMO | 1 week |
| 2 | CFO reviews media pricing for margin impact: (a) media revenue is incremental (doesn't reduce merchandise margin), (b) ensure media pricing is competitive vs. alternative advertising channels (Facebook, Google, radio), (c) target: media revenue at 30–50% gross margin after production and placement costs | CFO | CMO | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Annual: CMO benchmarks media rates against Philippine retail media market and digital advertising market; adjust rates upward as BuildRight's traffic and sales data become more valuable to vendors | CMO | COO | 1 day/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Rate card management in marketing module (W1999.1)
- Pricing governance with CFO approval (W1999.2)
- Integration with W40 (price management) for rate governance

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rate justification**: Vendors may question media rates; BuildRight must demonstrate ROI with sales lift data from past campaigns


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "print" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews media pricing for margin impact" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-385 (ensure controlled execution — Retail Media Platform & Inventory Management (PA-48.1).)
- operational: CMO review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 1 week initial; 1 day/year review

## W2000. In-Store Advertising Placement Setup & Maintenance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor books in-store media placement |
| **Frequency** | ~100–200 in-store placements/month across chain |
| **Volume** | ~3,000 in-store placements capacity |
| **Owner** | Visual Merchandiser |
| **Participants** | Visual Merchandiser, Store Manager, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visual Merchandiser receives placement order: (a) vendor, product, placement type, store list, duration, (b) creative assets: vendor provides artwork per BuildRight specifications (size, format, resolution, brand guidelines compliance), (c) if no creative: BuildRight in-house creative produces per vendor brief (additional fee) | Visual Merchandiser | Marketing Manager | Per placement |
| 2 | Production: (a) shelf talkers/wobblers: print per store count, (b) posters: print per format and store count, (c) endcap displays: coordinate with store for product placement and signage, (d) digital screen content: upload to content management system | Visual Merchandiser | Marketing Manager | 3–5 days |
| 3 | Distribution and installation: (a) ship printed materials to stores via DC replenishment, (b) Store Manager assigns Stock Associate for installation, (c) installation timeline: within 3 business days of receiving materials, (d) photo verification: Store Manager sends installation photo to Visual Merchandiser for confirmation | Visual Merchandiser / Store Manager | Marketing Manager | Per campaign |
| 4 | Maintenance: (a) weekly: Visual Merchandiser checks installation compliance via store photos, (b) damaged or missing materials: reorder and re-ship, (c) campaign end: Store Manager removes materials within 3 business days of campaign end date | Visual Merchandiser / Store Manager | Marketing Manager | Per campaign |

### System Touchpoints
- Placement order management with creative workflow (W2000.1)
- Distribution tracking via DC logistics (W2000.3)
- Photo verification of installation (W2000.3)
- Integration with W262 (store promotional setup) for physical execution

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Installation non-compliance**: Store staff may not install materials promptly or correctly; photo verification and compliance scoring per store needed


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "print per store count" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-send of "sends installation photo to Visual Merchandiser for confirmation" (replaces manual Step 3).
- System auto-validate of "checks installation compliance via store photos" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-193 (ensure retail-media billing & placement delivery.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 days per campaign setup; ongoing maintenance

## W2001. Ecommerce Sponsored Product & Banner Ad Position Management

| **Frequency** | ~50–100 ecommerce ad campaigns/month |
| **Volume** | ~50 digital ad positions |

| **Participants** | Digital Marketing Manager, Ecommerce Manager, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Digital Marketing Manager sets up sponsored product campaign: (a) vendor selects SKUs to sponsor, (b) targeting: category, search keywords, customer segments, (c) bidding: CPC model — vendor sets maximum CPC bid, (d) budget: daily or monthly cap, (e) ad creative: product image + promotional copy + price display | Digital Marketing Manager | CMO | 1–2 days/campaign |

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor or Account Manager books media placement |
| **Frequency** | ~50–100 bookings/month |
| **Volume** | ~3,050 total media positions (in-store + ecommerce + print) |
| **Owner** | Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Manager, Account Manager, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account Manager creates booking: (a) select media type and specific placement, (b) select store list (all 200, regional, specific stores), (c) select duration (1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, per catalog edition), (d) system checks availability: is placement already booked for requested period?, (e) if available: reserve placement, (f) if unavailable: offer alternatives (different store, different dates, different placement type) | Account Manager | Marketing Manager | 15–30 min/booking |
| 2 | Booking confirmation: (a) system generates booking confirmation with: placement details, rate, duration, total cost, creative submission deadline, installation timeline, (b) send to vendor for approval, (c) vendor approves (or requests changes), (d) booking locked — placement reserved | System / Account Manager | Marketing Manager | Per booking |
| 3 | Booking calendar management: (a) monthly view of all placements by store and media type, (b) identify unbooked inventory for promotional pricing or internal use, (c) overbooking prevention: system blocks double-booking of same placement | Marketing Manager | CMO | 1–2 hours/week |

- Integration with W13 (promotional calendar) for coordination

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Underutilization**: ~30–40% of media inventory may go unsold; offer promotional rates to fill inventory or use for BuildRight's own marketing


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "catalog edition" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "generates booking confirmation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-identify of "identify unbooked inventory for promotional pricing or internal use" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-385 (ensure controlled execution — Retail Media Platform & Inventory Management (PA-48.1).)

## W2005. Retail Media Conflict Resolution & Competitive Separation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Two competing vendors book adjacent or overlapping placements |
| **Frequency** | ~5–10 conflicts/month |
| **Volume** | Per conflict |
| **Owner** | Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Manager, Category Manager, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System detects competitive conflict: (a) two vendors in same product category (e.g., two paint brands) booked endcaps in same store during same period, (b) two competing appliance brands booked homepage banner in same week, (c) system flags conflict for Marketing Manager review | System | Marketing Manager | Automated |
| 2 | Marketing Manager resolves conflict: (a) competitive separation policy: competing brands should not be placed adjacent or in same visual zone during same period, (b) options: (i) offer different dates to one vendor, (ii) offer different stores (geographic separation), (iii) offer different placement types within same store (different departments), (c) if both vendors insist on same placement: first-booked priority, or highest-bid priority for contested placements | Marketing Manager | CMO | 30–60 min/conflict |
| 3 | Annual: review competitive separation policy with VP Merchandising: (a) is policy too restrictive (leaving revenue on table)?, (b) is policy too lenient (creating vendor dissatisfaction)?, (c) adjust based on vendor feedback and revenue impact | Marketing Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 day/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Automated competitive conflict detection (W2005.1)
- Booking system separation rules (W2005.2)
- Integration with W252 (item master) for competitor classification

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vendor pressure**: Major vendors may demand exclusive placements; balance revenue maximization with vendor relationship management


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-test of "contested placements" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-385 (ensure controlled execution — Retail Media Platform & Inventory Management (PA-48.1).)
- operational: Marketing Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 30–60 min per conflict; 1 day/year policy review
