## W2006. Vendor Media Campaign Briefing & Creative Development

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor books media campaign |
| **Frequency** | ~50–100 campaigns/quarter |
| **Volume** | Per campaign |
| **Owner** | Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Account Manager, Creative Designer, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account Manager conducts campaign briefing with vendor: (a) campaign objectives: brand awareness, new product launch, promotional sales lift, (b) target audience: DIY homeowners, trade professionals, specific category buyers, (c) key message and call-to-action, (d) campaign duration and channels, (e) creative assets available from vendor or BuildRight creative needed | Account Manager | Marketing Manager | 30–60 min |
| 2 | Creative development: (a) if vendor provides creative: review for BuildRight brand guidelines compliance, (b) if BuildRight produces: Creative Designer develops per vendor brief (additional production fee), (c) creative specifications: size, resolution, format per media type, (d) vendor approves final creative before production | Creative Designer | Marketing Manager | 3–5 days |
| 3 | Production and distribution: (a) print materials: produced and shipped to stores per W2000, (b) digital content: uploaded to CMS or ecommerce platform per W2001/W2003 | Account Manager / Visual Merchandiser | Marketing Manager | 3–5 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Campaign brief management with creative workflow (W2006.2)
- Integration with W14.1 (campaign planning) for promotional calendar alignment

### Time Estimate
- 30–60 min briefing; 3–5 days creative; 3–5 days production

## W2007. Multi-Channel Campaign Orchestration & Launch

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Campaign creative approved and ready for launch |
| **Frequency** | ~30–50 multi-channel campaigns/quarter |
| **Volume** | Per campaign |
| **Owner** | Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Manager, Visual Merchandiser, Digital Marketing Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Campaign orchestration: (a) simultaneous launch across booked channels: in-store placements, ecommerce ads, digital signage, (b) coordinate timing with vendor's promotional pricing activation per W13, (c) ensure product availability at stores running campaign (ATP check) | Marketing Manager | CMO | 1 day/campaign |
| 2 | Launch checklist: (a) in-store materials installed per W2000, (b) ecommerce ads live and serving, (c) digital signage content playing, (d) print catalog/flyer distributed (if applicable), (e) all placements verified with photo evidence or screenshot | Marketing Manager | CMO | Per launch |

### System Touchpoints
- Campaign launch management with channel coordination (W2007.1)
- Integration with W13 (promotions) for pricing alignment

### Time Estimate
- 1 day orchestration; 1–2 hours post-launch monitoring

## W2008. In-Store Campaign Compliance Monitoring & Photo Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Campaign active; weekly compliance cycle |
| **Frequency** | Weekly during active campaigns |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 active in-store campaigns |
| **Owner** | Visual Merchandiser |
| **Participants** | Visual Merchandiser, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weekly: Visual Merchandiser requests campaign compliance photos from stores: (a) Store Manager photographs each active placement, (b) uploads to campaign management system, (c) Visual Merchandiser reviews: (i) correct placement location, (ii) materials in good condition, (iii) products displayed correctly on endcaps | Visual Merchandiser / Store Manager | Marketing Manager | 15 min/store/week |
| 2 | Compliance scoring: (a) fully compliant: materials installed correctly and in good condition, (b) partially compliant: installed but with issues (wrong location, damaged materials), (c) non-compliant: not installed or removed, (d) store compliance score tracked per W67 (store performance) | Visual Merchandiser | Marketing Manager | 1–2 hours/week |
| 3 | Remediation: (a) non-compliant stores: re-ship materials and request re-installation within 3 days, (b) repeated non-compliance: escalate to Regional Manager, (c) compliance rate target: ≥ 90% of stores compliant within first week of campaign | Visual Merchandiser / Store Manager | Marketing Manager | Per campaign |

### System Touchpoints
- Photo upload and compliance review system (W2008.1)
- Store compliance dashboard (W2008.2)
- Integration with W67 (store performance review)

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 hours/week for Visual Merchandiser; 15 min/store/week for Store Manager

## W2009. Ecommerce Campaign Performance Optimization & A/B Testing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Ecommerce ad campaign active; optimization cycle |
| **Frequency** | Continuous during active campaigns |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 active ecommerce campaigns |
| **Owner** | Digital Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Digital Marketing Manager, Ecommerce Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daily monitoring: (a) impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, spend, conversions (sales attributed to ad), (b) ROAS: revenue from ad clicks ÷ ad spend, (c) flag campaigns with CTR < 0.5% or ROAS < 3x for optimization | Digital Marketing Manager | CMO | 30 min/day |
| 2 | Optimization actions: (a) adjust bids: increase CPC for high-performing keywords, decrease for low-performing, (b) A/B test: rotate banner creative, test different headlines, (c) targeting refinement: narrow audience segments for better conversion, (d) budget reallocation: shift spend from underperforming to overperforming placements | Digital Marketing Manager | CMO | 1–2 hours/week |
| 3 | Monthly: report optimization results to vendor with recommendations for next campaign cycle | Digital Marketing Manager | CMO | Per vendor |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce advertising analytics dashboard (W2009.1)
- A/B testing module (W2009.2)
- Integration with W50 (PIM) for product data and W19 (ecommerce) for sales attribution

### Time Estimate
- 30 min/day; 1–2 hours/week optimization

## W2010. Vendor Co-Op Advertising Fund Integration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor has co-op advertising fund agreement per W513 |
| **Frequency** | Per campaign; ~50–100 co-op funded campaigns/year |
| **Volume** | ~30–50% of media campaigns funded partly by vendor co-op |
| **Owner** | Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Account Manager, Marketing Manager, AP Clerk, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account Manager checks vendor co-op fund availability: (a) annual co-op budget per vendor agreement per W513, (b) YTD co-op spend, (c) remaining balance available for current campaign | Account Manager | Marketing Manager | Per campaign |
| 2 | Campaign cost allocation: (a) total campaign cost: BuildRight media rate + production costs, (b) vendor co-op contribution: per agreement (typically 50–100% of media cost), (c) BuildRight contribution: remaining portion, (d) system records co-op fund utilization per vendor | Account Manager / System | Marketing Manager | Per campaign |
| 3 | Post-campaign settlement: (a) AP Clerk processes co-op reimbursement claim to vendor: proof of execution (photos, screenshots), campaign performance summary, (b) vendor reimburses BuildRight per co-op terms, or (c) offset against AP (vendor credit memo) | AP Clerk | Accounting Manager | Per campaign |

### System Touchpoints
- Co-op fund tracking per vendor (W2010.1)
- Campaign cost allocation with co-op split (W2010.2)
- Integration with W513 (vendor co-op advertising) for fund management

### Time Estimate
- 15–30 min per campaign for co-op coordination

## W2011. Retail Media Campaign Reporting & Vendor Presentation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Campaign completion; monthly/quarterly reporting cycle |
| **Frequency** | Per campaign completion; monthly vendor reports |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 campaign reports/quarter |
| **Owner** | Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Manager, Account Manager, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates campaign performance report: (a) in-store: store compliance rate, estimated impressions (foot traffic × placement location), associated product sales lift (pre vs. during campaign), (b) ecommerce: impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, ROAS, attributed revenue, (c) print: distribution count, redemption rate (if promo code used) | System | Marketing Manager | Automated |
| 2 | Account Manager presents report to vendor: (a) campaign performance vs. objectives, (b) ROI analysis: vendor investment vs. sales lift, (c) recommendations for next campaign: optimization insights, (d) upsell: suggest additional placements or channels based on performance | Account Manager | Marketing Manager | 30–60 min/vendor |
| 3 | Quarterly: Marketing Manager compiles retail media network performance for CMO: (a) total media revenue, (b) fill rate: % of inventory sold, (c) vendor retention: % of vendors rebooking, (d) top-performing placements by revenue and vendor ROI | Marketing Manager | CMO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Automated campaign performance reporting (W2011.1)
- Integration with W35 (management reporting) for consolidated media revenue view

### Time Estimate
- 30–60 min per vendor presentation; 1 day/quarter consolidated

## W2012. Seasonal Media Package Development & Sales

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Seasonal planning cycle (aligned to BuildRight's seasonal calendar) |
| **Frequency** | 6 seasonal packages/year (bi-monthly) |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 seasonal packages sold/year |
| **Owner** | Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Manager, Account Manager, VP Merchandising, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing Manager develops seasonal media package: (a) season: summer (garden, outdoor, aircon), rainy (waterproofing, flood control), Ber-months (holiday, home improvement), etc., (b) package components: (i) endcap in all stores for 2 weeks, (ii) homepage banner for 1 week, (iii) catalog feature page, (iv) social media mention, (c) package pricing: 20–30% discount vs. à la carte booking, (d) limited availability: 5–10 packages per season | Marketing Manager | CMO | 1 week/season |
| 2 | Account Manager sells seasonal packages to vendors: (a) priority offer to existing media vendors, (b) present seasonal sales opportunity (higher foot traffic during seasonal peaks), (c) close deals before seasonal catalog deadline | Account Manager | Marketing Manager | 2–3 weeks/season |
| 3. Execution: (a) package campaigns launched per W2007 multi-channel orchestration, (b) all package components tracked as single campaign | Marketing Manager | CMO | Per season |

### System Touchpoints
- Seasonal package configuration and booking (W2012.1)
- Integration with W13 (promotions) for seasonal calendar alignment

### Time Estimate
- 1 week package design; 2–3 weeks selling

## W2013. Retail Media Campaign Exception & Quality Issue Resolution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Media campaign quality issue or exception |
| **Frequency** | ~10–20 quality issues/quarter |
| **Volume** | Per issue |
| **Owner** | Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Manager, Visual Merchandiser, Digital Marketing Manager, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Issue identification: (a) in-store: materials not installed, installed incorrectly, damaged, removed prematurely, (b) ecommerce: ad not serving, creative error, wrong product linked, (c) digital signage: screen malfunction, wrong content playing | Marketing Manager | CMO | Per issue |
| 2 | Resolution: (a) in-store: re-ship and re-install within 5 days, (b) ecommerce: fix within 24 hours, (c) digital signage: fix within 48 hours, (d) for all issues: extend campaign duration to compensate for downtime/issue period | Marketing Manager / Visual Merchandiser | CMO | Per issue |
| 3. Vendor compensation: (a) if issue caused by BuildRight: credit vendor for affected period (pro-rata refund or campaign extension), (b) if issue caused by vendor (late creative, wrong specs): no compensation, (c) document all issues for quality improvement tracking | Marketing Manager | CMO | Per issue |

### System Touchpoints
- Issue tracking and resolution workflow (W2013.1)
- Vendor compensation tracking (W2013.3)

### Time Estimate
- 30–60 min per issue
