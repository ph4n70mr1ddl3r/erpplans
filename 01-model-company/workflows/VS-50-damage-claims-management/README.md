# VS-50: Damage & Claims Management

> **Make & Move** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages the end-to-end lifecycle of product damage identification, documentation, claims processing, and financial recovery across BuildRight's supply chain. Covers damage occurring during inbound transit (vendor→DC), outbound transit (DC→store, store→customer), in-store (shelf damage, customer damage), and during storage. Integrates with carrier claims, vendor recovery, insurance claims, and shrinkage management to maximize recovery and minimize loss. Damage and shrinkage represent ~1–1.5% of sales (PHP 620M–930M annually), making effective claims management a significant P&L lever.
