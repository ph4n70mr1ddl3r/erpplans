## W3481. Remodel Fixture, Equipment & FF&E Procurement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Design package (W3477); capex approved (W3476) |
| **Frequency** | Per remodel |
| **Volume** | Fixtures, shelving, counters, equipment per store |
| **Owner** | Procurement / Store Design |
| **Participants** | Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34), Store Design, Facilities (links to VS-20.3), Finance (links to VS-40), Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store Design/Procurement compile the FF&E and equipment list from the design package (W3477): gondola/shelving, endcaps, service counters, paint-mixing station (links to VS-09.1), cutting equipment, tool-rental fixtures (links to VS-12.2), checkout counters (links to VS-08), backroom/storage, and specialty equipment | Store Design / Procurement | VP Store Ops | Per remodel |
| 2 | Procurement source via framework agreements / RFQ (links to VS-03/VS-34): vendor selection, lead-time management, import/customs for specialty fixtures (links to VS-02.2/VS-87), quality inspection (links to VS-31), and alignment to the project schedule (W3480); reuse/refurbish reusable fixtures where economical | Procurement / Vendor / Quality | VP Store Ops | Per remodel |
| 3 | Procurement/Facilities coordinate delivery sequencing to site (links to VS-04/VS-06), staging/storage, and handoff to installation (W3485); capture assets into the fixed-asset register (links to VS-35) and manage warranty/serials | Procurement / Facilities | VP Store Ops | Per remodel |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34); import/customs (links to VS-02.2/VS-87); quality (links to VS-31); asset register (links to VS-35); capex (links to VS-40)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lead-time risk**: Imported fixtures slip the schedule; mitigated by framework agreements and early order
- **Asset-registration gap**: Remodel fixtures not capitalized; mitigated by FA capture (links to VS-35)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "checkout counters" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-inspect of "inspection" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System workflow orchestration of "coordinate delivery sequencing to site" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: framework agreements and early order; operational: FA capture (links to VS-35)

### Time Estimate
- Aligned to schedule; ordering 4–10 weeks lead
