## W3489. Remodel Soft Launch / Grand Re-opening & Marketing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Handover complete (W3488); re-opening date |
| **Frequency** | Per remodel |
| **Volume** | ~30–40 re-openings/year |
| **Owner** | Marketing / Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing (links to VS-14), Store Manager (links to VS-07), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Loyalty (links to VS-13), Regional Manager (links to VS-07) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing/Store Manager plan the re-opening: soft-launch (staff/VIP/trade links to VS-43) followed by grand re-opening event, local marketing (links to VS-14.1), loyalty-member and trade-account outreach (links to VS-13/VS-43), re-opening promotions (links to VS-58/VS-14), and PR/community (links to VS-14.3/VS-104) | Marketing / Store Manager | CMO | Per remodel |
| 2 | Marketing/Merchandising execute re-opening offers, trade-area catchment marketing, and signage/POS materials (links to VS-14.1/W3485); ensure the loyalty engine (links to VS-13), POS (links to VS-08), and ecommerce inventory/price sync (links to VS-10) reflect the new store state | Marketing / Merchandising / IT | CMO | Per remodel |
| 3 | Store Manager/Marketing run the re-opening event, capture footfall/sales/feedback (links to VS-13/VS-44), and transition to steady-state operations (links to VS-07); report re-opening results to the program review (W3491/W3496) | Store Manager / Marketing | VP Store Operations | Per remodel |

### System Touchpoints
- Marketing (links to VS-14); loyalty (links to VS-13); POS (links to VS-08); ecommerce (links to VS-10); promotions (links to VS-58); PR/community (links to VS-14.3/VS-104)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Weak-launch risk**: Poor marketing caps the remodel's sales lift; mitigated by targeted trade-area campaign
- **Channel-inconsistency risk**: Online inventory/price out of sync with the new store; mitigated by ecommerce sync check (links to VS-10)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "loyalty-member and trade-account outreach" lists (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-verify of "ecommerce inventory/price sync" against the new store state (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-capture of "footfall/sales/feedback" into the re-opening scorecard (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-558 (ensure controlled execution — Re-merchandising, Re-opening & Remodel Performance Analytics (PA-109.3).)
- operational: targeted trade-area campaign; operational: ecommerce sync check (links to VS-10)

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per re-opening

## W3491. Post-Remodel Sales Lift & Performance Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Re-opening (W3489); monthly/quarterly cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + 6/12-month milestone |
| **Volume** | All recently-remodeled stores |
| **Owner** | FP&A / VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | FP&A (links to VS-17.4/VS-33.2), VP Store Operations, Merchandising (links to VS-01/VS-101), BI (links to VS-28.1), Regional Manager (links to VS-07) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A/BI track post-remodel performance: comp-sales lift vs pre-remodel baseline and vs non-remodeled control stores, traffic, ATV/basket, category mix (links to VS-01), gross margin (links to VS-101), inventory turn (links to VS-05), and conversion; normalize for seasonality and external factors | FP&A / BI | VP Store Operations | Monthly |
| 2 | FP&A/Merchandising reconcile actual lift to the business case (W3476): payback/IRR tracking, lost-sales recovery, and category-level contribution; identify laggards and root cause (assortment, pricing, marketing, macro) | FP&A / Merchandising | CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | VP Store Operations/FP&A report remodel performance to the program review (W3496) and executives (links to VS-33.2), re-baseline laggards, and feed insights to future remodel concepts (W3475) and the roadmap (W3473/W3495) | VP Store Operations / FP&A | COO / CFO | Quarterly/annual |

### System Touchpoints
- BI/FP&A (links to VS-28.1/VS-17.4/VS-33.2); merchandising (links to VS-01/VS-101); inventory (links to VS-05); capex/benefits (links to VS-40/W3561)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lift-attribution risk**: Macro/seasonality mistaken for remodel lift; mitigated by control-store comparison
- **Benefit-shortfall risk**: Lift below business case; mitigated by root-cause and corrective action


### Automation Opportunity

- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile actual lift to the business case" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "VP Store Operations/FP&A report remodel performance to the program" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-558 (ensure controlled execution — Re-merchandising, Re-opening & Remodel Performance Analytics (PA-109.3).)

### Time Estimate
- Monthly + 6/12-month review
