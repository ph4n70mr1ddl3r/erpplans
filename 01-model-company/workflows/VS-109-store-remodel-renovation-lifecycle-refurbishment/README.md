## Overview

Store Remodel, Renovation & Lifecycle Refurbishment workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **in-life store-asset renewal program** for the ~200 big-box stores (8,000–15,000
sqm selling area each, per profile §3.1 = ~1.6–3.0M sqm aggregate). A 200-store chain running at
PHP 62.3B revenue remodels or refurbishes stores on a roughly 5–7-year cycle to refresh the store
format, modernize departments and fixtures, expand or contract categories, upgrade
mechanical/electrical/POS technology, repair wear, and reposition the store for its trade area —
typically ~30–40 remodel events/year at PHP 8–25M each. This value stream owns the full remodel
lifecycle: portfolio strategy and lifecycle planning, store qualification and scope/concept
definition, design and fixture planning, business case and capex funding, permitting and
live-operations phasing, construction execution around a trading store, FF&E and signage rollout,
technology/POS refresh, merchandise reset and planogram implementation, soft-launch/re-opening and
marketing, post-remodel performance tracking, and program analytics/governance.

This is distinct from **VS-37 (Store Opening & Commissioning)** which brings a *new* store from
greenfield/shell to first trade (a net-new location), and from **VS-59 (Store Closure &
Decommissioning)** which winds a store down. It is distinct from **VS-20 (Real Estate &
Construction)** which delivers the *building shell / civil works* for new sites (and whose PA-20.2
engineering & construction is for new-build), and from **VS-97 (Corporate Real Estate & Property
Portfolio)** which is the *landlord/owner* investment view. It is distinct from **VS-40 (Capex &
Project Accounting)** which performs the *financial accounting* of remodel capex (request,
commitment tracking, CIP, asset turnover per W1811–W2752) — this value stream performs the
*operational* remodel program (scope/design/phasing/execution/re-merchandising/analytics), while
VS-40 capitalizes its spend. It is also distinct from **VS-55 (Planogram & Space Optimization)**
which maintains the *ongoing* planogram/space discipline and from **VS-07 (Store Operations)** which
runs the *steady-state* trading store. Store remodel/renovation is the *in-life asset-renewal*
discipline — distinct from new opening, closure, construction, property investment, capex
accounting, planogram maintenance, and daily store operations.

BuildRight's exposure is material and recurring: 200 stores × a 5–7-year cycle ≈ ~30–40
remodels/year, each a multi-week capital project executed around a live, revenue-generating store;
aggregate program spend on the order of PHP 300–700M/year. Remodels are the single largest lever
for comp-sales growth, format relevance, and asset-value preservation across the store base, and
they concentrate operational, safety, customer-experience, and capex-governance risk that no
existing value stream owns end-to-end.
