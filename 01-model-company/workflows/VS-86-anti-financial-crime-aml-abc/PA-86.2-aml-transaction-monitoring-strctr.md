## W2929. Cash Transaction Monitoring & Covered Transaction (PHP 500K Single-Day) Detection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Cash transaction in single business day |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~2.8M POS transactions/month; cash-heavy retail |
| **Owner** | Compliance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Compliance, Treasury, AR |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ERP monitors aggregate single-day cash transactions per customer; flags covered transactions exceeding PHP 500K (single or series) per AMLA | ERP automation | Compliance Analyst | Continuous |
| 2 | Compliance validates customer identity and transaction legitimacy; determines covered-transaction reportability | Compliance Analyst | Compliance Officer | Per alert |
| 3 | Compliance files Covered Transaction Report (CTR) with AMLC within 5 working days; archives filing | Compliance | Compliance Officer | Per covered txn |

### System Touchpoints
- Cash-transaction monitoring engine; CTR generator; AMLC portal

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Failure to file CTR incurs PHP 500K penalty per transaction; mitigated by automated monitoring


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitors aggregate single-day cash transactions per customer" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validates customer identity and transaction legitimacy" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-file of "files Covered Transaction Report" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-237 (ensure AML/KYC & sanctions compliance.)
- operational: automated monitoring

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 hours per covered transaction
