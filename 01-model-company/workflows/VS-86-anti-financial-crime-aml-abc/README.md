## Overview

Anti-Financial Crime, AML/KYC & Anti-Corruption workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — covering
the operational anti-financial-crime program: customer/vendor Know-Your-Customer (KYC) and
due diligence, politically exposed person (PEP) and sanctions screening, Anti-Money Laundering
(AML) transaction monitoring and AMLC reporting (CTR/STR), anti-bribery & anti-corruption
(ABC), gifts/benefits, and conflict-of-interest management under AMLA (RA 9160/RA 10365),
the Government Procurement Reform Act interface, and the Data Privacy Act.

While Internal Audit (VS-21) owns the *audit* of these controls (W159 ABC audit, W354 AML
audit), this value stream owns the **first-line financial-crime prevention program**:
day-to-day KYC, screening operations, monitoring, regulatory reporting, and investigations.
