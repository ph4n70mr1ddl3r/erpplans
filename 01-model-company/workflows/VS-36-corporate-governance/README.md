# VS-36: Corporate Governance & Board Management

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages corporate governance activities for BuildRight Depot's 5-entity holding company structure — including board meeting management, corporate record-keeping, SEC compliance filings, shareholder communication, equity and stock ledger management, related-party transaction disclosure, corporate policy governance, and entity-level compliance. Ensures the privately held Filipino family corporation meets all SEC Philippines, BIR, and LGU governance requirements while maintaining proper corporate formalities across BuildRight Holdings, Inc. and its four subsidiaries.
