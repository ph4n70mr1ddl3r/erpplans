## W5209. Project Material Goods Delivery Receipt (DR) Matching & Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Material delivery to project job site (VS-74); delivery completion |
| **Frequency** | Continuous per delivery dispatch |
| **Volume** | ~1,500–2,000 deliveries monthly across all projects |
| **Owner** | Project Billing Specialist / AR Clerk |
| **Participants** | Site Engineer, Fleet Driver (VS-06), 3PL Carrier (VS-56) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive signed original Goods Delivery Receipts (DR) from returning drivers. | Project Billing Specialist / AR Clerk | Project Billing Specialist / AR Clerk | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Inspect DR: verify contractor site engineer signature, stamp, date, and items accepted. | Project Billing Specialist / AR Clerk | Project Billing Specialist / AR Clerk | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Match DR to ERP billing invoice: verify SKU codes and quantities match. | Project Billing Specialist / AR Clerk | Project Billing Specialist / AR Clerk | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Attach scanned copy of signed DR and photo of delivered materials at site (GPS-tagged) to ERP invoice record. | Project Billing Specialist / AR Clerk | Project Billing Specialist / AR Clerk | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Fleet management system (VS-06), WMS dispatch (VS-04), B2B billing ledger (VS-16).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missing / Unsigned DRs**: Bank rejecting escrow draw requests due to lack of proof of delivery; mitigated by driver mobile photo-uploads at drop-off.

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile app barcode scanning and automatic document matching in ERP.

### Controls
- CTL-118 (ensure escrow draw & lien-release control.)
- CTL-774: Original signed DR archived; matching verified by AR supervisor before invoicing release.

### Time Estimate
- ~15 mins per delivery matching

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (logistics), VS-56 (3PL), VS-16 (O2C), VS-04 (DC ops)

## W5210. Bank Inspector Joint Quantity Survey (JQS) Staging & Schedule Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project materials delivery matching (W5209); scheduled bank inspection date |
| **Frequency** | Monthly or bi-weekly depending on contract |
| **Volume** | ~100–120 inspections managed monthly |
| **Owner** | Project Coordinator / B2B Technical Support |
| **Participants** | Bank Inspector, General Contractor Site Engineer, Geodetic Engineer (VS-115) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review scheduled inspection dates with the developer and bank escrow agent. | Project Coordinator / B2B Technical Support | Project Coordinator / B2B Technical Support | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Compile Joint Quantity Survey (JQS) folder: list all materials delivered, invoices, and matching delivery receipts. | Project Coordinator / B2B Technical Support | Project Coordinator / B2B Technical Support | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Schedule joint site walk-through with Bank Inspector, Contractor Site Engineer, and BuildRight representative. | Project Coordinator / B2B Technical Support | Project Coordinator / B2B Technical Support | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Conduct site audit: inspect delivered materials, measure quantities (lumber yard, rebar count, tile boxes), and address inspector queries. | Project Coordinator / B2B Technical Support | Project Coordinator / B2B Technical Support | 1-2 hours |

- Inspection calendar portal, technical specs database, scale logs (VS-115).

### Pain Points / Risks

### Automation Opportunity
- JQS scheduling workflow tool with email alerts.

### Controls
- CTL-118 (ensure escrow draw & lien-release control.)
- CTL-774: JQS worksheet signed by all three parties (Inspector, Contractor, BuildRight) at site; discrepancies resolved.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 days coordination and execution

### Cross-references
- links to VS-115 (calibration), VS-11 (trade project)

|---|---|---|---|---|

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monitor Trust Account bank feeds (W5204) for incoming credit transfers. | Accounts Receivable Specialist / Cash Application Clerk | Accounts Receivable Specialist / Cash Application Clerk | 1-2 hours |

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-774 (ensure controlled execution — Milestone Inspection, Joint Quantity Survey & Escrow Draw Processing (PA-181.2).)
- CTL-774: Daily cash reconciliation audited by Treasury supervisor; matching discrepancies reviewed.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project contract execution (W5201) specifying retainage; project milestone achievement |
| **Frequency** | Monthly accounting + event-driven release |
| **Volume** | ~50–60 retainage tracking accounts active |
| **Owner** | Project Accountant |
| **Participants** | AR Specialist, Developer Project Manager, Finance controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set up the Project Retainage subsidiary ledger: configure 5% to 10% auto-deduction from project invoices to retainage ledger. | Project Accountant | Project Accountant | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Perform monthly retention reconciliation: track accumulated retainage balance against project milestones. | Project Accountant | Project Accountant | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Prepare retainage release documentation: compile Certificate of Practical Completion, LGU occupancy permit, and final clear-title waiver. | Project Accountant | Project Accountant | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Submit release request to bank escrow agent; follow up on cash release and clear the retainage ledger balance. | Project Accountant | Project Accountant | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP GL retainage ledger (VS-17.1), AR billing database (VS-16), LGU permit tracking (VS-166).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Retainage unpaid / Delayed release**: Developer withholding retention funds due to minor defects, inflating DSO; mitigated by clear contract resolution clauses and defect warranty offsets.

### Automation Opportunity
- System automated retainage accrual postings.

### Controls
- CTL-774 (ensure controlled execution — Milestone Inspection, Joint Quantity Survey & Escrow Draw Processing (PA-181.2).)
- CTL-774: Retainage releases signed off by Corporate Controller; final ledger balances reconciled to zero.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-3 hours monthly review; 5-10 days release process

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.1 (GL close), VS-16 (O2C), VS-166 (license portfolio)
