# VS-181: B2B Project Financing, Escrow Account Orchestration & Lien Release

> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

B2B project financing, escrow account orchestration, and material lien release operations for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the commercial and financial program that supports large developer and general contractor accounts whose projects are funded through bank-managed escrow accounts: establishing bank-escrow integrations and portal connections; matching project purchase orders and contract pricing; forecasting and staging project-specific building material inventories; managing project credit limits and excess approvals; staging bank inspector Joint Quantity Surveys (JQS) to verify material placement; assembling and submitting escrow draw documentation; tracking fund settlements, cash applications, and match reconciliations; managing project retainage and milestone release; executing tripartite payment agreements; configuring project-restricted inventory locks; issuing conditional and unconditional progress waivers of lien; reconciliation of material returns against escrow draws; and managing project credit holds during escrow delays.

This matters at BuildRight's scale. Large B2B project sales (developers and general contractors) represent ~40% of BuildRight's revenue. These clients typically finance their construction projects through commercial banks, which release material payments from escrow accounts only upon verified construction milestones. Standard milestone billing in VS-11 and consumer credit in VS-154 do not cover the complex commercial workflows of **escrow bank draw management, joint quantity surveying, and material lien waivers** needed to capture and secure high-value B2B projects. This value stream prevents revenue leakage, reduces DSO, and unlocks developer sales.

This value stream owns the end-to-end program: mapping workflows, configuring system parameters, executing compliance actions, coordinating with external partners, and managing performance dashboards.
