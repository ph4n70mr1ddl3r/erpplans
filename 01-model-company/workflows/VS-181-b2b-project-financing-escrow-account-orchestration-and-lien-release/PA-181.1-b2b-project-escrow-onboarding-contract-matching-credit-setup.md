## W5201. Developer Bank-Escrow Agreement Review, Onboarding & Credit Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New B2B developer account contract negotiation (VS-11) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous per new developer project onboarding |
| **Volume** | ~50–60 major developer projects onboarded annually |
| **Owner** | B2B Credit Manager / Legal Operations (VS-100) |
| **Participants** | Developer Finance Director, Escrow Bank Agent, Legal (VS-100), AR Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collect and review the tripartite Escrow Agreement / Trust Agreement signed by the Developer, the Funding Bank (Escrow Agent), and the General Contractor. | B2B Credit Manager / Legal Operations (VS-100) | B2B Credit Manager / Legal Operations (VS-100) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Verify the escrow funding rules: verify payment release triggers, billing documentation checklists, and inspector audit schedules. | B2B Credit Manager / Legal Operations (VS-100) | B2B Credit Manager / Legal Operations (VS-100) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Set up the Developer Project Account in the ERP credit master; configure credit limits and payment terms matching the escrow release windows. | B2B Credit Manager / Legal Operations (VS-100) | B2B Credit Manager / Legal Operations (VS-100) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Onboard bank escrow contacts and set up secure communication protocols. | B2B Credit Manager / Legal Operations (VS-100) | B2B Credit Manager / Legal Operations (VS-100) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Credit master (VS-16), Legal document portal (VS-100), B2B CRM (VS-11).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vague release clauses**: Escrow bank agent delays payments due to ambiguous milestone definitions; mitigated by requiring standard bank-release formats before credit approval.

### Time Estimate
- ~5-10 working days

### Automation Opportunity
- Digital contract template parsing and credit score mapping.

### Controls
- CTL: Escrow agreements legal-approved; credit limit setup complies with Board Risk policy.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-11 (trade project), VS-100 (legal), VS-16 (O2C)

## W5202. Project Purchase Order Matching & Contract Price Alignment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Developer project onboarding (W5201); contract agreement signed |
| **Frequency** | Per project onboarding + contract amendments |
| **Volume** | ~50–60 contract configurations annually |
| **Owner** | B2B Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Developer Procurement Manager, Pricing Specialist (VS-01.2), Master Data Team (VS-29) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Upload the developer's Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and contracted unit pricing sheets into the ERP project pricing master. | B2B Account Manager | B2B Account Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Configure contract price locks in the system: ensure only the contracted SKUs can be billed to the project code at the negotiated price. | B2B Account Manager | B2B Account Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Set up price validation rules: prevent sales clerks from modifying project prices without Category Manager approval (W1394). | B2B Account Manager | B2B Account Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Match developer's corporate PO to the BuildRight contract code; verify SKU codes match. | B2B Account Manager | B2B Account Manager | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Pricing database (VS-01.2), ERP Master Data (VS-29), B2B CRM.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pricing mismatch / Billing rejection**: Wrong prices on billing invoices causing bank to reject the escrow draw; mitigated by automated pricing lock at the project master.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-3 working days

### Automation Opportunity
- Excel-to-ERP contract price uploader and validation scripts.

### Controls
- CTL: Contract prices verified by Category Manager; system blocks billing price changes.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-01.2 (pricing), VS-29 (master data), VS-11 (trade project)

## W5204. Bank Escrow Financing Trust Account System Mapping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Tripartite escrow agreement onboarded (W5201) |
| **Frequency** | Per project setup |
| **Volume** | ~50–60 bank mapping setups annually |
| **Owner** | Treasury Operations Manager (VS-18) |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, Bank System Administrator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify the bank trust account numbers and escrow routing codes with the funding bank. | Treasury Operations Manager (VS-18) | Treasury Operations Manager (VS-18) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Map the trust account codes in the ERP General Ledger: set up dedicated subsidiary ledgers for each project's escrow account. | Treasury Operations Manager (VS-18) | Treasury Operations Manager (VS-18) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Configure automatic bank statement feed (MT940/BAI2 formats) to track trust account balances. | Treasury Operations Manager (VS-18) | Treasury Operations Manager (VS-18) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Test system integration: run sample transaction to verify automatic matching of deposit notifications to the project trust ledger. | Treasury Operations Manager (VS-18) | Treasury Operations Manager (VS-18) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP GL (VS-17.1), Treasury Cash module (VS-18), bank system host.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Co-mingling of project funds**: Payments applied to wrong project accounts, violating trust covenants; mitigated by automated system validation of project identifier tags on bank wires.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 days configuration

### Automation Opportunity
- MT940 automatic bank statement parsing and cash application proposal.

### Controls
- CTL: Account mappings verified by Controller; bank reconciliations performed daily.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.1 (GL close), VS-18 (treasury)

## W5205. Project Credit Limit Allocation & Temporary Excess Approval

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project onboarding (W5201); temporary spike in material delivery schedule |
| **Frequency** | Per project onboarding + monthly reviews |
| **Volume** | ~100–120 active credit limits managed |
| **Owner** | B2B Credit Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, CFO, B2B Sales VP |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calculate base project credit limit: estimate monthly delivery volumes × 2 months (standard credit window). | B2B Credit Manager | B2B Credit Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Route credit proposal through hierarchy approval queue: Credit Manager up to CFO (depending on project size). | B2B Credit Manager | B2B Credit Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Assess temporary credit excess requests: if developer requests peak deliveries exceeding credit limit (e.g. bulk steel delivery), verify the escrow trust account balance holds sufficient funds. | B2B Credit Manager | B2B Credit Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Approve temporary credit excess in ERP with 30-day auto-expiry limits. | B2B Credit Manager | B2B Credit Manager | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Credit management screen (VS-16), GL accounts receivable (VS-17.1).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Developer default**: Exceeding credit limit for developers with unfunded escrow accounts; mitigated by requiring bank certificate of deposit before excess approvals.

### Time Estimate
- ~30 mins per credit review

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated system credit checks blocking orders when limit exceeded.

### Controls
- CTL: Temporary excess requires dual signature authorization; audit logs of credit limit modifications reviewed monthly.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (O2C), VS-17.1 (GL close)

## W5206. Developer Project Escrow Funding Verification & Collateral Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | First credit release request; quarterly credit reviews |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly per active project |
| **Volume** | ~100–120 active reviews annually |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst / B2B Risk Manager (VS-68) |
| **Participants** | Escrow Bank Agent, Legal Operations (VS-100), Developer Finance Team |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Request official Certificate of Escrow Balance from the funding bank. | Credit Analyst / B2B Risk Manager (VS-68) | Credit Analyst / B2B Risk Manager (VS-68) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Verify that developer has deposited the required project equity and bank loan releases into the trust account. | Credit Analyst / B2B Risk Manager (VS-68) | Credit Analyst / B2B Risk Manager (VS-68) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Assess project collateral: review chattel mortgages, bank guarantees (VS-116), or corporate parent guarantees submitted. | Credit Analyst / B2B Risk Manager (VS-68) | Credit Analyst / B2B Risk Manager (VS-68) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Generate project risk assessment report; update developer's risk tier classification (W1394) in ERP. | Credit Analyst / B2B Risk Manager (VS-68) | Credit Analyst / B2B Risk Manager (VS-68) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Credit risk portal (VS-68), Legal documents database (VS-100), bank systems.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Underfunded escrow**: Developer fails to deposit equity, blocking bank payouts; mitigated by monthly escrow balance audits.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 days per review

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL: Credit renewals blocked if bank escrow balance falls below 1.5× outstanding AR; risk reports shared with CFO.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (trade credit), VS-100 (legal), VS-116 (performance bonds)

## W5208. Project Onboarding Compliance Check (Anti-Money Laundering & Sanctions)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New developer account contract creation (W5201) |
| **Frequency** | Per project onboarding |
| **Volume** | ~50–60 compliance checks annually |
| **Owner** | Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO) (VS-86) |
| **Participants** | B2B Credit Analyst, Legal Operations (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct PEP (Politically Exposed Persons) and sanctions screening on the developer corporation, major shareholders, and board directors. | Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO) (VS-86) | Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO) (VS-86) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Verify beneficial ownership (KYC): identify and audit owners holding > 10% equity. | Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO) (VS-86) | Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO) (VS-86) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Perform source-of-funds check: verify the funding bank's license and check for any regulatory alerts on the project funding source. | Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO) (VS-86) | Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO) (VS-86) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Secure AMLCO clearance; file clean-status report in compliance folder before account activation. | Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO) (VS-86) | Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO) (VS-86) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Sanctions screening portal (VS-86), KYC documents archive (VS-88), AML audit logs.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Regulatory penalty**: Doing business with sanctioned entities or handling money-laundering funds, violating BSP/AMLC rules; mitigated by automated daily screen updates.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 working days

### Automation Opportunity
- API-based automatic screening against Dow Jones / World-Check databases.

### Controls
- CTL: Account setup blocked in ERP without AMLCO digital signature clearance.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-86 (anti-financial crime), VS-88 (document control), VS-100 (legal)
