## Overview

Legal Operations, Litigation & Intellectual Property Management workflows for BuildRight Depot
Corp. — governing the **General Counsel's office operating model** for active legal work across the
5-entity group: matter/case and outside-counsel management, the litigation lifecycle (pre-trial,
trial, judgment, appeal, enforcement), alternative dispute resolution, settlement/judgment
accounting, intellectual-property portfolio (trademarks, copyrights, industrial designs, trade
secrets for BuildRight's brand and private-label products per VS-41), IP enforcement against
infringers and counterfeiters (links to VS-71 anti-counterfeit), brand licensing and IP
commercialization, regulatory and government-investigation defense, contract-legal support,
corporate-legal advisory and entity management support, ethics/whistleblower investigation case
management, and legal-operations technology (e-billing) and cost analytics.

This is distinct from **VS-36 (Corporate Governance)** which handles board meetings, shareholders,
corporate policy, and entity-governance structure; from **VS-22 (Compliance & Regulatory)** which
handles permits/licenses, government-audit response, and regulatory-change management; and from
**VS-88 (Document Control & Records Retention)** which handles records lifecycle, legal-hold
mechanics, and e-discovery execution. This value stream owns the **legal-operations / litigator
work**: it initiates legal holds (executed by VS-88), manages active matters/cases and outside
counsel, prosecutes and defends litigation, owns the IP portfolio, and provides corporate-legal
advisory. The model company's board (per VS-36.1) receives periodic litigation updates — this
value stream is where that pipeline is operated.

BuildRight's exposure spans commercial/contract disputes (links to VS-11/VS-16), labor cases
before the NLRC (links to VS-84), consumer complaints escalating to DTI (links to VS-13),
property/lease disputes (links to VS-20/VS-42/VS-97), insurance/subrogation (links to VS-26), tax
protests (links to VS-79), customs disputes (links to VS-87), and IP enforcement against
counterfeit BuildRight-brand goods (links to VS-71/VS-41).
