## W3025. Vendor Shortage, Damage & Non-Conformance Claim Filing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor-attributable discrepancy confirmed |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 vendors; subset of receipts |
| **Owner** | Vendor Management Manager |
| **Participants** | Vendor Mgmt, AP, Quality, Inventory Control |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vendor Mgmt/AP files vendor claim via vendor portal (links to VS-03) with discrepancy details, evidence (W3020), quantity, and claimed value (cost + handling) | Vendor Mgmt / AP | Vendor Management Manager | Per claim (<vendor SLA) |
| 2 | Quality provides non-conformance detail (spec deviation, wrong item, defective); supports liability assertion | Quality | Quality Manager | Per claim |
| 3 | Vendor Mgmt tracks claim to vendor acknowledgment; pursues credit memo, replacement, or debit per purchase terms; updates damage case | Vendor Mgmt | Vendor Management Manager | Per claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor portal claim (links to VS-03); AP credit/debit (links to W7); damage case linkage

### Pain Points / Risks
- **SLA risk**: Claims filed past vendor notice window are denied; mitigated by automated claim-trigger at goods-receipt discrepancy


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-file of "files vendor claim via vendor portal" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-track of "tracks claim to vendor acknowledgment" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-500 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor, Carrier & Freight Claims Filing & Resolution (PA-90.2).)
- operational: automated claim-trigger at goods-receipt discrepancy

### Time Estimate
- Per claim

## W3026. Freight Carrier / 3PL Damage & Shortage Claim Filing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Carrier/3PL-attributable transit damage or shortage |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | Owned fleet (20%) + 3PL (80%); inter-island + last-mile |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | Logistics, Inventory Control, AP, 3PL/Carrier |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics files carrier/3PL claim with waybill, delivery exception, photos (W3020), and claimed value within carrier notice window (typically 7–15 days) | Logistics | Logistics Manager | Per claim (<window) |
| 2 | Inventory Control confirms transit-leg responsibility via seal/tracking evidence; differentiates pickup damage vs. in-transit vs. delivery | Inventory Control | Inventory Control Manager | Per claim |
| 3 | Logistics/AP track carrier settlement (credit/refund/offset against freight payable); reconcile to damage case; pursue denial disputes (W3031) | Logistics / AP | Logistics Manager | Per claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Carrier claim record; freight payable offset (links to W277); damage case linkage

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Notice-window risk**: Late filing forfeits carrier liability; mitigated by claim-trigger automation and short SLA


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics files carrier/3PL claim with waybill, delivery exception, photos" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-track of "Inventory Control confirms transit-leg responsibility via seal/tracking evidence" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile to damage case" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-500 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor, Carrier & Freight Claims Filing & Resolution (PA-90.2).)
- operational: claim-trigger automation and short SLA

### Time Estimate
- Per claim

## W3027. Import Container Damage, Short-Landing & Surveyor Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Import container discrepancy (damage, short-landing, contamination) |
| **Frequency** | ~400–600 TEUs/month; subset with discrepancy |
| **Volume** | Import vendors ~40% of COGS |
| **Owner** | Import & Customs Manager |
| **Participants** | Import/Customs, Logistics, Surveyor, Broker, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Import/Customs engages independent marine surveyor at port for container damage/short-landing; obtains survey report before container leaves terminal | Import / Customs | Import & Customs Manager | Per event (<port free-time) |
| 2 | Import/Customs/Broker files claim against ocean carrier, marine insurer, or vendor per bill of lading and Incoterms; preserves lien rights | Import / Customs / Broker | Import & Customs Manager | Per event |
| 3 | Finance/Logistics coordinate with marine insurance and customs (duty refund on damaged/destroyed imports per BIR rules); reconcile recovery | Finance / Logistics | Finance Manager | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Import/container record (links to VS-02/VS-87); marine survey report; customs duty adjustment (links to VS-87)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Evidence-window risk**: Surveyor not engaged before container leaves terminal forfeits claim; mitigated by port-discharge survey SOP


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-import of "Import/Customs engages independent marine surveyor at port for container…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "files claim against ocean carrier, marine insurer, or vendor per bill of lading…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "Logistics coordinate with marine insurance and customs" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-500 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor, Carrier & Freight Claims Filing & Resolution (PA-90.2).)
- operational: mandatory pre-gate-out port-discharge survey gate with findings photographed and attached to the claim file

### Time Estimate
- Per event; 1–4 weeks to resolve

## W3028. Claim Evidence Package, Liability Determination & Valuation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Claim filed or pre-filing preparation |
| **Frequency** | Per claim |
| **Volume** | Enterprise claims |
| **Owner** | Claims Analyst |
| **Participants** | Claims Analyst, Inventory Control, Finance, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claims Analyst assembles evidence package: damage case, photos, PO/ASN, waybill, survey, QC report, correspondence — per claim type template | Claims Analyst | Claims Manager | Per claim |

| 3 | Finance values claim at landed cost + attributable handling/logistics; validates against recovery potential and materiality threshold | Finance | Finance Manager | Per claim |

### Automation Opportunity

- System auto-document of "document apportionment and rationale" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "Finance values claim at landed cost + attributable handling/logistics" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-500 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor, Carrier & Freight Claims Filing & Resolution (PA-90.2).)
- operational: landed-cost-based valuation template

### Time Estimate
- Per claim

## W3029. Claim Negotiation, Settlement & Credit/Refund Resolution

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Counterparty responds to claim |
| **Frequency** | Per claim |
| **Volume** | Enterprise claims |
| **Owner** | Claims Manager |
| **Participants** | Claims Mgr, Vendor Mgmt/Logistics, AP/AR, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claims Mgr/Vendor Mgmt/Logistics negotiate settlement with counterparty; defends claim with evidence; agrees credit memo, refund, replacement, or freight offset | Claims Mgr / Vendor Mgmt / Logistics | Claims Manager | Per claim |

- System auto-log of "Logistics negotiate settlement with counterparty" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconciles to damage case and GL" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-flag of "flags residual for insurance/subrogation" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-75 (detect and recover freight & receiving damage.)
- operational: settlement SLAs and escalation tiers

### Time Estimate
- 2–12 weeks

## W3030. Subrogation, Insurance Recovery & Dual-Claim Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Claim settlement short of full recovery, or insured event |
| **Frequency** | Per qualifying claim |
| **Volume** | Subset of high-value claims |
| **Owner** | Risk & Insurance Manager |
| **Participants** | Risk/Insurance, Claims Mgr, Finance, Legal, Broker |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk/Insurance evaluates cargo/transit/goods-in-transit and property policies for recovery beyond counterparty settlement; avoids double-recovery | Risk / Insurance | Risk & Insurance Manager | Per claim |
| 2 | Risk/Insurance files insurer claim with evidence package (W3028) and settlement record; pursues subrogation against responsible carrier/vendor | Risk / Insurance | Risk & Insurance Manager | Per claim |
| 3 | Finance posts insurer recovery; reconciles total recovery vs. loss; coordinates vendor/carrier recovery (W3025/3026) and customer recovery (W3038) to net total | Finance | Finance Manager | Per claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance claim registry (links to W59/VS-26); subrogation case; recovery reconciliation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Double-recovery risk**: Claiming vendor + insurer for same loss; mitigated by net-recovery reconciliation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Risk/Insurance evaluates cargo/transit/goods-in-transit and property policies for recovery beyond" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "Risk/Insurance files insurer claim with evidence package" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconciles total recovery vs." (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-500 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor, Carrier & Freight Claims Filing & Resolution (PA-90.2).)
- operational: net-recovery reconciliation

### Time Estimate
- 4–16 weeks

## W3031. Claim Denial Dispute, Escalation & Legal Handoff

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Claim denied or settlement unacceptable |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Subset of claims |
| **Owner** | Claims Manager |
| **Participants** | Claims Mgr, Legal, Vendor Mgmt/Logistics, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claims Mgr documents denial rationale; assesses dispute viability (evidence strength, contract terms, materiality); escalates within escalation tiers | Claims Mgr | Claims Manager | Per claim |
| 2 | Vendor Mgmt/Logistics pursue commercial escalation with counterparty; Legal issues demand letter where contract supports | Vendor Mgmt / Logistics / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per claim |
| 3 | Legal evaluates litigation/arbitration for material claims; coordinates with finance write-off decision for non-recoverable amounts; closes case | Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Claim dispute case; contract repository (links to VS-03); legal matter management (links to VS-36)

- **Cost/benefit risk**: Disputing low-value claims costs more than recovery; mitigated by materiality threshold and tiered escalation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "documents denial rationale" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "Logistics pursue commercial escalation with counterparty" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System workflow orchestration of "coordinates with finance write-off decision for non-recoverable amounts" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-500 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor, Carrier & Freight Claims Filing & Resolution (PA-90.2).)
- operational: materiality threshold and tiered escalation

### Time Estimate
- 4–24 weeks for material disputes

## W3032. Vendor/Carrier Claim Aging, Recovery Analytics & Scorecard Feed

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly/monthly reporting |
| **Frequency** | Weekly + monthly |
| **Volume** | Enterprise open claims |
| **Owner** | Claims Manager |
| **Participants** | Claims Mgr, Supply Chain Analytics, Vendor Mgmt, Logistics, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claims Mgr maintains claim aging dashboard: open claims by counterparty, age, value, recovery rate, denial rate | Claims Mgr | Claims Manager | Weekly |
| 2 | Supply Chain Analytics produces recovery analytics: recovery % by vendor/carrier/lane, trend, and recovery-value leakage | Supply Chain Analytics | Supply Chain Analytics Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | Vendor Mgmt/Logistics feed claim performance into vendor scorecard (links to VS-67) and carrier performance review (links to VS-56); drives corrective action | Vendor Mgmt / Logistics | Vendor Management Manager | Monthly |

- Claim aging/recovery dashboard (links to VS-28); vendor scorecard feed (links to VS-67); carrier SLA (links to VS-56)

### Pain Points / Risks

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "counterparty, age, value, recovery rate, denial rate" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "Logistics feed claim performance into vendor scorecard" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-75 (detect and recover freight & receiving damage.)
- operational: aging alerts and recovery-rate KPIs

### Time Estimate
- Weekly/monthly
