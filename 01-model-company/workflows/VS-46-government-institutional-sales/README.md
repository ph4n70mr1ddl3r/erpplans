# VS-46: Government & Institutional B2G Sales

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages all business-to-government (B2G) and institutional sales operations for BuildRight Depot. Covers PHILGEPS registration, government procurement bidding, LGU partnerships, government account billing and collection, and institutional project sales. Government and institutional customers represent ~3–5% of revenue and require specialized compliance with Philippine procurement laws (RA 9184 Government Procurement Reform Act), PHILGEPS registration, and unique billing/collection procedures distinct from standard B2B trade sales.
