## W1958. Government Competitive Bidding Preparation & Submission

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Bidding opportunity identified per W1953 |
| **Frequency** | ~20–30 bids/year |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 200M–500M in annual government bids |
| **Owner** | Trade Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Account Manager, Category Manager, Pricing Analyst, Legal & Compliance Officer, VP Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bid/no-bid decision: (a) VP Store Operations reviews opportunity: (i) product availability — can BuildRight supply all requested items?, (ii) delivery feasibility — can BuildRight deliver to project site(s) within required timeline?, (iii) pricing competitiveness — can BuildRight offer competitive pricing given cost structure?, (iv) strategic fit — aligns with BuildRight's government sales strategy? | VP Store Operations | COO | 1–2 hours/opportunity |
| 2 | Bid preparation: (a) eligibility documents: compile per W1954, (b) technical proposal: product specifications, brand/model details, delivery schedule, warranty terms, product data sheets, (c) financial proposal: unit pricing per item, total bid price, validity period (typically 90 days), (d) Pricing Analyst calculates competitive pricing: (i) standard cost + margin, (ii) volume discount if applicable, (iii) delivery cost inclusion, (iv) target margin: minimum 15% for government bids | Trade Account Manager / Pricing Analyst | VP Store Operations | 3–5 days/bid |
| 3 | Bid submission: (a) submit through government procurement portal or physical submission per bidding documents, (b) ensure submission before deadline (late bids automatically disqualified), (c) obtain acknowledgment of receipt | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | 1 day/bid |
| 4 | Post-submission: (a) attend pre-bid conference if scheduled, (b) respond to clarifications from BAC, (c) attend bid opening (public opening — all bid prices visible to competitors), (d) await award decision | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | Per bid schedule |

### System Touchpoints
- Government bid tracking with status lifecycle (W1958.2)
- Pricing calculation for government bids (W1958.2d)
- Integration with W542 (POS quotation) for quotation format

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Bid price transparency**: Government bid opening reveals all bid prices to competitors; BuildRight's pricing strategy becomes visible; consider strategic pricing to balance competitiveness and margin protection


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews opportunity" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documents" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-submit of "submit through government procurement portal or physical submission per bidding…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-380 (ensure controlled execution — Government Bid, Quotation & Order Processing (PA-46.2).)
- operational: Trade Account Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 days per bid preparation; 1 day submission

## W1959. Government Shopping & Small Value Procurement Quotation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Government agency requests quotation for purchases below PHP 2M (shopping) or PHP 1M (small value) |
| **Frequency** | ~100–150 government quotations/year |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 50M–100M in annual shopping/small value sales |
| **Owner** | Trade Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Account Manager, Pricing Analyst, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Government procurement officer requests quotation: (a) verbal or written request with item list, quantities, delivery location, (b) shopping mode: agency canvasses at least 3 suppliers for each purchase, (c) small value procurement: simplified bidding for purchases below threshold | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | Per request |
| 2 | Trade Account Manager prepares quotation: (a) real-time ATP check for each item, (b) pricing: standard government pricing tier or project-specific pricing, (c) delivery timeline and fee, (d) validity period: 30 days standard, (e) quotation formatted per BuildRight standard with TIN, business permit details | Trade Account Manager / Pricing Analyst | VP Store Operations | 1–2 days/quotation |
| 3 | Store-level fulfillment for small orders: (a) if order can be fulfilled from a single store: direct store pickup or delivery, (b) Store Manager processes as trade account sale at POS with government account pricing, (c) if order requires DC fulfillment: process as standard sales order with delivery | Store Manager / Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | Per order |

### System Touchpoints
- Government quotation generation with ATP check (W1959.2)
- Store-level government order processing at POS (W1959.3)
- Integration with W542 (quotation generation) for format

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Quotation turnaround**: Government agencies may require quotations within 24–48 hours; need pre-approved pricing tier for quick turnaround


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-request of "requests quotation" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "check for each item" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "count sale at POS with government account pricing" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-191 (ensure B2G procurement compliance & collection.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 days per quotation

| **Frequency** | ~5–10 negotiated procurements/year |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 20M–50M in annual negotiated sales |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, Trade Account Manager, Category Manager, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Store Operations evaluates negotiated procurement opportunity: (a) verify justification: is this a legitimate case for negotiated procurement per RA 9184?, (b) assess BuildRight's position: is BuildRight the only supplier or one of few?, (c) pricing strategy: negotiated procurement allows direct negotiation — target higher margin than competitive bidding | VP Store Operations | COO | 1–2 days |

### Pain Points / Risks

- System auto-submit of "submit final offer with negotiated terms" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls

## W1961. Government Contract Award & Purchase Order Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Notice of Award received from government agency |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 contract awards/year |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 100M–300M in annual government contracts |
| **Owner** | Trade Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Account Manager, VP Store Operations, VP Supply Chain, Legal & Compliance Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive Notice of Award (NOA): (a) Trade Account Manager receives NOA from government procuring entity, (b) review contract terms: scope, pricing, delivery schedule, warranty, payment terms, penalty clauses, (c) Legal & Compliance Officer reviews contract terms for compliance and risk | Trade Account Manager / Legal & Compliance Officer | VP Store Operations | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Accept award and sign contract: (a) VP Store Operations approves contract acceptance, (b) sign contract within validity period (typically 10–20 calendar days from NOA receipt), (c) post performance security (if required): bank guarantee or surety bond typically 2–10% of contract value | VP Store Operations | COO | 1–3 days |
| 3 | Create sales order in ERP: (a) convert government PO/contract to BuildRight sales order, (b) allocate inventory per delivery schedule, (c) if items not in stock: create procurement request per W2 for vendor purchase, (d) coordinate delivery schedule with VP Supply Chain | Trade Account Manager / VP Supply Chain | VP Store Operations | 1–2 days |
| 4 | AR invoice processing: (a) generate government invoice per BIR requirements with TIN, (b) include: PO reference, contract reference, delivery receipt, acceptance certificate, (c) submit invoice through government agency's payment processing system | Trade Account Manager | AP/AR Manager | Per delivery milestone |

### System Touchpoints
- Government contract/sales order creation (W1961.3)
- AR invoice generation for government accounts (W1961.4)
- Integration with W8 (AR processing) for government billing

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Performance security cost**: Bank guarantee for 2–10% of contract value ties up working capital; negotiate minimum security requirement


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "review contract terms" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves contract acceptance" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-create of "Create sales order in ERP" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-380 (ensure controlled execution — Government Bid, Quotation & Order Processing (PA-46.2).)
- operational: Trade Account Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 days from NOA to contract signing

## W1962. Government Project Delivery & Site Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Government contract delivery schedule |
| **Frequency** | Per government contract delivery milestone |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 government contracts with multiple deliveries each |
| **Owner** | VP Supply Chain |
| **Participants** | VP Supply Chain, Trade Account Manager, DC Team, Delivery Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delivery planning: (a) VP Supply Chain plans delivery schedule per government contract, (b) coordinate with DC for pick/pack, (c) arrange delivery transport (own fleet or 3PL), (d) for project site deliveries: coordinate with government project manager for site access and unloading | VP Supply Chain | VP Store Operations | 2–3 days before delivery |
| 2 | Delivery execution: (a) deliver goods to government project site or designated location, (b) obtain delivery receipt signed by government receiving officer, (c) document delivery: photos of goods at site, signed DR, (d) for large deliveries: arrange unloading labor at site | DC Team / Delivery Partner | VP Supply Chain | Per delivery |
| 3 | Acceptance certificate: (a) government inspector verifies delivery against contract specifications, (b) for construction materials: quality inspection at site, (c) obtain acceptance certificate signed by government project manager, (d) acceptance certificate required for invoice submission and payment | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | Per delivery |
| 4 | Delivery exception handling: (a) if goods rejected: document reason, arrange replacement, negotiate timeline, (b) if partial delivery: adjust invoice accordingly, schedule remaining delivery, (c) penalty clauses: monitor on-time delivery performance to avoid government contract penalties (typically 1/10 of 1% per day of delay) | Trade Account Manager / VP Supply Chain | VP Store Operations | As needed |

### System Touchpoints
- Government delivery scheduling with milestone tracking (W1962.1)
- Delivery confirmation and acceptance documentation (W1962.3)
- Integration with W4 (DC outbound) and W52 (fleet) for logistics

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Site access**: Government project sites (schools, hospitals, military bases) may have restricted access; arrange access passes in advance
- **Delivery penalties**: Government contracts impose penalties for late delivery; buffer delivery timeline by 2–3 days


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-schedule of "schedule per government contract" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "document delivery" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-inspect of "inspector verifies delivery against contract specifications" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-380 (ensure controlled execution — Government Bid, Quotation & Order Processing (PA-46.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- Per delivery; planning 2–3 days ahead

## W1964. Government Bid Protest & Dispute Resolution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Bid protest filed by or against BuildRight; government contract dispute |
| **Frequency** | ~2–3 bid protests/disputes/year |
| **Volume** | Low frequency but high impact |
| **Owner** | VP Legal |
| **Participants** | VP Legal, Trade Account Manager, VP Store Operations, External Counsel |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | If BuildRight files protest (lost bid due to irregularity): (a) VP Legal reviews bid results for procedural irregularities per RA 9184, (b) if grounds found: file protest with BAC within prescribed period (typically 5–10 days from bid opening), (c) compile evidence: bid documents, pricing comparison, procedural violations | VP Legal | VP Store Operations | 3–5 days |
| 2 | If protest filed against BuildRight (competitor protests BuildRight's award): (a) VP Legal prepares defense: compile eligibility documents, technical compliance evidence, pricing justification, (b) respond to protest within prescribed period, (c) attend BAC hearing if required | VP Legal | VP Store Operations | 3–5 days |
| 3 | Contract performance dispute: (a) if government entity disputes delivery quality or timeline: document all communications, provide evidence of compliance, (b) negotiate resolution: replacement delivery, price adjustment, or mutual termination, (c) if unresolved: escalate to arbitration per contract dispute resolution clause, (d) Legal & Compliance Officer maintains dispute register per VS-36 | VP Legal / Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | Per dispute |

### System Touchpoints
- Bid protest tracking in legal module (W1964.1–2)
- Integration with VS-36 (corporate governance) for dispute management

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Blacklisting risk**: Persistent bid protests or contract disputes may lead to government blacklisting; exercise restraint and pursue only meritorious protests


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "documents, pricing comparison, procedural violations" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-compile of "compile eligibility documents, technical compliance evidence, pricing…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-escalate of "escalate to arbitration per contract dispute resolution clause" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-380 (ensure controlled execution — Government Bid, Quotation & Order Processing (PA-46.2).)
- operational: VP Legal review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 days per protest/dispute

## W1965. Government Catalog & Price List Maintenance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Price change; annual catalog update; government requirement |
| **Frequency** | Monthly price updates; annual catalog refresh |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–5,000 items in government catalog (subset of full assortment) |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Category Manager, Pricing Analyst, Trade Account Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager maintains government price list: (a) subset of full assortment most relevant to government procurement (cement, lumber, electrical, plumbing, paint, hardware), (b) government pricing tier: typically 5–15% below SRP for bulk government purchases, (c) price list format: item description, unit of measure, unit price, validity period, (d) update monthly with current pricing | Category Manager / Pricing Analyst | VP Merchandising | 1 day/month |
| 2 | Government catalog distribution: (a) print catalog: distribute to key government accounts annually, (b) digital catalog: maintain on BuildRight website or government portal, (c) PHILGEPS catalog: update product listings on PHILGEPS portal for government agency discovery | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | Per update |
| 3 | Annual catalog refresh: (a) add new products introduced during year, (b) remove discontinued products, (c) refresh product images and specifications, (d) update pricing per annual negotiation cycle, (e) distribute updated catalog to all registered government accounts | Category Manager / Trade Account Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 week/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Government price list management in pricing module (W1965.1)
- Integration with W40 (price changes) for price updates

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Price staleness**: If price list not updated frequently, government agencies may request pricing that is no longer current; monthly update cycle recommended


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-update of "update monthly with current pricing" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "print catalog" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "log refresh" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-380 (ensure controlled execution — Government Bid, Quotation & Order Processing (PA-46.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 1 day/month; 1 week/year for full refresh
