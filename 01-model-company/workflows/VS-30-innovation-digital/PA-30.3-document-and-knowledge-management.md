## W255. Electronic Document Storage & Retrieval (ERP-wide)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | A transaction is executed that requires supporting documentation (e.g., Goods Receipt, AP Invoice, Capex Request) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (thousands of documents attached daily) |
| **Volume** | ~5,000-10,000 document attachments/day across all entities |
| **Owner** | Respective Department Heads (Finance, Procurement, Logistics) |
| **Participants** | End Users, ERP Administrator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Document Capture**: User receives a physical document (e.g., vendor delivery receipt) and scans it, or receives a digital PDF (e.g., supplier invoice via email) | End User | Department Head | 2 min |
| 2 | **Transaction Attachment**: User drags-and-drops or uploads the document directly to the relevant ERP transaction screen (e.g., attaching the scanned DR to the ERP Goods Receipt record) | End User | Department Head | 1 min |
| 3 | **Metadata Tagging**: System automatically inherits metadata from the transaction (Transaction ID, Vendor Name, Date, Amount, User ID) for searchability | System | — | Automated |
| 4 | **Storage & Linking**: The document is stored in the ERP's secure document repository (or linked cloud storage) and visually indicated with a paperclip/attachment icon on the transaction record | System | — | Automated |
| 5 | **Retrieval**: Approvers or Auditors click the attachment icon during workflow review or audit processes to view the source document without requesting physical copies | Approver / Auditor | — | < 30 sec |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Document Management System (DMS) / Attachment framework
- Workflow approval screens (displaying attachments)

### Pain Points / Risks
- Users uploading massive uncompressed images, bloating database storage costs.
- Attaching documents to the wrong transaction, failing 3-way match audits.

### Staffing Implication
Thousands of documents attached daily across 200+ locations. Per-document time: ~3 min (scan + attach). Absorbed by end users as part of transaction processing. No incremental headcount.

### Time Estimate
**Total**: ~3 minutes per document capture; automated for metadata/storage

## W256. Enterprise Document Retention & Archiving Policy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | End of fiscal year and periodic archiving schedules |
| **Frequency** | Annual batch process |
| **Volume** | ~500,000+ documents archived annually |
| **Owner** | Legal & Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, IT Database Administrator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Policy Definition**: Legal Officer defines retention rules in the ERP DMS (e.g., Financial Invoices: 10 years per BIR regulation; HR records: 5 years post-separation) | Legal Officer | Legal Officer | 2–4 hours |
| 2 | **Automated Aging**: ERP DMS continuously monitors document creation dates against the retention policy matrix | System | — | Automated |
| 3 | **Archiving Run**: At fiscal year-end, IT DBA triggers the archiving utility. Documents exceeding the "active" threshold (e.g., 2 years) are compressed and moved to cold storage (cheaper storage tier), but remain accessible via slow-retrieval query | IT DBA | IT DBA | 4–8 hours |
| 4 | **Legal Hold**: For entities under active BIR audit or litigation, Legal Officer places a "Legal Hold" on the location/vendor, overriding automated deletion rules | Legal Officer | Legal & Compliance Officer | 30 min |
| 5 | **Purge/Destruction**: Documents reaching the absolute end of their legal retention period (e.g., 10 years + 1 day) are permanently purged from all tiers. IT generates a Certificate of Digital Destruction for compliance records | System | IT DBA | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Data Archiving Utility
- Cloud Storage Tiers (Hot vs. Cold)
- BIR compliance reporting

### Pain Points / Risks
- Accidental purging of documents under audit due to missed Legal Hold flags.
- Inability to retrieve 8-year-old proprietary formats after software upgrades.

### Staffing Implication
Annual batch process. Policy definition: 2-4 hours once. Archiving run: 4-8 hours per year-end. Legal hold management: ad-hoc, ~30 min per hold. Absorbed by IT DBA + Legal Officer.

### Time Estimate
**Total**: ~4-8 hours for annual archiving run; ~2-4 hours for initial policy definition

## W686. Document Approval Routing & Digital Signature Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Document requiring multi-level approval is created or received (contracts, purchase orders above threshold, board resolutions, BIR filings) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (~200-300 approval workflows/day enterprise-wide) |
| **Volume** | ~60,000-75,000 routed documents/year across 200 stores, 4 DCs, and HQ |
| **Owner** | ERP Administrator (system config); Department Heads (workflow ownership) |
| **Participants** | Originators, Approvers (multi-level), Legal, Compliance, Internal Audit |

### Background

BuildRight processes thousands of documents requiring approval each month — from PO approvals at stores, to vendor contract sign-offs at HQ, to board resolution execution per W243. While individual workflows (W7 AP, W36 vendor, W243 POA) include their own approval steps, no workflow governs the enterprise-wide approval routing infrastructure: how approval chains are configured, how digital signatures are managed, how delegation-of-authority (DOA) rules are enforced, and how approval audit trails are maintained for BIR and SEC compliance.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Originator submits document**: user creates or uploads document in ERP (contract, PO, expense report, BIR filing); system auto-classifies document type and identifies required approval chain based on DOA matrix (e.g., PO <PHP 50K: Store Manager approves; PHP 50K-500K: Category Manager + Procurement Manager; >PHP 500K: VP Procurement + CFO) | Originator | Department Head | 5 min |
| 2 | **System routes to first approver**: ERP pushes approval notification to assigned approver via email, mobile app, and in-app alert; includes document preview, amount, requester, and deadline; SLA timer starts (standard: 24 hours for routine, 4 hours for urgent, 1 hour for critical) | System | ERP Administrator | Automated |
| 3 | **Approver reviews and acts**: approver opens document, reviews content and supporting attachments, and selects Approve, Reject (with reason), or Request Revision; digital signature is applied automatically upon approval action using approver's registered credentials | Approver | — | 5-15 min |
| 4 | **Escalation for overdue approvals**: if approver does not act within SLA window, system escalates: (a) first escalation: reminder to approver + notification to approver's supervisor; (b) second escalation (after 2× SLA): auto-route to designated alternate approver per delegation rules; (c) critical items: escalation to Department Head and VP level | System | ERP Administrator | Automated |
| 5 | **Delegation-of-authority management**: approver going on leave configures delegation in system — designates alternate approver with same authority level, specifies delegation period; system routes approvals to delegate during delegation window; all delegate actions logged with original approver's authority | Approver / ERP Admin | Department Head | 10 min per delegation setup |
| 6 | **Multi-entity routing**: for documents affecting multiple BuildRight entities (e.g., intercompany agreements, shared services invoices), system routes to approvers in each relevant entity per W442 intercompany framework; all entity approvals must complete before document is finalized | System / Multi-entity Approvers | VP Legal | 1-2 days |
| 7 | **Digital signature verification**: system maintains digital signature registry for all authorized signatories: (a) registration: each signatory enrolls with verified identity (government ID + biometric); (b) usage: each approval action applies digital signature with timestamp, IP address, device ID; (c) verification: any party can verify signature authenticity; (d) BIR compliance: digital signatures comply with BIR e-invoicing requirements and Electronic Commerce Act (RA 8792) | ERP Administrator | VP Legal | Initial setup: 2 hours; ongoing: automated |
| 8 | **Audit trail & compliance reporting**: system maintains immutable audit trail for every approval action: who approved, when, from what device, with what authority level, any delegation chain; quarterly, Internal Audit reviews approval audit trails for segregation-of-duties violations per W359 SOD monitoring | System / Internal Audit | VP Legal | Quarterly: 4-6 hours |

### System Touchpoints

- ERP workflow engine with configurable approval routing rules (DOC-009)
- Digital signature module with RA 8792 compliance
- Delegation-of-authority (DOA) matrix configuration
- Mobile approval app with push notifications
- Audit trail module with immutable logging (W359 SOD integration)
- Multi-entity routing engine (W442 intercompany integration)
- BIR e-invoicing compliance module (FIN-010)

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Approval bottlenecks**: senior leaders (VP, C-suite) are single points of failure in approval chains; absence without delegation setup blocks time-sensitive transactions (BIR filings, vendor payments, store construction approvals)
- **Delegation abuse**: delegates approving items outside their normal authority scope; mitigated by audit trail monitoring and quarterly SOD review per W359
- **Digital signature legal validity**: Philippine courts and BIR may challenge digital signatures if not compliant with RA 8792 and NPC guidelines; mitigated by verified identity enrollment and secure credential management
- **Mobile approval security**: approvers using personal devices introduce risk of unauthorized approval if device is compromised; mitigated by MFA requirement and device registration

### Staffing Implication

- **ERP Administrator**: ~4-6 hours/month on DOA matrix updates, delegation troubleshooting, and digital signature enrollment. Absorbed within existing role.
- **Department Heads**: ~15-30 min/day reviewing and acting on approval queue items. Absorbed within existing management responsibilities.
- **No incremental headcount**.

### Time Estimate

- Per approval action: 5-15 min review + automated routing
- Delegation setup: 10 min per instance
- DOA matrix update: 2-3 hours/quarter
- Quarterly audit review: 4-6 hours

## W687. Document Template Management & Version Control

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New document type needed; existing template requires update (regulatory change, branding update, process improvement) |
| **Frequency** | Template creation: ad-hoc (~5-10 new templates/year); template updates: quarterly review cycle; version control: continuous |
| **Volume** | ~150-200 active document templates across all departments; ~20-30 template updates/year |
| **Owner** | ERP Administrator (technical); Department Heads (content) |
| **Participants** | Legal, Compliance, Department SMEs, Brand/Marketing |

### Background

BuildRight operates 200 stores and 4 DCs using standardized documents: BIR-registered invoices, delivery receipts, purchase orders, employment contracts, vendor agreements, safety checklists, and compliance forms. Without centralized template management, stores create ad-hoc versions of documents that deviate from corporate standards — incorrect BIR details, outdated legal terms, wrong pricing formats, or non-compliant safety forms. This workflow ensures all enterprise documents originate from controlled templates with version tracking and regulatory compliance.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Template request**: Department Head or SME submits template creation or update request via service portal: specifies document type, purpose, target users, required fields, regulatory requirements (BIR format, DOLE requirements, DENR forms) | Department SME | Department Head | 30 min |
| 2 | **Template design**: ERP Administrator or designated template designer creates template in ERP document engine: defines fields (static text, dynamic merge fields from ERP data, calculated fields), layout, formatting, header/footer (BuildRight branding, entity-specific details), and output format (PDF, Excel, print) | ERP Administrator | Department Head | 2-4 hours |
| 3 | **Legal & compliance review**: for templates with regulatory or legal implications (contracts, BIR forms, employment documents, safety forms): Legal reviews language and mandatory disclosures; Compliance verifies alignment with BIR, DOLE, DENR, SEC requirements; Brand/Marketing verifies corporate identity standards | Legal / Compliance | VP Legal | 1-3 days |
| 4 | **Testing & validation**: test template with sample data in UAT environment: verify dynamic field population (customer name, amounts, dates), formatting, page breaks, BIR-mandated print positions; validate output across print, PDF, and email channels | ERP Administrator | Department SME | 2-4 hours |
| 5 | **Version control & publishing**: ERP Administrator assigns version number (e.g., v2.1), records change log (what changed, why, who approved), publishes to production template library, and archives previous version with "superseded" status; system prevents use of superseded templates | ERP Administrator | Department Head | 30 min |
| 6 | **Distribution & training**: published template is available to all authorized users enterprise-wide; for significant changes, Department Head communicates changes to affected users; for BIR or regulatory template changes, Compliance issues mandatory compliance bulletin to all locations | Department Head / Compliance | VP Legal | 1-2 hours |
| 7 | **Quarterly template audit**: ERP Administrator runs template usage report: identifies templates not used in >6 months (candidates for deactivation), templates being bypassed (users creating documents outside template system), and templates approaching regulatory review dates (e.g., annual BIR form updates); presents findings to Department Heads | ERP Administrator | — | 4 hours/quarter |

### System Touchpoints

- ERP document template engine with version control (DOC-009)
- Dynamic merge field configuration (ERP data → document fields)
- Template library with role-based access control
- BIR e-invoicing template compliance module (FIN-010)
- Service portal for template request intake
- Template usage analytics and audit reporting

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Regulatory template changes outpace internal processes**: BIR, DOLE, and DENR issue updated form requirements (e.g., new BIR 2307 format, updated DOLE labor standards forms) with short compliance deadlines; if template updates lag, stores may submit non-compliant documents
- **Template workarounds**: users creating documents outside the template system (Word/Excel) to avoid perceived template rigidity, creating compliance and brand consistency risks
- **Multi-entity template complexity**: 5 BuildRight entities may require slightly different templates (different TIN, different registered address, different signatories) while maintaining consistent branding and compliance
- **Version confusion during transition**: when a new template version is published, in-flight transactions using the old template may need special handling

### Staffing Implication

- **ERP Administrator**: ~8-12 hours/month on template design, updates, and testing. Absorbed within existing role.
- **Legal/Compliance**: ~4-6 hours/month on template reviews. Absorbed within existing roles.
- **No incremental headcount**.

### Time Estimate

- New template: 4-8 hours (design + review + testing + publishing)
- Template update: 2-4 hours
- Quarterly template audit: 4 hours
- Annual template review cycle: 16-24 hours total

## W688. Contract & Agreement Lifecycle Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New contract or agreement required; existing contract approaching renewal/expiry; amendment or termination request |
| **Frequency** | ~30-50 new contracts/month; ~100-150 active contract renewals/year; ~20-30 amendments/year |
| **Volume** | ~800-1,000 active contracts across all entities (vendor contracts, lease agreements, service contracts, customer agreements, partnership MOUs, government contracts) |
| **Owner** | Legal Counsel |
| **Participants** | Requesting Department Head, Procurement (vendor contracts), Real Estate (leases), Finance (financial terms), VP Legal (high-value contracts), External Counsel |

### Background

BuildRight manages approximately 800-1,000 active contracts across its 5 entities: vendor supply agreements (800+ vendors per model-company profile), store lease agreements (200+ locations), 3PL logistics contracts, IT service agreements, customer wholesale agreements, and government procurement contracts. While W62 covers vendor contract management and W124 covers corporate governance, no workflow addresses the end-to-end contract lifecycle from request through execution, performance monitoring, renewal, and termination — applicable across all contract types.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Contract request intake**: Department Head submits contract request via legal portal: contract type (vendor, lease, service, customer, government, partnership), counterparty name, business purpose, estimated value, desired term, key commercial terms, target execution date | Department Head | Legal Counsel | 30 min |
| 2 | **Counterparty due diligence**: Legal or Compliance verifies counterparty: SEC/DTI registration check, BIR tax compliance (2303 certificate), litigation search, sanctions screening, existing relationship check (are there existing contracts with this counterparty across any BuildRight entity); for vendors: integrate with W620 vendor due diligence results | Legal Assistant | Legal Counsel | 2-4 hours |
| 3 | **Contract drafting**: Legal Counsel drafts contract using approved template per W687; incorporates BuildRight standard terms and conditions, Philippine law governing clause, BIR-compliant invoicing requirements, data privacy provisions per RA 10173, and force majeure clause (typhoon, earthquake, pandemic); for standard-value contracts below threshold: use pre-approved template with minimal customization | Legal Counsel | VP Legal | 2-8 hours |
| 4 | **Internal review & negotiation**: route contract to relevant stakeholders: Finance (payment terms, financial guarantees), Procurement (SLA metrics, delivery terms), IT (data privacy, technology clauses), Department Head (business terms); collect redlines and consolidate into negotiation position | Legal Counsel | VP Legal | 2-5 days |
| 5 | **Counterparty negotiation**: Legal Counsel or Department Head negotiates final terms with counterparty; all redlines tracked in contract management system; significant deviations from BuildRight standard terms flagged for VP Legal approval | Legal Counsel / Department Head | VP Legal | 1-14 days |
| 6 | **Approval & execution**: final contract routed for approval per DOA matrix per W686: contract value <PHP 1M: Department Head + Legal Counsel; PHP 1M-10M: VP + CFO; >PHP 10M: CEO + Board resolution per W243; executed via digital signature per W686 or wet-ink notarization if required by law (Philippine notarization required for real property leases >1 year, government contracts above threshold) | Legal Counsel | VP Legal / CEO | 1-3 days |
| 7 | **Contract registration & obligation setup**: executed contract registered in contract management system: key dates (commencement, expiry, renewal notice deadline), financial obligations (payment schedule, rate escalation), SLA metrics, special conditions; system sets up automated reminders for renewal notice deadlines (90, 60, 30 days before expiry) | Legal Assistant | Legal Counsel | 30 min |
| 8 | **Contract performance monitoring**: monthly, contract owner reviews SLA compliance and commercial performance against contracted terms; for vendor contracts: integrate with W669 vendor contract compliance; for lease agreements: integrate with W635 PFRS 16 lease accounting; for service contracts: review deliverable completion and SLA metrics | Contract Owner / Department Head | Legal Counsel | 2-4 hours/month |
| 9 | **Renewal, amendment, or termination**: 90 days before expiry, system alerts contract owner and Legal: (a) renewal: if performance satisfactory, initiate renewal per original or revised terms; negotiate rate adjustments (escalation clauses, market rate review); (b) amendment: document changes via formal amendment or change order signed by both parties; (c) termination: if performance unsatisfactory or business need ended, Legal issues termination notice per contract terms; manage transition/exit obligations | Legal Counsel / Contract Owner | VP Legal | 2-5 days per renewal/termination |
| 10 | **Annual contract portfolio review**: VP Legal and CFO conduct annual review of total contract portfolio: contract value by category, renewal pipeline, expiry concentration risk (too many contracts expiring simultaneously), standard term compliance rate, contract disputes summary, litigation exposure | VP Legal / CFO | CEO | 1 day/year |

### System Touchpoints

- Contract lifecycle management (CLM) system (DOC-010)
- Template management integration per W687
- Approval routing per W686
- Digital signature module per W686
- Vendor management integration per W620, W669
- Lease accounting integration per W635
- Calendar and reminder engine (renewal notices, expiry alerts)
- Contract analytics dashboard (portfolio value, renewal pipeline, SLA compliance)
- Document storage per W255

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Contract expiry oversight**: missed renewal notice deadlines resulting in unwanted auto-renewal at unfavorable terms or contract lapse disrupting operations (especially critical for 3PL logistics, IT services, and store leases)
- **Multi-entity contract fragmentation**: contracts signed by different BuildRight entities without centralized visibility — same vendor may have different terms across entities, losing volume leverage and creating compliance gaps
- **Philippine notarization requirements**: contracts requiring notarization (real property, government procurement above threshold) add 1-3 days to execution timeline and require physical document handling
- **Contract obligation leakage**: signed contracts with financial obligations (minimum purchase commitments, volume rebates, penalty clauses) not properly tracked, resulting in missed deadlines or uncollected penalties
- **Standard term erosion**: repeated negotiations systematically weakening BuildRight standard terms (liability caps, indemnification, termination rights) without visibility across the portfolio

### Staffing Implication

- **Legal Counsel**: ~20-30 hours/month on contract drafting, review, and negotiation. Absorbed within existing role.
- **Legal Assistant**: ~10-15 hours/month on due diligence, registration, and calendar management. Absorbed within existing role.
- **Department Heads**: ~4-8 hours/month each on contract review and business term negotiation. Absorbed within existing roles.
- **No incremental headcount**.

### Time Estimate

- New contract (standard): 2-5 days from request to execution
- New contract (complex/negotiated): 2-4 weeks
- Contract renewal: 2-5 days
- Monthly performance monitoring: 2-4 hours
- Annual portfolio review: 1 day
