# VS-164: Smart Locker & Automated Parcel Collection Network

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Smart-locker & automated parcel-collection network workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning
the discipline by which BuildRight deploys and operates a **network of automated, self-service
collection lockers** across (and beyond) its ~205 sites, through which customers collect BOPIS/
ship-to-locker orders, drop off returns, and collect third-party parcels — extending the in-store
BOPIS counter (VS-10) and the unattended-retail technology footprint (VS-149) into a genuine
**out-of-hours, low-friction, network-scale collection channel** that lifts omnichannel convenience
and captures a share of the growing parcel-pickup/returns economy.

This matters at BuildRight's omnichannel scale. The company runs **~42,900 ecommerce orders/month
(~515K/yr)** with a **BOPIS share** (profile §8.2) across all 200 stores, a **COD-heavy** market
(VS-142), a growing **marketplace presence** (VS-65/VS-95), and ~2.8M monthly in-store footfall — a
large base of customers for whom a fast, contactless, 24/7-capable pickup/returns option at a locker
(in-store, at the parking lot, at a transit hub, or in a partner condo/mall) removes the
queue-at-the-counter and the within-store-hours constraints that today limit BOPIS convenience and
drive abandonment. Lockers also unlock **returns drop-off** (a major friction in the VS-32 returns
chain) and a **third-party parcel pickup** revenue stream (BuildRight as a collection point for
outside carriers/marketplaces — the Amazon-Hub / Lazada-locker model). Across 200 stores + select
off-site locations, a locker network is a material customer-experience and footfall-driver asset
(locker pickups drive incremental store visits/baskets, linking VS-13/VS-14).

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. The defining terms — "smart locker network",
"automated parcel collection", "locker fleet operations", "parcel pickup locker", and "returns
locker" — appear in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers (the ~19 incidental "smart
locker" references are scattered across VS-10, VS-65, VS-93, VS-149 as single-step mentions with no
owning PA). The capability is **not** covered by **VS-10 (Ecommerce & Digital / BOPIS)**, which
owns the **BOPIS fulfillment transaction** (pick, stage, release at the customer-service counter
within store hours) — this value stream owns the **locker network infrastructure and the
locker-mediated collection/returns channel** that extends BOPIS beyond the counter and beyond store
hours. It is **not** covered by **VS-149 (Self-Checkout, Scan-&-Go & Unattended Retail Tech)**,
which owns **in-store unattended selling technology** (SCO, scan-&-go, vending) — this owns a
**network of outdoor/transit/off-site parcel lockers** with distinct fleet, siting, access-control,
and 3P-parcel-integration needs. It is **not** covered by **VS-93 (Dark Store & Micro-Fulfillment)**
or **VS-60 (Omnichannel Order Routing)**, which fulfill orders — this owns the **collection endpoint**
network. And it is **not** covered by **VS-32 (Returns & Reverse Logistics)**, which processes
returns — this owns the **locker returns-drop-off channel** that feeds VS-32. The closest existing
touchpoint is the BOPIS counter pickup (VS-10 W11) and the "smart locker and customer pickup
queuing" touchpoint (W247 in the system map) — single steps, not a network program.

This value stream owns the end-to-end locker-network discipline: network strategy & business model
(own-operate vs locker-vendor partnership like Luxer One/Quadient/Smartbox, site selection, host-
revenue for off-site lockers, 3P-parcel-pickup monetization); locker siting, procurement & deployment
(hardware, access-control integration, capacity planning); collection operations (BOPIS staging &
replenishment into lockers, access-code/QR management, returns drop-off, overflow/abandonment, 3P
parcel pickup integration, the customer pickup journey); and maintenance, ERP/ecommerce/loyalty
integration, accessibility (RA 7277/BP 344) & privacy (RA 10173), security/vandalism, uptime/SLA, and
network analytics/optimization. It integrates tightly with VS-10 (BOPIS/order management), VS-32
(returns), VS-65/VS-95 (marketplace), VS-149 (unattended tech), VS-80 (payment for any locker fees),
VS-13/VS-75 (loyalty/app), and VS-27 (locker IoT platform).
