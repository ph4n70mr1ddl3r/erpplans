## W4785. Preventive Maintenance, Cleaning & EVSE Health Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Calendar/metering interval; CSMS health alert (W4781); post-storm (VS-69) |
| **Frequency** | Per EVSE quarterly + continuous monitoring |
| **Volume** | ~all charge points across ~205 sites |
| **Owner** | EV Program Manager / Facilities (VS-138) |
| **Participants** | Facilities/EVSE technician, CPO/vendor (W4770), Energy (VS-108) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Run the PM schedule per EVSE: connector/cable wear inspection, cooling/fan, ground-fault/RCD test, emergency-stop test, firmware/OCPP health, metering check (VS-115), signage/wayfinding condition, and the electrical-safety visual; trigger from the CSMS asset register | Facilities / EVSE Technician | EV Program Manager / Facilities Lead | Quarterly |
| 2 | Monitor EVSE health continuously via the CSMS (W4781): uptime, fault codes, session-success rate, connector utilization, temperature, and connectivity; auto-flag degradation (e.g., rising fault rate, connector wear) before failure; coordinate with the CPO/vendor SLA | IT / Facilities / EV Program | EV Program Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Clean and maintain the bay: cable management, screen/camera cleaning, bay cleaning (VS-138), weatherproofing (tropical/outdoor), and post-typhoon inspection (VS-69) for water/debris damage; keep the bay safe and presentable (customer-experience VS-13) | Facilities / Store Ops | Facilities Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- CSMS health/asset (W4781); facilities/PM (VS-138 W240 pattern); metering (VS-115); CPO SLA; analytics (W4792)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Connector-wear risk**: Worn connectors → poor contact/safety/failed sessions; mitigated by wear inspection and replacement
- **Fault-degradation risk**: Unnoticed gradual degradation → surprise outages; mitigated by health monitoring and predictive maintenance (VS-133)
- **Storm-damage risk**: Typhoon/water damage (VS-69) → outage/safety; mitigated by post-storm inspection and weatherproofing

### Time Estimate
- ~per EVSE PM 1–2 hrs; ~continuous monitoring absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- CSMS health-driven PM scheduling; predictive-fault from pattern analytics; auto-fault ticketing (W4786)

### Controls
- CTL-140 (ensure EV-charging uptime & tariff compliance.)
- CTL: quarterly PM per EVSE; health monitored continuously; connectors/safety devices tested; bay maintained

### Cross-references
- links to VS-138 (facilities PM), VS-115 (metering), VS-69 (typhoon), W4781 (CSMS), W4786 (fault response), W4792 (analytics)

## W4786. Reactive Fault Triage, Field Repair & Vendor Dispatch

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | CSMS fault alert (W4781); customer-reported fault; session failure |
| **Frequency** | Per fault |
| **Volume** | ~30–80 faults/month at scale (scales with network size & utilization) |
| **Owner** | EV Program Manager / Facilities (VS-138) / IT (VS-27) |
| **Participants** | EVSE technician, CPO/vendor, Store Ops (VS-07), IT (VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Triage the fault from CSMS data: classify (hardware/electrical/software/connectivity/payment), severity (safety-critical vs degraded), and scope (single charger vs site vs network); safety-critical faults → immediate isolation/de-energization (W4787) | Facilities / IT / EV Program | EV Program Manager | Per fault |
| 2 | Dispatch repair: in-house technician for simple fixes (restart, connector, fuse, cleaning) or CPO/vendor field service under SLA (W4770); manage spare-parts (VS-31), substitute routing of drivers to adjacent chargers/sites, and the repair SLA | Facilities / CPO / Procurement | EV Program Manager | Per fault |
| 3 | Restore, retest, and return to service: verify the fix end-to-end (charge test), update the asset/fault record (VS-35/W4781), feed root-cause to PM/analytics (W4785/W4792), and if a recurring manufacturer defect, escalate to vendor/warranty (W4770) | Facilities / EV Program | EV Program Manager | Per fault |

### System Touchpoints
- CSMS fault/asset (W4781); facilities/service (VS-138); parts (VS-31); CPO SLA; analytics (W4792)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Long-downtime risk**: Slow fault response → lost revenue + poor CX; mitigated by SLA + spare-parts availability
- **Safety-fault-mishandling risk**: A safety-critical fault not isolated → injury/fire (W4787); mitigated by immediate isolation protocol
- **Recurring-defect risk**: Same fault repeating across units; mitigated by root-cause → vendor escalation

### Time Estimate
- ~per fault 1 hr–2 days depending on severity/parts

### Automation Opportunity
- CSMS auto-fault-ticket + dispatch routing; parts-availability check; charge-test automation; root-cause analytics

### Controls
- CTL-721 (ensure controlled execution — Operations, Maintenance, Compliance & Network Analytics (PA-163.3).)
- CTL: faults triaged & dispatched per SLA; safety-critical faults isolated immediately; fixes retested; root-cause fed to PM

### Cross-references
- links to VS-138 (facilities), VS-31 (parts), VS-27 (IT), W4770 (vendor SLA), W4781 (CSMS), W4785 (PM), W4787 (safety), W4792 (analytics)

## W4787. Charging-Site Safety, Emergency Response & Incident Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Safety-critical fault (W4786); fire/shock/arc incident; vehicle incident at a bay |
| **Frequency** | Per event (rare) |
| **Volume** | Rare: ~2–6 safety events/year, scaling with network size |
| **Owner** | EV Program Manager / Safety (VS-24) / Facilities (VS-138) |
| **Participants** | Safety Officer (VS-24), BFP, Facilities, Legal (VS-100), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Respond to the safety event: emergency-stop/isolation/de-energization of the affected EVSE, scene safety, call emergency services (BFP fire / medical) if needed, evacuate/cordon the bay, and notify Safety (VS-24), Facilities, Legal (VS-100), and Insurance (VS-26) for serious events | Facilities / Store Ops / Safety | Safety Officer / EV Program Manager | Per event (urgent) |
| 2 | Investigate and document: preserve the EVSE/fault data (CSMS W4781), gather statements/photos, coordinate with the EVSE vendor/CPO, and determine root cause (electrical, thermal, vehicle-side, misuse); manage any injury/property-damage claim (VS-26) and regulatory notification (BFP/DOE/DOLE per VS-24) | Safety / Facilities / Legal / CPO | Safety Officer / Legal | Per event |
| 3 | Remediate and prevent: corrective action on the affected EVSE and fleet-wide if systemic (e.g., firmware recall, connector model replacement), update the safety case/procedures, and feed lessons to continuous improvement (VS-133) and the audit (W4791) | Safety / Facilities / EV Program | Safety Officer / EV Program Manager | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Safety incident (VS-24); CSMS evidence (W4781); insurance claim (VS-26); legal (VS-100); facilities (VS-138); audit (W4791)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fire/shock risk**: High-voltage EVSE fire/shock → catastrophic injury/property damage; mitigated by safety design (GFCI/RCD, emergency-stop), PM (W4785), and isolation protocol
- **Evidence-loss risk**: Lost CSMS/evidence → failed investigation/claim; mitigated by immediate evidence preservation
- **Systemic-defect risk**: A defect affecting all units of a model; mitigated by fleet-wide corrective action

### Time Estimate
- ~per event 2–8 hrs response + investigation days-weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- CSMS auto-isolate on safety-fault; emergency-response playbook; auto-evidence preservation; fleet-wide defect detection

### Controls
- CTL-721 (ensure controlled execution — Operations, Maintenance, Compliance & Network Analytics (PA-163.3).)
- CTL: safety events isolated immediately; emergency services engaged; evidence preserved; corrective action + fleet-wide if systemic

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (safety), VS-26 (insurance/claims), VS-100 (legal), VS-138 (facilities), W4781 (CSMS evidence), W4785/W4786 (PM/fault), W4791 (audit), VS-133 (improvement)

### Cross-references

## W4789. Uptime/SLA Management, Availability Reporting & Penalty Administration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous SLA monitoring; monthly CPO review; SLA breach |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + monthly |
| **Volume** | 1 network SLA across all ~205 sites (uptime target ≥ 97%) |
| **Owner** | EV Program Manager / IT (VS-27) |
| **Participants** | CPO (W4770), IT (VS-27), Finance (VS-17), Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monitor uptime/SLA per the CPO contract (W4770) and internal targets: per-charger and network availability, session-success rate, time-to-repair (W4786), and fault frequency; track against the SLA thresholds (e.g., 97–99% availability) via the CSMS (W4781) | IT / EV Program | EV Program Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Administer SLA penalties/credits and vendor governance: where the CPO underperforms, apply contractual credits/penalties (W4770), manage performance improvement plans, and escalate persistent underperformance to re-compete (W4770); reconcile with Finance | EV Program / Finance / Legal | EV Program Manager / Legal | Monthly |
| 3 | Report availability to internal stakeholders (store ops, leadership) and publish charger availability to drivers (app VS-75 / roaming W4782) so drivers see live status; feed uptime performance to analytics (W4792) and the network-strategy review (W4769) | IT / EV Program / App team | EV Program Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- CSMS availability (W4781); CPO SLA/governance (W4770); finance (VS-17); legal (VS-100.3); app (VS-75); analytics (W4792)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **SLA-underperformance risk**: Persistent CPO underperformance → poor CX + lost revenue; mitigated by penalty administration and re-compete
- **Availability-opacity risk**: Drivers not seeing live status → failed trips; mitigated by live availability publishing
- **Penalty-dispute risk**: Disputed SLA measurements; mitigated by CSMS-sourced authoritative metrics

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous monitoring; ~monthly review absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- CSMS availability auto-dashboard; SLA-breach auto-penalty; live availability feed to app/roaming

### Controls
- CTL-140 (ensure EV-charging uptime & tariff compliance.)
- CTL: uptime monitored vs SLA; penalties/credits administered; live availability published; performance feeds strategy

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27 (IT), W4770 (CPO SLA), W4781 (CSMS), W4786 (repair), VS-75 (app), W4782 (roaming), W4769/W4792 (strategy/analytics)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly settlement; financial close (VS-17); CPO revenue-share recon |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + period close |
| **Volume** | ~monthly settlements across the network |
| **Owner** | Finance (VS-17/VS-80) / EV Program |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17), Treasury (VS-18), CPO, Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Settle host revenue: for own-operate, reconcile charging revenue (W4783) to metering (VS-115) and bank settlement (VS-80); for host-partner, reconcile the CPO revenue-share/lease settlement per the contract (W4770/W4772); manage CDR-based roaming settlements (W4782) | Finance / Treasury / EV Program | Finance Controller | Monthly |
| 2 | Reconcile and post: match revenue to sessions/metering, allocate costs (energy W4775, demand-charge, maintenance, insurance, capex amortization) per the unit economics (W4772), recognize revenue per PFRS 15 (VS-157) or lease accounting (VS-148), and post to GL (VS-17) with correct intercompany (VS-72) | Finance / Treasury | Finance Controller / CFO | Monthly |
| 3 | Manage exceptions and disputes: failed/reconciled CDRs (W4782), CPO settlement disputes, unreconciled variances (links VS-118 revenue assurance), and the period close (VS-17); feed financial performance to analytics (W4792) | Finance / EV Program | Finance Controller | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Payment/settlement (VS-80); GL/revenue (VS-17/VS-157); lease accounting (VS-148); treasury (VS-18); intercompany (VS-72); metering (VS-115); CPO contract (W4770); analytics (W4792)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Settlement-leakage risk**: Unreconciled CDRs/revenue-share → revenue loss (VS-118); mitigated by CDR validation and recon
- **Revenue-recognition risk**: Mis-applied PFRS 15/lease accounting (VS-157/VS-148); mitigated by Finance/Legal review
- **Cost-allocation risk**: Energy/demand-charge mis-allocated across entities (VS-72); mitigated by clear allocation

### Time Estimate
- ~monthly settlement 2–4 days; ~period close absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- CDR-to-GL auto-recon; revenue-recognition engine link (VS-157); variance exception dashboard (VS-118)

### Controls
- CTL-140 (ensure EV-charging uptime & tariff compliance.)
- CTL: revenue reconciled to metering/sessions; PFRS 15/lease accounting applied; intercompany allocated; variances investigated

### Cross-references

## W4792. Network Analytics, Utilization & Decarbonization Impact Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly review; annual strategy/capex (W4769/W4776); ESG reporting (VS-25) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly + annual |
| **Volume** | 1 group analytics view |
| **Owner** | EV Program Manager / Analytics (VS-28) |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17.4), ESG (VS-25), Marketing (VS-14), Store Ops (VS-07) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Track network performance: ports live, availability/uptime (W4789), utilization (sessions/port/day, kWh), session-success, revenue/host-revenue (W4790), cost per kWh, margin, basket-uplift (POS VS-08/VS-10 attributed to charging dwell), and customer NPS (VS-13); benchmark across sites/regions | Analytics / Finance / EV Program | EV Program Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | Track decarbonization impact: kWh delivered, renewable fraction (own solar W4780), tCO₂e enabled (displaced fuel), and contribution to BuildRight's Scope 1/2/3 + customer-Scope-3 reporting (VS-25); validate the green-attribution (W4780) to avoid double-counting | ESG / Analytics / Energy | ESG Lead / EV Program Manager | Monthly/quarterly |
| 3 | Report to leadership quarterly and feed the annual strategy/capex case (W4769/W4776): reallocate capex from under- to over-performing sites, adjust own-operate/host mix, drive continuous improvement (VS-133), and report decarbonization impact to the board/ESG stakeholders (VS-25/VS-36) | Analytics / EV Program / Strategy | VP Asset & Infra / COO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (VS-28); CSMS data (W4781); finance/FP&A (VS-17.4); POS/basket (VS-08/VS-10); ESG/GHG (VS-25); solar (VS-108); loyalty (VS-13)

- **Strategic-invisibility risk**: If basket-uplift/decarbonization not measured, the program is cut; mitigated by the KPI set

### Time Estimate
- ~monthly 2–3 days; ~quarterly leadership review absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- CSMS-to-analytics auto-feed; basket-uplift attribution (POS × charging-dwell); tCO₂e auto-calc; capex-reallocation recommendations

### Controls
- CTL-721 (ensure controlled execution — Operations, Maintenance, Compliance & Network Analytics (PA-163.3).)
- CTL: network performance + basket-uplift + decarbonization reported; green-attribution validated; outcomes feed strategy/capex/improvement
