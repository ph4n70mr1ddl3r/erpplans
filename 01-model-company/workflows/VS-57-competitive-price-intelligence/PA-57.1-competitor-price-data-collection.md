## W2214. Competitor Price Scraping & Collection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly collection cycle |
| **Frequency** | Weekly; ~500–1,000 price points |
| **Volume** | ~500–1,000 prices/week across 4–5 competitors |
| **Owner** | Pricing Analyst |
| **Participants** | Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing Analyst collects competitor prices: web scraping of competitor ecommerce sites, mystery-shop store visits for in-store prices, third-party price-intelligence feed; normalizes to BuildRight's SKU equivalents | Pricing Analyst | Category Manager | 4–6 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints
- Price-intelligence platform (links to VS-28 / W113, VS-44 / market research) — the scraped/mystery-shopped dataset
- Item/SKU matching (links to VS-29 / W252, W403 competitor master) — the competitor-SKU-to-BuildRight-SKU mapping
- Pricing master (links to VS-29 / W289) — the BuildRight price for comparison

### Pain Points / Risks
- **SKU-mismatch risk**: a competitor's "comparable" SKU isn't truly equivalent (different spec/pack), making the price comparison misleading; mitigated by the documented SKU-matching rule (equivalent spec, equivalent pack size)
- **Scraping-legality/ToS risk**: aggressive scraping violates competitor website ToS; mitigated by the compliant collection (respect robots.txt, use licensed third-party feeds, mystery-shopping within RA 10667 conduct limits)

### Automation Opportunity
- Scraping automated for ecommerce prices; SKU-match auto-suggested from the item-master mapping

### Controls
- CTL-175 (ensure competitor-price data integrity & price-match governance.)
- CTL (competition-law conduct — links to VS-129 / W2683); operational: Category Manager weekly collection-review

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/week Pricing Analyst = **~250 hours/year**.

## W2215. Price Matching Decision & Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Significant price gap identified (>10%) |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; ~20–30 decisions/month |
| **Volume** | ~20–30/month |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager reviews the >10%-gap SKUs; decides: match (lower price), differentiate (justify premium via value-add), or hold (transient competitor promo); executes the price change in the pricing master | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 30–60 min/decision |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing master (links to VS-29 / W289, VS-08 / W40 price-change execution) — the price change
- Margin guard (links to VS-17 / W85 product costing, VS-158) — the margin-floor check before a match
- Analytics (links to VS-57 / W2230 margin analysis) — the margin-impact view

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Race-to-the-bottom risk**: matching every competitor cut erodes category margin to zero; mitigated by the margin-floor guard (no price below the documented floor) and the differentiate-option
- **Transient-promo-matching risk**: BuildRight matches a competitor's 1-week promo as a permanent price cut, surrendering margin after the competitor's promo ends; mitigated by the promo-vs-permanent distinction (match a permanent cut; counter a promo with a promo)

### Automation Opportunity
- Gap auto-flagged at >10%; margin-impact auto-computed before the decision

### Controls
- CTL-175 (ensure competitor-price data integrity & price-match governance.)
- CTL (price-change authorization — links to VS-08 / W40); CTL (margin-floor guard); operational: VP Merchandising sign-off on matches below the margin floor

### Time Estimate
~45 min/decision × ~25/month ≈ ~1,125 min ≈ **~19 hours/month** Category Manager.

### System Touchpoints
- Promo-intelligence (links to VS-57 / W2214, VS-58 / W2260 competitor promo intelligence) — the competitor-promo dataset
- Promo planning (links to VS-58 / W2238 coupon design, VS-14 / W13 promotions) — the counter-promo
- Analytics (links to VS-57 / W2234 promo ROI) — the counter-promo ROI view

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Late-detection risk**: a competitor promo is detected days after launch, ceding sales; mitigated by the weekly (not monthly) monitoring cadence and the social-media alert
- **Over-reaction risk**: BuildRight launches a costly counter-promo for a minor competitor event; mitigated by the threat-assessment (is the competitor's promo material to BuildRight's category?)

### Automation Opportunity
- Competitor-promo auto-detected from circular/social-media monitoring; threat-level auto-assessed

### Controls
- CTL-400 (ensure controlled execution — Competitor Price Data Collection & Analysis (PA-57.1).)
- Operational: Category Manager counter-promo sign-off

### Time Estimate
~3 hours/event × ~8/year = **~24 hours/year** Pricing Analyst.

| **Volume** | ~50–100 rules |
| **Owner** | Pricing Analyst |
| **Participants** | Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing Analyst maintains the dynamic-pricing rules: competitor-price-follow rules (auto-adjust within a band), inventory-clearance rules (auto-markdown at threshold), time-based rules (happy-hour, slow-day); reviews rule performance and tunes | Pricing Analyst | Category Manager | 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing engine / rules (links to VS-29 / W289, VS-08 / W107 pricing hierarchy, W329 competitive tactical response) — the dynamic-pricing rules
- Competitor feed (links to VS-57 / W2214) — the competitor-price-follow input
- Inventory (links to VS-05 / W220 slow-moving) — the clearance-rule input

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rule-conflict risk**: two rules fire on the same SKU (competitor-follow lowers, inventory-clearance lowers further), producing an unintended deep discount; mitigated by the rule-precedence (one rule per SKU per period) and the conflict-check
- **Customer-trust risk**: too-frequent price changes (especially upward) erode customer trust ("the price is different every visit"); mitigated by the upward-change discipline (rare, justified) and the downward-change tolerance

### Automation Opportunity
- Rules auto-fired within bands; conflict auto-detected; performance auto-reported

### Controls
- CTL-400 (ensure controlled execution — Competitor Price Data Collection & Analysis (PA-57.1).)
- CTL (price-change authorization — links to VS-08); CTL (margin-floor guard); operational: Category Manager monthly rule-review sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month = **~60 hours/year** Pricing Analyst.

## W2220. Price Elasticity Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Semi-annual |
| **Frequency** | Semi-annual; ~50–100 SKUs |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 elasticity analyses |
| **Owner** | Analytics Manager |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analytics Manager computes the price-elasticity (the demand-response to a price change) for ~50–100 key SKUs from historical price-volume data; classifies elastic (price-sensitive) vs. inelastic (price-insensitive); informs the pricing strategy | Analytics Manager | VP Merchandising | 3–5 days/semi-annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113, W208 retail analytics) — the price-volume history for the elasticity model
- Pricing master (links to VS-29 / W289) — the historical prices
- Demand forecasting (links to VS-02 / W31) — the elasticity feeds the forecast

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Confounded-elasticity risk**: the elasticity model conflates the price effect with a concurrent promo or a competitor move; mitigated by the controlled-period selection (periods with only a price change, no confounders)
- **Stale-elasticity risk**: the elasticity from 2 years ago doesn't reflect a customer whose income/preferences shifted; mitigated by the semi-annual refresh

### Automation Opportunity
- Elasticity auto-computed from the price-volume history; confounded-periods auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-400 (ensure controlled execution — Competitor Price Data Collection & Analysis (PA-57.1).)
- Operational: Analytics Manager methodology sign-off; VP Merchandising review

### Time Estimate
~4 days/semi-annual = **~60–80 hours/year** Analytics Manager.
