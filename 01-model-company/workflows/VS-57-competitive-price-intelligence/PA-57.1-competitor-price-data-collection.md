## W2214. Competitor Price Scraping & Collection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly collection cycle |
| **Frequency** | Weekly; ~500–1,000 price points |
| **Volume** | ~500–1,000 prices/week across 4–5 competitors |
| **Owner** | Pricing Analyst |
| **Participants** | Pricing Analyst, Category Mgr, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analyst coordinates weekly collection: (a) automated web scraping for ecommerce prices, (b) mystery shopping for in-store (20 key stores/competitor), (c) marketplace monitoring (Lazada, Shopee); system normalizes and matches to BuildRight SKUs | Pricing Analyst / System | Category Manager | 1–2 days |
