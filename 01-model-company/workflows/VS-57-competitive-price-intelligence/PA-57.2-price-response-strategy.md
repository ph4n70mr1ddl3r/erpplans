## W2222. Rapid Price Response Workflow

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Competitor unexpected price move on key SKU |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; ~5–10 rapid responses/month |
| **Volume** | ~5–10/month |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Category Mgr, Pricing Analyst, VP Merch, IT |

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
