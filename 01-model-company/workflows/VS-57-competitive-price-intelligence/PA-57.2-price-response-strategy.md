## W2222. Rapid Price Response Workflow

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Competitor unexpected price move on key SKU |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; ~5–10 rapid responses/month |
| **Volume** | ~5–10/month |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R)) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager evaluates the competitor move; decides match/counter/hold; executes the price change within 24–48 hours; communicates to stores and ecommerce | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 2–4 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing master (links to VS-29 / W289, VS-08 / W40, W329 tactical response) — the rapid price change
- Competitor feed (links to VS-57 / W2214) — the triggering move
- Store/ecom comms (links to VS-63 / W571, VS-08 / W181 label) — the change distribution

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hasty-decision risk**: a 24-hour response is made without margin analysis, eroding profit; mitigated by the margin-floor guard in the rapid-response workflow
- **Change-fatigue risk**: too many rapid changes confuse stores and customers; mitigated by the rapid-response reserved for true key-SKU moves (not every competitor tweak)

### Automation Opportunity
- Competitor-move auto-alerted; margin-impact auto-computed; change auto-pushed on approval

### Controls
- CTL (price-change authorization); CTL (margin-floor guard); operational: VP Merchandising post-hoc review of rapid responses

### Time Estimate
~3 hours/response × ~7/month = **~20 hours/month** Category Manager.

### Controls
- CTL (promo-price authorization); CTL (DTI-permit compliance — links to W868); operational: Category Manager sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1.5 days/cycle × 18 = **~200 hours/year** Pricing Analyst.

## W2224. Markdown Optimization for Clearance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | End-of-season or slow-moving inventory |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; ~500–1,000 markdown decisions |
| **Volume** | ~500–1,000/month |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System recommends markdown depth per SKU based on sell-through, weeks-of-supply, and residual value; Category Manager approves; executes in pricing master | System / Category Manager | VP Merchandising | Automated + 2–3 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Markdown engine (links to VS-64 / W2390 sell-through trigger, VS-57 / W2224, VS-29 / W289) — the markdown recommendation
- Inventory (links to VS-05 / W220 slow-moving) — the sell-through and weeks-of-supply
- Pricing master (links to VS-29 / W289) — the markdown execution

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-markdown risk**: the markdown is deeper than needed to clear, eroding recoverable margin; mitigated by the sell-through-based recommendation (not a blanket percentage)
- **Cascading-markdown risk**: a markdown triggers a further markdown (death-spiral) as the SKU looks ever cheaper; mitigated by the markdown-count limit and the stop-loss floor

### Automation Opportunity
- Markdown auto-recommended from sell-through data; approved by Category Manager

### Controls
- CTL (price-change authorization); CTL (margin-floor / stop-loss); operational: Category Manager monthly sign-off

### Time Estimate
~3 hours/month Category Manager + system automated = **~36 hours/year**.

- CTL (price-override authorization — links to VS-08 / POS-006); CTL (margin-floor guard); operational: Regional Ops Manager approval; Store Ops Director audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly internal audit |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~200 audits/month |
| **Owner** | Pricing Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System compares POS transaction prices vs. the pricing master; flags mismatches; Pricing Analyst investigates root cause (label-not-applied, override-not-approved, sync-error); corrective action | System / Pricing Analyst | Category Manager | 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Price-compliance (links to VS-08 / W69 price-compliance audit, W2176 shelf-label audit) — the POS-vs-master comparison
- Pricing master (links to VS-29 / W289) — the authoritative price
- Analytics (links to VS-28 / W113) — the mismatch dataset

### Pain Points / Risks
- **DTI-exposure risk**: a systemic price-mismatch (shelf says one price, POS charges another) is a DTI consumer-protection violation (W2682); mitigated by the monthly audit catching the mismatch
- **Root-cause-neglect risk**: mismatches are corrected one-by-one without fixing the systemic cause; mitigated by the root-cause analysis and the system-fix

### Automation Opportunity
- POS-vs-master auto-compared; mismatch auto-flagged with root-cause-suggestion

### Controls
- CTL (price-compliance — links to VS-08 / W69); operational: Category Manager monthly sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month = **~60 hours/year** Pricing Analyst.

## W2229. Customer Price Match Guarantee

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer requests price match at POS |
| **Frequency** | ~3,000–5,000 requests/month |
| **Volume** | ~3K–5K/month |
| **Owner** | Cashier / Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer presents competitor price evidence (ad, screenshot); Cashier verifies per policy (same SKU, current, valid competitor); Manager approves if over threshold; POS applies the match; system logs the match for analytics | Cashier / Store Manager | Regional Ops Mgr | 3–5 min |

### System Touchpoints
- POS / price-match (links to VS-08 / W61 competitor price match, W5B) — the match application
- Competitor evidence verification (links to VS-57 / W2214) — the competitor-price validation
- Analytics (links to VS-28 / W113) — the match-frequency and margin-impact tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Match-fraud risk**: a customer fabricates competitor evidence (a photoshopped screenshot); mitigated by the verification policy (verifiable source, current date) and the manager-approval on high-value matches
- **Margin-erosion risk**: frequent matches on key SKUs erode margin; mitigated by the match-frequency analytics and the pricing-strategy adjustment if a SKU is chronically matched

### Automation Opportunity
- Competitor-price auto-verified from the intelligence feed (for digital evidence); match auto-logged

### Controls
- CTL (price-match authorization — links to VS-08 / POS-006); CTL (fraud guard); operational: Store Manager approval above threshold

### Time Estimate
~4 min/match × ~4,000/month = **~270 hours/month Cashier/Manager** (absorbed into checkout).
