## W2230. Gross Margin Analysis by Category

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly financial analysis |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; 13 categories |
| **Volume** | 13 category analyses |
| **Owner** | Finance Manager |
| **Participants** | CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Manager analyzes gross margin by category: revenue, COGS, markdowns, net margin %; identifies margin-eroding categories and recommends pricing/cost actions | Finance Manager | CFO | 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Cost accounting (links to VS-158 / W85, VS-17 / W26 budget) — the category margin dataset
- Pricing (links to VS-29 / W289, VS-57 / W2224 markdowns) — the margin drivers
- Analytics (links to VS-27 / W113) — the trend visualization

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-vs-volume-blindness risk**: a category shows high margin but low volume (small contribution); mitigated by the margin-and-contribution joint view
- **Markdown-distortion risk**: the margin reflects post-markdown actual, hiding the pre-markdown potential; mitigated by the pre-and-post-markdown margin view

### Automation Opportunity
- Category-margin auto-computed monthly; margin-eroder auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-402 (ensure controlled execution — Pricing Analytics & Margin Optimization (PA-57.3).)
- CTL-402 (cost-of-goods accuracy — links to VS-158); operational: CFO monthly sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month = **~60 hours/year** Finance Manager.

## W2232. Vendor Cost-Driven Price Adjustment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor cost change |
| **Frequency** | ~5–10/month |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 reviews/month |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vendor notifies cost change; Category Manager evaluates: pass-through (raise price), absorb (hold price, accept margin compression), or negotiate (challenge the cost increase); executes the decision | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1–2 hours/review |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor cost (links to VS-03 / W36, VS-158 / W85 landed cost) — the cost-change input
- Pricing master (links to VS-29 / W289) — the price-adjustment execution
- Margin guard (links to VS-57 / W2230) — the absorb-vs-pass-through margin impact

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pass-through-competitiveness risk**: passing through a cost increase prices BuildRight above competitors, losing volume; mitigated by the competitive-price check before the pass-through
- **Absorb-erosion risk**: absorbing repeated cost increases silently erodes category margin; mitigated by the margin-trend monitoring (W2230)

### Automation Opportunity
- Cost-change auto-flagged; margin-impact and competitive-position auto-computed

### Controls
- CTL-402 (ensure controlled execution — Pricing Analytics & Margin Optimization (PA-57.3).)
- CTL-402 (price-change authorization); CTL (vendor-cost accuracy — links to VS-158); operational: VP Merchandising sign-off on absorb decisions

### Time Estimate
~1.5 hours/review × ~7/month = **~10 hours/month** Category Manager.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-promotional analysis |
| **Frequency** | 18 analyses/year |
| **Volume** | 18/year |
| **Owner** | FP&A Analyst |
| **Participants** | CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A computes the promo ROI: incremental sales vs. baseline, promo cost (discount + marketing), margin impact, halo effect; recommends next-promo adjustments | FP&A Analyst | CFO | 4–6 hours/promo |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A (links to VS-17 / W26, VS-158 / W85) — the promo cost and incremental-sales
- Analytics (links to VS-27 / W113, VS-58 / W2254 campaign ROI) — the baseline and halo
- Promo (links to VS-14 / W13, VS-58) — the promo under analysis

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Baseline-confound risk**: the baseline is inflated by concurrent factors (a competitor closed, a typhoon drove demand), overstating the promo lift; mitigated by the controlled-baseline (comparable non-promo period)
- **Halo-overattribution risk**: the "halo" (promo drove sales of other items) is attributed without clean evidence; mitigated by the basket-analysis where the halo is measurable

### Automation Opportunity
- ROI auto-computed from the sales/cost datasets; baseline auto-selected

### Controls
- CTL-402 (ensure controlled execution — Pricing Analytics & Margin Optimization (PA-57.3).)
- CTL-402 (ROI-accuracy — links to VS-17); operational: CFO sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/promo × 18 = **~90 hours/year** FP&A Analyst.

- CTL-51: pricing-master data quality; operational: IT System Admin monthly sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month = **~60 hours/year** IT System Admin + Pricing Analyst.

| 1 | System compares prices across POS, ecommerce, and marketplace channels; flags inconsistencies; Ecom Ops Manager investigates root cause (sync error, channel-specific override, marketplace feed lag); remediates | System / Ecom Ops Manager | VP Marketing | 2–3 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints
- Cross-channel sync (links to VS-08 / POS-013, VS-10 ecommerce, VS-65 marketplace) — the price-sync across channels
- Pricing master (links to VS-29 / W289) — the authoritative single-source price
- Marketplace feed (links to VS-65 / W180 marketplace integration) — the marketplace price feed

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Customer-frustration risk**: a customer sees one price online and a different price in-store, eroding trust; mitigated by the consistency check and the rapid remediation

### Automation Opportunity
- Cross-channel comparison auto-run; inconsistency auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-402 (ensure controlled execution — Pricing Analytics & Margin Optimization (PA-57.3).)
- CTL-402 (price-consistency — links to VS-08); operational: Ecom Ops Manager weekly sign-off

~2.5 hours/week = **~130 hours/year** Ecom Ops Manager.
