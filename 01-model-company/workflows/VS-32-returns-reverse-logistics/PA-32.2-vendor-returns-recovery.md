## W1631. Vendor Return-to-Vendor (RTV) Shipment Planning & Consolidation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | RTV authorization created (from W91, W1280, or W1626 disposition) |
| **Frequency** | ~200–300 RTV shipments/month |
| **Volume** | Consolidated into ~50–80 vendor shipments/month |
| **Owner** | DC Returns Coordinator |
| **Participants** | DC Returns Coordinator, Receiving Clerk, Buyer, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System compiles RTV-eligible inventory: items with "Return to Vendor" disposition from W91 (damaged/defective), W1280 (excess merchandise), or W1626 (customer return assessment); grouped by vendor | System | DC Returns Coordinator | Automated |
| 2 | DC Returns Coordinator reviews RTV queue weekly: (a) prioritizes by: (i) vendor credit value (high-value RTVs processed first), (ii) vendor RTV policy deadline (some vendors require RTV within 30 days of delivery), (iii) perishable/shelf-life urgency (cement, chemicals), (b) checks vendor RTV requirements: (i) RMA required from vendor (contact Buyer to obtain), (ii) original packaging required, (iii) specific carrier or ship-to address, (c) consolidates multiple RTV items for same vendor into single shipment | DC Returns Coordinator | DC Supervisor | 2 hours/week |
| 3 | Buyer obtains RMA from vendor: (a) contacts vendor with RTV details (items, quantities, defect description, original PO reference), (b) vendor approves RMA with return authorization number, (c) vendor specifies return shipping terms (vendor pays, BuildRight pays, split) | Buyer | Category Manager | 30 min/vendor |
| 4 | DC Returns Coordinator prepares RTV shipment: (a) picks items from quarantine/damaged goods area, (b) packs per vendor requirements, (c) prints shipping label and packing list with RMA number, (d) stages for carrier pickup or vendor pickup | DC Returns Coordinator | DC Supervisor | 30 min/shipment |
| 5 | Shipment processed in system: (a) inventory deducted from "RTV Pending" status, (b) in-transit RTV inventory tracked, (c) AP Clerk notified of pending vendor credit | System | — | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- RTV-eligible inventory queue grouped by vendor (W1631.1)
- Vendor RTV policy database (W1631.2)
- RMA number tracking per vendor (W1631.3)
- RTV shipment processing with in-transit tracking (W1631.5)


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-compile (system compiles rtv-eligible inventory: items)
- auto-review (returns coordinator reviews rtv queue weekly:)
- auto-approve within tolerance ((b) vendor approves rma with return)

### Controls
operational: DC Returns Coordinator review and approval gate

## W1632. Vendor RTV Credit Recovery & Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | RTV shipment received by vendor |
| **Frequency** | ~50–80 vendor credit recoveries/month |
| **Volume** | Monthly RTV credit value: ~PHP 3–5M |
| **Owner** | AP Clerk |
| **Participants** | AP Clerk, Buyer, Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vendor confirms receipt of RTV shipment; issues credit note or replacement shipment; AP Clerk tracks confirmation status | AP Clerk | Controller | 15 min |
| 2 | AP Clerk matches vendor credit note to RTV record: (a) verifies credit amount matches expected value (purchase price or agreed settlement), (b) verifies credit note references correct PO and RMA numbers | AP Clerk | Controller | 10 min/credit |
| 3 | Credit applied: (a) system applies vendor credit against open AP (reduces amount due to vendor), (b) or vendor issues replacement shipment — processed as GR at no cost | AP Clerk / System | Controller | 5 min |
| 4 | Monthly: Controller reviews RTV recovery report: (a) total RTV shipments vs. total credits received, (b) recovery rate (credits received ÷ RTV value shipped — target: > 90%), (c) aging of unrecovered RTVs (> 60 days escalated to Buyer), (d) vendor RTV performance score (feeds into vendor scorecard per W44) | Controller | CFO | 1 hour/month |

### System Touchpoints
- RTV credit tracking with vendor confirmation status (W1632.1)
- AP credit matching against RTV record (W1632.2)
- Credit application against vendor AP (W1632.3)
- RTV recovery rate dashboard (W1632.4)


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-track (ap clerk tracks confirmation status)
- auto-record (to rtv record: (a) verifies credit)
- auto-issue (or vendor issues replacement shipment —)

### Controls
operational: AP Clerk review and approval gate

## W1633. Vendor Defective Product Investigation & Root Cause Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Defect rate exceeds threshold for a vendor/product (3+ defects of same type in 30 days) |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 investigations/month |
| **Volume** | Focus on high-impact defects (safety, high-value, high-volume) |
| **Owner** | Quality Manager |
| **Participants** | Quality Manager, Buyer, DC Quality Inspector, vendor quality team |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System alerts Quality Manager when defect pattern detected: (a) same vendor, same SKU, same defect type ≥ 3 occurrences in 30 days, (b) same vendor, different SKUs, similar defect type ≥ 5 occurrences in 30 days, (c) any safety-related defect pattern | System | Quality Manager | Automated |
| 2 | Quality Manager initiates investigation: (a) collects defect samples and photos from DC/stores, (b) reviews GR inspection records for affected lots, (c) reviews customer complaint records for affected products, (d) categorizes root cause hypothesis: (i) manufacturing defect, (ii) packaging inadequacy, (iii) shipping/handling damage, (iv) specification mismatch | Quality Manager | VP Supply Chain | 4 hours |
| 3 | Quality Manager and Buyer engage vendor: (a) share defect evidence and data, (b) request vendor root cause analysis, (c) agree on corrective action and timeline per W1610 (VCAR), (d) agree on immediate containment (hold shipments, substitute product, enhanced inspection) | Quality Manager / Buyer | VP Supply Chain | 2 hours |
| 4 | If vendor does not accept responsibility: (a) Quality Manager engages third-party testing laboratory for independent analysis (cost: PHP 10–30K per test), (b) test results shared with vendor for resolution, (c) if dispute continues: escalate to Category Manager and VP Merchandising for commercial resolution | Quality Manager | VP Supply Chain | As needed |
| 5 | Investigation closed when: (a) root cause identified and corrective action implemented, (b) vendor VCAR closed per W1610, (c) enhanced inspection shows defect rate returned to normal | Quality Manager | VP Supply Chain | 30 min |


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-detect (defect pattern detected: (a) same vendor,)
- auto-record (gr inspection records for affected lots,)
- auto-inspect (product, enhanced inspection))

### Controls
operational: Quality Manager review and approval gate

## W1634. Vendor Product Recall Return & Recovery Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Product recall decision from W1615 |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event (~1–2/year) |
| **Volume** | Variable — can involve thousands of units across 200 stores and 4 DCs |
| **Owner** | DC Returns Coordinator |
| **Participants** | DC Returns Coordinator, Store Managers, Compliance Officer, Buyer, Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliance Officer provides recall scope to DC Returns Coordinator: affected SKUs, lot/batch numbers, quantity to recover from stores and DCs | Compliance Officer | VP Supply Chain | Per W1616 |
| 2 | DC Returns Coordinator consolidates recalled product from DCs and stores: (a) DC: recalled product pulled from warehouse per W1616.3, (b) stores: recalled product consolidated and shipped to designated DC (store-to-DC reverse logistics), (c) customer returns of recalled product routed to designated DC | DC Returns Coordinator | VP Supply Chain | Variable |
| 3 | Recalled product disposition at DC: (a) **Return to vendor**: if vendor accepts return and provides credit — prepare consolidated RTV shipment per W1631, (b) **Destroy**: if product is unsafe for any use — arrange DENR-compliant destruction for chemicals or general destruction for non-hazardous items; document with destruction certificate, (c) **Hold for investigation**: retain samples for Quality Manager and Legal investigation | DC Returns Coordinator / Quality Manager | VP Supply Chain | Variable |
| 4 | Financial recovery: (a) Controller calculates total recall cost (product + handling + disposal + customer refunds), (b) Buyer negotiates vendor recovery: full credit, partial credit, or replacement, (c) insurance claim if applicable per W837, (d) unrecovered costs recorded as quality cost per W1614 | Controller / Buyer | CFO / VP Merchandising | Per recall event |


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-log ((store-to-dc reverse logistics), (c) customer returns)
- auto-document (non-hazardous items; document with destruction certificate,)
- auto-record (unrecovered costs recorded as quality cost)

### Controls
operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

## W1635. Vendor Seasonal/Promotional Return Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Season ends or promotion concludes; unsold seasonal/promotional inventory eligible for vendor return |
| **Frequency** | ~6–8 seasonal return events/year (aligned with Philippine seasonal calendar) |
| **Volume** | ~500–1,000 units per seasonal event |
| **Owner** | Buyer |
| **Participants** | Buyer, Category Manager, DC Returns Coordinator, AP Clerk |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buyer reviews seasonal/promotional vendor agreements for return eligibility: (a) some vendor contracts include seasonal return provisions (unsold seasonal items returnable within 30 days of season end), (b) identifies eligible items, quantities, and return terms | Buyer | Category Manager | 2 hours/season |
| 2 | DC Returns Coordinator consolidates unsold seasonal inventory from stores and DCs; identifies items meeting return criteria (unused, original packaging, within return window) | DC Returns Coordinator | DC Supervisor | 4 hours/season |
| 3 | Buyer obtains RMA from vendor; DC Returns Coordinator processes RTV shipment per W1631 | Buyer / DC Returns Coordinator | Category Manager | Per W1631 |
| 4 | AP Clerk processes vendor credit per W1632 | AP Clerk | Controller | Per W1632 |
| 5 | Items not eligible for vendor return: dispositioned per W93 (markdown/clearance) or W91 (donate/scrap) | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 hour/season |


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-review (buyer reviews seasonal/promotional vendor agreements)

### Controls
operational: Buyer review and approval gate

## W1636. Vendor Consignment Unsold Inventory Return & Settlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Consignment agreement review (typically quarterly) or vendor request |
| **Frequency** | ~12 consignment vendor reviews/quarter |
| **Volume** | ~300 SKUs on consignment from 12 key vendors |
| **Owner** | Buyer |
| **Participants** | Buyer, Category Manager, DC Returns Coordinator, AP Clerk |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quarterly: Buyer reviews consignment inventory performance: (a) sell-through rate per SKU per vendor, (b) aging of consignment inventory (> 180 days unsold), (c) margin contribution of consignment vs. owned inventory | Buyer | Category Manager | 2 hours/vendor |
| 2 | For unsold consignment inventory: (a) Buyer negotiates with vendor: (i) return unsold inventory to vendor, (ii) convert to owned inventory at discounted price, (iii) markdown and sell at clearance, (b) vendor selects option; agreement documented | Buyer | Category Manager | 1 hour/vendor |
| 3 | If vendor requests return: DC Returns Coordinator processes RTV per W1631; consignment inventory physically returned to vendor | DC Returns Coordinator | DC Supervisor | Per W1631 |
| 4 | Settlement: (a) AP Clerk calculates consignment settlement: (i) sold inventory — vendor payment at agreed consignment price, (ii) unsold returned inventory — no payment, (iii) markdown losses — per vendor agreement (vendor absorbs loss or shared), (b) system generates settlement report; payment processed per consignment terms | AP Clerk | Controller | 2 hours/quarter/vendor |


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-review (quarterly: buyer reviews consignment inventory performance:)
- auto-document (option; agreement documented)
- auto-request (if vendor requests return: dc returns)

### Controls
operational: Buyer review and approval gate

## W1637. Vendor Shipping Error & Wrong Item Return Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Goods receipt reveals wrong item, wrong quantity, or wrong specification received |
| **Frequency** | ~100–150 shipping errors/month (~2% of GRs) |
| **Volume** | ~25–35 per DC/month |
| **Owner** | Receiving Clerk |
| **Participants** | Receiving Clerk, DC Supervisor, Buyer, AP Clerk |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | At GR: Receiving Clerk identifies shipping error: (a) wrong item received (SKU doesn't match PO line), (b) wrong quantity (over-shipment or under-shipment), (c) wrong specification (e.g., wrong size, color, grade), (d) damaged in transit (carrier liability — separate from vendor error) | Receiving Clerk | DC Supervisor | 5 min/occurrence |
| 2 | Receiving Clerk records discrepancy in system: (a) creates GR discrepancy record with photos, (b) system blocks discrepancy quantity from available stock, (c) notifies Buyer of shipping error | Receiving Clerk | DC Supervisor | 5 min |
| 3 | Buyer contacts vendor to resolve: (a) **Wrong item**: vendor arranges pickup of wrong items and expedited shipment of correct items; no AP payment for wrong items, (b) **Over-shipment**: Buyer decides — accept excess (negotiate discount) or return excess per W1631, (c) **Under-shipment**: vendor ships remaining quantity on expedited basis; PO remains open, (d) **Wrong specification**: vendor arranges return and replacement; if spec difference is minor and acceptable to BuildRight, Buyer may negotiate price reduction instead | Buyer | Category Manager | 15 min |
| 4 | Resolution executed: (a) returns processed per W1631, (b) AP adjusted per W1632, (c) correct items received and processed per standard GR, (d) vendor shipping error rate tracked in vendor scorecard per W44 | Buyer / AP Clerk | Category Manager / Controller | Variable |


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-match ((sku doesn't match po line), (b))
- auto-record (receiving clerk records discrepancy in system:)
- auto-count (excess (negotiate discount) or return excess)

### Controls
operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules
