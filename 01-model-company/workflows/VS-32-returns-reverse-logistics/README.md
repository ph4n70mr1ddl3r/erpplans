# VS-32: Returns & Reverse Logistics

> **Make & Move** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages the complete reverse supply chain — from customer return initiation through disposition (restock, refurbish, return-to-vendor, scrap). Covers in-store returns, ecommerce returns, defective product handling, warranty claim processing, and the physical logistics of moving goods back through the supply chain. Distinct from VS-05 (which handles inventory disposition decisions) and VS-09 (which handles customer-facing service), this value stream focuses on the end-to-end operational flow of returned goods.
