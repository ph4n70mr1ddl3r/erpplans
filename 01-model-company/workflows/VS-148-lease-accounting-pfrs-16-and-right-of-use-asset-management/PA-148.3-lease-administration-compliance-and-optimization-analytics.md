## W4425. Lease Accounting Period-End Close, Reconciliation & Disclosures

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Month/quarter/year-end close |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly + annual |
| **Volume** | 5-entity lease portfolio each close |
| **Owner** | Lease Accounting / Controller / Consolidation (VS-17) |
| **Participants** | Controller, Consolidation (VS-17), Finance (VS-17.4), AP (VS-15), Treasury (VS-18), Audit (VS-21), External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Accounting close the period — run final engine (W4417), reconcile lease-accounting system to GL ROU/liability, to AP/cash (VS-15/VS-18), and to FA register (VS-35) | Lease Accounting / Controller | Controller | Per close |
| 2 | Lease Accounting/Consolidation run intercompany eliminations (W4424), validate consolidation, and prepare the **PFRS 16 disclosures** (maturity analysis, total cash outflow, ROU additions, depreciation/interest expense, variable/short-term/low-value expense, extensions/terminations/modifications) | Lease Accounting / Consolidation / Finance | Controller / CFO | Per close |
| 3 | Controller/Audit review & sign off, support external-audit (VS-21), and file the audited FS with SEC (links VS-22/VS-79) | Controller / Audit | CFO | Per close |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease-accounting engine (W4427); GL/consolidation (VS-17); AP/cash (VS-15/VS-18); FA (VS-35); BI (VS-28); disclosure templates; audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reconciliation-break risk**: lease-system vs GL mismatch → close delay/finding; mitigated by monthly reconciliation
- **Disclosure-incompleteness risk**: missing PFRS 16 disclosure → auditor/SEC finding; mitigated by disclosure template & checklist

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-reconciliation; disclosure-pack auto-generation; maturity-analysis engine; audit-support pack

### Controls
- CTL-111 (ensure PFRS 16 lease-accounting completeness.)
- CTL-676: system reconciled to GL/AP/cash/FA monthly; eliminations validated; PFRS 16 disclosures complete; reviewed & auditor-supported

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 days monthly; ~1–2 weeks annual

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (R2R/consolidation), VS-15 (AP), VS-18 (treasury), VS-35 (FA), VS-21 (audit), W4417 (engine), W4424 (elimination), W4428 (controls)

## W4429. Tax (VAT/WHT/Documentary Stamp) on Lease Payments

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Lease payment; tax review; BIR change |
| **Frequency** | Per payment + periodic review |
| **Volume** | Lease payments across ~205+ leases × multiple periods |
| **Owner** | Tax (VS-79) / Lease Accounting / Lease Admin (VS-42) |
| **Participants** | Tax (VS-79), Lease Accounting, Lease Admin (VS-42), AP (VS-15), Treasury (VS-18), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax/Lease Accounting determine the tax treatment per lease — VAT (12%) on residential/commercial rent as applicable, Expanded Withholding Tax (EWT) on rent, Documentary Stamp Tax (DST) on the lease contract, percentage tax considerations | Tax / Lease Accounting | Tax Lead / Controller | Per lease + on change |
| 2 | Lease Admin/AP withhold EWT on rent payments, file DST on lease contracts, and remit VAT/EWT via BIR (VS-79); issue/withhold BIR forms (2307) | Lease Admin / AP / Tax | Tax Lead | Per payment/contract |
| 3 | Tax/Lease Accounting reconcile tax to GL/AP (VS-15/VS-17), support BIR audit (VS-79/VS-21), and update on tax-law change | Tax / Lease Accounting / Audit | Tax Lead / Controller | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Tax/BIR eFPS (VS-79); lease-accounting (W4427); AP (VS-15); GL (VS-17); BIR forms; audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks

- **DST-miss risk**: DST not filed on lease contract → penalty; mitigated by contract-trigger DST filing

### Controls
- CTL-676 (ensure controlled execution — Lease Administration, Compliance & Optimization Analytics (PA-148.3).)

### Time Estimate

## W4430. Lessor/Property-Owner Counterparty Settlement (links VS-97/VS-72)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Intercompany lease; monthly settlement; third-party landlord payment |
| **Frequency** | Monthly settlement |
| **Volume** | Intercompany (Property Mgmt → Depot etc.) + third-party landlord settlements |
| **Owner** | Intercompany (VS-72) / Lease Accounting / Lease Admin (VS-42) |
| **Participants** | Intercompany (VS-72), Lease Admin (VS-42), Treasury (VS-18), AP/AR (VS-15/VS-16), Property Mgmt (VS-97), Consolidation (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Admin/Intercompany process rent/lease payments to third-party landlords (AP VS-15) and intercompany lease charges between the 5 entities (Property Mgmt as lessor, Depot as lessee) per arm's-length transfer pricing | Lease Admin / Intercompany | Controller / Treasurer | Monthly |
| 2 | Intercompany/Consolidation settle intercompany lease balances at month-end, match to the elimination (W4424), and reconcile to intercompany AR/AP (VS-72) | Intercompany / Consolidation | Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Lease Accounting/Property Mgmt reconcile lessee liability (W4414) to lessor receivable/asset (VS-97), resolve differences, and report consolidated (VS-17) | Lease Accounting / Property Mgr / Consolidation | Controller / CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Intercompany (VS-72); AP/AR (VS-15/VS-16); lease-accounting (W4427); consolidation (VS-17); treasury (VS-18); lessor/RE (VS-97)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Intercompany-mismatch risk**: lessee & lessor sides differ → settlement/elimination failure; mitigated by monthly match & reconciliation
- **Transfer-pricing risk**: non-arm's-length intercompany rent → tax exposure; mitigated by transfer-pricing policy

### Automation Opportunity
- Intercompany auto-billing & matching; settlement engine; lessee-vs-lessor reconciliation; transfer-pricing validation

### Controls
- CTL-111 (ensure PFRS 16 lease-accounting completeness.)
- CTL-676: rent/intercompany processed per transfer pricing; intercompany matched & settled monthly; lessee-vs-lessor reconciled; consolidated

### Time Estimate

## W4432. Lease vs Buy, Renewal & Optimization Decision Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Lease expiry; new asset need; capex decision; optimization review |
| **Frequency** | Per decision (~10–15 store + multiple equipment decisions/yr) |
| **Volume** | Renewal/new-lease/buy decisions across portfolio |
| **Owner** | Finance (VS-17.4) / Real Estate (VS-97) / Lease Accounting |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17.4), Real Estate (VS-97/VS-20), Treasury (VS-18), Capex (VS-40), Strategy (VS-33), Lease Accounting, Tax (VS-79) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance/Real Estate build the **lease-vs-buy** analysis — compare PFRS 16 ROU/liability + total-lease-cost vs purchase capex + depreciation + financing + residual, with tax/cash-flow modeling | Finance / Real Estate / Treasury | CFO / COO | Per decision |
| 2 | Finance/Real Estate assess renewal vs relocate vs downsize at expiry — market rent, renewal terms, strategic fit, total occupancy (W4431), and negotiate (VS-42) | Finance / Real Estate / Lease Admin | COO / CFO | Per expiry |
| 3 | Finance/Strategy execute optimization — consolidate sites, renegotiate outliers, right-size space, convert to own (VS-97) where economic — and measure benefit realization (VS-112) | Finance / Strategy / Real Estate | CFO / Strategy Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease-accounting/analytics (W4427/W4431); RE (VS-97); treasury (VS-18); capex (VS-40); strategy (VS-33); finance FP&A (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Sub-optimal-renewal risk**: auto-renew above market → value leakage; mitigated by market-rent benchmark & analysis
- **Wrong-lease-vs-buy risk**: leasing what should be owned (or vice versa) → higher TCO; mitigated by structured lease-vs-buy model

### Automation Opportunity
- Lease-vs-buy model with PFRS 16 impact; market-rent benchmark; renewal-decision dashboard; benefit-realization tracking

### Controls
- CTL-111 (ensure PFRS 16 lease-accounting completeness.)
- CTL-676: structured lease-vs-buy/renewal analysis; market-rent benchmarked; optimization measured; approved by Finance/Strategy

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 days per major decision

### Cross-references
- links to VS-97 (RE), VS-40 (capex), VS-18 (treasury), VS-33 (strategy), VS-42 (lease admin/negotiation), VS-112 (benefit), W4431 (analytics)
