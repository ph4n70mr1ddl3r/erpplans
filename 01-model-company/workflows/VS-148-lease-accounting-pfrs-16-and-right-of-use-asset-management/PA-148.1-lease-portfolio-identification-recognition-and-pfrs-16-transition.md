## W4409. Lease Accounting Strategy, Policy & PFRS 16/IFRS 16 Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | PFRS 16 adoption; policy review; audit finding |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on standard/PFRS change |
| **Volume** | One enterprise lease-accounting policy/framework |
| **Owner** | Controller / Lease Accounting (VS-17) / Finance (VS-17.4) |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17.4), Tax (VS-79), Treasury (VS-18), Audit (VS-21), Real Estate (VS-97/VS-20), IT/EA (VS-27/VS-113) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Controller/Lease Accounting set the PFRS 16 accounting policy — scope, recognition/exemption elections (short-term, low-value), discount-rate methodology (incremental borrowing rate vs implicit), lease-term (incl. renewal options reasonably certain), and component separation (lease vs non-lease vs service) | Controller / Lease Accounting | CFO / Audit Committee | Annual + on change |
| 2 | Controller/Audit document judgments & estimates (discount rate, term, variable payments, restoration), align with external auditor, and embed in the accounting manual | Controller / Audit / Lease Accounting | CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Lease Accounting/EA/IT translate policy into the lease-accounting system config (W4427), controls (W4428), and disclosure templates (W4425) | Lease Accounting / EA / IT | Controller / CIO | Per change |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease-accounting system (W4427); GL (VS-17); master data/contract (VS-29); tax (VS-79); audit (VS-21); EA (VS-113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Policy-inconsistency risk**: inconsistent elections/judgments across entities → audit finding; mitigated by single group policy & auditor alignment
- **Judgment-abuse risk**: aggressive discount-rate/term estimates distort liabilities; mitigated by documented methodology & auditor challenge

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual policy + on PFRS change

### Automation Opportunity
- Policy/config library; judgment-documentation templates; auditor-alignment workflow; disclosure templates

### Controls
- CTL: group PFRS 16 policy; elections documented & auditor-aligned; judgments supported; reviewed annually (W4428)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (R2R), VS-79 (tax), VS-18 (treasury), VS-21 (audit), W4427 (system), W4428 (controls), W4425 (disclosures)

## W4410. Lease Portfolio Discovery, Identification & Lease-Component Extraction

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Initial adoption; new contract; periodic refresh |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (per contract) + annual portfolio sweep |
| **Volume** | ~205 real-estate + equipment/fleet/IT leases across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Lease Accounting / Real Estate (VS-97/VS-20) / Procurement (VS-03/VS-34) |
| **Participants** | Real Estate (VS-97/VS-20), Procurement (VS-03/VS-34), Treasury (VS-18), IT (VS-99), Fleet (VS-06/VS-61), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Accounting coordinate a portfolio sweep — real-estate (VS-97/VS-20), equipment/forklifts (VS-138), fleet (VS-06/VS-61), IT/POS hardware (VS-99), fixtures — to identify every contract containing a lease (or embedded lease) | Lease Accounting / RE / Procurement / IT / Fleet | Controller | Annual + continuous |
| 2 | Lease Accounting/Legal determine whether each arrangement *contains a lease* (identified asset + right-to-direct-use + obtain-substantially-all-economic-benefits test), separating lease from service/non-lease components | Lease Accounting / Legal | Controller / VP Legal | Per contract |
| 3 | Lease Accounting register identified leases in the lease-accounting repository (W4426), assign unique IDs, owner, entity, and link to the contract record (VS-29) | Lease Accounting | Controller | Per lease |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease repository (W4426); contract/master (VS-29); RE (VS-97/VS-20); procurement (VS-03/VS-34); ITAM (VS-99); fleet (VS-06/VS-61); legal (VS-100)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missed-lease risk**: embedded leases in service contracts (e.g. leased copier, leased forklift-with-operator) not identified → under-stated liability; mitigated by portfolio sweep & lease-identification test
- **Component-mis-separation risk**: lease vs service mis-split → wrong recognition; mitigated by documented component-separation policy (W4409)

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days annual sweep; ~1–2 hrs per contract identification

### Automation Opportunity
- Contract repository scanning for lease indicators; lease-identification decision-tree; auto-registration to repository

### Controls
- CTL: portfolio sweep covers all lease types; lease-identification test applied; embedded leases extracted; registered in repository (W4426)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-97/VS-20 (RE), VS-03/VS-34 (procurement), VS-99 (ITAM), VS-06/VS-61 (fleet), VS-29 (contract), W4409 (policy), W4426 (repository)

## W4411. Lease Contract Data Capture & Abstracting (Terms, Payments, Options)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New/identified lease (W4410); amendment |
| **Frequency** | Per lease + on amendment |
| **Volume** | ~205+ leases abstracted; amendments ongoing |
| **Owner** | Lease Accounting / Lease Admin (VS-42) |
| **Participants** | Lease Admin (VS-42), Legal (VS-100), Real Estate (VS-97/VS-20), Treasury (VS-18), Tax (VS-79) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Accounting/Lease Admin abstract each contract — commencement/term/end, renewal/termination options, fixed payments, escalation/index, variable (turnover/usage), incentives/rent-free, initial direct costs, restoration/MAKE-GOOD, PWD/senior discounts, options-to-purchase | Lease Accounting / Lease Admin | Controller | Per lease |
| 2 | Legal/Lease Admin interpret ambiguous clauses (renewal certainty, termination penalty, CPI linkage, sublet consent), and confirm tax treatment (VAT/WHT, documentary stamp) with Tax | Legal / Lease Accounting / Tax | Controller / VP Legal | Per lease |
| 3 | Lease Accounting enter the abstract into the lease-accounting repository (W4426) with source-document link, evidence the data-quality check, and route for review | Lease Accounting | Controller | Per lease |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease repository (W4426); contract/legal (VS-100/VS-42); master data (VS-29); tax (VS-79); treasury (VS-18)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Abstracting-error risk**: wrong term/payment/escalation → wrong liability; mitigated by structured abstract template + dual review
- **Hidden-clause risk**: missed renewal/termination/MAKE-GOOD → wrong term/liability; mitigated by legal interpretation & full-clause capture

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 hrs per lease abstract (complexity-dependent)

### Automation Opportunity
- Abstract template with mandatory fields; contract-AI extraction; data-quality validation; legal-review workflow

### Controls
- CTL: structured abstract with all PFRS 16 data points; legal interpretation of ambiguous clauses; dual review; source-document linked

## W4412. Discount Rate, Lease Term & Payment Determination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New lease (W4411); remeasurement trigger (W4418) |
| **Frequency** | Per lease + on remeasurement |
| **Volume** | All recognized leases |
| **Owner** | Lease Accounting / Treasury (VS-18) |
| **Participants** | Treasury (VS-18), Finance (VS-17.4), Audit (VS-21), Real Estate (VS-97/VS-20) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/Lease Accounting determine the discount rate — lessee's incremental borrowing rate (IBR) by currency/term/entity (or implicit rate if readily determinable), using a documented methodology & market data | Treasury / Lease Accounting | Treasurer / Controller | Per lease + periodic refresh |
| 2 | Lease Accounting determine the lease term — non-cancellable period + periods covered by options reasonably certain to be exercised (renewal) / not to be exercised (termination), with documented assessment | Lease Accounting | Controller | Per lease + on change |
| 3 | Lease Accounting determine lease payments — fixed + escalation + variable-based-on-index/rate + exercise-purchase-option-if-reasonably-certain + penalties-for-termination + MAKE-GOOD, excluding variable-based-on-performance/usage | Lease Accounting / Treasury | Controller | Per lease |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease-accounting system (W4427); treasury (VS-18); audit (VS-21); market-rate data feed; BI (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Discount-rate-error risk**: wrong IBR → materially wrong liability (large PV effect over long retail leases); mitigated by documented IBR methodology & periodic refresh
- **Term-assessment-error risk**: wrong renewal-certainty → wrong term/liability; mitigated by documented assessment & evidence

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 hrs per lease determination; IBR refresh periodic

### Automation Opportunity
- IBR curve per entity/currency/term with market-data feed; renewal-certainty assessment template; payment-build engine

### Controls
- CTL: IBR methodology documented & refreshed; lease-term assessed with evidence; payment-build per PFRS 16; reviewed by Treasury/Audit

### Cross-references

## W4413. Lease Classification (Recognition Exemption vs On-Balance-Sheet)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New lease (W4411); policy election (W4409) |
| **Frequency** | Per lease |
| **Volume** | All leases classified |
| **Owner** | Lease Accounting / Controller |
| **Participants** | Controller, Audit (VS-21), Lease Admin (VS-42), Tax (VS-79) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Accounting apply recognition exemptions per policy (W4409) — short-term (≤12 months, no purchase option) and low-value (e.g. <USD 5,000) — electing by class of underlying asset | Lease Accounting | Controller | Per lease |
| 2 | Lease Accounting classify low-value/short-term as expense-on-straight-line (no ROU/liability) vs all other leases as on-balance-sheet; document the election and basis | Lease Accounting / Audit | Controller | Per lease |
| 3 | Lease Accounting flag subleases (W4421), sale-and-leaseback (W4421), and intercompany (W4424) for special classification | Lease Accounting | Controller | Per lease |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease-accounting system (W4427); policy (W4409); audit (VS-21); master data (VS-29)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-classification risk**: lease wrongly exempted → under-stated liability (audit finding); mitigated by exemption-class policy & documentation
- **Inconsistent-election risk**: per-asset vs per-class inconsistency; mitigated by class-of-asset election policy

### Time Estimate
- ~30 min per lease classification

### Automation Opportunity
- Classification rules-engine vs policy; exemption-evidence capture; sublease/SLB/intercompany auto-flag

### Controls
- CTL: exemptions applied per class-of-asset policy; election & basis documented; sublease/SLB/intercompany flagged; reviewed (W4428)

## W4414. Initial Recognition — ROU Asset & Lease Liability Calculation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Lease commencement (W4411/W4412/W4413) |
| **Frequency** | Per lease commencement |
| **Volume** | All on-balance-sheet leases (~205+ real-estate + equipment) |
| **Owner** | Lease Accounting / Controller |
| **Participants** | Controller, Finance (VS-17.4), Audit (VS-21), Treasury (VS-18), IT/EA (VS-27/VS-113) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Accounting calculate the **lease liability** at present value of unpaid lease payments (discounted per W4412) over the lease term (W4412) | Lease Accounting | Controller | Per lease |
| 2 | Lease Accounting calculate the **ROU asset** = liability + initial direct costs + estimated restoration/MAKE-GOOD − lease incentives received + prepayments, and post the journal (DR ROU asset / CR liability) | Lease Accounting / Controller | Controller | Per lease |
| 3 | Lease Accounting post the commencement journal to GL (VS-17), assign asset/sub-asset to the fixed-asset register (links VS-35), and evidence review | Lease Accounting / Controller | Controller | Per lease |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease-accounting system (W4427); GL (VS-17); fixed-asset register (VS-35); audit (VS-21); BI (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Calculation-error risk**: wrong PV/ROU build → mis-stated asset/liability; mitigated by system-calculated PV + dual review
- **Posting-error risk**: wrong GL/asset classification; mitigated by controlled posting & reconciliation (W4425)

### Time Estimate
- ~30–60 min per lease initial recognition

### Automation Opportunity
- PV auto-calculation; ROU-build engine; controlled journal posting to GL; FA-register auto-link

### Controls
- CTL: PV system-calculated; ROU-build per PFRS 16; controlled posting; reconciled to GL/FA register (W4425); reviewed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (GL), VS-35 (FA), VS-21 (audit), W4412 (rate/term/payment), W4417 (subsequent), W4425 (close), W4428 (controls)

## W4415. PFRS 16 Transition (Modified vs Full Retrospective)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Initial PFRS 16 adoption; new entity acquisition (VS-130) |
| **Frequency** | Per adoption/acquisition |
| **Volume** | One-time per entity/adoption; re-applied on M&A (VS-130) |
| **Owner** | Lease Accounting / Controller / Finance (VS-17.4) |
| **Participants** | Controller, Finance (VS-17.4), Audit (VS-21), Treasury (VS-18), IT (VS-27), External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Controller/Lease Accounting select & document the transition method — modified retrospective (cumulative adjustment to opening retained earnings, no restatement) vs full retrospective — per policy (W4409) and auditor guidance | Controller / Lease Accounting / External Auditor | CFO / Audit Committee | Per adoption |
| 2 | Lease Accounting calculate opening ROU asset & lease liability per the chosen method (e.g. modified-retrospective: ROU = liability, or liability less future-rent, with practical expedients), and post transition adjustment | Lease Accounting / Controller | Controller / CFO | Per adoption |
| 3 | Lease Accounting restate comparatives if full-retrospective, update disclosures (W4425), and reconcile to auditor sign-off | Lease Accounting / Audit / Finance | CFO | Per adoption |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease-accounting system (W4427); GL (VS-17); audit (VS-21); BI (VS-28); transition module

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transition-error risk**: wrong method/calculation → restatement; mitigated by method documentation & auditor sign-off
- **Comparative-restatement risk**: full-retrospective complexity; mitigated by system-restatement & dual review

### Time Estimate
- Weeks–months per adoption/M&A entity

### Automation Opportunity
- Transition-method engine; opening-balance calculator with practical expedients; comparative-restatement tool; disclosure auto-gen

### Controls
- CTL: transition method documented & auditor-approved; opening balances calculated; comparatives restated if required; disclosed (W4425)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (GL), VS-21 (audit), VS-130 (M&A), W4409 (policy), W4425 (disclosure), W4427 (system)
