## W4417. Subsequent Measurement — Depreciation, Interest & Payment Unwind

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly per lease |
| **Volume** | ~205+ on-balance-sheet leases × 12 months |
| **Owner** | Lease Accounting / Controller |
| **Participants** | Controller, Finance (VS-17.4), Treasury (VS-18), IT (VS-27), Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Accounting run the monthly engine — depreciation of ROU asset on straight-line over lease term; interest on lease liability (effective-interest method at discount rate W4412); reduction of liability for lease payment | Lease Accounting / System | Controller | Monthly |
| 2 | Lease Accounting post journals (DR depreciation / CR ROU; DR interest / CR liability; DR liability / CR cash/AP) to GL (VS-17) and reconcile to lease-accounting system & AP/cash (VS-15/VS-18) | Lease Accounting / Controller | Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Controller/Audit review the monthly P&L split (depreciation + interest, replacing former straight-line rent expense — a key PFRS 16 EBITDA effect), and investigate variances | Controller / Finance / Audit | CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease-accounting engine (W4427); GL (VS-17); AP/cash (VS-15/VS-18); BI (VS-28); audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Engine-error risk**: wrong depreciation/interest → mis-stated P&L/balance sheet; mitigated by system engine & reconciliation
- **P&L-distortion risk**: EBITDA jump from rent→depreciation+interest misunderstood by stakeholders; mitigated by clear reporting/communication

### Time Estimate
- Engine-run hours monthly across portfolio; ~1–2 days close

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated monthly engine; auto-journal posting to GL; AP/cash reconciliation; EBITDA-impact dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-675 (ensure controlled execution — Rou Asset and Lease Liability Measurement Modification and R.)
- CTL: system-calculated depreciation/interest; reconciled to GL/AP/cash (W4425); variances investigated; reviewed monthly

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (GL/R2R), VS-15 (AP), VS-18 (treasury), VS-21 (audit), W4412 (rate), W4414 (recognition), W4425 (close), W4428 (controls)

## W4418. Lease Modification, Reassessment & Remeasurement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Lease change (scope/payment/term); judgment change |
| **Frequency** | Per modification (~tens–hundreds/yr) |
| **Volume** | Modifications across the ~205+ lease portfolio |
| **Owner** | Lease Accounting / Lease Admin (VS-42) / Controller |
| **Participants** | Lease Admin (VS-42), Legal (VS-100), Treasury (VS-18), Real Estate (VS-97/VS-20), Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Admin/Legal identify & classify the modification — *separate* lease (additional ROU for added scope/rights) vs *modification/remasurement* of the existing lease (payment/term/Scope change) | Lease Admin / Legal / Lease Accounting | Controller | Per change |
| 2 | Lease Accounting remeasure the lease liability (revised payments at revised discount rate) and adjust the ROU asset correspondingly (or P&L for certain decreases), per PFRS 16 | Lease Accounting | Controller | Per change |
| 3 | Lease Accounting post the remeasurement journal, update the abstract (W4411) & repository (W4426), and disclose material modifications (W4425) | Lease Accounting | Controller | Per change |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease-accounting engine (W4427); lease admin/contract (VS-42/VS-100); GL (VS-17); repository (W4426); audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missed-modification risk**: side-letter/amendment not captured → stale liability; mitigated by lease-admin change-feed & periodic sweep
- **Mis-classification risk**: separate-lease vs modification mis-called → wrong accounting; mitigated by classification guidance & review

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 hrs per modification

### Automation Opportunity
- Lease-change intake & classification decision-tree; remeasurement engine; abstract/repository auto-update; disclosure flag

### Controls
- CTL-675 (ensure controlled execution — Rou Asset and Lease Liability Measurement Modification and R.)
- CTL: modifications identified & classified; liability remeasured at revised discount rate; ROU adjusted per PFRS 16; abstract/repository updated; reviewed (W4428)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-42 (lease admin), VS-100 (legal), VS-18 (treasury), W4411 (abstract), W4412 (rate), W4425 (disclosure), W4426 (repository)

| **Participants** | Lease Admin (VS-42), Treasury (VS-18), Finance (VS-17.4), Audit (VS-21), Real Estate (VS-97) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## W4421. Sublease & Sale-and-Leaseback (Lessor-Side) Accounting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Sublease executed; sale-and-leaseback (SLB) transaction |
| **Frequency** | Per transaction (~few/yr) |
| **Volume** | Subleases (e.g. BuildRight subletting surplus space) + occasional SLB |
| **Owner** | Lease Accounting / Controller / Real Estate (VS-97) |
| **Participants** | Real Estate (VS-97), Legal (VS-100), Finance (VS-17.4), Treasury (VS-18), Audit (VS-21), Tax (VS-79) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Accounting classify the sublease — BuildRight as **intermediate lessor**: retain lessee ROU/liability (scaled for right subleased) and recognize sublease income (finance or operating per IFRS 16 lessor model) | Lease Accounting / Real Estate | Controller / VP Real Estate | Per sublease |
| 2 | For SLB, Lease Accounting/Legal determine if the transfer qualifies as a **sale** under PFRS 15 — if sale: seller-lessee recognizes only proportionate ROU, buyer-lessor recognizes the asset; if not-sale: accounted as a financing | Lease Accounting / Legal / Finance | Controller / CFO / VP Legal | Per SLB |
| 3 | Lease Accounting post lessor-side/sublease journals, disclose (W4425), and coordinate with Property Mgmt (VS-97) for the lessor operating model | Lease Accounting / Real Estate | Controller | Per transaction |

### System Touchpoints

### Pain Points / Risks

### Time Estimate

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-675 (ensure controlled execution — Rou Asset and Lease Liability Measurement Modification and R.)

## W4422. Short-Term & Low-Value Lease Exemption Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New lease (W4413); policy threshold change |
| **Frequency** | Per lease + periodic review |
| **Volume** | Short-term & low-value leases (small equipment, IT, short tenancies) across portfolio |
| **Owner** | Lease Accounting / Controller / Procurement (VS-34) |
| **Participants** | Procurement (VS-34), ITAM (VS-99), Lease Admin (VS-42), Finance (VS-17.4), Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Accounting/Procurement identify leases qualifying for short-term (≤12 mo, no purchase option) or low-value (below threshold, e.g. <USD 5,000 standalone) exemption per policy (W4409) | Lease Accounting / Procurement / ITAM | Controller | Per lease |
| 2 | Lease Accounting account for exempt leases on a **straight-line expense** basis (no ROU/liability), disclose the expense (W4425), and monitor for changes (extension beyond 12 mo) that remove the exemption | Lease Accounting | Controller | Per period |
| 3 | Lease Accounting/Audit periodically test the low-value threshold & short-term assessment, and reclassify if a lease no longer qualifies | Lease Accounting / Audit | Controller | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease-accounting system (W4427); procurement (VS-34); ITAM (VS-99); GL (VS-17); audit (VS-21); repository (W4426)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Exemption-overuse risk**: leases wrongly exempted to avoid balance-sheet impact → audit finding; mitigated by threshold discipline & testing
- **Extension-blindness risk**: short-term lease extended >12 mo but still expensed → mis-statement; mitigated by extension monitoring

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min per exempt lease; periodic testing

### Automation Opportunity
- Exemption-rule engine; low-value threshold register; extension-monitor alert; expense-on-straight-line

### Controls
- CTL-675 (ensure controlled execution — Rou Asset and Lease Liability Measurement Modification and R.)
- CTL: exemptions applied per policy threshold; expensed straight-line; disclosed; monitored for extension; tested periodically (W4428)

### Cross-references

## W4424. Intercompany Lease Elimination & Consolidation Entries

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly consolidation; intercompany lease change |
| **Frequency** | Monthly per consolidation cycle |
| **Volume** | Intercompany leases across 5 entities (e.g. Property Mgmt → Depot) |
| **Owner** | Consolidation (VS-17) / Lease Accounting / Intercompany (VS-72) |
| **Participants** | Consolidation (VS-17), Intercompany (VS-72), Lease Accounting, Treasury (VS-18), Audit (VS-21), Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Consolidation/Intercompany identify intercompany leases (lessor & lessee within the 5-entity group — primarily Property Mgmt as lessor), and match lessee-ROU/liability to lessor-asset/income | Consolidation / Intercompany / Lease Accounting | Controller | Monthly |
| 2 | Consolidation post elimination entries — eliminate intercompany lease income/expense, eliminate intercompany ROU/lease-liability vs lessor-asset, eliminate unrealized profit, and eliminate intercompany receivable/payable | Consolidation / Lease Accounting | Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Consolidation validate the consolidated ROU/liability, reconcile to group FS (VS-17), and resolve any unmatched intercompany lease | Consolidation / Audit | Controller / CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Consolidation system (VS-17); intercompany (VS-72); lease-accounting (W4427); lessor/RE (VS-97); GL (VS-17); audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unmatched-intercompany risk**: lessor & lessee sides not matched → failed elimination → double-count; mitigated by intercompany-lease map & auto-match
- **Unrealized-profit-error risk**: profit on intercompany lease not eliminated; mitigated by elimination rules

### Time Estimate
- ~monthly consolidation hours

### Automation Opportunity
- Intercompany-lease auto-match; elimination rules engine; consolidated ROU/liability validation; exception workflow

### Controls
- CTL-675 (ensure controlled execution — Rou Asset and Lease Liability Measurement Modification and R.)
- CTL: intercompany leases identified & matched; eliminations posted; consolidated ROU/liability validated; reconciled to group FS; reviewed (W4428)

### Cross-references
