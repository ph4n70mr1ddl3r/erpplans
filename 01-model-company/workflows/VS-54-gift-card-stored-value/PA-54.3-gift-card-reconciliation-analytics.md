## W2158. Daily Gift Card Liability Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Daily across 200 stores + ecommerce |
| **Volume** | ~15,000–25,000 daily GC transactions |
| **Owner** | Accountant (AP/AR) |
| **Participants** | Accountant, Cashiers, Ecommerce Finance, Treasury |

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
