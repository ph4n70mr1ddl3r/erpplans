## W2150. In-Store Gift Card Redemption at POS

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer presents gift card as payment |
| **Frequency** | ~12,000–18,000 redemptions/month |
| **Volume** | ~12K–18K/month; avg PHP 1,800 |
| **Owner** | Cashier |
| **Participants** | Cashier, Customer, POS System, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cashier scans physical card or enters digital card number + PIN; POS validates (active, not expired, sufficient balance, not stolen) | Cashier | Store Manager | 30–60 sec |
| 2 | If full coverage: processes payment, reduces balance. If partial: applies gift card first, prompts additional tender (split payment) | POS System | Cashier | 30–60 sec |
| 3 | System records redemption: debits gift card liability, credits revenue; auto-deactivates zero-balance cards | System | Finance | Automated |
