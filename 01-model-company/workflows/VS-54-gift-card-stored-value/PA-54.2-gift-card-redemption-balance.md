## W2150. In-Store Gift Card Redemption at POS

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer presents gift card as payment |
| **Frequency** | ~12,000–18,000 redemptions/month |
| **Volume** | ~12K–18K/month; avg PHP 1,800 |
| **Owner** | Cashier |
| **Participants** | Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cashier scans the gift card; system displays the balance; customer confirms the amount to redeem; system applies the redemption, posts the liability→revenue conversion (the redeemed amount leaves gift-card liability and posts to revenue per PFRS 15), and prints the updated balance on the receipt | Cashier | Store Manager | 1–2 min |

### System Touchpoints
- POS / stored-value (links to VS-08 / W5B, W28, POS-013 real-time) — the redemption and the balance update
- Revenue recognition (links to VS-17 / W28, VS-157 / PFRS 15) — the liability→revenue posting at redemption
- Liability GL (links to VS-17 / W28) — the reduced liability balance

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Balance-dispute risk**: the system shows a different balance than the customer expects (a prior partial redemption forgotten), causing a dispute; mitigated by the balance-printed-on-receipt history and the W2152 balance-inquiry channel
- **Revenue-recognition-timing risk**: the redemption posts to revenue in the wrong period (cross-period redemption), distorting the monthly close; mitigated by the real-time posting and the W2158 daily reconciliation

### Automation Opportunity
- Redemption and revenue-posting fully automated in the POS flow; balance auto-printed on receipt

### Controls
- CTL-44: redemption/liability integrity; CTL (PFRS 15 revenue-recognition — links to VS-157); operational: Store Manager daily-close review

### Time Estimate
~1.5 min/redemption × ~15,000/month = **~375 hours/month Cashier** (absorbed into checkout).

## W2151. Online Gift Card Redemption

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer applies gift card at ecommerce checkout |
| **Frequency** | ~3,000–5,000 online redemptions/month |
| **Volume** | ~3K–5K/month; growing |
| **Owner** | Ecommerce System |
| **Participants** | Ecommerce Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer enters the gift card number/PIN at checkout; system validates the balance, applies the redemption, converts liability→revenue, and completes the order; balance updated in real-time across all channels (omnichannel stored-value) | Ecommerce System | Ecommerce Ops | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce / stored-value (links to VS-10 / W11, W180; VS-08 / W28) — the online redemption
- Omnichannel stored-value (links to VS-60 / W536 unified order management) — the real-time cross-channel balance
- Revenue recognition (links to VS-17 / W28, VS-157) — the liability→revenue posting

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fraud-risk**: a fraudster uses a gift-card number generated by a brute-force script (especially on low-PIN cards); mitigated by the redemption-attempt rate-limit and the fraud-monitoring (W2160)
- **Cross-channel-latency risk**: a redemption online doesn't reflect in-store immediately (or vice versa), allowing double-spend; mitigated by the real-time omnichannel stored-value (single authoritative balance)

### Automation Opportunity
- Redemption fully automated; balance real-time across channels

### Controls
- CTL-44: omnichannel balance integrity; CTL (ecommerce fraud — links to VS-125); operational: Ecommerce Ops monitoring

### Time Estimate
System automated; Ecommerce Ops ~4 hours/week = **~200 hours/year**.

## W2152. Gift Card Balance Inquiry & Statement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer requests balance via any channel |
| **Frequency** | ~8,000–12,000 inquiries/month |
| **Volume** | ~8K–12K/month across SMS, app, web, in-store |
| **Owner** | CSR / Self-Service |
| **Participants** | Customer Service Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer requests balance via SMS (text the card number), app/web (enter number+PIN), or in-store (CSR scans); system returns the current balance and recent transactions | System / CSR | Customer Service Mgr | Automated or 1–2 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Stored-value inquiry (links to VS-08 / W28, VS-75 app) — the balance API
- Self-service channels (links to VS-10 web, VS-75 app) — the customer-facing inquiry
- RA 10173 privacy (links to VS-91 / W53) — the card-number/PIN as access credentials (not PII in the traditional sense, but access-controlled)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Balance-enumeration risk**: a brute-force script uses the inquiry channel to enumerate valid card-numbers (then redeem); mitigated by the inquiry rate-limit and the PIN requirement
- **Channel-fragmentation risk**: the SMS channel shows a different balance than the app (sync lag); mitigated by the single authoritative stored-value balance

### Automation Opportunity
- Inquiry fully self-service across channels; rate-limited

### Controls
- CTL-392 (rate-limit / enumeration guard); CTL-44 (balance integrity); operational: Customer Service Manager monitoring

### Time Estimate
~1.5 min/inquiry (CSR-handled subset; most are self-service) × ~4,000/month = **~100 hours/month CSR**, declining as self-service grows.

## W2153. Lost/Stolen Gift Card Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer reports lost or stolen physical card |
| **Frequency** | ~200–400 reports/month (~1–2%) |
| **Volume** | ~200–400/month; primarily physical cards |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Rep |
| **Participants** | CSR, Customer Service Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CSR verifies the customer's identity and card ownership (proof of purchase, card number from the receipt); if the card is registered (loyalty-linked), the verification is straightforward; system freezes the card balance to prevent fraudulent redemption | CSR | Customer Service Mgr | 5–10 min |
| 2 | CSR issues a replacement card (new number) with the frozen balance transferred; the old card number is permanently deactivated; customer notified | CSR | Customer Service Mgr | 5–10 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Stored-value / card status (links to VS-08 / W28) — the freeze, transfer, and deactivation
- Loyalty / customer link (links to VS-13 / W17) — the registered-card ownership verification
- CRM (links to VS-13 / W41) — the lost-card case record

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fraudulent-claim risk**: a customer falsely claims a card is lost to double-spend (keep the original, get a replacement); mitigated by the proof-of-purchase requirement and the freeze-on-original (if the original is redeemed after the claim, it's flagged)
- **Unregistered-card-loss risk**: an unregistered card (gift recipient, cash purchase) can't be ownership-verified, so the balance is unrecoverable; mitigated by the loyalty-link encouragement at sale (register the card for lost-card protection)

### Automation Opportunity
- Freeze/transfer/deactivate fully system-controlled on CSR authorization; loyalty-link auto-suggested at sale

### Controls
- CTL-44: freeze/transfer integrity; CTL (fraudulent-claim guard — original-redemption-after-claim flag); operational: Customer Service Manager sign-off on replacements >PHP 5K

### Time Estimate
~12 min/case × ~300/month = **~60 hours/month CSR**.

## W2154. Gift Card Partial Redemption & Multi-Card Payment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer uses multiple gift cards for one transaction |
| **Frequency** | ~2,000–3,000 multi-card transactions/month |
| **Volume** | ~2K–3K/month; ~15% of redemptions |
| **Owner** | Cashier / POS System |
| **Participants** | Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cashier scans multiple gift cards; system applies each in sequence (partial redemption per card, draining each toward the transaction total); posts each liability→revenue conversion; prints the updated balance on each card's receipt | Cashier / POS System | Store Manager | 2–3 min |

### System Touchpoints
- POS / stored-value (links to VS-08 / W5B, W28) — the multi-card partial-redemption logic
- Revenue recognition (links to VS-17 / W28, VS-157) — the per-card liability→revenue posting
- Omnichannel balance (links to VS-60 / W536) — the real-time balance update per card

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transaction-stall risk**: a multi-card transaction with many small-balance cards stalls the checkout queue; mitigated by the multi-card-scan limit (e.g., max 5 cards per transaction) and the drain-to-zero auto-option
- **Balance-rounding-error risk**: partial redemption leaves a sub-cent balance that becomes unredeemable; mitigated by the drain-to-zero on balances <PHP 1

### Automation Opportunity
- Multi-card-scan with auto-sequence application; drain-to-zero auto-suggested

### Controls
- CTL-44: multi-card liability integrity; operational: Store Manager daily-close review

### Time Estimate
~2.5 min/transaction × ~2,500/month = **~100 hours/month Cashier** (absorbed into checkout).

### System Touchpoints

### Automation Opportunity
- Reload and liability-posting fully automated; AMLA threshold auto-prompted

- CTL-44: reload/liability integrity; CTL (AMLA KYC); operational: Store Manager daily-close review

### Time Estimate

## W2156. Gift Card Expiry & Breakage Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Gift card reaches 5-year expiry or monthly analysis |
| **Frequency** | Daily expiry; monthly breakage analysis |
| **Volume** | ~5–10% breakage; ~PHP 20–40M/year |
| **Owner** | Finance Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System flags cards reaching the 5-year-expiry window (per RA 7394 / consumer-protection practice); daily batch processes the expired balance to breakage revenue per PFRS 15 (Dr. Gift Card Liability / Cr. Breakage Revenue) | System / Finance | Finance Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Finance Manager performs monthly breakage analysis: actual breakage rate vs. the PFRS 15 expected-breakage estimate; adjusts the estimate if the actual diverges; reconciles the liability balance to the outstanding-card registry | Finance Manager | CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Stored-value / breakage (links to VS-08 / W28, VS-17 / W9A month-end) — the expiry batch and the breakage posting
- PFRS 15 revenue recognition (links to VS-157 / W911, VS-17 / W28) — the breakage-estimate methodology
- Liability reconciliation (links to VS-54 / W2158 daily reconciliation) — the liability-vs-outstanding-card reconciliation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Breakage-estimate-error risk**: the PFRS 15 expected-breakage estimate diverges from actual, creating a liability over/understatement; mitigated by the monthly actual-vs-estimate analysis and the estimate recalibration
- **Consumer-complaint risk**: a customer redeems an "expired" card (the 5-year window is a minimum, not a hard expiry under consumer-protection practice); mitigated by the goodwill-reinstatement policy (honor the expired card on customer request, reversing the breakage)

### Automation Opportunity
- Expiry batch auto-run daily; breakage-estimate-vs-actual auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-392 (PFRS 15 breakage accounting — links to VS-157); CTL-44 (liability integrity); operational: Finance Manager monthly sign-off; CFO quarterly review

### Time Estimate
~6 hours/month Finance Manager + daily batch automated = **~70–80 hours/year**.

## W2157. Gift Card Refund & Cancellation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer requests cancellation within cooling-off or error correction |
| **Frequency** | ~100–200 cancellations/month (~1%) |
| **Volume** | ~100–200/month |
| **Owner** | CS Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Customer Service Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CS Supervisor reviews the cancellation request: cooling-off period (within 24–48 hours of purchase, unredeemed), error correction (wrong denomination loaded), or fraud-claim reversal; verifies the card is unredeemed; processes the refund (reverse the liability, refund the original payment method) | CS Supervisor | Customer Service Mgr | 10–15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Stored-value / cancellation (links to VS-08 / W28, VS-16 / W101 refund) — the liability reversal and refund
- Payment reversal (links to VS-08 / W5B, VS-16 / W101) — the refund to the original payment method
- Fraud-monitoring (links to VS-54 / W2160) — the cancellation-frequency as a fraud signal

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Refund-fraud risk**: a fraudster buys a card with a stolen payment method, then requests a refund to a different (their own) payment method; mitigated by the refund-to-original-payment-method-only policy
- **Cancellation-abuse risk**: repeated purchase-cancel cycles (testing stolen cards); mitigated by the cancellation-frequency monitoring per customer

### Automation Opportunity
- Refund-to-original-payment auto-enforced; cancellation-frequency auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-44: cancellation/liability integrity; CTL (refund-to-original-payment guard); operational: Customer Service Manager sign-off on cancellations >PHP 5K

### Time Estimate
~12 min/cancellation × ~150/month = **~30 hours/month CS Supervisor**.
