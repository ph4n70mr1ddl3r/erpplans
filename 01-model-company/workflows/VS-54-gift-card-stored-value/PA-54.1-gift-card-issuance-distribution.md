## W2142. Physical Gift Card Stock Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Gift card inventory reorder point or new design launch |
| **Frequency** | Monthly reorder; quarterly new designs |
| **Volume** | ~50,000 cards printed/year; 10,000 in circulation |
| **Owner** | Marketing Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Ops Mgr, VP Marketing, Vendor, DC |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing Ops Manager monitors physical gift card stock across 200 stores; reorder triggers at minimum threshold; coordinates with card vendor for production (security features, sequential numbering, BuildRight branding) | Marketing Ops Mgr | VP Marketing | Monthly |
| 2 | Vendor produces cards with embedded security (sequential numbering registered to ERP at activation); ships to DC for distribution; DC allocates to stores based on sell-through rate | Vendor / DC | Marketing Ops Mgr | 2–3 weeks lead time |

### System Touchpoints
- Gift-card/stored-value module (links to VS-08 / W28 gift card liability, VS-29 / W296 service-item master) — the card-SKU and the activation registry
- Inventory (links to VS-05 / W6 cycle counting) — the physical-card stock tracking (distinct from the stored-value liability)
- DC distribution (links to VS-04 / W3) — card allocation to stores

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unactivated-card-loss risk**: physical cards lost/stolen before activation are worthless (no liability until activation), but loss signals weak controls; mitigated by the sequential-number registry and the store-level stock-count reconciliation
- **Design-obsolescence risk**: a quarterly new-design launch leaves the prior design as dead stock; mitigated by the design-phase-out plan (sell-through the old before activating the new)

### Automation Opportunity
- Stock-level auto-monitored with reorder at threshold; sequential-number range auto-registered to ERP on vendor shipment

### Controls
- CTL-44 (gift-card stock tracked separately from stored-value liability); CTL-06 (card-SKU master); operational: Marketing Ops monthly stock-reconciliation sign-off

### Time Estimate
~6 hours/month Marketing Ops + DC handling absorbed = **~70–90 hours/year**.

## W2143. Gift Card Sale & Activation at POS

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer purchases gift card at POS |
| **Frequency** | ~15,000–20,000 gift card transactions/month |
| **Volume** | ~15K–20K/month; avg PHP 2,000; ~PHP 30–40M/month |
| **Owner** | Cashier |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cashier scans the physical card; system prompts for load amount; customer pays; system activates the card (registers the sequential number to the stored-value liability) and prints a receipt; for cash purchases ≥PHP 50K, AMLA KYC applies (W2148) | Cashier | Store Manager | 1–2 min |
| 2 | System posts the sale: cash/card receipt to revenue-bank, load amount to gift-card liability (not revenue — revenue is recognized at redemption per PFRS 15) | System | Finance | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- POS / stored-value (links to VS-08 / W5B, W28 gift card liability, POS-013 real-time) — the activation and liability posting
- Stored-value liability GL (links to VS-17 / W28) — the liability account (Dr. Cash, Cr. Gift Card Liability)
- AMLA KYC (links to VS-86 / W2148) — the high-value-cash-purchase trigger

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Activation-failure risk**: the customer pays but the card doesn't activate (system glitch), so the stored-value never loads and the customer loses their money; mitigated by the activation-confirmation receipt and the W2158 daily liability reconciliation catching the discrepancy
- **Revenue-misrecognition risk**: the cashier posts the load as revenue (not liability), overstating revenue and understating liability; mitigated by the system-controlled posting (the cashier can't override the liability-vs-revenue split)

### Automation Opportunity
- Activation and liability-posting fully automated in the POS flow; AMLA KYC auto-prompted at the threshold

### Controls
- CTL-44 (liability-posting integrity); CTL (AMLA KYC on high-value cash — links to VS-86); operational: Store Manager daily-close review of gift-card activations

### Time Estimate
~1.5 min/transaction × ~17,500/month = **~440 hours/month Cashier time** (absorbed into the checkout role, not incremental headcount).

## W2144. Digital Gift Card Sale & Delivery

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer purchases digital gift card online |
| **Frequency** | ~3,000–5,000 digital cards/month; growing 20% QoQ |
| **Volume** | ~3K–5K/month; avg PHP 2,500 |
| **Owner** | Ecommerce Operations |
| **Participants** | Ecommerce Ops, Fraud Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer selects denomination and recipient (self or gift recipient) on ecommerce/app; pays online; system generates a digital card number, emails/SMSes it to the recipient, and posts the liability | Ecommerce System | Ecommerce Ops | Automated |
| 2 | For fraudulent-pattern transactions (rapid high-value purchases, stolen-card payment), the fraud filter (links to VS-125) holds the digital-card issuance pending review | Ecommerce System / Fraud Analyst | Ecommerce Ops | Automated + review |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce / stored-value (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace; VS-08 / W28) — the digital-card issuance and delivery
- Email/SMS gateway (links to VS-10) — the delivery channel
- Fraud filter (links to VS-125 / W266 ecommerce fraud) — the hold-and-review control

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stolen-payment-issuance risk**: a fraudster buys a digital card with a stolen credit card and redeems it before the chargeback lands; mitigated by the fraud-filter hold and the redemption-speed limit on newly-issued digital cards
- **Delivery-failure risk**: the email/SMS doesn't reach the recipient (typo, spam filter), and the customer disputes the charge; mitigated by the delivery-confirmation and the resend mechanism

### Automation Opportunity
- Digital-card issuance and delivery fully automated; fraud-hold auto-routed to the analyst worklist

### Controls
- CTL-44 (liability integrity); CTL (ecommerce fraud — links to VS-125 / W266); operational: Ecommerce Ops monitoring of the fraud-hold queue

### Time Estimate
System automated; Ecommerce Ops ~4 hours/week monitoring = **~200 hours/year**.

## W2145. Corporate Bulk Gift Card Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Corporate client requests bulk purchase |
| **Frequency** | ~20–30 corporate orders/month; peaks at Christmas |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 orders/month; 50–500 cards each |
| **Owner** | Corporate Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | Corporate Sales Mgr, VP Sales, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Corporate Sales Manager processes the bulk order: denomination, quantity, custom branding if requested, payment terms (often invoiced vs. prepaid); for bulk cash orders ≥ threshold, AMLA KYC applies | Corporate Sales Mgr | VP Sales | Per order |
| 2 | System bulk-activates the card range to the stored-value liability on payment receipt; delivers physical (to the corporate client) or digital (to the client's recipient list); invoices per the corporate payment terms | System / Corporate Sales Mgr | Finance | Per order |

### System Touchpoints
- Stored-value / bulk activation (links to VS-08 / W28, VS-16 / W8 AR for invoiced terms) — the bulk activation and billing
- CRM / corporate account (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-13 / W24 credit application) — the corporate client record
- AMLA KYC (links to VS-86 / W2148) — the bulk-cash threshold

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Bulk-activation-error risk**: a bulk activation loads the wrong amount or wrong card range, creating a large liability discrepancy; mitigated by the pre-activation validation (test activation on a sample before the full bulk run)
- **Corporate-default risk**: the corporate client is invoiced (not prepaid) and defaults on payment while the cards are already activated; mitigated by the credit-check (W24) and the prepaid-default for new corporate clients

### Automation Opportunity
- Bulk activation via file upload with pre-validation; invoice auto-generated per the corporate terms

### Controls
- CTL-44 (bulk-activation integrity); CTL-42 (corporate bank-detail-change guard on invoiced clients); operational: VP Sales sign-off on bulk orders >PHP 500K

### Time Estimate
~2 hours/order × ~25 orders/month = **~600 hours/year** Corporate Sales Manager.

## W2146. Gift Card Promotional Campaign

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Marketing launches gift card promotion (e.g., buy PHP 5K get PHP 500 bonus) |
| **Frequency** | ~4–6 campaigns/year aligned with seasonal calendar |
| **Volume** | ~4–6 campaigns/year; 30–50% lift during campaign |
| **Owner** | Marketing Promotions Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Promotions Mgr, VP Marketing, Finance, Finance Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing designs the promo (bonus structure, eligibility, channel); configures the bonus-load in the stored-value system; promotes across channels; tracks redemption and lift vs. baseline | Marketing Promotions Mgr | VP Marketing | Per campaign |
| 2 | Finance validates the bonus-load accounting (the bonus is a separate liability tranche with a different breakage/revenue-recognition treatment per PFRS 15) | Finance | Finance Manager | Per campaign |

### System Touchpoints
- Stored-value / promo config (links to VS-08 / W28, VS-58 / coupon, VS-14 / W13 promotions) — the bonus-load configuration
- Marketing/promo (links to VS-14 / W83 campaign) — the promo execution
- DTI permit (links to VS-22 / W868) — the "Per DTI Permit No." requirement on the promo

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Promo-fraud risk**: a fraudster exploits the bonus structure (buy PHP 5K, get PHP 500 bonus, redeem the PHP 5K for cash equivalent, keep the PHP 500); mitigated by the no-cashout-on-bonus term and the fraud-monitoring (W2160)
- **Revenue-misrecognition risk**: the bonus liability is commingled with the paid liability, distorting breakage accounting; mitigated by the separate liability tranche in step 2

### Automation Opportunity
- Bonus-load auto-applied at the promo threshold; separate-liability-tranche auto-configured

### Controls
- CTL-39 (promo-spend accuracy); CTL (DTI permit compliance — links to W868); operational: Finance sign-off on the bonus-liability tranche

### Time Estimate
~3 days/campaign × ~5 campaigns = **~100–120 hours/year** Marketing + Finance.

## W2148. Gift Card Compliance & Regulatory Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | BSP regulation update or annual review |
| **Frequency** | Annual comprehensive; ad-hoc for changes |
| **Volume** | BSP stored-value regulations; Consumer Act; AMLA |
| **Owner** | Legal & Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Legal Compliance Officer, VP Legal & Compliance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal & Compliance Officer monitors the regulatory framework: BSP stored-value-product rules (disclosure, expiry limits, consumer protection), RA 7394 Consumer Act (clear terms, no deceptive practices), RA 9160 AMLA (KYC on cash purchases ≥PHP 50K — single or aggregated), BIR revenue-recognition (PFRS 15 breakage) | Legal Compliance Officer | VP Legal | Annual + ad-hoc |
| 2 | Officer ensures BuildRight's program complies: T&Cs posted, expiry within the legal 5-year window (RA 7394 / consumer-protection practice), AMLA KYC triggered on the threshold, breakage accounting per PFRS 15 | Legal Compliance Officer | VP Legal | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance / T&Cs (links to VS-22 / W22.1 regulatory, VS-88 / W255 document) — the program T&Cs and disclosures
- AMLA KYC (links to VS-86 / W2143 trigger) — the high-value-cash KYC
- Breakage accounting (links to VS-17 / W28, W2156) — the PFRS 15 treatment

### Pain Points / Risks
- **AMLA-exposure risk**: a structured cash purchase (multiple sub-threshold cards to evade the PHP 50K KYC) goes undetected; mitigated by the aggregation-logic in the KYC trigger (single-customer, single-day aggregation)
- **Expiry-non-compliance risk**: a card expires before the legal 5-year window, triggering a consumer-protection complaint; mitigated by the 5-year-minimum-expiry setting enforced at the stored-value-system level

### Automation Opportunity
- AMLA-threshold auto-aggregated per customer/day; expiry auto-set to the 5-year minimum

### Controls
- CTL-211 (ensure stored-value float & liability integrity.)
- CTL (AMLA KYC — links to VS-86); CTL (consumer-protection / expiry — links to VS-22 / W2682); operational: VP Legal annual compliance sign-off

### Time Estimate
~40 hours/year Legal Compliance Officer + ad-hoc = **~50–60 hours/year**.

## W2149. Gift Card Denomination & Pricing Strategy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Semi-annual pricing review |
| **Frequency** | Semi-annual |
| **Volume** | Standard: PHP 300, 500, 1K, 2K, 3K, 5K, 10K |
| **Owner** | Category Manager (Gift Cards) |
| **Participants** | Category Manager, VP Marketing |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager reviews denomination performance: which denominations sell best, average load vs. face value, custom-load adoption; adjusts the standard-denomination set and the custom-load limits | Category Manager | VP Marketing | Semi-annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Stored-value / denomination config (links to VS-08 / W28, VS-29 / W296) — the standard-denomination master
- Analytics (links to VS-28 / W113) — the denomination-performance dataset
- POS (links to VS-08 / W5B) — the denomination selection at sale

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Denomination-mismatch risk**: the standard denominations don't match customer intent (e.g., no PHP 1,500 denomination for a typical gift amount), suppressing sales; mitigated by the custom-load option alongside the standard set
- **High-denomination-fraud risk**: the PHP 10K denomination is a fraud target (high value, portable); mitigated by the AMLA KYC on high-value cash purchases (W2143/2148)

### Automation Opportunity
- Denomination-performance auto-analyzed; denomination-set auto-suggested

### Controls
- CTL-391 (ensure controlled execution — Gift Card Issuance & Distribution (PA-54.1).)
- CTL (denomination config integrity); operational: VP Marketing sign-off on denomination changes

### Time Estimate
~1 day/semi-annual = **~16–20 hours/year** Category Manager.
