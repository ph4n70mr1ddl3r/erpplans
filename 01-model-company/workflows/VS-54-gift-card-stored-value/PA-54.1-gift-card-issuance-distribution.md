## W2142. Physical Gift Card Stock Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Gift card inventory reorder point or new design launch |
| **Frequency** | Monthly reorder; quarterly new designs |
| **Volume** | ~50,000 cards printed/year; 10,000 in circulation |
| **Owner** | Marketing Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Ops, Print Vendor, Store Manager, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing Ops monitors stock levels in ERP; reorder at 20 cards/store; places print order with denomination mix and seasonal design | Marketing Ops Mgr | VP Marketing | 1–2 hours |
| 2 | Vendor delivers to DC; DC distributes to stores; Store Manager receives, scans into inventory as inactive; stores in cash office safe | DC / Store Manager | Marketing Ops Mgr | 2–3 days |
| 3 | Quarterly: Finance reconciles physical stock vs. system; investigates discrepancies; lost/stolen cards deactivated | Finance | Marketing Ops Mgr | 2–3 hours |

## W2143. Gift Card Sale & Activation at POS

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer purchases gift card at POS |
| **Frequency** | ~15,000–20,000 gift card transactions/month |
| **Volume** | ~15K–20K/month; avg PHP 2,000; ~PHP 30–40M/month |
| **Owner** | Cashier |
| **Participants** | Cashier, Customer, POS System, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cashier scans inactive card barcode; POS prompts for load amount (min PHP 300, max PHP 50,000); activates card in real-time | Cashier | Store Manager | 1–2 min |
| 2 | Customer pays via any tender; transaction as gift card liability (not revenue); receipt with card number, balance, expiry, terms | Cashier / System | Finance | 30 sec |
| 3 | System sends optional SMS to purchaser confirming activation; gift card liability recorded in GL | System | Finance | Automated |
