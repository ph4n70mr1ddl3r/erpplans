## Overview

B2B Bulk-Project Custom Import, Indent Sourcing & Brokerage Operations for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the custom project-specific overseas procurement and logistics lifecycle for large B2B developers and institutional clients. This includes: custom sourcing specification scoping; foreign factory identification and quality vetting; pricing and margin matrix modeling; indent contract execution; Letter of Credit (LC) opening and bank trust receipt coordination; direct port-to-jobsite logistics; pre-shipment inspections; customs clearance and Bureau of Customs (BOC) entry; direct-to-jobsite container devanning; joint quantity surveys; progressive milestone billing; landed cost reconciliation; and supplier performance tracking.

BuildRight's aggregate B2B sales represent **~40% of total revenue (~PHP 25B annually)**. Developers and contractors routinely require bulk custom materials (such as custom marble tile layouts, high-spec structural steel, or project-specific elevators) that cannot be stocked in standard retail inventory. This value stream manages the customized indent-order import lifecycle where materials flow directly from port to jobsite, optimizing working capital and freight costs.
