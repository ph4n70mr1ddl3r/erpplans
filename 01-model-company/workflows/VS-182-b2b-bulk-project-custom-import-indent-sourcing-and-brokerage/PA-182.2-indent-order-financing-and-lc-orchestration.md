## W5233. Letter of Credit Opening and Trade Finance Allocation for Indent Orders

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Signed contract (W5228) and downpayment clearance (W5229) |
| **Frequency** | Per contract execution |
| **Volume** | ~40-50 LCs opened annually |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Import Buyer, Finance Director (VS-17.4), Partner Bank Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Submit LC application packet to partner bank matching contract terms. | Import Buyer | Treasury Manager | 2-3 hours |
| 2 | Allocate trade credit facility limit and reserve funding availability. | Treasury Manager | Treasury Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Review LC draft with Legal Operations (VS-100) to verify payment conditions. | Finance Director | Treasury Manager | 1 hour |
| 4 | Confirm bank issuance of LC and transmit SWIFT message to supplier. | Partner Bank Officer | Treasury Manager | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- Trade Finance module, Bank interface (VS-18).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Facility overcommitment risk**: Indent LC allocations exhaust trade limits needed for regular inventory sourcing; mitigated by maintaining dedicated B2B project credit lines.

### Time Estimate
- ~3-5 working days for bank issuance

### Automation Opportunity
- SWIFT message parsing and automated status matching in ERP.

### Controls
- CTL-01 (Prevent unauthorized purchases); Treasury Director check-off on credit facilities.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury), VS-100 (legal operations)

## W5234. Pro-Forma Invoice Verification and LC Amendment Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Supplier request for LC amendment or contract adjustment |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc per amendment request |
| **Volume** | ~10-15 amendments processed annually |
| **Owner** | Import Buyer |
| **Participants** | Treasury Manager, B2B Sourcing Manager, Partner Bank Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify validity of supplier amendment request (price change, delivery window extension). | Import Buyer | Import Buyer | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Calculate margin and project delivery schedule impact. | B2B Sourcing Manager | Import Buyer | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Submit approved amendment request to bank and pay amendment fees. | Treasury Manager | Import Buyer | 1 hour |
| 4 | Transmit bank SWIFT amendment notice to supplier bank. | Partner Bank Officer | Import Buyer | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- Trade Finance module, Partner Bank interface.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unauthorised cost escalation risk**: Uncontrolled LC amendments add fee overheads and delay shipment; mitigated by charging amendment fees back to supplier if they initiated change.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-4 working days

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated LC amendment workflow tracking with audit history.

### Controls
- CTL-01 (Prevent unauthorized purchases); CFO approval required for amendments affecting price or margin.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury), VS-101 (merchandise plan)

## W5235. Shipping Document Receipt Discrepancy Auditing and Bank Release

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vessel departure notification and doc submission to bank |
| **Frequency** | Per shipment dispatch |
| **Volume** | ~40-50 document reviews annually |
| **Owner** | Import Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Customs Broker Coordinator, Treasury Specialist, Partner Bank Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive shipping documents (Bill of Lading, Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, PSI). | Import Coordinator | Import Coordinator | 1 hour |
| 2 | Audit documents for consistency: exact matching of quantities, descriptions, weights, and LC terms. | Import Coordinator | Import Coordinator | 2-3 hours |
| 3 | Authorize bank to release shipping documents to Customs Broker upon zero discrepancies. | Treasury Specialist | Import Coordinator | 1 hour |
| 4 | Reconcile bank document fees and record in import ledger. | Import Coordinator | Import Coordinator | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Import Document Registry (W256), Partner Bank interface.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Discrepancy holding risk**: Inconsistent document descriptions delay customs release, causing port storage charges; mitigated by pre-clearance doc checks with shipping lines.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 days per document audit

### Automation Opportunity
- Optical Character Recognition (OCR) multi-document data reconciliation engine.

### Controls
- CTL-14 (3-way match validation controls); Import Director verification signature.

### Cross-references
- links to W256 (document archiving), VS-15 (P2P)

## W5236. Bank Trust Receipt Financing Execution for Indent Orders

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Document release from bank under TR terms |
| **Frequency** | Per qualified indent order import |
| **Volume** | ~20-30 TRs executed annually |
| **Owner** | Treasury Specialist |
| **Participants** | Treasury Manager, Partner Bank Officer, Cost Accountant |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Execute Trust Receipt (TR) agreement with partner bank for document release. | Treasury Specialist | Treasury Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Configure TR loan account in ERP: maturity date (typically 90-180 days), interest rate, principal amount. | Treasury Specialist | Treasury Specialist | 1 hour |
| 3 | Book trust receipt financing liability in General Ledger. | Cost Accountant | Treasury Specialist | 30 min |
| 4 | Track TR maturity calendar and schedule principal/interest payments. | Treasury Specialist | Treasury Specialist | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury Module, General Ledger (VS-17).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Interest expense leakage risk**: Lapsed TR payment dates trigger high penalty interest rates; mitigated by system automated maturity alerts 15 days before due date.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 day per TR execution

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated bank loan schedule import and interest accrual calculation.

### Controls
- CTL-19 (Ensure bank reconciliation completeness); CFO sign-off on TR facilities.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury), VS-170 (inventory pledge)

## W5237. Marine Insurance Coverage Mobilization and Port Risk Underwriting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Booking approval of shipment (W5232) |
| **Frequency** | Per cargo booking |
| **Volume** | ~40-50 declarations annually |
| **Owner** | Import Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Insurance Administrator (VS-26), Insurance Underwriter Broker |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Submit cargo declaration to marine insurance provider under open policy terms. | Import Coordinator | Import Coordinator | 1 hour |
| 2 | Verify insurance certificate: check coverage limit, port routing, and deductible levels. | Insurance Administrator | Import Coordinator | 1 hour |
| 3 | Confirm endorsement of policy matching Incoterms (typically FOB/EXW — BuildRight as buyer declares cargo under its own open policy into landed cost). | Insurance Underwriter Broker | Import Coordinator | 1 hour |
| 4 | Record insurance premium cost in landed cost calculator ledger. | Import Coordinator | Import Coordinator | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Landed Cost Engine (VS-158), Insurance Portal (VS-26).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Underinsured cargo risk**: Cargo damaged during rough sea transit is not fully covered due to valuation mismatch; mitigated by insuring cargo at CIF + 10% standard value.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 day per insurance booking

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated XML feed of cargo details from ERP shipment module to underwriter.

### Controls
- CTL-15 (landed cost audit controls); VP Supply Chain approval of cargo coverages.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-26 (insurance), VS-158 (product costing)

## W5238. Import Duties Excise Tax and 12 Percent VAT Payment Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customs duty assessment on the formal entry filed per W5239 (BOC e2m) |
| **Frequency** | Per customs entry assessment |
| **Volume** | ~40-50 payments processed annually |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | Import Coordinator, Partner Bank (AAB), Customs Broker |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive customs duties and VAT assessment sheet from Broker (via BOC portal). | Import Coordinator | Tax Accountant | 1 hour |
| 2 | Verify duties and excise-tax calculation against HS code rates and the VAT base (12% of the BOC dutiable value plus duty, excise and other charges per NIRC §107). | Tax Accountant | Tax Accountant | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Initiate online payment through bank portal to BOC collection account. | Tax Accountant | Tax Accountant | 1 hour |
| 4 | Secure bank confirmation receipt and transmit to Customs Broker. | Import Coordinator | Tax Accountant | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Tax Management module (VS-79), AAB Bank Portal (VS-18).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Incorrect HS classification risk**: Wrong tariff code leads to tax underpayment penalties; mitigated by securing official DTI-BPS and tariff commission classifications (VS-117/VS-87).

### Time Estimate
- ~3.5-4.5 hours for verification and payment release (per the step durations)

### Automation Opportunity
- Direct API connection to BOC payment rail for instant confirmation receipt generation.

### Controls
- CTL-22 (VAT compliance controls); Chief Accountant approval of tax payments.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-79 (tax), VS-18 (treasury), VS-87 (tariffs), VS-117 (DTI-BPS)

## W5239. Bureau of Customs Formal Entry Filing and Single Administrative Document Release

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Goods arrival at port / LC availability (W5236); formal entry due before release |
| **Frequency** | Per customs clearing cycle |
| **Volume** | ~40-50 clearances annually |
| **Owner** | Customs Broker Coordinator |
| **Participants** | In-house Customs Broker (VS-02.2), BOC Inspector |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | File formal import entry (Single Administrative Document - SAD) in BOC e2m system; the BOC assessment it returns triggers duty/VAT payment per W5238. | Customs Broker Coordinator | Customs Broker Coordinator | 2-3 hours |
| 2 | Coordinate physical container inspection (BOC Red Lane if triggered). | In-house Customs Broker | Customs Broker Coordinator | 2-4 hours |
| 3 | Verify duties/VAT paid per W5238 and assessment cleared, then secure Gate-Pass approval from port operator. | Customs Broker Coordinator | Customs Broker Coordinator | 1 hour |
| 4 | Download and file approved SAD in import document repository. | Customs Broker Coordinator | Customs Broker Coordinator | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- BOC e2m System, Document Registry (W256).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Customs inspection delay risk**: Port container yard congestion delays inspections; mitigated by booking inspections early morning at designated off-port clearance sites.

### Time Estimate
- ~3-5 working days standard customs duration

### Automation Opportunity
- BOC system status tracking dashboard with automated email notifications.

### Controls
- CTL-15 (landed-cost accuracy on imports); Compliance Director audit of brokerage logs.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-02.2 (import), W256 (document archiving)
