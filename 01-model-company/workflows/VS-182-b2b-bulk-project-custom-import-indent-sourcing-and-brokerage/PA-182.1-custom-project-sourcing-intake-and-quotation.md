## W5225. B2B Project Custom Sourcing Intake and Specifications Scoping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | B2B key account customer submits request for custom/bulk imported materials (VS-107, VS-11) |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc per project request |
| **Volume** | ~80-120 custom inquiries annually |
| **Owner** | B2B Sourcing Manager |
| **Participants** | Key Account Manager, Customer Project Design Specialist (VS-66), Technical Standards Officer (VS-117) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive custom material specifications from client. | Key Account Manager | B2B Sourcing Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Review technical feasibility and local regulatory compliance (BPS standards) with Technical Standards Officer. | Customer Project Design Specialist | B2B Sourcing Manager | 2-4 hours |
| 3 | Define final technical scope, packaging requirements, and target jobsite delivery dates. | B2B Sourcing Manager | B2B Sourcing Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Upload specifications sheet to ERP custom sourcing module. | B2B Sourcing Manager | B2B Sourcing Manager | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- B2B CRM module (VS-11), custom specification master (VS-29), project estimation module (VS-66).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Specification drift risk**: Customer specs change mid-process leading to incorrect orders; mitigated by sign-off gate in step 3.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-4 working days per intake review

### Automation Opportunity
- Customer portal for uploading design assets and CAD drawings directly linked to custom item module.

### Controls
- CTL-06 (vendor compliance check), technical compliance verification sign-off required prior to RFQ launch.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-11 (trade project), VS-107 (key account), VS-66 (design services), VS-117 (DTI-BPS)

## W5226. Overseas Supplier RFQ Factory Vetting and Sample Approval

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved specifications sheet (W5225) |
| **Frequency** | Per verified custom project inquiry |
| **Volume** | ~60-80 RFQs sent annually |
| **Owner** | Import Buyer |
| **Participants** | B2B Sourcing Manager, Global Sourcing Agent (VS-122), Quality Inspector (VS-31) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify qualified overseas suppliers and transmit RFQ package. | Import Buyer | Import Buyer | 2-3 hours |
| 2 | Review factory capability and ISO/accreditation records with Global Sourcing Agent. | Global Sourcing Agent | Import Buyer | 2-4 hours |
| 3 | Request prototype/production samples and track air-freight delivery. | Import Buyer | Import Buyer | 1 hour |
| 4 | Conduct physical and structural testing on received samples; issue sample evaluation report. | Quality Inspector | Import Buyer | 2-3 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Global Sourcing Portal (VS-122), Quality Management Module (VS-31).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Sample non-conformance risk**: Imported production materials differ from approved prototype; mitigated by retaining signed master samples in DC vault.

### Time Estimate
- ~10-15 working days lead time for overseas sample receipt and testing

### Automation Opportunity
- Automatic RFQ distribution and tracking system across registered import vendor panel.

### Controls
- CTL-06 (Prevent unauthorized vendor onboarding); Quality Manager sign-off on test results required.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-122 (global sourcing), VS-31 (quality management)

## W5227. Project Landing Cost Calculation Pricing and Margin Matrix Modeling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved sample evaluation report (W5226) |
| **Frequency** | Per approved sample |
| **Volume** | ~50-60 cost models generated annually |
| **Owner** | Logistics Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Import Buyer, Category Manager (VS-01.2), Freight Coordinator (VS-110) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gather FOB pricing, ocean freight rates, insurance, drayage, and customs tariff estimates. | Import Buyer | Logistics Finance Analyst | 2-3 hours |
| 2 | Input variables to Landed Cost Calculator; estimate duties, VAT, brokerage fees, and port charges. | Logistics Finance Analyst | Logistics Finance Analyst | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Apply margin matrix matching B2B project guidelines (target gross margin >=18% for custom bulk). | Category Manager | Logistics Finance Analyst | 1 hour |
| 4 | Generate project pricing proposal and submit to VP Merchandising for approval. | Logistics Finance Analyst | Logistics Finance Analyst | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Landed Cost Engine (VS-158), Pricing Module (VS-01.2).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cost underestimation risk**: Fluctuating ocean freight rates or tariff classifications erode planned margins; mitigated by utilizing real-time freight indexes and obtaining binding tariff rulings (VS-87).

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 days per costing cycle

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated API integration with logistics carriers for real-time freight and fuel surcharge updates.

### Controls
- CTL-15 (Ensure accurate landed cost on imports); VP Merchandising approval gate.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-158 (product costing), VS-01.2 (pricing), VS-110 (freight), VS-87 (tariffs)

- links to VS-100 (legal operations), VS-112 (PMO)

## W5229. Custom Order Pro-Forma Invoice Generation and Downpayment Collection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Signed indent contract (W5228) |
| **Frequency** | Per executed contract |
| **Volume** | ~40-50 orders processed annually |
| **Owner** | B2B Account Billing Clerk |
| **Participants** | Key Account Manager, Cashier (VS-08.1), AR Clerk (VS-16) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generate pro-forma invoice in ERP matching contract milestones (typically 30% downpayment). | B2B Account Billing Clerk | B2B Account Billing Clerk | 30 min |
| 2 | Transmit invoice to customer and track payment window (typically 5 business days). | Key Account Manager | B2B Account Billing Clerk | 30 min |
| 3 | Process downpayment collection (bank transfer, check, or escrow release). | Cashier | B2B Account Billing Clerk | 1 hour |
| 4 | Verify payment clearance in bank reconciliation and release order for production. | AR Clerk | B2B Account Billing Clerk | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Billing Module, AR Module (VS-16), Bank Reconciliation (VS-18).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Payment default risk**: Order sent to production before downpayment clears, creating cash exposure; mitigated by enforcing automated system production-lock until deposit clears.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-3 days for bank clearance

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated bank payment notification matching pro-forma reference numbers.

### Controls
- CTL-12 (Separate credit approval from sales execution); CTL-19 (Ensure bank reconciliation completeness).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (O2C), VS-18 (treasury), VS-173 (investor relations)

## W5232. Pre-Shipment Inspection Coordination and Quality Gate Sign-off

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Supplier notification of production completion |
| **Frequency** | Per completed production order |
| **Volume** | ~40-50 inspections coordinated annually |
| **Owner** | Quality Assurance Manager |
| **Participants** | Supplier Quality Liaison, Third-Party Inspector, B2B Sourcing Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Schedule third-party inspection (SGS, TUV, Bureau Veritas) at foreign factory location. | Supplier Quality Liaison | Quality Assurance Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Review Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI) report against specifications and approved samples. | Quality Assurance Manager | Quality Assurance Manager | 2-3 hours |
| 3 | Issue "Release for Shipment" certificate in ERP upon satisfactory PSI report. | Quality Assurance Manager | Quality Assurance Manager | 30 min |
| 4 | Authorize final container booking request with logistics partners. | B2B Sourcing Manager | Quality Assurance Manager | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Quality Module (VS-31), Supplier Portal (VS-03), shipping module (VS-110).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Defect container release risk**: Defective materials loaded into shipping containers before inspection is finalized; mitigated by requiring shipping lines to verify BuildRight's Quality Release Certificate before releasing empty containers to factory.

### Time Estimate
- ~3-5 working days for inspector dispatch and report analysis

### Automation Opportunity
- Automatic inspection trigger upon supplier production milestone closure.

### Controls
- CTL-24 (Detect receiving fraud); QA Director formal sign-off on PSI reports.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-31 (quality management), VS-110 (freight)
