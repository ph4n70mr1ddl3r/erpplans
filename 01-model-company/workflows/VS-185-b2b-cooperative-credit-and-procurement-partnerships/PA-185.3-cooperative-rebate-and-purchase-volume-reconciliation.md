## W5313. Quarterly Cooperative Purchase Volume Accumulation Audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarter-end closing cycle |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly per active cooperative account |
| **Volume** | ~80–120 account audits annually |
| **Owner** | Chief Accountant |
| **Participants** | Financial Analyst, Cooperative Finance Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Extract all closed invoice data associated with cooperative parent and child accounts. | Financial Analyst | Chief Accountant | 2 hours |
| 2 | Deduct returned items, cancelled orders, and disputed transactions. | Financial Analyst | Chief Accountant | 1 hour |
| 3 | Reconcile transaction values against bank receipts and cash collections. | Financial Analyst | Chief Accountant | 2 hours |
| 4 | Compile Quarterly Consolidated Sales Report. | Chief Accountant | Chief Accountant | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- General Ledger (VS-17), Accounts Receivable module, Reporting system.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Overpayment of rebates risk**: Calculating rebates on gross sales before deducting returns, causing margin erosion; mitigated by using net sales figures (excluding returns and taxes) for calculations.

### Automation Opportunity
- Quarterly sales extraction scripts compiling co-op accounts data.

### Controls
- CTL-787 (ensure controlled execution — Cooperative Rebate & Purchase Volume Reconciliation (PA-185.3).)
- operational: Month-end control standards; Chief Accountant audit validation.

### Time Estimate
- ~2 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (finance)

## W5314. Patronage Rebate Calculation & Tier Matching

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Quarterly per active cooperative account |
| **Volume** | ~80–120 rebate calculations annually |
| **Owner** | B2B Sales Director |
| **Participants** | Category Manager, Credit Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Match cooperative net purchase volume against tiered rebate percentage matrix in contract. | Credit Manager | B2B Sales Director | 1 hour |
| 2 | Compute quarterly rebate values. | Credit Manager | B2B Sales Director | 2 hours |
| 3 | Verify that the cooperative has met its contract credit terms. | Credit Manager | B2B Sales Director | 1 hour |
| 4 | Draft Rebate Approval Memo for Sales Director approval. | Credit Manager | B2B Sales Director | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- B2B Credit system, Category Pricing module (VS-39).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin compression risk**: Rebates granted to cooperatives with high margin exclusions or late payments, violating contract rules; mitigated by matching rebate eligibility to invoice payment dates.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated contract rebate tier matcher in CRM.

### Controls
- CTL-21: finance risk controls; B2B Sales Director sign-off.

### Time Estimate
- ~2 working days

### Cross-references

## W5316. Rebate Credit Memo Issuance and Voucher Disbursement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Posted rebate accrual journal entry (W5315) |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly per active cooperative account |
| **Volume** | ~80–120 disbursements annually |
| **Owner** | Accounts Payable Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Treasury Specialist, Cooperative Treasurer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Issue formal Credit Memo in ERP linked to cooperative parent account. | Accounts Payable Supervisor | Accounts Payable Supervisor | 1 hour |
| 2 | Apply credit to open invoice balances per co-op preference. | Accounts Payable Supervisor | Accounts Payable Supervisor | 1 hour |
| 3 | Transfer rebate funds to cooperative central bank account or issue cash voucher code. | Treasury Specialist | Accounts Payable Supervisor | 1 hour |
| 4 | Collect signed acknowledgement receipt from cooperative treasurer. | Accounts Payable Supervisor | Accounts Payable Supervisor | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- General Ledger (VS-17), Accounts Payable module (VS-15), Cash Book (VS-18).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Double disbursement risk**: Rebates applied as credit memo and also paid out in cash; mitigated by matching credit memos to unique voucher reference codes in ERP.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated credit memo generation and bank transfer processing.

### Controls
- CTL-787: ensure controlled execution — Cooperative Rebate & Purchase Volume Reconciliation (PA-185.3). — finance authorization controls; Finance Manager sign-off required for disbursements > PHP 500k.

### Time Estimate
- ~2 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-15 (P2P), VS-18 (treasury)
