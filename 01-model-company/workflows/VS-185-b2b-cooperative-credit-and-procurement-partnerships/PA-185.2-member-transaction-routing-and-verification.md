## W5305. Cooperative Member Digital POS Account Association & Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Cooperative member wishes to purchase goods using co-op credit line |
| **Frequency** | Daily per member transaction |
| **Volume** | ~12,000-15,000 purchases annually |
| **Owner** | Front-End Store Manager / POS Cashier |
| **Participants** | Cooperative Member, Co-op Verification Hotline Agent |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Present member credentials at checkout counter. | Cooperative Member | Front-End Store Manager / POS Cashier | 2 min |
| 2 | Scan member digital barcode on POS terminal. | POS Cashier | Front-End Store Manager / POS Cashier | 1 min |
| 3 | Query central co-op registry database to verify member eligibility. | Front-End Store Manager / POS Cashier | Front-End Store Manager / POS Cashier | 30 sec |
| 4 | If digital lookup fails, check membership status manually. | POS Cashier | Front-End Store Manager / POS Cashier | 3 minutes |

### System Touchpoints
- POS Checkout terminal (VS-08), B2B CRM module.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Identity theft purchase risk**: Non-members use cooperative credentials to make purchases, causing chargeback disputes; mitigated by requiring dual identification checks matching ID photo.

### Automation Opportunity
- Real-time API query to cooperative member databases returning member photo.

### Controls
- CTL-20: Ensure POS cash/credit accountability; Cashier ID matching protocols.

### Time Estimate
- ~3-5 min at checkout

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08 (POS), VS-107 (key account)

## W5306. Cooperative Member Authorization and Purchase Order Match

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Validated member request for high-value materials (> PHP 50,000) using co-op line |
| **Frequency** | Daily per high-value member transaction |
| **Volume** | ~3,000-4,000 transactions annually |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Lead |
| **Participants** | Co-op Secretary, Cooperative Member |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collect cooperative-issued Purchase Order (PO) or Authorization Letter from member. | Customer Service Lead | Customer Service Lead | 2 min |
| 2 | Match PO specifications (material list, budget limit, jobsite address) with POS basket items. | Customer Service Lead | Customer Service Lead | 5 min |
| 3 | Check PO authorization codes and signatories against co-op profile on system. | Customer Service Lead | Customer Service Lead | 3 min |
| 4 | Scan and attach the PO document to the POS invoice transaction in ERP. | Customer Service Lead | Customer Service Lead | 2 min |

### System Touchpoints
- POS terminal (VS-08), Document management portal (VS-88).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scope deviation risk**: Members purchase unauthorized retail items (appliances, consumer goods) on corporate construction POs; mitigated by blocking POS items not matching PO product classes.

### Automation Opportunity
- OCR scanner parsing PO details and matching against basket contents.

### Controls
- CTL-786: ensure controlled execution — Member Transaction Routing & Verification (PA-185.2). — legal authorization controls; Customer Service Lead PO validation.

### Time Estimate
- ~10-12 min

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08 (POS), VS-88 (document control)

## W5307. Real-time Credit Line Availability Check at Checkout

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | POS cashier initiates credit payment method on co-op account (W5305) |
| **Frequency** | Per co-op account checkout |
| **Volume** | ~12,000-15,000 checks annually |
| **Owner** | POS System Controller |
| **Participants** | POS Cashier, Credit Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | POS system calculates basket total (net of co-op B2B discounts and VAT exemptions). | POS System Controller | POS System Controller | 10 sec |
| 2 | System queries parent co-op credit ledger balance in central ERP database. | POS System Controller | POS System Controller | 10 sec |
| 3 | If requested transaction exceeds remaining available credit, block checkout and issue credit limit alert. | POS System Controller | POS System Controller | 10 sec |
| 4 | If limit is sufficient, hold transaction amount and proceed to invoice lock. | POS System Controller | POS System Controller | 10 sec |

### System Touchpoints
- POS Checkout terminal (VS-08), ERP Credit module (VS-68).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-limit credit risk**: POS terminal completes transactions while co-op account is in credit block status due to network delays; mitigated by offline POS credit blocking policies.

### Automation Opportunity
- Real-time credit inquiry interface integrated within cash register UI.

### Controls
- CTL-21: finance risk controls; Credit limits enforced at system database levels.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 min

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08 (POS), VS-68 (credit risk)

## W5312. Dispute Resolution for Member Unauthorized Purchases

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Cooperative flags transaction as unauthorized or disputes billing |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc per billing dispute |
| **Volume** | ~30-40 disputes annually |
| **Owner** | Customer Relations Officer |
| **Participants** | Cooperative Member, Accounts Receivable Clerk, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive dispute details: invoice number, disputed items, transaction date, co-op member name. | Customer Relations Officer | Customer Relations Officer | 1 hour |
| 2 | Retrieve transaction records: POS video logs, signature cards, scanned government IDs, PO attachments. | Accounts Receivable Clerk | Customer Relations Officer | 2 hours |
| 3 | Conduct review meeting with store manager and co-op officers to present evidence. | Customer Relations Officer | Customer Relations Officer | 2 hours |
| 4 | If unauthorized purchase is confirmed due to cashier error, issue credit memo and update internal controls. | Store Manager | Customer Relations Officer | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- Accounts Receivable module (VS-16), POS Logs (VS-08).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Bad-debt leakage risk**: Writing off disputed sales without proper verification of cashier compliance with ID check policies; mitigated by reviewing POS signature card matches.

### Automation Opportunity
- Online dispute registry for tracking audit trail and resolution history.

### Controls
- CTL-20: POS cash/credit accountability; credit memo approval matrix.

### Time Estimate
- ~3-5 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (O2C), VS-08 (POS)
