## Overview

B2B Cooperative Credit, Purchasing and Procurement Partnerships for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the wholesale sales and financial credit relationship with cooperative societies (agricultural, consumer, and credit cooperatives) in the provincial Philippine market. This includes: cooperative onboarding and board resolution review; CDA clearance checking; group credit limit allocation; cooperative member verification at POS; purchase tracking; quarterly rebate settlement; and joint community procurement projects.

With **170 stores located outside Metro Manila**, cooperatives represent massive economic groups. Establishing structured credit and procurement partnerships with cooperatives drives large-ticket purchasing for provincial infrastructure (warehouses, dryers, community facilities) and secures highly loyal customer cohorts.
