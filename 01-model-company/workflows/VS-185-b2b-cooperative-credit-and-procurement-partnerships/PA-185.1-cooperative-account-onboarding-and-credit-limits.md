## W5297. Cooperative Partner Account Creation & Initial Contact

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Regional sales team identifies candidate cooperative or cooperative contact request |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc per candidate co-op |
| **Volume** | ~30-40 inquiries annually |
| **Owner** | Cooperative Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Co-op Board Secretary, Regional Sales Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet with cooperative board representatives to present B2B wholesale pricing structures. | Cooperative Account Manager | Cooperative Account Manager | 2 hours |
| 2 | Collect co-op profiling sheet: membership size, asset size, sector focus. | Cooperative Account Manager | Cooperative Account Manager | 1 hour |
| 3 | Create cooperative prospect profile in B2B ERP system. | Cooperative Account Manager | Cooperative Account Manager | 30 min |
| 4 | Initiate onboarding compliance checklist workflow. | Regional Sales Manager | Cooperative Account Manager | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM / B2B customer database (VS-107).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Ineligible entity onboarding risk**: Onboarding fake or unregistered cooperatives, exposing firm to credit losses; mitigated by collecting formal contact credentials and profile sheets.

### Automation Opportunity
- Online partner inquiry form automatically creating CRM records.

### Controls
- CTL-785: cooperative compliance check; Sourcing/Sales Director contact validation.

### Time Estimate
- ~3 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-107 (key account), VS-11 (trade sales)

## W5300. Cooperative Financial Performance Review & Balance Sheet Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Validated CDA compliance status (W5299) |
| **Frequency** | Per cooperative onboarding |
| **Volume** | ~20-30 financial reviews annually |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst |
| **Participants** | Cooperative Treasurer, Credit Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collect three years of audited financial statements, tax returns, and current cash flow projections. | Credit Analyst | Credit Analyst | 1 hour |
| 2 | Calculate key financial ratios: debt-to-equity, current ratio, operating margin, and member capital size. | Credit Analyst | Credit Analyst | 3 hours |
| 3 | Conduct reference checks with primary deposit banks and existing supplier credit departments. | Credit Analyst | Credit Analyst | 2 hours |
| 4 | Compile Credit Assessment Report recommending credit limits. | Credit Analyst | Credit Analyst | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- B2B Credit module (VS-68), CRM.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Insolvent partner default risk**: Guaranty credit lines to cash-poor cooperatives, resulting in high bad-debt write-offs; mitigated by checking bank credit references and audited financial ratios.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated financial analysis spreadsheet parsing standard BIR XML financial statements.

### Controls
- CTL-785: finance risk controls; Credit Manager limit endorsement.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-3 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (credit risk), VS-17 (finance)

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per cooperative onboarding |
| **Volume** | ~20-30 contracts executed annually |
| **Owner** | Legal Counsel |
| **Participants** | Co-op Chairman, Partner Bank Representative, VP Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Draft tripartite agreement defining terms: credit line size, payment cycles, bank settlement routing, and member liability. | Legal Counsel | Legal Counsel | 3 hours |
| 2 | Share draft with co-op board and partner bank credit department for review. | Legal Counsel | Legal Counsel | 2 hours |
| 3 | Revise contract terms per feedback; coordinate final legal review. | Legal Counsel | Legal Counsel | 2 hours |
| 4 | Execute contract signing and notarize the tripartite agreement. | Legal Counsel | Legal Counsel | 2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract management portal (VS-100), Legal records system.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Contract enforceability risk**: Unclear credit terms or member liability clauses lead to legal disputes during payment recovery; mitigated by utilizing standardized legal templates approved by VP Legal.

### Automation Opportunity
- Electronic document signature routing.

### Controls
- CTL-785: ensure controlled execution — Cooperative Account Onboarding & Credit Limits (PA-185.1). — legal authorization controls; CFO final approval on credit limits > PHP 5M.

### Time Estimate
- ~5-10 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal operations), VS-68 (credit risk)

## W5302. Cooperative Group Credit Limit Allocation & System Activation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Executed tripartite agreement (W5301) |
| **Frequency** | Per approved cooperative account |
| **Volume** | ~20-30 accounts activated annually |
| **Owner** | Financial Systems Administrator |
| **Participants** | Chief Accountant, Credit Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create cooperative parent customer ledger account in ERP. | Financial Systems Administrator | Financial Systems Administrator | 1 hour |
| 2 | Input approved credit limit, billing cycle (typically net-30), and interest/penalty terms. | Financial Systems Administrator | Financial Systems Administrator | 1 hour |
| 3 | Generate unique cooperative customer code and assign to regional store registers. | Financial Systems Administrator | Financial Systems Administrator | 30 min |
| 4 | Verify credit limit status in ERP ledger; notify cooperative officers of account activation. | Credit Analyst | Financial Systems Administrator | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP General Ledger (VS-17), POS master data system (VS-29), Credit module.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **System limit overrun risk**: Cooperative accounts activated with incorrect credit limits, allowing over-purchasing; mitigated by requiring dual-person verification in ERP for credit limit setups.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated account activation notifications sent via email and SMS to co-op administrators.

### Controls
- CTL-785: month-end control standards; Chief Accountant credit configuration validation.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 day

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (finance), VS-29 (master data)

### Controls
- CTL-785: finance risk controls; Risk Specialist sign-off on surety bonds.

## W5304. Annual Cooperative Account Credit Limit Review & Renewal

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Cooperative account active for 12 months or nearing renewal date |
| **Frequency** | Annually per active cooperative customer |
| **Volume** | ~40-50 reviews annually |
| **Owner** | B2B Credit Director |
| **Participants** | Cooperative Treasurer, Credit Analyst, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Extract past-year purchase and payment performance: transaction volumes, delay days, collection issues. | Credit Analyst | B2B Credit Director | 2 hours |
| 2 | Request updated audited financial statement and current CDA certificate. | Credit Analyst | B2B Credit Director | 1 hour |
| 3 | Re-evaluate credit risks; calculate updated credit rating score. | Credit Analyst | B2B Credit Director | 2 hours |
| 4 | Adjust credit limit in ERP system and sign off on annual renewal agreement. | B2B Credit Director | B2B Credit Director | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- Credit module (VS-68), General Ledger (VS-17), CRM.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stale credit risk**: Deteriorating financial health of cooperative ignored during limit renewals, causing defaults; mitigated by requiring updated audited statements for limit renewals.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated review queues triggering email requests for financial documents 60 days before expiry.

### Controls
- CTL-785: finance risk controls; Credit Director renewal sign-off.

### Time Estimate
- ~5 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (credit risk), VS-17 (finance)
