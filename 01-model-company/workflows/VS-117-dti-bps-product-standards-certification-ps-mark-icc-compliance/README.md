# VS-117: DTI-BPS Product Standards Certification & PS Mark/ICC Compliance

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

DTI-BPS Product Standards Certification & PS Mark/ICC Compliance workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **mandatory Philippine product-certification program** operated by the Department of
Trade and Industry – Bureau of Philippine Standards (DTI-BPS) for regulated construction and
consumer products. BuildRight's assortment is heavily weighted to DTI-BPS-regulated categories:
deformed steel bars and other steel products, cement, PVC/CPVC pipes and fittings, electrical
products (wires, cables, circuit breakers, switches, extension cords), ceramic tiles, gypsum
board, paints and coatings, and household chemicals (~14% lumber/building materials, ~12% tiles,
~10% electrical, ~8% paint of the 35,000-SKU assortment per profile §6.2). Under Philippine law
(RA 4109, the Standards and Trade Laws, and DAO / Department Administrative Orders issued by
DTI-BPS), these products **cannot legally be sold** in the Philippines without active
certification: locally manufactured products require a **PS (Philippine Standard) Quality / Safety
Mark License** held by the manufacturer; imported regulated products require an **Import
Commodity Clearance (ICC)** per shipment, or a **Statement of Confirmation (SOC)** where the
foreign manufacturer holds a valid PS License. DTI-BPS conducts market surveillance ("Oplan"
monitoring), and non-compliance triggers product seizure, sales-stop orders, fines, customs
hold, and reputational/Consumer Act (RA 7394) exposure.

Today the program is reduced to a single workflow — **W447 (DTI-BPS Mandatory Product
Certification – ICC/SOC)** in VS-22.1 — which covers only the per-import-shipment clearance
process (application → documentary review → inspection/sampling → testing → certificate →
release → sticker application). The full enterprise program is unowned: PS-Mark license
management across BuildRight's ~800–1,000 vendors, regulated-product identification and standards
mapping, new-product certification gating at assortment, vendor PS-license verification and
renewal tracking, ICC sticker inventory and control, accredited-laboratory relationship and
testing strategy, DTI market-surveillance response and Oplan coordination, in-market compliance
monitoring across 200 stores, sample-failure disposition and re-export, vendor recovery and
penalty chargeback, certification cost/lead-time landed-cost analytics, and multi-entity (5
legal entities) certification coordination. This value stream consolidates the discipline:
standards governance and vendor PS-Mark licensing; import ICC/SOC clearance, testing and sticker
management; and market surveillance, compliance monitoring and certification analytics.

This is distinct from **VS-87 (Customs Trade Compliance & Tariff)** which clears goods through the
BOC and manages duty/tariff (this value stream manages the DTI-BPS product-certification that is
often a *prerequisite* to customs release of regulated goods, and the in-market certification
that governs ongoing saleability). It is distinct from **VS-31 (Quality Management & Product
Compliance)** which governs BuildRight's *internal* quality inspection and vendor quality
(this value stream governs the *external regulatory certification* of regulated products to PH
national standards). It is distinct from **VS-89 (Product Recall & Safety Corrective Action)**
which removes defective/safety products from market (this value stream prevents uncertified /
non-compliant regulated products from reaching or remaining on shelf, and feeds recall when a
certification non-conformance is the root cause). It is distinct from **VS-114 (DG/Hazmat
Compliance)** which governs dangerous-goods transport/handling (this value stream governs
product *standards certification*, a different regulatory regime). It is distinct from **VS-03
(Vendor Management)** which onboards and contracts vendors (this value stream manages the vendor's
*certification compliance* — PS License validity, renewal, testing cooperation). DTI-BPS product
standards certification is the *regulated-product market-access and certification* discipline —
distinct from customs, internal quality, recall, DG, and vendor management.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: a material share of the assortment (~44% of SKUs by category
mix) is DTI-BPS-regulated, ~40% is imported (per profile §6.5), and the company operates 200
stores and 5 legal entities across which certification status must be current and defensible.
Without a dedicated owner, BuildRight risks selling uncertified/non-compliant regulated products
(illegal sale, DTI seizure, fines, Consumer Act liability), customs hold and port demurrage on
imports lacking ICC, shelf removal and revenue loss during market-surveillance action, and
unrecoverable landed-cost inflation from reactive testing — risks that no existing value stream
owns end-to-end.
