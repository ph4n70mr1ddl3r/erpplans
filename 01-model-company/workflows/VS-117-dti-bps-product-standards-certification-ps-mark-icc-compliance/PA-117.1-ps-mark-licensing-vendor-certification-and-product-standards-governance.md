## W3665. DTI-BPS Certification Program Strategy & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual compliance planning (links to VS-22.3/VS-33.1); regulatory change; program review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise DTI-BPS certification program |
| **Owner** | Product Compliance Manager / Regulatory Officer |
| **Participants** | Product Compliance Manager, Regulatory Officer (links to VS-22), Merchandising/Category Mgrs (links to VS-01), Vendor Mgmt (links to VS-03), Import/Customs (links to VS-87/VS-02.2), Quality (links to VS-31), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Cross-Entity Shared Services (links to VS-72), Audit (links to VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Compliance Manager/Regulatory Officer define the DTI-BPS certification program strategy and governance: scope (regulated product categories — steel, cement, PVC, electrical, ceramics, gypsum, paint, chemicals), the instrument map (PS Mark License for local manufacture, ICC per import shipment, SOC for PS-licensed foreign makers), the regulatory map (RA 4109, DTI-BPS DAOs, I-SEAL portal, Consumer Act RA 7394), the operating model, and KPIs (regulatory non-conformance rate, ICC cycle-time, PS-license currency, sticker integrity, market-surveillance findings) | Product Compliance Manager / Regulatory Officer | VP Compliance | Annual |
| 2 | Product Compliance Manager establish the governance: Product Compliance Manager authority, the cross-functional responsibilities (Merchandising gates assortment, Vendor Mgmt manages license, Import coordinates ICC, Quality runs sampling, Legal advises), the certification policy, and the integration to regulatory compliance (VS-22), merchandising (VS-01), vendor management (VS-03), and customs (VS-87) | Product Compliance Manager / Regulatory Officer / Legal | VP Compliance | Annual |
| 3 | VP Compliance/CFO ratify the certification strategy and policy, approve the program budget (links to VS-17.4), and report certification posture and regulatory exposure to the executive risk committee (links to VS-21.2/VS-36) and board (links to VS-36); communicate the mandate | VP Compliance / Product Compliance Manager | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Regulatory/compliance (links to VS-22); merchandising/assortment (links to VS-01); vendor management (links to VS-03); customs/import (links to VS-87/VS-02.2); quality (links to VS-31); legal (links to VS-100.3); governance/board (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-owner risk**: Certification handled reactively per-import by the broker with no program view; mitigated by Product Compliance Manager authority
- **Scope-drift risk**: New regulated categories or standards added by DTI-BPS without BuildRight awareness; mitigated by standards monitoring (W3671)

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual

## W3666. Regulated-Product Identification & BPS Standards Mapping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program strategy (W3665); assortment change; new BPS standard |
| **Frequency** | Maintained + on change |
| **Volume** | DTI-BPS-regulated SKU subset (~10,000–15,000 SKUs across regulated categories) |
| **Owner** | Product Compliance Manager / Merchandising |
| **Participants** | Product Compliance Manager, Merchandising (links to VS-01), MDM (links to VS-29.1), Quality (links to VS-31), Vendor Mgmt (links to VS-03) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Compliance Manager/Merchandising maintain the regulated-product register: identify which of the 35,000 active SKUs fall under DTI-BPS mandatory certification (PNS-covered products), map each to its governing Philippine National Standard (PNS) and DAO, the certification type required (PS Mark / ICC / SOC / exempt), and the certificate/sticker obligation; integrate to item master (links to VS-29.1) with a "regulated" flag and cert-required attribute | Product Compliance Manager / Merchandising / MDM | Product Compliance Manager | Maintained |
| 2 | Product Compliance Manager classify each regulated SKU by origin (locally manufactured → vendor PS License; imported → ICC per shipment or SOC), certification status, and risk tier (high-risk structural/electrical vs. lower-risk); drive the standards mapping into assortment and sourcing decisions (links to VS-01/VS-03) | Product Compliance Manager / Merchandising | Product Compliance Manager | Maintained |
| 3 | Product Compliance Manager/MDM enforce the regulated flag downstream: gate PO creation, goods receipt, and POS saleability on certification status (links to W3669/W3681), and publish the regulated-product register to compliance, merchandising, and audit (links to W3672) | Product Compliance Manager / MDM | Product Compliance Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Item master/MDM (links to VS-29.1); merchandising/assortment (links to VS-01); vendor management (links to VS-03); quality (links to VS-31); PO/GR/POS gating (links to W3669/W3681)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-classification risk**: A regulated SKU mis-flagged as exempt reaches shelf uncertified; mitigated by standards mapping and MDM governance
- **Register-staleness risk**: New SKUs onboarded without certification flag; mitigated by assortment compliance gate (W3669)

### Time Estimate
- Maintained + continuous

| **Frequency** | Per vendor + ongoing (license validity 1–3 years) |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 vendors; regulated-category vendor subset |
| **Owner** | Product Compliance Manager / Vendor Management |
| **Participants** | Product Compliance Manager, Vendor Mgmt (links to VS-03), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Quality (links to VS-31), Vendor Scorecard (links to VS-67) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Compliance Manager/Vendor Mgmt capture and verify vendor/manufacturer PS Mark License data for regulated products: license number, scope (product/standard covered), validity period, issuing BPS office, and the supporting factory audit/testing evidence; verify authenticity via DTI-BPS and the vendor portal (links to W3670) | Product Compliance Manager / Vendor Mgmt | Product Compliance Manager | Per vendor |
| 2 | Product Compliance Manager maintain the PS-license register with expiry tracking, renewal reminders (90/60/30-day), and the license-to-SKU mapping; gate sourcing on valid license (no PO for a regulated locally-manufactured product without active PS License) | Product Compliance Manager / Vendor Mgmt / Merchandising | Product Compliance Manager | Ongoing |
| 3 | Product Compliance Manager manage PS-license lapse and renewal: coordinate vendor renewal, factory re-audit, and BPS re-issuance; on lapse, block new PO, assess in-stock sell-down authorization vs. Consumer Act, and feed to vendor scorecard (links to VS-67) and audit (links to W3672) | Product Compliance Manager / Vendor Mgmt | Product Compliance Manager | Per renewal |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor management/portal (links to VS-03.4); merchandising (links to VS-01); legal (links to VS-100.3); quality (links to VS-31); vendor scorecard (links to VS-67); PS-license register (links to W3672)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lapsed-license risk**: Selling a regulated product whose vendor PS License expired — illegal sale; mitigated by expiry tracking and PO gating
- **Counterfeit-license risk**: Vendor presents a forged/expired PS License; mitigated by DTI-BPS verification (W3670)

### Time Estimate
- Per vendor + ongoing

| **Frequency** | Per new regulated SKU |

| **Owner** | Product Compliance Manager / Merchandising |
| **Participants** | Product Compliance Manager, Merchandising (links to VS-01), Vendor Mgmt (links to VS-03), Quality (links to VS-31), Import (links to VS-02.2/VS-87), MDM (links to VS-29.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Compliance Manager/Merchandising assess every new product at assortment stage (links to VS-01.1 sample evaluation) for DTI-BPS regulation: determine if the product is PNS-covered, the certification type required (PS Mark / ICC / SOC / exempt), the vendor's certification posture, and the lead-time/cost impact on the new-product launch | Product Compliance Manager / Merchandising | VP Merchandising | Per new SKU |
| 2 | Product Compliance Manager gate assortment activation on certification readiness: block SKU activation to sellable/PO-able until the PS License is verified (local) or the ICC plan is confirmed (import); manage exempt products with documented exemption basis | Product Compliance Manager / MDM / Merchandising | Product Compliance Manager | Per new SKU |
| 3 | Product Compliance Manager/Import coordinate the certification path: trigger PS License application (vendor) or ICC/SOC import clearance (links to W3673), incorporate certification cost and lead-time into landed cost (links to VS-02.2/W144) and launch timing (links to VS-01.1), and confirm certification before first sale | Product Compliance Manager / Import / Merchandising | Product Compliance Manager | Per new SKU |

### System Touchpoints
- Merchandising/assortment (links to VS-01.1); item master/MDM (links to VS-29.1); vendor portal (links to W705); import/landed cost (links to VS-02.2/W144); ICC clearance (links to W3673)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Launch-block risk**: New product cannot sell pending certification, delaying revenue; mitigated by early assessment and parallel certification
- **Assortment-bypass risk**: New regulated SKU activated without certification gate; mitigated by MDM gating

### Time Estimate
- Per new SKU (2–6 weeks certification lead-time)

| 2 | Product Compliance Manager/Vendor Mgmt manage the PS License renewal lifecycle: trigger vendor renewal 90 days pre-expiry, coordinate factory re-audit and BPS re-issuance, track renewal to completion, and gate continued sourcing on renewal | Product Compliance Manager / Vendor Mgmt | Product Compliance Manager | Per renewal |

- **Renewal-lapse risk**: PS License lapses during renewal delay; mitigated by 90-day pre-expiry trigger

| **Participants** | Product Compliance Manager, Regulatory Officer (links to VS-22.3), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Vendor Mgmt (links to VS-03), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Government Affairs (links to VS-104) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Compliance Manager/Regulatory Officer monitor the DTI-BPS regulatory pipeline: new/updated PNS, DAOs adding/removing products from mandatory certification, ICC/SOC procedural changes, I-SEAL portal updates, sticker/fee changes, and market-surveillance programs (Oplan); source via DTI-BPS channels, associations (links to VS-104.2), and regulatory intelligence | Product Compliance Manager / Regulatory Officer | Product Compliance Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Product Compliance Manager assess the impact of each regulatory change on BuildRight's regulated-product register (W3666), vendors (W3667), and in-market stock; determine the compliance action (re-certify, re-test, re-mark, sell-down, withdraw) and the deadline; integrate to enterprise regulatory-change management (links to VS-22.3) | Product Compliance Manager / Merchandising / Vendor Mgmt | Product Compliance Manager | On change |
| 3 | Product Compliance Manager execute the change: update the standards mapping (W3666), brief merchandising/vendors, drive re-certification, and report regulatory-change impact and posture to governance (links to VS-22.3/VS-36) and government-affairs intelligence (links to VS-104) | Product Compliance Manager / Regulatory Officer | VP Compliance | On change |

### System Touchpoints
- Regulatory change management (links to VS-22.3); merchandising (links to VS-01); vendor management (links to VS-03); legal (links to VS-100.3); government affairs (links to VS-104); standards register (links to W3666)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Blind-spot risk**: A new DAO adding a product to mandatory certification goes unnoticed; mitigated by continuous monitoring and association intelligence
- **Late-response risk**: Re-certification initiated too late to meet the deadline, forcing sell-down/withdrawal; mitigated by impact assessment and deadline tracking

### Time Estimate
- Continuous + on publication
