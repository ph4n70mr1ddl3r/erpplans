## W3673. Import ICC/SOC Application & I-SEAL Portal Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Import PO for regulated product (links to VS-02.2/W2B); shipment departure |
| **Frequency** | Per import shipment of regulated goods |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 regulated import shipments/month (~400–600 TEUs/month total imports) |
| **Owner** | Import Coordinator / Product Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Import Coordinator, Product Compliance Manager, Customs Broker (links to VS-87), Buyer (links to VS-01), Vendor (links to VS-03), DC (links to VS-04) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Import Coordinator/Product Compliance Manager file the ICC or SOC application via DTI's I-SEAL portal upon shipment departure from origin: select ICC (foreign maker without PS License) vs. SOC (foreign maker holds valid PS License), attach the Bill of Lading, Packing List, Commercial Invoice, and Certificate of Origin, and pay the BPS fee | Import Coordinator / Product Compliance Manager | Product Compliance Manager | 1 hour/shipment |
| 2 | Import Coordinator manage the I-SEAL application lifecycle: track status, respond to BPS queries, manage the documentary-review loop (W3674), and coordinate timing against vessel arrival, customs release (links to VS-87), and DC delivery (links to VS-04) to minimize port dwell/demurrage (links to W249) | Import Coordinator / Customs Broker | Product Compliance Manager | Per shipment |
| 3 | Import Coordinator/Product Compliance Manager maintain the ICC/SOC application register, forecast upcoming regulated-import demand for BPS resource/lab planning (links to W3680), and report ICC cycle-time and clearance rate to program analytics (links to W3685) | Import Coordinator / Product Compliance Manager | Product Compliance Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Import tracker / I-SEAL portal integration; import PO (links to VS-02.2/W2B); customs (links to VS-87); DC receiving (links to VS-04); demurrage (links to W249); ICC register (links to W3672)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Application-error risk**: Incorrect ICC-vs-SOC selection or missing documents causes rejection and delay; mitigated by checklist and pre-submission review
- **Cycle-time risk**: Slow BPS review extends port dwell and demurrage cost; mitigated by early filing and lab planning (W3680)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-file of "file the ICC or SOC application via DTI's I-SEAL portal upon shipment departure…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documentary-review loop" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-import of "Import Coordinator/Product Compliance Manager maintain the ICC/SOC application…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: checklist and pre-submission review; operational: early filing and lab planning (W3680)

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 hours filing + application lifecycle 14–21 days

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Import Coordinator/Customs Broker assemble and submit the documentary package for BPS review: validated Bill of Lading, Packing List, Commercial Invoice, Certificate of Origin, the foreign maker's PS License (for SOC), test reports where required, and the product/standard identification | Import Coordinator / Customs Broker | Product Compliance Manager | Per shipment |
| 2 | Import Coordinator manage the BPS documentary-review loop: respond to queries, correct deficiencies, and confirm product-to-standard classification with BPS; coordinate with the vendor for missing/incorrect origin documents | Import Coordinator / Vendor | Product Compliance Manager | 1–3 days |

|---|---|---|---|---|

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-inspect of "inspection and sampling upon arrival" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "document the sample draw" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-post of "post-inspection" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: witnessed sampling and PNS procedure; operational: expediting testing (W3676)

### Time Estimate
- 4 hours inspection + coordination

## W3676. Accredited Laboratory Testing & Results Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | BPS sampling (W3675); test request |
| **Frequency** | Per shipment (and per PS License factory audit) |
| **Volume** | Per regulated shipment / per product re-test |
| **Owner** | Product Compliance Manager / Quality |
| **Participants** | Product Compliance Manager, Quality (links to VS-31), Accredited Lab (links to W3680), Import Coordinator, Vendor, DC |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Compliance Manager/Quality dispatch samples to the DTI-accredited laboratory and manage the test program: confirm the applicable PNS test methods (e.g., tensile/yield for steel, compressive strength for cement, electrical safety for wires/breakers), the sample integrity, and the test scope | Product Compliance Manager / Quality / Lab | Product Compliance Manager | Per shipment |
| 2 | Product Compliance Manager/Lab track testing to completion (5–10 days typical), manage re-test where a result is borderline/inconclusive, and capture the test report into the technical file (links to W3668) and ICC application (links to W3673) | Product Compliance Manager / Lab | Product Compliance Manager | 5–10 days |
| 3 | Product Compliance Manager/Quality evaluate results against the PNS acceptance criteria, determine pass/fail, and on fail trigger sample-failure disposition (links to W3679) and vendor recovery (links to W3686); on pass, proceed to certificate issuance (links to W3677) | Product Compliance Manager / Quality | Product Compliance Manager | Per shipment |

### System Touchpoints
- Quality/lab (links to VS-31/W625); technical file (links to W3668); ICC application (links to W3673); lab relationship (links to W3680); sample-failure (links to W3679)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Testing-delay risk**: Accredited-lab lead-times exceed 2 weeks, triggering demurrage (links to W249); mitigated by lab planning (W3680)
- **Borderline-result risk**: Results near the limit create re-test decisions; mitigated by clear acceptance criteria and re-test protocol


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-dispatch of "dispatch samples to the DTI-accredited laboratory and manage the test program" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "file" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
operational: lab planning (W3680); operational: clear acceptance criteria and re-test protocol

### Time Estimate
- 5–10 days per shipment

## W3677. ICC/SOC Certificate Issuance & Customs Release Coordination

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per shipment |
| **Volume** | Per regulated import shipment |
| **Owner** | Import Coordinator / Customs Broker |
| **Participants** | Import Coordinator, Customs Broker (links to VS-87), Product Compliance Manager, DC (links to VS-04), Finance (links to VS-17/W144), Records (links to VS-88) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Import Coordinator/Product Compliance Manager obtain the ICC/SOC certificate from BPS upon successful test, verify the certificate scope/quantity/validity, and coordinate ICC sticker issuance (links to W3678) for the certified units | Import Coordinator / Product Compliance Manager | Product Compliance Manager | 2 days |
| 2 | Customs Broker/Import Coordinator use the ICC/SOC certificate to finalize customs clearance (links to VS-87) — the BPS certificate is a prerequisite to BOC release of regulated goods — and coordinate delivery to DC (links to VS-04/VS-02.2) and putaway (links to W3678 sticker application) | Customs Broker / Import Coordinator / DC | Product Compliance Manager | 1 day |
| 3 | Import Coordinator/Finance capture the certification cost (BPS fees, testing, stickers) into landed cost (links to W144/VS-02.2) and archive the certificate to the ICC register (links to W3672) and technical file (links to W3668) per retention (links to VS-88) | Import Coordinator / Finance / Records | Product Compliance Manager | Per shipment |

### System Touchpoints
- Customs clearance (links to VS-87); DC receiving (links to VS-04); landed cost (links to W144/VS-02.2); sticker management (links to W3678); ICC register (links to W3672); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Release-delay risk**: Customs cannot release without the BPS certificate, extending dwell; mitigated by expediting the test/issuance chain
- **Certificate-scope risk**: Certificate quantity/scope mismatch with received goods; mitigated by verification


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "verify the certificate scope/quantity/validity, and coordinate ICC sticker…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-import of "Import Coordinator use the ICC/SOC certificate to finalize customs clearance" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-file of "file" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: expediting the test/issuance chain; operational: verification

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 days issuance + release

## W3679. Sample Failure, Re-export & Disposition Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Failed test result (W3676); BPS rejection |
| **Frequency** | On failure |
| **Volume** | Low frequency; high cost per event |
| **Owner** | Product Compliance Manager / Import |
| **Participants** | Product Compliance Manager, Import Coordinator, Customs Broker (links to VS-87), Vendor Mgmt (links to VS-03), Finance (links to VS-17), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Quality (links to VS-31), DC (links to VS-04) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Compliance Manager manage the failed-sample event: confirm the failure against the PNS criterion, place the affected lot on hold/stop-sell (links to W3684), notify BPS, and prevent release to customs/DC pending disposition | Product Compliance Manager / Import / Quality | Product Compliance Manager | On failure |
| 2 | Product Compliance Manager/Import execute the BPS-directed disposition: re-export or destruction under DTI supervision (the entire shipment may be affected), manage customs/drawback implications (links to VS-87.2), and coordinate with the vendor on root cause and recovery (links to W3686) | Product Compliance Manager / Import / Customs Broker / Vendor | Product Compliance Manager | Per event |
| 3 | Product Compliance Manager/Vendor Mgmt drive corrective action (vendor process fix, re-test, re-shipment), capture the failure cost (product loss, freight, demurrage, destruction, re-procurement) for vendor recovery (links to W3686), and feed root cause to quality (links to VS-31) and assortment decisions (links to VS-01) | Product Compliance Manager / Vendor Mgmt / Finance | Product Compliance Manager | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Stop-sell/hold (links to W3684); customs/drawback (links to VS-87.2); vendor recovery (links to W3686); quality/CAPA (links to VS-31); finance (links to VS-17); legal (links to VS-100.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lot-contamination risk**: A failed sample may invalidate the whole shipment; mitigated by lot segregation and BPS-directed disposition
- **Cost-recovery risk**: Vendor disputes recovery for a BPS-directed re-export/destruction; mitigated by contract terms and documented root cause


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-notify of "notify BPS, and prevent release to customs/DC pending disposition" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-import of "Import execute the BPS-directed disposition" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-capture of "capture the failure cost" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: lot segregation and BPS-directed disposition; operational: contract terms and documented root cause

### Time Estimate
- Per event (days to weeks)

## W3680. Testing Laboratory Relationship, Accreditation & Cost Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Lab sourcing (W3673); performance review; renewal |
| **Frequency** | Per lab + ongoing |
| **Volume** | DTI-accredited labs used for regulated-product testing |
| **Owner** | Product Compliance Manager / Quality |
| **Participants** | Product Compliance Manager, Quality (links to VS-31), Procurement (links to VS-34), Import Coordinator, Vendor Mgmt (links to VS-03), Vendor Scorecard (links to VS-67), Finance (links to VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Compliance Manager/Quality/Procurement qualify and select DTI-accredited testing laboratories: confirm BPS accreditation scope (the PNS test methods covered), capacity, turnaround, cost, and location; maintain the approved-lab list and diversify to avoid bottleneck | Product Compliance Manager / Quality / Procurement | Product Compliance Manager | Per lab |
| 2 | Product Compliance Manager/Quality manage the lab relationship and performance: turnaround SLA, test-quality, pricing, and capacity planning against the regulated-import pipeline (links to W3673); route tests to optimize cycle-time and cost | Product Compliance Manager / Quality | Product Compliance Manager | Ongoing |
| 3 | Product Compliance Manager/Finance manage testing cost: benchmark per-test cost across labs, consolidate volume for discount, allocate testing cost to landed cost (links to W144), and publish testing cost/cycle-time analytics to program reporting (links to W3685) and merchandising (links to VS-01) | Product Compliance Manager / Finance / Quality | Product Compliance Manager | Ongoing + quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Quality/lab (links to VS-31); procurement (links to VS-34); landed cost (links to W144); ICC pipeline (links to W3673); program analytics (links to W3685); vendor scorecard (links to VS-67)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lab-bottleneck risk**: Single lab with long lead-time constrains clearance; mitigated by diversification and capacity planning
- **Accreditation-lapse risk**: Lab loses BPS accreditation mid-engagement; mitigated by accreditation monitoring and backup labs


### Automation Opportunity
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approved-lab list and diversify to avoid bottleneck" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-route of "route tests to optimize cycle-time and cost" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "count, allocate testing cost to landed cost" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: diversification and capacity planning; operational: accreditation monitoring and backup labs

### Time Estimate
- Per lab + ongoing
