## W2398. Post-Season Sales Performance Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Season complete; all clearance finalized |
| **Frequency** | 6/year; 13 categories |
| **Volume** | 6 comprehensive analyses/year |
| **Owner** | Analytics Manager |
| **Participants** | Analytics Manager, Category Mgr, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analyzes: seasonal sales vs. plan by category, SKU, store, region; sell-through rate; markdown rate; gross margin; identifies best and worst performing seasonal items | Analytics Manager | Category Manager | 3–5 days/season |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113, W208 retail analytics) — the post-season performance dataset
- Forecast/plan (links to VS-02 / W31 demand forecasting, VS-64 / W2382 assortment plan) — the plan baseline
- Markdown (links to VS-64 / W2390–W2397) — the markdown-rate input

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Attribution-to-weather risk**: a rainy-season category's performance is confounded by an unusually dry season (external factor, not planning error); mitigated by the weather-normalized view where the prior-year-same-season comparison controls for the seasonal-pattern baseline
- **Winner-confirmation-bias risk**: a "best performer" is confirmed for next year without examining whether it was the product or a one-off (a viral social-media moment); mitigated by the 2-season confirmation before locking an item into the playbook

### Automation Opportunity
- Post-season analysis auto-assembled from the sales/markdown/forecast datasets; best/worst auto-ranked

### Controls
- CTL-423 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Season Analysis & Learning (PA-64.3).)
- Operational: Analytics Manager methodology sign-off; Category Manager review before the playbook update

### Time Estimate
~4 days/season × 6 seasons = **~190–240 hours/year** Analytics Manager.

## W2399. Seasonal Forecast Accuracy Review

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-season |
| **Frequency** | 6/year |
| **Volume** | 6 accuracy reviews/year |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Planner |
| **Participants** | Merch Planner, Analytics Manager, Category Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compares: forecasted demand vs. actual sales per seasonal SKU; calculates forecast accuracy (MAPE); identifies systematic over/under-forecasting patterns; improves next season's forecast model | Merchandising Planner / Analytics Manager | VP Merchandising | 2–3 days/season |

### System Touchpoints
- Demand forecasting (links to VS-02 / W31, VS-64 / W2382) — the forecast and the actuals
- Analytics (links to VS-27 / W113) — the MAPE computation and pattern analysis
- Seasonal calendar (links to VS-29 / W306) — the season framing for the comparison

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Model-overfit risk**: the forecast model is tuned to last season's outlier and over-corrects; mitigated by the multi-season rolling accuracy view (not single-season tuning)
- **New-SKU-no-baseline risk**: a first-year seasonal SKU has no prior-season baseline, making the forecast a guess; mitigated by the analogous-SKU proxy (a similar seasonal SKU's pattern as the baseline)

### Automation Opportunity
- MAPE auto-computed per SKU per season; systematic over/under-bias auto-flagged for the model update

### Controls
- CTL-423 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Season Analysis & Learning (PA-64.3).)
- Operational: VP Merchandising sign-off on the forecast-model change

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/season × 6 seasons = **~120–150 hours/year** Merch Planner + Analytics.

### Controls
- CTL-423 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Season Analysis & Learning (PA-64.3).)

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/season × 6 seasons = **~120–150 hours/year** Buyer + Category Manager (the file's 8–10 h/day convention).

## W2403. Seasonal Markdown Strategy Effectiveness

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-season |
| **Frequency** | 6/year |
| **Volume** | All seasonal markdowns |
| **Owner** | Pricing Analyst |
| **Participants** | Pricing Analyst, Merch Planner, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evaluates: markdown timing (was earlier markdown better?), depth (which discount level cleared inventory fastest?), phase progression effectiveness; recommends strategy improvements for next season | Pricing Analyst | Category Manager | 2–3 days/season |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing/markdown (links to VS-29 / W289, VS-64 / W2390–W2397) — the markdown dataset
- Analytics (links to VS-27 / W113) — the timing/depth effectiveness
- Forecast (links to VS-64 / W2399) — the forecast-vs-actual that informed the markdowns

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Confounded-timing risk**: an "earlier markdown was better" finding is confounded by an earlier season-start or a stronger promo; mitigated by the controlled comparison (same season-stage, same promo context)
- **Margin-vs-clearance trade-off blindness**: a deeper markdown cleared inventory faster but at a margin loss that a shallower markdown would have avoided; mitigated by the margin-recovered vs. inventory-cleared dual view

### Automation Opportunity
- Markdown-effectiveness auto-computed per phase/depth; next-season strategy auto-suggested

### Controls
- CTL-423 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Season Analysis & Learning (PA-64.3).)
- Operational: Category Manager + Finance joint sign-off on the strategy recommendation

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/season × 6 seasons = **~120–150 hours/year** Pricing Analyst.

## W2405. Seasonal Playbook Update

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual update after all seasons reviewed |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | Full seasonal playbook |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merch, Category Mgrs, Merch Planners, Buyers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Merchandising compiles learnings from all 6 seasons into updated seasonal playbook: forecast adjustments, assortment changes, vendor recommendations, markdown strategy refinements, display improvements; distributes to planning team for next year | VP Merchandising | COO | 5–10 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Knowledge/playbook repository (links to VS-30 / W381 knowledge management, VS-88 document control) — the versioned playbook
- Analytics inputs (links to VS-64 / W2398–W2404) — the per-season learnings
- Merchandising planning (links to VS-01, VS-64 / W2382) — the consumers of the updated playbook

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Playbook-as-shelfware risk**: the playbook is updated but not consulted at next year's planning (the learnings don't propagate); mitigated by the playbook referenced as the mandatory input at the W2382 seasonal-assortment-planning kickoff
- **Over-generalization risk**: a one-season outlier becomes a permanent playbook rule; mitigated by the 2-season confirmation threshold (per W2398) before a learning is promoted to the playbook

### Automation Opportunity
- Playbook-change-diff auto-generated from the per-season learnings; playbook-references auto-linked to the W2382 planning kickoff

### Controls
- CTL-423 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Season Analysis & Learning (PA-64.3).)
- Operational: COO sign-off on the annual playbook update; playbook referenced in the W2382 planning template

### Time Estimate
~7.5 days/year VP Merchandising + Category Mgr/Planner contributions absorbed = **~60–80 hours/year** VP Merchandising.
