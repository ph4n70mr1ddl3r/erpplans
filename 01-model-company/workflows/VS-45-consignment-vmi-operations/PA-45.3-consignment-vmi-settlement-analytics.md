## W1942. Consignment Sell-Through Settlement & Vendor Payment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly settlement cycle per consignment agreement |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~15–25 consignment vendors settled monthly |
| **Owner** | AP Manager |
| **Participants** | AP Manager, Category Manager, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates monthly consignment sell-through report per vendor: (a) total units sold by SKU in settlement period, (b) consignment cost per unit per SKU, (c) total settlement amount: units sold × consignment cost, (d) deductions: (i) returns processed, (ii) damages charged to vendor per agreement, (iii) promotional discounts shared per agreement, (e) net settlement amount | System | AP Manager | Automated |
| 2 | AP Manager reviews sell-through report: (a) verify unit counts against POS transaction data, (b) verify consignment costs per agreement (any price changes applied correctly?), (c) verify deductions (returns, damages, promo contributions), (d) flag any discrepancies for Category Manager review | AP Manager | Accounting Manager | 2–4 hours/month |
| 3 | AP Manager submits settlement statement to vendor: (a) via vendor portal (preferred), (b) settlement statement includes: sell-through detail, deductions, net amount, payment terms (typically Net 30 from settlement date), (c) vendor reviews and confirms within 10 business days | AP Manager | — | 1 day |
| 4 | Payment processing: (a) vendor invoice (settlement statement) processed through AP, (b) standard payment run per W7.9, (c) payment to vendor bank account, (d) GL posting: debit consignment AP (liability created at ownership transfer), credit cash/bank | AP Manager / System | Accounting Manager | Per payment run |

### System Touchpoints
- Automated sell-through settlement calculation (W1942.1)
- Settlement statement generation and vendor portal submission (W1942.3)
- Integration with W7 (AP processing) for payment

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Settlement disputes**: Vendor may dispute unit counts or deduction calculations; maintain detailed transaction-level sell-through data for audit support
- **Timing mismatch**: Sell-through period and payment period may not align; clear contractual settlement calendar prevents disputes

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 hours/month for review; payment processing absorbed in standard AP cycle

## W1943. Consignment Margin Analysis & Vendor Negotiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly consignment margin review |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~15–25 consignment vendors |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Category Manager, Pricing Analyst, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing Analyst generates quarterly consignment margin report: (a) gross margin % by consignment vendor, (b) gross margin % by consignment category (appliances, tiles), (c) compare consignment margin to owned-inventory margin for same categories, (d) margin trend: improving or declining by vendor, (e) identify vendors where consignment margin < owned-inventory margin by > 5 percentage points | Pricing Analyst | Category Manager | 1 day/quarter |
| 2 | Category Manager reviews margin analysis: (a) low-margin consignment vendors: negotiate improved consignment costs, (b) evaluate alternatives: can BuildRight purchase directly at better margin?, (c) high-margin consignment: consider expanding consignment range with vendor, (d) assess total value of consignment arrangement: (margin × volume) vs. (capital saved from not owning inventory) | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 day/quarter |
| 3 | Annual: Category Manager negotiates consignment cost adjustments with vendors based on: (a) volume commitment (higher volume = lower consignment cost), (b) competitive benchmarking (what margin do other retailers get?), (c) total relationship value (vendor also supplies non-consignment goods) | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | Per annual negotiation |

### System Touchpoints
- Consignment margin analytics (W1943.1)
- Integration with W85 (product costing & margin analysis) for consolidated margin view

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden costs**: Consignment may appear high-margin but operational costs (reconciliation, vendor management, aging management) reduce actual profitability; include operational overhead in margin analysis

### Time Estimate
- 2 days/quarter

## W1944. VMI Inventory Carrying Cost & Optimization Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Semi-annual VMI program optimization review |
| **Frequency** | Semi-annual |
| **Volume** | 12 VMI vendors; ~300 VMI SKUs |
| **Owner** | VP Supply Chain |
| **Participants** | VP Supply Chain, Category Manager, Finance Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Manager calculates VMI inventory carrying cost: (a) average VMI inventory value (at WAC) per month, (b) carrying cost rate: warehousing + insurance + obsolescence risk + cost of capital (~20–25% of average inventory value per year for Philippine retail), (c) compare VMI carrying cost to non-VMI carrying cost for similar SKUs | Finance Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1 day |
| 2 | VP Supply Chain evaluates VMI optimization: (a) inventory turns: VMI SKUs vs. non-VMI SKUs in same category, (b) stockout cost: lost sales from VMI stockouts vs. non-VMI stockouts, (c) procurement labor savings: VMI auto-replenishment reduces Buyer workload by ~X hours/month, (d) overstock cost: excess VMI inventory carrying cost above max level | VP Supply Chain | VP Merchandising | 1 day |
| 3 | Recommendations: (a) VMI SKUs with high overstock: tighten max levels or remove from VMI, (b) VMI SKUs with frequent stockouts: increase min levels or add supplier, (c) high-performing VMI vendors: expand VMI scope to additional SKUs, (d) low-performing VMI vendors: consider converting to standard procurement per W1947 | VP Supply Chain | VP Merchandising | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- VMI inventory analytics with carrying cost calculation (W1944.1)
- Integration with W35 (FP&A reporting) for carrying cost data

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Attribution difficulty**: Hard to isolate VMI program impact from overall supply chain performance; need control group (similar non-VMI SKUs) for comparison

### Time Estimate
- 3 days semi-annually

## W1946. Consignment Agreement Renewal & Terms Negotiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Consignment agreement expiration (typically annual) |
| **Frequency** | ~15–25 renewals/year |
| **Volume** | ~15–25 active consignment vendors |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Category Manager, VP Merchandising, VP Supply Chain, CFO, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager prepares renewal analysis: (a) past year performance: sell-through rate, margin, return rate, (b) compare consignment arrangement vs. direct purchase alternative, (c) vendor relationship: payment timeliness, dispute resolution, support quality, (d) market conditions: are there alternative vendors offering better consignment terms? | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days/vendor |
| 2 | Negotiation: (a) improved consignment cost (target 2–5% reduction annually), (b) expanded SKU range (add high-demand items to consignment), (c) improved terms: faster settlement, better damage responsibility allocation, (d) performance incentives: volume-based consignment cost reduction | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | Per negotiation |
| 3 | CFO approves consignment terms from financial perspective: (a) margin impact, (b) balance sheet impact (consignment stock is off-balance-sheet — vendor-owned), (c) settlement terms cash flow impact | CFO | VP Merchandising | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Consignment vendor performance analytics for renewal support (W1946.1)
- Integration with W230 (legal contract review) for agreement documentation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Renewal gap**: If agreement expires without renewal, consignment goods status becomes ambiguous; set renewal calendar alerts 60 days before expiration

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 days preparation per vendor; negotiation varies

## W1947. VMI-to-Standard Procurement Conversion Decision

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | VMI vendor consistently underperforming; strategic decision to internalize replenishment |
| **Frequency** | ~1–2 conversion decisions/year |
| **Volume** | 12 VMI vendors monitored |
| **Owner** | VP Supply Chain |
| **Participants** | VP Supply Chain, Category Manager, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Supply Chain identifies VMI vendor for conversion: (a) fill rate consistently < 93% (below 97% target for 2+ quarters), (b) stockout frequency impacting sales, (c) vendor unable or unwilling to improve, (d) internal replenishment capability available (Buyer capacity, demand planning tools) | VP Supply Chain | VP Merchandising | As needed |
| 2 | Decision analysis: (a) cost of conversion: additional Buyer labor, demand planning tool cost, (b) benefit of conversion: better fill rate, lower stockout, more control over replenishment timing, (c) risk of conversion: loss of vendor collaboration, BuildRight bears forecasting risk, (d) present business case to VP Merchandising | VP Supply Chain | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |
| 3 | If approved: (a) transition VMI SKUs to standard procurement per W2A, (b) establish ROP/EOQ parameters per W312, (c) notify vendor of VMI termination per contractual notice period, (d) monitor first 90 days closely for service level impact | VP Supply Chain / Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 2–4 weeks transition |

### System Touchpoints
- VMI-to-standard procurement parameter migration (W1947.3)
- Integration with W2A (auto-replenishment) for new parameter setup

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transition disruption**: Switching from VMI to internal replenishment may cause temporary stock level instability; plan transition during low-demand period

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 days analysis; 2–4 weeks transition execution

## W1948. Consignment & VMI Intercompany Settlement (Multi-Entity)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly IC settlement cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | Cross-entity consignment/VMI transactions across 5 legal entities |
| **Owner** | AP Manager |
| **Participants** | AP Manager, Accounting Manager, IC Settlement Team |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System identifies cross-entity consignment/VMI transactions: (a) consignment goods received by BuildRight Logistics DC but sold by BuildRight Depot store, (b) VMI stock at BuildRight Logistics DC transferred to BuildRight Depot stores, (c) ownership transfer revenue and COGS must be allocated to correct entities | System | AP Manager | Automated |
| 2 | AP Manager processes IC settlement for consignment/VMI: (a) consignment settlement: vendor payment from BuildRight Depot (selling entity), IC service fee from BuildRight Logistics (DC storage/service), (b) VMI settlement: standard PO payment from purchasing entity, IC transfer pricing for stock movements between entities, (c) post IC AP/AR per W14 | AP Manager | Accounting Manager | 1–2 days/month |
| 3 | Monthly IC reconciliation: (a) verify consignment/VMI IC balances match across entities, (b) resolve timing differences (sale in one entity, settlement in another), (c) elimination entries for consolidation per W9A | Accounting Manager | CFO | Per month-end close |

### System Touchpoints
- IC consignment/VMI transaction identification (W1948.1)
- IC settlement posting (W1948.2)
- Integration with W14 (IC settlement) and W9A (month-end close)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Entity allocation complexity**: Consignment goods received at Logistics entity, sold by Depot entity, settled by Holdings entity (if centralized); clear allocation rules needed

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 days/month

## W1949. Consignment & VMI Audit Trail & Compliance Documentation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Ongoing; annual audit preparation |
| **Frequency** | Continuous audit trail; annual documentation review |
| **Volume** | All consignment & VMI transactions |
| **Owner** | Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Accounting Manager, Internal Auditor, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System maintains continuous audit trail: (a) consignment: receipt → display → sale → ownership transfer → settlement, with timestamps, user IDs, and system references, (b) VMI: data sharing → replenishment → receipt → auto-confirmation → payment, with full traceability, (c) adjustments: damage, shrinkage, returns — all documented with reason codes and approvals | System | Accounting Manager | Automated |
| 2 | Annual: Accounting Manager prepares consignment & VMI documentation for external audit: (a) consignment inventory is vendor-owned — excluded from BuildRight's balance sheet inventory; provide vendor ownership documentation, (b) VMI inventory is BuildRight-owned — included in balance sheet at WAC; provide VMI agreement terms for audit review, (c) settlement reconciliation: consignment settlements matched to sell-through, VMI payments matched to GR, (d) provide sample of transactions for auditor testing | Accounting Manager | CFO | 2–3 days/year |
| 3 | Internal audit of consignment & VMI per W21: (a) verify consignment stock is correctly classified (not inadvertently included in owned inventory valuation), (b) verify VMI stock levels per physical count, (c) test settlement accuracy on sample basis, (d) review vendor agreements for compliance with BuildRight policies | Internal Auditor | CFO | Per annual audit plan |

### System Touchpoints
- Automated audit trail logging (W1949.1)
- Consignment/VMI compliance reports for audit (W1949.2)
- Integration with W21 (internal audit) for audit coverage

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Balance sheet misclassification**: If consignment stock is inadvertently valued (treated as owned), it inflates inventory on balance sheet; system must strictly enforce non-valuated treatment

### Time Estimate
- Continuous (automated); 2–3 days/year for audit preparation
