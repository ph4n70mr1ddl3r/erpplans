# VS-45: Consignment & Vendor-Managed Inventory Operations

> **Plan & Source** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages all consignment inventory and vendor-managed inventory (VMI) operations across BuildRight's supply chain. Consignment goods (major appliances, select tiles) are owned by the vendor until sold at POS, requiring non-valuated receipt, ownership transfer tracking, and sell-through-based settlement. VMI covers ~300 SKUs from 12 key vendors where the vendor monitors stock levels and triggers replenishment. Both models require specialized tracking, settlement, and analytics that differ from standard merchandise purchasing.
