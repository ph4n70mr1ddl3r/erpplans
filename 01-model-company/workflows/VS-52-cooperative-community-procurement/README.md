# VS-52: Cooperative & Community Enterprise Procurement

> **Plan & Source** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages BuildRight's program to develop and procure from Philippine MSMEs (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises), local cooperatives, artisan communities, and social enterprises. Aligned with BuildRight's ESG commitments (GOV-008), the DTI MSME development mandate, and BuildRight's private label sourcing strategy (VS-41). Covers local vendor identification and development, community sourcing for artisan and heritage products, cooperative capacity building, and social impact measurement. Targets: 15–20% of SKU sourcing from MSME/local vendors within 5 years.
