## W5121. Store Operations Support Center (Store Hotline) Setup, Staffing & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W5105); growth; support-demand analysis |
| **Frequency** | At setup + periodic review |
| **Volume** | ~1 support center serving 200 stores + 4 DCs |
| **Owner** | Director Field Retail Operations / Store Ops (VS-07) |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), HR (VS-103), IT (VS-27), Finance (VS-17.4), functional SMEs (Merch/HR/Facilities/LP) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design the support-center operating model: scope (operational issues, policy clarification, exception requests, cross-functional coordination — NOT IT-service-desk VS-27 nor HR-shared-services VS-103), channels (phone/hotline, chat, ticketing), hours, SLAs, and the tier structure | Director Field Retail Operations / Store Ops | Director Field Retail Operations / VP Store Operations | At setup + review |
| 2 | Staff & equip: staffing model from demand forecast, the SME/tier structure, the ticketing/CRM tool (distinct from VS-27 ITSM and VS-103 HR case), and training | Director Field Retail Operations / HR / IT | Director Field Retail Operations | At setup |
| 3 | Govern & launch: leadership sign-off (VS-36), the SLA/KPI baseline, the integration with field visits (W5114) and escalation (W5122), and go-live | Director Field Retail Operations / VP Store Operations | VP Store Operations | At setup |

### System Touchpoints
- Ticketing/case tool (new — distinct from VS-27 ITSM & VS-103 HR); store ops (VS-07); field dashboard (W5127); analytics (VS-28); governance (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scope-creep risk**: Becoming a duplicate IT/HR helpdesk; mitigated by clear scope boundaries & routing rules
- **Under-staffing risk**: Demand exceeds capacity → slow resolution; mitigated by demand-based staffing
- **Tool-proliferation risk**: Yet another system stores must use; mitigated by integration with existing channels (VS-63)

### Automation Opportunity
- Demand-based staffing model; integrated ticketing; channel consolidation

### Controls
- CTL-763 (ensure controlled execution — Store Support Center, Escalation & Field Operations Analytics (PA-177.3).)
- CTL-763: scope boundaries documented (not IT VS-27 / not HR VS-103); SLAs defined; leadership-approved (VS-36)

### Time Estimate
- ~6–10 weeks setup + ongoing

### Cross-references

## W5122. Store Issue Intake, Triage, Resolution & Escalation Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store contact (hotline/chat/ticket); field escalation (W5118); system alert |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~hundreds–thousands of issues/month across 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Store Support Center Lead / Director Field Retail Operations |
| **Participants** | Store Managers (VS-07), Regional/District Managers, functional leads (Merch/IT/HR/Facilities/LP), CX (VS-13) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake & triage: log the issue (channel, category, store, severity), classify, route to the right tier/function (Merch/IT/HR/Facilities/LP), and set the SLA | Support Center Agent | Support Center Lead | Per issue (minutes) |
| 2 | Resolve or escalate: tier-1 resolution (knowledge base W5126, policy clarification, routine exception), or escalate to tier-2 functional SME / Regional Manager / Director per the escalation matrix | Support Center Agent / Functional SME / Regional Manager | Support Center Lead | Per issue |
| 3 | Close & verify: confirm resolution with the store, capture the root-cause/lesson, feed recurring issues to CI (VS-133) and the knowledge base (W5126), and the SLA/quality reporting (W5120/W5127) | Support Center Lead / Analytics | Director Field Retail Operations | Per issue |

### System Touchpoints
- Ticketing/case tool (W5121); knowledge base (W5126); functional systems (Merch VS-01 / IT VS-27 / HR VS-103 / Facilities VS-138 / LP VS-23); analytics (VS-28); CI (VS-133)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-routing risk**: Issue routed to wrong function → delay; mitigated by triage classification & routing rules
- **SLA-breach risk**: Slow resolution → store downtime/CX impact; mitigated by SLA monitoring + escalation
- **Recurrence risk**: Same issue repeated → no root-cause fix; mitigated by step-3 root-cause + CI (VS-133)

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-classification/routing; SLA-monitoring; recurring-issue detection; knowledge-base suggestion

### Controls
- CTL-763 (ensure controlled execution — Store Support Center, Escalation & Field Operations Analytics (PA-177.3).)
- CTL-763: every issue logged & triaged; SLA monitored; root-cause captured for recurring issues (VS-133)

### Time Estimate
- ~minutes triage + variable resolution per issue

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27 (IT), VS-103 (HR), VS-138 (facilities), VS-23 (LP), VS-133 (CI); W5121 (center), W5126 (knowledge), W5127 (analytics)

## W5123. Exception & Override Authorization Handling (Store→HQ Approval Workflow)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store/field requests an exception to policy/standard (price override beyond limit, return-policy exception, markdown authorization, policy waiver, special-order approval) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~hundreds–thousands of exception requests/month |
| **Owner** | Store Support Center Lead / Director Field Retail Operations |
| **Participants** | Store Managers (VS-07), Regional/District Managers, Pricing (VS-01.2/VS-118), Finance (VS-17.4), Compliance (VS-22/VS-118), functional approvers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake the exception request: the request type, business justification, the policy/standard being excepted, the $/margin/precedent impact, and the requesting store/manager | Support Center Agent / Store Manager | Support Center Lead | Per request |
| 2 | Evaluate & authorize/deny: route to the approver per the exception-authorization matrix (dollar threshold, type, risk), the integrity/SoD check (no self-approval), and the compliance review where needed (VS-22/VS-118) | Approver / Compliance / Director Field Retail Operations | Per the matrix | Per request |
| 3 | Execute, log & monitor: execute the approved exception (system override), log for audit (VS-21), monitor exception-volume/spend analytics for abuse or policy-misfit, and feed policy review (VS-133) | Support Center / Finance / Analytics | Director Field Retail Operations | Per request + analytics |

### System Touchpoints
- Approval/workflow engine (links VS-15/VS-17 approval matrix); POS override (VS-08); pricing (VS-01.2/VS-118); audit log (VS-21); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Abuse risk**: Exceptions used to bypass controls (e.g. fake price overrides → shrink/leakage); mitigated by authorization matrix + SoD + analytics
- **Delay risk**: Slow exception approval → lost sale/customer; mitigated by SLA + tier structure
- **Policy-misfit risk**: High exception volume signals a broken policy; mitigated by exception-volume analytics → policy review (VS-133)

### Automation Opportunity
- Exception-request workflow with auto-routing; SoD check; exception-volume/pattern analytics

### Controls
- CTL-763 (ensure controlled execution — Store Support Center, Escalation & Field Operations Analytics (PA-177.3).)
- CTL-763: authorization matrix enforced; no self-approval (SoD); exceptions logged for audit (VS-21); volume monitored

### Time Estimate
- ~minutes-to-hours per request

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08 (POS override), VS-118 (price integrity), VS-21 (audit), VS-22 (compliance), VS-133 (CI/policy review); W5121 (center), W5127 (analytics)

| **Participants** | Store staff (VS-19), Store Managers (VS-07), HR/HRBP (VS-103), Labor Relations (VS-84), Ethics (VS-119), Regional Managers |

## W5125. Cross-Functional Store-Issue Coordination (Merch/IT/HR/Facilities/LP)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Multi-function store issue (e.g. product defect + recall, system outage + manual workaround, facility failure + safety, shrink ring + LP) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~10–15% of issues requiring multi-function coordination |
| **Owner** | Store Support Center Lead / Director Field Retail Operations |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01), IT (VS-27), HR (VS-103), Facilities (VS-138), LP (VS-23), Quality (VS-31), Recall (VS-89), functional leads |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Convene the cross-functional case: identify the functions involved, open a coordinated case (single ticket linking sub-tasks per function), and assign a single accountable coordinator | Support Center Lead / Director Field Retail Operations | Director Field Retail Operations | Per case |
| 2 | Coordinate execution: each function executes its piece (Merch recall/return VS-89, IT fix VS-27, HR staffing VS-103, Facilities repair VS-138, LP investigation VS-23), with the coordinator tracking the integrated resolution | Functional Leads / Coordinator | Support Center Lead | Per case |
| 3 | Close, debrief & systemize: confirm integrated resolution to the store, debrief the cross-functional case for systemic-fix (VS-133), and feed the coordination-effectiveness analytics (W5127) | Coordinator / Analytics | Director Field Retail Operations | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Coordinated case/multi-sub-ticket tool; functional systems (VS-01/VS-27/VS-103/VS-138/VS-23/VS-89); CI (VS-133); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fragmentation risk**: Each function works its piece in silos → no integrated resolution; mitigated by single coordinator + linked sub-tickets
- **Dropped-handoff risk**: A function drops its piece → incomplete fix; mitigated by coordinator tracking
- **Slow-coordination risk**: Convening functions is slow → store impact; mitigated by defined coordination protocol + SLA

### Automation Opportunity
- Multi-function case with linked sub-tasks; coordinator dashboard; handoff SLA tracking

### Controls
- CTL-763 (ensure controlled execution — Store Support Center, Escalation & Field Operations Analytics (PA-177.3).)
- CTL-763: single accountable coordinator; linked sub-tasks per function; integrated-resolution confirmation

### Time Estimate
- ~variable per case

### Cross-references
- links to VS-89 (recall), VS-27 (IT), VS-138 (facilities), VS-23 (LP), VS-31 (quality); W5122 (escalation), W5127 (analytics)
