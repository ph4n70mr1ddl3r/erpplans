# VS-177: Field Retail Operations, Regional/District Management & Multi-Store Retail Execution Network

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Field retail operations, regional/district management and the multi-store retail-execution network
for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **field-management discipline that oversees the 200-store
chain** between corporate strategy (VS-33) and single-store daily execution (VS-07): field
organization & operating-model design; regional/district territory design and store-group
assignment; field staffing, span-of-control and regional-manager workforce planning; field leader
onboarding, certification and capability building; multi-store P&L governance, store grading and
performance tiering; new-store stabilization and maturity-ramp field support (links VS-37);
underperforming/distressed-store diagnosis and turnaround; field compensation, incentive and
multi-store recognition; regional/district store-visit cadence and routing; store-visit execution
and retail-standards audit/scoring; store-manager coaching and development conversations; retail-
execution field audit (planogram/price/signage/merchandising standards — links VS-55/VS-118/VS-151);
competitor and market field intelligence (links VS-57); field action-item tracking and CAP closure;
field communication, regional meetings and best-practice sharing; the **store operations support
center (store hotline)** for store→HQ issue intake/triage/resolution/escalation; exception and
override authorization handling; store grievance/concern/feedback resolution; cross-functional
store-issue coordination (Merch/IT/HR/Facilities/LP); store knowledge base and field self-service
enablement; field and store-operations performance dashboard with comp-store analytics; and field
retail operations continuous improvement, benchmarking and program review.

This matters at BuildRight's scale. The model company runs **200 stores across six Philippine
regions** (Mindanao 60, Visayas 40, Luzon-outside-NCR 50, Metro Manila 30, North/Central Luzon 20)
staffed by ~5,800 store personnel, growing 10–15 stores/year, with a documented HQ "Store
Operations" department of ~24 regional managers / operations-standards / facilities-coordination
staff and 20 Regional Loss-Prevention officers (model-company-profile §3.3, §4). At that footprint
the field layer — regional/district managers each overseeing ~40–50 stores, running a monthly store-
visit cadence, coaching store managers, enforcing retail standards, diagnosing underperformers, and
stabilizing new stores — is the single controllable lever on comp-sales growth, format relevance,
standardized customer experience, and the consistent execution of every program HQ launches
(merchandising resets, promotions, new-product rollouts, remodels, compliance mandates). The
**Regional Manager** role already appears as the accountable/informed party across **~66 PA files**
in the existing workflow inventory (e.g. VS-07 store daily management, delay incident escalation,
delivery performance review; VS-37 store opening; VS-109 remodel; VS-146 mystery shopping
remediation) — yet the Regional Manager's *own* operating discipline (territory design, visit
cadence, field coaching, multi-store P&L, turnaround) had **no dedicated owner**. Likewise the
**store operations support center** (the HQ function 200 stores call/escalate to for operational
issues, policy clarification, exception approval, and cross-functional coordination) — distinct
from the IT service desk (VS-27 W48, IT-specific), HR shared services (VS-103, HR-specific), and
HQ→store broadcast/task management (VS-63, the *reverse* direction) — had no dedicated owner.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a unified program** — it exists only as scattered single
workflows ripe for elevation (the Pass-1/5/7/8/10/11/22/23 single-workflow-elevation pattern) and
scattered slices with no single owner (the VS-161 TPRM / VS-166 license-portfolio / VS-171 will-call
consolidation pattern): **W1030** (Multi-Store District Manager Weekly Operations Review & Compliance
Audit) buried in **VS-07.1 Store Daily Management**; **W2369** (Regional Operations Follow-Up) in
**VS-63.2**; **W3836** (Sales Coaching, Field Enablement & Store-Level Reinforcement) in **VS-124**;
**W1214** (Store Performance Scorecard Weekly Review & Regional Benchmarking); plus the Regional
Manager role embedded as a participant across ~66 PA files with no owning value stream for the field-
management work itself. The defining terms "field retail", "field operations", "field execution",
"retail execution", "multi-store management", "store operations support", "store support center",
"store hotline", "store escalation", "store operations center", "district manager", "field coach",
"store turnaround", and "new-store stabilization" each appear in **zero** PA files as dedicated
workflow headers. No value stream owns the end-to-end **field-management operating model**. It is
**not** covered by **VS-07 (Store Operations)**, which owns *single-store* daily management (PA-07.1
60 workflows) — this owns the *multi-store field layer above the store*. It is **not** covered by
**VS-33 (Strategic Planning)**, which owns corporate strategy and portfolio KPIs — this *executes*
strategy store-by-store in the field. It is **not** covered by **VS-63 (Store Communication & Task
Management)**, which owns the HQ→store broadcast and task-assignment *direction* — this also owns
the reverse store→HQ *support/escalation* direction and the field-visit/coaching discipline. It is
**not** covered by **VS-146 (Mystery Shopping)**, which is *independent assurance* — this is the
*first-line field-management* discipline that acts on those findings.

This value stream owns the end-to-end program: field strategy & operating model; territory design;
field staffing & workforce planning; field-leader onboarding & certification; multi-store P&L &
store grading; new-store stabilization (links VS-37); distressed-store turnaround; field comp &
incentive; visit cadence & routing; visit execution & standards audit; store-manager coaching;
retail-execution field audit; competitor/market field intelligence; field action-item tracking;
field communication & best-practice sharing; store support center (hotline); issue intake/triage/
resolution/escalation; exception/override authorization; store grievance resolution; cross-
functional store-issue coordination; store knowledge base & self-service; field/store performance
dashboard & comp-store analytics; and field-ops continuous improvement. It integrates tightly with
VS-07 (single-store execution), VS-37 (new-store opening), VS-109 (remodel), VS-33 (strategy), VS-63
(store communication/task), VS-146 (mystery shopping/assurance), VS-57 (competitive intelligence),
VS-13 (CX), VS-23 (LP), VS-27 (IT service desk), VS-103 (HR shared services), VS-134 (OCM), and
VS-28 (analytics).
