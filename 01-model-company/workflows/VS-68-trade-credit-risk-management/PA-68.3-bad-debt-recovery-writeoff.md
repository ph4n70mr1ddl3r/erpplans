## W2496. Bad Debt Write-Off Decision

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Account determined uncollectible after all recovery efforts |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 write-offs/year |
| **Volume** | ~10–15/year; ~PHP 5–15M total write-offs/year |
| **Owner** | Finance Director |
| **Participants** | Finance Dir, Credit Analyst, Legal, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Credit Analyst presents write-off recommendation: collection history, legal status, customer financial situation, recovery probability; Finance Director reviews; CFO approves; Board approves >PHP 1M per account | Credit Analyst / Finance Director | CFO | 2–3 days per write-off |
