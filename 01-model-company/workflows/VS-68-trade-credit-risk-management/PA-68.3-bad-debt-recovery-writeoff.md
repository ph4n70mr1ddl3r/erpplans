## W2494. Pre-Legal Collections & Negotiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Account reaches 90+ days past due |
| **Frequency** | ~50–80 accounts in pre-legal at any time |
| **Volume** | ~50–80 accounts |
| **Owner** | Collections Manager |
| **Participants** | Collections Mgr, Credit Analyst, Legal, VP Sales |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collections Manager conducts intensive collection: phone, email, in-person visit for high-value accounts; negotiates payment plan (structured payments over 3–6 months); documents all collection activity | Collections Manager | Finance Director | 4–6 hours/account |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 per occurrence
- Volume: ~50–80 accounts
- Frequency: ~50–80 accounts in pre-legal at any time
- Absorbed into the Collections Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
