## W2686. LGU Official Relationship & Engagement Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New LGU administration or periodic engagement |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly; election cycle renewal |
| **Volume** | 209 locations across ~50 LGUs |
| **Owner** | VP Legal |
| **Participants** | VP Legal, Store Managers, Regional Managers, Government Relations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Legal maintains LGU relationship strategy: key officials per location (mayor, councilors, BPLO head, fire marshal, barangay captains), tracks 3-year election cycles, plans engagement | VP Legal / Government Relations | CEO | Ongoing |
| 2 | Store Managers maintain day-to-day relationships: visits to barangay hall, event sponsorship, local employment priority, community support; Regional Managers handle city-level | Store Managers / Regional Managers | VP Legal | Ongoing |
| 3 | VP Legal conducts post-election engagement: meets new officials, introduces BuildRight, renews relationships; obtains updated contact directory every 3 years | VP Legal | CEO | Every 3 years |
