## W2678. Local Business Tax Computation & Payment Across LGUs

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly payment cycle |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly (or per LGU schedule) |
| **Volume** | 209 locations across ~50 LGUs |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | Tax Accountant, Finance Analyst, Store Managers, LGU Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Accountant compiles liability per location: gross receipts per store per LGU, applicable rate per ordinance (0.5–2%), tax base adjustments, quarterly installment calculation | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 3–5 days per quarter |
| 2 | Accountant prepares returns per LGU format; Store Manager submits to LGU Treasury; pays via cashier or bank; obtains official receipt | Tax Accountant / Store Manager | Tax Manager | 5–10 days per quarter |
| 3 | Finance reconciles: actual vs. budgeted, variance by LGU, identifies rate increases or new fees; Tax Manager incorporates into quarterly forecast | Finance / Tax Manager | CFO | Per quarter |
