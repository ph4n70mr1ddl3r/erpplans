## W2670. Multi-Location Business Permit Renewal Calendar

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual renewal season (January) |
| **Frequency** | Annual; 209 locations across ~50 LGUs |
| **Volume** | 209 locations |
| **Owner** | Legal Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Legal Compliance Officer, Store Managers, DC Ops, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal Compliance Officer maintains master permit calendar: renewal deadlines per LGU (typically Jan 20), fire safety cert, BFP occupancy, zoning clearance, barangay clearance, DENR clearance | Legal Compliance Officer | VP Legal | Ongoing |
| 2 | Officer initiates renewal 60 days before deadline: prepares LGU-specific requirements (SEC, mayor's permit, barangay clearance, fire cert, sanitary permit); coordinates with Store Managers | Legal Compliance Officer / Store Managers | VP Legal | 60 days before |
| 3 | Store Manager submits to LGU BPLO: pays local business tax (0.5–2% of gross receipts per LGU), obtains permit; uploads to ERP; Officer confirms all 209 locations renewed | Store Manager / Legal Compliance Officer | VP Legal | January each year |
