## W4873. Employee Pre-Hire Screening Integration & Offer-Conditional Workflow

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Candidate selection (VS-121); offer-conditional on screening clearance |
| **Frequency** | Per hire (~1,200–1,600 hires/yr) |
| **Volume** | ~1,200–1,600 employee screens/yr |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Talent Acquisition (VS-121) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19), Hiring Manager, Screening vendor (W4867), Legal (VS-100.3), Adverse-Action Board (W4871) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On candidate selection, trigger the screening order from the ATS/HRIS (VS-121): capture consent (W4868), determine the role-package (W4866/W4869), and order the package from the screening vendor; structure the offer as conditional-on-screening-clearance for sensitive roles | Talent Acquisition / Screening Program Manager | Hiring Manager / HR | Per hire |
| 2 | Execute & verify: vendor runs the package (NBI/employment/etc.), Screening verifies authenticity and the chain-of-custody (W4870); track TAT against the SLA; for clear results, clear for hire; for adverse findings, route to the Adverse-Action Board (W4871/W4880) | Screening Program Manager / Vendor | Screening Program Manager | Per hire |
| 3 | Record in the HRIS/worker record (VS-19/VS-29), file the evidence (W4872), set the re-screening schedule (W4877), and feed screening metrics to analytics (W4881); for adverse outcomes, manage the candidate per the fair-chance framework (W4880) | Screening Program Manager / HR / Records | Screening Program Manager | Per hire |

### System Touchpoints
- ATS/HRIS (VS-121/VS-19); screening platform/vendor (W4867); worker record (VS-29); records (VS-88); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hire-before-clear risk**: Onboarding before screening clears (esp. sensitive roles) → negligent-hiring exposure; mitigated by offer-conditional for sensitive roles
- **Delay risk**: Slow screening losing candidates (esp. in a tight labor market); mitigated by SLA + parallel where lawful
- **Consent-gap risk**: Screening without consent → unlawful processing (W4868); mitigated by consent-before-order

### Time Estimate
- ~per screen days (1–10, package-dependent)

### Automation Opportunity
- ATS → screening-order auto-trigger; consent e-signature; vendor-result ingestion; clear/flag workflow

### Controls
- CTL-732 (ensure controlled execution — Vetting Operations Across Workforce Categories (PA-167.2).)
- CTL: consent before order; sensitive roles offer-conditional; chain-of-custody; recorded in HRIS

### Cross-references
- links to VS-121 (recruiting), VS-19 (HR), VS-29 (worker record), W4866/W4868/W4869 (policy/consent/package), W4870 (evidence), W4877 (re-screening), W4880 (adverse-action)

## W4874. Contingent/Outsourced Worker Screening Governance & Agency Accountability

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contractor/outsourced-worker assignment (before site entry) (VS-98); agency onboarding |
| **Frequency** | Per worker assignment (~10–20% of store/DC labor) |
| **Volume** | ~600–1,200 contingent workers across 200 stores + 4 DCs (security, janitorial, promodizers, agency labor; ~10–20% of site labor per VS-98) |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Contingent Workforce Coordinator (VS-98) |
| **Participants** | Agencies (VS-98), Security (VS-159), Legal (VS-100.3), LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Govern the agency screening obligation (extends W3223): each agency certifies pre-engagement screening per BuildRight's role-package (cash-handling, security, child-adjacent critical), with BuildRight-specified checks (NBI, police, prior-employment, drug where lawful, court for cash/security); flow the obligation into the agency contract (VS-98) | Screening Program Manager / Contingent Coordinator / Legal / Procurement | VP Legal & Compliance / Contingent Workforce Mgr | Per agency + continuous |
| 2 | Verify agency screening quality: BuildRight audits agency screening records, verifies authenticity (NBI verification, sample re-check), and rejects uncertified workers; for high-risk roles (security, CIT VS-81, cash-handling, IT-admin) require enhanced screening + Security review (links to W3223/W4876) | Screening Program Manager / Security / Contingent Coordinator | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Continuous + per worker |
| 3 | Manage re-screening (W4877), adverse-action (W4880), and records (W4872) for contingent workers; ensure agency accountability for screening failures and feed screening-compliance to TPRM (VS-161), VS-98 governance, and analytics (W4881) | Screening Program Manager / Contingent Coordinator / TPRM | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Contingent workforce (VS-98); worker record (VS-29); agency/vendor master (VS-03); TPRM (VS-161); security (VS-159); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Agency-shirking risk**: Agencies certifying without actually screening; mitigated by BuildRight audits + authenticity verification
- **Insider-threat risk**: Unscreened contractor committing theft/fraud (W3229); mitigated by mandatory/enhanced screening
- **Accountability risk**: Screening failures unattributed; mitigated by agency-contract accountability

### Time Estimate
- ~per worker screening 1–5 days; agency governance continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Agency-screening-record upload/audit; authenticity-verification (NBI portal); worker-clear badge

### Controls
- CTL-131 (ensure lawful & consented personnel vetting.)
- CTL: agency contract specifies screening; BuildRight audits; high-risk roles enhanced; failures attributed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-98 (contingent, W3223), VS-161 (TPRM), VS-159 (security), VS-23 (LP), W4869 (package), W4877 (re-screening), W4880 (adverse-action)

## W4875. Vendor & 3PL Personnel Site-Access Vetting (Drivers, Delivery, Service)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor/3PL personnel granted site access (delivery drivers VS-56/VS-110, service technicians, installers VS-12); partner onboarding |
| **Frequency** | Per personnel + periodic |
| **Volume** | ~vendor/3PL personnel across 200 stores + 4 DCs |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Procurement (VS-03/VS-34) / Store/DC Ops |
| **Participants** | Vendors/3PLs (VS-56/VS-110), Security (VS-159), Legal (VS-100.3), LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the site-access vetting standard for vendor/3PL personnel: identity verification, driver-license check (links to W1400/W4754 driver-license verification), NBI/basic screening for recurring-site-access roles (delivery, service, installation), and the access-control integration (badging, escorts) | Screening Program Manager / Security / Procurement | VP Legal & Compliance / Chief Compliance & Security Officer | Continuous |
| 2 | Flow the standard into vendor/3PL contracts (VS-56/VS-110/VS-100.3): the partner certifies its personnel are vetted per the standard, BuildRight verifies (sample/license-authenticity), and access is granted only to cleared personnel; manage exceptions (one-time vs recurring access) | Procurement / Legal / Screening Program Manager / Security | VP Legal & Compliance | Per partner + continuous |
| 3 | Monitor & enforce: track cleared-personnel rosters, revoke access on personnel change, audit partner compliance, and feed vendor-screening compliance to TPRM (VS-161) and analytics (W4881); manage any incident involving vendor personnel (links to VS-23/VS-159) | Screening Program Manager / Security / TPRM / Store Ops | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor master (VS-03/VS-29); 3PL (VS-56/VS-110); access control/security (VS-159); driver-license (W1400/W4754); TPRM (VS-161); LP (VS-23)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unvetted-access risk**: A vendor/3PL person accessing sites unvetted → theft/safety incident; mitigated by the standard + access-control integration
- **Stale-roster risk**: Personnel changed but access not revoked; mitigated by roster tracking
- **Partner-non-compliance risk**: Partner not certifying/verifying; mitigated by contract + audits

### Time Estimate
- ~per personnel vetting hours–days; partner governance continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Cleared-personnel roster; access-control auto-grant/revoke; driver-license auto-verification (W1400/W4754); partner-compliance dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-732 (ensure controlled execution — Vetting Operations Across Workforce Categories (PA-167.2).)
- CTL: site-access vetting standard; partner contract flow-down; access only for cleared personnel; rosters current

### Cross-references
- links to VS-56/VS-110 (3PL), VS-159 (security/access), VS-23 (LP), VS-161 (TPRM), W1400/W4754 (driver-license), W4881 (analytics)

| **Frequency** | Per appointment + periodic re-vet |
| **Volume** | ~executives + sensitive roles |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Corporate Security (VS-159) / Legal (VS-100.3) |
| **Participants** | CHRO, Board/ nominating (VS-36), External counsel/vetting firm, DPO (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the enhanced-vetting package for executives/sensitive roles: full NBI/court, multi-source media/reputational, sanctions/PEP/litigation (links to VS-86/VS-100), conflicts-of-interest (links to VS-119/W426), credit-for-finance, reference interviews, education/employment verification, and (for security/CIT) enhanced criminal; lawful under RA 10173 with senior consent | Screening Program Manager / Corporate Security / Legal / DPO | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance / Board | Per role-type + continuous |
| 2 | Execute via specialized vetting firm/external counsel (where appropriate): conduct the enhanced package for board-appointed executives and sensitive roles, with strict confidentiality (VS-159), and route findings to the Adverse-Action Board (W4871) and nominating/governance committee (VS-36) | Corporate Security / External Vetting Firm / Legal | CHRO / Board Nominating | Per appointment |
| 3 | Periodically re-vet (W4877) sensitive roles, manage adverse findings with due process (W4880), and ensure confidentiality/privacy of sensitive screening data (VS-91/W4872); feed executive-vetting outcomes to governance (VS-36) | Screening Program Manager / Corporate Security / Legal | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Corporate security (VS-159); legal (VS-100.3); AML/sanctions (VS-86); ethics/COI (VS-119/W426); board governance (VS-36); privacy (VS-91)

- **Senior-negligent-appointment risk**: An unfit executive → fiduciary/reputational harm; mitigated by enhanced vetting
- **Confidentiality risk**: Executive screening data leaking → harm/liability; mitigated by strict confidentiality (VS-159/VS-91)
- **Privacy risk**: Over-intrusive enhanced vetting → RA 10173 exposure; mitigated by lawful basis + senior consent + proportionality

### Time Estimate
- ~per executive vetting weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- Enhanced-vetting checklist; multi-source monitoring; sanctions/PEP/litigation screening (VS-86); confidentiality workflow

### Controls
- CTL-732 (ensure controlled execution — Vetting Operations Across Workforce Categories (PA-167.2).)
- CTL: enhanced package per sensitive role; lawful basis + consent; confidential; findings to governance

### Cross-references

## W4877. Periodic Re-Screening, Continuous Monitoring & Triggered Re-Vet

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Re-screening cycle (annual/biennial per role); triggering event (arrest/conviction, sanctions listing, fraud/LP event, role change); continuous-monitoring alert |
| **Frequency** | Periodic + event-triggered |
| **Volume** | All screened workforce (periodic) + triggered events |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Security (VS-159) / HR |
| **Participants** | Contingent Coordinator (VS-98), Legal (VS-100.3), LP (VS-23), Labor Relations (VS-84) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the re-screening schedule (role-tier-based periodicity) and the continuous-monitoring feed (sanctions/PEP watchlist links to VS-86, criminal/court monitoring where lawful, media/reputational for sensitive roles); reconcile to the workforce roster (VS-29) | Screening Program Manager / Security / HR | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 2 | Manage triggered re-vet: on a triggering event (an arrest/conviction, a sanctions listing, an LP/fraud event VS-23/VS-125, a role change to higher-risk, a complaint), re-screen the individual with consent (W4868) and route findings to the Adverse-Action Board (W4871/W4880) | Screening Program Manager / Security / Legal | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |
| 3 | Decide & act: clear (no action), enhanced-monitor, restrict duties, or separate (W4880) per the individualized assessment and due process; document in the chain-of-custody (W4870) and feed re-screening outcomes to insider-risk (VS-23/VS-159) and analytics (W4881) | Screening Program Manager / HR / Security / Labor Relations | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Screening platform; workforce roster (VS-29); sanctions/AML (VS-86); LP/fraud (VS-23/VS-125); security/insider-risk (VS-159); labor (VS-84)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stale-screening risk**: A worker screened years ago now flagged (arrest/sanctions) undetected; mitigated by re-screening + continuous monitoring
- **Trigger-miss risk**: A triggering event (LP complaint, arrest) not triggering re-vet; mitigated by event-linkage from VS-23/VS-84
- **Due-process risk**: Acting on a triggering event without due process → NLRC exposure; mitigated by the individualized assessment (W4880)

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous monitoring; per triggered re-vet days

### Automation Opportunity
- Continuous-monitoring feed (sanctions/criminal/media); event → re-vet trigger; re-screening scheduler

### Controls
- CTL-131 (ensure lawful & consented personnel vetting.)
- CTL: periodic re-screening by role-tier; continuous monitoring on; triggers acted on with due process

### Cross-references
- links to VS-86 (sanctions/PEP), VS-23/VS-125 (LP/fraud), VS-159 (insider-risk), VS-84 (labor), W4868 (consent), W4880 (adverse-action)

### Steps

| 2 | Handle transfers: when an employee transfers across entities or regions, verify prior screening meets the new role/entity standard, re-screen gaps, port the screening record (chain-of-custody W4870) with consent (W4868), and update the workforce roster (VS-29) | HR Shared Services / Screening Program Manager | CHRO | Per transfer |

## W4880. Adverse-Action Decisioning, Disqualification Handling & NLRC/Due-Process

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Adverse screening finding (employee/contingent/vendor/executive); dispute/appeal; NLRC complaint |
| **Frequency** | Per adverse finding |
| **Volume** | ~adverse findings (subset of screens) |
| **Owner** | Adverse-Action Review Board (W4871) / Legal (VS-100.3) / Labor Relations (VS-84) |
| **Participants** | HR, Screening Program Manager, Security (VS-159), DPO (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On an adverse finding, conduct the individualized assessment (W4866 — nature/gravity/time-since/relevance-to-role/rehabilitation), issue the pre-adverse-action notice, allow the individual to respond/dispute/evidence rehabilitation, and verify the finding's accuracy (W4883 false-document risk) | Review Board / Legal / Screening Program Manager | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Per finding |
| 2 | Decide: clear (no action), conditional (enhanced monitoring/restrictions), or disqualify (withdraw offer / restrict duties / separate); document the decision and notice with due-process alignment (VS-84 NLRC); for separations, coordinate with VS-19 separation and VS-84 labor relations | Review Board / HR / Labor Relations / Legal | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Per finding |
| 3 | Manage appeals & NLRC complaints (VS-84): the appeal path, evidence preservation (W4872), and labor-case defense; track adverse-action outcomes and bias indicators (W4871) to governance (W4888) and analytics (W4881) | Legal / Labor Relations / Review Board | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Per appeal |

### System Touchpoints
- Screening/HRIS platform; legal (VS-100.3); labor relations (VS-84); privacy (VS-91); records (VS-88); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Wrongful-decision risk**: A wrongful disqualification → NLRC/human-rights claim + lost talent; mitigated by individualized assessment + due process
- **Bias/disparate-impact risk**: Adverse actions disparately affecting a protected group; mitigated by bias-indicator monitoring
- **Inconsistent-decision risk**: Similar findings decided differently; mitigated by the Review Board + framework

### Time Estimate
- ~per finding days; appeals/complaints weeks–months

### Automation Opportunity
- Individualized-assessment decision tool; pre-adverse-action notice automation; bias-indicator dashboard; appeal tracker

### Controls
- CTL-732 (ensure controlled execution — Vetting Operations Across Workforce Categories (PA-167.2).)
- CTL: individualized assessment; pre-adverse-action notice; due process; bias monitored
