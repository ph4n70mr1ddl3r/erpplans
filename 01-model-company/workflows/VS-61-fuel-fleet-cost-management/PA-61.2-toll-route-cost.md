## W2318. Toll Expense Management & Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; expressway routes |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 1–2M/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Fleet Supervisor, Logistics Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tracks toll per route vs. free alternatives: time saved vs. toll cost; recommends optimal: toll for time-sensitive, free for non-urgent/backhaul | Logistics Finance Analyst / Fleet Supervisor | Logistics Manager | 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Route/toll analytics (links to VS-06 / W196 route planning, VS-28 / W113) — the toll-vs-free comparison
- Toll/RFID (links to VS-61 / W2319 RFID account) — the toll-spend data
- Delivery scheduling (links to VS-06 / W196) — the time-sensitive-vs-non-urgent prioritization

### Pain Points / Risks
- **False-economy risk**: routing via free roads to save toll burns more fuel and time than the toll saves; mitigated by the time-vs-toll-vs-fuel composite (not toll alone)
- **Toll-spend-attribution risk**: toll spend isn't attributed per-route, hiding the high-toll routes; mitigated by the RFID-derived per-route attribution

### Automation Opportunity
- Toll-vs-free auto-compared per route; optimal-routing auto-suggested per delivery priority

### Controls
- CTL-413 (ensure controlled execution — Toll, Parking & Route Cost Management (PA-61.2).)
- Operational: Logistics Manager monthly sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month = **~60 hours/year** Logistics Finance Analyst.

### System Touchpoints

- TCO (links to VS-61 / W2325, W2326 annual TCO) — the maintenance cost feeds TCO
- Asset replacement (links to VS-35 / W39, VS-61 / W2327 replacement analysis) — the replace-vs-maintain decision

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Deferred-maintenance risk**: scheduled maintenance is deferred to save cost, causing a larger unscheduled failure later; mitigated by the PM-compliance tracking and the lifecycle-cost view (deferred PM is false economy)
- **Accident-repair-cost-distortion risk**: a single accident inflates a vehicle's annual maintenance cost, masking the underlying trend; mitigated by the accident-cost separated from routine maintenance

### Automation Opportunity
- Per-vehicle maintenance cost auto-tracked; replace-vs-maintain auto-suggested at the threshold

### Controls
- CTL-26 (asset-grade maintenance tracking); operational: VP Supply Chain replacement-decision sign-off
