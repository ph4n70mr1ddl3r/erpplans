## Overview

Sari-Sari Store & MSME Micro-Wholesale Program workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — covering
the dedicated channel serving the Philippines' ~1.3M sari-sari stores (neighborhood
micro-retailers), small contractors, and MSME resellers with right-sized assortments,
micro-distribution, credit, and digital enablement.

While Trade, Project & Wholesale (VS-11) serves formal resellers and large contractors, this
value stream serves the **micro-enterprise segment** with distinct economics: smaller basket
(PHP 2K–15K), higher frequency (weekly), cash/e-wallet payment, last-mile delivery to
micro-stores, MSME financing, and a "BuildRight Negosyo Partner" enablement program.
