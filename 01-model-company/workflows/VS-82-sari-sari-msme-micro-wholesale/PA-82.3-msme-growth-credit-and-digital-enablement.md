## W2841. MSME Micro-Credit & "Negosyo Loan" Origination (Partner Bank/SBCorp)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | MSME requests financing for inventory/capex |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; growing demand |
| **Volume** | ~5,000–10,000 financed MSMEs at maturity |
| **Owner** | MSME Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | MSME Sales, Partner Bank, Treasury, Compliance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MSME Sales refers qualified MSME to partner bank (or SBCorp/DTI microfinance program) for "Negosyo Loan"; provides purchase history as credit evidence | MSME Sales | MSME Sales Manager | Per referral |
| 2 | Partner bank underwrites using BuildRight transaction data (consented); disburses loan; funds may flow directly to BuildRight for inventory purchase | Partner Bank / Treasury | MSME Sales Manager | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Compliance ensures data-sharing consent and BSP Open Finance compliance; Treasury tracks partner-bank settlement and any revenue-share | Compliance / Treasury | CFO | Per program |

### System Touchpoints
- Consent management; partner-bank API; loan-repayment tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Regulatory risk**: Sharing MSME data without consent violates Data Privacy Act; mitigated by explicit consent capture


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-track of "tracks partner-bank settlement and any revenue-share" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-477 (ensure controlled execution — MSME Growth, Credit & Digital Enablement (PA-82.3).)
- operational: explicit consent capture

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per loan

## W2842. MSME Receivables Financing, Factor & Receivable Discounting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | MSME or partner financier requests early payment |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Eligible credit-term MSMEs |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury, Finance, MSME Sales, Partner Financier |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MSME requests early payment discount (e.g., 2% for 10-day vs. 30-day); Treasury evaluates cost vs. cash benefit | Treasury | Treasury Manager | Per request |
| 2 | Finance structures receivable discounting or factoring arrangement with partner financier; assigns receivables with notice | Finance / Partner Financier | CFO | Per program |
| 3 | Treasury settles discounted payment; reconciles factor advance and recourse; reports program yield and MSME adoption | Treasury | CFO | Per settlement |

### System Touchpoints
- Receivables financing module; partner financier API; GL settlement

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Credit risk**: Recourse factoring exposes BuildRight to MSME default; mitigated by non-recourse structuring where viable


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "discount" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-assign of "assigns receivables with notice" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconciles factor advance and recourse" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-477 (ensure controlled execution — MSME Growth, Credit & Digital Enablement (PA-82.3).)
- operational: non-recourse structuring where viable

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 days per arrangement
