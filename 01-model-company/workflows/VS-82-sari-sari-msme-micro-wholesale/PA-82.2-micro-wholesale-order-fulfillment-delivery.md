## W2833. MSME Multi-Channel Ordering (App/WhatsApp/Call/Sales Rep) Capture

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | MSME places order |
| **Frequency** | Weekly per active MSME |
| **Volume** | ~50,000 MSMEs; ~10,000–20,000 orders/week at maturity |
| **Owner** | MSME Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | MSME Sales, Customer Service, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MSME places order via preferred channel: mobile app (preferred), WhatsApp/SMS, call center, or field rep tablet; each captures standardized order data | MSME Partner / Customer Service | MSME Sales Manager | Per order |
| 2 | IT normalizes multi-channel orders into single ERP order queue; auto-applies tier pricing and default reorder list; validates SKU availability | IT / ERP automation | MSME Sales Manager | Per order |
| 3 | Customer Service handles exceptions (out-of-stock substitution, address correction); confirms order with MSME via channel of choice | Customer Service | MSME Sales Manager | Per order |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile order app; WhatsApp/SMS gateway; call center; ERP order queue

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-quality risk**: Unstructured WhatsApp orders cause errors; mitigated by app migration and structured capture templates


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "center, or field rep tablet" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validates SKU availability" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-206 (ensure MSME micro-wholesale credit & order integrity.)
- operational: app migration and structured capture templates

### Time Estimate
- 2–5 min per order

## W2834. MSME Order Validation, Credit Check & Confirmation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Order captured in ERP |
| **Frequency** | Per order |
| **Volume** | ~10,000–20,000 orders/week |
| **Owner** | AR Analyst |
| **Participants** | AR, MSME Sales, Credit |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ERP auto-validates order: tier pricing, credit limit (if on credit terms), overdue balance, delivery eligibility by location | ERP automation | AR Analyst | Per order |
| 2 | AR reviews flagged orders (over-limit, overdue, first-time large); approves/declines or routes to MSME Sales Manager | AR | AR Manager | Per flagged order |
| 3 | MSME Sales confirms order with MSME (amount, delivery date); triggers fulfillment release | MSME Sales | MSME Sales Manager | Per order |

### System Touchpoints
- Credit check engine; order release workflow; customer communication

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Credit risk**: Micro-credit defaults on small accounts; mitigated by conservative limits and credit-bureau-style scoring (W2921)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "ERP auto-validates order" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews flagged orders" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-206 (ensure MSME micro-wholesale credit & order integrity.)
- operational: conservative limits and credit-bureau-style scoring (W2921)

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 min per order

|---|---|---|---|---|

## W2836. Last-Mile Delivery to Sari-Sari / Micro-Account (Tricycle/Multicab)

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Order ready for delivery |
| **Frequency** | Daily; route-based |
| **Volume** | ~10,000–20,000 deliveries/week |
| **Owner** | Logistics Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Logistics, Driver/Courier, MSME Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics plans multi-drop routes using lightweight vehicles (tricycle, multicab, Lalamove) optimized for narrow streets and small baskets | Logistics | Logistics Supervisor | Daily |
| 2 | Driver/courier delivers to sari-sari store; verifies recipient; collects payment (COD) or e-wallet confirmation; captures digital POD | Driver/Courier | Logistics Supervisor | Per drop |
| 3 | Logistics tracks on-time delivery, route efficiency, and damage rate; reports MSME delivery satisfaction | Logistics | Logistics Supervisor | Daily |

### Pain Points / Risks

- System auto-collect of "Driver/courier delivers to sari-sari store" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-route of "route efficiency, and damage rate" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-476 (ensure controlled execution — Micro-Wholesale Ordering, Fulfillment & Delivery (PA-82.2).)
- operational: route density and lightweight vehicles

### Time Estimate

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per delivery |
| **Volume** | ~10,000–20,000 settlements/week |
| **Owner** | AR Analyst |
| **Participants** | AR, Driver/Courier, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Driver/courier collects COD cash or confirms e-wallet payment at delivery; issues BIR-compliant micro-receipt via mobile | Driver/Courier | AR Analyst | Per delivery |

- System auto-reconcile of "reconciles collected cash/e-wallet to order amount" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "tracks COD-vs-prepaid mix, write-off rate, and settlement timing" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-476 (ensure controlled execution — Micro-Wholesale Ordering, Fulfillment & Delivery (PA-82.2).)
- operational: dual reconciliation and courier accountability

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 min per delivery
