## W2913. SC/PWD/Solo Parent Tax Credit Computation & Monthly Claim Buildup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~140K–225K transactions/month |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | Tax Accountant, Finance, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ERP aggregates SC/PWD/Solo Parent discount amount and associated VAT component from mandatory book per entity/month | ERP automation | Tax Accountant | Monthly |
| 2 | Tax Accountant computes tax-credit-claimable amount (discount treated as deductible from gross sales or VAT output per BIR rules); validates computation methodology | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | Tax Manager approves monthly claim buildup; books tax-credit receivable; integrates into VAT return computation | Tax Manager | CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Mandatory book aggregation; tax-credit receivable GL; VAT return integration

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Recovery risk**: Under-claim forfeits rightful credit; mitigated by ERP-aggregated computation


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-count (sc/pwd/solo parent discount amount and associated)
- auto-log (validates computation methodology)
- rule-based auto-approval (tax manager approves monthly claim buildup;)

### Controls
operational: ERP-aggregated computation
### Time Estimate
- 2–3 days/month

## W2914. BIR Form 2552 (SC/PWD Book) Quarterly Compilation & Submission

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly close |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly per location/entity |
| **Volume** | ~205 locations |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, Tax Accountant, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Accountant compiles Book of Sales/Transactions for SC/PWD per location (or aggregated per BIR tolerance); validates against POS register | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Quarterly |
| 2 | Tax Manager prepares BIR Form 2552 (Quarterly Summary of Sales/Transactions for SC/PWD); reviews completeness | Tax Manager | CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | Tax Manager submits 2552 to BIR via eFPS/letter; archives confirmation; tracks acknowledgment | Tax Manager | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Book compilation; 2552 template; eFPS submission

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Late or incomplete 2552 forfeits credit and incurs penalty; mitigated by quarterly compilation SLA


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-validate (bir tolerance); validates against pos register)
- auto-review (for sc/pwd); reviews completeness)
- auto-submit (tax manager submits 2552 to bir)

### Controls
operational: quarterly compilation SLA
### Time Estimate
- 3–5 days/quarter

## W2915. Tax Credit Certificate (TCC) Application & Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual or large accumulated credit |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | Per entity |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, Tax Accountant, External Counsel |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Accountant prepares TCC application with supporting schedules (accumulated SC/PWD credit, 2552 history, VAT returns) | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Annual |
| 2 | Tax Manager files TCC application with BIR; responds to BIR verification queries; coordinates with External Counsel for complex cases | Tax Manager / External Counsel | CFO | Per application |
| 3 | Tax Manager tracks TCC issuance; applies TCC against future tax liabilities or pursues cash refund; books utilization | Tax Manager | CFO | Per TCC |

### System Touchpoints
- TCC application tracker; TCC utilization ledger

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Recovery risk**: Delayed/declined TCC ties up cash; mitigated by complete documentation and follow-up


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-count (tax accountant prepares tcc application)
- auto-file (tax manager files tcc application with)
- auto-track (tax manager tracks tcc issuance; applies)

### Controls
operational: complete documentation and follow-up
### Time Estimate
- Weeks to months per application

## W2918. BIR Audit of Statutory Discount Books & Tax Credit Defense

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | BIR LOA covering SC/PWD/TCC |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~1–2 audits/decade per entity |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, External Counsel, Store Ops, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Manager gathers mandatory books, POS records, ID-capture evidence, and 2552 history per audit scope | Tax Manager | CFO | Per audit |
| 2 | External Counsel defends tax-credit claims against BIR challenge (documentation gaps, eligibility disputes); negotiates resolution | External Counsel / Tax Manager | CFO | Per audit |
| 3 | Tax Manager settles any disallowed credits; updates controls to prevent recurrence; archives audit outcome | Tax Manager | CFO | Per audit |

### System Touchpoints
- Audit evidence repository; tax controversy tracker

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Financial risk**: Disallowed credits require repayment with surcharge; mitigated by rigorous documentation


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-record (books, pos records, id-capture evidence, and)
- auto-document (bir challenge (documentation gaps, eligibility disputes);)
- auto-update (disallowed credits; updates controls to prevent)

### Controls
operational: rigorous documentation
### Time Estimate
- Weeks to months per audit
