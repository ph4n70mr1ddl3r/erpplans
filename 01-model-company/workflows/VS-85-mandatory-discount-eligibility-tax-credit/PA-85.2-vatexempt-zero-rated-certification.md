## W2905. PEZA/BOI VAT-Zero-Rated Sale Customer Onboarding & Certificate Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New PEZA/BOI customer or renewal |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven + annual |
| **Volume** | ~50–150 registered entities |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | Tax Accountant, AR, Trade Sales |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales obtains PEZA/BOI Certificate of VAT Zero-Rating and registration at account setup; AR stores in customer master | Trade Sales / AR | Tax Manager | Per account |
| 2 | Tax Accountant validates certificate currency and scope; configures VAT-zero-rated treatment in customer/item tax code; audits renewal calendar | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Per account |
| 3 | Tax Accountant compiles zero-rated sales schedule monthly for 2550M; defends input VAT refund with BIR | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Customer master VAT treatment; certificate repository; 2550M schedule

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Audit risk**: Expired certificate disallows zero-rating; mitigated by annual renewal tracking


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-count (registration at account setup; ar stores)
- auto-validate (tax accountant validates certificate currency and)
- auto-compile (tax accountant compiles zero-rated sales schedule)

### Controls
operational: annual renewal tracking
### Time Estimate
- 30–60 min per account; ongoing monitoring

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

## W2911. Mixed Basket (Taxable + Exempt) Allocation & Apportionment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Single transaction with taxable and exempt items |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Common in trade/corporate sales |
| **Owner** | Pricing Analyst |
| **Participants** | Pricing, IT, AR |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing configures item-level VAT treatment in master (taxable standard, exempt, zero-rated); ensures POS/quote applies correctly per line | Pricing | VP Merchandising | Per rule |
| 2 | IT implements apportionment logic for shared charges (delivery, installation) between taxable and exempt lines per BIR rules | IT | Pricing Analyst | Per rule |
| 3 | AR validates final invoice tax computation; ensures correct VAT per line and correct ATC; reconciles to GL | AR | AR Manager | Per invoice |

### System Touchpoints
- Item VAT-treatment master; apportionment engine; invoice tax computation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Mis-allocation distorts VAT; mitigated by line-level configuration and audit


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-log (implements apportionment logic for shared charges)
- auto-validate (ar validates final invoice tax)

### Controls
operational: line-level configuration and audit
### Time Estimate
- Per-rule; per-invoice validation

| **Participants** | Tax Accountant, AR, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### System Touchpoints
- BIR invoice template; disclosure validation; EIS transmission; archive

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Missing disclosure invalidates exemption at audit; mitigated by template and sampling audit


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-generate (erp generates bir-compliant invoice with)
- auto-validate (tax accountant validates invoice format and)
- auto-document (linked exemption documentation; supports bir eis)

### Controls
operational: template and sampling audit
### Time Estimate
- Per-transaction automation; monthly audit
