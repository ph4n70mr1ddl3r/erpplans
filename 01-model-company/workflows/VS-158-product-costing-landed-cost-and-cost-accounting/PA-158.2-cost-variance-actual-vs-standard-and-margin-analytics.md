## W4657. Purchase-Price Variance (PPV) Analysis & Vendor Accountability

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | PO receipt where actual price ≠ standard; monthly variance review |
| **Frequency** | Per receipt (system) + monthly review |
| **Volume** | ~72,000 receipts/yr; ~ top-200 vendors = 85% COGS |
| **Owner** | Cost Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement (VS-03), Merchandising (VS-01), Vendor Mgmt (VS-67), Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture PPV at every receipt (actual purchase price − standard cost); post to the PPV P&L account and accumulate by vendor/category/SKU | Cost Accounting / System | Controller | Per receipt |
| 2 | Analyze PPV monthly: favorable vs. unfavorable by vendor/category; route unfavorable variances exceeding threshold to Procurement/Vendor Mgmt for vendor accountability and negotiation (VS-03.3/VS-67) | Cost Accounting / Procurement | Controller / VP Merchandising | Monthly |
| 3 | Feed recurring PPV into the next standard-cost roll (W4656) so the standard reflects the new actual; track PPV trend as a procurement-KPI input (VS-67 scorecard) | Cost Accounting / Procurement | Controller | Per roll |

### System Touchpoints
- PO receipt (VS-04); PPV ledger; vendor scorecard (VS-67); analytics (VS-28); GL (VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unaddressed-PPV risk**: Persistent unfavorable PPV on a vendor not acted on erodes margin silently; mitigated by threshold-based routing to Procurement
- **Standard-lag risk**: Standard not updated to reflect persistent PPV accumulates large revaluations; mitigated by feeding PPV into the roll

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days/month PPV analysis + vendor routing

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto PPV capture at receipt; threshold-based vendor alerts; PPV-to-roll feed

### Controls
- CTL-705 (ensure controlled execution — Cost Variance, Actual-vs-Standard & Margin Analytics (PA-158.2).)
- CTL-705: PPV captured at every receipt; unfavorable variances > threshold routed and reviewed; PPV feeds the standard roll

### Cross-references
- links to VS-03/VS-67 (vendor/scorecard), W4656 (roll), W4661 (margin), VS-17.1 (GL)

## W4658. Landed-Cost Variance vs. Standard & Import Rate Volatility Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Import landed-cost finalization (W4651); monthly variance review |
| **Frequency** | Per import shipment + monthly |
| **Volume** | ~400–600 import TEUs/month |
| **Owner** | Cost Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Imports (VS-02.2), Freight (VS-110), Treasury/FX (VS-18.3), Commodity (VS-106), Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compare actual landed cost (W4651) to standard landed cost; decompose the variance into freight, duty, brokerage, insurance, demurrage, and FX components; post to the appropriate variance accounts | Cost Accounting / Imports | Controller | Per shipment + monthly |
| 2 | Analyze import-rate volatility (ocean freight rates, FX, commodity-linked duty) vs. the commodity/FX hedge posture (VS-106/VS-18.3); quantify unhedged exposure and pass-through adequacy (VS-106.3) | Cost Accounting / Treasury / Commodity | Controller / CFO | Monthly |
| 3 | Feed landed-cost variance into the standard roll (W4656) and into commodity-risk decisions (VS-106); recommend forward-buy/hedging/duty-optimization actions (VS-87/VS-110/VS-106) | Cost Accounting / Treasury / Imports | Controller / CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Landed-cost module (W4651); freight audit (VS-110.3); FX/treasury (VS-18.3); commodity (VS-106); GL (VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unhedged-exposure risk**: Import FX/freight volatility not quantified vs. the hedge → surprise margin hits; mitigated by the volatility-vs-hedge analysis
- **Demurrage leakage risk**: Recurring demurrage/detention (W249) inflating landed cost without corrective action; mitigated by variance decomposition and routing to VS-110

### Time Estimate
- ~5–7 days/month landed-cost variance + volatility analysis

### Automation Opportunity
- Variance decomposition engine; volatility-vs-hedge dashboard; freight/FX-index feed

### Controls
- CTL-705 (ensure controlled execution — Cost Variance, Actual-vs-Standard & Margin Analytics (PA-158.2).)
- CTL-705: landed-cost variance decomposed by element; volatility vs. hedge quantified monthly; variance feeds the roll and commodity decisions

### Cross-references

## W4660. Inventory Adjustment, Write-Down & NRV Cost Impact

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Inventory adjustment (VS-05); shrinkage (VS-23); NRV review at close (W9) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + at close |
| **Volume** | ~ shrinkage <1.5% target; slow-moving/obsolete (SLOB) inventory |
| **Owner** | Cost Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Inventory (VS-05), Loss Prevention (VS-23), Controller, FP&A (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Post the cost impact of inventory adjustments (shrinkage, damage, count variances — VS-05.1/VS-23) to the correct cost account (write-off vs. variance) using the current standard cost | Cost Accounting / Inventory | Controller | Monthly |
| 2 | At each close (W9), perform the lower-of-cost-or-NRV review (PFRS/IAS 2): identify slow-moving/obsolete stock (W220), estimate NRV vs. carrying cost, and book the write-down provision | Cost Accounting / FP&A | Controller / CFO | Per close |
| 3 | Reconcile total inventory cost to the GL inventory account and the WAC verification (W9); analyze write-down trend and feed into assortment/SLOB decisions (VS-05.3) | Cost Accounting / FP&A | Controller | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory module (VS-05); shrinkage (VS-23); SLOB (W220); NRV review (W9); GL (VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **NRV-miss risk**: Failing to write down SLOB to NRV overstates inventory and assets — audit finding; mitigated by the per-close NRV review
- **Mis-classified-adjustment risk**: A shrinkage write-off booked as a variance distorts margin; mitigated by the cost-account classification rules

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days/month adjustment + NRV accounting

### Automation Opportunity
- Adjustment-to-cost-account auto-classification; SLOB/NRV candidate list with suggested provision; trend dashboards

### Controls
- CTL-705 (ensure controlled execution — Cost Variance, Actual-vs-Standard & Margin Analytics (PA-158.2).)
- CTL-705: adjustments classified per rules; NRV reviewed each close (W9); inventory cost reconciled to GL

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly margin review; pricing/markdown decisions |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + ad-hoc |
| **Volume** | ~35K SKUs × 200 stores × 5 entities (aggregated to category/region) |
| **Owner** | Cost Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01/VS-101), Pricing (VS-01.2), FP&A (VS-17.4), Insights (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Produce the margin analytics pack: gross margin % (net sales − COGS), initial markup (IMU), maintained markup (MMU) after markdowns, and GMROI by SKU/category/store/region/entity — using the cost layer (W4649/W4656) net of vendor funding (W4664) | Cost Accounting / FP&A / Insights | Controller / VP Merchandising | Monthly |
| 2 | Identify margin outliers (below category floor, eroding trend, store-specific) and route to Merchandising/Pricing for action (VS-01.2/VS-101/VS-64 markdown); validate cost-data accuracy before acting on a margin signal | Cost Accounting / Merchandising | Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Tie aggregate margin to the PFRS financials and the merchandise-financial plan (VS-101); feed maintained-margin into OTB and pricing strategy (VS-101/VS-01.2) | Cost Accounting / FP&A | Controller / CFO | Quarterly |

## W4662. Freight, Duty & Handling Cost Absorption vs. Billback Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly freight/duty cost review; vendor compliance chargeback decisions |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Freight/duty/handling spend (VS-110/VS-87); vendor compliance chargebacks (W245) |
| **Owner** | Cost Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Logistics (VS-06/VS-110), Imports (VS-87), Vendor Mgmt (VS-03/VS-67), Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Classify inbound freight/duty/handling cost as absorbed (into SKU cost per W4651/W4652) vs. billback-eligible (vendor non-compliance — wrong carrier, late shipment, packaging non-conformance per W245) | Cost Accounting / Logistics | Controller | Monthly |
| 2 | Bill back vendor-compliance-eligible cost (W245 chargebacks) and recover; analyze the absorb-vs-billback posture for net cost optimization (renegotiate Incoterms, routing-guide compliance — VS-110.2) | Cost Accounting / Vendor Mgmt | Controller / VP Merchandising | Monthly |
| 3 | Quantify unrecovered billback-eligible cost as margin leakage; route to vendor scorecard (VS-67) and freight-procurement (VS-110) for structural recovery | Cost Accounting / Logistics | Controller | Quarterly |

- Freight audit/pay (VS-110.3); vendor compliance chargeback (W245); landed cost (W4651); vendor scorecard (VS-67)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Absorbed-leakage risk**: Vendor-compliance cost absorbed into COGS instead of recovered erodes margin; mitigated by the billback classification and recovery
- **Chargeback-dispute risk**: Chargebacks disputed by vendors without documentation → non-recovery; mitigated by evidence-based chargebacks (routing-guide compliance VS-110.2)

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days/month absorb-vs-billback analysis

### Automation Opportunity
- Compliance-event-to-chargeback auto-flag; absorb-vs-billback dashboards; recovery-rate tracking

### Controls
- CTL-705 (ensure controlled execution — Cost Variance, Actual-vs-Standard & Margin Analytics (PA-158.2).)

## W4663. Cost-of-Goods-Sold Reconciliation, Cut-off & Period Matching

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Month-end close (W9A); COGS reconciliation |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 42–45B annual COGS; ~ every POS/sales transaction + every receipt/adjustment |
| **Owner** | Cost Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | GL/Close (VS-17.1), Inventory (VS-05), POS (VS-08), Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reconcile COGS to the inventory movement: opening inventory + receipts (at cost) + landed-cost adjustments − closing inventory = COGS; investigate any unexplained COGS variance | Cost Accounting / GL / Inventory | Controller | Per close |
| 2 | Perform COGS cut-off: ensure sales/shipments near period-end are matched to the corresponding COGS in the same period (and receipts in the correct period); clear cut-off exceptions | Cost Accounting | Controller | Per close |
| 3 | Reconcile COGS by entity/channel to revenue (margin tie-out) and to the PFRS financials; resolve any entity-allocation or intercompany COGS distortion (VS-72) | Cost Accounting / GL | Controller | Per close |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory module (VS-05); POS sales (VS-08); GL (VS-17.1); month-end close (W9A); intercompany (VS-72)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **COGS-variance risk**: An unexplained COGS-to-inventory variance hides a costing/inventory error; mitigated by the monthly reconciliation and variance investigation
- **Cut-off risk**: Period-boundary sales/receipts mismatched to COGS → period margin distortion; mitigated by cut-off testing

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 days/month COGS reconciliation + cut-off

### Automation Opportunity
- COGS-to-inventory-movement auto-reconciliation; cut-off-boundary flagging; entity/channel margin tie-out

### Controls
- CTL-115 (ensure cost-master & COGS reconciliation control.)
- CTL-705: COGS reconciled to inventory movement monthly; cut-off tested; entity COGS tied to revenue and PFRS financials

### Cross-references
- links to VS-05/VS-08 (inventory/POS), W9A (close), VS-72 (intercompany), W4661 (margin tie)

## W4664. Markdown, Promo & Vendor-Funding Net-Cost Impact Accounting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Markdown/promo run (VS-01.2/VS-64); vendor rebate/funding (VS-39); monthly review |
| **Frequency** | Per promo + monthly |
| **Volume** | 6 major catalog promos/yr + monthly hot deals; vendor rebates (VS-39); co-op advertising |
| **Owner** | Cost Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01.2/VS-64), Promo (VS-58), Rebate (VS-39), Pricing, Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Determine the correct accounting for vendor funding: reduction of cost of goods (net cost) vs. reduction of selling expense vs. other income — per PFRS and the documented policy; net rebates (VS-39) and co-op advertising against the funded SKU's cost | Cost Accounting / Rebate / Merchandising | Controller | Per funding type |
| 2 | Account for markdown/promo cost: temporary vs. permanent markdown, markdown reserve, and the net margin impact after vendor funding; post the markdown to margin or a markdown P&L account per policy | Cost Accounting / Pricing | Controller | Per promo |
| 3 | Reconcile vendor-funding accrual (VS-39) to actual receipts and to the net-cost reduction claimed; identify unfunded/unclaimed vendor funding as recovery opportunity (VS-39.3) | Cost Accounting / Rebate | Controller | Monthly |

- Markdown/promo module (VS-01.2/VS-64/VS-58); rebate accrual (VS-39); GL (VS-17.1); margin analytics (W4661)

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days/month markdown/vendor-funding accounting + reconciliation

### Automation Opportunity
- Funding-classification rules engine; accrual-to-receipt auto-reconcile; unclaimed-funding alert

### Controls
- CTL-705 (ensure controlled execution — Cost Variance, Actual-vs-Standard & Margin Analytics (PA-158.2).)
