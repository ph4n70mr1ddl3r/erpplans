## W4113. Technology Value Realization & Benefit Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Investment go-live; benefit cycle |
| **Frequency** | Per investment + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Enterprise technology investment portfolio |
| **Owner** | VP IT Finance / OpEx (VS-133) |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17.4), sponsors, OpEx (VS-133 W4065), OCM (VS-134 W4095) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP IT Finance maintain the value-realization register per investment (business case W4100 → realized), with owners and timelines, integrated with OpEx benefit tracking (VS-133 W4065) | VP IT Finance / OpEx / Finance | CIO / CFO | Per investment |
| 2 | VP IT Finance/Finance track realized value (revenue, cost, productivity, risk), reconcile to FP&A (VS-17.4), and attribute adoption-dependent value (VS-134 W4095) | VP IT Finance / Finance | VP IT Finance | Quarterly |
| 3 | VP IT Finance report value realization to the executive/Board (VS-36), flag shortfalls, and feed lessons into future cases (W4100) | VP IT Finance | CIO / CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Benefit register; FP&A (VS-17.4); OpEx benefit (VS-133 W4065); OCM ROI (VS-134 W4095); governance (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Benefit-overstate risk**: Claimed but unrealized; mitigated by Finance reconciliation and P&L linkage
- **Adoption-gap risk**: Value missed due to low adoption; mitigated by adoption tracking (VS-134)

### Automation Opportunity
- Benefit auto-reconciliation to GL/FP&A; value-realization dashboards

### Controls
- CTL-148 (ensure technology spend & license optimization.)
- CTL-637: benefit reconciled by Finance; adoption-dependency documented

### Time Estimate
- Per investment + quarterly

### Cross-references
- links to VS-133 W4065 (benefit), VS-134 W4095 (adoption ROI), VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-36 (governance)

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

## W4117. Technology Financial Month-End, Accruals & GL Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close cycle — Technology Financial Month-End |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | Enterprise technology spend (all entities) |
| **Owner** | VP IT Finance / Controllership (VS-17) |
| **Participants** | Finance/AP (VS-17/VS-15), IT (VS-27), interentity (VS-72) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP IT Finance/Finance accrue technology costs (usage-based cloud/SaaS not yet invoiced), allocate per TBM (W4097/W4102), and post to GL/COA (VS-17) within the 5-day close (links to VS-17.1) | VP IT Finance / Finance | CFO | Monthly |
| 2 | VP IT Finance/Finance reconcile cloud/SaaS/vendor invoices to accruals and allocations, resolve exceptions, and handle interentity (VS-72) | VP IT Finance / Finance | VP IT Finance | Monthly |
| 3 | VP IT Finance/Finance support the financial close (VS-17.1), audit (VS-21), and provide technology-spend reporting | VP IT Finance / Finance | CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- GL/close (VS-17.1); AP/invoice matching (VS-15); TBM (W4097); interentity (VS-72)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Accrual-error risk**: Usage-based spend mis-accrued; mitigated by usage data and reconciliation
- **Close-delay risk**: Technology accruals delay the 5-day close; mitigated by automation and SLAs

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated usage-based accruals; invoice-to-accrual reconciliation

### Controls
- CTL-148 (ensure technology spend & license optimization.)
- CTL-637: accruals reconciled to invoices; GL posting controls (VS-17)

### Time Estimate
- Monthly close window

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.1 (close), VS-15 (AP), VS-72 (interentity), VS-21 (audit)

## W4119. Technology Financial Risk, FX & Commitment Exposure Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Currency exposure; commitment cycle; risk review |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Enterprise technology commitments (USD-denominated cloud/SaaS, multi-year contracts) |
| **Owner** | VP IT Finance / Treasury (VS-18) |
| **Participants** | Treasury (VS-18), Finance (VS-17.4), Risk (VS-21.2), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP IT Finance/Treasury map technology financial exposures: USD-denominated cloud/SaaS (FX), multi-year commitments (commitment/termination risk), vendor concentration, and price-escalation clauses | VP IT Finance / Treasury / Risk | CFO | Quarterly |
| 2 | VP IT Finance/Treasury hedge/manage FX exposure on technology spend (aligned to group FX policy VS-18), manage commitment risk (exit terms, caps), and diversify concentration | VP IT Finance / Treasury | CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | VP IT Finance report technology financial risk to risk/treasury governance (VS-21.2/VS-18) and the Board (VS-36) | VP IT Finance / Treasury | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/FX (VS-18); risk register (VS-21.2); contracts (VS-100); TBM (W4097)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **FX-volatility risk**: PHP/USD moves spike cloud/SaaS cost; mitigated by hedging aligned to group policy
- **Commitment-risk**: Multi-year commitment traps spend; mitigated by exit terms and staged commitments

### Automation Opportunity
- FX-exposure analytics on tech spend; commitment-portfolio risk modeling

### Controls
- CTL-637 (ensure controlled execution — Technology Value Realization & Financial Analytics (PA-135.3).)
- CTL-637: FX hedging per group policy (VS-18); commitment review with Legal/Risk

### Time Estimate
- Quarterly + continuous

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury/FX), VS-21.2 (risk), VS-100 (contracts), VS-36 (governance)
