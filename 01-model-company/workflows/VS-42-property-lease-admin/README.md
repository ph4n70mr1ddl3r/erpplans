# VS-42: Property & Lease Administration

> **Asset & Infrastructure** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages all real estate leasing operations for BuildRight Property Management, Inc. — the legal entity that owns or leases store sites, DC facilities, and office space on behalf of the BuildRight group. Covers lease negotiation and administration, rent payment processing, landlord-tenant relationship management, property tax and LGU compliance, lease accounting (IFRS 16 / PFRS 16), property insurance, and lease renewal/expiry management. With 200 stores across 4 regions, 4 DCs, and HQ, BuildRight Property Management, Inc. manages ~205 leased or owned properties with annual rent obligations of ~PHP 2–4 billion.
