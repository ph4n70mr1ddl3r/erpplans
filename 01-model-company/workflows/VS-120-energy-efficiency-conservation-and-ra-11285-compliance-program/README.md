# VS-120: Energy Efficiency, Conservation & RA 11285 Compliance Program

## Overview

Energy Efficiency, Conservation & RA 11285 Compliance Program workflows for BuildRight Depot
Corp. — governing the **enterprise energy-management and statutory energy-efficiency program**
across ~205 large sites (200 big-box stores at 8,000–15,000 sqm each, 4 distribution centers at
25,000–40,000 sqm, and HQ) under Philippine Republic Act 11285 (the Energy Efficiency and
Conservation Act, 2019) and its IRR. Each BuildRight store is a large energy consumer (HVAC,
refrigeration, lighting, paint/chemical ventilation, lumber-yard and yard lighting, equipment)
and DCs operate refrigeration, material-handling, and high-bay lighting — together making
BuildRight a **"Designated Establishment"** under RA 11285, with statutory obligations to
designate an **Energy Efficiency Officer (EEO)** / **Energy Manager**, conduct mandatory
**energy audits** (Type-1 every 3 years; Type-2 annually for the largest consumers), prepare and
submit an **Energy Conservation Plan (ECP)** and annual energy-consumption reporting to the
Department of Energy (DOE) via the **ECC**/energy-conservation framework, and implement energy-
conservation measures (ECM). Non-compliance risks DOE penalties, adverse assessment, and
reputational exposure, while effective energy management is also a material cost lever (energy is
a significant operating expense across ~205 large sites) and decarbonization lever (links to
VS-25 ESG and VS-108 renewable).

Today this program is **unowned as a discipline**: energy is referenced incidentally across ~14
PA files — consumption monitoring (W692/W1543 in VS-25.1, W701/W1563 in VS-20.3), the single
utility-bill workflow (W111), own-generation (VS-108), and facility operations (VS-20.3) — but
no value stream owns the RA 11285 compliance program (EEO designation, DOE registration/reporting,
mandatory energy audit, conservation plan), the energy-management-system discipline (ISO 50001),
the ECM investment pipeline, energy procurement/retail-competition (RES/WESM), measurement &
verification (M&V), or energy-performance analytics. This value stream consolidates the
discipline: EEC program strategy/designated-establishment/governance; energy audit,
measurement & conservation-plan management; and ECM delivery, performance optimization and
compliance analytics.

This is distinct from **VS-20.3 (Facility Maintenance & Equipment)** which *maintains* building
systems (this value stream *optimizes* those systems' energy performance and governs the energy-
efficiency compliance program). It is distinct from **VS-25 (ESG & Sustainability)** which
reports the environmental footprint (this value stream manages the *operational energy program*
whose data feeds ESG reporting and decarbonization). It is distinct from **VS-108 (On-Site
Renewable Energy & Prosumer)** which generates clean energy (this value stream manages demand-side
energy efficiency/conservation that *reduces* consumption and integrates with own-generation).
It is distinct from **VS-34 (Expense Procurement)** which buys goods/services transactionally
(this value stream manages the energy *commodity procurement and retail-competition strategy*).
It is distinct from **VS-109 (Store Remodel)** which renews stores (this value stream manages the
ongoing energy performance of the existing and remodeled estate). Energy efficiency &
conservation is the *demand-side energy-management and RA 11285 statutory-compliance*
discipline — distinct from facilities maintenance, ESG reporting, own-generation, expense
procurement, and store remodel.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: ~205 large energy-consuming sites subject to RA 11285, a
material energy cost line, decarbonization commitments, and a 4-DC + 200-store distributed
estate. Without a dedicated owner, BuildRight risks DOE non-compliance (penalties, adverse
assessment), over-spending on energy, missed ECM savings, and understated ESG performance —
risks that no existing value stream owns end-to-end.
