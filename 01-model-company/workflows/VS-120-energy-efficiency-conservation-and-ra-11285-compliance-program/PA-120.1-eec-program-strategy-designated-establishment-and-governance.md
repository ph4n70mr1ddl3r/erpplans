## W3737. Energy Efficiency & Conservation Program Strategy & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual planning (links to VS-33.1); RA 11285 obligation; program review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise EEC program (~205 sites) |
| **Owner** | Energy Efficiency Officer (EEO) / Sustainability |
| **Participants** | EEO, Sustainability (links to VS-25), Facilities (links to VS-20.3), Store Ops (links to VS-07), Procurement (links to VS-34), Finance (links to VS-17.4), Renewable (links to VS-108), Cross-Entity (links to VS-72), Compliance (links to VS-22), Audit (links to VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EEO/Sustainability define the energy-efficiency & conservation (EEC) program strategy and governance: scope (~205 sites — 200 stores, 4 DCs, HQ), the RA 11285 compliance map (designated-establishment status, energy audit, conservation plan, DOE reporting), the energy-management-system approach (ISO 50001 EnMS), the ECM investment strategy, and KPIs (energy intensity kWh/sqm, cost, audit compliance, ECM savings, GHG reduction) | EEO / Sustainability | VP Operations / Sustainability | Annual |
| 2 | EEO establish the governance: EEO authority, the energy team (Facilities, Store Ops, Procurement, Finance, Sustainability), cross-functional responsibilities, and integration to facilities (VS-20.3), ESG (VS-25), renewable (VS-108), procurement (VS-34), and compliance (VS-22) | EEO / Facilities / Sustainability | VP Operations | Annual |
| 3 | Executive committee ratify the EEC strategy, approve the program budget and ECM investment envelope (links to W3743), and report energy posture to the executive risk/sustainability committee (links to VS-21.2/VS-25.3) and board (links to VS-36); communicate the mandate | VP Operations / EEO | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Sustainability/ESG (links to VS-25); facilities (links to VS-20.3); store ops (links to VS-07); procurement (links to VS-34); finance (links to VS-17.4); renewable (links to VS-108); compliance (links to VS-22); governance (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-owner risk**: Energy managed reactively per-site with no program/DOE view; mitigated by EEO authority
- **Compliance-blindness risk**: RA 11285 obligations unknown/unmet; mitigated by compliance map (W3738)

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual

- Annual + on new site
