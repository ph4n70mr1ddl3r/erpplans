## W5169. EPR Plastic Waste Recovery Certificate (PRC) Verification & Matching

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly compliance close; receipt of PRC from PRO or recovery partners |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~40–50 certificates verified quarterly |
| **Owner** | EPR Compliance Manager / Internal Auditor (VS-21) |
| **Participants** | PRO Representatives, Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collect all original PRCs released by partners and the PRO. | EPR Compliance Manager / Internal Auditor (VS-21) | EPR Compliance Manager / Internal Auditor (VS-21) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Verify authenticity: match security seals, certificate IDs, and registered signatures against NSWMC registry. | EPR Compliance Manager / Internal Auditor (VS-21) | EPR Compliance Manager / Internal Auditor (VS-21) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Match certified weights against net distributed plastic footprint (W5157) for the corresponding quarter. | EPR Compliance Manager / Internal Auditor (VS-21) | EPR Compliance Manager / Internal Auditor (VS-21) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Calculate the net recovery status: determine if target volume (e.g. 20%) is met or if a recovery gap exists. | EPR Compliance Manager / Internal Auditor (VS-21) | EPR Compliance Manager / Internal Auditor (VS-21) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- NSWMC database, ERP reporting portal (VS-28), Audit tracking (VS-21).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fraudulent Certificates**: Counterfeit certificates submitted by unscrupulous partners; mitigated by independent verify call to issuing plant controller.

### Automation Opportunity
- Certificate validation registry checks.

### Controls
- CTL-769 (ensure controlled execution — EPR Credit Trading, Compliance Auditing & Annual Reporting (PA-179.3).)
- CTL-769: Dual sign-off (Compliance Manager + Head of Internal Audit & Risk) required for quarterly PRC reconciliation report.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-3 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21 (internal audit), VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-28 (analytics)

## W5170. EPR Recovery Credit Purchase Agreement (RCPA) Negotiation & Trading

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly reconciliation (W5169) showing a recovery volume deficit |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per deficit |
| **Volume** | ~1–2 credit purchase agreements annually |
| **Owner** | Director of Supply Chain / EPR Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | EPR Credit Sellers (other companies, PROs), Legal Operations (VS-100), Treasury (VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assess credit market: source excess recovery credits from companies exceeding their targets or specialized PRO pools. | Director of Supply Chain / EPR Compliance Manager | Director of Supply Chain / EPR Compliance Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Negotiate Recovery Credit Purchase Agreement (RCPA): define price per metric ton, validation guarantees, and indemnification against NSWMC rejection. | Director of Supply Chain / EPR Compliance Manager | Director of Supply Chain / EPR Compliance Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Draft the RCPA contract and route for board approval (VS-36) if transaction value exceeds standard limits. | Director of Supply Chain / EPR Compliance Manager | Director of Supply Chain / EPR Compliance Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Release payment through Treasury and obtain the endorsed Plastic Recovery Certificates. | Director of Supply Chain / EPR Compliance Manager | Director of Supply Chain / EPR Compliance Manager | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP AP (VS-15), treasury payment systems (VS-18), Legal contract library (VS-100).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Invalid credits**: Credits rejected by NSWMC due to seller double-counting; mitigated by strict contract indemnity and pre-audit of seller's audit logs.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-769 (ensure controlled execution — EPR Credit Trading, Compliance Auditing & Annual Reporting (PA-179.3).)
- CTL-769: Seller certificates validated by legal before escrow payout; transactions board-authorized.

### Time Estimate
- ~10-15 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal), VS-18 (treasury), VS-15 (P2P), VS-36 (governance)
