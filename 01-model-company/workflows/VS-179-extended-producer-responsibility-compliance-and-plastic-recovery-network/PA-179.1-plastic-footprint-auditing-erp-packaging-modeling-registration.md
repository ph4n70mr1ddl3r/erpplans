## W5153. Plastic Packaging Footprint Baseline Audit & Material Taxonomy Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | EPR program launch; annual compliance planning (VS-25) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + strategy refresh |
| **Volume** | 1 comprehensive audit of 35,000 active SKUs |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Director / EPR Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Merchandising Category Managers (VS-01), Master Data Team (VS-29), Procurement (VS-03), External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define plastic material categories per RA 11898 (rigid plastic, flexible single-layer, flexible multi-layer, polystyrene). | Sustainability Director / EPR Compliance Manager | Sustainability Director / EPR Compliance Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Audit packaging weight and type for all active SKUs, focusing on private label products and transit packaging (stretch wraps, bubble wraps). | Sustainability Director / EPR Compliance Manager | Sustainability Director / EPR Compliance Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Establish the BuildRight Plastic Packaging Taxonomy and map SKUs to their respective packaging profiles. | Sustainability Director / EPR Compliance Manager | Sustainability Director / EPR Compliance Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Calculate the baseline annual plastic footprint (in metric tons) using trailing 12-month sales and import volumes. | Sustainability Director / EPR Compliance Manager | Sustainability Director / EPR Compliance Manager | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Item Master (VS-29), Sales ledger (VS-16), Imports database (VS-02.2/VS-87).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Incomplete SKU data**: Stale packaging weight database leading to under-reporting; mitigated by mandatory supplier packaging disclosures during onboarding.

### Time Estimate
- ~4-6 weeks for initial audit

### Automation Opportunity
- System calculation matching SKU sales to packaging weight taxonomy profiles.

### Controls
- CTL: Baseline footprint signed off by external environmental auditor; taxonomy mapping locked in master data.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-25 (ESG), VS-29 (master data), VS-03 (procurement), VS-87 (customs)

## W5154. ERP Item Master Plastic/Packaging Weight Fields Configuration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved plastic baseline audit (W5153); new product creation process (VS-01.1) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous per new SKU onboarding |
| **Volume** | ~5,000–8,000 new SKUs configured annually |
| **Owner** | Master Data Manager |
| **Participants** | Master Data Analysts (VS-29), Product Developers (VS-41), Category Managers (VS-01) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Configure new fields in the ERP Item Master for packaging material type, unit packaging weight (grams), and recycling tier. | Master Data Manager | Master Data Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Update the product onboarding workflow: require suppliers to fill in packaging weight fields before SKU generation approval. | Master Data Manager | Master Data Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Run data purification campaigns to fill in missing packaging fields for legacy SKUs. | Master Data Manager | Master Data Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Perform monthly system checks to ensure new SKUs have completed packaging metadata fields. | Master Data Manager | Master Data Manager | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Item Master (VS-29), Product Information Management (PIM) system (VS-137).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Suppliers providing false packaging weights**: Over/under-calculation of footprint; mitigated by random sample weighing at DC quality control.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 hours per SKU configuration batch

### Automation Opportunity
- API verification enforcing non-zero entry in packaging weight fields before SKU release.

### Controls
- CTL: Master data validation rules prevent SKU creation without packaging weight verification.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-29 (master data), VS-137 (PIM), VS-41 (private label), VS-01.1 (assortment)

## W5156. Private Label Packaging Material Substitution & Eco-Design Review

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Product development review (VS-41); high plastic footprint optimization mandate |
| **Frequency** | Bi-annual review |
| **Volume** | ~100–150 private label SKUs reviewed annually |
| **Owner** | Private Label Brand Manager / Sustainability Lead |
| **Participants** | Product Development Team (VS-41), Packaging Engineers (VS-111), Procurement, Suppliers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify private label products with high plastic footprint (e.g. plastic handles, blister packs, bubble wrap). | Private Label Brand Manager / Sustainability Lead | Private Label Brand Manager / Sustainability Lead | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Assess alternative materials: evaluate biodegradable plastics, recycled plastics, molded pulp, or cardboard alternatives. | Private Label Brand Manager / Sustainability Lead | Private Label Brand Manager / Sustainability Lead | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Conduct engineering trials: test structural integrity, shelf-life, and moisture barrier properties of alternative packaging (VS-111). | Private Label Brand Manager / Sustainability Lead | Private Label Brand Manager / Sustainability Lead | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Execute packaging transition plan: approve design, update supplier tooling, and update the ERP Item Master with new packaging weights. | Private Label Brand Manager / Sustainability Lead | Private Label Brand Manager / Sustainability Lead | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- PIM system (VS-137), PL development portal (VS-41.1), Packaging specifications database (VS-111).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Material failure / Product damage**: Alternative packaging fails, increasing damage claims; mitigated by mandatory stress testing in DC packaging lab (VS-111).

### Time Estimate
- ~4-8 weeks per product design review

### Automation Opportunity
- Simulation modeling for estimating carbon footprint reductions from material changes.

### Controls
- CTL: Eco-design design sign-off by Quality and Sustainability Directors; updated specifications uploaded to document control (VS-88).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-41 (private label), VS-111 (packaging), VS-137 (PIM), VS-88 (document control)

## W5157. Outbound Retail Plastic/Packaging Weight Calculation & Transaction Matching

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | EOD transaction processing; monthly close data aggregation |
| **Frequency** | Daily batch + monthly consolidation |
| **Volume** | ~2.8M transactions processed monthly across 200 stores |
| **Owner** | EPR Data Analyst |
| **Participants** | IT Systems Engineer (VS-27), POS Admin (VS-08), Accounts Receivable (VS-16) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Execute daily database script: multiply POS SKU quantities sold by their Item Master packaging weight (W5154). | EPR Data Analyst | EPR Data Analyst | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Aggregate packaging weight by plastic type (rigid, single-layer, multi-layer) and sales channel (POS walk-in, ecommerce delivery, B2B trade delivery). | EPR Data Analyst | EPR Data Analyst | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Apply transaction rules: subtract returns/refund transactions to calculate net monthly plastic footprint distributed. | EPR Data Analyst | EPR Data Analyst | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Generate monthly packaging volume reports for reconciliation against the recovery database. | EPR Data Analyst | EPR Data Analyst | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Sales module (VS-16), POS database (VS-08), ERP Reporting engine (VS-28).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data lag / Sync failure**: POS transactions missing packaging metadata; mitigated by daily data error reports flagging SKUs with missing weights.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 hour daily automated batch execution

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated SQL ETL script running nightly to aggregate footprint metrics.

### Controls
- CTL: Footprint calculations reconciled to monthly sales volume reports; discrepancies investigated.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (O2C), VS-08 (POS), VS-27 (IT), VS-28 (analytics)

| **Frequency** | Annual budgeting + monthly allocation |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 10M–25M annual recovery budget |
| **Owner** | Real Estate & Operations Controller / EPR Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance FP&A (VS-17.4), Category Managers (VS-01), Merchandising VP |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Estimate annual EPR compliance cost: compute PRO fees, partner recovery fees, logistics costs, and audit fees based on footprint forecast. | Real Estate & Operations Controller / EPR Compliance Manager | Real Estate & Operations Controller / EPR Compliance Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Create cost allocation model: allocate EPR expenses to departments/categories based on their plastic packaging footprint share. | Real Estate & Operations Controller / EPR Compliance Manager | Real Estate & Operations Controller / EPR Compliance Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Integrate plastic fees into private label product costing sheets as an overhead landed cost component (VS-158). | Real Estate & Operations Controller / EPR Compliance Manager | Real Estate & Operations Controller / EPR Compliance Manager | 1-2 hours |

- ERP Budgeting module (VS-33.1), Cost accounting system (VS-158), GL (VS-17.1).

### Controls
- CTL: Allocation approved by Controller; monthly variance reporting shared with Merchandising VP.
