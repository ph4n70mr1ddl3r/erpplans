## W5161. Retail Store In-Store Plastic Waste Sorting, Collection & Storage

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store daily operations; store cleanup routines (VS-07) |
| **Frequency** | Daily collection |
| **Volume** | 200 stores collecting ~2–3 metric tons of plastic waste monthly |
| **Owner** | Store Manager / Store LP Officer (VS-23) |
| **Participants** | Stock Associates (VS-07), Store Staff, Cleaning Service (VS-98) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set up clearly labeled Customer Recycling Bins at checkout areas and customer service counters. | Store Manager / Store LP Officer (VS-23) | Store Manager / Store LP Officer (VS-23) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Sort packaging waste in receiving area: separate clean LDPE stretch wrap, bubble wrap, plastic bands, and PET containers generated during receiving operations. | Store Manager / Store LP Officer (VS-23) | Store Manager / Store LP Officer (VS-23) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Compact and bale sorted plastics using store compaction equipment; label bales with store ID, weight, and date. | Store Manager / Store LP Officer (VS-23) | Store Manager / Store LP Officer (VS-23) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Stage compacted plastic bales in designated dry storage zones to prevent moisture and fire hazards (VS-24). | Store Manager / Store LP Officer (VS-23) | Store Manager / Store LP Officer (VS-23) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Store operations logs (VS-07), Safety checklists (VS-24), Security system (VS-23).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fire Hazard**: Stored plastics ignite in the receiving backroom; mitigated by store fire safety standards (sprinkler coverage, clearance zones) per VS-24.2.

### Automation Opportunity
- Digital scale weight logging with barcode tagging.

### Controls
- CTL-768 (ensure controlled execution — Outbound Plastic Recovery Partnerships & Waste-to-Energy Co-Processing (PA-179.2).)
- CTL-768: Weekly safety inspection checks storage zones; store weight logs verified by Regional LP (VS-23).

### Time Estimate
- ~30 mins daily per store

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07 (store ops), VS-23 (loss prevention), VS-98 (contingent workforce), VS-24 (HSE)

| **Frequency** | Weekly / Bi-weekly pickup |
| **Volume** | ~60–80 metric tons consolidated monthly across 4 DCs |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) |
| **Participants** | Fleet Drivers, DC Receiving Team (VS-04), 3PL Logistics Partner (VS-56) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 4 | Bale loose LGU/junk shop plastics at the DC bale facility; arrange full truckload transfers to final co-processing/recycling plants. | Logistics Manager / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) | Logistics Manager / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints

- Fleet tracking and routing algorithms for backhaul optimization.

### Controls
- CTL-170 (ensure EPR recovery & registration compliance.)
- CTL-768: DC scale verification against store dispatch weights; variances over 5% investigated.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 hours per pickup window

### Cross-references
- links to VS-04 (DC ops), VS-06 (logistics), VS-56 (3PL), VS-110 (freight procurement)

## W5168. Multi-Region Recovery Operations Safety & OSH Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | EPR logistics / baling operations; DOLE OSH audit (VS-24) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous safety enforcement + quarterly audits |
| **Volume** | 4 DCs and 200 store collection zones monitored |
| **Owner** | DC Safety Officer / OSH Manager (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | Store Managers, Logistics operators, DOLE Representative |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for operating plastic baling and compaction machinery. | DC Safety Officer / OSH Manager (VS-24) | DC Safety Officer / OSH Manager (VS-24) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Train staff: run mandatory safety training on machine lock-out/tag-out, bale handling, and ergonomic lifting. | DC Safety Officer / OSH Manager (VS-24) | DC Safety Officer / OSH Manager (VS-24) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Enforce PPE compliance: require safety boots, heavy gloves, safety glasses, and ear protection around balers (VS-169). | DC Safety Officer / OSH Manager (VS-24) | DC Safety Officer / OSH Manager (VS-24) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Conduct quarterly fire risk assessments in dry plastic storage zones; verify fire extinguishers are serviced and clear. | DC Safety Officer / OSH Manager (VS-24) | DC Safety Officer / OSH Manager (VS-24) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- OSH training logs, safety audit portal (VS-24), Employee training records (VS-19.4).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Laceration / Compaction injury**: Worker hand trapped in baler; mitigated by mandatory dual-button activation controls on all balers.

### Automation Opportunity
- Baler safety sensor logs integration with central OSH dashboard.

### Controls
- CTL-768 (ensure controlled execution — Outbound Plastic Recovery Partnerships & Waste-to-Energy Co-Processing (PA-179.2).)
- CTL-768: Baling machines inspected weekly; lock-out/tag-out keys managed by shift supervisors.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 hours per quarterly safety audit

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-169 (PPE), VS-19.4 (HR training)
