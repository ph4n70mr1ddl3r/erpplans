## Overview

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) compliance operations and the post-consumer plastic recovery network for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the statutory program under the EPR Act of 2022 (RA 11898) to calculate, collect, recover, and recycle plastic packaging across the group's retail operations and private label supply chain: establishing equivalent plastic/packaging weight taxonomies and SKU master fields; auditing baseline plastic footprints from imports and local procurement; conducting eco-design reviews of private label packaging for material substitution; setting up in-store recycling collection bins; collaborating with LGUs, Materials Recovery Facilities (MRFs), junk shops, and informal waste collectors; transporting and baling recovered plastics; executing co-processing agreements with cement kilns and mechanical recyclers; securing audited waste-to-energy and recovery certifications; purchasing plastic recovery credits (PRCs); drafting and submitting annual EPR compliance reports to the National Solid Waste Management Commission (NSWMC); managing PRO service levels; and reporting sustainability KPIs to the Board.

This matters at BuildRight's scale. Under **RA 11898 (EPR Act of 2022)**, large enterprises in the Philippines must recover and recycle 20% to 80% of their single-use plastic/packaging waste annually. Failure to register and comply carries heavy fines (up to PHP 20M) and potential business permit cancellations. While VS-73 handles basic store-level waste segregation and municipal disposal, it does not cover the compliance structure, footprint auditing, recovery agreements, credit trading, and NSWMC reporting mandated by the EPR law. The new value stream establishes this critical statutory compliance program.

This value stream owns the end-to-end program: mapping workflows, configuring system parameters, executing compliance actions, coordinating with external partners, and managing performance dashboards.
