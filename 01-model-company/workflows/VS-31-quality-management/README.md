# VS-31: Quality Management & Product Compliance

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Ensures product quality and regulatory compliance across the full merchandise lifecycle — from vendor sourcing and incoming goods inspection through in-store quality monitoring and product recall execution. Critical for a hardware/DIY retailer selling building materials (structural integrity), electrical items (fire safety), paint/chemicals (DENR/FPA regulation), and tools (consumer safety).
