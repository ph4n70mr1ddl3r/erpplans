## W1615. Product Recall Assessment & Decision

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor recall notice, regulatory directive (DTI, DENR, FDA), or internal safety investigation finding |
| **Frequency** | ~2–4 recall assessments/year (most do not result in full recall) |
| **Volume** | Low frequency but high impact per event |
| **Owner** | Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Compliance Officer, VP Merchandising, Quality Manager, Category Manager, Legal Counsel, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recall trigger received: (a) vendor issues recall notice (e.g., power tool safety defect, paint lead content exceedance), (b) DTI/DENR/FDA issues product safety advisory, (c) internal quality investigation identifies safety risk (e.g., customer injury report linked to product), (d) media/social media report of product safety issue | Compliance Officer / Quality Manager | VP Merchandising | 30 min |
| 2 | Compliance Officer convenes Recall Assessment Team (Compliance, Quality, Category Manager, Legal, Supply Chain); initial assessment: (a) identify affected product(s) and lot/batch numbers, (b) determine affected inventory locations (DCs, stores, in-transit), (c) determine if product has been sold to customers (sales history by lot/batch), (d) assess risk severity: (i) **Critical** — injury or death risk (electrical shock, structural failure, toxic exposure), (ii) **High** — property damage risk or non-critical injury, (iii) **Medium** — product does not meet specification but no immediate safety risk, (iv) **Low** — labeling or documentation issue | Compliance Officer | VP Merchandising | 2 hours |
| 3 | System identifies: (a) all inventory locations holding affected lot/batch, (b) quantity on hand vs. quantity sold, (c) customer purchase records for sold items (POS transaction history with lot/batch traceability), (d) ecommerce order records with customer contact information | System | Compliance Officer | 30 min |
| 4 | Recall decision: (a) **Full Recall** (Critical/High severity) — remove all affected product from sale; notify all customers who purchased affected product; public advisory, (b) **Market Withdrawal** (Medium severity) — remove affected product from sale; customer notification via targeted communication; no public advisory unless escalated, (c) **Stock Correction** (Low severity) — replace or relabel affected inventory; no customer notification required, (d) **No Action** — risk assessment does not warrant recall; document decision with rationale | Recall Assessment Team | CEO / COO (Critical); VP Merchandising (High/Medium) | 1–2 hours |
| 5 | If Full Recall or Market Withdrawal: system immediately blocks affected SKU/lot from: (a) POS sale, (b) ecommerce availability, (c) store replenishment, (d) transfer orders | System / Compliance Officer | VP Supply Chain | 15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Lot/batch traceability across all inventory locations (W1615.3)
- Customer purchase history with lot/batch linkage (W1615.3)
- Product sale block at POS, ecommerce, and replenishment (W1615.5)
- Recall assessment record with decision documentation (W1615.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lot traceability gaps**: If lot/batch numbers are not consistently captured at GR (especially for DSD items), affected product cannot be precisely identified, forcing broader recall scope
- **Customer identification**: Many POS transactions are cash walk-in without loyalty card; only loyalty members and ecommerce customers can be directly notified
- **Speed of decision**: Critical recalls require CEO/COO authorization; if executives are unavailable, delay increases customer risk exposure
- **Media amplification**: Social media can escalate a minor product issue into a brand crisis before the recall assessment is complete

### Time Estimate
- Step 1: 30 min
- Step 2: 2 hours
- Step 3: 30 min
- Step 4: 1–2 hours
- Step 5: 15 min

## W1616. Product Recall Execution & Customer Notification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall decision (Full Recall or Market Withdrawal) from W1615 |
| **Frequency** | ~1–2 full recall executions/year |
| **Volume** | Variable — from single SKU/lot to multi-SKU recall |
| **Owner** | Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Compliance Officer, Store Operations, Marketing, Customer Service, DC Operations, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliance Officer creates Recall Execution Plan: (a) recall scope (SKU, lot/batch, date range), (b) affected locations (DCs and stores with affected inventory), (c) customer notification scope (purchased customers identifiable via loyalty/ecommerce records), (d) disposition plan for recalled product (return to vendor, quarantine for investigation, or destruction), (e) timeline: store-level product removal within 24 hours; customer notification within 48 hours | Compliance Officer | VP Merchandising | 2–4 hours |
| 2 | Store-level execution: (a) system sends recall alert to all affected store managers with product images, lot numbers, and instructions, (b) Store Manager assigns Stock Associates to pull affected product from sales floor and backroom, (c) Stock Associates scan and remove affected items; system moves to "Recalled" inventory status, (d) Store Manager confirms removal completion within 24 hours | Store Manager / Stock Associates | VP Store Operations | 2–4 hours/store |
| 3 | DC-level execution: (a) DC Supervisor pulls affected lots from available stock and quarantine zones, (b) segregates recalled product in designated recall staging area, (c) confirms removal completion | DC Supervisor | VP Supply Chain | 2–4 hours/DC |
| 4 | Customer notification: (a) **Loyalty members**: system generates notification list of loyalty members who purchased affected product; sends SMS/email with product details, risk explanation, and return instructions (full refund or replacement), (b) **Ecommerce customers**: system sends email to all ecommerce customers who purchased affected product; includes return shipping label for home delivery customers, (c) **General public** (Full Recall only): Marketing posts recall notice on buildright.com.ph, social media, and in-store poster; press release if DTI directs public notification | Marketing / Customer Service | CMO / VP Merchandising | 4–8 hours |
| 5 | In-store recall signage posted at: (a) entrance, (b) affected product aisle/shelf location, (c) customer service counter | Store Manager | VP Store Operations | 30 min/store |
| 6 | Store and DC submit daily recall progress reports until all affected product accounted for: (a) units pulled from shelf, (b) units returned by customers, (c) units unaccounted for (sold but not yet returned) | Store Manager / DC Supervisor | Compliance Officer | 15 min/day during recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Recall alert broadcast to affected stores with product details and lot numbers (W1616.2)
- Inventory status change to "Recalled" at store and DC (W1616.2–3)
- Loyalty member purchase history query and notification generation (W1616.4)
- Ecommerce customer notification with return label generation (W1616.4)
- Daily recall progress tracking dashboard (W1616.6)


### Pain Points / Risks
- **Slow-shelf-clearance risk**: affected units keep selling at 200 stores while paper notices circulate; mitigated by system recall alerts with product images and lot numbers, store pull confirmation, and POS transaction-level block on affected SKU/lots (steps 2–3)
- **Notification-gap risk**: identifiable customers who bought the hazard are never told; mitigated by loyalty-purchase-based notification lists (target >95% of identifiable customers) with SMS/email return instructions (step 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "records" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-scan of "scan and remove affected items" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-generate of "generates notification list of loyalty members who purchased affected product" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-336 (ensure controlled execution — Product Recall & Safety Compliance (PA-31.3).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- Step 1: 2–4 hours
- Step 2: 2–4 hours/store
- Step 3: 2–4 hours/DC
- Step 4: 4–8 hours
- Step 5: 30 min/store
- Step 6: 15 min/day during recall

## W1617. Product Recall Effectiveness Verification & Closure

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall execution reaches steady state (all locations confirmed removal; customer returns declining) |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | ~1–2 per year |
| **Owner** | Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Compliance Officer, Quality Manager, Legal, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliance Officer reviews recall effectiveness metrics: (a) % of affected inventory removed from stores and DCs (target: 100%), (b) % of identifiable customers notified (target: > 95%), (c) customer return rate of recalled product (target: > 50% for Critical recalls), (d) time from recall decision to store removal completion (target: < 24 hours) | Compliance Officer | VP Merchandising | 2 hours |
| 2 | Physical verification: Quality Manager or Internal Audit conducts spot checks at 5–10 stores to confirm recalled product is not on shelf; confirms recall signage is posted | Quality Manager / Internal Audit | Compliance Officer | 1 day |
| 3 | Disposition of recalled product: (a) vendor-liable: coordinate return to vendor per W88 with credit recovery, (b) BuildRight-liable: arrange destruction with DENR-compliant disposal for chemicals (W82) or general destruction with documentation for non-hazardous items, (c) investigation samples: retain 5–10 units for Quality Manager and Legal investigation | Quality Manager / DC Supervisor | VP Supply Chain / Legal | 2–4 hours |
| 4 | Financial settlement: (a) Controller calculates total recall cost (product cost + handling + disposal + customer refunds + vendor chargeback/recovery), (b) processes insurance claim if applicable (product liability insurance per W837), (c) records quality cost per W1614 | Controller | CFO | 4 hours |
| 5 | Recall closure: Compliance Officer prepares Recall Close-Out Report: (a) root cause summary, (b) effectiveness metrics, (c) total cost and recovery, (d) corrective actions implemented to prevent recurrence (vendor VCAR per W1610, procurement specification changes, inspection protocol updates), (e) lessons learned | Compliance Officer | VP Merchandising / CEO | 4 hours |
| 6 | Post-recall monitoring: Quality Manager monitors the affected SKU/category for 6 months post-recall for recurrence; monthly check of customer complaints for recalled product | Quality Manager | Compliance Officer | 30 min/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Recall effectiveness dashboard with removal, notification, and return rate metrics (W1617.1)
- Recalled product disposition tracking (W1617.3)
- Financial impact tracking linked to quality cost analysis (W1614) and insurance (W837) (W1617.4)
- Post-recall monitoring and complaint tracking (W1617.6)


### Pain Points / Risks
- **False-closure risk**: a recall declared closed with product still on shelf or returns still flowing; mitigated by effectiveness metrics (100% inventory removal, >95% notification, declining returns) plus Internal Audit shelf spot-checks at 5–10 stores (steps 1–2)
- **Residue risk**: recalled product re-enters sellable stock via customer returns or found stock after closure; mitigated by SKU/lot-level quarantine flags and DENR-compliant destruction or vendor-return disposition with documented credit recovery (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews recall effectiveness metrics" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "checks at 5–10 stores to confirm recalled product is not on shelf" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "documentation for non-hazardous items," (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-336 (ensure controlled execution — Product Recall & Safety Compliance (PA-31.3).)
- operational: Compliance Officer review and approval gate

- Step 2: 1 day
- Step 3: 2–4 hours
- Step 4: 4 hours

## W1618. Regulatory Incident Reporting & Government Notification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Product safety incident requiring government notification (customer injury, environmental release, regulatory violation) |
| **Frequency** | Rare (~1–3 incidents/year) |
| **Volume** | Low frequency, high regulatory impact |
| **Owner** | Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Compliance Officer, Legal Counsel, VP Merchandising, Quality Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Incident reported: (a) customer injury in-store or from product use, (b) environmental release (chemical spill at DC or store), (c) regulatory inspection finding (BIR, DENR, DOLE, DTI), (d) product safety complaint with injury allegation | Compliance Officer | VP Legal / CEO | Immediate |
| 2 | Compliance Officer classifies incident per reporting requirement: (a) DTI — consumer product safety incident (RA 7394), (b) DENR — hazardous material release (PD 1586, RA 6969), (c) FDA — if applicable product categories, (d) DOLE — workplace safety incident (if employee injured), (e) BIR — tax/compliance violation finding, (f) LGU — local ordinance violation | Compliance Officer | VP Legal | 1 hour |
| 3 | Prepare regulatory notification: (a) incident description with factual details (no admission of liability), (b) product identification (SKU, lot/batch, vendor), (c) actions taken (product removal, customer notification, remediation), (d) timeline of events, (e) Legal Counsel reviews before submission | Compliance Officer / Legal Counsel | CEO | 4–8 hours |
| 4 | Submit notification to appropriate agency within required timeline (DTI: within 48 hours; DENR: within 24 hours for significant release; DOLE: within 48 hours for serious workplace incident) | Compliance Officer | VP Legal | 1 hour |
| 5 | Respond to government follow-up: (a) provide additional information as requested, (b) facilitate government inspection if directed, (c) implement corrective actions ordered by agency, (d) Legal Counsel manages any formal investigation or enforcement proceedings | Compliance Officer / Legal Counsel | CEO | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Incident logging with classification and regulatory reporting requirements (W1618.2)
- Document management for regulatory correspondence (DOC-001)
- Corrective action tracking (W1610)
- Regulatory reporting calendar and deadline tracking (W1618.4)


### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missed-deadline risk**: agency notification windows (DTI 48 hours; DENR 24 hours for significant release; DOLE 48 hours) blow while facts are still being gathered; mitigated by classification against each agency's requirement at intake and submission within the shortest applicable timeline (steps 2, 4)
- **Self-incrimination risk**: a notification drafted as an admission of liability damages later defense; mitigated by factual, no-admission drafting reviewed by Legal before submission (step 3)

## W1619. Product Safety Complaint Triage & Escalation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer complaint involving product safety concern (injury, fire, electrical shock, chemical exposure, structural failure) |
| **Frequency** | ~20–40 safety complaints/month across all channels |
| **Volume** | ~250–500 per year; most resolved at Tier 1; ~5–10 escalated to full safety investigation |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Manager |
| **Participants** | Customer Service Agent, Customer Service Manager, Quality Manager, Compliance Officer, Legal Counsel |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Service Agent receives safety complaint via phone, email, in-store, or social media: (a) records complaint details (customer info, product details, incident description, photos if available, injury/property damage details), (b) classifies severity: (i) **Critical** — injury requiring medical attention, fire, structural collapse, (ii) **High** — near-miss with injury potential, significant property damage risk, (iii) **Medium** — product malfunction without immediate safety risk, (iv) **Low** — quality issue not safety-related (route to standard complaint handling) | Customer Service Agent | Customer Service Manager | 10 min |
| 2 | **Critical severity**: immediate escalation to Customer Service Manager and Compliance Officer; product images and incident details forwarded within 1 hour; customer advised to stop using product and offered immediate refund/replacement | Customer Service Agent / Customer Service Manager | Compliance Officer | 15 min |
| 3 | Compliance Officer reviews Critical/High complaints within 4 hours: (a) determines if complaint is isolated or part of a pattern (searches complaint database for similar reports on same SKU/lot), (b) if pattern detected (2+ similar complaints within 30 days): initiates recall assessment per W1615, (c) if isolated: initiates product investigation — request customer return the product for Quality Manager inspection | Compliance Officer | VP Legal | 30 min/complaint |
| 4 | Quality Manager inspects returned product: (a) visual and functional examination, (b) comparison to specification and control sample, (c) root cause hypothesis (manufacturing defect, misuse, installation error, design flaw), (d) if needed: sends to external testing laboratory for material/functional analysis | Quality Manager | Compliance Officer | 1–4 hours |
| 5 | Investigation conclusion: (a) **Product defect confirmed**: route to VCAR (W1610) and potential recall (W1615), (b) **Customer misuse/installation error**: customer education; no product action; consider updating product instructions or warning labels, (c) **Inconclusive**: monitor for additional complaints; keep case open for 90 days | Compliance Officer / Quality Manager | VP Merchandising | 30 min |
| 6 | Monthly: Compliance Officer reviews all safety complaint data — trend analysis by category, vendor, and complaint type; reports to Quality Manager and Category Managers for proactive quality improvement | Compliance Officer | VP Legal | 2 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Safety complaint logging with severity classification (W1619.1)
- Pattern detection across complaint database (W1619.3)
- Investigation case management with lifecycle tracking (W1619.4–5)
- Safety complaint trend analytics (W1619.6)


### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pattern-blindness risk**: isolated-looking complaints on the same SKU/lot hide a systemic defect (~5–10 escalate yearly from ~250–500); mitigated by the complaint-database pattern search on same-SKU/same-lot reports at Critical/High review within 4 hours (step 3)
- **Evidence-loss risk**: the failed unit is discarded or repaired by the customer before inspection; mitigated by product retrieval with visual/functional examination against specification and control sample (step 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "records complaint details" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews Critical/High complaints within 4 hours" (replaces manual Step 3).
- System auto-inspect of "inspects returned product" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-336 (ensure controlled execution — Product Recall & Safety Compliance (PA-31.3).)
- operational: Customer Service Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- Step 1: 10 min
- Step 2: 15 min
- Step 3: 30 min/complaint
- Step 4: 1–4 hours
- Step 5: 30 min

## W1620. Product Safety Testing & Certification Renewal Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Certification renewal cycle, new product introduction, or quality investigation |
| **Frequency** | ~50–100 certification renewals/year; ~20–30 new product certifications |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 SKUs requiring product safety certification |
| **Owner** | Quality Manager |
| **Participants** | Quality Manager, Category Manager, Buyer, third-party testing laboratory |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System tracks product safety certification status per SKU: (a) certification type (PS mark, ICC, FPA, DENR, FDA), (b) issuing body (DTI-BPS, FPA, DENR-EMB), (c) certification number and validity period, (d) renewal requirements (testing, documentation, fees) | System | Quality Manager | Automated |
| 2 | 120 days before certification expiry: system generates renewal task; Quality Manager coordinates with Category Manager and Buyer to: (a) determine if SKU will remain active (discontinuing SKUs do not need renewal), (b) collect product samples for testing, (c) engage accredited testing laboratory (preferably DTI-BPS recognized lab) | Quality Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1 hour/SKU |
| 3 | Testing laboratory conducts product testing per applicable Philippine National Standard (PNS): (a) electrical products: PNS IEC standards (safety, performance), (b) paint/chemical: PNS paint standards (lead content, volatile organic compounds), (c) construction materials: PNS structural standards (cement, steel, tiles), (d) timeline: 30–60 days for standard testing | Testing Laboratory | Quality Manager | 30–60 days |
| 4 | Test results received: (a) **Pass**: Quality Manager submits test report and application to DTI-BPS/FPA/DENR for certification renewal, (b) **Fail**: Quality Manager notifies Category Manager and Buyer; product cannot be sold after current certification expires; vendor notified for corrective action per W1610; inventory disposition decision needed (sell through before expiry, RTV, or scrap) | Quality Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2 hours/SKU |
| 5 | Renewal approval received from issuing body; update certification record in system; update product master with new certification validity period | Quality Manager | — | 30 min |
| 6 | Annual: Quality Manager reviews certification portfolio — (a) percentage of active SKUs with current certification, (b) upcoming renewal wave and resource planning, (c) testing cost analysis, (d) recommendation for SKU rationalization (SKUs with high certification cost relative to revenue) | Quality Manager | VP Supply Chain | 4 hours/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Product certification master per SKU with validity tracking (W1620.1)
- Automated renewal task generation with 120-day lead time (W1620.2)
- Test result repository with pass/fail documentation (W1620.4)
- Certification portfolio dashboard (W1620.6)


### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cert-lapse risk**: SKUs keep selling on lapsed PS/ICC/FPA/DENR certification between expiry and renewal; mitigated by the 120-day renewal task and the Pass/Fail gate completing renewal before expiry, with discontinuation routing for retiring SKUs (steps 2, 4)
- **Fail-handling risk**: product that failed re-testing continues flowing to stores; mitigated by Category Manager/Buyer notification on Fail with vendor-corrective or discontinuation decision before stock release (step 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "documentation, fees" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "generates renewal task" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-test of "Testing laboratory conducts product testing per applicable Philippine National…" (replaces manual Step 3).

- operational: Quality Manager review and approval gate

- Step 2: 1 hour/SKU
- Step 3: 30–60 days

## W1621. Consumer Product Safety Monitoring & Market Surveillance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Ongoing monitoring cycle |
| **Frequency** | Weekly scan; monthly summary |
| **Volume** | Continuous monitoring of regulatory feeds, social media, and competitor actions |
| **Owner** | Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Compliance Officer, Quality Manager, Marketing, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliance Officer monitors regulatory feeds: (a) DTI Consumer Safety Notices and advisories, (b) DENR environmental compliance updates, (c) FDA product safety alerts (for applicable categories), (d) Philippine Consumer Act amendments and implementing rules, (e) ASEAN product safety harmonization initiatives | Compliance Officer | VP Legal | 2 hours/week |
| 2 | Marketing monitors social media and online forums for: (a) product safety complaints mentioning BuildRight or carried brands, (b) viral posts about product issues in hardware/DIY category, (c) competitor recall notices or safety issues that may affect BuildRight customers using similar products | Marketing | Compliance Officer | 1 hour/week |
| 3 | Quality Manager monitors international product safety databases for vendors also supplying BuildRight: (a) EU RAPEX (rapid alert system for dangerous products), (b) US CPSC (Consumer Product Safety Commission) recalls, (c) Australian ACCC product safety recalls; if a vendor's product is recalled in another market, assess whether same product/lot is sold by BuildRight | Quality Manager | Compliance Officer | 1 hour/week |
