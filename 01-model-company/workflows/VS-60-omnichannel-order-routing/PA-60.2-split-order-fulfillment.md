## W2294. Multi-Source Fulfillment Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Split order across sources |
| **Frequency** | ~12K–17K/month |
| **Volume** | ~12K–17K/month |
| **Owner** | Order Management System |
| **Participants** | Ecom Ops Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System orchestrates: triggers pick at store, DC, sends drop-ship PO; tracks sub-orders against parent; determines delivery strategy: consolidate at DC, separate deliveries, or consolidate at store | System | Ecom Ops Manager | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- Order orchestration (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, W534 multi-origin) — the multi-source coordination
- Inventory (links to VS-05 / W105) — the per-source ATP
- Delivery (links to VS-56 / W548, VS-06 logistics) — the consolidation-vs-separate decision

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Orchestration-desync risk**: the sub-orders trigger out of sync (store picks before the drop-ship PO sends), producing inconsistent delivery; mitigated by the atomic-orchestration (all sub-orders trigger together)
- **Consolidation-missed-opportunity risk**: sub-orders ship separately when consolidation would have been cheaper; mitigated by the W2295 consolidation logic

### Automation Opportunity
- Orchestration fully automated with consolidation-preference

### Controls
- CTL-44 (sub-order orchestration integrity); operational: Ecom Ops Manager split-order monitoring

### Time Estimate
Automated; Ecom Ops Manager ~4 hours/month = **~50 hours/year**.

## W2295. Consolidated Delivery Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Sub-orders consolidated |
| **Frequency** | ~5K–8K/month |
| **Volume** | ~5K–8K/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Ecom Ops Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System identifies consolidation: same DC, same customer; holds first sub-order 24 hours for consolidation; ships together; reduces cost and improves experience; tracks consolidation rate | System / Logistics Coord | Ecom Ops Manager | Automated + ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Consolidation engine (links to VS-60 / W2294, VS-56 / W592) — the same-customer/same-DC consolidation logic
- Delivery (links to VS-06 / W52 fleet, VS-56) — the consolidated shipment
- Analytics (links to VS-28 / W113) — the consolidation-rate tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hold-too-long risk**: the 24-hour hold for consolidation delays the first sub-order past its SLA; mitigated by the SLA-priority override (ship immediately if the SLA is at-risk)
- **Consolidation-never-arrives risk**: the second sub-order is delayed, so the held first sub-order waits 24 hours then ships anyway, gaining nothing; mitigated by the hold-window-limit and the consolidation-rate monitoring

### Automation Opportunity
- Consolidation auto-identified; SLA-priority override auto-applied

### Controls
- CTL-410 (ensure controlled execution — Split-Order & Mixed-Basket Fulfillment (PA-60.2).)
- CTL (SLA-priority override integrity); operational: Logistics Coordinator consolidation-rate review

### Time Estimate
Automated + ~3 hours/month Logistics Coord = **~40 hours/year**.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System cancels specific sub-order(s), restores inventory, processes partial refund, keeps remaining active; Finance adjusts: partial refund, revenue for fulfilled only, promo adjustments | CSA / System / Finance | CS Supervisor | 10–15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Order cancellation (links to VS-10 / W98, VS-16 / W101 refund) — the partial cancellation and refund
- Inventory (links to VS-05 / W105) — the restored ATP
- Finance (links to VS-17 / W9, VS-157) — the partial-revenue and promo-adjustment

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cancellation-after-ship risk**: the customer cancels a sub-order that already shipped, requiring a return-not-delivery; mitigated by the cancellation-window (before ship-confirm) and the return-flow fallback
- **Promo-adjustment-complexity risk**: a promo that required the full basket (buy-A-get-B) now needs adjustment on a partial cancellation; mitigated by the promo-rule engine handling the partial-cancel case

### Automation Opportunity
- Partial cancellation auto-processed; promo-adjustment auto-computed

### Controls
- CTL-44 (partial-cancel integrity); CTL-39 (promo-adjustment accuracy); operational: CS Supervisor sign-off on partial-cancels >PHP 5K

### Time Estimate
~12 min/case × ~2,000/month = **~400 hours/month CSA** (distributed).

## W2301. Same-Day & Express Fulfillment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer selects express |
| **Frequency** | ~3K–5K/month |
| **Volume** | ~3K–5K/month |
| **Owner** | Ecommerce Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Staff, 3PL, Ecom Ops Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Routes only to stores with stock in radius; cutoff 12 PM; premium 3PL (GrabExpress, Lalamove Priority); store picks/packs in 2 hours; 3PL pickup in 1 hour; delivery in 4 hours; premium SLA tracking | System / Store Staff / 3PL | Ecom Ops Manager | Same day |

### System Touchpoints
- Express routing (links to VS-60 / W2286 routing, W548 on-demand) — the in-radius-with-stock routing
- Premium 3PL (links to VS-56 / W548) — the GrabExpress/Lalamove integration
- SLA (links to VS-60 / W2292) — the premium-SLA clock (4-hour)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **SLA-miss risk**: the 4-hour SLA is missed due to pick/pack/3PL-pickup delay, requiring a refund of the express premium; mitigated by the cutoff (12 PM) and the in-radius-with-stock precondition
- **Store-capacity risk**: express orders surge during peak, overwhelming store pick capacity; mitigated by the express-volume-cap per store per hour

### Automation Opportunity
- Express routing auto-filtered (in-radius-with-stock); SLA auto-tracked with premium-refund on miss

### Controls
- CTL-410 (ensure controlled execution — Split-Order & Mixed-Basket Fulfillment (PA-60.2).)
- CTL (express-SLA integrity); operational: Ecom Ops Manager express-SLA-miss review

### Time Estimate
~Same-day per order; Ecom Ops Manager ~4 hours/day during express window = **~1,000 hours/year** (absorbed into ops).
