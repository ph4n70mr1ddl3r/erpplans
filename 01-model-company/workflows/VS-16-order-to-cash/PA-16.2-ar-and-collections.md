## W889. Customer AR Aging Analysis & Collection Prioritization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly collection cycle; daily for high-risk accounts |
| **Frequency** | Weekly (comprehensive); daily (exception monitoring) |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 trade accounts + ~200 corporate accounts; ~3,500 AR invoices/month |
| **Owner** | AR Supervisor |
| **Participants** | AR Clerk, Sales Rep, Finance Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates AR aging report by bucket (current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–120, > 120 days) segmented by entity, customer tier, and collection officer | System | — | Automated |
| 2 | AR Clerk reviews aging and flags accounts requiring action: (a) accounts crossing 30-day threshold for first reminder, (b) accounts in 61–90 day bucket for escalation, (c) accounts > 90 days for write-off assessment per W81 | AR Clerk | AR Supervisor | 1 hour/week |
| 3 | AR Clerk prioritizes collection queue by: (a) amount at risk (largest balances first), (b) aging severity, (c) customer tier (corporate > trade), (d) payment history score, (e) sales rep input on customer situation | AR Clerk | AR Supervisor | 30 min/week |
| 4 | AR Supervisor assigns collection accounts to AR Clerks balanced by load and territory | AR Supervisor | — | 15 min/week |
| 5 | Weekly collection review meeting: AR Supervisor, AR Clerks, and Sales Reps review top 20 overdue accounts, agree on action plan per account (call, visit, demand letter, legal referral) | AR Supervisor | Finance Manager | 1 hour/week |
| 6 | System generates DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) and collection effectiveness index (CEI) metrics for management reporting per W35 | System | — | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- AR aging report generation with configurable bucket definitions
- Automated exception alerts: accounts crossing aging thresholds, payments > 10 days overdue
- Collection queue management with assignment and tracking
- DSO and CEI calculation integrated with management reporting (W35)
- Customer account notes and collection history log

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Manual prioritization bias**: relying on AR Clerk judgment may miss systemic collection issues in smaller accounts
- **Sales-Collections tension**: Sales Reps may resist aggressive collection on their accounts for relationship reasons
- **Data freshness**: AR aging is only as current as the last posting; mid-day payments may not reflect


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "generates AR aging report by bucket" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews aging and flags accounts requiring action" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-collect of "collection queue by" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: AR Supervisor review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
~3 hours/week for comprehensive aging review and prioritization across ~5,400 accounts

### Staffing Implication
At ~5,400 active AR accounts with weekly collection cycles, 2–3 dedicated AR Clerks for collections + 1 AR Supervisor is adequate. Collection call volume: ~150–200 calls/week based on aging distribution.

## W1200. Trade Account Monthly Statement Review, Credit Limit Recalibration & Churn Prevention

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly trade account review cycle (first week of month) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly comprehensive review; weekly for high-risk accounts |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 active trade accounts; ~200 corporate accounts |
| **Owner** | AR Supervisor |
| **Participants** | AR Supervisor, Credit Analyst, Trade Sales Manager, Store Manager, Controller |

### Background

Trade (contractor/builder) and corporate (developer/institutional) accounts represent 40% of BuildRight Depot's revenue but carry significant credit risk. Trade customers operate in a cyclical, project-based business with irregular cash flows; contractor defaults are common in the Philippine construction industry. This workflow manages the monthly proactive review of trade account health — not just collection of overdue amounts (covered by W889, W890) but forward-looking credit limit management, churn detection, and relationship retention.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Monthly trade account health report**: System generates "Trade Account Health Report" for all active trade and corporate accounts: (a) current balance and aging (30/60/90/120+ days), (b) credit utilization (outstanding ÷ credit limit), (c) payment behavior score (on-time % over trailing 6 months), (d) purchase volume trend (current month vs. trailing 3-month average), (e) days sales outstanding (DSO), (f) accounts flagged for: (i) credit utilization > 80%, (ii) payment score < 70%, (iii) purchase volume decline > 30%, (iv) new account < 6 months with first late payment | System | AR Supervisor | Automated |
| 2 | **Account segmentation and prioritization**: AR Supervisor categorizes accounts into four segments: (a) **Healthy** (green): utilization < 60%, payment score > 90%, stable or growing purchases → standard monitoring, (b) **Watch** (yellow): utilization 60–80% or payment score 70–90% or purchase decline 15–30% → proactive outreach and possible credit limit review, (c) **At Risk** (orange): utilization > 80% or payment score < 70% or purchase decline > 30% → immediate credit limit review and collection escalation per W889, (d) **Churn Risk** (blue): previously healthy account with sudden purchase volume decline > 40% over 2 months → sales outreach for retention | AR Supervisor | Controller | 2 hours/month |
| 3 | **Credit limit recalibration**: For Watch and At Risk accounts: Credit Analyst reviews account profile: (a) updates financial standing based on recent payment behavior and trade references, (b) recalculates appropriate credit limit based on: (i) trailing 6-month average monthly purchases, (ii) payment history, (iii) years of relationship, (iv) external credit data if available, (c) recommends: (i) credit limit increase (for healthy growing accounts), (ii) credit limit decrease (for deteriorating accounts), (iii) credit hold (for accounts with 120+ day overdue per W229), (d) Controller approves changes > 20% or > PHP 500K | Credit Analyst | Controller | 15 min/account |
| 4 | **Churn prevention outreach**: For Churn Risk accounts: Trade Sales Manager contacts customer within 5 business days: (a) determines reason for volume decline (project completed, competitor switch, financial difficulty, seasonal slowdown), (b) for competitor switch: offers competitive re-engagement package (discount, extended terms, or loyalty bonus points), (c) for financial difficulty: explores restructured payment plan or reduced credit limit, (d) for project completion: discusses upcoming project pipeline and material needs; documents outcome in CRM | Trade Sales Manager | VP Store Ops | 20 min/account |
| 5 | **Store-level account relationship review**: For trade accounts serviced primarily through specific stores: Store Manager reviews top 10 trade accounts at their store monthly: (a) discusses account health with Sales Associates who interact with the customer, (b) identifies opportunities for cross-sell or upsell based on purchase history, (c) escalates any relationship concerns (complaints, competitor interest) to Trade Sales Manager | Store Manager | Trade Sales Manager | 30 min/month |
| 6 | **Executive review**: Quarterly: Controller presents trade portfolio summary to CFO: (a) total trade receivable balance and aging distribution, (b) bad debt expense trend and allowance adequacy, (c) portfolio quality distribution (Healthy/Watch/At Risk/Default), (d) credit limit changes summary, (e) churn rate and recovery rate, (f) recommended reserve adjustments and write-off proposals | Controller | CFO | 2 hours/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Automated Trade Account Health Report with multi-dimensional scoring (W1200.1)
- Credit limit recalibration module with approval workflow (W1200.3)
- CRM integration for churn prevention outreach tracking (W1200.4)
- Trade portfolio analytics dashboard for executive review (W1200.6)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Contractor default in Philippine construction**: Philippine construction industry has high contractor failure rate; small contractors may abandon projects and leave suppliers unpaid; mitigated by conservative initial credit limits (W160), regular recalibration, and credit insurance for limits > PHP 1M
- **Personal relationships overriding credit discipline**: Trade Sales Managers may resist credit limit reductions on long-standing customer relationships; mitigated by system-driven segmentation in Step 2 and Controller approval requirement in Step 3
- **Seasonal volume fluctuations misread as churn**: construction activity is seasonal in the Philippines (slow during rainy season Jun–Aug); mitigated by comparing to same-month prior year rather than trailing 3-month average
- **Manual effort for 5,200 accounts**: reviewing every trade account monthly is labor-intensive; mitigated by automated health scoring and segmentation that focuses manual effort on flagged accounts only


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count health report" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review," (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-calculate of "calculates appropriate credit limit based on" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: conservative initial credit limits (W160), regular recalibration, and credit insurance for limits > PHP 1M; operational: system-driven segmentation in Step 2 and Controller approval requirement in Step 3; operational: comparing to same-month prior year rather than trailing 3-month average

### Staffing Implication
- **AR Supervisor**: ~6 hours/month on account segmentation and prioritization. Absorbed within existing role.
- **Credit Analyst**: ~15 hours/month on credit limit recalibration (for ~100 Watch/At Risk accounts). Absorbed within existing role.
- **Trade Sales Manager**: ~10 hours/month on churn prevention outreach (~30 accounts). Absorbed within existing role.
- **Store Managers**: ~30 min/month per store on top-10 trade account review. Absorbed within existing role.
- **No permanent incremental headcount**.

## W1414. Customer Trade Account Credit Insurance Premium Review, Claims Filing & Recovery Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual policy renewal; quarterly premium review; or trade account write-off event (W81) triggering insurance claim |
| **Frequency** | Annual policy renewal; quarterly premium reconciliation; claims as needed (estimated 5–10 claims/year for accounts written off > PHP 500,000) |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 trade accounts insured under group credit insurance policy; ~5–10 claims/year for significant bad debts |
| **Owner** | AR Supervisor |
| **Participants** | AR Supervisor, Controller, CFO, Credit Insurance Underwriter, Insurance Broker, Legal Head |

### Background

BuildRight extends trade credit to ~5,200 trade and ~200 corporate accounts, creating significant accounts receivable exposure. Credit insurance protects BuildRight against non-payment due to customer insolvency, bankruptcy, or protracted default. At BuildRight's scale (monthly AR of ~PHP 500M–1B across trade/corporate accounts), credit insurance is a prudent risk management tool that converts uncertain bad debt risk into a known annual premium cost. Philippine trade credit insurance is provided by underwriters such as Coface, Euler Hermes (Allianz Trade), and local insurers, typically covering 75–90% of the insured receivable.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Policy selection and renewal**: annually, AR Supervisor and Controller evaluate credit insurance options: (a) review current policy coverage (% of receivable insured, maximum tenor, country/customer exclusions), (b) obtain competitive quotes from 2–3 underwriters, (c) negotiate premium rate (typically 0.15–0.40% of insured receivable volume), (d) CFO approves policy selection and premium budget; policy covers 80% of approved receivable balances up to a portfolio limit | AR Supervisor / Controller | CFO | 2 days/year |
| 2 | **Customer coverage submission**: AR Supervisor submits the complete trade account portfolio to the underwriter for coverage assessment: (a) each customer's credit limit request (aligned with BuildRight's internal credit limits per W24), (b) customer financial statements (for corporate accounts > PHP 5M credit limit), (c) payment history (aging and days sales outstanding per customer), (d) industry risk classification; underwriter approves, reduces, or declines coverage per customer | AR Supervisor | Underwriter | 1 week/annual cycle |
| 3 | **Quarterly premium reconciliation**: AR Supervisor reconciles quarterly premium invoice from underwriter: (a) premium = insured receivable balance at quarter-end × premium rate, (b) verify insured receivable balance matches AR sub-ledger, (c) verify no premium charged for cash/advance-payment customers, (d) submit premium invoice for payment per W7; typical annual premium at 0.25% rate on ~PHP 500M average insured AR = ~PHP 1.25M/year | AR Supervisor | Controller | 2 hours/quarter |
| 4 | **New customer coverage request**: when a new trade account is onboarded (W460) or an existing account's credit limit is increased (W1300), AR Supervisor submits a coverage request to the underwriter: (a) customer name and TIN, (b) requested credit limit, (c) customer trade references, (d) payment terms; underwriter responds within 5–10 business days with approved coverage limit | AR Supervisor | — | 30 min/request |
| 5 | **Claim filing — protracted default**: when a trade account is identified for write-off (W81) after exhaustion of collection efforts (W108, W812): (a) AR Supervisor gathers claim documentation: original invoice copies, delivery receipts, proof of demand letters, statement of account, collection history log, customer TIN and registration, (b) submits claim to underwriter within policy notification period (typically 60–90 days from due date or 180 days from invoice date), (c) AR Supervisor certifies that BuildRight has complied with all policy conditions (collection efforts, notice of overdue, retention of title if applicable) | AR Supervisor | Controller | 4–8 hours/claim |
| 6 | **Claim filing — insolvency/bankruptcy**: if a trade account customer files for bankruptcy or is placed under receivership: (a) Legal Head provides court filing documentation, (b) AR Supervisor submits claim with proof of insolvency (court order, SEC petition, published notice), (c) claim must be filed within 30 days of knowledge of insolvency event per most policy terms | AR Supervisor / Legal Head | CFO | 4–8 hours/claim |
| 7 | Underwriter reviews claim: (a) verifies customer was covered under policy at time of sale, (b) verifies credit limit was not exceeded, (c) verifies BuildRight complied with notification requirements, (d) may request additional documentation or conduct independent investigation; claim processing time: 30–90 days | Underwriter | — | External |
| 8 | **Claim settlement**: underwriter approves claim and pays indemnity: 80% of approved receivable (per policy terms); AR Supervisor receives payment, posts to GL: Dr. Cash / Cr. Credit Insurance Recovery (other income); remaining 20% uninsured portion remains as BuildRight's loss per W81 | AR Supervisor / Treasury | Controller | 30 min |
| 9 | **Subrogation**: if the customer later pays (full or partial), BuildRight must remit the proportional recovery to the underwriter per subrogation clause; AR Supervisor tracks subrogation obligations in system | AR Supervisor | — | 15 min/recovery |

### System Touchpoints
- Credit insurance policy master: underwriter details, policy number, coverage %, premium rate, portfolio limit, renewal date (W1414.1)
- Customer coverage tracker: per-customer approved insurance limit from underwriter, linked to credit limit master (W24, W1414.2)
- Quarterly premium reconciliation: AR sub-ledger balance × premium rate vs. underwriter invoice (W1414.3)
- Claim filing workspace: document checklist, submission tracking, underwriter communication log (W1414.5–6)
- Claim settlement posting: cash receipt with GL coding to credit insurance recovery account (W1414.8)
- Subrogation obligation tracker: customer recovery events linked to settled claims (W1414.9)
- Annual policy performance dashboard: premiums, claims, recovery, loss ratio (W1414.10)
- Integration with W24 (credit application — credit limit source), W81 (bad debt write-off — claim trigger), W108 (collections — collection history for claim), W812 (field collection — escalation documentation), W460 (customer onboarding — coverage request trigger), W1300 (credit limit review — coverage adjustment)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Underwriter coverage reductions**: the credit insurer may reduce or withdraw coverage for specific customers (especially small contractors during economic downturns), leaving BuildRight exposed to bad debt risk without insurance protection for those accounts
- **Claim denial risk**: underwriters may deny claims if BuildRight cannot prove it followed the policy's notification and collection procedures (e.g., failure to notify underwriter of overdue account within 60-day window, or continuing to ship to a customer after knowledge of financial difficulty)
- **Premium cost vs. bad debt loss**: at ~PHP 1.25M/year in premiums, the insurance is cost-effective only if BuildRight's annual bad debt write-offs would exceed PHP 1.56M without insurance (80% recovery × PHP 1.56M = PHP 1.25M); at a 0.5% bad debt rate on ~PHP 6B annual trade AR, expected bad debts are ~PHP 30M — insurance is highly cost-effective
- **Policy exclusions**: credit insurance typically excludes government accounts, related-party transactions, and foreign currency receivables — precisely the categories with elevated default risk

## W1420. Trade Account Monthly Statement Generation, Aging Analysis & Field Collection Route Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly AR cycle (1st business day of the month); or weekly for high-risk accounts |
| **Frequency** | Monthly statement generation (all 5,200 trade accounts); weekly aging review (Watch and At-Risk accounts per W1200 segmentation); daily field collection visits for overdue accounts |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 trade accounts generating ~3,500 AR invoices/month; ~500–800 accounts in active collection at any time; ~30–50 field collection visits per week across all regions |
| **Owner** | AR Supervisor |
| **Participants** | AR Supervisor, Collection Officer, Trade Sales Manager, Regional Sales Coordinator, Controller |

### Background

BuildRight Depot's trade and corporate accounts (5,200 trade + 200 corporate) represent ~40% of total revenue (~PHP 2.5B/month) and are the primary source of accounts receivable risk. Unlike retail POS transactions (cash/card/e-wallet — immediate payment), trade accounts have Net 30–60 day payment terms, creating a receivables portfolio of ~PHP 500M–1B at any time. Effective collection requires more than sending statements — it requires field-level relationship management: (a) Philippine trade customers (contractors, builders) often prefer face-to-face collection, (b) collection officers must travel to customer sites across the Philippines (200 store regions across Mindanao, Visayas, Luzon), (c) route planning must optimize travel time to maximize collection visits per day, (d) collection activity data must feed back into credit scoring per W24/W1200. This workflow manages the monthly statement cycle, aging analysis, and field collection route planning for BuildRight's trade receivables.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Monthly statement generation** (1st business day): System generates monthly statements for all 5,200 trade accounts: (a) opening balance, (b) invoices raised during month (with invoice number, date, amount, PO reference), (c) payments received during month, (d) closing balance, (e) payment due date per terms (Net 30 or Net 60), (f) statements emailed automatically to customer contact on file; hard copy mailed to customers without email (estimated ~10% of trade accounts) | System / AR Clerk | AR Supervisor | 2–3 hours (review + exception handling) |
| 2 | **Weekly aging analysis**: AR Supervisor reviews weekly aging report: (a) current (0–30 days), (b) 31–60 days, (c) 61–90 days, (d) 90+ days, (e) identify accounts moving to next aging bucket — these are prioritized for collection, (f) compare to prior week: is overall AR health improving or deteriorating? | AR Supervisor | Controller | 1–2 hours/week |
| 3 | **Collection prioritization and route planning**: Collection Officer plans weekly field collection route: (a) prioritize by: (i) accounts 90+ days overdue (highest priority), (ii) accounts moving from 60→90 days, (iii) accounts with broken payment promises, (iv) high-value accounts (> PHP 500K overdue), (b) group visits by geographic area to minimize travel time (cluster customers by city/municipality), (c) schedule 6–8 visits per day per collection officer, (d) Trade Sales Manager provides customer relationship context (personal issues, project delays affecting payment) | Collection Officer / Trade Sales Manager | AR Supervisor | 2–3 hours/week |
| 4 | **Field collection execution**: Collection Officer visits trade account customer: (a) present statement and discuss outstanding invoices, (b) obtain commitment: (i) specific payment date, (ii) partial payment collected on-site (cash or check), (iii) payment plan for large overdue amounts, (c) update collection notes in system: visit date, customer contact, commitment details, payment collected, (d) if customer refuses or is unreachable: escalate to W812 (field collection — formal demand) | Collection Officer | AR Supervisor | 6–8 visits/day |
| 5 | **Collection performance tracking**: (a) AR Supervisor tracks weekly collection metrics: (i) total collections vs. target, (ii) DSO (days sales outstanding) trend, (iii) collection effectiveness index (CEI), (iv) field visit conversion rate (visits resulting in payment commitment), (b) Controller reviews monthly: are we on track to keep bad debt below 0.5% of trade revenue? (c) escalate to CFO if DSO exceeds 45 days for 2+ consecutive months | AR Supervisor | Controller | 2–3 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints
- Monthly statement generator: auto-generated from AR sub-ledger with email/print distribution (W1420.1)
- Aging analysis dashboard: real-time aging buckets with drill-down to invoice level, trend charts, exception highlighting (W1420.2)
- Collection route planner: geographic clustering of overdue accounts, Google Maps/fleet routing integration, visit scheduling (W1420.3)
- Field collection mobile app: visit logging, payment collection recording, customer signature capture, GPS check-in (W1420.4)
- Collection performance dashboard: DSO, CEI, aging distribution, collection target vs. actual (W1420.5)
- Integration with W24 (credit scoring — collection activity feeds credit review), W1200 (trade account health — segmentation input), W108 (collections — formal demand letters), W812 (field collection — escalation), W81 (bad debt write-off — terminal action)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Philippine collection culture**: Filipino business culture emphasizes personal relationships (utang na loob, pakikisama); trade customers may prioritize paying suppliers with whom they have deeper personal connections over those who send formal demand letters; mitigated by Trade Sales Manager involvement in collection visits
- **Geographic dispersion**: trade customers are spread across the Philippines' 7,641 islands; collection officers in Visayas and Mindanao may need to travel by ferry/boat to reach customers on smaller islands, limiting daily visit capacity
- **Seasonal contractor cash flow**: Philippine construction slows during rainy season (June–August); contractors may delay payments not because they are insolvent but because their own project payments are delayed; requires judgment to distinguish temporary delay from actual default
- **Manual field collection effort**: at ~30–50 visits/week, field collection is labor-intensive; opportunity for mobile app automation and GPS route optimization

- System auto-review of "reviews weekly aging report" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-route of "route planning" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: Trade Sales Manager involvement in collection visits

### Time Estimate
- Monthly statement generation: 2–3 hours/month (mostly automated)
- Weekly aging analysis: 1–2 hours/week = 52–104 hours/year
- Weekly route planning: 2–3 hours/week = 104–156 hours/year
- Field collection execution: 6–8 visits/day × 4–5 days/week × 2–3 hours/visit = ~40–60 hours/week for 2–3 collection officers
- Performance tracking: 2–3 hours/week = 104–156 hours/year

## W1540. Trade Account Monthly Statement Generation, Aging Analysis & Collection Priority Scoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Monthly statements for ~5,200 trade accounts; aging analysis weekly |
| **Volume** | ~3,500 AR invoices/month generating ~5,200 statements |
| **Owner** | AR Supervisor |
| **Participants** | AR Supervisor, AR Clerk, Collection Agent, Trade Account Manager, Credit Manager |

### Background

With ~5,200 trade/corporate accounts generating ~PHP 1.86B in monthly B2B revenue (30% of total), effective AR management is critical for BuildRight's cash flow. Monthly statement generation provides each trade customer with a comprehensive account summary, while aging analysis and collection priority scoring ensure that collection efforts are focused on the highest-risk accounts. This workflow bridges the gap between invoice-level processing (W8) and escalated collections (W108) with systematic prioritization.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Monthly statement generation**: System generates statements for all active trade accounts — opening balance, all invoices and credit memos for the month, payments received, closing balance, current amount due, overdue amount by aging bucket (Current, 1–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days, > 90 days); statements formatted per BIR requirements with BuildRight TIN, customer TIN, and invoice references | System | AR Supervisor | Automated |
