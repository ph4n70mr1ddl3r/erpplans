## W3225. SLA Performance & Vendor Scorecard Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly review cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly business review |
| **Volume** | ~30–60 agencies/contractors |
| **Owner** | Contingent Workforce Manager |
| **Participants** | Contingent Workforce Mgr, User Dept, Procurement (links to VS-34/VS-67), Finance, Agency |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contingent Workforce Mgr/Procurement compile scorecard per contractor: SLA/KPI performance (W3212), quality audit scores, coverage/reliability (W3220), HSE (W3219/W3228), compliance attestations (W3210/W3226), invoice accuracy (W3221), customer/user satisfaction | Contingent Workforce Mgr / User Dept | CHRO | Monthly |
| 2 | Conduct quarterly business review (QBR) with each contractor: present scorecard, discuss improvement actions, credit/penalty application (W3212), recognition for top performers, improvement plans for underperformers, forward demand (W3209); document and follow up | Contingent Workforce Mgr / Procurement | CHRO | Quarterly |
| 3 | Drive performance into contract decisions: feed scorecard into renewal/re-bid (W3216), preferred-supplier status, vendor master (links to VS-67/VS-03.3 vendor scorecard methodology), and category strategy (W3209) | Contingent Workforce Mgr / Procurement | CHRO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Scorecard/BI (links to VS-28/VS-67); procurement (links to VS-34); contract (W3213)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric-manipulation risk**: Contractor games KPIs; mitigated by balanced scorecard and audit
- **Relationship deterioration**: Punitive scorecard use damages partnership; mitigated by constructive QBR approach

### Time Estimate
- Monthly compile + quarterly QBR

## W3226. Misclassification Audit & DOLE Inspection Defense

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual compliance audit; DOLE inspection/notice; worker regularization complaint |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | All engagements; ~1–5 DOLE/complaint events/year |
| **Owner** | Labor Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Labor Compliance Mgr, Legal, HR (links to VS-84), Internal Audit (links to VS-21), Contingent Workforce Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Labor Compliance/Internal Audit (links to VS-21) conduct periodic misclassification audit: sample engagements, apply four-fold test (W3215), review agency DOLE/SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG remittance evidence, wage-order compliance, contract terms vs actual practice; identify risk-rated findings | Labor Compliance Mgr / Internal Audit | CHRO | Annual + event |
| 2 | Remediate findings: convert confirmed employees (W3230), restructure engagements (W3211), strengthen supervisor training, recover solidary exposure where possible; track to closure | Labor Compliance Mgr / HR / Legal | CHRO | Per finding |
| 3 | On DOLE inspection or NLRC complaint (links to VS-84.2/VS-100), defend: produce contracts, agency licenses/remittance proof, classification rationale, four-fold-test documentation, supervisor-training evidence; manage conciliation/settlement per VS-84.2 DOLE process; reserve for loss (links to VS-100 W3277) | Labor Compliance Mgr / Legal | General Counsel | Event-driven |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/audit (links to VS-21/VS-22); legal matter (links to VS-100); payroll (links to VS-19.2); risk register (links to VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **DOLE-finding risk**: Labor-only-contracting finding → mass regularization and back-pay; mitigated by annual audit and structuring discipline (W3211)
- **Solidary-liability realization**: BuildRight pays agency's unpaid obligations; mitigated by remittance verification and indemnity recovery

### Time Estimate
- 4–8 weeks/annual audit; event-driven for inspections

## W3227. Contingent Workforce Spend Analytics & Cost Control

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close; quarterly cost review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Portfolio-wide contingent spend |
| **Owner** | Contingent Workforce Manager |
| **Participants** | Contingent Workforce Mgr, Finance (links to VS-17.4/VS-34.3), Procurement, User Depts |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance/Contingent Workforce Mgr compile spend analytics: by category (security/janitorial/promodizer/etc.), by location, by agency, by GL/cost center (links to VS-34.3), vs budget (links to VS-33) and prior period; compute cost per unit (per store, per sqm, per shift) | Finance Analyst / Contingent Workforce Mgr | CFO | Monthly |
| 2 | Analyze drivers and opportunities: rate vs benchmark (W3213), overtime/premium spend, idle/overstaffing, SLA-credit recovery, category consolidation, demand-management (reduce unnecessary contingent hours), insource/outsource (W3231) | Contingent Workforce Mgr / Procurement | CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | Execute cost actions: renegotiate rates (W3213), consolidate suppliers, eliminate low-value spend, enforce PO compliance (W3217); track savings vs target and report to finance/exec | Contingent Workforce Mgr / Finance | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Spend analytics/BI (links to VS-28); expense monitoring (links to VS-34.3); FP&A (links to VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Maverick-spend risk**: Off-contract engagements inflate cost and compliance risk; mitigated by PO gating and analytics visibility
- **Budget-overrun risk**: Unplanned contingent usage exceeds budget; mitigated by demand planning (W3209) and monthly tracking

### Time Estimate
- Monthly + quarterly

## W3228. Incident, Injury & Contractor Claims Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contractor-worker or contractor-caused incident |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~50–200 incidents/year (minor to serious) |
| **Owner** | Safety Manager |
| **Participants** | Safety Mgr (links to VS-24), Risk & Insurance (links to VS-26), Legal, User Dept, Agency, Contractor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Respond to incident: first aid/medical, secure scene, document (photos, witness statements, time/location), notify Safety and agency; for serious injury/fatality, notify DOLE per VS-24.1 (occupational accident reporting) and preserve evidence; contractor-worker medical care is agency/ECC responsibility | Safety Officer / User Dept | Safety Mgr | Per incident |
| 2 | Investigate root cause: Safety/Agency joint investigation, determine cause (worker error, equipment, process, environment), corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), assign responsibility (BuildRight vs contractor vs shared); document for HSE metrics (links to VS-24.1) and DOLE | Safety Mgr / Agency | Safety Mgr | Per incident |
| 3 | Manage claims and recovery: contractor workers' compensation via agency/ECC; third-party/property-damage claims via BuildRight/contractor insurance (W3214); subrogation where third party at fault; legal matter for disputes (links to VS-100); reserve for loss (W3277) | Risk & Insurance Mgr / Legal | VP Finance | Per incident |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE/incident (links to VS-24); insurance/claims (links to VS-26); legal (links to VS-100); risk register (links to VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Liability-attribution risk**: Disputed responsibility for incident/cost; mitigated by thorough investigation and contract terms (W3213/W3214)
- **Underreporting risk**: Minor incidents unreported, masking systemic issues; mitigated by reporting culture and analytics (W3225)

### Time Estimate
- Per incident; days–weeks for serious

## W3229. Contractor Fraud, Theft & Background Investigation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Loss event (links to VS-23); anomaly; tip/complaint |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~10–50 investigations/year |
| **Owner** | Loss Prevention Manager |
| **Participants** | Loss Prevention Mgr (links to VS-23), Security, Legal, User Dept, Agency, HR |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Loss Prevention (links to VS-23.1) detect and triage: shrinkage anomaly, cash-handling discrepancy (links to VS-08/VS-81), asset theft (links to VS-35/VS-99), timesheet/deliverable fraud (W3220), collusion with BuildRight employees; initiate investigation per VS-23 methodology | Loss Prevention Officer | Loss Prevention Mgr | Per case |
| 2 | Investigate: evidence collection (CCTV links to VS-23.2, access logs, transaction data), interview (with agency present — not direct BuildRight discipline), coordinate with agency for worker removal/cooperation; preserve chain of custody for potential criminal/civil action (links to VS-100) | Loss Prevention / Legal / Agency | Loss Prevention Mgr | Per case |
| 3 | Resolve: recovery (civil/criminal per RA 7895 Anti-Fencing for stolen goods), agency charge-back/disqualification (W3216), tighten controls (access, supervision, screening W3223), feed lessons to risk register (links to VS-21.2) and scorecard (W3225) | Loss Prevention / Legal | Loss Prevention Mgr | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Loss prevention (links to VS-23); CCTV/access (links to VS-23.2); legal (links to VS-100); risk register (links to VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Recidivism risk**: Problematic worker re-deployed by agency; mitigated by shared industry watchlists and agency contractual obligation
- **Boundary-violation risk**: BuildRight directing discipline during investigation; mitigated by agency involvement and legal guidance

### Time Estimate
- Per case; days–weeks

## W3230. Conversion-to-Regular & Headcount Boundary Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Misclassification finding (W3226); 5-month/6-month statutory thresholds; strategic insource decision (W3231) |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~50–200 conversions/year |
| **Owner** | Labor Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Labor Compliance Mgr, HR (links to VS-19), Payroll (links to VS-19.2), Finance, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Labor Compliance/HR identify conversion triggers: workers approaching statutory engagement-duration thresholds (6-month constructive-regularization per W555 context), misclassification findings (W3226), strategic insourcing (W3231), performance-based conversion offer; compute back-pay/ benefits liability (W3215) | Labor Compliance Mgr / HR | CHRO | Per case |
| 2 | HR/Recruitment (links to VS-19.1) execute conversion: offer regular employment, onboard as employee (master data, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG enrollment, contract), calculate and pay back-wages/benefits/13th-month/SIL per NLRC/DOLE order where applicable; recover from agency per indemnity where contractually liable | HR / Payroll / Legal | CHRO | Per case |
| 3 | Manage headcount boundary: maintain ratio guardrails (W3209), update workforce plan (links to VS-19.3), ensure converted roles reflected in headcount/budget (links to VS-33/VS-17.4); monitor for future triggers | Labor Compliance Mgr / Finance | CHRO | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Employee master/payroll (links to VS-19); workforce planning (links to VS-19.3); finance/budget (links to VS-17.4/VS-33)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mass-conversion risk**: Systematic misclassification → large back-pay and headcount shock; mitigated by structuring discipline (W3211) and early conversion
- **Budget-disruption risk**: Unplanned conversions strain budget; mitigated by boundary management and reserves

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 weeks per conversion (longer for contested/back-pay cases)

## W3231. Outsourced Function Transition & Insourcing/Outsourcing Decisions

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategic review (W3209); performance/compliance failure; cost benchmarking; capability-build |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~1–5 function transitions/year |
| **Owner** | Contingent Workforce Manager |
| **Participants** | Contingent Workforce Mgr, HR, User Dept, Finance, Legal, Procurement, Change (links to VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build business case for insource/outsource: total-cost comparison (W3209), capability/quality/control considerations, risk (W3226), strategic alignment, capital/transition cost (W3188 for facilities, VS-99 for technology); obtain approval per authority matrix | Contingent Workforce Mgr / Finance | CEO | 4–8 weeks |
| 2 | Plan and execute transition: for insource — recruit/hire (links to VS-19.1), train (links to VS-19.4), build capability, convert where applicable (W3230), terminate outsource contract (W3216); for outsource — select agency (W3210), structure contract (W3211), transfer function/knowledge, redeploy employees | Contingent Workforce Mgr / HR / User Dept | CHRO | 8–24 weeks |
| 3 | Stabilize and measure: post-transition review, KPI baseline and tracking, lessons-learned, update workforce plan (links to VS-19.3) and category strategy (W3209); manage any residual legal/employee-relations issues (links to VS-84) | Contingent Workforce Mgr / Finance | CHRO | 8–12 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Workforce planning (links to VS-19.3); finance (links to VS-17.4); change/portfolio (links to VS-33)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transition-disruption risk**: Service gap or quality dip during handover; mitigated by phased transition and overlap
- **Conversion-dispute risk**: TUPE-equivalent obligations and worker claims; mitigated by legal structuring and engagement

### Time Estimate
- 4–9 months per function transition

## W3232. Workforce Composition Strategy & Integrated Workforce Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual strategic cycle; major operating-model change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | Enterprise-wide integrated workforce |
| **Owner** | CHRO |
| **Participants** | CHRO, Contingent Workforce Mgr, HR (links to VS-19), Finance (links to VS-17.4), Strategy (links to VS-33), User Depts |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CHRO/Strategy (links to VS-33) build integrated workforce plan: total workforce (employees + contingent + outsourced functions), composition targets by function/location, build/buy/borrow decisions, capability/skills strategy, future-state for growth (10–15 new stores/year) and automation (links to VS-30); reconcile with budget (links to VS-33/VS-17.4) | CHRO / Contingent Workforce Mgr / Strategy | CEO | 6–10 weeks |
| 2 | Finance model total cost of workforce: employee comp+benefits (links to VS-19.2), contingent spend (W3227), outsourced-function cost (W3231), management overhead, compliance/risk premium; scenario analysis (growth, downturn, automation) | Finance Analyst / CHRO | CFO | 3–5 weeks |
| 3 | Approve and cascade composition strategy; integrate into VS-19 hiring plan, VS-34 procurement plan, and VS-33 strategic plan; monitor composition KPIs quarterly (employee-to-contingent ratio, contractor conversion rate, insource/outsource balance) and adjust | Exec Committee | CEO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategic plan/portfolio (links to VS-33); workforce planning (links to VS-19.3); FP&A (links to VS-17.4); BI (links to VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Composition-imbalance risk**: Over-contingent or over-employee in either direction; mitigated by integrated planning and ratio KPIs
- **Capability-gap risk**: Future skills not built; mitigated by capability strategy (links to VS-19.4)

### Time Estimate
- 8–14 weeks/annual cycle
