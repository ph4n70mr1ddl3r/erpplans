## W3209. Contingent Labor Strategy & Workforce Category Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual workforce planning (links to VS-19.3); new operating need; cost/strategic review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~8–12 contingent categories (security, janitorial, promodizer, merchandising crew, construction labor, delivery helper, IT contractor, etc.) |
| **Owner** | Contingent Workforce Manager |
| **Participants** | Contingent Workforce Mgr, CHRO/HR (links to VS-19), Store Ops (links to VS-07), DC Ops (links to VS-04), Procurement (links to VS-34), Finance, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contingent Workforce Mgr/HR (links to VS-19.3) assess demand by category: which functions are core (employee) vs non-core (contingent/outsource), headcount equivalents by location/shift, seasonality (Christmas promodizers per W555 context), peak/event demand (store openings VS-37, inventory counts); define make-vs-buy (employee vs contractor vs outsourced function) | Contingent Workforce Mgr / HR | CHRO | 4–6 weeks |
| 2 | Finance/Procurement (links to VS-34) build category cost model: fully-loaded employee cost vs contingent rate vs outsourced-function price; total-cost-of-ownership incl. management overhead, compliance, and risk premium; size addressable spend | Finance Analyst / Procurement | CHRO | 2–4 weeks |
| 3 | Executive committee (CHRO/COO/CFO) approves strategy, sourcing model per category, authority for insource/outsource transitions (W3231), and contingency-to-regular ratio guardrails (W3230); integrate to VS-33 workforce plan and VS-19 hiring plan | Exec Committee | CEO | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Workforce planning (links to VS-19.3); procurement/category (links to VS-34); FP&A (links to VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-reliance risk**: Excessive contingent dependency signals misclassification exposure and erodes service quality; mitigated by ratio guardrails (W3230)
- **Strategic-misalignment risk**: Outsourcing a core capability loses control; mitigated by make-vs-buy discipline


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "headcount equivalents by location/shift, seasonality" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves strategy, sourcing model per category, authority for…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-523 (ensure controlled execution — Contingent Workforce Strategy, Sourcing & Compliance (PA-98.1).)
- operational: ratio guardrails (W3230); operational: make-vs-buy discipline

### Time Estimate
- 6–10 weeks/annual cycle

## W3210. Agency & Service Contractor Sourcing & Qualification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New category need; contract expiry; market test |
| **Frequency** | Per category cycle (every 1–3 years) |
| **Volume** | ~30–60 agencies/contractors active; ~8–12 category RFPs/year |
| **Owner** | Contingent Workforce Manager |
| **Participants** | Contingent Workforce Mgr, Procurement (links to VS-34), Legal, Finance, Safety (links to VS-24), User Depts |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement/Contingent Workforce Mgr (links to VS-34) run sourcing: market scan, RFx, shortlist agencies/contractors; evaluate on legal/DOLE compliance (valid DOLE license where required — security agency per RA 5487, etc.), financial stability, capacity, references, HSE record (links to VS-24), wage/benefits compliance of their workers | Procurement / Contingent Workforce Mgr | CHRO | 4–8 weeks |
| 2 | Legal/Safety qualify: verify agency business permits (links to VS-22), DOLE registration, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG remittance proof for their workers, police/NBI clearance program, insurance (W3214), HSE program; perform site reference checks; confirm no labor-only-contracting red flags (W3211) | Legal / Safety | VP Legal & Compliance | 2–4 weeks |
| 3 | Award and onboard preferred supplier per category: master services agreement (W3213), rate card, SLA (W3212), compliance attestations, system access for time/invoice (W3221); add to vendor master (links to VS-29) and contingent supplier register | Procurement / Contingent Workforce Mgr | CHRO | 2–4 weeks |

### System Touchpoints

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Capacity risk**: Agency cannot scale to peak (Christmas, store openings); mitigated by multi-supplier strategy

- System auto-validate of "checks" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-test of "attestations, system access for time/invoice" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-523 (ensure controlled execution — Contingent Workforce Strategy, Sourcing & Compliance (PA-98.1).)
- operational: upfront qualification and periodic audit (W3226); operational: multi-supplier strategy

### Time Estimate
- 6–12 weeks per category sourcing

## W3211. DOLE D.O. 174 Labor-Only Contracting Compliance & Contract Structuring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New/renegotiated service contract; classification review; DOLE inspection |
| **Frequency** | Per contract + periodic review |
| **Volume** | ~30–60 active service contracts |
| **Owner** | Labor Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Labor Compliance Mgr, Legal, HR (links to VS-84), Procurement (links to VS-34), Contingent Workforce Mgr, Agency |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Labor Compliance/Legal structure each contract to be legitimate job-contracting (not labor-only): confirm contractor has substantial capital/investment (tools, equipment, premises), exercises direct control over its workers (hiring, firing, assignment, payroll, discipline), performs work distinct from BuildRight's core business where possible; avoid provisions implying employer-of-record (set hours, supervise day-to-day, integrate into BuildRight org) | Labor Compliance Mgr / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per contract |
| 2 | Legal draft contract with compliance safeguards: principal-contractor relationship (not BuildRight–worker), contractor warrants DOLE/SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG/13th-month/wage compliance and indemnifies BuildRight, audit rights, recordkeeping, right-to-direct-reserved-to-contractor clause; align with VS-34 service-provider contract standards | Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per contract |
| 3 | Labor Compliance maintain contracting-risk register and train operations supervisors (Store Mgrs/DC Mgrs) on supervision boundaries — direct BuildRight supervisors must not assume employer functions (hire/fire/pay/direct-discipline) over contractor workers, which would convert them to BuildRight employees under the four-fold test; periodic audit (W3226) | Labor Compliance Mgr / HR | CHRO | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract/CMS (links to VS-22/VS-88); compliance register (links to VS-22); risk register (links to VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Labor-only finding risk**: DOLE/NLRC deems arrangement labor-only → workers become BuildRight employees with backwages, benefits, regularization; mitigated by structuring discipline, four-fold-test training, and audit
- **Solidary-liability risk**: BuildRight jointly liable for agency's unpaid wages/benefits; mitigated by remittance verification (W3210/W3226) and indemnity


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "Labor Compliance/Legal structure each contract to be legitimate job-contracting" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System continuous audit of "audit rights, recordkeeping, right-to-direct-reserved-to-contractor clause" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-test of "test" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-121 (ensure lawful contracting & worker classification.)
- operational: structuring discipline, four-fold-test training, and audit; operational: remittance verification (W3210/W3226) and indemnity

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per contract structuring; ongoing training/audit

## W3215. Worker Classification & Co-Employment Risk Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New engagement; reclassification request; regulatory change; audit |
| **Frequency** | Per engagement + periodic |
| **Volume** | All ~30–60 contractor engagements + individual escalations |
| **Owner** | Labor Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Labor Compliance Mgr, Legal, HR (links to VS-84), Tax (links to VS-79), Contingent Workforce Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Labor Compliance/Legal apply the four-fold test (selection/engagement, payment of wages, power of dismissal, power of control) and economic-reality factors to each engagement and to individual-worker escalations (e.g., a long-tenured "contractor" worker seeking regularization); document classification rationale | Labor Compliance Mgr / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per case |
| 2 | Assess tax/ benefits exposure of misclassification: back wages, 13th month, SIL, premium pay, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG, tax withholding; quantify potential liability and reserve (links to VS-100 legal loss contingency W3277); plan remediation (convert to regular per W3230, or restructure engagement) | Labor Compliance Mgr / Finance / Legal | CFO | Per case |
| 3 | Implement remediation and controls: convert confirmed employees to regular (W3230) with back-pay; restructure borderline engagements; train supervisors (W3211); update risk register and monitor via audit (W3226) | Labor Compliance Mgr / HR / Legal | CHRO | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Classification/risk register (links to VS-21.2); legal matter (links to VS-100); payroll conversion (links to VS-19.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Misclassification-liability risk**: NLRC regularization cases with back-wages and moral/exemplary damages; mitigated by four-fold-test discipline and proactive remediation
- **Tax-exposure risk**: BIR reclassification of contractors to employees; mitigated by documentation and tax review

- System auto-monitor of "monitor via audit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls

- operational: four-fold-test discipline and proactive remediation; operational: documentation and tax review
