## W4297. Daily Delivery Manifest, Load Planning & Dispatch

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Day's bulky deliveries confirmed (W4291) |
| **Frequency** | Daily per DC/store catchment |
| **Volume** | Per-day manifests × ~each serving DC/store; each manifest = multi-stop two-man route |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Dispatch / DC Ops (VS-04) / Logistics (VS-06.3) |
| **Participants** | DC Ops (VS-04), Store Ops (VS-07), Crews/Drivers, 3PL (VS-56), Customer Service (VS-13) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dispatch/DC build the day's manifest from confirmed orders — sequence by time-window + zone, group by vehicle type (W4294), and assign two-man crews by install-license match (W4293) | Dispatch / DC Ops | Head of Bulky Delivery | Daily |
| 2 | DC Ops pick/stage bulky items, verify against manifest (item, install-kit, haul-away flag), load in reverse-stop order with securing equipment, and release to crew with e-manifest + customer instructions | DC Ops / Crew | DC Manager | Daily |
| 3 | Dispatch push e-manifest to crew device, send customer ETA notifications (VS-75/VS-13), and track in real-time against the route | Dispatch / Customer Service | Head of Bulky Delivery | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- WMS/pick (VS-04); logistics/routing (VS-06.3); dispatch/e-manifest; customer comms (VS-13/VS-75)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Load-sequence risk**: wrong stop order → re-handle/damage/time loss; mitigated by reverse-stop load plan
- **Mismatch risk**: wrong item/install-kit loaded → failed install; mitigated by manifest-vs-load verification at staging

### Time Estimate
- Load ~20–40 min/vehicle; dispatch build ~30–60 min/zone/day

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto time-window sequencing; e-manifest to crew device; load-verification scan; live ETA to customer

### Controls
- CTL: manifest-vs-load verification at staging; crew-license match before dispatch (W4293)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-04 (DC), VS-06.3 (routing), VS-13/VS-75 (comms), W4291 (scheduling), W4293 (crew), W4294 (vehicle)

## W4298. In-Home Delivery Execution, Site Protection & Handoff

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Crew arrives at customer address |
| **Frequency** | Per bulky stop |
| **Volume** | Bulky-delivery stop volume (manifests × stops) |
| **Owner** | Two-Man Crew / Bulky-Delivery Operations |
| **Participants** | Driver/Installer, Customer, Customer Service (VS-13), LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crew verify customer/identity and site access, lay floor/wall protection, and plan the carry path (stairs/elevator/clearances) per the pre-delivery check (W4292) | Crew | Crew Lead | Per stop |
| 2 | Crew team-lift/roll the item to the install location using equipment (W4294), set in place, remove packaging, and collect for haul-away/recycling where applicable (W4305) | Crew | Crew Lead | Per stop |
| 3 | Crew hand off to installer if separate (W4299) or complete placement, brief the customer on use/care, and capture handoff confirmation | Crew / Installer | Crew Lead | Per stop |

### System Touchpoints
- e-POD/crew app (VS-06.3); order (VS-16); customer comms (VS-13); LP/exception (VS-23)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Property-damage risk**: appliance scratches floor/wall during carry; mitigated by site protection + trained carry path
- **Access-blocker risk**: unforeseen obstruction (narrow stair, low elevator) blocks delivery; mitigated by pre-delivery access check + on-site re-plan/escalation (W4302)

### Time Estimate
- ~15–45 min/stop depending on item/site

### Automation Opportunity
- Crew-app carry-path checklist; photo e-POD of placement; auto-prompt haul-away/recycling capture

### Controls
- CTL: site protection used; team-lift SOP; handoff captured (e-POD) before crew departure

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06.3 (delivery app), VS-13 (service), VS-23 (exception), W4299 (install), W4305 (haul-away), W4301 (acceptance)

## W4299. Appliance & White-Goods Installation, Hookup & Commissioning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Item install-required flag (W4292); delivery handoff (W4298) |
| **Frequency** | Per install-tagged bulky order |
| **Volume** | Install-tagged orders (split-AC, water heater, washing machine, cooker, rangehood, dishwasher) |
| **Owner** | Licensed Installer / Bulky-Delivery Service Ops / Installation Services (VS-12) |
| **Participants** | Installer (W4293), Crew, Customer Service (VS-13), Quality (VS-31), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Installer perform the hookup/commissioning per category SOP — electrical (cooker/washer), plumbing (washer/dishwasher/water heater), refrigerant split-AC (vacuum/charge/leak-test per RA 9275), gas (cooker — gas-safe), and venting (rangehood/dryer) | Installer | Crew Lead / Service Mgr | Per install |
| 2 | Installer test function, leak/safety-check (gas leak, refrigerant leak, electrical, level/stability), brief the customer on operation/maintenance, and hand over manuals/warranty card | Installer | Service Mgr | Per install |
| 3 | Installer register the install/warranty (VS-53), capture install evidence (photos, serial, completion), and post the install-fee/service revenue (VS-16/VS-17) | Installer / Service Ops | Service Mgr | Per install |

### System Touchpoints
- Install/service (VS-12); warranty (VS-53); order/revenue (VS-16/VS-17); quality (VS-31); HSE (VS-24)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Refrigerant/gas-safety risk**: improper split-AC refrigerant or gas hookup → leak/fire/health + RA 9275 violation; mitigated by licensed installer (W4293) + leak-test SOP
- **Warranty-void risk**: install not registered → manufacturer warranty dispute; mitigated by mandatory install/warranty registration at completion

### Time Estimate
- ~30–120 min/install depending on type (split-AC longest)

### Automation Opportunity
- Category install-SOP on installer app; mandatory leak-test photo capture; auto warranty-registration; install-fee auto-post

### Controls
- CTL: installer licensed (W4293); leak/safety test documented; warranty registered (VS-53); install evidence captured

### Cross-references
- links to VS-12 (install services), VS-53 (warranty), VS-16/VS-17 (revenue), VS-31 (quality), VS-24 (HSE), W4293 (license), W4300 (quality)

## W4300. Installation Quality, Damage/Defect-on-Delivery & Field Resolution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Damage found at delivery; defect-on-arrival; install-quality issue |
| **Frequency** | Per exception (benchmark 1–3% of bulky deliveries) |
| **Volume** | ~1–3% of bulky stops → damage/defect exceptions |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Service Ops / Quality (VS-31) / Damage Claims (VS-90) |
| **Participants** | Crew/Installer, Customer Service (VS-13), Quality (VS-31), Vendor (VS-03), Claims (VS-90), LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crew/Installer detect and document damage/defect at the point of delivery/install (photo, serial, nature), offer the customer options (accept-as-is with concession, refuse, swap), and capture the decision on e-POD | Crew / Installer | Crew Lead / Service Mgr | Per exception |
| 2 | Service Ops/Quality triage — transit damage (vs VS-06/VS-56 carrier), manufacturing defect (vs VS-31/VS-03 vendor DOA), or install error — and trigger recovery: redelivery (W4302), vendor RMA (VS-90/VS-31), or field repair | Service Ops / Quality | Service Mgr | Per exception |
| 3 | Claims/LP file the claim against the liable party (carrier VS-90, vendor VS-03), process customer concession/refund/swap (VS-16/VS-118), and feed root-cause to prevention (W4296) | Claims / LP / Service Ops | Service Mgr | Per exception |

### System Touchpoints
- e-POD/exception (VS-06.3); quality/RMA (VS-31); damage claims (VS-90); vendor (VS-03); order/refund (VS-16/VS-118); LP (VS-23)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Evidence risk**: damage not documented at delivery → unrecoverable claim (vs carrier/vendor) and customer dispute; mitigated by mandatory photo+decision capture on e-POD at the doorstep
- **Liability-attribution risk**: transit vs DOA vs install error unclear → claim ping-pong; mitigated by triage SOP and serial/evidence trail

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min per field exception; claim resolution days–weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- e-POD damage-photo → auto-claim draft; serial-based DOA check; concession/refund workflow; root-cause tagging

### Controls
- CTL: damage/defect documented at delivery (e-POD evidence); claim filed vs liable party (VS-90/VS-03); concession within RA authority (VS-118)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-31 (quality), VS-90 (damage claims), VS-03 (vendor), VS-16/VS-118 (refund), VS-23 (LP), W4296 (analytics), W4302 (redelivery)

## W4302. Failed/Rescheduled Delivery, Redelivery & Customer-Not-At-Home Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer not at home; access blocked; item unfit; customer reschedule |
| **Frequency** | Per failed attempt (benchmark 3–8% of bulky deliveries) |
| **Volume** | ~3–8% of bulky stops → failed/rescheduled |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Service Ops / Customer Service (VS-13) |
| **Participants** | Crew, Customer Service (VS-13), Logistics (VS-06), Finance (VS-118), Revenue Assurance (VS-118) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crew report failed-attempt reason (not-at-home, access, unfit site, refused) with evidence (photo/attempt log) and item returned-to-DC/store or held | Crew | Crew Lead / Service Mgr | Per attempt |
| 2 | Customer Service contact customer, reschedule into a new slot (W4291), and apply any re-delivery/attempt fee per policy (W4295) within RA authority | Customer Service | Service Mgr | Per attempt |
| 3 | Revenue Assurance/Finance track failed-attempt cost, apply cancellation/restocking where customer cancels, and feed repeat-failure analytics to prevention (W4296) | Revenue Assurance / Finance | CFO / Service Mgr | Per attempt |

### System Touchpoints
- e-POD/attempt (VS-06.3); scheduling (W4291); order/refund (VS-16/VS-118); CX (VS-13)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Capacity-waste risk**: failed attempts consume crew/vehicle capacity; mitigated by reminder comms + slot-deposit/no-show policy
- **Customer-experience risk**: opaque reschedule → churn/chargeback; mitigated by proactive contact + clear re-delivery policy

### Time Estimate
- ~10–20 min per failed-attempt resolution

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto pre-delivery reminder (SMS/app); no-show fee auto-apply; self-serve reschedule; failed-attempt cost analytics

### Controls
- CTL: failed-attempt evidenced; re-delivery fee per policy (W4295); cancellation/restocking within authority (VS-118)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-13 (service), VS-06.3 (delivery), VS-16/VS-118 (refund), W4291 (reschedule), W4295 (fees), W4296 (analytics)

- CTL: 3PL specialist terms + insurance/bonding (VS-26); SLA-governed; HSE-compliant (W4304)

## W4304. Field Service Safety, DOLE OSH, Heavy-Lifting & Customer-Premises Liability

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program operation; incident; HSE review |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + per incident |
| **Volume** | All bulky-delivery field operations + incidents |
| **Owner** | HSE (VS-24) / Bulky-Delivery Operations / Risk (VS-21.2) |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), HR (VS-19/VS-98), Insurance (VS-26), Legal (VS-100.3), Risk (VS-21.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HSE/Delivery Ops set the field-service safety standard (manual-handling/team-lift, lift-gate use, appliance securing, electrical/gas/refrigerant install safety, customer-premises conduct) per DOLE OSH | HSE / Delivery Ops | HSE Mgr / COO | Annual |
| 2 | Crews/Installers execute pre-task hazard check, use required PPE/equipment (W4294), and report incidents/near-miss immediately | Crew / Installer | Crew Lead | Per task |
| 3 | HSE/Legal/Insurance manage incidents (injury, property damage, customer-premises liability), investigate, file insurance (VS-26), and feed the risk register (VS-21.2) | HSE / Legal / Insurance / Risk | HSE Mgr / CRO | Per incident |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE (VS-24); HR (VS-19/VS-98); risk/ERM (VS-21.2); insurance (VS-26); legal (VS-100.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Injury risk**: heavy-lifting/appliance-handling injury to crew; mitigated by team-lift + lift-gate + OSH training + PPE
- **Customer-premises-liability risk**: property damage/personal injury at customer site; mitigated by site protection, conduct SOP, and insurance (VS-26)

### Time Estimate
- Standard ~2 weeks; pre-task check ~5 min/task; per-incident investigation variable

### Automation Opportunity
- Pre-task hazard-check app; incident-reporting mobile; insurance-claim workflow; ERM auto-feed

### Controls
- CTL: field-service SOP per DOLE OSH; PPE/lift-gate used; incidents reported & insured (VS-26); risks in ERM (VS-21.2)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-19/VS-98 (crew), VS-21.2 (ERM), VS-26 (insurance), VS-100.3 (legal), W4293 (training), W4294 (equipment)
