# VS-143: Bulky & White-Goods Delivery, Installation, Haul-Away & Recycling Operations

> **Make & Move** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Bulky & White-Goods Delivery, Installation, Haul-Away & Recycling workflows for BuildRight Depot
Corp. — owning the enterprise **bulky-item last-mile + reverse-logistics discipline** for
merchandise that cannot move on the standard replenishment/parcel network: home appliances
(1,750 SKUs, 5% of assortment — aircons, refrigerators, washing machines, water heaters, kitchen
appliances), home décor & furniture (1,750 SKUs), large-format tile, lumber/material packs, solar
water heaters, and oversized tools/equipment. These items share a distinct operational signature:
**scheduled** delivery to a customer address (not shelf sell-through), **two-person** heavy-lifting
crews, customer **time-window** booking, **in-home installation** and hookup, **old-unit
haul-away** at the doorstep, and **refrigerant/e-waste/scrap recycling** of the removed unit.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. Bulky/appliance delivery and haul-away appear
in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers. The capability is sprinkled as single steps
inside VS-06.3 (generic last-mile/3PL), VS-12 (generic installation & tool rental — which does not
own the appliance reverse-chain), VS-32 (returns/reverse — which owns *customer-initiated*
returns, not doorstep haul-away of the old unit on a *new* delivery), VS-07 (store ops), and VS-73
(waste — which owns store-generated waste, not appliance end-of-life recycling). No value stream
owns the end-to-end **bulky delivery + installation + haul-away + recycling program**: the
specialized fleet/crew capacity, the customer scheduling/time-window engine, the two-man
installation commissioning, the field damage/defect-on-delivery resolution, the old-unit haul-away
eligibility/consent, the refrigerant recovery (ODS / RA 9275) and e-waste/scrap recycling
disposition, the EPR (RA 11898) documentary trail, and the recovery-asset/refurbish economics.

This is distinct from **VS-06.3 (Last-Mile & Delivery Partners)** which delivers the standard
parcel/basket ecommerce order and owns the 3PL last-mile relationship — this value stream owns the
**bulky/heavy/installed** last-mile that needs lift-gate vehicles, two-man crews, and customer
premises access. It is distinct from **VS-12 (Installation & Services)** which owns tool rental and
general install dispatch — this value stream owns the **appliance/white-goods in-home commissioning
plus the reverse haul-away** that only happens on a bulky delivery. It is distinct from **VS-32
(Returns & Reverse Logistics)** which owns customer *returns* — this value stream owns the
**old-for-new haul-away** executed at the moment of delivery (a forward-logistics event with a
reverse payload), plus the appliance end-of-life recycling chain. It is distinct from **VS-73
(Store Waste & Circular Economy)** which owns store-generated waste — this value stream owns the
**customer-end-of-life appliance** recycling/refrigerant-recovery chain and its recovery economics.
It is distinct from **VS-56 (Third-Party Delivery Partner)** which manages the generic 3PL
relationship — this value stream owns the **specialized bulky-delivery capacity** (own crews +
niche bulky 3PLs).

BuildRight's exposure is structural: at ~5% appliances + ~5% furniture of a 35K-SKU assortment
carried across 200 stores, with ecommerce (VS-10) and trade/project (VS-11/VS-107) channels also
selling bulky goods, the enterprise runs a continuous stream of scheduled bulky deliveries — each
a multi-visit, two-person, customer-premises, cash-or-credit event with installation, damage,
haul-away, and recycling obligations, where a failed window or a mishandled refrigerant charge
directly costs a sale (VS-16), a return (VS-32), a safety incident (VS-24), and a DENR/compliance
exposure (VS-114/VS-73). RA 9275 (Clean Air Act) prohibits venting ODS refrigerants and RA 11898
(EPR Act) makes producers accountable for end-of-life — both attach to the doorstep haul-away this
value stream owns.
