## W4289. Bulky & White-Goods Delivery Program Strategy, Channel Scope & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Growth in appliance/furniture mix; ecommerce expansion (VS-10); channel strategy review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on strategy change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise bulky-delivery program across appliances (1,750 SKUs), furniture (1,750), large-format tile, lumber packs, solar water heaters |
| **Owner** | Head of Bulky/Home Delivery / Logistics (VS-06) / Commercial |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01), Ecommerce (VS-10), Trade (VS-11), Store Ops (VS-07), Finance (VS-17.4), 3PL (VS-56) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics/Commercial set the bulky-delivery strategy — which SKUs/categories are "bulky-deliverable," which channels offer in-home delivery+install, the own-crew vs 3PL mix, and the install/haul-away service scope per category | Head of Bulky Delivery / Logistics | COO | Annual |
| 2 | Logistics/Merchandising map the end-to-end flow (order → schedule → dispatch → deliver → install → haul-away → recycle) and the handoffs to VS-06.3 (last-mile), VS-12 (install), VS-32 (returns), VS-73 (waste/recycle) | Logistics / Merchandising | COO | Annual |
| 3 | Logistics document the strategy, obtain approval, and cascade to capacity (W4290), scheduling (W4291), pricing (W4295), and 3PL terms (W4303) | Head of Bulky Delivery | COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Order/ecommerce (VS-10); logistics/WMS (VS-06); merchandising (VS-01); 3PL (VS-56); finance (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scope-mismatch risk**: in-home install offered where licensing/safety exposure is too high (e.g. split-type AC refrigerant work); mitigated by category-level service-scope gating (W4292) and licensed-installer requirement (W4293)
- **Handoff-ambiguity risk**: install vs deliver vs haul-away ownership unclear → customer ping-pong; mitigated by mapped end-to-end flow & single-bucket order status

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual strategy

### Automation Opportunity
- Bulky-mix/cost scenario modeling; channel profitability by delivery type; auto-flag items requiring specialist install

### Controls
- CTL-659 (ensure controlled execution — Bulky Delivery Network Scheduling and Capacity (PA-143.1).)
- CTL: strategy approved (COO); service-scope risk-tiered (W4292); licensed-installer gate (W4293)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06.3 (last-mile), VS-10 (ecommerce), VS-12 (install), VS-32 (returns), VS-56 (3PL), VS-73 (recycle), W4290 (capacity), W4295 (pricing)

## W4290. Bulky Delivery Network, Capacity & Crew Sizing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4289); volume/peak forecast; network change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + quarterly tuning |
| **Volume** | Bulky-delivery network across 200 stores / 4 DCs / catchment zones |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Network Planning / Logistics Capacity (VS-06) |
| **Participants** | DC Ops (VS-04), Store Ops (VS-07), 3PL (VS-56), Finance (VS-17.4), Fleet (VS-06.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Network Planning forecast bulky-delivery volume by zone/category/season (appliance AC peaks in summer, furniture in ber-months) and size crew, vehicle, and lift-gate capacity against the forecast | Network Planning | Head of Bulky Delivery | Annual |
| 2 | Network Planning/DC Ops define the staging model — DC-bulked, store-pooled, or cross-dock — and the two-man crew shift pattern covering customer time-windows incl. weekends | Network Planning / DC Ops / Store Ops | Head of Bulky Delivery | Annual |
| 3 | Network Planning/Finance size own vs 3PL capacity split (build for base, flex to 3PL for peak) and feed capex/lease decisions to VS-06.1/VS-35 | Network Planning / Finance | COO / CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Logistics/capacity planning (VS-06); DC/WMS (VS-04); store ops (VS-07); fleet (VS-06.1); 3PL (VS-56); finance (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-capacity risk**: peak (summer AC, ber-month furniture) demand exceeds crew capacity → cancellations/lost sales; mitigated by seasonal capacity plan + 3PL flex pool
- **Crew-utilization risk**: oversized own-fleet idles off-peak; mitigated by own-base + 3PL-peak split

### Time Estimate
- ~3 weeks annual + ~1 day/quarter tuning

### Automation Opportunity
- Demand-based crew/vehicle sizing; zone heatmap of bulky demand; peak-flex auto-trigger to 3PL

### Controls
- CTL-659 (ensure controlled execution — Bulky Delivery Network Scheduling and Capacity (PA-143.1).)
- CTL: capacity sized to forecast with peak flex (3PL); crew shift covers statutory rest (VS-19.3/VS-84)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (logistics), VS-04 (DC), VS-07 (stores), VS-06.1 (fleet), VS-56 (3PL), W4289 (strategy), W4296 (analytics)

## W4291. Customer Delivery Scheduling, Time-Window & Routing Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Bulky order placed (VS-10/VS-11/VS-16) |
| **Frequency** | Per order (~thousands/month) |
| **Volume** | ~per-order booking; enterprise = bulky-order volume × time-windows/day |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Scheduling / Customer Service (VS-13) |
| **Participants** | Ecommerce (VS-10), Trade (VS-11), Logistics (VS-06.3), Store Ops (VS-07), Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scheduling system offers the customer available delivery date/time-windows by zone (AM/PM/2-hour slots) constrained by crew/vehicle capacity (W4290) and item install-eligibility (W4292) | Scheduling / Ecommerce | Head of Bulky Delivery | Per order |
| 2 | Customer confirms slot; system reserves crew+vehicle capacity, sends confirmation + pre-delivery instructions (measure/access/parking), and holds the slot | Scheduling / Customer Service | Head of Bulky Delivery | Per order |
| 3 | Logistics auto-routes the day's bulky deliveries into two-man crew manifests (multi-stop, time-window-constrained) and pushes to dispatch (W4297) | Logistics Routing | Head of Bulky Delivery | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Scheduling/booking engine (VS-10/VS-06); order (VS-16); customer comms (VS-13/VS-75); routing (VS-06.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-promise risk**: slot sold without real crew capacity → failed window; mitigated by capacity-linked slot calendar (no sell beyond W4290 capacity)
- **Access risk**: customer site unmeasured (door/stair/elevator) → crew cannot deliver; mitigated by mandatory pre-delivery access check & customer instruction

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 min per booking; routing ~30–60 min/day/zone

### Automation Opportunity
- Capacity-linked self-serve slot booking; auto multi-stop time-window routing; pre-delivery access-check checklist/prompt

### Controls
- CTL-659 (ensure controlled execution — Bulky Delivery Network Scheduling and Capacity (PA-143.1).)
- CTL: slots capacity-constrained; pre-delivery access instruction mandatory; confirmation audit-trail

### Cross-references
- links to VS-10 (ecommerce), VS-06.3 (routing), VS-13 (service), VS-75 (app), W4290 (capacity), W4297 (dispatch)

## W4292. Bulky Item Order Capture, Eligibility & Pre-Delivery Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer adds bulky/install item to basket (VS-10/VS-11) |
| **Frequency** | Per bulky order |
| **Volume** | Bulky-order volume (appliances + furniture + large tile + lumber packs) |
| **Owner** | Merchandising (VS-01) / Bulky-Delivery Ops / Ecommerce (VS-10) |
| **Participants** | Trade (VS-11), Logistics (VS-06), Compliance (VS-114 for DG), Finance (VS-16) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandising/Delivery Ops tag each bulky SKU with delivery attributes (weight/dim, lift-gate req, two-man flag, install-required flag, install-license req e.g. split-AC refrigerant, haul-away-eligible flag, DG flag) | Merchandising / Delivery Ops | Head of Merch Ops | Per setup |
| 2 | Order capture enforces eligibility at basket — install/DG items need licensed installer availability (W4293), correct address/zone, stock at serving DC/store, and customer consent for install/haul-away | Ecommerce / Trade | Head of Bulky Delivery | Per order |
| 3 | Delivery Ops verify pre-conditions before confirm — site access, electrical/plumbing readiness for install, payment/credit cleared (VS-16/VS-68), and required permits (e.g. condo delivery elevator booking) | Delivery Ops / Customer Service | Head of Bulky Delivery | Per order |

### System Touchpoints
- Merchandising/item master (VS-01/VS-29); ecommerce/order (VS-10/VS-16); logistics (VS-06); compliance DG (VS-114); credit (VS-68)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-tagged-item risk**: heavy/gas/refrigerant item sold without install-license gate → unsafe/illegal install; mitigated by SKU attribute gating + installer-availability check
- **Readiness risk**: install attempted at unprepared site → failed/return; mitigated by pre-delivery readiness verification

### Time Estimate
- Tagging ~1 day/quarter; per-order verification ~5–10 min

### Automation Opportunity
- Attribute-driven eligibility at basket; installer-availability auto-check; pre-delivery readiness checklist

### Controls
- CTL-81 (ensure bulky delivery compliance & e-POD closure.)
- CTL: install/DG items installer-gated (W4293); readiness verified; credit cleared (VS-16/VS-68)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-01 (item attrs), VS-10/VS-16 (order), VS-114 (DG), VS-68 (credit), W4293 (installers), W4299 (install)

## W4293. Two-Man Crew, Driver & Installer Authorization, Training & Certification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Crew onboarding; licensing expiry; new install category |
| **Frequency** | Per crew/periodic renewal |
| **Volume** | All bulky-delivery two-man crews + drivers + installers |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Operations / HR (VS-19) / HSE (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19), HSE (VS-24), Logistics (VS-06), Contingent (VS-98), Compliance (VS-114) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delivery Ops/HR authorize each crew member by competency — heavy-lifting/Manual-Handling (DOLE OSH), customer-premises conduct, and specialist install licenses (DTI/TESDA HVAC-refrigerant for split-AC, gas/electrical for cookers/water heaters) | Delivery Ops / HR / HSE | Head of Bulky Delivery | Per crew |
| 2 | HSE/HR train crews on heavy-lifting technique, appliance securing, DG items, and customer-premises safety/liability (coordinate VS-24/VS-98 for outsourced crews) | HSE / HR / Delivery Ops | Head of Bulky Delivery / HSE | Per onboarding |
| 3 | Delivery Ops/Compliance track license/cert expiry, enforce no-work-without-valid-cert, and revoke authorization on breach | Delivery Ops / Compliance | Head of Bulky Delivery | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- HR/competency (VS-19/VS-98); HSE (VS-24); compliance/licensing (VS-114); logistics (VS-06)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unlicensed-install risk**: split-AC/gas appliance installed by uncertified crew → safety/insurance void + RA 9275 refrigerant exposure; mitigated by license gate + expiry tracking
- **Injury risk**: manual-handling injury from heavy appliances; mitigated by lift-gate (W4294), team-lift SOP, and OSH training

### Time Estimate
- Onboarding ~2–3 days/crew; renewal tracking continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Competency/license registry with expiry auto-alert; auto-block assignment of unlicensed crew to licensed-install orders

### Controls
- CTL-81 (ensure bulky delivery compliance & e-POD closure.)
- CTL: install work gated by valid license; heavy-lift team-lift + lift-gate; OSH training current (VS-24)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-19/VS-98 (crew/contingent), VS-24 (HSE), VS-114 (DG/refrigerant), W4294 (equipment), W4299 (install)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fleet/Delivery Ops specify and provision the right vehicle mix (lift-gate trucks for appliances, box trucks for furniture, flatbeds for lumber/large tile) with securing equipment (straps, blankets, dollies, appliance hand-trucks) | Fleet / Delivery Ops | Head of Bulky Delivery | Per lifecycle |

### Time Estimate

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls

## W4296. Bulky Delivery Service-Level, Capacity Analytics & Peak (Ber-Month) Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Operating performance; peak season planning; SLA review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly review + annual peak plan |
| **Volume** | Enterprise bulky-delivery performance + capacity utilization |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Operations / Logistics Analytics (VS-06) / Finance (VS-17.4) |
| **Participants** | DC Ops (VS-04), Store Ops (VS-07), 3PL (VS-56), CX (VS-13), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delivery Ops/Analytics track SLA KPIs (on-time-window %, first-time-success %, install-complete %, failed-window %, damage-on-delivery %, haul-away completion %) and crew/vehicle utilization vs plan | Delivery Ops / Analytics | Head of Bulky Delivery | Monthly |

### Time Estimate

### Automation Opportunity
