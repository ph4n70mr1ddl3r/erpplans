## W4305. Old-Appliance Haul-Away Policy, Eligibility & Customer Consent

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Bulky order with haul-away-eligible item (W4292); customer requests haul-away |
| **Frequency** | Per haul-away-eligible bulky order |
| **Volume** | Haul-away-eligible orders (appliances, large furniture) × opt-in rate |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Service Ops / Commercial / Sustainability (VS-25) |
| **Participants** | Ecommerce (VS-10), Trade (VS-11), Customer Service (VS-13), Compliance (VS-114), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Commercial/Sustainability set the haul-away policy — eligibility (1-for-1 on like-category new purchase), accepted condition, exclusions (e.g. biohazard/heavily-infested, oversized non-appliance), fee/subsidy, and customer-consent/data-clearing requirement | Commercial / Sustainability / Service Ops | COO / Sustainability Lead | Annual |
| 2 | Order capture offers haul-away at purchase (VS-10/VS-11), captures customer consent + item description (type/age/condition, refrigerant flag), and books it onto the delivery manifest (W4297) | Ecommerce / Trade / Service Ops | Service Mgr | Per order |
| 3 | Crew execute haul-away at delivery (W4298) — verify the old unit matches consent, ensure data-bearing devices are data-cleared per consent, and load for reverse logistics (W4308) | Crew | Crew Lead | Per delivery |

### System Touchpoints
- Order/ecommerce (VS-10/VS-16); delivery/POD (VS-06.3); compliance (VS-114); sustainability/ESG (VS-25)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unauthorized-removal risk**: crew hauls a unit not covered by consent → customer dispute/theft allegation; mitigated by consent capture + on-site verification
- **Condition-rejection risk**: customer expects haul-away of a unit outside policy (biohazard/oversized); mitigated by clear eligibility + in-app condition check

### Automation Opportunity
- Haul-away opt-in at checkout; consent + condition e-capture; manifest auto-inclusion; eligibility-rule engine

### Controls
- CTL-661 (ensure controlled execution — Haul-Away, Old-Unit Recycling, Reverse Logistics & Analytics (PA-143.3).)
- CTL-661: haul-away consent captured & verified on-site; eligibility enforced; data-clearing on data-bearing devices (VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- Policy ~2 weeks annual; per-order capture ~3 min; on-site haul-away ~10–15 min

### Cross-references
- links to VS-10/VS-16 (order), VS-114 (compliance), VS-25 (sustainability), VS-91 (data clearing), W4297 (manifest), W4308 (reverse logistics)

## W4306. Refrigerant Recovery, ODS/RA 8749 & DENR-Compliant Appliance Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Haul-away of refrigerant-bearing appliance (refrigerator, freezer, AC, water cooler) |
| **Frequency** | Per refrigerant-bearing haul-away |
| **Volume** | Refrigerant-bearing haul-aways (subset of appliance haul-aways) |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Service Ops / HSE (VS-24) / Compliance (VS-114) |
| **Participants** | Licensed Installer (W4293), DENR-accredited recycler, Compliance (VS-114), ESG (VS-25), Risk (VS-21.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HSE/Compliance set the ODS (ozone-depleting substance / RA 8749 Clean Air Act) handling standard — recover (not vent) refrigerant at removal, use certified recovery equipment, route only to DENR-accredited recyclers | HSE / Compliance | HSE Mgr / Compliance Lead | Annual |
| 2 | Licensed crew/recovery-tech recover refrigerant on removal (split-AC de-install, fridge compressor), label/tag the recovered charge, and document quantity/type | Recovery Tech / Installer | Crew Lead / HSE | Per unit |
| 3 | Compliance/ESG verify the recovered unit + refrigerant is delivered to a DENR-accredited recycler (W4307/W4308), retain the chain-of-custody record, and report ODS recovery for ESG (VS-25) | Compliance / ESG | Compliance Lead | Per unit |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE (VS-24); compliance/DG (VS-114); ESG (VS-25); risk (VS-21.2); reverse-logistics (VS-32/VS-73)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Venting risk**: refrigerant vented at removal → RA 8749 violation, fines, environmental harm; mitigated by recovery-equipment mandate + licensed handler (W4293) + accredited-recycler routing
- **Chain-of-custody risk**: recovered unit untraceable → compliance/ESG reporting failure; mitigated by tagged chain-of-custody record to accredited recycler

### Automation Opportunity
- Refrigerant-recovery record app; auto chain-of-custody to accredited recycler; ESG ODS-recovery reporting

### Controls
- CTL-81 (ensure bulky delivery compliance & e-POD closure.)
- CTL-661: refrigerant recovered not vented (RA 8749); licensed handler (W4293); accredited-recycler routing; chain-of-custody retained

### Time Estimate
- Recovery ~20–40 min/unit; documentation ~10 min/unit

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-114 (DG/compliance), VS-25 (ESG), VS-21.2 (risk), W4293 (license), W4307 (recycling), W4308 (reverse)

## W4307. E-Waste, Scrap Metal & Appliance Recycling/Disposition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Hauled-away old units consolidated for disposition |
| **Frequency** | Continuous consolidation + periodic disposition |
| **Volume** | Hauled-away appliance/e-waste/furniture units |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Reverse Logistics / Sustainability (VS-25) / Waste (VS-73) |
| **Participants** | Reverse Logistics (VS-32), DENR-accredited recycler/vendor, Compliance (VS-114), ESG (VS-25), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reverse Logistics/Sustainability classify each hauled unit for disposition — refurb/resale (W4311), parts-harvest, scrap-metal recovery, e-waste (RA 6969/DENR), or responsible disposal — by type/condition | Reverse Logistics / Sustainability | Service Mgr / Sustainability Lead | Continuous |
| 2 | Reverse Logistics route to the right channel — accredited e-waste recycler (DENR), scrap-metal partner, refurb partner — and process via W4308 consolidation | Reverse Logistics / Vendor Mgmt | Service Mgr | Continuous |
| 3 | ESG/Finance capture recovery value (scrap/refurb/parts), disposal cost, and the recycling/landfill-diversion outcome for ESG reporting (VS-25) and program economics (W4310) | ESG / Finance | Sustainability Lead / CFO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Reverse logistics (VS-32); waste/recycle (VS-73); compliance (VS-114); ESG (VS-25); finance (VS-17.4); vendor (VS-03)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-disposal risk**: e-waste to non-accredited channel → DENR/RA 6969 violation; mitigated by classification + accredited-recycler routing
- **Value-leak risk**: refurb/resale-eligible units scrapped → lost recovery; mitigated by disposition classification (W4311)

### Automation Opportunity
- Disposition decision-tree by type/condition; recovery-value tracking; ESG landfill-diversion auto-report

### Controls
- CTL-661 (ensure controlled execution — Haul-Away, Old-Unit Recycling, Reverse Logistics & Analytics (PA-143.3).)
- CTL-661: e-waste to DENR-accredited recycler; disposition classified; recovery/diversion documented (VS-25)

### Time Estimate
- Classification ~5–10 min/unit; disposition batched weekly

### Cross-references
- links to VS-32 (reverse), VS-73 (waste/recycle), VS-114 (compliance), VS-25 (ESG), W4306 (refrigerant), W4310 (economics), W4311 (refurb)

## W4308. Haul-Away Reverse Logistics, Consolidation & Vendor Scrap Partner

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Hauled units at DC/store ready for consolidation |
| **Frequency** | Periodic consolidation runs |
| **Volume** | Consolidated hauled-unit volume to recyclers |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Reverse Logistics / DC Ops (VS-04) / 3PL (VS-56) |
| **Participants** | DC Ops (VS-04), Store Ops (VS-07), Reverse Logistics (VS-32), Scrap/Recycler Vendor, 3PL (VS-56), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DC/Store Ops consolidate hauled units at the designated reverse-staging area, verify against haul-away records (W4305), and stage by disposition (W4307) | DC Ops / Store Ops / Reverse Logistics | DC/Store Mgr | Periodic |
| 2 | Reverse Logistics/Vendor schedule the consolidator/scrap-metal/e-waste partner pickup or 3PL reverse-leg, with chain-of-custody handoff and weigh/count verification | Reverse Logistics / Vendor Mgmt / 3PL | Service Mgr | Periodic |
| 3 | Finance/Reverse Logistics reconcile partner settlement (scrap value, e-waste fee), post recovery/cost to the GL (VS-17), and close the reverse leg | Finance / Reverse Logistics | Finance Mgr | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- WMS/reverse staging (VS-04/VS-32); 3PL (VS-56); vendor (VS-03); finance/GL (VS-17); ESG (VS-25)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reconciliation risk**: hauled units not matched to records → leakage/unaccounted disposal; mitigated by record-vs-staging verification + weigh/count handoff
- **Cost-leakage risk**: reverse-leg cost untracked → economics distortion; mitigated by partner settlement reconciliation to GL

### Automation Opportunity
- Reverse-staging scan vs haul-away record; partner pickup scheduling; settlement auto-reconcile to GL

### Controls
- CTL-661 (ensure controlled execution — Haul-Away, Old-Unit Recycling, Reverse Logistics & Analytics (PA-143.3).)
- CTL-661: record-vs-staging verification; chain-of-custody handoff; partner settlement reconciled (VS-17)

### Time Estimate
- Consolidation ~hours/week per site; reconciliation ~2–4 hrs/cycle

### Cross-references
- links to VS-04 (DC), VS-32 (reverse), VS-56 (3PL), VS-03 (vendor), VS-17 (GL), W4305 (records), W4307 (disposition)

## W4309. Recycling Compliance, EPR (RA 11898) & Documentary Trail

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Annual reporting + continuous record-keeping |
| **Volume** | Enterprise haul-away/recycling compliance record |
| **Owner** | Compliance (VS-22/VS-114) / Sustainability (VS-25) / Legal (VS-100.3) |
| **Participants** | Compliance (VS-22/VS-114), ESG (VS-25), Legal (VS-100.3), DENR-accredited recyclers, Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliance/ESG map BuildRight's EPR (RA 11898 Extended Producer Responsibility) obligations for end-of-life appliances/e-waste and the documentary trail required (quantity, type, chain-of-custody, accredited-processor proof) | Compliance / ESG | Compliance Lead / Sustainability Lead | Annual |
| 2 | Compliance/Sustainability compile the EPR/recycling record (haul-away quantities, refrigerant recovery W4306, e-waste to accredited recycler W4307, recovery/diversion W4310) and submit required reports to DENR/EMB | Compliance / ESG | Compliance Lead | Annual |
| 3 | Audit/Legal verify the documentary trail, retain records per policy (VS-88), and respond to any DENR/audit inquiry | Audit / Legal / Compliance | Compliance Lead / VP Legal | Annual + per inquiry |

### Controls
- CTL-81 (ensure bulky delivery compliance & e-POD closure.)
- CTL-661: EPR (RA 11898) record complete; chain-of-custody to accredited processor; records retained (VS-88)

### Time Estimate

### Cross-references

## W4311. Bulky Reverse-Logistics Asset Recovery, Refurb/Open-Box & Liquidation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Hauled unit or customer-return assessed as refurb/resale-eligible |
| **Frequency** | Continuous assessment + periodic liquidation |
| **Volume** | Refurb/resale-eligible units (subset of hauled-away + VS-32 returns) |
| **Owner** | Bulky-Delivery Reverse Logistics / Reverse (VS-32) / Clearance (VS-64) |
| **Participants** | Reverse Logistics (VS-32), Quality (VS-31), Refurb Partner, Clearance (VS-64), Finance (VS-17.4), LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reverse Logistics/Quality assess each candidate unit for refurb/resale eligibility (cosmetic vs functional, safety, warranty/legality of resale, data-bearing clearing VS-91) per VS-32.3 | Reverse Logistics / Quality | Service Mgr | Per unit |
| 2 | Reverse Logistics route eligible units — in-house/partner refurb, certified-refurb relabel, open-box liquidation via VS-64 channels, or parts-harvest — and price for recovery | Reverse Logistics / Clearance | Service Mgr | Per unit |
| 3 | Finance/LP track recovery vs scrap-baseline, ensure resale compliance (refurb disclosure, no resale of unsafe/counterfeit), and prevent leak/diversion | Finance / LP | CFO / LP Lead | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Reverse logistics (VS-32); quality (VS-31); clearance (VS-64); finance (VS-17.4); LP (VS-23); privacy (VS-91)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unsafe-resale risk**: unsafe/uncertified refurbished unit resold → safety/Consumer Act (RA 7394) exposure; mitigated by refurb eligibility/safety assessment + disclosure
- **Diversion risk**: refurb-eligible units diverted to scrap/personal gain; mitigated by LP monitoring (VS-23) & recovery tracking

### Automation Opportunity
- Refurb-eligibility scoring; recovery-vs-scrap analytics; refurb-disclosure auto-label; LP exception monitoring

### Controls
- CTL-661 (ensure controlled execution — Haul-Away, Old-Unit Recycling, Reverse Logistics & Analytics (PA-143.3).)
- CTL-661: refurb safety-assessed & disclosed; recovery tracked; LP-monitored (VS-23); data cleared (VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- Assessment ~10–20 min/unit; refurb cycle days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-32 (reverse), VS-31 (quality), VS-64 (clearance), VS-23 (LP), VS-91 (privacy), W4307 (disposition), W4310 (economics)
