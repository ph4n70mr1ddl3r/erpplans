## W3425. Strategic Account Definition, Tiering & Selection Criteria

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual KAM strategy review; segment/revenue change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + periodic re-tier |
| **Volume** | ~200 corporate + ~500 top trade accounts (~40% of revenue) |
| **Owner** | Head of Strategic Accounts |
| **Participants** | Head of Strategic Accounts, VP Sales/Trade (links to VS-11), Finance (links to VS-17.4), CRM/Master Data (links to VS-29/VS-13.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Head of Strategic Accounts define the strategic-account segmentation model: tiering criteria (revenue, GM$, growth potential, strategic importance, complexity, credit exposure links to VS-68), tier definitions (e.g., Platinum/Gold/Silver key accounts), and the KAM-service-level per tier (dedicated team, executive sponsor, cadence); align to corporate strategy (links to VS-33) and segment plan (links to VS-11.1) | Head of Strategic Accounts / Finance | VP Sales / COO | Annual |
| 2 | Finance/CRM compute the account-level revenue/GM$/margin and growth analytics from actuals (links to VS-17.4/VS-13.3/VS-101.3), apply the tiering criteria, and produce the candidate strategic-account list with tier assignments and exception flags | Finance / CRM Analyst | Head of Strategic Accounts | Annual |
| 3 | Head of Strategic Accounts/VP Sales validate the tiering with regional/trade leadership, approve the strategic-account roster and tier assignments, publish to account teams (W3428), and update the CRM/account master (links to VS-29/VS-13.3); set the re-tier cadence | Head of Strategic Accounts / VP Sales | COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM/account master (links to VS-13.3/VS-29); finance/revenue analytics (links to VS-17.4/VS-101.3); trade accounts (links to VS-11.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Tiering-distortion risk**: Over-/under-tiering mis-allocates scarce account resources; mitigated by data-driven criteria and validation
- **Roster-staleness risk**: Outdated roster misses emerging accounts / retains declining ones; mitigated by periodic re-tier


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "counts define the strategic-account segmentation model" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-assign of "assignments and exception flags" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "validate the tiering with regional/trade leadership, approve the…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: data-driven criteria and validation; operational: periodic re-tier

### Time Estimate
- 3–4 weeks annual

## W3426. Strategic Account Identification, Qualification & Onboarding to KAM

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New high-potential account (won project links to VS-11.2/VS-46); re-tier promotion; acquisition target |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + quarterly intake |
| **Volume** | ~20–40 new strategic accounts/year |
| **Owner** | Head of Strategic Accounts |
| **Participants** | Head of Strategic Accounts, Account Managers, Trade/Regional Sales (links to VS-11), Credit (links to VS-16.1/VS-68), CRM (links to VS-13.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales/Account Managers identify and qualify candidate strategic accounts: fit with tiering criteria (W3425), growth potential, strategic value, decision-maker access, credit-worthiness (links to VS-16.1/VS-68), and competitive position; document the qualification rationale and opportunity size | Sales / Account Managers | Head of Strategic Accounts | Per account |
| 2 | Head of Strategic Accounts approve onboarding to the KAM program, assign the account team and executive sponsor (W3428), set up the account in CRM (links to VS-13.3) with tier/service-level, and ensure credit/contract/CRM master alignment (links to VS-29/VS-16.1) | Head of Strategic Accounts / CRM | VP Sales | Per account |
| 3 | Account Manager conduct the onboarding meeting with the customer: introduce the account team and sponsor, confirm mutual expectations and cadence (W3430), kick off the Key Account Plan (W3427), and integrate any active projects/orders (links to VS-11.2/VS-46) into the account view | Account Manager / Sponsor | Head of Strategic Accounts | Per account |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM (links to VS-13.3); account/credit master (links to VS-29/VS-16.1); project/order (links to VS-11.2/VS-46)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-qualification risk**: Onboarding an account that doesn't warrant KAM wastes resources; mitigated by clear criteria and review
- **Channel-conflict risk**: KAM overlaps regional/trade sales causing confusion; mitigated by clear ownership and coordination (W3431)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "document the qualification rationale and opportunity size" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "counts approve onboarding to the KAM program, assign the account team and…" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
operational: clear criteria and review; operational: clear ownership and coordination (W3431)

### Time Estimate
- Per account; 2–4 weeks

| **Frequency** | Annual + mid-year refresh |
| **Volume** | One KAP per strategic account (~700 KAPs) |
| **Owner** | Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Account Manager, Executive Sponsor, Customer counterparts, Category/Specialist teams (links to VS-01/VS-66/VS-77), Finance (links to VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account Manager build the Key Account Plan: customer profile and business context, wallet/share analysis, revenue/GM$ target and growth thesis, category/product opportunity (links to VS-01), project pipeline (links to VS-11.2/VS-77), competitor position, key stakeholders and relationship gaps (W3429), risks/issues, and 12-month action plan with owners | Account Manager / Finance | Head of Strategic Accounts | Annual |
| 2 | Account Manager/Sponsor conduct the Joint Business Planning (JBP) session with the customer: align on mutual targets, project pipeline, pricing/contract terms (links to W3434), service-level expectations, product/category roadmap, and joint marketing/CSR (links to VS-14/VS-135); capture commitments and action items | Account Manager / Sponsor / Customer | Head of Strategic Accounts | Annual |
| 3 | Account Manager finalize and lock the KAP/JBP in CRM (links to VS-13.3), cascade targets to category/specialist teams (links to VS-01/VS-66/VS-77), integrate to the revenue/margin plan (links to VS-33.1/VS-101.3), and set the review cadence (W3430); refresh mid-year | Account Manager | Head of Strategic Accounts | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM (links to VS-13.3); revenue/margin planning (links to VS-33.1/VS-101.3); project pipeline (links to VS-11.2); category (links to VS-01)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Plan-on-shelf risk**: KAP not actioned/reviewed; mitigated by cadence (W3430) and executive sponsorship
- **Mis-alignment risk**: KAP targets inconsistent with customer reality or BuildRight plan; mitigated by JBP and finance integration


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count Manager build the Key Account Plan" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-capture of "capture commitments and action items" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review cadence" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: cadence (W3430) and executive sponsorship; operational: JBP and finance integration

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 weeks per KAP; annual

| **Volume** | ~5–20 stakeholders per strategic account |
| **Owner** | Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Account Manager, Executive Sponsor, CRM (links to VS-13.3), Customer stakeholders |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account Manager build the account relationship map: identify all key stakeholders (decision-makers, influencers, users, blockers, procurement/finance), their roles, power/interest, sentiment, BuildRight counterpart, and relationship strength; capture in CRM (links to VS-13.3) | Account Manager | Head of Strategic Accounts | Per account |
| 2 | Account Manager/Sponsor develop the stakeholder engagement plan: coverage of each stakeholder by the right BuildRight counterpart, relationship-building actions, gap-closure (uncovered/weak stakeholders), and exec-to-exec engagement for top stakeholders; execute against the plan | Account Manager / Sponsor | Head of Strategic Accounts | Continuous |
| 3 | Account Manager monitor stakeholder changes (turnover, reorg, sentiment shift), update the map, and trigger escalation/relationship-recovery (links to W3446) where a key stakeholder is at-risk or hostile; feed insights to the KAP (W3427) and governance review (W3430) | Account Manager | Head of Strategic Accounts | Continuous |

## W3430. Account Governance Cadence: QBR, Operating Reviews & Joint Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Established cadence per tier |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly (QBR) + monthly/weekly operating reviews |
| **Volume** | ~700 accounts across tiers |
| **Owner** | Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Account Manager, Sponsor, Customer counterparts, internal specialists |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account Manager prepare the operating review: performance vs KAP/JBP targets (revenue, GM$, share, projects, service-levels links to VS-67/VS-06), open issues/actions, pipeline (links to VS-11.2), credit/AR status (links to VS-16/VS-68), and risks; meet customer monthly/weekly per tier | Account Manager | Head of Strategic Accounts | Monthly/Weekly |
| 2 | Account Manager/Sponsor conduct the Quarterly Business Review (QBR) with customer decision-makers: performance review, strategic alignment, pipeline/pipeline-conversion, joint initiatives, escalations, and forward plan; capture minutes, actions, and commitments | Account Manager / Sponsor | Head of Strategic Accounts | Quarterly |
| 3 | Account Manager cascade QBR outcomes to internal teams (W3431), update the KAP (W3427) and CRM (links to VS-13.3), track action closure, and report account-health and at-risk signals to the account-governance review (links to VS-33.2) and retention workflow (W3446) | Account Manager | Head of Strategic Accounts | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM (links to VS-13.3); performance/scorecard (links to VS-67/VS-101.3); pipeline (links to VS-11.2); credit/AR (links to VS-16/VS-68)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cadence-slippage risk**: Missed QBRs signal disengagement; mitigated by tier-based cadence and tracking
- **Data-unpreparedness risk**: Reviews without data erode credibility; mitigated by pre-review analytics prep


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "count Manager/Sponsor conduct the Quarterly Business Review" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track action closure, and report account-health and at-risk signals to the…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: tier-based cadence and tracking; operational: pre-review analytics prep

### Time Estimate
- Monthly/Weekly + quarterly

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Account Manager coordinate multi-site/multi-entity delivery (e.g., a developer with projects across regions/entities per VS-72): align store/DC capacity (links to VS-07/VS-04), logistics (links to VS-06/VS-74), pricing consistency (links to VS-01.2/VS-57), and intercompany settlement (links to VS-17.2/VS-72); ensure seamless customer experience | Account Manager / Operations | Head of Strategic Accounts | Continuous |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliance/Legal screen strategic accounts for regulatory/sanctions/PEP/adverse-media exposure (links to VS-86.1 KYC/CDD/PEP/sanctions), government-account eligibility (links to VS-46), anti-bribery risk (links to VS-86.3), and conflict-of-interest (e.g., account affiliated with a BuildRight executive/family); document findings | Compliance / Legal / AFC | Head of Strategic Accounts | Per account + annual |

### Time Estimate
