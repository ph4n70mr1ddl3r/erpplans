## Overview

Strategic Key Account & Enterprise Customer Management (KAM) workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **strategic-relationship discipline** for BuildRight's highest-value business
customers: the ~200 corporate/institutional accounts (developers, large enterprises, government
agencies — 10% of revenue, ~PHP 6.2B per profile §9.1) and the top ~500 strategic trade accounts
(large contractors, builders, interior designers, architects — the upper tier of the 30%-of-revenue
Trade/B2B segment). This value stream owns the dedicated account-management program that
distinctively sits above transactional sales: strategic-account selection and tiering, account
planning and joint business planning (JBP), dedicated account teams and executive sponsorship,
enterprise contract and pricing governance, account-level profitability and mix management,
customer success and adoption, strategic growth and share-of-wallet, retention and at-risk
management, and key-account analytics. It addresses the reality that ~40% of revenue is
concentrated in ~5,200 B2B accounts (per profile §9.2), where a handful of strategic accounts
materially move the P&L and where disciplined relationship management is the primary lever for
growth, retention, and margin.

This is distinct from **VS-11 (Trade, Project & Wholesale)** which executes *transactional* trade
account setup, project/quotation/wholesale order processing, and bid response — the operational
engine KAM plans into and governs, but not the strategic relationship itself. It is distinct from
**VS-43 (Trade Professional Program)** which operates the *loyalty/incentive program* for the broad
trade population (tiered benefits, points, training) and from **VS-46 (Government & Institutional
Sales)** which executes *government bidding/accreditation/billing* mechanics. It is also distinct
from **VS-13 (Customer Experience & Loyalty)** which serves the *mass B2C/B2B support* function
(contact center, complaints, loyalty ops) and from **VS-16/VS-68 (Order-to-Cash / Trade Credit
Risk)** which process *credit, billing, and collection*. KAM owns the *strategic account
relationship, growth, and P&L* discipline that orchestrates VS-11/VS-16/VS-43/VS-46/VS-13 toward
the outcome of maximizing the value of each strategic account to BuildRight and of BuildRight to
each account.

BuildRight's exposure is concentrated and high-stakes: the top-20 corporate accounts alone can
represent a material share of revenue and GM$; losing a strategic developer/hospitality/government
account to a competitor (or to disintermediation by direct vendor import) directly hits EBITDA.
Strategic accounts also drive category-mix outcomes (links to VS-101), project-pipeline visibility
(links to VS-11.2/VS-77), credit exposure concentration (links to VS-68), and brand reputation
(links to VS-14.3). Existing workflows touch account management transactionally (e.g., VS-11.1
trade-account lifecycle, VS-46 government accounts, W103 trade sales pipeline, W162 project
quotation); no value stream owned the dedicated strategic key-account program.
