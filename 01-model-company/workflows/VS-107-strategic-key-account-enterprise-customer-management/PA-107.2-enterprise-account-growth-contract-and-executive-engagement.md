## W3433. Share-of-Wallet, Opportunity & White-Space Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | KAP development (W3427); annual/quarterly account review |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~700 strategic accounts; focus on top tiers |
| **Owner** | Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Account Manager, Finance (links to VS-17.4), Market Intelligence (links to VS-44/VS-57/VS-33.3), Category (links to VS-01) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account Manager/Finance estimate the customer's total addressable spend in BuildRight's categories (construction/renovation/operations) from project scale (links to VS-11.2/VS-66), industry benchmarks, and market intelligence (links to VS-44/VS-33.3); compute current share-of-wallet from actuals (links to VS-17.4/VS-13.3) | Account Manager / Finance / MI | Head of Strategic Accounts | Quarterly |
| 2 | Account Manager/Category identify white-space: categories/products not currently bought, project phases not served, geographies/sites not covered, and competitor-served spend; quantify the opportunity ($, GM$, strategic value) and prioritize | Account Manager / Category | Head of Strategic Accounts | Quarterly |
| 3 | Account Manager integrate the share-of-wallet and white-space opportunity into the KAP growth thesis (W3427), build the pursuit plan (links to W3436), and track share-growth over time; report to the QBR (W3430) | Account Manager | Head of Strategic Accounts | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance/revenue analytics (links to VS-17.4); CRM/CDP (links to VS-13.3); market intelligence (links to VS-44/VS-57/VS-33.3); category (links to VS-01)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Estimation-error risk**: Wrong addressable-spend estimate distorts the growth plan; mitigated by triangulation and customer JBP
- **White-space-inaction risk**: Identified opportunities not pursued; mitigated by pursuit plan and QBR tracking


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Account Manager/Finance estimate the customer's total addressable spend in…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-identify of "Account Manager/Category identify white-space" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track share-growth over time" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-551 (ensure controlled execution — Enterprise Account Growth, Contract & Executive Engagement (PA-107.2).)
- operational: triangulation and customer JBP; operational: pursuit plan and QBR tracking

### Time Estimate
- Quarterly

## W3434. Enterprise Pricing, Contract & Trading-Terms Negotiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | KAP/JBP (W3427); contract renewal; new large opportunity |
| **Frequency** | Per contract + periodic |
| **Volume** | ~200 enterprise contracts |
| **Owner** | Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Account Manager, Pricing (links to VS-01.2/VS-57), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Finance (links to VS-101.3/VS-68), Customer procurement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account Manager/Finance build the enterprise pricing/contract proposal: volume/mix assumptions, tiered/rebate structure (links to VS-39), price relative to SRP/trade (links to VS-01.2/VS-11), margin impact (links to VS-101.3 W3286), credit terms (links to VS-16.1/VS-68), SLAs, and duration; align to authority matrix (links to VS-36) | Account Manager / Pricing / Finance | Head of Strategic Accounts | Per contract |
| 2 | Account Manager/Legal negotiate trading terms with customer procurement: price/discount/rebate, volume commitment/take-or-pay, payment terms, exclusivity/preferred-supplier, SLA/penalty, termination, and risk allocation; execute the master agreement and price book (links to VS-100.3/W230/W402) | Account Manager / Legal | Head of Trade / Account Management / CFO | Per contract |
| 3 | Finance/Master Data load the enterprise price book/contract into the ERP (links to W289 pricing master, W402 contract master, VS-11.1/VS-163 project price books), validate application to orders (links to VS-11.2/VS-08), and monitor contract compliance and margin realization (W3441) | Finance / Master Data / Sales Ops | Finance Controller | Per contract |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing master (links to W289); contract master (links to W402); project price book (links to VS-11.1/W163); legal (links to VS-100.3); rebate (links to VS-39)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-erosion risk**: Over-discounting erodes margin (links to VS-101.3); mitigated by margin modeling and authority limits
- **Contract-leakage risk**: Agreed terms not applied at POS/order (links to VS-08/VS-11.2); mitigated by price-book loading and compliance monitoring


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "enterprise pricing/contract proposal" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Account Manager/Legal negotiate trading terms with customer procurement" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "validate application to orders" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-551 (ensure controlled execution — Enterprise Account Growth, Contract & Executive Engagement (PA-107.2).)
- operational: margin modeling and authority limits; operational: price-book loading and compliance monitoring

### Time Estimate
- Per contract; 2–6 weeks

- CTL-551 (ensure controlled execution — Enterprise Account Growth, Contract & Executive Engagement (PA-107.2).)
