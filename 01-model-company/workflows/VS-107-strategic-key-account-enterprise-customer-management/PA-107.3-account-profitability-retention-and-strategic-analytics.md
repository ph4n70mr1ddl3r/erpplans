## W3441. Account-Level Profitability, Cost-to-Serve & Margin Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close (links to VS-17.1); KAP/QBR (W3427/W3430) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~700 strategic accounts |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Finance |
| **Participants** | Merchandising Finance, Account Managers, FP&A (links to VS-17.4), Logistics Finance (links to W277), BI (links to VS-28.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandising Finance/BI compute account-level profitability: revenue, GM$ and GM% (net of discounts/rebates links to VS-39/VS-11.1, landed cost links to VS-02.2/VS-87), cost-to-serve (logistics links to VS-06/VS-74/VS-77 staging, special handling, account-team cost, financing/credit cost links to VS-68), returns (links to VS-32), and net contribution; allocate shared costs fairly | Merchandising Finance / BI | Head of Strategic Accounts | Monthly |
| 2 | Merchandising Finance/Account Manager analyze profitability drivers: product/category mix (links to VS-01/VS-101.3), pricing-vs-tier (links to W3434/VS-01.2), service mix, project margin (links to VS-11.2/VS-40), and cost-to-serve hotspots; flag unprofitable accounts for action | Merchandising Finance / Account Manager | Head of Strategic Accounts | Quarterly |
| 3 | Account Manager use profitability analytics to inform pricing/contract renewal (W3434), mix-shift, service redesign, and tier reassignment (W3425); report profitability to the QBR (W3430) and finance review (links to VS-17.4/VS-101.3) | Account Manager / Merchandising Finance | Head of Strategic Accounts | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance/revenue analytics (links to VS-17.4/VS-101.3); BI (links to VS-28.1); costing (links to VS-85); logistics finance (links to W277); CRM (links to VS-13.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cost-allocation-distortion risk**: Unfair cost-to-serve allocation mis-ranks accounts; mitigated by driver-based allocation
- **Margin-ignoring risk**: Growing revenue at the expense of net contribution (links to VS-101.3); mitigated by net-profitability view


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logistics links to VS-06/VS-74/VS-77 staging, special handling, account-team…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "count Manager analyze profitability drivers" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-552 (ensure controlled execution — Account Profitability, Retention & Strategic Analytics (PA-107.3).)
- operational: driver-based allocation; operational: net-profitability view

### Time Estimate
- Monthly + quarterly
