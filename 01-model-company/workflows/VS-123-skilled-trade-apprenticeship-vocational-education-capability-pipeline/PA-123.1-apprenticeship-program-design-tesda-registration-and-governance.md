## W3809. Trade-Capability Strategy & Apprenticeship Program Charter

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual people strategy (links to VS-33.1/VS-103.3); capability gap |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise trade-capability strategy |
| **Owner** | Trade Capability / L&D / Store Ops |
| **Participants** | Trade Capability, L&D (links to VS-19.4), Store Ops (links to VS-07), In-Store Services (links to VS-09), Installation (links to VS-12), HR (links to VS-19/VS-103), Workforce Planning (links to VS-103.3), Strategy (links to VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Capability/L&D/Store-Ops define the trade-capability strategy: identify the critical trade roles and skills BuildRight must master (lumber/yard, tiles/flooring, plumbing, electrical, paint/tinting, hand/power tools, hardware/fasteners; plus fabrication/estimation/installation), assess current capability gaps, and set the target capability posture and build-vs-buy approach | Trade Capability / L&D / Store Ops | CHRO / COO | Annual |
| 2 | Trade Capability/L&D charter the apprenticeship program as the primary capability-build vehicle: program objectives (certified trade capability, succession for trade-master roles, scalable service quality), target cohorts/scale, the TESDA-registered model (links to W3811), and the link to workforce planning (links to VS-103.3) and store growth (links to VS-37) | Trade Capability / L&D / HR | CHRO | Annual |
| 3 | CHRO/Executive ratify the trade-capability strategy and apprenticeship charter, approve the program budget (links to W3812), and cascade to category/store leadership as a BuildRight capability priority | CHRO / Trade Capability | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- L&D (links to VS-19.4); workforce planning (links to VS-103.3); store ops (links to VS-07); in-store services (links to VS-09); installation (links to VS-12); strategy (links to VS-33); governance (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Capability-gap risk**: Trade knowledge gaps unaddressed, hurting service quality; mitigated by capability strategy
- **Ad-hoc risk**: Apprenticeship run informally without charter/governance; mitigated by formal charter


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-post of "posture and build-vs-buy approach" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-register of "registered model" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the program budget" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-598 (ensure controlled execution — Apprenticeship Program Design, TESDA Registration & Governance (PA-123.1).)
- operational: capability strategy; operational: formal charter

### Time Estimate
- 3–4 weeks annual
