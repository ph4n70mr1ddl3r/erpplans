## W3817. Apprenticeship Cohort Planning, Scheduling & Resource Allocation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Cohort intake (W3814); program calendar |
| **Frequency** | Per cohort + ongoing |
| **Volume** | Apprentice cohorts per year |
| **Owner** | Trade Capability / L&D |
| **Participants** | Trade Capability, L&D (links to VS-19.4), Store Ops (links to VS-07), DC Ops (links to VS-04), HR (links to VS-19), Finance (links to VS-17.4), In-Store Services (links to VS-09) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Capability/L&D plan cohorts: cohort size/intake aligned to capability needs and store-growth (links to VS-37/VS-103.3), intake calendar, theory/practical schedule, store/DC rotation slots, and instructor/mentor capacity (links to W3820) | Trade Capability / L&D / Store Ops | Trade Capability Lead | Per cohort |
| 2 | Trade Capability/Store-Ops allocate apprentices to stores/DCs balancing learning value, store capacity, trade coverage, and regional distribution, and schedule rotation across departments (lumber, tiles, plumbing, electrical, paint, tools) | Trade Capability / Store Ops / DC Ops | Trade Capability Lead | Per cohort |
| 3 | Trade Capability/Finance allocate program resources: instructors, materials, tools/PPE kits (links to W172/W3823), facilities, and budget tracking (links to W3812) | Trade Capability / Finance | Trade Capability Lead | Per cohort |

### System Touchpoints
- L&D (links to VS-19.4); workforce planning (links to VS-103.3); store ops (links to VS-07); DC (links to VS-04); finance (links to VS-17.4); PPE (links to W172)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Capacity-mismatch risk**: Too many apprentices for store/mentor capacity; mitigated by capacity planning
- **Rotation-gap risk**: Apprentice stuck in one department; mitigated by rotation scheduling


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-schedule of "schedule, store/DC rotation slots, and instructor/mentor capacity" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-allocate of "allocate apprentices to stores/DCs balancing learning value, store capacity,…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "tracking" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: capacity planning; operational: rotation scheduling

### Time Estimate
- Per cohort
