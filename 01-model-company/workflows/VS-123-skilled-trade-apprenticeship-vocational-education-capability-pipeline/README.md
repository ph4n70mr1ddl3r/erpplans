# VS-123: Skilled-Trade Apprenticeship, Vocational Education & Capability Pipeline

> **People** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Skilled-Trade Apprenticeship, Vocational Education & Trade-Capability Pipeline workflows for
BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **structured build-up of BuildRight's most differentiating
human capability**: knowledgeable trade staff across lumber & building materials, tiles &
flooring, plumbing, electrical, paint & finishes, hand/power tools, and hardware/fasteners
departments (in ~200 stores), plus the specialist fabrication, estimation, and installation skills
that underpin BuildRight's in-store-services (VS-09), installation (VS-12), and customer
project/design (VS-66) offerings. In a Philippine market where formal trade certification is
delivered through the **TESDA** (Technical Education and Skills Development Authority, RA 7796)
framework and its **Training Regulations / CoCs / NCs** (Certificates of Competency / National
Certificates), and where knowledgeable trade frontline talent is scarce, BuildRight's
differentiation and service revenue depend on a deliberate, structured capability pipeline rather
than ad-hoc on-the-job training alone.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**: VS-19.4 runs general **employee** learning,
cross-training, and competency/certification (W51/W567/W683/W1042/W1127), VS-12.3 runs
**customer-facing** workshops and a single TESDA school career-day participation workflow
(W1556), and VS-43.3 runs trade **customer** training — but no value stream owns BuildRight's own
**structured apprenticeship and vocational capability pipeline**: TESDA-registered
apprenticeship/dual-training program design and compliance, earn-while-you-learn cohort
operations, mentor/master-tradesperson network, trade-skill curriculum and certification
sponsorship, vocational-school/SHS partnership and feeder management, instructor/master-trainer
pipeline, and trade-capability analytics. 'apprenticeship program' appears in **zero** PA files;
'talent pipeline' in one; 'vocational' in four (incidental).

This is distinct from **VS-19.4 (Learning & Development)** which owns general employee training
and competency (this value stream owns the *structured, certified, trade-specific apprenticeship
and vocational pipeline*). It is distinct from **VS-12.3 W1556 (Youth & Student Skilled Trade
Exposure / TESDA partnership)** which is a *participation/awareness* workflow for customer and
community engagement (this value stream owns BuildRight's *own* apprenticeship program and
vocational feeder). It is distinct from **VS-43.3 (Trade Customer Training & Community)** which
trains BuildRight's trade **customers** (this value stream builds BuildRight's *own* trade
capability). It is distinct from **VS-121 (Talent Acquisition & Employer Brand)** which attracts
talent generally (this value stream owns the *structured capability-building* of trade staff once
attracted, and the vocational feeder that supplies them). Skilled-trade apprenticeship, vocational
education & capability pipeline is the *certified trade-capability-building* discipline — distinct
from general L&D, customer-facing workshops, trade-customer training, and general talent
attraction.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: trade-knowledgeable frontline staff directly drive customer
service quality, basket size, custom-fabrication/estimation accuracy, installation revenue, and
the credible "home-building partner" positioning, yet such talent is scarce in the Philippine
market. Without a dedicated owner, BuildRight risks inconsistent trade knowledge, over-reliance on
individual hero staff, weak succession for trade-master roles, and an inability to scale
trade-quality service as the chain grows — risks that no existing value stream owns end-to-end.
