# VS-188: Trade Reseller Floor-Plan & Dealer Inventory Financing

> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Trade reseller floor-plan & dealer inventory financing for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **financing program in which BuildRight funds the inventory its trade/MSME/cooperative reseller customers hold for resale**, advancing the purchase cost to the supplier and carrying the unit-level loan secured by that inventory until the reseller sells it and pays down (curtails) the floor-plan. This covers the dealer-financing product strategy and risk appetite; dealer eligibility, credit underwriting, and floor-plan limit setting; the security agreement and collateral perfection under the Civil Code pledge/RA 9266-equivalent; dealer onboarding and inventory-reporting integration; floor-plan loan origination, dual-payee disbursement to the supplier, and unit-level collateral registration; dealer stocking-list audit and field examination; sold-unit curtailment and paydown; interest/markup billing and settlement; dealer collections, out-of-trust (OOT) detection, workout, repossession, and disposition; portfolio risk rating, provisioning, and analytics; and BSP/SEC/lending-company compliance and Truth-in-Lending (RA 3765) disclosure.

BuildRight operates as a wholesaler to its trade base (~5,000 trade accounts incl. sari-sari/MSME per VS-82, cooperatives per VS-94/VS-185, and reseller/dealer sub-distributors), and ~40% of revenue is B2B — a base for whom inventory purchase is the binding constraint on growth. A floor-plan program (the model used by manufacturers/wholesalers to finance dealer inventory in autos, equipment, and building materials) releases that constraint, lifts BuildRight's wholesale volume and loyalty, and earns financing margin — while exposing BuildRight to dealer default, out-of-trust (dealer sells without paying down), and collateral-recovery risk. This is distinct from VS-105 (supply-chain finance on BuildRight's *own payables*), VS-170 (BuildRight's *own* inventory pledged as ABL collateral), VS-38 (consumer credit), VS-154 (home-loan brokerage), VS-181 (B2B project escrow), and VS-16/VS-68 (the reseller trade-AR and credit-risk functions that this program *feeds*). No value stream owned BuildRight as a **floor-plan/dealer-inventory lender** — 'floor plan', 'dealer financing', 'inventory financing', and 'reseller financing' each appeared in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers.
