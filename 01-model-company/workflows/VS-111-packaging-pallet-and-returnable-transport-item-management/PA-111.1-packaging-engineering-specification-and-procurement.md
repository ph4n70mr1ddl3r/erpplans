## W3521. Packaging Strategy, Standards & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual SCM/sustainability planning (links to VS-33.1/VS-25); EPR/regulatory change (links to VS-22) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Group packaging/pallet estate |
| **Owner** | Packaging Engineering / SCM |
| **Participants** | Packaging Eng, SCM/Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34), Merchandising (links to VS-01/VS-41), Logistics (links to VS-06), Sustainability (links to VS-25), Quality (links to VS-31), Finance (links to VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Packaging Eng/SCM set the packaging strategy and standards: packaging policy, standard materials and specs, modular/standardized carton and pallet sizes (to optimize cube links to VS-110/W3541), right-sizing and over-packaging elimination, private-label packaging standards (links to VS-41), and brand/graphics requirements (links to VS-14); align to sustainability (links to VS-25) and EPR obligations (W3526) | Packaging Eng / SCM | COO | Annual |
| 2 | Packaging Eng/Merchandising define the packaging governance: who specifies/approves packaging (vendor vs BuildRight for private label), change-control, packaging-BOM in the item master (links to VS-29), and performance metrics (damage, cost, sustainability); set the pallet/RTI strategy (W3529) | Packaging Eng / Merchandising | COO | Annual |
| 3 | SCM/Finance approve the packaging cost-reduction and sustainability targets (links to W3542/W3526), align to the budget (links to VS-33.1) and commodity outlook (links to VS-106 — corrugate/plastic are commodity-derived), and report to governance (W3544) | SCM / Finance | COO / CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Item/packaging master (links to VS-29); merchandising/private label (links to VS-01/VS-41); logistics/cube (links to VS-110/W3541); sustainability (links to VS-25); commodity (links to VS-106); budget (links to VS-33.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Standardization-absence risk**: Ad-hoc packaging inflates cost and cube; mitigated by standards and modular sizes
- **Policy-non-compliance risk**: Packaging breaches EPR/brand standards; mitigated by governance and audit (W3539)


### Automation Opportunity
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves packaging" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "report to governance" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-79 (ensure RTI & pallet asset reconciliation.)
- operational: standards and modular sizes; operational: governance and audit (W3539)

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 weeks annual

| **Frequency** | Per SKU/project + ongoing |
| **Volume** | 35K active SKUs + private-label pipeline |
| **Owner** | Packaging Engineer |
| **Participants** | Packaging Eng, Merchandising (links to VS-01), Private Label (links to VS-41), Quality (links to VS-31), Supplier (links to VS-03), Marketing (links to VS-14), Master Data (links to VS-29) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Packaging Eng specify the product (consumer/unit) packaging per SKU: material (corrugate, blister, clamshell, bag), size/dimensions, weight, protection level, shelf appeal/graphics (links to VS-14), barcoding (links to VS-29/VS-71), and regulatory/labeling (W3527); right-size to minimize waste and cube | Packaging Eng / Private Label | VP Merchandising | Per SKU |
| 2 | Packaging Eng/Quality test the packaging: drop/compression/vibration/shelf-life testing (links to VS-31), transit-simulation (links to W3523), compatibility with the product (chemicals/paint links to VS-24.3), and consumer/ergonomic validation; iterate to spec | Packaging Eng / Quality | VP Merchandising | Per SKU |
| 3 | Packaging Eng/Master Data load the packaging spec and packaging-BOM (unit pack, inner, master/carton, pallet layer, GTIN hierarchy links to VS-29/VS-71) into the item master, publish to procurement (W3524) and vendors (W3525), and maintain through the product lifecycle (links to VS-01.1) | Packaging Eng / Master Data | VP Merchandising | Per SKU |

### System Touchpoints
- Item/packaging master & GTIN (links to VS-29/VS-71); quality testing (links to VS-31); merchandising/private label (links to VS-01/VS-41); graphics (links to VS-14)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-specification risk**: Inadequate packaging causes damage (links to VS-90/VS-31); mitigated by testing
- **Over-specification risk**: Over-packaging wastes cost/material; mitigated by right-sizing


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-test of "test the packaging" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-562 (ensure controlled execution — Packaging Engineering, Specification & Procurement (PA-111.1).)
- operational: testing; operational: right-sizing

### Time Estimate
- Per SKU/project

## W3523. Transport & Protective Packaging Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Damage analytics (W3540); new lane/mode/product; import packaging |
| **Frequency** | Project + ongoing |
| **Volume** | Transport packaging across inbound/line-haul/last-mile |
| **Owner** | Packaging Engineer / Logistics |
| **Participants** | Packaging Eng, Logistics (links to VS-06/VS-110), DC Operations (links to VS-04), Import (links to VS-02.2/VS-87), Quality (links to VS-31), Supplier |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Packaging Eng/Logistics design the transport/protective packaging per lane/mode: export/import packaging (container-load, moisture/vapor barrier, desiccant for ocean transit links to VS-02.2/W3503), palletization pattern and stretch-wrap/strapping, corner-boards/edge-protection, dunnage/void-fill, and fragile/hazardous handling (links to VS-24.3); optimize pallet build (W3529) and cube (links to W3541) | Packaging Eng / Logistics | VP Merchandising / Logistics Mgr | Per project |
| 2 | Packaging Eng/Quality validate the transport design via transit/damage testing and real damage data (W3540): drop, vibration, compression, humidity, tilt; iterate to reduce damage claims (links to VS-90/W3511) and protect high-value/fragile categories (appliances, tiles, power tools) | Packaging Eng / Quality | Logistics Manager | Per project |
| 3 | Packaging Eng publish the transport-packaging spec to vendors (inbound, W3525) and DC/store (outbound handling), capture the spec in the lane/SKU master (links to VS-29/VS-110), and track damage reduction (W3540) and freight-cube improvement (W3541) | Packaging Eng / Logistics | Logistics Manager | Per project |

### System Touchpoints
- Lane/SKU master (links to VS-29/VS-110); import (links to VS-02.2/VS-87); quality (links to VS-31); damage (links to VS-90/W3511/W3540); hazmat (links to VS-24.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transit-damage risk**: Inadequate transport packaging drives claims (links to VS-90); mitigated by transit testing and damage analytics
- **Import-moisture risk**: Ocean-transit moisture damages goods; mitigated by barrier/desiccant packaging


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics design the transport/protective packaging per lane/mode" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validate the transport design via transit/damage testing and real damage data" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track damage reduction" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-562 (ensure controlled execution — Packaging Engineering, Specification & Procurement (PA-111.1).)
- operational: transit testing and damage analytics; operational: barrier/desiccant packaging

### Time Estimate
- Per project
