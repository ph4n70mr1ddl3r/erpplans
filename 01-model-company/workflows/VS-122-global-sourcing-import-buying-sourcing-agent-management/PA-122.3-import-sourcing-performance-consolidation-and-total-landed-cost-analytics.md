## W3801. Import Order Consolidation, Container Planning & Consolidated Buying

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Sourcing plan (W3785); buying cycle |
| **Frequency** | Per buying cycle (continuous) |
| **Volume** | ~400–600 TEUs/month |
| **Owner** | Global Sourcing / Supply Planning |
| **Participants** | Global Sourcing, Supply Planning (links to VS-02), Buyers (links to VS-01), Freight (links to VS-110), Logistics Finance (links to W3788), DC Ops (links to VS-04) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Global Sourcing/Supply-Planning plan consolidated import container buying: aggregate category/entity demand into container-load POs, sequence vendor POs by origin/port/lead time to fill containers and reduce partial-load (LCL) cost, and balance against demand/lead time (links to VS-02.1) | Global Sourcing / Supply Planning | VP Merchandising | Per cycle |
| 2 | Global Sourcing/Buyers place consolidated import POs (links to VS-02.2/W2B): multi-vendor/multi-category consolidation at origin, container booking, and origin consolidation-point coordination (agent/office links to W3793/W3794) | Global Sourcing / Buyers / Freight | VP Merchandising | Per cycle |
| 3 | Global Sourcing/Freight optimize container utilization (cube/weight) and routing (links to W3803), and track container fill-rate / consolidation savings (links to W3807) | Global Sourcing / Freight / Logistics Finance | VP Merchandising | Per shipment |

### System Touchpoints
- Supply planning (links to VS-02); import PO (links to VS-02.2); freight (links to VS-110); finance/landed cost (links to VS-17.4); DC (links to VS-04)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **LCL-excess risk**: Poor consolidation inflates LCL freight cost; mitigated by container planning
- **Over-consolidation risk**: Waiting to fill containers delays availability; mitigated by demand/service-level balance

### Time Estimate
- Per cycle (continuous)

| **Volume** | Enterprise import landed-cost analytics |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Global Sourcing/Logistics-Finance track total-landed-cost (TLC) variance: actual vs planned FOB, freight, duty, brokerage, financing, shrink per SKU/source/route (links to W3788), identify variance drivers, and feed corrective action | Global Sourcing / Logistics Finance | VP Merchandising / Finance | Monthly |
| 2 | Global Sourcing/Finance measure realized import savings (negotiated FOB savings, consolidation savings, freight optimization, duty/PTA savings) vs target and confirm flow-through to margin/retail (links to VS-101/VS-3796), preventing "negotiated but not realized" leakage | Global Sourcing / Finance / Merchandise Finance | VP Merchandising / CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | Global Sourcing/Merchandising link TLC to category margin and pricing decisions (links to VS-01.2/VS-101), ensuring import sourcing supports the 28–32% gross-margin and 6–8x turn targets | Global Sourcing / Merchandising | VP Merchandising | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance/costing (links to VS-17.4); commodity (links to VS-106); freight (links to VS-110); customs (links to VS-87); treasury (links to VS-18); merchandise finance (links to VS-101); pricing (links to VS-01.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Variance-blindness risk**: TLC drifts without detection; mitigated by variance tracking
- **Savings-leakage risk**: Negotiated savings absorbed before reaching margin; mitigated by realized-savings tracking and margin linkage

### Time Estimate
- Monthly + quarterly
