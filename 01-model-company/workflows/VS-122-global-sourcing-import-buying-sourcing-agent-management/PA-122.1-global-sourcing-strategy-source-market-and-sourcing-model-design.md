## W3785. Global Sourcing Strategy & Import Category Sourcing Plan

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual merchandise/financial plan (links to VS-101); category strategy cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise import sourcing (~40% of COGS) |
| **Owner** | Global Sourcing / VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | Global Sourcing, Merchandising/Buyers (links to VS-01/VS-19), Supply Planning (links to VS-02), Vendor Mgmt (links to VS-03), Quality (links to VS-31), Finance (links to VS-17.4/VS-106), Compliance (links to VS-87/VS-22), Strategy (links to VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Global Sourcing/Merchandising define the global sourcing strategy: which categories/SKUs to import vs source locally (informed by landed cost, lead time, quality, IP, volume), the import category sourcing plan per category (steel/cement/lumber, tiles, electrical, paint/coatings, power tools, appliances, hardware), target source countries, and the make/buy/consolidation approach, aligned to the merchandise financial plan (links to VS-101) and assortment (links to VS-01.1) | Global Sourcing / Merchandising | VP Merchandising | Annual |
| 2 | Global Sourcing/Finance model the import business case per category: FOB cost, freight, duty, brokerage, inland, financing (LC cost), shrink/damage, and overhead against local-source alternatives; confirm the import landed-cost advantage and margin contribution (links to VS-106 commodity, VS-110 freight) | Global Sourcing / Finance | VP Merchandising / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | VP Merchandising/Executive ratify the global sourcing strategy, approve the sourcing-model and source-market decisions (links to W3786/W3787), and cascade import sourcing targets to buyers/category managers | VP Merchandising / Global Sourcing | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Merchandising (links to VS-01); supply planning (links to VS-02); finance/costing (links to VS-17.4/VS-106); freight (links to VS-110); strategy (links to VS-33)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fragmented-buying risk**: Each category/buyer imports independently with no enterprise leverage; mitigated by consolidated category sourcing plan
- **Margin-leakage risk**: Import landed cost not modeled end-to-end; mitigated by total-landed-cost framework (links to W3788)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "countries, and the make/buy/consolidation approach, aligned to the merchandise…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-import of "import business case per category" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the sourcing-model and source-market decisions" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: consolidated category sourcing plan; operational: total-landed-cost framework (links to W3788)

### Time Estimate
- 4–6 weeks annual

## W3787. Sourcing-Model Decision: Direct vs Sourcing Agent vs Overseas Buying Office

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W3785); new category; model review |
| **Frequency** | Per category + on review |
| **Volume** | Enterprise sourcing-model portfolio |
| **Owner** | Global Sourcing / Procurement Strategy |
| **Participants** | Global Sourcing, Merchandising (links to VS-01), Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34), Finance (links to VS-17.4), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Quality (links to VS-31), Compliance (links to VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Global Sourcing/Strategy evaluate and decide the sourcing model per category/vendor: (a) **direct** import (BuildRight buys ex-works/FOB from the factory — highest control, lowest unit cost, highest overhead); (b) **sourcing agent** (a third-party agent sources, consolidates, and manages vendors on BuildRight's behalf — links to W3793); (c) **overseas buying office** (BuildRight's own staff/office in the source country — links to W3794); based on volume, complexity, control, cost, and capability | Global Sourcing / Strategy / Finance | VP Merchandising | Per category |
| 2 | Global Sourcing/Legal structure the chosen model contractually: direct supply agreement, sourcing-agent agreement (scope, commission, exclusivity, IP, anti-bribery links to VS-86.3, data links to VS-91), or overseas-office establishment/legal-entity (links to VS-100/VS-36); define KPIs and governance | Global Sourcing / Legal | VP Merchandising / CFO | Per model |
| 3 | Global Sourcing/Finance publish a periodic sourcing-model ROI review (unit cost, overhead, quality, control, risk by model) and recommend model migration (e.g., agent → direct as volume grows) to governance (links to W3808) | Global Sourcing / Finance | VP Merchandising | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal/contracts (links to VS-100.3/W688); vendor master (links to VS-03); finance (links to VS-17.4); ABC (links to VS-86.3); data privacy (links to VS-91); governance (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Model-mismatch risk**: Direct buying without capability, or agent over-reliance; mitigated by model-decision framework
- **Agent-capture risk**: Sourcing agent controls vendor/price information; mitigated by governance and direct audits (links to W3796)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "country — links to W3794" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: model-decision framework; operational: governance and direct audits (links to W3796)

### Time Estimate
- Per category + annual review

## W3788. Total-Landed-Cost Sourcing Framework, Incoterms & Payment-Terms Strategy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Sourcing decision (W3785/W3787); cost review |
| **Frequency** | Per sourcing decision + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Enterprise landed-cost framework |
| **Owner** | Global Sourcing / Logistics Finance |
| **Participants** | Global Sourcing, Logistics Finance (links to VS-17.4), Freight (links to VS-110), Customs (links to VS-87), Treasury (links to VS-18), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Commodity (links to VS-106) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Global Sourcing/Logistics-Finance maintain the total-landed-cost (TLC) framework and calculator: FOB/ex-works unit cost, ocean freight & surcharges (BAF/CAF), duty/tariff (links to VS-87), brokerage, insurance, inland, port/handling, LC/financing cost (links to VS-18), shrink/damage allowance, and overhead → landed cost per unit and per SKU, by source/vendor/route | Global Sourcing / Logistics Finance | VP Merchandising / Finance | Maintained |
| 2 | Global Sourcing/Legal define the Incoterms strategy (FOB, EXW, CIF, DDP) and payment-terms strategy (LC at sight, usance LC, TT, open account, CAD) per source/vendor/category, balancing cost, control, risk, and cash flow (links to VS-18/VS-105); negotiate into contracts | Global Sourcing / Legal / Treasury | VP Merchandising | Per contract |
| 3 | Global Sourcing/Finance compare landed cost across sources/vendors/routes and recommend the lowest-TLC sourcing that meets quality/lead-time/risk constraints (links to W3807), tracking actual vs planned landed cost | Global Sourcing / Finance | VP Merchandising | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Freight (links to VS-110); customs/tariff (links to VS-87); treasury/LC (links to VS-18/VS-105); commodity (links to VS-106); finance/costing (links to VS-17.4); legal (links to VS-100.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden-cost risk**: Sourcing on FOB price alone ignores freight/duty/financing; mitigated by TLC framework
- **Cash-flow risk**: Aggressive LC terms strain working capital; mitigated by payment-terms strategy (links to VS-105)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics-Finance maintain the total-landed-cost" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "count, CAD" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-route of "routes and recommend the lowest-TLC sourcing that meets quality/lead-time/risk…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: TLC framework; operational: payment-terms strategy (links to VS-105)

### Time Estimate
- Per decision + quarterly
