## W2694. Construction Project Material Requirements Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contractor engages BuildRight for project supply |
| **Frequency** | Per project; ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects) |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 projects; PHP 2M–100M each |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Mgr, Category Managers, Supply Planning, Procurement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Manager works with contractor: reviews blueprint and bill of quantities, identifies BuildRight-supplied categories, estimates quantities per phase | Trade Sales Manager / Contractor | VP Merchandising | 2–5 days |
| 2 | Supply Planning validates availability: checks inventory vs. requirements, identifies long-lead items (imported tiles: 60–90 days), raises advance POs | Supply Planning | VP Supply Chain | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Trade Sales Manager produces phased delivery schedule: divides project into phases, allocates quantities, aligns delivery dates with timeline; obtains contractor sign-off | Trade Sales Manager | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-06 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scope-creep risk**: the project scope expands beyond the original quotation, eroding margin; mitigated by the change-order process and the scope-confirmation sign-off
- **Data-quality risk**: incomplete or inaccurate master data (pricing, inventory, customer) corrupts the workflow output; mitigated by the master-data governance (VS-29 / W291) and the periodic audit

### Time Estimate
- ~2–5 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~20–30 projects; PHP 2M–100M each
- Frequency: Per project; ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects)
- Absorbed into the Trade Sales Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls
- CTL-51 (master-data quality — links to VS-29 / W291); Operational: Trade Sales Manager sign-off on the workflow output

|---|---|

| **Participants** | Trade Sales Mgr, Category Manager, Pricing Analyst, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Manager negotiates price lock: fixed price for project duration (3–12 months) or escalation formula tied to published index, with maximum cap | Trade Sales Manager | VP Merchandising | Per project negotiation |

### System Touchpoints

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-erosion risk**: pricing miscalculation (commission, discount, surcharge) erodes net margin below target; mitigated by the margin-floor guard and pre-launch validation
- **Data-quality risk**: incomplete or inaccurate master data (pricing, inventory, customer) corrupts the workflow output; mitigated by the master-data governance (VS-29 / W291) and the periodic audit

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects)
- Frequency: Per project; monthly monitoring
- Absorbed into the Trade Sales Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Automation Opportunity

## W2701. Project Account Credit & Payment Terms Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project PO creation or contract negotiation |
| **Frequency** | Per project |
| **Volume** | ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects) |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Mgr, Credit Manager, Finance, Contractor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Manager negotiates payment terms: milestone-based, progress billing, retention (5–10%), credit terms (Net 30/60); Credit Manager approval per authorization matrix | Trade Sales Manager / Credit Manager | VP Merchandising / CFO | Per project |
| 2 | Finance sets up project billing: creates project account, links phase delivery to billing milestones, configures retention tracking, establishes credit limit | Finance / Credit Manager | CFO | Per project |
| 3 | Trade Sales Manager monitors payment health: tracks billed vs. collected, escalates overdue payments, ensures retention release upon completion | Trade Sales Manager / Finance | VP Merchandising | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Credit-default risk**: a trade account defaults on extended credit, creating a bad-debt write-off; mitigated by the credit-scoring model and the credit-limit monitoring
- **Phase-mismatch risk**: material arrives ahead of the construction phase needing it (or late), inflating staging cost and delaying the project; mitigated by the phasing plan tied to the construction schedule

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects)
- Frequency: Per project
- Absorbed into the Trade Sales Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Automation Opportunity
- Credit-score auto-computed from the financial-data feed; credit-limit auto-suggested from the scoring model; collection-aging auto-tracked with escalation

### Controls
- CTL-44 (duplicate-invoice/payment guard); CTL (credit-authorization — links to VS-16 / W24); Operational: Trade Sales Manager sign-off on the workflow output
