## W3641. Surety & Guarantee Program Strategy & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual finance/treasury planning (links to VS-33.1/VS-18); business/B2G strategy |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise surety/guarantee program |
| **Owner** | Surety Program Manager / Treasury |
| **Participants** | Surety Program Manager, Treasury (links to VS-18), Finance (links to VS-17), B2G Sales (links to VS-46), B2B/Project Sales (links to VS-11), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Risk (links to VS-21.2), Cross-Entity Shared Services (links to VS-72), Insurance (links to VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surety Program Manager/Treasury define the surety program strategy and governance: scope (bid/performance/payment/warranty bonds, bank guarantees, LC-for-guarantee, cash retention/escrow), the instrument-mix strategy (surety bond vs. bank guarantee vs. cash vs. LC by contract type), the regulatory map (RA 9184 government procurement, Insurance Code for surety, BSP for bank guarantees), the operating model, and the KPIs (facility utilization, premium cost, encumbrance, claim rate, release cycle-time) | Surety Program Manager / Treasury | CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Surety Program Manager establish the governance: Surety Program Manager role/authority, the cross-functional responsibilities (Sales initiates the need, Treasury/Finance arranges the instrument, Legal reviews terms, PMO/Operations delivers the contract), the surety policy, and the integration to treasury (VS-18), B2G (VS-46), B2B (VS-11), and risk (VS-21.2) | Surety Program Manager / Treasury / Legal | CFO | Annual |
| 3 | CFO/CEO ratify the surety strategy and policy, approve the authority matrix (W3647) and facility envelope (W3642), allocate the program budget (links to VS-17.4), and report surety posture and exposure to the executive risk committee (links to VS-21.2/VS-36) and board (links to VS-36); communicate the mandate | CFO / Surety Program Manager | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury (links to VS-18); finance (links to VS-17); B2G/B2B sales (links to VS-46/VS-11); legal (links to VS-100.3); risk (links to VS-21.2); governance (links to VS-36); cross-entity (links to VS-72)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-owner risk**: Bonds arranged ad-hoc by Sales with no program view; mitigated by Surety Program Manager authority
- **Instrument-mix-suboptimality risk**: Defaulting to cash retention or bank guarantee when surety bond is cheaper/frees credit; mitigated by instrument-mix strategy


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews terms, PMO/Operations delivers the contract" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the authority matrix" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-107 (ensure bond encumbrance & renewal control.)
operational: Surety Program Manager authority; operational: instrument-mix strategy

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 weeks annual

## W3642. Surety Facility, Line & Counter-Indemnity Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Facility strategy (W3641); capacity need; renewal |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on need |
| **Volume** | Surety facility / line across providers |
| **Owner** | Treasury / Surety Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury (links to VS-18), Surety Program Manager, Finance (links to VS-17), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Surety Companies/Banks, Risk (links to VS-21.2), IC/BSP regulators |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/Surety Program Manager arrange the surety facility/line: aggregate surety capacity across surety companies (IC-regulated) and bank-guarantee lines (BSP-regulated, links to VS-18.2), sized to BuildRight's projected B2G/B2B pipeline, multi-year, and renewable; negotiate terms, limit, sub-limits by bond type/contract size, and the counter-indemnity/collateral requirements | Treasury / Surety Program Manager | CFO | Annual + on need |
| 2 | Treasury/Legal negotiate the General Agreement of Indemnity (GAI) / counter-indemnity with surety providers (BuildRight indemnifies the surety for any bond paid), and the bank-guarantee facility agreement (including cash-margin/collateral terms); manage the corporate authorization, board resolution (links to VS-36), and entity-level signatories for the 5 entities | Treasury / Legal / Surety/Bank | CFO / CEO | Per facility |
| 3 | Treasury/Surety Program Manager manage the facility lifecycle: utilization tracking (W3654), renewal/extension, limit optimization (W3645), and provider diversification; report facility health, utilization, and capacity to governance (W3661) and treasury/cash planning (links to VS-18.1) | Treasury / Surety Program Manager | CFO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/banking (links to VS-18.2); finance (links to VS-17); legal (links to VS-100.3); governance/board (links to VS-36); cash positioning (links to VS-18.1); encumbrance register (links to W3654)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Insufficient-capacity risk**: Facility too small to support bids, causing bid rejection or cash tie-up; mitigated by pipeline-based sizing
- **Indemnity-exposure risk**: GAI exposes BuildRight to full bond amount on any claim; mitigated by risk assessment and collateral strategy (W3644)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "counter-indemnity/collateral requirements" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-track of "tracking" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-577 (ensure controlled execution — Surety Facility Strategy, Provider Management & Program Governance (PA-116.1).)
operational: pipeline-based sizing; operational: risk assessment and collateral strategy (W3644)

### Time Estimate
- Annual + per facility
