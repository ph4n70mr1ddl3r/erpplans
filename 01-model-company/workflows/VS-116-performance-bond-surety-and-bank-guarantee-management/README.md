# VS-116: Performance Bond, Surety & Bank Guarantee Management

> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Performance Bond, Surety & Bank Guarantee Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **contractual-security and surety program** that underpins BuildRight's participation
in government (B2G) and large enterprise/project (B2B) business. The model company earns ~10% of
revenue from corporate/institutional customers and a further ~30% from trade/project B2B (profile
§9.1); Philippine government procurement under RA 9184 (Government Procurement Reform Act, operated
by VS-46) and large B2B/project contracts routinely require **bid bonds** (to secure a tender),
**performance bonds** (5–30% of contract value, to secure delivery), **payment bonds** (to secure
supplier/subcontractor payment), **warranty bonds** (to secure the warranty period), and
**retention/maintenance money** — secured by surety bonds from insurance/surety companies or by
bank guarantees / letters of credit / cash retention that encumber BuildRight's credit facilities.
At the scale of BuildRight's B2G/B2B activity, multiple concurrent bonds and guarantees are
outstanding at any time, encumbering on the order of **PHP 5M–50M+ in bank guarantees
simultaneously** (per the VS-46 workflow note) and tying up credit facility capacity that otherwise
supports operations. Yet no value stream owns the surety program: VS-46 (B2G Sales) processes the
*bid* and references the bond as one step; VS-11 (Trade/Project/Wholesale) references tender/performance
bonds in project bids; and VS-18 (Treasury) manages the bank/facility relationship — but no value
stream owns the surety facility strategy, the bond application/issuance/tracking lifecycle, the
encumbrance and counter-indemnity management, the bond release/closeout, the claim/default response,
or the surety analytics. This value stream consolidates the discipline: surety facility strategy and
provider management, counter-indemnity and collateral, pricing/cost optimization, capacity and
encumbrance planning, policy/authority/audit; bid/performance/payment/warranty bond application and
issuance, bank guarantee and LC-for-guarantee, cash retention/escrow, bond tracking and encumbrance
register, multi-entity coordination, and amendment/renewal; and bond release/retrieval/closeout,
bond claim and default response, counter-indemnity call and subrogation/recovery, dispute/litigation,
surety spend/encumbrance analytics, provider performance, contract/bond compliance analytics, and
program maturity.

This is distinct from **VS-46 (Government & Institutional B2G Sales)** which *bids and delivers*
the government contract (this value stream secures the contractual obligations with bonds/
guarantees, and manages the surety/bond lifecycle around the bid). It is distinct from **VS-11
(Trade, Project & Wholesale)** which *sells and delivers* B2B/project business (this value stream
secures the project-performance obligations where the customer requires it). It is distinct from
**VS-18 (Treasury & Cash)** which *manages cash, banking, and the credit facility* (this value
stream manages the *encumbrance* of that facility by guarantees/bonds and the surety-provider
relationship). It is distinct from **VS-16 (Order-to-Cash / Trade Credit)** which *extends trade
credit* to customers (this value stream secures *BuildRight's own performance* to customers, the
mirror of trade credit). It is distinct from **VS-68 (Trade Credit Risk)** which insures
BuildRight's *receivables* (this value stream secures BuildRight's *payables/obligations*). It is
distinct from **VS-72 (Cross-Entity Shared Services)** which *bills intercompany* (this value
stream coordinates bonds across the 5 entities where multi-entity bidding occurs). It is distinct
from **VS-100 (Legal Operations)** which *manages litigation* (this value stream manages the
contractual-security instruments and any claim/default, with legal support). Performance bond,
surety & bank guarantee management is the *contractual-security and encumbrance* discipline —
distinct from B2G/B2B sales, treasury, trade credit, cross-entity billing, and legal operations.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: B2G and large B2B/project revenue (~40% combined of
PHP 62.3B) require contractual security that consumes surety capacity, encumbers bank facilities,
carries premium cost, ties up cash retention, and — in the event of dispute or default — exposes
the company to bond claim, counter-indemnity call, and significant financial loss. Without a
dedicated owner, BuildRight risks bid rejection (no bond), excessive facility encumbrance,
over-payment of surety premiums, unreleased bonds after contract completion (capital trapped),
unmanaged claim exposure, and mis-coordination across the 5 entities — risks that no existing value
stream owns.
