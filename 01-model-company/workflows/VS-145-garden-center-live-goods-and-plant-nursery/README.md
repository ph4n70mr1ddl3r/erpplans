# VS-145: Garden Center, Live Goods & Plant Nursery Operations

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Garden Center, Live Goods & Plant Nursery workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the
**live-goods operating discipline** for the Garden & Outdoor category (~3% of active SKUs =
~1,050 live-plant/nursery SKUs) carried across 200 store garden centers and outdoor yards. Live
plants are **perishable biological merchandise**: they require daily care (watering, pruning,
light/acclimatization), decline visibly with age/stress, suffer pest/disease, and carry high
mortality/shrink — fundamentally unlike the hard-goods inventory lifecycle (VS-05) or standard
replenishment (VS-02). They are also frequently **vendor-consigned / pay-by-scan**, seasonally
cycled (ber-months poinsettias, Valentine, Holy Week, rainy-season), and regulated
(phytosanitary/BPI, pesticides/FPA, restricted/invasive species).

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. The defining terms — "live goods", "plant
nursery", "plant care", "live-goods shrink/mortality", "live-goods markdown-on-decline" — appear
in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers; only the single workflow **W1487 (Garden
Center Seasonal Assortment Rotation & VMI)** in VS-07.1 exists, plus incidental mentions across
~10 PA files (assortment, store daily ops, facilities, compliance, ESG). No value stream owns the
end-to-end **live-goods program**: live-goods assortment & seasonal calendar, grower sourcing &
phytosanitary, nursery/acclimatization, daily in-store care, plant-health/pest-disease, decline-
triggered markdown, mortality/shrink, consignment/pay-by-scan settlement, pesticide/FPA &
restricted-species compliance, and live-goods sustainability/ESG.

This is distinct from **VS-05 (Inventory Lifecycle)** which owns **hard-goods** inventory
transactions/valuation (live goods need *biological care*, not just transactions). It is distinct
from **VS-02 (Supply Planning)** which owns standard replenishment (live goods are *seasonal,
consigned, and care-sensitive*, not min/max-replenished). It is distinct from **VS-07 (Store
Operations)** which owns general store daily ops (live goods need *specialist horticultural
daily care*). It is distinct from **VS-73 (Store Waste & Circular Economy)** which owns general
store waste (live-goods mortality/composting is a *biological-disposition* discipline with
composting/phytosanitary specificity). It is distinct from **VS-45 (Consignment/VMI)** which owns
the consignment *mechanism* generically (live-goods consignment needs *mortality-attribution* and
*pay-by-scan-on-sell-through* specificity).

BuildRight's exposure is structural: ~1,050 live SKUs across 200 store garden centers = a large
perishable inventory base with documented retail live-goods shrink benchmarks of 8–20% (vs the
≥97% hard-goods accuracy target) — unowned mortality/markdown directly erodes the 28–32% gross
margin and the category's role as a traffic/attach driver; unowned pesticide/restricted-species
handling creates FPA/BPI/DENR and safety exposure; and the seasonal cycles (poinsettias,
Valentine, Holy Week) are concentrated sell-through windows where overstock becomes mortality.
