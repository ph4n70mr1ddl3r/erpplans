## W4337. Live-Goods & Garden Center Strategy, Assortment & Seasonal Calendar

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual category plan; seasonal cycle; performance review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + seasonal cycles |
| **Volume** | ~1,050 live-goods SKUs across 200 store garden centers |
| **Owner** | Garden Buyer / Merchandising (VS-01) / Garden Ops |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01), Store Ops (VS-07), Supply (VS-02), Marketing (VS-14), Vendor (VS-03) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Garden Buyer/Merch set the live-goods strategy & assortment — core/perennial range, seasonal event range (poinsettias ber-months, Valentine, Holy Week, rainy-season), and the regional/climate fit (Mindanao vs Luzon) | Garden Buyer / Merch | Head of Merch | Annual |
| 2 | Garden Buyer/Supply build the seasonal calendar (sow/grow/land/peak/clear windows) aligned to grower lead times and store capacity, and size seasonal buy against forecast | Garden Buyer / Supply | Head of Merch | Per season |
| 3 | Merch/Ops document the strategy, cascade to sourcing (W4338), pricing/markdown (W4341), and seasonal promo (W4344) | Garden Buyer / Merch | Head of Merch | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Merchandising/assortment (VS-01); supply (VS-02); marketing (VS-14); vendor (VS-03); master data (VS-29)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Climate-mismatch risk**: assortment wrong for region → mortality; mitigated by regional/climate assortment fit
- **Timing risk**: seasonal land-date missed → missed peak sell-through → markdown/mortality; mitigated by seasonal calendar aligned to grower lead times

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual + ~1 week/season

### Automation Opportunity
- Regional assortment-fit analytics; seasonal-calendar vs sell-through modeling; forecast-based buy sizing

### Controls
- CTL: assortment region/climate-fit; seasonal calendar aligned to lead times; buy sized to forecast

### Cross-references
- links to VS-01 (merchandising), VS-02 (supply), VS-14 (marketing), VS-03 (vendor), W4338 (sourcing), W4341 (markdown), W4344 (seasonal promo)

## W4338. Live Plant Sourcing, Grower/Vendor Management & Phytosanitary Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Sourcing cycle; new grower; seasonal buy |
| **Frequency** | Per sourcing cycle + ongoing vendor mgmt |
| **Volume** | Live-goods grower/vendor base (local + import) |
| **Owner** | Garden Buyer / Vendor Mgmt (VS-03) / Quality (VS-31) |
| **Participants** | Vendor (VS-03), Quality (VS-31), Supply (VS-02), Customs (VS-87 for import), Compliance (BPI/phytosanitary VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Garden Buyer/Vendor Mgmt source & qualify growers (capacity, quality, certification, pest-free status, sustainability), and negotiate seasonal capacity + standing orders | Garden Buyer / Vendor Mgmt | Head of Merch | Per cycle |
| 2 | Quality/Compliance verify phytosanitary compliance — BPI (Bureau of Plant Industry) import permit & phytosanitary certificate for imported live plants, pest-free status, restricted-species check (W4357) | Quality / Compliance | Quality Lead / Compliance Lead | Per shipment |

- **Capacity risk**: grower cannot deliver seasonal peak; mitigated by multiple qualified growers + standing orders

### Time Estimate
- Qualification ~weeks; per-shipment verification hours

### Automation Opportunity
- Grower scorecard; phytosanitary/permit tracking; restricted-species check at intake; capacity-forecast sharing

### Controls
- CTL: growers qualified & scored; phytosanitary/BPI compliance for imports; restricted-species gated (W4357)

## W4339. Live-Goods Inbound Receiving, Acclimatization & Quarantine

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Live-goods delivery to DC/store garden center |
| **Frequency** | Per live-goods receipt (seasonal peaks) |
| **Volume** | Live-goods receipts (seasonal-batched) |
| **Owner** | Garden Ops / DC Inbound (VS-04) / Quality (VS-31) |
| **Participants** | DC Ops (VS-04), Store Ops (VS-07), Quality (VS-31), Vendor (VS-03), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Garden Ops/Quality receive live goods — inspect survival/health on arrival (vs grower), check pest/disease, verify phytosanitary (W4338), and reject/sub-standard stock | Garden Ops / Quality | Garden Ops Lead / Quality Lead | Per receipt |
| 2 | Garden Ops acclimatize received plants (shade/light/humidity transition) and quarantine suspect/infested stock before floor placement | Garden Ops | Garden Ops Lead | Per receipt |
| 3 | Garden Ops/DC post receipt to inventory (VS-05) with received condition, attribute health-grade, and trigger any vendor claim (W4353) for sub-standard/dead-on-arrival | Garden Ops / DC | Garden Ops Lead | Per receipt |

### System Touchpoints
- DC/WMS (VS-04); quality (VS-31); inventory (VS-05); vendor claims (VS-90/VS-31); HSE (VS-24)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transit-shock risk**: plants stressed/damaged in transit → post-receipt mortality; mitigated by survival-on-arrival inspection + acclimatization
- **Pest-introduction risk**: infested stock placed on floor → spread; mitigated by quarantine before placement

### Time Estimate
- Inspection ~min/lot; acclimatization ~1–7 days/lot

### Automation Opportunity
- Health-grade capture on receipt; quarantine-workflow; survival-on-arrival analytics; vendor DOA-claim auto-draft

### Controls
- CTL: survival/health inspected on arrival; quarantine for suspect stock; DOA claimed vs vendor (VS-90/VS-31)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-04 (DC), VS-31 (quality), VS-05 (inventory), VS-90 (claims), VS-24 (HSE), W4338 (phytosanitary), W4353 (shrink/claim)

## W4341. Live-Goods Pricing, Markdown-on-Decline & Shrink Policy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Pricing cycle; decline detected; shrink write-off |
| **Frequency** | Pricing periodic + decline-triggered markdown |
| **Volume** | ~1,050 live SKUs pricing + decline-markdown flow |
| **Owner** | Pricing (VS-01.2) / Garden Ops / Finance (VS-17.4) |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01), Garden Ops, Revenue Assurance (VS-118), Finance (VS-17.4), Marketing (VS-14) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing/Garden set live-goods pricing (cost + perishability + care-cost premium) and the markdown-on-decline ladder (healthy → slight-decline → marked-down → clearance → dispose) | Pricing / Garden Ops | Head of Merch / CFO | Periodic |
| 2 | Garden Ops apply decline-triggered markdown per the ladder as plants age/stress (W4354), and document markdown reason/grade for margin & vendor-consignment attribution | Garden Ops | Garden Ops Lead | Continuous |
| 3 | Finance/RA track live-goods markdown/shrink margin impact, detect leakage (un-marked decline sold at full price, or over-markdown), and feed prevention | Finance / RA | CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing/merchandising (VS-01.2); revenue assurance (VS-118); finance (VS-17.4); inventory/markdown (VS-05/VS-64)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-leak risk**: declined plants sold at full price (customer dissatisfaction) or over-marked-down; mitigated by decline-ladder & RA monitoring
- **Attribution risk**: markdown/shrink not attributed (BuildRight vs consignor); mitigated by documented grade for consignment settlement (W4342)

### Time Estimate
- Pricing periodic; decline-markdown daily as triggered

### Automation Opportunity
- Health-grade → markdown-ladder auto-suggest; age/decline-triggered markdown; RA leakage detection; consignment attribution

### Controls
- CTL: decline-markdown ladder applied; markdown documented/attributed; margin RA-monitored (VS-118)

### Cross-references

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL: consignment ownership tracked until scan; mortality/markdown attributed per terms; settlement reconciled
