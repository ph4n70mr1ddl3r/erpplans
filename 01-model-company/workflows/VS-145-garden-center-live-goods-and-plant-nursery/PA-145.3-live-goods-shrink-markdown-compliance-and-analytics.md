## W4353. Live-Goods Shrink, Mortality & Write-Off Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Plant death/unsellable detected; periodic write-off |
| **Frequency** | Continuous detection + periodic write-off |
| **Volume** | Live-goods mortality/shrink (benchmark 8–20% of live stock) |
| **Owner** | Garden Ops / Inventory (VS-05) / Finance (VS-17.4) / LP (VS-23) |
| **Participants** | Garden Ops, Inventory (VS-05), Finance (VS-17.4), LP (VS-23), Vendor (VS-03) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Garden staff detect dead/unsellable plants, document cause (care, transit, vendor-quality, pest/disease), and segregate for disposition (W4355) or vendor-claim (W4338) | Garden Staff / Garden Ops | Garden Ops Lead | Continuous |
| 2 | Inventory/Finance write off the dead stock from inventory (VS-05) at cost, attribute to BuildRight/consignor/vendor per terms (W4342), and post shrink | Inventory / Finance | Finance Mgr | Periodic |
| 3 | LP/Finance analyze shrink trends (by store/cause/vendor), file vendor claims (VS-90), and feed prevention (care, sourcing, transit) | LP / Finance / Garden Ops | LP Lead / CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory (VS-05); finance/GL (VS-17.4); LP/shrink (VS-23); vendor claims (VS-90); consignment (VS-45)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unattributed-shrink risk**: mortality not attributed → cost falls on BuildRight not liable vendor; mitigated by cause-documentation & vendor-claim
- **Late-write-off risk**: dead stock left on floor → display quality/inventory distortion; mitigated by daily detection & periodic write-off

### Time Estimate
- Detection folded into W4345; write-off periodic

### Automation Opportunity
- Mortality-cause capture app; auto write-off with attribution; shrink-trend analytics; vendor-claim auto-draft

### Controls
- CTL-667 (ensure controlled execution — Live-Goods Shrink, Markdown, Compliance & Analytics (PA-145.3).)
- CTL: dead stock detected daily & written off; cause documented; attributed per terms; vendor-claim filed (VS-90)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-05 (inventory), VS-17.4 (finance), VS-23 (LP), VS-90 (claims), W4342 (attribution), W4355 (disposal), W4359 (analytics)

## W4354. Decline/Markdown Trigger, Aging & Clearance

| **Volume** | Decline-markdown flow across live stock |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Cycle-count cycle; inventory-close; accuracy concern |
| **Frequency** | Frequent cycle count (live-goods drift faster than hard goods) |
| **Volume** | ~1,050 live SKUs × 200 stores cycle counting |
| **Owner** | Inventory (VS-05) / Garden Ops / Store Ops (VS-07) |
| **Participants** | Inventory (VS-05), Garden Ops, Store Ops (VS-07), Finance (VS-17.4), LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inventory/Garden Ops set live-goods cycle-count frequency (higher than hard-goods weekly, given mortality/drift) and the count method (by health-zone/SKU) | Inventory / Garden Ops | Inventory Lead / Garden Ops Lead | Standard |
| 2 | Garden staff/Inventory execute the count, reconcile to system (VS-05), investigate variance (mortality un-written W4353, theft VS-23, scan-error), and adjust | Garden Staff / Inventory | Garden Ops Lead / Inventory Lead | Per cycle |
| 3 | Finance/LP analyze accuracy/variance trends, attribute loss, and feed prevention (care, write-off timeliness, security) | Finance / LP / Inventory | CFO / LP Lead | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory/WMS (VS-05); finance (VS-17.4); LP (VS-23); store ops (VS-07)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Accuracy-drift risk**: live-goods count drifts (mortality/decline) faster than hard-goods cycle; mitigated by higher-frequency live-goods count
- **Variance-attribution risk**: variance cause unclear (mortality vs theft); mitigated by investigation SOP & LP

### Time Estimate
- ~hours/store/cycle (frequent)

### Automation Opportunity
- Live-goods cycle-count app by health-zone; variance auto-investigation; accuracy dashboard; theft-pattern analytics

### Controls
- CTL-91 (ensure live-goods compliance & inventory accuracy.)
- CTL: live-goods cycle-counted at higher frequency; variance investigated; loss attributed
