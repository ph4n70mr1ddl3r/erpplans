## W4345. Garden Center Daily Care, Watering, Pruning & Rotation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily store operation (garden center) |
| **Frequency** | Daily per store garden center |
| **Volume** | 200 store garden centers × daily care cycles |
| **Owner** | Garden Center Staff / Store Ops (VS-07) / Garden Ops |
| **Participants** | Garden Staff, Store Ops (VS-07), Garden Ops, Vendor (VS-03), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Garden staff execute the daily care schedule — water by plant need (species/season), inspect/prune, remove dead material, and rotate stock (oldest/declining to front for sell-through) | Garden Staff | Garden Ops Lead / Store Mgr | Daily |
| 2 | Garden staff monitor micro-climate (sun/shade/heat), adjust protection (shade net, misting) for hot/rainy conditions, and flag declining stock for markdown (W4341) | Garden Staff | Garden Ops Lead | Daily |
| 3 | Store Ops/Garden Ops verify care execution (checklist), track plant condition, and feed shrink/markdown data (W4353) | Store Ops / Garden Ops | Store Mgr / Garden Ops Lead | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Store ops/task (VS-07/VS-63); care-schedule app; inventory/markdown (VS-05/VS-64)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mortality-from-neglect risk**: skipped watering/care → mortality/shrink; mitigated by daily care checklist & accountability
- **Heat/rain-damage risk**: unprotected plants damaged by hot/rainy PH weather; mitigated by micro-climate protection

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 hrs/store/day depending on garden-center size

### Automation Opportunity
- Digital care-checklist by plant need; condition-photo tracking; auto-flag decline for markdown; weather-linked care alerts

### Controls
- CTL: daily care checklist executed; declining stock flagged for markdown (W4341); care verified

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07 (store ops), VS-63 (task), VS-05/VS-64 (inventory/markdown), W4340 (nursery), W4341 (markdown), W4353 (shrink)

## W4347. Plant Health Monitoring, Pest/Disease & Treatment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Pest/disease detected; routine monitoring |
| **Frequency** | Continuous monitoring + per outbreak |
| **Volume** | Per-store plant-health across live stock |
| **Owner** | Garden Ops / Garden Staff / Quality (VS-31) |
| **Participants** | Garden Staff, Quality (VS-31), HSE (VS-24), Pest-control Vendor, Compliance (FPA VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Garden staff monitor plant health daily (yellowing, wilting, spots, pests), identify pest/disease early, and isolate affected plants | Garden Staff | Garden Ops Lead | Daily |
| 2 | Garden Ops/staff treat per protocol (biological/preferred-first, pesticide per FPA W4356), apply safely with PPE, and document treatment | Garden Ops / Staff | Garden Ops Lead / HSE | Per case |
| 3 | Quality/HSE verify treatment efficacy & safety, escalate severe outbreak, and feed prevention/protocol improvement | Quality / HSE | Quality Lead / HSE Mgr | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Quality (VS-31); HSE (VS-24); compliance/FPA (VS-22); pest-control vendor (VS-34)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Outbreak-spread risk**: undetected pest/disease spreads across stock → mass mortality; mitigated by daily monitoring + isolation
- **Pesticide-safety risk**: unsafe pesticide use → staff/customer/environment exposure + FPA violation; mitigated by FPA-compliant treatment & PPE (W4356)

### Time Estimate
- Monitoring folded into W4345; treatment ~min–hours/outbreak

### Automation Opportunity
- Pest/disease identification aid; treatment-protocol app; outbreak-alert workflow; efficacy tracking

### Controls
- CTL: daily health monitoring; affected plants isolated; treatment FPA-compliant with PPE (W4356)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-31 (quality), VS-24 (HSE), VS-22 (FPA compliance), W4340 (nursery), W4356 (pesticide handling), W4353 (shrink)

## W4352. Customer Returns, Plant Guarantee & Replacement Policy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer return/claim (dead/declined plant under guarantee) |
| **Frequency** | Per return/claim |
| **Volume** | Live-goods returns/claims (proportional to guarantee take-up) |
| **Owner** | Customer Service (VS-13) / Garden Ops / Returns (VS-32) |
| **Participants** | Customer Service (VS-13), Garden Ops, Returns (VS-32), Store Ops (VS-07), Finance (VS-118) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Garden Buyer/Finance set the plant guarantee policy (guarantee period, replacement-vs-refund, customer-care-burden, exclusions) balancing service vs abuse | Garden Buyer / Finance | Head of Merch / CFO | Periodic |
| 2 | Customer Service/Garden Ops process the return/claim — verify eligibility/receipt, assess cause (stock-quality vs customer-care), and replace/refund per policy | Customer Service / Garden Ops | Store Mgr / Garden Ops Lead | Per claim |
| 3 | Returns/Finance route the returned plant to disposal/compost (W4355) or vendor-claim (W4338), track guarantee cost/abuse, and feed prevention | Returns / Finance | Returns Lead / CFO | Per claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Returns (VS-32); customer service (VS-13); finance/RA (VS-118); inventory (VS-05); waste/compost (VS-73/W4355)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Abuse risk**: guarantee exploited (replace customer-neglected plants) → cost; mitigated by cause-assessment & policy limits
- **Cause-attribution risk**: stock-quality vs customer-care unclear → wrong vendor-claim/customer outcome; mitigated by assessment SOP

### Time Estimate
- ~10–20 min/claim

### Automation Opportunity
- Guarantee-eligibility check; cause-assessment workflow; guarantee-cost/abuse analytics; auto-route to disposal/vendor-claim

### Controls
- CTL: guarantee policy applied; cause assessed; abuse monitored; returned plant routed per disposition

### Cross-references
- links to VS-32 (returns), VS-13 (service), VS-118 (RA), VS-05 (inventory), W4338 (vendor claim), W4355 (disposal), W4348 (advisory)
