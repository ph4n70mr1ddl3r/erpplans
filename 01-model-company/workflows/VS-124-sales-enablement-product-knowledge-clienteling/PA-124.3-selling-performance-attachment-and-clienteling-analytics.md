## W3849. Selling-Effectiveness KPI Framework & Associate/Store Performance Scorecard

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W3833); reporting cycle |
| **Frequency** | Maintained + monthly/quarterly |
| **Volume** | Enterprise selling-effectiveness scorecard |
| **Owner** | Sales Enablement / Analytics |
| **Participants** | Sales Enablement, Analytics (links to VS-28.1), Store Ops (links to VS-07), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Finance (links to VS-17.4), People Analytics (links to VS-103.3), HR (links to VS-19) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Enablement/Analytics define the selling-effectiveness KPI framework: basket size/units, conversion, attach rate (service/accessory/link-sell), trade-pro identification, loyalty enrollment/identification, product-knowledge certification coverage (links to W3842), and selling-quality score (links to W3847), at store/department/associate level where data permits | Sales Enablement / Analytics / Store Ops | Sales Enablement Lead | Maintained |
| 2 | Sales Enablement/Analytics produce store/department/associate selling scorecards (with appropriate fairness, data-quality, and privacy controls — links to VS-91), and feed into coaching (links to W3836) and recognition/incentive (links to W3838) | Sales Enablement / Analytics / HR | Sales Enablement Lead | Monthly |
| 3 | Sales Enablement/Store-Ops run performance conversations using the scorecard (recognize top performers, coach middle, improve bottom), with fairness and lawful handling of associate-level data (links to VS-84/VS-91) | Sales Enablement / Store Ops / HR | Sales Enablement Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28.1); POS data (links to VS-08); people analytics (links to VS-103.3); data privacy (links to VS-91); labor relations (links to VS-84); recognition (links to W3838)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric-gaming risk**: Associates game narrow metrics; mitigated by balanced scorecard
- **Fairness/privacy risk**: Associate-level metrics misused or unlawfully handled; mitigated by fairness controls and RA 10173 (links to VS-91)


### Automation Opportunity
- workflow automation; system-driven processing with exception handling

### Controls
- CTL-603 (ensure controlled execution — Selling Performance, Attachment & Clienteling Analytics (PA-124.3).)
- operational: balanced scorecard; operational: fairness controls and RA 10173 (links to VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- Maintained + monthly

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Enablement/Analytics analyze basket (size, units, composition, category mix), conversion (footfall → transaction where measurable), and attach (primary item → accessory/service/link-sell) by store/department/category/region/time, using POS/ecommerce data (links to VS-08/VS-10) | Sales Enablement / Analytics | Sales Enablement Lead | Weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- POS (links to VS-08); ecommerce (links to VS-10); merchandising/pricing (links to VS-01.2/VS-57); in-store services (links to VS-09); merchandise finance (links to VS-101); analytics (links to VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Opportunity-blindness risk**: Attach/basket gaps unseen; mitigated by analytics
- **Action-gap risk**: Insights not translated to action; mitigated by playbook/coaching linkage


### Automation Opportunity

- System auto-identify of "identify attach/basket opportunities" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "report to commercial" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-603 (ensure controlled execution — Selling Performance, Attachment & Clienteling Analytics (PA-124.3).)
- operational: analytics; operational: playbook/coaching linkage
