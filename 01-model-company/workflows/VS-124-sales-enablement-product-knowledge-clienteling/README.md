# VS-124: Sales Enablement, Product Knowledge Mastery & Clienteling

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Sales Enablement, Product-Knowledge Mastery & Clienteling workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
owning the **associate-side selling effectiveness discipline** that converts ~2.8M monthly POS
transactions, ~PHP 1,800 average transaction value, ~40% B2B/trade revenue, and ~600K loyalty
members into basket size, conversion, attachment, and trade-pro capture. Across ~5,800 store
staff and ~200 stores, associate effectiveness — product knowledge mastery, consultative selling,
clienteling (serving identified customers with their full profile/history at the point of sale),
attachment/linked selling, and category-specific expertise (lumber, tiles, plumbing, electrical,
paint, tools, appliances) — is the single largest controllable lever on basket size, service
quality, and the credible "home-building partner" positioning.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**: VS-19.4 runs general employee training and
competency/certification (the *platform*), product knowledge and product training are referenced
incidentally across ~33 and ~13 PA files respectively (sprinkled across VS-19.4 L&D, VS-07 store
ops, VS-09 in-store services), and VS-13 owns loyalty/CX — but no value stream owns the **sales
enablement discipline**: selling-skills curriculum and coaching, product-knowledge mastery and
certification (role/department-specific), clienteling tools and the customer-360 at the point of
sale, attachment/linked/category-selling playbooks, trade-pro consultative selling, sales
performance coaching and quality-assurance, and selling-effectiveness/clienteling analytics.
'clienteling' appears in **zero** PA files; 'sales enablement' in two; 'selling skills' in one.

This is distinct from **VS-19.4 (Learning & Development)** which owns general employee training
and the competency/LMS platform (this value stream owns the *sales-specific enablement, product-
knowledge mastery, and clienteling* that runs on top of that platform). It is distinct from
**VS-13 (Customer Experience & Loyalty)** which owns the loyalty program, complaints, and CRM
(this value stream owns the *associate's selling behavior and tools* that leverage that customer
data at the point of sale). It is distinct from **VS-07 (Store Operations)** which runs daily
store execution (this value stream owns the *selling-effectiveness capability* of the people in
those stores). It is distinct from **VS-09 (In-Store Customer Services)** which delivers custom
fabrication, estimation, and advisory *services* (this value stream owns the *selling skills and
product knowledge* that surface those services to customers and attach them). It is distinct from
**VS-123 (Skilled-Trade Apprenticeship)** which builds trade *capability* (this value stream
owns the *selling effectiveness* of trade-knowledgeable and all frontline staff). Sales
enablement, product-knowledge mastery & clienteling is the *associate selling-effectiveness*
discipline — distinct from general L&D, loyalty/CRM, store operations, in-store services, and
trade-capability building.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: ~5,800 frontline staff serving 2.8M monthly transactions and
~40% B2B revenue, where a small uplift in basket size, conversion, or attachment moves millions
of pesos monthly, and where inconsistent product knowledge and selling quality directly degrade
service quality, attach rate, trade-pro stickiness, and the brand promise. Without a dedicated
owner, BuildRight risks inconsistent selling quality across 200 stores, under-attachment of
services/accessories, weak trade-pro capture, and an inability to operationalize the customer-360
for clienteling — risks that no existing value stream owns end-to-end.
