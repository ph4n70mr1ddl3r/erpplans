# VS-39: Vendor Rebate & Incentive Management

> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages the full lifecycle of vendor-funded rebates, incentives, co-op advertising funds, promotional markdown allowances, volume discounts, and special pricing agreements across BuildRight Depot's ~800–1,000 active vendors. At a retailer of this scale, vendor rebates can represent 2–4% of COGS (PHP 800M–1.8B annually) and significantly impact gross margin. Covers rebate agreement negotiation and setup, accrual and estimation, claim submission and verification, settlement and payment application, and analytics. Ensures all vendor incentives are properly captured, accrued, and reconciled — preventing margin leakage from unclaimed or under-claimed rebates.
