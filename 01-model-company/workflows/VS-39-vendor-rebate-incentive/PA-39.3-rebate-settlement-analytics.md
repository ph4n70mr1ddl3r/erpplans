## W1803. Rebate Settlement Processing (Credit Note & Cash)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor approves rebate claim (W1793) or co-op reimbursement (W1799) |
| **Frequency** | ~100–200 settlements/quarter |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 800M–1.8B in annual rebate settlements |
| **Owner** | AP Manager |
| **Participants** | AP Manager, Treasury, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vendor settlement received in one of two forms: (a) **credit note**: vendor issues credit note against outstanding AP; reduces amount owed to vendor on next payment cycle, (b) **cash payment**: vendor wires payment to BuildRight bank account | Vendor → System | AP Manager | Per settlement |
| 2 | For credit note settlement: (a) AP Manager verifies credit note matches approved claim amount (or documents variance), (b) credit note entered in AP system against vendor account, (c) next payment to vendor reduced by credit note amount, (d) system posts: debit AP, credit rebate income | AP Manager / System | Accounting Manager | 10 min/credit note |
| 3 | For cash settlement: (a) Treasury confirms receipt in bank account, (b) Treasury applies payment to rebate receivable in system, (c) system posts: debit cash, credit rebate receivable, (d) rebate income already recognized at accrual — no additional P&L impact | Treasury / System | AP Manager | 10 min/payment |
| 4 | Partial settlement handling: (a) if vendor settles less than claimed amount: AP Manager investigates reason (documentation gap, calculation dispute, vendor policy limit), (b) unresolved variance escalated to Category Manager for vendor negotiation, (c) any unrecoverable portion reversed from rebate income | AP Manager | Accounting Manager | As needed |
| 5 | Monthly: AP Manager reconciles all rebate settlements to claims and accruals; unresolved items > 90 days escalated to CFO | AP Manager | CFO | 1 day/month |


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-capture of "entered in AP system against vendor account," (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-post of "posts" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: AP Manager review-and-approval gate; periodic reconciliation against source documents

## W1804. Rebate Income Recognition & GL Posting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close; rebate accrual or settlement processing |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~100–200 active agreements |
| **Owner** | Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Accounting Manager, AP Manager, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rebate income recognition method (BuildRight policy): (a) rebates treated as **reduction of COGS** (IAS 2 / PAS 2 treatment — most common for retail), (b) alternative: treat as other income (IAS 18 / PAS 18) — less preferred as it inflates gross margin, (c) consistently applied across all rebate types, (d) accrued on a systematic basis (monthly) to match purchase period, (e) recognized when: (i) amount can be measured reliably, (ii) receipt is probable (vendor financial health assessed) | Accounting Manager | CFO | Policy-level |
| 2 | Monthly journal entries: (a) **accrual**: debit rebate receivable, credit COGS reduction (contra-COGS), (b) **settlement**: debit AP (credit note) or cash (cash payment), credit rebate receivable, (c) **adjustment**: debit/credit COGS reduction for accrual variances, (d) entries posted per vendor agreement for traceability | AP Manager / System | Accounting Manager | Automated after review |
| 3 | Financial statement presentation: (a) rebate income shown as reduction of COGS in P&L (net presentation), (b) rebate receivable shown as current asset in balance sheet (if material), (c) note disclosure: rebate income policy, significant agreements, and contingent rebates | Accounting Manager | CFO | At reporting date |


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-match of "match purchase period," (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-post of "posted per vendor agreement for traceability" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

## W1805. Rebate Vendor Scorecard & Partnership Tiering

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual vendor scorecard review (Q4) |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | Top 100 vendors by rebate value |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising, Category Managers, CFO, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Merchandising develops vendor partnership tiering based on rebate and overall vendor performance: (a) **Platinum** (top 10 vendors — ~45% of COGS): highest rebate rates, co-op budgets, strategic collaboration, annual business planning together, (b) **Gold** (next 20 vendors): good rebate terms, regular co-op marketing, quarterly business reviews, (c) **Silver** (next 30 vendors): standard rebate terms, semi-annual reviews, (d) **Standard** (remaining vendors): basic rebate or no rebate, transactional relationship | VP Merchandising | CFO | 1 week |
| 2 | Vendor rebate scorecard metrics: (a) total rebate income earned, (b) rebate as % of purchases (effective margin improvement), (c) claim settlement rate (% of claimed amount actually received), (d) settlement speed (days from claim to payment), (e) co-op marketing funds utilized, (f) dispute rate (% of claims disputed by vendor), (g) year-over-year trend | Category Managers / FP&A | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Tiering used for: (a) next year's negotiation leverage (higher tiers get more favorable terms), (b) allocation of limited shelf space, (c) new store opening rebate solicitation, (d) co-op marketing budget allocation | VP Merchandising | — | Annual |


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews," (replaces manual Step 1).

### Controls
operational: VP Merchandising review and approval gate

| **Frequency** | Annual + quarterly updates |
| **Volume** | 1 annual projection + 3 quarterly updates |
| **Owner** | FP&A |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Annual rebate income projection: (a) per agreement: project annual purchase volume × applicable rebate rate = projected rebate income, (b) aggregate by vendor, category, and total, (c) include co-op marketing income projection, (d) include markdown allowance recovery projection, (e) apply historical realization rate (% of accrued rebates actually received): typically 85–95%, (f) net projected rebate income = gross projection × realization rate | FP&A | VP Merchandising | 1 week |

## W1808. Rebate Audit & Compliance Review

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual internal audit plan; external audit |
| **Frequency** | Annual internal audit; external audit annually |
| **Volume** | 1 comprehensive audit/year |
| **Owner** | Internal Audit |
| **Participants** | Internal Audit, AP Manager, VP Merchandising, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Internal audit scope for rebates: (a) verify rebate accrual methodology is appropriate and consistently applied, (b) sample 20–30 rebate agreements: verify terms, accrual calculation accuracy, claim documentation, settlement recording, (c) test for unclaimed rebates (vendors with agreements but no claims filed), (d) verify co-op marketing fund utilization and documentation, (e) check for fictitious rebate claims (fraud risk: claiming rebates for purchases not made) | Internal Audit | VP Internal Audit | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | Key audit findings typically addressed: (a) missed claims: vendor agreements exist but claims not submitted (direct margin leakage), (b) accrual accuracy: over/under accrual due to incorrect threshold assumptions, (c) documentation gaps: claims denied by vendor due to insufficient proof, (d) process inefficiency: manual claim process causes delays and errors | Internal Audit | VP Internal Audit | Report within 2 weeks |
| 3 | External auditor review: (a) rebate income is material (PHP 800M–1.8B annually); receives significant audit attention, (b) test accrual estimation methodology (management judgment area), (c) confirm settlements with vendors (sample of top vendors), (d) assess collectibility of rebate receivables at year-end | External Auditor | CFO | During annual audit |
| 4 | Management response: AP Manager and VP Merchandising address audit findings; implement process improvements per W1810 | AP Manager / VP Merchandising | CFO | Within 30 days |


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logy is appropriate and consistently applied," (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documentation gaps" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: Internal Audit review and approval gate

## W1810. Rebate Process Automation & System Enhancement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual process improvement cycle; internal audit recommendation |
| **Frequency** | Continuous improvement |
| **Volume** | Ongoing |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising, IT, AP Manager, CIO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify automation opportunities: (a) auto-accrual: system calculates rebate accrual directly from purchase data without manual intervention (currently semi-automated; target: fully automated for standard agreements), (b) auto-claim generation: system generates rebate claim worksheets automatically at period end, (c) vendor portal integration: claims submitted electronically via vendor portal with auto-populated data, (d) settlement matching: auto-match vendor credit notes to claims using AI/ML pattern matching, (e) alerts: automatic notification when vendor crosses volume threshold, when claim is overdue, when accrual rate changes | VP Merchandising / IT | CIO | Continuous |
| 2 | Prioritize automation based on: (a) volume of manual effort saved (high: auto-accrual for 200 agreements), (b) error reduction (high: manual calculation errors on tier thresholds), (c) margin leakage prevention (high: missed claims due to manual process gaps), (d) implementation complexity (medium: vendor portal integration requires vendor cooperation) | VP Merchandising / CIO | CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | Implement in phases: (a) Phase 1: auto-accrual and auto-claim generation (3–6 months), (b) Phase 2: vendor portal integration for claim submission (6–12 months), (c) Phase 3: AI-powered settlement matching and anomaly detection (12–18 months) | IT | CIO | 18 months |


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-calculate of "calculates rebate accrual directly from purchase data without manual…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-match of "matching and anomaly detection" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules
