## Overview

Disaster relief supply chain logistics and humanitarian aid coordination for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the operational protocol to support local recovery and reconstruction during natural disasters (typhoons, earthquakes, volcanic eruptions): forecasting and pre-positioning building materials (lumber, galvanized sheets, nails, cement, solar kits, generators) at regional distribution centers; fast-tracking procurement and credit approvals for government relief agencies (NDRRMC, DSWD) and NGOs; staging and kitting disaster-relief home rebuilding kits; allocating emergency transport capacity and routing through LGU disaster checkpoints; executing price freeze mandates under the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) regulations for areas under a State of Calamity; configuring anti-hoarding controls in the POS system; applying for casualty tax deductions with the BIR; coordinating store re-openings and structural safety clearances in devastated areas; and managing emergency driver safety and priority fuel allocations.

This matters at BuildRight's scale. The Philippines is highly prone to natural disasters. As the primary provider of building materials, BuildRight plays a critical role in immediate relief and rebuilding. Currently, VS-69 focuses on internal store BCP and securing physical assets, but no value stream coordinates the **outbound supply chain logistics** for disaster relief and government/NGO aid, nor the strict pricing compliance controls required under DTI State of Calamity price freeze laws. This value stream establishes BuildRight's disaster-relief operational capability.

This value stream owns the end-to-end program: mapping workflows, configuring system parameters, executing compliance actions, coordinating with external partners, and managing performance dashboards.
