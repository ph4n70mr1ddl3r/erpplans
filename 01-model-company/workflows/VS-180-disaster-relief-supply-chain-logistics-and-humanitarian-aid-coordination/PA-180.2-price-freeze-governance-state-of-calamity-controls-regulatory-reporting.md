## W5185. LGU State of Calamity Declaration Trigger & Pricing Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | National government (NDRRMC) or LGU Council declaring a State of Calamity in store regions |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per declaration |
| **Volume** | ~15–20 declarations monitored annually |
| **Owner** | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Pricing Lead (VS-01.2) |
| **Participants** | DTI Liaison, Legal Operations (VS-100), Store Managers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monitor official Gazette and NDRRMC releases for State of Calamity declarations. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Pricing Lead (VS-01.2) | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Pricing Lead (VS-01.2) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Identify affected BuildRight store locations within the declared cities, municipalities, or provinces. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Pricing Lead (VS-01.2) | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Pricing Lead (VS-01.2) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Verify the official date and timing of the declaration (which triggers the statutory 60-day price freeze under the Price Act, RA 7581). | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Pricing Lead (VS-01.2) | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Pricing Lead (VS-01.2) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Issue formal price freeze notification to the Pricing Team, Store Managers, and the CFO. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Pricing Lead (VS-01.2) | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Pricing Lead (VS-01.2) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- LGU permit tracking system (VS-76), Pricing configuration engine (VS-01.2), Legal database (VS-100).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Delayed detection of declaration**: Store continues selling at updated prices, violating law; mitigated by automated alerts from government RSS feeds.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 hours per declaration event

### Automation Opportunity
- Web scraper monitoring LGU/NDRRMC calamity declarations and sending email alerts to pricing team.

### Controls
- CTL-83 (ensure calamity pricing & relief-channel compliance.)
- CTL-771: Declaration log maintained by Legal; affected store list signed off by Compliance Officer.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-76 (LGU compliance), VS-01.2 (pricing), VS-100 (legal)

## W5186. ERP Price Freeze Configuration & Store-Level Price Lock Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | State of Calamity notification (W5185) |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per calamity |
| **Volume** | ~15–20 configurations annually |
| **Owner** | Pricing Administrator / IT Systems Engineer (VS-27) |
| **Participants** | Pricing Analyst, POS Administrator (VS-08), Store Operations (VS-07) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retrieve price list for the affected stores: lock selling prices of basic necessities and prime commodities (GI sheets, lumber, cement, nails, electrical wires) to their prevailing prices prior to calamity declaration. | Pricing Administrator / IT Systems Engineer (VS-27) | Pricing Administrator / IT Systems Engineer (VS-27) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Configure price locks in ERP: disable manual price overrides and promotional discounts for frozen SKUs at the affected store group. | Pricing Administrator / IT Systems Engineer (VS-27) | Pricing Administrator / IT Systems Engineer (VS-27) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Push price database updates to store POS terminals; verify update confirmation from all affected registers. | Pricing Administrator / IT Systems Engineer (VS-27) | Pricing Administrator / IT Systems Engineer (VS-27) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Instruct store staff to print and display the price freeze compliance list at store entrance and customer service counters. | Pricing Administrator / IT Systems Engineer (VS-27) | Pricing Administrator / IT Systems Engineer (VS-27) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Pricing database (VS-01.2), POS price master (VS-08), WMS pricing lookup.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **System sync error**: POS terminals fail to receive price locks, selling at higher prices; mitigated by mandatory POS EOD verification checks.

### Time Estimate
- ~2 hours configuration and deployment

### Automation Opportunity
- System-wide price lock script executed by store group tag.

### Controls
- CTL-771 (ensure controlled execution — Price Freeze Governance, State of Calamity Controls & Regulatory Reporting (PA-180.2).)
- CTL-771: Price freeze database verified against pre-calamity transactional prices; audit trail archived.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-01.2 (pricing), VS-08 (POS), VS-27 (IT), VS-07 (store ops)

## W5187. Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) Price Compliance Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DTI request; weekly price monitoring requirements during State of Calamity |
| **Frequency** | Weekly during calamity period (up to 60 days) |
| **Volume** | ~80–120 reports filed annually |
| **Owner** | Regulatory Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Pricing Analyst (VS-01.2), Store Managers, DTI Inspectors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aggregate price lists: compile actual POS transaction prices for basic commodities sold at affected stores. | Regulatory Compliance Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Prepare the DTI Price Compliance Report matching actual retail prices to pre-calamity baselines. | Regulatory Compliance Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | File report with the local DTI provincial office and upload to the DTI compliance portal. | Regulatory Compliance Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Host DTI field inspectors at store sites; provide access to POS registers, inventory ledgers, and price displays. | Regulatory Compliance Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- DTI submission portal, POS transaction database (VS-08), pricing master.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Failing DTI audit**: Discrepancies between store price tag and POS database; mitigated by daily store manager price audits.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-3 hours per report

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated generation of pricing compliance report sheets from ERP data.

### Controls
- CTL-83 (ensure calamity pricing & relief-channel compliance.)
- CTL-771: Report signed off by LGU Liaison; copy of DTI inspection certificates filed in document control (VS-88).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08 (POS), VS-01.2 (pricing), VS-88 (document control)

## W5188. Price Freeze Audit, Margin Impact Analysis & Financial Provisioning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Activation of price locks (W5186); monthly financial close |
| **Frequency** | Monthly during calamity period |
| **Volume** | Reconciliation of affected store groups (~20–40 stores) |
| **Owner** | Finance FP&A Analyst / Tax Accountant (VS-79) |
| **Participants** | Category Managers (VS-01), Controller, Treasury (VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calculate margin dilution: compare actual purchase costs (spiking due to emergency shipping) against frozen selling prices. | Finance FP&A Analyst / Tax Accountant (VS-79) | Finance FP&A Analyst / Tax Accountant (VS-79) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Analyze purchase price adjustments (PPA): identify vendors increasing costs during freeze period. | Finance FP&A Analyst / Tax Accountant (VS-79) | Finance FP&A Analyst / Tax Accountant (VS-79) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Prepare the Margin Impact Report: compute EBITDA dilution and inventory writedowns for affected branches. | Finance FP&A Analyst / Tax Accountant (VS-79) | Finance FP&A Analyst / Tax Accountant (VS-79) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Set up financial provisions in the General Ledger (VS-17.1) for expected margin drops. | Finance FP&A Analyst / Tax Accountant (VS-79) | Finance FP&A Analyst / Tax Accountant (VS-79) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP GL (VS-17.1), AP ledger (VS-15), Cost accounting database (VS-158).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Gross margin squeeze**: Input costs exceed frozen selling prices, causing net losses; mitigated by vendor price freeze agreements and sourcing adjustments.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-3 days per monthly close review

### Automation Opportunity
- System calculation of cost-vs-frozen-price margin differences.

### Controls
- CTL-83 (ensure calamity pricing & relief-channel compliance.)
- CTL-771: Provision entries verified by Controller; margin variance reports shared with CFO.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.1 (GL close), VS-15 (P2P), VS-158 (product costing), VS-01 (merch strategy), VS-18 (treasury), VS-79 (tax)

## W5189. Anti-Hoarding & Quantity-Limit Rules POS Configuration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | State of Calamity declaration (W5185); DTI advisory on basic necessity limits |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per calamity |
| **Volume** | ~15–20 configurations annually |
| **Owner** | POS Administrator / IT Developer (VS-27) |
| **Participants** | Zoning Officer (VS-76), POS Cashiers (VS-08), Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define quantity limit rules: set maximum allowable purchase quantities per customer for basic necessities (e.g. max 5 sheets of GI roofing, 10 bags of cement, 1 generator). | POS Administrator / IT Developer (VS-27) | POS Administrator / IT Developer (VS-27) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Configure limits in the POS software: block scanning of quantities above limit in a single transaction/customer profile. | POS Administrator / IT Developer (VS-27) | POS Administrator / IT Developer (VS-27) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Implement cashier override protocol: require manager PIN authorization (W5122) for legitimate trade-pro or LGU bulk sales. | POS Administrator / IT Developer (VS-27) | POS Administrator / IT Developer (VS-27) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Display quantity limit advisories at checkout lanes and power aisles. | POS Administrator / IT Developer (VS-27) | POS Administrator / IT Developer (VS-27) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- POS configuration module (VS-08), Loyalty customer database (VS-13).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **POS bypass / Split transactions**: Customers creating multiple loyalty profiles or cash bills to bypass limits; mitigated by cashier training and POS limit rules based on matching credit cards / phone numbers.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 hours

### Automation Opportunity
- POS automated prompt blocking transaction when quantity exceed limits.

### Controls
- CTL-771 (ensure controlled execution — Price Freeze Governance, State of Calamity Controls & Regulatory Reporting (PA-180.2).)
- CTL-771: Manager overrides logged and reviewed weekly by Loss Prevention (VS-23).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08 (POS), VS-27 (IT), VS-76 (LGU compliance), VS-13 (loyalty), VS-23 (loss prevention)

| **Frequency** | Event-driven |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify imported cargo (e.g. lumber, steel coil) in transit for disaster relief. | Customs Compliance Manager / Tariff Specialist (VS-87) | Customs Compliance Manager / Tariff Specialist (VS-87) | 1-2 hours |

## W5192. Disaster Tax Relief & BIR Casualty Loss Deduction Certification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Physical damage to stores or DCs; write-off of ruined inventory |
| **Frequency** | Annual tax return preparation (Q1) |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 loss certifications annually |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager (VS-79) |
| **Participants** | External Auditor, Store Manager, Loss Prevention (VS-23), Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct physical inventory of ruined and damaged stock; take photos and compile police/LP reports (VS-23). | Tax Manager (VS-79) | Tax Manager (VS-79) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Draft inventory write-off report; post casualty loss adjustments in ERP asset ledger (VS-35). | Tax Manager (VS-79) | Tax Manager (VS-79) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | File formal claim for Casualty Loss Deduction at the BIR Revenue District Office (RDO) within 45 days of disaster. | Tax Manager (VS-79) | Tax Manager (VS-79) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Secure BIR Commissioner's validation clearance; attach to corporate income tax return to deduct losses from taxable income. | Tax Manager (VS-79) | Tax Manager (VS-79) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Tax module (VS-79), Fixed assets ledger (VS-35), Inventory write-off system (VS-05).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Loss deduction denied**: BIR rejects claim due to inadequate proof of damage; mitigated by securing notary-certified damage photos and external auditor verified write-off manifests.

### Time Estimate
- ~30-45 days

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-771 (ensure controlled execution — Price Freeze Governance, State of Calamity Controls & Regulatory Reporting (PA-180.2).)
- CTL-771: Loss write-off approved by CFO; BIR filing certificate signed by tax agent.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-79 (tax), VS-35 (fixed assets), VS-05 (inventory lifecycle), VS-23 (loss prevention)
