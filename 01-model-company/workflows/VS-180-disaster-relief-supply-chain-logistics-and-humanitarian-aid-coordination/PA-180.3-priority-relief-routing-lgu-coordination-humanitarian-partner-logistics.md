## W5193. LGU Disaster Operations Center (DRRMC) Collaboration & Communication

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Activation of disaster response plan (VS-69); storm landfall imminent |
| **Frequency** | Continuous during disaster response |
| **Volume** | Coordination with ~20–30 municipal/provincial DRRMCs |
| **Owner** | Government Affairs Specialist / Regional Store Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | DRRMC Officers, Store Managers, LP team (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish contact with the LGU Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (DRRMC) Command Center. | Government Affairs Specialist / Regional Store Operations Manager | Government Affairs Specialist / Regional Store Operations Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Provide BuildRight point-of-contact, store emergency numbers, and inventory status list (generators, water, tools). | Government Affairs Specialist / Regional Store Operations Manager | Government Affairs Specialist / Regional Store Operations Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Receive road status, utility updates, security briefs, and localized disaster declarations from DRRMC. | Government Affairs Specialist / Regional Store Operations Manager | Government Affairs Specialist / Regional Store Operations Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Coordinate joint efforts: arrange for store parking lots to be used as evacuation staging, or set up mobile charging stations. | Government Affairs Specialist / Regional Store Operations Manager | Government Affairs Specialist / Regional Store Operations Manager | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- LGU directory, emergency comm channel (radio/satellite), LP dashboard (VS-23).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Communication breakdown**: Mobile networks down, losing touch with LGU; mitigated by pre-positioning satellite phones and two-way radios at key regional stores.

### Automation Opportunity
- Shared LGU contact sheet and communication log in cloud portal.

### Controls
- CTL-772 (ensure controlled execution — Priority Relief Routing, LGU Coordination & Humanitarian Partner Logistics (PA-180.3).)
- CTL-772: Joint plans signed by Regional Manager; LGU authorization badges distributed to store staff.

### Time Estimate
- Continuous real-time tracking

### Cross-references
- links to VS-69 (typhoon response), VS-23 (loss prevention), VS-76 (LGU compliance)

## W5194. DSWD (Social Welfare) & Red Cross Priority Relief Delivery Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Event-driven per dispatch order |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 major delivery operations per storm |
| **Owner** | Logistics Dispatch Supervisor (VS-06) / 3PL Coordinator (VS-56) |
| **Participants** | Fleet Drivers, DSWD Warehouse Receiver, Port Liaison Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Secure emergency dispatch manifest: verify destination DSWD/Red Cross drop-off facility or disaster zone. | Logistics Dispatch Supervisor (VS-06) / 3PL Coordinator (VS-56) | Logistics Dispatch Supervisor (VS-06) / 3PL Coordinator (VS-56) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Apply DSWD priority transport banner on delivery trucks to bypass traffic checkpoints and port queues. | Logistics Dispatch Supervisor (VS-06) / 3PL Coordinator (VS-56) | Logistics Dispatch Supervisor (VS-06) / 3PL Coordinator (VS-56) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Execute delivery: transport relief kits or building supplies, monitor vehicle GPS, and communicate safety status. | Logistics Dispatch Supervisor (VS-06) / 3PL Coordinator (VS-56) | Logistics Dispatch Supervisor (VS-06) / 3PL Coordinator (VS-56) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Obtain DSWD/Red Cross signed Goods Receipt Slip and delivery receipt verification. | Logistics Dispatch Supervisor (VS-06) / 3PL Coordinator (VS-56) | Logistics Dispatch Supervisor (VS-06) / 3PL Coordinator (VS-56) | 1-2 hours |

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-772 (ensure controlled execution — Priority Relief Routing, LGU Coordination & Humanitarian Partner Logistics (PA-180.3).)
- CTL-772: Signed receipts scanned and uploaded to ERP project tracking database; driver logs reconciled.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 days transit window

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (logistics), VS-56 (3PL), VS-110 (freight procurement)

- CTL-772 (ensure controlled execution — Priority Relief Routing, LGU Coordination & Humanitarian Partner Logistics (PA-180.3).)

### Cross-references

### System Touchpoints
- Facilities database (VS-138), OSH logs (VS-24), IT service portal (VS-27).

### Pain Points / Risks

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-772 (ensure controlled execution — Priority Relief Routing, LGU Coordination & Humanitarian Partner Logistics (PA-180.3).)

## W5198. Emergency Fleet Fuel Allocation & Auxiliary Power Logistics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Widespread power grid failure post-disaster; fuel stations offline |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven post-disaster |
| **Volume** | Reconciliation covering affected DCs and store groups |
| **Owner** | Fuel & Fleet Cost Manager (VS-61) / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) |
| **Participants** | Store Managers, DC Managers, Fuel Supplier, LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assess fuel status: audit diesel levels in store/DC backup generators and fleet vehicles. | Fuel & Fleet Cost Manager (VS-61) / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) | Fuel & Fleet Cost Manager (VS-61) / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Coordinate fuel tankers: purchase priority bulk diesel from partner oil companies (VS-61) and direct deliveries to affected sites. | Fuel & Fleet Cost Manager (VS-61) / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) | Fuel & Fleet Cost Manager (VS-61) / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Set up emergency fuel allocation rules: prioritize fuel for store generators (to prevent server/security outages) and fleet trucks delivering relief kits. | Fuel & Fleet Cost Manager (VS-61) / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) | Fuel & Fleet Cost Manager (VS-61) / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Log fuel consumption at stores/DCs; audit fuel transfers to prevent siphoning/theft (VS-23). | Fuel & Fleet Cost Manager (VS-61) / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) | Fuel & Fleet Cost Manager (VS-61) / Fleet Supervisor (VS-06) | 1-2 hours |

- Fleet fuel portal (VS-61), DC operations system (VS-04), LP logs (VS-23).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fuel running out**: Store goes completely offline, losing security system; mitigated by maintaining 72-hour fuel buffer in all store backup generators.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automatic generator fuel level sensor warnings to logistics control tower.

### Controls
- CTL-772 (ensure controlled execution — Priority Relief Routing, LGU Coordination & Humanitarian Partner Logistics (PA-180.3).)
- CTL-772: Bulk fuel deliveries verified by weighbridge / dip-stick; fuel disbursements log signed by LP.

### Time Estimate
- Continuous monitoring during crisis

### Cross-references
- links to VS-61 (fuel management), VS-06 (logistics), VS-23 (loss prevention), VS-04 (DC ops)

| **Frequency** | Per dispatch batch |
| **Volume** | ~30–40 dispatches monitored per storm |
| **Owner** | COD Controller (VS-142) / Workforce Vetting Specialist (VS-167) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vet drivers for high-risk disaster routes: verify license status, health clearance, and defensive driving history. | COD Controller (VS-142) / Workforce Vetting Specialist (VS-167) | COD Controller (VS-142) / Workforce Vetting Specialist (VS-167) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Issue cash box and mobile POS: instruct drivers on cash handling for COD relief orders (VS-142). | COD Controller (VS-142) / Workforce Vetting Specialist (VS-167) | COD Controller (VS-142) / Workforce Vetting Specialist (VS-167) | 1-2 hours |

- CTL-772 (ensure controlled execution — Priority Relief Routing, LGU Coordination & Humanitarian Partner Logistics (PA-180.3).)
- CTL-772: Drivers vetted before dispatch; cash box counts verified by cashier and supervisor.

## W5200. Post-Disaster Supply Chain Recovery, Lessons-Learned & BCP Update

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | De-activation of disaster response plan (typically 30 days post-storm) |
| **Frequency** | Post-event |
| **Volume** | 1 comprehensive review per major storm |
| **Owner** | Director of Business Continuity / Risk Manager (VS-26) |
| **Participants** | Supply Chain VP, Store Ops VP, CFO, Logistics Director, LP Director |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | De-mobilize emergency logistics zones at DCs; return fleet vehicles to standard delivery schedules. | Director of Business Continuity / Risk Manager (VS-26) | Director of Business Continuity / Risk Manager (VS-26) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Conduct post-disaster audit: review margin losses, inventory write-offs, transport delays, and compliance issues. | Director of Business Continuity / Risk Manager (VS-26) | Director of Business Continuity / Risk Manager (VS-26) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Host the Lessons-Learned Workshop: gather feedback from store managers, drivers, and procurement team. | Director of Business Continuity / Risk Manager (VS-26) | Director of Business Continuity / Risk Manager (VS-26) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Update the Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and S&OP staging models based on findings; submit updates to Board Risk Committee (VS-36). | Director of Business Continuity / Risk Manager (VS-26) | Director of Business Continuity / Risk Manager (VS-26) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- BCP document portal (VS-26), Risk dashboard (VS-21), Board portal (VS-36).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Repeating past mistakes**: Supply bottlenecks recurrent in next storm; mitigated by mandatory BCP updates and staging parameter adjustments within 30 days.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-772 (ensure controlled execution — Priority Relief Routing, LGU Coordination & Humanitarian Partner Logistics (PA-180.3).)
- CTL-772: BCP updates signed off by Risk Manager and COO; training scheduled for next season.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 weeks review

### Cross-references
- links to VS-26 (BCP), VS-21 (internal audit), VS-36 (governance)
