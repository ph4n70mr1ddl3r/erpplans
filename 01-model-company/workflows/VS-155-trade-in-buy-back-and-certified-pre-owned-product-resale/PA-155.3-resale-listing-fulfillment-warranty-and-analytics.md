## W4593. Certified Pre-Owned Listing, Content & Channel Syndication

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Item certified (W4589); listing cycle |
| **Frequency** | Per item + ongoing |
| **Volume** | Listings across certified resaleable items |
| **Owner** | Merchandising (VS-01) / PIM (VS-137) / Ecommerce (VS-10) |
| **Participants** | Marketing (VS-14), Auto-ID (VS-151), Pricing (VS-01.2), Marketplace (VS-65) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandising/PIM create the certified-pre-owned listing (grade, condition description, photos, certified warranty W4583, price W4581, serial), clearly marked "certified pre-owned" | Merchandising / PIM | VP Merch | Per item |
| 2 | Ecommerce/Marketplace syndicate the listing to channels (web VS-10, marketplace VS-65, in-store VS-07, loyalty VS-13), with accurate availability (VS-05/W4584) | Ecommerce / Marketplace / Stores | VP Merch / CMO | Per item |
| 3 | Marketing/Merchandising manage listing lifecycle (refresh, de-list on sale, re-grade on aging), and ensure no misrepresentation as new (VS-118/VS-22) | Marketing / Merchandising | VP Merch / CMO | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- PIM (VS-137); ecommerce (VS-10); marketplace (VS-65); stores (VS-07); loyalty (VS-13); auto-ID (VS-151); revenue assurance (VS-118)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Misrepresentation risk**: Listed/marked as new → consumer-protection/fraud (VS-118/VS-22); mitigated by clear certified-pre-owned labeling & integrity check
- **Availability-drift risk**: Listed item sold elsewhere → cancellation (VS-13); mitigated by real-time availability sync (VS-05)

### Time Estimate
- Per item ~10–15 min listing

### Automation Opportunity
- Listing/content automation from certification data; multi-channel syndication; availability sync

### Controls
- CTL: listings clearly "certified pre-owned" (not new) (VS-118/VS-22); availability accurate (VS-05); lifecycle managed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-137 (PIM), VS-10/VS-65 (channels), VS-07/VS-13 (store/loyalty), VS-151 (auto-ID), VS-118 (integrity), W4581 (price), W4589 (certification)
