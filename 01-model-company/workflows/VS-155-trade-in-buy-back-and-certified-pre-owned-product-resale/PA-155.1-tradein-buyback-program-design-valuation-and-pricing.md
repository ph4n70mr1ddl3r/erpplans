## W4577. Trade-In Program Strategy, Scope & Economics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (VS-33); ESG/circular goal (VS-25/VS-73); category review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | One trade-in program strategy |
| **Owner** | Merchandising (VS-01) / Circular-economy Lead / Commercial |
| **Participants** | Service Center (VS-12.1), Returns (VS-32), ESG (VS-25), Pricing (VS-01.2), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandising/Circular Lead set the trade-in strategy & scope (which categories: power tools, appliances, equipment; trade-in vs buy-back; geographic/channel rollout), aligned to ESG (VS-25/VS-73) | Merchandising / Circular Lead | VP Merch | Annual |
| 2 | Finance/Merchandising build the program economics (trade-in-driven new-purchase lift, refurbishment cost, resale margin, circular impact), and define success metrics vs do-nothing | Finance / Merchandising | CFO / VP Merch | Annual |
| 3 | Merchandising/ESG obtain approval, align to brand positioning (sustainability/affordability), and cascade to design (W4578–W4581) | Merchandising / ESG | VP Merch / CMO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy (VS-33); ESG/circular (VS-25/VS-73); merchandising (VS-01); pricing (VS-01.2); finance (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Negative-margin risk**: Refurbishment cost exceeds resale value; mitigated by unit-economics modeling (W4582)
- **Scope-overreach risk**: Taking back un-refurbishable items; mitigated by category/condition eligibility (W4578)

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual strategy

### Automation Opportunity
- Program-economics modeling; category-ROI analytics; ESG-impact quantification

### Controls
- CTL: strategy ESG-aligned (VS-25/VS-73); economics modeled & approved (VS-36); scope refurbishable

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-25/VS-73 (ESG/circular), VS-01/VS-01.2 (merch/pricing), VS-17.4 (finance), W4578–W4582 (design)

## W4579. Trade-In Valuation Engine & Condition-Grading Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Trade-in inquiry; valuation rule update |
| **Frequency** | Per inquiry + periodic tuning |
| **Volume** | Valuations across eligible trade-in items |
| **Owner** | Merchandising (VS-01.2) / Service Center (VS-12.1) / Pricing |
| **Participants** | Stores (VS-07), Data (VS-28), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing/Service Center build the condition-grading model (e.g., A/B/C/for-parts) with objective criteria (function, cosmetic, age, battery/condition), and the valuation engine (grade × model × resale-value-minus-refurb cost) | Pricing / Service Center / Data | VP Merch | Annual + tuning |
| 2 | Stores/Service Center apply the grading at counter (inspect, grade, system-generated valuation), presenting the trade-in value to the customer | Stores / Service Center | Store Manager | Per inquiry |
| 3 | Pricing/Data tune the valuation on resale/refurbishment outcomes (W4599), avoiding over/under-valuation, and refresh for model/age depreciation | Pricing / Data / Finance | VP Merch | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing (VS-01.2); service center (VS-12.1); stores (VS-07); data (VS-28); finance (VS-17.4); valuation system (W4584)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-valuation risk**: Trade-in value > recoverable resale value → loss; mitigated by valuation-minus-refurb-cost engine & tuning
- **Grading-inconsistency risk**: Subjective grading across stores; mitigated by objective criteria & training

### Time Estimate
- Per inquiry ~10–20 min grading; engine build weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- Valuation-engine app; objective-grading checklist; outcome-feedback tuning

### Controls
- CTL: valuation = resale-value-minus-refurb-cost (W4582); grading objective/consistent; tuned on outcomes (W4599)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-01.2 (pricing), VS-12.1 (service center), VS-07 (stores), VS-28 (data), W4582 (unit economics), W4584 (systems), W4599 (analytics)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service Center/Finance build the refurbishment cost model (labor, parts, testing, certification, by model/category/grade) and the yield model (% that reach certified vs for-parts/recycle VS-73) | Service Center / Finance / Data | VP Merch / CFO | Per model |

- **Hidden-refurb-cost risk**: Underestimated parts/labor → margin loss; mitigated by detailed cost model & tracking
- **Low-yield risk**: Few items reach certified → program uneconomic; mitigated by yield modeling & eligibility tuning

### Time Estimate
- Per model ~weeks; continuous tuning

### Automation Opportunity
- Refurb-cost/yield analytics; unit-economics dashboards; profitability-driven eligibility tuning

### Controls
- CTL: refurb cost & yield modeled per category/grade; unit economics positive (or exited); valuation/pricing cost-based
