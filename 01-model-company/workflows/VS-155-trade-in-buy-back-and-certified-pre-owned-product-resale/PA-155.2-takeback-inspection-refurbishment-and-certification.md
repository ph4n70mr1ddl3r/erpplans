## W4585. Customer Take-Back Intake & Custody Transfer

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer trade-in acceptance (W4580); buy-back |
| **Frequency** | Per item |
| **Volume** | Take-back intakes across stores/service centers |
| **Owner** | Stores (VS-07) / Service Center (VS-12.1) |
| **Participants** | POS (VS-08), Inventory (VS-05), Compliance (VS-22), Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stores/Service Center take physical custody of the trade-in item, verify ownership/identity (anti-theft — avoid receiving stolen goods VS-23), and confirm eligibility (W4578) | Stores / Service Center | Store Manager | Per item |
| 2 | Stores/POS issue the trade-in credit (W4580), record the take-back (customer, item, valuation, channel), and transfer title/ownership to BuildRight | Stores / POS / Compliance | Store Manager | Per item |
| 3 | Inventory/Stores move the item to pre-owned intake stock (VS-05/W4584) and route to inspection (W4586), with custody chain documented | Inventory / Stores | Inventory Manager | Per item |

### System Touchpoints
- Stores (VS-07); POS (VS-08); inventory (VS-05/W4584); LP/anti-theft (VS-23); compliance (VS-22)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stolen-goods risk**: Receiving stolen property → legal/loss (VS-23/VS-100); mitigated by ownership/ID verification & serial check
- **Title-transfer risk**: Ambiguous ownership → later dispute; mitigated by documented title transfer & terms

### Time Estimate
- ~10–15 min per item

### Automation Opportunity
- Intake/ownership-verification app; serial-vs-stolen-list check; custody-chain capture

### Controls
- CTL: ownership/identity verified (anti-theft VS-23); eligibility confirmed (W4578); title transferred & documented

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07/VS-08 (store/POS), VS-05 (inventory), VS-23 (LP), VS-22 (compliance), W4578 (eligibility), W4580 (credit), W4586 (inspection)

### Automation Opportunity

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service Center/Merchandising triage each item to a disposition (refurbish-to-certify W4588, parts-harvest W4590, recycle/dispose VS-73) based on inspection (W4586) & unit economics (W4582) | Service Center / Merchandising | Service Center Manager | Per item |

- **Triage-bottleneck risk**: Items pile up pre-disposition; mitigated by triage SLA & capacity

### Time Estimate
- ~per item minutes

### Automation Opportunity
- Economics-driven triage rules; disposition-routing app; capacity/analytics

### Controls
- CTL: disposition economics-driven (W4582); routes correct (refurb/parts/recycle); recorded for analytics/inventory

### Cross-references

- **Labeling-error risk**: Certified item mislabeled; mitigated by Auto-ID/serial control (VS-151)

### Time Estimate
- ~30–60 min per item

### Automation Opportunity
- Certification-test checklist; certificate/warranty generation; certified-label/serial issuance (VS-151)

### Controls
- CTL: certification testing rigorous w/ reject criteria; certificate & warranty issued (W4583); labeled/serial-tracked (VS-151)
