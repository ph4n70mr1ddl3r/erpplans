## Overview

Competition & Antitrust Compliance workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the enterprise
discipline of competing lawfully under the **Philippine Competition Act (RA 10677)** and the
**Philippine Competition Commission (PCC)**. As the largest hardware/DIY/home-improvement
big-box retailer in its market (200 stores, ~PHP 62.3B annual revenue, ~35,000 active SKUs,
~800–1,000 vendors, ~40% B2B revenue, 5 legal entities), BuildRight has structural exposure to
competition-law risk on multiple fronts: it sets and matches prices and runs competitive-price
intelligence (VS-57), it negotiates trade/resale pricing and terms with vendors and resellers
(VS-01.2, VS-11, VS-43, VS-82), it coordinates with competitors and vendors inside industry
associations (VS-104.2), it buys from ~1,000 suppliers with potential buyer-power exposure
(VS-03), it operates a 3P marketplace and retail-media network (VS-95, VS-48), and it pursues
growth that may include acquisitions, JVs, and store/market consolidation (VS-130). Each is a
distinct antitrust vector under RA 10677, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and PCC
guidelines (agreements restricting competition, abuse of dominant position, and mergers and
acquisitions that substantially lessen competition).

Today this discipline is **owned only as a single workflow** — W2683 (Philippine Competition Law
Compliance) in VS-76.2 — supplemented by scattered antitrust guardrail references in VS-104
(association/coalition conduct), VS-46 (bid-rigging awareness), VS-71.3 (data-sharing limits),
VS-100.3 (legal advisory), and VS-119 (ethics violation scope). No value stream owns the
**end-to-end competition-compliance program**: market-power assessment, pricing/conduct risk
controls, vertical-restraint/RPM and channel-pricing contract controls, association-meeting
protocols, merger-notification pre-filing, PCC engagement and investigation defense, leniency/
settlement, penalty/damages management, remediation/monitoring, and board-level reporting. This
follows the same single-workflow-elevation pattern used in Pass 1 (VS-89 from W776), Pass 5
(VS-105 from W324), Pass 7 (VS-116), and Pass 8 (VS-117 from W447, VS-118 from W348).

This is distinct from **VS-57 (Competitive Price Intelligence)** which *gathers* competitor price
data lawfully (this value stream owns the *conduct controls* that ensure that data and BuildRight's
pricing responses never cross into coordination/fixing with competitors). It is distinct from
**VS-118 (Revenue Assurance)** which protects *pricing execution integrity* (this value stream
protects *competitive-conduct legality*). It is distinct from **VS-104 (Government Affairs &
Industry Relations)** which manages external stakeholder *relationships* including association
membership (this value stream owns the *antitrust guardrails* around that activity). It is distinct
from **VS-86 (Anti-Financial Crime/ABC)** which addresses bribery/corruption (this value stream
addresses competition-specific violations). It is distinct from **VS-130 (Corporate Development &
M&A)** which executes transactions (this value stream owns the merger-notification/antitrust-
clearance *gate* on those transactions). Competition & antitrust compliance is the *lawful-
competition* discipline — distinct from price intelligence, revenue assurance, external affairs,
financial crime, and corporate development.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: a dominant-share retailer with pervasive pricing, vendor,
channel, association, and M&A activity faces real PCC enforcement exposure (fines up to PHP 250M
for first-time corporate offenders and a full year of preceding-year revenues for repeat/large
offenders under RA 10677), private damages actions, reputational harm, and contract
unenforceability — risks that no existing value stream owns end-to-end and that today rest on a
single workflow.
