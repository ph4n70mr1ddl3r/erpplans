## W2881. Grievance Intake, Classification & Step-1 Conference

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee or union files grievance |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~5–20 grievances/month |
| **Volume** | All employees |
| **Owner** | HR Manager |
| **Participants** | HR, Employee, Union Steward, Supervisor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR receives grievance (written/verbal via union or individual); logs in grievance case system; classifies (wage, discipline, working condition, interpretation) | HR | HR Manager | Per grievance |
| 2 | HR convenes Step-1 conference with employee, union steward, and immediate supervisor within CBA-prescribed timeline (often 5–7 days) | HR | HR Manager | Per grievance |
| 3 | HR documents Step-1 decision; communicates to employee/union; escalates to Step-2 if unresolved | HR | HR Manager | Per grievance |

### System Touchpoints
- Grievance case system; CBA-prescribed timelines; documentation repository

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Process risk**: Missed CBA timeline forfeits management defense; mitigated by SLA-bounded case system


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs in grievance case system" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documents Step-1 decision" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-217 (ensure labor-relations statutory compliance.)
operational: SLA-bounded case system

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 hours per grievance

## W2883. Preventive Suspension, Due Process & Notice Workflow (2-Notice Rule)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Serious offense allegation requiring investigation |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~10–30 cases/year |
| **Volume** | All employees |
| **Owner** | HR Manager |
| **Participants** | HR, LR Director, Legal, Supervisor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR issues Notice of Preventive Suspension (not exceeding 30 days per Labor Code) with written grounds; ensures pay rules per law | HR | HR Manager | Per case |
| 2 | HR serves First Notice (Notice to Explain) specifying charges and giving employee opportunity to respond within reasonable period (typically 5 days) | HR | HR Manager | Per case |
| 3 | HR documents employee response; prepares for hearing and decision; safeguards against procedural defects | HR / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Disciplinary case system; notice templates; timeline tracker

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Legal risk**: Procedural defect (no 2-notice) makes dismissal illegal; mitigated by standardized workflow and legal review


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-issue of "issues Notice of Preventive Suspension" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documents employee response" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-482 (ensure controlled execution — Grievance Handling, Labor Dispute & DOLE Conciliation (PA-84.2).)
operational: standardized workflow and legal review

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 hours per case

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LR Director conducts administrative hearing; presents evidence; allows employee (with counsel/union rep) to cross-examine and present defense | LR Director | VP Legal & Compliance | Per hearing |

### Controls
