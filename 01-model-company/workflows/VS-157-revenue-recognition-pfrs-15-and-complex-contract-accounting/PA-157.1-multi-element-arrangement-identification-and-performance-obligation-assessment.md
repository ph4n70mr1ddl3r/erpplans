## W4625. Revenue-Stream & Contract-Portfolio Register Maintenance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New channel/revenue stream; quarterly close; annual policy refresh |
| **Frequency** | Continuous register maintenance + quarterly reconcile |
| **Volume** | 1 enterprise revenue-stream register; ~20+ distinct revenue types across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager (Controller org) |
| **Participants** | Controller, CFO, Channel owners (VS-10/11/13/45/47/48/53/54/92/95/142/154/155/156), Tax (VS-79), External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue Accounting maintains the enterprise revenue-stream & contract-portfolio register: each stream mapped to (a) owning channel/VS, (b) entity, (c) PFRS 15 treatment summary, (d) performance obligation(s), (e) recognition trigger, (f) gross/net posture, (g) deferred vs. current, (h) disclosure category | Revenue Accounting Manager | Controller | Continuous |
| 2 | Each channel owner confirms its streams (POS cash-and-carry, BOPIS, delivery, installation, subscription, retail media, marketplace commission, consignment sell-through, gift card, loyalty, trade-in, COD, VAS/bills-payment, construction-finance referral) are captured and unchanged/changed | Channel Owners / Revenue Accounting | CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | Revenue Accounting reconciles the register to GL revenue accounts and to the W487 legacy scenario list; flags any stream lacking a documented PFRS 15 decision or any GL account lacking an owning stream | Revenue Accounting Manager / Controller | Controller | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Revenue/Contract module (revenue-stream master, performance-obligation tags); GL account mapping (links VS-17.1); legacy W487 register (VS-15.1); channel subledgers (VS-10/13/54)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Shadow-revenue risk**: A new stream (e.g., a Pass-17 service) books revenue with no PFRS 15 decision on record → mis-recognition accumulates silently; mitigated by register-to-GL reconciliation and the W4645 pre-launch gate
- **Orphan-account risk**: GL revenue accounts with no owning stream hide errors; mitigated by account-to-stream mapping and quarterly reconcile

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 days/quarter register maintenance + reconcile; absorbed in Revenue Accounting

### Automation Opportunity
- Contract/revenue-stream register auto-populated from ERP contract & channel modules; GL-revenue-account coverage exception report; change-detection alert when a channel posts a new revenue GL

### Controls
- CTL: every revenue stream has a documented PFRS 15 decision (links VS-21 financial-controls audit); register reconciled to GL quarterly; new streams gated by W4645

### Cross-references
- elevates W487 (VS-15.1); links to VS-17.1 (GL), VS-13/VS-54 (loyalty/gift card), W4643 (disclosure), W4645 (new-stream gate)

## W4626. Contract Identification, Combination & Modification Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New/custom contract; contract modification; bundling of separate contracts |
| **Frequency** | Per significant contract + periodic review of template contracts |
| **Volume** | ~hundreds of B2B/project/template contracts/yr; ~5–10 complex combinations/yr |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), Channel owners, Tax (VS-79), Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue Accounting/Legal determine whether a contract exists under PFRS 15 (enforceable rights/obligations, commercial substance, collectibility probable) for atypical arrangements (trade-in W4577, construction-finance referral W4553, COD W4265, design-service MOUs VS-66) | Revenue Accounting / Legal | Controller / VP Legal | Per contract |
| 2 | Assess contract combination (multiple contracts with same customer entered near in time with single commercial objective — common in project/B2B VS-11/VS-46) and contract modification (change-scope/price — is it a separate contract or a cumulative catch-up?) | Revenue Accounting / Legal | Controller | Per modification |
| 3 | Document the identification/combination/modification conclusion and the resulting performance-obligation remeasurement; route consequential changes to W4641 close cycle and W4645 if a new pattern | Revenue Accounting Manager | Controller | Per contract |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract lifecycle (VS-100.3); project/job module (VS-11.2); revenue-stream register (W4625); modification journal (VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-combined-contracts risk**: Treating one commercial deal as several contracts (or vice versa) mis-allocates the transaction price across obligations; mitigated by Legal+Accounting joint assessment and documented criteria
- **Modification mis-classification risk**: A change order treated as a new contract when it should be a cumulative catch-up (or the reverse) distorts period revenue; mitigated by the modification-decision template

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 hours per complex contract; ~5–10 days/yr aggregate for combinations/modifications

### Automation Opportunity
- Contract-clause extraction to flag combination/modification triggers; modification decision-template workflow linked to the project module

### Controls
- CTL: contract-identification decision documented for atypical arrangements; modification classification reviewed by Controller; Legal sign-off on combined-contract conclusions

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100.3 (legal/contracts), VS-11.2/VS-46.2 (project/B2B), VS-66 (design), W4577/W4553/W4265 (Pass-17 streams), W4641 (close)

## W4627. Performance-Obligation Identification for Bundled Product + Service Arrangements

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New bundle/kit launch (VS-92); installation-with-product sale (VS-12); design+build (VS-66) |
| **Frequency** | Per bundle SKU/service combo + annual review |
| **Volume** | ~ Kit/bundle SKUs in catalog; ~ every product+install sale (VS-12) |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01.1/VS-92), Services (VS-12/VS-66), Pricing, Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each bundle (VS-92) and product+installation sale (VS-12), identify the distinct performance obligations: is the product distinct from the installation service? Is the design service (VS-66) distinct from materials? Apply the "distinct" criteria (capable of being used alone; identifiable in the contract) | Revenue Accounting / Merchandising / Services | Controller | Per bundle/combo |
| 2 | Determine recognition timing per obligation: product = point in time (delivery/POS, control transfer); installation = point in time (completion) or over-time if it creates an asset with no alternative use + right to payment; design = over-time/point-in-time per terms | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Per combo |
| 3 | Tag the kit/BOM (VS-92.1) and the service-work-order (W544) with the performance-obligigation structure so POS/order posting routes revenue to the correct obligation and period | Revenue Accounting / IT | Controller / CIO | Per setup |

### System Touchpoints
- Kit/BOM module (VS-92.1); service work order (W544); POS/order-to-cash posting (VS-08/VS-16); revenue obligation tags

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden-service-in-product risk**: Recognizing the entire bundle at product delivery overstates period revenue when installation is a separate obligation not yet complete; mitigated by obligation split at the SKU/work-order level
- **Over-time mis-application risk**: Applying over-time recognition to installation that does not meet the no-alternative-use/right-to-payment test → premature revenue; mitigated by the criteria checklist

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 hours per new bundle/combo; ~3–5 days/yr aggregate

### Automation Opportunity
- Performance-obligation tagging on the kit/BOM master; auto-split of bundle revenue at POS based on obligation tags

### Controls
- CTL: distinct-obligation assessment documented per bundle; recognition-timing criteria checklist; obligation tags reviewed at kit setup

### Cross-references
- links to VS-92 (kitting), VS-12 (installation), VS-66 (design), VS-01.1 (assortment), W544 (service work order), W4634 (allocation)

## W4628. Principal-vs-Agent (Gross-vs-Net) Determination for Marketplace, Retail Media, VAS & COD

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New marketplace/retail-media/VAS/COD flow; annual gross/net review |
| **Frequency** | Per flow + annual |
| **Volume** | ~7 principal-vs-agent decisions across VS-48/VS-95/VS-156/VS-142/VS-154/VS-155/VS-11(3P) |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Channel owners (VS-48/95/156/142/154/155), Legal (VS-100.3), Tax (VS-79), Controller, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each flow where BuildRight sits between a third party and a customer (marketplace 3P seller VS-95, retail media VS-48, VAS/financial-agency VS-156, COD VS-142, construction-finance referral VS-154, trade-in resale VS-155), apply the principal-vs-agent indicators: control of the good/service before transfer; primary obligor; inventory risk; pricing discretion; credit risk | Revenue Accounting / Channel / Legal | Controller / VP Legal | Per flow |
| 2 | Conclude gross (recognize full revenue + offsetting cost) vs. net (recognize only commission/fee) and document the rationale + indicator evidence; for VAS (VS-156) confirm BuildRight is agent of the principal; for marketplace confirm BuildRight is platform operator (net on commission) | Revenue Accounting Manager | Controller / CFO | Per flow |
| 3 | Configure the ERP/POS/ecommerce posting to gross or net per the conclusion; reflect in the disclosure schedule (W4643) and re-assess annually or on flow change | Revenue Accounting / IT | Controller / CIO | Per setup + annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Marketplace/retail-media/VAS/COD subledgers; POS/ecommerce revenue posting (VS-08/VS-10); GL gross-vs-net account structure; disclosure schedule (W4643)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Gross-vs-net error risk**: Mis-stating a net commission flow as gross inflates reported revenue (and COGS) — a top external-audit and BSP/SEC-disclosure focus; mitigated by the documented indicator analysis and annual re-assessment
- **Inconsistent-posting risk**: A gross/net conclusion not reflected in system posting → books diverge from the accounting decision; mitigated by IT config + Controller sign-off

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per new flow decision; ~3–5 days annual re-assessment across all flows

### Automation Opportunity
- Principal-vs-agent decision template with indicator scoring; auto-routing of commission vs. gross revenue at the transaction per the decision

### Controls
- CTL: documented indicator-based principal-vs-agent decision per flow (reviewed by External Auditor); system posting matches the conclusion; re-assessed annually

### Cross-references
- links to VS-48 (retail media), VS-95 (marketplace), VS-156 (VAS agency), VS-142 (COD), VS-154 (construction finance), VS-155 (trade-in), W4643 (disclosure), W4645 (gate)

## W4629. Consignment & VMI Revenue-Timing & Sell-Through Recognition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Consignment/VMI setup (VS-45); monthly sell-through settlement |
| **Frequency** | Monthly settlement + per setup |
| **Volume** | ~300 VMI/consignment SKUs from 12 vendors; monthly settlements |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-45), Vendor Mgmt (VS-03), Controller, Tax (VS-79) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm revenue is NOT recognized at consignment-stock receipt (goods remain vendor-owned per VS-45.1); recognize only at customer sell-through (W543 trigger) — verify the inventory ownership flag and the sell-through feed | Revenue Accounting / Merchandising | Controller | Per settlement |
| 2 | Compute consignment/VMI revenue at sell-through price, validate against the POS sell-through report (W543) and the vendor settlement (VS-45.3), and confirm BuildRight is principal (gross) for consignment sales to the consumer | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Post revenue + AP/vendor-payable at sell-through; reconcile the consignment inventory balance (vendor-owned) against recognized revenue and the monthly vendor statement; investigate any unmatched sell-through | Revenue Accounting / AP | Controller | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Consignment/VMI module (VS-45); POS sell-through trigger (W543); AP/vendor settlement (VS-45.3); consignment inventory ownership flag (VS-05)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Premature-recognition risk**: Recognizing consignment revenue at store receipt (as if purchased) overstates revenue and inventory; mitigated by the ownership-flag gate and sell-through trigger
- **Unreconciled-sell-through risk**: POS sell-through and vendor settlement mismatch → revenue or payable error; mitigated by the monthly three-way reconcile (POS × settlement × inventory)

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days/month consignment/VMI revenue accounting

### Automation Opportunity
- Sell-through-triggered auto-posting of consignment revenue + vendor payable; exception dashboard for unmatched sell-through

### Controls
- CTL: ownership-flag prevents premature recognition; three-way reconcile (POS × vendor settlement × consignment inventory) monthly; principal/gross posture documented

### Cross-references
- links to VS-45 (consignment/VMI), W543 (sell-through trigger), VS-45.3 (settlement), VS-05 (inventory), W4647 (contract-liability reconcile)

## W4630. Layaway, Customer-Deposit & Bill-and-Hold Recognition Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Layaway/deposit transaction (VS-08/VS-11); bill-and-hold request; annual review |
| **Frequency** | Per arrangement type + quarterly deposit review |
| **Volume** | Layaway/custom-order deposits on project & special orders (~thousands/yr); bill-and-hold rare |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Store/Trade ops (VS-07/VS-11), AR (VS-16), Controller, Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assess revenue recognition for customer deposits & layaway: deposit = contract liability (deferred) until control transfers (delivery for layaway; milestone for project deposits W546); do NOT recognize at cash receipt | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Per type |
| 2 | For bill-and-hold (customer pays but asks BuildRight to hold inventory), apply the PFRS 15 criteria (commercial reason, segregated, ready for transfer, timing fixed) before recognizing at billing rather than delivery | Revenue Accounting / Trade Ops | Controller | Per case |
| 3 | Reconcile the customer-deposit/contract-liability GL balance to the open layaway/deposit/POU (point-of-use) list quarterly; release to revenue at the control-transfer trigger | Revenue Accounting / AR | Controller | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Layaway/deposit module (VS-08/VS-11/VS-16); contract-liability GL (VS-17.1); project deposit W546; AR (VS-16)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Deposit-as-revenue risk**: Booking customer deposits as revenue at cash receipt overstates revenue and understates contract liability; mitigated by the deferred-revenue posting rule and quarterly reconcile
- **Bill-and-hold abuse risk**: Recognizing bill-and-hold revenue without meeting all criteria → premature revenue (a classic audit finding); mitigated by the mandatory criteria checklist

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days/quarter deposit/contract-liability reconciliation; bill-and-hold cases ad-hoc

### Automation Opportunity
- Deposit-to-deferred-revenue auto-posting; control-transfer-triggered release; contract-liability aging dashboard

### Controls
- CTL: deposits posted to contract liability (not revenue); bill-and-hold criteria checklist; contract-liability GL reconciled to open-deposit list quarterly

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08/VS-11/VS-16 (layaway/deposit/AR), W546 (project deposit), W4647 (contract-liability reconcile), W4635 (gift-card breakage — analogous deferred model)

## W4631. Loyalty-Points, Rewards & Deferred-Revenue Performance Obligation Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Loyalty program change (VS-13); new reward tier/partner; annual fair-value refresh |
| **Frequency** | Per change + annual fair-value reset |
| **Volume** | 1 loyalty program; 600K members; ~ points earned on every one of 2.8M monthly POS transactions |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Loyalty (VS-13), Marketing (VS-14), Actuarial/Analytics, Controller, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm loyalty points are a separate performance obligation (distinct from the underlying sale); allocate a portion of the transaction price to points at each earning transaction based on standalone selling price (W4633/W4636) — defer that portion | Revenue Accounting / Loyalty | Controller | Per setup + ongoing |
| 2 | Set the breakage/forfeiture estimate and the points fair value; recognize deferred revenue to revenue when points are redeemed (or expire/forfeit) per W4636; review the estimate each period | Revenue Accounting / Analytics | Controller / CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | Reconcile the points-deferred-revenue GL to the loyalty-engine points liability (W104/W17) and the redemption feed monthly; investigate redemption-rate drift | Revenue Accounting / Loyalty ops | Controller | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Loyalty engine (VS-13 W17/W104); POS earning feed (VS-08); deferred-revenue GL (VS-17.1); redemption feed

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Actuarial-estimation risk**: The points fair value and breakage rate drive a material deferred-revenue balance and are estimation-heavy → audit scrutiny and period volatility; mitigated by documented methodology (W4636) and quarterly true-up
- **Engine-to-GL drift risk**: Loyalty-engine points liability and GL deferred revenue diverge → mis-statement; mitigated by monthly reconcile

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days/quarter points accounting + estimate refresh; monthly ~1 day reconcile

### Automation Opportunity
- Transaction-price auto-allocation to points at POS; engine-to-GL auto-reconcile with exception routing; redemption-triggered revenue release

### Controls
- CTL: documented SSP/breakage methodology (External-Auditor reviewed); engine-to-GL monthly reconcile; estimate refreshed quarterly

### Cross-references
- links to VS-13 (loyalty), W104/W17 (points liability), W4633/W4636 (SSP/breakage measurement), W4647 (reconcile), W4643 (disclosure)

## W4632. Project & Government Over-Time-vs-Point-in-Time Recognition Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project/contract milestone (VS-11/VS-46); annual policy review |
| **Frequency** | Per project type + periodic |
| **Volume** | ~ Project/B2B/G2G contracts (VS-11/VS-46); material project revenue (~30% trade + 10% corporate) |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade/Government sales (VS-11/VS-46), Project Finance, Controller, Tax (VS-79), External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For project/government contracts (VS-11.2/VS-46.2), assess over-time recognition: does BuildRight create an asset with no alternative use AND an enforceable right to payment for performance to date? If both → over-time (input/output measure of progress); else point-in-time at delivery/handover | Revenue Accounting / Project Finance | Controller | Per contract |
| 2 | Select and document the measure of progress (cost-to-cost input method typical for project supply; output/milestone if more faithful); compute % complete, contract revenue, and the onerous-contract check (PAS 37) each period | Revenue Accounting / Project Finance | Controller / CFO | Per period |
| 3 | Reconcile over-time/point-in-time revenue to billings (W165/W1288 progress billing) and the contract asset/liability (billed-but-unrecognized vs. recognized-but-unbilled); investigate cumulative catch-up at each measurement date | Revenue Accounting / AR | Controller | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/job module (VS-11.2); progress billing (W165/W1288); contract asset/liability GL (VS-17.1); government module (VS-46)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Measure-of-progress error risk**: An inappropriate input/output measure mis-states period revenue (front-loading or back-loading); mitigated by documented method selection and periodic review
- **Onerous-contract miss risk**: Failing to recognize a PAS 37 onerous-contract provision on a loss project understates the liability; mitigated by the periodic onerous check tied to margin monitoring (VS-11.2 W918)
- **Billings-vs-revenue drift risk**: Billings and revenue diverge without a clean contract asset/liability split → cut-off errors; mitigated by monthly reconcile

### Time Estimate
- ~3–6 days/quarter project/government revenue accounting + measure-of-progress review; per-contract ~2–4 hours

### Automation Opportunity
- Cost-to-cost % complete auto-computation from the project module; contract asset/liability auto-derivation from billings-vs-revenue; onerous-contract trigger on margin breach

### Controls
- CTL-114 (ensure PFRS 15 recognition & cut-off control.)
- CTL: documented over-time/point-in-time decision per contract; measure-of-progress reviewed by Controller; contract asset/liability reconciled monthly; onerous-contract provision assessed each period

### Cross-references
- links to VS-11.2/VS-46.2 (project/government), W165/W1288 (progress billing), W918 (project margin), W4641 (close), W4643 (disclosure)
