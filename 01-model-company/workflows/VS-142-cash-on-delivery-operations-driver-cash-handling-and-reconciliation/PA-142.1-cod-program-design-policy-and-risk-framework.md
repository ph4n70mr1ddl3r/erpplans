## W4265. COD Program Strategy, Channel Scope & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Ecommerce growth (VS-10); payment-mix review; market/competitive pressure |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on strategy change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise COD program across ecommerce, MSME (VS-82), trade delivery (VS-74) |
| **Owner** | Head of Payment Operations / Ecommerce Finance / Commercial |
| **Participants** | Ecommerce (VS-10), Last-Mile (VS-06.3/VS-56), Finance (VS-17.4), Treasury (VS-18), Fraud (VS-125) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payment Ops/Commercial set the COD strategy and channel scope (which channels/segments/geographies offer COD vs pre-pay only), the COD share target, and the operating model (own-crew collect vs 3PL-driver collect vs cash-handling partner) | Head of Payment Ops / Ecommerce | CFO / COO | Annual |
| 2 | Payment Ops/Last-Mile map the cash-chain design (driver → 3PL branch/depot → BuildRight → bank), the custody handoff points, and the reconciliation architecture (W4272), aligned to VS-06.3/VS-56 | Payment Ops / Last-Mile / Treasury | CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Payment Ops document the strategy, obtain approval, and cascade to policy (W4266), pricing (W4267), and 3PL terms (W4268) | Head of Payment Ops | CFO / COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/order (VS-10); payment ops (VS-80); last-mile/3PL (VS-06.3/VS-56); treasury (VS-18); fraud (VS-125)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Channel-mismatch risk**: COD offered where cost/fraud too high; mitigated by segment/geography scoping & risk-tier (W4266)
- **Chain-ambiguity risk**: Custody handoffs unclear → loss attribution disputes; mitigated by mapped cash-chain & handoff SLA

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual strategy

### Automation Opportunity
- COD-share/cost scenario modeling; channel-segment profitability analytics

### Controls
- CTL: strategy approved (CFO/COO); scope risk-tiered (W4266); cash-chain/handoffs documented

### Cross-references
- links to VS-10 (ecommerce), VS-80 (payment ops), VS-06.3/VS-56 (last-mile/3PL), VS-18 (treasury), VS-125 (fraud), W4266 (policy), W4272 (architecture)

## W4266. COD Order Eligibility, Limit & Risk-Tier Policy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4265); fraud/loss data; customer/segment change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + periodic tuning |
| **Volume** | Eligibility/limit rules across the order base |
| **Owner** | Payment Operations / Fraud (VS-125) / Ecommerce (VS-10) |
| **Participants** | Fraud (VS-125), CRM/CDP (VS-126), Risk (VS-21.2), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payment Ops/Fraud set COD eligibility (new vs returning, verified address, account age, blacklist) and per-order/per-customer COD value limits and item-type rules (no COD on ultra-high-value/DG) using fraud history (VS-125) and CDP signals (VS-126) | Payment Ops / Fraud | Head of Payment Ops | Annual |
| 2 | Payment Ops/Fraud define the risk-tier (low/med/high) with differentiated limits, authentication (OTP/address verification), and pre-pay nudge for high-risk; integrate to order capture (VS-10) | Payment Ops / Fraud / Ecommerce | Head of Payment Ops | Annual |
| 3 | Fraud/Payment Ops tune limits on loss/refusal data (W4287), manage blacklists/whitelists, and monitor eligibility-policy performance | Fraud / Payment Ops | Head of Payment Ops | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Fraud/rules engine (VS-125); CDP/customer signals (VS-126); order capture (VS-10); risk (VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-extension risk**: COD to high-risk buyers → refusal/fraud loss; mitigated by risk-tier limits & blacklist
- **Friction risk**: Limits too tight → lost legitimate sales; mitigated by tuning on conversion/loss data

### Time Estimate
- ~2 weeks annual + continuous tuning

### Automation Opportunity
- Risk-scoring → auto limit/auth; address/identity verification; dynamic blacklist; conversion-vs-loss optimization

### Controls
- CTL: eligibility/limits risk-based (VS-125); high-risk pre-pay/auth; blacklist maintained

### Cross-references
- links to VS-125 (fraud), VS-126 (CDP), VS-10 (order capture), VS-21.2 (risk), W4265 (strategy), W4287 (loss data)

## W4268. Driver/3PL COD Authorization, Bonding & Contract Terms

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | 3PL contracting (VS-56); own-crew onboarding; loss/audit finding |
| **Frequency** | Per contract + onboarding |
| **Volume** | All drivers/3PLs authorized to collect COD cash |
| **Owner** | Procurement (VS-34) / Payment Ops / Last-Mile (VS-06.3/VS-56) |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), Finance (VS-17.4), HR (VS-98 for own crew), Insurance (VS-26), Fraud (VS-125) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement/Legal embed COD cash-handling terms in 3PL contracts (cash custody responsibility, remittance SLA, bonding/security deposit, liability for loss/theft, audit rights) and in own-crew terms (coordinate VS-98) | Procurement / Legal / Last-Mile | CFO / COO | Per contract |
| 2 | Payment Ops/Last-Mile authorize specific drivers/crews to collect COD (ID, training on W4270 controls, fidelity bonding, daily cash limit per driver), and revoke on breach | Payment Ops / Last-Mile / HR | Head of Payment Ops | Per driver |
| 3 | Finance/Insurance structure bonding/insurance coverage (fidelity, cash-in-transit for the inward chain) via VS-26, and track exposure vs bonded limits | Finance / Insurance | CFO | Annual + ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/contract (VS-34); legal (VS-100.3); 3PL mgmt (VS-56); HR/contingent (VS-98); insurance (VS-26); fraud (VS-125)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unbonded-driver risk**: Unauthorized/unbonded driver collects cash → theft exposure; mitigated by authorization + bonding gate
- **Liability-ambiguity risk**: 3PL vs BuildRight loss responsibility unclear; mitigated by explicit contract terms

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks per contract cycle; per-driver authorization ~1 day

### Automation Opportunity
- Driver-authorization registry with bonding/expiry tracking; auto-revoke on breach; exposure dashboards

### Controls
- CTL: drivers authorized, trained (W4270) & bonded; contract liability explicit; insurance in place (VS-26)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-34 (procurement), VS-56/VS-06.3 (3PL/last-mile), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-98 (own crew), VS-26 (insurance), VS-125 (fraud), W4270 (controls)

## W4270. COD Cash-Handling Controls, SoD & Custody Standard

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Authorization (W4268); control review; audit finding |
| **Frequency** | Standard definition + continuous adherence |
| **Volume** | Cash-handling standard for all COD-collection staff |
| **Owner** | Payment Ops / Internal Audit (VS-21) / Last-Mile (VS-06.3) |
| **Participants** | Audit (VS-21), Fraud (VS-125), 3PL/own drivers, Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payment Ops/Audit define the COD cash-handling standard (exact-change policy, receipt/POD capture, dual-control where feasible, daily remittance limit, tamper-evident bag, no-cash-overnight rule), with segregation of duties (collector ≠ reconciler ≠ poster) | Payment Ops / Audit | Head of Payment Ops | Annual |
| 2 | Payment Ops train drivers/3PL/own crew on the standard (coordinate W4268 authorization & VS-98), and embed proof capture (POD + cash amount photo/digital) into the delivery app (VS-06.3) | Payment Ops / Last-Mile / HR | Head of Payment Ops | Per onboarding |
| 3 | Audit/Fraud monitor adherence (exception reports, spot checks, exception-based analytics VS-23/VS-125), investigate deviations, and enforce consequence | Audit / Fraud / Payment Ops | Head of Payment Ops / CRO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Audit/internal control (VS-21); fraud/exception (VS-125/VS-23); delivery app/POD (VS-06.3); finance (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Theft/skim risk**: Cash handled without control; mitigated by SoD, daily remittance, tamper-evident custody, proof capture
- **Evidence risk**: No proof of cash collected → unrecoverable dispute; mitigated by mandatory POD/cash-amount capture

### Time Estimate
- ~2 weeks standard; ongoing adherence monitoring

### Automation Opportunity
- Digital POD with cash-amount capture; exception-based monitoring; SoD enforcement in workflow

### Controls
- CTL: SoD (collect/reconcile/post segregated); daily remittance; tamper-evident custody; POD evidence; audit-monitored (VS-21)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21 (audit), VS-125/VS-23 (fraud/exception), VS-06.3 (delivery app/POD), VS-98 (crew), W4268 (authorization), W4274 (collection)
