## W4273. COD Order Handoff, Delivery Note & Amount-Due Computation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | COD order released for fulfillment (VS-10.2) |
| **Frequency** | Per COD order (~13,000–21,000/month) |
| **Volume** | Each COD order with an amount-due for collection |
| **Owner** | Ecommerce/Order Mgmt (VS-10) / Last-Mile Dispatch (VS-06.3) |
| **Participants** | Payment Ops, Pricing (VS-01.2), Delivery Driver/3PL (VS-56) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Order Mgmt/Payment Ops compute the exact COD amount-due (item total + COD fee W4267 − pre-paid deposit − loyalty redemption + delivery fee), apply risk-tier auth (W4266), and freeze the amount on the delivery note | Order Mgmt / Payment Ops | Head of Payment Ops | Per order |
| 2 | Order Mgmt/Last-Mile generate the COD delivery note (amount-due, item list, customer ID, payment-instruction) and hand off to the driver/3PL via the delivery app (VS-06.3), flagging COD vs pre-paid | Order Mgmt / Dispatch | Head of Payment Ops | Per order |
| 3 | Payment Ops monitor COD handoff completeness (every COD order has a locked amount-due + assigned driver), and flag undelivered/COD-released exceptions | Payment Ops | Head of Payment Ops | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Order/ecommerce (VS-10); pricing/fee (VS-01.2/W4267); delivery app (VS-06.3); risk auth (W4266); loyalty (VS-13)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Wrong-amount risk**: Driver collects wrong amount → dispute/loss; mitigated by system-computed locked amount-due on the note
- **Handoff-gap risk**: COD order released without amount/driver assignment; mitigated by handoff-completeness monitoring

### Time Estimate
- Automated per order; monitoring part of daily ops

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto amount-due computation & lock; COD-flag handoff to delivery app; handoff-completeness dashboard

### Controls
- CTL: amount-due system-computed & locked; risk-tier auth applied (W4266); handoff completeness monitored

### Cross-references
- links to VS-10 (order), VS-01.2/W4267 (fee), VS-06.3 (delivery app), VS-13 (loyalty), W4266 (auth), W4274 (collection)

## W4274. Doorstep Cash Collection, Receipting & POD Capture

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Delivery to COD customer |
| **Frequency** | Per COD delivery (~13,000–21,000/month) |
| **Volume** | Each COD cash collection at the doorstep |
| **Owner** | Delivery Driver / 3PL (VS-56) / Last-Mile Ops (VS-06.3) |
| **Participants** | Customer, Driver/3PL, Payment Ops, Fraud (VS-125) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Driver deliver the order, state the exact amount-due (per W4273), collect cash, verify denomination/authenticity (counterfeit check), and confirm exact amount or process short-pay (W4276) | Driver / 3PL | Last-Mile Ops | Per delivery |
| 2 | Driver capture proof of delivery + cash collected (digital POD with amount, customer signature/photo receipt per W4270 standard), issue customer receipt (BIR-compliant per W4271), and update order cash-status in the app | Driver / 3PL | Last-Mile Ops | Per delivery |
| 3 | Payment Ops/Fraud monitor collection exceptions (no-cash, short-pay, counterfeit flag), trigger refusal/return handling (W4276), and feed data to reconciliation (W4277) | Payment Ops / Fraud | Head of Payment Ops | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Delivery app/POD (VS-06.3); order cash-status (W4272); receipting (VS-79/W4271); fraud/exception (VS-125)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Counterfeit/short-pay risk**: Fake bill or underpayment; mitigated by denomination check + short-pay handling (W4276)
- **No-evidence risk**: Cash collected without POD → unrecoverable; mitigated by mandatory digital POD/receipt

### Time Estimate
- ~3–8 min/collection at the doorstep

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile POD with amount capture & customer signature; digital receipt (BIR-compliant); counterfeit-detection guidance; cash-status auto-update

### Controls
- CTL: amount verified vs locked amount-due (W4273); POD/receipt captured (W4270/W4271); exceptions handled (W4276)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06.3 (delivery/POD), VS-56 (3PL), VS-79/W4271 (receipt), VS-125 (fraud), W4270 (controls), W4273 (amount-due), W4276 (short-pay), W4277 (reconciliation)

## W4275. Driver/3PL Daily Cash Custody, Remittance & Deposit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | End of driver/3PL shift, daily |
| **Frequency** | Daily per driver/depot |
| **Volume** | Daily cash custody & remittance across the driver/3PL network |
| **Owner** | Last-Mile Ops (VS-06.3) / 3PL (VS-56) / Payment Ops |
| **Participants** | Drivers, 3PL depot/branch, Treasury (VS-18), Bank, Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Driver/3PL secure the day's collected cash (tamper-evident bag per W4270, no-cash-overnight rule), reconcile to POD records, and remit to the 3PL depot/branch or BuildRight cash office within the SLA | Driver / 3PL / Cash Office | Last-Mile Ops / Payment Ops | Daily |
| 2 | 3PL depot/cash office verify & accept the remittance (count, match to POD list, sign handoff), deposit to bank (VS-18) or arrange CIT pickup (VS-81), and transmit the cash file to BuildRight (W4278) | 3PL / Cash Office / Treasury | Head of Payment Ops | Daily |
| 3 | Treasury/Payment Ops confirm bank deposit (deposit slip/bank feed VS-18), track unremitted-cash aging (W4269 float), and escalate overdue remittance per SLA | Treasury / Payment Ops | CFO | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Delivery app/POD (VS-06.3); treasury/bank (VS-18); CIT (VS-81); 3PL cash file (W4278); GL (VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Holding/robbery risk**: Cash held overnight/en route; mitigated by no-cash-overnight, tamper-evident custody, CIT (VS-81)
- **Float risk**: Slow remittance ties up working capital; mitigated by remittance SLA & aging tracking (W4269)

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min/driver end-of-shift; deposit within SLA

### Automation Opportunity
- POD→remittance auto-match; bank-deposit feed reconciliation; unremitted-cash aging dashboard & alerts

### Controls
- CTL: tamper-evident custody; no-cash-overnight; remittance SLA; deposit verified (VS-18); aging tracked

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06.3 (delivery), VS-18 (treasury/bank), VS-81 (CIT), W4270 (controls), W4269 (float), W4278 (3PL file), W4279 (GL posting)

## W4276. COD Refusal, Short-Pay & Failed-Delivery Cash Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer refuses delivery, short-pays, or is unreachable |
| **Frequency** | Daily (~COD refusal rate 5–15% in PH ecommerce) |
| **Volume** | Refused/short-pay/failed COD orders |
| **Owner** | Last-Mile Ops (VS-06.3) / Payment Ops / Returns (VS-32) |
| **Participants** | Driver/3PL, Customer Support (VS-13), Returns (VS-32), Fraud (VS-125), Inventory (VS-05) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Driver/3PL record refusal/short-pay/failed-delivery with reason & evidence (photo/attempt log), collect any partial cash due (with POD per W4274), and return the goods per the RTV process (VS-32) | Driver / 3PL / Returns | Last-Mile Ops | Per event |
| 2 | Payment Ops/Returns process the order outcome (cancel/return to stock VS-32/VS-05, partial-payment posting, restocking fee per policy), and flag repeat-refusal customers to fraud (VS-125) | Payment Ops / Returns / Fraud | Head of Payment Ops | Per event |
| 3 | Customer Support (VS-13) follow up on failed deliveries (reschedule, address correction), and Fraud/Payment Ops analyze refusal patterns (W4287) to tune eligibility (W4266) | Customer Support / Fraud / Payment Ops | Head of Payment Ops | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Delivery app (VS-06.3); returns/RTV (VS-32); inventory (VS-05); fraud (VS-125); customer support (VS-13); order (VS-10)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Abuse risk**: Serial refusers tie up capacity/inventory; mitigated by refusal tracking & eligibility tuning (W4266)
- **Recovery risk**: Partial cash/stock not reconciled; mitigated by partial-payment posting & RTV (VS-32)

### Time Estimate
- ~5–15 min/event; analysis periodic

### Automation Opportunity
- Refusal-reason capture & pattern analytics; auto-eligibility tuning; RTV/inventory auto-update

### Controls
- CTL: refusal/short-pay evidenced; partial cash posted; stock returned (VS-32); repeat-refusal flagged (VS-125)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06.3 (delivery), VS-32 (returns/RTV), VS-05 (inventory), VS-125 (fraud), VS-13 (support), W4266 (eligibility), W4274 (collection), W4287 (analytics)

## W4277. Daily COD Cash-vs-Order Reconciliation & Exception Resolution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily close (VS-17.1); receipt of remittance/3PL file |
| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | All COD orders dispatched/collected that day (~thousands/day) |
| **Owner** | Finance / Payment Ops / Cash Applications (VS-16) |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17.4), GL (VS-17.1), AR/Cash App (VS-16), Last-Mile (VS-06.3), Fraud (VS-125) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance/Cash App reconcile cash collected (POD W4274 + 3PL remittance W4275 + bank deposit W4279) against each COD order's amount-due (W4273), at order level, daily | Finance / Cash App | Finance Controller | Daily |
| 2 | Finance/Payment Ops investigate exceptions (over/short, unmatched cash, missing POD, duplicate), route to driver/3PL/Fraud, and resolve within SLA | Finance / Payment Ops / Fraud | Finance Controller | Daily |
| 3 | Finance post the reconciled cash to the GL/revenue (W4279), provision unremitted/unreconciled balances (W4269), and report reconciliation health (W4287) | Finance / GL | CFO | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- GL/reconciliation (VS-17.1); AR/cash app (VS-16); delivery/POD (VS-06.3); bank (VS-18); fraud (VS-125); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unreconciled-cash risk**: Cash without an order match → leakage/write-off; mitigated by daily order-level reconciliation & aging
- **Manual-effort risk**: High-volume manual matching; mitigated by automation (W4272 architecture)

### Time Estimate
- ~0.5–1 FTE daily at scale; continuous exception handling

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto cash-to-order matching (POD+remittance+deposit vs amount-due); exception auto-flag; auto-aging

### Controls
- CTL: daily order-level reconciliation; exceptions resolved within SLA; unreconciled provisioned (W4269); SoD (W4270)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.1 (GL), VS-16 (AR/cash app), VS-06.3 (POD), VS-18 (bank), VS-125 (fraud), W4269 (provision), W4273 (amount-due), W4279 (posting), W4287 (reporting)

## W4278. 3PL COD Cash File Ingest, Match & Discrepancy Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | 3PL daily cash-file transmission |
| **Frequency** | Daily per 3PL |
| **Volume** | Cash files per 3PL partner (VS-56) |
| **Owner** | Payment Ops / IT (VS-27) / Finance (VS-17.4) |
| **Participants** | 3PL (VS-56), IT (VS-27), Finance, Last-Mile (VS-06.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT/Payment Ops ingest the 3PL daily cash file (per-order collected amount, remittance, deposit reference) via the agreed format/exchange (W4272), validate structure & completeness | IT / Payment Ops | Head of Payment Ops | Daily |
| 2 | Payment Ops/Finance match the 3PL file to BuildRight POD/orders (W4273/W4274), flag discrepancies (missing orders, amount mismatch, missing deposit), and raise to the 3PL per contract (W4268) | Payment Ops / Finance | Finance Controller | Daily |
| 3 | Payment Ops track discrepancy resolution/SLA, apply contract holdback/penalty (W4268), and feed 3PL performance to scorecard (VS-67) and reconciliation (W4277) | Payment Ops / Finance | CFO | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- 3PL integration (VS-56/W4272); delivery/POD (VS-06.3); finance/GL (VS-17.1); vendor scorecard (VS-67); IT (VS-27)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-quality risk**: Incomplete/late 3PL file → reconciliation gap; mitigated by file-validation & SLA
- **Discrepancy risk**: 3PL-reported cash ≠ order cash; mitigated by matching + contract holdback (W4268)

### Time Estimate
- Automated ingest; ~1–2 hours/day discrepancy handling

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto file ingest & validation; auto-match with discrepancy flag; holdback auto-deduction

### Controls
- CTL: 3PL file validated & matched; discrepancies raised/SLA-tracked; holdback applied (W4268)

### Cross-references

## W4279. COD Bank Deposit Verification & GL Cash Posting

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Bank deposit (VS-18); daily close (VS-17.1) |
| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | Bank deposits of COD cash across accounts |
| **Owner** | Treasury (VS-18) / GL (VS-17.1) / Finance |
| **Participants** | Treasury (VS-18), GL (VS-17.1), Cash App (VS-16), Bank, Payment Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/GL verify the bank deposit (deposit slip vs bank statement/feed VS-18) against the remittance/cash file (W4275/W4278), confirming cash reached the bank | Treasury / GL | Finance Controller | Daily |
| 2 | GL post the reconciled COD cash to revenue/AR (recognize at delivery per W4271), clear the COD receivable/float, and post COD fee/handling revenue (W4267) | GL / Finance | CFO | Daily |
| 3 | Finance reconcile GL cash to bank (VS-17.1 bank rec), investigate variances, and ensure VAT/tax coding per VS-79 | Finance / GL / Tax | Finance Controller | Daily/Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/bank (VS-18); GL/revenue (VS-17.1); AR/cash app (VS-16); tax/BIR (VS-79); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Deposit-gap risk**: Cash remitted but not deposited → theft/loss; mitigated by deposit-vs-remittance verification
- **Recognition-error risk**: Revenue mis-timed/mis-coded; mitigated by recognize-at-delivery rule & VAT coding (VS-79)

### Time Estimate
- Automated posting; ~1–2 hours/day verification

### Automation Opportunity
- Bank-feed auto-reconciliation; GL auto-posting with revenue/VAT coding; bank-rec automation

### Controls
- CTL: deposit verified vs remittance; revenue recognized at delivery (W4271); VAT-coded (VS-79); bank rec closed

### Cross-references

## W4280. COD Physical Cash Security, Robbery/Loss Response & Recovery

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Robbery/loss/security incident involving COD cash |
| **Frequency** | Incident-driven |
| **Volume** | Security incidents across the COD cash chain |
| **Owner** | Security (VS-23) / Payment Ops / Insurance (VS-26) / Loss Prevention |
| **Participants** | Security (VS-23), Insurance (VS-26), Legal (VS-100), Fraud (VS-125), Treasury (VS-18), Police |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Driver/3PL/Security execute the incident response (safety first, secure scene, notify Security/Payment Ops/police), per the cash-security SOP (W4270) | Driver / Security | Head of Payment Ops / Security | Immediate |
| 2 | Security/Fraud investigate (root cause, custody breakdown, internal vs external), document, and log to the loss/security system (VS-23/VS-125) | Security / Fraud | CRO / COO | Per incident |
| 3 | Insurance/Legal file the claim (VS-26), pursue recovery (3PL contract W4268, fidelity bond, police/legal VS-100), provision/write off unrecovered loss (W4269), and apply corrective action | Insurance / Legal / Finance | CFO / CRO | Per incident |

### System Touchpoints
- Security/incident (VS-23); insurance/claims (VS-26); fraud (VS-125); legal (VS-100); treasury/GL (VS-18/VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Loss risk**: Robbery/theft of COD cash; mitigated by custody controls (W4270), CIT (VS-81), insurance (VS-26)
- **Recovery risk**: Unrecovered loss distorts earnings; mitigated by claims/recovery & provisioning (W4269)

### Time Estimate
- Immediate response; investigation ~1–5 days/incident

### Automation Opportunity
- In-app SOS/panic; incident→security/insurance workflow; loss-provision auto-suggestion

### Controls
- CTL: cash-security SOP followed; custody controls (W4270); claim/recovery pursued (VS-26); loss provisioned (W4269)

### Cross-references
