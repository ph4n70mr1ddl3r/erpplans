## W4713. Assignment Payroll, Multi-Currency & Home/Host Split-Pay Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Assignment start; payroll cycle; mid-assignment change |
| **Frequency** | Per pay cycle (semi-monthly) |
| **Volume** | ~10–20 active assignees on multi-currency/split pay |
| **Owner** | Mobility Payroll Manager / Payroll (VS-19.2) |
| **Participants** | Payroll (VS-19.2), Treasury/FX (VS-18.3), Comp (VS-102), Tax (VS-79), assignment-payroll vendor (W4700) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set up the assignment pay structure: home-country pay vs. host-country pay split (per the package W4699), multi-currency, and the home/host entity payroll feeds (VS-72); configure in payroll (VS-19.2) and the assignment-payroll vendor | Mobility Payroll / Payroll / Treasury | Head of Global Mobility / CFO | Per assignment |
| 2 | Run assignment payroll each cycle: home and host components, FX conversion (VS-18.3), allowances (premium, COLA, hardship), and tax/equalization entries (W4714); reconcile split-pay across entities | Mobility Payroll / Payroll | Head of Global Mobility / CFO | Per cycle |
| 3 | Manage mid-assignment changes (package, currency, entity), year-end reconciliation, and hand-off to repatriation/localization payroll (W4718); ensure SoD and audit trail | Mobility Payroll / Payroll | Head of Global Mobility / CFO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Payroll (VS-19.2); treasury/FX (VS-18.3); intercompany (VS-72); comp (VS-102); assignment-payroll vendor

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Split-pay-error risk**: Home/host split mis-computed → under/over-payment and intercompany distortion; mitigated by the package configuration and reconciliation
- **FX-volatility risk**: Multi-currency pay exposed to FX; mitigated by Treasury FX coordination (VS-18.3)

### Time Estimate
- ~per cycle absorbed in payroll; ~per assignment setup 2–3 days

### Automation Opportunity
- Assignment-payroll configuration; multi-currency/FX automation; split-pay reconciliation

### Controls
- CTL-712 (ensure controlled execution — Assignment Administration, Tax/Payroll, Repatriation & Analytics (PA-160.3).)
- CTL: split-pay per package; FX from governed source (VS-18.3); reconciled across entities; SoD

### Cross-references
- links to VS-19.2 (payroll), VS-18.3 (FX), VS-72 (intercompany), W4699 (package), W4714 (tax)

## W4714. Assignment Tax: Resident/Non-Resident, Tax-Treaty, 19-Series & Equalization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Assignment start/each tax year; tax filing; residency change |
| **Frequency** | Per tax year + per filing |
| **Volume** | ~15–25 active assignees' tax positions |
| **Owner** | Mobility Tax Manager / Tax (VS-79) |
| **Participants** | Tax (VS-79), Payroll (VS-19.2), Treasury (VS-18), assignment-tax vendor, foreign worker, BIR |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Determine each assignee's tax residency (Philippine resident vs. non-resident; home-country residency), apply tax-treaty relief where applicable, and the 19-series (final withholding on non-residents) vs. regular graduated tax treatment | Mobility Tax Manager / Tax | VP Tax / CFO | Per tax year |
| 2 | Operate tax-equalization (the assignee bears home-country tax as if at home; BuildRight bears/recoveres the host-country up/downside) or tax-protection per policy (W4697); compute the equalization settlement annually | Mobility Tax Manager / Payroll | VP Tax / CFO | Annual + per cycle |
| 3 | File the assignee's Philippine tax (BIR — resident or 19-series non-resident W4715) and coordinate home-country filing/relief; retain tax records (VS-88) and support BIR audit (VS-79) | Mobility Tax Manager / Tax | VP Tax / CFO | Per filing + audit |

### System Touchpoints
- Tax (VS-79); payroll (VS-19.2); treasury (VS-18); assignment-tax vendor; records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Double-taxation risk**: Assignee taxed in both jurisdictions without treaty relief → cost and inequity; mitigated by treaty application
- **Equalization-error risk**: Equalization mis-computed → assignee/employer cost disputes; mitigated by documented methodology and annual settlement

### Time Estimate
- ~per assignee 3–5 days/year tax + equalization

### Automation Opportunity
- Residency/treaty decision support; equalization calculator; tax-filing automation

### Controls
- CTL-712 (ensure controlled execution — Assignment Administration, Tax/Payroll, Repatriation & Analytics (PA-160.3).)
- CTL: residency/treaty determined (Tax-led); equalization per policy; filings and records retained (VS-88)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-79 (tax/BIR), W4697 (policy), W4713 (payroll), W4715 (statutory), VS-88 (records)
