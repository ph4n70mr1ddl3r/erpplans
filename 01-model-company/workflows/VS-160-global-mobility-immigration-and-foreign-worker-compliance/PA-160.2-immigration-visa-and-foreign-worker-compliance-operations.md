## W4705. DOLE Alien Employment Permit (AEP) Application & Renewal Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New foreign hire; AEP expiry (annual/biennial); role change |
| **Frequency** | Per foreign worker + renewal cycle |
| **Volume** | ~15–25 active foreign workers needing AEP across the 5 entities (seconded experts per VS-122, executives) |
| **Owner** | Immigration Operations Lead |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19), hiring manager, Legal (VS-100.3), DOLE Regional Office, immigration agent (W4700) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare and file the DOLE AEP application: proof of the foreign worker's qualifications, the justification for hiring a foreign national (no available qualified Filipino — DOLE requirement), the position, and the employer's documents; engage the DOLE Regional Office (via immigration agent W4700) | Immigration Operations / HR / Legal | Head of Global Mobility / VP Legal | Per application |
| 2 | Track the AEP issuance and conditions (validity, role/entity scope); file renewals before expiry per the compliance calendar (W4703); manage AEP conditions (role/entity changes require amendment) | Immigration Operations | Head of Global Mobility | Per renewal |
| 3 | Retain AEP records (VS-88); support DOLE inspection/audit (W4711) and ensure the foreign worker does not commence productive work before AEP issuance (compliance) | Immigration Operations / Legal | Head of Global Mobility / VP Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Immigration/case management; compliance calendar (W4703); HR (VS-19); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-justification risk**: DOLE may deny the AEP if BuildRight cannot justify the absence of a qualified Filipino (developed via VS-123); mitigated by robust justification and local-talent-development evidence
- **Pre-AEP-work risk**: A foreign worker starting before AEP issuance → DOLE penalty; mitigated by the no-work-before-AEP rule

### Time Estimate
- ~per AEP application 3–6 weeks (DOLE-dependent); ~renewal absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- AEP-application document assembly; renewal-alert automation; justification-evidence library

### Controls
- CTL-130 (ensure immigration & foreign-worker compliance.)
- CTL: AEP justification documented; no work before AEP; renewals before expiry (compliance calendar)

### Cross-references
- links to W4700 (agents), W4703 (calendar), W4706 (9G/I-Card), W4711 (audit), VS-123 (local talent)

## W4706. Bureau of Immigration 9G Pre-Arranged Employee Visa & ACR I-Card Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | AEP issuance (W4705); visa/I-Card expiry; entry/exit |
| **Frequency** | Per foreign worker + renewal |
| **Volume** | ~ active 9G visa holders |
| **Owner** | Immigration Operations Lead |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19), Legal (VS-100.3), BI, immigration agent (W4700), foreign worker |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On AEP issuance (W4705), file the BI 9G Pre-Arranged Employee Commercial Visa and ACR I-Card application (with the employment contract, AEP, and supporting documents); manage the foreign worker's entry/visa status (downgrading/conversion from tourist/9a where applicable) | Immigration Operations / Agent / Legal | Head of Global Mobility / VP Legal | Per application |
| 2 | Track visa/I-Card validity, conditions, and entry/exit; file renewals before expiry (W4703); manage exit-clearing (Emigration Clearance Certificate / SSDC where applicable) on assignment end (W4718) | Immigration Operations | Head of Global Mobility | Per renewal + exit |
| 3 | Retain visa/I-Card records (VS-88); support BI audit (W4711); ensure no overstay (BI penalty, deportation, officer liability) | Immigration Operations / Legal | Head of Global Mobility / VP Legal | Continuous |

- **Overstay risk**: Visa/I-Card expiry unnoticed → overstay (BI fines, blacklist, deportation); mitigated by the compliance calendar + alerts (W4703)
- **Wrong-status-work risk**: A tourist/9a visa holder doing productive work → immigration violation; mitigated by the 9G-before-work rule

### Time Estimate
- ~per 9G/I-Card application 4–8 weeks (BI-dependent); ~renewal absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- Visa/I-Card application document assembly; expiry-alert automation; entry/exit tracking

### Controls
- CTL-711 (ensure controlled execution — Immigration, Visa & Foreign-Worker Compliance Operations (PA-160.2).)
- CTL: 9G/I-Card secured before productive work; renewals before expiry; no overstay (compliance calendar)

## W4707. Special Work Permit (SWP), Treaty-Trader (9D) & Other Visa Category Selection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Short-term/specialized foreign-worker need; treaty-based engagement |
| **Frequency** | Per case |
| **Volume** | ~ short-term experts, treaty-based assignees, CAI (commercial arrangements) cases |
| **Owner** | Immigration Operations Lead |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19), Legal (VS-100.3), Tax (VS-79), hiring manager, BI |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select the appropriate immigration category for each case: Special Work Permit (SWP) for short-term specialized work (<6 months); Treaty-Trader/Investor (9D) where a qualifying treaty/business exists; 9G for standard employment; Special Visa for Employment Generation (SVEG) where applicable | Immigration Operations / Legal / Tax | Head of Global Mobility / VP Legal | Per case |
| 2 | File and track the chosen category with its specific documents, conditions, and tax implications (category affects residency/tax treatment W4714); manage transitions between categories | Immigration Operations / Agent | Head of Global Mobility | Per case |
| 3 | Document the category-selection rationale (avoid category-shopping that misrepresents the engagement); retain records (VS-88); support audit (W4711) | Immigration Operations / Legal | Head of Global Mobility / VP Legal | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Immigration/case management; tax (VS-79); compliance calendar (W4703); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Category-mismatch risk**: Choosing a category that doesn't fit the actual engagement (e.g., SWP for long-term work) → violation; mitigated by the category-selection framework
- **Tax-treatment-mismatch risk**: Visa category and tax treatment misaligned → tax exposure (W4714); mitigated by Tax involvement in selection

### Time Estimate
- ~per case 1–2 weeks selection + filing

### Automation Opportunity
- Category-decision tool with tax/immigration implications; document assembly per category

### Controls
- CTL-130 (ensure immigration & foreign-worker compliance.)
- CTL: category-selection rationale documented; category fits actual engagement; tax-treatment aligned (W4714)

### Cross-references
- links to W4714 (tax), W4703 (calendar), W4711 (audit), VS-79/VS-100.3 (tax/legal)

## W4712. Foreign-Worker Wage, Minimum-Wage & Anti-Discrimination Compliance (DOLE)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Foreign-worker hire; wage review; DOLE audit |
| **Frequency** | Per hire + periodic |
| **Volume** | ~ active foreign workers |
| **Owner** | Head of Global Mobility / Comp & Benefits |
| **Participants** | Comp (VS-102), Payroll (VS-19.2), Legal (VS-100.3), Labor Relations (VS-84), DOLE |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ensure foreign workers receive at least the applicable Philippine regional minimum wage and statutory benefits (SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG, 13th-month, leave) per DOLE — foreign workers enjoy the same labor protections; structure compensation per VS-102 | Comp / Global Mobility / Payroll | Head of Global Mobility / CHRO | Per hire + periodic |
| 2 | Apply anti-discrimination and equal-treatment principles; manage any DOLE concern about foreign-worker displacement of Filipino labor (the AEP justification W4705 addresses this); coordinate with Labor Relations (VS-84) | Global Mobility / Legal / Labor Relations | Head of Global Mobility / VP Legal | Continuous |
| 3 | Retain compensation records (VS-88); support DOLE wage/labor audit; assure via Internal Audit (VS-21) | Comp / Payroll / Global Mobility | Head of Global Mobility / CHRO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Comp (VS-102); payroll (VS-19.2); HR (VS-19); labor relations (VS-84); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Sub-minimum-wage risk**: A foreign worker paid below minimum/without statutory benefits → DOLE violation; mitigated by the wage-parity rule
- **Displacement concern risk**: DOLE/labor concern about foreign-worker displacement of nationals; mitigated by robust AEP justification (W4705) and local-talent development (VS-123)

### Time Estimate
- ~per hire absorbed; ~periodic review absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- Wage-parity check at payroll setup; statutory-benefit automation; displacement-justification library

### Controls
- CTL-711 (ensure controlled execution — Immigration, Visa & Foreign-Worker Compliance Operations (PA-160.2).)
- CTL: foreign workers paid ≥ minimum wage with full statutory benefits; anti-discrimination applied; AEP justification robust
