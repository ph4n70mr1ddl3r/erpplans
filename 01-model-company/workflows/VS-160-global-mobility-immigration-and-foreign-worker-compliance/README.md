## Overview

Global Mobility, Immigration & Foreign Worker Compliance workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
owning the enterprise **global-mobility & immigration discipline** by which BuildRight moves talent
across borders and within the Philippine archipelago compliantly — securing DOLE **Alien
Employment Permits (AEP)**, Bureau of Immigration **9G Pre-Arranged Employee Commercial Visas** and
**ACR I-Cards** for foreign workers (sourcing agents/seconded experts from VS-122, foreign
executives/directors, specialized technicians), managing cross-border and domestic
provincial-transfers, administering assignment payroll/tax/statutory benefits and repatriation, and
discharging employer **duty-of-care** — all within Philippine immigration/labor/tax law and
BuildRight's family-corporation governance.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. "Immigration", "9G visa", "alien employment",
"expatriate", "international assignment", and "employee mobility" each appear in **zero** PA files
as dedicated workflow headers (a single incidental "work permit" reference exists); "relocation"
appears ~22 times but in store-relocation/staff-housing contexts (VS-144), not cross-border
mobility. VS-19 covers Philippine employees, VS-98 covers contingent workforce, VS-122 covers the
vendor-side of global sourcing, VS-103 covers domestic HR shared services — but no value stream
owns the **foreign-worker immigration & global-mobility program**.

This is distinct from **VS-19 (Hire-to-Retire)** which manages **Philippine-national employees** —
this value stream manages the **immigration, assignment, and cross-border compliance** of foreign
and mobile workers. It is distinct from **VS-122 (Global Sourcing)** which is the **vendor/buying
office** side — this value stream handles BuildRight's **own foreign employees and assignees**. It
is distinct from **VS-98 (Contingent Workforce)** which manages **outsourced/contract labor** — this
value stream manages **sponsored foreign employees on assignment**. It is distinct from **VS-144
(Employee Accommodation)** which provides **housing** — this value stream administers the
**assignment** (and coordinates housing as one element). It is distinct from **VS-141 (Employee
Transport)** which handles **daily commute** — this value stream handles **relocation and travel
security** (and links to VS-159 for travel-risk).

BuildRight's exposure is structural: a Filipino-owned family corporation that operates global
sourcing (VS-122 — likely with seconded sourcing experts), may employ foreign executives/specialists
under its 5-entity structure, runs provincial inter-island transfers across a 200-store footprint,
and is expanding 10–15 stores/year — and Philippine immigration/labor law (DOLE AEP, BI 9G/I-Card,
minimum-wage parity for foreign workers, tax-treaty/19-series residency rules) carries real
compliance, penalty, and officer-personal-liability exposure.
