# VS-38: Consumer Credit & Financing

> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages consumer installment and financing programs for big-ticket purchases at BuildRight Depot — including partnerships with banks and financing companies, in-house credit evaluation for promotional schemes, "buy now pay later" (BNPL) integration, credit card installment conversion, and home improvement loan referral programs. Critical for driving sales in high-value categories (appliances, power tools, kitchen/bathroom renovation packages, air conditioning units) where the average purchase price exceeds typical consumer cash availability. Supports the 5% Home Appliances and 5% Home Décor & Furniture categories while also enabling trade professionals to offer financing to their end-customers.
