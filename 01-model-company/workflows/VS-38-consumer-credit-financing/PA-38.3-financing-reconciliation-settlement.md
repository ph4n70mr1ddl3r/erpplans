## W1779. Financing Partner Daily Settlement Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily settlement received from financing partners (T+1 to T+3) |
| **Frequency** | Daily (~5–8 partners × 365 days = ~2,000 settlements/year) |
| **Volume** | ~8,000–12,000 installment transactions/day across all stores |
| **Owner** | Treasury |
| **Participants** | Treasury, Revenue Accountant, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Financing partner settles daily transactions: (a) bank installment: acquiring bank remits net amount (gross − MDR) to BuildRight bank account, (b) non-card financing: partner remits approved transaction amounts, (c) BNPL provider: remits via designated settlement account, (d) settlement file received electronically (per partner: CSV, API, or bank statement) | Financing Partner → System | Treasury | Automated (T+1 to T+3) |
| 2 | System auto-matches settlement records to POS transactions: (a) match on: transaction reference, date, amount, terminal ID, (b) matched transactions: system posts receipt to GL (debit cash, credit AR-financing partner), (c) unmatched transactions: flagged for investigation (unreconciled items queue) | System | Treasury | Automated |
| 3 | Treasury investigates unmatched items: (a) timing differences (settlement covers different date range than POS), (b) amount discrepancies (MDR calculation differences, promotional rate misapplication), (c) missing transactions (POS transaction not received by partner), (d) resolves within 5 business days; escalates to partner for unresolved items | Treasury | Accounting Manager | 1–2 hours/day |
| 4 | Daily reconciliation report: matched count/amount, unmatched count/amount, aging of unreconciled items; Accounting Manager reviews material discrepancies | Treasury | Accounting Manager | 30 min/day |


### System Touchpoints
- Partner settlement capture — daily net remittance (gross − MDR) per partner (~2,000 settlements/year) (step 1)
- Auto-match engine — transaction reference, date, amount, terminal ID (step 2)
- Unmatched-item investigation — timing, amount, MDR variance (step 3)
- Daily reconciliation report with aging of unreconciled items (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unmatched-aging risk**: small differences accumulate into an unresolvable mass; mitigated by aging-of-unreconciled reporting reviewed daily (step 4)
- **False-match risk**: auto-matching binds the wrong records; mitigated by multi-key matching with review thresholds (step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count," (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "records to POS transactions" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-escalate of "escalates to partner for unresolved items" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: Treasury review-and-approval gate; periodic reconciliation against source documents

### Time Estimate
- Step 1: Automated (T+1 to T+3)
- Step 2: Automated
- Step 3: 1–2 hours/day
- Step 4: 30 min/day

## W1780. Monthly Financing Partner Statement Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly statement received from financing partner |
| **Frequency** | Monthly (~5–8 partner statements) |
| **Volume** | ~5–8 reconciliations/month |
| **Owner** | Treasury |
| **Participants** | Treasury, Revenue Accountant, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Financing partner provides monthly statement: (a) total transactions processed, (b) total settlement amount remitted, (c) total MDR/fees charged, (d) chargebacks and reversals, (e) promotional subsidy adjustments | Financing Partner | Treasury | By 10th of following month |
| 2 | Treasury reconciles monthly statement to internal records: (a) total transaction amount: partner total vs. POS total for installment transactions, (b) total settlement received: partner total vs. bank deposits, (c) MDR charged: partner calculation vs. contracted rate × transaction volume, (d) promotional adjustments: verify against approved promotional campaigns, (e) net settlement: partner total vs. GL clearing account balance | Treasury | Accounting Manager | 1 day/partner |
| 3 | Discrepancies investigated and resolved: (a) MDR rate misapplication: compare contracted rate to actual rate charged per transaction, (b) promotional rate discrepancies: verify correct promotional MDR applied during campaign periods, (c) settlement timing: adjust for T+2 or T+3 settlements crossing month-end, (d) adjustments: partner issues credit note or debit memo for confirmed discrepancies | Treasury / Financing Partner | Accounting Manager | 1–2 weeks |
| 4 | Accounting Manager reviews and approves monthly reconciliation; adjusts GL entries for any reconciling items; reconciliation filed for audit trail | Accounting Manager | CFO | 1 day |


### System Touchpoints
- Monthly statement reconciliation — transactions, settlements, MDR totals vs. internal records (steps 1–2)
- Discrepancy investigation — MDR rate misapplication per transaction (step 3)
- GL adjustments and filed reconciliation with Accounting Manager approval (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **MDR-misapplication leakage**: the partner charges above the contracted rate; mitigated by per-transaction rate comparison (step 3)
- **Reconciliation-theater risk**: reconciliations filed with items still open; mitigated by approval gated on resolution or documented plan (step 4)

## W1781. Financing Chargeback & Dispute Resolution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Chargeback received from financing partner (customer disputes transaction) |
| **Frequency** | ~50–100 chargebacks/year |
| **Volume** | ~0.1% of installment transactions |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Treasury, Legal Counsel, Financing Partner, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Financing partner notifies BuildRight of chargeback: (a) customer claims: merchandise not received, defective, or not as described, (b) fraud claim: customer denies making the purchase, (c) processing error: duplicate charge, incorrect amount | Financing Partner → Treasury | — | Event-driven |
| 2 | Treasury alerts Store Manager for investigation: (a) pull transaction details (receipt, POS log, sales associate), (b) check delivery status (for ecommerce), (c) review CCTV footage if fraud claim, (d) check return history for the transaction | Store Manager / Treasury | — | 1–3 days |
| 3 | BuildRight responds to financing partner: (a) provide evidence supporting the transaction (signed receipt, delivery confirmation, product description match), (b) if BuildRight accepts chargeback (legitimate dispute): accept loss, process return, reverse transaction, (c) if BuildRight contests: submit representment with evidence to partner, (d) partner makes final determination | Treasury / Store Manager | Accounting Manager | 5–10 days |
| 4 | If chargeback upheld: (a) revenue reversed, (b) inventory recovered (if merchandise returned), (c) chargeback fee (typically PHP 500–1,500) charged as expense, (d) if chargeback overturned: transaction reinstated, funds returned to BuildRight | Treasury / System | Accounting Manager | Per resolution |


### System Touchpoints
- Chargeback intake and categorization — not received, defective, not as described (step 1)
- Evidence assembly — receipt, POS log, delivery confirmation, CCTV (step 2)
- Response submission to the partner within dispute windows (step 3)
- Upheld-chargeback accounting — revenue reversal, inventory recovery, fee (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Evidence-loss risk**: transaction evidence purged before the dispute lands; mitigated by evidence retention windows matched to dispute timelines (step 2)
- **Repeat-fraud risk**: the same customer/terminal pattern generates serial chargebacks; mitigated by chargeback pattern analysis feeding Loss Prevention (step 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "log, sales associate" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-submit of "submit representment with evidence to partner," (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: Store Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- Step 1: Event-driven
- Step 2: 1–3 days
- Step 3: 5–10 days
- Step 4: Per resolution

## W1782. MDR Fee Tracking & Expense Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly management reporting |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~5–8 partners × monthly reports |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, Treasury, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System tracks MDR fees per transaction: (a) standard credit card MDR: 1.5–2.0%, (b) installment MDR: 2.5–4.0% (higher for longer terms and 0% promotions), (c) BNPL fee: 2–4%, (d) non-card financing fee: 3–5%, (e) fees recorded as selling expense per transaction, allocated to store cost center | System | — | Automated |
| 2 | Monthly MDR expense report: (a) total MDR expense by partner, (b) MDR as % of financed sales, (c) MDR rate comparison vs. contracted rates (identify overcharging), (d) MDR by store (identify stores with higher installment usage and higher MDR cost), (e) trend analysis: is MDR cost increasing as % of sales? | FP&A | CFO | 1 day/month |
| 3 | CFO uses MDR data for partner renegotiation: (a) aggregate volume provides leverage for better rates, (b) identify partners with highest MDR for targeted negotiation, (c) assess if MDR cost is justified by incremental sales generated by financing | CFO | CEO | Quarterly |


### System Touchpoints
- Per-transaction MDR tracking by tender type — standard 1.5–2.0%, installment 2.5–4.0% (step 1)
- Monthly MDR expense report — by partner, % of financed sales, actual vs. contract rate (step 2)
- Renegotiation analytics — volume leverage and outlier identification (step 3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rate-creep risk**: actual MDR drifts above contracted rates; mitigated by monthly contract-rate comparison (step 2)
- **Mix-shift blindness**: longer installment terms quietly raise blended MDR; mitigated by term-mix analysis in the expense report (steps 1–2)

| **Owner** | Revenue Accountant |
| **Participants** | Revenue Accountant, Cashier, Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Multi-tender scenarios: (a) customer purchases PHP 20K appliance: pays PHP 5K cash, PHP 15K on 12-month installment, (b) customer uses loyalty points + credit card installment, (c) customer uses GCash + BNPL for remaining balance | Cashier | — | Per transaction |
| 2 | System records each tender separately: (a) cash portion: deposited daily, (b) installment portion: settled by financing partner at T+1 to T+3, (c) loyalty points: liability reduction, (d) e-wallet: settled per e-wallet provider terms | System | — | Automated |

## W1784. Financing Partner Payment Exception Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Settlement delay, partial settlement, or settlement error from financing partner |
| **Frequency** | ~10–20 exceptions/month |
| **Volume** | Low volume but requires timely resolution |
| **Owner** | Treasury |
| **Participants** | Treasury, Financing Partner, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exception types: (a) settlement not received by expected date (T+2 or T+3), (b) settlement amount differs from expected (short settlement), (c) duplicate settlement (partner pays twice), (d) settlement for wrong merchant (BuildRight receives another merchant's settlement), (e) partner system outage (no settlement file) | Treasury | — | Event-driven |
| 2 | Treasury contacts partner for resolution: (a) escalates to partner's merchant support, (b) documents communication, (c) tracks exception in reconciliation log, (d) target resolution: 5 business days | Treasury | Accounting Manager | 1–3 days |
| 3 | GL adjustment if needed: (a) if settlement permanently short: record loss or adjust MDR, (b) if settlement delayed but expected: no adjustment (accrue), (c) if duplicate: return excess to partner | Accounting Manager | CFO | Per exception |
| 4 | Chronic issues (> 3 exceptions in 30 days from same partner): CFO escalates to partner management; considers partner termination if systemic | CFO | — | As needed |


### System Touchpoints
- Exception intake and classification — settlement delay, short settlement, error (~10–20/month) (step 1)
- Partner escalation tracking with documented communication (step 2)
- GL adjustment policy — loss recognition or accrual for expected settlement (step 3)
- Chronic-issue escalation — > 3 exceptions in 30 days triggers CFO action (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Chronic-partner risk**: repeated short settlements erode revenue silently; mitigated by the >3-in-30-days CFO escalation including termination consideration (step 4)
- **GL-distortion risk**: unresolved exceptions accumulate as phantom receivables; mitigated by the adjustment policy with accrual/loss recognition (step 3)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ecommerce financing flow: (a) customer selects installment/BNPL on buildright.com.ph, (b) payment gateway (PayMongo/Dragonpay) processes installment with financing partner, (c) BuildRight Digital Commerce Inc. collects payment from gateway, (d) Digital Commerce Inc. remits to BuildRight Depot Inc. per intercompany agreement (daily or weekly), (e) Depot Inc. recognizes revenue at fulfillment (per intercompany arrangement) | System | Treasury | Automated |
