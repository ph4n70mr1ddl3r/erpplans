## W1763. Financing Partner Selection & Agreement Negotiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual financing partner review or new partner needed |
| **Frequency** | Annual review; new agreements every 2–3 years |
| **Volume** | ~5–8 active financing partnerships |
| **Owner** | CFO |
| **Participants** | CFO, Treasury, VP Merchandising, CMO, Legal Counsel |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CFO identifies financing partner categories needed: (a) bank installment partners: BDO, BPI, Metrobank, Chinabank, EastWest (credit card installment conversion), (b) financing companies: Home Credit, RFC, Savers (for non-card installment), (c) BNPL providers: GCash GGives, Maya (digital-first installment), (d) government housing loan programs: Pag-IBIG Fund (home improvement loans), (e) in-house deferred payment for trade accounts (covered by VS-16) | CFO | CEO | 1 week |
| 2 | Negotiate terms with each partner: (a) merchant discount rate (MDR): % of transaction paid to partner (target: 1.5–3.5% depending on promo), (b) 0% interest promotional periods: partner subsidizes interest for 3/6/12-month terms (higher MDR for longer 0% periods), (c) settlement period: days until BuildRight receives payment (target: T+1 to T+3), (d) minimum transaction amount for installment (typically PHP 3,000–5,000), (e) maximum installment term (6/12/18/24/36 months), (f) chargeback and dispute resolution process, (g) marketing co-op fund contribution from partner | CFO / Treasury | CEO | 2–4 weeks |
| 3 | Legal Counsel reviews and executes financing agreement: (a) BSP compliance (if partner is regulated financial institution), (b) Data Privacy Act compliance for customer data sharing, (c) liability and indemnification terms, (d) termination provisions | Legal Counsel | CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 4 | IT integrates partner into POS system: (a) installment payment method added to POS terminal, (b) real-time approval interface with financing partner, (c) settlement reconciliation automated, (d) promotional rate tables configured in POS | IT / POS Vendor | CIO | 2–4 weeks |


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-count (for trade accounts (covered by vs-16))
- auto-review (legal counsel reviews and executes financing)

### Controls
operational: CFO review-and-approval gate; periodic reconciliation against source documents

## W1766. Consumer Credit Application Processing at POS

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer requests installment payment at POS |
| **Frequency** | ~8,000–12,000 installment transactions/month across 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~3–5% of POS transactions use installment financing |
| **Owner** | Cashier |
| **Participants** | Cashier, Customer, Financing Partner (real-time) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer selects items and requests installment payment at checkout; Cashier confirms eligibility: (a) total transaction amount meets minimum (typically ≥ PHP 3,000–5,000), (b) items are eligible for installment (not clearance, not services), (c) customer presents valid credit card (for bank installment) or applies for non-card financing | Cashier | — | 2 min |
| 2 | For credit card installment: (a) Cashier selects installment payment method in POS, (b) selects term (3/6/12/24/36 months per promo), (c) swipes/taps customer credit card, (d) POS sends installment conversion request to acquiring bank, (e) approval received in real-time (3–10 seconds), (f) receipt printed showing installment details (monthly amount, term, total cost) | Cashier | — | 3–5 min |
| 3 | For non-card financing (e.g., Home Credit): (a) customer presents valid ID and provides personal information, (b) Sales Associate assists customer with financing partner application (tablet or kiosk), (c) financing partner performs credit assessment (real-time, 5–15 minutes), (d) approval/rejection communicated to customer, (e) if approved: transaction processed at POS with partner payment method, (f) customer signs financing agreement with partner (not BuildRight) | Sales Associate / Cashier | Store Manager | 15–30 min |
| 4 | Transaction completed: (a) BuildRight receives full transaction amount from financing partner at settlement (minus MDR), (b) customer's payment obligation is with financing partner (not BuildRight), (c) no AR risk for BuildRight on consumer installment transactions | System | — | Automated |

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Application rejection**: Customer selects merchandise, applies for financing, and is rejected — creates frustration; Sales Associate should offer alternatives (shorter term, lower amount, different partner)
- **Processing time**: Non-card financing applications take 10–15 minutes; queue management needed at busy periods
- **MDR impact**: Installment MDR (2.5–4%) is higher than regular card-present MDR (1.5–2%); gross margin impact must be factored into pricing

---



### Automation Opportunity
- auto-validate (payment at checkout; cashier confirms eligibility:)
- auto-generate ((f) receipt printed showing installment details)
- rule-based auto-approval ((e) if approved: transaction processed at)

### Controls
operational: Cashier review and approval gate

| **Frequency** | ~2,000–4,000 BNPL transactions/month (growing rapidly) |
| **Volume** | ~1–2% of total transactions (increasing trend) |
| **Owner** | CMO (digital channels) / VP Store Operations (in-store) |
| **Participants** | Cashier, Customer, BNPL Provider (GCash GGives, Maya, etc.) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer selects BNPL at checkout: (a) in-store: Cashier selects BNPL payment method in POS, generates QR code or asks customer to open BNPL app, (b) ecommerce: customer selects BNPL at payment page, redirected to BNPL provider for approval, (c) customer authenticates with BNPL provider (biometric/PIN), (d) BNPL provider approves transaction (instant for existing users; new user onboarding 3–5 minutes) | Cashier / System | — | 3–5 min |
| 2 | Transaction completed: (a) BuildRight receives payment from BNPL provider at T+1 settlement, minus BNPL fee (typically 2–4%), (b) customer's repayment obligation is with BNPL provider, (c) no AR risk for BuildRight | System | — | Automated |
| 3 | Returns on BNPL transactions: (a) customer returns item at store, (b) BuildRight processes return in POS, (c) system sends refund instruction to BNPL provider, (d) BNPL provider adjusts customer's payment schedule or issues refund, (e) BuildRight receives credit note from BNPL provider | Cashier / System | Store Manager | 10 min |
| 4 | Monthly reconciliation: Treasury reconciles BNPL settlement statements with POS transaction records per W1779 | Treasury | CFO | Monthly |


### Automation Opportunity

- auto-send ((c) system sends refund instruction to)
- auto-record (pos transaction records per w1779)

| **Frequency** | Continuous monitoring; ad-hoc for regulatory changes |

| **Participants** | Legal Counsel, Compliance Officer, CFO, DPO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal Counsel monitors regulatory environment for consumer financing: (a) BSP regulations on installment sales and credit, (b) DTI consumer protection requirements (Truth in Lending Act), (c) Data Privacy Act requirements for customer financial data sharing, (d) Anti-Money Laundering (AMLA) requirements for large cash transactions, (e) financing company licensing requirements (BuildRight is merchant, not lender — but must verify partners are properly licensed) | Legal Counsel | VP Legal | Continuous |
| 2 | Compliance with Truth in Lending Act (RA 3765): (a) ensure all promotional financing displays clearly state: principal amount, interest rate (or "0% interest"), installment term, monthly amortization, total amount payable, (b) no hidden fees passed to customer, (c) promotional terms clearly communicated at POS and in marketing materials | Legal Counsel / Marketing | CMO | Per promotion |
| 3 | Data privacy compliance for financing applications: (a) customer consent obtained before sharing personal data with financing partner, (b) financing partner data processing agreement in place, (c) customer data retained only per retention policy, (d) DPO monitors financing data flows per W1735 | DPO / Legal Counsel | VP Legal | Continuous |
| 4 | Annual review: Legal Counsel assesses all financing partnerships for regulatory compliance; flags any partner concerns (license status, complaint patterns, regulatory actions) to CFO | Legal Counsel | CFO | 1 day/year |
