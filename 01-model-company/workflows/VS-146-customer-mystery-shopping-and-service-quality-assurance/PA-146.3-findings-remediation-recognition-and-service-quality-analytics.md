## W4377. Store/Unit Service-Findings Feedback & Corrective Action

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Triage finding (W4376) |
| **Frequency** | Per finding + batch feedback |
| **Volume** | All service findings routed to units |
| **Owner** | Service Quality / Store Ops (VS-07) / CX (VS-13) |
| **Participants** | Service Quality, Store/Unit Leadership, CX (VS-13), HR (VS-103), Sales Enablement (VS-124) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service Quality route findings to the responsible unit/store with evidence & score, within a feedback SLA (days) | Service Quality | Service Quality Lead | Per finding |
| 2 | Unit leadership acknowledge, plan corrective action (coaching, retraining, process fix), and execute within an agreed timeframe | Unit Leadership / Store Ops | Store Mgr / Unit Lead | Per finding |
| 3 | Service Quality/HR verify corrective-action completion, confirm behavior change in subsequent shops, and close the finding | Service Quality / HR | Service Quality Lead | Per finding |

### System Touchpoints
- Service-quality system (W4368); store ops (VS-07); HR (VS-103); sales enablement (VS-124); CX (VS-13)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-action risk**: findings acknowledged but not actioned → repeat failure; mitigated by closure verification & behavior-change confirmation
- **Feedback-delay risk**: slow feedback → lost learning moment; mitigated by feedback SLA

### Time Estimate
- Feedback ~min; corrective action days–weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- Findings-routing workflow with SLA; corrective-action plan tracking; behavior-change confirmation via subsequent shops

### Controls
- CTL: findings routed within SLA; corrective action verified & closed; behavior change confirmed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07 (store), VS-103 (HR), VS-124 (enablement), VS-13 (CX), W4376 (triage), W4378 (root-cause)

## W4378. Root-Cause Service Failure Analysis & Remediation Plan

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Repeat/critical/systemic finding; pattern detected |
| **Frequency** | Per pattern + periodic |
| **Volume** | Systemic/pattern service-failure analyses |
| **Owner** | Service Quality / OpEx (VS-133) / CX (VS-13) |
| **Participants** | Service Quality, OpEx (VS-133), CX (VS-13), Store Ops (VS-07), HR (VS-103), Strategy (VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service Quality/OpEx identify patterns & systemic failures (repeated across stores/regions/associates), and run root-cause analysis (5-why, process VS-133) | Service Quality / OpEx | Head of Service Quality / OpEx Lead | Per pattern |
| 2 | Cross-functional team design the remediation plan (training VS-124, process redesign VS-133, staffing VS-19.3, tools, policy) addressing root cause | Service Quality / OpEx / Store Ops / HR | COO / CHRO | Per pattern |
| 3 | Service Quality/OpEx track remediation impact on subsequent service scores, and standardize/scale successful fixes | Service Quality / OpEx | Head of Service Quality / OpEx Lead | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Service-quality system (W4368); OpEx (VS-133); CX (VS-13); store ops (VS-07); HR (VS-103); strategy (VS-33)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Symptom-fix risk**: addressing symptoms not root cause → recurrence; mitigated by root-cause analysis (VS-133)
- **Scale-miss risk**: successful fix not standardized → inconsistent; mitigated by standardization/scaling

### Time Estimate
- ~days–weeks per systemic analysis

### Automation Opportunity
- Pattern detection across findings; root-cause analytics; remediation-impact tracking; standardization workflow

### Controls
- CTL: systemic failures root-caused (VS-133); remediation addresses root cause; impact tracked & scaled

### Cross-references
- links to VS-133 (OpEx), VS-13 (CX), VS-07 (store), VS-124 (training), VS-19.3 (WFM), W4377 (corrective action), W4384 (improvement)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR/Service Quality link service-quality KPI (W4364) to recognition/incentive (service-excellence awards, variable-pay component VS-102.3) within fairness/ethics (W4365) | HR / Service Quality | CHRO / CFO | Periodic |

### Time Estimate
- Administered per cycle

### Automation Opportunity
- KPI-to-incentive auto-link; recognition workflow; ROI analytics; fairness/gaming monitoring

### Controls
- CTL: incentive linked to validated KPI (W4364/W4375); integrity-controlled (W4381); balanced recognition; dispute path (W4380)

## W4380. Service-Finding Dispute, Validation & Appeal

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Associate/unit disputes a finding |
| **Frequency** | Per dispute |
| **Volume** | Service-finding disputes (proportional to findings) |
| **Owner** | Service Quality / HR (VS-84) / Labor Relations (VS-84) |
| **Participants** | Service Quality, HR (VS-103), Labor Relations (VS-84), Store Ops (VS-07), CX (VS-13) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Associate/unit raise a dispute on a finding (factual error, context, fairness) via a defined channel within a window | Associate / Unit | Service Quality Lead / HR | Per dispute |
| 2 | Service Quality/HR re-validate the evidence (W4375), investigate context, and determine uphold/correct/withdraw with due process (W4365) | Service Quality / HR | Head of Service Quality / CHRO | Per dispute |
| 3 | Service Quality/HR communicate outcome, adjust records/scores if corrected, and ensure no retaliation for disputing | Service Quality / HR | Head of Service Quality / CHRO | Per dispute |

### System Touchpoints
- Service-quality system (W4368); HR (VS-103); labor relations (VS-84); CX (VS-13)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unfair-finding risk**: erroneous finding harms associate; mitigated by re-validation & due process
- **Retaliation risk**: associate penalized for disputing; mitigated by anti-retaliation (W4365/VS-119)

### Time Estimate
- ~hours–days per dispute

### Automation Opportunity
- Dispute-intake workflow; evidence re-validation; outcome-tracking; anti-retaliation monitoring

### Controls
- CTL: dispute channel available; evidence re-validated; due process (W4365); anti-retaliation enforced

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103 (HR), VS-84 (labor), VS-119 (ethics/anti-retaliation), W4365 (ethics), W4375 (validation), W4381 (integrity)

## W4381. Mystery-Shopper Integrity, Fraud & Collusion Control

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program operation; anomaly detected; audit cycle |
| **Frequency** | Continuous monitoring + periodic audit |
| **Volume** | All shopper/vendor activity |
| **Owner** | Service Quality / Audit (VS-21) / Risk (VS-21.2) / LP (VS-23) |
| **Participants** | Service Quality, Audit (VS-21), Risk (VS-21.2), LP (VS-23), Vendor (VS-34), Ethics (VS-119) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audit/Service Quality define integrity controls — randomized re-audit, evidence-spot-check, geo/time verification, shopper rotation, duplicate/gaming detection, vendor-integrity SLA | Audit / Service Quality / Risk | Audit Lead / Head of Service Quality | Annual |
| 2 | Audit/LP monitor for fraud/collusion (fabricated visits, leaked schedules, shopper-associate collusion, score-manipulation), investigate anomalies, and quarantine suspect data (W4375) | Audit / LP / Service Quality | Audit Lead / LP Lead | Continuous |
| 3 | Service Quality/Ethics enforce consequence on confirmed fraud/collusion (vendor penalty, shopper removal, associate action per due process W4380), and feed prevention | Service Quality / Ethics / Legal | Head of Service Quality / Ethics Lead | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Audit (VS-21); risk (VS-21.2); LP (VS-23); ethics (VS-119); vendor (VS-34); service-quality system (W4368)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fabrication risk**: fabricated shop reports → unreliable data & wrong decisions; mitigated by evidence-verification & re-audit
- **Collusion risk**: shopper-associate collusion → gamed scores; mitigated by rotation, no-notice, gaming detection

### Time Estimate
- Monitoring continuous; investigation per anomaly

### Automation Opportunity
- Geo/time verification; duplicate/fabrication detection; gaming-pattern analytics; integrity-scorecard

### Controls
- CTL: integrity controls (re-audit, evidence-verification, rotation); fraud/collusion investigated; suspect data quarantined; consequence enforced

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21 (audit), VS-21.2 (risk), VS-23 (LP), VS-119 (ethics), W4363 (methodology), W4375 (QC), W4380 (dispute)
