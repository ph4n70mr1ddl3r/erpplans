## W4369. Mystery-Shop Visit Scheduling, Scenario & Shopper Deployment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Coverage plan (W4361); visit cycle |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (randomized visits) |
| **Volume** | Visits = stores × frequency × scenarios |
| **Owner** | Service Quality / Vendor (Agency) |
| **Participants** | Service Quality, Vendor/Shoppers, CX (VS-13), Store Ops (VS-07), Privacy (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service Quality/Vendor generate the randomized visit schedule — store/time/associate coverage per methodology (W4363), scenario assignment, and shopper-profile match | Service Quality / Vendor | Head of Service Quality | Per cycle |
| 2 | Vendor deploy & brief shoppers on the scenario script, evidence-capture protocol (W4367), conduct code (W4365), and submit timeline | Vendor / Service Quality | Head of Service Quality | Per visit |
| 3 | Service Quality monitor coverage completion, manage no-shows/replacements, and ensure randomization/integrity (W4381) | Service Quality / Vendor | Head of Service Quality | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Service-quality system (W4368); vendor (VS-34); privacy (VS-91); CX (VS-13)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Predictability risk**: schedule detected → gamed performance; mitigated by randomized scheduling & shopper rotation
- **Shopper-mismatch risk**: shopper profile unrealistic → compromised validity; mitigated by profile-scenario match

### Automation Opportunity
- Randomized scheduling engine; scenario-shopper auto-match; coverage-completion dashboard; integrity monitoring

### Controls
- CTL-669 (ensure controlled execution — Mystery Shopping, Service Audit & Measurement Operations (PA-146.2).)
- CTL-669: visits randomized & rotated; shoppers profile-matched; coverage & integrity monitored (W4381)

### Time Estimate
- Scheduling automated; per-visit deployment minutes

### Cross-references
- links to VS-34 (vendor), VS-91 (privacy), VS-13 (CX), W4363 (methodology), W4367 (privacy), W4381 (integrity)

## W4370. In-Store Mystery Shopping Execution & Evidence Capture

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Scheduled in-store visit |
| **Frequency** | Per visit |
| **Volume** | In-store mystery-shop visits |
| **Owner** | Mystery Shopper (Vendor) / Service Quality |
| **Participants** | Shopper, Service Quality, Store Ops (VS-07) [unaware], CX (VS-13) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shopper execute the in-store scenario (greeting, inquiry, product-knowledge probe, consultative-selling test, checkout, return/complaint W4373), observing the rubric (W4362) | Shopper | Service Quality Lead | Per visit |
| 2 | Shopper capture evidence within lawful limits (W4367) — observed behaviors, timings (queue/wait), and permitted photo/audio per consent | Shopper | Service Quality Lead | Per visit |
| 3 | Shopper submit the completed shop report + evidence to the system (W4368) within timeline, for validation/scoring (W4375) | Shopper / Vendor | Service Quality Lead | Per visit |

### System Touchpoints
- Service-quality/evidence system (W4368); privacy (VS-91); CX (VS-13); store ops (VS-07)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Detection risk**: shopper recognized → gamed service; mitigated by shopper rotation & profile-realism
- **Evidence-unlawfulness risk**: evidence captured beyond consent → privacy violation; mitigated by lawful-limits protocol (W4367)

### Automation Opportunity
- Shopper reporting app with rubric; lawful-evidence capture; timed-behavior logging; auto-submit to validation

### Controls
- CTL-669 (ensure controlled execution — Mystery Shopping, Service Audit & Measurement Operations (PA-146.2).)
- CTL-669: scenario per rubric (W4362); evidence within lawful limits (W4367); report submitted timely

### Time Estimate
- ~30–60 min/visit + reporting

### Cross-references
- links to VS-91 (privacy), VS-13 (CX), W4362 (rubric), W4367 (privacy), W4375 (validation/scoring), W4381 (integrity)

## W4375. Service-Quality Data Validation, Scoring & Quality Control

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Shop/audit report submitted |
| **Frequency** | Per submission + batch QC |
| **Volume** | All shop/audit reports |
| **Owner** | Service Quality / Quality (VS-31) |
| **Participants** | Service Quality, Vendor (VS-34), Quality (VS-31), BI (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service Quality/Vendor validate each submitted report — completeness, evidence presence/quality, rubric-alignment, and plausibility (vs integrity W4381) | Service Quality / Vendor | Service Quality Lead | Per submission |
| 2 | Service Quality score the report vs the rubric (W4362/KPI W4364), apply weighting, and aggregate to touchpoint/store/region scores | Service Quality | Service Quality Lead | Per submission |
| 3 | Quality/BI run batch QC (inter-rater reliability, outlier detection, evidence-spot-check), flag anomalies, and quarantine suspect data (W4381) | Quality / BI | Quality Lead / Service Quality Lead | Per batch |

### System Touchpoints
- Service-quality system (W4368); quality (VS-31); BI (VS-28); vendor (VS-34)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scoring-inconsistency risk**: inconsistent scoring → unreliable comparison; mitigated by rubric & inter-rater reliability QC
- **Fabricated-data risk**: fabricated shop reports; mitigated by validation, evidence-spot-check, integrity controls (W4381)

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-validation (completeness/evidence); rubric-based scoring; inter-rater reliability analytics; outlier/fabrication detection

### Controls
- CTL-669 (ensure controlled execution — Mystery Shopping, Service Audit & Measurement Operations (PA-146.2).)
- CTL-669: reports validated & evidence-checked; scored per rubric; QC'd for consistency & integrity (W4381)

### Time Estimate
- Validation/scoring ~min/report; batch QC periodic

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21 (quality), VS-28 (BI), VS-34 (vendor), W4362 (rubric), W4364 (KPI), W4376 (triage), W4381 (integrity)

## W4376. Findings Triage, Severity Classification & Escalation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Validated finding (W4375) |
| **Frequency** | Per finding + batch |
| **Volume** | All service findings |
| **Owner** | Service Quality / CX (VS-13) / Store Ops (VS-07) |
| **Participants** | Service Quality, CX (VS-13), Store Ops (VS-07), HR (VS-103), Ethics (VS-119), Leadership |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service Quality triage each finding — severity (critical/major/minor), type (behavior, knowledge, process, recovery, compliance), and the responsible unit/store/associate | Service Quality | Service Quality Lead | Per finding |
| 2 | Service Quality escalate critical/major findings (safety, legal, ethics, repeat-critical) to the right owner (Store Ops/HR/Ethics/Leadership) per governance (W4365) | Service Quality | Head of Service Quality | Per finding |
| 3 | Service Quality route all findings to feedback/remediation (W4377) and track to closure, with dispute/appeal path (W4380) | Service Quality / CX / Store Ops | Head of Service Quality | Per finding |

### System Touchpoints
- Service-quality system (W4368); CX (VS-13); store ops (VS-07); HR (VS-103); ethics (VS-119)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Triage-error risk**: mis-classified severity → under/over-escalation; mitigated by severity rubric & review
- **No-closure risk**: findings not actioned/closed → repeat failure; mitigated by closure tracking

### Automation Opportunity
- Severity auto-classification; routing rules; critical-finding auto-escalation; closure-tracking dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-669 (ensure controlled execution — Mystery Shopping, Service Audit & Measurement Operations (PA-146.2).)
- CTL-669: findings tried by severity; critical escalated per governance (W4365); closure tracked; dispute path available (W4380)

### Time Estimate
- ~min/finding triage; escalation timely

### Cross-references
- links to VS-13 (CX), VS-07 (store), VS-103 (HR), VS-119 (ethics), W4365 (governance), W4377 (remediation), W4380 (dispute)
