## W4361. Service-Quality Assurance Strategy, Scope & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Service-quality strategy; competitive pressure; CX review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on strategy change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise service-quality assurance program across all customer touchpoints |
| **Owner** | Head of Service Quality / CX (VS-13) / Quality (VS-21) |
| **Participants** | CX (VS-13), Store Ops (VS-07), Ecommerce (VS-10), Trade (VS-11), Sales Enablement (VS-124), Strategy (VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service Quality/CX set the assurance strategy — scope (in-store, ecommerce/app, B2B/field, contact center), cadence, in-house vs agency shopper model, and the link to CX/operations/HR outcomes | Head of Service Quality / CX | COO / CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | Service Quality/Ops map the customer journeys & touchpoints to be assured, align to the service standards (W4362), and define coverage (all stores × frequency) | Service Quality / Store Ops / CX | Head of Service Quality | Annual |
| 3 | Service Quality document the strategy, obtain approval, and cascade to standards (W4362), methodology (W4363), KPI (W4364), and governance (W4365) | Head of Service Quality | COO / CHRO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- CX (VS-13); store ops (VS-07); ecommerce (VS-10); trade (VS-11); sales enablement (VS-124); strategy (VS-33)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scope-mismatch risk**: assuring only in-store while ecommerce/B2B drift; mitigated by full-touchpoint scope
- **Gaming risk**: predictable shop schedule → stores perform only on shop days; mitigated by randomized/variable cadence (W4363)

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual strategy

### Automation Opportunity
- Coverage-vs-touchpoint modeling; randomized-cadence scheduling; link to CX/HR outcomes analytics

### Controls
- CTL-668 (ensure controlled execution — Service Quality Assurance Strategy Standards and Design (PA-.)
- CTL: strategy approved (COO/CHRO); scope covers all touchpoints; cadence randomized (W4363)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-13 (CX), VS-07 (store), VS-10 (ecommerce), VS-11 (trade), VS-124 (enablement), W4362 (standards), W4363 (methodology)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

- **Adoption risk**: standards not communicated → associates unaware; mitigated by training & comms

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual standards/rubric

### Automation Opportunity
- Standards/rubric library; behavior-based scoring templates; standards pushed to associate training (VS-124)

### Controls
- CTL-668 (ensure controlled execution — Service Quality Assurance Strategy Standards and Design (PA-.)
- CTL: standards observable & rubric evidence-based; standards communicated & trained; reviewed on change

## W4363. Mystery Shopping & Service-Audit Program Design & Methodology

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4361); methodology review |
| **Frequency** | Annual design + continuous operation |
| **Volume** | One mystery-shop/service-audit methodology |
| **Owner** | Service Quality / Quality (VS-21) |
| **Participants** | Service Quality, CX (VS-13), Vendor (VS-34), Store Ops (VS-07), Risk (VS-21.2), Privacy (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service Quality design the methodology — scenario types (purchase, return, complaint, inquiry, B2B quote, omnichannel), shopper profile (segment-realistic), randomized store/time/associate coverage, and frequency | Service Quality | Head of Service Quality | Annual |
| 2 | Service Quality/Vendor build the scenario scripts & evidence-capture protocol (what to observe, evidence type, lawfulness per W4367), and the data-validation/quality-control steps | Service Quality / Vendor | Head of Service Quality | Annual |
| 3 | Service Quality/Risk define anti-gaming/anti-collusion controls (variable timing, shopper rotation, no-notice, evidence verification W4381) and pilot before rollout | Service Quality / Risk | Head of Service Quality / CRO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Service-quality system (W4368); vendor (VS-34); risk (VS-21.2); privacy (VS-91); CX (VS-13)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Gaming/collusion risk**: predictable methodology → gamed results; mitigated by randomization, rotation, evidence verification (W4381)
- **Validity risk**: weak scenario/evidence → unreliable measurement; mitigated by validated scripts & QC

### Time Estimate
- ~3 weeks annual design + pilot

### Automation Opportunity
- Randomized scenario/store/time assignment; shopper rotation; scenario-script library; evidence-capture app

### Controls
- CTL-163 (ensure service-quality audit independence.)
- CTL: methodology randomized & evidence-based; anti-collusion controls (W4381); piloted before rollout

### Cross-references
- links to VS-34 (vendor), VS-21.2 (risk), VS-91 (privacy), VS-13 (CX), W4362 (standards), W4381 (integrity)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Time Estimate

### Controls
- CTL-163 (ensure service-quality audit independence.)
