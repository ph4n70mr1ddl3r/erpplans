# VS-146: Customer Mystery Shopping & Service Quality Assurance Program

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Customer Mystery Shopping & Service Quality Assurance workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
owning the **independent service-quality assurance discipline** that measures, verifies, and
improves the service delivered across BuildRight's customer touchpoints: 200 stores running 2.8M
monthly POS transactions, ecommerce/app/digital channels (~42,900 orders/month), B2B/trade/field-
sales (~5,000 trade + 200 corporate accounts), and the contact center. Service quality (greeting,
product knowledge, consultative selling, queue/wait, cleanliness, recovery, omnichannel
consistency) is a primary controllable lever on conversion, basket, loyalty, repeat-purchase, and
the "Your Home Building Partner" positioning — yet it is only credible as an **independent,
objective, evidence-based assurance**, not as a self-reported store metric.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. "Mystery shopping", "service audit", "service
quality assurance", and "service standards compliance" appear in **zero** PA files as dedicated
workflow headers (only ~13 incidental mentions). The capability is sprinkled as single steps
inside VS-13 (Customer Experience — owns the *reactive* complaint/VOC), VS-07 (Store Operations —
owns the *self-reported* standards walk), VS-21 (Internal Audit — *financial/control* assurance),
VS-133 (OpEx — *process* improvement), and VS-124 (Sales Enablement — *selling-skills* training).
No value stream owns the **independent service-quality assurance program**: the service-standards
framework & scoring rubric, the mystery-shop & service-audit methodology, the third-party shopper
deployment across in-store/omnichannel/B2B/contact-center journeys, evidence capture & scoring,
findings triage/remediation, recognition/incentive linkage, shopper-integrity/fraud control, and
service-quality analytics/reporting to leadership.

This is distinct from **VS-13 (Customer Experience & Loyalty)** which owns the *reactive* customer
experience (complaints, VOC, NPS, loyalty) — this value stream owns the *proactive, independent,
audited* measurement of service delivery. It is distinct from **VS-07 (Store Operations)** which
owns *self-reported* standards execution — this value stream owns the *independent verification* of
those standards. It is distinct from **VS-21 (Internal Audit)** which provides *financial/control*
assurance — this value stream provides *service-quality* assurance. It is distinct from **VS-133
(Operational Excellence)** which drives *process* improvement — this value stream drives
*service-behavior* improvement. It is distinct from **VS-124 (Sales Enablement)** which builds
*selling skills* — this value stream *measures* whether those skills are deployed. It is distinct
from **VS-23 (Loss Prevention)** exception monitoring which targets *loss* — this value stream
targets *service-quality* outcomes.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: at 2.8M monthly transactions across 200 stores and a service-
differentiated positioning in a competitive PH retail market, un-measured service quality drifts —
a single under-performing store/associate erodes conversion and repeat-purchase invisibly, and the
absence of independent assurance means management relies on self-reported or complaint-driven
signals (which under-represent the silent majority of dissatisfied-but-non-complaining customers).
The program also carries its own integrity risk (shopper collusion, fabricated visits, gaming) that
must be controlled, and its own compliance dimension (RA 10173 consent/privacy on shopper
recordings, fairness to associates).
