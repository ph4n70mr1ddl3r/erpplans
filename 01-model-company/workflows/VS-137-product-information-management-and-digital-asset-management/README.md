# VS-137: Product Information Management (PIM) & Digital Asset Management (DAM)

> **Technology & Data** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Product Information Management (PIM) & Digital Asset Management (DAM) workflows for
BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the enterprise **product-content discipline** that creates,
governs, and syndicates authoritative, channel-ready rich product data and digital assets for a
**35,000-active-SKU** (≈55,000 in the master) hardware/home-improvement assortment sold across
**six consumer-facing channels** (in-store POS/lookup, web, mobile app, marketplace presence on
Lazada/Shopee, retail-media, and the monthly print flyer) and one **B2B/trade** channel. At
BuildRight's scale, product content is a direct revenue lever: complete, accurate, spec-rich
content drives ecommerce conversion, marketplace win-rate, associate consultative selling, and the
"D.I.Y. how-to partner" positioning — while missing or wrong content (an un-declared SDS, a wrong
load rating on a ladder, a stale SRP) causes lost sales, returns, and regulatory exposure under
the Consumer Act (RA 7394) and DTI-BPS labeling rules.

Today this discipline is **entirely unowned as a program**: the defining terms 'product
information management' / 'PIM' and 'digital asset management' / 'DAM' appear scattered across
~30 PA files (most heavily VS-01 assortment, VS-10 ecommerce, VS-09 in-store services, VS-48
retail media, VS-29 master data) with **zero dedicated workflow headers** — every channel
*maintains its own copy* of product data, asset files live on shared drives and vendor emails, and
no value stream owns the **product-content operating model**: the canonical product model and
attribute taxonomy, the onboarding of new-product data from merchandising/vendors, the digital-asset
lifecycle (images/renders/360°/video/spec sheets/SDS/certificates), rich-content authoring
(how-to guides, comparison tables), translation/multilingual content, channel-specific
publication rules, content quality/completeness scoring, vendor content SLAs, and content
performance analytics.

This is distinct from **VS-29 (Master Data Management)** which governs the *transactional* masters
(item core, vendor, customer, price) that drive ERP posting — this value stream owns the *rich,
channel-facing content layer* (marketing copy, specs, assets, how-to) that surrounds those masters.
It is distinct from **VS-10 (Ecommerce)** which *consumes* content to render the storefront — this
value stream owns the *authoring and syndication* of that content to all channels. It is distinct
from **VS-48 (Retail Media)** which *monetizes* the storefront audience — this value stream owns
the *product content* that retail-media ads link to. It is distinct from **VS-01.1 (Assortment/
Product Lifecycle)** which decides *which* SKUs to range — this value stream owns *how those SKUs
are described and shown* across every touchpoint. PIM/DAM is the *product-content* discipline —
distinct from transactional master data, channel storefronts, media monetization, and ranging.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: ~44% DTI-BPS-regulated assortment requiring standards/ICC
disclosure, ~8–10% dangerous-goods assortment (paint/solvents ~2,800 SKUs, adhesives, aerosols,
garden/agro chemicals) requiring GHS-labeled Safety Data Sheets (SDS), catch-weight/technical
items (lumber board-foot, wire per meter, tiles per sq-m) requiring precise spec data, and a
multi-channel growth ambition (ecommerce 2.9%→rising, marketplace expansion, retail-media launch)
that makes consistent, high-quality product content the gating dependency.
