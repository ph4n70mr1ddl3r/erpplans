## W4153. Digital Asset Repository, Taxonomy & Metadata

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DAM strategy refresh; asset growth; architecture review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on major change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise DAM (35,000 SKUs × multiple assets = hundreds of thousands of images/specs/SDS/docs) |
| **Owner** | DAM Manager / Enterprise Architecture (VS-113) |
| **Participants** | Marketing (VS-14), Ecommerce (VS-10), IT (VS-27), Vendor (asset source) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DAM/EA define the digital-asset repository architecture — asset classes (image/360°/video/PDF spec sheet/SDS/certificate/3D), taxonomy, metadata schema (SKU, channel, usage-rights, locale, version), and storage/CDN strategy | DAM Manager / EA | Director Ecommerce / CIO | Annual |
| 2 | DAM/Marketing establish the asset-to-product linkage model (each asset bound to SKU + channel + role), naming/numbering conventions, and search/retrieval access for all consuming teams | DAM Manager / Marketing | DAM Manager | Annual |
| 3 | DAM/IT govern the repository (backup, retention, access control per VS-27.3), integrate with PIM (W4145) and consuming channels, and document via EA (VS-113) | DAM Manager / IT | CIO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- DAM platform; CDN/storage (VS-27); PIM link (W4145); access control (VS-27.3); EA repository (VS-113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lost-asset risk**: Assets scattered on shared drives/emails, unfindable; mitigated by central DAM with bound metadata
- **Orphan-asset risk**: Assets with no SKU linkage (unusable); mitigated by mandatory metadata on ingest

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 weeks setup; ongoing repository administration ~0.5 FTE

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-tagging/metadata extraction (AI, governed VS-128); duplicate/variant detection

### Controls
- CTL-642 (ensure controlled execution — Digital Asset Management, Rich Content & SDS/Certificate Lifecycle (PA-137.2).)
- CTL: DAM access-controlled & backed up (VS-27.3); metadata mandatory on ingest

### Cross-references
- links to VS-113 (EA), VS-27 (IT/storage/CDN), VS-27.3 (security), VS-128 (AI tagging), VS-14 (marketing assets)

## W4154. Product Image & Media Production Lifecycle

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New SKU; rebrand; channel requirement (marketplace image spec); refresh cycle |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (~150–230 new SKUs/mo + refresh backlog) |
| **Volume** | Multiple images/SKU (main, alternate, lifestyle, in-context); ~35,000 SKUs |
| **Owner** | DAM Manager / Studio Lead |
| **Participants** | Studio/photography, Marketing (VS-14), Vendor (supplied assets), Ecommerce (VS-10) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DAM/Studio define the image standard per category (count, angles, white-background vs lifestyle, resolution, marketplace/Lazada-Shopee specs links to VS-65) and the production plan prioritizing A/B items | Studio Lead / Marketing | DAM Manager | Per category |
| 2 | DAM/Studio shoot/produce or ingest vendor-supplied media, post-process (crop/retouch/color-manage), and ingest into DAM with bound metadata (W4153) | Studio Lead / DAM | DAM Manager | Per SKU |
| 3 | DAM/Marketing review/approve (brand standards, accuracy), version (W4158), and publish to channels (W4159); re-shoot on quality-fail | DAM Manager / Marketing | DAM Manager | Per SKU |

### System Touchpoints
- DAM ingest/processing; studio tools; PIM link; marketplace specs (VS-65); retail-media (VS-48)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inconsistency risk**: Mixed styles/quality across the catalog; mitigated by per-category standard and approval gate
- **Vendor-asset-quality risk**: Low-quality supplied images; mitigated by standard enforcement and re-shoot budget

### Time Estimate
- ~0.5–1.5 hrs/SKU (shoot+process) × prioritized backlog; ~2–3 FTE studio at scale

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto background-removal/retouch (AI, governed VS-128); marketplace-spec auto-formatting; bulk ingest

### Controls
- CTL-642 (ensure controlled execution — Digital Asset Management, Rich Content & SDS/Certificate Lifecycle (PA-137.2).)
- CTL: brand-approval gate before publish; marketplace-spec compliance check

### Cross-references

## W4155. Safety Data Sheet (SDS) & Regulatory Certificate Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DG/regulated SKU onboarding; SDS revision from vendor; regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (onboarding + periodic refresh) |
| **Volume** | ~8–10% DG assortment (paint/solvents ~2,800 SKUs + adhesives/aerosols/agro/cleaning); ~44% DTI-BPS-regulated requiring PS/ICC cert |
| **Owner** | DAM Manager / Regulatory Compliance (VS-22/VS-114/VS-117) |
| **Participants** | Compliance (VS-22/VS-114/VS-117), Quality (VS-31), Vendor (SDS source), Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DAM/Compliance capture the SDS and regulatory certificates (PS/ICC links to VS-117, GHS labels links to VS-114) for each DG/regulated SKU, bound to the product record with version/expiry metadata | DAM Manager / Compliance | Compliance Lead | Per SKU |
| 2 | DAM/Compliance validate SDS accuracy/completeness (GHS hazard phrases, Philippine localization W4150), publish to the PDP (downloadable SDS) and to internal/associate access, and ensure channel-presence (marketplace/legal) | DAM Manager / Compliance | Compliance Lead | Per SKU |
| 3 | DAM/Compliance monitor SDS/cert expiry and vendor revisions, trigger refresh, maintain the audit trail (links to VS-88 records, VS-114 W3593 DG inventory), and support recall (VS-89) | DAM Manager / Compliance / Quality | Compliance Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- DAM doc repository; PDP SDS download; DG inventory (VS-114 W3593); DTI-BPS cert (VS-117); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missing-SDS risk**: DG sold without accessible SDS → regulatory/liability exposure; mitigated by publication gate for DG
- **Stale-SDS risk**: Outdated SDS (formula change); mitigated by expiry tracking and vendor-revision monitoring

### Time Estimate
- ~0.5–1 hr/regulated SKU onboarding + refresh monitoring ~0.3 FTE

### Automation Opportunity
- SDS expiry auto-alerts; vendor-revision auto-ingest; GHS-phrase localization (governed VS-128)

### Controls
- CTL-149 (ensure product content accuracy & rights compliance.)
- CTL: publication gate — no DG publish without current SDS (links to VS-114/VS-117); audit trail retained (VS-88)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-114 (DG/GHS), VS-117 (DTI-BPS/PS-ICC), VS-22 (compliance), VS-31 (quality), VS-89 (recall), VS-88 (records), VS-100.3 (legal)

## W4159. Channel Content Publishing & Distribution Rules

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Content ready-to-publish (gate passed W4147); channel change |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (~150–230 new SKUs/mo + updates) |
| **Volume** | 6 channels (POS/store, web, app, marketplace, retail-media, print) + B2B |
| **Owner** | PIM Content Lead / Ecommerce Operations (VS-10) |
| **Participants** | Ecommerce (VS-10), Marketplace (VS-65), Retail Media (VS-48), Marketing/Print (VS-14), IT (VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PIM/Ecommerce define channel-specific publication rules — which content/asset fields each channel consumes, transforms (image spec W4154, copy length W4149), and the publication SLA per channel | PIM Content Lead / Ecommerce | Director Ecommerce | Per channel |
| 2 | PIM/Ecommerce publish approved content to channels via the syndication pipeline (web/app PDP push links to VS-10, marketplace feed links to VS-65, retail-media links to VS-48, print catalog extract links to VS-14) after the gate (W4147) | PIM Content Lead / Ecommerce Ops | Director Ecommerce | Per SKU/change |
| 3 | PIM/Ecommerce handle channel errors/rejections (marketplace feed fail, spec mismatch), re-publish, and verify propagation/availability per channel (W4160) | PIM Content Lead / Ecommerce Ops | Director Ecommerce | Per exception |

### System Touchpoints
- PIM syndication pipeline; ecommerce PDP (VS-10); marketplace feed (VS-65); retail-media (VS-48); print/catalog (VS-14 W1270); integration bus (VS-27/VS-113.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Channel-drift risk**: One channel out of sync (stale price/spec); mitigated by centralized syndication + propagation verification
- **Feed-failure risk**: Marketplace/feed silently fails; mitigated by error monitoring and re-publish

### Time Estimate
- Automated pipeline; ~0.5 FTE ops + exception handling

### Automation Opportunity
- Event-driven auto-publish on gate-pass; per-channel transform rules; error auto-retry/alert

### Controls
- CTL-642 (ensure controlled execution — Digital Asset Management, Rich Content & SDS/Certificate Lifecycle (PA-137.2).)
- CTL: publication only after completeness gate (W4147); propagation verified per channel

### Cross-references

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Continuous (pre-publish) + periodic audit |
| **Volume** | All published SKUs × channels |
| **Owner** | PIM Content Lead / QA |
| **Participants** | Ecommerce (VS-10), Marketplace (VS-65), Marketing (VS-14), Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PIM/QA run pre-publish QA — completeness gate (W4147), rendering check on web/storefront/app/marketplace, image-load, spec-display, and mobile-responsiveness | PIM QA / Ecommerce | PIM Content Lead | Per publish |

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min/SKU pre-publish (batch-automated); periodic audit ~1 FTE

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated rendering/screenshot QA across channels; broken-link/image auto-scan; defect auto-routing
