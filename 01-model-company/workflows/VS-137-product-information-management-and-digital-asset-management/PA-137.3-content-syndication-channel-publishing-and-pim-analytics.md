## W4161. Marketplace & Third-Party Channel Content Syndication

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Marketplace presence (VS-65); channel onboarding; catalog change |
| **Frequency** | Continuous feed |
| **Volume** | Lazada/Shopee and other marketplace catalogs (subset/full of 35,000 SKUs) |
| **Owner** | PIM Content Lead / Ecommerce Marketplace (VS-65) |
| **Participants** | Marketplace ops (VS-65), Ecommerce (VS-10), IT (VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PIM/Ecommerce map BuildRight's canonical content to each marketplace's required taxonomy/attributes (Lazada/Shopee categories differ), and configure the feed transform rules per channel | PIM Content Lead / Marketplace Ops | Director Ecommerce | Per channel |
| 2 | PIM/Ecommerce run the syndication feed (product/price/availability/asset), monitor marketplace acceptance/rejections, and remediate category-mapping errors | PIM Content Lead / Ecommerce Ops | Director Ecommerce | Continuous |
| 3 | PIM/Ecommerce handle marketplace-specific content (promotional copy, bundle listings, sponsored-content assets links to VS-48 retail media), and measure channel performance (W4163) | PIM Content Lead / Marketplace Ops | Director Ecommerce | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Marketplace feed/API (VS-65); PIM syndication (W4159); retail-media (VS-48); integration bus (VS-27/VS-113.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mapping-error risk**: Wrong category mapping → listing rejected/hidden; mitigated by mapping governance + acceptance monitoring
- **Channel-conflict risk**: Inconsistent content across channels; mitigated by canonical-source syndication

### Time Estimate
- Automated feed; ~0.5–1 FTE marketplace ops + exception handling

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto category-mapping suggestions; feed-error auto-classification; bulk listing templates

### Controls
- CTL: marketplace content sourced from canonical PIM (no manual channel-side edits); feed-error SLA

### Cross-references
- links to VS-65 (marketplace), VS-48 (retail media), VS-10 (ecommerce), W4159 (publishing), VS-113.2 (integration)

## W4162. Price, Promotion & Availability Content Sync

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Price change (VS-57/VS-16); promotion (VS-58); availability change (VS-05) |
| **Frequency** | Real-time / near-real-time |
| **Volume** | 35,000 SKUs × 6 channels; ~2.8M monthly POS + ~42,900 ecommerce orders/mo |
| **Owner** | PIM Content Lead / Ecommerce Operations (VS-10) / Pricing (VS-57) |
| **Participants** | Pricing (VS-57), Ecommerce (VS-10), Promotion (VS-58), Inventory (VS-05) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PIM/Ecommerce/Pricing sync price content (SRP, trade price, promo price) from the authoritative pricing source (VS-57/VS-16) to all channels in real-time, with display-price computation (VAT-inclusive, mandatory-discount links to VS-85) | PIM Content Lead / Pricing / Ecommerce Ops | Director Ecommerce | Real-time |
| 2 | PIM/Ecommerce sync promotion content (badges, callouts, validity links to VS-58) and availability (per-store real-time stock links to VS-10 §8.4) consistently across channels | PIM Content Lead / Ecommerce Ops | Director Ecommerce | Real-time |
| 3 | PIM/Ecommerce monitor sync latency/errors, reconcile price/availability discrepancies (links to VS-118 revenue-assurance price integrity), and escalate stale data | PIM Content Lead / Ecommerce Ops | Director Ecommerce | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing source (VS-57/VS-16); promotion engine (VS-58); inventory availability (VS-05/VS-10 §8.4); PIM syndication (W4159); revenue assurance (VS-118)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stale-price risk**: Channel shows wrong price → customer dispute/revenue leakage; mitigated by real-time sync + revenue-assurance monitoring (VS-118)
- **Availability-mismatch risk**: Online shows stock when out → failed fulfillment; mitigated by real-time per-store availability

### Time Estimate
- Automated real-time pipeline; ~0.3 FTE monitoring + exception

### Automation Opportunity
- Event-driven real-time price/promo/availability push; discrepancy auto-detection & alert

### Controls
- CTL: single authoritative pricing source; real-time sync with discrepancy monitoring (VS-118)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-57 (pricing), VS-16 (price types), VS-58 (promotions), VS-05 (inventory), VS-85 (mandatory discount), VS-118 (revenue assurance), VS-10 (availability §8.4)

## W4163. Product Content Performance & SEO Analytics

| **Frequency** | Weekly/monthly |

| **Participants** | SEO (VS-10), Marketing (VS-14), Ecommerce (VS-10), Insights (VS-44) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PIM/Ecommerce/SEO measure content performance — PDP views, search ranking, click-through, bounce, time-on-page, search-term gaps (zero-result queries) — per SKU/category/channel | PIM Content Lead / SEO / Analytics | Director Ecommerce | Weekly |
| 2 | PIM/SEO identify content gaps (low-completeness SKUs, missing keywords, poor images) and prioritize enrichment (links to W4149/W4156) by revenue/traffic impact | PIM Content Lead / SEO | Director Ecommerce | Monthly |
| 3 | PIM/Marketing report content-performance KPIs, A/B test content variants, and feed learnings into category/content strategy (VS-01/VS-14) | PIM Content Lead / Marketing | Director Ecommerce | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- SEO/web analytics (VS-10); BI/analytics (VS-28); insights (VS-44); PIM completeness (W4147)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Blind-spot risk**: Low-performing content not identified → lost revenue; mitigated by per-SKU performance analytics
- **Vanity-metric risk**: Optimizing views not conversion; mitigated by tying content KPIs to conversion/attach (W4164)

### Time Estimate
- Weekly/monthly; ~1 FTE analytics

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto content-gap detection from search analytics; A/B-test automation; SEO-scoring per SKU

### Controls
- CTL: content KPIs tied to revenue/conversion (not vanity metrics); A/B-test governance

### Cross-references
- links to VS-10 (SEO/ecommerce analytics), VS-28 (BI), VS-44 (insights), VS-14 (marketing), W4149/W4156 (enrichment)
