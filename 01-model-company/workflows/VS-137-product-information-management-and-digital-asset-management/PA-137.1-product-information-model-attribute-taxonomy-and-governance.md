## W4145. Product Information Model & Canonical Catalog Architecture

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | PIM strategy refresh; channel expansion; architecture review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on major change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise catalog model (35,000 active SKUs; ~55,000 in master) |
| **Owner** | PIM Product Owner / Enterprise Architecture (VS-113) |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01), Ecommerce (VS-10), Master Data (VS-29), Marketing (VS-14), IT (VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PIM/EA define the canonical product information model: the single source-of-truth product record (core identity, classification, specs, marketing, commerce, compliance, relationships) consumed by all channels, and its relationship to the ERP item master (VS-29 MDM-021) | PIM Owner / EA | Director Ecommerce / CIO | Annual |
| 2 | PIM/EA design the catalog architecture (category hierarchy, family/variant model, channel-specific views, fallback/inheritance rules) and the publication pipeline to each consuming channel | PIM Owner / Ecommerce | Director Ecommerce | Annual |
| 3 | PIM/EA document the model, obtain architecture-board approval (VS-113), and sequence rollout with OCM (VS-134) and the data-engineering team (VS-28.2) | PIM Owner / EA / IT | CIO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- PIM platform record model & category service; ERP item master link (VS-29 MDM-021); EA repository (VS-113); integration bus (VS-27/VS-113.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fragmentation risk**: Each channel keeps its own copy → drift, conflicts; mitigated by canonical-model single-source-of-truth design
- **Over-modeling risk**: Schema too complex to maintain at 35K-SKU scale; mitigated by category-template inheritance and fallback rules

### Time Estimate
- 4–6 weeks annual; ongoing model maintenance ~0.2 FTE

### Automation Opportunity
- Schema-driven catalog generation from category templates; model-drift detection alerts

### Controls
- CTL-641 (ensure controlled execution — Product Information Model, Attribute Taxonomy & Governance (PA-137.1).)
- CTL: canonical model approved by Architecture Board (VS-113); change-controlled schema versioning

### Cross-references
- links to VS-29 (MDM), VS-113 (EA), VS-10 (ecommerce), VS-01 (merchandising), VS-28.2 (data eng), VS-134 (OCM)

## W4146. Attribute Taxonomy, Spec Schema & Category Modeling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Category review; new assortment; channel need (e.g., marketplace spec) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + per category |
| **Volume** | ~12 categories × tens of attributes each (e.g., tiles: material/size/thickness/PEI/water-absorption) |
| **Owner** | PIM Content Lead / Category Management (VS-01) |
| **Participants** | Category managers (VS-01), Ecommerce (VS-10), Trade specialists |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PIM/Category define the attribute set per category — distinguishing (search/driving, e.g., power-tool wattage) vs supporting (display) attributes, units, allowed values, and mandatory-for-channel flags | PIM Content Lead / Category Mgr | PIM Owner | Per category |
| 2 | PIM/Category model category-specific spec schemas and facets (filter taxonomy) aligned to how customers/shopper search (links to VS-10 search, VS-44 insights W2831) and to DTI-BPS regulated attributes (VS-117) | PIM Content Lead / Ecommerce | PIM Owner | Per category |
| 3 | PIM publish the category template to the PIM platform, train merchandising/enrichment teams, and govern attribute change requests | PIM Content Lead | PIM Owner | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- PIM category/attribute model; ecommerce facet/search config (VS-10); market-research insights (VS-44); DTI-BPS attributes (VS-117)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inconsistency risk**: Same spec named differently across categories (e.g., "wattage" vs "power"); mitigated by a controlled attribute dictionary
- **Missing-facet risk**: Key shopper filters absent → lost conversion; mitigated by insights-driven facet design

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days/category × ~12 categories = ~3 weeks/yr; ongoing

### Automation Opportunity
- Attribute-dictionary reuse across categories; facet-gap detection from search analytics

### Controls
- CTL-641 (ensure controlled execution — Product Information Model, Attribute Taxonomy & Governance (PA-137.1).)
- CTL: attribute dictionary change-controlled; category templates reviewed by Category Mgr

### Cross-references
- links to VS-01 (category mgmt), VS-10 (search/facets), VS-44 (insights), VS-117 (DTI-BPS specs)

## W4147. Data Governance, Ownership & Completeness Standards

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program setup; quality review; audit |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly governance + continuous monitoring |
| **Volume** | 35,000 SKUs × completeness scorecard |
| **Owner** | PIM Product Owner / Data Governance (VS-28.2) |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01), Ecommerce (VS-10), Legal/Compliance (VS-22/VS-91), Quality (VS-31) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PIM/Data Governance assign content ownership (RACI per attribute family — e.g., specs to merchandising, marketing copy to marketing, assets to DAM team, compliance fields to legal/quality) and publish data-steward roles | PIM Owner / Data Gov | Director Ecommerce | Quarterly |
| 2 | PIM define completeness/quality scoring per SKU and per channel (mandatory-field coverage, image-count threshold, spec completeness, SDS presence for DG), and a channel-publication gate (no publish below threshold) | PIM Owner / Ecommerce | Director Ecommerce | Quarterly |
| 3 | PIM report content-quality KPIs to governance (links to VS-28.2 data quality W2392, VS-133 OpEx), remediate with owners, and audit a sample for accuracy | PIM Owner / Audit (VS-21) | Director Ecommerce | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- PIM completeness scoring; data-quality framework (VS-28.2 W2392); audit (VS-21); OpEx (VS-133)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Orphan-data risk**: No owner for an attribute → never maintained; mitigated by RACI per attribute family
- **Incomplete-publish risk**: Gaps reach customers (no image, no spec); mitigated by publication gates

### Time Estimate
- ~0.5 FTE governance; quarterly reviews

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto completeness scoring; publication-gate enforcement; owner-assignment alerts

### Controls
- CTL-149 (ensure product content accuracy & rights compliance.)
- CTL: content RACI documented; channel-publication gate enforced (no publish < threshold); sample audit (VS-21)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-28.2 (data quality), VS-21 (audit), VS-133 (OpEx), VS-31 (quality), VS-91 (privacy/consent)

## W4148. New-Product Content Onboarding & Item Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New SKU ranged (NPI/assortment) links to VS-01.1; new vendor item |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (~5–8% SKU growth/yr = ~1,750–2,800 new active SKUs/yr) |
| **Volume** | ~150–230 new SKUs/month onboarding to content |
| **Owner** | PIM Content Lead / Merchandising Onboarding (VS-01.1) |
| **Participants** | Buyers (VS-01), Vendor (content source), DAM team, Ecommerce (VS-10), Compliance (VS-22/VS-117) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On NPI/range decision (VS-01.1), PIM create the product record skeleton from the category template (W4146), capture core identity/classification, and trigger the content-intake tasks to owners | PIM Content Lead / Buyer | PIM Owner | Per SKU, ~15 min |
| 2 | PIM/Buyer/Vendor capture specs, marketing copy, images, and compliance fields (SDS for DG, PS/ICC cert for DTI-BPS-regulated links to VS-117), validating against completeness standards (W4147) | PIM Content Lead / Vendor / Compliance | Category Mgr | Per SKU, ~1–3 hrs |
| 3 | PIM pass the completeness gate (W4147), publish to channels (W4159), and verify rendering on web/storefront before go-live | PIM Content Lead / Ecommerce | PIM Owner | Per SKU, ~15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- PIM onboarding workflow; supplier portal (W4152); item master (VS-29); ecommerce storefront (VS-10); DTI-BPS/SDS repos (VS-117/VS-114)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Launch-block risk**: SKU ranged but content incomplete → cannot sell online; mitigated by NPI→content SLA and gate
- **Garbage-in risk**: Vendor data unstructured/inaccurate; mitigated by structured intake and validation

### Time Estimate
- ~1.5–3.5 hrs/SKU × ~150–230 SKUs/mo ≈ ~1.5 FTE

### Automation Opportunity
- Vendor-content auto-ingest via portal templates; completeness-gate auto-trigger of tasks; pre-publish rendering check

### Controls
- CTL-641 (ensure controlled execution — Product Information Model, Attribute Taxonomy & Governance (PA-137.1).)
- CTL: completeness gate before publish; DG/DTI-BPS compliance fields validated (VS-114/VS-117)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-01.1 (NPI/assortment), VS-29 (item master), VS-117 (DTI-BPS), VS-114 (DG/SDS), VS-10 (storefront)

| **Frequency** | Continuous |

| **Owner** | PIM Content Lead / Content Marketing (VS-14) |
| **Participants** | Copywriters, Trade specialists, SEO (VS-10), Ecommerce (VS-10) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PIM/Content author/enrich marketing content — short/long description, how-to copy, selling points, SEO keywords — distinct from spec data and tuned per channel (web SEO vs marketplace vs print brevity) | Content Writer / SEO | PIM Content Lead | Per SKU |
| 2 | PIM/Trade specialist add consultative content — use-cases, project guides, "what you'll need" bundles (links to VS-124 selling, VS-09.1 how-to W1136) — for high-consideration categories | Trade Specialist | PIM Content Lead | Per SKU |

### Time Estimate
- ~0.5–2 hrs/SKU for enriched A/B items; prioritized backlog

### Automation Opportunity
- GenAI draft copy from specs (governed per VS-128); template-based how-to generation; translation auto-draft

### Controls
- CTL-641 (ensure controlled execution — Product Information Model, Attribute Taxonomy & Governance (PA-137.1).)
- CTL: legal/brand approval for claims; GenAI content governed under VS-128 with human review

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Channel need; import product with foreign-language source data |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + on import onboarding |
| **Volume** | ~40% imported SKUs (foreign-language source); Filipino/English localization |
| **Owner** | PIM Content Lead / Localization Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Content team, Sourcing (VS-122), Ecommerce (VS-10) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PIM/Localization localize imported-product content (source in Chinese/English/other) to the BuildRight standard (Filipino/English as applicable), including marketing copy, specs, and safety warnings | Localization Coord / Content | PIM Content Lead | Per SKU |
| 2 | PIM/Localization standardize units of measure and catch-weight presentation (board-foot for lumber, per-meter for wire, per-sq-m for tiles — links to VS-115 metrology) consistently across channels | Localization Coord / Merchandising | PIM Content Lead | Per category |
| 3 | PIM/Localization localize safety/DG warnings and GHS phrases for the Philippine market (links to VS-114 DG, VS-117 DTI-BPS labels), validate with compliance, and publish | Localization Coord / Compliance | PIM Owner | Per SKU |

### System Touchpoints
- PIM localization/translation module; DAM localized assets (W4153); DG/GHS label data (VS-114); weights/measures (VS-115)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-translation risk**: Wrong unit/spec/phrase (esp. safety); mitigated by bilingual review and compliance sign-off on DG
- **Inconsistent-UoM risk**: Catch-weight shown differently across channels; mitigated by UoM standard (links to VS-115)

### Time Estimate
- ~0.5–1 hr/SKU for imported items; ~40% of ~1,750–2,800 new SKUs/yr

### Automation Opportunity
- Machine-translation pre-draft with human post-edit (governed VS-128); UoM normalization rules

### Controls
- CTL-641 (ensure controlled execution — Product Information Model, Attribute Taxonomy & Governance (PA-137.1).)
- CTL: DG/safety phrases compliance-validated (VS-114); bilingual review on safety-critical content

### Cross-references

### Time Estimate
- ~0.5 hr/review × hundreds of submissions/mo ≈ ~0.5 FTE

### Automation Opportunity
- Portal template auto-validation; exception auto-routing; scorecard auto-feed

### Controls
- CTL-149 (ensure product content accuracy & rights compliance.)
