## W5041. Customer Storage Inquiry, Reservation & Unit Assignment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer inquiry (online/phone/store); lead generation |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; demand-driven |
| **Volume** | ~3,000–6,000 inquiries/month at maturity × conversion ~25–40% |
| **Owner** | Property Manager (Storage) / Store Services Lead |
| **Participants** | Customer Service (VS-13), Marketing (VS-14), CRM/Loyalty (VS-13), Property Manager, Yield Mgmt (W5039) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture the inquiry & assess need: storage size estimate (by room count / item list), term, site preference, climate/vehicle/container need, and capture the lead in CRM (VS-13) | Customer Service / Property Mgr | Property Manager | Per inquiry |
| 2 | Reserve the unit & confirm availability: check live availability (vs W5039 occupancy), hold the unit, quote the street rate + fees (W5035), and collect the reservation deposit | Property Mgr / O2C | Property Manager | Per inquiry |
| 3 | Convert to lease handoff: send the quote/lease-preview, schedule move-in (W5042), and feed conversion analytics (W5054) and retention (VS-13) | Customer Service / Property Mgr | Property Manager | Per inquiry |

### System Touchpoints
- Storage PMS availability/reservation; CRM/lead (VS-13); rate/quoting (W5035); O2C/deposit (VS-16); web/app self-service (VS-10/VS-75)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-sized-unit risk**: Wrong size estimate → move-in churn or upgrade friction; mitigated by size-estimator tool
- **Availability-mismatch risk**: Reserved unit double-booked → customer complaint; mitigated by real-time availability lock
- **Lead-leakage risk**: Unconverted inquiries; mitigated by CRM follow-up + conversion analytics (W5054)

### Time Estimate
- ~10–20 min per inquiry; ~3,000–6,000/month

### Automation Opportunity
- Online size-estimator & self-service reservation; availability auto-lock; lead→lease conversion scoring

### Controls
- CTL: deposit collected before hold; availability lock prevents double-booking; rate quote RA 7394-compliant (VS-118)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-13 (CRM/CX), VS-10/VS-75 (self-service), VS-16 (O2C), VS-118 (price integrity); W5035 (pricing), W5039 (availability), W5042 (move-in), W5054 (analytics)

## W5042. Customer Move-In, Lease Execution, Access Credential & Insurance Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Reservation confirmed (W5041); scheduled move-in date |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~1,000–2,000 move-ins/month at maturity |
| **Volume** | 1 move-in per new tenant |
| **Owner** | Property Manager (Storage) / Store Services Lead |
| **Participants** | Legal (lease — VS-100/W5038), O2C (VS-16), Insurance (VS-26), IT/Security (access — VS-27/VS-23), Compliance (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Execute the lease & collect setup: e-sign the master lease (W5038), capture tenant ID & data-privacy consent (RA 10173 — VS-91), collect security deposit + first period, and enroll auto-pay (W5044) | Property Mgr / O2C / Compliance | Property Manager | Per move-in |
| 2 | Issue access credentials & insurance: provision the access-credential (gate code/unit lock PIN/RFID — W5043), confirm tenant insurance or sell the damage waiver (W5038), and brief the tenant on rules/prohibited goods (W5051) | IT/Security / Insurance / Property Mgr | Property Manager | Per move-in |
| 3 | Record move-in & activate: log the move-in inspection (empty-unit condition photos), activate billing, and feed occupancy (W5039) and analytics (W5054) | Property Mgr / O2C | Property Manager | Per move-in |

### System Touchpoints
- Storage PMS lease/tenant master; e-signature; access-control provisioning (VS-27/VS-23/W5043); insurance (VS-26); O2C/deposit (VS-16); privacy/consent (VS-91); image repository (links VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Uninsured-tenant risk**: Tenant storing goods without insurance/waiver → BuildRight liability on loss; mitigated by mandatory insurance/waiver gate
- **Access-credential-misuse risk**: Shared/leaked gate codes → unauthorised access; mitigated by per-tenant credentials + audit (W5043)
- **Privacy risk**: Tenant ID/data captured without RA 10173 consent; mitigated by consent gate (VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- ~20–30 min per move-in

### Automation Opportunity
- E-signature + access-provisioning auto-flow; insurance/waiver gating; move-in inspection photo-capture mobile app

### Controls
- CTL: lease executed & deposit collected before access; insurance/waiver mandatory; access per-tenant; RA 10173 consent captured (VS-91)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal), VS-16 (O2C), VS-26 (insurance), VS-91 (privacy), VS-27/VS-23 (access); W5038 (lease), W5043 (access), W5044 (billing), W5051 (prohibited goods)

## W5043. Storage Facility Daily Access Control, Security Monitoring & Audit Trail

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Every tenant/visitor site entry; continuous security monitoring |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (24/7 access windows common) |
| **Volume** | ~10–100 access events/day per site × ~50 sites |
| **Owner** | Property Manager (Storage) / Security & LP (VS-23/VS-159) |
| **Participants** | Security/LP (VS-23/VS-159), IT (VS-27), Facilities (VS-138), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Control access: validate each entry via gate access-control (credential/plate reader), log unit-door open/close, and flag anomalies (after-hours, wrong-unit access, loitering) | Security / Property Mgr | Property Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Monitor CCTV & alarms: live/scheduled CCTV review (VS-23), unit-door-alarm response, and incident escalation to LP (VS-23) or corporate security (VS-159) and police if needed | Security / LP | Property Manager / LP Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Maintain the audit trail: reconcile access logs to active leases (flag access by delinquent/moved-out tenants — links W5044/W5047), and retain CCTV/log evidence (retention VS-88/W5050) | Security / Property Mgr / Compliance | Property Manager | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Access-control/gate system (VS-27); CCTV/VMS (VS-23/VS-159); unit-door alarm; storage PMS access-log; LP case management (VS-23); evidence retention (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Theft risk**: Unauthorised access to units → tenant loss & claims; mitigated by per-tenant credentials + CCTV + door alarms
- **Audit-gap risk**: Access logs not reconciled to lease status → ex-tenant access; mitigated by daily reconciliation
- **Safety risk**: Lone-tenant/after-hours incidents; mitigated by lighting, CCTV, emergency call-points (VS-24)

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous; ~30–60 min/site/day log review

### Automation Opportunity
- ANPR/credential→lease auto-match; door-alarm→CCTV auto-tag; anomaly-detection analytics on access logs

### Controls
- CTL-99 (ensure storage lien & prohibited-goods control.)
- CTL: access per active lease only (reconciled); CCTV/alarm evidence retained (VS-88); incident escalation to LP (VS-23)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-23/VS-159 (security/LP), VS-27 (access IT), VS-24 (HSE), VS-88 (retention); W5042 (credentials), W5044 (delinquency), W5050 (security ops)

## W5044. Storage Tenant Billing, Auto-Pay, Late-Fee & Delinquency Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly billing cycle; missed payment; delinquency timeline |
| **Frequency** | Monthly billing + continuous delinquency follow-up |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of active tenants × monthly; ~5–15% delinquency at any time |
| **Owner** | O2C / Property Manager (Storage) |
| **Participants** | O2C (VS-16), Treasury (VS-18), Property Manager, Legal (lien — VS-100/W5047), Customer Service (VS-13) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bill & collect: generate monthly rent invoices, process auto-pay (card/direct-debit/e-wallet — VS-80), apply street-vs-in-place rates (W5039), and reconcile cash (VS-16) | O2C / Property Mgr | O2C Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | Manage late/delinquent: apply late fees per lease (W5038), send dunning notices (5/15/30-day), suspend gate access at threshold, and pre-qualify for lien (W5047) | O2C / Property Mgr / Legal | O2C Manager / Property Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Report delinquency & reserve: aged-delinquency reporting, bad-debt provision (VS-17), and hand-off to lien/disposition (W5047) or write-off with FP&A (VS-17.4) | O2C / FP&A | O2C Manager | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Storage PMS billing module; O2C/AR (VS-16); payment ops/acquirer (VS-80); treasury/cash (VS-18); FP&A/bad-debt (VS-17.4); dunning automation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Bad-debt risk**: Tenant abandonment + lost lien window → uncollectible rent; mitigated by timely dunning + lien (W5047)
- **Access-overlock risk**: Locking out a paying tenant in error → complaint/liability; mitigated by access-suspension control tied to verified delinquency
- **Revenue-leakage risk**: In-place-rate not stepping up; auto-pay failures unnoticed; mitigated by rate-engine (W5039) + failed-payment alerts

### Time Estimate
- ~monthly billing run; ~continuous delinquency follow-up (~10–20 min/delinquent case)

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-pay + failed-payment retry; dunning sequence automation; access-suspension auto-trigger at threshold

### Controls
- CTL: late fees per lease; access-suspension tied to verified delinquency; bad-debt provision (VS-17); lien only after legal notice (W5047)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (O2C), VS-80 (payments), VS-18 (treasury), VS-17/VS-17.4 (R2R/FP&A); W5038 (lease), W5039 (rates), W5043 (access), W5047 (lien)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Tenant move-out notice; end of lease; container return (W5045) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~800–1,500 move-outs/month at maturity |
| **Volume** | 1 per vacating tenant/container |
| **Owner** | Property Manager (Storage) / O2C (VS-16) |
| **Participants** | O2C (VS-16), Property Manager, Facilities (VS-138), Customer Service (VS-13) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Schedule & vacate: confirm move-out date, terminate access (W5043), and confirm the unit is fully emptied/clean (or flag for lien if abandoned — W5047) | Property Mgr | Property Manager | Per move-out |
| 2 | Inspect & damage-assess: compare move-in vs move-out condition (photos), assess damage (door/wall/lock/floor), and quote repair/cleaning charges against the deposit | Property Mgr / Facilities | Property Manager | Per move-out |
| 3 | Settle & refund: apply deposit less damages (per lease W5038), process refund (VS-16/O2C), issue final statement, and update occupancy (W5039) + churn analytics (W5054) | O2C / Property Mgr | O2C Manager / Property Manager | Per move-out |

- ~30–45 min per move-out (inspection + settlement)

### Automation Opportunity
- Move-in/move-out photo-comparison; damage-quote template; automated deposit settlement

### Controls
- CTL-99 (ensure storage lien & prohibited-goods control.)

## W5047. Abandoned-Unit Contents Disposition, Lien Sale & Regulatory Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Tenant delinquency past lien threshold (W5044); abandonment confirmed |
| **Frequency** | ~2–8% of move-outs (abandoned); ~hundreds/year at maturity |
| **Volume** | 1 lien/disposition per abandoned unit |
| **Owner** | Property Manager (Storage) / Legal (VS-100) |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100), O2C (VS-16), Compliance (VS-22), Security (VS-23), Facilities (VS-138), Privacy (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish abandonment & perfect the lien: verify delinquency past the lease threshold (W5038/W5044), serve the statutory/contractual lien notice (Civil Code), and overlock the unit (W5043) | Property Mgr / Legal | Property Manager / General Counsel | Per case |
| 2 | Conduct the lien sale/disposition: auction/sell/dispose contents per lease & law, document the sale (bid records, proceeds), and apply proceeds against the owed balance | Property Mgr / Legal / O2C | Property Manager / General Counsel | Per case |
| 3 | Settle, document & retain: refund any surplus to the tenant (or escheat unclaimed per unclaimed-property rules), document the chain-of-custody/evidence, and retain records (VS-88) | O2C / Legal / Compliance | O2C Manager / General Counsel | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Storage PMS lien/disposition module; O2C/recovery (VS-16); legal/case (VS-100); compliance (VS-22); evidence retention (VS-88); privacy (VS-91)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unlawful-disposition risk**: Disposing/selling contents without perfected lien → conversion/trespass-to-goods liability; mitigated by Legal-governed lien notice & sale
- **Privacy/data risk**: Personal documents found in disposed contents (RA 10173); mitigated by secure-shred protocol (links VS-88)
- **Surplus/escheat risk**: Unclaimed sale surplus not handled per unclaimed-property law; mitigated by escheat accounting

### Time Estimate
- ~2–6 weeks per case (notice period + sale)

### Automation Opportunity
- Lien-threshold auto-detection → notice generation; lien-sale documentation templates

### Controls
- CTL-99 (ensure storage lien & prohibited-goods control.)
- CTL: lien perfected per Civil Code before disposition (Legal); sale documented & evidenced; surplus/escheat handled; personal data secure-shredded (VS-91)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal), VS-16 (O2C), VS-22 (compliance), VS-88 (records), VS-91 (privacy); W5038 (lease/lien terms), W5043 (access/overlock), W5044 (delinquency)
