## W5033. Storage Program Strategy, Product Portfolio & Business-Model Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy refresh (VS-33); new business-line approval; site/portfolio review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | 1 program across 5 entities; phased rollout across up to ~50 of the 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Director, Storage & Rental Services / VP Strategy (VS-33) |
| **Participants** | Strategy (VS-33), Real Estate (VS-97), Finance/FP&A (VS-17.4), Legal (VS-100), Store Ops (VS-07), Marketing (VS-14), Risk (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the storage business strategy & operating model: the product portfolio (on-site self-storage units, drive-up units, climate-controlled, vehicle/boat/RV parking, portable containers, mobile on-demand storage), the make-vs-partner-vs-franchise model, the rollout sequence (which of the 200 stores have the parking/yard footprint and the demand), and the KPIs (occupancy, rate/unit, NOI margin, cross-sell attach) | Director Storage / Strategy | VP Strategy / CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Build the business case & capital model: capex per unit, breakeven occupancy, payback, cannibalisation check vs BuildRight's own backroom space (VS-04), and the recurring-revenue/NOI contribution to the group P&L (links VS-17.4) | Director Storage / FP&A | CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Govern & approve: board/leadership sign-off (links VS-36), legal-entity placement (which of the 5 entities owns the storage — typically Property Mgmt Inc.), and the regulatory/tax posture (VAT on rentals, percentage-tax vs VAT, LGU business-permit scope) with Legal/Tax (VS-79) | Director Storage / Legal / Tax | CFO / CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A/strategy (VS-17.4/VS-33); real-estate/portfolio (VS-97); fixed-asset/capex (VS-35/VS-40); legal entity master (VS-29); tax (VS-79); storage property-management module (new capability — W5038/W5039)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Demand-misread risk**: Building storage where renovation/project demand won't fill units → stranded capex; mitigated by site-level demand modeling & phased pilot
- **Entity/tax-structure risk**: Wrong legal entity or VAT/percentage-tax treatment on rentals → restate & penalty exposure (VS-79); mitigated by Tax sign-off at step 3
- **Cannibalisation risk**: Diverting backroom space needed for omnichannel fulfillment (VS-04/VS-93); mitigated by space-allocation governance with DC/Store Ops

### Time Estimate
- ~6–10 weeks annual strategy + per-site business case (~2 weeks/site)

### Automation Opportunity
- Site-suitability scoring (parking area, trade-density, competitor-storage gap) from GIS/customer-data; capex/payback template tied to real-estate & FP&A actuals

### Controls
- CTL-752 (ensure controlled execution — Storage Portfolio Strategy Site and Unit Capacity Planning (.)
- CTL: business case board/leadership-approved (VS-36); entity & tax-structure Tax-reviewed (VS-79); capex within VS-35/VS-40 approval matrix

## W5034. Storage Site Selection, Real-Estate Acquisition & Unit-Capacity Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy approval (W5033); expansion plan; site availability |
| **Frequency** | Per candidate site; ~5–10 new storage sites/year at maturity |
| **Volume** | ~200 candidate store sites screened; ~10–20% shortlisted |
| **Owner** | Director, Storage & Rental Services / Real Estate (VS-97) |
| **Participants** | Real Estate (VS-97), Store Ops (VS-07), Strategy (VS-33), Legal (VS-100), Facilities (VS-138), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Screen candidate sites against the storage criteria: available parking/yard/side-lot area (vs selling-area need VS-07), access (drive-up vs interior), zoning/LGU allowance for storage use, population & competitor-storage density, and B2B-trade density (contractors needing jobsite staging) | Real Estate / Director Storage | Director Storage | Per site |
| 2 | Acquire/secure the footprint: own-build on BuildRight land (VS-20), lease adjacent/under-used land (VS-42), or partner with a third-party facility (W5040); negotiate terms, secure LGU clearance, and confirm BFP/zoning feasibility for a storage occupancy | Real Estate / Legal | Director Storage / VP Real Estate | Per site |
| 3 | Plan unit capacity & layout: the unit count and size mix (5'×5' to 10'×30'+ climate/drive-up/RV), aisle/corridor, office, and the build-out scope handed to facility design (W5037) | Director Storage / Facilities | Director Storage | Per site |

### System Touchpoints
- Real-estate/portfolio & GIS (VS-97); site/property master (VS-29); lease administration (VS-42); zoning/LGU permit tracking (VS-166); capacity-planning analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Zoning/permitting risk**: Storage use not permitted at the site → blocked build or LGU violation; mitigated by zoning/LGU feasibility check at step 2 (links VS-166)
- **Capacity-mismatch risk**: Wrong unit-size mix for local demand → chronic vacancy in large units, waitlist in small; mitigated by demand-driven mix modeling
- **Footprint-conflict risk**: Storage consuming customer parking needed for basket/large-item pickup (VS-171/VS-162); mitigated by Store Ops sign-off

### Time Estimate
- ~4–8 weeks/site for screening + acquisition + capacity plan

### Automation Opportunity
- GIS-based demand/supply scoring; automated zoning/LGU-permit status feed; capacity-mix optimizer

### Controls
- CTL-752 (ensure controlled execution — Storage Portfolio Strategy Site and Unit Capacity Planning (.)
- CTL: site acquisition within VS-97 real-estate approval; lease terms Legal-reviewed (VS-42); LGU/BFP feasibility confirmed before capex commit

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Set the rate card & pricing architecture: street rate vs in-place rate, discounts (first-month-free, long-term), auto-pay discount, insurance/coverage options, and the price-integrity/disclosure (RA 7394) compliance of advertised rates | Pricing / FP&A / Legal | Director Storage / CFO | At launch + quarterly |

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks initial rate card; ~1 week quarterly review

### Automation Opportunity

## W5039. Storage Occupancy/Yield Management & Dynamic Rate Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Live program; monthly/weekly yield cycle; occupancy/vacancy movement |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + periodic rate review |
| **Volume** | ~all occupied units across ~50 sites × monthly cycles |
| **Owner** | Director, Storage & Rental Services / Revenue/Yield Mgmt |
| **Participants** | Pricing (VS-01.2), FP&A (VS-17.4), Property Manager, Analytics (VS-28), Marketing (VS-14) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monitor occupancy & demand by site/unit-type: physical vs economic occupancy, vacancy by size, lead-to-lease conversion, and seasonality (links W5055) | Yield Mgmt / Analytics / Property Mgr | Director Storage | Weekly/Monthly |
| 2 | Optimize rates dynamically: street-rate vs in-place-rate spread, move-in discounts vs occupancy, and site-level rate adjustments that maximise revenue per available unit (RevPAU) — governed within the rate-card framework (W5035) | Yield Mgmt / Pricing | Director Storage | Monthly |
| 3 | Act on churn & delinquency: identify at-risk/move-out tenants, trigger retention outreach (Marketing VS-14), and feed the delinquency workflow (W5044) | Yield Mgmt / Property Mgr / O2C | Director Storage | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Storage PMS yield/revenue module; analytics/BI (VS-28); pricing engine (VS-01.2); competitor intel (VS-57); CRM/loyalty (VS-13); O2C/delinquency (VS-16/W5044)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Yield-leakage risk**: Holding street rates flat as occupancy rises → foregone revenue; mitigated by dynamic rate engine
- **Discount-addiction risk**: Over-reliance on first-month-free → brand/price erosion; mitigated by discount-discipline in W5035
- **Churn-blindness risk**: Undetected move-out drivers → occupancy erosion; mitigated by churn analytics + retention outreach

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous; ~1–2 days/month per site for rate review

### Automation Opportunity
- RevPAU-optimizing dynamic rate engine; churn-risk scoring → automated retention trigger

### Controls
- CTL-752 (ensure controlled execution — Storage Portfolio Strategy Site and Unit Capacity Planning (.)
- CTL: rate changes within W5035 framework; discounts margin-controlled; churn/delinquency reconciled to O2C (VS-16)
