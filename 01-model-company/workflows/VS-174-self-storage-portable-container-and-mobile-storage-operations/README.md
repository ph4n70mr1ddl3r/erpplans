# VS-174: Self-Storage, Portable Container & Mobile-Storage Operations

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Self-storage, portable-container and mobile-storage rental operations for BuildRight Depot Corp.
— owning the **customer-facing storage-rental program** that converts BuildRight's ~205
parking-rich sites, its ~40% B2B trade base (contractors/builders/developers), and its ~55% B2C
homeowner base (doing renovations that displace household goods) into a recurring-revenue, dwell-
and-basket-linked service: storage portfolio & business-model strategy; site selection and
unit/container capacity planning; unit-type mix, pricing and yield/occupancy management; portable
and mobile (PODS-style) container fleet strategy, procurement and delivery; storage facility
build-out, security and access infrastructure; the **storage lease/rental agreement, rate card
and terms administration**; the customer rental lifecycle (inquiry, reservation, move-in, access,
billing, delinquency, move-out); portable-container delivery/placement/relocation/pickup; the
unit inspection, damage and final-settlement workflow; **abandoned-unit contents disposition,
lien sale and legal/regulatory compliance**; storage customer service; and storage occupancy,
revenue, yield, delinquency and cross-sell analytics.

This matters at BuildRight's structure, scale and customer mix. The group operates ~205 large
sites (200 stores at 8,000–15,000 sqm + 4 DCs + HQ) with substantial under-used parking, yard and
side-lot area across provincial/island locations, and serves a customer base for whom storage is
a *direct consequence of the purchase* — a homeowner renovating a kitchen (appliance/furniture
VS-143), a contractor staging materials and tools for a multi-week jobsite (VS-77), a homeowner
decluttering to sell, a trade pro between projects, or a disaster-affected household needing
temporary space (VS-69). Big-box and home-improvement retail globally (and storage-adjacent
operators) monetise exactly this adjacency — self-storage is one of the highest-margin, lowest-
churn recurring-revenue businesses in retail real estate (90%+ occupancy, ~PHP 1,500–4,000/month
per unit), and portable/mobile storage extends it to the doorstep. At even a modest pilot of 50
stores × ~80 units + a ~200-container mobile fleet, the program is a multi-hundred-million-PHP
recurring stream that also lifts large-item attach (VS-143/VS-162), basket (renovation +
storage), and dwell time. Ungoverned, it leaks value through mispriced/under-occupied units,
delinquency, security/theft loss, fire/life-safety exposure, and abandoned-unit legal liability.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a unified program** — the defining terms "self-storage",
"storage unit", "portable storage", "mobile storage", "storage container", "storage rental",
"storage lien", and "storage lease" each appear in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow
headers and in **zero** VS directory names. Adjacent coverage exists only as scattered,
conflated slices: BuildRight's own warehouse space is operated by **VS-04 (DC & Warehouse)** for
*merchandise* (not customer storage); customer **bulk-material staging** (VS-77) stages
*purchased materials at the jobsite*; the **smart-locker** network (VS-164) handles *small
parcels*; **returnable transport items** (VS-111) track *BuildRight's own pallets*; **corporate
real estate** (VS-97) manages the *store/DC portfolio*; and **vehicle rental** (VS-162) rents
*titled motor vehicles to self-drive*. No value stream owns the **customer storage-rental
operating model** — the facility/unit/container fleet, the rental lease and billing, the access/
security program, the lien/disposition discipline, and the storage yield analytics. It is **not**
covered by **VS-97 (Corporate Real Estate)**, which manages BuildRight's operating sites — this
operates a *customer-paid storage service on top of* that real estate. It is **not** covered by
**VS-04 (DC & Warehouse)**, which stores *merchandise inventory* — this stores *customers'
household/business goods under a rental lease*. It is **not** covered by **VS-164 (Smart
Locker)**, which is *small-parcel automated collection* — this is *full-unit and mobile-container
bulk storage with a landlord-tenant relationship*. And it is **not** covered by **VS-162
(Customer Truck/Van Rental)** or **VS-12.2 (Tool Rental)**, which rent *vehicles and tools* —
this rents *space*.

This value stream owns the end-to-end program: storage business strategy & portfolio design;
storage site selection, real-estate acquisition and unit-capacity planning; unit-type mix,
pricing and yield/occupancy management; portable/mobile container fleet strategy, procurement and
sizing; storage facility design, build-out, security and access infrastructure; the lease
agreement, rate card and terms; reservation, move-in, access-credential and insurance setup;
daily access control, security monitoring and audit trail; tenant billing, auto-pay, late-fee and
delinquency; portable-container delivery, placement, relocation and pickup; move-out, unit
inspection, damage assessment and final settlement; **abandoned-unit lien, contents disposition
and regulatory compliance**; storage customer service; facility housekeeping and maintenance;
stored-goods restrictions and hazardous-item inspection; **BFP fire/life-safety and emergency
preparedness**; portable-container fleet maintenance and lifecycle; occupancy/revenue/yield/
delinquency analytics; seasonality/demand forecasting; and program ROI / cross-sell/basket
analytics. It integrates tightly with VS-97 (real-estate siting), VS-138 (facility maintenance),
VS-23/VS-159 (security), VS-24 (HSE), VS-143/VS-162 (large-item attach), VS-77 (contractor
staging), VS-16 (rental O2C), VS-15 (P2P for build-out), VS-35 (fixed assets), VS-91 (tenant
data privacy), and VS-26 (business continuity).
