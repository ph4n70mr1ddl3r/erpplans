# VS-166: Regulatory License, Permit & Accreditation Portfolio Management

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Unified regulatory license, permit & accreditation portfolio-management workflows for BuildRight
Depot Corp. — owning the **enterprise-wide cross-domain discipline** that maintains a single
register of **every statutory license, permit, certification, and accreditation** held by the
5 entities across ~205 sites (200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ + off-site lockers/EV chargers), with a
governing **renewal calendar, expiry-risk engine, evidence repository, and compliance dashboard**
— so that no go-to-market-blocking or site-closing authorization lapses unnoticed.

This matters at BuildRight's distributed, multi-regulated scale. The group holds a large,
heterogeneous, and constantly-renewing portfolio of regulatory authorizations: **LGU business
permit / mayor's permit** per store (200 stores across 6 regions) and per DC, **barangay
clearance**, **BFP Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC)** and annual fire-safety permit,
**DENR Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC)** / certificate of non-coverage and hazardous-
waste generator registration, **BIR registration + Computerized Accounting System (CAS) permit +
Authority to Print (ATP)**, **DTI-BNR / DTI-BPS licenses**, **PCAB contractor license** (VS-165),
**PhilGEPS registration**, **FDA-adjacent / FPA / BPI** for garden/agro, **PEZA/BOI incentive
registration** where applicable, **SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG employer registration**, **DOLE OSH /
labor standards registrations**, **LTO vehicle registrations & LTFRB freight license** (fleet
VS-06), **driver licenses**, **PCAB/PRC/TESDA personnel credentials**, **BSP agency accreditation
for VAS/bills-payment** (VS-156), **customs brokerage / importer accreditation**, **energy
retailer/RCOA**, **LGU sanitary/health/building permits**, and **data-protection registration
(NPC)** — plus the per-site signage, mechanical, electrical, and occupancy permits. At ~205
sites × dozens of permit types each, the portfolio numbers in the **thousands of active
authorizations**, each with its own expiry/renewal cycle, renewal owner, fee, and penalty for
lapse. A single lapsed LGU business permit, BFP FSIC, or BIR registration can **close a store or
block its sales**; a lapsed B2G eligibility document forfeits bid access; a lapsed personnel
credential voids service delivery.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a unified program**. Each compliance domain owns its
*execution slice* — VS-22 (compliance & regulatory) executes permits and inspection response,
VS-76 (LGU compliance) owns local permits, VS-138 (IFM) owns facility/fire permits, VS-114 (DG)
owns hazmat permits, VS-117 (DTI-BPS) owns product-standard licenses, VS-79 (tax) owns BIR/CAS,
VS-46 (B2G) owns PhilGEPS, VS-165 (PCAB) owns contractor licensing, VS-06 (fleet) owns vehicle/
driver licenses, VS-145 (garden) owns FPA/BPI — but **no value stream owns the unified
cross-domain license/permit portfolio**: the single register, the cross-domain renewal calendar,
the expiry-risk engine, the evidence repository, the regulator-change monitor, the inspection-
response hub, and the executive compliance dashboard. The defining terms — "license portfolio",
"permit portfolio", "regulatory inventory", "permit register", "license register", "compliance
calendar", and "centralized regulatory obligation register" — appear in **zero** PA files as
dedicated workflow headers (the existing 4 "renewal tracking" headers — W1547 product certs,
W1400 driver, W4735 COI/attestation, W2910 tax-exempt — are each a single-domain slice).

This value stream owns the end-to-end portfolio discipline **without re-executing the domain
permits** (those stay in their owning VSs): portfolio strategy & operating model (centralized vs
distributed, ownership/accountability matrix); the unified license/permit/accreditation
inventory & register; regulatory-intelligence obligation mapping by site/entity/activity;
governance & ownership matrix; the renewal calendar & expiry-risk engine; the evidence repository
& audit trail; regulatory-change monitoring & impact assessment; renewal-cycle execution
coordination and multi-site renewal campaigns (200 stores × LGU/BFP); new-site/new-activity
license acquisition (links to VS-37/VS-109 store lifecycle); the regulator-inspection/audit
coordination hub; lapsed-permit remediation & business-continuity workarounds; third-party
permit/compliance service-provider (fixer/consultant/agent) management; inter-agency multi-permit
coordination; permit-variation/scope-change & site-transfer/closure handling; the executive
compliance dashboard & board reporting; penalty/fine management; portfolio cost analytics;
audit-ready evidence & regulator data requests; license/permit data governance & master-data
sync (VS-29); operating-model optimization; internal-audit coordination & SOX/COSO-style
assurance; and program maturity/continuous improvement. It integrates tightly with every
domain compliance VS (VS-22/76/79/114/117/138/145/165/46/06), VS-29 (master data), VS-88
(records), VS-21 (audit/risk), VS-36 (board governance), and VS-166's analytics feed VS-28.
