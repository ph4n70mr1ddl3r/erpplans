## W4849. Renewal-Cycle Execution & Cross-Domain Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Calendar alert (W4845); renewal cycle |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (per authorization's cycle) |
| **Volume** | All renewable authorizations (~thousands/yr) |
| **Owner** | Compliance Operations / Domain Renewal Owners |
| **Participants** | Domain compliance owners (VS-22/76/79/114/117/138/145/165/46/06), Finance (fees), Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On calendar alert (W4845), the domain owner prepares the renewal package (forms, supporting documents, fee) per the authorization's renewal rule and the evidence standard (W4846); coordinate any cross-domain dependencies (e.g., BIR CAS renewal needs current mayor's permit) | Domain Owner / Compliance Operations | Domain Accountable Owner | Per renewal |
| 2 | Submit & track: file with the regulator (or via third-party provider W4854), track processing, respond to queries, and obtain the renewed authorization within the lead-time; coordinate the fee payment (VS-17) and update the register (W4842) on receipt | Domain Owner / Compliance Operations / Finance | Domain Accountable Owner | Per renewal |
| 3 | Close the renewal: record renewed authorization + evidence in the repository (W4846), reset the calendar (W4845), and confirm on the dashboard; escalate any delay to remediation (W4853) before lapse | Compliance Operations / Domain Owner | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Per renewal |

### System Touchpoints
- GRC calendar/workflow (W4845); register (W4842); evidence repository (W4846); finance (VS-17); domain compliance VSs

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Dependency-miss risk**: Renewal blocked by an unmet cross-domain dependency (e.g., expired mayor's permit blocking BIR CAS); mitigated by dependency mapping
- **Processing-delay risk**: Regulator backlog delaying renewal past expiry; mitigated by lead-time headroom + remediation (W4853)
- **Evidence-lag risk**: Renewed authorization not recorded → calendar not reset → false alert; mitigated by the update SLA

### Time Estimate
- ~per renewal hours–days (class-dependent); operation continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Renewal-package auto-assembly; dependency mapping; submission-status tracker; calendar auto-reset on receipt

### Controls
- CTL-729 (ensure controlled execution — Renewal Execution, Inspection Coordination & Multi-Site Campaigns (PA-166.2).)
- CTL: renewal filed within lead-time; cross-domain dependencies resolved; renewed authorization recorded

### Cross-references
- links to W4842/W4845/W4846 (register/calendar/evidence), VS-17 (finance), W4853 (remediation), W4854 (providers), all domain compliance VSs

## W4850. Multi-Site Permit Renewal Campaigns (LGU Business Permit, Mayor's Permit, BFP FSIC)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual LGU business-permit renewal season (Jan); annual BFP FSIC; recurring multi-site cycles |
| **Frequency** | Annual per site (200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ) for LGU; periodic for BFP |
| **Volume** | ~205 sites × LGU business permit/mayor's permit annually; ~205 × BFP FSIC |
| **Owner** | Compliance Operations / Store Ops (VS-07) / LGU Compliance (VS-76) |
| **Participants** | Store Managers, DC Managers, Real Estate (VS-97), Finance (VS-17 fees), Third-party providers (W4854) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plan the annual campaign: the ~205-site LGU business-permit/mayor's-permit renewal schedule by LGU (different LGU deadlines & requirements), the BFP FSIC cycle, owners per site (Store/DC Manager + central support), the standard package, fee budget, and the third-party-provider coverage (W4854); sequence by LGU deadline | Compliance Operations / Store Ops / LGU Compliance | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Campaign planning |
| 2 | Execute the campaign across sites: distribute packages to Store/DC Managers (or providers), track submission per site, manage LGU-specific variations (barangay clearance, sanitation, fire-safety eval), collect renewed permits, and reconcile to the calendar (W4845) and register (W4842) | Compliance Operations / Store Managers / Providers | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Campaign window |
| 3 | Close the campaign: confirm 100% coverage, remediate any lapses (W4853), report campaign results (on-time %, lapses, cost) to governance (W4864), and capture LGU-process learnings for next year | Compliance Operations / Analytics | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Post-campaign |

### System Touchpoints
- GRC campaign workflow; calendar (W4845); register (W4842); store ops (VS-07); LGU compliance (VS-76); finance (VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scale risk**: 205 sites × LGU variation overwhelming execution; mitigated by campaign discipline + providers
- **Deadline-variation risk**: Different LGU deadlines causing some sites to lapse; mitigated by per-LGU sequencing
- **Store-engagement risk**: Store Managers deprioritizing permit renewal; mitigated by central support + accountability (W4844)

### Time Estimate
- ~campaign window 4–8 weeks annually; planning 1–2 weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- Campaign-workflow with per-LGU rules; site-status tracker; package auto-distribution; coverage dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-729 (ensure controlled execution — Renewal Execution, Inspection Coordination & Multi-Site Campaigns (PA-166.2).)
- CTL: 100% site coverage targeted; per-LGU deadlines tracked; lapses remediated; campaign results reported

### Cross-references
- links to VS-76 (LGU compliance), VS-07 (store ops), VS-138 (BFP/fire), W4842/W4845 (register/calendar), W4853 (remediation), W4854 (providers)

| **Volume** | ~15–25 new-site/activity acquisitions per year (10–15 new stores + remodels/DC changes) |
| **Owner** | Compliance Operations / Regulatory Intelligence (W4843) |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), Real Estate (VS-97), Government Affairs (VS-104), domain owners, providers (W4854) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On new site/activity, generate the required-permit checklist from the obligation map (W4843) for that site type/activity/geography (LGU business permit, barangay, BFP, DENR ECC if applicable, BIR branch/CAS, sanitary, signage, occupancy, plus activity-specific e.g., BSP accreditation for VAS, NPC registration for data); sequence by the go-live timeline (VS-37) | Regulatory Intelligence / Compliance Operations | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Per new site/activity |
| 2 | Execute acquisition in parallel with site build/open (VS-37/VS-109): assign owners (W4844), file applications, manage LGU/regulator processing, and obtain authorizations before go-live; coordinate the fee budget (VS-17) and providers (W4854) | Compliance Operations / Domain Owners / Providers / Real Estate | Compliance Portfolio Manager / COO | Per new site/activity |

### System Touchpoints
- Obligation map (W4843); store lifecycle (VS-37/109); register (W4842); calendar (W4845); government affairs (VS-104); finance (VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Checklist-gap risk**: A required permit omitted from the acquisition; mitigated by the obligation map (W4843)
- **Timeline-miss risk**: Regulator processing exceeding the go-live timeline; mitigated by early start + provider support

### Time Estimate
- ~per new site acquisition 4–12 weeks (lead-time dependent)

### Automation Opportunity
- Site/activity → checklist engine (W4843); parallel-acquisition tracker; go-live gate (permit-clear to open)

### Controls
- CTL-729 (ensure controlled execution — Renewal Execution, Inspection Coordination & Multi-Site Campaigns (PA-166.2).)
- CTL: obligation-map checklist applied; authorizations obtained before go-live; recorded in register

### Cross-references

## W4853. Expiring/Lapsed Permit Remediation & Business-Continuity Workaround

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | At-risk renewal (W4845); lapsed permit; regulator notice of lapse/operating-without-permit |
| **Frequency** | Ad hoc |
| **Volume** | ~rare lapse events; ~any lapse is critical |
| **Owner** | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Domain Owner / Legal (VS-100.3) |
| **Participants** | COO, Store/DC Ops (VS-07/VS-04), Finance, BCP (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Detect the lapse/at-risk (calendar alert non-response W4845, regulator notice, or self-identified); assess business impact (store-closing, bid-blocking, fines, operating-without-permit offense) and escalate to CCO/COO immediately | Compliance Operations / Domain Owner / Legal | Compliance Portfolio Manager / VP Legal & Compliance / COO | Per event (immediate) |
| 2 | Remediate: expedite the renewal (W4849), engage the regulator for a cure/grace period where available, and where the lapse is operational (e.g., lapsed LGU business permit) deploy the business-continuity workaround (e.g., temporary-operation permit, adjacent-site fulfillment, BCP VS-26) to sustain operations; manage any penalty (W4858) | Domain Owner / Legal / Store Ops / BCP | Compliance Portfolio Manager / COO | Per event |
| 3 | Reinstate & prevent recurrence: obtain the renewed authorization, reset the calendar (W4845), conduct root-cause (ownership failure W4844, calendar failure, provider failure W4854), strengthen controls, and report the incident to governance (W4864) and analytics (W4862) | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Internal Audit | VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Calendar/alerts (W4845); register (W4842); BCP (VS-26); legal (VS-100.3); finance penalty (VS-17); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Operating-without-permit risk**: Continued operation during lapse → offense + fines + reputational harm; mitigated by continuity workaround + expedited cure
- **Store-closure risk**: A lapsed LGU/BFP permit forcing closure; mitigated by BCP workaround
- **Recurrence risk**: Same lapse recurring; mitigated by root-cause review + control strengthening

### Time Estimate
- ~per event containment 1–5 days; reinstatement weeks; review ~2 weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- At-risk → escalation workflow; continuity-playbook trigger; root-cause tracker

### Controls
- CTL-729 (ensure controlled execution — Renewal Execution, Inspection Coordination & Multi-Site Campaigns (PA-166.2).)
- CTL: lapse escalated immediately; continuity workaround deployed; reinstatement tracked; root-cause reviewed

### Cross-references
- links to W4845 (calendar), W4849 (renewal), VS-26 (BCP), VS-100.3 (legal), W4844 (ownership), W4858 (penalty), W4862/W4864 (analytics/governance)

| 2 | Engage per need (W4849/W4851/W4852): assign the provider, track the deliverable (permit obtained/renewed), verify authenticity, and reconcile the fee (VS-17); enforce the ABC/anti-corruption standard (no bribery of officials — links to VS-86/VS-119) and the data-privacy standard (VS-91) for any data shared | Compliance Operations / Domain Owner / Provider | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Per engagement |

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-729 (ensure controlled execution — Renewal Execution, Inspection Coordination & Multi-Site Campaigns (PA-166.2).)
- CTL: providers DD'd (TPRM/ABC); ABC clauses in contract; deliverable verified; performance monitored

### Cross-references

## W4855. Inter-Agency Coordination & Multi-Permit Concurrent Renewals

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Permits requiring multi-regulator sign-off (e.g., new store needs LGU + BFP + DENR + BIR); concurrent renewal cycles; conflicting regulator requirements |
| **Frequency** | Per multi-permit need |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 multi-permit acquisitions per year (one per new store) plus renewal campaigns |
| **Owner** | Compliance Operations / Regulatory Intelligence (W4843) |
| **Participants** | Domain owners, Government Affairs (VS-104), Real Estate (VS-97), Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Map the multi-permit dependency graph for the need (e.g., a new store: SEC/DTI → LGU business permit needs BFP FSIC + barangay clearance + DENR ECC + sanitary; BIR branch needs LGU permit); identify the critical path and sequencing constraints | Regulatory Intelligence / Compliance Operations | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Per need |
| 2 | Coordinate concurrent renewals/acquisitions across regulators: sequence filings, manage cross-regulator dependencies (permit B needs permit A), resolve conflicting requirements, and leverage Government Affairs (VS-104) relationships to smooth bottlenecks | Compliance Operations / Domain Owners / Government Affairs | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Per need |
| 3 | Track the multi-permit bundle to completion, update the register (W4842), and capture inter-agency-process learnings (e.g., a region where DENR-BFP sequencing is slow) for the obligation map (W4843) and analytics (W4862) | Compliance Operations / Regulatory Intelligence | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Per need |

### System Touchpoints
- Obligation map (W4843); register (W4842); government affairs (VS-104); legal (VS-100.3); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Dependency-bottleneck risk**: A slow regulator blocking downstream permits; mitigated by dependency mapping + Government Affairs
- **Conflict risk**: Conflicting regulator requirements (e.g., BFP vs DENR on a site); mitigated by Legal/Government Affairs resolution
- **Sequencing-error risk**: Filing out of sequence → rejection; mitigated by the dependency graph

### Time Estimate
- ~per multi-permit bundle weeks–months

### Automation Opportunity
- Multi-permit dependency-graph engine; critical-path tracker; inter-agency-process library

### Controls
- CTL-729 (ensure controlled execution — Renewal Execution, Inspection Coordination & Multi-Site Campaigns (PA-166.2).)
- CTL: dependency graph followed; conflicts escalated via Government Affairs/Legal; bundle tracked to completion

## W4856. Permit Variation, Scope Change & Site-Transfer/Closure Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Business/activity change (scope expansion, new product line, signage change, floor-area change); site transfer/closure (VS-59); entity restructuring |
| **Frequency** | Per change |
| **Volume** | ~30–60 permit variations/changes per year |
| **Owner** | Compliance Operations / Domain Owners |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), Real Estate (VS-97), Legal (VS-100.3), Government Affairs (VS-104) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Detect the change-trigger (scope/activity/scope/signage/area change, store closure VS-59, transfer, entity restructuring) and assess permit impact from the obligation map (W4843): which authorizations need variation/amendment/surrender/new-application | Regulatory Intelligence / Compliance Operations / Domain Owner | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Per change |
| 2 | Execute the variation: file amendments with the relevant regulators (e.g., expanded floor area → amended building/occupancy permit + BFP re-eval; new product line → DTI-BPS/DENR if regulated), obtain approvals before the changed activity, and surrender/cancel permits on site closure (VS-59) | Domain Owner / Legal / Compliance Operations | Compliance Portfolio Manager / VP Legal & Compliance | Per change |
| 3 | Update the register (W4842), evidence (W4846), and calendar (W4845); confirm no orphaned/invalid permits remain after closure/transfer; feed variation-volume and closure-compliance to analytics (W4862) and governance (W4864) | Compliance Operations | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Per change |

### System Touchpoints
- Obligation map (W4843); register (W4842); calendar (W4845); store closure (VS-59); real estate (VS-97); legal (VS-100.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unreported-change risk**: A scope/area change not triggering a permit variation → non-compliance; mitigated by change-trigger detection (links to store ops/real estate)
- **Closure-orphan risk**: Permits not surrendered on site closure → stale records/fees; mitigated by closure checklist
- **Timeline risk**: Changed activity starting before the variation is approved; mitigated by a pre-activity gate

### Time Estimate
- ~per variation weeks (regulator-dependent)

### Automation Opportunity
- Change-trigger detection from store/real-estate events; variation-checklist engine; closure-permit-surrender checklist

### Controls
- CTL-729 (ensure controlled execution — Renewal Execution, Inspection Coordination & Multi-Site Campaigns (PA-166.2).)
- CTL: change-triggers assessed for permit impact; variations approved before changed activity; closure permits surrendered

### Cross-references
- links to VS-59 (store closure), VS-97 (real estate), VS-07 (store ops), W4843 (obligation map), W4842/W4845/W4846 (register/calendar/evidence), W4862 (analytics)
