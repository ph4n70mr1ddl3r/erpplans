## W2270. Inventory Liquidation Planning & Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Month 2–4 of closure |
| **Frequency** | Per closure; 90-day window |
| **Volume** | ~35K SKUs; ~PHP 15–25M inventory |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Planner |
| **Participants** | Marketing, Store Manager, Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Designs strategy: transfer high-demand to nearby stores, progressive markdown (10→25→50→75%), bulk sale for residual; Marketing promotes; system tracks sell-through; remaining to DC or bulk | Merch Planner / Marketing / Store Manager | Category Manager | 1–2 weeks + 90 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory/disposition (links to VS-05 / W220 slow-moving, W91 damaged/obsolete, VS-64 / W2390 markdown engine) — the liquidation strategy
- Transfers (links to VS-05 / W22 store transfers) — the high-demand transfer to nearby stores
- Analytics (links to VS-27 / W113) — the sell-through tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inventory-write-down risk**: the liquidation doesn't clear enough inventory, forcing a large write-down; mitigated by the aggressive progressive-markdown schedule
- **Transfer-disruption risk**: transferring high-demand items to nearby stores overwhelms their receiving capacity; mitigated by the phased transfer aligned with the receiving-store capacity

### Automation Opportunity
- Markdown-schedule auto-configured from sell-through data; transfer-need auto-identified from demand-at-nearby-stores

### Controls
- CTL-26: asset-grade inventory disposition; CTL-44 (inventory-count integrity); operational: Category Manager sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1.5 weeks/closure planning + 90-day execution absorbed into store-ops = **~60 hours/closure** Merch Planner.

## W2271. Fixed Asset Recovery & Disposal

| **Frequency** | Per closure |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 5–10M in fixtures/equipment per closure (per W807) |
| **Owner** | Facilities Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Finance, COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inventories assets: POS, shelving, forklifts, tools, office equipment; determines reuse vs. disposal; reusable transferred to new stores/DCs; disposable sold or scrapped; Finance adjusts asset register | Facilities Coordinator / Finance | COO | 3–5 days + 2–3 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Fixed-asset register (links to VS-35 / W399 fixed-asset master, W184 fixed-asset audit) — the asset inventory and disposal
- Transfer/disposal (links to VS-35 / W39 asset disposal) — the asset movement
- Finance/GL (links to VS-17 / W9, W39) — the asset-register adjustment and gain/loss posting

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Asset-loss risk**: assets are scrapped/sold below recoverable value due to time pressure; mitigated by the reuse-first policy and the new-store-demand check before disposal
- **Asset-register-inaccuracy risk**: the disposed assets aren't removed from the register, inflating the asset base; mitigated by the atomic disposal-and-register-update

### Automation Opportunity
- Asset-inventory auto-generated from the fixed-asset register; reuse-demand auto-checked against new-store pipeline

### Controls
- CTL-26: asset disposal authorization — links to VS-35; CTL-184 (fixed-asset audit); operational: COO sign-off

### Time Estimate
~4 days + ~2.5 weeks/closure = **~140 hours/closure** Facilities + Finance.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per closure |
| **Volume** | 1 lease per store |
| **Owner** | COO / Legal |
| **Participants** | CEO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Negotiates termination: executes clause, pays penalty, coordinates site restoration, schedules handover inspection; Legal ensures documentation: inspection report, deposit return, mutual release | COO / Legal | CEO | 2–4 weeks + 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease (links to VS-42 / W117 lease admin, VS-97 real estate) — the lease-termination execution
- Legal (links to VS-100 / W230 contract review) — the mutual-release documentation
- Finance (links to VS-17 / W118 rent payment) — the final rent settlement and deposit recovery

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Restoration-cost risk**: the landlord claims extensive restoration (beyond the lease's "make good" obligation), inflating the cost; mitigated by the documented condition-at-handover (photos, inspection report) and the lease-term review
- **Deposit-forfeiture risk**: the landlord withholds the security deposit on disputable grounds; mitigated by the mutual-release clause in the termination documentation

### Automation Opportunity
- Termination-checklist auto-assembled from the lease terms; restoration-scope auto-referenced from the lease

### Controls
- CTL-221 (ensure controlled store-closure execution.)
- CTL-407: lease-termination accuracy — links to VS-42; operational: CEO sign-off on termination terms >PHP 1M

### Time Estimate
~3 weeks/closure (~120 hours) × ~2.5/year = **~300 hours/year** COO + Legal.

| **Volume** | ~29 employees per store |
| **Owner** | HR Director |
| **Participants** | CHRO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identifies redeployment: transfers to nearby stores (priority performers), DC, HQ, new openings; for non-redeployed: 30-day notice, severance (0.5–1 month/year), clearance, final pay, DOLE reporting | HR Director | CHRO | 2–3 weeks + 4–6 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- HR/separation (links to VS-19 / W43 separation & offboarding, W10 payroll) — the redeployment and separation processing
- DOLE (links to VS-19 / W43, VS-84 labor relations) — the DOLE-compliant separation
- Nearby-store capacity (links to VS-19 / W34 shift scheduling) — the redeployment-availability check

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Labor-dispute risk**: a non-redeployed employee disputes the separation (claims it was constructive dismissal); mitigated by the DOLE-compliant 30-day notice, the documented severance, and the redeployment-first policy
- **Redeployment-capacity-gap risk**: nearby stores can't absorb all redeployed employees; mitigated by the DC/HQ/new-opening alternatives and the phased-redeployment timeline

### Automation Opportunity
- Redeployment-options auto-identified from the nearby-store vacancies; severance auto-calculated per DOLE formula; DOLE-reporting auto-generated

### Controls
- CTL-407: DOLE-compliant separation — links to VS-19 / W43, VS-84; CTL-172 (PPE/payroll-deduction on separation); operational: CHRO sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2.5 weeks + ~5 weeks/closure = **~300 hours/closure** HR Director + team.

## W2274. IT Systems Decommissioning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Month 5 of closure |
| **Frequency** | Per closure |
| **Volume** | 3 POS terminals + network |
| **Owner** | IT Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Ops Manager, CIO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Closes POS terminals, disconnects network/VPN, deactivates store in ERP, archives data per retention, secure data destruction; removes from store locator | IT Ops Manager | CIO | 3–5 days + 2–3 days |

### System Touchpoints
- POS/network (links to VS-27 / W265 POS-terminal management, W366 network) — the hardware decommissioning
- ERP/location master (links to VS-29 / W254 location master) — the store deactivation
- Data retention (links to VS-88 / W256, the 10-year retention rule) — the archived data
- IT-asset (links to VS-99 / W99 IT-asset lifecycle) — the terminal asset-status update

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-breach risk**: the decommissioned POS terminals retain customer/payment data that isn't securely wiped; mitigated by the secure-data-destruction (not just format)
- **Orphaned-system risk**: the store is deactivated in ERP but the POS terminals remain connected (zombie), creating a security surface; mitigated by the network-disconnection synchronized with the deactivation

### Automation Opportunity
- Decommissioning-checklist auto-sequenced; data-destruction auto-verified; store-locator auto-updated

### Controls
- CTL-407: data-security on decommissioning — links to VS-27, VS-91; CTL-26 (IT-asset disposition — links to VS-99); operational: CIO sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 days/closure × ~2.5/year = **~100 hours/year** IT Ops.
