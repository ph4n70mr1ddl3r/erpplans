## W2262. Store Performance Trigger Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly store performance review |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly; 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~2–3 flagged/quarter |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System flags stores: sales/sqm <60% of chain avg for 4 quarters, negative contribution margin 2+ quarters, lease renewal with unfavorable terms; VP presents to Executive Committee | System / VP Store Ops | CEO | 1–2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-performance scorecard (links to VS-07 / W67 monthly performance review, VS-177 field-retail analytics) — the performance dataset
- Lease master (links to VS-42 / W117 lease admin, VS-97 real estate) — the lease-renewal trigger
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113, W231 QBR) — the 4-quarter trend

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Premature-trigger risk**: a store is flagged during a transient dip (a competitor promo, a road construction) rather than structural decline; mitigated by the 4-quarter duration and the root-cause analysis before flagging
- **Late-trigger risk**: a structurally failing store isn't flagged because its absolute revenue still looks acceptable (a declining trend within an acceptable level); mitigated by the trend (not just absolute) trigger

### Automation Opportunity
- Triggers auto-computed quarterly; flagged stores auto-presented with the supporting data

### Controls
- CTL-184 (fixed-asset verification — the store as a fixed asset); operational: CEO/ExComm review of flagged stores

### Time Estimate
~1.5 days/quarter = **~6 days/year** VP Store Ops.

## W2263. Closure Business Case Development

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Executive Committee approves evaluation |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; ~2–3/year |
| **Volume** | ~2–3 business cases/year |
| **Owner** | FP&A Director |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A develops: closure costs (lease penalty, severance, inventory write-down, asset disposal) vs. savings (rent, staff, utilities); payback period, NPV; VP Real Estate evaluates lease; Legal reviews; HR estimates severance | FP&A Director / VP Real Estate / Legal / HR | CFO | 5–10 days |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A/cost model (links to VS-17 / W26 budget, VS-40 / W21 capex, VS-158 / W85) — the closure-cost and savings model
- Lease (links to VS-42 / W117, VS-97) — the lease-penalty evaluation
- HR/severance (links to VS-19 / W43 separation) — the severance estimate

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden-cost-omission risk**: the business case misses a cost (a long-tail liability, a vendor termination penalty); mitigated by the cross-functional review (Real Estate, Legal, HR, Finance)
- **Savings-overestimate risk**: the savings are optimistic (assume all customers migrate, but some don't); mitigated by the customer-retention assumption drawn from historical closure data (W2279)

### Automation Opportunity
- Closure-cost template auto-assembled from the lease/HR/asset data; savings-scenario auto-modeled

### Controls
- CTL-21 (capex/financial-commitment approval — links to VS-40); operational: CFO sign-off on the business case

### Time Estimate
~7.5 days/business-case × ~2.5/year = **~150 hours/year** FP&A + cross-functional team.

### System Touchpoints

### Controls
- CTL (DOLE compliance — links to VS-19 / W43, VS-84); CTL (communication-accuracy); operational: CEO sign-off
