# VS-59: Store Closure & Decommissioning

> **Asset & Infrastructure** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages the process of closing underperforming BuildRight Depot stores, covering closure decision analysis, lease termination, inventory liquidation, asset recovery, staff redeployment, and post-closure site management. Inverse of VS-37 (Store Opening). Targets: ~2–3 store closures/year with 6-month timeline.
