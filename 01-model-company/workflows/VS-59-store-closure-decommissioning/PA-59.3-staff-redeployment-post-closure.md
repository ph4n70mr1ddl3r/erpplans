## W2278. Redeployed Employee Onboarding at New Location

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee accepts transfer |
| **Frequency** | Per closure; ~15–20 redeployments |
| **Volume** | ~15–20 per closure |
| **Owner** | HR Manager |
| **Participants** | Receiving Store Manager, CHRO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR processes movement in system, updates location, arranges relocation support; receiving Store Manager plans orientation; 30-day check-in to assess adjustment | HR Manager / Receiving Store Manager | CHRO | 3–5 days + 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS (links to VS-19 / W15 onboarding, W292 employee master) — the transfer processing
- Nearby-store capacity (links to VS-19 / W34 shift scheduling) — the receiving-store staffing plan
- Relocation (links to VS-19 / W43 separation, VS-141 employee transport) — the relocation support

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Adjustment-failure risk**: the redeployed employee doesn't adjust to the new store (longer commute, different culture) and resigns; mitigated by the 30-day check-in and the relocation/commute support
- **Receiving-store-disruption risk**: the influx of redeployed employees disrupts the receiving store's team dynamics; mitigated by the phased onboarding and the receiving-Store-Manager orientation plan

### Automation Opportunity
- Transfer auto-processed in HRIS; 30-day-check-in auto-scheduled

### Controls
- CTL-408 (ensure controlled execution — Staff Redeployment & Post-Closure Analytics (PA-59.3).)
- CTL (employee-master update — links to VS-19 / W292); operational: CHRO sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 days/closure × ~2.5/year + ~1 week adjustment = **~100 hours/year** HR + receiving Store Managers.

## W2279. Customer Migration Success Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly post-closure |
| **Frequency** | Monthly for 12 months |
| **Volume** | ~15K–30K customers per store |
| **Owner** | Loyalty Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Loyalty Manager, VP Marketing |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System tracks: loyalty members from closed store → purchases at nearby stores; calculates retention and spend change; re-engagement campaigns for non-migrated customers | System / Loyalty Manager | VP Marketing | 2–3 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM/CDP (links to VS-13 / W156 CDP, VS-126) — the customer-migration tracking
- Marketing (links to VS-14 / W83 campaign) — the re-engagement campaigns
- Analytics (links to VS-28 / W113) — the retention and spend-change computation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Migration-failure risk**: customers don't migrate to the nearest store (they go to a competitor instead); mitigated by the re-engagement campaigns and the welcome offer
- **Attribution-ambiguity risk**: a loyalty member's purchase at a nearby store may not be attributable to the closure (they were already shopping there); mitigated by the pre-closure-shop-pattern baseline

### Automation Opportunity
- Migration auto-tracked from the loyalty-ID; retention auto-computed; non-migrated auto-flagged for re-engagement

### Controls
- CTL-408 (ensure controlled execution — Staff Redeployment & Post-Closure Analytics (PA-59.3).)
- CTL (customer-data privacy — links to VS-91 / W53); operational: VP Marketing monthly review

### Time Estimate
~2.5 hours/month × 12 months/closure = **~30 hours/closure** Loyalty Manager.

## W2280. Post-Closure Financial Impact Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly post-closure |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly for 4 quarters |
| **Volume** | Full impact per closure |
| **Owner** | FP&A Director |
| **Participants** | CEO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compares actual costs vs. estimates; tracks quarterly savings vs. projected; calculates actual payback; reviews surrounding store impact; validates network P&L | FP&A Director | CEO | 2–3 days/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A (links to VS-17 / W26 budget, VS-59 / W2263 business case) — the actual-vs-estimate comparison
- Nearby-store P&L (links to VS-17 / W67 store performance) — the surrounding-store impact
- Analytics (links to VS-28 / W113) — the payback computation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Savings-non-realization risk**: the projected savings don't materialize (nearby stores' costs increase from absorbing the volume); mitigated by the network-P&L view (not just the closed-store P&L)
- **Estimate-vs-actual-divergence risk**: the original business-case estimates were wrong, undermining future closure decisions; mitigated by the estimate-vs-actual reconciliation feeding the next closure model

### Automation Opportunity
- Actual-vs-estimate auto-compared; payback auto-computed; network-P&L auto-assembled

### Controls
- CTL-408 (ensure controlled execution — Staff Redeployment & Post-Closure Analytics (PA-59.3).)
- CTL (financial-impact accuracy — links to VS-17); operational: CEO quarterly review

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/quarter × 4 quarters/closure = **~80 hours/closure** FP&A Director.

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113, VS-33 / competitive intelligence) — the regional sales assessment
- Competitor monitoring (links to VS-57 / W2214 competitor price intelligence) — the competitor-activity data
- Store-performance (links to VS-07 / W67) — the nearby-store performance data

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Competitor-opportunity risk**: a competitor opens in the vacated trade area, capturing the demand BuildRight expected to absorb at nearby stores; mitigated by the defensive-action recommendation (aggressive nearby-store investment, targeted customer retention)
- **Attribution-confound risk**: the regional sales change is confounded by a market shift (a recession, a new entrant) unrelated to the closure; mitigated by the controlled-comparison (market-level baseline)

### Automation Opportunity
- Regional-sales auto-compared pre/post; competitor-activity auto-monitored

### Controls
- CTL-408 (ensure controlled execution — Staff Redeployment & Post-Closure Analytics (PA-59.3).)

### Time Estimate
~3 days/closure × 2 assessments × ~2.5/year = **~120 hours/year** Analytics Manager.

|---|---|---|---|---|

### System Touchpoints
- HR analytics (links to VS-19 / HR-028 engagement survey, VS-103 people analytics) — the survey and retention data
- Performance (links to VS-19 / W72 employee performance) — the post-redeployment performance

## W2284. Network Optimization Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual network review |
| **Frequency** | Annual; 200 stores |
| **Volume** | Full network |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, FP&A Director, CEO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reviews all store performance, identifies future closure candidates, evaluates new opportunities in vacated areas; FP&A models scenarios; optimal network plan to Executive Committee | VP Store Operations / FP&A Director | CEO | 5–10 days + 3–5 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-performance (links to VS-07 / W67, VS-177 field-retail analytics) — the full-network performance
- Network optimization (links to VS-33 / strategic planning, VS-136 supply-chain network design) — the network-modeling
- Site selection (links to VS-20 / W116 site selection) — the new-opportunity evaluation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Network-optimization-as-shrinkage risk**: the analysis recommends closures to optimize the network, but the market interprets it as BuildRight retreating (brand-damage); mitigated by the paired-growth-narrative (for every closure, a new-opening or a nearby-store-investment)
- **Closure-trigger-inertia risk**: the network analysis identifies candidates but no action follows (ExComm defers); mitigated by the decision-deadline tied to the annual-plan cycle

### Automation Opportunity
- Network-performance auto-assembled; scenario-modeling auto-run

### Controls
- CTL-349: board governance — links to VS-36; operational: CEO/ExComm sign-off on the network plan

### Time Estimate
~7.5 days/year + ~4 days modeling = **~90 hours/year** VP Store Operations + FP&A.
