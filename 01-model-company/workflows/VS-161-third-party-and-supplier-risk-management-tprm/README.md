> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Third-Party & Supplier Risk Management (TPRM) workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the
enterprise **third-party risk management discipline** by which BuildRight maintains a single,
unified view of, and risk control over, **every external party** on which its operations depend —
~800–1,000 merchandise vendors, ~80% third-party logistics/fleet (VS-06/VS-56), SaaS/cloud and IT
providers (VS-27/VS-99), payment/acquirer partners (VS-80), BPO/contact-center and managed-service
providers, CIT and security-guard agencies (VS-81/VS-159), sourcing agents (VS-122), marketplace
sellers (VS-95), professional services and EPC contractors — across **all risk domains**
(financial solvency, cyber/infosec, operational resilience, regulatory/compliance, data-privacy,
ESG/human-rights, geographic/geopolitical, concentration, fourth-party) and the full lifecycle
(inventory → tiering → due diligence → continuous monitoring → resilience/exit → governance).

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. Each risk domain owns its **slice**: VS-21.2
W1328 covers supply-chain vendor criticality/concentration (operational/disruption slice only);
VS-21.1 W334 audits vendor risk/compliance (assurance slice); VS-86.3 W2942 covers ABC due
diligence on intermediaries (bribery slice); VS-91.2 W3052 covers data-processor privacy oversight
(privacy slice); VS-125.1 W3863 covers fraud-vendor governance (fraud slice); VS-119.3 W3733 covers
supply-chain integrity DD (integrity slice); VS-131 covers human-rights DD (human-rights slice);
VS-03/VS-67 cover vendor onboarding/performance (commercial slice). But no value stream owns the
**unified, cross-domain enterprise TPRM program** — the single third-party inventory, the
cross-domain risk-tiering methodology, the continuous-monitoring platform, the fourth-party/fifth-
party mapping, the concentration-risk view across ALL third-party types, the resilience/exit
discipline, and the regulator-facing evidence (BSP data-processor, NPC, BIR, DOLE).

This is distinct from **VS-03 (Vendor Management)/VS-67 (Scorecard)** which manage the
**commercial/merchandise vendor relationship and performance** — this value stream owns the
**enterprise risk view across all third-party types and all risk domains**. It is distinct from
**VS-21.2 W1328 (Third-Party Risk Assessment)** which is scoped to **supply-chain disruption** —
this value stream extends the same discipline enterprise-wide across all third parties and all risk
domains, and feeds W1328 with the unified tiering. It is distinct from each **domain slice**
(ABC/privacy/fraud/integrity/human-rights) — this value stream provides the **common inventory,
tiering, monitoring, and governance backbone** that each domain plugs into.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: a PHP 62.3B retailer with ~800–1,000 merchandise vendors, ~80%
outsourced logistics, heavy SaaS/cloud and payment dependencies, BPO, CIT, sourcing agents, and a
growing digital perimeter (ecommerce, marketplace, retail media, AI/ML) — a single significant
third-party failure (financial collapse, cyber breach, regulatory action, concentration shock,
modern-slavery exposure) can halt operations, breach data, or trigger regulator action. Modern TPRM
is a board-level discipline (it is explicitly cited in BSP, NPC, and international frameworks like
ISO 31000 / NIST CSF / SOC 2 supply-chain criteria).
