## W4737. Concentration-Risk Mitigation: Dual-Source, Multi-Vendor & Alternate Qualification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Concentration limit breach (W4725); Critical single-source; risk-reduction initiative |
| **Frequency** | Per breach + continuous qualification |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 Critical sole/single-source categories and single-provider services |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager / Supply (VS-02.3) / Procurement (VS-03) |
| **Participants** | Supply Planning (VS-02.3 W763), Procurement (VS-03), Quality (VS-31), business owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For concentration-limit breaches (W4725) and Critical single-source/sole-provider exposure, develop mitigation: qualify alternate sources/vendors, build safety stock, design substitutable specs, or formally risk-accept where mitigation is infeasible | TPRM Program Manager / Supply / Procurement | TPRM Lead / CRO / COO | Per breach |
| 2 | Execute alternate-source/vendor qualification (quality VS-31, commercial VS-03, resilience DD W4733) and transition volume to reduce concentration to within appetite; coordinate dual-sourcing policy with merchandising (VS-01/VS-03) | Supply / Procurement / Quality | TPRM Lead / COO / VP Merchandising | Per qualification |
| 3 | Monitor concentration against limits (W4725); track mitigation progress; escalate unmitigated concentration above appetite to the board (W4728) | TPRM Program Manager | TPRM Lead / CRO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Concentration analytics (W4725); supply (VS-02.3 W763); procurement (VS-03); quality (VS-31); board reporting (W4728)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mitigation-infeasibility risk**: No alternate source exists for a Critical sole-source (e.g., a proprietary component) → residual concentration; mitigated by formal risk acceptance and compensating resilience (safety stock, contract)
- **Slow-qualification risk**: Alternate qualification too slow → prolonged concentration; mitigated by proactive qualification pipeline

### Time Estimate
- ~per qualification 4–12 weeks; ~monitoring continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Concentration-limit-breach workflow; qualification-pipeline tracking; mitigation-progress dashboards

### Controls
- CTL-715 (ensure controlled execution — Concentration, Resilience, Exit & TPRM Governance/Analytics (PA-161.3).)
- CTL: concentration breaches mitigated or formally risk-accepted; alternates qualified; progress tracked

### Cross-references
- links to W4725 (concentration), VS-02.3 W763 (vendor diversification), VS-31/VS-03 (qualification), W4728 (board)

## W4738. Critical-Third-Party Resilience, Stress-Test & Tabletop Exercise

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual resilience cycle; Critical third party; BCP exercise (VS-26) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on significant change |
| **Volume** | ~40–60 Critical third parties and concentration points |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager / BCP (VS-26) |
| **Participants** | BCP (VS-26), Supply (VS-02.3), IT/DR (VS-27.2), business owners, the third party, Cyber (VS-27.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each Critical third party, conduct resilience stress-tests: scenario analysis (financial failure, cyber breach, operational outage, geopolitical, concentration shock) and assess BuildRight's ability to continue operations within required RTO/RPO (W4733) | TPRM Program Manager / BCP / Business Owners | TPRM Lead / CRO / COO | Annual |
| 2 | Run tabletop exercises (joint with the third party where possible, and with BCP VS-26) simulating Critical third-party failure; test failover, alternates (W4737), communication, and customer/regulator notification | BCP / TPRM Program Manager / Business Owners | TPRM Lead / CRO / COO | Annual |
| 3 | Capture exercise findings; remediate resilience gaps (alternate source, safety stock, contract RTO, BCP update VS-26); track to closure (W4741); report resilience posture to the board (W4728) | TPRM Program Manager / BCP | TPRM Lead / CRO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- BCP (VS-26); supply disruption (VS-02.3 W729); IT DR (VS-27.2 W55); concentration (W4725); board (W4728)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Untested-resilience risk**: A Critical third party's failure assumed survivable without testing → catastrophic surprise; mitigated by annual stress-test/tabletop
- **Third-party-non-participation risk**: A third party unwilling to participate in joint exercises; mitigated by contract clauses and independent scenario analysis

### Time Estimate
- ~annual 2–3 weeks across Critical third parties

### Automation Opportunity
- Exercise-scenario library; findings/remediation tracking; resilience-posture dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-715 (ensure controlled execution — Concentration, Resilience, Exit & TPRM Governance/Analytics (PA-161.3).)
- CTL: Critical third parties stress-tested annually; findings remediated; resilience posture board-reported

### Cross-references
- links to W4733 (RTO/RPO evidence), W4737 (alternates), VS-26 (BCP), VS-02.3 W729 (disruption response), W4741 (remediation)

## W4739. Third-Party Exit, Transition & Succession Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Planned exit (performance, risk, strategic); termination; Critical third party |
| **Frequency** | Per exit |
| **Volume** | ~30–60 exits/year (planned and unplanned, mostly tail suppliers) |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager / Procurement (VS-03) |
| **Participants** | Procurement (VS-03), Legal (VS-100.3), IT (VS-27), business owners, alternate vendor (W4737) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For planned exits (performance/risk/strategic) of material third parties, develop an exit/transition plan: alternate vendor (W4737), data/service migration, transition period, knowledge transfer, and continuity safeguards | TPRM Program Manager / Procurement / Business / IT | TPRM Lead / CRO / COO | Per exit |
| 2 | Maintain succession/exit readiness for Critical third parties (even when no exit is planned) so an unplanned termination (breach W4736, financial failure) can be executed within RTO; include contract exit clauses (step-in, transition assistance, data return) | TPRM Program Manager / Legal | TPRM Lead / VP Legal | Continuous |
| 3 | Execute the transition; manage offboarding (W4740); capture lessons and update DD/tiering/contracts (W4741); settle final accounts (VS-15) and contract close-out | TPRM Program Manager / Procurement / Legal / Finance | TPRM Lead / COO | Per exit |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement (VS-03); contracts (VS-100.3); IT/data migration (VS-27); offboarding (W4740); AP close-out (VS-15)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Exit-impossibility risk**: A Critical third party that cannot be exited (lock-in, no alternate) → entrenched risk; mitigated by succession planning and exit clauses
- **Transition-disruption risk**: A poorly-managed transition disrupts operations; mitigated by the transition plan and continuity safeguards

### Time Estimate
- ~per material exit 4–12 weeks planning + transition

### Automation Opportunity
- Exit/transition playbook templates; succession-readiness scoring; transition-milestone tracking

### Controls
- CTL-715 (ensure controlled execution — Concentration, Resilience, Exit & TPRM Governance/Analytics (PA-161.3).)
- CTL: material exits have a transition plan; Critical third parties have exit readiness; contract exit clauses in place

### Cross-references
- links to W4737 (alternates), W4740 (offboarding), VS-03/VS-15 (procurement/AP), VS-59/VS-130 (closure/M&A context)

## W4740. Offboarding, Data-Return/Deletion & Access-Revocation Assurance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Third-party exit (W4739); termination; contract end |
| **Frequency** | Per offboarding |
| **Volume** | ~30–60 offboardings/year |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager / IT Security (VS-27.3) |
| **Participants** | IT/Security (VS-27.3), Privacy (VS-91), Legal (VS-100.3), Procurement (VS-03), the third party |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On exit/termination, execute offboarding: revoke system access (credentials, VPN, API keys, physical access), recover assets, and disable integrations per VS-27.3 | IT Security / TPRM Program Manager | TPRM Lead / CIO | Per offboarding |
| 2 | Ensure data return and certified deletion of BuildRight/customer/employee data held by the third party (and fourth parties W4724) per the DPA (VS-91) and contract; obtain deletion certification | TPRM Program Manager / Privacy / Legal | TPRM Lead / DPO / VP Legal | Per offboarding |
| 3 | Verify offboarding completion (access revoked, data returned/deleted, assets recovered, final accounts settled VS-15); manage residual obligations (audit rights, confidentiality); retain records (VS-88) | TPRM Program Manager / IT / Legal / Finance | TPRM Lead | Per offboarding |

### System Touchpoints
- Access management (VS-27.3); privacy/DPA (VS-91); contracts (VS-100.3); AP close-out (VS-15); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lingering-access risk**: A terminated vendor retaining system access → breach risk; mitigated by prompt access revocation and verification
- **Data-retention risk**: A vendor retaining BuildRight data post-exit against the DPA → privacy exposure; mitigated by certified deletion

### Time Estimate
- ~per offboarding 1–3 weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- Offboarding checklist; automated access-revocation workflow; deletion-certification tracking

### Controls
- CTL-715 (ensure controlled execution — Concentration, Resilience, Exit & TPRM Governance/Analytics (PA-161.3).)
- CTL: access revoked and verified; data returned/deleted with certification; residual obligations managed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27.3 (access/security), VS-91 (data/DPA), W4724 (fourth-party data), W4739 (exit), VS-15 (AP)

## W4741. TPRM Findings, Remediation & Corrective-Action Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DD finding (W4729); monitoring alert (W4734); incident (W4736); audit |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~40–80 open findings at any time across the third-party base |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Domain owners, business owners, Internal Audit (VS-21), the third parties |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Log all TPRM findings (DD gaps, monitoring alerts, attestation gaps, incidents) in a central register with severity, owner, due-date, and third party; assign corrective actions | TPRM Program Manager / Domain Owners | TPRM Lead / CRO | Continuous |
| 2 | Track corrective actions to closure: third-party remediation (plan, evidence, verification) and BuildRight compensating controls; escalate overdue/high-severity items; link to contract levers (credit, termination W4739) | TPRM Program Manager / Domain Owners / Legal | TPRM Lead / CRO | Continuous |
| 3 | Assure closure quality (verified, not just declared); feed findings/remediation into tiering (W4723), board reporting (W4728), and KRIs (W4743); retain evidence (VS-88) | TPRM Program Manager / Internal Audit | TPRM Lead / CRO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Findings/remediation register; domain-owner systems; contracts (VS-100.3); audit (VS-21); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Open-finding-accumulation risk**: Findings logged but not closed → unmanaged risk; mitigated by tracking + escalation
- **Closure-without-verification risk**: A third party declaring remediation without evidence → false closure; mitigated by verified closure

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous; ~review cadence absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- Findings/remediation register with escalation; third-party remediation-evidence portal; verified-closure workflow

### Controls
- CTL-715 (ensure controlled execution — Concentration, Resilience, Exit & TPRM Governance/Analytics (PA-161.3).)
- CTL: findings logged with owner/due-date; tracked to verified closure; overdue escalated; feeds tiering/reporting

### Cross-references
- links to W4729/W4734/W4736 (sources), W4723 (tiering), W4728 (board), W4743 (KRIs), VS-21 (audit)

| 2 | On regulatory request/inspection, assemble the required TPRM evidence (DD, monitoring, attestations, incident records, contract terms); manage the response with Compliance/Legal (VS-22/VS-100.3) | TPRM Program Manager / Compliance / Legal | TPRM Lead / VP Legal | Per request |

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous readiness; ~per request 1–3 weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- Regulator-evidence binder auto-assembled by regulator; findings tracking

### Controls
- CTL-715 (ensure controlled execution — Concentration, Resilience, Exit & TPRM Governance/Analytics (PA-161.3).)

## W4743. TPRM Analytics, Heat-Map & KRIs

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Periodic reporting; board cycle (VS-36/W4728); KRI threshold |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + continuous |
| **Volume** | 1 TPRM analytics/heat-map/KRI set |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager / CRO |
| **Participants** | Analytics (VS-28), ERM (VS-21.2), domain owners, Board (VS-36) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain TPRM analytics: third-party inventory by type/tier/risk-domain, a risk heat-map (Critical/High exposure by domain), concentration metrics (W4725), DD/monitoring coverage, findings/remediation (W4741), and incident trends (W4736) | TPRM Program Manager / Analytics | TPRM Lead / CRO | Quarterly |
| 2 | Maintain TPRM KRIs (Critical-third-party count, concentration ratio, DD-coverage %, attestation-lapse count, incident frequency/severity, remediation-overdue count) integrated with ERM KRIs (VS-21.2); alert on threshold breach | TPRM Program Manager / CRO | CRO | Continuous |
| 3 | Report the heat-map/KRIs to the board (W4728) and leadership; drive risk-reduction initiatives from the analytics (W4737/W4738); assure data quality | TPRM Program Manager / CRO / Analytics | CRO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics (VS-28); ERM/KRIs (VS-21.2); TPRM platform; board reporting (VS-36/W4728)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vanity-metric risk**: Activity metrics (DDs done) without outcome KRIs (residual exposure, incidents) → false assurance; mitigated by outcome-oriented KRIs
- **KRI-isolation risk**: TPRM KRIs not integrated with ERM → fragmented risk view; mitigated by ERM integration (VS-21.2)

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days/quarter analytics + reporting

### Automation Opportunity
- TPRM analytics/heat-map dashboards; KRI threshold-alerting; ERM integration

### Controls
- CTL-715 (ensure controlled execution — Concentration, Resilience, Exit & TPRM Governance/Analytics (PA-161.3).)
- CTL: outcome-oriented KRIs ERM-integrated; heat-map board-reported; analytics drives risk reduction

### Cross-references
- links to VS-28/VS-21.2 (analytics/ERM), W4725 (concentration), W4728 (board), W4741 (findings)

| **Frequency** | Annual + continuous |
| **Volume** | 1 TPRM program |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager / CRO |
| **Participants** | Internal Audit (VS-21), Compliance (VS-22), External Auditor, Op Excellence (VS-133) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assure the TPRM program via Internal Audit (VS-21) and the third-party vendor audit (VS-21.1 W334): coverage completeness, DD/monitoring effectiveness, findings closure, and governance; manage audit findings (W4741) | TPRM Program Manager / Internal Audit | CRO | Annual |
| 2 | Continuously improve (VS-133): benchmark to standards (ISO 31000, NIST CSF supply-chain, SOC 2), refresh the framework/taxonomy (W4722), and adopt automation (monitoring, DD); capture lessons from incidents (W4736) | TPRM Program Manager / Op Excellence | CRO | Continuous |

- Audit (VS-21/VS-21.1 W334); compliance (VS-22); op excellence (VS-133); ERM/BCP (VS-21.2/VS-26); board (VS-36)

- **Program-stagnation risk**: A TPRM program not benchmarked/improved → declining effectiveness vs. evolving third-party risk; mitigated by benchmarking and continuous improvement (VS-133)

### Time Estimate
- ~annual 1–2 weeks assurance + improvement

### Automation Opportunity
- Program-maturity assessment; benchmark library; improvement-backlog tracking

### Controls
- CTL-715 (ensure controlled execution — Concentration, Resilience, Exit & TPRM Governance/Analytics (PA-161.3).)
