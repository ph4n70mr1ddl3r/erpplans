## W4729. Risk-Based Due Diligence Library & Assessment Workflow (Pre-Contract & Periodic)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New Critical/High third party; periodic re-DD; material change |
| **Frequency** | Pre-contract + periodic (tier-driven cadence) |
| **Volume** | ~80–120 Critical/High third parties (deepest DD); ~300–500 Medium (standard); remainder Low (light) |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Domain owners (ABC/privacy/cyber/ESG/financial), Procurement (VS-03), Legal (VS-100.3), business owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the risk-based DD library: tier-driven DD packs (Critical/High = full cross-domain DD; Medium = standard; Low = light), each combining the domain-specific DD (cyber W4730, privacy W4731, ABC W4732, resilience W4733, financial/ESG W4727) | TPRM Program Manager / Domain Owners | TPRM Lead / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Continuous |
| 2 | Run the DD assessment workflow per third party pre-contract and periodically: issue the pack, collect responses/evidence, score, and feed findings into tiering (W4723) and contract terms | TPRM Program Manager / Domain Owners / Procurement | TPRM Lead / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Per third party |
| 3 | Document DD conclusions and residual risk; obtain risk acceptance for high-residual third parties per the appetite (W4722); retain evidence (VS-88) | TPRM Program Manager / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Per third party |

### System Touchpoints
- TPRM platform (DD library/workflow); domain-owner systems; procurement (VS-03); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **DD-fatigue risk**: Same DD requested redundantly by each domain → vendor fatigue and inconsistent conclusions; mitigated by the unified DD library
- **Risk-acceptance-untracked risk**: High-residual risk accepted informally → unmanaged; mitigated by documented risk acceptance per appetite

### Time Estimate
- ~per Critical/High DD 1–3 weeks; ~Medium/Low lighter

### Automation Opportunity
- Tier-driven DD pack assembly; vendor portal for DD response; findings-to-tiering integration

### Controls
- CTL-714 (ensure controlled execution — Due Diligence, Continuous Monitoring & Evidence Lifecycle (PA-161.2).)
- CTL: tier-driven DD applied; findings feed tiering; high-residual risk formally accepted; evidence retained

### Cross-references
- links to W4722/W4723 (appetite/tiering), W4730–W4733 (domain DD), W4727 (financial/ESG), VS-88 (records)

## W4730. Cybersecurity & IT-Controls Attestation (SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / PCI DSS) Collection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New IT/SaaS/payment third party; attestation expiry; cyber DD |
| **Frequency** | Per third party + annual attestation refresh |
| **Volume** | ~80–150 IT/SaaS/cloud/payment/marketplace third parties handling data or integrated to BuildRight systems |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager (joint with Cyber VS-27.3) |
| **Participants** | Cyber (VS-27.3 W367), IT (VS-27), Privacy (VS-91), the third party |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each IT/SaaS/payment third party, collect cybersecurity & IT-controls attestation appropriate to the data/risk: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, PCI DSS (for payment data), or a security questionnaire for lower-tier; extend enterprise-wide the cyber-vendor slice in VS-27.3 W367 | TPRM Program Manager / Cyber / IT | TPRM Lead / CIO | Per third party + annual |
| 2 | Review the attestation for coverage and gaps (scope, exceptions, qualified opinions); require remediation/compensating controls for gaps; coordinate with cyber (VS-27.3) on penetration-test/monitoring integration | TPRM Program Manager / Cyber | TPRM Lead / CIO | Per attestation |
| 3 | Track attestation expiry and refresh (W4735); manage third-party cyber incidents (W4736) and integration-risk (VS-27.3/VS-113); retain evidence (VS-88) | TPRM Program Manager / Cyber / IT | TPRM Lead / CIO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Cyber (VS-27.3 W367); attestation repository; IT integration risk (VS-27/VS-113); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Attestation-scope-mismatch risk**: A SOC 2 that doesn't cover the service BuildRight uses → false assurance; mitigated by scope review
- **Unremediated-gap risk**: A cyber gap not remediated → breach exposure; mitigated by remediation/compensating-control requirement

### Time Estimate
- ~per attestation collection+review 2–5 days

### Automation Opportunity
- Attestation repository with scope-mapping; expiry-alerts; gap-remediation tracking

### Controls
- CTL-714 (ensure controlled execution — Due Diligence, Continuous Monitoring & Evidence Lifecycle (PA-161.2).)
- CTL: attestation appropriate to data/risk; scope reviewed; gaps remediated; refresh tracked (W4735)

### Cross-references
- extends VS-27.3 W367 (cyber-vendor governance); links to VS-113 (integration), VS-91 (privacy), W4735 (renewal), W4736 (incidents)

## W4731. Data-Privacy & Processor Due Diligence (DPA, Transfer Mechanism)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New data-handling third party; privacy review (VS-91); sub-processor change |
| **Frequency** | Per third party + on change |
| **Volume** | ~80–150 data-handling third parties (SaaS, BPO, payment, marketplace, CDP/fraud) |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager (joint with Privacy/DPO VS-91) |
| **Participants** | Privacy/DPO (VS-91.2 W3052), Legal (VS-100.3), Cyber (VS-27.3), the third party |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each data-handling third party, conduct privacy & processor DD: Data Processing Agreement (DPA), purpose limitation, sub-processor disclosure (W4724), cross-border transfer mechanism (vs. Philippine DPA RA 10173 and destination law), data-subject-rights support, breach-notification obligations — extends VS-91.2 W3052 enterprise-wide | TPRM Program Manager / DPO / Legal | TPRM Lead / DPO | Per third party |
| 2 | Coordinate Privacy Impact Assessment (VS-91.2 DPIA) for high-risk data processing; ensure the DPA covers BuildRight's obligations (VS-91) and the processor's; manage cross-border transfers | TPRM Program Manager / DPO / Legal | TPRM Lead / DPO / VP Legal | Per third party |
| 3 | Monitor sub-processor changes (a processor adding a fourth party per VS-91.2 W3052 requires notice/objection); manage privacy incidents (W4736) and data-return/deletion on exit (W4740) | TPRM Program Manager / DPO | TPRM Lead / DPO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Privacy/DPIA (VS-91.2 W3052); contracts (VS-100.3); cyber (VS-27.3); records (VS-88); TPRM platform

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-DPA risk**: A processor handling personal data without a DPA → RA 10173 violation; mitigated by mandatory DPA before data handling
- **Cross-border-transfer risk**: Personal data transferred abroad without a valid mechanism → violation; mitigated by transfer-mechanism assessment

### Time Estimate
- ~per data-handling third party 2–4 days DD

### Automation Opportunity
- DPA/template library; sub-processor-change workflow; DPIA integration (VS-91.2)

### Controls
- CTL-714 (ensure controlled execution — Due Diligence, Continuous Monitoring & Evidence Lifecycle (PA-161.2).)
- CTL: DPA in place before data handling; transfer mechanism valid; sub-processor changes managed; extends W3052

### Cross-references
- extends VS-91.2 W3052 (data-processor oversight); links to W4724 (fourth party), W4740 (exit data-return), VS-91 (privacy program)

## W4733. Operational, Resilience & BCP/DR Evidence Collection (RTO/RPO, Substitutes)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New Critical third party; BCP review (VS-26); periodic refresh |
| **Frequency** | Per Critical third party + annual |
| **Volume** | ~40–60 Critical third parties whose failure would disrupt operations |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager (joint with BCP VS-26) |
| **Participants** | BCP (VS-26), Supply (VS-02.3), IT/DR (VS-27.2 W55), business owners, the third party |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each Critical third party, collect operational-resilience & BCP/DR evidence: the third party's BCP/DR plan, RTO/RPO, recovery-site, dependency on its own fourth parties (W4724), substitute availability, and recent test results — extends VS-21.2 W1328/W334 enterprise-wide | TPRM Program Manager / BCP / Supply | TPRM Lead / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Per Critical third party + annual |
| 2 | Assess whether the third party's RTO/RPO meets BuildRight's requirements (operations continuity, VS-26); require gaps be remediated or compensating resilience (alternate source W4737, safety stock) | TPRM Program Manager / BCP | TPRM Lead / Head of Internal Audit & Risk / COO | Per third party |
| 3 | Coordinate resilience testing (joint exercise W4738); integrate third-party BCP into BuildRight's BCP (VS-26) and supply-chain disruption response (VS-02.3 W729); retain evidence (VS-88) | TPRM Program Manager / BCP / Supply | TPRM Lead / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- BCP (VS-26); supply disruption (VS-02.3 W729); IT DR (VS-27.2 W55); resilience testing (W4738); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **False-resilience-assurance risk**: A third party's BCP claim not tested → failure in a real event; mitigated by evidence and joint testing (W4738)
- **RTO-mismatch risk**: A third party's RTO longer than BuildRight can tolerate → disruption; mitigated by the RTO/RPO assessment

### Time Estimate
- ~per Critical third party 1–2 days evidence collection

### Automation Opportunity
- Resilience-evidence repository; RTO/RPO-vs-requirement matching; test-result tracking

### Controls
- CTL-714 (ensure controlled execution — Due Diligence, Continuous Monitoring & Evidence Lifecycle (PA-161.2).)
- CTL: Critical third-party BCP/DR evidence collected; RTO/RPO meets BuildRight requirements; tested jointly (W4738); extends W1328/W334

### Cross-references
- extends VS-21.2 W1328/W334; links to VS-26 (BCP), VS-02.3 (supply disruption), VS-27.2 W55 (IT DR), W4738 (testing)

## W4734. Continuous Monitoring Platform: Financial, Cyber, Compliance, Performance Feed

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous; alert threshold |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (24/7 for Critical) |
| **Volume** | ~80–120 Critical/High third parties monitored continuously |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Cyber (VS-27.3), Financial (Treasury VS-18/SCF VS-105), ABC (VS-86), performance owners (VS-67) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Operate the continuous-monitoring platform: feeds of financial health (credit/financials), cyber posture (breach news, rating services), compliance (sanctions/adverse-media W4732, certifications W4735), and performance (SLA/scorecard VS-67) for Critical/High third parties | TPRM Program Manager / Domain Owners | TPRM Lead / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Continuous |
| 2 | Triage alerts (financial deterioration, breach, sanctions hit, SLA breach, news event); escalate material changes to the tiering (W4723) and domain slices; trigger re-DD (W4729) where warranted | TPRM Program Manager / Domain Owners | TPRM Lead / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Per alert |
| 3 | Maintain monitoring within data/legal bounds (VS-91/VS-100.3); retain intelligence (VS-88); report monitoring KRIs (W4743) | TPRM Program Manager | TPRM Lead / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Continuous-monitoring platform; financial/cyber/compliance/performance feeds; domain-owner systems; records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Alert-fatigue risk**: High alert volume without triage → real signals missed; mitigated by tuned thresholds and domain-triage
- **Point-in-time-DD-only risk**: DD done once at onboarding and never refreshed → stale risk view; mitigated by continuous monitoring

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous (platform + analyst triage)

### Automation Opportunity
- Integrated monitoring platform; alert-triage workflow; re-DD auto-trigger

### Controls
- CTL-714 (ensure controlled execution — Due Diligence, Continuous Monitoring & Evidence Lifecycle (PA-161.2).)
- CTL: Critical/High continuously monitored; alerts triaged; material changes escalate to tiering/DD; monitoring lawful

### Cross-references

## W4736. Third-Party Incident, Breach & Performance-Degradation Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Third-party incident (cyber breach, financial failure, operational outage, compliance breach, performance degradation) |
| **Frequency** | Per incident |
| **Volume** | ~25–50 incidents/year across the third-party base (mostly minor lapses) |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager / Head of Internal Audit & Risk |
| **Participants** | Cyber IR (VS-27.3 W383), Privacy (VS-91), BCP (VS-26), Fraud (VS-125), Legal (VS-100.3), business owners, the third party |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake and triage third-party incidents: cyber breach (coordinate VS-27.3 W383), privacy breach (VS-91 72-hour notification), operational outage (VS-26 BCP activation), financial failure (supply disruption VS-02.3), compliance breach (VS-22), performance degradation (VS-67) | TPRM Program Manager / Domain Owners | TPRM Lead / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Per incident |
| 2 | Manage incident response: activate BuildRight's response per the affected domain, demand the third party's incident response, assess BuildRight impact (data/operations/customers), and coordinate notification (regulators, customers, board W4728) as required | TPRM Program Manager / Domain Owners / Legal | TPRM Lead / Head of Internal Audit & Risk / VP Legal | Per incident |
| 3 | Conduct post-incident review; capture lessons; adjust tiering (W4723), DD (W4729), monitoring (W4734), and contracts; manage third-party accountability (remediation, credit, termination W4740) | TPRM Program Manager / Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Legal | TPRM Lead / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Post-incident |

### System Touchpoints
- Cyber IR (VS-27.3 W383); privacy breach (VS-91); BCP (VS-26); fraud (VS-125); legal (VS-100); board (W4728)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Slow-incident-detection risk**: A third-party breach detected late → enlarged impact and missed notification deadlines (NPC 72-hour); mitigated by continuous monitoring (W4734) and breach-notification clauses
- **Accountability-vacuum risk**: A third-party incident with no clear BuildRight owner → uncoordinated response; mitigated by the ownership matrix (W4728)

### Time Estimate
- ~per incident 1–4 weeks response + review

### Automation Opportunity
- Incident-intake workflow; cross-domain response coordination; breach-notification-deadline tracking

### Controls
- CTL-714 (ensure controlled execution — Due Diligence, Continuous Monitoring & Evidence Lifecycle (PA-161.2).)
- CTL: third-party incidents triaged and managed per domain; notification deadlines met (VS-91); post-incident lessons captured

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27.3 W383 (cyber IR), VS-91 (privacy breach), VS-26 (BCP), VS-125 (fraud), W4728 (ownership/board), W4740 (termination)
