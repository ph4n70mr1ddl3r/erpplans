## W4721. Enterprise Third-Party Inventory & Relationship Register

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New third-party onboarding; periodic inventory reconcile; TPRM setup |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + quarterly reconcile |
| **Volume** | 1 enterprise register; ~800–1,000 merchandise vendors + ~hundreds of non-merchandise/SaaS/3PL/payment/BPO/CIT/professional third parties |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager / Third-Party Risk Lead |
| **Participants** | Procurement (VS-03/VS-34), Vendor Mgmt (VS-03), IT (VS-27), Finance (VS-17), Compliance (VS-22), business owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the enterprise third-party inventory & relationship register: every external party with type (merchandise vendor, 3PL, SaaS/cloud, payment, BPO, CIT, security agency, sourcing agent, marketplace seller, professional/EPC), owning business/function, contract, spend, criticality tier (W4723), data-access posture (W4726), fourth-party exposure (W4724), and risk-domain scores | TPRM Program Manager / Procurement / IT | TPRM Lead / CRO | Continuous |
| 2 | Reconcile the register to procurement (VS-03/VS-34), AP (VS-15), IT/SaaS (VS-99), and contracts (VS-100.3) quarterly; flag third parties transacting without a register entry ("shadow third parties") | TPRM Program Manager / Procurement / Finance | TPRM Lead / CRO | Quarterly |
| 3 | Govern the register as the single source feeding each risk-domain slice (ABC VS-86.3, privacy VS-91.2, fraud VS-125.1, integrity VS-119.3, supply VS-21.2 W1328, audit VS-21.1 W334) | TPRM Program Manager | TPRM Lead / CRO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- TPRM platform/register; procurement/vendor (VS-03/VS-34); AP (VS-15); IT/SaaS (VS-99); contracts (VS-100.3); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Shadow-third-party risk**: A SaaS/BPO/provider transacting without a register entry → unmanaged risk; mitigated by the AP/procurement reconcile that surfaces unregistered spend
- **Fragmented-inventory risk**: Each function keeping its own vendor list → no enterprise view; mitigated by the single register

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous; ~3–5 days/quarter reconcile

### Automation Opportunity
- Register auto-populated from procurement/AP/IT/contracts; shadow-third-party detection from AP spend

### Controls
- CTL-713 (ensure controlled execution — Third-Party Inventory, Tiering & Cross-Domain Risk Framework (PA-161.1).)
- CTL: single enterprise register; reconciled quarterly to AP/procurement/IT; feeds all risk-domain slices

### Cross-references
- links to VS-03/VS-34 (procurement), VS-15 (AP), VS-99 (IT asset), VS-100.3 (contracts), W4723 (tiering); consolidates W1328/W334/W2942/W3052/W3863/W3733 scope

## W4722. TPRM Framework, Risk Taxonomy & Cross-Domain Risk Appetite

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | TPRM setup; annual framework review; ERM refresh (VS-21.2) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | 1 TPRM framework |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager / CRO |
| **Participants** | CRO (VS-21.2), Compliance (VS-22), Cyber (VS-27.3), Privacy (VS-91), Legal (VS-100.3), Procurement (VS-03) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the TPRM framework and risk taxonomy: risk domains (financial, cyber/infosec, operational/resilience, regulatory/compliance, data-privacy, ESG/human-rights, geographic/geopolitical, concentration, fourth-party), inherent/residual scoring methodology, and risk-appetite per domain aligned to ERM (VS-21.2) | TPRM Program Manager / CRO | CRO / CEO | Annual |
| 2 | Map each risk domain to its domain-owner workflow (ABC VS-86.3, privacy VS-91.2, fraud VS-125.1, integrity VS-119.3, human-rights VS-131, supply VS-21.2, cyber VS-27.3, financial VS-105/VS-68); define how the TPRM backbone feeds and is fed by each | TPRM Program Manager / Domain Owners | CRO | Annual |
| 3 | Obtain leadership endorsement (CRO/CEO) and align to international standards (ISO 31000, NIST CSF supply-chain, SOC 2); publish the framework and train business/procurement owners | TPRM Program Manager / CRO | CRO / CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ERM/risk taxonomy (VS-21.2); TPRM platform; domain-owner systems (VS-86/VS-91/VS-125/VS-131/VS-27.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Domain-silo risk**: Each domain scoring in isolation → no cross-domain view of a single third party; mitigated by the cross-domain taxonomy and shared register
- **Appetite-misalignment risk**: TPRM appetite not aligned to ERM → inconsistent decisions; mitigated by ERM alignment (VS-21.2)

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks annual framework

### Automation Opportunity
- Cross-domain scoring model; appetite-linked tiering; domain-owner workflow integration

### Controls
- CTL-167 (ensure third-party risk tiering & oversight.)
- CTL: cross-domain taxonomy ERM-aligned; domain-owners mapped; framework leadership-endorsed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2 (ERM), VS-86/VS-91/VS-125/VS-131/VS-27.3 (domain owners), W4723 (tiering)

## W4723. Third-Party Tiering & Criticality Methodology (Inherent/Residual, All Domains)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New third party; annual re-tier; material change |
| **Frequency** | Per third party + annual re-tier |
| **Volume** | ~all enterprise third parties (W4721) |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Business/function owners, domain owners, CRO (VS-21.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tier each third party by inherent risk across all domains (W4722): spend/materiality, criticality to operations (sole/single-source, revenue-impact), data-access (W4726), fourth-party exposure (W4724), and domain-specific exposure; assign Criticality Tier (Critical/High/Medium/Low) driving the DD depth (PA-161.2) and monitoring cadence | TPRM Program Manager / Domain Owners | TPRM Lead / CRO | Per third party |
| 2 | Compute residual risk after controls/DD (PA-161.2); escalate high-residual third parties; this extends enterprise-wide the supply-side tiering in VS-21.2 W1328 (which is scoped to merchandise-supply criticality) | TPRM Program Manager / CRO | CRO | Annual re-tier |
| 3 | Maintain tiering transparency: document the scoring inputs and rationale; re-tier on material change (new data-access, acquisition, performance/financial deterioration); feed tiering to W1328 and each domain slice | TPRM Program Manager | TPRM Lead / CRO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- TPRM platform (scoring/tiering); ERM (VS-21.2); register (W4721); domain-owner systems

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-tiering risk**: A critical third party tiered low → insufficient DD/monitoring → undetected risk; mitigated by the multi-factor inherent scoring
- **Static-tiering risk**: A tier not updated after a material change → stale risk view; mitigated by the material-change trigger and annual re-tier

### Time Estimate
- ~per third party 0.5–1 day initial; ~annual re-tier absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- Multi-factor scoring engine; material-change detection (data-access/spend/news); tier-driven DD/monitoring automation

### Controls
- CTL-713 (ensure controlled execution — Third-Party Inventory, Tiering & Cross-Domain Risk Framework (PA-161.1).)
- CTL: inherent/residual scoring documented; re-tier on material change + annual; extends W1328 enterprise-wide

### Cross-references
- extends VS-21.2 W1328 (supply-side tiering); links to W4721 (register), W4722 (taxonomy), W4724/W4726 (inputs)

## W4724. Fourth-Party (Sub-Processor / Sub-Contractor) Risk Identification & Mapping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New third party with sub-contracting; annual mapping; data-processor flow |
| **Frequency** | Per third party + annual |
| **Volume** | ~ fourth parties (SaaS sub-processors, 3PL sub-carriers, BPO sub-vendors) |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Privacy (VS-91), Cyber (VS-27.3), Procurement (VS-03), Legal (VS-100.3), third parties |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify fourth parties (sub-processors, sub-contractors) for each Critical/High third party — especially data-processing fourth parties (SaaS cloud/hosting sub-processors per VS-91) and logistics sub-carriers (VS-06/VS-56); require disclosure in contracts | TPRM Program Manager / Privacy / Procurement | TPRM Lead / CRO | Per third party + annual |
| 2 | Map fourth-party exposure: which BuildRight data/processes touch which fourth party; assess concentration (a fourth party serving many BuildRight third parties = hidden concentration W4725) and flow-down obligations (privacy VS-91, security, ABC) | TPRM Program Manager / Privacy / Cyber | TPRM Lead / CRO | Annual |
| 3 | Require flow-down of DD/controls/audit rights to fourth parties in contracts; monitor fourth-party changes (a third party adding a sub-processor per VS-91.2 W3052); manage unacceptable fourth-party exposure (W4739/W4740) | TPRM Program Manager / Legal / Procurement | TPRM Lead / VP Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- TPRM platform (fourth-party mapping); privacy (VS-91.2 W3052); contracts (VS-100.3); cyber (VS-27.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden-fourth-party risk**: A SaaS vendor's cloud sub-processor unknown to BuildRight → unmanaged privacy/security risk; mitigated by disclosure requirements and mapping
- **Hidden-concentration risk**: A single fourth party serving many BuildRight third parties → systemic concentration (W4725); mitigated by fourth-party mapping

### Time Estimate
- ~per Critical/High third party 1–2 days mapping; ~annual refresh

### Automation Opportunity
- Fourth-party disclosure/mapping platform; flow-down clause library; sub-processor-change alerts (VS-91.2)

### Controls
- CTL-167 (ensure third-party risk tiering & oversight.)
- CTL: fourth parties disclosed and mapped; flow-down obligations in contracts; unacceptable exposure managed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-91.2 W3052 (data-processor/sub-processor), VS-100.3 (contracts), W4725 (concentration), W4739 (resilience)

## W4725. Concentration, Single-Source & Geographic Risk Exposure Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual analysis; new significant third party; geopolitical event |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on event |
| **Volume** | 1 concentration view across all third-party types and geographies |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager / CRO |
| **Participants** | Supply Planning (VS-02), Procurement (VS-03), Treasury (VS-18), Cyber (VS-27.3), Sourcing (VS-122) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analyze concentration across all third-party types: sole/single-source vendors (cement, steel, cable), single 3PL/payment/SaaS/cloud providers, geographic concentration (single port/region/country of origin — VS-02.2/VS-110), and fourth-party concentration (W4724) | TPRM Program Manager / Supply / Procurement / Cyber | TPRM Lead / CRO | Annual + on event |
| 2 | Quantify concentration exposure (revenue/COGS/operations at risk from a single point of failure); set concentration limits per risk-appetite (W4722); extend the supply-side concentration view in VS-21.2 W1328 to all third-party types | TPRM Program Manager / CRO | CRO / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Drive mitigation for excessive concentration (dual-source/multi-vendor W4737, alternate qualification, geographic diversification); monitor concentration as a KRI (W4743) and report to the board (W4728) | TPRM Program Manager / Supply / Procurement | TPRM Lead / CRO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- TPRM platform (concentration analytics); supply (VS-02.3 W558/W763); treasury (VS-18); cyber (VS-27.3); ERM KRIs (VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden-concentration risk**: Concentration obscured by entity/contract structure (e.g., two "different" vendors owned by one parent, or shared fourth party); mitigated by the unified concentration view
- **Concentration-limit-breach risk**: Concentration exceeding appetite unaddressed; mitigated by limits and mitigation routing (W4737)

### Time Estimate
- ~annual 1–2 weeks; ~event-triggered absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- Concentration analytics across register/fourth-party/geography; limit-breach alerts; KRI dashboards

### Controls
- CTL-167 (ensure third-party risk tiering & oversight.)
- CTL: concentration analyzed annually + on event; limits per appetite; breaches mitigated (W4737); extends W1328

### Cross-references
- extends VS-21.2 W1328 (supply concentration); links to VS-02.3 (supply risk), W4724 (fourth party), W4737 (mitigation), W4743 (KRIs)

## W4726. Third-Party Data Classification & Data-Access/Processing Risk Scoping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New third party; data-access change; privacy review (VS-91) |
| **Frequency** | Per third party + on change |
| **Volume** | ~ all third parties with data access (SaaS, BPO, payment, marketplace, fraud/CDP providers) |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager (joint with Privacy VS-91) |
| **Participants** | Privacy (VS-91), Cyber (VS-27.3), Data Governance (VS-28), Legal (VS-100.3), business owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each third party, scope data-access: what BuildRight/customer/employee data the third party (and fourth parties W4724) can access/process (PII VS-91, payment data PCI, credentials, IP); classify the data sensitivity | TPRM Program Manager / Privacy / Cyber | TPRM Lead / DPO | Per third party |
| 2 | Determine the data-protection posture required (DPA, transfer mechanism for cross-border, encryption, access controls, retention/disposal) per VS-91; scope privacy DD (W4731) and cyber DD (W4730) accordingly | TPRM Program Manager / Privacy / Cyber / Legal | TPRM Lead / DPO / CIO | Per third party |
| 3 | Monitor data-access changes (scope creep, new data type, sub-processor W4724); manage unauthorized data-access/use; ensure data-return/deletion on exit (W4740) | TPRM Program Manager / Privacy / Cyber | TPRM Lead / DPO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Privacy/DPIA (VS-91.2); cyber (VS-27.3); data governance (VS-28); contracts (VS-100.3); TPRM platform

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Scope-creep risk**: A third party expanding data use beyond the DPA; mitigated by change-monitoring

### Time Estimate
- ~per data-handling third party 1–2 days scoping

### Automation Opportunity
- Data-access inventory/scoping tool; DPA/scope-change monitoring; sensitivity-driven DD depth

### Controls
- CTL-167 (ensure third-party risk tiering & oversight.)
- CTL: data-access scoped and classified; DPA/transfer mechanism per VS-91; changes monitored; exit data-return (W4740)

### Cross-references

## W4728. TPRM Governance, Ownership Matrix & Board Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Governance review; board cycle (VS-36); significant event |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + on event |
| **Volume** | 1 TPRM governance & reporting cadence |
| **Owner** | TPRM Program Manager / CRO |
| **Participants** | CRO (VS-21.2), Compliance (VS-22), business/function owners, Board Risk Committee (VS-36) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the TPRM governance & ownership matrix: each third party has a business owner (the function that uses it) and a risk-domain owner (privacy/cyber/ABC/etc.); the TPRM Program owns the backbone; define escalation paths | TPRM Program Manager / CRO | CRO | Annual + continuous |
| 2 | Report TPRM to the board risk committee (VS-36) quarterly: third-party inventory summary, Critical/High exposure, concentration (W4725), significant findings (PA-161.2), resilience posture (PA-161.3); integrate into ERM reporting (VS-21.2) | TPRM Program Manager / CRO | CRO / Board | Quarterly |
| 3 | Manage significant TPRM events (Critical third-party failure, breach, concentration shock) with executive/board notification per the ERM escalation (VS-21.2/W4694); assure via Internal Audit (VS-21) | TPRM Program Manager / CRO / Internal Audit | CRO / Board | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- ERM/board reporting (VS-21.2/VS-36); TPRM platform; audit (VS-21); incident/crisis (VS-159 W4694)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Ownership-vacuum risk**: A third party with no clear business/risk owner → unmanaged; mitigated by the ownership matrix
- **Board-blindness risk**: Board unaware of significant third-party exposure → governance failure; mitigated by quarterly reporting

### Time Estimate
- ~quarterly 2–3 days reporting; ~governance continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Ownership-matrix workflow; board-report auto-roll; significant-event escalation

### Controls
- CTL-167 (ensure third-party risk tiering & oversight.)
- CTL: every third party has a business + risk-domain owner; board reported quarterly; significant events escalated (VS-21.2)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2/VS-36 (ERM/board), VS-21 (audit), VS-159 W4694 (incident escalation), W4743 (KRIs)
