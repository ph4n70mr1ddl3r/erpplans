## W2126. In-Store Warranty Claim Initiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer brings defective product with proof of purchase to store |
| **Frequency** | ~8,000–10,000 claims/month across 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~8K–10K/month; peaks Jan–Feb, Jun–Jul |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Rep |
| **Participants** | CSR, Dept Supervisor, Store Manager, CRM |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CSR verifies warranty in CRM; inspects product; photographs defect; checks serial number match | CSR | Store Manager | 10–15 min |
| 2 | System classifies claim: standard (auto-approve), borderline (route to Supervisor), out-of-warranty (offer paid repair) | System | CSR | Automated |
| 3 | CSR offers resolution: immediate replacement, store credit, service center repair, or DC/vendor replacement (7–14 days) | CSR | Customer | 5–10 min |
| 4 | Customer selects resolution; system processes inventory adjustment, creates claim record, notifies vendor | CSR / System | Store Manager | 5 min |
