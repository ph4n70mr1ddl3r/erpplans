## W2118. POS Warranty Capture at Checkout

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer purchases warranty-eligible product at POS or online |
| **Frequency** | ~420,000 warranty-eligible transactions/month |
| **Volume** | ~420K transactions/month across 200 stores + ecommerce |
| **Owner** | Cashier / POS System |
| **Participants** | Cashier, Customer, POS System, CRM |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | POS auto-identifies warranty-eligible SKU at scan; prompts cashier; captures transaction date, SKU, serial number, store, loyalty ID; generates warranty record in CRM | POS System | Cashier | 30–60 sec |
| 2 | Cashier prints warranty card with terms, duration, claim instructions, and registration QR code; customer scans for digital warranty wallet | Cashier | Store Manager | 30 sec |
| 3 | For serial-numbered items: cashier scans or enters serial number; system validates format and links to item+transaction record | Cashier | Cashier | 15–30 sec |
| 4 | System pushes registration to vendor portal (real-time for top 20 vendors; batch daily for others); customer receives SMS/email confirmation | System | IT Integration Lead | Automated |
