# VS-91: Consumer Data Privacy & Data Protection Program

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Consumer Data Privacy & Data Protection Program workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — governing
the privacy of personal data collected from ~600,000 loyalty members, ~5,400 B2B contacts,
~42,900 monthly ecommerce buyers, ~515,000 annual ecommerce orders, marketplace-channel buyers,
app users, and CCTV-captured visitors — across 200 stores, 4 DCs, the web/app, marketplaces, and
third-party processors — in compliance with the Philippine **Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)**, NPC
Implementing Rules, and applicable cross-border transfer rules.

While **VS-22** owns regulatory permits/licenses and **VS-27** owns IT security operations, and
**W647** (in VS-19) handles employee data privacy operations, this value stream owns the
**consumer data privacy program**: NPC registration and DPO operations, personal data inventory
and Records of Processing Activities (ROPA), consent/preference management, data subject rights
(DSAR) fulfillment, privacy impact assessments (PIA/DPIA), vendor/processor privacy due
diligence, and personal data breach detection and 72-hour NPC notification.
