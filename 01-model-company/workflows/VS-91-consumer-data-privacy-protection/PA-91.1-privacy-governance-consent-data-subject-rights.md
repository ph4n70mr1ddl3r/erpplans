## W3041. Data Privacy Program Governance, NPC Registration & DPO Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program setup, annual review, or regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + annual review |
| **Volume** | Enterprise-wide; 1 DPO, ~5 legal entities |
| **Owner** | Data Protection Officer (DPO) |
| **Participants** | DPO, Legal, Compliance, IT Security, HR, Marketing, Customer Service |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DPO maintains the privacy program: charter, privacy policy, data governance framework, and NPC registration/renewal for all 5 legal entities and personal-data processing | DPO | General Counsel | Annual + event |
| 2 | DPO chairs the Privacy Steering Committee (Legal, IT Security, HR, Marketing, CX); sets risk appetite, priorities, and resourcing; reports to board (links to VS-36) | DPO | General Counsel | Quarterly |
| 3 | DPO maintains privacy policy and notices across touchpoints (web/app, store signage, loyalty enrollment, CCTV) and ensures lawful-basis documentation per processing activity | DPO / Legal | DPO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Privacy policy/notice CMS; NPC registration record; board reporting (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Governance risk**: Program under-resourced or fragmented; mitigated by dedicated DPO and steering committee


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-report (and resourcing; reports to board (links)
- auto-document (ensures lawful-basis documentation per processing activity)

### Controls
operational: dedicated DPO and steering committee

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; 1–2 weeks annual

## W3044. Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) Fulfillment & Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer/subject submits access request |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Growing volume as awareness rises |
| **Owner** | Data Protection Officer (DPO) |
| **Participants** | DPO, Customer Service, IT, Business Process Owners, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Service/DPO log DSAR; verify subject identity (anti-fraud); acknowledge within statutory window; route to relevant data custodians | Customer Service / DPO | DPO | Per request (<ack SLA) |
| 2 | IT/Business Process Owners retrieve all personal data held (per ROPA W3042) across systems; compile response package; DPO reviews for third-party data | IT / Process Owners / DPO | DPO | Per request (<fulfill SLA) |
| 3 | DPO delivers response to subject securely; logs fulfillment; updates DSAR register; coordinates related rectification/erasure (W3045) | DPO | DPO | Per request |

### System Touchpoints
- DSAR case register; data retrieval across systems (links to VS-29); secure delivery

### Pain Points / Risks
- **SLA risk**: Missing statutory fulfillment window; mitigated by case tracking and cross-system retrieval runbooks


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-log (customer service/dpo log dsar; verify subject)
- auto-review (package; dpo reviews for third-party data)
- auto-update (logs fulfillment; updates dsar register; coordinates)

### Controls
operational: case tracking and cross-system retrieval runbooks; operational: retrieval runbooks

### Time Estimate
- Per request; fulfill within statutory window

## W3045. Data Rectification, Erasure & Objection Request Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Subject requests correction, deletion, or objection to processing |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Subset of DSARs |
| **Owner** | Data Protection Officer (DPO) |
| **Participants** | DPO, IT, Business Process Owners, Legal, Records |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DPO assesses request: rectification (correct inaccurate), erasure (delete where no lawful basis/legal hold), objection (stop specific processing) | DPO | DPO | Per request |
| 2 | IT/Process Owners execute correction/deletion across systems and processor copies; preserve records subject to legal hold or BIR/SEC retention (links to VS-88) | IT / Process Owners / Records | DPO | Per request |
| 3 | DPO notifies subject of action; notifies third-party recipients of rectification/erasure obligation; logs and confirms completion | DPO | DPO | Per request |

### System Touchpoints
- Rectification/erasure workflow; legal hold (links to VS-88); processor notification; DSAR register

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Incomplete-erasure risk**: Copies retained by processors; mitigated by ROPA-driven processor notification and verification


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-request (dpo assesses request: rectification (correct inaccurate),)
- auto-record (copies; preserve records subject to legal)
- auto-log (rectification/erasure obligation; logs and confirms completion)

### Controls
operational: ROPA-driven processor notification and verification

### Time Estimate
- Per request

## W3048. Minors' Data, Sensitive Data & Special Category Processing Controls

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Processing involving minors or sensitive/special-category data |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven + annual review |
| **Volume** | Limited; high-sensitivity |
| **Owner** | Data Protection Officer (DPO) |
| **Participants** | DPO, Legal, HR, Marketing, Customer Service |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DPO/Legal identify processing involving minors (e.g., student/community programs) or sensitive data (health-related wellness, biometrics, government IDs); apply heightened controls | DPO / Legal | DPO | Per processing + annual |
| 2 | IT/Process Owners apply enhanced controls: explicit consent, access restriction, encryption, minimization, and shorter retention for sensitive data | IT / Process Owners | DPO | Per processing |
| 3 | DPO audits special-category processing for compliance; restricts use; documents lawful basis (consent or limited legitimate grounds) | DPO | DPO | Annual + event |

### System Touchpoints
- Enhanced access controls (links to VS-27); encryption; consent with explicit flag; retention override

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Heightened-risk data exposure**: Sensitive data breach carries severe consequences; mitigated by minimization and enhanced controls


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-identify (dpo/legal identify processing involving minors)
- auto-document (restricts use; documents lawful basis (consent)

### Controls
operational: minimization and enhanced controls

### Time Estimate
- Per processing; annual audit
