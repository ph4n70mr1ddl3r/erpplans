## W2646. Mobile App Feature Roadmap & Prioritization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly planning cycle |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | 1 app (iOS + Android); ~300K active users |
| **Owner** | Digital Product Manager |
| **Participants** | Digital Product Mgr, CIO, CMO, IT Development |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Digital Product Manager collects feature requests: customer feedback, business stakeholders, competitive analysis, analytics insights; maintains prioritized backlog | Digital Product Manager | CIO | Ongoing |
| 2 | Product Manager prioritizes using RICE framework; presents quarterly roadmap to CIO and CMO for approval | Digital Product Manager | CIO / CMO | 1–2 weeks/quarter |
| 3 | IT Development implements per agile: 2-week sprints, UAT, staged rollout (10% → 50% → 100%); Product Manager monitors adoption post-launch | IT Development / Digital Product Manager | CIO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile app (links to VS-75 / digital engagement app, VS-10 / ecommerce)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Adoption-risk**: the digital feature launches but customers/staff don't adopt it, wasting the investment; mitigated by the user-testing and the adoption-incentive
- **Data-quality risk**: incomplete or inaccurate master data (pricing, inventory, customer) corrupts the workflow output; mitigated by the master-data governance (VS-29 / W291) and the periodic audit

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 weeks per occurrence
- Volume: 1 app (iOS + Android); ~300K active users
- Frequency: Quarterly
- Absorbed into the Digital Product Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Automation Opportunity
- Self-service workflows (scan-and-go, product lookup, self-checkout) reducing staff intervention; feature-adoption auto-tracked from app telemetry

### Controls
- CTL-51 (master-data quality — links to VS-29 / W291); Operational: Digital Product Manager sign-off on the workflow output

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Continuous; weekly review |
| **Volume** | ~300K active users; ~50K daily sessions |
| **Owner** | IT Application Support |
| **Participants** | IT App Support, Digital Product Manager, CIO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT monitors performance: crash rate (<0.5%), load time (<3 sec), API response time, battery consumption; uses Firebase/Crashlytics; alerts for degradation | IT Application Support | CIO | Continuous |
| 2 | Support analyzes bottlenecks: slow image loading, API timeouts, memory leaks; prioritizes fixes; hotfixes for critical issues within 24 hours | IT App Support / Digital Product Manager | CIO | Ongoing |
| 3 | Product Manager reviews weekly: DAU/MAU (>20%), session duration (>3 min), screen flow, feature usage heat map; identifies UX optimization opportunities | Digital Product Manager | CIO / CMO | 1–2 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile app (links to VS-75 / digital engagement app, VS-10 / ecommerce)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113 BI platform, W231 QBR dashboard)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric-gaming risk**: a metric is gamed (marking complete before actual completion, excluding unfavorable data); mitigated by the source-system ground truth and the cross-metric consistency check
- **Data-quality risk**: incomplete or inaccurate master data (pricing, inventory, customer) corrupts the workflow output; mitigated by the master-data governance (VS-29 / W291) and the periodic audit

### Time Estimate
- ~Product Manager reviews per occurrence
- Volume: ~300K active users; ~50K daily sessions
- Frequency: Continuous; weekly review
- Absorbed into the IT Application Support role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Automation Opportunity
- Dashboard auto-refreshed from source systems; anomalies auto-flagged; report auto-distributed to stakeholders

### Controls
- CTL-51 (master-data quality — links to VS-29 / W291); Operational: IT Application Support sign-off on the workflow output
