# VS-108: On-Site Renewable Energy & Prosumer Asset Operations

> **Asset & Infrastructure** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

On-Site Renewable Energy & Prosumer Asset Operations workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **own-generation asset discipline** for BuildRight as a *producer and prosumer* of
renewable energy at its own sites: rooftop and carport solar PV (and emerging storage) across the
~200 big-box stores (8,000–15,000 sqm selling area each, per profile §3.1 = ~1.6–3.0M sqm of
roof), 4 distribution centers (25,000–40,000 sqm each per profile §3.2), and the Davao HQ —
installed to cut grid-electricity cost (the Philippines has among the highest commercial
electricity rates in Asia), hedge against volatile/frequent power interruptions (links to
VS-07/W470 brownout protocol), and deliver the decarbonization commitments under the ESG/sustainability
program (links to VS-25). This value stream owns the full renewable-asset lifecycle: generation
investment and feasibility, capex project development and EPC, asset registration and interconnection
/net-metering with the DU (distribution utility) and ERC, generation operations and monitoring,
self-consumption optimization, export-to-grid and net-metering settlement, asset O&M and
performance, renewable-energy certificate (REC) and decarbonization accounting, and the renewable
portfolio analytics and governance.

This is distinct from **VS-70 (Solar & Renewable Energy Product Operations)** which merchandises,
sells, and coordinates installation of solar/renewable products *to BuildRight's customers* —
BuildRight as retailer of solar goods. It is distinct from **VS-35 (Fixed Asset Management)** which
performs *accounting* (registration, depreciation, disposal) for all fixed assets including the
renewable assets this value stream operates (but not the generation/operating discipline). It is
distinct from **VS-20.3 / VS-07.2 (Facility Maintenance)** which maintains facilities/equipment
generically (and where W111 "Store Energy & Utility Consumption Management" and W173 "Store-Level
Solar Energy Monitoring" today introduce energy/solar at a single-workflow level; this value
stream decomposes the full prosumer-asset program). It is also distinct from **VS-25.1
(Environmental Monitoring)** which reports *consumption and emissions* (the downstream metric this
value stream improves) and from **VS-97 (Corporate Real Estate Portfolio)** which manages the
*property* as a real-estate asset (the roof is the site, not the generation business). On-site
renewable & prosumer operations is the *generation-asset operating* discipline — BuildRight as a
grid-interactive prosumer — distinct from selling solar products, fixed-asset accounting, facility
maintenance, environmental reporting, and property portfolio management.

BuildRight's exposure is material and growing: ~205 large rooftops in a high-irradiance,
high-tariff market; multi-MW aggregate generation potential; PHP-hundreds-of-millions in capex
over the build-out; direct OpEx reduction on a major cost line; and ESG/SEC climate-disclosure
relevance. The transition from pure-consumer to prosumer introduces new operational, regulatory
(ERC/DU net-metering, RE Act, REC), accounting (PFRS/IFRS 16 for leased-asset models, government
incentives, asset retirement), and risk disciplines that no existing value stream owns.
