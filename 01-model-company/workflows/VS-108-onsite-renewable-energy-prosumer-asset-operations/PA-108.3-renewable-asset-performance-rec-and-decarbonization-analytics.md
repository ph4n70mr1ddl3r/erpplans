## W3465. Renewable Energy Certificate (REC) / Carbon Offset Generation, Registry & Trading

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Generation (W3457); REC market opportunity; ESG disclosure cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly/quarterly |
| **Volume** | Portfolio generation (MWh/yr) |
| **Owner** | Head of Sustainability |
| **Participants** | Head of Sustainability (links to VS-25), Energy Manager, Finance (links to VS-17), Legal (links to VS-100.3), REC Registry/Broker |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability/Energy Manager determine REC/offset eligibility and ownership per ownership model (W3455): own-and-operate → BuildRight owns RECs; PPA/lease → attribute ownership per contract; register eligible generation with the recognized REC registry (e.g., I-REC) | Sustainability / Energy Manager / Legal | Head of Sustainability | Per site/period |
| 2 | Sustainability/Finance create/retire/trade RECs per strategy: retire to support BuildRight's own Scope 2/RE100 claim (W3466), or sell/trade for revenue where the claim is not needed; manage registry account, serialization, and transactions | Sustainability / Finance / Broker | CFO | Periodic |
| 3 | Sustainability/Legal document REC creation/retire/trade for audit (links to VS-21) and ESG disclosure (links to VS-25.3/W3470), ensure no double-counting (own-use vs sold), and report REC outcome to the performance review (W3468) and governance (W3472) | Sustainability / Legal / Finance | Head of Sustainability | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- REC registry; ESG metrics (links to VS-25.1/W192); finance/GL (links to VS-17.1); legal (links to VS-100.3); audit (links to VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Double-counting risk**: Claiming and selling the same REC; mitigated by clear attribution and registry discipline
- **Attribute-loss risk**: PPA/lease cedes REC ownership unintentionally; mitigated by contract clause (W3455)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-register of "register eligible generation with the recognized REC registry" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "account, serialization, and transactions" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document REC creation/retire/trade for audit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-555 (ensure controlled execution — Renewable Asset Performance, REC & Decarbonization Analytics (PA-108.3).)
- operational: clear attribution and registry discipline; operational: contract clause (W3455)

### Time Estimate
- Periodic

## W3466. GHG Emissions Reduction Accounting & Scope 2 Attribution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual GHG inventory (links to VS-25.1/W192); ESG reporting cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual + quarterly tracking |
| **Volume** | Portfolio generation vs consumption |
| **Owner** | Head of Sustainability |
| **Participants** | Head of Sustainability (links to VS-25), Energy Manager, Finance (links to VS-17.4), External Assurance (links to VS-21/W341) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability/Energy Manager compute renewable contribution to Scope 2 (location-based and market-based): self-generated MWh (W3457) reducing grid import, and retired RECs (W3465) reducing market-based Scope 2; reconcile to the GHG inventory (links to VS-25.1/W192) | Sustainability / Energy Manager | Head of Sustainability | Annual |
| 2 | Sustainability/Finance compute the emissions avoided (tCO2e) vs the grid emission factor, validate methodology against GHG Protocol/SEC/IFRS S2 and external assurance (links to VS-21/W341 ESG assurance audit), and document for disclosure | Sustainability / Finance / Assurance | Head of Sustainability | Annual |
| 3 | Sustainability report Scope 2 reduction and progress vs the decarbonization target (links to VS-25.1/VS-25.3) to the ESG review and integrate to climate disclosure (W3470); feed outcomes to the renewable roadmap (W3449) and governance (W3472) | Sustainability | Head of Sustainability / CSO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ESG/GHG metrics (links to VS-25.1/W192); SCADA generation; finance (links to VS-17.4); assurance (links to VS-21/W341)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Methodology-error risk**: Wrong emission factor/methodology mis-states reduction; mitigated by GHG Protocol adherence and assurance
- **Claim-vs-actual risk**: Claiming reduction without REC retirement (market-based); mitigated by attribution discipline (W3465)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "Sustainability/Energy Manager compute renewable contribution to Scope 2" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "methodology against GHG Protocol/SEC/IFRS S2 and external assurance" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review and integrate to climate disclosure" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-555 (ensure controlled execution — Renewable Asset Performance, REC & Decarbonization Analytics (PA-108.3).)
- operational: GHG Protocol adherence and assurance; operational: attribution discipline (W3465)

### Time Estimate
- Annual + quarterly
