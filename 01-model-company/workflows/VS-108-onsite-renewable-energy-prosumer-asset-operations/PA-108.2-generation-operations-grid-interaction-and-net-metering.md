## W3457. Daily Generation Monitoring, SCADA & Performance Surveillance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous (SCADA); daily operations |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + daily |
| **Volume** | ~80 commissioned solar sites today, growing toward the ~205-rooftop potential (per the README) |
| **Owner** | Energy Manager |
| **Participants** | Energy Manager, Facilities (links to VS-20.3), O&M Vendor, BI (links to VS-28.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Manager/Facilities monitor generation via SCADA/monitoring platform: real-time kW/kWh, performance ratio, inverter status, alarms, and per-string performance across all sites; aggregate to portfolio view in BI (links to VS-28.1) | Energy Manager / Facilities | Energy Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Energy Manager detect and triage under-performance/anomalies (soiling, shading, string/inverter fault, degradation) vs expected yield (W3456); prioritize by impact and dispatch O&M (W3459) or warranty (W3460) | Energy Manager | Energy Manager | Daily |
| 3 | Energy Manager publish the daily/weekly generation and performance report (actual vs expected, availability, faults), feed consumption-vs-generation matching (W3458) and net-metering settlement (W3463), and report portfolio performance to governance (W3471) | Energy Manager / BI | Head of Sustainability | Weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- SCADA/monitoring platform; BI (links to VS-28.1); energy/asset management; facility (links to VS-20.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Monitoring-blindspot risk**: Undetected under-performance erodes savings; mitigated by SCADA and anomaly detection
- **Alert-fatigue risk**: Too many alarms cause ignoring; mitigated by smart alerting and prioritization


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitor generation via SCADA/monitoring platform" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-dispatch of "dispatch O&M" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-match of "matching" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-135 (ensure renewable asset registration & net-metering settlement.)
- operational: SCADA and anomaly detection; operational: smart alerting and prioritization

### Time Estimate
- Continuous + daily

## W3458. Self-Consumption Optimization & Load Matching

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Generation/consumption analytics (W3457); tariff review |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + periodic optimization |
| **Volume** | All sites |
| **Owner** | Energy Manager |
| **Participants** | Energy Manager, Facilities (links to VS-20.3/VS-07), Store Ops (links to VS-07), Finance (links to VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Manager/Analytics profile site load vs generation: consumption pattern (store/DC load, HVAC, lighting, refrigeration links to VS-20.3), generation curve, self-consumption ratio, and export volume; identify load-shifting and sizing opportunities | Energy Manager / Analytics | Energy Manager | Periodic |
| 2 | Energy Manager/Facilities optimize self-consumption (highest-value use of generated kWh): load-shifting (HVAC, water heating, charging) to solar hours, BESS charge/discharge scheduling (W3460), and avoidance of peak/grid import where tariffs penalize it; coordinate with store/DC ops (links to VS-07/VS-04) | Energy Manager / Facilities | Energy Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Energy Manager/Finance measure savings achieved (self-consumed kWh × avoided tariff + export kWh × export rate per W3463), validate against the business case (W3451), and tune the optimization as tariffs/load change; report to the performance review (W3471) | Energy Manager / Finance | Head of Sustainability | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- SCADA/BMS (links to VS-20.3); energy analytics (links to VS-28.1); store/DC ops (links to VS-07/VS-04); finance/savings (links to VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low-self-consumption risk**: Exporting cheap and importing expensive erodes savings; mitigated by load-shifting and BESS
- **Disruption risk**: Load-shifting harms store operations/comfort; mitigated by coordination and guardrails


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-file of "profile site load vs generation" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "generated kWh" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "Energy Manager/Finance measure savings achieved" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-554 (ensure controlled execution — Generation Operations, Grid Interaction & Net-Metering (PA-108.2).)
- operational: load-shifting and BESS; operational: coordination and guardrails

### Time Estimate
- Continuous + periodic

| **Frequency** | Event-driven |

| **Owner** | Energy Manager |
| **Participants** | Energy Manager, Facilities (links to VS-20.3), Store/DC Ops (links to VS-07/VS-04), HSE (links to VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Manager/Facilities design islanding/resilience capability where justified: grid-forming inverter + BESS, critical-load prioritization (POS links to VS-08, lighting, refrigeration, safety), transfer/islanding logic, and compliance with DU/ERC anti-islanding rules (links to W3462) | Energy Manager / Facilities / EPC | Energy Manager | Per site |
| 2 | During a grid outage, the system islands (if equipped) or the site falls back to generator/UPS (links to VS-07/W470); Facilities/Ops manage the switchover, confirm critical loads sustained, and coordinate with store/DC ops (links to VS-07/VS-04) and HSE (links to VS-24) | Facilities / Store Ops / HSE | Energy Manager | Event-driven |
| 3 | Energy Manager/Facilities validate post-outage grid reconnection and system recovery, capture resilience performance (uptime sustained, kWh served), and report resilience value vs the business case (W3451) and BCP (links to VS-26); tune islanding logic | Energy Manager / Facilities | Head of Sustainability | Event-driven |

### Controls

| **Frequency** | Per site + on regulation change |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Manager/Compliance register the renewable system with the DU and ERC for net-metering (RE Act / net-metering rules): submit application, specs, single-line diagram, structural/electrical certification, and fees; secure the Certificate of Completion and net-metering agreement | Energy Manager / Compliance / DU | Energy Manager | Per site |
| 2 | Energy Manager/Legal execute the net-metering/interconnection agreement with the DU: import/export meter installation (bi-directional), export pricing/terms, compliance with DU/ERC technical standards, and anti-islanding; ensure multi-site consistency across DUs/regions (links to VS-76) | Energy Manager / Legal / DU | Energy Manager | Per site |

- **Rule-change risk**: Net-metering/export-pricing changes alter economics; mitigated by regulatory monitoring (links to VS-22.3) and scenario planning

## W3463. Net-Metering Settlement, Export Billing & Import/Export Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly DU billing cycle; generation/consumption data (W3457) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | All net-metered sites |
| **Owner** | Energy Manager |
| **Participants** | Energy Manager, Finance/AP (links to VS-15/VS-17), Facilities, DU |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Manager/Finance reconcile the monthly DU bill against SCADA/meter data: import kWh (billed at retail tariff), export kWh (credited at export rate), net position, demand charges, and other charges; validate meter readings and rate application | Energy Manager / Finance | Energy Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | Finance/AP process the net-metered electricity bill (links to VS-15.1/W7), post the electricity expense (reduced by self-generation) and export credit to GL (links to VS-17.1), and resolve billing disputes with the DU (links to VS-90/W244); allocate cost to the correct entity/site (links to VS-72) | Finance / AP | Finance Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Energy Manager/Finance compute the net savings (avoided import + export credit − charges vs baseline grid-only) per site and portfolio, validate against the business case (W3451) and optimization (W3458), and report to the performance review (W3471) | Energy Manager / Finance | Head of Sustainability | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- SCADA/meter; AP/GL (links to VS-15.1/VS-17.1); DU billing; cost allocation (links to VS-72)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Billing-error risk**: DU mis-billing (wrong rate, meter error) over-charges; mitigated by reconciliation and dispute (links to VS-90)
- **Export-rate-volatility risk**: Changing export pricing alters net-metering value; mitigated by self-consumption optimization (W3458)

- System auto-allocate of "allocate cost to the correct entity/site" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "Energy Manager/Finance compute the net savings" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
