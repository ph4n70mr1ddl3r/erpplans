## W3449. Renewable Generation Portfolio Strategy & Site Prioritization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual ESG/energy strategy (links to VS-25); portfolio capacity planning |
| **Frequency** | Annual + multi-year roadmap |
| **Volume** | ~205 candidate sites (200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ) |
| **Owner** | Head of Sustainability / Energy Manager |
| **Participants** | Energy Manager, Head of Sustainability (links to VS-25), Real Estate (links to VS-20/VS-97), Finance (links to VS-17/VS-40), Facilities (links to VS-20.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Manager/Sustainability develop the renewable-portfolio strategy: target self-generation %, capex envelope and phasing, ownership-model preference (own/lease/PPA per W3455), alignment to the ESG/decarbonization target (links to VS-25.1/W192 GHG) and grid-resilience need (links to VS-07/W470), and store/DC/HQ prioritization criteria | Energy Manager / Sustainability | COO / CSO | Annual |
| 2 | Energy Manager/Real Estate score and prioritize candidate sites: roof area/condition/orientation/shading, structural capacity, site consumption profile (links to W111), DU/tariff and net-metering feasibility (links to W3462), and business-case attractiveness (W3451); produce the multi-year build roadmap | Energy Manager / Real Estate | Head of Sustainability | Annual |
| 3 | CFO/COO approve the portfolio strategy and roadmap, sequence capex against the corporate capex plan (links to VS-40/VS-33.1) and working capital (links to VS-105), and set the program governance (links to W3472); integrate to the ESG/sustainability plan (links to VS-25.3) | CFO / COO | CEO / Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Energy/asset master (links to VS-35/W399); ESG metrics (links to VS-25.1/W192); facility/real estate (links to VS-20/VS-97); capex (links to VS-40)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Site-mis-prioritization risk**: Low-yield sites selected ahead of high-yield; mitigated by rigorous scoring (W3450)
- **Capex-conflict risk**: Renewable capex crowds out store-growth capex; mitigated by portfolio governance and phasing


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-renew of "renewable-portfolio strategy" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "profile" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the portfolio strategy and roadmap, sequence capex against the…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-553 (ensure controlled execution — Renewable Generation Investment, Capex & Project Development (PA-108.1).)
- operational: rigorous scoring (W3450); operational: portfolio governance and phasing

### Time Estimate
- 4–6 weeks annual

## W3450. Site Feasibility, Solar Resource Assessment & Structural/Capacity Evaluation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Site shortlisted (W3449) |
| **Frequency** | Per site |
| **Volume** | ~10–30 sites/year per roadmap |
| **Owner** | Energy Manager |
| **Participants** | Energy Manager, EPC/Engineering consultant, Real Estate (links to VS-20), Facilities (links to VS-20.3), Structural Engineer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy Manager/EPC conduct site feasibility: solar resource assessment (irradiance/yield via PVsyst/satellite data), roof survey (area, orientation, tilt, shading, penetration), structural evaluation (roof load capacity for PV + access), electrical/interconnection point, and site consumption matching | Energy Manager / EPC | Head of Sustainability | Per site |
| 2 | EPC/Engineering size the system: kWp capacity, inverter type, optional battery storage (BESS), mounting (roof/carport/ground), generation estimate (kWh/yr), self-consumption ratio, and export potential; identify constraints (heritage, CAAP height clearance links to VS-22/W480, structural reinforcement) | EPC / Engineering | Energy Manager | Per site |
| 3 | Energy Manager compile the feasibility report with go/no-go recommendation, expected generation, capex/payback (W3451), risks, and interconnection/net-metering feasibility (W3462); obtain Real Estate/Facilities sign-off and proceed to business case | Energy Manager / Real Estate / Facilities | Head of Sustainability | Per site |

### System Touchpoints
- Energy modeling tools; facility/asset master (links to VS-20/VS-35); permits (links to VS-22); CAAP clearance (links to VS-22/W480)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Yield-overestimate risk**: Optimistic yield understates payback; mitigated by PVsyst and conservative assumptions
- **Structural-risk**: Roof cannot bear the load without costly reinforcement; mitigated by structural evaluation upfront


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-match of "matching" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-export of "export potential" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-compile of "compile the feasibility report with go/no-go recommendation, expected…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-553 (ensure controlled execution — Renewable Generation Investment, Capex & Project Development (PA-108.1).)
- operational: PVsyst and conservative assumptions; operational: structural evaluation upfront

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 weeks per site
