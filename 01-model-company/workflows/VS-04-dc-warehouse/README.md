# VS-04: DC & Warehouse Operations

> **Make & Move** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

DC & warehouse operations: inbound (receiving/putaway), outbound (pick/pack/dispatch), and operations management across 4 DCs.

## Why it matters

4 DCs gate the entire 35,000-SKU flow to 200 stores; receiving accuracy underpins AP 3-way match and inventory accuracy (≥97% target); throughput drives store availability.

- **Owner**: VP Supply Chain / DC Operations Managers
