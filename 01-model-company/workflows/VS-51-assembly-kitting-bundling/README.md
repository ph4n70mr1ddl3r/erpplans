# VS-51: Product Assembly, Kitting & Bundling

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages in-store and DC-level product assembly, custom kit creation, and promotional bundle operations for BuildRight Depot. Covers furniture and fixture assembly for display and customer delivery, custom kit creation (e.g., bathroom combo kit, tool starter set), promotional bundle assembly (buy-one-take-one packs, gift sets), and kit SKU lifecycle management. Kitting and bundling increase basket size by 15–25% per transaction and are critical for competitive differentiation in Philippine hardware retail.
