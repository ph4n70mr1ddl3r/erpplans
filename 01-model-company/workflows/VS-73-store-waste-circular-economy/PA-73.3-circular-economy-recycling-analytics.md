## W2614. Vendor Packaging Take-Back Program Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New vendor onboarding or annual sustainability program review |
| **Frequency** | Annual program; per vendor onboarding |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 vendors |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Coord, Procurement, Vendor, Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Coordinator designs packaging take-back program: identifies high-volume packaging vendors; negotiates returnable packaging (reusable crates, pallets) instead of single-use | Sustainability Coordinator / Procurement | COO | 2–3 months |
| 2 | Procurement incorporates packaging requirements into vendor contracts: returnable packaging for top 50 vendors, packaging reduction targets, penalties for excessive waste | Procurement | VP Merchandising | Per contract cycle |
| 3 | DC Ops manages returnable packaging logistics: tracks crate/pallet inventory, schedules vendor pickups, reconciles packaging deposits; Coordinator reports packaging waste reduction metrics | DC Ops / Sustainability Coordinator | VP Supply Chain | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor contract module — returnable-packaging clause + packaging-reduction KPI in vendor agreements (links to VS-03 vendor management; W62 contract lifecycle)
- Returnable Transport Item (RTI) module — crate/pallet pool tracking + deposit reconciliation (links to VS-111 Packaging/Pallet/RTI; W270)
- ERP sustainability module — packaging-waste-reduction metric (links to W2619 ESG; RA 11898 EPR)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Deposit-loss risk**: returnable crates/pallets not returned ⇒ deposit forfeited and program uneconomic; mitigated by RTI pool reconciliation (VS-111) and deposit clawback in vendor settlement
- **Adoption risk**: vendors resist returnable packaging; mitigated by top-50-vendor focus and packaging-reduction penalty leverage

### Automation Opportunity
- Vendor contract auto-flag if high-volume packaging vendor without take-back clause at renewal; RTI deposit auto-reconcile against vendor settlement

### Controls
- CTL-06: vendor onboarding — packaging clause check; operational control: monthly RTI deposit reconciliation by DC Ops

### Time Estimate
~2–3 months initial program design + per-contract-cycle negotiation + monthly RTI reconciliation (~1–2 days/month network). Top-50-vendor focus concentrates effort.

## W2615. Recycling Revenue Optimization & Vendor Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly recycling contract review |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 recycling vendor contracts |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Coord, Procurement, Finance, Recycling Vendors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Coordinator reviews recycling vendor performance: collection reliability, pricing competitiveness, manifest compliance; benchmarks against market rates | Sustainability Coordinator / Procurement | COO | 2–3 days/quarter |
| 2 | Coordinator optimizes recycling revenue: consolidates volumes across nearby stores for better pricing, negotiates volume-based rate increases, explores higher-value recycling streams | Sustainability Coordinator / Procurement | COO | 1 week |
| 3 | Finance tracks recycling revenue per store: PHP 2K–10K/month; includes in store P&L; Coordinator reports network-wide recycling revenue trend quarterly | Finance / Sustainability Coordinator | Finance Controller | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor scorecard module — recycling-vendor performance (reliability, price, manifest compliance) (links to VS-67 vendor scorecard)
- Financials — recycling-revenue per store in P&L (links to W2619); vendor master (W287)
- ERP sustainability module — recycling revenue trend dashboard

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Revenue-underperformance risk**: un-benchmarked rates ⇒ stores under-credited vs. market; mitigated by quarterly benchmarking and volume consolidation
- **Manifest-compliance risk**: recycler with poor manifest discipline exposes BuildRight's compliance record; mitigated by manifest-compliance KPI in vendor scorecard

### Automation Opportunity
- Quarterly auto-build recycling-vendor scorecard from collection reliability + price + manifest data; auto-flag below-market rates

### Controls
- CTL-39 analog (settlement accuracy — recycling revenue validated before reporting); operational control: quarterly Finance validation of recycling revenue per store

### Time Estimate
~2–3 days/quarter review + 1 week optimization + Finance batch. Network recycling revenue ≈ PHP 2–10K/store/mo × 200 ≈ PHP 4.8–24M/yr.

### Time Estimate
~quarterly ideation + 1–2 months/initiative evaluation + ongoing marketing. Low volume, strategic.

## W2617. Store-Level Composting & Organic Waste Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store with garden center generates organic waste |
| **Frequency** | Daily at stores with garden centers; ~80 stores |
| **Volume** | ~80 stores with garden centers |
| **Owner** | Garden Center Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Garden Center Supervisor, Store Manager, Sustainability Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Garden Center Supervisor collects organic waste: dead plants, soil, leaves, trimmings; separates from general waste; stages in composting area or bags for community garden donation | Garden Center Supervisor | Store Manager | 15–20 min/day |
| 2 | Supervisor manages on-site composting: turns pile weekly, monitors temperature, processes into compost in 8–12 weeks; bags finished compost for sale at PHP 50–100 per bag | Garden Center Supervisor | Store Manager | 30 min/week |
| 3 | Sustainability Coordinator tracks organic waste diversion across garden center stores: composting volume, compost sales revenue; includes in ESG report | Sustainability Coordinator | COO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP sustainability module — organic-waste diversion volume + compost sales (links to W2619 ESG)
- Inventory module — compost-bag SKU at PHP 50–100 (catch-weight; links to W463)
- Garden center / nursery module (links to VS-07 store operations)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pest/odor risk**: poorly managed compost pile attracts pests/odors ⇒ store-experience and health risk; mitigated by weekly turn + temperature monitoring
- **Revenue-visibility risk**: compost sales untracked; mitigated by SKU-coded bag at POS

### Automation Opportunity
- Organic-diversion auto-rollup into ESG; compost-bag SKU auto-created at finished-compost stage

### Controls
- CTL-450 (ensure controlled execution — Circular Economy & Recycling Analytics (PA-73.3).)
- Operational control: weekly pile-turn + temperature log signed by Supervisor

### Time Estimate
~15–20 min/store/day collection + 30 min/week composting × ~80 composting-program stores ≈ ~1,000–1,600 staff-min/day; quarterly rollup ~1 day.

## W2618. Customer Reusable Bag & Packaging Reduction Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily POS operations or sustainability campaign |
| **Frequency** | Daily at POS; quarterly campaigns |
| **Volume** | 2.8M monthly transactions |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Cashier, Marketing, Sustainability Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cashier implements reusable bag program: offers BuildRight-branded bags (PHP 25–50 each), charges PHP 2 per plastic bag per local ordinance, encourages BYO bag; tracks in POS | Cashier | Store Manager | Per transaction |
| 2 | Marketing designs seasonal reusable bag campaigns: limited editions, trade professional heavy-duty bags, loyalty member free bag promotions | Marketing | CMO | Quarterly campaigns |
| 3 | Sustainability Coordinator reports bag program impact: plastic bag reduction (target: 20% YoY), reusable bag sales, LGU ordinance compliance across 200 locations | Sustainability Coordinator | COO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- POS — reusable-bag SKU (PHP 25–50) + plastic-bag fee (PHP 2) tender line with LGU-ordinance tax code (links to VS-08 POS; W5B in-store selling)
- ERP sustainability module — plastic-bag count + reduction trend; LGU-ordinance fee remittance (links to VS-76 multi-LGU compliance)
- Loyalty module — free-bag promo for members (links to VS-13)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **LGU-compliance risk**: ~200 locations across LGUs with differing plastic-bag ordinances ⇒ fee mis-collection and LGU fine exposure; mitigated by LGU-specific fee configuration in tax master (W293) per location
- **Friction risk**: bag fee annoyances at checkout harm NPS; mitigated by reusable-bag alternative and loyalty free-bag promo

### Automation Opportunity
- Location-aware POS auto-apply correct LGU plastic-bag fee from tax master; auto-remit fee per LGU schedule

### Controls
- CTL-34 analog (statutory fee remittance accuracy); operational control: monthly LGU-fee remittance reconciliation per location

### Time Estimate
Per-transaction (~seconds) absorbed at POS + quarterly campaign design + monthly reporting. Volume: 2.8M monthly transactions × plastic-bag rate.

## W2619. Corporate Waste Reduction Target & ESG Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual ESG reporting cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | BuildRight group |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Coord, COO, CFO, ESG Committee |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Coordinator compiles annual waste reduction metrics: total waste generated, diversion rate, recycling revenue, hazardous waste, carbon emissions avoided, waste intensity per sqm | Sustainability Coordinator | COO | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | CFO and COO review waste metrics vs. targets; validates data; Coordinator prepares external ESG disclosure per GRI Standards (Waste 306) | CFO / COO | CEO | 1 week |
| 3 | Coordinator publishes in annual sustainability report; submits to SEC if required; presents to ESG Committee; sets next-year targets | Sustainability Coordinator | COO / Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP sustainability / BI module — annual waste-metric rollup (GRI 306) from the per-store ledger (W2598–W2605, W2606–W2610) (links to VS-25 ESG; VS-28 analytics)
- Reporting/disclosure module — GRI 306 + SEC sustainability report (links to VS-17 R2R external reporting)
- ESG metrics master (W406) — emission factors and intensity denominators

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Disclosure-accuracy risk**: ESG/GRI 306 misstatement from poor upstream waste data ⇒ greenwashing/regulatory exposure; mitigated by CFO/VP validation (step 2) and the W2612 audit + VS-25 ESG assurance
- **Target-miss risk**: public targets missed ⇒ reputational risk; mitigated by monthly W2605 tracking and mid-year corrective action

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-build GRI 306 disclosure from the per-store waste ledger with cross-check to disposal-cost and recycling-revenue data; variance exceptions flagged

### Controls
- CTL-34: reporting accuracy; W2612 (audit); operational control: CFO sign-off before external publication

### Time Estimate
~2–3 weeks annual compilation + validation + disclosure. This workflow is the annual rollup of the entire VS-73 waste-data chain; its integrity depends on upstream W2598–W2618 quality.

## W2621. Waste-to-Energy Feasibility & Biomass Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual sustainability strategy review |
| **Frequency** | Annual evaluation; ad-hoc for pilot |
| **Volume** | 4 DCs; 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Coord, Facilities, COO, External Consultant |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Coordinator evaluates waste-to-energy potential: anaerobic digestion for organic waste, biomass boiler for wood waste, solar panel waste recycling; engages consultant for feasibility study | Sustainability Coordinator / External Consultant | COO | 2–3 months |
| 2 | For feasible projects: Coordinator develops business case with ROI; presents to CFO and COO for approval | Sustainability Coordinator / Finance | CFO | 1 month |
| 3 | For approved pilots: manages implementation, tracks results, reports to COO; scales successful pilots; documents for ESG reporting | Sustainability Coordinator | COO | 6–12 months |

### System Touchpoints
- Capex/project module — feasibility + business case + pilot capex (links to VS-40 capex; VS-112 PMO; VS-108 renewable energy for own-generation overlap)
- ERP sustainability module — pilot results + ESG carbon-avoidance reporting (links to W2619; VS-25)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **White-elephant risk**: pilot approved on weak ROI ⇒ stranded capex; mitigated by CFO/COO business-case gate and scale-only-on-success discipline
- **Overlap risk**: waste-to-energy overlaps VS-108 (own renewable generation) scope; mitigated by clear boundary — VS-73 owns waste-feedstock conversion, VS-108 owns grid-interactive generation assets

### Automation Opportunity
- Feasibility-study → capex-request auto-template with ROI model; pilot-result dashboard feeding scale/sunset decision

### Controls
- CTL-60 analog (investment policy compliance — capex approval tiers); operational control: scale decision requires pilot ROI validation

### Time Estimate
~2–3 months feasibility + 1 month business case + 6–12 months pilot (if approved). Low frequency, strategic; ~annual evaluation.
