## W2614. Vendor Packaging Take-Back Program Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New vendor onboarding or annual sustainability program review |
| **Frequency** | Annual program; per vendor onboarding |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 vendors |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Coord, Procurement, Vendor, Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Coordinator designs packaging take-back program: identifies high-volume packaging vendors; negotiates returnable packaging (reusable crates, pallets) instead of single-use | Sustainability Coordinator / Procurement | VP Operations | 2–3 months |
| 2 | Procurement incorporates packaging requirements into vendor contracts: returnable packaging for top 50 vendors, packaging reduction targets, penalties for excessive waste | Procurement | VP Merchandising | Per contract cycle |
| 3 | DC Ops manages returnable packaging logistics: tracks crate/pallet inventory, schedules vendor pickups, reconciles packaging deposits; Coordinator reports packaging waste reduction metrics | DC Ops / Sustainability Coordinator | VP Supply Chain | Monthly |
