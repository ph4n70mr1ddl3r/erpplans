## W2606. Paint & Chemical Waste Storage & Disposal

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Paint mixing station waste accumulation or quarterly disposal cycle |
| **Frequency** | Daily accumulation; quarterly disposal |
| **Volume** | 200 stores with paint stations |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Paint Section Lead, DENR-Certified Hazardous Waste Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Paint Section Lead manages daily paint waste: leftover tint, contaminated thinner, paint spills, empty cans with residue; collects in designated hazardous waste containers with proper labeling per DENR requirements | Paint Section Lead | Store Manager | 15–20 min/day |
| 2 | Store Manager monitors hazardous waste accumulation: tracks volume against DENR storage limits (max 90-day accumulation), ensures containers properly sealed and labeled | Store Manager | Regional Manager | 15 min/week |
| 3 | DENR-certified hazardous waste vendor conducts quarterly pickup; issues manifest document; Store Manager signs and archives for 5-year compliance record; Finance processes disposal cost (PHP 30–50K/quarter per store) | Hazwaste Vendor / Store Manager / Finance | VP Operations | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP sustainability / hazwaste sub-ledger — accumulation start-date, volume, category, 90-day-limit countdown alert (links to W2609 DENR permit; W2612 audit)
- Paint mixing/colorant module — colorant/thinner consumption linkage (W168) feeding waste-volume estimate
- Document management — 5-year manifest archive (links to VS-88 records retention; legal-hold capable)
- Financials (AP) — disposal-cost posting per store (PHP 30–50K/qtr × 200 ≈ PHP 24–40M/yr)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Regulatory risk**: exceeding the DENR 90-day accumulation limit (DAO 2013-22) triggers fines/permit risk; mitigated by the 90-day countdown alert and quarterly vendor pickup cadence
- **Manifest-integrity risk**: missing/mis-matched manifest forfeits the compliance record and the vendor-cost deduction; mitigated by mandatory manifest capture before vendor payment (operational control)

### Automation Opportunity
- Accumulation-date → auto 90-day countdown → auto-trigger vendor pickup work order at day 75; manifest upload → auto-block vendor invoice payment until matched to logged accumulation

### Controls
- CTL-06 (DENR-certified vendor onboarding); CTL-44 (duplicate-invoice guard on disposal invoices); operational control: manifest-vs-invoice match before payment

### Time Estimate
~15–20 min/store/day accumulation + 15 min/week monitoring + quarterly pickup (~2 hrs/store/qtr) + Finance batch. ~PHP 24–40M/yr network disposal cost.

## W2607. Solvent & Flammable Material Waste Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Solvent waste from paint station or equipment cleaning |
| **Frequency** | Daily accumulation; monthly disposal |
| **Volume** | ~200 stores |
| **Owner** | Department Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Dept Supervisor, Maintenance Staff, Safety Officer, DENR-Certified Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Department Supervisor segregates solvent waste: used thinner, mineral spirits, acetone; stores in approved flammable liquid containers in flammable storage cabinet | Dept Supervisor | Store Manager | 10–15 min/day |
| 2 | Safety Officer inspects flammable storage monthly: verifies container integrity, ventilation, fire suppression, signage, PPE availability | Safety Officer | Store Manager | 30 min/month |
| 3 | DENR-certified vendor collects flammable waste per schedule; issues transport manifest; waste tracked cradle to grave in ERP sustainability module | Hazwaste Vendor / Safety Officer | VP Operations | Monthly/quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP sustainability module — solvent-waste cradle-to-grave ledger with manifest reference (links to VS-114 DG/hazmat; VS-24.3 fixed-site hazmat storage)
- Flammable-storage / Fire Code module — BFP Fire Code compliance records, cabinet inspection log (links to W476 FSIC)
- HSE module — monthly Safety Officer inspection record (links to VS-24 HSE; W141 safety inspection)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fire risk**: flammable solvent waste in unapproved containers or near ignition sources → BFP Fire Code violation and fire risk; mitigated by approved-cabinet requirement + monthly Safety inspection
- **Cradle-to-grave risk**: gap in manifest chain ⇒ RA 6969 liability if waste is mishandled downstream; mitigated by cradle-to-grave ledger and DENR-certified vendor (CTL-06)

### Automation Opportunity
- Monthly Safety inspection checklist on mobile with photo evidence → auto-file to Fire Code compliance record; manifest auto-linked to disposal cost

### Controls
- CTL-06 (vendor certification); operational control: monthly Safety Officer cabinet inspection with signed checklist; BFP FSIC linkage (W476)

### Time Estimate
~10–15 min/store/day segregation + 30 min/month inspection + monthly/quarterly collection. Network: ~200 stores × daily = absorbed into paint-station routine.

## W2608. Battery & Lead-Acid Waste Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Forklift battery replacement or customer battery return |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; 3–5 forklift batteries/store/year |
| **Volume** | 4 DCs + 200 stores |
| **Owner** | DC Operations Manager / Store Manager |
| **Participants** | DC Ops / Store Manager, DENR-Certified Lead Recycler, Safety Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DC Ops / Store Manager manages spent lead-acid batteries: stores upright in acid-resistant containment, labels as hazardous waste, contacts certified lead recycler | DC Ops / Store Manager | VP Operations | Per replacement event |
| 2 | Lead recycler collects batteries: weighs and issues receipt with DENR manifest; pays lead recovery credit (PHP 500–1,500 per battery) | Lead Recycler / Finance | Finance Controller | Per collection |
| 3 | Sustainability Coordinator tracks battery waste: ensures 100% go to certified recyclers per RA 6969 (Toxic Substances and Hazardous and Nuclear Wastes Control Act) | Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | Quarterly review |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP sustainability module — battery-waste ledger with DENR manifest + lead-recovery credit (links to W2612 audit; VS-114 DG)
- Fixed-asset / fleet module — forklift battery asset retirement (links to W188 fleet spare parts; CTL-26)
- Financials (AR/misc income) — lead-recovery credit posting (PHP 500–1,500/battery)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Environmental liability**: lead-acid batteries sent to uncertified recycler violate RA 6969 and create soil/groundwater liability; mitigated by 100%-certified-recycler rule (CTL-06) and manifest trail
- **Acid-burn/injury risk**: handling cracked/leaking batteries; mitigated by acid-resistant containment + PPE (VS-24)

### Automation Opportunity
- Forklift battery asset retirement → auto-trigger certified-recycler work order; auto-recover lead credit against asset NBV

### Controls
- CTL-06 (certified recycler onboarding); CTL-26 (asset disposal); operational control: 100% manifest-traceability review quarterly

### Time Estimate
Per-event (~1–2 hrs) × ~3–5/store/yr × ~204 locations ≈ ~600–1,000 events/yr network; quarterly review ~1 day.

## W2609. DENR Hazardous Waste Permit & Registration Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual permit renewal cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | 205 locations |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Coord, Legal, VP Operations, DENR-EMB |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Coordinator maintains DENR Environmental Compliance Certificate and Hazardous Waste Generator Registration for all 205 locations; tracks renewal deadlines; compiles required documentation | Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | 1–2 months before renewal |
| 2 | Legal reviews submissions for completeness; ensures compliance with DENR DAO 2013-22 and RA 6969; Coordinator submits to DENR-EMB regional office | Legal / Sustainability Coordinator | VP Legal | Per renewal cycle |
| 3 | Coordinator archives all DENR permits and correspondence in document management system; maintains 5-year rolling compliance record per location | Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Regulatory/permit module — DENR ECC + HW Generator Registration per location with expiry countdown (links to VS-22 regulatory permits; CTL-41 permit-compliance control)
- Location master (W254) — permit status per location; links to VS-76 multi-LGU compliance
- Document management — permit archive (VS-88 records retention; legal-hold capable)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lapse risk**: expired DENR registration ⇒ illegal hazardous-waste generation status, fines, operational risk; mitigated by 60-day expiry alert (CTL-41) and renewal-cycle tracking
- **Multi-location inconsistency risk**: 205 locations across DENR-EMB regions with varying requirements; mitigated by central permit ledger and Legal pre-review

### Automation Opportunity
- Permit-expiry → 90/60/30-day auto-alert → auto-generate renewal work order with pre-filled prior-year submission; permit-status block on new-location hazwaste generation if lapsed

### Controls
- CTL-41 (permit-compliance alerting — directly applies); operational control: Legal completeness review before submission

### Time Estimate
~1–2 months/location-cycle prep (spread across 205 locations, batched by region) + ongoing archive. Central Coordinator effort ~2–3 FTE-days/month steady-state.

| **Frequency** | Monthly for store lighting; quarterly customer events |
| **Volume** | 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Maintenance Staff |
| **Participants** | Maintenance Staff, Store Manager, DENR-Certified Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintenance Staff replaces burnt-out CFL bulbs and fluorescent tubes; carefully handles to prevent breakage; stores spent bulbs in dedicated CFL collection containers | Maintenance Staff | Store Manager | Per replacement |
| 2 | Store Manager hosts quarterly customer CFL/bulb collection: accepts household CFLs, fluorescent tubes; partners with DENR-certified recycler; offers loyalty points | Store Manager / Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | Quarterly |
| 3 | DENR-certified vendor collects mercury-containing waste: issues manifest; Coordinator tracks volume and ensures no mercury waste enters general waste stream | Vendor / Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | Per collection |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP sustainability module — mercury-waste volume + manifest (links to W2612 audit; RA 6969)
- Loyalty module — bonus-points for customer drop-off (links to VS-13)
- Facility/lighting asset module — lighting replacement cycle (links to VS-20.3 facility maintenance)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mercury-release risk**: broken CFL/tube releases mercury vapor — health + environmental hazard; mitigated by dedicated unbreakable collection containers and breakage-response procedure
- **Contamination risk**: mercury waste entering general stream ⇒ RA 6969 violation; mitigated by separate stream + manifest

### Automation Opportunity
- Lighting-asset replacement log → auto-generate mercury-waste accumulation entry; loyalty auto-issue points at customer drop-off

### Controls
- CTL-06 (certified vendor); operational control: dedicated-container + no-general-stream rule audited in W2612

### Time Estimate
Per-replacement (~10 min) absorbed into facility routine + quarterly customer event (~1 day/store) + per-collection manifest. Network ~200 stores.

## W2611. Spill Response & Containment Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Chemical spill at store or DC |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; 2–5 spills per year across network |
| **Volume** | 200 stores + 4 DCs |
| **Owner** | Safety Officer |
| **Participants** | Safety Officer, Store/DC Manager, Maintenance Staff, DENR (if significant) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First responder activates spill response: dons PPE from spill kit, identifies spilled material, contains spill using absorbent materials, prevents entry into drains | Nearest Staff / Safety Officer | Store/DC Manager | Immediate |
| 2 | Safety Officer assesses spill severity: minor (<5 liters) — staff cleans; major (>5 liters or hazardous) — evacuates area, calls emergency contractor, reports to DENR if environmental contamination possible | Safety Officer | VP Operations | 15–60 min |
| 3 | Safety Officer documents spill: incident report with photos, root cause analysis, corrective actions; updates spill kit inventory; submits to VP Operations and Insurance if damage occurred | Safety Officer | VP Operations | 1–2 days post-incident |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE/incident module — spill incident record with severity, photos, root cause, corrective action (links to VS-24 HSE W140 OHS incident management; VS-114 DG spill)
- ERP sustainability module — environmental-contamination reporting to DENR-EMB if significant (RA 6969; links to W433 DENR SMR/CMR)
- Insurance/claims module — damage claim if applicable (links to VS-26 insurance)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Escalation risk**: minor spill mis-classified and not escalated ⇒ environmental contamination + DENR reporting failure; mitigated by severity-decision tree (5-liter/hazardous threshold) and Safety Officer authority to escalate
- **Drain-entry risk**: spill reaching storm drain ⇒ serious environmental incident; mitigated by drain-protection in spill kits and containment-first response

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile spill-report app with severity-decision tree → auto-escalate to DENR reportable threshold → auto-notify Insurance/VP; spill-kit inventory auto-decrement

### Controls
- CTL-449 (ensure controlled execution — Hazardous Waste Denr Compliance (PA-73.2).)
- Operational control: spill-kit inventory check in monthly Safety inspection; incident-report dual-signoff (Safety Officer + Store Manager); insurance evidence within 48 hrs

### Time Estimate
Immediate response (minutes) + 15–60 min assessment + 1–2 days documentation. ~2–5 events/yr network but each carries high severity tail-risk.

## W2612. Hazardous Waste Compliance Audit & Record Keeping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Internal audit cycle or DENR inspection |
| **Frequency** | Annual internal; DENR inspection ad-hoc |
| **Volume** | 205 locations |
| **Owner** | Internal Auditor |
| **Participants** | Internal Auditor, Sustainability Coordinator, VP Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Internal Auditor reviews hazardous waste compliance at sampled locations (20–30/year): verifies storage compliance, reviews disposal manifests, confirms vendor DENR certification, checks training records | Internal Auditor | VP Internal Audit | 2–3 weeks/year |
| 2 | Sustainability Coordinator addresses findings: corrects non-compliance within 30 days; critical findings corrected within 7 days | Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | 30 days |
| 3 | Coordinator maintains 5-year hazardous waste records per location: generation logs, disposal manifests, vendor certifications, inspection reports, training records; accessible for DENR inspection at any time | Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Internal audit module — hazwaste compliance audit program + findings/CAPA tracking (links to VS-21 internal audit W339 regulatory compliance audit; W333 CAPA)
- Document management — 5-year hazwaste record retention per location (links to VS-88; legal-hold capable; DENR-inspection-ready)
- ERP sustainability module — source generation logs and manifests under audit

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inspection-readiness risk**: DENR ad-hoc inspection finds incomplete manifests ⇒ fines/permit risk; mitigated by inspection-ready 5-year archive and annual internal audit
- **Finding-drift risk**: audit findings not remediated within SLA; mitigated by 30/7-day CAPA tracking (W333)

### Automation Opportunity
- DENR-inspection "readiness dashboard" — real-time completeness check of manifests/certs/training per location, with red flags for any gap

### Controls
- CTL-41 (permit compliance); W333 (CAPA tracking); operational control: annual internal audit sampling 20–30 locations

### Time Estimate
~2–3 weeks/year internal audit + finding remediation (30-day SLA) + ongoing archive maintenance. ~20–30 locations sampled/yr.

## W2613. Asbestos & Special Hazardous Material Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Renovation project involving asbestos-containing materials |
| **Frequency** | Rare; event-driven during renovations |
| **Volume** | 10–15 renovation projects/year |
| **Owner** | Facilities Manager |
| **Participants** | Facilities Mgr, Licensed Contractor, DENR, Safety Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities Manager conducts asbestos survey before renovation; if found: engages DENR-licensed removal contractor; obtains DENR permit | Facilities Manager / Licensed Contractor | VP Operations | 2–4 weeks pre-renovation |
| 2 | Licensed contractor performs removal with full PPE and containment; Safety Officer monitors compliance | Licensed Contractor / Safety Officer | Facilities Manager | Per project |
| 3 | Contractor transports to DENR-licensed asbestos landfill; issues disposal manifest; Facilities Manager archives documentation permanently | Licensed Contractor / Facilities Manager | VP Operations / VP Legal | Post-project |

### System Touchpoints
- Capex/project module — asbestos survey as a mandatory remodel gate (links to VS-109 store remodel; VS-112 PMO stage-gate; VS-40 capex)
- HSE module — containment/PPE compliance monitoring (links to VS-24; VS-114 DG)
- Document management — permanent asbestos-disposal archive (links to VS-88; legal-hold)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Health-liability risk**: unlicensed removal or fiber release exposes workers/customers to asbestos ⇒ severe health + legal liability; mitigated by DENR-licensed-contractor requirement + containment + survey-before-renovation gate
- **Discovery-late risk**: asbestos found mid-renovation ⇒ project stop-work and cost/schedule overrun; mitigated by mandatory pre-renovation survey

### Automation Opportunity
- PMO stage-gate block: remodel project cannot pass "demolition" gate without completed asbestos survey + DENR permit (if found) attached

### Controls
- CTL-06 (DENR-licensed contractor onboarding); operational control: Safety Officer containment monitoring; permanent manifest archive

### Time Estimate
~2–4 weeks pre-renovation survey + per-project removal (days-weeks) + documentation. ~10–15 projects/yr, low frequency but high-severity tail.
