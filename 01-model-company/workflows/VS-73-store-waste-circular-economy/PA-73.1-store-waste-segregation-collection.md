## W2598. Store-Level Waste Segregation & Daily Collection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily store operations |
| **Frequency** | Daily per store |
| **Volume** | 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Maintenance Staff, Stock Associates |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintenance Staff segregates daily store waste into categories: cardboard/paper, plastic, wood/pallets, metal, organic, general waste; uses color-coded bins per waste category | Maintenance Staff | Store Manager | 30–45 min/day |
| 2 | Stock Associates manage sellable waste: damaged but repairable items marked for RTV, slightly damaged items for clearance sale, demo/display units for periodic sale event; routes items to appropriate disposition channel | Stock Associate | Department Supervisor | 15–20 min/day |
| 3 | Maintenance Staff compacts cardboard using on-site baler; stores compacted bales for scheduled recycler pickup; weighs and logs waste volumes per category in ERP sustainability module; Store Manager reviews daily waste volumes | Maintenance Staff / Store Manager | Store Manager | 15–20 min/day |

## W2599. Cardboard & Paper Waste Baling & Recycling Revenue

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Cardboard baler full or weekly recycling pickup |
| **Frequency** | Weekly per store |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × ~2 tons cardboard/month |
| **Owner** | Maintenance Staff |
| **Participants** | Maintenance Staff, Store Manager, Recycling Vendor, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintenance Staff operates cardboard baler: breaks down all shipping cartons from receiving, removes tape and staples, feeds into baler, secures bales with wire ties; stores bales in designated recycling area | Maintenance Staff | Store Manager | 30 min/day |

| 3 | Finance processes recycling revenue: rates at PHP 2–4/kg for cardboard; generates monthly recycling income report per store; annual recycling revenue ~PHP 5–10K per store | Finance / Store Manager | Finance Controller | Monthly |
