## W2598. Store-Level Waste Segregation & Daily Collection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily store operations |
| **Frequency** | Daily per store |
| **Volume** | 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Maintenance Staff, Stock Associates |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintenance Staff segregates daily store waste into categories: cardboard/paper, plastic, wood/pallets, metal, organic, general waste; uses color-coded bins per waste category | Maintenance Staff | Store Manager | 30–45 min/day |
| 2 | Stock Associates manage sellable waste: damaged but repairable items marked for RTV, slightly damaged items for clearance sale, demo/display units for periodic sale event; routes items to appropriate disposition channel | Stock Associate | Department Supervisor | 15–20 min/day |
| 3 | Maintenance Staff compacts cardboard using on-site baler; stores compacted bales for scheduled recycler pickup; weighs and logs waste volumes per category in ERP sustainability module; Store Manager reviews daily waste volumes | Maintenance Staff / Store Manager | Store Manager | 15–20 min/day |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP sustainability/ESG module — per-store waste-volume ledger by category (links to VS-25 ESG reporting; feeds W2619 corporate ESG rollup)
- Inventory disposition module — RTV (W88), clearance/markdown (W93), and demo-stock (W97) routing for "sellable waste" flagged in step 2
- Baler weigh-scale integration (catch-weight capture at W294 UOM master) — auto-post bale weight to the waste ledger

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-quality risk**: hand-keyed waste volumes are inconsistent across 200 stores, corrupting the ESG diversion-rate metric (W2619) and recycling-revenue reporting (W2615); mitigated by weigh-scale integration and mandatory category capture before baler cycle completes
- **Mis-routing risk**: sellable waste sent to general waste instead of RTV/clearance forfeits recovery value; mitigated by disposition decision tree at the point of damage flagging (links to W91 damaged-goods disposition)

### Automation Opportunity
- Weigh-scale → ERP auto-posting (eliminates hand-keyed volumes); mobile damage-flag → disposition-suggestion (auto-routes to RTV/clearance/donation per rules)

### Controls
- CTL-26 (control fixed-asset disposals — applies to any asset-grade item in the waste stream); operational control: dual-signoff on weekly waste-volume log by Maintenance Staff + Store Manager

### Time Estimate
~45–65 min/store/day (steps 1 + 3) × 200 stores ≈ 9,000–13,000 staff-min/day network-wide; step 2 (sellable-waste triage) ~15–20 min/store/day absorbed into receiving staff routine.

## W2599. Cardboard & Paper Waste Baling & Recycling Revenue

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Cardboard baler full or weekly recycling pickup |
| **Frequency** | Weekly per store |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × ~2 tons cardboard/month |
| **Owner** | Maintenance Staff |
| **Participants** | Maintenance Staff, Store Manager, Recycling Vendor, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintenance Staff operates cardboard baler: breaks down all shipping cartons from receiving, removes tape and staples, feeds into baler, secures bales with wire ties; stores bales in designated recycling area | Maintenance Staff | Store Manager | 30 min/day |
| 2 | Recycling vendor collects bales per weekly schedule; weighs on certified scale; issues weight ticket; Maintenance Staff logs collection in ERP: weight, date, vendor, collection receipt number | Recycling Vendor / Maintenance Staff | Store Manager | 15 min/collection |
| 3 | Finance processes recycling revenue: rates at PHP 2–4/kg for cardboard; generates monthly recycling income report per store; annual recycling revenue ~PHP 5–10K per store (at the ~0.4–0.9 t/store/month stream consistent with W502; the ~2 t/month figure would yield ~PHP 48–96K) | Finance / Store Manager | Finance Controller | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP sustainability module — recycling-revenue sub-ledger per store/vendor with weight-ticket reference (links to W2615 recycling revenue optimization)
- Financials (AR/miscellaneous income) — recycling revenue posting to store P&L; vendor master (W287) for recycler
- Weigh-scale/ticket capture — weight ticket number stored as the audit artifact

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Revenue-leakage risk**: weight tickets lost or hand-keyed incorrectly ⇒ store under-credited (PHP 5–10K/store/yr); mitigated by mandatory weight-ticket-number capture and Finance reconciliation of vendor payment to logged weights (links to VS-118 revenue assurance discipline)
- **Safety risk**: baler crushing injury if operated without guard or by untrained staff; mitigated by LOTO procedure and training prerequisite (links to VS-24 HSE)

### Automation Opportunity
- Vendor portal weight-ticket upload → auto-match to logged bales → auto-generate recycling-revenue accrual per store (eliminates monthly manual Finance step)

### Controls
- CTL-44 (detect/prevent duplicate payments — applies if recycler invoices rather than weight-ticket-settles); operational control: monthly Finance reconciliation of recycler payment vs. sum of logged weights

### Time Estimate
~30 min/store/day baling (absorbed into Maintenance routine) + 15 min/weekly collection + ~monthly Finance batch (~1 day/month network-wide). Cardboard revenue ≈ PHP 5–10K/store/yr × 200 = ~PHP 1–2M/yr network.

## W2600. Plastic & Shrink Wrap Waste Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily waste segregation cycle |
| **Frequency** | Daily per store |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × ~200 kg plastic/month |
| **Owner** | Maintenance Staff |
| **Participants** | Maintenance Staff, Sustainability Coordinator, Waste Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintenance Staff collects and segregates plastic waste: shrink wrap, product packaging plastic, plastic bags, stretch film; separates by recyclability (clean film vs. contaminated) | Maintenance Staff | Store Manager | 15–20 min/day |
| 2 | Sustainability Coordinator evaluates plastic recycling options: identifies vendors accepting mixed plastics, negotiates collection rates, explores closed-loop programs with major vendors | Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | Quarterly |
| 3 | Maintenance Staff tracks plastic waste volume in ERP sustainability module; Coordinator reports plastic diversion rate (target: >60% recycled) | Maintenance Staff / Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP sustainability module — plastic-volume + diversion-rate ledger (links to W2619 ESG; RA 11898 EPR reporting exposure)
- Vendor master / contract module — plastic-recycler contracts and rates (links to W2615)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: RA 11898 (Extended Producer Responsibility) and LGU single-use-plastic ordinances (PHP 2/bag) impose reporting/fee obligations; untracked plastic volume exposes BuildRight to LGU fines; mitigated by category-level volume capture and the W2618 bag-reduction program
- **Contamination risk**: mixed/contaminated film rejected by recycler ⇒ diversion-rate target missed and disposal cost incurred; mitigated by clean-vs-contaminated segregation at step 1

### Automation Opportunity
- Monthly auto-rollup of plastic diversion rate per store vs. target, with bottom-quartile store flagging in the W2605 analytics dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-448 (ensure controlled execution — Store Waste Segregation & Collection (PA-73.1).)
- Operational control: quarterly Sustainability Coordinator audit of plastic segregation quality at sampled stores (feeds W2612 compliance audit)

### Time Estimate
~15–20 min/store/day segregation + quarterly vendor negotiation (~2–3 days/quarter network-wide). ~200 kg/store/month × 200 = ~40 tons/month network plastic stream.

## W2601. Pallet & Wood Waste Recovery & Reuse

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Pallet accumulation at receiving dock |
| **Frequency** | Weekly per store |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × ~50 pallets/month |
| **Owner** | Receiving Clerk |
| **Participants** | Receiving Clerk, Maintenance Staff, Logistics, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receiving Clerk manages pallet accumulation: sorts by condition — reusable, repairable, waste; reusable pallets staged for vendor pickup or DC return | Receiving Clerk | Store Manager | 15 min/shipment |
| 2 | Maintenance Staff repairs damaged pallets; repurposes irreparable pallet wood for store displays, garden center planters, or customer DIY project bins | Maintenance Staff | Store Manager | 1–2 hours/week |
| 3 | Logistics tracks pallet return rate to DC: target >80% returned or reused; Store Manager logs pallet inventory and disposition in ERP | Logistics / Store Manager | VP Supply Chain | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Returnable Transport Item (RTI) / pallet pool module — pallet ID, condition, location, return status (links to VS-111 Packaging/Pallet/RTI management; W270 pallet tracking)
- Inventory/transfer module — pallet DC-return transfer orders (W22); reverse-logistics consolidation (W176)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Asset-loss risk**: pallets are a floating asset; poor return-rate tracking forfeits the deposit/pool value (target >80% return); mitigated by RTI module and the VS-111 pallet-pool reconciliation
- **Safety risk**: repairing damaged pallets (nails/splinters) without PPE; mitigated by PPE requirement (links to VS-24)

### Automation Opportunity
- Barcode/RFID pallet ID scan at receiving → auto-decision (return/repair/scrap) → auto-generate DC-return transfer if return threshold met

### Controls
- CTL-448 (ensure controlled execution — Store Waste Segregation & Collection (PA-73.1).)
- Operational control: monthly pallet-inventory reconciliation (logged vs. physical) by Store Manager; variances escalated (links to W248 inventory variance investigation)

### Time Estimate
~15 min/shipment sort + 1–2 hrs/store/week repair + monthly reconciliation. ~50 pallets/store/month × 200 = ~10,000 pallets/month network flow.

## W2602. Lumber Yard Waste & Offcut Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily lumber yard operations |
| **Frequency** | Daily at stores with lumber yards |
| **Volume** | ~150 stores with lumber yards (one register with W1015) |
| **Owner** | Lumber Yard Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Lumber Yard Supervisor, Stock Associate, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lumber Yard Supervisor manages wood waste from cutting operations: collects offcuts, sawdust, and damaged lumber; sorts by size — usable offcuts for customer sale, sawdust for composting, waste wood for biomass recycler | Lumber Yard Supervisor | Store Manager | 20–30 min/day |
| 2 | Stock Associate packages usable offcuts into bundles for clearance sale: priced at PHP 50–100 per bundle; popular with DIY customers; generates ~PHP 5K–10K/month per store | Stock Associate | Department Supervisor | 15 min/day |
| 3 | Supervisor tracks lumber waste rate: calculates % becoming waste (target: <5%); implements waste reduction measures | Lumber Yard Supervisor | Store Manager | Monthly review |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory module — offcut/bundle item creation (non-stock/variant) with catch-weight pricing (links to W463 catch-weight; W294 UOM)
- ERP sustainability module — lumber waste-rate % KPI; biomass/compost diversion volume
- Cutting/fabrication module (links to VS-09 custom fabrication W169 lumber cutting) — yield/kerf data feeding waste-rate %

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Yield/margin risk**: high waste % (>5%) signals poor cutting yield or poor inbound lumber grade, eroding the lumber-category margin; mitigated by waste-rate KPI and root-cause back to vendor grade (links to VS-31 quality / W110 supplier CAPA)
- **Revenue-recognition risk**: offcut bundle sales (PHP 5–10K/store/mo) untracked ⇒ lost visibility and shrink; mitigated by SKU-coded bundle items at POS

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-calc waste % from cutting-module yield data; auto-create offcut-bundle SKU when usable-offcut threshold reached; auto-price by bundle-weight

### Controls
- CTL-448 (ensure controlled execution — Store Waste Segregation & Collection (PA-73.1).)
- Operational control: monthly waste-rate review with vendor-grade correlation (feeds VS-67 vendor scorecard)

### Time Estimate
~35–45 min/store/day (steps 1+2) × ~120 lumber-yard stores ≈ ~4,200–5,400 staff-min/day; monthly review ~30 min/store. Offcut revenue ≈ PHP 5–10K/store/mo × 120 = ~PHP 7.2–14.4M/yr.

## W2603. E-Waste & Battery Collection & Disposal

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Electronic equipment end-of-life or battery collection event |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly e-waste collection; ad-hoc for equipment |
| **Volume** | 200 stores |
| **Owner** | IT Support / Store Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Support, Store Manager, DENR-Certified E-Waste Vendor, Sustainability Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Support collects end-of-life electronic equipment: POS terminals, barcode scanners, RF guns, computers, printers; stores in designated e-waste staging area; coordinates with DENR-certified e-waste recycler | IT Support / Store Manager | CIO | Ongoing |
| 2 | Store Manager hosts quarterly customer e-waste collection event: accepts batteries, CFL bulbs, small electronics; partners with LGU environmental office; offers loyalty points incentive | Store Manager / Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | Quarterly |
| 3 | Sustainability Coordinator tracks e-waste diversion: ensures compliance with DENR DAO 2013-22 (Revised Procedures and Standards for the Management of Hazardous Wastes under RA 6969) | Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- IT Asset Management / fixed-asset module — end-of-life IT asset retirement with data-wipe attestation (links to VS-99 ITAM; W131 IT asset lifecycle; CTL-26 data-wipe control per RA 10173)
- ERP sustainability module — e-waste volume + DENR manifest reference (links to W2609 hazwaste permit; W2612 compliance audit)
- Loyalty module — bonus-points issuance for customer e-waste drop-off (links to VS-13 loyalty)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-breach risk**: POS terminals/RF guns/scanners retired without certified data wipe leak customer/payment data (RA 10173, 72-hour breach notification — links to VS-91); mitigated by mandatory wipe-and-certify before asset leaves the store (CTL-26)
- **Compliance risk**: e-waste given to uncertified vendor violates DENR DAO 2013-22; mitigated by vendor DENR-certification check (CTL-06 vendor onboarding) and manifest archiving (W2612)

### Automation Opportunity
- IT asset retirement request → auto-block disposal until data-wipe attestation uploaded; auto-issue DENR manifest from ITAM + sustainability modules

### Controls
- CTL-26 (fixed-asset disposal + IT data wipe per RA 10173); CTL-06 (vendor DENR-certification onboarding); manifest 5-year archive (operational)

### Time Estimate
Ad-hoc IT collection ~ongoing (absorbed); quarterly customer event ~1 day/store/event × 200; ~quarterly diversion tracking. Manifest archive ongoing.

## W2604. Construction & Renovation Waste Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store renovation or construction project |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; 10–15 renovations/year |
| **Volume** | 10–15 events/year |
| **Owner** | Facilities Manager |
| **Participants** | Facilities Mgr, Contractor, Sustainability Coordinator, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities Manager includes waste management plan in renovation project spec: requires contractor to segregate waste, use certified waste haulers, comply with DENR construction waste regulations | Facilities Manager / Contractor | VP Operations | Per project |
| 2 | Contractor executes waste segregation during renovation; Sustainability Coordinator spot-checks compliance | Contractor / Sustainability Coordinator | Facilities Manager | Throughout project |
| 3 | Facilities Manager compiles waste diversion report: total waste generated, % diverted from landfill (target: >50%), recycling revenue, disposal costs | Facilities Manager | VP Operations | Post-project |

### System Touchpoints
- Capex/project module — waste-management plan as a mandatory project-spec section (links to VS-40 capex accounting; VS-109 store remodel program; VS-112 PMO stage-gate)
- ERP sustainability module — project-level waste-diversion % and disposal-cost capture
- Vendor/contract module — certified-hauler requirement enforced via vendor master (CTL-06)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Spec-circumvention risk**: contractor mixes waste to cut hauling cost ⇒ diversion target missed and DENR exposure; mitigated by contract penalty clause and Sustainability spot-checks at step 2
- **Cost-overrun risk**: disposal cost under-budgeted in capex; mitigated by mandatory waste-plan + cost line in the project spec (links to VS-40 W1811 capex request)

### Automation Opportunity
- PMO stage-gate block: capex project cannot pass "mobilization" gate without an approved waste-management plan and certified-hauler vendor attached

### Controls
- CTL-06 (certified-hauler vendor onboarding); operational control: post-project diversion report reconciled to hauler manifests

### Time Estimate
~per-project spec (~1–2 days) + spot-checks (absorbed) + post-project report (~1 week). ~10–15 events/yr × ~10 days effort ≈ ~100–150 person-days/yr.

## W2605. Store Waste Analytics & Reduction Target Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly sustainability reporting cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Coordinator, Store Managers, VP Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Coordinator compiles store waste analytics: waste generated per store by category, waste diversion rate, recycling revenue, disposal cost, waste per sqm; benchmarks stores against each other | Sustainability Coordinator | VP Operations | 2–3 days |
| 2 | Coordinator identifies top and bottom performing stores; investigates high-waste stores; implements targeted reduction programs | Sustainability Coordinator / Store Manager | VP Operations | 1–2 days |
| 3 | VP Operations reviews waste reduction progress vs. annual targets (diversion rate: 60%, recycling revenue growth: 10%, waste-to-landfill reduction: 15%) | VP Operations | COO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP sustainability / BI module — store waste dashboard (diversion %, recycling revenue, waste/sqm, vs. target) (links to VS-28 analytics; W113 BI)
- Reporting layer — monthly VP/COO review pack; feeds W2619 annual ESG/GRI 306 disclosure

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric-gaming risk**: stores under-report general waste to hit diversion targets ⇒ ESG misstatement and GRI 306 disclosure risk; mitigated by disposal-cost cross-check (hauler invoices vs. reported volumes) and the W2612 audit
- **Target-miss risk**: structural high-waste stores (e.g. lumber yards) miss uniform targets; mitigated by store-cluster benchmarking (step 1) rather than flat targets

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-build the monthly dashboard from the per-store waste ledger (W2598–W2604) with bottom-quartile flagging and disposal-cost cross-check exceptions

### Controls
- CTL-34 analog (statutory/ESG reporting accuracy — reconciliation discipline); operational control: VP Operations sign-off on monthly metric pack before ESG rollup

### Time Estimate
~3–5 days/month network-wide for steps 1–2 + monthly VP review. This workflow is the analytical rollup of W2598–W2604, so its accuracy depends entirely on upstream data quality.
