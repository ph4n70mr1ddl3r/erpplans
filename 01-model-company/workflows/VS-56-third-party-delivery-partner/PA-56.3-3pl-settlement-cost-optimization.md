## W2206. Monthly 3PL Invoice Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | 3PL partner submits monthly invoice |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; ~10–15 invoices |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 30–50M/month total logistics |
| **Owner** | Logistics Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Logistics Finance Analyst, Logistics Mgr, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analyst matches invoice vs. system delivery records: validates count, distances, surcharges, SLA penalties; investigates discrepancies with partner; approves adjusted invoice; payment Net 30 | Logistics Finance Analyst | Finance Manager | 4–6 hours/month |
