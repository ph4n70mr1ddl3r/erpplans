# VS-35: Fixed Asset Management

> **Asset & Infrastructure** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages the complete lifecycle of fixed assets across BuildRight Depot's 5 legal entities — from acquisition and capitalization through depreciation, physical verification, maintenance tracking, transfer, and retirement/disposal. Covers 200 stores, 4 DCs, corporate HQ, fleet vehicles, IT equipment, POS terminals (600), RF guns, forklifts, store fixtures, and office equipment. Ensures PFRS-compliant fixed asset accounting, accurate asset registers, and effective capital asset stewardship across ~PHP 8–10B in gross fixed assets.
