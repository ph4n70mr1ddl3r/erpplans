# VS-44: Consumer Insights & Market Research

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages all market research and consumer insight operations that inform BuildRight's merchandising, marketing, store operations, and strategic planning decisions. Covers customer satisfaction measurement, market trend analysis, competitive intelligence, product category research, store experience research, and demographic/trade area analysis. Provides the data-driven foundation for decision-making across all value streams, with particular focus on understanding the Filipino home improvement consumer (DIY homeowner, trade professional, and corporate buyer) and the competitive landscape in Philippine hardware/home improvement retail.
