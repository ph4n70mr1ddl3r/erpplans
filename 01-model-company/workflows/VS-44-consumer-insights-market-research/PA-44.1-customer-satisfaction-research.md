## W1902. Quarterly Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) Survey Design & Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly survey cycle |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly survey execution |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–5,000 responses per quarter (email, SMS, in-store) |
| **Owner** | Market Research Analyst |
| **Participants** | Market Research Analyst, CMO, VP Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market Research Analyst designs CSAT survey instrument: (a) overall satisfaction: "How satisfied are you with your recent BuildRight experience?" (1–5 scale), (b) satisfaction drivers: product availability, store cleanliness, staff helpfulness, checkout speed, pricing competitiveness, product quality, returns experience, (c) open-ended: "What could BuildRight do to improve your experience?", (d) segment questions: DIY homeowner vs. trade professional vs. corporate buyer, (e) channel: email to loyalty members, SMS to recent POS customers, in-store tablet survey at exit | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 2–3 days |
| 2 | Survey distribution: (a) email survey to random sample of 10,000 loyalty members per quarter, (b) SMS survey to 5,000 recent POS customers (purchase in last 30 days), (c) in-store tablet at 20 stores (rotated quarterly) for exit survey, (d) incentive: 50 loyalty points for completion; target response rate ≥ 15% | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 week |
| 3 | Analysis: (a) overall CSAT score by quarter and trend, (b) CSAT by store cluster and region, (c) CSAT by customer segment (DIY, trade, corporate), (d) driver analysis: which satisfaction drivers have the strongest correlation with overall CSAT?, (e) text analytics on open-ended responses: identify top 10 improvement themes, (f) statistical significance testing for quarter-over-quarter changes | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 week |
| 4 | Presentation and action planning: (a) CMO presents findings to VP Store Operations and VP Merchandising, (b) identify top 3 improvement priorities per quarter, (c) assign action items to relevant departments with timelines, (d) track action item completion for next quarter's survey | CMO | COO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Survey platform integrated with loyalty/CRM for distribution (W1902.2)
- CSAT analytics dashboard with trend and driver analysis (W1902.3)
- Action item tracking linked to department KPIs (W1902.4)
- Integration with W13.1 (customer complaints), W67 (store performance), W1550 (VOC analysis)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Response bias**: Satisfied customers are more likely to respond; dissatisfied customers may not; supplement with in-store intercept and post-return surveys for balanced data

### Staffing Implication
- **Market Research Analyst**: CSAT survey adds ~3 weeks/quarter = ~12 weeks/year — absorbed if dedicated role; otherwise outsourced (~PHP 200K–400K/quarter to research agency)

### Time Estimate
- Survey design: 2–3 days/quarter
- Distribution: 1 week/quarter
- Analysis: 1 week/quarter
- Presentation: 1 day/quarter
- Total: ~5 weeks/quarter × 4 = ~20 weeks/year

## W1903. Net Promoter Score (NPS) Monthly Measurement & Trend Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly NPS measurement cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly measurement |
| **Volume** | ~1,000–3,000 responses/month |
| **Owner** | Market Research Analyst |
| **Participants** | Market Research Analyst, CMO, VP Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly: system sends NPS survey to random sample of customers within 48 hours of purchase: (a) single question: "On a scale of 0–10, how likely are you to recommend BuildRight to a friend or colleague?", (b) follow-up: "What is the primary reason for your score?", (c) classification: Promoters (9–10), Passives (7–8), Detractors (0–6), (d) NPS = % Promoters − % Detractors | System / Market Research Analyst | CMO | Automated + 1 day/month |
| 2 | Monthly analysis: (a) overall NPS score and trend, (b) NPS by store, region, and customer segment, (c) NPS by purchase channel (in-store, ecommerce, B2B), (d) theme analysis of verbatim responses, (e) identify stores or segments with declining NPS for targeted intervention | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 day/month |
| 3 | Quarterly: CMO presents NPS trend to executive team: (a) target NPS: ≥ 50 (good) → ≥ 70 (excellent) for Philippine retail, (b) benchmark against Philippine retail industry NPS (if available), (c) correlation analysis: NPS vs. same-store sales growth, (d) "close the loop": contact Detractors who provided contact information to address their issues per W13.1 | CMO | CEO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Automated NPS survey distribution triggered by purchase event (W1903.1)
- NPS analytics with segmentation and trend tracking (W1903.2)
- Detractor "close the loop" workflow integrated with W13.1 (W1903.3d)
- Integration with W13 (customer experience), W67 (store performance), W1550 (VOC analysis)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Survey fatigue**: Monthly NPS surveys may annoy frequent shoppers (trade professionals buying weekly); throttle survey frequency per customer to max once per quarter

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by Market Research Analyst

### Time Estimate
- Monthly: ~2 days/month
- Quarterly presentation: 1 day
- Total: ~30 days/year

## W1904. Customer Effort Score (CES) Measurement for Key Service Touchpoints

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-interaction survey at key touchpoints |
| **Frequency** | Continuous measurement; monthly reporting |
| **Volume** | Touchpoints: returns, special orders, BOPIS pickup, delivery, complaints |
| **Owner** | Market Research Analyst |
| **Participants** | Market Research Analyst, VP Store Operations, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System triggers CES survey after key service interactions: (a) returns: "How easy was it to return your item today?" (1–7 scale, 1 = very difficult, 7 = very easy), (b) BOPIS pickup: "How easy was it to pick up your online order?", (c) delivery: "How easy was it to schedule and receive your delivery?", (d) complaint resolution: "How easy was it to resolve your issue?", (e) special order: "How easy was it to place and receive your special order?" | System | Market Research Analyst | Automated |
| 2 | Monthly analysis: (a) CES by touchpoint and trend, (b) identify touchpoints with CES < 5 (high effort = poor experience): priority for process improvement, (c) CES by store for returns and BOPIS (identifies stores with poor service execution), (d) correlate CES with repeat purchase behavior | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 day/month |
| 3 | Quarterly: VP Store Operations reviews low-CES touchpoints: (a) identify root cause of high customer effort, (b) implement process improvements to reduce effort (simplify return process, improve BOPIS pickup signage, streamline complaint resolution), (c) track CES improvement post-intervention | VP Store Operations | COO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- CES survey triggered by service interaction type (W1904.1)
- CES analytics by touchpoint and store (W1904.2)
- Integration with W12 (returns), W11 (BOPIS), W19 (delivery), W13.1 (complaints), W38 (special orders)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low response rate for post-service surveys**: Customers who complete a return or complaint are often eager to leave; those with smooth experiences may not respond; low response rates skew data

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount

### Time Estimate
- Monthly analysis: 1 day/month
- Quarterly review: 1 day/quarter
- Total: ~20 days/year

## W1905. In-Store Customer Intercept Survey & Shopper Journey Mapping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Semi-annual shopper research program |
| **Frequency** | Semi-annual; 2-week data collection period |
| **Volume** | ~500–1,000 intercept interviews across 10 stores per cycle |
| **Owner** | Market Research Analyst |
| **Participants** | Market Research Analyst, Research Agency (field interviewers), VP Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market Research Analyst designs intercept survey and shopper journey study: (a) shopper profile: purpose of visit, frequency, companion type, time spent in store, (b) journey mapping: which departments did they visit? In what order? What was their path through the store?, (c) conversion: did they find what they were looking for? If not, why? Did they buy anything?, (d) basket analysis: what did they buy vs. what they intended to buy?, (e) store experience: signage clarity, department navigation, staff interaction quality, checkout experience, (f) competitor comparison: where else do they shop for home improvement? Why? | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 week |
| 2 | Execute fieldwork: (a) engage research agency for trained interviewers at 10 stores (5 urban, 5 provincial), (b) 2-week data collection: intercept customers at store exit, (c) observational component: track shopper movement patterns through store (heatmap analysis) | Research Agency | Market Research Analyst | 2 weeks |
| 3 | Analysis: (a) shopper journey maps: typical paths for DIY vs. trade customers, (b) conversion rate by department: what % of visitors to each department make a purchase?, (c) friction points: where do customers get frustrated or leave without buying?, (d) cross-shop analysis: which competitor stores do BuildRight customers also visit?, (e) recommendations: store layout optimization, signage improvement, department staffing realignment | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 week |
| 4 | VP Store Operations and VP Merchandising review findings: (a) incorporate into planogram optimization (W1848), (b) address navigation and signage issues, (c) adjust department staffing based on traffic patterns, (d) consider store layout modifications for high-friction areas | VP Store Operations / VP Merchandising | COO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Shopper journey mapping analytics (W1905.3a)
- Department conversion analytics from POS data (W1905.3b)
- Heatmap visualization of store traffic patterns (W1905.2c)
- Integration with W67 (store performance), W1848 (planogram), W1116 (complaint trend analysis)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Sample bias**: Exit intercepts only capture customers who visited the store, not potential customers who chose competitors; supplement with non-customer research for competitive insight

### Staffing Implication
- **Research Agency cost**: ~PHP 200K–500K per semi-annual study

### Time Estimate
- Design: 1 week
- Fieldwork: 2 weeks
- Analysis: 1 week
- Review: 1 day
- Total per cycle: ~4–5 weeks; ~8–10 weeks/year

## W1906. Post-Purchase Experience Survey & Product Satisfaction Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Continuous; monthly reporting |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–5,000 post-purchase surveys/month |
| **Owner** | Market Research Analyst |
| **Participants** | Market Research Analyst, Category Manager, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System sends post-purchase survey 7 days after transaction: (a) email to loyalty members and online customers, (b) SMS to POS customers with mobile number on file, (c) questions: (i) "Are you satisfied with the product(s) you purchased?", (ii) "Did the product meet your expectations?", (iii) "Would you recommend this product to a friend?", (iv) "Any issues or concerns with the product?", (v) product-specific questions based on category (e.g., "How is the paint coverage?" for paint, "How is the tool durability?" for power tools) | System | Market Research Analyst | Automated |
| 2 | Monthly analysis: (a) product satisfaction score by category and SKU, (b) identify SKUs with satisfaction score < 3.5/5.0 for Category Manager review, (c) product return correlation: are low-satisfaction SKUs also returned more frequently?, (d) feed into private label quality monitoring (W1839) for BuildRight-branded products | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 day/month |
| 3 | Quarterly: Category Manager reviews product satisfaction data: (a) national brand products with low satisfaction: discuss with vendor, consider alternative products, (b) private label products with low satisfaction: escalate to QA Manager per W1839, (c) customer satisfaction trends inform assortment decisions per W679 (assortment optimization) | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Automated post-purchase survey triggered by transaction event (W1906.1)
- Product satisfaction analytics by SKU with return rate correlation (W1906.2)
- Integration with W12 (returns), W1839 (private label quality), W679 (assortment optimization)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low response rate**: Most customers don't respond to post-purchase emails; loyalty point incentives improve response rate

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by Market Research Analyst

### Time Estimate
- Monthly analysis: 1 day/month
- Quarterly review: 1 day
- Total: ~15 days/year

## W1907. Customer Complaint Root Cause Analysis & Voice of Customer (VOC) Integration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly complaint data aggregation |
| **Frequency** | Monthly analysis; quarterly deep-dive |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–5,000 customer complaints/month across all channels |
| **Owner** | Market Research Analyst |
| **Participants** | Market Research Analyst, VP Store Operations, Category Manager, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly: Market Research Analyst aggregates complaint data from all channels: (a) in-store complaints (logged by CSR per W13.1), (b) call center complaints (logged by customer service), (c) email complaints, (d) social media complaints (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter mentions), (e) ecommerce reviews and ratings, (f) categorize complaints: product quality, pricing, staff behavior, store cleanliness, out-of-stock, delivery, returns, wait time, others | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 day/month |
| 2 | Root cause analysis: (a) top 10 complaint categories by volume and severity, (b) complaint rate per store: identify stores with complaint rates > 2× chain average, (c) Pareto analysis: 80% of complaints from which 20% of causes?, (d) trend analysis: are complaint volumes increasing or decreasing by category?, (e) specific product complaints: SKUs with highest complaint rates for Category Manager review, (f) staff behavior complaints: identify specific stores or departments for targeted training | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 day/month |
| 3 | Quarterly deep-dive: (a) Market Research Analyst presents comprehensive complaint analysis to VP Store Operations, VP Merchandising, and CMO, (b) identify systemic issues (process, training, product, policy) vs. isolated incidents, (c) recommend specific corrective actions with measurable targets, (d) track corrective action effectiveness: did complaint rate decrease after intervention? | Market Research Analyst / CMO | COO | 1 day/quarter |
| 4 | VOC integration: (a) integrate complaint insights with CSAT, NPS, and post-purchase data for holistic customer experience view, (b) feed insights into relevant workflows: product quality → W1839, staff training → W41, pricing → W40, inventory availability → W4, (c) monthly VOC summary distributed to all department heads | Market Research Analyst | CMO | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Multi-channel complaint aggregation from CRM, call center, social media, and ecommerce (W1907.1)
- Complaint categorization and analytics dashboard (W1907.2)
- Root cause analysis module with Pareto and trend analysis (W1907.2)
- VOC integration dashboard combining CSAT, NPS, CES, complaint, and post-purchase data (W1907.4)
- Integration with W13.1 (customer complaints), W1116 (complaint trend analysis), W1550 (VOC analysis)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Social media complaint volume**: BuildRight's Facebook page may receive 50–100+ comments/day; without social media monitoring tool, many complaints go unnoticed; invest in social media listening tool

### Staffing Implication
- **Market Research Analyst**: Complaint analysis adds ~2–3 days/month — absorbed by dedicated analyst role

### Time Estimate
- Monthly aggregation and analysis: 2 days/month
- Quarterly deep-dive: 1 day/quarter
- VOC integration: ~2 hours/month
- Total: ~35 days/year

## W1908. Mystery Shopping Program Execution & Compliance Scoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly mystery shopping cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly mystery shopping at 20–30 stores (rotated) |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 mystery shops/month; each store visited at least once per quarter |
| **Owner** | Market Research Analyst |
| **Participants** | Market Research Analyst, VP Store Operations, Mystery Shopping Agency, Store Managers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market Research Analyst manages mystery shopping program: (a) engage mystery shopping agency (Philippine-based with nationwide coverage), (b) evaluation criteria: (i) greeting and acknowledgment by Sales Associate, (ii) product knowledge: can associate answer basic product questions?, (iii) store cleanliness and organization, (iv) shelf price accuracy vs. POS price, (v) checkout experience: queue time, cashier friendliness, transaction accuracy, (vi) trade account recognition (if applicable), (vii) BOPIS experience (if applicable), (viii) fitting/estimation service availability (if applicable), (c) scoring rubric: 100-point scale per evaluation | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 day setup + ongoing |
| 2 | Mystery shopping agency executes monthly visits: (a) 20–30 stores per month (rotated to ensure all 200 stores covered quarterly), (b) mystery shoppers: mix of profiles (DIY homeowner, trade professional, new customer, returning customer), (c) scenarios: purchase specific item, ask product question, return an item, inquire about special order, (d) results submitted within 48 hours of visit | Mystery Shopping Agency | Market Research Analyst | Monthly |
| 3 | Monthly: Market Research Analyst analyzes mystery shopping results: (a) overall compliance score by store and trend, (b) scores by evaluation category: identify weak areas (e.g., product knowledge scoring lowest chain-wide), (c) recognize top-performing stores and associates, (d) flag stores with scores < 70% for VP Store Operations intervention, (e) distribute store-level results to Store Managers (confidentially) | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 day/month |
| 4 | Store Manager receives results and implements improvements: (a) address specific deficiencies identified in mystery shop report, (b) recognize associates who scored well, (c) retrain associates who scored poorly in specific areas, (d) target improvement in next quarter's mystery shop | Store Manager | VP Store Operations | Per store |

### System Touchpoints
- Mystery shopping scorecard management and result tracking (W1908.1–3)
- Store-level analytics with trend tracking (W1908.3)
- Score distribution to Store Managers through store manager app (W1908.3e)
- Integration with W67 (store performance), W41 (training), W575 (weekly store review)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mystery shopper detection**: Store associates may recognize repeat mystery shoppers, especially in smaller provincial stores; rotate shoppers and agencies periodically

### Staffing Implication
- **Mystery shopping agency cost**: ~PHP 1,500–3,000 per visit × 240–360 visits/year = ~PHP 360K–1.08M/year
- No incremental BuildRight headcount

### Time Estimate
- Program management: ~1 day/month
- Monthly analysis: ~1 day/month
- Total: ~25 days/year

## W1909. Store-Level Customer Experience Benchmarking & Improvement Action Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly CX benchmark reporting |
| **Frequency** | Monthly benchmarking; quarterly action planning |
| **Volume** | 200 stores benchmarked on CX metrics |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, Market Research Analyst, Store Managers, Regional Managers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly: Market Research Analyst generates store-level CX benchmark report: (a) composite CX score per store: weighted average of NPS, CSAT, CES, mystery shop score, complaint rate, (b) rank stores by CX score: top 20 and bottom 20, (c) trend: improving or declining vs. previous month/quarter, (d) identify outlier stores: (i) high sales but low CX (profitable but unsustainable — customer experience will erode sales), (ii) low sales but high CX (room to grow sales with improved traffic — good experience foundation) | Market Research Analyst | VP Store Operations | 1 day/month |
| 2 | Quarterly: VP Store Operations conducts CX action planning for bottom 20 stores: (a) review CX scorecard with each Store Manager and Regional Manager, (b) identify specific CX gaps per store: is it staffing, training, facility condition, inventory availability?, (c) develop 90-day improvement plan: specific actions, responsible parties, measurable targets, (d) allocate resources: additional training (W41), facility improvements (W47), staffing adjustments (W34) | VP Store Operations | COO | 1 day/quarter |
| 3 | Quarterly: VP Store Operations recognizes top 20 CX-performing stores: (a) include in monthly store performance review per W67, (b) financial incentive: bonus for Store Manager and team (PHP 10K–30K), (c) feature in internal newsletter as best practice examples, (d) share improvement techniques with bottom-performing stores | VP Store Operations | COO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-level CX composite scoring dashboard (W1909.1)
- CX trend tracking with ranking and percentile distribution (W1909.1)
- 90-day improvement plan tracking per store (W1909.2)
- Integration with W67 (store performance), W575 (weekly store review), W72 (employee performance)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric gaming**: Store Managers may focus on improving survey scores rather than actual customer experience (e.g., selectively inviting satisfied customers to respond); ensure data collection is randomized and representative

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by VP Store Operations and Market Research Analyst

### Time Estimate
- Monthly benchmark: 1 day/month
- Quarterly action planning: 1 day/quarter
- Quarterly recognition: 1 day/quarter
- Total: ~18 days/year
