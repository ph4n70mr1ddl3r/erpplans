## W3353. National Government Stakeholder Mapping & Relationship Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual planning; new stakeholder; leadership change |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + annual |
| **Volume** | National stakeholder map (Congress, agencies, regulators) |
| **Owner** | Government Affairs Manager |
| **Participants** | Govt Affairs Mgr, VP Legal & Compliance, CEO, Entity leadership |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Govt Affairs Mgr build and maintain the national stakeholder map: legislators (Senators, Representatives, relevant committees — trade, labor, finance, ways & means), executive agencies (DTI, DOLE, BIR, BSP, NTC, DENR, DTI-BPS, ARTA, FDA), regulators, and local-government leagues at the national level | Govt Affairs Mgr | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 2 | Govt Affairs Mgr cultivate and manage relationships: briefings, meetings, site visits, information sharing, and BuildRight positioning as a credible industry voice; coordinate executive participation (W3359) | Govt Affairs Mgr / Leadership | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 3 | Govt Affairs Mgr track engagement (CRM-style), relationship health, and issues per stakeholder; brief leadership and coordinate with LGU relationship work (links to VS-76.3) and labor liaison (links to VS-84.3 W2894) | Govt Affairs Mgr | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Stakeholder/CRM tool; records (links to VS-88); governance (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Relationship-gap risk**: Key stakeholders unknown/unengaged when a policy issue arises; mitigated by proactive mapping
- **Ethics risk**: Improper relationship cultivation creates bribery/conflict exposure (links to VS-86.3/W3360); mitigated by ethics policy


### Automation Opportunity
- System workflow orchestration of "coordinate executive participation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track engagement" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-541 (ensure controlled execution — National Government Relations & Public Policy Advocacy (PA-104.1).)
- operational: proactive mapping; operational: ethics policy

### Time Estimate
- Continuous
