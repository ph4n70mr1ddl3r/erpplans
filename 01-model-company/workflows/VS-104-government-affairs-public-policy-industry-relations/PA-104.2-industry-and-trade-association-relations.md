## W3361. Industry Association Membership Strategy, Selection & Dues Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual review; new association opportunity |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on opportunity |
| **Volume** | Portfolio of memberships (retail, chambers, supply-chain, sector) |
| **Owner** | Government Affairs Manager |
| **Participants** | Govt Affairs Mgr, VP Legal & Compliance, affected business units, Procurement/Finance (links to VS-34/VS-17.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Govt Affairs Mgr maintain the membership strategy and portfolio: assess each association's value (advocacy access, benchmarking, standards, networking, brand) vs cost (dues, time); select/maintain/exit memberships | Govt Affairs Mgr | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 2 | Govt Affairs Mgr/Procurement manage dues, contracts, and renewals (links to VS-34.2); approve per authority matrix (links to VS-36); assign BuildRight representatives and engagement plan per association | Govt Affairs Mgr / Procurement | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 3 | Govt Affairs Mgr track membership utilization and ROI (W3367), and report the portfolio to leadership; coordinate labor-association engagement (ECOP/FWE) with labor relations (links to VS-84.3 W2893) | Govt Affairs Mgr | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Membership tracking; AP/dues (links to VS-34/VS-17.1); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Wasted-dues risk**: Memberships underused; mitigated by ROI evaluation (W3367)
- **Misaligned-membership risk**: Joining associations inconsistent with BuildRight values/strategy; mitigated by review


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-assign of "assign BuildRight representatives and engagement plan per association" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track membership utilization and ROI" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-542 (ensure controlled execution — Industry & Trade Association Relations (PA-104.2).)
- operational: ROI evaluation (W3367); operational: review

### Time Estimate
- Annual
