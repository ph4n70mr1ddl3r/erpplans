## W3369. Public Affairs Strategy & Issue Management (Non-Crisis)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Emerging public issue; complements VS-14.3 crisis comms |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + per issue |
| **Volume** | Active public-affairs issues |
| **Owner** | Government Affairs Manager |
| **Participants** | Govt Affairs Mgr, Comms/PR (links to VS-14.3), Legal (links to VS-100), affected business units, Leadership |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Govt Affairs Mgr/Comms identify and track emerging public issues affecting BuildRight's license to operate (consumer/retail, labor, environmental, tax, supply chain, data); assess materiality and stakeholder sentiment | Govt Affairs Mgr / Comms | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 2 | Govt Affairs Mgr/Comms manage the issue: position, messaging, stakeholder engagement (government W3356, industry W3358, community W3371, media W3373), and escalation to crisis comms when warranted (links to VS-14.3 W134/W1562) | Govt Affairs Mgr / Comms | VP Legal & Compliance | Per issue |
| 3 | Govt Affairs Mgr capture outcomes and lessons, update issue playbook, and feed insights to risk register (links to VS-21.2) and reputation monitoring (W3370) | Govt Affairs Mgr | VP Legal & Compliance | Per issue |

### System Touchpoints
- Issue tracking; comms/PR (links to VS-14.3); risk register (links to VS-21.2); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Issue-escalation risk**: Failing to manage an issue before it becomes a crisis (links to VS-14.3); mitigated by early identification and engagement
- **Mixed-messaging risk**: Inconsistent BuildRight voice across stakeholders; mitigated by coordinated messaging


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-track of "track emerging public issues affecting BuildRight's license to operate" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-issue of "issue" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "monitoring" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: early identification and engagement; operational: coordinated messaging

### Time Estimate
- Per issue
