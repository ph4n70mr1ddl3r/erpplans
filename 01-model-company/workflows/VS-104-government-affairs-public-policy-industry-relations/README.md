## Overview

Government Affairs, Public Policy & Industry Relations workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **corporate external-affairs function** for the 5-entity group at the national, policy,
and industry level: national-government stakeholder mapping and relationship management,
legislative and regulatory horizon scanning and impact assessment, public-policy position
development and advocacy-campaign execution, engagement with Congress and national executive
agencies (DTI, DOLE, BIR/Bureau of Internal Revenue, BSP, NTC, DENR, DTI-BPS, ARTA) at the
*policy* level, cross-industry coalition building and joint advocacy, industry/trade-association
membership, leadership and committee representation, industry-standard and best-practice
development participation, peer-retailer benchmarking and knowledge exchange, industry-event
participation, public-affairs strategy and non-crisis issue management, corporate positioning and
stakeholder narrative, community and stakeholder relations at the national brand level, think-tank
and academic partnerships, political/regulatory risk assessment, and external-affairs annual
planning and board reporting.

This is distinct from **VS-76 (Multi-Region LGU Compliance)** which handles *local-government*
(LGU/municipal/city/barangay) permits, local business tax, and local relationships for 200 store
locations; from **VS-22 (Compliance & Regulatory)** which executes *permit/license operations* and
responds to government audits/inspections; from **VS-84.3 (Labor Relations)** which owns
*labor-specific* policy advocacy and labor-coalition/industry-association engagement (W2893 ECOP/FWE,
W2894 DOLE/BIR/SSS labor liaison); from **VS-36 (Corporate Governance)** which handles board and
entity governance; from **VS-14.3 (Brand/PR/Corporate Communications)** which handles
media/PR/crisis-communications execution; and from **VS-100 (Legal Operations)** which handles
litigation and legal-matter management. This value stream owns the *proactive corporate
government-affairs and industry-relations program* at the national/policy level — shaping the
rules BuildRight operates under and the industry ecosystem it participates in — beyond labor and
beyond local permitting.

BuildRight's exposure spans a PHP 62.3B retailer operating under Philippine national regulation
(BIR tax reform/e-invoicing per VS-79, DTI consumer protection, DOLE labor standards, BSP payments,
DENR environmental, customs/tariff per VS-87, data privacy per VS-91), participation in retail and
supply-chain industry associations (e.g., Philippine Retailers Association, chambers of commerce,
industry coalitions), 200 stores across regions requiring consistent national policy engagement,
and a board that expects periodic external-affairs reporting (links to VS-36.1). No prior value
stream owned the national corporate government-affairs / industry-relations discipline.
