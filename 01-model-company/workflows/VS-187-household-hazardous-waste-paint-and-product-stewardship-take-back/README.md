# VS-187: Household Hazardous Waste, Paint & Used-Product Stewardship Take-Back Program

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Household hazardous waste (HHW), leftover paint, and used-product stewardship take-back program for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **customer-facing take-back and stewardship program** that accepts back the residual hazardous/regulated materials BuildRight sells: leftover latex and oil-based paint (~2,800 paint SKUs, 8% of assortment); used motor oil, lubricants and filters; lead-acid and lithium batteries; fluorescent, CFL and mercury-vapor lamps; pesticides and garden/agro chemicals; solvents and adhesives; consumer e-waste, power tools and appliances; and propane/LPG cylinders (in coordination with VS-175). The program covers stewardship strategy and brand/manufacturer co-funding; DENR-EMB hazardous-waste generator/treater registration and RA 6969 compliance; the take-back partner/transporter/TSD-facility network; in-store collection-point design and acceptance policy; the per-stream collection, safe storage, and consolidation-to-pickup lifecycle; recovery, reuse (latex reblending/donation), recycling, and compliant disposal; DENR-EMB annual hazardous-waste reporting and NSWMC/EPR alignment; and diversion-rate, recycling-yield, and program-impact analytics.

This is distinct from VS-73 (store *operational* waste — cardboard, plastic, food, BuildRight's own refuse), VS-179 (EPR compliance scoped to *plastic* packaging under RA 11898), VS-114 (dangerous-goods/hazmat *transport* of salable merchandise), VS-24 (employee/site HSE), and VS-25 (ESG *reporting* of the footprint). No value stream owned the *customer take-back of leftover paint, used oil, batteries, lamps, pesticides, and e-waste* — a recognized retailer product-stewardship discipline (the PaintCare / Call2Recycle / lamp-and-battery take-back model) that diverts hazardous material from landfill, satisfies DENR-EMB hazardous-waste obligations (RA 6969, RA 8749 Clean Air, RA 9275 Clean Water), strengthens BuildRight's ESG/community posture, drives footfall, and closes the loop on the hazardous products it sells. At ~200 provincial stores, ~2,800 paint SKUs, and a hazardous-chemical-heavy assortment, an ungoverned take-back program risks DENR-EMB violations, store-level safety incidents (a lithium-battery or paint-fire), and a missed circular-economy margin from reblended paint and recovered materials.
