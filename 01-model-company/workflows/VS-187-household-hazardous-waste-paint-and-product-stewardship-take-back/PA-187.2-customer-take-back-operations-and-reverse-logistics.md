## W5353. Customer Paint Take-Back, Latex/Oil Sorting & Usable Paint Reblending (PaintCare)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer returns leftover paint at the take-back counter |
| **Frequency** | ~1,500–4,000 paint take-backs/store/yr (highest-volume stream) |
| **Volume** | ~1–5 containers per visit; ~300k–800k containers/yr group-wide |
| **Owner** | Take-Back Counter Associate |
| **Participants** | HSE Officer, Paint Dept Supervisor, Reblending Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify eligibility/quantity per policy (W5350); inspect container (labeled, not mixed with other waste). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 2 min |
| 2 | Sort by type: water-based (latex/acrylic) vs. oil/solvent-based; separate non-paint (thinners, stains) to the solvent stream (W5357). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 1 min |
| 3 | Stage in the ventilated flame cabinet (W5349); log container count/volume to the store hazardous-waste ledger. | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 2 min |
| 4 | Issue loyalty points/incentive per policy (W5350). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 1 min |
| 5 | Flag usable volumes for reblending/donation (W5362) vs. recycling/disposal. | Paint Dept Supervisor | Take-Back Counter Associate | per batch |

### System Touchpoints
- Take-back transaction record (per customer, per container); hazardous-waste ledger (paint stream); loyalty incentive (VS-13).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-sort risk**: oil-based paint routed to the latex reblending batch contaminates it and ruins the reblended product; mitigated by sort-at-counter SOP and a separate cabinet section per type.
- **Fume/fire risk**: accumulated solvent-based paint in an unventilated cabinet creates fume/fire exposure; mitigated by ventilated flame cabinet and quantity caps per the BFP FSIC.

### Time Estimate
- ~6 min per visit; ~5,000/yr/store at a busy store → ~500 person-hours/yr/store.

### Automation Opportunity
- Barcode/label scan auto-classifies paint type from the merchandise master (links VS-01/VS-137) and logs to the ledger.

### Controls
- CTL-23 (fraud detection — water-filled containers); CTL-41 (paint hazardous-waste handling).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-09 (paint dept/mixing), VS-13 (loyalty), VS-114 (DG containment)

## W5354. Used Motor Oil, Lubricant & Filter Collection & Bulk Consolidation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer (DIY/auto/contractor) returns used oil/filters |
| **Frequency** | ~200–600 oil take-backs/store/yr |
| **Volume** | ~1–10 liters per visit; ~150k–400k liters/yr group-wide |
| **Owner** | Take-Back Counter Associate |
| **Participants** | HSE Officer, Oil Re-refinery Partner, Store Receiving |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify the container is used oil/lube only (no coolant/solvent/fuel mixed — refusal script if contaminated). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 3 min |
| 2 | Transfer to the bunded oil-rated drum in the containment area (W5349); log liters to the ledger. | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 5 min |
| 3 | Drain/collect used oil filters into a separate rated container; log count. | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 3 min |
| 4 | Issue incentive per policy (W5350). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 1 min |
| 5 | Monitor drum fill level; schedule bulk consolidation pickup before capacity (W5360). | HSE Officer | Take-Back Counter Associate | weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- Hazardous-waste ledger (used-oil stream, liters); drum fill-level tracking; bulk pickup scheduling (W5360).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Contamination risk**: coolant/solvent/fuel mixed into used oil makes it un-re-refinable and hazardous; mitigated by a verification/refusal step and customer guidance.
- **Spill risk**: a drum overflow or spill contaminates the store/site (Clean Water Act RA 9275); mitigated by bunding, fill-level monitoring, and spill kits.

### Time Estimate
- ~12 min per visit; ~400/yr/store → ~80 person-hours/yr/store.

### Automation Opportunity
- Drum fill-level sensor auto-schedules pickup before capacity; liters auto-logged via flow meter.

### Controls
- CTL-41 (used-oil hazardous-waste handling); CTL-21 (volume-accuracy on the ledger).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-114 (DG), VS-24 (HSE/spill), VS-61 (fleet oil cross-link), VS-175 (propane/gas adjacency)

## W5355. Lead-Acid & Lithium Battery Collection, Safe Storage & Fire-Separation Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer returns used batteries (auto, power-tool, appliance, electronics) |
| **Frequency** | ~300–800 battery take-backs/store/yr |
| **Volume** | ~1–5 units per visit; ~150k–400k batteries/yr group-wide |
| **Owner** | Take-Back Counter Associate |
| **Participants** | HSE Officer, Battery Recycler Partner, BFP Fire Safety |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify chemistry: lead-acid (auto/UPS) vs. lithium-ion (power tools/electronics) — distinct fire/storage profiles. | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 2 min |
| 2 | Inspect for damage/swelling/leak; tape terminals of lithium cells to prevent short-circuit. | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 3 min |
| 3 | Stage lead-acid in acid-rated containment; lithium in fire-separated, non-conductive bins with sand/quench provision (BFP Fire Code). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 3 min |
| 4 | Log count/weight to the ledger per chemistry; issue incentive (W5350). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 2 min |
| 5 | Monitor for swelling/thermal event; isolate any suspect cell in a quench container. | HSE Officer | Take-Back Counter Associate | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Hazardous-waste ledger (battery stream by chemistry); fire-alarm/suppression integration; recycler-partner scheduling (W5360).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Thermal-runaway/fire risk**: a damaged/swollen lithium cell short-circuits and ignites in the containment area — a severe retail fire (BFP, life safety); mitigated by terminal-taping, fire-separated non-conductive bins, and a quench-container isolation procedure.
- **Acid-leak risk**: a cracked lead-acid battery leaks acid in the containment area; mitigated by acid-rated containment and spill kits.

### Time Estimate
- ~10 min per visit; ~500/yr/store → ~80 person-hours/yr/store.

### Automation Opportunity
- Chemistry auto-suggested from the merchandise master barcode; suspect-cell isolation checklist.

### Controls
- CTL-41 (battery hazardous-waste handling); operational: BFP fire-separation commissioning.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-114 (DG), VS-24 (HSE/fire), VS-179 (EPR/e-waste), VS-143 (appliance take-back cross-link)

## W5356. Fluorescent, CFL & Mercury-Vapor Lamp Collection & Mercury Containment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer returns spent fluorescent tubes, CFLs, or mercury-vapor lamps |
| **Frequency** | ~100–300 lamp take-backs/store/yr |
| **Volume** | ~1–10 lamps per visit; ~50k–150k lamps/yr group-wide |
| **Owner** | Take-Back Counter Associate |
| **Participants** | HSE Officer, Mercury/Lamp Recycler Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accept intact lamps only; broken lamps follow the spill/breakage procedure (W5366). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 2 min |
| 2 | Place in mercury-tight lamp containers (rigid tubes/boxes) to prevent breakage and vapor release. | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 3 min |
| 3 | Log count/type to the ledger; issue incentive (W5350). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 2 min |
| 4 | Store in the ventilated containment area away from customer access and stock. | HSE Officer | Take-Back Counter Associate | ongoing |
| 5 | Schedule pickup by the mercury/lamp recycler before container capacity (W5360). | HSE Officer | Take-Back Counter Associate | per fill |

### System Touchpoints
- Hazardous-waste ledger (lamp stream); mercury-recycler scheduling; breakage-incident link (W5366).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mercury-exposure risk**: a broken fluorescent releases mercury vapor in the store — a health and Clean Air Act (RA 8749) exposure; mitigated by mercury-tight rigid containers and a defined breakage/spill procedure (W5366).
- **Breakage-in-storage risk**: fragile lamps crushed in storage release mercury; mitigated by rigid containers and no-stacking rules.

### Time Estimate
- ~7 min per visit; ~200/yr/store → ~25 person-hours/yr/store.

### Automation Opportunity
- Fill-level-sensed containers auto-schedule pickup.

### Controls
- CTL-41 (mercury hazardous-waste handling); operational: breakage/spill procedure (W5366).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-114 (DG), VS-78 (green building/clean-air adjacency)

## W5357. Pesticide, Garden Chemical & Solvent Take-Back & Restricted-Substance Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer returns leftover pesticides, garden/agro chemicals, solvents, adhesives, thinners |
| **Frequency** | ~100–300 take-backs/store/yr |
| **Volume** | ~1–4 containers per visit; ~50k–120k containers/yr group-wide |
| **Owner** | Take-Back Counter Associate |
| **Participants** | HSE Officer, FPA/DENR Partner, Compliance Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify the container is labeled/identifiable; refuse unidentified/unlabeled chemicals (policy W5350). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 3 min |
| 2 | Identify restricted/controlled pesticides (FPA-regulated) and route to the restricted-substance protocol (separate manifest, FPA-licensed handler). | Take-Back Associate | Compliance Specialist | 5 min |
| 3 | Stage in the ventilated flame cabinet by compatibility group (no incompatible mixing); log to the ledger. | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 3 min |
| 4 | Issue incentive per policy (W5350). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 1 min |
| 5 | Monitor cabinet fill; schedule pickup by the licensed chemical handler (W5360). | HSE Officer | Take-Back Counter Associate | weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- Hazardous-waste ledger (pesticide/solvent stream, by compatibility group); FPA/DENR handler scheduling; restricted-substance manifest flag.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Incompatible-mixing risk**: mixing incompatible chemicals (e.g. bleach and ammonia-based) in one cabinet causes reaction/fumes; mitigated by compatibility-group separation and labeling.
- **Restricted-substance risk**: mishandling FPA-controlled pesticides is a specific regulatory violation; mitigated by the restricted-substance protocol and FPA-licensed handlers.

### Time Estimate
- ~12 min per visit; ~200/yr/store → ~40 person-hours/yr/store.

### Automation Opportunity
- Barcode auto-classifies compatibility group and restricted-substance status from the merchandise master.

### Controls
- CTL-41 (pesticide/solvent hazardous-waste handling); CTL-34 (FPA-restricted-substance compliance).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-114 (DG), VS-24 (HSE), VS-145 (garden/chemical adjacency), VS-117 (DTI-BPS chemical standards)

## W5358. Consumer E-Waste, Power Tool & Appliance Take-Back & Data-Bearing Device Sanitization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer returns used electronics, power tools, small appliances |
| **Frequency** | ~100–400 take-backs/store/yr |
| **Volume** | ~1–3 units per visit; ~60k–200k units/yr group-wide |
| **Owner** | Take-Back Counter Associate |
| **Participants** | HSE Officer, E-Waste Recycler Partner, Privacy/DPO (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accept consumer e-waste/small appliances; route white-goods/large appliances to VS-143 haul-away/recovery. | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 3 min |
| 2 | Identify data-bearing devices (phones, tablets, smart tools, storage); route to data-sanitization before recycling (RA 10173). | Take-Back Associate | Privacy/DPO | 5 min |
| 3 | Sanitize/destroy data-bearing media; issue a sanitization certificate to the customer. | E-Waste Recycler Partner | Privacy/DPO | per device |
| 4 | Remove embedded batteries to the battery stream (W5355); log unit/count to the ledger. | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 3 min |
| 5 | Issue incentive per policy (W5350); stage for e-waste recycler pickup (W5360). | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 2 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Hazardous-waste ledger (e-waste stream); data-sanitization certificate register (VS-91/VS-88); e-waste recycler scheduling (W5360).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-privacy risk**: recycling a customer's data-bearing device without sanitization breaches RA 10173 and exposes customer data; mitigated by mandatory sanitization + certificate.
- **Embedded-battery risk**: an e-waste unit with an undetected lithium battery causes a fire in the e-waste bin; mitigated by battery removal before staging.

### Time Estimate
- ~15 min per visit (more if data device); ~250/yr/store → ~60 person-hours/yr/store.

### Automation Opportunity
- Data-device detection prompt at intake; sanitization certificate auto-generated and emailed.

### Controls
- CTL-27 (customer data safeguards / sanitization); CTL-41 (e-waste handling).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-91 (privacy), VS-88 (records/sanitization cert), VS-143 (appliance haul-away), VS-99 (IT asset disposal), VS-155 (trade-in/resale cross-link)

## W5359. Propane/LPG Cylinder Take-Back, Condemned-Cylinder Handling & Cross-link to VS-175

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer returns an unwanted/old/damaged LPG cylinder or it is condemned at the cage (VS-175) |
| **Frequency** | ~50–150 cylinder take-backs/store/yr |
| **Volume** | ~1–4 cylinders per visit; ~20k–60k cylinders/yr group-wide |
| **Owner** | Take-Back Counter Associate |
| **Participants** | LPG Program Lead (VS-175), HSE Officer, Cylinder Disposal Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify the cylinder is empty/vented before acceptance; refuse pressurized/valve-intact unknowns to the VS-175 venting protocol. | Take-Back Associate | LPG Program Lead | 5 min |
| 2 | Inspect for condemnation criteria (rust, valve damage, out-of-test); route serviceable cylinders to the VS-175 pool, condemned to scrap. | Take-Back Associate | LPG Program Lead | 5 min |
| 3 | Log the cylinder to the hazardous-waste/asset ledger (cross-link VS-175 cylinder fleet); resolve deposit/float if applicable. | Take-Back Associate | Take-Back Counter Associate | 3 min |
| 4 | Stage condemned cylinders in the BFP-rated cage (VS-175) for the disposal partner. | HSE Officer | Take-Back Counter Associate | per cylinder |
| 5 | Coordinate pickup with the licensed cylinder-scrap partner (W5360). | LPG Program Lead | Take-Back Counter Associate | per batch |

### System Touchpoints
- Hazardous-waste ledger (cylinder stream) cross-linked to the VS-175 cylinder-fleet ledger; deposit/float resolution (VS-175).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pressurized-cylinder risk**: accepting a pressurized/valve-intact cylinder of unknown contents is a fire/explosion hazard; mitigated by the venting-verification gate and routing to the VS-175 protocol.
- **Cylinder-fleet-leakage risk**: take-back cylinders not reconciled to the VS-175 pool distort the deposit-float and asset ledger; mitigated by the cross-linked ledger and deposit resolution.

### Time Estimate
- ~15 min per visit; ~100/yr/store → ~25 person-hours/yr/store.

### Automation Opportunity
- Cylinder barcode/ID scan auto-reconciles to the VS-175 fleet ledger and resolves deposit.

### Controls
- CTL-26 (cylinder-asset reconciliation); CTL-41 (cylinder hazardous-waste handling).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-175 (propane/LPG program), VS-114 (DG), VS-24 (HSE/fire)

## W5360. Hazardous Waste Consolidation, Manifest Generation & Licensed Transporter Pickup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store stream-container fill threshold reached; scheduled pickup cycle |
| **Frequency** | ~2–8 pickups/store/month across streams |
| **Volume** | ~5,000–15,000 pickup events/yr group-wide |
| **Owner** | HSE Officer |
| **Participants** | Store Staff, TSD Transporter (W5347), Compliance Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review the store hazardous-waste ledger; confirm containers at/over pickup threshold by stream. | HSE Officer | HSE Officer | 30 min |
| 2 | Schedule the licensed transporter for the streams due; confirm transporter accreditation (W5347). | Compliance Specialist | HSE Officer | 30 min |
| 3 | Generate the DENR-format manifest from the ledger (W5351); stage containers at the pickup point. | Store Staff | HSE Officer | 45 min |
| 4 | Hand over with transporter signature/timestamp and spill-response acknowledgment; release containers. | TSD Transporter | HSE Officer | 30 min |
| 5 | Confirm pickup in the ledger; track to TSD receipt (W5351 chain-of-custody); restage containment. | Compliance Specialist | HSE Officer | per pickup |

### System Touchpoints
- Hazardous-waste manifest/chain-of-custody module (W5351); transporter-accreditation link; store ledger pickup-threshold alerts.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-accumulation risk**: missing a pickup threshold leaves hazardous waste accumulating past permitted storage time/quantity (DENR-EMB limit); mitigated by threshold-based pickup alerts and scheduled cycles.
- **Manifest-handover risk**: releasing waste without a complete manifest breaks the chain-of-custody and BuildRight's cradle-to-grave liability; mitigated by a no-release-without-signed-manifest gate.

### Time Estimate
- ~2 hours per pickup batch at store; ~60/store/yr → ~120 person-hours/yr/store.

### Automation Opportunity
- Fill-threshold alerts auto-schedule pickup; manifest auto-generated and transporter-accredited at scheduling.

### Controls
- CTL-41 (hazardous-waste manifest/transporter compliance); operational: no-release-without-signed-manifest gate.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-114 (DG transport), VS-161 (TPRM transporter), VS-06 (logistics scheduling), VS-88 (manifest records retention)
