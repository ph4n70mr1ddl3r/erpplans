# VS-87: Customs Trade Compliance & Tariff Optimization

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Customs Trade Compliance & Tariff Optimization workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — covering
the trade-compliance program for ~40% of COGS sourced through imports (~400–600 TEUs/month,
~PHP 1.4B/month import value): HS tariff classification, customs valuation, rules of origin,
ASEAN/AFTA and RCEP preference utilization, duty drawback, customs bonded warehouse
operations governance, customs broker management, and Bureau of Customs (BOC) audit defense.

While Supply Planning (VS-02, PA-02.2) executes import operations and logistics, this value
stream owns the **trade compliance and tariff strategy**: classification accuracy, duty
optimization, preference claims, and BOC regulatory compliance and audit defense.
