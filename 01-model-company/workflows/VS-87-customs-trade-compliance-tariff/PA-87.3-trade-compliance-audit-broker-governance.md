## W2961. Customs Broker Selection, Accreditation & Performance Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Broker onboarding or renewal |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~3–5 customs brokers |
| **Owner** | Trade Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Compliance, Procurement, Logistics |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement vets customs broker: BOC accreditation (CAB-CBP), PRC license, experience, references, insurance | Procurement | Trade Compliance Manager | Per onboarding |
| 2 | Trade Compliance/Logistics contracts broker; defines SLA (clearance time, error rate, availability) and KPIs | Trade Compliance / Logistics | Trade Compliance Manager | Per contract |
| 3 | Trade Compliance monitors broker performance quarterly; reviews errors, delays, and complaints; manages renewal/termination | Trade Compliance | Trade Compliance Manager | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Broker accreditation register; SLA tracker; performance scorecard

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Service risk**: Poor broker performance delays clearance and incurs demurrage; mitigated by SLA and quarterly review


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics contracts broker" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "reviews errors, delays, and complaints" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-492 (ensure controlled execution — Trade Compliance Audit, Broker Governance & ADC/NPCA (PA-87.3).)
- operational: SLA and quarterly review

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 days/quarter
