## W2953. FTA Preference Strategy & Annual Duty Savings Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual strategy review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | All imported categories |
| **Owner** | Trade Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Compliance, Procurement, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Compliance analyzes duty savings opportunity across FTAs (AFTA, RCEP, ATIGA, ACFTA, AKFTA, AJCEP, AANZFTA, IPAPTA) | Trade Compliance | Trade Compliance Manager | Annual |
| 2 | Procurement incorporates FTA preference in sourcing decisions (e.g., shift volume to FTA-partner country for eligible SKUs) | Procurement | VP Merchandising | Annual |
| 3 | Finance quantifies realized duty savings vs. baseline MFN duty; reports to Executive Committee | Finance | CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- FTA savings model; sourcing decision support; savings report

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cost risk**: Missed preference over-pays duty; mitigated by annual savings analysis


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-analyze (trade compliance analyzes duty savings opportunity)
- auto-count (to fta-partner country for eligible skus))
- auto-report (mfn duty; reports to executive committee)

### Controls
operational: annual savings analysis

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks annual

## W2955. Duty Drawback Scheme Application & Re-export Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Re-export of previously imported goods or unused imported goods |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Limited (returned/unused imports) |
| **Owner** | Trade Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Compliance, Finance, Logistics, BOC |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Compliance identifies duty drawback opportunity on re-exported or unused-destroyed imports per Customs Modernization and Tariff Act | Trade Compliance | Trade Compliance Manager | Per case |
| 2 | Finance prepares drawback claim with import entry evidence, re-export proof, duty paid evidence | Finance | Trade Compliance Manager | Per claim |
| 3 | Trade Compliance files drawback claim with BOC; tracks adjudication; books refund | Trade Compliance | Trade Compliance Manager | Per claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Drawback claim tracker; import/export evidence; refund GL

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Recovery risk**: Missed drawback forfeits rightful refund; mitigated by identification and claim process


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-import (or unused-destroyed imports per customs modernization)
- auto-generate (finance prepares drawback claim with)
- auto-file (trade compliance files drawback claim with)

### Controls
operational: identification and claim process

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 days per claim

## W2958. Duty Deferment, Bond & Guarantee Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Import requiring bond/guarantee |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~400–600 TEUs/month |
| **Owner** | Treasury Analyst |
| **Participants** | Treasury, Trade Compliance, Surety/Bank |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury maintains customs bond/surety/guarantee facility (re-usable bond for CBW, transshipment, protest) | Treasury | Treasury Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Trade Compliance applies bond to import entry as required; tracks bond utilization against facility limit | Trade Compliance | Trade Compliance Manager | Per entry |
| 3 | Treasury reconciles bond utilization; renews facility; addresses claims/calls on bond | Treasury | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Customs bond facility; bond utilization tracker

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Operational risk**: Bond facility exhaustion delays import clearance; mitigated by utilization monitoring


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-test (cbw, transshipment, protest))
- auto-track (as required; tracks bond utilization against)
- auto-reconcile (treasury reconciles bond utilization; renews)

### Controls
operational: utilization monitoring

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; quarterly review
