## W4891. Music Royalty Licensing — FILSCAP / OPM / IP Compliance & Statutory Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch (W4889); annual license renewal; playlist change (W4890); audit |
| **Frequency** | Annual license + continuous playlist-coverage verification |
| **Volume** | ~3–6 licensing societies (FILSCAP + sound-recording/mechanical/international) × group-wide coverage across 205 sites |
| **Owner** | Legal (VS-100.3) / In-Store Media Manager / Finance |
| **Participants** | FILSCAP & other PROs, Commercial music provider (W4892), Legal (VS-100), IP (VS-100.2), Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify all required music-public-performance licenses for public performance of copyrighted musical works and sound recordings in 200 stores under RA 8293 (IP Code): FILSCAP (Filipino + affiliated international societies for musical works), and the sound-recording/mechanical rights where applicable; scope per site-type and confirm the commercial music service's blanket-license coverage vs BuildRight's direct obligation | Legal / In-Store Media Manager | VP Legal / VP Marketing | Annual |
| 2 | Obtain/maintain the licenses: complete the FILSCAP establishment survey (no. of sites, floor area, ambient vs featured use), negotiate the annual blanket license fee (typically area/no.-of-speakers-based), execute agreements, and pay; retain certificates and renewal calendar (links VS-166) | Legal / Finance / In-Store Media Manager | VP Legal / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Verify ongoing compliance & statutory reporting: cross-check programmed playlists (W4890) against license coverage, submit any required music-use reports to FILSCAP/societies, manage the renewal calendar (W4889 of VS-166) to prevent lapse, and maintain the audit-ready evidence file for VS-21/internal-audit and any FILSCAP inspection (W4908) | Legal / In-Store Media Manager / Audit | VP Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- License/contract repository (VS-100.3/VS-166); AP/payment (VS-15/VS-17); playlist/CMS (W4892); legal/IP (VS-100.2); audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Infringement risk**: Unlicensed public performance → statutory damages per work + injunction (RA 8293); mitigated by the blanket license + coverage verification
- **Lapse risk**: A missed FILSCAP renewal leaves 200 stores unlicensed; mitigated by the renewal calendar (VS-166) + annual true-up
- **Double-coverage risk**: Paying both BuildRight-direct and via the music service for the same right; mitigated by the scoping step

### Time Estimate
- ~initial licensing 2–4 weeks; ~annual renewal 3–5 days; ~continuous coverage verification absorbed into W4890 refresh

### Automation Opportunity
- Playlist-vs-license coverage auto-check; renewal-calendar auto-alert (VS-166); fee-area auto-calc from site master

### Controls
- CTL-96 (ensure music-licensing & audio compliance.)
- CTL: FILSCAP blanket license current for all 205 sites; playlist coverage verified before publish; renewal calendar tracked; audit-ready evidence

### Cross-references
- links to RA 8293 (IP Code); VS-100/VS-100.2 (legal/IP), VS-15/VS-17 (AP), VS-21 (audit), VS-166 (license portfolio), W4890 (programming), W4892 (provider), W4908 (audit)
