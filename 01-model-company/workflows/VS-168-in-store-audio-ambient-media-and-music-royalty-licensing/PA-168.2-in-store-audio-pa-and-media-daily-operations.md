## W4897. PA / Paging System Daily Operations & Live Announcement Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store open (links VS-07.1); customer page; department/receiving/security call; emergency |
| **Frequency** | Daily, ~10–30 announcements/store/day |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × ~10–30 live announcements/day → ~60,000–180,000 announcements/month group-wide |
| **Owner** | Store Manager / Assistant Store Manager / Customer-Service Rep |
| **Participants** | Store staff (VS-07), In-Store Media Manager (VS-168), LP (VS-23), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Execute the standard announcement protocol per VS-07.1 (opening, customer service, department calls, receiving, coded security alerts, closing): use the standard opening, no slang/jokes/personal messages, no full customer names for privacy (use order-number reference) unless consented, and the coded-LP protocol (links VS-23) | Various Staff / Customer-Service Rep / Assistant SM | Store Manager | 30 sec–1 min each |
| 2 | Manage the PA system: confirm microphone health, override background music during announcements (auto-ducking), respect volume/quiet-hour limits, and log security/coded announcements for LP review (VS-23) | Assistant SM / LP | Store Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Handle exceptions & privacy: manage consent for customer-name paging (RA 10173), avoid paging sensitive info, escalate any misuse, and feed PA-usage/quality issues to the In-Store Media Manager (VS-168) | Store Manager / In-Store Media Manager | Store Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- PA/microphone/automation (VS-27/VS-138); music auto-ducking (W4898); LP logging (VS-23); privacy (VS-91); store ops (VS-07.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Privacy risk**: Paging a customer's full name without consent (RA 10173); mitigated by the order-number protocol + consent
- **LP-compromise risk**: Misuse of coded-LP announcements; mitigated by the coded protocol + logging
- **Quality risk**: Untrained/garbled announcements harm CX; mitigated by the standard protocol + audit (W4908)

### Time Estimate
- ~30 sec–1 min per announcement × ~10–30/day ≈ 5–30 staff-min/store/day; absorbed into store-ops staffing (VS-07)

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-ducking; order-number lookup for BOPIS/loyalty pages; text-to-speech for routine announcements; LP-coded-announcement logging

### Controls
- CTL-735 (ensure controlled execution — In-Store Audio, PA & Media Daily Operations (PA-168.2).)
- CTL: standard announcement protocol; privacy/order-number rule; coded-LP logged; volume/quiet-hour limits

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07.1 (daily management), VS-23 (LP), VS-91 (privacy), VS-24/VS-147 (safety PA), W4898 (music ducking), W4902 (emergency override), W4908 (audit)

## W4898. Background Music Daily Operations, Scheduling & Volume / Zone Control

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store open (links VS-07.1); daypart change; playlist update (W4890) |
| **Frequency** | Daily, continuous during trading hours |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × ~12 trading hours/day × zone/daypart playback |
| **Owner** | Store Manager / Assistant Store Manager |
| **Participants** | In-Store Media Manager (VS-168), Store Ops (VS-07), Commercial music provider (W4892) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start/stop the music per opening/closing (links VS-07.1): confirm the player is online and on-schedule (W4890), set the trading-hours daypart, and verify the zone coverage (sales floor, pro lounge, garden center; exclude paint/chemical aisles if scent-sensitive W4894) | Assistant SM | Store Manager | Open/close |
| 2 | Monitor volume & zoning: keep volume within the brand standard (audible but conversational), auto-duck for PA (W4897), respect quiet hours, and manage zone-specific levels (calmer checkout/pro-lounge); log any override | Assistant SM / Store Staff | Store Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Handle player/content exceptions: offline fallback (local cache per W4892), report outages/content issues to IT (VS-27) and the In-Store Media Manager, and confirm royalty-covered content (W4891) is playing | Assistant SM / IT / In-Store Media Manager | Store Manager | Per exception |

### System Touchpoints
- Music player/CMS (W4892/VS-27); PA auto-ducking (W4897); analytics (VS-28); store ops (VS-07); IT (VS-27)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Volume-nuisance risk**: Too-loud music harms conversation/consultative selling (VS-124); mitigated by the brand standard + zoning
- **Offline-music risk**: Cloud player down during network outage → silent store; mitigated by local-cache fallback
- **Override-abuse risk**: Stores self-changing playlists/volume → off-brand (W4890); mitigated by limited override + audit

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 staff-min/store/day (open/close + spot-checks); absorbed into VS-07 staffing

### Automation Opportunity
- Daypart auto-scheduling; auto-ducking; volume-cap enforcement; offline-fallback auto-engage

### Controls
- CTL-96 (ensure music-licensing & audio compliance.)
- CTL: brand-standard volume + zoning; override limited & logged; offline fallback; royalty-covered content verified

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07 (store ops), VS-27 (IT), W4890 (programming), W4891 (royalty), W4892 (provider), W4897 (PA ducking), W4900 (equipment), W4908 (audit)

## W4902. Emergency Override & Public-Address Incident Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Fire/alarm (VS-24); evacuation; security incident (VS-23/VS-147); typhoon/disaster (VS-69); AMBER/missing-child |
| **Frequency** | Rare (drill + incident) |
| **Volume** | ~205 sites × drills + rare real incidents |
| **Owner** | Store Manager / HSE (VS-24) / LP (VS-23) / Crisis Mgmt (VS-14.3) |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), LP (VS-23), Facilities (VS-138), In-Store Media Manager (VS-168), Customer Safety (VS-147) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Override all in-store media on emergency trigger: cut background music, interrupt signage with emergency messaging, and broadcast the pre-recorded or live emergency announcement (evacuation, shelter-in-place, lockdown) per the incident type; the PA must override every other audio source | Store Manager / HSE / LP | Store Manager | Immediate |
| 2 | Execute the incident protocol per VS-24/VS-147/VS-69: coordinate evacuation/shelter, manage customer safety (VS-147), engage LP/security (VS-23) and crisis comms (VS-14.3), and maintain the emergency PA until all-clear | HSE / LP / Store Manager / Crisis Mgmt | Store Manager | Per incident |
| 3 | Test, document & improve: run periodic emergency-override drills (links VS-24 tabletop/W1204), verify the override works on every site, log incidents/drills, and feed findings to the In-Store Media Manager and HSE for protocol improvement | HSE / In-Store Media Manager / Facilities | HSE Lead | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Emergency PA/override (VS-27/VS-138); fire/life-safety (VS-24); BMS (VS-138); LP (VS-23); crisis comms (VS-14.3); customer safety (VS-147)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Override-failure risk**: Emergency PA failing to cut music → delayed evacuation, life-safety liability; mitigated by drill-testing every site
- **Panic-communication risk**: Confused/garbled emergency announcements; mitigated by pre-recorded standard messages + trained staff
- **Drill-neglect risk**: Override untested → fails when needed; mitigated by the periodic drill + verification

### Time Estimate
- ~per drill 30–60 min; ~per incident variable; ~override test absorbed into VS-24 drills

### Automation Opportunity
- One-touch emergency override (cut music + interrupt signage + broadcast); auto-trigger from fire panel (VS-24); drill-scheduling

### Controls
- CTL-735 (ensure controlled execution — In-Store Audio, PA & Media Daily Operations (PA-168.2).)
- CTL: emergency override tested every site per drill cycle; pre-recorded standard messages; BCP-drill-verified (VS-24)

### Cross-references
