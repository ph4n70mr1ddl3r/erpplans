# VS-168: In-Store Audio, Ambient Media & Music Royalty Licensing

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

In-store audio, ambient media & music royalty licensing workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
owning the **enterprise-wide customer-facing ambient-experience and in-store media discipline**
across ~205 sites (200 stores at 8,000–15,000 sqm + 4 DCs will-call + HQ): the licensed background
music program (FILSCAP/OPM and international-society royalty licensing), the public-address (PA)
paging and live-announcement operations, the in-store digital-signage content programming, the
ambient scent/sensory program, the AV equipment lifecycle, and the analytics that link ambient
media to footfall, dwell, basket, and the "Your Home Building Partner" customer experience.

This matters at BuildRight's scale and exposure. Playing copyrighted sound recordings and musical
works across 200 stores is a **statutory licensing obligation** under RA 8293 (Intellectual
Property Code) administered by the **FILSCAP** (Filipino Society of Composers, Authors and
Publishers) and the international PRO/sound-recording societies — unlicensed public performance
carries per-work statutory damages and injunctive exposure (the gap-analysis candidate search
found 'music licensing' in **0** PA files as a dedicated header and only a single incidental
'FILSCAP' system-list reference in VS-07.1). Background music and in-store media are also a
material opex line (commercial music service + royalty + equipment + content production across
205 sites), a proven footfall/dwell/basket lever, and a brand-consistency and accessibility
(RA 7394 price-integrity announcements, RA 7277/BP 344) surface.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. The defining terms — "music licensing",
"FILSCAP", "background music", "in-store audio", "ambient media", and "music programming" — appear
in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers. The only existing coverage is the *step* of
making live PA announcements and switching off background music embedded inside the store
opening/closing workflow (VS-07.1 daily management) and a single FILSCAP mention in that file's
system list — no value stream owns the **ambient-media operating model**: the royalty-license
program, the commercial music service, the PA/paging standard & protocol, the in-store
digital-signage content (distinct from VS-48 vendor-paid retail-media ads), the ambient-scent
program, the AV equipment lifecycle, the customer-experience analytics, or the multi-entity
governance. It is **not** covered by **VS-14 (Marketing)**, which owns brand/campaign/PR — this
owns the *always-on in-store ambient channel*. It is **not** covered by **VS-07 (Store
Operations)**, which embeds one PA-announcement step — this owns the *media program and
royalty/asset discipline*. It is **not** covered by **VS-48 (Retail Media Network)**, which sells
*vendor-paid* advertising inventory — this owns *BuildRight's own* background music, PA, signage
content, and scent. It is **not** covered by **VS-138 (IFM)**, which maintains the *building* —
this owns the *content and royalty program* on the AV assets (with equipment maintenance as one
workflow linking to VS-138). And it is **not** covered by **VS-27 (IT)**, which operates the
*network* — this owns the *media programming and licensing*.

This value stream owns the end-to-end ambient-media discipline: strategy & operating model; music
programming & brand-sound standards; **music royalty licensing (FILSCAP/IP) & statutory
reporting**; commercial music-service vendor selection; in-store digital-signage content strategy;
ambient scent; budget & vendor co-funding; multi-entity standards; **PA/paging daily operations &
live-announcement protocol**; background-music daily scheduling/zoning; digital-signage publishing;
**AV equipment preventive maintenance** (links VS-138/VS-35); seasonal/event media deployment
(links VS-14/VS-64); **emergency override protocol** (links VS-24/VS-147); Filipino/regional
localization (links VS-14); content refresh & asset library; in-store media analytics; equipment
lifecycle & refresh capex (links VS-40); technology & integration architecture (links VS-27/VS-113);
**royalty compliance audit & true-up**; accessibility & regulatory compliance (RA 7394, RA 10173);
vendor SLA & TPRM (links VS-161); innovation (AI music, programmatic signage); and program
assurance. It integrates tightly with VS-14 (marketing/brand), VS-07 (store ops), VS-138 (facilities
AV maintenance), VS-35 (asset), VS-27/VS-113 (tech), VS-48 (retail media), VS-13 (CX), VS-24/VS-147
(emergency/safety PA), and VS-161 (TPRM).
