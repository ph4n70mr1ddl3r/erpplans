## W4817. PCAB Licensing Strategy, Entity Eligibility & Contractor-Category Decision

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch; new service line (installation VS-12, design-build VS-66, B2G delivery-and-install VS-46); entity added; annual strategy review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on trigger |
| **Volume** | 1 group strategy; ~1–5 of the 5 entities requiring a PCAB license; ~2–4 license categories (Regular AAA/AA/A/B + Specialty) |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance / PCAB Compliance Lead |
| **Participants** | COO, VP Operations, VP Engineering (VS-20), Trade Sales (VS-11), Government Sales (VS-46), Finance (VS-17), HR (VS-19.4 technical personnel) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Determine where BuildRight acts as a *contractor* (vs pure material supplier) across its service lines — installation (VS-12), design-build (VS-66), bulky install (VS-143), B2G delivery-and-install (VS-46/VS-11.2), own-site construction (VS-20) — and map each to the PCAB license-required trigger (engaging in the business of contracting; execution of construction/install scope for an owner) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / Trade Sales | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 2 | Decide the licensing structure per entity: which of the 5 entities (Depot, Logistics, Digital Commerce, Property Mgmt, Holdings) holds which license; the category/size needed (Regular vs Specialty; AAA/AA/A/B/BB/Gen-B by max single-project value); and the affiliate/branch-coverage approach; validate against capitalization, equipment, and technical-personnel requirements | PCAB Compliance Lead / Finance / HR / Engineering | COO / VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 3 | Approve the strategy & multi-year license roadmap; integrate into corporate compliance plan (VS-22/VS-166), B2G market strategy (VS-46), and the installation/design service strategy (VS-12/VS-66); set KPIs (license coverage %, renewal on-time %, project-registration timeliness, unlicensed-work incidents) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Strategy | COO / CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/license register (VS-166); legal matter/contract (VS-100.3); finance capitalization (VS-17); HR technical personnel (VS-19.4); B2G pipeline (VS-46)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-licensing risk**: A service line run without the required category/size license → unlicensed-contracting offense (Sec. 6/8) and bid disqualification; mitigated by the scope-to-license mapping and category headroom
- **Category-cap risk**: License size too small for the largest target project → blocked bid or illegal over-limit contracting; mitigated by capacity analysis and timely upgrade (W4819)
- **Strategic-invisibility risk**: PCAB compliance cut from strategy if not tied to bid-win/install-revenue; mitigated by the KPI set and board reporting (W4839)

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 weeks annual strategy; entity/category decision ~1–2 weeks per entity

### Automation Opportunity
- Scope-to-license trigger mapping; capacity-vs-category decision support; license-coverage gap dashboard (links to VS-166)

### Controls
- CTL-168 (ensure PCAB licensing validity & capacity limits.)
- CTL: legal review of contractor-scope determination; entity/category decision approved by COO + VP Legal; coverage reviewed annually

### Cross-references
- links to VS-46/VS-11 (B2G bids), VS-12/VS-66 (installation/design), VS-20 (own construction), VS-22/VS-166 (compliance/portfolio), VS-116 (bonding); sets inputs for W4818–W4824

## W4818. PCAB License Application, Documentation Package & Registration Issuance (RA 4566)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy approved (W4817); new entity licensed; category added |
| **Frequency** | Per license application (initial) |
| **Volume** | ~1 application per entity/category at launch; ~rare new-category applications thereafter |
| **Owner** | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal (VS-100.3) |
| **Participants** | Finance (audited FS), HR (technical personnel), Engineering (equipment list), External consultant/fixer, CIAP/PCAB |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assemble the RA 4566 / PCAB IRR documentation package per entity & category: SEC/DTI registration, BIR tax clearance, current mayor's permit (per entity HQ + branches), audited financial statements demonstrating net worth/capitalization, list of technical personnel (civil engineer, ECE, architect — PRC-licensed per W600), equipment inventory, business address, and the application form with required fees | PCAB Compliance Lead / Finance / HR / Engineering | VP Legal & Compliance | Per application |
| 2 | Submit to CIAP/PCAB, track evaluation, respond to deficiencies (show-cause, additional documents, personnel/equipment validation), and coordinate the PCAB evaluation/inspection; manage external consultant/fixer per VS-166 third-party-provider governance | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / External Consultant | VP Legal & Compliance | Per application |
| 3 | Receive the Certificate of Registration/License, record in the compliance register (VS-166) with license number, category, validity, conditions, and authorized scope; publish license proof to bidding/PhilGEPS (VS-46) and the installation/design service teams (VS-12/VS-66); trigger renewal on the calendar (W4820) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per application |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/license register (VS-166); legal matter (VS-100.3); finance FS (VS-17); HR personnel (VS-19.4); B2G/PhilGEPS (VS-46)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Documentation-deficiency risk**: Incomplete capitalization/personnel/equipment evidence → application rejection or delay; mitigated by the package checklist and pre-submission review
- **Personnel-shortfall risk**: License requires named PRC-licensed technical staff who may resign; mitigated by HR retention and backup-credentialing (W4834)
- **Timeline risk**: PCAB processing delays blocking a bid window; mitigated by early application and renewal headroom

### Time Estimate
- ~3–6 months per initial application (evaluation cycle)

### Automation Opportunity
- Documentation checklist automation; personnel/equipment/capitalization auto-validated against license master; renewal-trigger on issuance

### Controls
- CTL-168 (ensure PCAB licensing validity & capacity limits.)
- CTL: complete documentation package per RA 4566/PCAB IRR; external consultant governed (VS-166); license recorded before any in-scope work begins

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100.3 (legal), VS-166 (register), VS-17 (finance), VS-19.4 (personnel), VS-46 (PhilGEPS), W4817 (strategy), W4820 (renewal)

## W4819. License Category, Classification & Size/Capacity Upgrade Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Larger project opportunity exceeding current category cap; capacity/capitalization growth; strategic expansion (W4817) |
| **Frequency** | Per upgrade event + annual review |
| **Volume** | ~rare upgrades (e.g., B → A → AA as B2G/project scale grows) |
| **Owner** | PCAB Compliance Lead / Trade Sales (VS-11) / Finance |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), Engineering, HR, CIAP/PCAB |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify the upgrade trigger (a specific bid/project exceeding the current category's max single-contract value, or strategic capacity growth), confirm the target category/size, and assess eligibility (capitalization, equipment, technical personnel, track record of completed projects) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Finance / HR / Trade Sales | VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |
| 2 | Prepare & submit the upgrade application (additional capitalization evidence, completed-projects list, personnel/equipment add), track PCAB evaluation, and respond to deficiencies | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |
| 3 | On approval, update the license master (VS-166), notify bidding/sales (VS-46/VS-11) of the new capacity, and feed the upgrade economics (application cost vs incremental bid-access) to analytics (W4839) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Finance | VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/license register (VS-166); finance capitalization (VS-17); B2G pipeline (VS-46); HR personnel (VS-19.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cap-exceedance risk**: Bidding/contracting above the licensed category → illegal over-limit contracting; mitigated by bid-vs-capacity pre-check (W4828)
- **Upgrade-delay risk**: Slow upgrade missing a key bid window; mitigated by forward capacity planning
- **Cost-benefit risk**: Upgrading for a one-off project that doesn't recur; mitigated by upgrade-economics review

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 months per upgrade event

### Automation Opportunity
- Bid-value-vs-category-cap auto-check; capacity-growth threshold alert; upgrade-economics model

### Controls
- CTL-168 (ensure PCAB licensing validity & capacity limits.)
- CTL: upgrade application complete & approved by VP Legal; capacity validated before bidding at the new level

### Cross-references
- links to VS-46/VS-11 (bids), VS-166 (register), VS-17 (finance), W4817 (strategy), W4828 (bid cap-check)

## W4820. PCAB Annual License Renewal, Registration Compliance & Expiry Calendar

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual license-renewal cycle (PCAB registration renewed annually); 60/90-day pre-expiry alert |
| **Frequency** | Annual per license |
| **Volume** | ~1–5 licenses (entities/categories) × annual renewal |
| **Owner** | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal |
| **Participants** | Finance (fees, updated FS), HR (personnel currency), Engineering (equipment), Compliance Portfolio (VS-166) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the PCAB renewal calendar in the compliance register (VS-166): license number, expiry, renewal owner, required documents, fee, and the 60/90/30-day pre-expiry alerts; reconcile annually with CIAP/PCAB published schedules | PCAB Compliance Lead / Compliance Portfolio | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 2 | Prepare & submit the annual renewal: updated mayor's permit, current BIR tax clearance, updated audited FS, current technical-personnel roster (W4834), equipment list, paid annual registration fee, and proof of completed continuing requirements (insurance, safety program W4827); respond to PCAB queries | PCAB Compliance Lead / Finance / HR / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 3 | Receive renewed Certificate of Registration, update the license master (VS-166) and the bid-eligibility evidence (VS-46); escalate any lapse-risk to COO/Legal and trigger the lapse-prevention/remediation workflow (W4824) if a renewal is delayed | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/renewal calendar (VS-166); finance fees/FS (VS-17); HR personnel (VS-19.4); B2G eligibility (VS-46)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lapse risk**: A missed renewal lapses the license → cannot lawfully contract and is dropped from the PhilGEPS/bid eligibility list; mitigated by tiered alerts and early filing
- **Personnel-currency risk**: Renewal requires current PRC-licensed staff; resignations between cycles block renewal; mitigated by backup credentialing (W4834)
- **Fee/FS risk**: Outstanding fees or stale FS → renewal rejection; mitigated by finance pre-check

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 weeks prep per annual renewal; calendar maintenance continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Expiry calendar with tiered alerts (VS-166); renewal-package auto-assembly from current FS/permit/personnel; PhilGEPS eligibility auto-sync

### Controls
- CTL-168 (ensure PCAB licensing validity & capacity limits.)
- CTL: annual renewal filed before expiry; evidence recorded (VS-166); lapse-risk escalated

### Cross-references
- links to VS-166 (calendar/register), VS-17 (finance), VS-19.4 (personnel), VS-46 (PhilGEPS), W4824 (lapse remediation)

## W4822. Multi-Entity License Coverage, Affiliate & Entity-Level Licensing Decisions

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New entity (5-entity group); a service moving to a different entity; intercompany scope (VS-72) |
| **Frequency** | On entity/structure change + annual |
| **Volume** | 5 entities; ~which-entity-does-the-work decisions |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance / PCAB Compliance Lead |
| **Participants** | Tax (VS-79 intercompany/transfer pricing), Treasury (VS-18), Trade Sales (VS-11), Shared Services (VS-72) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Determine the license-holding entity per service line: e.g., Depot Inc. for retail installation (VS-12) and B2G delivery-and-install (VS-46), Logistics Inc. if it performs scope, Property Mgmt Inc. for its own build (VS-20); assess branch coverage vs separate licenses; reconcile with the intercompany/chargeback model (VS-72) and transfer pricing (VS-79) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / Tax / Treasury | VP Legal & Compliance | Per change |
| 2 | Manage affiliate/branch registration where a single license must enumerate branch/store locations doing in-scope work (200 stores) — register the branches authorized to perform installation under the entity license; update on store open (VS-37)/close (VS-59)/remodel (VS-109) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Store Ops (VS-07) / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 3 | Govern cross-entity contracting: when one licensed entity performs work for another BuildRight entity (intercompany), document the contractor-of-record, license applicability, and arm's-length terms; feed to intercompany settlement (VS-72) and consolidated reporting (VS-17) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / Tax | VP Legal & Compliance / CFO | Per arrangement |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/license register (VS-166); intercompany (VS-72); tax/TP (VS-79); store lifecycle (VS-37/VS-59/VS-109); finance consolidation (VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Entity-mismatch risk**: Work performed by an unlicensed entity while the license sits elsewhere → unlicensed contracting; mitigated by the entity-per-service mapping
- **Branch-coverage risk**: A store doing installation not enumerated under the branch registration; mitigated by branch-list reconciliation on store open/close
- **Intercompany-tax risk**: Cross-entity contracting mispriced for transfer pricing; mitigated by tax review (VS-79)

### Time Estimate
- ~per change 1–2 weeks; branch-list reconciliation continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Entity-license coverage matrix; branch-list auto-sync from store lifecycle (VS-37/VS-59); intercompany-license applicability check (VS-72)

### Controls
- CTL-168 (ensure PCAB licensing validity & capacity limits.)
- CTL: license-holding entity decided per service line; branches enumerated; intercompany contracting arm's-length + tax-reviewed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-72 (intercompany), VS-79 (tax/TP), VS-37/VS-59/VS-109 (store lifecycle), VS-166 (register), W4817 (strategy)

## W4824. License Lapse/Revocation Prevention, Condition Breach Reporting & Reinstatement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Renewal delayed (W4820); condition breach (personnel/equipment/insurance shortfall); PCAB show-cause/suspension/revocation notice; complaint |
| **Frequency** | Ad hoc (lapse/breach events) |
| **Volume** | ~rare events; ~any lapse is critical |
| **Owner** | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal (VS-100.3) |
| **Participants** | COO, Trade Sales (VS-11), HR (VS-19.4), Finance, Insurance (VS-26/VS-116) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Detect the lapse/breach risk: expiry alert missed (W4820), condition shortfall (lost PRC engineer, lapsed insurance, equipment disposed), or a PCAB notice (show-cause, suspension, revocation); assess business impact (active bids VS-46, in-flight projects W4825, ongoing install VS-12) and escalate to COO/Legal immediately | PCAB Compliance Lead / Compliance Portfolio / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance / COO | Per event (immediate) |
| 2 | Contain & remediate: for a renewal delay, expedite filing and, where lawful, pause new in-scope contracting until reinstated; for a condition breach, cure within the PCAB cure period (replace personnel W4834, restore insurance/equipment); for a suspension/revocation notice, file a response/appeal (W4821/W4838) and manage active-project continuity (transfer scope, subcontract under a licensed partner W4829) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / HR / Finance / Trade Sales | VP Legal & Compliance / COO | Per event |
| 3 | Reinstate & prevent recurrence: obtain reinstatement/renewed license, update the register (VS-166) and bid eligibility (VS-46), conduct a root-cause review (calendar failure, personnel-retention gap), strengthen controls, and report the incident/lesson to governance (W4840) and analytics (W4839) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / Internal Audit | VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance register/alerts (VS-166); legal matter (VS-100.3); HR personnel (VS-19.4); insurance (VS-26/VS-116); B2G eligibility (VS-46)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unlawful-continuation risk**: Contracting during a lapse → additional offense + bid/contract invalidity; mitigated by pause-new-contracting protocol
- **Active-project disruption risk**: Suspension halting in-flight B2G/install projects; mitigated by continuity/subcontract plan
- **Recurrence risk**: Same lapse recurring; mitigated by root-cause review and control strengthening

### Time Estimate
- ~per event containment 1–5 days; reinstatement weeks–months; review ~2 weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- Condition-breach detection (personnel/insurance/equipment shortfalls); lapse-impact model on active bids/projects; reinstatement workflow

### Controls
- CTL: lapse/breach escalated immediately; new in-scope contracting paused until reinstated where required; root-cause review documented

### Cross-references
- links to VS-166 (alerts/register), VS-100.3 (legal/appeal), VS-26/VS-116 (insurance/bond), VS-46 (bids), VS-12 (install), W4820 (renewal), W4829 (subcontract continuity), W4834 (personnel), W4838 (penalty), W4840 (governance)
