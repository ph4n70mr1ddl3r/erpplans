# VS-165: PCAB Contractor Licensing & RA 4566 Construction Contractor Compliance

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Philippine Contractors Accreditation Board (PCAB) contractor-licensing & RA 4566 compliance
workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the discipline by which BuildRight **as a
licensed construction contractor** obtains, maintains, and renews its own **PCAB
license/registration** under **Republic Act No. 4566 (the Contractors' License Law, 1965)** and
the Construction Industry Authority of the Philippines (CIAP) / PCAB Implementing Rules, and
discharges the **contractor-statutory obligations** that attach to performing construction,
installation, and design-build work — across the activities where BuildRight acts as a
*contractor* (not merely a *material supplier*).

This matters at BuildRight's scale and business mix. BuildRight performs **installation
services** (VS-12 — appliance, power-tool, solar, HVAC, water-heater, fixture install),
**customer project & design-build services** (VS-66), **government/institutional delivery-and-
install bids** (VS-46, VS-11.2 — explicitly noting that "PCAB license … may be required for
delivery-and-install bids"), **bulky white-goods delivery & installation** (VS-143), and
**construction of its own stores** (VS-20) — a body of work in which BuildRight is routinely the
*contractor of record* executing construction/installation scope for a customer or project owner.
Under RA 4566, no person/entity may "engage in the business of contracting" in the Philippines
without a valid PCAB license; doing so is an **unlawful act punishable by fine and imprisonment**
(Sec. 6/8), can render contracts unenforceable, and disqualifies the entity from public bidding
(RA 9184 eligibility). At ~30% trade + ~10% corporate/project revenue (profile §9.1) with a
growing delivery-and-install and B2G pipeline, BuildRight's own PCAB licensing is a **go-to-
market precondition** and a board-level statutory exposure.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. The defining terms — "PCAB license",
"contractor accreditation", "RA 4566", "PCAB project registration", "contractor's license
registration", and "PCAB renewal" — appear in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers
for BuildRight's *own* license. Every existing PCAB reference verifies *someone else's* license:
trade-professional **customer** PCAB verification for the Pro Badge (W590 in VS-09.1, VS-43.1),
**vendor/contractor** eligibility documents for PhilGEPS/B2G bids (VS-11.2, VS-46.1), and
**employee** PCAB credential tracking (VS-19.4) — but no value stream owns BuildRight's
*own* contractor-license lifecycle. The capability is **not** covered by **VS-46 (Government &
Institutional B2G Sales)**, which executes the *bid/sale* and lists "PCAB license if applicable"
as one eligibility checkbox (W162) — this owns the *licensing program* that produces and keeps
current that checkbox across every entity and bid. It is **not** covered by **VS-12
(Installation & Services)** or **VS-66 (Customer Project & Design)**, which *perform* the
installation/design work — this owns the *license and statutory contractor compliance* that
legally authorizes that work. It is **not** covered by **VS-20 (Real Estate & Construction)**,
which builds BuildRight's *own* stores (an owner-builder context) — this owns the contractor
license used for *customer-facing* contracted work. It is **not** covered by **VS-22
(Compliance & Regulatory)**, which executes permit/license *transactions* and inspection
response generally (VS-166 owns the unified portfolio) — this owns the *specialized PCAB
contractor-licensing regime* with its distinct board, project-registration, bonding, and
construction-safety obligations. And it is **not** covered by **VS-116 (Performance Bond,
Surety & Bank Guarantee)**, which manages the *financial instrument* — this owns the *license*
that the bond secures and the statutory scheme that requires it.

This value stream owns the end-to-end PCAB contractor-compliance discipline: licensing strategy
& entity eligibility (which of the 5 entities need a license, category/size, regular vs
specialty); application, documentation & issuance (RA 4566 + PCAB IRR — SEC/DTI registration,
BIR tax clearance, mayor's permit, audited FS, technical personnel, equipment, capitalization);
category/classification & upgrade; annual renewal & registration-compliance calendar; CIAP/PCAB
board engagement & multi-entity coverage; **project registration (PCAB-required project
registration per contract/notice of award)**; performance/surety bonding linkage (VS-116);
construction safety & health program (COSP) / DOLE D.O. 13 compliance for licensed work; bidding
license proof & PhilGEPS sync; subcontractor license cascading; warranty/acceptance/retention;
**construction payment security (mechanic's-lien rights, lien waivers, payment-bond claims)**;
CIAP/LGU inspection response; license-condition monitoring (insurance, technical capacity, CPD);
employee/Engineer-of-Record credentialing; **unlicensed-contracting risk control** (the
material-supply-vs-install scope boundary); joint-venture/consortium & foreign-contractor
licensing (links to VS-160); penalty/violation & reinstatement; and contractor-compliance
analytics & governance. It integrates tightly with VS-46/VS-11 (B2G bids), VS-12/VS-66
(installation/design work), VS-20 (own construction), VS-116 (surety bonds), VS-24 (construction
safety), VS-22/VS-166 (permits/portfolio), VS-100.3 (contracts), and VS-160 (foreign/JV).
