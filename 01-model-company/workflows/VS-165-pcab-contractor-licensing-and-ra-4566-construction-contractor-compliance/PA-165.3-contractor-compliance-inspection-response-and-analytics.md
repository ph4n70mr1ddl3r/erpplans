## W4833. CIAP/PCAB & LGU Construction Inspection/Audit Response & Corrective Action

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | PCAB/CIAP audit; LGU Office of the Building Official (OBO) inspection; DOLE construction-safety inspection (W4827); COA audit (B2G); complaint-triggered inspection |
| **Frequency** | Ad hoc + scheduled |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 inspections/audits per year across licensed projects + entity-level audits |
| **Owner** | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal (VS-100.3) / HSE (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | Engineering (VS-20), Installation (VS-12), Finance (VS-40), Records (VS-88), Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive & log the inspection/audit notice (PCAB, LGU-OBO, DOLE, COA), scope it (entity, project W4825, license condition), assign the response owner, and assemble the evidence packet from the audit trail (W4823) and records (VS-88) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / Records | VP Legal & Compliance | Per event (immediate) |
| 2 | Host the inspection/audit, respond to findings, and negotiate/correct: immediate correction of construction-safety/scope/records issues, written response to PCAB/CIAP, and a corrective-action plan; coordinate with HSE (VS-24) and the project team | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / HSE / Engineering | VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |
| 3 | Track corrective-action closure, document the outcome in the audit trail (W4823) and legal matter record (VS-100.3), manage any resulting penalty (W4838), and feed inspection findings to governance (W4840) and analytics (W4839) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / Internal Audit | VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance audit trail (W4823); legal matter (VS-100.3); records (VS-88); HSE/incident (VS-24); internal audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unprepared-inspection risk**: Disorganized records → escalated finding; mitigated by the audit-ready repository (W4823)
- **Finding-escalation risk**: An uncorrected finding becoming a penalty/suspension (W4838); mitigated by prompt corrective action
- **Cross-regulator risk**: Parallel PCAB/LGU/DOLE findings compounding; mitigated by coordinated response

### Time Estimate
- ~per inspection 1–3 weeks (response + correction)

### Automation Opportunity
- Audit-notice → evidence-packet auto-assembly; corrective-action tracker; finding→penalty alert

### Controls
- CTL-727 (ensure controlled execution — Contractor Compliance, Inspection Response & Analytics (PA-165.3).)
- CTL-727: inspection response owner assigned; findings corrected & closed; outcome documented

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous license-condition monitoring; condition shortfall alert; renewal (W4820) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + monthly review |
| **Volume** | All license conditions across entities/categories |
| **Owner** | PCAB Compliance Lead / Compliance Portfolio (VS-166) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19.4 personnel), Finance (VS-26/VS-116 insurance), Engineering (VS-20 equipment), Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the license-condition matrix per license: required insurance (comprehensive liability, contractor's all-risk, surety facility VS-116), technical-personnel complement (named PRC engineers/architects W4835), equipment capacity, capitalization, and continuing-education (CPD) units; map each to evidence in the audit trail (W4823) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Compliance Portfolio | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 2 | Monitor conditions continuously: insurance currency (links to W4735 COI renewal tracking), personnel retention (resignation → shortfall alert), equipment availability, and CPD-unit accumulation; raise shortfall alerts with cure-period tracking | PCAB Compliance Lead / HR / Finance / Engineering | VP Legal & Compliance | Monthly |
| 3 | On shortfall, cure within the PCAB cure period (replace personnel W4835, restore insurance/equipment, complete CPD), document the cure, and escalate to lapse/breach handling (W4824) if uncured; report condition-health to governance (W4840) | PCAB Compliance Lead / HR / Finance | VP Legal & Compliance | Per shortfall |

### System Touchpoints

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous monitoring; monthly review ~1 day

### Automation Opportunity
- Condition-matrix dashboard with shortfall alerts; personnel-resignation → condition-impact alert; insurance-expiry sync (W4735)

### Controls
- CTL-727 (ensure controlled execution — Contractor Compliance, Inspection Response & Analytics (PA-165.3).)
- CTL-727: license conditions monitored continuously; shortfalls cured within cure period; condition-health reported

### Cross-references

## W4836. Unlicensed-Contracting Risk Control — In-Scope/Out-of-Scope Boundary & Material-Supply-vs-Install Decision

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New service/offering; bid scope ambiguity; sales-contract drafting; compliance review |
| **Frequency** | Per offering/bid/contract + periodic policy refresh |
| **Volume** | All in-scope-vs-out-of-scope decisions |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance / PCAB Compliance Lead |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales (VS-11), Installation (VS-12), Design (VS-66), Procurement (VS-03), Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the in-scope/out-of-scope (contracting vs material-supply) decision framework: what work is "contracting" requiring a PCAB license (execution of construction/install scope, design-build, delivery-and-install on B2G) vs pure material supply (sale of goods without installation scope); codify the boundary in policy and sales guidance | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous + annual |
| 2 | Apply at bid/contract drafting: review each bid (VS-46/VS-11) and customer contract (VS-100.3) for scope that would cross into contracting (incidental install, "free" setup, design services, B2G delivery-and-install); require license confirmation (W4828) or restructure to stay out of scope; train sales on the boundary | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / Trade Sales / Procurement | VP Legal & Compliance | Per bid/contract |
| 3 | Audit compliance with the boundary: review actual performed work vs contracted scope to detect quiet scope-creep into unlicensed contracting; manage exceptions; feed boundary-disputes to regulatory liaison (W4821) and findings to analytics (W4839) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Internal Audit (VS-21) / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal contract (VS-100.3); trade project (VS-11); installation (VS-12); design (VS-66); compliance register (VS-166); audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Quiet-scope-creep risk**: A material-supply deal quietly including install → unlicensed contracting offense; mitigated by contract review and the decision framework
- **Sales-pressure risk**: Sales overpromising install to win a deal without license check; mitigated by training and bid-gating
- **Adverse-interpretation risk**: PCAB interpreting a hybrid deal as contracting; mitigated by documentation and liaison (W4821)

### Time Estimate
- ~framework annual; ~per bid/contract review 1–3 hours

### Automation Opportunity
- Scope-classification decision tool from contract text; bid-gate on contracting-scope flag; scope-creep audit query

### Controls
- CTL-727 (ensure controlled execution — Contractor Compliance, Inspection Response & Analytics (PA-165.3).)
- CTL-727: contracting-scope contracts require license confirmation; sales trained; boundary audited periodically

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100.3 (contracts), VS-46/VS-11 (bids), VS-12/VS-66 (install/design), VS-21 (audit), VS-166 (register), W4821 (liaison), W4828 (bid-gate), W4839 (analytics)
