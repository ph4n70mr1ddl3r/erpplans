## W4825. PCAB Project Registration per Contract & Notice-of-Award Filing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Notice of Award / contract execution for an in-scope licensed project (B2G, B2B project, design-build VS-66) |
| **Frequency** | Per project contract |
| **Volume** | ~B2G + project contracts requiring registration per year (subset of B2G/project pipeline VS-46/VS-11) |
| **Owner** | PCAB Compliance Lead / Project Sales (VS-11.2) / Legal (VS-100.3) |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales, Finance (VS-40 project accounting), Insurance/Bond (VS-116), CIAP/PCAB |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On Notice of Award/contract execution, confirm the project is in licensed scope (W4836), the license category covers the contract value (W4819/W4828), and the license is current (W4820); capture project data (owner, location, scope, contract value, duration, category-applicability) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Trade Sales / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per project |
| 2 | File the PCAB-required project registration per the contract/notice of award within the prescribed period (register the project against the entity license), pay any per-project registration fee, and manage the acknowledgment/certificate; coordinate bonds (W4826) and the safety program (W4827) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / Finance | VP Legal & Compliance | Per project |
| 3 | Record the registration in the compliance audit trail (W4823) and the project accounting/contract records (VS-40/VS-11/VS-100.3); set project-milestone/warranty obligations (W4830) and closeout (W4832) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Finance / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per project |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance register (VS-166); legal contract (VS-100.3); project accounting (VS-40/VS-11); bonds (VS-116); audit trail (W4823)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Late-registration risk**: Missing the registration window → compliance finding/penalty or contract issues; mitigated by per-contract trigger and SLA
- **Category-mismatch risk**: Registering a project exceeding the license category; mitigated by the capacity pre-check (W4828)
- **Record-gap risk**: Unregistered-in-records project surfacing at audit/claim time; mitigated by the audit trail (W4823)

### Time Estimate
- ~per project 2–5 days registration + coordination

### Automation Opportunity
- Contract-execution → project-registration auto-trigger; capacity-vs-category auto-check; registration-acknowledgment filed to audit trail

### Controls
- CTL: every in-scope contract project-registered before work start; capacity pre-checked; recorded in audit trail

### Cross-references
- links to VS-46/VS-11 (B2G/project), VS-100.3 (contract), VS-40 (project accounting), VS-116 (bond), W4819/W4828 (capacity), W4823 (audit trail), W4830 (warranty)

## W4826. Performance & Surety Bonding for PCAB-Licensed Project Work

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project award (W4825); bid bond at tender (VS-116); contract/bond requirement |
| **Frequency** | Per project |
| **Volume** | ~B2G + large B2B project bonds per year |
| **Owner** | PCAB Compliance Lead / Treasury-Surety (VS-116) / Legal |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-18 facility), Surety/Bank, Insurance (VS-26), Trade Sales (VS-11) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify the bonding requirement per project (bid bond at tender VS-116, performance bond 5–30% of contract, payment bond, warranty bond, retention) and confirm it against the license conditions (W4820) and the contract (VS-100.3) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Treasury-Surety / Trade Sales | VP Legal & Compliance / CFO | Per project |
| 2 | Arrange & issue the bond via the surety facility (VS-116): application, counter-indemnity/collateral, premium, encumbrance on credit facility; deliver to owner/beneficiary and record encumbrance | Treasury-Surety / Finance / Legal | CFO | Per project |
| 3 | Track bond validity across the project + warranty period, manage reduction/release at acceptance (W4830) and final release at warranty expiry, and manage any bond claim/call (W4831/VS-116); feed bond economics to analytics (W4839) | Treasury-Surety / PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal | CFO / VP Legal & Compliance | Per project + continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Surety/bond facility (VS-116); treasury/cash (VS-18); legal contract (VS-100.3); project accounting (VS-40); insurance (VS-26)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Facility-encumbrance risk**: Bonds tying up credit capacity that otherwise supports operations; mitigated by facility planning (VS-116)
- **Bond-call risk**: Owner calling the bond on dispute/default → cash/credit hit; mitigated by performance management (VS-11) and claim defense (VS-116)
- **Release-failure risk**: Bond not released at acceptance/warranty expiry → stranded capacity; mitigated by release tracking

### Time Estimate
- ~per bond 1–2 weeks arrangement; tracking continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Bond-requirement auto-detection from contract; facility-encumbrance dashboard (VS-116); release-trigger at acceptance/warranty expiry

### Controls
- CTL: bond per contract/license requirement; facility encumbrance tracked (VS-116); release at milestones

### Cross-references
- links to VS-116 (surety), VS-18 (treasury), VS-100.3 (contract), VS-40 (project accounting), VS-26 (insurance), W4825 (registration), W4830 (warranty/release), W4831 (claims)

## W4827. Construction Safety & Health Program (COSP) & DOLE D.O. 13 Compliance for Licensed Work

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Licensed project start (W4825); PCAB/DOLE requirement; annual program refresh |
| **Frequency** | Per project + annual program |
| **Volume** | All licensed construction/install projects |
| **Owner** | PCAB Compliance Lead / HSE (VS-24) / Safety Officer |
| **Participants** | DOLE-qualified Safety Officer, Engineering (VS-20), Installation (VS-12), HR (VS-19.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish/maintain the Construction Safety & Health Program (COSP) required for licensed contracting: DOLE D.O. 13 (Guidelines Governing Occupational Safety and Health in the Construction Industry) compliance — safety officer (SO1/SO2/SO3 per project size), safety program, PPE, incident reporting, and the OSH construction requirements; integrate with VS-24 HSE | PCAB Compliance Lead / HSE / Safety Officer | VP Legal & Compliance / VP Engineering | Per project + annual |
| 2 | Deploy per project: site-specific safety plan, qualified safety officer, worker safety induction/orientation, daily safety briefing, incident reporting; verify for installation crews (VS-12/VS-143) on customer sites; coordinate with the project owner's safety program | HSE / Safety Officer / Installation / Engineering | VP Engineering | Per project |
| 3 | Report safety performance to DOLE/PCAB as required, manage any construction incident (links to VS-24 incident management), and feed safety-record to license conditions (W4834) and analytics (W4839) | HSE / PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance / VP Engineering | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE/incident (VS-24); compliance register (VS-166); HR personnel (VS-19.4); installation (VS-12/VS-143); engineering (VS-20)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **D.O. 13 non-compliance risk**: Missing safety officer/program → DOLE/PCAB finding, project stoppage, license risk; mitigated by per-project COSP
- **Cross-site-crew risk**: Installation crews on customer sites outside BuildRight HSE control; mitigated by deployment standards
- **Incident-license risk**: A serious construction incident triggering license scrutiny; mitigated by safety program + incident management (VS-24)

### Time Estimate
- ~per project COSP setup 1–2 weeks; continuous during project

### Automation Opportunity
- COSP template per project size; safety-officer assignment from HR roster; incident → license-condition alert

### Controls
- CTL: DOLE-qualified safety officer per project; COSP documented; incidents reported and managed (VS-24)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE/incident), VS-166 (register), VS-12/VS-143 (installation), VS-20 (engineering), VS-19.4 (personnel), W4834 (conditions)

## W4828. Bidding/Bid-Support License Proof, Capacity Pre-Check & PhilGEPS Eligibility Sync

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Bid/no-bid decision (VS-46/VS-11.2); B2G eligibility-document refresh; annual PhilGEPS sync |
| **Frequency** | Per bid + annual |
| **Volume** | ~government/large-B2B bids per year requiring license proof |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales (VS-11.2) / Government Sales (VS-46) / PCAB Compliance Lead |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), Finance (capacity), Surety (VS-116 bond) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | At bid/no-bid, confirm BuildRight's license is current (W4820), the project is in licensed scope (W4836), and the license category/size covers the bid value (capacity pre-check W4819); if not, decide upgrade (W4819), JV/partner (W4837), or no-bid | Trade Sales / Government Sales / PCAB Compliance Lead | VP Legal & Compliance / VP Merchandising | Per bid |
| 2 | Produce bid-support license proof: current PCAB Certificate of Registration, category documentation, and the eligibility pack (PhilGEPS W162, mayor's permit, BIR tax clearance, audited FS, Omnibus Sworn Statement); sync to PhilGEPS profile (VS-46.1) so it is current for every bid | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / Government Sales | VP Legal & Compliance | Per bid + annual |
| 3 | On award (W4825), confirm the license-under-which the work will be performed and the entity (W4822); on loss, capture license-related bid-loss learnings to analytics (W4839) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Trade Sales | VP Legal & Compliance | Per bid |

### System Touchpoints
- B2G/PhilGEPS (VS-46); trade project pipeline (VS-11); compliance register (VS-166); legal (VS-100.3); finance capacity (VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-capacity-bid risk**: Bidding above the licensed category → disqualification or illegal award; mitigated by the capacity pre-check
- **Stale-eligibility risk**: PhilGEPS/eligibility documents expired at bid time; mitigated by annual sync (W4820) + pre-bid check
- **Scope-creep risk**: A material-supply bid quietly becoming delivery-and-install requiring a license; mitigated by scope-boundary review (W4836)

### Time Estimate
- ~per bid 2–4 hours license-proof + capacity check; annual PhilGEPS sync ~1 day

### Automation Opportunity
- Bid-value-vs-category auto-check; eligibility-pack auto-assembly; PhilGEPS-profile sync alert

### Controls
- CTL: license current + capacity covers bid value before submission; eligibility pack current in PhilGEPS

### Cross-references
- links to VS-46/VS-11 (bids), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-166 (register), W4819 (capacity), W4820 (currency), W4822 (entity), W4825 (registration), W4836 (scope)

## W4829. Subcontractor License Verification, Approved-Subcontractor List & Cascading Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Subcontracting part of a licensed project; approved-subcontractor refresh; subcontractor onboarding |
| **Frequency** | Per subcontract + list maintenance |
| **Volume** | ~subcontractors used on licensed projects |
| **Owner** | PCAB Compliance Lead / Trade Sales (VS-11) / Procurement (VS-03/VS-34) |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), HSE (VS-24), TPRM (VS-161), Subcontractors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the approved-subcontractor list for licensed work: verify each subcontractor holds the required PCAB license/category for the subcontracted scope (the cascading-license obligation), PRC personnel, insurance, HSE program (VS-24), and TPRM (VS-161); pre-qualify via Procurement/Legal | PCAB Compliance Lead / Procurement / Legal / TPRM | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous + per onboarding |
| 2 | Per subcontract, verify the subcontractor's license is current and category-covers the subcontract scope, execute the subcontract (with license-flow-down, safety W4827, payment W4831, warranty W4830 clauses), and notify the owner/PCAB where the contract requires subcontractor disclosure | PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal / Trade Sales | VP Legal & Compliance | Per subcontract |
| 3 | Monitor subcontractor performance/compliance during the work (safety, quality, license currency), manage any subcontractor lapse/default (replace with continuity), and feed subcontractor record to analytics (W4839) and TPRM (VS-161) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Trade Sales / TPRM | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor/subcontractor master (VS-03/VS-29); TPRM (VS-161); legal contract (VS-100.3); HSE (VS-24); compliance register (VS-166)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cascading-license risk**: An unlicensed subcontractor performing licensed scope on BuildRight's project → BuildRight's liability; mitigated by verification + flow-down
- **Continuity risk**: Subcontractor lapse/default mid-project; mitigated by approved-list depth
- **Quality/safety risk**: Subcontractor non-compliance; mitigated by monitoring + HSE (VS-24)

### Time Estimate
- ~per subcontractor onboarding 1–2 weeks verification; list maintenance continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Subcontractor-license auto-verification (PCAB portal); approved-list dashboard; flow-down-clause library

### Controls
- CTL: subcontractor PCAB-licensed for scope; license/safety/payment flow-down in subcontract; monitored

### Cross-references
- links to VS-03/VS-34 (procurement), VS-161 (TPRM), VS-100.3 (subcontract), VS-24 (HSE), VS-29 (master), W4823 (audit trail), W4839 (analytics)

| **Trigger** | Project completion/substantial completion; acceptance; warranty period; retention release |
| **Frequency** | Per project (completion + warranty tail) |
| **Volume** | All licensed projects (completion + warranty tail) |
| **Owner** | Project Sales (VS-11) / PCAB Compliance Lead / Installation (VS-12) |
| **Participants** | Owner/customer, Finance (retention VS-40), Surety (VS-116 bond release), Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manage project completion & acceptance: punch-list/defect-snag resolution, the owner's acceptance certificate, and substantial-completion; document per the contract (VS-100.3) and project accounting (VS-11/VS-40) | Trade Sales / Installation / PCAB Compliance Lead | VP Merchandising / VP Legal & Compliance | Per project |
| 2 | Track the warranty/retention period: the PCAB/contract warranty obligation, retention money (VS-11 W165/W1134), and the warranty bond (W4826); respond to warranty/defect claims during the period with remediation (VS-12) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Installation / Finance | VP Legal & Compliance / CFO | Per project (warranty tail) |
| 3 | At warranty expiry/retention release: obtain final acceptance, release retention (VS-11 W1134) and the warranty bond (W4826), close the project-registration in the audit trail (W4823), and complete statutory closeout for B2G (W4832) | PCAB Compliance Lead / Finance / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance / CFO | Per project |

### System Touchpoints
- Project accounting/retention (VS-11/VS-40); surety/bond (VS-116); legal contract (VS-100.3); installation/warranty (VS-12); compliance audit trail (W4823)

- **Warranty-claim risk**: Defect claims during warranty requiring remediation cost; mitigated by quality (VS-31) and retention hold

- **Acceptance-dispute risk**: Owner withholding acceptance; mitigated by punch-list discipline and contract clarity

### Time Estimate
- ~per project completion 1–2 weeks; warranty tail months–years; release ~1–2 weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- Warranty-period tracker; retention/bond-release triggers at acceptance/expiry; defect-claim workflow

### Controls
- CTL: acceptance documented; warranty/retention tracked; retention & bond released at expiry; project-registration closed

## W4831. Construction Payment Security — Mechanic's-Lien Rights, Lien Waivers & Payment-Bond Claims

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project progress billing (VS-11); payment receipt; non-payment/default; owner/lender request for lien waiver |
| **Frequency** | Per billing milestone + on payment event |
| **Volume** | All licensed project payment milestones |
| **Owner** | PCAB Compliance Lead / AR (VS-16) / Legal (VS-100.3) |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-16 AR, VS-40 project), Surety (VS-116 payment bond), Owner/GC, Subcontractors (W4829) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manage lien rights & waivers across the project: on each progress billing, provide the conditional/unconditional lien waiver (per Civil Code / PD 1529 construction-lien practice) upon payment, and preserve lien rights for unpaid scope; coordinate with AR (VS-16) and the project accounting (VS-11/VS-40) | PCAB Compliance Lead / AR / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance / CFO | Per milestone |
| 2 | On non-payment/default, assess remedies: mechanic's-lien filing (within the statutory period on the improved property), payment-bond claim (on B2G/bonded work via VS-116), suspension/termination per contract, and escalation to collections/litigation (VS-16/VS-100); preserve evidence (W4823) | Legal / AR / PCAB Compliance Lead / Treasury-Surety | VP Legal & Compliance / CFO | Per default event |
| 3 | Manage subcontractor/supplier payment cascade: ensure BuildRight's subcontractors (W4829) are paid for work for which BuildRight has been paid (the payment-bond / cascading-payment obligation), collect their lien waivers, and avoid double-liability; report payment-security outcomes to analytics (W4839) | Finance / PCAB Compliance Lead / Legal | CFO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- AR/collections (VS-16); project accounting (VS-11/VS-40); surety/payment bond (VS-116); legal/litigation (VS-100); compliance audit trail (W4823)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lost-lien-rights risk**: Providing an unconditional waiver before payment, or missing the filing period → unsecured receivable; mitigated by conditional-until-paid waiver discipline
- **Cascade-liability risk**: BuildRight liable to unpaid subcontractors/suppliers on a project BuildRight wasn't paid for; mitigated by pay-when-paid structuring and cascade management
- **Bond-claim risk**: Payment-bond claim disputes/cost; mitigated by documentation (VS-116)

### Time Estimate
- ~per milestone 1–2 hours waiver management; per default event days–weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- Milestone → conditional-lien-waiver generation; lien-rights expiry tracker; subcontractor-payment cascade reconciliation

### Controls
- CTL: conditional-until-paid waiver discipline; lien rights tracked; subcontractor cascade reconciled; bond claims documented

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (AR/collections), VS-11/VS-40 (project accounting), VS-116 (payment bond), VS-100 (litigation), W4823 (evidence), W4829 (subcontractor), W4839 (analytics)

### Automation Opportunity
