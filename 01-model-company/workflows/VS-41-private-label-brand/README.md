# VS-41: Private Label & Exclusive Brand Management

> **Plan & Source** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages the end-to-end lifecycle of BuildRight-branded private label products and exclusive brand partnerships — from product concept and market analysis through factory sourcing, quality assurance, packaging design, pricing strategy, launch, and ongoing performance management. Private label products are critical for margin improvement (typically 15–25% higher gross margin vs. national brands), customer loyalty (exclusive products available only at BuildRight), and supply chain resilience (reduced dependence on national brand vendors). At BuildRight's scale (~35,000 active SKUs), a private label portfolio targeting 8–12% of active SKUs (~2,800–4,200 SKUs) across key categories (hardware fasteners, hand tools, paint, adhesives, cleaning supplies, storage, garden tools, PPE) supports the company's margin targets while differentiating from competitors like CitiHardware and Wilcon Depot.
