## W4489. Label Production Fleet Operations & Consumable Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Fleet ops; consumable reorder; new store (VS-37) |
| **Frequency** | Daily ops + per-store replenishment |
| **Volume** | Label printers + media (thermal rolls, tags, ribbons) across 200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ |
| **Owner** | Auto-ID Lead / IT (VS-27) / Stores (VS-07) |
| **Participants** | ITAM (VS-99), Procurement (VS-34), DC (VS-04), IFM (VS-138) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stores/IT operate the per-store label-printer fleet (load media, replace ribbons, clean printheads), following the W4485 standard and IFM preventative maintenance (VS-138) | Stores / IT | Store Manager / ITAM Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Procurement/Auto-ID Lead manage consumable supply (media/ribbon min-max per store, central reorder, vendor consolidation VS-34), with shrink-controlled media (links to VS-23 — media is a fraud/counterfeit vector) | Procurement / Auto-ID Lead | Procurement Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | IT/ITAM track printer fleet health, PM, and refresh via VS-99, and feed failures to the service desk (VS-27.1) | IT / ITAM | CIO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- ITAM fleet (VS-99); procurement/consumables (VS-34); IFM PM (VS-138); service desk (VS-27.1); shrink control (VS-23)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stockout-of-media risk**: Store runs out of label stock → no price tags (RA 7394 risk); mitigated by min-max auto-reorder
- **Counterfeit-media risk**: Unauthorized media/labels enable tag-switch fraud (VS-23); mitigated by controlled supply & serialized media

### Time Estimate
- ~15 min/day per store media ops; ~weekly consumable replenishment

### Automation Opportunity
- Printer media-level monitoring → auto-reorder; predictive PM on printhead wear; consumable spend analytics

### Controls
- CTL: media supply chain-controlled (VS-23); printer fleet PM'd (VS-138/VS-99); RA 7394 stock continuity

### Cross-references
- links to VS-99 (ITAM), VS-34 (procurement), VS-138 (IFM), VS-27.1 (service desk), VS-23 (shrink), W4485 (hardware standard)

## W4490. Centralized Label/Price-Tag Print Job Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Price/promo change; new-item setup; reprint request |
| **Frequency** | Daily (price/promo), per onboarding |
| **Volume** | Print jobs across 35K SKUs × 200 stores; bi-monthly sale events |
| **Owner** | Auto-ID Lead / Pricing (VS-01.2) |
| **Participants** | IT (VS-27), PIM (VS-137), Stores (VS-07), Marketing (VS-14) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing/Auto-ID Lead generate print jobs from the PIM/template library (VS-137/W4483) — shelf-edge, hang-tag, adhesive — filtered by store/section/SKU and effective date | Pricing / Auto-ID Lead | VP Merch | Per change |
| 2 | IT distribute print jobs to store/DC printers (push to RF/PC printers), confirm print receipt, and log the job for reconciliation (W4496) | IT / Auto-ID Lead | CIO | Per change |
| 3 | Stores execute the print job, apply labels (W4491/W4492), and close the job with application confirmation/compliance (links to VS-63 task mgmt) | Stores | Store Manager | Per change |

### System Touchpoints
- PIM/template (VS-137); pricing (VS-01.2); store task mgmt (VS-63); IT distribution (VS-27); promo (VS-58)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missed-print risk**: Job not printed/applied → stale price (RA 7394/VS-118 risk); mitigated by receipt confirmation + compliance close
- **Volume-spike risk**: Bi-monthly sale events overwhelm printers; mitigated by staged job scheduling

### Time Estimate
- Per job minutes; bi-monthly events ~0.5 day/store

### Automation Opportunity
- Price-change-driven auto job generation; print-receipt confirmation; compliance-close in task app (VS-63)

### Controls
- CTL: print job receipt-confirmed & application-compliance-closed; RA 7394 price current (VS-118)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-137 (PIM), VS-01.2/VS-58 (pricing/promo), VS-63 (task mgmt), VS-27 (IT), W4491/W4492 (application), W4496 (reconciliation)

## W4491. Shelf-Edge Label & Planogram Price Application

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Price change; reset; new-item; audit gap |
| **Frequency** | Daily + on reset |
| **Volume** | Shelf-edge labels across ~2.3–4.4 SKUs/sqm × ~1.6–3.0M sqm (profile §6.1) |
| **Owner** | Stores (VS-07) / Auto-ID Lead |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01), Planogram (VS-55), Pricing (VS-01.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stock associates apply shelf-edge price labels per the print job/planogram (VS-55), matching label to facing and verifying barcode/price/unit correctness (RA 7394) | Stock Associate | Department Supervisor | Daily/on reset |
| 2 | Supervisors verify shelf-edge compliance vs planogram (VS-55) and price accuracy (W4486), and flag/reprint mismatches | Department Supervisor | Store Manager | Daily |
| 3 | Stores capture shelf-edge compliance in the task app (VS-63) and feed exceptions to Revenue Assurance (VS-118) | Stores | Store Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Planogram (VS-55); pricing (VS-01.2); store task mgmt (VS-63); revenue assurance (VS-118); W4486 conformance

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-facing risk**: Label under wrong SKU → scan/price error (VS-118); mitigated by label-to-facing verification
- **Stale-label risk**: Old price left after change → over/under-charge + RA 7394 violation; mitigated by replace-old-first rule

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 hr/day per store; resets ~0.5–1 day

### Automation Opportunity
- RF-guided label-to-facing scan-match; stale-label detector (price-change vs applied); shelf-edge image audit

### Controls
- CTL: label-to-facing verified; stale labels replaced; RA 7394 price accuracy audited (W4486/VS-118)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-55 (planogram), VS-01.2 (pricing), VS-63 (task mgmt), VS-118 (revenue assurance), W4486 (conformance), W4490 (print job)

| **Frequency** | Per receipt + daily |
| **Volume** | Hang-tags/adhesive on tools/hardware/appliances; bulk-yard tags on lumber/cement/tiles |
| **Owner** | Stores (VS-07) / DC (VS-04) |
| **Participants** | Receiving (VS-07.3), Merchandising (VS-01), LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Lumber/bulk-yard staff apply bulk-yard tags (price/board-foot, lot) to lumber/cement/tiles, with catch-weight/variable format (W4494) and lot traceability (W4482) | Stock Associate / Yard Staff | Department Supervisor | Daily |
| 3 | LP/Stores ensure high-value items carry the EAS tag per policy (W4484) at tagging time (source-tagging preferred W4498) | LP / Stores | LP Director / Store Manager | Per item |

### System Touchpoints
- Receiving (VS-07.3/VS-04); WMS putaway (VS-04); LP/EAS (VS-23/W4484); lot trace (VS-29/W4482); catch-weight (VS-08)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Untagged-at-receipt risk**: Item hits floor without tag → can't sell/scan; mitigated by receipt tagging gate

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 min/receipt batch; yard tagging continuous

### Automation Opportunity
- Receipt-driven tag-print trigger; durable-media selection by environment; yard-tag re-issue on cycle-count gap

### Controls
- CTL: items tagged at receipt before floor; high-value EAS-tagged (W4484); bulk-tag durable & lot-traced

### Cross-references

## W4494. Catch-Weight / Variable-Measure Labeling at POS & Cutting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Catch-weight sale (lumber/wire/nails); cutting (pipe/sheet) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (2.8M monthly POS, catch-weight subset) |
| **Volume** | Variable-measure labels at 600 POS + cutting stations every store |
| **Owner** | Stores (VS-07/VS-09) / Auto-ID Lead |
| **Participants** | POS (VS-08), Custom fabrication (VS-09.1), Calibration (VS-115), Revenue Assurance (VS-118) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cashiers/cutting staff weigh/measure catch-weight items on calibrated scales (VS-115) and print the variable-measure label (weight/length, unit price, total, barcode) using the W4482 format | Cashier / Cutting Staff | Department Supervisor | Per sale |
| 2 | POS scans the variable-measure barcode to ring exact price (links to VS-08), preventing keying error (VS-118) | Cashier | Department Supervisor | Per sale |
| 3 | Calibration/Auto-ID Lead ensure scales/printers are calibrated (VS-115) and variable-format labels pass the W4486 read-rate standard | Calibration / Auto-ID Lead | Master Data Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- POS catch-weight (VS-08); cutting/fabrication (VS-09.1); calibration (VS-115); revenue assurance (VS-118); W4482 format

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mismeasure risk**: Uncalibrated scale → wrong price (revenue loss/overcharge VS-118); mitigated by VS-115 calibration gate
- **Keying-error risk**: Manual weight entry → error; mitigated by variable-barcode scan (no manual)

### Time Estimate
- ~30–60 sec per catch-weight item

### Automation Opportunity
- Scale→label→POS auto-posting (no manual entry); calibration-expiry lock on scale

### Controls
- CTL: scales calibrated (VS-115); variable-measure scanned not keyed; price = weight × unit verified (VS-118)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08 (POS), VS-09.1 (cutting), VS-115 (calibration), VS-118 (revenue assurance), W4482 (format), W4486 (read-rate)

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
