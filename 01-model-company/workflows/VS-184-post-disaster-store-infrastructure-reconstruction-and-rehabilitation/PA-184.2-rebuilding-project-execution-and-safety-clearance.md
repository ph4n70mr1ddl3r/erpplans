## W5281. Post-Disaster Rebuilding CAPA Budget Approval and Financing Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved BOQ (W5275) and insurance clearance (W5278) |
| **Frequency** | Per approved reconstruction project |
| **Volume** | ~2-3 budgets set up annually |
| **Owner** | Finance Director |
| **Participants** | Facilities Director, CFO, Budget Analyst (VS-33.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compile reconstruction budget request: BOQ, contractor estimates, permit fees, contingency buffer. | Facilities Director | Finance Director | 2-3 hours |
| 2 | Verify funding source allocation: insurance recovery offsets vs. corporate CAPEX cash. | Finance Director | Finance Director | 2 hours |
| 3 | Review budget against corporate performance metrics and annual plans. | Budget Analyst | Finance Director | 2-3 hours |
| 4 | Release approved project budget in ERP and initialize project code. | Finance Director | Finance Director | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- CAPEX module (VS-40), Budget tracker (VS-33.1).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rebuilding budget overflow risk**: Project costs exceed budget due to lack of contingency funds; mitigated by adding a mandatory 15% disaster contingency buffer to all reconstruction budgets.

### Time Estimate
- ~3-5 working days

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated budget constraint matching on project code creation.

### Controls
- CTL-02 (Capex authorization limits); Board approval required for rebuilds > PHP 50M.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-40 (capex), VS-33.1 (strategy)
