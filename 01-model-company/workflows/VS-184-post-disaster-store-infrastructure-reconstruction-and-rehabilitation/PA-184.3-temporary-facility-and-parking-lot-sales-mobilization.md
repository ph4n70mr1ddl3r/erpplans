## W5289. Temporary Container Sales Office Site Selection and Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store tagged Red or Yellow (W5274) with reconstruction timeline > 30 days |
| **Frequency** | Per approved temporary site mobilization |
| **Volume** | ~1-2 setups annually |
| **Owner** | Facilities Coordinator / Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Construction Contractor, LP Officer, LGU Permit Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify secure layout area in parking lot or adjacent clear lot for temporary setup. | Store Manager | Facilities Coordinator / Store Manager | 2 hours |
| 2 | Mobilize mobile office containers (typically 20-foot shipping container offices). | Construction Contractor | Facilities Coordinator / Store Manager | 1 day |
| 3 | Connect utilities (temporary electrical drop, water, network cabling). | Construction Contractor | Facilities Coordinator / Store Manager | 1 day |
| 4 | Install secure locks, safe box, and perimeter lighting. | LP Officer | Facilities Coordinator / Store Manager | 4 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Facilities Module, Asset registry (VS-35).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unsecure office risk**: Temporary office burglarized due to poor locking systems; mitigated by installing heavy steel doors, deadbolts, and dedicated night guard patrol.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-28: petty cash security controls; LP Director sign-off required.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-3 working days setup duration

### Cross-references
- links to VS-35 (fixed assets), VS-138 (facilities)

## W5290. Mobile POS Terminal Routing and Network Activation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Temporary office structure setup (W5289) |
| **Frequency** | Per temporary site activation |
| **Volume** | ~1-2 activations annually |
| **Owner** | IT Field Support Engineer |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Network Analyst, Security Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deploy mobile POS terminal units, receipt printers, and barcode scanners to site. | IT Field Support Engineer | IT Field Support Engineer | 2-3 hours |
| 2 | Establish secure network tunnel via dual cellular LTE/5G SIM routers. | IT Field Support Engineer | IT Field Support Engineer | 2 hours |
| 3 | Verify POS software transactions processing, receipt printing, and e-wallet connectivity. | Store Manager | IT Field Support Engineer | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Audit security logs ensuring terminal transactions encryption. | Network Analyst | IT Field Support Engineer | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- IAM Module (VS-27), POS Transaction module (VS-08).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transaction offline loss risk**: Unstable cellular connection drops transactions sync, causing cash discrepancies; mitigated by deploying POS terminals configured with offline database store-and-forward features.

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-switching dual SIM cellular failover routing integration.

### Controls
- CTL-20: Ensure POS cash accountability; IT Director terminal activation approval.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 day setup

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27 (IT operations), VS-08 (POS)

## W5291. Temporary Utility Connection and Safety Accreditation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Successful structural container placement (W5289) |
| **Frequency** | Per temporary office mobilization |
| **Volume** | ~1-2 hookups annually |
| **Owner** | Facilities Engineering Lead |
| **Participants** | LGU electrical inspector, private utility partner representative, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Apply for temporary electrical and water connection with local distribution utilities. | Facilities Engineering Lead | Facilities Engineering Lead | 2 hours |
| 2 | Run armored cabling from nearest unaffected substation to distribution panel on container office. | Contractor MEP Engineer | Facilities Engineering Lead | 4 hours |
| 3 | Install safety grounding rod and verify electrical insulation values. | Contractor MEP Engineer | Facilities Engineering Lead | 2 hours |
| 4 | Coordinate safety walk-through inspection with LGU building official; obtain temporary utility safety sign-off. | Facilities Engineering Lead | Facilities Engineering Lead | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- LGU Permitting system (VS-76), Facilities maintenance log (VS-138.2).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Electrocution risk**: Water leaks near temporary electrical panel causing short circuits; mitigated by positioning utility connections on opposite sides of the container and testing ground resistance.

### Automation Opportunity
- Electronic municipal safety inspection scheduling.

### Controls
- CTL-784: personnel safety verification; engineering supervisor formal sign-off.

### Time Estimate
- ~2 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-76 (LGU compliance), VS-138 (facilities)

## W5292. Emergency Vault and Cash Staging Security Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Utilities active at temporary container office (W5291) |
| **Frequency** | Per temporary office mobilization |
| **Volume** | ~1-2 setups annually |
| **Owner** | Loss Prevention Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Cashier, Armored Car Driver |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bolt heavy-duty fireproof drop-safe to reinforced steel framing of the container structure. | Loss Prevention Supervisor | Loss Prevention Supervisor | 2 hours |
| 2 | Install cellular-communicating alarm system and high-definition IP camera facing the safe area. | LP Specialist | Loss Prevention Supervisor | 3 hours |
| 3 | Program access codes for authorized store managers only; test emergency panic button link to local PNP. | Loss Prevention Supervisor | Loss Prevention Supervisor | 1 hour |
| 4 | Issue keys for safe deposit boxes and verify cash custody sign-off registers. | Store Manager | Loss Prevention Supervisor | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- LP Alarm portal, Cash Management module (VS-81).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cash transport theft risk**: Criminal elements target cash transfer from temporary container to armored truck in parking area; mitigated by enforcing dual-custody LP escort and parking CIT truck directly outside the container.

### Automation Opportunity
- Alarm system monitoring with automated status updates sent to corporate security dashboard.

### Controls
- CTL-20: Cash handling controls; LP Manager safe code approval.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 day

### Cross-references
- links to VS-81 (cash-in-transit), VS-23 (loss prevention)

| **Frequency** | Per temporary office sales cycle |
| **Volume** | ~1-2 inventory staging setups annually |
| **Owner** | Store Inventory Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Stock Associate, DC Dispatcher, Regional Merchandiser |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define core SKU list for temporary container: cement, plywood, nails, tarps, electrical wire, hand tools. | Regional Merchandiser | Store Inventory Supervisor | 2 hours |

## W5296. Temporary Facility Decommissioning and Site Re-entry Checklist

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Reconstructed main store facility commissioned (W5288) |
| **Frequency** | Per temporary facility shutdown |
| **Volume** | ~1-2 shutdowns annually |
| **Owner** | Facilities Coordinator / Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Logistics Coordinator, LP Specialist, Utility Technician |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Terminating utility connections (electrical, water) and coordinate technician meter disconnection. | Utility Technician | Facilities Coordinator / Store Manager | 2 hours |
| 2 | De-authorize mobile POS network tunnels and remove all terminal hardware, cash safe, and LP cameras. | LP Specialist | Facilities Coordinator / Store Manager | 4 hours |
| 3 | Clear remaining physical inventory from parking lot cages and return to main store shelves. | Logistics Coordinator | Facilities Coordinator / Store Manager | 6 hours |
| 4 | Demobilize office container units from parking lot; execute final site restoration and cleanup. | Facilities Coordinator | Facilities Coordinator / Store Manager | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Store Status system, Fixed Asset module (VS-35), IT Asset module (VS-99).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Asset abandonment risk**: High-value security equipment or POS terminals left behind in container during logistics pickup; mitigated by matching physical equipment serial numbers against original staging manifest.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated pickup scheduling alerts integrated with container leasing partner systems.

### Controls
- CTL-26: Asset disposal controls; Store Manager final decommissioning clearance.

### Time Estimate
- ~2 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-35 (fixed assets), VS-99 (IT assets), VS-138 (facilities)
