# VS-184: Post-Disaster Store Infrastructure Reconstruction & Rehabilitation

> **Asset & Infrastructure** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Post-disaster store infrastructure reconstruction, structural assessment, insurance coordination, and site rehabilitation for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the medium-to-long term recovery lifecycle for stores and DCs affected by extreme weather, earthquakes, or fire. This includes: physical damage surveys and geodetic hazard mapping; insurance claim coordination and loss adjusters audits; CAPA engineering budget approval; contractor mobilization and site safety fencing; Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) and LGU structural safety re-certification; temporary parking lot container sales office setup; utility restoration coordination; and site handover to operations.

Operating **200 big-box stores** across an archipelago exposed to an average of **20 typhoons per year** and seismic activities makes structural resilience and rapid rehabilitation critical. This value stream manages the post-calamity infrastructure reconstruction process to restore trading capabilities safely and optimize insurance recoveries.
