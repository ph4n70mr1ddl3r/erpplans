# VS-49: Dark Store & Micro-Fulfillment Operations

> **Make & Move** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages dark store (mini-warehouse for ecommerce fulfillment only, no retail customers) and micro-fulfillment center operations for BuildRight's growing ecommerce channel. Dark stores serve as hyper-local fulfillment hubs for rapid BOPIS, home delivery, and ship-from-store orders, reducing delivery times from 2–5 days to same-day or next-day in key metro areas. Targets dark store deployment in Metro Manila, Cebu, and Davao to support ecommerce growth targets of 3→7% of revenue over 3 years.
