## W4329. Dormitory Preventive & Reactive Maintenance Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | PM cycle; resident maintenance request; breakdown |
| **Frequency** | PM scheduled + reactive per request |
| **Volume** | Per-site maintenance across housed units/systems |
| **Owner** | Facilities/Housing Ops (VS-138/VS-20.3) |
| **Participants** | Facilities (VS-138), Maintenance Vendor/Technician, Housing Ops, HSE (VS-24), Asset (VS-35) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities/Housing Ops set the dormitory PM schedule (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, fixtures, appliances, structure) and the reactive work-order process | Facilities / Housing Ops | Housing Ops Lead | Standard |
| 2 | Residents/staff log maintenance requests; Facilities/vendor execute PM & reactive repair, and verify completion with the resident | Facilities / Vendor / Resident | Housing Ops Lead | Per request |
| 3 | Housing Ops/HSE track resolution SLA, recurring failures, and safety-critical (electrical/gas/fire-system) priority, and feed asset lifecycle (W4332) | Housing Ops / HSE | Housing Ops Lead / HSE Mgr | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Facilities/FM CAFM (VS-138); maintenance work-order (VS-20.3); asset (VS-35); HSE (VS-24)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Habitability risk**: unresolved maintenance (no water/AC/electrical) → welfare/DOLE-OSH issue; mitigated by SLA & priority for safety/habitability
- **Safety-system risk**: fire/electrical/gas system failure; mitigated by PM priority & inspection (W4333)

### Time Estimate
- PM scheduled; reactive ~resolve within SLA (24–72 hrs)

### Automation Opportunity
- Resident maintenance-request app; PM scheduler; SLA-tracking; recurring-failure analytics

### Controls
- CTL: PM current; reactive within SLA; safety-critical prioritized; habitability maintained

### Cross-references
- links to VS-138 (FM), VS-20.3 (maintenance), VS-35 (asset), VS-24 (HSE), W4324 (housekeeping), W4332 (asset), W4333 (fire/life-safety)

## W4334. Housing Closure, Move-Out Inspection & Deposit Refund

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee separation/transfer (VS-19); end of assignment |
| **Frequency** | Per move-out |
| **Volume** | Move-outs proportional to turnover/transfers |
| **Owner** | Housing Ops / HR (VS-19/VS-103) / Finance (VS-17.4) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19/VS-103), Housing Ops, Facilities (VS-138), Finance (VS-17.4), Asset (VS-35), LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR/Housing Ops trigger move-out on separation/transfer (VS-19), schedule the move-out, and notify the resident of the inspection & handover | HR / Housing Ops | Housing Ops Lead | Per move-out |
| 2 | Housing Ops/Facilities conduct move-out inspection (unit condition, FF&E inventory W4332, keys/access return, outstanding charges), and document with photos | Housing Ops / Facilities | Housing Ops Lead | Per move-out |
| 3 | Finance/Housing Ops assess resident-caused damage, deduct from deposit/final-pay (VS-19.2) within policy, release the unit for re-allocation (W4322), and settle | Finance / Housing Ops | Finance Mgr / Housing Ops Lead | Per move-out |

### System Touchpoints
- HR/separation (VS-19); HRIS (VS-103); housing/occupancy (W4320); payroll/final-pay (VS-19.2); asset (VS-35); finance (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Damage-recovery risk**: resident-caused damage un-deducted → cost leak; mitigated by move-out inspection & documented deduction
- **Orphan-unit risk**: separated employee's unit not released → idle/blocked; mitigated by separation-triggered move-out & re-allocation

### Time Estimate
- ~30–60 min inspection; settlement days

### Automation Opportunity
- Separation-triggered move-out workflow; inspection-checklist app with photos; damage-deduction auto to final-pay; unit auto-release

### Controls
- CTL: move-out on separation (VS-19); inspection documented; damage deducted within policy; unit released

### Cross-references
