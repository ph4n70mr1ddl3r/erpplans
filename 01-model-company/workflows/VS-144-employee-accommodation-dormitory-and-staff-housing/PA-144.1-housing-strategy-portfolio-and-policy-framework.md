## W4313. Employee Housing Strategy, Eligibility & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Expansion (VS-37); turnover/retention review; site-staffing gap |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on expansion |
| **Volume** | Enterprise housing strategy across ~205 sites and 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Head of People Services / HR (VS-103) / Facilities (VS-138) / Strategy (VS-33) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19/VS-103), Store/DC Ops (VS-07/VS-04), Facilities (VS-138), Finance (VS-17.4), Property (VS-97) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | People Services/Strategy set the housing strategy — eligibility (transferee, new-province hire, night-shift, key-role), coverage (which sites need housing), and operating model (own/lease/manage vs vendor-managed vs housing allowance) | People Services / Strategy / HR | CHRO / COO | Annual |
| 2 | HR/Ops map housing need per site (commute feasibility per VS-141, rental-market thinness, shift pattern) and size the housing portfolio & budget against the expansion plan (10–15 new stores/yr) | HR / Ops / Finance | CHRO / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | People Services document the strategy, obtain approval, and cascade to portfolio (W4314), policy (W4315), cost (W4317), and governance (W4319) | People Services | CHRO / COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- HR/HRIS (VS-103); strategy (VS-33); finance (VS-17.4); facilities (VS-138); property (VS-97)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-investment risk**: housing built/leased where commute is feasible → idle cost; mitigated by need-mapping tied to VS-141 commute feasibility
- **Equity risk**: inconsistent eligibility → grievance/perception of favoritism; mitigated by transparent eligibility criteria (W4315)

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual strategy

### Automation Opportunity
- Site-housing-need model from HRIS + commute data (VS-141); expansion-driven portfolio sizing; cost-benefit scenario modeling

### Controls
- CTL-662 (ensure controlled execution — Housing Strategy, Portfolio & Policy Framework (PA-144.1).)
- CTL: strategy approved (CHRO/COO/CFO); eligibility transparent; need tied to commute/retention data

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103 (HR shared services), VS-33 (strategy), VS-138 (facilities), VS-97 (property), VS-141 (commute), W4314 (portfolio)

## W4314. Housing Portfolio Planning, Lease-vs-Own & Site Selection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4313); new-site opening (VS-37); lease expiry |
| **Frequency** | Per lifecycle + annual plan |
| **Volume** | Housing portfolio across sites (units/beds) |
| **Owner** | Housing Portfolio / Real Estate (VS-97/VS-20) / Facilities (VS-138) |
| **Participants** | Property (VS-97), Facilities (VS-138), Finance (VS-40/VS-17.4), Legal (VS-100.3), Procurement (VS-34) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Portfolio/RE plan the housing footprint per site — units/beds needed, lease-vs-own-vs-vendor-managed decision, location (proximity/safety/access to site), and build/lease cost | Portfolio / RE / Finance | Head of People Services / CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Property/Legal source & lease/purchase units or contract a vendor-managed dormitory, with terms (rent, maintenance, safety compliance, exit) per VS-97/VS-34 | Property / Legal / Procurement | CFO / VP Legal | Per lifecycle |
| 3 | Facilities/Asset register housing units as assets/leases (VS-35/VS-42), track capacity vs need, and feed capex/opex to VS-40/VS-17.4 | Facilities / Asset / Finance | CFO | Continuous |

- Real estate (VS-97); lease admin (VS-42); asset (VS-35); procurement (VS-34); finance/capex (VS-40/VS-17.4); legal (VS-100.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Location risk**: housing far/unsafe from site → low take-up/safety exposure; mitigated by proximity/safety site-selection criteria
- **Lease-vs-own risk**: leasing where long-term need is stable → cost over time; mitigated by lifecycle cost analysis vs expansion horizon

### Time Estimate
- Annual plan ~2 weeks; per-site sourcing ~4–8 weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- Capacity-vs-need dashboard; lease-vs-own lifecycle costing; vendor-managed-dormitory marketplace scan

### Controls
- CTL-662 (ensure controlled execution — Housing Strategy, Portfolio & Policy Framework (PA-144.1).)
- CTL: site selection on proximity/safety; lease-vs-own lifecycle-costed; assets/leases registered (VS-35/VS-42)

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR/Legal draft the housing policy & resident agreement — eligibility, allocation, occupancy terms, house rules (conduct, visitors, quiet hours, prohibited items/acts), termination/eviction, fee/deduction, and welfare/anti-harassment | HR / Legal / Housing Ops | CHRO / VP Legal | Annual |

### Time Estimate

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-662 (ensure controlled execution — Housing Strategy, Portfolio & Policy Framework (PA-144.1).)

## W4316. Housing Allocation, Assignment & Occupancy Entitlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per assignment + ongoing occupancy |
| **Volume** | Housing-eligible employee population × assignment cycles |
| **Owner** | Housing Ops / HR (VS-19/VS-103) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19/VS-103), Store/DC Ops (VS-07/VS-04), Facilities (VS-138), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR/Housing Ops verify employee eligibility per policy (W4315) and the site's housing availability (W4314), and apply allocation priority (transferee, key-role, hardship) | HR / Housing Ops | Housing Ops Lead | Per assignment |
| 2 | Housing Ops assign the unit/bed, issue the resident agreement (W4315), schedule move-in/check-in (W4321), and record occupancy in the housing system | Housing Ops | Housing Ops Lead | Per assignment |
| 3 | HR/Housing Ops manage move-out/transfer (W4334), release the unit, and update occupancy/cost records | HR / Housing Ops | Housing Ops Lead | Per move-out |

### System Touchpoints
- HR/HRIS (VS-19/VS-103); housing/occupancy system; finance (VS-17.4); facilities (VS-138)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Favoritism risk**: allocation not transparent → grievance; mitigated by documented eligibility/priority criteria
- **Idle-unit risk**: assigned units held by separated/transferred employees; mitigated by move-out trigger from HRIS (VS-19 separation/transfer)

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min per assignment; move-out ~30–60 min

### Automation Opportunity
- HRIS-driven eligibility & auto-allocation; vacancy dashboard; move-out auto-trigger on separation/transfer (VS-19)

### Controls
- CTL-662 (ensure controlled execution — Housing Strategy, Portfolio & Policy Framework (PA-144.1).)
- CTL: allocation per transparent criteria; occupancy reconciled to HRIS; move-out on separation triggers release

### Cross-references
- links to VS-19/VS-103 (HR), VS-07/VS-04 (ops), W4315 (policy), W4321 (check-in), W4334 (move-out)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Policy (W4315); finance close; entity/role change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + monthly processing |
| **Volume** | Enterprise housing cost/recovery across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | HR (VS-19.2 payroll) / Finance (VS-17.4/VS-72) / Housing Ops |
| **Participants** | Payroll (VS-19.2), Finance (VS-17.4), Shared Services (VS-72), Tax (VS-79), HR (VS-103) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance/HR set the housing cost model — full cost (rent/utilities/maintenance/housekeeping), subsidy % by eligibility tier, employee share, and salary-deduction treatment (incl. BIR/tax per VS-79 for de-minimis/benefit-in-kind) | Finance / HR / Tax | CFO / CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | Payroll/Shared Services process the monthly salary deduction and the entity cost allocation (which entity bears the housing cost — Depot vs Logistics vs Property, per intercompany VS-72) | Payroll / Shared Services | Finance Mgr | Monthly |

### Time Estimate

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-662 (ensure controlled execution — Housing Strategy, Portfolio & Policy Framework (PA-144.1).)

## W4318. Housing Safety, Welfare & DOLE/OSH Compliance Standard

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Housing operation; incident; regulatory review |
| **Frequency** | Standard + continuous adherence |
| **Volume** | All housing units/residents |
| **Owner** | HSE (VS-24) / Housing Ops / Compliance (VS-22) |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), Facilities (VS-138), Fire/BFP, DOLE, HR (VS-103), Insurance (VS-26), Risk (VS-21.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HSE/Housing Ops set the housing safety standard — fire/life-safety (BFP Fire Code: exits, extinguishers, smoke detection, no overcrowding), structural, electrical, sanitation/ventilation, capacity (floor-area/person), and DOLE OSH on employer-provided housing | HSE / Housing Ops / Facilities | HSE Mgr / CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | Facilities/HSE inspect each housing unit to standard, remediate deficiencies (W4329), and maintain inspection records | Facilities / HSE | Housing Ops Lead / HSE Mgr | Periodic |
| 3 | HSE/HR/Insurance manage welfare incidents (injury, fire, harassment, illness), investigate, file insurance (VS-26), and feed the risk register (VS-21.2) | HSE / HR / Insurance / Risk | HSE Mgr / CRO | Per incident |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE (VS-24); facilities (VS-138); compliance (VS-22); insurance (VS-26); risk (VS-21.2); HR (VS-103)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fire/overcrowding risk**: substandard fire-safety/overcrowded dormitory → BFP/OSH violation, mass-casualty exposure; mitigated by BFP-standard inspection + capacity rule
- **Welfare/liability risk**: resident injury/harassment on employer-provided housing; mitigated by welfare standard + insurance (VS-26) + incident response

### Time Estimate
- Standard ~2 weeks; inspection ~1–2 hrs/unit; per-incident variable

### Automation Opportunity
- Inspection checklist app with deficiency auto-work-order; capacity-vs-occupancy alert; incident-reporting & insurance workflow

### Controls
- CTL-128 (ensure dormitory safety & welfare compliance.)
- CTL: BFP fire-safety & DOLE-OSH housing standard met; capacity not exceeded; incidents reported & insured (VS-26)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-138 (facilities), VS-22 (compliance), VS-26 (insurance), VS-21.2 (risk), W4329 (maintenance), W4333 (fire/life-safety)
