## W4321. Dormitory Daily Operations, Check-In/Out & Front-Desk Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Resident assignment (W4316); daily operation |
| **Frequency** | Daily + per check-in/out |
| **Volume** | Per-site daily ops × ~housed-sites; check-in/out on assignment/move-out |
| **Owner** | Dormitory Warden/House Parent / Housing Ops |
| **Participants** | Housing Ops, HR (VS-103), Facilities (VS-138), Security (VS-23), Resident |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warden/Housing Ops run daily front-desk operations — check-in (verify assignment W4316, signed agreement W4315, orientation, key/access issuance, bed/unit handover) and check-out (key return, inspection W4334) | Warden / Housing Ops | Housing Ops Lead | Per check-in/out |
| 2 | Warden/Housing Ops maintain the daily log (occupancy, incidents, maintenance requests, visitor register W4325), conduct rounds, and report exceptions | Warden / Housing Ops | Housing Ops Lead | Daily |
| 3 | Housing Ops reconcile daily occupancy to the housing system/HRIS (W4320) and escalate anomalies (unauthorized occupant, separation not released) | Housing Ops | Housing Ops Lead | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Housing/occupancy system (W4320); HRIS (VS-103); facilities/maintenance (VS-138); security/access (VS-23)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unauthorized-occupant risk**: non-employee/unassigned person housed → liability/security; mitigated by assignment-verified check-in & access control (W4325)
- **Orphan-occupancy risk**: separated employee still housed; mitigated by HRIS-reconciled daily occupancy (W4320)

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min/check-in; rounds ~1–2 hrs/day/site

### Automation Opportunity
- Digital check-in/out with assignment verification; access-card issuance; daily-occupancy auto-reconcile to HRIS

### Controls
- CTL-663 (ensure controlled execution — Dormitory/Accommodation Operations, Occupancy & Resident Welfare (PA-144.2).)
- CTL: check-in assignment-verified; daily occupancy reconciled to HRIS; visitor register maintained (W4325)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103 (HR), VS-138 (facilities), VS-23 (security), W4316 (assignment), W4320 (system), W4334 (move-out)

## W4322. Occupancy Management, Bed Allocation & Waitlist

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vacancy; waitlisted applicant; capacity change |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | Per-site bed/occupancy management |
| **Owner** | Housing Ops / HR (VS-103) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-103), Store/DC Ops (VS-07/VS-04), Facilities (VS-138), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Housing Ops maintain real-time bed/unit availability by site, apply allocation priority (W4316), and manage the waitlist for over-subscribed sites | Housing Ops | Housing Ops Lead | Continuous |
| 2 | Housing Ops balance gender/role-appropriate allocation (separate quarters where required), respect capacity limits (W4318), and document assignments | Housing Ops / HR | Housing Ops Lead | Per allocation |
| 3 | Housing Ops/Finance forecast occupancy vs capacity to flag need for additional units (W4314) or cost recovery gaps, and report utilization | Housing Ops / Finance | Housing Ops Lead / CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Housing/occupancy system (W4320); HRIS (VS-103); finance (VS-17.4); facilities (VS-138)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-allocation risk**: beds assigned beyond capacity/safety limit → overcrowding/BFP violation; mitigated by capacity-enforced allocation (W4318)
- **Waitlist-opaque risk**: unclear waitlist order → grievance; mitigated by documented priority criteria

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; ~hours/week/site

### Automation Opportunity
- Real-time bed-availability dashboard; waitlist by priority; capacity-enforced allocation; utilization analytics

### Controls
- CTL-663 (ensure controlled execution — Dormitory/Accommodation Operations, Occupancy & Resident Welfare (PA-144.2).)
- CTL: allocation within capacity (W4318); priority documented; gender/role-appropriate; occupancy tracked

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103 (HR), VS-07/VS-04 (ops), W4314 (portfolio), W4316 (assignment), W4318 (capacity), W4320 (system)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warden/HR receive welfare concerns/grievances (roommate conflict, harassment, unsafe condition, mental-health), log confidentially, and acknowledge to the resident | Warden / HR / Housing Ops | Housing Ops Lead / HR Mgr | Per case |
| 2 | HR/Labor Relations investigate per VS-84/VS-119 (harassment/ethics escalation), take interim protection measures, and mediate/resolve | HR / Labor Relations / Ethics | CHRO / Ethics Lead | Per case |

- **Retaliation risk**: resident penalized for reporting; mitigated by anti-retaliation (VS-119) & monitoring

### Time Estimate
- ~1–4 hrs per case; complex cases longer

### Automation Opportunity
- Confidential grievance channel; case-management workflow; VS-119 integration; welfare pulse-survey

### Controls
- CTL-663 (ensure controlled execution — Dormitory/Accommodation Operations, Occupancy & Resident Welfare (PA-144.2).)
- CTL: grievance channel confidential; harassment escalated (VS-84/VS-119); anti-retaliation monitored

### Cross-references

| **Participants** | HR/EX (VS-103), People Analytics (VS-103.3), Housing Ops, Store/DC Ops (VS-07/VS-04), Strategy (VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

### Time Estimate
- Quarterly review ~1–2 days

### Automation Opportunity
- Housing-satisfaction pulse; retention-attribution analytics; retention-ROI dashboard; action-tracking
