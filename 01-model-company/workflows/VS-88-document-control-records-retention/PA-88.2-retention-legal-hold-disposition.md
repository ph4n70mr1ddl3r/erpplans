## W2977. Retention Schedule Maintenance by Record Class (BIR/SEC/DOLE/NPC)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual review or regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~200–300 record classes |
| **Owner** | Records Manager |
| **Participants** | Records Mgr, Compliance, Legal, Tax Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Records Manager maintains retention schedule mapping each record class to mandated retention: BIR accounting/tax 10 years, DOLE payroll 3–5+ years, SEC corporate permanent/specified, NPC per purpose | Records Manager | Compliance Officer | Annual |
| 2 | Compliance/Legal/Tax validate retention against current regulations; updates on regulatory change | Compliance / Legal / Tax Manager | Compliance Officer | Per change |
| 3 | IT configures retention policy in document management system; enforces automatic disposition hold until retention expires | IT | Records Manager | Per change |

### System Touchpoints
- Retention schedule master; policy enforcement engine

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Premature destruction violates retention law; mitigated by policy-enforced retention


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "Records Manager maintains retention schedule mapping each record class to…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validate retention against current regulations" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document management system" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-239 (ensure records-retention & legal-hold compliance.)
- operational: policy-enforced retention

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks annual

## W2978. Retention Clock Start, Pause & Event-Driven Extension

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Continuous |

| **Owner** | Records Manager |
| **Participants** | Records Mgr, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

## W2979. Legal Hold Issuance, Scope & Release Management

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Litigation, investigation, or regulatory inquiry |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~5–20 holds/year |
| **Volume** | Per matter |
| **Owner** | Legal Counsel |
| **Participants** | Legal, Records Manager, IT, Custodians |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal issues legal hold notice defining scope (custodians, date range, keywords, record classes); communicates to custodians | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | Per hold |
| 2 | Records Manager/IT applies hold in repository (suspends retention clock and disposition); preserves relevant records and metadata | Records Manager / IT | Records Manager | Per hold |

### Pain Points / Risks

### Automation Opportunity

- System auto-document of "documents hold lifecycle" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-239 (ensure records-retention & legal-hold compliance.)
- operational: hold suspension and custodian notice

### Time Estimate
- 1–4 hours per hold issuance/release
