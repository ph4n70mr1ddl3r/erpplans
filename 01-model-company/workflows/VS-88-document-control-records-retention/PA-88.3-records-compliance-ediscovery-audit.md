## W2985. e-Discovery Request Response, Collection & Production

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Court order, subpoena, or regulatory e-Discovery request |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~5–15 requests/year |
| **Volume** | Per request |
| **Owner** | Legal Counsel |
| **Participants** | Legal, Records Manager, IT, Custodians |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal parses request scope (custodians, date range, keywords, record classes); coordinates with Records Manager/IT | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | Per request |
| 2 | IT/Records Manager collects relevant ESI (electronically stored information) preserving metadata and chain of custody; de-duplicates and reviews for privilege | IT / Records Manager | Legal Counsel | Per request |
| 3 | Legal produces responsive records in required format; documents privilege log; archives production | Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per request |

### System Touchpoints
- e-Discovery collection tool; chain-of-custody log; privilege log

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Legal risk**: Incomplete production or broken chain of custody; mitigated by defensible process and documentation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "record classes" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews for privilege" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "log" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: defensible process and documentation

### Time Estimate
- Days to weeks per request

## W2986. BIR Records Inspection (LOA) Support & Production

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | BIR Letter of Authority covering books/records |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~2–4 audits/year |
| **Volume** | Multi-year scope |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, Records Manager, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Manager validates LOA scope; coordinates with Records Manager to assemble books, e-books, invoices, schedules for 10-year look-back | Tax Manager | CFO | Per audit |
| 2 | Records Manager produces records (digital preferred; physical where required) in BIR-accepted format; maintains inventory and chain of custody | Records Manager | Tax Manager | Per audit |
| 3 | Tax Manager/Legal responds to BIR queries; defends record completeness and integrity; archives audit correspondence | Tax Manager / Legal | CFO | Per audit |

### System Touchpoints
- BIR audit production repository; chain-of-custody log

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Incomplete/unavailable records trigger BIR assessment; mitigated by 10-year retention and retrieval readiness


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "Records Manager to assemble books, e-books, invoices, schedules for 10-year…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System continuous audit of "audit correspondence" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: 10-year retention and retrieval readiness

### Time Estimate
- Weeks per audit
