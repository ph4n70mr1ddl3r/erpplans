## W3857. Enterprise Fraud Management Strategy, Risk Appetite & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual strategy (links to VS-33.1); board risk appetite refresh |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise fraud program (1 program; ~PHP 62.3B revenue protected) |
| **Owner** | Fraud Management / CFO |
| **Participants** | Fraud Management, Finance/Treasury (links to VS-18), Internal Audit (links to VS-21), Loss Prevention (links to VS-23), Risk (links to VS-21/VS-26), Legal (links to VS-100), IT Security (links to VS-27.3), Privacy/DPO (links to VS-91), Payments (links to VS-80), Ecommerce (links to VS-10), Loyalty (links to VS-13), HR (links to VS-19/VS-103), ABC (links to VS-86) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fraud Management/Finance define the enterprise fraud strategy and risk appetite: target fraud-loss rate (% of revenue, channel-level), acceptable false-positive rate, the cross-channel operating model (central fraud team + channel investigators + store/regional LP), and the governance committee reporting to the Audit & Risk Committee (links to VS-21/VS-36) | Fraud Management / Finance / Risk | CFO / CRO | Annual |
| 2 | Fraud Management establish the policy framework: fraud policy, investigation standards, evidence handling, escalation thresholds, law-enforcement referral criteria, employee-fraud handling (links to VS-19.1 discipline/VS-119 speak-up), and interplay with AML (links to VS-86), privacy (links to VS-91), and revenue assurance (links to VS-118) | Fraud Management / Legal / Privacy | CFO | Annual |
| 3 | CFO/Executive ratify the strategy, approve the fraud budget (links to W3864), and cascade accountability to channel owners (Store Ops links to VS-07, Ecommerce links to VS-10, Payments links to VS-80, Loyalty links to VS-13) | CFO / Fraud Management | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Fraud risk register & appetite (links to VS-21/VS-26); audit committee reporting (links to VS-36); ABC (links to VS-86); privacy (links to VS-91); payments (links to VS-80); ecommerce (links to VS-10); loyalty (links to VS-13); LP (links to VS-23)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-owner risk**: Fraud handled reactively per channel with no enterprise loss-rate visibility; mitigated by central fraud strategy and KPI scorecard
- **Appetite-misalignment risk**: Over-tight controls raise false positives and hurt checkout conversion; mitigated by explicit false-positive appetite and tuning (links to W3862)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "enterprise fraud strategy and risk appetite" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "countability to channel owners" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-109 (ensure cross-channel fraud detection & case governance.)
- operational: central fraud strategy and KPI scorecard; operational: explicit false-positive appetite and tuning (links to W3862)

### Time Estimate
- 3–4 weeks annual strategy + ongoing governance

## W3858. Fraud Risk Assessment & Cross-Channel Fraud Typology Catalog

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W3857); emerging-scheme intelligence |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on new-scheme alert |
| **Volume** | 1 enterprise fraud-risk assessment; ~25–40 fraud typologies catalogued |
| **Owner** | Fraud Management / Risk |
| **Participants** | Fraud Management, Risk (links to VS-21), Internal Audit (links to VS-21), Loss Prevention (links to VS-23), Channels (links to VS-07/VS-10/VS-80/VS-13), Insights (links to VS-28.1/VS-44) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fraud Management/Risk run the cross-channel fraud risk assessment: identify fraud typologies per channel (POS sweethearting/price-override, barcode-switch, return-without-receipt, refund-to-card, coupon stacking, loyalty-point farming, gift-card cash-out, COD non-receipt claims, friendly-fraud chargebacks, account takeover, trade-account first-party, employee collusion), rate each by likelihood × impact, and map existing controls | Fraud Management / Risk / Channels | Fraud Management Lead | Annual |
| 2 | Fraud Management publish the fraud typology catalog and control matrix (typology → detection signal → rule/model → case workflow → recovery path), with owners and KRIs, and register gaps into the detection backlog (links to W3859) | Fraud Management / Internal Audit | Fraud Management Lead | Annual |
| 3 | Fraud Management/Insights refresh on emerging schemes (seasonal peaks, new tender types, new channel launches links to VS-75/VS-95) and feed threat intel (links to W3861) | Fraud Management / Insights | Fraud Management Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Risk register (links to VS-21); LP exception analytics (links to VS-23); ecommerce fraud signals (links to VS-10); payment chargeback data (links to VS-80); loyalty/gift-card data (links to VS-13/VS-54); insights (links to VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Blind-spot risk**: New schemes (e.g., social-engineering on COD, marketplace-3P fraud links to VS-95) undetected until loss materializes; mitigated by typology refresh and consortium intel
- **Double-counting risk**: Same loss counted as shrink (VS-23) and fraud; mitigated by clear typology ownership


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "technologies per channel" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-detect of "detection signal → rule/model → case workflow → recovery path" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-109 (ensure cross-channel fraud detection & case governance.)
- operational: typology refresh and consortium intel; operational: clear typology ownership

### Time Estimate
- 4–6 weeks annual + continuous scheme monitoring

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

| 2 | Fraud Management/Data Science develop and deploy ML models (transaction anomaly, return-abuse, account-takeover, chargeback-risk, gift-card velocity) with feature stores, model registry, and champion/challenger (links to VS-30.2/W3862) | Fraud Management / Data Science | Fraud Management Lead | Continuous |
| 3 | Fraud Management orchestrate decisions (approve/decline/step-up/challenge/review/case-open) with consistent customer friction (links to VS-13 CX) and route to case management (links to W3860) | Fraud Management / Channels | Fraud Management Lead | Continuous |

- System auto-review of "review/case-open" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-109 (ensure cross-channel fraud detection & case governance.)
- operational: synchronous low-latency API and async case fallback; operational: model governance (links to W3862)

### Time Estimate
- Maintained platform + ~2–4 days per new model/rule cycle

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Continuous |

| **Owner** | Fraud Management |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fraud Management triage alerts into cases (duplicate-suppress, severity-score, assign owner/investigator), link to customer/transaction/device identity (links to VS-126 identity resolution), and set SLA by severity | Fraud Investigators | Fraud Management Lead | Per case |
| 2 | Investigators gather evidence across systems (POS journal links to VS-08, CCTV links to VS-23, ecommerce logs links to VS-10, payment/chargeback data links to VS-80, loyalty/gift-card history links to VS-13/VS-54, returns history links to VS-32), interview/store-side checks via LP (links to VS-23), and document chain-of-custody | Fraud Investigators / LP / Channels | Fraud Management Lead | Per case |
| 3 | Investigators adjudicate (confirm/dismiss), apply outcomes (block/flag customer, reverse points/gift balance, restock hold, refer for recovery links to W3874 or law-enforcement links to W3877), and close with root-cause and learning fed to rules (links to W3862) | Fraud Investigators / Legal | Fraud Management Lead | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Case management system; POS journal (links to VS-08); CCTV/LP (links to VS-23); ecommerce logs (links to VS-10); payments/chargebacks (links to VS-80); loyalty/gift-card (links to VS-13/VS-54); returns (links to VS-32); identity (links to VS-126); legal matter (links to VS-100)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Evidence-scatter risk**: Evidence scattered across POS/ecommerce/CCTV with no single case view; mitigated by unified case management and identity link (links to VS-126)
- **SLA-breach risk**: High alert volumes overwhelm investigators; mitigated by triage severity and ML prioritization


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-assign of "assign owner/investigator" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "logs links to VS-10, payment/chargeback data links to VS-80, loyalty/gift-card…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-flag of "flag customer, reverse points/gift balance, restock hold, refer for recovery…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-109 (ensure cross-channel fraud detection & case governance.)
- operational: unified case management and identity link (links to VS-126); operational: triage severity and ML prioritization

### Time Estimate
- ~20–90 min/case; ~8,000–15,000 cases/yr → ~5,000–12,000 investigator-hrs/yr
