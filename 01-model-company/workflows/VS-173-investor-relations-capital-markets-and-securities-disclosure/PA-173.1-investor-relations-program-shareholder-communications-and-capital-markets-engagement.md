## W5009. IR Program Strategy, Investor Audience & Equity-Story / Capital-Markets Narrative Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch; annual IR plan; capital-markets event; strategic-plan refresh (VS-33); transaction (VS-130) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | 1 IR program across the 5 entities × investor/analyst/credit/lender audiences |
| **Owner** | VP Investor Relations / CFO / Strategy (VS-33) |
| **Participants** | CFO/CEO, Strategy (VS-33), Treasury (VS-18), R2R (VS-17), Governance (VS-36), Marketing/Comms (VS-14), ESG (VS-25), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the IR strategy & operating model: the investor/analyst/credit/lender audiences BuildRight engages, the equity story and capital-markets narrative (positioning, growth thesis, margin/cash-conversion story, multi-entity/intercompany clarity links VS-72), the disclosure posture (voluntary vs mandatory), the IR calendar (earnings, ASHM, ratings, events), and the IR KPIs (coverage, sentiment, valuation gap) | VP IR / Strategy / CFO | CFO / CEO | Annual |
| 2 | Build the equity-story & narrative materials: the core IR deck, fact book, segment/financial summaries, capital-structure and liquidity narrative (links VS-18/VS-170), and the consistency check vs the strategic plan (VS-33), annual report (W5018), and management accounts (VS-17) | VP IR / Finance / Strategy | VP IR | Annual |
| 3 | Govern & align: leadership/board sign-off on the narrative and forward statements (links VS-36 governance), alignment of investor messaging with internal strategy, and the disclosure-controls linkage (W5017) so every external statement traces to approved, disclosed information | VP IR / CFO / Legal / Governance | CFO / CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- IR/CRM platform; investor database (W5010); FP&A/strategy (VS-33/VS-17.4); treasury (VS-18); legal/disclosure (VS-100/W5017); IR website (W5014)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Narrative-drift risk**: Investor messaging diverging from strategy/internal data → credibility loss; mitigated by strategy alignment & approval
- **Forward-statement risk**: Guidance/projections without disclosure-control backing → securities-law exposure (W5017); mitigated by disclosure-governance gate
- **Consistency risk**: Equity story conflicting with annual report/accounts (VS-17); mitigated by reconciliation

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual IR plan; quarterly narrative refresh

### Automation Opportunity
- Narrative/drafting templates tied to FP&A actuals; IR KPI dashboards; audience-segmentation analytics

### Controls
- CTL-749 (ensure controlled execution — Investor Relations Program, Shareholder Communications & Capital-Markets Engagement (PA-173.1).)
- CTL: narrative board/leadership-approved; forward statements disclosure-controlled (W5017); messaging reconciled to accounts/strategy

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-17/VS-17.4 (accounts/FP&A), VS-18/VS-170 (capital structure), VS-36 (governance), VS-100 (legal), W5010 (database), W5014 (website), W5017 (disclosure controls), W5018 (annual report)

## W5011. Periodic Investor Communications — Earnings, Results & Guidance Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly/annual close (VS-17); guidance cycle; investor calendar |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + annual |
| **Volume** | ~4 quarterly + 1 annual results cycle per year × audiences |
| **Owner** | VP Investor Relations / CFO |
| **Participants** | CFO/CEO, R2R (VS-17), FP&A (VS-17.4/VS-33), Treasury (VS-18), Legal (VS-100), ESG (VS-25) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare results & earnings communications: the results press release, investor presentation, financial & segment commentary, MD&A-style narrative, and guidance/range — all reconciled to the closed books (VS-17) and reviewed through disclosure controls (W5017) before release | VP IR / Finance / FP&A / Legal | CFO | Per cycle |
| 2 | Conduct the results engagement: earnings call/webcast (Q&A prep, script, hot-topics), investor portal publication (W5014), and analyst follow-up — all on fair-disclosure basis (W5017) | VP IR / CFO / CEO | CFO | Per cycle |
| 3 | Manage guidance & forward statements: set/affirm/withdraw guidance with disclosure-control sign-off (W5017), track consensus (W5013), and manage any guidance update/withdrawal as a disclosure event (W5019) | VP IR / CFO / Legal | CFO / CEO | Per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- R2R/close (VS-17); FP&A (VS-17.4); disclosure-controls (W5017); IR website/webcast (W5014); consensus (W5013); legal (VS-100)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Disclosure-breach risk**: Releasing material info in earnings without fair disclosure (W5017); mitigated by simultaneous public release
- **Guidance-miss risk**: Guidance not met → credibility/litigation risk; mitigated by conservative, control-backed ranges
- **Numbers-mismatch risk**: Investor comms disagreeing with filed accounts (VS-17); mitigated by reconciliation & close tie-out

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days prep per quarterly cycle; ~half-day earnings engagement

### Automation Opportunity
- Investor-deck generation from FP&A actuals; webcast/Q&A logging; consensus tracking; disclosure-checklist workflow

### Controls
- CTL-749 (ensure controlled execution — Investor Relations Program, Shareholder Communications & Capital-Markets Engagement (PA-173.1).)
- CTL: results reconciled to closed books; forward statements disclosure-controlled (W5017); fair simultaneous release; script/legal-reviewed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (close/accounts), VS-17.4/VS-33 (FP&A/strategy), W5017 (disclosure controls), W5014 (website), W5013 (consensus), W5019 (ad-hoc disclosure), W5018 (annual report)
