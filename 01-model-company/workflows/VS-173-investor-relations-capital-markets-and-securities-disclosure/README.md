# VS-173: Investor Relations, Capital Markets & Securities Disclosure

> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Investor relations (IR), capital-markets engagement and securities/corporate-disclosure workflows
for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **shareholder-, analyst- and market-facing communications
and disclosure program** that sustains the group's equity story, valuation, cost of capital and
securities-law compliance: the IR strategy and capital-markets narrative; investor/ownership
intelligence and targeting; earnings, results and guidance communications; investor events,
roadshows and analyst days; sell-side analyst and credit-rating-agency relationships; the IR
website/portal; **corporate disclosure controls and material-event/SEC-statutory disclosure**; the
**annual stockholders' meeting (ASHM) investor/proxy materials**; insider-list and
securities-trading-window compliance (RA 8799, the Securities Regulation Code); the **share
register, stock ledger and cap-table/ownership administration**; the **dividend program
(declaration-to-payment, DRIP/election, unclaimed property)**; shareholder services; and IR
perception/analytics.

This matters at BuildRight's structure, scale and ownership. The group is a ~PHP 62.3B-revenue
holding company operating across **5 legal entities** (Holdings, Depot, Logistics, Digital
Commerce, Property Mgmt) with material institutional ownership, bank/debt and capital-markets
counterparties (links VS-170 ABL / VS-18 treasury), a credit-rating posture that sets borrowing
cost across every facility, and ESG-rated institutional investors (links VS-25). Even as a
non-listed group, BuildRight issues securities to investors/lenders, files statutory disclosures
with the **Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)** and the Bureau of Internal Revenue, holds an
**Annual Stockholders' Meeting (ASHM)**, pays dividends (links W327), maintains a share/stock
ledger and cap table, and is bound by the **Securities Regulation Code (RA 8799)** on
insider-trading, selective-disclosure and related-party reporting — with lender covenants, bond/
facility indentures and the general information sheet (GIS) all demanding timely, accurate,
fair-disclosure-grade investor communications. Mis-management starves the equity/debt story,
mis-prices the cost of capital, breaches a securities-law disclosure duty (RA 8799 — civil and
criminal officer liability), triggers an insider-trading or selective-disclosure violation, or
loses a lender/credit-rating/investor at exactly the moment liquidity is needed (links VS-26 BC,
VS-130 M&A).

Today this discipline is **unowned as a unified program** — the defining terms "investor
relations", "SEC reporting", "SEC filing", "securities disclosure", "equity story", "capital
markets narrative", "sell-side analyst", "credit rating agency", "investor website", "proxy
statement", and "cap table" each appear in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers.
Adjacent coverage exists only as **scattered single-workflow slices** across five other value
streams — the same scattered-slice pattern consolidated into VS-161 (TPRM) and VS-166 (license
portfolio): **W327** (External Shareholder Dividend *Declaration & Payment*, in VS-18.1
treasury) posts the cash but owns neither the dividend *program* nor shareholder services;
**W482** (Annual Stockholders' Meeting *Management*, in VS-17.1 record-to-report) runs the
meeting *logistics/admin* but not the investor/proxy/disclosure content; **W4684** (Corporate
Event, AGM & High-Profile Appearance *Security*, in VS-159 corporate security) protects the AGM
but does not run the investor-facing event; **W4524** (Foundation *Annual Report*, in VS-152 CSR)
reports the *foundation* not the group; and **W3373** (Media & Analyst Relations for
*Policy/Industry Topics*, in VS-104 government affairs) manages policy/industry-analyst
relations, not financial analyst/investor relations. No value stream owns the **investor-relations
/ capital-markets / securities-disclosure program**. It is **not** covered by **VS-18 (Treasury &
Cash)**, which owns cash/banks/FX/forecasting and the dividend cash-mechanic (W327) — this owns
the *investor/analyst relationship and securities-disclosure discipline*. It is **not** covered by
**VS-17 (Record-to-Report)**, which closes the books and runs ASHM admin (W482) — this owns the
*investor narrative, disclosure content and share-register/cap-table program*. It is **not**
covered by **VS-104 (Government Affairs)**, which manages policy/regulator advocacy (W3373) — this
owns *financial-market* (sell-side/credit/investor) relations. It is **not** covered by **VS-36
(Corporate Governance)**, which governs the board — this serves the *shareholder/market*
audience and feeds disclosure governance to it. And it is **not** covered by **VS-25 (ESG)** or
**VS-152 (CSR)**, which own sustainability/CSR reporting — this owns the *investor-channel*
communication of it and the group annual report.

This value stream owns the end-to-end program: IR strategy & equity-story/capital-markets
narrative; investor/shareholder database, ownership intelligence & targeting; periodic
(earnings/results/guidance) investor communications; investor events, analyst days, roadshows &
conferences; sell-side analyst & credit-rating-agency relationship & consensus management; the IR
website/portal & digital disclosure channels; investor-inquiry CRM & proactive outreach;
ESG-investor & sustainable-finance communications (links VS-25); **corporate disclosure controls,
materiality & selective/fair-disclosure (Reg-FD-equivalent) program**; **annual report, GIS &
SEC-statutory investor disclosure**; material-event/ad-hoc disclosure & press-release/rumor
control; **ASHM investor notice, proxy statement & information circular (links W482)**;
capital-markets/securities-offering transaction disclosure (links VS-18/VS-130); **insider list,
trading-window & securities-trade compliance (RA 8799 SRC)**; related-party, beneficial-ownership
& group-structure disclosure; disclosure governance & board reporting (links VS-36); **share
register, stock ledger & cap-table/ownership administration**; stock transfer & investor-position
reconciliation; **dividend & distribution program — declaration-to-payment, DRIP/election &
unclaimed property (links W327)**; shareholder services & self-service; investor-perception/
sentiment analytics; peer/valuation/capital-structure benchmarking; IR budget/ROI; and
crisis/activist/transaction IR response (links VS-130). It integrates tightly with VS-18
(treasury/dividend/facility), VS-17 (R2R/ASHM/debt accounting), VS-36 (governance/board),
VS-25/VS-152 (ESG/CSR reporting), VS-104 (policy/regulator comms), VS-130 (M&A/transactions),
VS-100 (legal — RA 8799 securities law), VS-21 (audit/disclosure assurance), VS-79 (BIR), and
VS-26 (business continuity).
