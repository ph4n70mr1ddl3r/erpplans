## W5025. Share Register, Stock Ledger & Ownership / Cap-Table Administration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Share issuance/issuance event; cap-table change; record date; transaction (VS-130); periodic reconciliation |
| **Frequency** | Per share event + periodic reconciliation |
| **Volume** | ~share movements × 5 entities × shareholders |
| **Owner** | Company Secretary (VS-36) / VP Investor Relations / General Counsel (VS-100) |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100), Finance/R2R (VS-17), Treasury (VS-18), Transfer Agent, Compliance (VS-22), Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the share register & stock ledger: the register of shareholders/members, stock ledger, issued/outstanding/treasury shares, classes/series, par value, and the cap table per entity (5 entities — links VS-72), ensuring legal integrity of ownership records (the ASHM/dividend/RPT foundation) | Company Secretary / Legal / Transfer Agent | Company Secretary / General Counsel | Per event + periodic |
| 2 | Administer the cap table & ownership: authorized vs issued vs outstanding, option/warrant/convertible tracking (links VS-102 W3324 LTI), dilution analysis, beneficial-owner mapping (W5023), and ownership intelligence feed (W5010) | VP IR / Legal / Finance | Company Secretary | Continuous + periodic |
| 3 | Reconcile & assure: reconcile the register/ledger to the equity accounts (VS-17) and intercompany/group structure (VS-72), set record dates (links W5020 ASHM, W5027 dividend), and assure (VS-21); manage discrepancies as ownership-record risk | Company Secretary / Finance / Audit | Company Secretary / CFO | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Share-register/cap-table system; equity accounts (VS-17); intercompany/group (VS-72); legal (VS-100); governance (VS-36); audit (VS-21); ownership intelligence (W5010)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Register-error risk**: Inaccurate share register → invalid votes/dividends/ownership disputes; mitigated by reconciliation & controls
- **Dilution-blindness risk**: Untracked options/convertibles diluting cap table; mitigated by cap-table admin
- **Multi-entity-complexity risk**: 5-entity cap table inconsistencies (links VS-72); mitigated by group reconciliation

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous/periodic; ~reconciliation per close

### Automation Opportunity
- Cap-table automation; register-to-equity-account reconciliation; record-date automation; dilution modeling

### Controls
- CTL-751 (ensure controlled execution — Shareholder Services, Equity Records, Dividend & IR Analytics (PA-173.3).)
- CTL: register/ledger legally maintained; reconciled to equity accounts (VS-17) & group (VS-72); record dates set; audited (VS-21)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-36 (company secretary), VS-100 (legal), VS-17 (equity accounts), VS-72 (group/intercompany), VS-21 (audit), VS-102/W3324 (LTI/options), W5010 (ownership), W5020 (ASHM record date), W5023 (beneficial ownership), W5027 (dividend record date)
