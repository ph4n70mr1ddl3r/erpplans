## W1974. Subscription Service Product Definition & Pricing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New subscription service product development |
| **Frequency** | ~2–4 new subscription products/year |
| **Volume** | Target: 5–8 subscription service products |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, Category Manager, Pricing Analyst, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Store Operations defines subscription service product: (a) service type: (i) pest control (quarterly visits, PHP 3,000–8,000/year), (ii) aircon cleaning/maintenance (bi-annual, PHP 2,500–5,000/year), (iii) water heater maintenance (annual, PHP 1,500–3,000/year), (iv) generator servicing (semi-annual, PHP 3,000–6,000/year), (v) plumbing check-up (annual, PHP 2,000–4,000/year), (vi) electrical safety inspection (annual, PHP 2,000–4,000/year), (b) service tier: basic (inspection only), standard (inspection + minor repair), premium (inspection + all repairs + parts), (c) service frequency: annual, semi-annual, quarterly, (d) geographic coverage: initially Metro Manila + Davao; expand to all 200 store catchment areas | VP Store Operations | COO | 1–2 weeks/product |
| 2 | Pricing Analyst develops subscription pricing: (a) cost model: service partner cost + BuildRight margin + platform fee, (b) competitive benchmarking: market rates for equivalent services in Philippines, (c) subscription discount: 10–20% below a-la-carte pricing to incentivize subscription, (d) annual vs. monthly billing: annual upfront (preferred — higher cash collection) with monthly payment option, (e) target margin: 25–35% per service visit | Pricing Analyst | CFO | 3–5 days/product |
| 3 | CFO approves pricing and revenue model: (a) deferred revenue treatment per PFRS 15 (customer pays upfront, revenue recognized per service visit), (b) breakage estimation for unused services (customers who don't schedule all visits), (c) annual margin contribution projection | CFO | COO | 1–2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Subscription product definition in service item master (W1974.1)
- Pricing configuration with tier structure (W1974.2)
- Integration with MDM-015 (service item governance) for product setup

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Service quality dependency**: BuildRight's brand reputation rides on service partner quality; poor service execution damages subscription retention


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "VP Store Operations defines subscription service product" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Pricing Analyst develops subscription pricing" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-schedule of "schedule all visits" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-382 (ensure controlled execution — Subscription Product Design & Setup (PA-47.1).)
- operational: VP Store Operations review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks per new subscription product

## W1976. Subscription SKU & Service Item Master Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Subscription product approved per W1974 |
| **Frequency** | Per new subscription product |
| **Volume** | ~5–8 subscription service items |
| **Owner** | IT Analyst |
| **Participants** | IT Analyst, VP Store Operations, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Analyst creates subscription service items in item master: (a) service SKU with subscription indicator, (b) pricing: annual subscription price, per-visit service cost (internal), (c) revenue recognition: deferred revenue model — customer pays annual fee upfront, revenue recognized per visit completed, (d) billing schedule: annual upfront or monthly recurring, (e) service frequency: number of visits per subscription period, (f) service duration: estimated hours per visit | IT Analyst | VP Store Operations | 1 day/product |
| 2 | Accounting Manager configures financial posting: (a) customer payment: debit cash, credit deferred revenue (liability), (b) per service visit completed: debit deferred revenue, credit service revenue + output VAT, (c) service partner cost: debit COGS, credit AP (service partner), (d) unused service (breakage): after subscription period expires, remaining deferred revenue recognized as breakage income | Accounting Manager | CFO | 1 day/product |
| 3 | POS configuration: (a) subscription enrollment at POS: scan service item, collect annual fee, print enrollment confirmation with service schedule, (b) online enrollment: ecommerce integration per ECOM-008, (c) loyalty points: subscription purchase earns points per standard rules | IT Analyst | VP Store Operations | 1 day/product |

### System Touchpoints
- Service item master creation per MDM-015 (W1976.1)
- Deferred revenue configuration (W1976.2)
- POS subscription enrollment (W1976.3)
- Integration with W282 (subscription billing) for recurring billing mechanics

### Pain Points / Risks
- **System complexity**: Subscription billing with deferred revenue recognition is more complex than standard POS selling; requires careful financial configuration


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-create of "creates subscription service items in item master" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Accounting Manager configures financial posting" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-scan of "scan service item, collect annual fee, print enrollment confirmation with…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-192 (ensure subscription billing & dunning integrity.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 3 days per subscription product

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | POS enrollment flow: (a) Sales Associate identifies subscription opportunity during customer interaction (e.g., customer buying aircon → offer maintenance subscription), (b) scan subscription service SKU at POS, (c) system prompts: (i) customer details (link to loyalty account or new enrollment), (ii) service address, (iii) preferred visit schedule, (iv) payment: annual upfront or monthly, (d) customer signs subscription agreement (digital or paper), (e) payment collected, (f) confirmation with first visit date sent via SMS/email | VP Store Operations / IT Analyst | COO | 1 week design |

## W1978. Subscription Revenue Model & Deferred Revenue Configuration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Subscription product financial setup |
| **Frequency** | Per product setup; quarterly review |
| **Volume** | ~5–8 subscription products |
| **Owner** | Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Accounting Manager, CFO, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accounting Manager configures deferred revenue model per PFRS 15: (a) contract liability setup: customer payment → deferred revenue (liability), (b) revenue recognition trigger: service visit completed → deferred revenue → service revenue, (c) VAT treatment: output VAT recognized at service completion (not at payment), (d) breakage: unperformed services after subscription expiry → breakage income, (e) refund: pro-rata refund if customer cancels — deferred revenue reduced, cash refunded | Accounting Manager | CFO | 1–2 days/product |
| 2 | Monthly: Accounting Manager reviews deferred revenue schedule: (a) total deferred revenue balance, (b) deferred revenue by subscription product, (c) revenue recognized in month (service visits completed), (d) new enrollments added to deferred revenue, (e) breakage recognized (subscriptions expired with unused visits) | Accounting Manager | CFO | 1 day/month |
| 3 | Quarterly: CFO reviews subscription financials with COO: (a) subscription revenue growth, (b) deferred revenue trend, (c) breakage rate (target: < 10% of deferred revenue), (d) subscription margin vs. target | CFO | COO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Deferred revenue configuration in financial module (W1978.1)
- Monthly deferred revenue schedule (W1978.2)
- Integration with W487 (revenue recognition review) for PFRS 15 compliance

### Pain Points / Risks
- **PFRS 15 complexity**: Subscription revenue recognition requires careful configuration; errors lead to audit findings


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Accounting Manager configures deferred revenue model per PFRS 15" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews deferred revenue schedule" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-382 (ensure controlled execution — Subscription Product Design & Setup (PA-47.1).)
- operational: Accounting Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 days setup per product; 1 day/month review

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
