## W1990. Service Visit Scheduling & Dispatch Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Subscription enrollment; scheduled service visit due |
| **Frequency** | ~2,000–5,000 service visits/month (projected) |
| **Volume** | ~5,000–10,000 subscribers × 2–4 visits/year |
| **Owner** | Service Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Service Coordinator, Service Partner, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates service visit schedule: (a) based on subscription frequency: quarterly, semi-annual, annual, (b) customer's preferred visit window (from enrollment), (c) service partner availability calendar, (d) geographic optimization: batch visits by area to minimize partner travel time | System | Service Coordinator | Automated |
| 2 | Service Coordinator reviews and confirms schedule: (a) verify partner availability, (b) check customer communication preferences (SMS/email/app), (c) send visit confirmation to customer: date, time window (e.g., 9 AM–12 PM), partner name, (d) send dispatch order to service partner: customer address, service type, tier, special instructions | Service Coordinator | VP Store Operations | 1–2 hours/day |
| 3 | Day-of coordination: (a) partner confirms arrival estimate via app, (b) system sends "technician on the way" notification to customer with ETA, (c) if partner reports delay: reschedule notification to customer | Service Coordinator / System | VP Store Operations | Per visit |

### System Touchpoints
- Automated service visit scheduling (W1990.1)
- Service partner dispatch portal (W1990.2)
- Integration with W544 (POS service work order) for service order creation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scheduling conflicts**: Customer not available at scheduled time; allow easy rescheduling via app/SMS


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "generates service visit schedule" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews and confirms schedule" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-send of "sends "technician on the way" notification to customer with ETA," (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-384 (ensure controlled execution — Service Fulfillment Quality (PA-47.3).)
operational: Service Coordinator review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 hours/day for coordinator; scheduling automated

| **Volume** | ~200–500 quality audits/month |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, Quality Inspector, Service Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System calculates per-visit quality score: (a) customer rating (1–5 stars from W1991.3): weight 40%, (b) service report completeness (all fields filled, photos attached): weight 20%, (c) on-time arrival (within scheduled window): weight 20%, (d) customer complaint (if any): weight 20% (complaint = 0, no complaint = full score) | System | VP Store Operations | Automated |
| 2 | Random quality audit (~10% of visits): (a) VP Store Operations selects random sample, (b) Quality Inspector calls customer for feedback: (i) was technician professional?, (ii) was work completed to satisfaction?, (iii) would you recommend BuildRight services?, (c) review service report and photos for quality standards compliance | Quality Inspector | VP Store Operations | 15–20 min/audit |
| 3 | Monthly partner scorecard: (a) average quality score by partner, (b) customer rating trend, (c) complaint rate, (d) on-time rate, (e) share with partner for continuous improvement | VP Store Operations | COO | 1 day/month |

- Automated quality scoring (W1993.1)
- Partner scorecard dashboard (W1993.3)
- Integration with W44 (vendor scorecard) for consolidated partner tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Audit resource**: Quality calls to 200–500 customers/month require dedicated Quality Inspector or outsourcing


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-calculate of "calculates per-visit quality score" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review service report and photos for quality standards compliance" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-384 (ensure controlled execution — Service Fulfillment Quality (PA-47.3).)
operational: VP Store Operations review and approval gate

| **Volume** | Per complaint |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Rep |
| **Participants** | Customer Service Rep, Service Coordinator, Service Partner, VP Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer reports service complaint: (a) channel: phone, email, app, in-store, (b) complaint categories: (i) technician no-show or late, (ii) incomplete service, (iii) property damage during service, (iv) rude/unprofessional technician, (v) billing issue, (c) severity: minor (inconvenience), major (property damage, safety issue) | Customer Service Rep | VP Store Operations | 10–15 min |
| 2 | Service Coordinator investigates: (a) review service report and photos, (b) contact service partner for their version, (c) for property damage: arrange inspection and repair, (d) for incomplete service: schedule revisit within 48 hours | Service Coordinator | VP Store Operations | 30–60 min |

| 4 | Partner accountability: (a) repeated complaints from same partner: escalation meeting, (b) 3+ complaints in 30 days: temporary suspension of new visits, (c) unresolved major complaint: partner termination per W1975 agreement | VP Store Operations | COO | As needed |

- Partner penalty tracking (W1994.4)

### Controls
- CTL-384 (ensure controlled execution — Service Fulfillment Quality (PA-47.3).)

## W1996. Service Visit No-Show & Rescheduling Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer or partner no-show for scheduled visit |
| **Frequency** | ~100–200 no-shows/month (projected ~5–10% rate) |
| **Volume** | Per occurrence |
| **Owner** | Service Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Service Coordinator, Customer, Service Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer no-show: (a) technician arrives at scheduled time, customer not available, (b) technician waits 15 minutes, attempts customer contact, (c) if no response: mark as customer no-show, (d) system logs no-show with timestamp, (e) reschedule: system sends rescheduling link to customer | Service Partner / System | Service Coordinator | 15–30 min |
| 2 | Partner no-show: (a) partner fails to arrive within scheduled window, (b) system detects: no GPS check-in from partner app within 30 min of scheduled start, (c) Service Coordinator contacts partner immediately, (d) if partner cannot come: emergency dispatch from backup partner or reschedule customer, (e) partner no-show penalty: PHP 500–1,000 deduction from partner payment per no-show | Service Coordinator | VP Store Operations | 30–60 min |
| 3 | Rescheduling: (a) system offers 3 alternative time slots, (b) customer selects preferred slot, (c) partner notified of rescheduled visit, (d) if rescheduling within same billing period: no impact on revenue recognition, (e) if rescheduling pushes visit to next billing period: deferred revenue timing adjusted | Service Coordinator / System | VP Store Operations | 5–10 min |

### System Touchpoints
- GPS-based no-show detection (W1996.2)
- Automated rescheduling workflow (W1996.3)
- Integration with W1990 (scheduling) for reschedule

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Customer frustration**: Partner no-shows are highly damaging to subscription satisfaction; maintain strict SLA and backup partner availability


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs no-show with timestamp," (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "check-in from partner app within 30 min of scheduled start," (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-schedule of "scheduled visit," (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-384 (ensure controlled execution — Service Fulfillment Quality (PA-47.3).)
operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 15–60 min per no-show incident

## W1997. Subscription Service Annual Program Review & Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual subscription program review |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | Consolidated program review |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, CMO, CFO, COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Store Operations compiles annual program review: (a) subscriber count: target vs. actual, (b) revenue: subscription revenue + deferred revenue balance, (c) margin: actual vs. target (25–35%), (d) retention rate: % of subscribers renewed, (e) churn analysis: why are customers leaving?, (f) service quality: average customer rating, complaint rate, partner performance, (g) enrollment funnel: awareness → inquiry → enrollment conversion rate | VP Store Operations | COO | 2–3 days |
| 2 | CFO reviews financial performance: (a) deferred revenue as % of total revenue, (b) breakage rate: % of subscribers not using all visits, (c) partner cost trend: are partner costs increasing?, (d) cash flow impact: annual upfront payments improve cash flow; monthly payments create receivables, (e) ROI of subscription program: incremental profit vs. investment | CFO | COO | 1 day |
| 3 | Strategic decisions: (a) expand: add new service types or territories, (b) optimize: adjust pricing, tiers, partner network, (c) sunset: discontinue underperforming subscription products, (d) invest: allocate budget for Year 2 subscriber growth target | COO | CEO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Subscription program analytics (W1997.1)
- Integration with W35 (executive reporting) for board presentation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Program sustainability**: If churn exceeds enrollment growth, subscription base shrinks; continuous improvement in service quality and value proposition essential


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-create of "create receivables," (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-allocate of "allocate budget for Year 2 subscriber growth target" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-384 (ensure controlled execution — Service Fulfillment Quality (PA-47.3).)
operational: VP Store Operations review and approval gate
