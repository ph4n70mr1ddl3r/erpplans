# VS-47: Subscription & Recurring Home Services

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages subscription-based and recurring home service offerings for BuildRight Depot. Covers service product design (pest control, HVAC maintenance, water heater maintenance, generator servicing), subscription enrollment at POS and online, recurring billing and renewal lifecycle, service fulfillment scheduling, and quality assurance. This value stream supports BuildRight's strategic initiative to generate predictable recurring revenue beyond one-time product sales, targeting the growing Philippine middle-class homeownership segment.
