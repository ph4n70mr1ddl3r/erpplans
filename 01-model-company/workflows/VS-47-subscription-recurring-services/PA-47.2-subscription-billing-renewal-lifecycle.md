## W1982. Subscription Monthly Billing & Payment Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly billing cycle for monthly-payment subscribers |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–5,000 monthly subscribers (projected Year 1) |
| **Owner** | AR Clerk |
| **Participants** | AR Clerk, System, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates monthly subscription charges: (a) for monthly-payment subscribers: auto-charge credit card or e-wallet on file, (b) billing date: 1st of month (or enrollment anniversary date), (c) amount: monthly subscription fee (annual fee ÷ 12), (d) GL posting: debit accounts receivable, credit deferred revenue | System | AR Clerk | Automated |
| 2 | Payment processing: (a) credit card: auto-charge via payment gateway, (b) GCash/Maya: auto-debit via partner API, (c) success: post cash receipt — debit cash, credit AR, (d) failure: trigger dunning per W1986 | System | AR Clerk | Automated |
| 3 | Monthly reconciliation: (a) AR Clerk reconciles subscription billing vs. payments received, (b) failed payments: total failed charges and follow-up actions, (c) deferred revenue: new enrollments added, revenue recognized from completed visits, net deferred revenue balance | AR Clerk | Accounting Manager | 2–3 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Automated monthly subscription billing (W1982.1)
- Payment gateway integration for auto-charge (W1982.2)
- Integration with W267 (digital payment reconciliation) for payment matching

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Payment failure rate**: Philippine credit card auto-charge failure rates can be 5–10% (expired cards, insufficient funds); robust dunning process needed


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-count (posting: debit accounts receivable, credit deferred)
- auto-post ((c) success: post cash receipt —)
- auto-reconcile (ar clerk reconciles subscription billing vs.)

### Controls
operational: periodic reconciliation against source documents

### Time Estimate
- Automated billing; 2–3 hours/month reconciliation

## W1984. Subscription Cancellation & Pro-Rata Refund Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer requests subscription cancellation |
| **Frequency** | ~30–50 cancellations/month (projected) |
| **Volume** | Target churn rate: < 30% annually |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Rep |
| **Participants** | Customer Service Rep, AR Clerk, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer requests cancellation: (a) channel: in-store, phone, email, online, (b) Customer Service Rep verifies identity and subscription details, (c) cancellation reason captured (mandatory): (i) service quality, (ii) price, (iii) no longer needed, (iv) moving, (v) competitor switch, (vi) other | Customer Service Rep | VP Store Operations | 10–15 min |
| 2 | Pro-rata refund calculation: (a) annual subscription: refund = (remaining unused months ÷ 12) × annual fee, less: (i) visits already completed at a-la-carte rate (if a-la-carte rate > subscription pro-rata rate), (ii) cancellation fee per subscription agreement (if applicable), (iii) promotional discount clawback (if customer received enrollment discount), (b) monthly subscription: cancel next billing cycle; no mid-month refund | System / AR Clerk | Accounting Manager | 5–10 min |
| 3 | Refund processing: (a) AR Clerk processes refund per W101 (customer refund & credit processing), (b) refund method: same as original payment (credit card → credit card refund; GCash → GCash refund; cash at POS → cash refund), (c) reverse deferred revenue for unused portion, (d) cancellation confirmation sent to customer via email | AR Clerk | Accounting Manager | 10–15 min |
| 4 | Retention attempt: (a) before processing cancellation, Customer Service Rep offers retention options: (i) service credit for next visit, (ii) temporary pause instead of cancellation, (iii) downgrade to lower tier, (b) track retention attempt outcome | Customer Service Rep | VP Store Operations | 5–10 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Cancellation request with reason capture (W1984.1)
- Pro-rata refund auto-calculation (W1984.2)
- Integration with W101 (customer refund) for refund processing

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Refund disputes**: Customers may dispute refund calculation (especially a-la-carte rate vs. subscription rate for completed visits); clear subscription terms and transparent calculation essential


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-request (customer requests cancellation: (a) channel:)
- auto-count ((iii) promotional discount clawback (if customer)
- auto-track (tier, (b) track retention attempt outcome)

### Controls
operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 15–30 min per cancellation

## W1986. Subscription Payment Failure & Dunning Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly auto-charge payment failure |
| **Frequency** | ~200–500 failed payments/month (5–10% failure rate projected) |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–5,000 monthly-billed subscribers |
| **Owner** | AR Clerk |
| **Participants** | AR Clerk, Customer Service Rep, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System detects payment failure: (a) credit card declined, (b) e-wallet insufficient balance, (c) bank account issue, (d) system tags subscription as "payment due" | System | AR Clerk | Automated |
| 2 | Dunning sequence: (a) Day 1: SMS notification — "Your payment didn't go through. Please update your payment method.", (b) Day 3: email with payment update link, (c) Day 7: customer service call, (d) Day 14: second call + final notice, (e) Day 30: subscription suspended (service visits paused) | System / Customer Service Rep | AR Clerk | Automated + manual calls |
| 3 | Payment recovery: (a) customer updates payment method (online or in-store), (b) system retries charge, (c) if successful: reactivate subscription, clear overdue status, (d) if customer unresponsive after 60 days: auto-cancel subscription per W1984 (no refund — subscription was partially used) | AR Clerk / System | Accounting Manager | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Automated dunning notification sequence (W1986.2)
- Payment method update self-service (W1986.3)
- Integration with W108 (collection escalation) for escalation path

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Service interruption**: Suspending service visits due to payment failure may lead to customer churn; balance firm collection with retention


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-count ((c) bank account issue, (d) system)
- auto-update (through. please update your payment method.",)

### Controls
operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- Automated dunning; 10–15 min per manual call

| **Frequency** | ~20–50 pause requests/month |
| **Volume** | ~5–10% of subscribers may pause per year |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Rep |
| **Participants** | Customer Service Rep, AR Clerk, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer requests pause: (a) reason: travel, renovation, temporary move, financial difficulty, (b) pause duration: 1–6 months maximum, (c) Customer Service Rep verifies subscription is in good standing | Customer Service Rep | — | 10 min |
| 2 | System processes pause: (a) pause start date and end date recorded, (b) monthly billing suspended during pause period, (c) annual subscription: pause extends subscription expiry by pause duration, (d) service visits cancelled during pause, (e) customer notified of pause confirmation with reactivation date | System | AR Clerk | Automated |
| 3 | Reactivation: (a) system sends reactivation reminder 7 days before pause end date, (b) customer confirms reactivation (or requests extension), (c) billing resumes, (d) next service visit scheduled, (e) if customer does not respond: auto-reactivate after 14 days grace period | System / Customer Service Rep | AR Clerk | Automated + 10 min manual |

- auto-send ((a) system sends reactivation reminder 7)

### Controls
operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

| **Participants** | CMO, System, Customer Service Rep |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System sends automated communications at lifecycle events: (a) enrollment confirmation with welcome guide, (b) service visit reminder: 48 hours and 2 hours before scheduled visit, (c) service completion confirmation with satisfaction survey, (d) renewal notice: 30/14/7 days before expiry, (e) payment confirmation (or failure notice), (f) subscription anniversary: thank you + loyalty points bonus | System | CMO | Automated |

- **Communication fatigue**: Too many messages lead to opt-outs; limit to essential lifecycle communications + 1 promotional message per month


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-send (system sends automated communications at)
- auto-review (quarterly: cmo reviews communication effectiveness: (a))

### Controls
operational: CMO review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
