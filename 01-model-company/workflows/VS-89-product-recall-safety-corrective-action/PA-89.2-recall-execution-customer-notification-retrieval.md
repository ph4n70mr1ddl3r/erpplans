## W3001. Multi-Channel Customer Recall Notice & Public Communication

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall execution start |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | Reaches up to 600,000 loyalty members + ecommerce buyers (segmented to affected) |
| **Owner** | Marketing Communications Manager |
| **Participants** | Comms, Product Safety, Legal, Customer Service, Loyalty/CRM, DPO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Comms/Product Safety draft customer recall notice (plain-language hazard, affected SKU/lot, remedy, contact channel); Legal reviews; DPO approves use of customer contact data and lawful basis | Comms / Product Safety | Marketing Communications Manager | <48 hr |
| 2 | Marketing/CRM distributes notice via targeted channels: email/SMS to affected loyalty members and ecommerce buyers, in-app push (VS-75), website banner, social media, and press release for high-severity | Marketing / CRM / Loyalty | Marketing Communications Manager | Per recall |
| 3 | Customer Service staffs dedicated recall hotline/chat; logs inquiries; routes remedy requests; escalates safety incidents; closes loop with Product Safety | Customer Service | Customer Service Manager | Through recall |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM/loyalty segment & broadcast (links to VS-13); ecommerce customer messaging (links to VS-10); recall case logging

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reach risk**: Notice fails to reach affected customers (no email, guest checkout); mitigated by multi-channel approach and public notice for high-severity

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 days to launch; ongoing

## W3002. Store-Level Recall Execution, POS Block & Shelf Removal

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall stop-sale (W3000) and customer notice |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event, across 200 stores |
| **Volume** | 200 stores execute concurrently |
| **Owner** | Store Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Ops, Store Mgr/Staff, LP, Inventory Control |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store Ops pushes recall task to all affected stores via store communication/task system (links to VS-63) with SKU/lot, photos, shelf location, and action deadline | Store Ops | Store Operations Manager | <4 hr |
| 2 | Store staff locate and remove affected stock from shelf/yard/backroom; quarantine with recall label; confirm POS block active; complete task with photo proof | Store Staff | Store Manager | <24 hr |
| 3 | LP/Store Ops audits a sample of stores for execution completeness; re-trains non-compliant stores; reports exceptions to Recall Committee | LP / Store Ops | Store Operations Manager | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Store task/compliance system (links to VS-63); POS block; inventory quarantine; task photo proof

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Execution risk**: Missed units on shelf or in outdoor lumber yard; mitigated by photo-proof task completion and LP audit sampling

### Time Estimate
- <24 hr chain-wide; ongoing audit

## W3003. Ecommerce & Marketplace Recall Handling (Listing Block, Buyer Notify)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall stop-sale (W3000) |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | Ecommerce + marketplace channels (VS-10, VS-65) |
| **Owner** | Ecommerce Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Ecommerce Ops, Marketplace Mgr, IT, Customer Service |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ecommerce Ops/IT suspends product listing on web/app and all marketplace channels (Shopee/Lazada/etc.); cancels pending unshipped orders; prevents new orders | Ecommerce Ops / IT | Ecommerce Operations Manager | <4 hr |
| 2 | Ecommerce Ops notifies all buyers of affected SKU/lot within the recall window (even if order delivered); provides remedy and return instructions; updates order records | Ecommerce Ops / Customer Service | Ecommerce Operations Manager | Per recall |
| 3 | Marketplace Mgr files marketplace-platform recall notices and complies with channel-specific recall takedown procedures; reconciles platform settlement | Marketplace Manager | Ecommerce Operations Manager | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce catalog/listing control (links to VS-10); marketplace integration (links to VS-65); order recall flag

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Channel risk**: Listing remains live on a marketplace; mitigated by automated multi-channel listing block and platform-level notices

### Time Estimate
- <24 hr to execute across channels

## W3004. Customer Return, Refund, Repair & Replacement Processing (Recall)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer presents recalled product |
| **Frequency** | Continuous through recall |
| **Volume** | Proportional to units in-field and recovery % |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Manager |
| **Participants** | Customer Service, Store Ops, Finance, AR |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Service/Store receives returned/recalled product; verifies SKU/lot against recall scope; logs to recall case with customer identity (privacy-minimized) | Customer Service / Store | Customer Service Manager | Per return |
| 2 | Finance processes remedy per recall matrix: cash refund (BIR-compliant), store credit, replacement, or repair booking; processes refund via original tender or store credit per W101 | Finance / AR | Finance Manager | Per return |
| 3 | Store/Customer Service quarantines returned unit to recall bin; updates recall recovery count; closes customer loop with confirmation | Store / Customer Service | Customer Service Manager | Per return |

### System Touchpoints
- Recall return/remedy workflow; refund/credit (links to W101); recall case update; quarantine inventory

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Remedy risk**: Inconsistent remedy application causes customer disputes; mitigated by pre-defined remedy matrix and system-enforced options

### Time Estimate
- 5–15 min per return

## W3005. Recall Product Retrieval Logistics & Reverse Consolidation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarantined recall stock accumulates at stores/DCs |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | Reverse flows from 200 stores + ecommerce to consolidation point |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | Logistics, DC Ops, Store Ops, 3PL |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics plans reverse consolidation: store→DC or store→vendor/destroyer routing; schedules pickups leveraging existing fleet/3PL (links to VS-06/VS-56) | Logistics | Logistics Manager | Per recall |
| 2 | DC Ops receives and tallies consolidated recall stock against per-store quarantine counts; reconciles variances; secures pending disposition | DC Ops | DC Operations Manager | Per receipt |
| 3 | Logistics documents chain-of-custody for regulator/insurance evidence; supports vendor return, salvage, or destruction routing per W3010 | Logistics | Logistics Manager | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Reverse logistics order (links to VS-32); WMS quarantine tally; chain-of-custody log

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Accountability risk**: Loss of chain-of-custody undermines regulator/insurance evidence; mitigated by serialized tracking and reconciliation

### Time Estimate
- Through recall duration

## W3006. Trade / B2B Account Recall Outreach & Bulk Recovery

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall affects B2B/trade/government delivered goods |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | Up to ~5,000 trade accounts + ~200 corporate/government accounts (segmented) |
| **Owner** | Trade Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Account Mgr, Sales, Project Mgmt, Customer Service |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Account Mgr/Sales identifies B2B/trade/government accounts that purchased affected SKU/lot; segments by jobsite/installation status | Trade Account Mgr / Sales | Trade Sales Manager | Per recall |
| 2 | Account Mgr contacts affected accounts directly; arranges bulk recovery, on-site replacement, or contractor notification for installed goods; coordinates remedy | Trade Account Mgr | Trade Sales Manager | Per recall |
| 3 | Customer Service/Project Mgmt logs recovery per account; handles project retention/warranty implications and milestone-billing adjustments where relevant | Customer Service / Project Mgmt | Trade Sales Manager | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- B2B account/order history (links to VS-11/VS-43); recall case linkage; project billing adjustment (links to VS-40)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Installed-goods risk**: Recalled product already installed on jobsite; mitigated by account-level outreach and on-site remedy coordination

### Time Estimate
- Through recall duration

## W3007. Field / Job Site Recall Recovery for Delivered Goods

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recalled goods delivered to customer/construction job site |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | Subset of recall with DSD/delivery |
| **Owner** | Field Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Field Ops, Logistics, Contractor Jobsite Delivery (VS-74), Customer Service |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Field Ops/Logistics identifies deliveries (DSD, contractor jobsite, ecommerce delivery) of affected SKU/lot; plans field recovery visits | Field Ops / Logistics | Field Operations Manager | Per recall |
| 2 | Field Ops coordinates jobsite access (links to VS-74), recovers or replaces product on-site, documents condition and quantity recovered | Field Ops | Field Operations Manager | Per visit |
| 3 | Field Ops/Customer Service reconciles field recovery with recall case; handles partial-recovery cases and customer disputes | Field Ops / Customer Service | Field Operations Manager | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Delivery/job-site records (links to VS-74); recall case; field service mobile app

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Access/dispute risk**: Customer refuses access or disputes ownership; mitigated by pre-coordination and documented remedy offer

### Time Estimate
- Per field visit; through recall

## W3008. Recall Progress Tracking, Daily Status Reporting & Effectiveness Measurement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall execution ongoing |
| **Frequency** | Daily during active recall |
| **Volume** | One dashboard per active recall |
| **Owner** | Product Safety & Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Product Safety Mgr, Data Analytics, Recall Committee |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data Analytics/Product Safety maintain recall dashboard: units in-field, units recovered (by channel/store), notification reach %, remedy mix, cost-to-date | Data Analytics / Product Safety | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Product Safety reports daily status to Recall Committee; flags under-performing stores/channels; triggers corrective follow-up | Product Safety | General Counsel | Daily |
| 3 | Product Safety measures effectiveness vs. targets (W2999); documents evidence package for regulator close-out and insurance | Product Safety | Regulatory Compliance Manager | Through recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Recall analytics/dashboard (links to VS-28); effectiveness evidence repository

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Visibility risk**: Slow recovery goes undetected; mitigated by daily dashboard and effectiveness targets

### Time Estimate
- Daily through recall
