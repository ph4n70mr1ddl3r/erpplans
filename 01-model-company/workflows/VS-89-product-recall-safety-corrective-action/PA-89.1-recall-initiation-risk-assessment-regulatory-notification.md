## W2993. Product Safety Issue Intake, Triage & Hazard Classification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Safety complaint, regulator alert, vendor notice, or field incident |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven (~30–60 candidate issues/year) |
| **Volume** | ~30–60 issues triaged; ~3–8 escalate to formal recall |
| **Owner** | Product Safety & Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Product Safety Mgr, Quality, Customer Service, Vendor Mgmt, Legal, Store Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Service / Store Ops / Quality logs safety issue in recall case system (source, SKU, lot, incident description, photos, injury/property damage flag) | Customer Service / Store Ops / Quality | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Per issue (<24 hr) |
| 2 | Product Safety Mgr triages and classifies hazard (electrical/fire, chemical/toxic, mechanical/laceration, structural/failure, labeling/allergen) and assigns preliminary severity (Class I–III per Consumer Act / DTI-BPS) | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Quality Manager | Per issue |
| 3 | Quality/Vendor Mgmt confirms affected SKU, lot, vendor, and whether the issue is isolated or systemic; duplicates against existing open recalls | Quality / Vendor Mgmt | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Per issue |

### System Touchpoints
- Recall case management; quality non-conformance module; item/lot/serial master; vendor master linkage

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Detection risk**: Safety issues lost in generic complaint queues; mitigated by mandatory hazard-classification field and dedicated triage queue


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs safety issue in recall case system" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-assign of "assigns preliminary severity" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-issue of "issue is isolated or systemic" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: mandatory hazard-classification field and dedicated triage queue; operational: hazard-classification field and dedicated triage queue

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 hours per issue

## W2994. Recall Risk Assessment & Corrective Action Decision

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Triaged issue flagged Class I/II or systemic |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~8–15 risk assessments/year |
| **Owner** | Product Safety & Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Product Safety Mgr, Quality, Legal, Merchandising, Insurance, DPO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Safety Mgr performs risk assessment: probability of harm × severity × exposure (units in market, time since sale); documents likelihood of regulatory mandatory action | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Quality Manager | 1–3 days |
| 2 | Legal/Insurance assesses liability, product-liability exposure, and policy notification triggers; DPO screens affected-customer data needed for notification | Legal / Insurance / DPO | General Counsel | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Recall Committee reviews assessment; decides corrective action type (full recall, stop-sale, field repair, consumer notice, no-action); documents rationale and decision authority | Recall Committee | General Counsel / COO | Per decision |

### System Touchpoints
- Risk assessment template; decision/authority log; document repository

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-reaction risk**: Classifying a serious hazard as lower-tier; mitigated by documented severity matrix and committee review


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "documents likelihood of regulatory mandatory action" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews assessment" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: documented severity matrix and committee review

### Time Estimate
- 2–5 days

## W2995. DTI-BPS / FDA Regulatory Notification & Filing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall decision confirmed (Class I/II) |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | ~3–8 recalls/year |
| **Owner** | Regulatory Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Regulatory Officer, Product Safety Mgr, Legal, Tax/Records |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Regulatory Compliance Officer prepares and files notification with DTI-BPS (consumer product safety) and/or FDA (regulated goods), within mandated timelines, including product, hazard, scope, and remedy | Regulatory Compliance Officer | Regulatory Compliance Manager | Per filing (<72 hr for mandatory) |
| 2 | Legal/Regulatory notifies relevant LGUs and, for imports, Bureau of Customs and country-of-origin regulators; confirms any PS/ICC mark suspension | Legal / Regulatory | Regulatory Compliance Manager | Per recall |
| 3 | Records Mgr retains regulatory correspondence and filings per BIR/SEC 10-year and Consumer Act retention rules | Records Manager | Regulatory Compliance Manager | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Regulatory filing portal integration; records retention; compliance calendar

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Late or incomplete regulator notification triggers penalties; mitigated by compliance calendar and pre-cleared templates


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "prepares and files notification with DTI-BPS" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "country-of-origin regulators" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "Records Mgr retains regulatory correspondence and filings per BIR/SEC 10-year…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: compliance calendar and pre-cleared templates

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 days

## W2996. Recall Strategy, Scope & Lot/Shipment Traceability Definition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall decision + regulatory notification filed |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | ~3–8 recalls/year |
| **Owner** | Supply Chain Planning Manager |
| **Participants** | Supply Planning, Quality, Merchandising, IT, Store Ops, Ecommerce |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Supply Planning defines recall scope: affected SKU(s), lots/batches, date ranges, receiving locations (DCs, stores), and sales channel exposure (in-store, ecommerce, marketplace, B2B) | Supply Planning | Supply Chain Planning Manager | Per recall |
| 2 | IT/Supply Planning traces affected lots/serials through inventory and sales history (DC receipts, store transfers, POS sales, ecommerce orders, trade/gov deliveries) to quantify units in-field vs. on-hand | IT / Supply Planning | Supply Chain Planning Manager | Per recall |
| 3 | Merchandising/Supply Planning sets recall strategy: full retrieval, consumer replacement, refund, or repair; defines remedy matrix and recovery logistics | Merchandising / Supply Planning | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Lot/serial/shipment traceability; inventory & sales history; omnichannel order history; recall scope record

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scope risk**: Incomplete lot/trace data leaves affected units in market; mitigated by full chain-of-custody traceability and IT-assisted queries


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logistics" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: full chain-of-custody traceability and IT-assisted queries

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 days

## W2997. Recall Committee Activation & Cross-Functional Command

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Confirmed Class I/II recall |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | ~3–8 activations/year |
| **Owner** | General Counsel |
| **Participants** | GC, COO, CMO, CIO, Product Safety, Quality, Supply Chain, LP, Finance, HR/Comms |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | General Counsel activates Recall Committee; confirms roles, decision authority, and cadence (daily war-room for high-severity); assigns incident commander | General Counsel | CEO / COO | Per activation (<24 hr) |
| 2 | Committee sets recall KPIs and status reporting: units recovered vs. in-field, % notification reach, customer contact rate, SLA to resolution | Recall Committee | General Counsel | Per recall |
| 3 | Comms/HR coordinates internal and external spokesperson, media holding statement, and store/DC staff briefing script | Comms / HR | CMO | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Incident command / war-room tooling; status dashboard; communication broadcast (links to VS-63)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Coordination risk**: Slow committee activation delays response; mitigated by pre-defined activation authority and standing committee roster


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-assign of "assigns incident commander" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-report of "reporting" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System workflow orchestration of "coordinates internal and external spokesperson, media holding statement, and…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: pre-defined activation authority and standing committee roster

### Time Estimate
- Activation <1 day; ongoing through recall

## W2998. Vendor / Manufacturer Coordination & Corrective Action Agreement

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall scope confirms vendor/manufacturer responsibility |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | ~3–8 vendor engagements/year |
| **Owner** | Vendor Management Manager |
| **Participants** | Vendor Mgmt, Merchandising, Legal, Quality, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vendor Mgmt formally notifies vendor/manufacturer of recall decision; demands corrective action, cost responsibility, and root-cause report | Vendor Mgmt | Vendor Management Manager | Per recall (<48 hr) |

- **Cost-recovery risk**: Vendor disputes liability; mitigated by purchase contract recall/indemnification clauses and documented evidence package


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-report of "report" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "logistics, reimbursement" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
operational: purchase contract recall/indemnification clauses and documented evidence package

### Time Estimate
- 1–4 weeks

## W2999. Recall Effectiveness Check Planning & Sample Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall execution underway |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | ~3–8 plans/year |
| **Owner** | Product Safety & Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Product Safety Mgr, Quality, Data Analytics, Store Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Safety Mgr defines effectiveness-check criteria (target recovery %, time-bound targets per regulator guidance) and sampling approach to verify customer reach | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Quality Manager | Per recall |
| 2 | Data Analytics/Store Ops designs audit sample (stores, channels, customer segments) and verifies POS block, shelf removal, and notification delivery | Data Analytics / Store Ops | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Per recall |
| 3 | Product Safety Mgr documents effectiveness results; identifies gaps; drives corrective follow-up before regulator close-out | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Regulatory Compliance Manager | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Recall effectiveness analytics; POS block verification report; sample design tool

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Effectiveness risk**: Recall declared complete while units remain in market; mitigated by quantitative effectiveness targets and independent sampling


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "check criteria" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System continuous audit of "audit sample" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "documents effectiveness results" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: quantitative effectiveness targets and independent sampling

### Time Estimate
- Through recall duration

## W3000. Stop-Sale, Quarantine & Inventory Hold Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall decision (or pre-decision precautionary hold) |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | ~3–8 holds/year affecting 200 stores + 4 DCs + ecommerce |
| **Owner** | Inventory Control Manager |
| **Participants** | Inventory Control, DC Ops, Store Ops, Ecommerce, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inventory Control/IT places system-wide stop-sale (POS block, ecommerce/marketplace listing suspension, PO/replenishment freeze) on affected SKU/lot across all locations | Inventory Control / IT | Inventory Control Manager | <4 hr |
| 2 | DC Ops/Store Ops physically quarantine on-hand stock to segregated recall bin/area; apply recall hold labels; prevent sale or shipment | DC Ops / Store Ops | DC Operations Manager / Store Manager | <24 hr |
| 3 | Inventory Control reconciles quarantined quantities vs. system stock; investigates variances; secures high-value/regulated goods under additional LP control | Inventory Control / Loss Prevention | Inventory Control Manager | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory hold/quarantine status; POS item block; ecommerce/marketplace listing control (links to VS-10/VS-65); WMS recall bin

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Leakage risk**: Stock sold after stop-sale decision; mitigated by immediate system-wide block before physical quarantine


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-reconcile of "reconciles quarantined quantities vs." (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: immediate system-wide block before physical quarantine

### Time Estimate
- <24 hr to execute chain-wide
