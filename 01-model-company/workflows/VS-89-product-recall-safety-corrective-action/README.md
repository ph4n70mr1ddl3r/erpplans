## Overview

Product Recall & Safety Corrective Action Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the end-to-end product safety recall lifecycle across 35,000 active SKUs, ~800–1,000
vendors, 200 stores, 4 DCs, and 5 legal entities, in compliance with the Philippine **Consumer
Act (RA 7394)**, **DTI-BPS** product certification (ICC/PS Mark) requirements, **FDA** oversight
of regulated items (paints/chemicals, electrical goods adjacent to consumer safety), and the
**Data Privacy Act** for affected-customer data used during notification.

While **VS-31** owns product quality and **VS-71** owns anti-counterfeit authentication, and
**W776** (in VS-09) handles the store-level customer-notification *execution* step, this value
stream owns the **recall program**: hazard intake, risk assessment, regulatory notification,
recall scope/strategy, customer communication orchestration, product retrieval, vendor
reimbursement, destruction, root-cause/CAPA, and post-recall surveillance — across physical and
ecommerce/marketplace channels and across B2C, trade, and government accounts.
