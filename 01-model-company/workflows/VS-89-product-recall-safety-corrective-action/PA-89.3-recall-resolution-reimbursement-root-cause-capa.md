## W3009. Vendor Reimbursement, Credit & Cost Recovery for Recalls

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall recovery and disposition complete |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | ~3–8 recoveries/year |
| **Owner** | Finance Manager (AP) |
| **Participants** | Finance/AP, Vendor Mgmt, Legal, Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance/AP compiles recall cost ledger: refunds/credits issued, retrieval logistics, destruction, communications, regulatory, and internal labor — by vendor/SKU | Finance / AP | Finance Manager | Per recall |
| 2 | Vendor Mgmt/Legal presents cost ledger to vendor under the corrective action agreement (W2998); negotiates credit memo, debit, or cash reimbursement | Vendor Mgmt / Legal | Vendor Management Manager | Per recall |
| 3 | Finance/AP posts vendor credit/debit memo; reconciles to recall case; tracks recovery aging; escalates disputes to Legal | Finance / AP | Finance Manager | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- AP credit/debit memo (links to W7/W100); recall cost ledger; vendor recovery aging (links to VS-67)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Recovery risk**: Vendor disputes or delays reimbursement; mitigated by contract indemnification clauses and documented cost ledger


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logistics, destruction, communications, regulatory, and internal labor — by…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-escalate of "escalates disputes to Legal" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-152 (ensure recall regulatory reporting & closure.)
- operational: contract indemnification clauses and documented cost ledger

### Time Estimate
- 2–8 weeks

## W3010. Recalled Product Destruction, Recycling & Disposal Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recalled stock dispositioned for destruction (not returnable/salvageable) |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | Proportional to recall |
| **Owner** | Quality Manager |
| **Participants** | Quality, Logistics, EHS, Records, Licensed Disposal Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quality/Logistics selects licensed destruction/disposal vendor; confirms method (incineration, crushing, certified recycling) appropriate to product and DENR/EHS rules (links to VS-24) | Quality / Logistics | Quality Manager | Per recall |
| 2 | Disposal Vendor destroys/recycles stock; issues certificate of destruction with quantities and serial/lot evidence; EHS verifies hazardous-goods handling | Disposal Vendor / EHS | Quality Manager | Per batch |
| 3 | Quality/Records retains destruction certificates and evidence in recall audit file; updates inventory write-off (W3022-equivalent) and removes quarantined stock | Quality / Records | Quality Manager | Per recall |

### System Touchpoints
- Disposal vendor record; certificate of destruction; inventory write-off (links to VS-05); EHS compliance (links to VS-24)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Diversion risk**: Recalled product re-enters market via disposal chain; mitigated by licensed vendor, certificate of destruction, and serialization


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics selects licensed destruction/disposal vendor" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-issue of "issues certificate of destruction with quantities and serial/lot evidence" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "Records retains destruction certificates and evidence in recall audit file" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-152 (ensure recall regulatory reporting & closure.)
- operational: licensed vendor, certificate of destruction, and serialization

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 weeks

## W3011. Recall Root Cause Analysis & CAPA (Corrective & Preventive Action)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall recovery substantially complete |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event |
| **Volume** | ~3–8 RCAs/year |
| **Owner** | Quality Manager |
| **Participants** | Quality, Vendor Mgmt, Merchandising, Engineering/Technical, Product Safety |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quality leads root cause analysis (5-Why / fishbone) with vendor/manufacturer and internal stakeholders; identifies design, process, supplier, or specification cause | Quality | Quality Manager | Per recall |
| 2 | Quality/Vendor Mgmt define CAPA: corrective (fix cause) and preventive (avoid recurrence) actions, owners, and deadlines; verify against DTI-BPS re-certification (W447) | Quality / Vendor Mgmt | Quality Manager | Per recall |
| 3 | Quality tracks CAPA to closure; verifies effectiveness; updates vendor scorecard (links to VS-67) and QA process controls | Quality | Quality Manager | Through closure |

### System Touchpoints
- CAPA / non-conformance module (links to VS-31); vendor scorecard (links to VS-67)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Recurrence risk**: Root cause not fully corrected; mitigated by formal CAPA tracking and effectiveness verification


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "verify against DTI-BPS re-certification" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "tracks CAPA to closure" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-498 (ensure controlled execution — Recall Resolution, Reimbursement & Root Cause / CAPA (PA-89.3).)
- operational: formal CAPA tracking and effectiveness verification

### Time Estimate
- 4–12 weeks

## W3012. Post-Recall Surveillance & Re-Occurrence Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall closed / CAPA implemented |
| **Frequency** | Defined surveillance window (3–12 months) |
| **Volume** | Per closed recall |
| **Owner** | Product Safety & Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Product Safety, Quality, Customer Service, Data Analytics |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Safety/Customer Service monitor incoming complaints/returns for the recalled SKU/lot and similar SKUs; watch for re-occurrence signals | Product Safety / Customer Service | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Through window |
| 2 | Quality/Product Safety verify CAPA effectiveness via targeted audits and field data; confirms corrected product requalified (W447) | Quality / Product Safety | Quality Manager | Through window |
| 3 | Product Safety reports surveillance outcome to Recall Committee and regulator where required; closes or re-opens recall | Product Safety | VP Legal & Compliance | At window end |

### System Touchpoints
- Complaint/return trend analytics (links to VS-28/VS-32); CAPA effectiveness tracker

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Recurrence risk**: Recurrence missed due to alerting gaps; mitigated by automated SKU/lot complaint-rate alerts


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitor incoming complaints/returns for the recalled SKU/lot and similar SKUs" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "verify CAPA effectiveness via targeted audits and field data" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "reports surveillance outcome to Recall Committee and regulator where required" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-498 (ensure controlled execution — Recall Resolution, Reimbursement & Root Cause / CAPA (PA-89.3).)
- operational: automated SKU/lot complaint-rate alerts

### Time Estimate
- Through surveillance window

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Regulatory Officer compiles close-out report: scope, units recovered, effectiveness %, disposition, CAPA status; submits to DTI-BPS/FDA per their termination process | Regulatory Compliance Officer | Regulatory Compliance Manager | Per recall |

### Controls
- CTL-498 (ensure controlled execution — Recall Resolution, Reimbursement & Root Cause / CAPA (PA-89.3).)
- operational: formal regulator acknowledgment requirement

### Time Estimate

## W3016. Recall Lessons Learned, Process & Quality System Improvement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recall closed and surveillance complete |
| **Frequency** | Per recall event + quarterly portfolio review |
| **Volume** | ~3–8 lessons-learned/year |
| **Owner** | Product Safety & Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | Product Safety, Quality, Merchandising, Supply Chain, IT, Internal Audit |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Safety facilitates lessons-learned review: what worked, gaps (detection speed, traceability, reach), and systemic improvements | Product Safety | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Per recall |
| 2 | Stakeholders translate findings into process/system improvements: vendor onboarding QA gates, lot-traceability enhancements, notification automation, shelf-removal task rigor | Stakeholders | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Per recall |
| 3 | Internal Audit/Product Safety track improvement actions to closure; update recall playbook and training; feed trends into enterprise risk register (links to VS-21) | Internal Audit / Product Safety | Product Safety & Compliance Manager | Through closure |

### System Touchpoints
- Lessons-learned/action tracker; enterprise risk register (links to VS-21); training/L&D (links to VS-19)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Repeat-failure risk**: Lessons not embedded into process; mitigated by tracked action items and playbook updates


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "Product Safety facilitates lessons-learned review" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-capture of "enterprise risk register" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-498 (ensure controlled execution — Recall Resolution, Reimbursement & Root Cause / CAPA (PA-89.3).)
- operational: tracked action items and playbook updates

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per recall
