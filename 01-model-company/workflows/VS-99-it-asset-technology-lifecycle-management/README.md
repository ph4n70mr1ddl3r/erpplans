## Overview

IT Asset & Technology Lifecycle Management (ITAM) workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — governing
the **lifecycle of BuildRight's information-technology hardware and software assets** across 200
stores, 4 DCs, and HQ: **600 POS terminals, RF/handheld scanners, mobile devices, laptops/desktops,
network and Wi-Fi gear, servers/storage, printers/peripherals, and the full software estate
(operating systems, applications, SaaS subscriptions, cloud services, and open-source components)**.

This is distinct from **VS-27 (IT Operations & Security)** which runs the IT service desk,
infrastructure, and cybersecurity operations; from **VS-35 (Fixed Asset Management)** which handles
financial fixed-asset accounting, capitalization, and depreciation; from **VS-34 (Expense &
Non-Merchandise Procurement)** which handles purchasing transactions; and from **VS-29 (Master Data
Management)** which governs item/customer/vendor masters. This value stream owns the **ITAM
operating model**: hardware and software asset discovery and inventory (reconciliation to a
configuration management database / CMDB), technology procurement standardization and catalog,
device provisioning/imaging and deployment at scale across 205+ locations, mobile/RF/peripheral
management, maintenance/break-fix and spare-pool operations, technology refresh and rollout,
secure asset retirement with data sanitization and e-waste disposal (links to VS-73/VS-91), lost-
and stolen-asset recovery, software asset management (SAM) and license optimization, SaaS
portfolio and cloud-spend (FinOps) management, vendor true-up and compliance-audit defense,
open-source license compliance, license forecasting and budgeting, IT asset security hardening and
end-of-life/vulnerability remediation, total-cost-of-ownership (TCO) and utilization analytics,
and ITAM governance/stewardship.

With ~2,500+ endpoints and 600 POS units distributed across the archipelago, disciplined ITAM is
critical to control TCO, ensure license compliance (avoiding costly vendor true-ups and BSA/audit
exposure), satisfy data-privacy obligations on retired devices (links to VS-91), and maintain a
hardened, supportable technology base.
