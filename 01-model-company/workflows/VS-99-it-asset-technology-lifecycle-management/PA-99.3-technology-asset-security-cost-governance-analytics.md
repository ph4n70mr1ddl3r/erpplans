## W3249. IT Asset Security Hardening & Configuration Baseline

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New device deployment (W3235); policy update; security findings |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + policy cycle |
| **Volume** | All endpoints, POS, servers, network |
| **Owner** | Security Manager |
| **Participants** | Security Mgr (links to VS-27.3), IT Ops (links to VS-27), IT Asset Mgr, Development (links to VS-27.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Security/IT Ops define and maintain hardening baselines per asset class (POS, endpoint, server, network, mobile): CIS/industry benchmarks, BuildRight-specific standards (encryption, AV/EDR, patch level, MDM, least-privilege, disabled services/ports), approved software allow-list; version and publish baselines | Security / IT Ops | Security Manager | Per policy cycle |
| 2 | Deploy and enforce baselines: build into imaging (W3235), enforce via configuration management and MDM (W3236), monitor drift via scanning (links to VS-27.3), auto-remediate or ticket; gate new assets on baseline compliance before deployment | IT Ops / Security | Security Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Audit and report baseline compliance: measure % compliant by class/location, remediate exceptions, report to IT governance (W3253) and security risk register (links to VS-21.2/VS-27.3); align to data-privacy safeguards (links to VS-91) on devices handling personal data | Security / IT Asset Mgr | Security Manager | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Configuration/security tools (links to VS-27.3); MDM (W3236); imaging (W3235); risk register (links to VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Configuration-drift risk**: Devices fall out of baseline over time; mitigated by continuous scanning and auto-remediation
- **POS-constraint risk**: Hardening must coexist with POS offline/legacy requirements (per POS-013); mitigated by class-specific baselines


### Automation Opportunity
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approved software allow-list" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-scan of "scanning" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "report baseline compliance" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: continuous scanning and auto-remediation; operational: class-specific baselines

### Time Estimate
- Per policy cycle; continuous monitoring

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Continuous + event-driven |
| **Volume** | Subset of estate approaching EOL or with unpatched vulnerabilities |
| **Owner** | Security Manager |
| **Participants** | Security Mgr (links to VS-27.3), IT Ops (links to VS-27), IT Asset Mgr, Finance, Business Owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Security/IT Asset Mgr identify EOL/vulnerable assets: track vendor end-of-support dates per asset (W3233), scan for known vulnerabilities (CVEs per W3247), assess exploitability and exposure; prioritize by risk (critical/high first) | Security / IT Asset Mgr | Security Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Remediate: patch/upgrade where supported, isolate/compensating-controls where patch unavailable, accelerate refresh (W3238) for EOL assets, decommission where uneconomic; manage business disruption and change control (links to VS-27.1 W132/W495) | IT Ops / Security / IT Asset Mgr | Security Manager | Per finding |
| 3 | Track remediation SLA and report: open vulnerabilities/EOL inventory, mean-time-to-remediate, exceptions with risk acceptance and expiry; feed risk register (links to VS-21.2) and audit support (W3256) | Security / IT Asset Mgr | Security Manager | Monthly |

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unpatched-vulnerability risk**: Exploit of known CVE causes breach (links to VS-27.3/VS-91); mitigated by SLA and prioritization
- **EOL-support gap**: Vendor no longer patches; mitigated by refresh acceleration and isolation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-scan of "scan for known vulnerabilities" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-track of "Track remediation SLA and report" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: SLA and prioritization; operational: refresh acceleration and isolation

### Time Estimate
- Per finding; continuous tracking

## W3251. IT Asset Total Cost of Ownership Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly cost review; catalog/refresh decisions |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + per decision |
| **Volume** | Full hardware/software estate |
| **Owner** | IT Asset Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Asset Mgr, Finance (links to VS-17.4), IT Ops (links to VS-27), Procurement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Asset Mgr/Finance build TCO model per asset class/model: acquisition (capex), software/licenses (W3241), deployment/config (W3235), support/maintenance/break-fix (W3237), operations (power, network, management), refresh/disposal (W3238/W3239); annualize over lifecycle | IT Asset Mgr / Finance | IT Asset Manager | Quarterly |
| 2 | Analyze TCO drivers and comparisons: cost per device, per store, per user; compare models/vendors/strategies (own vs lease per VS-96 parallel, standard vs premium); identify cost outliers and optimization opportunities (W3242/W3244) | IT Asset Mgr / Finance | IT Asset Manager | Quarterly |
| 3 | Feed TCO into decisions: catalog selection (W3234), refresh policy (W3238), make/buy/lease, vendor selection (W3255); report TCO trends and optimization savings to IT governance and finance | IT Asset Mgr / Procurement | IT Asset Manager | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- TCO analytics/BI (links to VS-28); finance (links to VS-17.4); CMDB (W3233); procurement (links to VS-34)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden-cost risk**: Undercounting support/operations understates true TCO; mitigated by comprehensive TCO model
- **Model-oversimplification risk**: Aggregate TCO hides class-level variation; mitigated by granular modeling


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-analyze of "Analyze TCO drivers and comparisons" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "log selection" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: comprehensive TCO model; operational: granular modeling

### Time Estimate
- Quarterly; ongoing refinement

| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | Full estate |
| **Owner** | IT Asset Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Asset Mgr, Finance (links to VS-72/VS-17), IT Ops (links to VS-27), Entity/Dept Owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Asset Mgr measure utilization: device uptime/usage, software-feature usage (W3242), cloud resource utilization (W3244), capacity vs demand; identify under-utilized assets and over-provisioning | IT Asset Mgr / IT Ops | IT Asset Manager | Quarterly |

- System auto-count of "countability, report unit economics to entity finance" (replaces manual Step 3).

operational: utilization analytics; operational: transparent usage data and methodology

| **Volume** | Hundreds–thousands of disposals/year |
| **Owner** | IT Asset Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Asset Mgr, Privacy/DPO (links to VS-91), Sustainability (links to VS-73/VS-25), Finance (links to VS-35), Legal, Audit (links to VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ensure data-privacy compliance on disposal: certified sanitization (NIST 800-88) for all data-bearing media (W3239), retain sanitization certificates, manage data-subject obligations (links to VS-91 — secure destruction of personal data per RA 10173); DPO oversight | IT Asset Mgr / DPO | IT Asset Manager | Per disposal |
| 2 | Ensure e-waste/environmental compliance: use DENR-accredited TSD vendors (links to VS-73.2 hazardous/e-waste), document chain-of-custody and disposal method, comply with hazardous-waste registration; manage CSR/donation with sanitization and end-use controls | IT Asset Mgr / Sustainability | IT Asset Manager | Per disposal |
| 3 | Ensure financial/BIR compliance: derecognize assets (links to VS-35.3), document disposal for tax (gain/loss, links to VS-79), retain records per BIR 10-year requirement (links to VS-88); support external audit (links to VS-22.2) and privacy/environmental audits | IT Asset Mgr / Finance / Legal | IT Asset Manager | Per disposal |

### System Touchpoints
- Privacy (links to VS-91); environment (links to VS-73/VS-25); fixed assets (links to VS-35); tax (links to VS-79); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Privacy-breach risk**: Improper sanitization exposes personal data (links to VS-91 NPC); mitigated by certified sanitization and certificates
- **Environmental-fine risk**: Improper e-waste disposal violates DENR rules (links to VS-73.2); mitigated by accredited vendor and documentation

- System auto-record of "records per BIR 10-year requirement" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls

### Time Estimate
