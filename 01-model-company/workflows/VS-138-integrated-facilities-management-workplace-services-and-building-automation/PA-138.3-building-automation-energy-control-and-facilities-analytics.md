## W4185. Building Management System (BMS) & IoT Integration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Site opening (VS-37); retrofit; BMS-strategy refresh |
| **Frequency** | Per deployment + continuous operation |
| **Volume** | ~205 sites × BMS/IoT (HVAC, lighting, energy meters, sensors) |
| **Owner** | Facilities Technology Lead / IT (VS-27) / EA (VS-113) |
| **Participants** | Energy (VS-120), Engineering (VS-20.2), InfoSec (VS-27.3), IT (VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities/IT/EA define the BMS/IoT architecture and standards (controllers, protocols, IoT sensors, central monitoring) for stores/DCs/HQ, integrated with the energy program (VS-120) | Facilities Tech Lead / IT / EA | CIO / Head of Facilities | Per strategy |
| 2 | Facilities/IT deploy the BMS at sites (HVAC/lighting/meter control, environmental sensors), connect to central monitoring, and secure it (OT security links to VS-27.3) | Facilities Tech Lead / IT / Provider | Facilities Tech Lead | Per site |
| 3 | Facilities/Energy operate the BMS (monitoring, alarms, control), integrate data into analytics (VS-28/VS-120), and maintain the platform lifecycle (W4168-style governance) | Facilities Tech Lead / Energy Officer | Head of Facilities | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- BMS/IoT platform; energy monitoring (VS-120); OT security (VS-27.3); analytics (VS-28); EA standards (VS-113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **OT-security risk**: BMS/IoT exposed to cyber-attack (building hijack); mitigated by OT security (VS-27.3) and segmentation
- **Integration-debt risk**: Proprietary siloed BMS per site; mitigated by architecture standards (VS-113)

### Time Estimate
- Per-site deployment weeks; continuous operation

### Automation Opportunity
- IoT auto-monitoring/alerting; BMS-data auto-ingest to analytics; predictive-maintenance from sensor data

### Controls
- CTL-646 (ensure controlled execution — Building Automation, Energy Control & Facilities Analytics (PA-138.3).)
- CTL: BMS OT-security assessed/segmented (VS-27.3); platform governed (VS-113)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-120 (energy), VS-27.3 (OT security), VS-113 (EA), VS-28 (analytics), VS-20.2 (engineering), VS-37 (new-site BMS)

|---|---|---|---|---|
