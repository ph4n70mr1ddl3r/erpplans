## Overview

Integrated Facilities Management (IFM), Workplace Services & Building Automation workflows for
BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the enterprise **facilities/physical-environment discipline** that
keeps **~205 sites** habitable, safe, compliant, and cost-efficient: 200 big-box stores
(8,000–15,000 sqm selling area each), 4 distribution centers (~130,000 sqm aggregate), the Davao
HQ (~3,000 sqm), and supporting yards/lots. Across this footprint run *hard* building services
(HVAC, lighting, fire/life-safety, plumbing, elevators/escalators, refrigeration, building
management systems) and *soft* workplace services (janitorial/cleaning, pest control, security
guarding, landscaping/grounds, waste handling, cafeteria/canteen, mailroom) — each currently
bought, scheduled, and tracked in fragments with no single owner.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**: facility/building-service terms are sprinkled
across ~30 PA files — VS-20.3 (facility maintenance & equipment), VS-07.2 (store equipment/
utilities), VS-120 (energy efficiency), VS-98 (outsourced-services workforce), VS-73 (store waste),
VS-23 (physical security), VS-34 (transactional procurement) — with **zero dedicated workflow
headers** for the *integrated* facilities-management operating model. No value stream owns the IFM
strategy and provider governance, the consolidated hard+soft service catalog and SLA framework,
preventive/reactive maintenance coordination across 205 sites, vendor/contractor management for
facilities services, workplace/canteen/mailroom services, building-automation/BMS integration, the
facilities helpdesk and work-order system, space/occupancy management, or facilities cost/performance
analytics.

This is distinct from **VS-20.3 (Facility Maintenance & Equipment)** which covers *equipment*
maintenance (POS, paint mixers, cutting equipment, forklifts) — this value stream owns the *building
fabric and soft workplace services* (the shell, HVAC, cleaning, pest, security, grounds, cafeteria).
It is distinct from **VS-120 (Energy Efficiency)** which owns the *energy/RA 11285* program — this
value stream owns the *BMS/building-automation* that executes energy control and the broader
facilities environment. It is distinct from **VS-23.2 (Physical Security)** which owns *loss-
prevention surveillance/access* — this value stream owns the *guarding-service procurement and
coordination* (the two coordinate on the on-site guard force). It is distinct from **VS-98
(Contingent Workforce)** which governs the *outsourced-labor* employment model — this value stream
governs the *facilities-service contracts* delivered by that labor. IFM is the *physical-
environment* discipline — distinct from equipment maintenance, energy management, loss-prevention,
and workforce governance.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: 205 sites across an archipelago (uneven local contractor
quality), tropical climate (HVAC, mold, pest pressure), typhoon/seismic exposure (fabric integrity),
fire/life-safety obligations (BFP Fire Code, OSH), and a material opex line where fragmented,
overlapping service contracts leak value and where a failed AC or fire-system directly closes a
store.
