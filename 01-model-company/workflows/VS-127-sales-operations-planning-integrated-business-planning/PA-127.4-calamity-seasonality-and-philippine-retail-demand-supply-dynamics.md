> **Why this process area exists.** The generic monthly S&OP/IBP cycle in PA-127.1–PA-127.3 is
> deliberately *retail- and country-agnostic*. BuildRight Depot operates in a setting that the
> generic cycle does not, on its own, own: a Philippine typhoon belt where a single PAGASA Signal
> No. 3+ storm produces a multi-fold spike in flood-control SKUs (sandbags, plywood, nails, water
> pumps, flashlights, tarps) across whole regions; a demand calendar dominated by the **ber-months**
> (September–December) renovation-and-gift surge and the **summer** aircon/garden/dry-season
> construction peak; a **4-DC archipelago network** where a port closure or regional disruption
> must be absorbed by reallocating inventory across islands; lumpy **B2B/trade-project** and
> **new-store-opening** demand that does not behave like steady-state replenishment; ~300
> **VMI/consignment** SKUs whose ownership and replenishment signal cross a vendor boundary; and
> **government-mandated price events** (RA 7581 Price Act calamity freezes, election-period SRPs,
> DTI basic-goods mandates) that override the commercial price/promo plan on demand. This process
> area specializes the consensus cycle for those dynamics — it does not duplicate PA-127.1–.3; it
> feeds them. Owned by the **S&OP/IBP sub-team within Supply Chain & Logistics** (`model-company-profile.md` §3.3), which was stood up specifically to close the prior "unowned-as-a-program" gap.

## W5489. Typhoon & Calamity Demand-Surge Forecasting and Pre-Positioning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | PAGASA tropical-cyclone advisory (low-pressure area → Signal); pre-season (April–May) calamaty-readiness cycle |
| **Frequency** | Continuous monitoring (Jun–Nov peak) + pre-season build + per-storm response |
| **Volume** | ~20 named storms/yr entering PAR; flood-control SKU surge of 3–10× baseline across the affected region's ~20–60 stores |
| **Owner** | S&OP/IBP Lead (Supply Chain & Logistics) |
| **Participants** | Demand Planning, Supply Planning, DC Operations (links to VS-04), Logistics/Fleet (links to VS-06/VS-110), Sourcing (links to VS-122/VS-03), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Store Ops (links to VS-07/VS-177), Disaster Response/BCP (links to VS-69), Quality (links to VS-31) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Demand Planning maintain a flood-control/calamity SKU cluster (sandbags, plywood, lumber, nails/screws, tarps, water pumps, flashlights/batteries, PVC pipes, waterproofing, generators) and a per-region surge model calibrated on prior storms (Haiyan/Yolanda, Odette/Rai, Karding) — base demand × Signal-strength × region exposure — refreshed each pre-season | Demand Planning | S&OP/IBP Lead | Pre-season + per storm |
| 2 | On PAGASA forecast track, S&OP/IBP run the surge scenario for the likely landfall region: forecast SKU spike, days-of-supply gap vs on-hand + in-transit, and the pre-positioning plan (push build-forward from the nearest DC, expedite local sourcing links to VS-122/VS-03, inter-island transfer links to W5492) timed against the 45–90-day import lead where import SKUs are involved | S&OP/IBP / Supply Planning | S&OP/IBP Lead | 4–8 hrs per storm scenario |
| 3 | S&OP/IBP/Supply execute pre-positioning (DC special-build, store forward-stocking of the calamity cluster, 3PL capacity pre-book links to VS-06/VS-110), set allocation/rationing rules for the event, interface the DTI price-freeze constraint (links to W5496), and run the post-storm sell-down/rebalancing to avoid stranded post-event stock | S&OP/IBP / DC Ops / Store Ops | S&OP/IBP Lead | Per storm + 1–2 wks sell-down |

### System Touchpoints
- S&OP/what-if platform (links to W3917); demand-sensing signals incl. PAGASA feed (links to W3910); supply/import (links to VS-02/VS-122); DC/WMS (links to VS-04); fleet/3PL (links to VS-06/VS-110); inter-island transfer (links to W5492); DTI price-freeze (links to W5496); BCP/disaster response (links to VS-69)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pre-position-too-late risk**: import flood-control SKUs (45–90-day lead) arrive after the storm window; mitigated by pre-season build and local-source qualification per region
- **Over-preposition/stranded-stock risk**: storm veers off, leaving region overstocked; mitigated by post-event inter-island rebalancing (W5492) and the seasonal sell-down into steady-state demand
- **Price-gouging/compliance risk**: surge demand collides with the RA 7581 calamity price freeze; mitigated by the W5496 interface so pre-positioning never sets up an un-frozen price

### Automation Opportunity
- PAGASA-feed → surge-scenario auto-run (forecast SKU spike, gap, pre-position plan) on Signal declaration, replacing manual Step 2
- Auto-flag of flood-control cluster SKUs whose on-hand + in-transit < modeled surge demand by region

### Controls
- operational: per-region surge model calibrated on prior storms and refreshed pre-season; operational: post-event rebalancing to prevent stranded stock; operational: RA 7581 price-freeze interface (W5496)

### Time Estimate
- Pre-season model refresh ~3–5 days/yr; per-storm scenario + pre-positioning ~8–16 hrs; post-storm sell-down/rebalancing absorbed in the weekly cycle

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-compression risk**: a freeze on calamity SKUs compresses margin exactly when demand spikes; mitigated by quantifying the gap for exec S&OP and SCF/financing (links to VS-105)
- **Non-compliance/penalty risk**: selling frozen SKUs above the ceiling or failing the DTI price-check; mitigated by the price-freeze master control and compliance monitoring (VS-118/W107)
- **Promo-conflict risk**: an active promo conflicts with the freeze; mitigated by Step 2 promo suspension/re-price

### Automation Opportunity
- Mandate-declaration → frozen-SKU scope + margin/demand-impact auto-assessment replacing manual Step 1
- Auto-suspension of conflicting promos on freeze-declaration
