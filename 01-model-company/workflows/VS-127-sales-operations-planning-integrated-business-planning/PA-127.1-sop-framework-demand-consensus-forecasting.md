## W3905. S&OP/IBP Strategy, Operating Rhythm & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual planning cycle (links to VS-33.1); executive mandate |
| **Frequency** | Annual design + monthly cycle |
| **Volume** | 1 enterprise S&OP/IBP process (~12–18 month rolling horizon) |
| **Owner** | S&OP / Demand & Supply Planning |
| **Participants** | S&OP Lead, Merchandising (links to VS-01), Supply Planning (links to VS-02), Marketing (links to VS-14), Finance/FP&A (links to VS-17.4/VS-101), Store Ops (links to VS-07), Logistics (links to VS-06/VS-110), Sourcing/Import (links to VS-122/VS-87), Strategy (links to VS-33), Commodity (links to VS-106) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | S&OP/Demand-Supply design the S&OP/IBP operating model: horizon (18–24 month rolling), granularity (category/region/SKU-family monthly → key-SKU weekly), the five-step monthly cycle (demand review → supply review → pre-S&OP → exec S&OP → plan publication), product-treatment segments (A/B/C links to profile §6.6), and KPIs (forecast accuracy, plan adherence, service level, inventory turns) | S&OP Lead / Planning | COO / VP Merchandising | Annual |
| 2 | S&OP establish governance: S&OP chair (typically Demand-Supply Planning leader), executive sponsor (COO), the demand-consensus participants (Sales/Marketing/Merchandising/Finance/Supply), decision rights, escalation path, and integration to corporate planning (links to VS-33) and merchandise financial planning (links to VS-101) | S&OP Lead / COO | COO | Annual |
| 3 | COO/Executive ratify the cadence, calendar (monthly cycle aligned to financial close), and the single-number principle (one agreed plan), and cascade to functions | COO / S&OP Lead | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- S&OP/IBP platform; supply planning (links to VS-02); merchandise financial planning (links to VS-101); corporate planning (links to VS-33); logistics (links to VS-06/VS-110); sourcing (links to VS-122/VS-87)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-single-number risk**: Each function brings its own forecast; mitigated by consensus cycle and single-number governance
- **Cadence-drift risk**: Cycle slips under operational pressure; mitigated by fixed calendar and exec sponsorship


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-review (cycle (demand review → supply review)

### Controls
operational: consensus cycle and single-number governance; operational: fixed calendar and exec sponsorship

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual design + monthly cycle facilitation

## W3906. Demand Planning & Statistical Baseline Forecasting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly cycle; data refresh |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + weekly refresh |
| **Volume** | ~35,000 active SKUs × 200 stores/4 DCs; ~55,000 SKU master |
| **Owner** | Demand Planning |
| **Participants** | Demand Planning, Data Science (links to VS-30.2), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Supply Planning (links to VS-02), Insights (links to VS-28.1), IT (links to VS-27/VS-28), Consumer Insights (links to VS-44) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Demand Planning/Data Science generate the statistical baseline forecast per SKU/region: time-series models (seasonal, trend, Croston for slow-movers), hierarchical reconciliation (category → SKU, region → store), outlier cleansing, and incorporate the seasonal calendar (links to profile §13.2 and VS-64) | Demand Planning / Data Science | S&OP Lead | Monthly |
| 2 | Demand Planning segment SKUs by treatment (A-items statistical + override; B-items statistical; C-items aggregate/profile) to balance effort, and incorporate causal factors (price links to VS-01.2, weather/typhoon links to VS-69, holidays, store openings links to VS-37) | Demand Planning / Merchandising | S&OP Lead | Monthly |
| 3 | Demand Planning publish the baseline to the consensus review (links to W3907) with confidence intervals, model lineage, and exceptions for review | Demand Planning | S&OP Lead | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Forecasting/ML platform (links to VS-30.2); sales history (links to VS-08/VS-28); pricing (links to VS-01.2); seasonal (links to VS-64/VS-69); store openings (links to VS-37); insights (links to VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Model-staleness risk**: Out-of-date models mis-forecast seasonal shifts; mitigated by monthly retrain and bias tracking (links to W3911)
- **Granularity-cost risk**: Forecasting all 35K SKUs at store-level is costly; mitigated by ABC segmentation


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-generate (planning/data science generate the statistical baseline)
- auto-file (statistical; c-items aggregate/profile) to balance effort,)
- auto-review (the consensus review (links to w3907))

### Controls
operational: monthly retrain and bias tracking (links to W3911); operational: ABC segmentation

### Time Estimate
- Monthly cycle + ~1 week model maintenance

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Demand Planning sense near-term demand shifts from real-time signals: POS velocity (links to VS-08), ecommerce trend (links to VS-10), weather/typhoon (links to VS-69 — flood-control spikes), local events/construction booms, and competitor stock-outs (links to VS-57) | Demand Planning / Insights / Store Ops | S&OP Lead | Weekly |
| 2 | Demand Planning translate sensed shifts into short-cycle forecast adjustments (0–4 week horizon) for A-items and event-driven categories, and trigger expedite/rebalancing with Supply/Logistics (links to VS-02/VS-06/VS-74) | Demand Planning / Supply | S&OP Lead | Weekly |
| 3 | Demand Planning monitor sensing-accuracy and avoid over-reaction (nervousness), with thresholds for when to adjust vs hold the plan (links to W3911) | Demand Planning | S&OP Lead | Weekly |

### System Touchpoints

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Plan-nervousness risk**: Over-reacting to short-term signals destabilizes the plan and vendor orders; mitigated by thresholds and exception handling

### Controls
operational: thresholds and exception handling; operational: longer-horizon sensing for imports
