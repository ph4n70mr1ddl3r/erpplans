# VS-127: Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP) & Integrated Business Planning

## Overview

Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP) & Integrated Business Planning (IBP) workflows for BuildRight
Depot Corp. — owning the **monthly cross-functional consensus planning cycle** that balances
demand and supply across 35,000 active SKUs, 200 stores, 4 DCs, ~40% import component (45–90-day
import lead times), ~PHP 42–45B annual COGS, the Philippine seasonal calendar (rainy-season,
ber-months, summer), and a 6–8x inventory-turn target. S&OP/IBP converts the demand forecast,
supply constraints, inventory targets, and financial plan into a single agreed volume/margin plan
that drives procurement, capacity, inventory investment, and the merchandise financial plan.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**: 'S&OP' is referenced across ~20 PA files and
'demand planning/forecasting' across ~24–31 with no dedicated owner; the formal terms
'integrated business planning', 'IBP', and 'sales and operations planning' each appear in **zero**
PA files. S&OP activity is sprinkled as single steps inside VS-02 (Supply Planning — the
*operational* replenishment/ROP execution), VS-101 (Merchandise Financial Planning — the
*financial* OTB/margin plan), VS-33 (Strategic Planning — the *corporate* plan), and VS-106
(Commodity Risk — input-cost hedging) — but no value stream owns the **S&OP/IBP process**: the
monthly demand-consensus cycle, statistical baseline forecasting, new-product/event forecasting,
demand sensing, supply/capacity review, pre-S&OP reconciliation, scenario/what-if planning,
executive S&OP decision meeting, constrained-plan publication, and financial reconciliation to the
corporate plan.

This is distinct from **VS-02 (Supply Planning)** which executes operational replenishment/ROP/EOQ
and DC/store supply (this value stream owns the *forward consensus plan* that feeds those
operations). It is distinct from **VS-101 (Merchandise Financial Planning)** which owns the
financial open-to-buy/markdown/margin plan in pesos (this value stream owns the *unit/volume
demand-supply reconciliation* that the financial plan is built on). It is distinct from **VS-33
(Strategic Planning)** which owns the corporate strategy and annual budget (this value stream owns
the *monthly operating-plan cycle* that bridges strategy to execution). It is distinct from **VS-01
(Merchandise Strategy)** which owns assortment/pricing (this value stream owns the *volume plan by
SKU/category/region*). It is distinct from **VS-106 (Commodity Risk)** which hedges input costs
(this value stream owns the *volume plan* that commodity exposure is sized from). S&OP/IBP is the
*demand-supply consensus planning* discipline — distinct from operational supply, merchandise
finance, corporate strategy, assortment, and commodity risk.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: 35,000 SKUs across 200 stores and 4 DCs with island-geography
supply constraints, ~40% imports with 45–90-day lead times (demand errors compound over long
lead times), heavy seasonality (rainy-season flood-control, ber-months, summer aircon/garden),
and a 6–8x turn target that requires precise inventory investment. Without a dedicated S&OP/IBP
owner, BuildRight risks forecast fragmentation (each function with its own number), bullwhip-
amplified orders to vendors and import shipments, excess slow-mover inventory vs stockouts of
A-items, margin leakage from unplanned markdowns, and a disconnect between the volume plan and the
financial plan — risks that no existing value stream owns end-to-end.
