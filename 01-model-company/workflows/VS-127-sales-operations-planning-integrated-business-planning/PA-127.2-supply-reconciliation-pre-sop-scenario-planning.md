## W3913. Supply Review & Capacity/Constraints Plan

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Consensus demand (links to W3907) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | Supply plan across 4 DCs, ~800–1,000 vendors, 2–3x weekly replenishment |
| **Owner** | Supply Planning / S&OP |
| **Participants** | Supply Planning (links to VS-02), S&OP Lead, Logistics/DC (links to VS-04/VS-06), Sourcing/Vendor (links to VS-03/VS-122), Store Ops (links to VS-07), Finance (links to VS-17.4), Commodity (links to VS-106) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Supply Planning translate the consensus demand (links to W3907) into a supply plan: vendor capacity, PO/release schedule (links to VS-03/VS-02), import-container plan (links to VS-122/VS-02.2), DC throughput capacity (links to VS-04), and transportation capacity (links to VS-06/VS-110) | Supply Planning / Sourcing / Logistics | S&OP Lead | Monthly |
| 2 | Supply Planning identify supply constraints (vendor capacity limits, MOQ, import lead times 45–90 days links to VS-122, DC/transport capacity, commodity availability links to VS-106, DG/hazmat transport links to VS-114) and quantify the gap to demand | Supply Planning / Functions | S&OP Lead | Monthly |
| 3 | Supply Planning document supply options to close gaps (alternate vendors links to VS-03/VS-122, expedites, capacity expansion, substitution) and feed pre-S&OP (links to W3916) | Supply Planning / Sourcing | S&OP Lead | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Supply planning (links to VS-02); procurement/vendor (links to VS-03); sourcing/import (links to VS-122); DC (links to VS-04); logistics (links to VS-06/VS-110); commodity (links to VS-106); DG transport (links to VS-114)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Capacity-blind risk**: Demand plan ignores vendor/DC/transport capacity limits; mitigated by explicit supply review
- **Import-lead-time risk**: 45–90 day import lead means today's decision affects supply months out (links to VS-122); mitigated by long-horizon planning


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-translate of "translate the consensus demand into a supply plan" drafts (replaces manual Step 1 builds).
- System auto-import of "Supply Planning identify supply constraints" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document supply options to close gaps" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-611 (ensure controlled execution — Supply Reconciliation, Pre-S&OP & Scenario Planning (PA-127.2).)
- operational: explicit supply review; operational: long-horizon planning

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 days/month supply review
