## W3921. Financial Reconciliation to Plan (Revenue/Margin/Inventory Budget)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Plan publication (W3920); financial cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Financial reconciliation across the plan horizon |
| **Owner** | S&OP / Finance |
| **Participants** | S&OP Lead, Finance/FP&A (links to VS-17.4), Merchandising (links to VS-101), Treasury (links to VS-18), Strategy (links to VS-33), Supply (links to VS-02), Commodity (links to VS-106) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | S&OP/Finance translate the constrained volume plan (links to W3920) into financial terms: revenue (× price links to VS-01.2), COGS (× cost incl. commodity/FX links to VS-106/VS-18.3), gross margin (links to VS-101), and inventory investment, reconciled to the corporate budget (links to VS-33/VS-17.4) | S&OP / Finance / Merchandising | S&OP Lead / FP&A | Monthly |
| 2 | S&OP/Finance quantify plan-vs-budget gaps (revenue/margin/inventory/EBITDA links to profile §9.4), identify financial risk (markdown need links to VS-101, commodity exposure links to VS-106, FX links to VS-18.3), and feed gap-closing to exec S&OP (links to W3912/W3919) | S&OP / Finance / Commodity | S&OP Lead / FP&A | Monthly |
| 3 | S&OP/Finance document the financial implications of plan decisions (e.g., expedite cost, inventory build cost, markdown cost) for the decision record and the IBP steering | S&OP / Finance | S&OP Lead | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A (links to VS-17.4); merchandise financial plan (links to VS-101); pricing (links to VS-01.2); commodity (links to VS-106); FX (links to VS-18.3); treasury (links to VS-18); strategy (links to VS-33)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Volume-finance-disconnect risk**: Volume plan looks good but misses financial targets; mitigated by monthly financial reconciliation
- **Hidden-cost risk**: Plan decisions carry uncosted expedites/builds/markdowns; mitigated by decision costing


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-reconcile of "reconciled to the corporate budget" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "file §9.4" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record and the IBP steering" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-72 (ensure S&OP consensus & financial reconciliation.)
operational: monthly financial reconciliation; operational: decision costing

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 days/month financial reconciliation
