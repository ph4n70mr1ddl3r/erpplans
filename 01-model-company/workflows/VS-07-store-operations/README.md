# VS-07: Store Operations

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Store operations: daily management, facility & safety, receiving & replenishment, and staffing across 200 stores.

## Why it matters

200 stores × ~29 staff × 2–3 shifts; 2.8M monthly POS transactions originate here. The largest value stream (149 workflows); daily execution governs revenue, safety, and customer experience.
