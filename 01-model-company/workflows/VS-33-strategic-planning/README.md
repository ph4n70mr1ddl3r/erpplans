# VS-33: Strategic Planning & Corporate Performance Management

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Covers corporate-level strategic planning, annual business planning, KPI definition and cascading, board governance, competitive intelligence, and executive performance reporting. While VS-28 covers BI/analytics tooling and VS-17 covers financial reporting, this value stream addresses the planning and governance processes that set direction for the entire organization.
