## W4473. Employee Assistance Program (EAP) & Referral to Rehabilitation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Positive result eligible for rehab (W4471); self-referral; supervisor referral |
| **Frequency** | Per referral |
| **Volume** | EAP/referral cases (self- and case-driven) |
| **Owner** | Occupational Health/EAP (VS-83.3) / HR (VS-103) / Labor Relations (VS-84) |
| **Participants** | EAP (VS-83.3), Occupational Health (VS-83), HR (VS-103), Labor Relations (VS-84), Legal (VS-100), Compliance (VS-22), Vendor (W4460) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EAP/Occupational Health/HR offer the rehabilitation pathway (per DOLE rehabilitation emphasis & policy W4457) to eligible positive cases and self-referrals, confidentially, with no retaliation | EAP / Occupational Health / HR | CHRO | Per case |
| 2 | EAP/Occupational Health assess the employee, refer to the rehabilitation provider network (W4475), coordinate leave/treatment, and manage the return-to-work agreement (W4474) | EAP / Occupational Health / HR | Occupational Health Lead / CHRO | Per case |
| 3 | EAP/HR manage the case through treatment (confidentially W4462), coordinate return-to-duty/follow-up testing (W4474), and document for DOLE (W4477) | EAP / HR / Occupational Health | CHRO | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- EAP (VS-83.3); occupational health (VS-83); HRIS/case (VS-103); labor relations (VS-84); vendor (W4475); records (VS-88); privacy (VS-91)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rehab-not-offered risk**: punitive-only handling → DOLE/cultural issue + repeat impairment; mitigated by mandatory rehab-pathway offer
- **Confidentiality-breach risk**: rehab status disclosed → stigma/claim; mitigated by need-to-know & privacy (W4462)

### Time Estimate
- ~case-management hours; treatment duration varies

### Automation Opportunity
- EAP referral workflow; provider-network matching; leave/treatment coordination; return-to-work-agreement tracking

### Controls
- CTL: rehabilitation pathway offered to eligible cases; confidential & no-retaliation; provider-network referral; return-to-work agreement (W4474); documented

### Cross-references
- links to VS-83.3 (EAP), VS-83 (occupational health), VS-84 (labor), VS-91 (privacy), W4457 (policy), W4471 (case), W4474 (return), W4475 (provider)

## W4474. Return-to-Duty, Return-to-Work Agreement & Follow-Up Testing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Completion of rehabilitation (W4473) |
| **Frequency** | Per returning employee |
| **Volume** | Return-to-duty cases (subset of rehab cases) |
| **Owner** | Occupational Health (VS-83) / HR (VS-103) / Labor Relations (VS-84) |
| **Participants** | Occupational Health (VS-83), HR (VS-103), Labor Relations (VS-84), EAP (VS-83.3), HSE (VS-24), Legal (VS-100), Vendor (W4460) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Occupational Health/HR execute a negative **return-to-duty test** before the employee resumes any safety-sensitive duty, and confirm fitness/clearance | Occupational Health / Vendor / HR | Occupational Health Lead / CHRO | Per return |
| 2 | Labor Relations/HR/EAP put a **return-to-work agreement** in place — conditions, follow-up testing schedule (unannounced, per DOT-equivalent/DOLE guidance), EAP continuation, and consequences of relapse | Labor Relations / HR / EAP / Legal | CHRO / VP Legal | Per return |
| 3 | Occupational Health/HR administer the **follow-up testing** schedule over the defined period, monitor performance/behavior, and manage any relapse (W4471); document confidentially | Occupational Health / HR / Vendor | Occupational Health Lead / CHRO | Per schedule |

### System Touchpoints
- Occupational health (VS-83); HRIS/case (VS-103); labor relations (VS-84); EAP (VS-83.3); vendor (W4460); records (VS-88); privacy (VS-91); HSE (VS-24)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Premature-return risk**: employee returned to safety-sensitive duty before negative return-to-duty test → repeat incident; mitigated by return-to-duty-test gate
- **Follow-up-lapse risk**: follow-up testing lapses → undetected relapse; mitigated by scheduled unannounced follow-up

### Time Estimate
- ~case-management hours over the follow-up period

### Automation Opportunity
- Return-to-duty-test gate; return-to-work-agreement workflow; follow-up-testing scheduler (unannounced); relapse-alert

### Controls
- CTL: negative return-to-duty test before resuming safety-sensitive duty; return-to-work agreement; scheduled unannounced follow-up testing; relapse managed (W4471)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-83 (occupational health), VS-84 (labor), VS-83.3 (EAP), W4457 (policy), W4466 (random), W4471 (case/relapse), W4473 (rehab)

### Time Estimate

## W4476. Confidentiality, Records Retention & RA 10173 Data Protection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program operation; records lifecycle; privacy review |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + per review |
| **Volume** | All drug-program records (~thousands of test/case records) |
| **Owner** | Privacy/DPO (VS-91) / Records (VS-88) / HR (VS-103) / Occupational Health (VS-83) |
| **Participants** | DPO (VS-91), Records (VS-88), HR (VS-103), Occupational Health (VS-83), Legal (VS-100), Compliance (VS-22), IT (VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DPO/Records/HR set the confidentiality & retention policy — drug-test/case records as sensitive health data under RA 10173, restricted access (HR/Occupational Health/Legal need-to-know), retention per DOLE/legal (VS-88), and destruction | DPO / Records / Legal / HR | DPO / VP Legal / CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | HR/Occupational Health/IT enforce restricted access, secure storage/transfer, legal-hold (VS-100), and DSAR handling (VS-91) for drug-program records | HR / Occupational Health / IT / DPO | CHRO / CIO | Continuous |
| 3 | DPO/Compliance audit confidentiality compliance, manage any breach (VS-91.3), and ensure records available for DOLE/legal (W4477/W4472) within retention | DPO / Compliance / Records | DPO / VP Legal | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Privacy/DPO (VS-91); records (VS-88); HRIS (VS-103); occupational health (VS-83); legal-hold (VS-100); IT security (VS-27.3); breach (VS-91.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Disclosure risk**: drug record accessed by unauthorized → RA 10173/NPC exposure + stigma/claim; mitigated by need-to-know access & audit
- **Retention-failure risk**: records destroyed prematurely / kept too long → DOLE/legal or privacy issue; mitigated by retention policy (VS-88)

### Time Estimate
- ~annual framework; continuous adherence

### Automation Opportunity
- Restricted-access health-data vault; access-audit; retention/destruction automation; DSAR workflow; legal-hold integration

### Controls
- CTL: drug records treated as sensitive health data; restricted need-to-know access; retained per policy (VS-88); DSAR handled; breach-managed; audited

### Cross-references

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Policy review; impairment case; regulation change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | One impairment policy (beyond illicit drugs) |
| **Owner** | HR (VS-103) / HSE (VS-24) / Occupational Health (VS-83) / Legal (VS-100) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-103), HSE (VS-24), Occupational Health (VS-83), Legal (VS-100), Compliance (VS-22), Labor Relations (VS-84), Benefits (VS-102) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR/HSE/Occupational Health/Legal extend the impairment policy beyond illicit drugs — **alcohol** (working under the influence), **prescription/OTC medication** impairment (safety-sensitive roles), and any legal-substance impairment, with testing/handling protocol | HR / HSE / Occupational Health / Legal | CHRO / VP Legal | Annual + on change |
| 2 | Occupational Health/HR handle medication-impairment in safety-sensitive roles — legitimate-use accommodation vs safety (engage employee/physician, reassignment), balancing RA 10524/disability and safety duty | Occupational Health / HR / Legal / Benefits | CHRO / VP Legal | Per case |
| 3 | HSE/HR enforce alcohol/impairment rules (no working under the influence, post-incident alcohol testing W4468), train supervisors (W4461), and review on regulation/case learning | HSE / HR / L&D | HSE Lead / CHRO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS/policy (VS-103); HSE (VS-24); occupational health (VS-83); legal (VS-100); labor (VS-84); benefits (VS-102); compliance (VS-22)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Medication-accommodation-conflict risk**: disability/legitimate-use vs safety duty tension; mitigated by individualized assessment & legal/medical engagement
- **Alcohol-blindness risk**: program covers only drugs not alcohol → impairment gap; mitigated by explicit alcohol policy & testing

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks annual policy; per-case handling

### Automation Opportunity
- Impairment-policy library; medication-accommodation case workflow; supervisor recognition training; alcohol-breathalyzer integration (where lawful)

### Controls
- CTL: impairment policy covers alcohol/prescription/any-impairment; medication-accommodation assessed individually; safety duty protected; trained; reviewed

### Cross-references
