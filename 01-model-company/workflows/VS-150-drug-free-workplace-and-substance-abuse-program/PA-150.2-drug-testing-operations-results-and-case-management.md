## W4465. Pre-Employment Drug Testing (links VS-121 recruitment)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Candidate at offer/contingent-offer stage |
| **Frequency** | Per hire (~1,200–1,600 hires/yr) |
| **Volume** | ~1,200–1,600 pre-employment tests/yr |
| **Owner** | Talent Acquisition (VS-121) / Occupational Health (VS-83) / HR |
| **Participants** | Talent Acquisition (VS-121), Occupational Health (VS-83), HR (VS-103), Vendor (W4460), Compliance (VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Talent Acquisition include drug-test as a contingent-offer condition, schedule with the DOH-accredited vendor (W4460), and obtain candidate consent (W4462) | Talent Acquisition / Occupational Health | TA Lead / CHRO | Per hire |
| 2 | Vendor collect specimen per chain-of-custody (W4469), lab test, MRO review (W4470), and return result to Occupational Health/HR confidentially | Vendor / Occupational Health | Occupational Health Lead | Per test |
| 3 | Occupational Health/HR act on result — negative → proceed to hire; positive/refusal → withdraw offer per policy (W4457) and due process (W4471), with confidentiality (W4462) | Occupational Health / HR / Legal | CHRO / VP Legal | Per result |

### System Touchpoints
- TA/recruitment (VS-121); occupational health (VS-83); HRIS (VS-103); vendor (W4460); privacy (VS-91); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hire-before-result risk**: candidate starts before result → impaired new hire; mitigated by contingent-offer-gating
- **Privacy risk**: result disclosed to hiring manager; mitigated by need-to-know (HR/Occupational Health only)

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 days turnaround per test; ~minutes scheduling

### Automation Opportunity
- Contingent-offer-gating workflow; vendor scheduling/integration; result-to-HRIS (restricted); consent capture

### Controls
- CTL: drug-test a contingent-offer condition; DOH-accredited vendor + chain-of-custody; result confidential to HR/Occupational Health; offer-gated on result

### Cross-references
- links to VS-121 (TA), VS-83 (occupational health), VS-91 (privacy), W4457 (policy), W4460 (vendor), W4469 (collection), W4470 (MRO), W4471 (case)

## W4466. Random Drug Testing — Selection, Notice & Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Random-testing cycle (DOLE D.O. 53-04 requires random testing) |
| **Frequency** | Per cycle (e.g. periodic; safety-sensitive higher rate) |
| **Volume** | Random sample of workforce incl. all safety-sensitive roles (W4459) |
| **Owner** | Occupational Health (VS-83) / HR (VS-103) / HSE (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | Occupational Health (VS-83), HR (VS-103), HSE (VS-24), Vendor (W4460), Security (VS-23), Compliance (VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Occupational Health/HR run a documented **random** selection (computer-generated, unbiased) covering the required proportion of the workforce (higher for safety-sensitive W4459), per D.O. 53-04 | Occupational Health / HR | CHRO / Compliance Lead | Per cycle |
| 2 | Occupational Health/HR/Security notify selected employees (unannounced, same-day where feasible to prevent evasion), arrange collection with vendor, and manage the process confidentially | Occupational Health / HR / Security / Vendor | CHRO | Per cycle |
| 3 | Vendor collect/test (W4469/W4470), Occupational Health/HR act on results (negative → resume; positive → W4471), and document the cycle for DOLE (W4477) | Vendor / Occupational Health / HR | CHRO | Per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS (VS-103); occupational health (VS-83); random-selection engine; vendor (W4460); security/access (VS-23); records (VS-88); compliance (VS-22)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Selection-bias risk**: non-random selection → legal/labor challenge + DOLE finding; mitigated by documented computer-generated random
- **Evasion risk**: advance notice → evasion; mitigated by unannounced/same-day notification

### Time Estimate
- Per-cycle planning/execution days

### Automation Opportunity
- Computer-generated random selection (audit-trail); unannounced-notification workflow; same-day collection scheduling; cycle documentation

### Controls
- CTL: documented unbiased random selection; safety-sensitive proportion met; unannounced notification; chain-of-custody; documented for DOLE (W4477)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-83 (occupational health), VS-103 (HRIS), VS-24 (HSE), W4459 (safety-sensitive), W4460 (vendor), W4469 (collection), W4477 (DOLE documentation)

## W4467. Reasonable-Suspicion & For-Cause Drug Testing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Observed signs of impairment; behavior/performance; safety event precursor |
| **Frequency** | Per event (on suspicion) |
| **Volume** | For-cause tests (variable; triggered by observation) |
| **Owner** | Supervisor/Manager / HR (VS-103) / HSE (VS-24) / Occupational Health (VS-83) |
| **Participants** | Supervisors/Managers, HR (VS-103), HSE (VS-24), Occupational Health (VS-83), Security (VS-23), Legal (VS-100), Vendor (W4460) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trained supervisor observes & documents specific, articulable signs of impairment (appearance/behavior/speech/coordination/performance/safety), per reasonable-suspicion training (W4461) | Supervisor / Manager | Supervisor / HSE Lead | Immediate |
| 2 | Supervisor/HR/Occupational Health confer, confirm reasonable suspicion (second observer where possible), arrange immediate for-cause test with vendor, and manage the employee safely (remove from safety-sensitive duty) | Supervisor / HR / Occupational Health / HSE / Vendor | HR / HSE Lead / CHRO | Immediate |
| 3 | Occupational Health/HR/Legal act on result (positive → W4471; negative → resume with appropriate handling), document the reasonable-suspicion record, and protect against wrongful-accusation/discrimination (W4462) | Occupational Health / HR / Legal | CHRO / VP Legal | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS/case (VS-103); occupational health (VS-83); HSE (VS-24); security (VS-23); legal (VS-100); vendor (W4460); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Untrained-observation risk**: non-articulable suspicion → wrongful test/labor challenge; mitigated by supervisor training (W4461) & documentation
- **Safety-while-impaired risk**: employee left in safety-sensitive duty during process → incident; mitigated by immediate removal from duty

### Time Estimate
- Immediate response; documentation hours

### Automation Opportunity
- Reasonable-suspicion documentation app (observation checklist); second-observer workflow; immediate-test scheduling; removal-from-duty tracking

### Controls
- CTL: trained supervisor; articulable documented signs; second observer where possible; immediate removal from safety-sensitive duty; documented; confidentiality/due process

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103 (HR), VS-83 (occupational health), VS-24 (HSE), VS-100 (legal), W4461 (training), W4468 (post-incident), W4471 (case)

## W4468. Post-Incident & Post-Accident Drug Testing (links VS-24/VS-147)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Workplace accident/incident (employee or customer); near-miss of severity |
| **Frequency** | Per incident |
| **Volume** | Post-incident tests (~accident/near-miss rate × severity threshold) |
| **Owner** | HSE (VS-24) / Occupational Health (VS-83) / HR (VS-103) |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), Occupational Health (VS-83), HR (VS-103), Store Ops (VS-07), Customer Safety (VS-147), Legal (VS-100), Vendor (W4460) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HSE/Supervisor trigger post-incident testing per the threshold (any injury/property-damage/near-miss of defined severity, esp. involving safety-sensitive roles/drivers), immediately after securing the scene & care (VS-24/VS-147) | HSE / Supervisor | HSE Lead / CHRO | Immediate |
| 2 | HSE/Occupational Health/Vendor arrange immediate collection (within the detection window), chain-of-custody (W4469), MRO (W4470), and manage the employee (removal from duty, medical care) | HSE / Occupational Health / Vendor | HSE Lead / Occupational Health Lead | Immediate |
| 3 | HSE/HR/Legal use the result in the incident investigation (VS-24/W4402), disciplinary/case (W4471), and any regulatory/insurance notification (VS-26/VS-147 W4405), with confidentiality | HSE / HR / Legal | HSE Lead / VP Legal | Per incident |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE incident (VS-24); occupational health (VS-83); HRIS/case (VS-103); customer safety (VS-147); vendor (W4460); legal (VS-100); insurance (VS-26)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Detection-window-miss risk**: delayed collection → negative despite impairment; mitigated by immediate post-incident testing
- **Wrongful-test risk**: testing without threshold/basis → labor challenge; mitigated by defined threshold policy (W4457)

### Time Estimate
- Immediate response within hours; documentation/investigation hours

### Automation Opportunity
- Post-incident testing trigger from incident system (VS-24/VS-147); immediate vendor dispatch; chain-of-custody; result-to-investigation link

### Controls
- CTL: defined post-incident testing threshold; immediate collection within detection window; chain-of-custody; employee removed from duty & cared for; result used per due process

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE/incident), VS-147 (customer incident), VS-83 (occupational health), VS-26 (insurance), W4457 (policy/threshold), W4469 (collection), W4471 (case)

## W4469. Specimen Collection, Chain-of-Custody & Laboratory Testing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Any test event (W4465–W4468) |
| **Frequency** | Per test |
| **Volume** | All drug tests (~thousands/yr) |
| **Owner** | Occupational Health (VS-83) / Vendor (W4460) |
| **Participants** | Occupational Health (VS-83), Vendor (collection/lab), HR (VS-103), HSE (VS-24), Security (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vendor collect the specimen per DOH/D.O. 53-04 procedure — identity verification, observed/split-specimen protocol, tamper-evidence, and the **chain-of-custody** form signed at every transfer | Vendor / Occupational Health | Occupational Health Lead | Per test |
| 2 | Vendor transport to the DOH-accredited lab, perform the initial screen + confirmatory test (GC-MS or equivalent) on positives, and maintain chain-of-custody/evidence integrity | Vendor / Lab | Occupational Health Lead | Per test |
| 3 | Occupational Health/Vendor return the result + chain-of-custody record to MRO (W4470) and HR (confidentially, W4462), and retain per records policy (VS-88) | Occupational Health / Vendor | Occupational Health Lead | Per test |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor/lab system (W4460); occupational health (VS-83); chain-of-custody record; records (VS-88); privacy (VS-91); compliance (VS-22)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Chain-of-custody-break risk**: gap → challengeable/invalid result (labor/litigation loss); mitigated by tamper-evidence & signed transfer
- **Specimen-integrity risk**: tampering/substitution; mitigated by observed/split-specimen protocol

### Time Estimate
- ~collection minutes; lab turnaround days

### Automation Opportunity
- Chain-of-custody e-form with tamper-evidence; identity verification; lab-integration; split-specimen tracking; evidence-vault

### Controls
- CTL: identity verified; chain-of-custody maintained at every transfer; tamper-evidence/split-specimen; DOH-accredited lab; evidence retained (VS-88)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-83 (occupational health), VS-91 (privacy), VS-88 (records), W4460 (vendor), W4470 (MRO), W4471 (case)

## W4470. MRO Review, Results Validation & Confirmation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Lab result returned (W4469) |
| **Frequency** | Per test |
| **Volume** | All drug-test results reviewed by MRO |
| **Owner** | MRO (Vendor W4460) / Occupational Health (VS-83) |
| **Participants** | MRO, Occupational Health (VS-83), HR (VS-103), Legal (VS-100), Compliance (VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MRO review every positive/invalid result — interview the employee for legitimate medical explanation (valid prescription), verify prescription/medication, and distinguish true-positive from legitimate-medical-use | MRO / Occupational Health | MRO / Occupational Health Lead | Per positive |
| 2 | MRO validate the chain-of-custody/evidence integrity (W4469), confirm via split-specimen re-test on employee request, and issue the verified result | MRO / Lab | MRO | Per result |
| 3 | MRO/Occupational Health return the verified result to HR (confidentially W4462) for action (W4471), retain records (VS-88), and ensure due process (W4471) | MRO / Occupational Health / HR | Occupational Health Lead / CHRO | Per result |

### System Touchpoints
- MRO/lab system (W4460); occupational health (VS-83); HRIS/case (VS-103); records (VS-88); privacy (VS-91); legal (VS-100)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **False-positive risk**: legitimate-medical-use mis-read as positive → wrongful discipline; mitigated by MRO interview & prescription verification
- **Due-process-gap risk**: result acted on before MRO validation; mitigated by MRO-first validation gate

### Time Estimate
- ~MRO review hours per positive; prescription verification

### Automation Opportunity
- MRO-review workflow with prescription-verification; split-specimen re-test tracking; validated-result-to-HRIS (restricted); due-process gate

### Controls
- CTL: MRO reviews every positive; legitimate-medical-use distinguished; chain-of-custody validated; split-specimen on request; acted on only after validation

### Cross-references
- links to VS-83 (occupational health), VS-91 (privacy), VS-88 (records), W4460 (vendor/MRO), W4469 (collection), W4471 (case/due process)

## W4471. Positive/Refusal Result Case Management & Due Process

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Verified positive / refusal-to-test result (W4470) |
| **Frequency** | Per positive/refusal |
| **Volume** | Positive/refusal cases (deterrence-dependent) |
| **Owner** | HR (VS-103) / Labor Relations (VS-84) / Legal (VS-100) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-103), Labor Relations (VS-84), Legal (VS-100), Occupational Health (VS-83), HSE (VS-24), Security (VS-23), CHRO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR/Labor Relations open the case, notify the employee (confidentially W4462), provide the result & evidence, and afford **due process** (right to explain, split-specimen re-test W4470, counsel/union representation per CBA VS-84) | HR / Labor Relations / Legal | CHRO / VP Legal | Per case |
| 2 | HR/Labor Relations/Legal determine the outcome per policy (W4457) & due process — rehabilitation pathway (W4473) for first-time/non-safety-critical; disciplinary/suspension; or separation (W4472) for severe/safety-sensitive/refusal/recurrent | HR / Labor Relations / Legal / CHRO | CHRO / VP Legal | Per case |
| 3 | HR/Legal document the case (records VS-88), manage confidentiality, handle any labor challenge/NLRC (W4472), and feed learning to deterrence analytics (W4478) | HR / Legal / Labor Relations | CHRO / VP Legal | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS/case (VS-103); labor relations (VS-84); legal (VS-100); occupational health (VS-83); records (VS-88); privacy (VS-91); security (VS-23)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Due-process-violation risk**: wrongful/precipitate discipline → NLRC case (W4472); mitigated by due-process protocol & documentation
- **Disparate-treatment risk**: inconsistent outcomes → discrimination claim; mitigated by policy-consistency & review

### Time Estimate
- ~days–weeks per case

### Automation Opportunity
- Case-management workflow with due-process steps; outcome-consistency check; documentation/records; labor-challenge tracking

### Controls
- CTL: case opened confidentially; due process afforded (explain/re-test/representation); outcome per policy & consistent; documented; NLRC-ready

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103 (HR), VS-84 (labor), VS-100 (legal), VS-88 (records), VS-91 (privacy), W4457 (policy), W4470 (MRO), W4472 (discipline/NLRC), W4473 (rehab)

## W4472. Disciplinary, Separation & Legal (NLRC) Handling of Positive Cases

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Outcome decision (W4471) for severe/safety-sensitive/recurrent/refusal cases |
| **Frequency** | Per case |
| **Volume** | Disciplinary/separation cases (subset of positives) |
| **Owner** | Labor Relations (VS-84) / HR (VS-103) / Legal (VS-100) |
| **Participants** | Labor Relations (VS-84), HR (VS-103), Legal (VS-100), CHRO, Security (VS-23), Occupational Health (VS-83) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Labor Relations/HR execute disciplinary/suspension or separation per policy (W4457), the CBA (VS-84), and the Labor Code (due process: notice → hearing → decision), with full documentation | Labor Relations / HR / Legal | CHRO / VP Legal | Per case |
| 2 | Legal/Labor Relations manage any NLRC/DOLE challenge (illegal dismissal, due-process challenge), prepare defense, and represent BuildRight (VS-100) | Legal / Labor Relations | VP Legal | Per challenge |
| 3 | HR/Security manage access/exit, final pay (VS-19.2), and any reference/records (VS-88) with confidentiality (W4462); Occupational Health/HR coordinate rehabilitation alternative where applicable (W4473) | HR / Security / Legal / Occupational Health | CHRO / VP Legal | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Labor relations (VS-84); legal matter (VS-100); HRIS/payroll/final-pay (VS-19.2/VS-103); security/access (VS-23); records (VS-88); privacy (VS-91)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Illegal-dismissal risk**: procedural/substantive due-process failure → NLRC reinstatement/back-pay; mitigated by Labor Code-compliant due process & documentation
- **Reputational/cultural risk**: punitive-only handling harms culture; mitigated by rehabilitation alternative where appropriate (W4473)

### Time Estimate
- ~days–weeks per case; NLRC extends months

### Automation Opportunity
- Disciplinary/separation workflow with Labor Code due-process; documentation/records; NLRC-case management (VS-100); final-pay coordination

### Controls
- CTL: discipline/separation per policy/CBA/Labor Code due process; documented; NLRC-defense ready; access/exit/final-pay managed; confidentiality maintained

### Cross-references
