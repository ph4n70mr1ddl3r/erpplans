## W5089. Customer Print-Job Intake, File Check, Quotation & Order Confirmation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer job submission (counter or portal W5086) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of jobs/month at maturity |
| **Owner** | Reprographics Counter Associate / Store Services Lead |
| **Participants** | Counter Associate, Trade Pro/Account (VS-43), Customer Service (VS-13), Legal (attestation — W5087) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake & capture: receive the file (counter upload or portal W5086), capture job specs (size/media/color/copies/finishing/binding), and capture the customer authorship/IP attestation (W5087) | Counter Associate / Customer Service | Store Services Lead | Per job |
| 2 | File check & pre-flight: validate format (PDF/DWG/DXF), pre-flight (scale/size/color), detect corruption, and confirm printability before quoting | Counter Associate / IT | Store Services Lead | Per job |
| 3 | Quote & confirm: auto/assisted quote per tariff (W5083), confirm with customer, take deposit/prepaid/credit (W5096), and release to production (W5090) | Counter Associate / O2C | Store Services Lead | Per job |

### System Touchpoints
- Print-management/job system (W5086); pricing/tariff (W5083); O2C/prepaid (W5096/VS-16); attestation (W5087); CRM (VS-13)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Bad-file risk**: Corrupt/unsupported file → failed/late job; mitigated by pre-flight check
- **Mis-quote risk**: Quote below cost (W5083); mitigated by tariff-based quoting
- **IP risk**: Reproducing infringing material; mitigated by attestation (W5087)

### Automation Opportunity
- Portal auto-pre-flight & quoting (W5086); attestation e-capture; quote→order auto-flow

### Controls
- CTL-759 (ensure controlled execution — Customer Reprographics Job Lifecycle (PA-176.2).)
- CTL-759: attestation captured (W5087); pre-flight before quote; tariff-based pricing (W5083)

### Time Estimate
- ~5–15 min/job intake + quoting

### Cross-references
- links to VS-13 (CRM), VS-16 (O2C), VS-43 (trade pro); W5083 (pricing), W5086 (portal), W5087 (IP), W5090 (production), W5096 (billing)

## W5090. Large-Format Plan Printing, Plotting & Print-Quality Control

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Confirmed job (W5089) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of prints/month at maturity |
| **Owner** | Reprographics Operator / Store Services Lead |
| **Participants** | Operator, Counter Associate, Quality check, Metrology (VS-115) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set up & print: load media/ink (W5084), set print parameters (scale/DPI/color/profile), send to plotter, and monitor the run for media/ink faults | Operator | Store Services Lead | Per job |
| 2 | Quality-control: inspect output (scale accuracy W5097, color, line definition, no banding/bleed), and re-print defects before finishing (W5092) | Operator / Quality | Store Services Lead | Per job |
| 3 | Log & handoff: log consumables consumed (W5084/W5099), record job completion, and route to finishing/pickup (W5092/W5094) | Operator | Store Services Lead | Per job |

### System Touchpoints
- Print-management/job system; plotter; consumables inventory (W5084); quality/dimensional check (W5097); analytics (W5099)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Defect risk**: Banding/bleed/scale error → rework/waste; mitigated by QC + calibration (VS-115/W5088)
- **Media/ink-fault risk**: Mid-run fault → delay; mitigated by monitoring + safety stock (W5084)
- **Consumables-tracking risk**: Untracked consumption → cost illusion; mitigated by per-job logging (W5099)

### Automation Opportunity
- Plotter status/ink-level monitoring; auto-defect detection; consumables auto-deduct per job

### Controls
- CTL-101 (ensure reprographics IP & file-handling control.)
- CTL-759: QC before finishing (W5097); calibration current (VS-115/W5088); consumables logged (W5099)

### Time Estimate
- ~varies by job (minutes to hours)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-115 (calibration); W5084 (consumables), W5088 (maintenance), W5092 (finishing), W5097 (quality), W5099 (analytics)
