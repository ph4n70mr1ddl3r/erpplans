# VS-176: Blueprint, Reprographics & Large-Format Plan Printing Services

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Blueprint, reprographics and large-format plan-printing/scanning service operations for BuildRight
Depot Corp. — owning the **contractor- and design-professional-facing reprographics service** that
converts BuildRight's ~40% B2B trade base (contractors, builders, developers, architects,
engineers, government-project buyers) and its design/pro capabilities into an in-store value-added
service: reprographics service strategy & catalog; large-format printer/plotter/scanner equipment
selection and procurement; reprographics pricing, cost-per-sqm and tariff strategy; print media and
consumables procurement/inventory; service-counter and self-service workstation setup; cloud/mobile
print-submission portal and file-format standardization; copyright/IP and sensitive-document
handling; equipment maintenance and calibration; customer print-job intake/quotation and order
confirmation; large-format plan printing/plotting and print-quality control; document
scanning/archiving and digitization; plan copying/binding/folding and multi-set reproduction;
customer file storage, reprint-on-demand and version control; job notification, pickup, delivery
and will-call; bulk/tender bid-set and project plan-room reproduction; job billing and
prepaid/credit-term management; print quality and dimensional verification; equipment performance
and consumables-yield analytics; **customer-document confidentiality, RA 10173 data-privacy and
secure disposal**; software-license/cloud-subscription administration; customer satisfaction and
rework; demand seasonality/capacity forecasting; and program ROI/cross-sell analytics.

This matters at BuildRight's B2B mix and service positioning. ~40% of revenue is B2B (trade,
project, government) and the professionals who buy from BuildRight — contractors, architects,
engineers, developers, LGU/project buyers — live in large-format drawings: site plans, floor plans,
structural/electrical/plumbing plans, shop drawings, bid sets, permit sets, as-builts. Large-format
plan printing/scanning/copying (reprographics) is a recognized contractor-facing in-store service
that directly supports BuildRight's "Your Home Building Partner" positioning and its design/pro
capabilities (VS-66 customer project & design, VS-43 trade pro program, VS-46 government/institutional
bids, VS-140 field sales). At ~40% B2B revenue and a busy bid/permit cycle, the service is a
recurring, margin-accretive, dwell- and attach-driving counter that also pulls trade-pro traffic into
the store; a mis-sized or unreliable service (a down plotter on bid day) directly costs a customer's
project. It also carries document-confidentiality, copyright/IP, and RA 10173 data-privacy exposure
(client plans, signed documents, government bids contain sensitive/personal data).

Today this discipline is **unowned as a unified program** — the defining terms "blueprint",
"reprographics", "large-format printing", "plan printing", "plotter", "engineering print", "plan
copying", and "plan scanning" each appear in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers and in
**zero** VS directory names. Adjacent coverage exists only as scattered, conflated references:
**VS-66 (Customer Project & Design Services)** *creates* the design/plans (not prints them at
scale); **VS-14 (Marketing)** produces BuildRight's *own* promotional print; **VS-09 (In-Store
Services)** covers *physical custom fabrication* (glass/pipe/countertop cutting) but not document
reprographics; **VS-46 (Government & Institutional Sales)** and **VS-11 (Trade/Project/Wholesale)**
consume plan-room/bid-set needs as a step but don't operate the reprographics service. No value
stream owns the **reprographics service operating model** — the equipment fleet, the consumables,
the print-job lifecycle, the file/IP/confidentiality controls, and the cost/quality analytics. It is
**not** covered by **VS-66 (Customer Project & Design)**, which *designs* the project — this *prints/
reproduces* the design output (and that of any external architect/engineer). It is **not** covered
by **VS-14 (Marketing)**, which produces *BuildRight's promotional print* — this is a *paid customer
document-reproduction service*. It is **not** covered by **VS-09 (In-Store Services)**, which covers
*physical fabrication* — this is *digital-document reproduction*. And it is **not** covered by
**VS-29 (Master Data) / VS-137 (PIM/DAM)**, which govern *product* data/assets — this operates a
*customer-document service*.

This value stream owns the end-to-end program: reprographics strategy & catalog; equipment
(printer/plotter/scanner) selection, procurement and setup; pricing, cost-per-sqm and tariff;
print-media/consumables procurement and inventory; service-counter & self-service workstation setup;
cloud/mobile print-submission portal and file-format standardization; **copyright/IP and
sensitive-document handling**; equipment maintenance, vendor service contract and calibration
(links VS-115); customer print-job intake, file-check, quotation and order confirmation; large-format
plan printing/plotting and quality control; document scanning, archiving and digitization; plan
copying, binding, folding and multi-set reproduction; customer file storage, reprint-on-demand and
version control; job notification, pickup, delivery and will-call; **bulk/tender bid-set and project
plan-room reproduction**; job billing and prepaid/credit-term management; print quality and
dimensional verification; equipment performance and consumables-yield analytics; **customer-document
confidentiality, RA 10173 data-privacy and secure disposal**; software-license/cloud-subscription
administration; customer satisfaction and rework; demand seasonality/capacity forecasting; and
program ROI/cross-sell analytics. It integrates tightly with VS-66 (design output), VS-43 (trade pro),
VS-46/VS-11 (bids/projects), VS-140 (field sales), VS-09 (in-store services), VS-14 (marketing
print), VS-03/VS-34 (equipment/consumables procurement), VS-35/VS-99 (equipment assets/IT), VS-115
(calibration), VS-91 (data privacy), VS-100 (copyright/IP legal), VS-16 (O2C), and VS-28 (analytics).
