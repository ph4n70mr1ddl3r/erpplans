## W1668. Store Supplies & Consumables Procurement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly replenishment cycle or low stock alert |
| **Frequency** | Monthly blanket PO releases; weekly ad-hoc requests |
| **Volume** | ~200 stores × ~50 supply SKUs = ~10,000 supply items/month |
| **Owner** | Procurement Specialist — Non-Merchandise |
| **Participants** | Procurement Specialist, Store Managers, AP Clerk |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement Specialist manages store supplies catalog: (a) shelf labels and price tags, (b) receipt paper rolls (thermal), (c) shopping bags (paper and plastic per LGU regulations — some Philippine cities ban plastic bags), (d) cleaning supplies, (e) cash register tape, (f) signage materials, (g) go-bags (security bags for cash transport), (h) first aid kit supplies, (i) packing materials (stretch wrap, boxes for ecommerce) | Procurement Specialist | VP Store Operations | Ongoing |
| 2 | Monthly: system generates store supply replenishment suggestions based on: (a) standard supply allocation per store (e.g., 10 rolls of receipt paper per POS terminal per month), (b) usage rate from prior months, (c) special events (promotional signage materials for sale events) | System | Procurement Specialist | Automated |
| 3 | Procurement Specialist reviews and adjusts replenishment quantities; releases blanket PO to contracted suppliers; suppliers deliver to DC for consolidation with merchandise shipments to stores | Procurement Specialist | VP Store Operations | 4 hours/month |
| 4 | Store Managers submit ad-hoc supply requests through system (e.g., unexpected supply consumption, broken equipment replacement); approved by Regional Manager; Procurement Specialist processes via express order | Store Manager / Regional Manager | Procurement Specialist | 15 min/request |

### System Touchpoints
- Store supplies catalog with standard allocation parameters per store (W1668.1)
- Automated replenishment suggestion engine (W1668.2)
- Blanket PO release against annual supply contracts (W1668.3)
- Ad-hoc supply requisition with approval workflow (W1668.4)

## W1669. IT Equipment & Software Procurement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | IT procurement plan, equipment replacement cycle, or ad-hoc request |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly planned procurement; ad-hoc for replacements |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 IT purchase orders/quarter |
| **Owner** | IT Procurement Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Procurement Manager, CIO, IT team, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CIO develops annual IT procurement plan: (a) POS hardware refresh schedule (600 terminals; target 5-year lifecycle = ~120 replacements/year), (b) server and network infrastructure upgrades, (c) employee laptop/desktop refresh (357 HQ + DC office staff), (d) software license renewals (annual), (e) new technology projects (per strategic plan W1645), (f) barcode scanners, RF guns, printers for stores and DCs | CIO | CFO | 1 week |
| 2 | IT Procurement Manager executes procurement: (a) obtain 3 quotes for items > PHP 50K, (b) evaluate: specifications, total cost of ownership, warranty, vendor support capability in Philippines, (c) recommend vendor to CIO, (d) create purchase requisition per W1675 | IT Procurement Manager | CIO | 2 hours/PO |
| 3 | Software procurement specifics: (a) SaaS subscriptions: annual license cost, user count, renewal date, auto-renewal flag, (b) perpetual licenses: maintenance and support fees, upgrade rights, (c) open source: license compliance review by IT, (d) all software procurement requires IT security review before purchase | IT Procurement Manager | CIO | Per item |
| 4 | IT asset tracking: all IT equipment recorded in asset register with: (a) asset tag, (b) assigned user/location, (c) warranty expiry, (d) depreciation schedule, (e) maintenance schedule | IT Procurement Manager / IT Team | CIO | Ongoing |

## W1670. Uniform, PPE & Safety Equipment Procurement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual uniform refresh, new hire onboarding, or PPE replenishment |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly bulk orders; ad-hoc for new hires |
| **Volume** | ~1,200–1,600 new hires/year × 3 sets uniform + quarterly PPE replenishment |
| **Owner** | HR Operations |
| **Participants** | HR Operations, Procurement Specialist, Store Managers, Safety Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR Operations manages uniform and PPE catalog: (a) uniforms: polo shirts (3 per employee), slacks/pants (2 per employee), safety shoes (1 per employee), ID lanyards, name badges, (b) PPE per role: hard hats (lumber yard staff), gloves (receiving clerks), safety goggles (cutting services), masks (paint mixing), high-visibility vests (stockroom), harnesses (high-bay access), (c) seasonal PPE: raincoats (monsoon season), cooling towels (summer) | HR Operations | CHRO | Ongoing |
| 2 | Quarterly: HR Operations forecasts uniform and PPE needs: (a) new hire demand (from recruitment pipeline per W292), (b) replacement demand (wear/tear cycle ~6 months for active roles), (c) regulatory PPE replenishment (per DOLE requirements), (d) creates purchase requisition per W1675 | HR Operations | Procurement Specialist | 1 day/quarter |
| 3 | Procurement Specialist sources from contracted uniform supplier and PPE suppliers; manages delivery to DC for distribution to stores and HQ | Procurement Specialist | VP Store Operations | 2 days/quarter |
| 4 | Store Manager distributes uniforms to new hires during onboarding (W292); PPE issued per role and tracked per employee in system (W1670.4 of HR-017) | Store Manager | HR Operations | 15 min/new hire |

## W1671. Marketing Materials & Print Procurement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Promotional calendar event, campaign launch, or in-store signage refresh |
| **Frequency** | 6 major promotional events/year + monthly marketing activities |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 marketing procurement orders/year |
| **Owner** | Marketing Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Operations, CMO, Procurement Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing Operations Manager manages marketing materials procurement: (a) promotional flyers and catalogs (6 major sale events × 200 stores = 1,200 print runs), (b) in-store signage: banners, shelf talkers, danglers, window displays, (c) outdoor signage: tarpaulins, billboards, (d) digital assets: not physical procurement but vendor management for creative agency, photography, video production, (e) loyalty program materials: membership cards, welcome kits, (f) brand materials: store exterior signage (for new stores), uniforms (coordinated with HR) | Marketing Operations | CMO | Ongoing |
| 2 | Per campaign: Marketing Operations creates print specification: (a) quantity per store (varies by store size and traffic), (b) total print run (200 stores × allocation + 10% buffer), (c) paper quality, dimensions, finish, (d) delivery deadline (must arrive at DC 5 days before campaign launch for distribution to stores), (e) creates purchase requisition per W1675 | Marketing Operations | CMO | 2 hours/campaign |
| 3 | Procurement Specialist obtains quotes from contracted print vendors; evaluates quality, price, and delivery timeline; places order | Procurement Specialist | Marketing Operations | 1 day/campaign |
| 4 | Print materials delivered to DC; consolidated with store replenishment shipments; Store Manager receives and deploys per marketing instructions | DC Operations / Store Manager | Marketing Operations | Per campaign |

## W1672. Facility Maintenance Supplies & Services Procurement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Maintenance schedule, equipment failure, or seasonal maintenance needs |
| **Frequency** | Monthly recurring services; ad-hoc for repairs |
| **Volume** | ~500–700 maintenance POs/month across 205 locations |
| **Owner** | Facilities Manager |
| **Participants** | Facilities Manager, Store Managers, DC Supervisor, Procurement Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities Manager manages contracted maintenance services: (a) aircon/HVAC cleaning and maintenance (quarterly per store — 200 stores × 4 = 800 service visits/year), (b) elevator/escalator maintenance (stores with multi-level format), (c) fire suppression system inspection (annual per BFP requirement), (d) pest control (monthly per store/DC — 205 locations × 12 = 2,460 service visits/year), (e) generator maintenance (per W808), (f) plumbing and electrical repairs, (g) floor maintenance (polishing, repair) | Facilities Manager | VP Store Operations | Ongoing |
| 2 | Store Managers submit maintenance service requests through system: (a) describe issue with photo, (b) urgency level: routine (7-day SLA), urgent (48-hour SLA), emergency (4-hour SLA), (c) system routes to Facilities Manager for approval and vendor dispatch | Store Manager | Facilities Manager | 10 min/request |
| 3 | Facilities Manager dispatches contracted vendor; tracks service completion; vendor submits service report and invoice; Facilities Manager confirms completion and approves invoice for payment | Facilities Manager | VP Store Operations | 15 min/request |
| 4 | Maintenance supplies procurement: (a) light bulbs (LED replacements), (b) cleaning chemicals, (c) paint for touch-ups, (d) plumbing fittings, (e) electrical components (switches, breakers), (f) ordered monthly through blanket PO from contracted suppliers | Facilities Manager | Procurement Specialist | 4 hours/month |

## W1674. Emergency & Urgent Non-Merchandise Purchase Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Urgent operational need (equipment breakdown, storm damage, safety issue) |
| **Frequency** | ~50–100 emergency purchases/month across all locations |
| **Volume** | ~1–2 per store/month; higher during typhoon season |
| **Owner** | Store Manager (store-level); DC Supervisor (DC-level); Department Head (HQ) |
| **Participants** | Requesting manager, Finance, Procurement Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Requesting manager creates emergency purchase request in system: (a) description and justification, (b) estimated cost, (c) urgency level: (i) **Emergency** — safety or operational continuity threat (e.g., roof leak during typhoon, POS terminal failure, security system failure), (ii) **Urgent** — significant operational impact if not resolved within 48 hours (e.g., aircon failure in peak summer, forklift breakdown at DC), (d) emergency requests bypass standard 3-quote requirement for items < PHP 50K | Requesting Manager | — | 10 min |
| 2 | Approval workflow: (a) < PHP 10K: Store Manager or DC Supervisor self-approve, (b) PHP 10–50K: Regional Manager or VP approval, (c) > PHP 50K: CFO approval, (d) emergency flag expedites approval — SLA: < 2 hours for emergency, < 4 hours for urgent | Approver | — | Per SLA |
| 3 | Requesting manager procures from available vendor (often local supplier for speed); submits receipt and documentation for reimbursement or direct payment | Requesting Manager | Finance | Variable |
| 4 | Post-emergency: Finance reviews emergency purchases monthly for: (a) total spend by location, (b) pattern analysis (same location recurring emergencies → indicates need for planned maintenance or capital investment), (c) policy compliance (were emergency procedures followed?) | Finance | CFO | 2 hours/month |

## W1675. Non-Merchandise Purchase Requisition to Payment Workflow

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Any non-merchandise purchase need identified |
| **Frequency** | ~1,500–2,000 non-merchandise purchase requisitions/month |
| **Volume** | ~8–10 per location/month + HQ departmental purchases |
| **Owner** | Procurement Specialist |
| **Participants** | Requesting employee, Approving manager, Procurement Specialist, AP Clerk |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Requesting employee creates purchase requisition in system: (a) item/service description, (b) quantity and estimated cost, (c) preferred vendor (if known), (d) delivery location, (e) required-by date, (f) budget category and cost center | Requesting Employee | — | 10 min |
| 2 | Approval workflow (tiered by amount): (a) < PHP 5K: direct supervisor, (b) PHP 5–50K: department head, (c) PHP 50–500K: VP + Finance Manager, (d) > PHP 500K: CFO + CEO, (e) budget check: system verifies spend against departmental budget; over-budget requisitions require VP + Finance approval | System / Approver | — | Per SLA (24 hours standard) |
| 3 | Procurement Specialist processes approved requisition: (a) < PHP 50K: order from contracted or preferred vendor (no competitive quote required), (b) PHP 50–500K: obtain minimum 3 quotes, (c) > PHP 500K: formal bidding process with bid evaluation committee, (d) create PO and send to vendor | Procurement Specialist | Finance Manager | 1–5 days |
| 4 | Goods/service received: (a) requesting employee confirms receipt and quality in system, (b) for services: confirm service completion and satisfaction, (c) system performs 2-way match (PO → Invoice) for non-merchandise purchases (no GR for services) | Requesting Employee | — | 5 min |
| 5 | AP Clerk processes payment per standard P2P workflow (W89/W90) — 2-way match for non-PO items; 3-way match for PO-based items with goods receipt | AP Clerk | Controller | Per W89 |
