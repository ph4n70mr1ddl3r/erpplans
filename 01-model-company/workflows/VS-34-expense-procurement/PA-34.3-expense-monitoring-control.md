## W1683. Monthly Department Expense Budget vs. Actual Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly financial close cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~15 departments × ~30 expense line items = ~450 data points |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, all department heads, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates monthly expense budget vs. actual report per department by Day 3: (a) actual spend by expense category vs. monthly budget and YTD budget, (b) variance amount and %, (c) forecast for remaining months (based on run rate and known commitments) | System | FP&A | Automated |
| 2 | FP&A reviews departmental expense reports: (a) flags variances > 5% of monthly budget or > PHP 100K, (b) identifies root cause categories (one-time vs. recurring), (c) assesses YTD implications for full-year budget | FP&A | CFO | 2 hours |
| 3 | Department heads review their expense reports: (a) explain significant variances to FP&A, (b) identify corrective actions if over budget, (c) confirm or adjust remaining year forecast | Department Heads | FP&A | 1 hour/department |
| 4 | CFO reviews consolidated expense performance at monthly operating review (W1653): (a) total OpEx vs. budget, (b) department-level highlights, (c) action items for over-budget departments | CFO | CEO | Per W1653 |

## W1684. Store-Level Controllable Expense Monitoring & Benchmarking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly store scorecard generation (W1655) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | 200 stores |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, Regional Managers, Store Managers, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System calculates monthly controllable expense KPIs per store: (a) utilities per sqm (electricity, water), (b) maintenance cost as % of revenue, (c) supplies cost per transaction, (d) shrinkage as % of revenue, (e) overtime cost as % of payroll, (f) total controllable expense as % of revenue | System | VP Store Operations | Automated |
| 2 | VP Store Operations benchmarks stores: (a) compare each store to cluster average (stores of similar size and format), (b) compare each store to chain average, (c) identify top 10 and bottom 10 stores by controllable expense ratio, (d) trend analysis (improving vs. declining) | VP Store Operations | COO | 2 hours/month |
| 3 | Regional Manager coaches bottom 10 stores: (a) review specific expense categories driving high costs, (b) identify root causes (e.g., high electricity → aging aircon units; high maintenance → deferred maintenance), (c) agree on action plans with Store Managers | Regional Manager | VP Store Operations | 30 min/store |
| 4 | Best practice sharing: VP Store Operations communicates expense management practices from top 10 performing stores to bottom 10 through regional manager network | VP Store Operations | — | 1 hour/month |

## W1685. Company Expense Card & Corporate Credit Card Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Corporate credit card program for authorized personnel |
| **Frequency** | Monthly statement reconciliation; ongoing compliance monitoring |
| **Volume** | ~50–80 corporate cards issued (C-Suite, department heads, regional managers, selected store managers) |
| **Owner** | Finance — Treasury |
| **Participants** | Cardholder, Finance, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Corporate card issuance: (a) authorized positions: C-Suite, VP-level, department heads, regional managers, selected store managers (for emergency purchases), (b) credit limit per card tier: Executive (PHP 500K), Department Head (PHP 200K), Regional Manager (PHP 100K), Store Manager (PHP 50K), (c) cardholder signs cardholder agreement with usage policy | Finance | CFO | Per issuance |
| 2 | Monthly: cardholder reconciles statement: (a) match each transaction to expense category and budget code, (b) attach receipts/invoices for all transactions > PHP 1,000, (c) submit reconciled statement to Finance by 5th of month | Cardholder | Finance | 30 min/month |
| 3 | Finance reviews reconciliation: (a) verify receipts attached, (b) check for policy violations (personal expenses, unauthorized merchants, excessive amounts), (c) process payment (auto-debit from corporate bank account) | Finance | CFO | 4 hours/month |
| 4 | Quarterly: Finance audits corporate card usage: (a) compliance rate (receipts provided ÷ total transactions), (b) policy violation rate, (c) average spend per card, (d) unusual patterns | Finance | CFO | 4 hours/quarter |

## W1686. Travel & Business Entertainment Expense Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee business travel or entertainment event |
| **Frequency** | ~200–300 travel/entertainment expense reports/month |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 per department per month |
| **Owner** | HR / Finance |
| **Participants** | Traveling employee, Approving manager, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Travel authorization: (a) employee creates travel request in system: destination, dates, purpose, estimated cost, (b) approval: < PHP 20K by direct supervisor; < PHP 50K by department head; > PHP 50K by VP, (c) travel booking: through designated travel agency or approved booking platform (for cost control) | Employee / Approver | Finance | 30 min |
| 2 | Per diem and reimbursement rates (Philippine standard): (a) domestic travel per diem: PHP 1,500/day (covers meals and incidentals), (b) hotel: actual cost up to tier limit (Executive: PHP 5K/night; Manager: PHP 3K/night; Staff: PHP 2K/night), (c) transportation: economy class for domestic flights; business class for international only with VP approval; local transport: taxi/Grab with receipts | Finance | CFO | Policy |
| 3 | Post-travel: employee submits expense report within 5 business days: (a) itemized expenses with receipts, (b) per diem calculation (days × rate), (c) actual vs. pre-approved budget comparison, (d) approver reviews and approves | Employee / Approver | Finance | 30 min |
| 4 | Finance processes reimbursement: (a) verify compliance with policy, (b) deduct advances if any, (c) reimburse via payroll or separate bank transfer, (d) GL posting to correct department and expense category | Finance | CFO | 10 min/report |

## W1687. Utility Expense Monitoring & Energy Cost Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly utility bill processing |
| **Frequency** | Monthly bill review; quarterly energy optimization review |
| **Volume** | 200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ = ~205 utility accounts (electricity); ~205 water accounts |
| **Owner** | Facilities Manager |
| **Participants** | Facilities Manager, VP Store Operations, Store Managers, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly: Finance processes utility bills: (a) electricity bills from Meralco (Metro Manila/Luzon), Davao Light (Mindanao), VECO (Cebu), and other regional electric cooperatives, (b) water bills from local water districts, (c) system records bill amount per location per month | Finance / System | Facilities Manager | 2 days/month |
| 2 | Facilities Manager monitors utility KPIs: (a) electricity cost per sqm per month (benchmark: PHP 80–120/sqm/month for warehouse retail), (b) water cost per sqm per month, (c) trend analysis: month-over-month and year-over-year, (d) flags locations with > 20% increase vs. same month prior year | Facilities Manager | VP Store Operations | 4 hours/month |
| 3 | Energy optimization opportunities: (a) LED lighting retrofit (stores still using fluorescent — ROI ~18 months), (b) aircon temperature standardization (set at 24–25°C per DOE recommendation), (c) motion-sensor lighting in backroom/warehouse areas, (d) solar panel installation for stores with large roof area (Philippine solar viability is high — ROI ~4–5 years), (e) generator fuel optimization per W808 | Facilities Manager | VP Store Operations / CFO | Quarterly |
| 4. | Annual: Facilities Manager and Finance evaluate energy optimization investments: (a) total utility spend (~PHP 300–500M/year for 200 stores + 4 DCs), (b) savings from implemented measures, (c) proposed new investments with ROI analysis, (d) present to CFO for capex budget inclusion | Facilities Manager / Finance | CFO | 1 week/year |

## W1688. Petty Cash Management & Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Petty cash disbursement or replenishment request |
| **Frequency** | Daily disbursements; bi-weekly or monthly replenishment |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × PHP 20K float + 4 DCs × PHP 50K float = PHP 6M total petty cash |
| **Owner** | Store Manager (store); DC Supervisor (DC) |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, DC Supervisor, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Petty cash disbursement (per FIN-017): (a) employee requests petty cash for small expense (< PHP 5K per transaction; > PHP 5K requires purchase requisition per W1675), (b) Store Manager or DC Supervisor approves and disburses cash, (c) employee provides receipt within 3 business days, (d) custodian records transaction in petty cash log | Store Manager / DC Supervisor | Finance | 5 min/transaction |
| 2 | Petty cash replenishment: (a) when float drops below 30% (PHP 6K for stores; PHP 15K for DCs), custodian submits replenishment request with all receipts attached, (b) Finance reviews receipts for compliance, (c) approves replenishment, (d) fund transfer or cash delivery via CIT service per FIN-025 | Custodian / Finance | Controller | 30 min |
| 3 | Monthly reconciliation: (a) custodian counts physical cash on hand, (b) system calculates expected balance (float − disbursements + replenishments), (c) variance documented and investigated, (d) > PHP 500 variance requires Store Manager explanation, (e) Finance audits random sample of 10 stores per month for petty cash compliance | Custodian / Finance | Controller | 30 min/location |

## W1689. Expense Policy Compliance Monitoring & Audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly compliance monitoring cycle; quarterly internal audit |
| **Frequency** | Monthly monitoring; quarterly audit |
| **Volume** | ~1,500–2,000 expense transactions/month |
| **Owner** | Internal Audit |
| **Participants** | Internal Audit, Finance, department heads, Loss Prevention |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly: Finance monitors expense policy compliance: (a) transactions without proper approval, (b) transactions exceeding budget without override approval, (c) duplicate expense claims, (d) expense reports submitted late (> 5 business days after travel), (e) missing receipts on corporate card transactions, (f) petty cash transactions exceeding PHP 5K limit | Finance | Controller | 4 hours/month |
| 2 | Quarterly: Internal Audit conducts expense audit: (a) sample 50–100 expense transactions across departments, (b) verify compliance with approval thresholds, documentation requirements, and policy limits, (c) test petty cash at 10 random stores, (d) test corporate card transactions for 10 cardholders, (e) report findings with severity rating and corrective actions | Internal Audit | VP Internal Audit | 1 week/quarter |
| 3 | Findings communicated to department heads; corrective actions tracked to closure; repeat findings escalated to CFO and CEO | Internal Audit / Finance | CFO | Ongoing |
| 4. Annual: Finance reviews and updates expense policy based on: (a) audit findings and common compliance gaps, (b) changes in Philippine tax regulations (BIR requirements for deductible expenses), (c) organizational changes (new roles, new locations), (d) communicate policy updates to all employees | Finance | CFO | 1 week/year |
