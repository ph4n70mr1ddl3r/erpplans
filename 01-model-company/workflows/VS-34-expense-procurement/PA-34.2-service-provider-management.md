## W1676. Annual Service Contract Renewal & Renegotiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contract expiry alert (90 days before expiry) |
| **Frequency** | ~80–120 contract renewals/year |
| **Volume** | ~8–10 renewals/month |
| **Owner** | Procurement Specialist |
| **Participants** | Procurement Specialist, Contract Owner (department head), Finance, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System alerts Procurement Specialist of upcoming contract expirations (90 days); contract owner (department head) receives notification to assess service quality and renewal interest | System | Procurement Specialist | Automated |
| 2 | Contract owner assesses: (a) service quality over past year (per SLA monitoring W1678), (b) pricing competitiveness (market benchmark), (c) strategic fit (are needs changing?), (d) recommends: renew, renegotiate, or retender | Contract Owner | — | 2 hours/contract |
| 3 | **Renew**: if service quality satisfactory and pricing competitive, Procurement Specialist renews at existing or inflation-adjusted terms (CPI + 1–2% typical for Philippine service contracts) | Procurement Specialist | Contract Owner | 2 hours |
| 4 | **Renegotiate**: if service quality issues or pricing above market, Procurement Specialist: (a) obtains competitive quotes from alternative providers, (b) negotiates improved terms with current provider (using competitive quotes as leverage), (c) presents options to contract owner with recommendation | Procurement Specialist | Contract Owner / Finance | 1 week |
| 5 | **Retender**: if significant dissatisfaction or high-value contract (> PHP 5M/year), Procurement Specialist conducts formal bidding process: (a) prepare request for proposal (RFP) with specifications, (b) invite 3–5 qualified bidders, (c) evaluate bids: price (40%), quality/capability (30%), track record (20%), terms (10%), (d) recommend to contract owner | Procurement Specialist | Contract Owner / Finance / Legal | 4 weeks |
| 6 | Finance approves contract value; Legal reviews contract terms; signing authority per corporate policy (Board resolution for contracts > PHP 10M) | Finance / Legal | CFO / CEO | 1 week |

## W1677. New Service Provider Evaluation & Onboarding

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New service need identified or retender decision from W1676 |
| **Frequency** | ~20–30 new service provider evaluations/year |
| **Volume** | ~2–3 per month |
| **Owner** | Procurement Specialist |
| **Participants** | Procurement Specialist, Contract Owner, Finance, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contract Owner defines service requirements: (a) scope of services, (b) expected volume and frequency, (c) SLA expectations, (d) geographic coverage (nationwide across 200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ), (e) budget range | Contract Owner | Procurement Specialist | 4 hours |
| 2 | Procurement Specialist sources potential providers: (a) market research (Philippine market), (b) industry referrals, (c) invite 3–5 providers to bid, (d) provide RFP with detailed requirements | Procurement Specialist | Contract Owner | 1 week |
| 3 | Evaluate proposals: (a) capability assessment: can they serve 200+ locations across Philippine islands?, (b) financial stability: check business registration, financial references, (c) reference checks: contact existing clients (preferably in retail), (d) pilot or site visit if high-value contract | Procurement Specialist / Contract Owner | Finance | 1 week |
| 4 | Select provider; negotiate contract terms: (a) pricing and payment terms, (b) SLAs with penalty/incentive clauses, (c) termination provisions, (d) insurance requirements, (e) data privacy compliance (RA 10173 if provider handles customer data), (f) Legal review | Procurement Specialist / Legal | CFO | 1 week |
| 5 | Onboard provider: (a) system setup (vendor master, contract record), (b) service commencement communication to all affected locations, (c) training/orientation on BuildRight processes and systems (if applicable), (d) 30-day post-commencement review | Procurement Specialist / Contract Owner | — | 1 week |

## W1678. Service Level Agreement (SLA) Monitoring & Performance Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Ongoing monitoring cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly SLA reporting; quarterly review meetings with providers |
| **Volume** | ~40–60 active service contracts with SLAs |
| **Owner** | Contract Owner (department head) |
| **Participants** | Contract Owner, Procurement Specialist, Service Provider |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System tracks SLA metrics per contract (automated where possible): (a) response time (e.g., maintenance: 4-hour emergency response), (b) resolution time (e.g., IT helpdesk: 24-hour resolution), (c) uptime (e.g., POS systems: 99.9%), (d) delivery accuracy (e.g., 3PL: on-time delivery %), (e) quality metrics (e.g., cleaning: inspection score), (f) customer satisfaction (e.g., security: incident rate) | System / Contract Owner | — | Automated + monthly |
| 2 | Monthly: Contract Owner reviews SLA performance report: (a) SLA achievement rate per metric, (b) trend analysis (improving/declining), (c) SLA breaches with root cause, (d) penalty/incentive calculations per contract terms | Contract Owner | Procurement Specialist | 1 hour/month |
| 3 | Quarterly: Contract Owner and Procurement Specialist meet with service provider for SLA review: (a) present performance data, (b) discuss improvement areas, (c) agree on action plan for underperforming areas, (d) document meeting minutes with action items | Contract Owner / Procurement Specialist | — | 1 hour/provider |
| 4 | SLA breach penalties applied: (a) Procurement Specialist calculates penalty per contract terms, (b) Finance deducts penalty from next payment, (c) chronic breach (> 3 consecutive months) triggers contract review per W1676 | Procurement Specialist / Finance | CFO | Per breach |

## W1679. Professional Services Engagement & Statement of Work Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Need for specialized professional services (consulting, legal, audit, design) |
| **Frequency** | ~30–50 professional services engagements/year |
| **Volume** | ~3–4 per month |
| **Owner** | Engaging Department Head |
| **Participants** | Department Head, Procurement Specialist, Finance, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Department Head identifies need for professional services: (a) management consulting (e.g., organizational restructuring, process improvement), (b) legal services (e.g., contract review, litigation, regulatory compliance), (c) external audit (e.g., financial statement audit, internal audit co-sourcing), (d) design services (e.g., store layout, marketing creative), (e) engineering (e.g., structural assessment, construction management) | Department Head | C-Suite sponsor | 2 hours |
| 2 | Department Head creates statement of work (SOW): (a) objectives and deliverables, (b) scope and exclusions, (c) timeline and milestones, (d) resource requirements (consultant hours, team composition), (e) acceptance criteria, (f) budget estimate | Department Head | — | 4 hours |
| 3 | Procurement Specialist sources and evaluates providers: (a) for recurring needs (legal, audit): use retained firms, (b) for project-based needs: obtain 2–3 proposals, (c) evaluate: expertise, track record, team composition, fees, availability, (d) recommend to engaging department head | Procurement Specialist | Department Head | 1 week |
| 4 | Engagement setup: (a) sign SOW or engagement letter, (b) NDA (non-disclosure agreement) signed per W243, (c) system creates service PO with SOW reference, (d) budget allocated from department OpEx | Department Head / Legal | CFO | 2 days |
| 5 | During engagement: Department Head monitors progress against SOW milestones; approves deliverables; authorizes milestone payments upon acceptance | Department Head | C-Suite sponsor | Ongoing |
| 6 | At engagement completion: (a) final deliverable accepted, (b) final invoice processed, (c) provider performance evaluation recorded, (d) lessons learned documented | Department Head | — | 2 hours |

## W1680. Third-Party Logistics (3PL) Service Contract Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | 3PL contract management cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly monitoring; quarterly review; annual renewal |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 active 3PL contracts (80% of fleet is third-party per model company profile) |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Coordinator, VP Supply Chain, Procurement Specialist, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics Coordinator manages 3PL contracts for: (a) DC-to-store delivery trucks (80% of outbound fleet = ~80–100 trucks), (b) last-mile delivery partners (Lalamove, Transportify for ecommerce), (c) inter-island ferry transport, (d) port drayage (container pickup from port to DC) | Logistics Coordinator | VP Supply Chain | Ongoing |
| 2 | Monthly SLA tracking: (a) on-time pickup rate (target: > 95%), (b) on-time delivery rate (target: > 95%), (c) damage rate during transport (target: < 0.5%), (d) vehicle availability rate, (e) driver compliance (uniform, PPE, behavior), (f) cost per delivery vs. contract rate | Logistics Coordinator | VP Supply Chain | 4 hours/month |
| 3 | Monthly freight cost reconciliation: (a) verify 3PL invoices against delivery records, (b) rate verification per contract, (c) fuel surcharge calculation verification (per DOE fuel price index), (d) penalty deduction for SLA breaches | Finance / Logistics Coordinator | CFO | 1 day/month |
| 4 | Annual contract review per W1676; retender if performance below SLA or market rates more competitive | Logistics Coordinator / Procurement Specialist | VP Supply Chain | Per W1676 |

## W1681. Security & Cleaning Service Provider Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Ongoing service management cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly monitoring; quarterly review |
| **Volume** | ~200 store security guards + 200 store cleaning contracts + 4 DC security + HQ security |
| **Owner** | Facilities Manager |
| **Participants** | Facilities Manager, Store Managers, Security Agency, Cleaning Agency |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities Manager manages security service: (a) contracted security agency provides guards for all 200 stores (typically 1 guard per store during operating hours; 2 for high-risk locations), (b) DC security: 24/7 coverage at 4 DCs, (c) HQ security: reception and after-hours, (d) guard rotation and deployment scheduling, (e) guard licensing verification (per SOGO - Supervisory Office for Security and Investigation Agencies) | Facilities Manager | VP Store Operations | Ongoing |
| 2 | Monthly: Store Manager rates security guard performance: (a) attendance and punctuality, (b) alertness and professionalism, (c) incident response, (d) report submission; ratings aggregated by security agency for quarterly review | Store Manager | Facilities Manager | 15 min/month |
| 3 | Cleaning service management: (a) contracted cleaning service provides daily cleaning for all stores (before/after hours), (b) DC deep cleaning quarterly, (c) monthly cleanliness inspection by Facilities Manager or Regional Manager (scored 1–5) | Facilities Manager | VP Store Operations | Ongoing |
| 4 | Quarterly: Facilities Manager reviews security and cleaning agency performance with Procurement Specialist; addresses issues; plans contract renewals per W1676 | Facilities Manager / Procurement Specialist | VP Store Operations | 2 hours/quarter |

## W1682. Service Provider Contract Termination & Transition Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contract non-renewal decision or early termination due to performance failure |
| **Frequency** | ~5–10 terminations/transitions/year |
| **Volume** | Variable |
| **Owner** | Procurement Specialist |
| **Participants** | Procurement Specialist, Contract Owner, Legal, Finance, incoming provider |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contract Owner initiates termination: (a) documents reason (performance failure, strategic change, cost reduction), (b) reviews contract termination provisions (notice period — typically 30–90 days; early termination fees), (c) obtains approval from VP and Finance | Contract Owner | Procurement Specialist | 1 day |
| 2 | Legal reviews termination for compliance with contract terms and Philippine labor law (if outsourced staff at BuildRight locations are affected — security guards, cleaners may have tenure rights under DOLE DO 174) | Legal | VP Legal | 2 days |
| 3 | Procurement Specialist provides formal notice to provider per contract terms; initiates transition planning with incoming provider (identified per W1677) | Procurement Specialist | Contract Owner | 1 day |
| 4 | Transition execution: (a) phase-out schedule (overlapping with new provider for 2–4 weeks), (b) asset return (if provider has BuildRight equipment/materials), (c) final invoice and payment settlement, (d) outstanding SLA penalty deduction, (e) knowledge transfer to incoming provider | Procurement Specialist / Contract Owner | — | 2–4 weeks |
| 5 | Post-transition: Contract Owner monitors new provider performance closely for first 30 days; compares to predecessor performance | Contract Owner | — | 30 days |
