# VS-34: Expense & Non-Merchandise Procurement

> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Manages procurement of all non-merchandise goods and services required to operate 200 stores, 4 DCs, and corporate HQ — including store supplies, cleaning materials, IT equipment, marketing/print materials, uniform/PPE, facility maintenance services, and professional services. Distinct from VS-03 (merchandise procurement for resale), this value stream addresses the operational expenditure (OpEx) side of purchasing.
