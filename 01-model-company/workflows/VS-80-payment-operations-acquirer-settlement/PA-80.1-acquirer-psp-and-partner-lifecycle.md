## W2777. Acquirer & Card Scheme Registration, MID/MCC Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New store opening, new tender type, or acquirer expansion |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~10–15 new stores/year + periodic |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × ~4 acquirer relationships (BDO, BPI, Metrobank, Chinabank) |
| **Owner** | Payment Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Payment Ops Mgr, IT, Store Ops, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payment Ops Manager submits merchant application to acquiring bank per entity/location; provides SEC docs, BIR 2303, mayor's permit, store photos; specifies MID and MCC | Payment Ops Manager | Finance Director | 2–4 weeks/round |
| 2 | IT configures terminal profiles (Visa/Mastercard/JCB/UnionPay/BancNet accept marks), MID/TID, and settlement routing in POS terminal management system | IT | Payment Ops Manager | 1–2 days/store |
| 3 | Payment Ops Manager validates test transactions (live + reversal) end-to-end before go-live; archives acquirer confirmation and fee schedule | Payment Ops Manager | Finance Director | 1 day/store |

### System Touchpoints
- POS terminal management system; acquirer onboarding portal; merchant master

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Onboarding risk**: Incomplete KYC/permit docs delay store opening; mitigated by 8-week pre-opening checklist


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-submit (ops manager submits merchant application to)
- auto-file (configures terminal profiles (visa/mastercard/jcb/unionpay/bancnet accept marks),)
- auto-validate (ops manager validates test transactions (live)

### Controls
operational: 8-week pre-opening checklist

### Time Estimate
- 4–6 weeks per store payment setup

## W2778. E-Wallet (GCash/Maya) Merchant Onboarding & QR/Intent Integration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New e-wallet partner or store enablement |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven + annual review |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × 2 e-wallets; ~15% of POS tender |
| **Owner** | Payment Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Payment Ops Mgr, Ecommerce, IT, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payment Ops Manager registers merchant accounts with GCash/Maya per entity; configures dynamic QR, merchant-intent API, and cash-in/cash-out capability | Payment Ops Manager | Finance Director | 2–3 weeks |
| 2 | IT integrates e-wallet API with POS (QR generation, payment notification callback, status polling) and ecommerce checkout; certifies sandbox testing | IT / Ecommerce | Payment Ops Manager | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Finance validates daily settlement files (DCS) vs. POS capture; reconciles e-wallet fee deductions (1.5–2%) and rebates; archives partner agreements | Finance | Finance Director | Per settlement |

- E-wallet merchant portal; POS QR/intent integration; daily settlement file ingestion

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Operational risk**: QR/payment-intent API downtime causes checkout failure; mitigated by fallback to manual QR + status reconciliation


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-count (registers merchant accounts with gcash/maya per)
- auto-validate (and ecommerce checkout; certifies sandbox testing)
- auto-file (daily settlement files (dcs) vs. pos)

### Controls
operational: fallback to manual QR + status reconciliation

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 weeks per partner onboarding

## W2783. Payment Partner Offboarding & Business Continuity Cutover

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Partner termination, breach, or replacement |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; rare |
| **Volume** | ~1–2 events/decade |
| **Owner** | Payment Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Payment Ops Mgr, IT, Store Ops, Finance, BCM |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payment Ops Manager plans offboarding timeline with replacement partner onboarding; coordinates dual-running window; notifies card schemes and terminals of MID change | Payment Ops Manager / BCM | CFO | 2–4 weeks planning |
| 2 | IT executes terminal profile updates in waves; tests fallback routing; ensures in-flight settlements and pending disputes are mapped to new partner | IT | Payment Ops Manager | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Finance closes old partner GL accounts; reconciles final settlement and reserves; archives documentation; updates BCM runbook with lesson learned | Finance / BCM | CFO | 1 month |

### System Touchpoints
- Terminal management system; settlement reconciliation; BCM runbook

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Continuity risk**: Cutover gap causes tender outage at POS; mitigated by dual-running window and rollback plan


### Automation Opportunity
- workflow orchestration (partner onboarding; coordinates dual-running window; notifies)
- auto-file (executes terminal profile updates in waves;)
- auto-document (reserves; archives documentation; updates bcm runbook)

### Controls
operational: dual-running window and rollback plan

### Time Estimate
- 6–10 weeks per offboarding

## W2784. Multi-Entity Merchant Settlement Hierarchy & GL Mapping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New entity, new partner, or restructure |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven + annual validation |
| **Volume** | 5 entities × ~10 partners |
| **Owner** | Payment Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Payment Ops Mgr, Finance, Master Data |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Master Data maintains settlement hierarchy mapping MID/Partner/Entity to GL clearing, fee, and bank settlement accounts; validates uniqueness and completeness | Master Data | Payment Operations Manager | Per change |
| 2 | Finance configures per-entity settlement GL posting rules; ensures intercompany clearing for cross-entity transactions (e.g., Digital Commerce collects, Depot fulfills) | Finance | Finance Director | Per change |
| 3 | Payment Ops Manager runs annual integrity test: simulate settlement file across all entities; verify GL postings and bank credits reconcile to expected | Payment Ops Manager / Finance | CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Settlement hierarchy master; GL posting rules; intercompany clearing engine

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reconciliation risk**: Mis-mapped MID causes wrong-entity revenue recognition; mitigated by annual integrity test and segregation controls


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-validate (settlement accounts; validates uniqueness and completeness)
- auto-post (settlement gl posting rules; ensures intercompany)
- auto-file (simulate settlement file across all entities;)

### Controls
operational: annual integrity test and segregation controls

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per annual validation
