> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Payment Operations, Acquirer & Settlement Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
covering the partner lifecycle and settlement operations for the full tender mix supporting
~2.8M POS transactions and ~42,900 ecommerce orders/month: cash, credit/debit cards (Visa,
Mastercard, JCB, UnionPay, BancNet), e-wallets (GCash, Maya), direct debit (PESONet/InstaPay),
BNPL, and corporate charge accounts.

While treasury (VS-18) owns cash positioning and bank-account management, and POS (VS-08)
handles tender capture, this value stream owns the **payment acceptance and settlement program**:
acquirer/PSP onboarding, terminal fleet configuration, batch settlement reconciliation,
chargeback representment, interchange/MDR cost optimization, and payment fraud governance.
