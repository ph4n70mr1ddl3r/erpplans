## W4049. Operational Excellence Strategy, Charter & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program stand-up; annual strategy refresh; CEO/Board mandate |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on strategic change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise program (1 OpEx function serving 5 entities) |
| **Owner** | VP Operational Excellence / Chief Transformation Officer |
| **Participants** | COO, CFO (links to VS-17.4), CIO (links to VS-27), CHRO (links to VS-103), Internal Audit (links to VS-21), PMO (links to VS-112), all function VPs |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP OpEx define the operating model: a centralized OpEx CoE with embedded improvement leads in Stores, DC, Supply Chain, Finance; the relationship to audit findings (VS-21), the PMO (VS-112), innovation (VS-30), and HR/OCM (VS-134); and the value-driver targets (margin, turns, cycle-time, NPS) | VP OpEx / COO Office | CEO / COO | Annual |
| 2 | VP OpEx author the charter: scope (all cross-functional processes), decision rights, the improvement-pipeline governance, and the benefit-realization accountability to Finance (links to VS-17.4) | VP OpEx / Finance | COO / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | CEO/Board ratify the OpEx charter, approve resourcing, and set it as a board-level performance lever (links to VS-36 governance and VS-33 corporate performance) | CEO / Board | Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy/performance (links to VS-33); governance (links to VS-36); finance/benefit tracking (links to VS-17.4); project portfolio (links to VS-112)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-owner risk**: Improvement done ad-hoc by individuals with no compounding benefit; mitigated by a chartered CoE and pipeline governance
- **Sub-optimization risk**: Local improvement that harms end-to-end flow; mitigated by enterprise process architecture (W4050) and value-stream lens

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual

### Automation Opportunity
- Strategy/charter workflow automation; benefit-target cascading from corporate plan into OpEx scorecard

### Controls
- CTL-629 (ensure controlled execution — OpEx Strategy, Governance & Improvement Methodology (PA-133.1).)
- CTL governance: OpEx charter and value targets approved by Board; benefit realization reconciled by Finance (segregation between OpEx sponsor and Finance verifier)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21 (audit findings feed), VS-30 (innovation/automation), VS-33 (strategy), VS-112 (PMO), VS-134 (OCM)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OpEx maintain the enterprise process architecture (value streams → process areas → workflows) mapped to the value-stream-index, assign a single process owner (RACI A) per end-to-end process, and resolve ownership gaps/overlaps | OpEx CoE / Process Owners | VP OpEx | Maintained |

- Maintained; ~1 FTE ongoing + annual review

## W4052. Improvement Opportunity Pipeline & Prioritization (Impact-Effort)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous (intake) + monthly review |
| **Frequency** | Continuous intake; monthly prioritization |
| **Volume** | Enterprise pipeline (hundreds of opportunities/yr from stores, DC, functions, audit, VOC) |
| **Owner** | VP Operational Excellence |
| **Participants** | OpEx CoE, process owners, Internal Audit (links to VS-21), CX/VOC (links to VS-13/VS-44), Finance (links to VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OpEx maintain a single improvement-opportunity pipeline ingesting from all sources: store/DC frontline suggestions (W4056), audit findings (VS-21), VOC/complaints (VS-13), process-mining bottlenecks (W4058), and strategy gaps (VS-33) | OpEx CoE / Process Owners | VP OpEx | Continuous |
| 2 | OpEx/Finance score each opportunity on impact (margin/turn/cycle-time/NPS/risk) and effort, and build the impact-effort matrix for prioritization | OpEx / Finance | VP OpEx | Monthly |
| 3 | OpEx publish the prioritized backlog, assign sponsors/leads, and feed approved items into the project stage-gate (W4053) and capacity plan | OpEx CoE | COO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Opportunity pipeline / idea-management tool; benefit modeling (links to VS-17.4); audit findings (links to VS-21); VOC (links to VS-13)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lost-idea risk**: Frontline suggestions disappear; mitigated by single pipeline and feedback to submitter (W4056)
- **Bias risk**: Prioritization skewed to loud/large items; mitigated by standardized impact-effort scoring

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; monthly review ~1 day

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated intake from audit/VOC/process-mining feeds; impact-effort auto-scoring against historical benefit data

### Controls
- CTL-629 (ensure controlled execution — OpEx Strategy, Governance & Improvement Methodology (PA-133.1).)
- CTL: prioritization criteria documented and applied consistently; sponsor assignment recorded

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21 (audit), VS-13 (VOC), VS-44 (insights), VS-33 (strategy)

## W4053. Cross-Functional Improvement Project Governance & Stage-Gate

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved opportunity entering execution |
| **Frequency** | Per project + weekly portfolio review |
| **Volume** | Dozens of active improvement projects/yr across all functions |
| **Owner** | VP Operational Excellence |
| **Participants** | Project leads/sponsors, PMO (links to VS-112), Finance (links to VS-17.4), OCM (links to VS-134), Audit (links to VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OpEx/PMO charter each improvement project (problem statement, scope, baseline, target, team, benefit case) and gate it through Define→Measure→Analyze→Improve→Control with sign-offs | OpEx / PMO / Project Lead | Sponsor / VP OpEx | Per project |
| 2 | OpEx coordinate cross-functional dependencies, integrate OCM for the people side (links to VS-134), and manage capacity against the portfolio | OpEx / OCM / PMO | VP OpEx | Weekly |
| 3 | OpEx/Finance verify benefit realization at Control/handover (links to W4065), transition to process owner for sustainment, and archive lessons (W4071) | OpEx / Finance / Process Owner | VP OpEx / CFO | Per project close |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/CI workflow tool (links to VS-112 PMO); benefit tracking (links to VS-17.4); OCM (links to VS-134)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stall risk**: Projects stall at Analyze/Improve; mitigated by stage-gate time-boxes and sponsor accountability
- **No-handover risk**: Improvement not sustained post-project; mitigated by Control-stage handover to process owner (W4092)

### Time Estimate
- Per-project governance ~5–10% of project effort; weekly portfolio review

### Automation Opportunity
- Stage-gate workflow automation; automated dependency/capacity conflict detection

### Controls
- CTL-162 (ensure improvement benefit realization.)
- CTL: stage-gate sign-off by sponsor; benefit verified by Finance (segregation); control-stage handover documented

### Cross-references
- links to VS-112 (PMO), VS-134 (OCM), VS-17.4 (finance)

- CTL-629 (ensure controlled execution — OpEx Strategy, Governance & Improvement Methodology (PA-133.1).)
