## W4129. Inventory Strategy, Postponement & Pooling Policy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy refresh; category review; network change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + per category |
| **Volume** | 35,000 active SKUs across 4 DCs + 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Network Strategy Lead / Merchandising (VS-01) |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01), Supply Planning (VS-02), Finance (VS-17.4/VS-105) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Network Strategy/Merchandising define the inventory strategy per category: where to stock (DC vs store vs both), postponement (e.g., paint tinting at store links to VS-09), pooling, and make-to-stock vs make-to-order | Network Strategy Lead / Merchandising / Supply Planning | VP Supply Chain | Annual |
| 2 | Network Strategy set the inventory positioning policy (centralized vs decentralized, forward-stocking, cross-dock for fast-movers links to VS-04) aligned to network design (W4121) | Network Strategy Lead | VP Supply Chain | Annual |
| 3 | Network Strategy document the strategy, align with merchandising/finance on the turn/service trade-off, and feed into MEIO (W4130) and parameter governance (W4135) | Network Strategy Lead / Finance | VP Supply Chain / COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Network/inventory model (W4122/W4130); merchandising (VS-01); supply planning (VS-02); MDM (VS-29)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-positioning risk**: Inventory in the wrong echelon (slow at store, stockout at DC); mitigated by echelon-aware strategy
- **Postponement-overlook risk**: Missing postponement opportunities (e.g., central stock + configure-to-order); mitigated by category review

### Automation Opportunity
- Strategy-to-policy cascading; positioning analytics

### Controls
- CTL-639 (ensure controlled execution — Multi-Echelon Inventory Optimization & Service-Level Engineering (PA-136.2).)
- CTL-639: inventory strategy approved by Merchandising/Supply Chain/Finance

### Time Estimate
- Annual + per category

### Cross-references
- links to VS-01 (merchandising), VS-02 (supply), VS-04 (DC/cross-dock), VS-09 (postponement/fabrication)

## W4130. Multi-Echelon Inventory Optimization (MEIO) Modeling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Network/inventory decision; parameter refresh |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + periodic refresh |
| **Volume** | ~7.2M parameter sets (per SKU per location); 35K SKUs × 205 locations |
| **Owner** | Network Strategy Lead / Supply Chain Analytics (VS-28) |
| **Participants** | Analytics (VS-28), Supply Planning (VS-02), Merchandising (VS-01) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Network Strategy/Analytics build and maintain the MEIO model: demand variability and lead-time variability per SKU per echelon, dependency between echelons (DC buffers protect stores), and the service-level target per segment (W4132) | Network Strategy Lead / Analytics | VP Supply Chain | Maintained |
| 2 | Network Strategy/Analytics run MEIO optimization to find the least-inventory-cost deployment that meets service targets across all echelons (vs siloed per-location safety stock), and quantify working-capital/service impact | Network Strategy Lead / Finance | VP Supply Chain | Per refresh |
| 3 | Network Strategy output optimized parameters (safety stock, ROP, EOQ, min/max) to MDM (W4135) for execution by supply planning (VS-02), and validate post-implementation (W4138) | Network Strategy Lead / Supply Planning | VP Supply Chain | Per refresh |

### System Touchpoints
- MEIO optimization tool; BI/analytics (VS-28); demand planning (VS-127/VS-02); MDM (VS-29 MDM-021); ERP replenishment (VS-02)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Silo-optimization risk**: Optimizing each location independently misses echelon synergy; mitigated by true MEIO
- **Adoption risk**: Optimized parameters not adopted in execution; mitigated by MDM governance (W4135) and monitoring (W4138)

### Automation Opportunity
- MEIO auto-refresh from demand/lead-time data; parameter auto-publication to MDM

### Controls
- CTL-639 (ensure controlled execution — Multi-Echelon Inventory Optimization & Service-Level Engineering (PA-136.2).)
- CTL-639: MEIO assumptions documented; parameters governed before publishing

### Time Estimate
- Maintained; per refresh weeks

### Cross-references
- links to VS-02 (supply/replenishment), VS-127 (S&OP/demand), VS-29 MDM-021 (parameters), VS-28 (analytics)

## W4132. ABC/XYZ Segmentation & Differentiated Inventory Policy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Quarterly + on change |
| **Volume** | 35,000 active SKUs (A: top 20% = 80% revenue; B: 30% = 15%; C: 50% = 5%) |
| **Owner** | Merchandising / Supply Planning (VS-02) |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01), Supply Planning (VS-02), Analytics (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandising/Analytics segment SKUs by value (ABC by revenue/contribution) and variability (XYZ by demand variability), producing a 3×3 matrix, refreshed quarterly | Merchandising / Analytics | VP Merchandising | Quarterly |
| 2 | Merchandising/Supply Planning define differentiated inventory policy per segment (AX: tight control, high service, frequent review; CZ: minimal/no stock, make-to-order); set service-level targets per segment (W4131) | Merchandising / Supply Planning | VP Supply Chain | Quarterly |
| 3 | Merchandising/Supply Planning publish segmentation to MDM (W4135), drive policy into replenishment (VS-02), and review segment migration over time | Merchandising / Supply Planning | VP Merchandising | Quarterly |

- BI/analytics (VS-28); merchandising (VS-01); MDM (VS-29); ERP replenishment (VS-02)

- **One-size-fits-all risk**: Same policy for fast/slow movers (over-invest in C, under-serve A); mitigated by differentiated policy
- **Stale-segmentation risk**: Segments not refreshed as SKUs move; mitigated by quarterly refresh

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-segmentation from sales/variability data; policy-recommendation engine

### Controls
- CTL-639 (ensure controlled execution — Multi-Echelon Inventory Optimization & Service-Level Engineering (PA-136.2).)
- CTL-639: segmentation reviewed by Merchandising; policy per segment documented

### Time Estimate

| **Frequency** | Quarterly + on decision |
| **Volume** | ~50% of SKUs are C-items (long tail); obsolescence exposure |

| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-01), Supply Planning (VS-02), Inventory Lifecycle (VS-05), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandising/Supply Planning identify slow-movers/long-tail (CZ segment) and obsolescence risk via aging analysis (links to VS-05 inventory aging), and quantify carrying cost and write-down exposure | Merchandising / Supply Planning / Finance | VP Merchandising | Quarterly |
| 2 | Merchandising optimize slow-movers: centralize stocking (stock at DC only, store-on-request), convert to non-stock/special-order (links to VS-09), bundle/clearance (links to VS-64/VS-32), or discontinue (links to VS-01 lifecycle) | Merchandising / Supply Planning | VP Merchandising | Quarterly |

### Automation Opportunity
- Aging/velocity auto-scoring; slow-mover policy recommendations

### Controls
- CTL-80 (ensure inventory parameter & network optimization governance.)
- CTL-639: write-downs per accounting policy (VS-17); slow-mover decisions documented

### Time Estimate
