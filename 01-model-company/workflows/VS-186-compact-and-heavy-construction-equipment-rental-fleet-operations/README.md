## Overview

Compact & heavy construction equipment rental fleet operations for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **customer-facing short-term rental of self-operated construction equipment** that BuildRight *owns as a fleet* and rents out by the day/week/month to contractors, developers, and homeowners: mini-excavators and skid-steer loaders; concrete mixers, pumps and vibrators; boom lifts, scissor lifts and scaffolding towers; plate compactors, breakers and jackhammers; mobile generators, light towers, air compressors and dewatering pumps; tillers and site-clearance equipment; and towable equipment (trailers, welders).

This is distinct from VS-12.2 (Tool Rental — hand/power tools from Bosch/Makita/DeWalt/Hilti), VS-12.1 (installation & repair labour), the single scaffolding-rental workflow **W1062** and the construction-equipment *brokerage* workflow **W1094** in VS-09 (which books third-party partners rather than operating a BuildRight-owned fleet), VS-162 (pickup-truck/van self-haul rental — titled motor vehicles), VS-96 (multi-year equipment *leasing/finance*), and VS-175 (propane/LPG cylinder exchange). At ~40% B2B trade/project revenue, a large lumber/building-materials and tool/equipment assortment, and ~200 provincial stores near active construction sites, a compact-equipment rental fleet is a recognized big-box discipline (the "Home Depot Rentals" model) that converts "I can't do this job without a machine" basket abandonment into a completed sale, captures contractor recurring rental spend, and lifts large-material attach — while carrying acute safety (DOLE-OSH, BFP Fire Code), roadworthiness/registration (LTO/LTFRB for registered equipment), and asset-loss exposure on capital equipment worth PHP 0.5M–8M per unit.
