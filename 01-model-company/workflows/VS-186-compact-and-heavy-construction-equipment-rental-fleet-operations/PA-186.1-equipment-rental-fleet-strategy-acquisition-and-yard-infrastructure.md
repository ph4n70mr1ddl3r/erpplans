## W5321. Compact & Heavy Equipment Rental Fleet Strategy, Category Mix & Business Case

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Executive decision to enter/expand BuildRight-owned construction-equipment rental (beyond power tools and the W1094 brokerage model) |
| **Frequency** | Annual strategy refresh; ad-hoc expansion reviews |
| **Volume** | 1 program strategy per year; ~1 category-mix decision per quarter |
| **Owner** | COO (rental P&L owner) |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising, CFO, COO, Store Operations Director, Supply Planning |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quantify unmet demand from trade-account and project-quote data (lost sales where customer needed equipment BuildRight did not own — links VS-11/VS-107). | Rental Category Manager | COO | 1 week |
| 2 | Build demand model by equipment class (earthmoving, concrete, access/lift, power/light/compressed-air, compaction) mapped to provincial construction activity and PHP/rental-day revenue. | Rental Category Manager | COO | 1 week |
| 3 | Define category mix and store-tier fleet footprint (full-service vs. satellite vs. hub-and-spoke across 200 stores). | COO | COO | 3 days |
| 4 | Build business case: capex (PHP 0.5M–8M/unit), utilization hurdle (~60–70%), payback, and attach uplift to material sales. | Finance Business Partner | CFO | 1 week |
| 5 | Present capex envelope and stage-gate to capital committee (per VS-40). | COO | CFO | 2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Fixed Asset / Rental Fleet module (asset class "Construction Equipment Rental"); capex request & approval workflow (VS-40 W1811).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stranded-capex risk**: over-buying a fleet class that under-utilizes (e.g. a boom lift at a store with no high-rise trade base) erodes the ~60–70% utilization hurdle; mitigated by demand modeling from lost-sale and quote data and a hub-and-spoke rollout that concentrates high-value units at regional hubs with inter-store rebalancing (W5344).
- **Cannibalization risk**: pricing the fleet so low it cannibalizes the W1094 brokerage margin or VS-12.2 tool rental; mitigated by explicit rate-card separation and a documented value boundary in the strategy.

### Automation Opportunity
- Lost-sale/quote-to-demand pipeline: flag every B2B quote containing an equipment-rental need line and feed a rolling demand forecast per equipment class.

### Controls
- CTL-02: prevent unauthorized capex — stage-gate approval; CTL-35 (budget adherence vs. approved envelope).

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks elapsed for the annual refresh; ~120 person-hours.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-12.2 (tool rental), VS-40 (capex), VS-107 (key accounts), VS-11 (trade/project)

## W5322. Construction Equipment Vendor Selection, Procurement & Fleet Acquisition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved category-mix and capex envelope (W5321) |
| **Frequency** | Per fleet-acquisition wave (~2–4 waves/year) |
| **Volume** | ~15–40 units per wave; ~60–120 units added/yr in steady state |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager (Equipment) |
| **Participants** | Rental Fleet Manager, TESDA-qualified mechanic, Finance, Vendor (Komatsu/Kubota/Bobcat/JCB/Wacker Neuson/etc.) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Issue RFP to equipment OEMs/dealers for rental-grade units with parts availability and after-sales support in the Philippines. | Procurement Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2 weeks |
| 2 | Evaluate on durability, hour-rating, local parts depots, dealer service network, and rental-program support (per W224 vendor selection). | Rental Fleet Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1 week |
| 3 | Negotiate price, warranty, and fleet service agreement; confirm PHP landed cost including freight to DC (per VS-02.2). | Procurement Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1 week |
| 4 | Raise PO; receive units at the assigned hub DC; inspect against spec and register assets (W5324). | Receiving Clerk | Rental Fleet Manager | 3 days |
| 5 | Acceptance sign-off and asset capitalization to the Rental Fleet GL class. | Finance Business Partner | CFO | 2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/PO module (VS-03/VS-15); Fixed Asset capitalization with rental-fleet asset class and serial/engine-number capture; 3-way match (PO→GR→invoice).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stranded-fleet risk**: buying a brand/model with no Philippines parts depot grounds units for weeks on a single failed hydraulic seal; mitigated by a mandatory local-parts-availability and dealer-service-network check in the RFP scoring, and a minimum 2-day spares kit on order at acquisition.
- **Spec-mismatch risk**: units arrive under-powered for PH jobsite conditions (e.g. a mini-excavator too heavy for typical residential slab access); mitigated by TESDA-qualified mechanic input in evaluation and a documented spec sheet per category.

### Automation Opportunity
- Parts-availability and service-network data fed directly from OEM dealer portals into the RFP scorecard; auto-raise PO from approved wave list.

### Controls
- CTL-06: vendor onboarding; CTL-14 (3-way match); CTL-26 (fixed asset capitalization control).

### Time Estimate
- ~6–10 weeks elapsed per wave; ~80 person-hours.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-03 (vendor mgmt), VS-15 (P2P), VS-35 (fixed assets), VS-02.2 (inbound freight)

## W5323. Rental Equipment Yard, Wash Bay & Heavy-Equipment Storage Infrastructure Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New hub store selected for an equipment-rental footprint (W5321) |
| **Frequency** | Per hub/satellite setup (~10–20 sites/yr as the network rolls out) |
| **Volume** | 1 yard-build per site |
| **Owner** | Facilities & Real Estate Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, HSE Officer, BFP-accredited Fire Safety Inspector, Corporate Security (VS-159) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design the rental yard: compacted/graded laydown, security lighting, CCTV coverage, fenced perimeter, and equipment-tier separation. | Facilities Manager | Store Manager | 1 week |
| 2 | Build wash-down bay with oil-water interceptor and sump (DENR-ECC compliant for wash runoff, per VS-24/VS-187). | Facilities Manager | HSE Officer | 2 weeks |
| 3 | Install fueling/battery-charge station, spill kit, and fire suppression rated for diesel/equipment (BFP Fire Code FSIC). | Facilities Manager | BFP Inspector | 2 weeks |
| 4 | Commission the yard; sign off HSE and BFP FSIC before first unit intake. | HSE Officer | Store Manager | 3 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Facilities/CAPEX project module; BFP FSIC and DENR-ECC permit records (links VS-166 permit portfolio).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Environmental liability risk**: equipment wash-down runoff (oil, hydraulic fluid, concrete slurry) discharged without an interceptor triggers DENR-EMB violation under RA 9275 (Clean Water Act); mitigated by an oil-water interceptor + sump and a quarterly effluent check.
- **Theft risk**: high-value capital equipment (a PHP 5M boom lift) parked in an inadequately lit/fenced yard is a prime target; mitigated by fenced/lighted/CCTV yard design and telematics kill-switch (W5324).

### Automation Opportunity
- Yard-design template and BOM auto-generated per tier (hub/satellite) from the approved footprint.

### Controls
- CTL-26: asset safeguarding at the yard; operational: BFP FSIC sign-off before commissioning.

### Time Estimate
- ~6–8 weeks elapsed per yard; capital project, ~PHP 2–6M per site.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-20 (construction), VS-138 (facilities), VS-166 (permits), VS-24 (HSE), VS-159 (security)

## W5324. Heavy Equipment Rental Fleet Asset Registration, Tracking & Telematics Provisioning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Unit received at hub DC (W5322) |
| **Frequency** | Per new unit intake |
| **Volume** | ~60–120 units/yr |
| **Owner** | Rental Fleet Manager |
| **Participants** | IT/IoT Engineer, Mechanic, Asset Accountant |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture unit identity: OEM, model, serial, engine number, hour-meter baseline, photos, and any VIN/registration. | Mechanic | Rental Fleet Manager | 1 hour |
| 2 | Install telematics/IoT unit (GPS, hour-meter feed, geofence, kill-switch where fitted). | IT/IoT Engineer | Rental Fleet Manager | 2 hours |
| 3 | Register the unit in the Rental Fleet module as a unique rentable asset with utilization/maintenance counters. | Rental Fleet Manager | Rental Fleet Manager | 30 min |
| 4 | Link telematics feed to the ERP rental-lifecycle events (pickup, return, geofence breach). | IT/IoT Engineer | Rental Fleet Manager | 1 hour |
| 5 | Capitalize asset and reconcile to the PO/invoice (per VS-35). | Asset Accountant | CFO | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental Fleet asset master (per-unit serial/hour-meter); telematics/IoT platform ingesting GPS + hour-meter + geofence events into ERP; Fixed Asset register.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Invisible-fleet risk**: a unit without telematics is "lost" the moment it leaves the yard — no usage billing accuracy, no theft recovery; mitigated by mandatory telematics provisioning before first rental and a kill-switch on units > PHP 1M.
- **Hour-meter drift risk**: maintenance scheduled on hour-meter readings that the telematics under-reports causes missed PM and in-field breakdowns; mitigated by daily hour-meter-feed health checks and variance alerts.

### Automation Opportunity
- Telematics feed auto-populates the rental asset's hour-meter and location, eliminating manual readings; geofence breach auto-creates a recovery ticket.

### Controls
- CTL-26: asset registration; operational: dual-control telematics-provisioning sign-off (IT + Mechanic).

### Time Estimate
- ~4–5 hours per unit; ~60 units/yr → ~300 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-35 (fixed assets), VS-27 (IoT/IT ops), VS-99 (technology lifecycle)

## W5325. Equipment Rental Rate Card, Pricing Structure & Competitor Benchmarking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Fleet intake (W5322) and quarterly margin review |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly rate review; ad-hoc per competitor move |
| **Volume** | 1 rate card per quarter covering ~25–40 equipment classes |
| **Owner** | Rental Pricing Analyst |
| **Participants** | COO, Finance Business Partner, Competitive Intelligence (VS-57) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gather competitor rental rates (specialty rental houses, peer big-box) by class and region. | Rental Pricing Analyst | COO | 3 days |
| 2 | Build hourly/daily/weekly/monthly rate per class covering capex amortization, utilization, fuel/wear, and target margin. | Rental Pricing Analyst | Finance Business Partner | 3 days |
| 3 | Define CDW (collision damage waiver) fee, delivery fee, and late/overtime rate. | Rental Pricing Analyst | COO | 1 day |
| 4 | Load rate card to POS/rental module; publish to the digital booking channel (W5328). | Rental Pricing Analyst | COO | 1 day |
| 5 | Monitor realized rate vs. rate card weekly and flag erosion. | Rental Pricing Analyst | COO | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental pricing/rate-card module (per-class hourly/daily/weekly); POS rental pricing; competitive-price-intel feed (VS-57).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-recovery risk**: a daily rate that does not cover capex amortization at the realized utilization bleeds margin silently; mitigated by a per-class break-even utilization model in the rate build and weekly realized-rate monitoring.
- **Price-leadership risk**: undercutting the brokerage (W1094) channel or being uncompetitive vs. specialty rental houses; mitigated by competitor benchmarking and a documented positioning band.

### Automation Opportunity
- Competitor-rate scraping (VS-57) feeds directly into the rate-build workbook; rate-card changes propagate to POS and the booking channel in one publish.

### Controls
- CTL-07: prevent unauthorized pricing changes — rate-card change approval; CTL-35 (margin adherence).

### Time Estimate
- ~2 weeks per quarterly review; ~10 person-hours/week ongoing monitoring.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-57 (competitive price intelligence), VS-118 (revenue assurance/pricing integrity)

## W5326. Rental Operator Certification, Customer Pre-Qualification & Credit/License Vetting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New customer applies for self-operated equipment rental; periodic re-qualification |
| **Frequency** | Per new renter; annual re-qualification |
| **Volume** | ~3,000–6,000 first-time renters/yr; ~15,000 active renters in the base |
| **Owner** | Rental Operations Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Credit Analyst (VS-68), HSE Officer, Compliance Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture renter identity, valid government ID, and (for B2B) PCAB license and business registration. | Rental Counter Staff | Rental Operations Supervisor | 20 min |
| 2 | Run credit check / credit-card pre-auth / trade-account standing (links VS-68/VS-16). | Credit Analyst | Rental Operations Supervisor | 30 min |
| 3 | Verify operator competency: valid license/TCO where the class requires it (e.g. heavy-vehicle-restricted units), or issue an in-store competency check + signed acknowledgment. | HSE Officer | Rental Operations Supervisor | 20 min |
| 4 | Capture and store the signed rental agreement, CDW election, and operator certification in the rental customer record (VS-88). | Rental Counter Staff | Rental Operations Supervisor | 15 min |
| 5 | Flag any reject (failed credit, no competency, sanctioned party) and prevent rental-lifecycle start. | Rental Operations Supervisor | Rental Operations Supervisor | 10 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental customer master + credit-hold integration (VS-68/VS-16); document vault (VS-88); sanctions/PEP check (VS-86) for large deposits.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Negligent-rental risk**: renting an aerial lift or mini-excavator to an untrained operator who injures a third party exposes BuildRight to liability and DOLE findings; mitigated by mandatory competency check or signed operator-acknowledgment (W5330 safety briefing) and CDW/insurance (W5327).
- **Credit-loss risk**: a renter absconds with a PHP 5M unit on a weak credit check; mitigated by credit-card pre-auth / trade-account standing and deposit hold sized to the unit value.

### Automation Opportunity
- Online pre-qualification with ID upload and credit-card pre-auth that opens/holds the rental account before the customer reaches the counter.

### Controls
- CTL-12: separate credit approval from sales execution — counter staff cannot override a credit reject; CTL-27 (customer data safeguards on the captured IDs).

### Time Estimate
- ~1.5 hours per first-time renter; ~3,000/yr → ~4,500 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (credit risk), VS-16 (order-to-cash), VS-86 (AML/KYC), VS-167 (background screening)

## W5327. Heavy Equipment Insurance, Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) & Liability Program Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Fleet intake (W5322) and annual insurance renewal |
| **Frequency** | Annual policy renewal; per-unit endorsement on intake |
| **Volume** | 1 master policy/yr covering the fleet; ~60–120 unit endorsements/yr |
| **Owner** | Risk & Insurance Manager |
| **Participants** | CFO, Insurance Broker, Captive (VS-153), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define coverage: physical damage (comprehensive), third-party liability, and hired/borrowed operator coverage, sized to unit values (PHP 0.5M–8M). | Risk & Insurance Manager | CFO | 1 week |
| 2 | Structure CDW as an optional customer fee that shifts the deductible/first-loss to the renter (per RA 10607 non-life insurance rules / the captive). | Risk & Insurance Manager | CFO | 3 days |
| 3 | Negotiate master policy + premiums with broker/insurer; consider captive participation (VS-153) for the predictable first-loss layer. | Insurance Broker | CFO | 2 weeks |
| 4 | Configure per-rental CDW election and deductible into the rental rate card (W5325) and POS. | Rental Pricing Analyst | Risk & Insurance Manager | 2 days |
| 5 | Establish claims protocol linking to incident management (W5341) and recovery (W5334). | Risk & Insurance Manager | CFO | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance policy/claims register (links VS-26); rental POS CDW election; captive underwriting ledger (VS-153).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Uninsured-loss risk**: a unit rented without CDW/elected coverage and damaged off-site leaves BuildRight to absorb the full repair; mitigated by a default-on CDW (opt-out only) or mandatory deposit sized to the deductible.
- **Mis-priced-CDW risk**: a CDW fee that does not cover the actuarial first-loss erodes margin; mitigated by annual loss-ratio review against the CDW revenue pool.

### Automation Opportunity
- Per-rental CDW election auto-posted to the policy/claims register and to the captive ledger.

### Controls
- CTL-26: asset insurance coverage; operational: default-on CDW with documented opt-out.

### Time Estimate
- ~6 weeks elapsed per renewal; ~60 person-hours.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-26 (insurance), VS-153 (captive), VS-100 (legal), VS-159 (security/investigations)

## W5328. Equipment Rental Catalog, Merchandising & Digital Booking Channel Integration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Fleet intake (W5322) and rate-card publication (W5325) |
| **Frequency** | Per intake wave; ongoing availability sync |
| **Volume** | ~60–120 catalog SKUs maintained; ~3,000–6,000 digital bookings/yr |
| **Owner** | Rental Category Manager |
| **Participants** | PIM/DAM team (VS-137), Ecommerce (VS-10), Marketing (VS-14) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Author rental product content per class: specs, load/capacity ratings, photos, operator requirements, and safety video (per VS-137 PIM/DAM). | Rental Category Manager | Rental Category Manager | 2 hours/class |
| 2 | Publish to web/app booking channel with real-time availability by store/hub (links VS-10). | Ecommerce Manager | Rental Category Manager | 1 week |
| 3 | Merchandise in-store: yard signage, "Rent It" shelf-edge tags on merchandise that pairs with equipment (e.g. a breaker hammer near the jackhammer rental). | Store Manager | Rental Category Manager | ongoing |
| 4 | Integrate online booking → rental reservation → counter handover workflow (W5329). | Ecommerce Manager | Rental Category Manager | 2 weeks |
| 5 | Sync availability across channels to prevent double-booking (links VS-60 omnichannel). | Ecommerce Manager | Rental Category Manager | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- PIM/DAM (VS-137); ecommerce/web/app booking (VS-10); rental availability/reservation module; omnichannel inventory sync (VS-60).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-information risk**: an outdated load rating or omitted operator-license requirement in the catalog causes a wrong rental and a safety/return dispute; mitigated by PIM governance and a mandatory operator-requirement field per class.
- **Double-booking risk**: a unit booked online and rented in-store simultaneously; mitigated by real-time availability sync to a single rental-asset ledger (not a channel-local copy).

### Automation Opportunity
- Real-time availability from the rental-asset ledger published to all booking channels; automated "frequently rented together" merchandising.

### Controls
- CTL-788 (ensure controlled execution — Fleet Strategy, Acquisition & Yard Infrastructure (PA-186.1).)
- operational: any operator-license/ID data captured online is safeguarded; operational: PIM content review before publish.

### Time Estimate
- ~2 hours/class for content; ~3 weeks for initial channel integration; ongoing sync automated.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-137 (PIM/DAM), VS-10 (ecommerce), VS-14 (marketing), VS-60 (omnichannel)
