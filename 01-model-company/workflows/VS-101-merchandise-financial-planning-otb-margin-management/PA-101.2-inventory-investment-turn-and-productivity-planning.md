## W3289. Ending Stock & Weeks-of-Supply (WOS) Target Planning by Category

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Seasonal/annual plan (W3281/W3282) |
| **Frequency** | Seasonal |
| **Volume** | ~40 categories |
| **Owner** | Merchandise Planner |
| **Participants** | Merchandise Planner, Category Managers, Supply Planning (links to VS-02.1), Allocation (links to VS-55) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandise Planner set EOM stock targets and weeks-of-supply (WOS) by category using forward demand (links to VS-02.1), lead time (domestic 7–21d / import 45–90d per VS-02.2), safety-stock policy (A/B/C per profile §6.6), and seasonality | Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Seasonal |
| 2 | Category Managers confirm category WOS supports service-level targets and key-item availability; reconcile to receipt plan (W3284) and store-cluster needs (W3302) | Category Managers / Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Seasonal |
| 3 | Merchandise Planner publish WOS/EOM targets to supply planning and allocation; monitor actual WOS weekly (W3288) and flag over/under-stock | Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Seasonal |

### System Touchpoints
- Merchandise planning; demand planning (links to VS-02.1); allocation (links to VS-55); BI (links to VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-stock risk**: Excess WOS ties up working capital and ages inventory (W3294); mitigated by OTB discipline (W3283)
- **Stockout risk**: Insufficient WOS on A-items loses sales; mitigated by safety-stock policy and reforecast (W3288)


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-file ((a/b/c per profile §6.6), and seasonality)
- auto-reconcile (key-item availability; reconcile to receipt plan)
- auto-monitor (and allocation; monitor actual wos weekly)

### Controls
operational: OTB discipline (W3283); operational: safety-stock policy and reforecast (W3288)
### Time Estimate
- Seasonal

## W3290. Inventory Turn & GMROI Target Setting & Performance Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual plan; monthly performance review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~40 categories |
| **Owner** | Merchandise Planner |
| **Participants** | Merchandise Planner, Category Managers, FP&A (links to VS-17.4), Finance (links to VS-17.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandise Planner set inventory-turn targets (corporate 6–8x per profile §12.3) and GMROI (gross-margin return on inventory investment) targets by category, benchmarking to category role (destination vs routine vs seasonal) | Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Annual |
| 2 | Merchandise Planner/Finance track actual turn and GMROI monthly by category, isolate drivers (sales velocity, stock level, margin), and flag underperformers for action (markdown W3285, cancel W3284, transfer links to VS-05.2) | Merchandise Planner / Finance | VP Merchandising | Monthly |
| 3 | VP Merchandising review category turn/GMROI with Category Managers quarterly; reallocate OTB and space (links to VS-55.3) toward higher-productivity categories and exit chronic underperformers (W3293) | VP Merchandising | CEO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Merchandise planning; GL/inventory valuation (links to VS-17.1); BI (links to VS-28.1); space analytics (links to VS-55.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Capital-trap risk**: Capital trapped in low-turn categories erodes ROIC; mitigated by GMROI discipline and space reallocation
- **Margin-vs-turn trade-off risk**: Chasing turn via markdown erodes margin; mitigated by maintained-margin modeling (W3286)


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-file (6–8x per profile §12.3) and gmroi)
- auto-track (merchandise planner/finance track actual turn and)
- auto-review (vp merchandising review category turn/gmroi with)

### Controls
operational: GMROI discipline and space reallocation; operational: maintained-margin modeling (W3286)
### Time Estimate
- Monthly/quarterly

## W3291. Stock-to-Sales Ratio & Flow Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Seasonal plan; flow/receipt scheduling |
| **Frequency** | Seasonal |
| **Volume** | ~40 categories × 12 months |
| **Owner** | Merchandise Planner |
| **Participants** | Merchandise Planner, Supply Planning (links to VS-02.1), DC ops (links to VS-04), Buyers (links to VS-03) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandise Planner set monthly stock-to-sales ratio targets by category (beginning stock as a multiple of the month's planned sales) to ensure flow supports demand without overstock | Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Seasonal |
| 2 | Merchandise Planner/Supply Planning convert stock-to-sales targets into a receipt flow plan timed to lead times (domestic/import) and DC capacity (links to VS-04.3); smooth receipts to avoid inbound peaks | Merchandise Planner / Supply Planning | VP Merchandising | Seasonal |
| 3 | Merchandise Planner monitor actual stock-to-sales weekly (W3288) and adjust flow (chase/cancel/transfer) to maintain target | Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- Merchandise planning; demand/supply planning (links to VS-02.1); DC capacity (links to VS-04.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Flow-mismatch risk**: Receipt timing off vs demand creates stockouts or overstock; mitigated by flow planning and reforecast
- **Inbound-congestion risk**: Receipt peaks overwhelm DC inbound (links to VS-04.1); mitigated by smoothing


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-import (lead times (domestic/import) and dc capacity)
- auto-monitor (merchandise planner monitor actual stock-to-sales weekly)

### Controls
operational: flow planning and reforecast; operational: smoothing
### Time Estimate
- Seasonal + weekly

| **Frequency** | Quarterly + on demand |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Over-investment risk**: Inventory grows faster than cash/turn supports; mitigated by working-capital-constrained scenario
- **Under-investment risk**: Over-tight constraint starves growth and loses sales; mitigated by scenario trade-off analysis


### Automation Opportunity

- auto-review (monthly cash review (links to vs-18.1))

### Controls
operational: working-capital-constrained scenario; operational: scenario trade-off analysis
