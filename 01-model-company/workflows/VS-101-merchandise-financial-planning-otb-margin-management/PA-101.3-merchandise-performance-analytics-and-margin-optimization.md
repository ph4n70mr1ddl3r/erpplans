## W3297. Weekly Merchandise P&A Reporting & Variance Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly close of sales/receipts |
| **Frequency** | Weekly |
| **Volume** | All departments/categories |
| **Owner** | Merchandise Planner |
| **Participants** | Merchandise Planner, Category Managers, BI (links to VS-28.1), Finance (links to VS-17.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandise Planner/BI produce weekly merchandise P&A pack: sales, GM%, receipts, markdowns, EOM stock, sell-through, turn, WOS by department/category vs plan and vs last year; variance flags vs threshold | Merchandise Planner / BI | VP Merchandising | Weekly |
| 2 | Category Managers analyze variance root cause (demand, price, mix, stockout, promo, weather per VS-69) and propose corrective action (chase/cancel/markdown/transfer) against OTB (W3283) and markdown budget (W3285) | Category Managers | VP Merchandising | Weekly |
| 3 | Merchandise Planner consolidate actions and feed to weekly P&A review (W3288); archive the pack for audit (links to VS-21.1) and trend analysis | Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- BI/reporting (links to VS-28.1); merchandise planning; POS/analytics (links to VS-08); GL (links to VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Latency risk**: Slow P&A cycle delays corrective action; mitigated by weekly cadence and automation
- **Attribution risk**: Misread variance causes wrong action; mitigated by disciplined root-cause analysis


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-flag (year; variance flags vs threshold)
- auto-analyze (category managers analyze variance root cause)
- auto-review (weekly p&a review (w3288); archive the)

### Controls
operational: weekly cadence and automation; operational: disciplined root-cause analysis

### Time Estimate
- Weekly
