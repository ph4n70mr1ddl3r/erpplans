## W3883. Customer Identity Resolution & Profile Stitching

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Ingestion (W3882); new identifier |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~600K loyalty + ~515K ecommerce customers/yr + cash walk-ins; identity graph maintained |
| **Owner** | CDP / Data Science |
| **Participants** | CDP Team, Data Science (links to VS-30.2), Data Engineering (links to VS-28/VS-27), Loyalty (links to VS-13), Ecommerce (links to VS-10), Trade (links to VS-11/VS-16), Privacy (links to VS-91), Fraud (links to VS-125/W3861) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CDP/Data Science build identity resolution: deterministic matching on verified identifiers (loyalty ID, email, phone, trade account, card token) and probabilistic matching on device/behavior/name/address for anonymous-to-known stitching (links to VS-125 device graph for fraud), with confidence scoring and survivorship rules | CDP / Data Science | CDP Lead | Maintained |
| 2 | CDP stitch profiles into the resolved identity graph, surface duplicate/split identities for review, and handle household/B2B-account-group resolution (links to VS-107 key account hierarchy) | CDP / Data Science | CDP Lead | Continuous |
| 3 | CDP/Privacy ensure resolution complies with RA 10173 (lawful basis, no unlawful re-identification of anonymized data, cross-entity sharing limits — links to VS-91/VS-72) and document the resolution logic for DSAR response (links to W3900) | CDP / Privacy | CDP Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Identity graph engine; data warehouse (links to VS-28); loyalty (links to VS-13); ecommerce (links to VS-10); trade/AR (links to VS-16); key account (links to VS-107); fraud device graph (links to VS-125); privacy (links to VS-91)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-merge risk**: Probabilistic match merges two distinct customers (wrong personalization, privacy breach); mitigated by confidence thresholds and survivorship rules
- **Under-merge risk**: Same customer split across records defeats the single view; mitigated by deterministic-first matching


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count, card token" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review, and handle household/B2B-account-group resolution" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "logic for DSAR response" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: confidence thresholds and survivorship rules; operational: deterministic-first matching

### Time Estimate
- Maintained identity graph + continuous tuning
