### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CDP/Marketing build and maintain segments on the profile (links to W3884/W3887): RFM, lifecycle stage, value tier, trade-pro vs DIY, category-affinity (links to VS-01), channel-preference, win-back/lapse-risk, new-mover/home-builder, seasonal (links to VS-13.2), and ad-hoc campaign segments | CDP / Marketing / Insights | CDP Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- CDP segments; golden profile (links to W3884); consent (links to W3885); merchandising (links to VS-01); loyalty (links to VS-13); trade (links to VS-11/VS-107); insights (links to VS-28.1/VS-44); data governance (links to VS-28.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stale-segment risk**: Segments not refreshed deliver wrong targets; mitigated by daily/real-time refresh

### Automation Opportunity

- System auto-document of "document segment lineage for governance" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "counts/quality for activation" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-608 (ensure controlled execution — Customer Segmentation, Activation & Personalization Foundation (PA-126.2).)
