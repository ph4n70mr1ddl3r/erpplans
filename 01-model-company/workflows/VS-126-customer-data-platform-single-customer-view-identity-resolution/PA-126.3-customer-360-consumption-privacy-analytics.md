## W3897. Customer 360 Consumption & Self-Service Access

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Consumer need (marketing/sales/service); self-service request |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~100–300 entitled users; thousands of 360 lookups/day |
| **Owner** | CDP / Data Governance |
| **Participants** | CDP Team, Marketing (links to VS-14), Loyalty (links to VS-13), Sales Enablement/Clienteling (links to VS-124), Trade/Key Account (links to VS-11/VS-107), Contact Center (links to VS-13), Insights (links to VS-28.1), Privacy (links to VS-91), IT (links to VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CDP publish the Customer 360 view for entitled consumers: contact-center agent screen-pop (links to VS-13), clienteling view at POS (links to VS-124/VS-08), key-account 360 (links to VS-107), marketing self-service segmentation (links to W3889), and trade-pro view (links to VS-43) | CDP / IT / Data Governance | CDP Lead | Maintained |
| 2 | CDP/Privacy govern 360 access: role-based views, field-level masking of sensitive attributes (links to VS-91/W3888), purpose-limitation per consumer role, and audit logging of all lookups | CDP / Privacy / Security | CDP Lead | Continuous |
| 3 | CDP/Insights train consumers and self-service adoption (links to VS-19.4/VS-124 enablement), monitor usage and value, and refine the 360 with consumer feedback | CDP / Insights / Consumers | CDP Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Customer 360 app; contact center (links to VS-13); clienteling (links to VS-124); POS (links to VS-08); key account (links to VS-107); loyalty (links to VS-13); privacy/access (links to VS-91/W3888); analytics (links to VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-exposure risk**: 360 exposes PII to over-broad audience; mitigated by role-based views and masking (links to VS-91)
- **Adoption risk**: Consumers bypass 360 for old tools; mitigated by enablement and UX


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "center agent screen-pop" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "logging of all lookups" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "monitor usage and value, and refine the 360 with consumer feedback" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-609 (ensure controlled execution — Customer 360 Consumption, Privacy & Governance Analytics (PA-126.3).)
- operational: role-based views and masking (links to VS-91); operational: enablement and UX

### Time Estimate
- Maintained 360 + continuous adoption effort
