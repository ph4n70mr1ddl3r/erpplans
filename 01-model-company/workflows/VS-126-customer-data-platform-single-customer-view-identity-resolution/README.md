# VS-126: Customer Data Platform, Single Customer View & Identity Resolution

> **Technology & Data** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Customer Data Platform, Single Customer View & Identity Resolution workflows for BuildRight Depot
Corp. — owning the **customer-data platform (CDP) discipline** that builds and activates the single,
consented, reconciled customer profile across ~600,000 loyalty members, ~5,200 trade accounts,
~200 corporate accounts, ~515,000 ecommerce orders/year, 2.8M monthly POS transactions, ~600K
customer digital engagement app users (links to VS-75), and trade/B2B/clienteling touchpoints —
delivering identity resolution, the golden customer record, segmentation, personalization,
next-best-action, journey orchestration, and consent/privacy governance as a reusable platform.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**: 'customer data platform' is referenced across
~12 PA files and 'CDP' across ~23 with no dedicated owner; identity resolution appears in 2 PA
files, single customer view in 1, and 'customer golden record' in **zero**. The capability is
sprinkled across VS-13 (Customer Experience & Loyalty — loyalty CRM and points), VS-29 (Master
Data — customer master), VS-107 (Strategic Key Account — account 360), VS-10 (Ecommerce — online
customer), and VS-75 (Digital Engagement App) — but no value stream owns the **CDP platform and
the single-customer-view discipline**: cross-source ingestion and stitching, deterministic +
probabilistic identity resolution, golden-record mastery, consent/preference center, segmentation,
activation/syndication, personalization/recommendation engine, journey orchestration, CLV/churn
modeling, attribution/experimentation, DSAR/consumer-rights workflow, and CDP ROI/value realization.

This is distinct from **VS-29 (Master Data Management)** which owns the foundational customer
master and data-quality framework (this value stream owns the *unified, resolved, activated*
customer profile that consumes that master). It is distinct from **VS-13 (Customer Experience &
Loyalty)** which owns the loyalty program, complaints, and CRM *operations* (this value stream
owns the *customer-data platform and analytics layer* that powers those operations). It is
distinct from **VS-91 (Consumer Data Privacy)** which owns consent/privacy compliance (this value
stream owns the *CDP consent-preference center and DSAR workflow* as platform capabilities,
working alongside privacy). It is distinct from **VS-107 (Strategic Key Account)** which owns
account relationships (this value stream owns the *B2B/customer 360 data product* consumed by
account teams). It is distinct from **VS-28 (Data, Analytics & BI)** which owns the enterprise
data warehouse and BI (this value stream owns the *customer-domain-specific CDP* with real-time
activation). The CDP is the *customer-data-platform and single-customer-view* discipline —
distinct from foundational MDM, loyalty ops, privacy, key-account, and enterprise BI.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: a multi-channel retailer with ~600K loyalty members, growing
ecommerce, clienteling ambition (links to VS-124), a trade-pro program (links to VS-43), and
~5,400 B2B accounts, where the inability to resolve a single customer across cash/loyalty/ecommerce/
trade/app touchpoints directly blocks personalization, loyalty earn accuracy, retention, CLV-
based decisions, and consent-compliant marketing under RA 10173. Without a dedicated owner,
BuildRight risks duplicate/unresolved customer records, inconsistent personalization, consent
drift and privacy exposure, marketing-technology sprawl, and missed cross-sell/retention — risks
that no existing value stream owns end-to-end.
