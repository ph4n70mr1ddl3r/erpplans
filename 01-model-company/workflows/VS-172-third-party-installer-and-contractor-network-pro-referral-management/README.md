# VS-172: Third-Party Installer & Contractor Network (Pro-Referral) Management

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Third-party installer & contractor network (pro-referral) management workflows for BuildRight
Depot Corp. — owning the **vetted third-party trade professional network and customer referral
("Find-a-Pro") marketplace** that connects customers (who bought materials but need someone to
install/build) with screened, licensed, insured installers and contractors across the trade
specialties BuildRight sells into (electrical, plumbing, carpentry, tiling, paint, HVAC/solar,
appliance install, roofing, masonry): network strategy & trade-coverage design, contractor
recruitment and vetting/onboarding (PCAB RA 4566 license, insurance, references, TPRM), the
customer request → matchmaking → routing → lead handoff lifecycle, referral economics (lead
fees/commission), project tracking and customer ratings, installer performance/scorecard, quality
and warranty/dispute resolution, license/insurance monitoring, and network analytics.

This matters at BuildRight's positioning and revenue model. BuildRight's tagline is **"Your Home
Building Partner"** and ~55% of revenue is B2C walk-in — many homeowners and small contractors
buy materials but lack the trade skill, license, or capacity to install them, and project
non-completion is a leading cause of basket abandonment and product return. A recognized big-box
discipline (the "Find-a-Pro" / installer-network / home-services referral model) directly lifts
basket size, conversion, project completion, attach (financing VS-154, design VS-66), and the
credible partner positioning — while a mis-managed referral (unlicensed/uninsured installer,
shoddy work, no-show) damages the brand, creates vicarious/selection liability, and triggers
complaints and warranty disputes. It complements BuildRight's **own** installation crews (VS-12)
by covering trades/geographies/overflow BuildRight doesn't staff directly, and is already assumed
in the model by the existing **W1472** (Trade Account Referral Program, Contractor Network
Development & Lead Tracking) in VS-11.1.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a unified program**. The only existing dedicated workflow
is the single **W1472** in **VS-11.1 Trade Account Management**, which tracks the *trade-account
side* of referral/lead-tracking but not the end-to-end *installer network & pro-referral
operating model*; "contractor network" appears in 7 PA files, "installer network" in 2, and
"preferred contractor"/"pro referral" in 0–1 — with no dedicated owner. This follows the same
single-workflow-elevation pattern used throughout the gap analysis (VS-89 Product Recall elevated
from W776; VS-105 SCF from W324; VS-129 Competition from W2683). It is **not** covered by **VS-12
(Installation & Services)**, which runs BuildRight's *own* installation crews — this owns the
*third-party network* that complements them. It is **not** covered by **VS-43 (Trade Professional
Program)**, which serves the *contractor as a customer* (loyalty/pricing) — this owns the
*contractor as a service provider* to BuildRight's customers. It is **not** covered by **VS-95
(Marketplace Operator)**, which is a 3P marketplace for *goods* sellers — this is a referral
marketplace for *services*. It is **not** covered by **VS-11 (Trade/Project/Wholesale)**, which
executes B2B *sales* (and hosts W1472) — this owns the *installer network & customer-referral
operating model*. And it is **not** covered by **VS-66 (Customer Project & Design)**, which does
the *design* — this owns *finding the builder* to execute it.

This value stream owns the end-to-end program: network strategy & trade-coverage design;
contractor recruitment, vetting & onboarding (PCAB RA 4566 license VS-165, insurance, references,
TPRM VS-161, ethics VS-119/VS-131); tiers/segments & agreement; the customer "Find-a-Pro"
request intake; matchmaking/routing by trade/location/rating/capacity; lead handoff, quote &
booking; referral economics (lead fee/commission) & settlement; project tracking & customer
rating/feedback; installer performance scorecard; quality, warranty & dispute resolution;
license/insurance continuous monitoring; pay-for-performance; network coverage/health analytics;
data privacy (customer/contact data shared with installers, RA 10173); and continuous
improvement. It integrates tightly with VS-12 (own installation), VS-43 (trade program),
VS-11/VS-107 (B2B/key accounts), VS-66 (project/design), VS-95 (marketplace), VS-165 (PCAB
licensing), VS-161 (TPRM), VS-53 (warranty), VS-13 (CX/complaints), VS-91 (privacy), VS-100
(legal/contracts), VS-154 (project financing), and VS-09 (in-store services).
