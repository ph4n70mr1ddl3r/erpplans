## W5001. Customer Rating, Review & Feedback Collection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project completion (W4998) |
| **Frequency** | Per completed project |
| **Volume** | ~400–800 ratings per month (per completed project) × network |
| **Owner** | CX (VS-13) / Pro-Referral Network Manager |
| **Participants** | CX (VS-13), Customer, Marketplace (VS-95), Privacy (VS-91), Installer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Solicit the rating/feedback: post-completion (W4998), request the customer rate the installer/work (CSAT/NPS, workmanship, timeliness, professionalism, value) via the platform/app/SMS (links VS-13 W65) | CX / Marketplace | Network Manager / CX Lead | Per project |
| 2 | Manage reviews & authenticity: publish reviews on the installer profile (W4990) per consent, verify authenticity (prevent fake/retaliatory reviews — links VS-125), and manage the installer's right-of-reply | Marketplace / CX / Legal / DPO | Network Manager | Per review |
| 3 | Analyze & act: aggregate ratings into the scorecard (W5002), identify low-rated installers for review/improvement (W5005), and feed CX analytics and the Find-a-Pro ranking (W4994) | CX / Analytics / Network Manager | COO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- CX/VOC (VS-13 W65); marketplace/reviews (VS-95); privacy/consent (VS-91); analytics (VS-28); fake-review (VS-125)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low-response risk**: Few ratings → weak signal; mitigated by prompt solicitation and incentives
- **Fake-review risk**: Inauthentic reviews mislead (VS-125); mitigated by authenticity controls
- **Bias/manipulation risk**: Reviews manipulated against/for installers; mitigated by governance

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-solicit on completion; review-authenticity detection; auto-aggregate to scorecard; ranking feed

### Controls
- CTL-748 (ensure controlled execution — Network Performance, Quality, Compliance & Analytics (PA-172.3).)
- CTL-748: ratings solicited post-completion; reviews authentic; consent respected; scorecard updated

### Time Estimate
- ~per-project solicitation automated; ~review moderation continuous

### Cross-references
- links to VS-13 (CX/W65), VS-91 (privacy), VS-125 (fake-review), VS-95 (marketplace), W4990 (profile), W4994 (ranking), W4998 (completion), W5002 (scorecard)

## W5002. Installer Performance Scorecard & KPI Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Periodic review; ratings (W5001); quality findings (W5003) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly deep-dive |
| **Volume** | ~1 scorecard per installer × network |
| **Owner** | Pro-Referral Network Manager / Analytics |
| **Participants** | Analytics (VS-28), CX (VS-13), Quality (VS-31), Network Manager, Installer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build the scorecard: per-installer KPIs — rating (W5001), response/quote/completion time, acceptance rate, completion verification (W4998), quality/defect (W5003/W5004), complaints (W5005), compliance (W5006), and referral-fee/revenue contribution | Analytics / Network Manager / CX / Quality | Network Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | Review & rank: review the scorecard per installer, rank within trade/area, identify top/bottom performers, and manage tiers (W4989) and preferred status | Network Manager / Analytics / Trade Program | COO | Monthly |
| 3 | Act on performance: recognition for top performers, improvement plans for under-performers (links VS-67/VS-133 improvement), tier changes, and removal (W5005) for persistent poor performance | Network Manager / Quality / Legal | COO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (VS-28); CX (VS-13); quality (VS-31); marketplace (VS-95); CRM (VS-11); scorecard platform

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric-gaming risk**: Installers gaming KPIs; mitigated by balanced multi-dimensional scorecard
- **Inaction risk**: Scorecard not actioned → poor performers retained; mitigated by tier/removal process
- **Unfairness risk**: Scorecard penalizes installers unfairly; mitigated by transparency and right-of-reply

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-build of "Build the scorecard: per-installer KPIs" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-rank of "rank within trade/area, identify top/bottom performers" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "improvement plans for under-performers" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-748 (ensure controlled execution — Network Performance, Quality, Compliance & Analytics (PA-172.3).)
- CTL-748: multi-dimensional scorecard; reviewed monthly; top/bottom acted on; tiers managed

### Time Estimate
- ~monthly scorecard automated; ~quarterly deep-dive 1–2 weeks

### Cross-references
- links to VS-28 (analytics), VS-31 (quality), VS-67/VS-133 (improvement), W4989 (tier), W5001 (rating), W5003/W5004 (quality/warranty), W5005 (removal), W5006 (compliance)

## W5004. Warranty, Defect Liability & Post-Installation Service Obligation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Defect found (W5003); customer warranty claim; defect-liability period |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~30–60 warranty/defect claims per year across the network |
| **Owner** | Warranty (VS-53) / Pro-Referral Network Manager / Installer |
| **Participants** | Warranty (VS-53), Installer, CX (VS-13), Quality (VS-31), Finance (VS-17), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define warranty/defect-liability terms: the workmanship warranty period/scope per trade (in the installer agreement W4989), the materials warranty (links VS-53 product warranty), and the defect-liability/retention mechanism | Warranty / Legal / Network Manager | COO / Legal | Per program |
| 2 | Manage claims: receive the customer defect/warranty claim, diagnose (workmanship vs materials vs customer), assign remediation to the installer (or alternate W5000), and track to resolution (links VS-53) | Warranty / CX / Installer | Network Manager | Per claim |
| 3 | Recover cost & learn: recover remediation cost from the installer (retention/bond/fee per agreement), feed the scorecard (W5002) and QA (W5003), and manage disputes (W5005) | Warranty / Finance / Legal / Network Manager | COO | Per claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Warranty (VS-53); quality (VS-31); CX (VS-13); finance (VS-17/VS-16); legal (VS-100); surety/bond (VS-116)

- **Installer-non-remediation risk**: Installer won't fix → customer harm; mitigated by retention/bond and alternate remediation
- **Cost-recovery risk**: Remediation cost not recovered from installer; mitigated by retention/bond mechanism

### Automation Opportunity
- Claim intake/workflow; defect-liability tracking; cost-recovery from retention; scorecard feed

### Controls
- CTL-748 (ensure controlled execution — Network Performance, Quality, Compliance & Analytics (PA-172.3).)
- CTL-748: warranty terms in agreement; claims tracked to resolution; cost recovered; scorecard updated

### Time Estimate

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~1 risk/privacy assurance cycle across the network |
| **Owner** | Pro-Referral Network Manager / TPRM (VS-161) / DPO (VS-91) / Internal Audit (VS-21) |
| **Participants** | TPRM (VS-161), DPO/Privacy (VS-91), Internal Audit (VS-21), Legal (VS-100), Security (VS-27.3/VS-159) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manage network risk via TPRM: tier installers by risk (W4989), monitor fourth-party/subcontractor risk, concentration risk (single installer/area), continuity/exit (alternate W5000), and feed the enterprise TPRM program (VS-161) | TPRM / Network Manager / Analytics | TPRM Lead / COO | Annual + continuous |
| 2 | Assure data privacy: govern customer-data shared with installers (consent W4993, DPA in agreement W4989, installer data-handling, breach response links VS-91), installer access/credentials (VS-27), and DSAR handling (VS-91 W271) | DPO / Legal / IT Security / Network Manager | DPO | Continuous |

### Automation Opportunity
- TPRM tiering/monitoring; privacy/DPA workflow; access/credential governance; audit-test automation

### Controls
- CTL-748 (ensure controlled execution — Network Performance, Quality, Compliance & Analytics (PA-172.3).)

### Time Estimate
