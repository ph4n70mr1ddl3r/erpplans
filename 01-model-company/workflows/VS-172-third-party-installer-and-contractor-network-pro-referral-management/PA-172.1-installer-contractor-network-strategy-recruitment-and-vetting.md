## W4985. Installer & Contractor Network Strategy, Trade-Coverage & Capacity Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch; annual strategy; coverage gap; format/region expansion (VS-37) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on launch |
| **Volume** | 1 network program across 200 trade-areas × trade specialties |
| **Owner** | VP Services / Pro-Referral Network Manager / Strategy (VS-33) |
| **Participants** | Services (VS-12), Store Ops (VS-07), B2B/Key Accounts (VS-11/VS-107), Trade Program (VS-43), Strategy (VS-33), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the installer-network strategy & operating model: the referral-vs-managed-marketplace model, the trade specialties covered (electrical, plumbing, carpentry, tiling, paint, HVAC/solar, appliance, roofing, masonry), the own-crew (VS-12) vs network split, the geographic/trade-coverage targets, the referral economics (W4997), and the positioning ("Your Home Building Partner") | Network Manager / Strategy / Services / B2B | VP Services / COO | Annual |
| 2 | Design trade-coverage & capacity: per trade-area (region/province) the required installer count/capacity by specialty, the tier model (preferred/standard), the service-level targets (response, quote, completion), and the build-vs-partner decision per trade | Network Manager / Strategy / Store Ops | VP Services | Annual |
| 3 | Build the business case & govern: referral-fee/commission economics, attach/CLV impact, the platform/ops cost, KPIs (coverage %, fill-rate, rating, conversion), board/leadership approval, and integration with services (VS-12), trade (VS-43), and store opening (VS-37) | Network Manager / Finance / Strategy | VP Services / CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy/FP&A (VS-33/VS-17.4); services (VS-12); B2B/trade (VS-11/VS-43); marketplace platform (VS-95); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Coverage-gap risk**: Insufficient installers in a trade/area → unfulfilled referrals; mitigated by coverage design (W4991)
- **Channel-conflict risk**: Network installers competing with BuildRight's own crews (VS-12); mitigated by the build-vs-partner split
- **Economics risk**: Referral fees too low/high → installer disinterest or customer friction; mitigated by economics modeling (W4997)

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual strategy; ~quarterly review

### Automation Opportunity
- Coverage/capacity modeling; trade-area analytics; economics simulator; KPI dashboards

### Controls
- CTL: strategy board-approved; coverage targets set; build-vs-partner split defined; KPIs tracked

### Cross-references
- links to VS-12 (own crews), VS-11/VS-43/VS-107 (B2B/trade/key accounts), VS-95 (marketplace), VS-33 (strategy), VS-37 (expansion), W4991 (coverage), W4997 (economics)

## W4987. Contractor Vetting, PCAB License (RA 4566) & Insurance Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Candidate progression (W4986); periodic re-verification (W5006) |
| **Frequency** | Per candidate + periodic re-verification |
| **Volume** | ~vetting per candidate × network + periodic re-checks |
| **Owner** | Pro-Referral Network Manager / Compliance (VS-165/VS-22) / Legal (VS-100) |
| **Participants** | Compliance (VS-165/VS-22), Legal (VS-100), TPRM (VS-161), Quality (VS-31), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify licenses & legal eligibility: PCAB contractor license (RA 4566 — links VS-165, verify it's the *contractor's* license, current, correct category), DTI/SEC registration, BIR registration, mayor's/LGU permit, and trade-specific licenses (electrical license, plumbing, FPA for pesticides, etc.); verify against official registers | Compliance / Legal / Network Manager | Compliance Lead | Per candidate |
| 2 | Verify insurance & bonding: public-liability/professional indemnity insurance (current, adequate limits, BuildRight named/added insured where required), performance bond/surety for larger projects (links VS-116), workers' compensation/SSS for the installer's crew, and coverage adequacy | Insurance / Legal / Network Manager | Compliance Lead | Per candidate |
| 3 | Document & gate onboarding: capture verification evidence, gate onboarding (W4989) on verified license+insurance, set re-verification (W5006), and manage exceptions/remediation; feed TPRM (VS-161) | Compliance / Legal / TPRM | Compliance Lead | Per candidate |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/license (VS-165/VS-22/VS-166); legal (VS-100); TPRM (VS-161); insurance (VS-26); surety (VS-116); marketplace platform (VS-95)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unlicensed risk**: Referring an unlicensed contractor (RA 4566) → illegal contract, customer harm, BuildRight liability; mitigated by PCAB verification gate
- **Uninsured risk**: Installer without adequate insurance → uncovered liability; mitigated by insurance verification
- **Lapse risk**: License/insurance lapses after onboarding; mitigated by periodic re-verification (W5006)

### Time Estimate
- ~per-candidate vetting 1–3 weeks (verification turnaround)

### Automation Opportunity
- License/insurance register auto-verify; lapse-monitoring (W5006); onboarding gate; TPRM integration

### Controls
- CTL-98 (ensure installer-network vetting & referral integrity.)
- CTL: PCAB/insurance verified before onboarding; evidence captured; onboarding gated; re-verification scheduled (W5006)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-165 (PCAB/RA 4566), VS-166 (license portfolio), VS-100 (legal), VS-161 (TPRM), VS-26 (insurance), VS-116 (surety), W4988 (DD), W4989 (onboarding), W5006 (re-verification)

## W4989. Installer Network Onboarding, Agreement & Tier Classification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vetting approved (W4987/W4988); tier change |
| **Frequency** | Per installer + tier changes |
| **Volume** | ~onboarding per installer × network |
| **Owner** | Pro-Referral Network Manager / Legal (VS-100) |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100), Services (VS-12), Trade Program (VS-43), IT (VS-27), Finance (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Execute the installer agreement: the referral/marketplace agreement (distinct from employment — clarifies BuildRight is not the installer's employer, mitigating co-employment/DOLE D.O. 174 risk links VS-98), the referral-fee/commission terms (W4997), the service-level/quality/warranty commitments, the code-of-conduct (W4992), insurance/indemnity, data-privacy (DPA, customer-data handling VS-91), and termination | Legal / Network Manager / Installer | VP Services / Legal | Per installer |
| 2 | Classify tier & set profile: tier assignment (preferred/standard/ probation based on W4987/W4988), trade specialties, service area, capacity, pricing/rate card, and the marketplace profile/catalog (W4990) | Network Manager / Trade Program / Installer | Network Manager | Per installer |
| 3 | Onboard to systems & launch: provision the marketplace/platform account (VS-95/VS-27), the lead-routing/CRM (W4994), the settlement/billing (W4997), train on the referral process/code-of-conduct (W4992), and launch with probationary monitoring (W5003) | IT / Network Manager / Training / Finance | Network Manager | Per installer |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal/contract (VS-100); marketplace platform (VS-95); CRM/lead (VS-11/VS-107); finance/settlement (VS-17/VS-16); privacy/DPA (VS-91); IT (VS-27)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Misclassification risk**: Agreement implying employment → co-employment (VS-98); mitigated by clear referral-agreement framing
- **Onboarding-failure risk**: Installer live without training/systems → poor referrals; mitigated by the onboarding gate
- **Profile-error risk**: Wrong trade/area/capacity → mis-routing (W4994); mitigated by profile verification

### Time Estimate
- ~per-installer onboarding 1–2 weeks (agreement + setup + training)

### Automation Opportunity
- Agreement e-sign/template; tier-classification rules; profile/catalog auto-build; system-provisioning workflow

### Controls
- CTL: agreement executed (referral, not employment); tier/profile verified; onboarding gate; probationary monitoring

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal/contract), VS-98 (co-employment risk), VS-95 (marketplace), VS-91 (DPA/privacy), W4987/W4988 (vetting), W4990 (profile), W4992 (code-of-conduct), W4997 (economics), W5003 (monitoring)

| **Volume** | ~1 coverage analysis across 200 trade-areas × trade specialties |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
