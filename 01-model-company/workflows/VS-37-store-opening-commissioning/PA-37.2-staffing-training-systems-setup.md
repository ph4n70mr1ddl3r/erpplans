## W1746. Store Manager Selection & Appointment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New store project charter approved (W1737); Store Manager needed 3 months before opening |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 selections/year |
| **Volume** | 1 Store Manager per new store |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, Regional Manager, CHRO, HR Recruitment |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Store Operations and Regional Manager identify Store Manager candidate: (a) preferred: promote experienced Assistant Store Manager from existing store (internal), (b) alternative: experienced Department Supervisor ready for promotion, (c) external hire: only if no qualified internal candidates (hardware/DIY retail experience required) | VP Store Operations / Regional Manager | CHRO | 2–4 weeks |
| 2 | Selection criteria: (a) minimum 2 years retail management experience (preferably hardware/home improvement), (b) demonstrated P&L awareness, (c) leadership and team management skills, (d) operational knowledge (receiving, inventory, POS, customer service), (e) willingness to relocate (if needed for new store location) | VP Store Operations | COO | 1 week |
| 3 | CHRO processes appointment: (a) offer letter with compensation package (Store Manager salary range per company structure), (b) transfer documentation (if internal), (c) appointment effective 3 months before store opening, (d) assigned to existing store for orientation during transition period | CHRO | VP Store Operations | 1 week |
| 4 | New Store Manager assigned to project: (a) participates in all pre-opening meetings per W1744, (b) begins learning store-specific details (location, trade area, layout, systems), (c) supports hiring of store team per W1747 | Store Manager | VP Store Operations | 3 months pre-opening |


### System Touchpoints
- Candidate identification — internal Assistant Store Manager promotion preferred (step 1)
- Selection criteria — 2+ years retail management, P&L literacy, leadership (step 2)
- Appointment processing — offer, compensation, training plan (step 3)
- Project assignment — participation in all W1744 pre-opening meetings (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Untested-manager risk**: a first-time Store Manager struggles through the most demanding 90 days of the store's life; mitigated by the training plan and Regional Manager mentoring (steps 3–4)
- **Fit risk**: an external hire without hardware/home-improvement context misreads the trade customer; mitigated by category-experience selection criteria (step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-identify of "identify Store Manager candidate" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documentation" (replaces manual Step 3).
- System auto-assign of "assigned to project" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-353 (ensure controlled execution — Store Staffing, Training & Systems Setup (PA-37.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
Per selection ~1–2 weeks of concentrated effort (candidate identification over a 2–4-week elapsed window + criteria evaluation 1 week + CHRO appointment processing 1 week; the 3-month pre-opening assignment follows per W1747) × ~10–15 selections/year ≈ 10–30 weeks/year.

## W1747. Store Staff Recruitment & Hiring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store Manager appointed (W1746); 2 months before opening |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 hiring campaigns/year |
| **Volume** | 28 staff per new store (29 total minus Store Manager already appointed) |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, HR Recruitment, Regional Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store Manager and HR Recruitment plan hiring for 28 positions: (a) 1 Assistant Store Manager, (b) 4 Department Supervisors (Lumber, Plumbing/Electrical, Tiles, Tools/Hardware), (c) 12 Sales Associates, (d) 3 Cashiers, (e) 2 Receiving Clerks, (f) 4 Stock Associates, (g) 1 Customer Service Rep, (h) 1 Maintenance/Utility | Store Manager / HR Recruitment | CHRO | 1 week |
| 2 | Recruitment channels: (a) internal postings (for supervisor promotions from existing stores), (b) local job postings (barangay bulletin boards, local Facebook groups, job boards), (c) walk-in applications at store site during construction, (d) referrals from existing store staff, (e) recruitment agency for specialized roles (paint mixing, tool experts) | HR Recruitment | Store Manager | 2–3 weeks |
| 3 | Interview and selection: (a) Store Manager screens applications, (b) Regional Manager interviews supervisor candidates, (c) practical assessments: cashier candidates test on POS simulator, sales associates assessed on product knowledge, (d) background checks and reference verification, (e) offers extended; target: all staff hired 4 weeks before opening | Store Manager / Regional Manager | VP Store Operations | 2–3 weeks |
| 4 | Pre-employment requirements collected: (a) SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG numbers, (b) TIN (Tax Identification Number), (c) NBI clearance, (d) medical certificate, (e) barangay clearance, (f) diploma/transcripts, (g) emergency contact information | HR Recruitment | Store Manager | 2 weeks |


### System Touchpoints
- Hiring plan for 28 positions — ASM, Department Supervisors, cashiers, receiving, sales associates (step 1)
- Multi-channel recruitment — internal postings, barangay boards, local job sites (step 2)
- Structured interview and selection (step 3)
- Pre-employment requirements pipeline — SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, TIN, NBI clearance (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-hire-at-opening risk**: positions still vacant when the doors open; mitigated by starting 2 months out with internal-posting fast tracks (step 1)
- **Clearance-delay risk**: hires blocked pending NBI/government numbers miss payroll setup; mitigated by collecting requirements progressively during selection (step 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-post of "postings" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "checks and reference verification," (replaces manual Step 3).
- System workflow notification of "contact information" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-353 (ensure controlled execution — Store Staffing, Training & Systems Setup (PA-37.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
7–9 weeks elapsed per occurrence (1 week + 2–3 weeks + 2–3 weeks + 2 weeks) — at ~10–15 hiring campaigns/year ≈ 10–15 occurrences/year.

### Steps

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Day 2 — Store-specific orientation: (a) store tour: layout, zones, receiving area, breakroom, emergency exits, (b) safety orientation: fire exits, extinguisher locations, emergency procedures, (c) introduction to all team members, (d) work schedule and shift assignments, (e) uniform and PPE issuance, (f) locker assignments | Store Manager | — | 1 day |

## W1749. POS & Systems Training for New Store Staff

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Staff onboarded (W1748); 3 weeks before opening |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 training sessions/year |
| **Volume** | 29 staff per store (role-specific training) |
| **Owner** | IT Training Lead |
| **Participants** | IT Training Lead, Store Manager, all store staff |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cashier training (3 Cashiers + 1 backup from Customer Service Rep): (a) POS login and navigation, (b) barcode scanning (1D and 2D), (c) transaction types: cash, card, e-wallet, charge account, (d) multi-tender transactions (split payment), (e) returns and exchanges, (f) price override with manager authorization, (g) loyalty points earning and redemption, (h) weight-based items (nails, screws by kg), (i) cut-to-length items (lumber, pipes), (j) paint mixing custom SKU generation, (k) age-restricted product prompts, (l) end-of-day: Z-report, cash count, reconciliation, deposit, (m) offline POS operation | IT Training Lead | Store Manager | 3 days |
| 2 | Receiving Clerk training (2 Clerks): (a) PO/ASN lookup on handheld tablet, (b) goods receipt processing: scan, verify quantities, note discrepancies, (c) DSD receiving procedures, (d) return-to-vendor processing, (e) receiving exception handling (damaged goods, wrong items) | IT Training Lead | Store Manager | 2 days |
| 3 | Stock Associate training (4 Associates): (a) RF gun operation for cycle counting, (b) inventory lookup and stock inquiry, (c) replenishment from backroom, (d) BOPIS order picking and staging, (e) shelf label printing and price tag updates | IT Training Lead | Store Manager | 2 days |
| 4 | Store Manager and Assistant Manager training: (a) all above modules, plus: (b) store dashboard and KPI monitoring, (c) approval workflows (price overrides, expense approvals, overtime), (d) daily store reporting, (e) cash management and deposit verification, (f) incident reporting in system | IT Training Lead | Regional Manager | 2 days |


### System Touchpoints
- Role-based systems training modules — cashier (POS, scanning, tenders), receiving (GR, ASN), stock (RF gun), manager (dashboards, approvals) (steps 1–4)
- Hands-on POS/RF practice environment (steps 1, 3)
- Manager approval-workflow training (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Untrained-at-live risk**: a cashier meets their first real transaction on opening day; mitigated by cross-training a backup cashier per shift and hands-on practice (step 1)
- **Skill-regression risk**: trained weeks early and forgotten by opening; mitigated by proficiency checks at the W1752/W1753 verification stages (steps 1, 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-scan of "scanning" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "verify quantities, note discrepancies," (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-generate of "printing and price tag updates" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-353 (ensure controlled execution — Store Staffing, Training & Systems Setup (PA-37.2).)
- operational: IT Training Lead review-and-approval gate; periodic reconciliation against source documents

### Time Estimate
9 days elapsed per occurrence (3 days + 2 days + 2 days + 2 days) — at ~10–15 training sessions/year ≈ 10–15 occurrences/year.

| **Frequency** | ~10–15 training sessions/year |
| **Volume** | 29 staff per store |

### Steps

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Sales Associates receive category-specific training assigned to their department: (a) basic product knowledge for their assigned zone, (b) where to find product information in the system (specs, dimensions, availability), (c) how to direct customers to the right department, (d) basic project estimation concepts (for DIY customers asking "how much do I need?") | Department Supervisors | Store Manager | 2 days |
| 3 | Vendor-led training for specialized categories: (a) paint mixing — paint vendor trains designated staff on color matching, mixing ratios, equipment operation, (b) power tools — vendor demonstrates safe operation and customer demonstration techniques, (c) appliances — vendor trains on installation requirements, warranty terms, energy efficiency ratings, (d) tiles — vendor trains on tile selection guidance (size, material, application) | Vendor Trainers | Store Manager | 1–2 days |

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Wrong-advice risk**: staff give incorrect product guidance to trade professionals, damaging credibility on day one; mitigated by readiness assessment with remediation (step 4)

- System auto-match of "matching, mixing ratios, equipment operation," (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-353 (ensure controlled execution — Store Staffing, Training & Systems Setup (PA-37.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

## W1751. Safety & Compliance Training for New Store

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Staff onboarded (W1748); 2 weeks before opening |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 training sessions/year |
| **Volume** | 29 staff per store |
| **Owner** | Safety Officer |
| **Participants** | Safety Officer, Store Manager, all store staff, Local Fire Department |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | General safety orientation: (a) fire safety: extinguisher use (PASS technique), evacuation routes and assembly points, (b) earthquake safety: drop-cover-hold, building evacuation, (c) typhoon preparedness: store securing procedures, emergency supplies, (d) first aid basics: wound care, burn treatment, CPR awareness, (e) PPE usage: when and how to use gloves, hard hats, safety glasses, masks | Safety Officer | Store Manager | 1 day |
| 2 | Category-specific safety: (a) paint and chemicals: SDS (Safety Data Sheet) reading, spill response, proper storage, ventilation requirements, (b) lumber yard: safe lifting techniques, forklift awareness, stacking safety, (c) power tools: demonstration safety, blade guard checks, electrical safety, (d) electrical section: no live wire handling by staff, customer referral to licensed electrician, (e) receiving dock: truck safety, forklift-pedestrian awareness, manual handling | Safety Officer / Department Supervisors | Store Manager | 1 day |
| 3 | Compliance-specific training: (a) price tag display requirements (Consumer Act RA 7394), (b) return policy and customer rights, (c) age-restricted product handling (solvents, cutting tools), (d) data privacy: customer information handling per RA 10173, (e) anti-money laundering awareness for large cash transactions (> PHP 500K) | Legal Counsel / Compliance Officer | Store Manager | 0.5 day |
| 4 | Fire drill conducted with local fire department; store emergency plan posted in breakroom and receiving area; safety committee formed (Store Manager + 2 staff volunteers) | Safety Officer / Local Fire Dept | Store Manager | 0.5 day |


### System Touchpoints
- General safety orientation — fire (PASS, evacuation), earthquake, first aid (step 1)
- Category-specific safety — SDS reading, spill response, chemical storage (step 2)
- Compliance training — RA 7394 price display, return policy, customer rights (step 3)
- Fire drill with the local fire department; safety committee formed (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Day-one-incident risk**: an emergency in opening week with an unprepared crew; mitigated by drills and posted emergency plans before opening (step 4)
- **Regulatory-violation risk**: price-tag or returns handling violates Consumer Act rules from day one; mitigated by Legal-led compliance training (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "preparedness" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "checks, electrical safety," (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-post of "posted in breakroom and receiving area" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-188 (prevent premature store go-live.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
3 days elapsed per occurrence (1 day + 1 day + 0.5 day + 0.5 day) — at ~10–15 training sessions/year ≈ 10–15 occurrences/year.

## W1752. Store System Configuration & Go-Live Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | IT infrastructure installed (W1741); 1 week before opening |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 verifications/year |
| **Volume** | 1 system verification per new store |
| **Owner** | IT Infrastructure Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Infrastructure Manager, IT Applications, Store Manager, Store Opening Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System configuration verification: (a) store location active in ERP with correct master data, (b) pricing zone and tax configuration verified, (c) POS terminals connected, logged in, and processing test transactions, (d) RF guns connected and scanning correctly, (e) CCTV recording and transmitting to central monitoring, (f) network connectivity tested (primary + backup), (g) offline POS capability tested (simulate outage for 30 min), (h) loyalty system integration tested (points earn and redeem), (i) ecommerce BOPIS integration tested (order received at store, pick confirmed) | IT Infrastructure Team | IT Infrastructure Manager | 2 days |
| 2 | Data verification: (a) 35,000 SKU master loaded and pricing verified (sample 100 SKUs across categories), (b) store replenishment parameters active, (c) GL cost center and profit center posting correctly, (d) intercompany setup for BuildRight Depot Inc. entity | IT Applications / Finance | IT Infrastructure Manager | 1 day |
| 3 | User acceptance testing (UAT): (a) Store Manager processes full end-to-end test: receive goods → shelve → sell at POS → tender → print receipt → end-of-day close → cash reconciliation → daily report, (b) all transaction types tested (cash, card, e-wallet, return, loyalty redemption, weight-based, cut-to-length), (c) any issues logged and resolved before opening | Store Manager / IT | IT Infrastructure Manager | 1 day |
| 4 | Go-live sign-off: IT Infrastructure Manager and Store Manager sign system readiness confirmation; Store Opening Coordinator updates critical path per W1744 | IT Infrastructure Manager / Store Manager | CIO | 1 day |


### System Touchpoints
- System configuration verification — store master, pricing zone, tax setup (step 1)
- Data verification — 35,000-SKU master loaded; 100-SKU pricing sample across categories (step 2)
- End-to-end UAT — receive → shelve → sell → tender → post (step 3)
- Go-live sign-off by IT Infrastructure Manager and Store Manager (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pricing-error risk**: a wrong pricing zone misprices the entire store on opening day; mitigated by the 100-SKU sample verification before sign-off (step 2)
- **Untested-flow risk**: a receiving or payment flow fails mid-morning on day one; mitigated by the full end-to-end UAT gate (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-scan of "scanning correctly," (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-capture of "enter and profit center posting correctly," (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-generate of "print receipt → end-of-day close → cash reconciliation → daily report," (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-188 (prevent premature store go-live.)
- operational: periodic reconciliation against source documents

### Time Estimate
5 days elapsed per occurrence (2 days + 1 day + 1 day + 1 day) — at ~10–15 verifications/year ≈ 10–15 occurrences/year.

## W1754. Store Cash Float & Financial Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Systems verified (W1752); 1 week before opening |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 setups/year |
| **Volume** | 1 financial setup per new store |
| **Owner** | Treasury |
| **Participants** | Treasury, Store Manager, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury opens bank deposit account for new store: (a) bank chosen per company banking relationship (BDO, BPI, Metrobank, or Chinabank — closest branch to store location), (b) corporate resolution per W1717 for account opening, (c) Store Manager and Assistant Manager listed as authorized signatories for deposits only (withdrawals require HQ approval) | Treasury | CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | Petty cash float established: (a) PHP 20,000 petty cash fund per store policy per W1688, (b) cash float for 3 POS terminals: PHP 5,000 per terminal (PHP 15,000 total), (c) Store Manager receives and signs for cash floats; records in cash management system | Treasury / Store Manager | Accounting Manager | 1 day |
| 3 | GL setup: Accounting Manager verifies new store cost center and profit center are active in GL chart of accounts; intercompany billing setup for lease payments to Property Management entity; payroll entity assignment confirmed (BuildRight Depot Inc. for store staff) | Accounting Manager | CFO | 1 day |
| 4 | Daily cash deposit process established: (a) Store Manager prepares deposit after end-of-day close, (b) armored car or bank branch deposit per location arrangement, (c) deposit slip uploaded to treasury system, (d) treasury confirms deposit next business day | Store Manager / Treasury | Accounting Manager | Ongoing |


### System Touchpoints
- Bank deposit account opening per company banking relationships (step 1)
- Cash floats — PHP 20,000 petty cash per W1688 plus per-terminal POS floats (step 2)
- GL setup verification — cost/profit centers, intercompany billing activation (step 3)
- Daily cash deposit process — armored car or bank branch (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cash-process-gap risk**: day-one sales with no deposit process or floats established; mitigated by completing all cash setup before the W1752 sign-off (steps 1–2)
- **IC-billing omission**: store costs never bill between entities from day one; mitigated by GL/intercompany verification at setup (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count for new store" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "records in cash management system" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-capture of "enter and profit center are active in GL chart of accounts" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-188 (prevent premature store go-live.)
- operational: Treasury review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
1.3–2.3 weeks elapsed per occurrence (1–2 weeks + 1 day + 1 day); cadence-carried steps as authored (Ongoing) — at ~10–15 setups/year ≈ 10–15 occurrences/year.
