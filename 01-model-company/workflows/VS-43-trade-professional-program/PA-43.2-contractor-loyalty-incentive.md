## W1886. Contractor Loyalty Program Tier Design & Benefit Structure Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual program review; competitive benchmarking |
| **Frequency** | Annual review; redesign every 2–3 years |
| **Volume** | 4 loyalty tiers across ~5,200 trade accounts |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, CMO, CFO, Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Annual: VP Store Operations reviews contractor loyalty tier structure: (a) current tiers: Bronze (PHP 0–500K/year), Silver (PHP 500K–2M), Gold (PHP 2M–10M), Platinum (PHP 10M+), (b) evaluate tier distribution: are too many accounts in Bronze (devaluing program) or too few in Platinum (unattainable)?, (c) competitive benchmarking: what do CitiHardware, Wilcon Depot, and ACE Hardware offer trade professionals?, (d) member satisfaction survey: do contractors value current benefits? What benefits would increase loyalty? | VP Store Operations | COO | 1 week |
| 2 | VP Store Operations and CMO redesign benefit structure: (a) pricing benefits: trade discount by tier (Bronze 5%, Silver 8%, Gold 12%, Platinum 15%), (b) service benefits: priority delivery scheduling, dedicated phone line, extended store hours access, (c) credit benefits: higher credit limits, longer payment terms for higher tiers, (d) experiential benefits: exclusive contractor events, product training, jobsite visits by Category Managers, (e) partner benefits: discounts from partner businesses (insurance, fuel, vehicle rental), (f) financial modeling: cost of benefits per tier vs. incremental margin from higher spend | VP Store Operations / CMO | CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | CFO approves program financial model: (a) total program cost (discounts, benefits, points liability) vs. incremental revenue from tier-based spend uplift, (b) target ROI: program cost should not exceed 2–3% of B2B revenue, (c) points liability impact on balance sheet per W1893 | CFO | CEO | 1–2 days |
| 4 | IT configures loyalty tier rules in POS and CRM: (a) tier thresholds and auto-upgrade/downgrade rules, (b) benefit activation per tier, (c) points earning rates per tier (higher tiers earn points faster), (d) test configuration before go-live | IT | CIO | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Loyalty tier management module with auto-classification (W1886.4a)
- Benefit activation per tier in POS, CRM, and ecommerce (W1886.4b)
- Financial modeling for program ROI (W1886.3)
- Integration with W1549 (contractor annual spend tier review), W17 (consumer loyalty — separate program), W104 (loyalty financial governance)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Tier compression**: If tier thresholds are too low, most accounts qualify for Silver/Gold, diluting the exclusivity that drives aspirational spending
- **Benefit cost escalation**: Generous benefits (e.g., 15% Platinum discount) may erode margin below acceptable levels on high-volume accounts; ensure minimum margin floor per category


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews contractor loyalty tier structure" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "count by tier" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves program financial model" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-371 (ensure controlled execution — Contractor Loyalty, Incentive & Volume Program (PA-43.2).)
operational: VP Store Operations review and approval gate

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by VP Store Operations, CMO, CFO, and IT

### Time Estimate
- Annual review: 1 week
- Benefit redesign (if applicable): 1–2 weeks
- Financial approval: 1–2 days
- IT configuration: 1–2 weeks
- Total: ~3–6 weeks/year

## W1887. Contractor Points Earning, Redemption & Statement Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contractor purchase at POS; points redemption request |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~15,000 B2B transactions/month generating points |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 trade accounts earning and redeeming points |
| **Owner** | Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Account Manager, Cashier, Trade Professional, Loyalty System |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contractor makes purchase at POS using trade account card: (a) system identifies trade account and loyalty tier, (b) points earned per spend: Bronze 1 point per PHP 100, Silver 1.5 per PHP 100, Gold 2 per PHP 100, Platinum 3 per PHP 100, (c) system posts points to contractor's loyalty account with transaction reference, (d) points visible in contractor's online portal and mobile app | System | — | Automated |
| 2 | Contractor redeems points: (a) at POS: contractor presents trade account card and requests points redemption; cashier enters redemption amount; system converts points to peso value (1 point = PHP 1.00), (b) online: contractor redeems points for merchandise or gift items through trade portal, (c) points deduction posted to loyalty account; redemption value reduces transaction total or issued as store credit | Cashier / System | — | 2–3 min |
| 3 | Monthly: system generates contractor loyalty statement: (a) points earned this month and year-to-date, (b) points redeemed this month and year-to-date, (c) current points balance and peso equivalent, (d) current tier and progress to next tier, (e) next tier benefits preview, (f) statement sent via email and available in trade portal | System / Account Manager | VP Store Operations | Automated + 1 day/month |
| 4 | Quarterly: Account Manager reviews points liability for assigned accounts: (a) total unredeemed points per account, (b) accounts with high points balances that may indicate dissatisfaction (earning but not redeeming = not engaging with program benefits), (c) accounts approaching tier upgrade — proactive communication to encourage next-tier spend | Account Manager | VP Store Operations | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Loyalty points engine integrated with POS and trade account system (W1887.1)
- Points earning rate configuration per tier (W1887.1b)
- Redemption processing at POS and online (W1887.2)
- Monthly statement generation and distribution (W1887.3)
- Integration with W17 (consumer loyalty — same engine, different program), W5B (POS selling), W10.1 (ecommerce platform)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Points liability growth**: Unredeemed points accumulate as a liability on the balance sheet; if redemption rate is low, liability grows unchecked; encourage redemption through targeted promotions


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count card" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-capture of "enters redemption amount" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review," (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-371 (ensure controlled execution — Contractor Loyalty, Incentive & Volume Program (PA-43.2).)
operational: Account Manager review and approval gate

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — automated system with AM oversight

### Time Estimate
- Points processing: automated
- Monthly statement review: 1 day/month
- Quarterly points liability review: 1 day/quarter
- Total: ~20 days/year

## W1888. Contractor Volume Rebate & Spend-Based Incentive Calculation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual/quarterly volume rebate calculation period end |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly calculation for Gold/Platinum; annual for Bronze/Silver |
| **Volume** | ~200 Gold/Platinum accounts eligible for volume rebates |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, Account Manager, Finance, Trade Professional |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A calculates volume rebate per eligible account: (a) total qualifying spend during period (exclude returns, taxes, non-merchandise), (b) apply rebate rate per spend tier: (i) PHP 2M–5M: 1% rebate, (ii) PHP 5M–10M: 1.5%, (iii) PHP 10M–20M: 2%, (iv) PHP 20M+: 2.5%, (c) rebate paid as credit memo to trade account (applied against future purchases) or as direct refund | FP&A | CFO | 1–2 days/period |
| 2 | Account Manager communicates rebate to trade professional: (a) present rebate calculation with supporting spend data, (b) explain how rebate will be applied (credit memo or refund), (c) encourage reinvestment: suggest using rebate credit for upcoming project materials | Account Manager | VP Store Operations | 30 min/account |
| 3 | Finance processes rebate: (a) credit memo: Dr. Trade Promotion Expense / Cr. Accounts Receivable — Trade Account, (b) refund: Dr. Trade Promotion Expense / Cr. Cash, (c) update trade account balance | Finance | Controller | Per AP process |

### System Touchpoints
- Volume rebate calculation engine with configurable tier rates (W1888.1)
- Rebate credit memo generation linked to AR (W1888.3a)
- Integration with W39 (vendor rebate — similar logic, vendor side), W85 (product costing — margin impact)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin erosion**: Cumulative effect of trade discount (5–15%) + loyalty points (1–3%) + volume rebate (1–2.5%) can reduce effective margin below acceptable levels; FP&A must monitor total trade program cost per account


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-communication of "communicates rebate to trade professional" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-update of "update trade account balance" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-371 (ensure controlled execution — Contractor Loyalty, Incentive & Volume Program (PA-43.2).)
operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by FP&A and Account Managers

### Time Estimate
- Calculation: 1–2 days/period
- Communication: ~100 hours/period
- Processing: per AP
- Total: ~300–500 hours/year

## W1893. Contractor Loyalty Program Financial Governance & Liability Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly financial close; annual loyalty liability review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly liability posting; annual review |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 accounts with cumulative points liability |
| **Owner** | Finance |
| **Participants** | Finance, VP Store Operations, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly: Finance posts loyalty points liability: (a) Dr. Loyalty Program Expense / Cr. Deferred Revenue — Loyalty Points (for points issued during the month valued at redemption rate), (b) upon redemption: Dr. Deferred Revenue — Loyalty Points / Cr. Revenue (or contra-revenue), (c) estimate breakage: % of points that will never be redeemed based on historical pattern (typically 15–25% for trade programs) | Finance | Controller | Per W9A |
| 2 | Quarterly: Finance reviews loyalty liability adequacy: (a) total liability balance vs. redemption trend, (b) is breakage estimate still appropriate? Adjust if actual redemption patterns change, (c) liability as % of B2B revenue — flag if > 3% (program cost growing faster than revenue) | Finance | CFO | 1 day/quarter |
| 3 | Annual: External Auditor tests loyalty liability: (a) verify liability calculation methodology, (b) test breakage assumption against historical data, (c) assess completeness of points outstanding | External Auditor | CFO | Per audit cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Loyalty points liability module with automated monthly posting (W1893.1)
- Breakage estimation engine (W1893.1c)
- Liability adequacy review analytics (W1893.2)
- Integration with W104 (loyalty financial governance — consumer program), W9A (month-end close), W9B (year-end close)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Liability overstatement/understatement**: If breakage estimate is wrong, loyalty liability is misstated on the balance sheet; regular calibration with actual redemption data is essential


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-post of "posts loyalty points liability" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews loyalty liability adequacy" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "logy," (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-371 (ensure controlled execution — Contractor Loyalty, Incentive & Volume Program (PA-43.2).)
operational: Finance review and approval gate

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by Finance

### Time Estimate
- Monthly posting: per W9A (automated)
- Quarterly review: 1 day/quarter
- Total: ~5 days/year
