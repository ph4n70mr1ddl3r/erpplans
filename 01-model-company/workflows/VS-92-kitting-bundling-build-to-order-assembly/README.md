## Overview

Kitting, Bundling & Build-to-Order Assembly Operations workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the definition, build, inventory, pricing, and performance of pre-packaged **Kit / Bundle**
item types (per model company §6.4) across 35,000 active SKUs, 4 DCs, 200 stores, and the
ecommerce/marketplace channels. Covers centralized DC kit-build work orders, store-level display
assembly and light value-add (grill/furniture assembly, sample bundles, contractor combo packs),
bill-of-materials (BOM) management, component allocation, bundle pricing/promotions, and
kit/bundle sell-through and margin analytics.

While **VS-09 PA-09.1** owns customer-requested **custom fabrication** (cut-to-length, key
cutting, glass cutting) and **VS-01** owns assortment/price strategy, this value stream owns the
**build-to-stock kit/bundle operations**: kit product strategy and BOM, component allocation and
build planning, centralized and store-level assembly execution, tear-down and component recovery,
and bundle pricing/promotion performance — distinct from on-demand custom services.
