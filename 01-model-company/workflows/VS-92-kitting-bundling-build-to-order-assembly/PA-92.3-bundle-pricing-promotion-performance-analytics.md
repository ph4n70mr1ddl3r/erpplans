## W3081. Bundle Promotion Execution, Price Override & POS Application

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Promotion event or price change |
| **Frequency** | Per promotion cycle |
| **Volume** | Bi-monthly sale events + monthly hot deals (per §13.3) |
| **Owner** | Pricing Manager |
| **Participants** | Pricing, Merchandising, Marketing, IT/POS, Ecommerce |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing configures bundle promotion in price book (links to W40): bundle SKU/promo logic, price, validity, channel scope, and loyalty applicability | Pricing | Pricing Manager | Per promo |
| 2 | IT/POS deploy price change to POS (600 terminals) and ecommerce/marketplace (links to VS-08/VS-10/VS-65); validate price applies correctly at scan | IT / POS | IT Operations Manager | Per promo |
| 3 | Pricing/Store Ops monitor promotion execution; handle price overrides per authorization (links to POS-010); correct configuration errors | Pricing / Store Ops | Pricing Manager | Per promo |

### System Touchpoints
- Price book (links to W40); POS deployment (links to VS-08); ecommerce/marketplace price sync (links to VS-10/VS-65)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Deployment risk**: Promotion not applied at POS or channel; mitigated by pre-launch test transactions


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logic, price, validity, channel scope, and loyalty applicability" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-scan of "IT/POS deploy price change to POS" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "monitor promotion execution" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-507 (ensure controlled execution — Bundle Pricing, Promotion & Performance Analytics (PA-92.3).)
- operational: pre-launch test transactions

### Time Estimate
- Per promotion
