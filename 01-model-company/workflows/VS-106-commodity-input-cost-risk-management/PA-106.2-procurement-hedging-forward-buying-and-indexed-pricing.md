## W3409. Commodity Mitigation Strategy Selection & Hedge Program Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual program design (links to W3406); exposure review (W3404) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + periodic |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 hedged/mitigated commodities |
| **Owner** | CFO |
| **Participants** | CFO, Treasury Manager, Procurement Director, Category Managers (links to VS-01), Risk (links to VS-21.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/Procurement evaluate mitigation options per commodity: financial hedge (futures/forwards/swaps), physical hedge / strategic inventory (W3410), indexed/floating contract (W3411), vendor pass-through clause (W3414), long-term supply agreement (W3415), or accept (self-insure); select based on exposure size (W3404), market liquidity, cost, accounting (W3413), and risk appetite (W3406) | Treasury Manager / Procurement | CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Treasury design the hedge/mitigation program per selected commodity: hedge ratio (% of exposure covered), instrument, tenor matching the purchase plan (links to VS-101 W3284 receipts), trigger/threshold for activation, and the board authority limit (links to W3406); coordinate FX hedges (links to VS-18.3/W321) to avoid interaction gaps (W3408) | Treasury Manager / Risk | CFO | Annual |
| 3 | CFO/Board approve the mitigation program, authority matrix, and accounting designation (links to W3413); document the hedge strategy and rationale for audit (links to VS-21) and PFRS 9 hedge documentation (W3413) | CFO | Board / Audit Committee | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/risk systems (links to VS-18/VS-21.2); merchandise plan (links to VS-101); policy repository (links to VS-186)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Instrument-mismatch risk**: Hedging the wrong instrument/tenor leaves exposure or creates basis risk; mitigated by tenor matching and basis analysis
- **Over-hedge risk**: Hedging beyond physical exposure becomes speculation; mitigated by hedge-ratio policy and board limit


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "counting" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-match of "matching the purchase plan" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document the hedge strategy and rationale for audit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-548 (ensure controlled execution — Procurement Hedging, Forward Buying & Indexed-Pricing Strategy (PA-106.2).)
operational: tenor matching and basis analysis; operational: hedge-ratio policy and board limit

### Time Estimate
- 4–6 weeks annual

| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising, Buyers (links to VS-03.2), Supply Planning (links to VS-02.1), Treasury Manager, Finance (links to VS-101) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement/Merchandising identify forward-buy opportunities from the market outlook (W3407): commodities/SKUs with high price-rise probability, supply-allocation risk, or strategic importance; quantify the buy (volume, $, storage capacity links to VS-04, OTB impact links to VS-101 W3283) and the carrying-cost vs expected price-gain trade-off | Procurement / Merchandising / Treasury | VP Merchandising | Opportunity-driven |
| 2 | Buyers/Merchandising execute the forward buy via PO/blanket (links to VS-03.2 W2C) or pre-buy agreement; coordinate storage at DC (links to VS-04) and financing/cash (links to VS-18/VS-105 WC impact); respect OTB and markdown-budget controls (links to VS-101 W3283/W3285) and inventory-turn guardrails (per profile §12.3) | Buyers / Supply Planning | VP Merchandising | Per buy |

## W3411. Indexed / Formula / Floating Vendor Contract Structuring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategic-vendor contract (links to VS-03.3); commodity exposure (W3401) |
| **Frequency** | Per contract + periodic |
| **Volume** | Top ~50–100 commodity-exposed vendors |
| **Owner** | Procurement Director |
| **Participants** | Procurement Director, Category Managers, Vendor (links to VS-03.3), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Managers/Procurement design the indexed/floating contract structure per vendor/commodity: select the index (W3403), pricing formula (base + index multiplier), adjustment frequency (monthly/quarterly), cap/floor/collar, raw-material component weight (W3402), and notice/audit rights; benchmark to market practice (links to VS-03.3) | Category Managers / Procurement / Finance | Procurement Director | Per contract |
| 2 | Procurement/Legal negotiate and execute the indexed contract clause (links to VS-100.3/W230), ensure it is enforceable, audit-able, and consistent with the exposure map (W3401) and pass-through strategy (W3414); document the formula and index in the contract master (links to VS-402) | Procurement / Legal | Procurement Director | Per contract |
| 3 | Category Managers/Finance load the indexed formula into the price/cost master (links to VS-289 pricing master, VS-292 service/non-stock master), validate initial price, and set up the periodic re-price process (W3414); monitor formula integrity over the contract life | Category Managers / Finance | Procurement Director | Per contract |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor contract (links to VS-03.3/W230/W402); pricing master (links to VS-289); legal (links to VS-100.3); costing (links to VS-85)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Formula-complexity risk**: Over-complex formula is hard to administer/audit; mitigated by simplicity and clear indices
- **Asymmetric-clause risk**: Formula lets vendor raise but not lower price; mitigated by symmetric design and benchmarking (W3403)

- System auto-document of "document the formula and index in the contract master" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "validate initial price, and set up the periodic re-price process" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-548 (ensure controlled execution — Procurement Hedging, Forward Buying & Indexed-Pricing Strategy (PA-106.2).)
operational: simplicity and clear indices; operational: symmetric design and benchmarking (W3403)

### Time Estimate
- Per contract; 2–4 weeks

| 2 | Finance perform periodic (monthly) effectiveness testing and MTM: compute hedge effectiveness, post hedge MTM and effective/ineffective portions to GL (links to VS-17.1/W9A), reclassify to COGS/margin when the hedged purchase occurs, and document for audit; handle de-designation/discontinuance per PFRS 9 | Finance / Treasury | Finance Controller | Monthly |
