## W3417. Input-Cost-to-Price Pass-Through Governance & SRP/Cost Adjustment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Cost change (W3414); margin-plan variance (links to VS-101.3); quarterly pricing review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly/quarterly + on cost change |
| **Volume** | ~35,000 SKUs; ~40 categories |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising, Category Managers, Pricing (links to VS-01.2/VS-57), Merchandising Finance (links to VS-101.3), Master Data (links to VS-29) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Managers/Pricing analyze the cost change (from indexed formula W3414, vendor surcharge, landed cost links to VS-02.2, or hedge settlement W3413) and model the maintained-margin impact (links to VS-101 W3286); determine the pass-through decision: full pass-through, partial (absorb/share), or hold price | Category Managers / Pricing | VP Merchandising | Per change |
| 2 | Category Managers/Pricing execute the SRP/cost change per VS-01.2/W40 (price hierarchy, authority matrix links to VS-36), update shelf labels (links to VS-08 W63/W181), ecommerce/marketplace prices (links to VS-10/VS-65/VS-95 price parity), and comply with DTI price-tag/price-freeze rules (links to VS-22/W468); respect OTB/markdown-budget controls (links to VS-101 W3283/W3285) | Category Managers / Pricing / Master Data | VP Merchandising | Per change |
| 3 | Merchandising Finance track realized pass-through vs decision, measure margin retention, and report commodity-driven price/cost variance to the merchandise P&A (links to VS-101 W3288) and margin analytics (W3419); feed learning to the next decision | Merchandising Finance / Category Managers | VP Merchandising | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Price management (links to VS-01.2/W40/W107); shelf labels (links to VS-08 W63); ecommerce price (links to VS-10/VS-65/VS-95); margin/OTB (links to VS-101.3/W3286); DTI compliance (links to VS-22/W468)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-pass-through risk**: Absorbing cost erodes margin/EBITDA (links to VS-101.3); mitigated by disciplined margin modeling and competitor check (W3420)
- **Over-pass-through risk**: Passing through beyond market harms price-competitiveness (links to VS-57) and volume; mitigated by competitor sensitivity (W3420)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-analyze of "analyze the cost change" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-update of "update shelf labels" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track realized pass-through vs decision, measure margin retention, and report…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-70 (ensure commodity exposure & hedge accounting accuracy.)
- operational: disciplined margin modeling and competitor check (W3420); operational: competitor sensitivity (W3420)

### Time Estimate
- Per change; monthly/quarterly

## W3418. Rapid Commodity-Shock Price Response & Margin Recovery

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Market shock (W3407); sudden cost surge; supply disruption (links to VS-69/VS-02.3) |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Affected categories/SKUs |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising, Category Managers, Pricing (links to VS-01.2), Treasury (links to VS-18), Supply (links to VS-02), Marketing (links to VS-14) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/Procurement alert the rapid-response group on a commodity shock (e.g., +20% steel, oil spike, cement allocation); quantify exposure impact (W3404), hedge coverage (W3412), inventory position (links to VS-05), and competitor posture (links to VS-57) | Treasury Manager / Procurement | VP Merchandising | Event-driven |
| 2 | VP Merchandising/Pricing decide the price response: emergency SRP adjustment (links to VS-01.2), temporary surcharge, promo hold, or hold price using inventory/hedge buffer; execute via VS-01.2/W40 within authority, respect DTI rules (links to VS-22/W468), and communicate to stores/ecommerce | VP Merchandising / Pricing | CEO | Event-driven |
| 3 | Merchandising/Marketing manage customer communication (links to VS-14.3) and competitor response (links to VS-57); Treasury execute emergency hedge/buy (links to W3410/W3412) if warranted; track margin recovery and report to executives (links to VS-33.2) and the post-event review (W3416) | Merchandising / Treasury / Marketing | CFO / CEO | Event-driven |

### System Touchpoints
- Price management (links to VS-01.2); hedge/buy (links to W3410/W3412); inventory (links to VS-05); competitor (links to VS-57); comms (links to VS-14.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-erosion risk**: Slow response locks in loss; mitigated by pre-defined triggers and authority
- **Reputational risk**: Visible opportunistic pricing during a crisis (e.g., post-typhoon links to VS-69) attracts DTI/consumer backlash (links to VS-22/W469); mitigated by measured, value-driven response


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-post of "Treasury/Procurement alert the rapid-response group on a commodity shock" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-communication of "communicate to stores/ecommerce" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "Merchandising/Marketing manage customer communication" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-549 (ensure controlled execution — Input-Cost Pass-Through, Margin Protection & Risk Analytics (PA-106.3).)
- operational: pre-defined triggers and authority; operational: measured, value-driven response

### Time Estimate
- Event-driven; hours-to-days
