## Overview

Commodity & Input-Cost Risk Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — governing the
**procurement-finance discipline** that identifies, measures, and mitigates BuildRight's exposure
to volatile raw-material and input-commodity prices across a 35,000-SKU, ~PHP 42–45B COGS
assortment heavily weighted to commodity-intensive categories: lumber & building materials (14% of
SKUs — steel rebar, cement, plywood, lumber), hardware & fasteners (14% — steel-based nails,
screws, bolts), electrical (10% — copper wire, conduit), tiles & flooring (12% — clay, cement,
adhesive), plumbing (12% — copper/brass/PVC fittings), paint & finishes (8% — titanium dioxide,
resins, oil-derived solvents), and power tools/appliances (oil-derived plastics, rare-earth
motors, batteries). This value stream owns the end-to-end risk discipline: commodity-exposure
identification and sensitivity mapping (which SKUs/categories/vendors move with which commodity),
market-price intelligence and benchmarking (LME steel/copper, regional cement, oil/forex),
exposure measurement and the commodity risk register, mitigation strategy selection (forward
buying / physical hedge, indexed/floating contracts, vendor pass-through clauses, financial
hedges, price-laddering, strategic inventory), margin-protection and pass-through governance
(SRP/cost adjustment per VS-01.2/VS-101), and commodity-risk analytics and board reporting.

This is distinct from **VS-02 (Supply Planning)** which executes *operational* demand forecasting,
S&OP, import/customs, and replenishment (PA-02.3 supply-chain risk touches disruption risk, not
commodity-price risk), from **VS-18.3 (FX & Investments)** which hedges *currency* exposure only
(not the underlying commodity), from **VS-101 (Merchandise Financial Planning)** which owns the
*margin/OTB/markdown plan* into which commodity-driven cost variance flows (but not the hedging
decision), from **VS-03.3 (Vendor Performance & Contracts)** which negotiates *vendor terms*
(the contractual vehicle for indexed pricing, but not the market-risk view), and from
**VS-57 (Competitive Price Intelligence)** which monitors *competitor retail prices* (downstream
of input cost). Commodity & input-cost risk is the *raw-material price-risk* discipline that sits
between vendor contracting, treasury, and merchandise finance.

BuildRight's exposure is material: ~40% import component (links to VS-02.2/VS-87), a strong
steel/copper/lumber/cement content, oil-derived plastics and paint, and PHP/USD and PHP/CNY FX
interaction (links to VS-18.3). A 10% commodity-price swing on steel/copper/cement categories
alone can move COGS by ~PHP 0.5–1.5B and directly threatens the 28–32% gross-margin and 12–14%
EBITDA targets (per profile §9.4). Existing workflows touch commodity price only incidentally
(e.g., VS-02.3 W1260 import-risk, VS-101 W3286 margin modeling); no value stream owned the
dedicated commodity-risk program.
