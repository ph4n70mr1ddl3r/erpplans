## W4209. Hosted Trade Day & Contractor Event Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Trade-day calendar (W4194); trade-pro engagement cycle |
| **Frequency** | Periodic (per cluster/store/region) |
| **Volume** | Trade-pro members + contractors (~5,200 trade accounts base) |
| **Owner** | Event Marketing Manager / Trade-Pro Program (VS-43) / Trade Sales |
| **Participants** | Trade-Pro (VS-43), Trade Sales (VS-11), Merchandising (VS-01), Store Ops (VS-07), Vendors (W4197) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Event Marketing/Trade-Pro plan the hosted trade day (theme — e.g., "Pro Pricing Days", product focus, offer, venue — store/DC/external), and target the trade-pro base (VS-43) and prospects (W4196) | Event Marketing Mgr / Trade-Pro | Event Marketing Mgr | Per event |
| 2 | Event Marketing/Merchandising/Vendors execute the trade day (exclusive offers, demos W4203, vendor reps, consultations/estimates, training sessions), and capture leads (W4214) | Event Marketing Mgr / Trade Sales / Vendor | Event Marketing Mgr | Per event |
| 3 | Event Marketing/Trade-Pro measure trade-day results (attendance, offers redeemed, leads, incremental sales), nurture attendees (W4215), and report ROI (W4216) | Event Marketing Mgr / Trade-Pro / Trade Sales | Event Marketing Mgr | Post-event |

### System Touchpoints
- Trade-pro base (VS-43); CRM/leads (W4214); merchandising/offers (VS-01/VS-58); trade pricing (VS-16)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low-relevance risk**: Trade-day theme/offers don't resonate; mitigated by trade-pro insight (VS-43/VS-44)
- **No-follow-up risk**: Attendees not nurtured → no conversion; mitigated by nurture workflow (W4215)

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks per trade day; run 1–multi days

### Automation Opportunity
- Trade-pro targeting/invitation; offer-redemption tracking; lead-to-nurture auto-handoff

### Controls
- CTL-649 (ensure controlled execution — Field Events Trade Days and Lead Roi Analytics (PA-139.3).)
- CTL: trade-day offers within pricing governance (VS-16/VS-58); leads routed (W4214)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-43 (trade-pro), VS-11 (trade sales), VS-01/VS-58 (offers), VS-16 (trade pricing), W4203 (demos), W4214/W4215 (lead/nurture)

## W4214. Lead Capture, Qualification & CRM Routing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Event lead captured (booth W4204, digital W4213, trade-day W4209) |
| **Frequency** | During + immediately post-event |
| **Volume** | Hundreds–thousands of leads per major show |
| **Owner** | Event Marketing Manager / Trade Sales (VS-11/VS-107) / Sales Ops |
| **Participants** | Booth staff, Trade Sales (VS-11), Key Accounts (VS-107), CDP (VS-126), Privacy (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Event/Booth staff capture leads (badge-scan/app/form) with consent (VS-91), qualify on the spot (segment, project, urgency, value), and tag with source/event/offer | Event Lead / Booth Staff / Sales Ops | Event Marketing Mgr | During event |
| 2 | Sales Ops route qualified leads to the right owner (trade sales VS-11, key account VS-107, store, trade-pro VS-43) via CRM within the lead-SLA (same-day/24h), and de-dupe against CDP (VS-126) | Sales Ops / Trade Sales | Event Marketing Mgr | Post-event |
| 3 | Sales Ops track lead disposition (contacted, qualified-out, opportunity created), monitor SLA/routing accuracy, and report lead-pipeline (W4216) | Sales Ops / Trade Sales | Event Marketing Mgr | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Lead-capture tool; CRM (VS-11/VS-107); CDP/de-dupe (VS-126); consent (VS-91); trade-pro (VS-43)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lead-leakage risk**: Captured leads never routed/contacted → wasted event spend; mitigated by same-day SLA + disposition tracking
- **Duplication risk**: Same lead routed twice; mitigated by CDP de-dupe (VS-126)

### Time Estimate
- Real-time capture; routing within SLA; ~0.5 FTE sales-ops per major show

### Automation Opportunity
- Lead-capture→CRM auto-routing; AI qualification scoring (governed VS-128); SLA-breach alerts; de-dupe

### Controls
- CTL-89 (ensure event compliance & asset recovery.)
- CTL: leads captured with consent (VS-91); routed within SLA; disposition tracked; de-duped (VS-126)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-11/VS-107 (CRM/sales), VS-126 (CDP/de-dupe), VS-43 (trade-pro), VS-91 (consent), VS-128 (AI scoring), W4215 (nurture)

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Post-event + monthly/quarterly |
| **Volume** | All events × portfolio metrics |
| **Owner** | Event Marketing Manager / Analytics (VS-28) / Finance (VS-17.4) |
| **Participants** | Analytics (VS-28), Finance (VS-17.4), Trade Sales (VS-11/VS-107), Insights (VS-44), CMO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Event Marketing/Analytics measure event performance vs objectives (W4200) — leads, qualified %, meetings, cost-per-lead, pipeline/sales attributed, NPS/brand-lift, attendance vs target | Event Marketing Mgr / Analytics | Event Marketing Mgr | Post-event |

### Time Estimate
- Post-event ~1 week; monthly/quarterly portfolio ~1 FTE analytics

### Automation Opportunity
- Event-performance dashboards; revenue-attribution automation; portfolio-ROI optimization

### Controls
- CTL-89 (ensure event compliance & asset recovery.)
- CTL: event ROI measured with attribution rigor; portfolio reviewed quarterly; budget reallocated to ROI
