## W4193. Event Marketing Strategy & Channel Role

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual marketing strategy (VS-14); B2B-growth objective |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on strategy change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise event-channel strategy (~40% B2B revenue base) |
| **Owner** | Event Marketing Manager / Marketing (VS-14) |
| **Participants** | CMO, Trade Sales (VS-11/VS-107), Trade-Pro Program (VS-43), Merchandising (VS-01), Insights (VS-44) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Event Marketing define the role of events in the B2B/customer-acquisition mix (trade shows vs hosted trade days vs store events vs sponsorships), aligned to marketing strategy (VS-14) and B2B-growth goals | Event Marketing Mgr / Marketing | CMO | Annual |
| 2 | Event Marketing/Insights assess the event landscape (PhilConstruct, Worldbex, CebuCon, regional shows, association events) and audience fit vs BuildRight's trade/corporate segments (links to VS-44 insights, VS-43 trade-pro) | Event Marketing Mgr / Insights | CMO | Annual |
| 3 | Event Marketing document the strategy, channel role, and investment envelope, obtain approval, and cascade to the annual portfolio/calendar (W4194) | Event Marketing Mgr / Finance | CMO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Marketing strategy (VS-14); insights (VS-44); trade-pro base (VS-43); finance (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-strategy risk**: Events done ad-hoc, no portfolio logic; mitigated by documented channel strategy
- **Audience-mismatch risk**: Sponsoring events BuildRight's buyers don't attend; mitigated by audience-fit assessment

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks annual strategy

### Automation Opportunity
- Event-landscape/audience database; strategy-template automation

### Controls
- CTL-647 (ensure controlled execution — Event Marketing Strategy, Portfolio & Calendar Management (PA-139.1).)
- CTL: event strategy approved by CMO; aligned to marketing/B2B strategy (VS-14)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-14 (marketing), VS-44 (insights), VS-43 (trade-pro), VS-11/VS-107 (trade sales), W4194 (portfolio)
