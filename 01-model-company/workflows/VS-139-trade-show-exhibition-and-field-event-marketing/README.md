## Overview

Trade Show, Exhibition & Field Event Marketing workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the
enterprise **event/field-marketing discipline** that plans, executes, and measures BuildRight's
presence at construction/home-building trade shows and industry exhibitions (PhilConstruct,
Worldbex, CebuCon, regional shows), its own hosted field events (contractor trade days, product
launches, project clinics, store grand openings), and partner/sponsored events — all aimed at the
**~40% B2B revenue base** (30% professional/trade + 10% corporate/institutional): contractors,
builders, developers, architects, interior designers, and government-project buyers. For a
hardware/home-improvement retailer whose differentiation is being the credible "home-building
partner", trade shows and field events are a primary B2B lead-generation, relationship, and
brand-building channel where six- to seven-figure PHP is spent each year and where a poorly-run
event wastes spend and squanders high-value trade-pro attention.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**: trade-show/exhibition/event-marketing terms are
sprinkled across ~9 PA files (VS-01 merchandise strategy, VS-02 supply planning, VS-03 vendor
management, VS-14 marketing, VS-30 innovation, VS-41 private label, VS-43 trade-professional
program, VS-44 consumer insights, VS-70 solar) with **zero dedicated workflow headers** — each
event is run ad-hoc by whichever team happens to sponsor it, with no owned end-to-end discipline
for the event portfolio/calendar, sponsorship/booth logistics, lead capture/follow-up, vendor
co-funding/co-exhibition, compliance (LGU/exhibition permits, ABC, prize-promo rules), or
event-ROI measurement. No value stream owns the **event-marketing operating model**.

This is distinct from **VS-14 (Marketing)** which owns brand/promotion/digital channels at the
audience level — this value stream owns the *event/field* channel (physical, time-bound,
relationship-driven). It is distinct from **VS-43 (Trade-Professional Program)** which manages the
trade-pro *membership/relationship* — this value stream owns the *events* that recruit, engage, and
sell to that base. It is distinct from **VS-107 (Strategic Key Account)** which manages named
enterprise accounts — this value stream owns the *event touchpoints* that generate and nurture those
relationships. It is distinct from **VS-37 (Store Opening)** which commissions a new store — this
value stream owns the *event program* around grand-opening and ongoing store events.
Trade-show/field-event marketing is the *event channel* discipline — distinct from broad marketing,
trade-pro membership, key-account management, and store commissioning.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: a high-consideration, relationship-driven B2B business where
trade shows (PhilConstruct draws ~100K+ construction-industry attendees) and contractor trade days
are where projects and trade accounts are won, where ~PHP millions/yr are spent on
booths/sponsorship/samples, where vendors co-fund, and where lead follow-up failure converts the
entire spend to zero return.
