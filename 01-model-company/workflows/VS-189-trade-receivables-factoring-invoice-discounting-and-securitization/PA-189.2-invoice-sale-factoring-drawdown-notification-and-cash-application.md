## W5401. Eligible Invoice Batch Selection, Pooling & Submission to Factor

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Invoice issuance (VS-16); scheduled submission cycle |
| **Frequency** | Daily/weekly submission batches |
| **Volume** | ~3,500 AR invoices/month eligible; ~42,000/yr |
| **Owner** | AR/Factoring Operations Lead |
| **Participants** | Credit Analyst, Factor/Funder |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select eligible invoices per criteria (W5395): approved customer, within age, undisputed, within concentration. | AR/Factoring Ops Lead | Credit Manager | per batch |
| 2 | Pool into a submission batch with required data (customer, invoice, amount, terms, due date). | AR/Factoring Ops Lead | Credit Manager | per batch |
| 3 | Gate eligibility automatically (W5395); exclude ineligible (over-age, disputed, over-limit). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per batch |
| 4 | Submit the batch to the factor via the factoring platform (W5398); obtain acceptance. | AR/Factoring Ops Lead | Credit Manager | per batch |
| 5 | Record submission and update invoice status to "submitted/assigned" in AR (VS-16). | AR/Factoring Ops Lead | Credit Manager | per batch |

### System Touchpoints
- Factoring eligibility/submission engine; AR (VS-16); concentration/limit check (W5395).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Ineligible-submission risk**: submitting ineligible invoices causes funder rejection, fees, and covenant impact; mitigated by automated eligibility-gating.
- **Double-financing risk**: an invoice submitted twice (to two factors) is fraud; mitigated by a single assigned-status flag in AR preventing re-submission.

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 hours per batch; ~50 batches/yr → ~150 person-hours/yr.

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-batch eligible invoices and submit straight-through to the factor.

### Controls
- CTL-23 (fraud detection on double-financing); CTL-21 (submission accuracy); CTL-14/CTL-44 (duplicate guard).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (AR/invoicing), VS-68 (credit/eligibility), VS-125 (fraud)

## W5402. Factor Funding/Drawdown, Advance Posting & Bank Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Factor accepts and funds a submission (W5401) |
| **Frequency** | Per submission batch |
| **Volume** | ~50 drawdowns/yr (batched) |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance, Factor/Funder, Bank |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive the factor advance (advance rate × eligible batch) net of fees/reserve to BuildRight's account. | Treasury Manager | Treasury Manager | per drawdown |
| 2 | Post the advance to cash and the receivables-sale/factoring GL accounts (VS-17/VS-157/W5397). | Finance | Financial Controller | per drawdown |
| 3 | Reconcile the advance to the accepted submission and facility terms (rate, advance, reserve). | Treasury Manager | Financial Controller | per drawdown |
| 4 | Update facility utilization and available headroom (W5394). | Treasury Manager | Treasury Manager | per drawdown |
| 5 | Investigate and resolve any funding discrepancy with the factor. | Treasury Manager | Financial Controller | per exception |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/funding (VS-18); GL posting (VS-17); factoring accounting (W5397); bank reconciliation (CTL-19).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Funding-discrepancy risk**: an advance short of the expected amount (fees, reserve, rejection) mis-states cash and receivables; mitigated by per-drawdown reconciliation.
- **Mis-posting risk**: posting the advance to the wrong GL account distorts the financials and derecognition; mitigated by defined GL mapping (W5397).

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 hours per drawdown; ~50/yr → ~150 person-hours/yr.

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-post the advance from the bank feed and reconcile to the accepted submission.

### Controls
- CTL-19 (bank reconciliation); CTL-21 (posting accuracy); CTL-30 (close).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury/cash), VS-17 (GL), VS-157 (revenue/sale accounting)

## W5403. Customer Invoice Reissuance/Re-assignment & Remit-To Instruction Update

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Notification factoring (W5396); customer remit-to change |
| **Frequency** | Per notified customer/segment; per remit change |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 customer remit records; ~changes |
| **Owner** | AR Lead |
| **Participants** | Customer, Trade Account Manager (VS-107), Factor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For notification factoring: update the customer master remit-to to the factor/lockbox (W5396). | AR Lead | Credit Manager | per customer |
| 2 | Reissue/re-assign invoices (where required) with the new remit-to and assignment notice. | AR Lead | Credit Manager | per invoice |
| 3 | Communicate the change to the customer (Trade Account Mgmt) to avoid mis-directed payment (W5404). | Trade Account Manager | Credit Manager | per customer |
| 4 | Update AR (VS-16) and customer master (VS-29) with the assignment/remit status. | AR Lead | Credit Manager | per customer |
| 5 | Verify the customer's next payment routes correctly to the factor/lockbox. | AR Lead | Credit Manager | per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Customer master remit-to (VS-29); AR reassignment (VS-16); lockbox (W5398); communication (VS-14/VS-107).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-directed-payment risk**: a customer paying the old remit-to (BuildRight) instead of the factor causes mis-application and reconciliation breaks; mitigated by clear customer communication and remit-to controls.
- **Stale-remit risk**: an un-updated remit-to on a customer record causes ongoing mis-direction; mitigated by assignment-status flags and verification.

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min per customer setup; ~changes ongoing.

### Automation Opportunity
- Remit-to auto-updated on assignment with customer-notification template.

### Controls
- CTL-21 (remit-to accuracy); CTL-27 (customer data); operational: payment-routing verification.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (AR), VS-29 (master data), VS-107 (accounts), VS-14 (comms)

## W5404. Lockbox/Cash Receipt Processing, Customer Payment Matching & Clearing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer payment to the factor/lockbox (notification) or BuildRight (silent) |
| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | ~3,500 AR payments/month |
| **Owner** | Cash Application Lead |
| **Participants** | Factor/Funder, AR (VS-16), Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive lockbox/factor remittance data with customer/invoice references. | Cash Application Lead | Credit Manager | daily |
| 2 | Match payments to the assigned invoices in AR (VS-16); clear the receivable and the factoring advance. | Cash Application Lead | Credit Manager | daily |
| 3 | Route the collected funds per the facility (to the factor / to BuildRight net of advance). | Treasury Manager | Credit Manager | daily |
| 4 | Investigate and resolve unapplied/short/over-payments and unidentified remittances. | Cash Application Lead | Credit Manager | per exception |
| 5 | Reconcile the lockbox/factor account daily (CTL-19). | Cash Application Lead | Financial Controller | daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Lockbox/factor remittance feed; AR cash application (VS-16); treasury routing (VS-18); bank reconciliation.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unapplied-cash risk**: unmatched payments distort AR and the factoring reconciliation; mitigated by remittance-detail matching and daily reconciliation.
- **Float/routing risk**: collected funds not routed per the facility create exposure; mitigated by defined routing and reconciliation.

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 person-hours/day cash application; ~1,500 person-hours/yr.

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-matching of lockbox remittance to assigned invoices; auto-routing.

### Controls
- CTL-19 (bank/lockbox reconciliation); CTL-20 (cash accountability); CTL-21 (matching accuracy).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (AR/cash application), VS-18 (treasury), VS-80 (payments)

## W5405. Non-Recourse Credit Risk Event, Customer Dispute & Short-Pay Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer dispute, short-pay, or credit risk event on a factored invoice |
| **Frequency** | ~dilution rate of submitted invoices |
| **Volume** | ~1–5% of factored invoices |
| **Owner** | Credit Manager |
| **Participants** | Factor/Funder, Trade Credit Insurer (VS-68), AR, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify the event: dispute, short-pay, or customer credit deterioration on a factored invoice. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per event |
| 2 | For non-recourse: file a claim with the trade-credit insurer (VS-68) within the policy window. | Credit Manager | Credit Manager | per event |
| 3 | For recourse: prepare for buyback at recourse-period expiry (W5407) or negotiate cure with the customer. | Credit Manager | Credit Manager | per event |
| 4 | Resolve the dispute/short-pay with the customer (links VS-16 dispute management). | AR Lead | Credit Manager | per event |
| 5 | Update dilution analytics and reserve/holdback (W5399/W5406). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Credit-risk module (VS-68); trade-credit insurance claims (VS-68/VS-26); AR dispute (VS-16); dilution analytics.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Uninsured-loss risk**: a non-recourse claim filed late (past the insurance window) is denied, leaving BuildRight/ factor exposed; mitigated by prompt claim filing within policy windows.
- **Recourse-buildup risk**: recourse events accumulating signal portfolio deterioration and future buybacks; mitigated by dilution analytics and reserve review (W5399).

### Time Estimate
- ~2–6 person-hours per event; ~1,000/yr → ~4,000 person-hours/yr.

### Automation Opportunity
- Event-driven claim workflows with insurance-window alerts.

### Controls
- CTL-37/CTL-21 (credit-insurance claim validity); CTL-23 (fraud detection on disputes); CTL-21 (dilution accuracy).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (trade credit/insurance), VS-26 (insurance), VS-16 (AR/disputes), VS-125 (fraud)

## W5406. Dilution Reconciliation, Reserve Release & Holdback Settlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer payment/close of factored invoice; periodic reserve settlement |
| **Frequency** | Per invoice close; periodic settlement |
| **Volume** | ~42,000 invoice closes/yr |
| **Owner** | Finance (Receivables Finance) |
| **Participants** | Factor/Funder, Credit Manager, Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On invoice payment/close, reconcile actual vs. expected (full vs. short-pay/dispute). | Finance | Credit Manager | per invoice |
| 2 | Compute dilution (short-pay/dispute as % of invoice) and impact on the reserve/holdback (W5399). | Finance | Credit Manager | per batch |
| 3 | Release the reserve/holdback to BuildRight net of dilution per the facility terms. | Treasury Manager | Credit Manager | per settlement |
| 4 | Post reserve release/holdback settlement to GL (VS-17). | Finance | Financial Controller | per settlement |
| 5 | Report dilution vs. facility threshold to the funder and credit committee (W5410). | Credit Manager | CFO | monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Factoring reserve/holdback module; GL (VS-17); dilution analytics (VS-68).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reserve-dispute risk**: disagreement with the factor on dilution/reserve release causes cash delay; mitigated by transparent dilution reconciliation and terms.
- **Mis-posting risk**: incorrect reserve-release posting distorts the financials; mitigated by defined GL mapping.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 person-hours per settlement batch; ~12/yr.

### Automation Opportunity
- Reserve auto-released on invoice close net of computed dilution.

### Controls
- CTL-21 (dilution/reserve accuracy); CTL-31 (adequate reserve); CTL-30 (close).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (GL), VS-68 (dilution/credit), VS-18 (treasury/settlement)

## W5407. Recourse Period Expiry, Unpaid-Invoice Buyback & Repurchase Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recourse period expiry on an unpaid factored invoice (recourse factoring) |
| **Frequency** | ~1–3% of recourse factored invoices |
| **Volume** | ~500–1,500 buybacks/yr |
| **Owner** | Credit Manager |
| **Participants** | Factor/Funder, AR (VS-16), Finance, Collections |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify invoices unpaid past the recourse period (recourse factoring). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per cycle |
| 2 | Repurchase/buyback the unpaid invoice from the factor per the recourse agreement (W5394). | Finance | Credit Manager | per buyback |
| 3 | Re-establish the receivable on BuildRight's books (re-recognize under PFRS 9 — W5397) and pursue collection (VS-16/VS-68). | Finance | Financial Controller | per buyback |
| 4 | Apply the recourse reserve/holdback (W5399) and provision for likely loss (W5412). | Finance | CFO | per buyback |
| 5 | Monitor recourse activity for substance/re-characterization signals (W5397/W5415). | Financial Controller | CFO | monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Factoring recourse engine; AR re-recognize (VS-16/VS-157); GL/provisioning (VS-17); collections (VS-16/VS-68).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Recourse-buildup risk**: high recourse activity signals the sale is in substance a borrowing (re-characterization) and erodes the working-capital benefit; mitigated by substance monitoring (W5415).
- **Loss risk**: bought-back invoices that never collect become losses; mitigated by provisioning (W5412) and collection effort.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 person-hours per buyback; ~1,000/yr → ~1,500 person-hours/yr.

### Automation Opportunity
- Recourse-expiry auto-triggers buyback and re-recognition workflows.

### Controls
- CTL-21 (buyback accuracy); CTL-31 (adequate recourse reserve/provision); CTL-29 (consolidated reporting).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (AR/collections), VS-68 (credit), VS-157 (re-recognize), VS-17 (GL)

## W5408. Securitization Pool Reporting, Investor/Trustee Reporting & Servicing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Securitization instrument selected (W5393); periodic investor reporting |
| **Frequency** | Monthly investor/trustee reporting |
| **Volume** | ~1 securitization pool; ~12 reports/yr |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Trustee/SPV, Investors, Finance, Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aggregate pool performance: collections, dilution, delinquencies, and credit events (W5405). | Finance | Treasury Manager | per cycle |
| 2 | Prepare investor/trustee reports per the pooling-and-servicing agreement (covenants, triggers, waterfalls). | Finance | Treasury Manager | per cycle |
| 3 | Reconcile pool cash flows and remit per the waterfall (senior fees, interest, principal, residual). | Treasury Manager | Financial Controller | per cycle |
| 4 | Manage pool covenants/triggers (e.g. excess spread, delinquency triggers) and report breaches. | Treasury Manager | CFO | per cycle |
| 5 | Coordinate with the trustee/SPV and legal on pool administration (VS-100). | Legal Counsel | Treasury Manager | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Securitization pool/servicing module; investor/trustee reporting; treasury/waterfall (VS-18); GL (VS-17).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Covenant/trigger-breach risk**: breaching a pool trigger (delinquency, excess spread) accelerates amortization/penalties; mitigated by trigger monitoring and early action.
- **Reporting-error risk**: inaccurate investor/trustee reports breach the PSA and expose to investor claims; mitigated by reconciled reporting and review.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 weeks elapsed per monthly cycle.

### Automation Opportunity
- Pool-performance auto-aggregated and investor reports auto-generated per the PSA.

### Controls
- CTL-21 (pool-reporting accuracy); CTL-19 (cash/waterfall reconciliation); CTL-29 (consolidated/off-BS reporting).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury/waterfall), VS-17 (GL), VS-100 (legal/SPV), VS-173 (investor/capital-markets disclosure)
