# VS-189: Trade Accounts Receivable Factoring, Invoice Discounting & Receivables Securitization

> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Trade accounts receivable factoring, invoice discounting & receivables securitization for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **working-capital program in which BuildRight sells/finances its own trade and B2B accounts receivable** to a factor/funder (bank or specialized financier) for immediate cash, at a discount, either with recourse (BuildRight repurchases unpaid invoices) or non-recourse (the factor bears the customer credit risk, typically credit-insured). The program covers the receivables-financing strategy and instrument selection (factoring vs. invoice discounting vs. securitization); factor/funder selection and master-agreement; eligible-receivable criteria and advance-rate framework; notification strategy (notification vs. silent/confidential factoring); the PFRS 9 receivables-sale/derecognition/true-sale accounting design; the factoring/securitization platform and lockbox/remittance infrastructure; dilution/reserve/holdback modeling and credit-protection sourcing; eligible-invoice batch submission and drawdown; customer notification/remit-to changes and lockbox cash receipt processing; non-recourse credit events, disputes, and recourse-period buybacks; factor reconciliation, fee/interest settlement, and recourse/bad-debt reserve; and portfolio cash-conversion-cycle, DSO, cost-of-funds, and profitability analytics.

At ~40% B2B revenue (~PHP 25B/yr), ~5,200 trade accounts, Net 30–90 terms, and a large COD/ecommerce AR base, trade receivables are BuildRight's second-largest balance-sheet asset after inventory, and the cash-conversion cycle they create is a primary working-capital constraint. Factoring/discounting/securitization releases that working capital immediately, diversifies funding, and can transfer customer credit risk — while carrying recourse/buyback, customer-relationship (notification), true-sale/derecognition, and BSP/SEC/TinL compliance exposure. This is distinct from VS-105 (supply-chain finance / reverse factoring on BuildRight's *own payables* — the mirror image), VS-170 (AR *pledged as ABL collateral* but not sold), VS-18 (cash/treasury operations), VS-16/VS-68 (the AR and credit-risk functions whose receivables are sold), and VS-173 (investor/capital-markets disclosure). No value stream owned BuildRight as a **seller of its own trade receivables** — 'factoring', 'invoice discounting', 'receivable sale', and 'securitization' each appeared in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers (the lone 'securitization' reference was W3181 *lease-portfolio* securitization in VS-96).
