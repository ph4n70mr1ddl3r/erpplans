## W4433. Self-Checkout & Unattended Retail Strategy, Scope & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy review; wage/queue pressure; format evolution |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on strategic change |
| **Volume** | One enterprise SCO/scan-&-go/unattended strategy across 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Head of Frontend/Store Ops (VS-07/VS-08) / Strategy (VS-33) / IT (VS-27) |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), POS (VS-08), Strategy (VS-33), IT/EA (VS-27/VS-113), Loss Prevention (VS-23), Finance (VS-17.4), HR (VS-19/VS-103) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strategy/Frontend set the SCO/scan-&-go strategy — objective (wage savings, queue reduction, trade-pro experience), format mix (banked SCO, mobile scan-&-go, locker/vending, unattended), phased rollout by store profile | Strategy / Frontend / Store Ops | COO / CIO | Annual |
| 2 | Finance/LP model the business case — capex (VS-40), wage offset, expected SCO loss-rate vs staffed, ROI/payback, and the loss-prevention investment to keep SCO shrink acceptable | Finance / LP / Strategy | CFO / COO | Annual |
| 3 | Strategy/Frontend document the strategy, obtain approval, and cascade to technology (W4434), layout (W4435), loss-prevention (W4437), change management (W4440) | Strategy / Frontend | COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy (VS-33); POS (VS-08); store-ops (VS-07); IT/EA (VS-27/VS-113); LP (VS-23); finance FP&A (VS-17.4); capex (VS-40)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Loss-rate-blindness risk**: SCO deployed without loss modeling → shrink blowout erodes wage savings; mitigated by upfront loss-rate modeling & LP design (W4437)
- **Format-mismatch risk**: wrong format per store profile (e.g. scan-&-go in low-digital-province store); mitigated by store-profile-segmented rollout

### Automation Opportunity
- Business-case modeler with loss-rate sensitivity; store-profile-segmented rollout planner; ROI tracker

### Controls
- CTL-677 (ensure controlled execution — Self-Checkout & Scan-&-Go Strategy, Design & Technology (PA-149.1).)
- CTL-677: strategy approved (COO/CIO/CFO); business case incl. loss-rate; format segmented by store profile; phased rollout

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual strategy + business case

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-08 (POS), VS-27/VS-113 (IT/EA), VS-23 (LP), VS-17.4 (FP&A), W4434 (technology), W4437 (loss), W4440 (change)

## W4437. Weight-Security, Computer-Vision & Loss-Prevention Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | SCO/scan-&-go design (W4434/W4436); loss modeling |
| **Frequency** | Per design + continuous tuning |
| **Volume** | SCO loss-prevention design across 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Loss Prevention (VS-23) / Frontend (VS-08) / IT (VS-27) |
| **Participants** | LP (VS-23), Frontend (VS-08), IT (VS-27), BI (VS-28), Security (VS-27.3), Vendor (VS-34) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LP/IT specify the weight-security design — bagging-area scale cross-check (expected vs actual weight per item), item-recognition camera, and intervention-prompt on mismatch (the core SCO anti-scan-avoidance control) | LP / IT / Frontend | LP Lead / CIO | Per design |
| 2 | LP/IT layer computer-vision/analytics — SKU-recognition (e.g. produce/bulk), barcode-swap detection (cheap barcode on expensive item), "sweethearting" (attendant override pattern), and exception-casework (W4450) | LP / IT / BI | LP Lead | Per design + tuning |
| 3 | LP/Frontend tune thresholds (false-positive vs detection), train attendants on intervention protocol, and review loss data (W4455) to iterate | LP / Frontend | LP Lead / COO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- SCO weight-scale (VS-115 calibration); computer-vision; LP/exception (VS-23/W4450); BI (VS-28); CCTV (VS-23.2); security (VS-27.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Weight-spoofing risk**: customer manipulates scale or bags after scan → scan-avoidance; mitigated by item-recognition + intervention
- **Barcode-swap risk**: cheap barcode scanned on expensive item; mitigated by SKU-recognition CV + swap-detection

### Automation Opportunity
- Weight-cross-check engine; CV SKU-recognition; barcode-swap detection; exception-case auto-routing (W4450); threshold auto-tuning

### Controls
- CTL-677 (ensure controlled execution — Self-Checkout & Scan-&-Go Strategy, Design & Technology (PA-149.1).)
- CTL-677: weight-security + item-recognition; barcode-swap/sweethearting detection; intervention protocol trained; loss reviewed (W4455)

### Time Estimate
- Weeks design; continuous tuning
