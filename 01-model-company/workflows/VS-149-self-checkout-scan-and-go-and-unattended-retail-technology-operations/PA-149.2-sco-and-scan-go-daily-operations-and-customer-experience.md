## W4441. SCO & Scan-&-Go Lane Daily Open/Close & Staffing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store open/close; shift |
| **Frequency** | Daily per store |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × 2 (open/close)/day |
| **Owner** | Frontend Supervisor (VS-08) / Store Ops (VS-07) |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), Frontend (VS-08), Cashiers/Attendants, IFM (VS-138), IT (VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Frontend open the SCO pod — terminal boot/health check, scale/calibration check (VS-115), cash-recycler load/float (W4445), signage, and attendant station setup | Frontend Supervisor / Attendant | Store Manager | ~20–30 min/open |
| 2 | Frontend staff the pod — attendant ratio (1:4–6 SCO), intervention-ready, monitoring; open/close scan-&-go & unattended formats per W4439 | Frontend Supervisor | Store Manager | Per shift |
| 3 | Frontend close the pod — cash-recycler pull/reconcile (W4445), transaction settlement, terminal close, and exception handoff (W4450) | Frontend Supervisor / Attendant | Store Manager | ~20–30 min/close |

### System Touchpoints
- SCO terminal (VS-08); calibration (VS-115); cash-recycler (W4445); POS-ERP (VS-08); exception (W4450); IT (VS-27)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Calibration-drift risk**: un-calibrated scale at open → false interventions/scan-avoidance; mitigated by open calibration check (VS-115)
- **Under-staffing risk**: attendant ratio too thin → intervention queue/friction; mitigated by ratio standard & peak staffing (W4448)

### Time Estimate
- ~40–60 min open+close/day × 200 stores; staffing built into frontend model

### Automation Opportunity
- Terminal-health/calibration auto-check on boot; cash-recycler load/reconcile workflow; attendant-ratio/peak scheduler

### Controls
- CTL-678 (ensure controlled execution — SCO & Scan-&-Go Daily Operations & Customer Experience (PA-149.2).)
- CTL: terminal health & calibration checked at open; cash-recycler loaded/reconciled; attendant ratio met; exceptions handed off

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08 (POS), VS-115 (calibration), VS-07 (store), W4445 (cash), W4448 (peak), W4450 (exception)

## W4443. SCO Intervention, Age-Verification & Override Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | SCO flag (age/hazmat/controlled/weight/price-override); customer request |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (per flagged item) |
| **Volume** | Intervention events = flag-rate × SCO volume |
| **Owner** | SCO Attendant / Frontend (VS-08) / Compliance (VS-22) |
| **Participants** | Attendants, Frontend (VS-08), Compliance (VS-22), DG (VS-114), LP (VS-23), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Attendant respond to flag — age-restricted (ID-verify per W4438), DG/hazmat (block/authorize per W4438), catch-weight (weigh/measure VS-115), weight-mismatch (re-scan/inspect), price-override (manager auth per SoD) | Attendant | Frontend Supervisor / Store Manager | Per flag |
| 2 | Attendant/Manager authorize override within authority matrix (W4453), log the override (exception W4450), and apply the price/quantity change | Attendant / Manager | Frontend Supervisor | Per override |
| 3 | Compliance/LP audit intervention/override patterns for abuse (links W4449/W4450) and tune the flag set (W4438) | Compliance / LP | Compliance Lead / LP Lead | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- SCO item-control (VS-08/W4438); DG (VS-114); calibration (VS-115); LP/exception (VS-23/W4450); override-auth (SoD); audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Override-abuse risk**: attendant/manager overrides used to fraud/discount; mitigated by authority matrix (SoD) & override logging (W4450)
- **Age-verification-failure risk**: minor served; mitigated by ID-check protocol & audit

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 min per intervention

### Automation Opportunity
- Flag-to-attendant dispatch; ID-verification capture; override-auth workflow with SoD; override-pattern analytics

### Controls
- CTL-92 (ensure self-checkout loss & compliance control.)
- CTL: flags actioned per policy; override within authority matrix & logged; age verified; patterns audited (W4449/W4450)
