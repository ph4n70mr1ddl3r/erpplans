# VS-149: Self-Checkout, Scan-&-Go & Unattended Retail Technology Operations

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Self-Checkout (SCO), Scan-&-Go and Unattended Retail Technology Operations workflows for
BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **self-service/unattended checkout operating discipline** that
is a strategic growth lever for a 200-store, 600-terminal, 2.8M-monthly-transaction big-box
retailer under wage (DOLE minimum-wage/13th-month) pressure and a customer base increasingly
conditioned to self-service. The discipline spans traditional banked SCO lanes (fixed terminals
with cashier-assisted intervention), mobile **scan-&-go** (customer scans with their own/device-
supplied handset and pays without unloading), computer-vision/camera-only "just-walk-out" concepts,
and **unattended/contactless** formats (24-hr micro-stores, locker/click-&-collect, vending), each
with distinct technology, loss-prevention, cash, accessibility, and compliance profiles.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. "scan-and-go", "scan-go", "unattended",
"just-walk-out", and "self-checkout" appear in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers
(only scattered single-step mentions). The capability is conflated with VS-08 (POS & Checkout —
owns the *staffed* POS transaction, not the SCO/scan-&-go *operating program*), VS-07 (Store
Operations — owns *store* execution, not the SCO *technology/discipline*), VS-23 (Loss Prevention
— owns *shrink/theft* broadly, not the SCO-specific *sweethearting/swap/weight-security* program),
VS-09.3 (In-Store Amenities — touches the *customer amenity* aspect only), and VS-109 (Store
Remodel — owns *deployment as a project*, not the in-life *program*). No value stream owns the
end-to-end SCO/scan-&-go/unattended program: strategy/acceptance, technology/architecture, lane
layout/capacity, weight-security/computer-vision/loss-prevention design, age-restricted/hazmat
intervention, daily open/close/staffing, customer assistance, cash-recycler handling, hardware/
software uptime, queue management, fraud detection, refund/void integrity, cash reconciliation,
BIR CAS/e-invoicing/Consumer Act compliance, accessibility, and analytics.

This is distinct from **VS-08 (POS & Checkout)** which owns the *staffed* POS transaction — this
value stream owns the *self-service/unattended* checkout and the distinct loss/cash/accessibility
profile it introduces. It is distinct from **VS-23 (Loss Prevention)** broad shrink program — this
value stream owns the SCO-specific *scan-avoidance/swap/sweethearting/weight-violation* controls.
It is distinct from **VS-80 (Payment Operations)** — this value stream owns the *in-lane* SCO
payment/cash-handling and tender integrity, while VS-80 owns the *acquirer/settlement* rails. It is
distinct from **VS-109 (Store Remodel)** project deployment — this value stream owns the *in-life
operating* program. It is distinct from **VS-147 (Customer Safety)** — this value stream owns the
*transaction* side of the self-service experience, while VS-147 owns *premises safety*.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: SCO/scan-&-go without a dedicated program leaks margin (SCO
loss benchmarks 2–5× staffed-POS shrink from scan-avoidance, swap, weight-manipulation), creates
cash/tender-reconciliation exposure (cash-recycler variances, COD/float), risks Consumer Act
(RA 7394) price-tag/integrity and BIR CAS/e-invoicing non-compliance, and can harm the
"Your Home Building Partner" experience if intervention queues form. A well-run program, by
contrast, materially reduces frontline wage cost and queue time while improving the trade-pro and
DIY self-service experience.
