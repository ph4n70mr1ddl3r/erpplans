## W2630. Job Site Delivery Unloading & Material Placement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Truck arrives at construction site |
| **Frequency** | ~300–500 deliveries/month |
| **Volume** | ~300–500/month |
| **Owner** | Driver / Delivery Crew |
| **Participants** | Driver, Delivery Crew, Contractor Site Foreman |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Driver arrives at site: contacts foreman, confirms unloading location, verifies site access and safety (hard hat area, PPE required); positions truck | Driver | Logistics Coordinator | 15–20 min |
| 2 | Delivery crew and contractor personnel unload: hand-bomb materials if no forklift/crane; place in designated staging area per contractor direction; separate by type and phase | Delivery Crew / Contractor Personnel | Driver | 30–90 min |
| 3 | Site foreman verifies delivery against manifest: counts items, notes damage or shortage, signs receipt; Driver photographs delivered materials as proof | Site Foreman / Driver | Logistics Coordinator | 15–20 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-06 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Delivery-failure risk**: a delivery misses its window (traffic, access, scheduling), disrupting the customer/project; mitigated by the real-time tracking and the rescheduling protocol
- **Data-quality risk**: incomplete or inaccurate master data (pricing, inventory, customer) corrupts the workflow output; mitigated by the master-data governance (VS-29 / W291) and the periodic audit

### Time Estimate
- ~15–20 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~300–500/month
- Frequency: ~300–500 deliveries/month
- Absorbed into the Driver / Delivery Crew role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Automation Opportunity
- Delivery scheduling auto-optimized from the route/dock/traffic data; real-time tracking auto-pushed to customer and store; POD auto-captured digitally

### Controls
- CTL-51: master-data quality — links to VS-29 / W291; Operational: Driver / Delivery Crew sign-off on the workflow output

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

| **Volume** | ~50–80/month |

| **Participants** | Logistics Coord, Driver, Contractor, 3PL Dump Truck Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics Coordinator arranges bulk delivery (sand, gravel, cement): requires dump trucks; coordinates with DSD vendor or 3PL; confirms contractor has designated dump area | Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 1 day |
| 2 | Driver delivers: positions dump truck, tips load carefully; contractor foreman visually estimates quantity; photographs tipped load | Driver / Contractor Foreman | Logistics Coordinator | 30–60 min |
| 3 | System processes bulk delivery: catch-weight recorded with estimated weight; Finance bills based on delivered weight; Logistics tracks cost per cubic meter | System / Finance / Logistics | Finance Controller | Per delivery |

### Time Estimate

- Absorbed into the Logistics Coordinator role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
