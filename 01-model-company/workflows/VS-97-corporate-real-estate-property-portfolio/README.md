## Overview

Corporate Real Estate & Property Portfolio Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **BuildRight Property Management, Inc.** entity's role as **property owner, developer,
and lessor** of store sites, distribution centers, offices, and ancillary real estate across the
200-store / 4-DC / 1-HQ footprint (~205+ locations across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao).

This value stream owns the **landlord / property-investor side** of the real estate equation. It is
distinct from **VS-20 (Real Estate & Construction)** which handles site selection and BuildRight's
construction of stores, from **VS-42 (Property & Lease Administration)** which handles BuildRight
Depot, Inc. as **lessee** (rent payment, CAM reconciliation as tenant, lease accounting under PFRS
16 lessee model), and from **VS-35 (Fixed Asset Management)** which handles financial fixed-asset
accounting and depreciation. This value stream owns the **lessor / property-portfolio operating
model**: property acquisition and investment underwriting, ground-up property development and
tenant-improvement build-out, investment property valuation and fair-value measurement (PFRS 40),
landlord-side leasing and tenant management (both intercompany to BuildRight Depot Inc. at
arm's-length transfer pricing and, where applicable, third-party tenants such as pad/outlot
occupants — banks, ATMs, food concessionaires, courier drop-points per W177), landlord-side rent
billing and CAM recovery, real property tax and LGU compliance as **owner**, property-level P&L
and net operating income (NOI) reporting, capital improvements and reserve governance, property
insurance and risk transfer, and portfolio performance / yield / valuation analytics.

BuildRight's corporate structure (model-company-profile §2) explicitly designates Property
Management Inc. as a real-estate leasing entity that owns store/DC/office sites and leases them to
BuildRight Depot, Inc. Consolidated reporting under PFRS requires investment-property fair-value
accounting, intercompany rent at arm's length (links to VS-72 cross-entity transfer pricing), and
elimination of intercompany rent and profit on intercompany property transactions.
