### System Touchpoints
- Talent CRM (links to W3770); L&D/apprenticeship (links to VS-19.4/VS-123); loyalty/marketing (links to VS-14); analytics (links to VS-28.1); data privacy (links to VS-91)

- **Low-yield risk**: Expensive partnerships with poor conversion; mitigated by school-ROI analytics

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-track of "tracks, and regional schools near store/DC locations" (replaces manual Step 1).

- System auto-report of "report to TA governance" (replaces manual Step 3).
