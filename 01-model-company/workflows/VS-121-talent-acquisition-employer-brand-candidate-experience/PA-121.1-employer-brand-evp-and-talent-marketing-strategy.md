## W3761. Employer Brand Strategy, Positioning & EVP Development

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual TA planning (links to VS-33.1); EVP refresh; brand-health decline |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise employer brand & EVP |
| **Owner** | TA Marketing / Employer Brand Lead |
| **Participants** | TA Marketing, CHRO/HR (links to VS-19/VS-103), Marketing/Brand (links to VS-14.3), Store Ops (links to VS-07), Comms (links to VS-14.3), DEI (links to VS-103.2/W719), Finance (links to VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TA Marketing/CHRO define the employer brand strategy and employee value proposition (EVP): research-based positioning ( BuildRight as the Filipino home-building partner and a builder of careers — trade mastery, stability, growth, family orientation), the EVP pillars (pay & benefits links to VS-102, learning & career links to VS-19.4, purpose/impact links to VS-25, culture links to VS-103.2), target talent segments (store frontline, trade-knowledgeable, DC/logistics, corporate/digital), and the brand promise to candidates | TA Marketing / CHRO | CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | TA Marketing/Brand operationalize the EVP into messaging, visual identity, and proof points (real employee stories, career-path examples, recognition links to W630/W973), and align it to the consumer/corporate brand (links to VS-14.3) so the employer brand is consistent across career site, social, store, and external communications | TA Marketing / Brand | CHRO | Annual + ongoing |
| 3 | CHRO/Executive ratify the employer brand strategy and EVP, approve the talent-marketing budget envelope (links to W3768), and cascade the EVP to all hiring managers and recruiters as the standard candidate value proposition | CHRO / TA Marketing | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- HR/TA platform (links to VS-103.3); brand/marketing (links to VS-14.3); recognition (links to W630/W973); rewards (links to VS-102); L&D (links to VS-19.4); governance (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Generic-brand risk**: EVP indistinguishable from competitors, weak attraction; mitigated by research-based, segment-specific positioning
- **Promise-vs-reality risk**: EVP overstates the actual employee experience, driving early attrition; mitigated by proof-point validation and EX alignment (links to VS-103.2)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "TA Marketing/CHRO define the employer brand strategy and employee" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the talent-marketing budget envelope" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-592 (ensure controlled execution — Employer Brand, EVP & Talent Marketing Strategy (PA-121.1).)
- operational: research-based, segment-specific positioning; operational: proof-point validation and EX alignment (links to VS-103)

### Time Estimate
- 4–6 weeks annual + ongoing
