## W5557. Recruitment Fraud & Fake Job-Offer Scam Response, Takedown & Victim-Guidance Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Discovery of a scam impersonating BuildRight recruitment — fake job offers, "training fee" / "processing fee" demands, fake onboarding portals or hiring group chats using the company name, logo, or careers-site look-alikes — reported by victims, employees, or channel monitoring |
| **Frequency** | A social-era integrity event that arrives in bursts: a few episodes per year, each clustering dozens to hundreds of victim contacts around a single fake hiring campaign |
| **Volume** | 1 campaign × the victim surface (10–500 contacts) × the takedown surfaces (social pages and group chats, look-alike domains, SMS/e-mail blasts) × the response desk (TA Marketing, IP Counsel, the store-facing script) |
| **Owner** | TA Marketing / Employer Brand Lead |
| **Participants** | IP Counsel (W3272 takedown machinery), Corporate Communications (W134 advisory; W143 media), Corporate Security (VS-159 threat intake), IT Security (phishing-domain coordination, VS-27.3), Store Managers and HR front desk (walk-in victim script), Talent Acquisition Operations (offer-verification registry, W3762), Data Protection Officer (VS-91 if harvested personal data surfaces), PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group |

### Background

'Recruitment scam'/'fake job'/'job scam' appear in zero PA files. PA-100.2's digital-IP machinery (W3272) watches and takes down impersonating sites and accounts — but through the brand-abuse lens, aimed at customers and phishing; W3764 manages employer-review-site reputation; nobody owns the *recruitment-fraud event*, whose victims arrive at BuildRight's own door: walk-in victims at stores who already paid a "training fee", job-seekers whose government IDs and personal data were harvested into a fake onboarding portal (a privacy incident in its own right), and social threads treating the scam as evidence BuildRight really is hiring. The response pattern is distinctive — same-day never-charges-fees advisory, a simultaneous multi-surface takedown wave, a victim-guidance script that protects the brand without blaming victims, and a PNP-ACG referral pack — and this workflow is its single owner, riding W3272 for enforcement, W3762 for the verification surface, and W5551's sensitive-case handling where a scammer turns out to be an insider.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Report intake & evidence capture**: screenshots, URLs, sender numbers, payment channels, and any fake offer letters preserved to the case; victim reports logged with contact consent for follow-up | TA Marketing / HR Front Desk / Corporate Security | TA Marketing / Employer Brand Lead | Same day |
| 2 | **Same-day scam advisory publication**: careers-site banner (W3762) plus official social channels and the W3764 monitoring surfaces: BuildRight never charges application, training, or processing fees; no offer is valid unless it appears in the official candidate portal; only official channels announce hiring | Corporate Communications / TA Marketing | CHRO | Within 24 hours |
| 3 | **Takedown wave**: platform reports filed simultaneously on all discovered surfaces via the W3272 machinery (social pages, group chats, marketplaces), registrar action on look-alike domains with IT Security, escalations tracked to closure | IP Counsel / IT Security | VP Legal & Compliance | 1–14 days |
| 4 | **Victim-guidance desk**: one standard script — stop paying, report to the platform and the PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group, submit no further personal data, and how to verify any real BuildRight offer; empathy-first, never blame; no ad-hoc commitments of reimbursement | TA Marketing / Customer Service (W258-class routing) | CHRO | Per episode |
| 5 | **Store & employee briefing**: walk-in script for store staff and HR front desks (where to route victims, what not to say), and an employee advisory so associates recognize and report fresh scam surfaces | TA Marketing / Store Operations | VP Store Operations | 1–2 days |
| 6 | **Law-enforcement referral**: evidence pack (captures, payment channels, platform metadata, victim count estimate) filed with the PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group; W1337-class coordination where the campaign links to organized fraud | Corporate Security / IP Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | 1–2 weeks |
| 7 | **Verification registry & content hardening**: the W3762 portal offer-verification path re-confirmed as the single source of truth; scam patterns folded into candidate-experience FAQ content and onboarding literacy material | Talent Acquisition Operations / TA Marketing | TA Marketing / Employer Brand Lead | 1–2 weeks |
| 8 | **Post-episode review**: channel monitoring rules tuned (W3764), employer-brand health checked (W3765), recurrence watch left running, and the episode filed as a VS-159 threat-intelligence record | TA Marketing / Corporate Security | CHRO | 2–4 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Careers-site advisory banner objects and offer-verification records (links to W3762)
- Social-listening and review-site monitoring queues (links to W3764)
- Brand-protection takedown case records and enforcement logs (links to W3272)
- Security threat-intake case (links to VS-159) and phishing-domain coordination records (links to VS-27.3)
- PNP referral evidence pack assembled from the case record

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Victim-harm escalation risk**: victims keep paying while the advisory waits for a perfect statement; mitigated by Step 2's within-24-hours advisory rule
- **Takedown-delay risk**: serial single-platform reports let a fake campaign run for weeks; mitigated by Step 3's simultaneous multi-surface wave with tracked escalations
- **Brand-silence risk**: silence is read as the offers being real; mitigated by the explicit never-charges-fees advisory and verified-offer rule
- **Misdirection risk**: store staff improvising "we're investigating" or accepting victim documents on the spot; mitigated by Step 5's script-and-routing briefing
- **Data-harvesting risk**: victims' government IDs and personal data already sit in the fake portal — a privacy exposure in its own right; mitigated by Step 4's no-further-data guidance and the VS-91 breach-assessment path when BuildRight-side data is implicated

### Automation Opportunity
- Social-listening rule on brand-name + hiring/job keywords with automatic case creation and evidence-capture checklist (replaces manual Step 1 discovery)
- One-click takedown pack assembly from the case record (platform report texts, registrar request, PNP referral annexes) — replaces manual Step 3/6 drafting

### Controls
- CTL-592 (ensure controlled execution — Employer Brand, EVP & Talent Marketing Strategy (PA-121.1).)
- operational: advisory published within 24 hours of confirmed scam; every takedown, referral, and victim-contact action logged on the case with evidence preserved

### Time Estimate
Per episode: ≈ 20–60 staff-hours over 2–4 weeks (advisory, takedown wave, victim desk, referral pack). Steady-state monitoring is absorbed by the W3764 listening cadence — no incremental headcount.

### Staffing Implication
- Absorbed by the TA Marketing bench with IP Counsel, Corporate Communications, and Corporate Security support; no incremental headcount.
