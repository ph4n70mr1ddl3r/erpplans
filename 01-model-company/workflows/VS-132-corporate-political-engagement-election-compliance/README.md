# VS-132: Corporate Political Engagement, Election Compliance & Public Affairs Governance

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Corporate Political Engagement & Election Compliance workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning
the enterprise discipline of engaging in politics and public affairs **lawfully and accountably**
across all 5 legal entities, 200 stores, and 13+ Philippine regions. As a PHP 62.3B-revenue
retailer operating under multiple regulators and LGUs, participating in industry and policy
advocacy (links to VS-104), and pursuing government/institutional business (links to VS-46),
BuildRight has structural exposure to Philippine political-engagement law: the **Omnibus Election
Code**, **RA 9006 (Fair Election Act)** and **COMELEC resolutions** (which strictly regulate and
largely prohibit corporate contributions to candidates/parties, election-period advertising, and
partisan activity), the **Anti-Graft and Corrupt Practices Act (RA 3019)** and **Government
Procurement Reform Act (RA 9184)** (which govern gifts/interactions with public officials and
prohibit bribery of foreign/public officials, overlapping with VS-86 ABC), the **Anti-Red Tape
Act / Ease of Doing Business Act (RA 11032)**, and the ethical/transparency expectations of
investors, lenders, B2B customers, and the board.

Today this discipline is **entirely unowned as a program**: VS-104 (Government Affairs & Industry
Relations) manages external *relationships* and policy *advocacy*, VS-86 (ABC) manages
*bribery/corruption*, VS-119 (Ethics/Speak-Up) manages *misconduct*, and VS-46 (B2G) executes
*government sales* — but no value stream owns the **corporate political-engagement governance
program**: a political-engagement policy and risk assessment, political-contribution and donation
controls and disclosure, lobbying/advocacy governance and disclosure, trade-association
membership review for political alignment, election-period compliance protocol, campaign-finance
and prohibited-donation controls, election-period marketing/advertising restrictions,
candidate/official interaction and anti-graft controls, grassroots/employee political-activity
governance, political-intelligence monitoring, crisis political/reputational management, and
transparency/board oversight. 'political contribution', 'election compliance', and 'COMELEC'
each appeared in **zero** (or only one incidental) PA file; political activity is genuinely
unowned as a discipline.

This is distinct from **VS-104 (Government Affairs & Industry Relations)** which manages the
*relationship and advocacy* activity (this value stream owns the *governance and compliance
controls* over that activity, including disclosure). It is distinct from **VS-86 (Anti-Financial
Crime / ABC)** which addresses *bribery and corruption* (this value stream addresses the broader
*political activity* — contributions, lobbying, endorsements, election-period conduct — much of
which is lawful but regulated, while overlapping areas defer to VS-86). It is distinct from
**VS-119 (Ethics/Speak-Up)** which owns enterprise *misconduct* handling (this value stream owns
the *political-activity-specific* controls and reporting). It is distinct from **VS-46 (B2G
Sales)** which executes *government sales/bidding* (this value stream governs the *political and
public-official interaction* environment around that business). It is distinct from **VS-14.3
(Brand PR & Corporate Communications)** which manages *communications* (this value stream governs
*political communications and election-period advertising restrictions*). Corporate political
engagement & election compliance is the *lawful-and-accountable-political-activity* discipline —
distinct from government affairs relationships, financial crime, ethics, government sales, and
corporate communications.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: a large, multi-region, multi-entity corporation operating in
a politically active environment, with substantial government sales, LGU relationships, and
industry-advocacy activity, faces real exposure to election-law violations (corporate
contributions are largely prohibited and carry criminal liability), anti-graft violations
(gifts/favors to officials), reputational harm from partisan alignment, and transparency
expectations — risks that no existing value stream owns end-to-end and that today sit implicitly
across VS-104/VS-86/VS-119.
