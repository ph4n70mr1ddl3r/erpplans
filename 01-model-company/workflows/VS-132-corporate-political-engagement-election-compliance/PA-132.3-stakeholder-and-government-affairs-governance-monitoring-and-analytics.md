## W4041. Political-Engagement Stakeholder & Relationship Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Engagement plan; relationship lifecycle |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | Multi-stakeholder (national, regional, LGU, regulator) |
| **Owner** | VP Govt Affairs |
| **Participants** | Govt Affairs (links to VS-104), Legal (links to VS-100), regional teams, B2G Sales (links to VS-46), Corporate Communications (links to VS-14.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Govt Affairs maintain the stakeholder/relationship map and CRM for political-engagement stakeholders (officials, regulators, LGUs links to VS-76, associations links to VS-104.2), with relationship owners, interaction history, and conduct compliance (links to W4037 anti-graft and W4038) | Govt Affairs / regional teams | VP Govt Affairs | Continuous |
| 2 | Govt Affairs manage engagements per the conduct standards (links to W4037) and the lobbying/advocacy registry (links to W4028), ensuring consistent, accountable, and documented interaction | Govt Affairs | VP Govt Affairs | Continuous |
| 3 | Govt Affairs/Comms coordinate stakeholder messaging with corporate communications (links to VS-14.3) and brand/reputation (links to VS-25), and report relationship health to leadership | Govt Affairs / Comms | VP Govt Affairs | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Govt affairs (links to VS-104); legal (links to VS-100); LGU (links to VS-76); B2G (links to VS-46); comms (links to VS-14.3); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inconsistent-engagement risk**: Multiple parties engaging officials inconsistently; mitigated by single owner and CRM
- **Conduct-breach risk**: Engagements crossing anti-graft lines; mitigated by conduct standards

### Time Estimate
- Continuous
