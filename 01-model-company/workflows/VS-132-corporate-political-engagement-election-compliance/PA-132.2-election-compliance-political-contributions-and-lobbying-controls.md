## W4033. Election-Period Compliance Protocol (Omnibus Election Code, RA 9006, COMELEC)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Election period declared (national/local/Barangay/SK/BARMM); COMELEC resolution |
| **Frequency** | Per election cycle |
| **Volume** | All 5 entities, 200 stores, all regions |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance |
| **Participants** | Legal (links to VS-100), Compliance (links to VS-119), Govt Affairs (links to VS-104), Marketing (links to VS-14), Store Ops (links to VS-07), Corporate Communications (links to VS-14.3), regional teams |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Legal activate the election-period compliance protocol on COMELEC election-period declaration: communicate restrictions across all entities/stores/regions (no corporate contributions, no partisan activity, election-period advertising rules, no use of company resources for partisanship), and align Marketing/Comms/Store-Ops to the restrictions | VP Legal / Compliance / Govt Affairs | CEO | Per cycle |
| 2 | Legal/Marketing implement election-period advertising/marketing restrictions (COMELEC rules on election propaganda, airtime/print limits, blackout periods, and the risk that BuildRight promotions/ads could be construed as indirect candidate/party support), with pre-publication review of sensitive campaigns | Legal / Marketing / Comms | VP Legal | Per cycle |
| 3 | Legal/Compliance monitor compliance during the cycle, manage incidents (links to VS-119), and document the cycle for disclosure (links to W4032) and lessons (links to W4040) | Legal / Compliance | VP Legal | Per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal (links to VS-100); marketing/comms (links to VS-14/VS-14.3); store ops (links to VS-07); ethics (links to VS-119); records (links to VS-88); governance (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inadvertent-violation risk**: Marketing/activity construed as electioneering; mitigated by protocol and pre-publication review
- **Inconsistent-application risk**: Some stores/regions unaware; mitigated by enterprise-wide communication


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-communication of "communicate restrictions across all entities/stores/regions" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "print limits, blackout periods, and the risk that BuildRight promotions/ads…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document the cycle for disclosure" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-161 (ensure election-period & political-contribution compliance.)
operational: protocol and pre-publication review; operational: enterprise-wide communication

### Time Estimate
- Per cycle (weeks to months)

## W4034. Campaign-Finance, Contribution-Limit & Prohibited-Donation Controls

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contribution request; spend decision; election cycle |
| **Frequency** | On request + maintained controls |
| **Volume** | Heavily restricted |
| **Owner** | VP Legal / CFO |
| **Participants** | Legal, Finance (links to VS-17.4), Compliance (links to VS-119), ABC (links to VS-86), Govt Affairs (links to VS-104), CEO, Board |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal/Finance maintain the campaign-finance/contribution control: the prohibition on direct/indirect corporate contributions to candidates, parties, and election campaigns under the Omnibus Election Code §89–95 and RA 9006 (with criminal liability for officers/agents); the screening of any spend that could be indirect support; and Board authority for any political-adjacent spend | Legal / Finance / Compliance | VP Legal / CFO | Maintained |
| 2 | Legal/Compliance review flagged spend (sponsorships, donations, in-kind, candidate-linked events/charities, advertising near elections) for electoral nexus, apply ABC (links to VS-86) and anti-graft (links to W4037) controls, and approve or decline with documented rationale | Legal / Compliance | CEO / Board | Per request |
| 3 | Finance/Legal record and disclose any permitted political-adjacent spend (links to W4032) and report to the Board Audit Committee (links to VS-36) | Finance / Legal | Board Audit Committee | Per event + periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance (links to VS-17.4); ABC (links to VS-86); ethics (links to VS-119); records (links to VS-88); governance (links to VS-36); disclosure (links to W4032)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Criminal-liability risk**: Prohibited contribution exposing officers; mitigated by prohibition, screening, and Board authority
- **Indirect-support risk**: Spend functioning as indirect contribution; mitigated by nexus review


### Automation Opportunity

- System auto-record of "record and disclose any permitted political-adjacent spend" (replaces manual Step 3).
