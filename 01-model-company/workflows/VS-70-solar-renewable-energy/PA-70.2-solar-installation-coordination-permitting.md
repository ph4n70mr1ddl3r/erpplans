## W2534. Solar Site Survey & Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer confirms interest in solar system purchase |
| **Frequency** | ~80–120 surveys/month |
| **Volume** | ~80–120/month across all regions |
| **Owner** | Solar Installation Partner |
| **Participants** | Solar Installer, Customer, Solar Sales Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Solar Sales Coordinator schedules site survey with certified installation partner within 5 business days of lead qualification; provides customer brief with preliminary system sizing from in-store consultation | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 30 min |
| 2 | Installation partner conducts on-site assessment: roof structural integrity, orientation and tilt angle, shading analysis (trees, buildings), electrical panel capacity, meter location, conduit routing; takes photos and measurements; uses mobile app to capture data into ERP | Solar Installer | Solar Sales Coordinator | 2–3 hours |
| 3 | Installer produces final system design: panel layout, inverter placement, cable routing, structural mounting requirements; generates updated quotation with actual costs (scaffolding, structural reinforcement, electrical upgrades if needed); Coordinator presents to customer for approval | Solar Installer / Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 1–2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile site survey app with photo capture and measurement logging
- Solar design tool integration with ERP product catalog
- Quotation update with site-specific cost adjustments

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Structural issues**: Older roofs may require reinforcement, adding PHP 20K–50K to project cost
- **Survey delays**: Limited installer capacity in provincial areas (Visayas, Mindanao)

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 hours on-site; 1–2 days for design and quotation

| **Frequency** | ~40–60 installations/month |
| **Volume** | ~40–60/month across all regions |
| **Owner** | Solar Sales Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Solar Sales Coord, Installation Partner, Logistics, Store Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinator confirms customer order and creates installation work order in ERP; assigns to installation partner based on geographic coverage, certification level (ERC-licensed electrician required), and current workload; confirms installation date with customer (typically 2–4 weeks lead time) | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 1–2 hours |
| 2 | Coordinator coordinates material delivery: generates pick list for store/DC, schedules delivery to customer site 1–2 days before installation, confirms installation partner has required tools and mounting hardware; Logistics arranges special delivery (panels require careful handling) | Solar Sales Coordinator / Logistics | VP Supply Chain | 1 day |
| 3 | Installation partner arrives on-site, confirms material receipt and completeness, executes installation per approved design; updates work order status in mobile app with progress photos at key milestones (mounting, panel placement, inverter installation, wiring) | Solar Installer | Solar Sales Coordinator | 1–3 days (system size dependent) |

- Installation work order management with partner assignment
- Delivery scheduling integration with installation timeline

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Weather delays**: Rainy season (Jun–Nov) delays rooftop installation work
- **Partner capacity**: Limited number of ERC-licensed installers in provincial areas

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

## W2536. Net Metering Application & DU Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Solar system installation completed and passed inspection |
| **Frequency** | ~40–60 applications/month |
| **Volume** | ~40–60/month across all DU service areas |
| **Owner** | Solar Sales Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Solar Sales Coord, Customer, DU (Distribution Utility), ERC |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinator prepares net metering application package: ERC certificate of compliance, electrical plan/diagram, installation photos, installer license, customer documents (ID, proof of ownership, DU account number); submits to DU per their specific process (Meralco online portal, provincial DU walk-in) | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 1–2 days |
| 2 | DU conducts inspection and meter change (bi-directional meter); Coordinator tracks application status; follows up with DU every 5 business days; manages customer expectations (DU processing: 2–8 weeks depending on utility) | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 2–8 weeks |
| 3 | Upon DU approval and bi-directional meter installation: Coordinator confirms net metering activation; customer begins earning export credits; Coordinator closes installation work order and triggers warranty registration (W2541) | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Net metering application tracking with DU-specific process templates
- Work order status with net metering stage
- Customer notification system for milestone updates

### Pain Points / Risks
- **DU processing delays**: Provincial DUs may take 8+ weeks for net metering approval
- **Application rejection**: Incomplete electrical diagrams or non-compliant installations require rework

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence; 2–8 weeks for DU processing

## W2537. Solar Installation Quality Inspection & Commissioning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Solar installation physical work completed |
| **Frequency** | ~40–60 inspections/month |
| **Volume** | ~40–60/month |
| **Owner** | Quality Inspector |
| **Participants** | Quality Inspector, Solar Installer, Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quality Inspector (BuildRight or third-party) conducts commissioning inspection: verifies panel mounting torque, wiring compliance (Philippine Electrical Code), inverter installation, grounding system, DC/AC disconnect accessibility, label compliance, fire safety clearance | Quality Inspector | Category Manager | 2–4 hours |
| 2 | Inspector performs electrical testing: open-circuit voltage measurement per string, insulation resistance test, ground continuity test, inverter startup and grid synchronization; records all test results in commissioning report via mobile app | Quality Inspector | Category Manager | 1–2 hours |
| 3 | If passed: Inspector signs off commissioning certificate; triggers net metering application (W2536) and warranty registration (W2541). If failed: issues punch list to installer, schedules re-inspection within 5 business days | Quality Inspector | Category Manager | 30 min – 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- Commissioning checklist and test result recording via mobile app
- Work order stage progression (Installation → Commissioning → Net Metering)
- Punch list tracking for failed inspections

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Electrical safety**: Improper wiring creates fire risk; mitigated by mandatory inspection
- **Inspector availability**: Limited qualified inspectors in provincial areas create bottleneck

### Time Estimate
- 3–6 hours per inspection

## W2538. Solar Electrical Permit & LGU Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Solar installation work order created |
| **Frequency** | ~40–60 permits/month |
| **Volume** | ~40–60/month across ~50 different LGUs |
| **Owner** | Solar Sales Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Solar Sales Coord, Customer, LGU Building Official, Licensed Engineer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinator prepares LGU permit application: electrical plan signed by licensed Professional Electrical Engineer (PEE), structural certification for roof loading, building permit (if required by LGU for solar installations), fire safety clearance; requirements vary by LGU | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 1–3 days |
| 2 | Coordinator submits application to LGU Building Official; tracks approval timeline (typically 5–15 business days); coordinates with Licensed Electrical Engineer for any revisions or additional documentation requested | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 5–15 business days |
| 3 | Upon permit approval: installation can proceed; LGU inspection scheduled post-installation for final occupancy/electrical clearance; Coordinator ensures all documentation archived in ERP for future reference and warranty claims | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- LGU permit tracking with jurisdiction-specific requirements
- Document management for permits and certifications
- Licensed engineer credential tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **LGU variability**: Each of the ~50 LGUs with BuildRight stores has different solar permit requirements and processing times
- **Engineer availability**: PEE sign-off required; limited PEEs in some provincial areas

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence; 5–15 days for LGU processing

| **Frequency** | ~40–60 deliveries/month |
| **Volume** | ~40–60/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Coord, Store/DC Ops, Installation Partner, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics Coordinator picks and stages installation materials at originating location (store or DC): panels (fragile), inverter, mounting rails, cables, connectors, breakers, conduits; creates consolidated shipment for site delivery | Logistics Coordinator / DC Ops | Logistics Manager | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Coordinator schedules special delivery: panels require flat-bed or covered truck (no stacking); arranges delivery 1–2 days before installation; confirms customer site access (roof access, parking for truck, crane/hoist availability for 2+ story buildings) | Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 1 day |
| 3 | Delivery executed; installation partner or customer signs goods receipt; Coordinator confirms material completeness vs. BOM; any missing items trigger emergency store-to-site replenishment within 24 hours | Logistics Coordinator / Installation Partner | Logistics Manager | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Pick/pack with special handling instructions for solar components
- TMS with special vehicle requirements
- BOM-based delivery completeness check

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Panel damage in transit**: ~2–3% breakage rate; requires careful handling and insurance
- **Site access**: Urban installations (Metro Manila) face parking and access restrictions

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

- Work order closure with revenue posting
- Warranty registration trigger

### Pain Points / Risks
- **DU scheduling delays**: Bi-directional meter installation may take 4–8 weeks after application approval
- **Customer confusion**: Net metering billing complexity requires clear explanation

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

## W2541. Solar Installation Warranty Registration & Handover

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Solar system commissioned and grid-connected |
| **Frequency** | ~40–60 registrations/month |
| **Volume** | ~40–60/month |
| **Owner** | Solar Sales Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Solar Sales Coord, Customer, Vendor, Installation Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinator registers warranties: panel performance warranty (25-year linear, 12-year product), inverter warranty (5–10 years), battery warranty (5–10 years), installation workmanship warranty (2 years); enters serial numbers, installation date, and contractor details into ERP warranty module | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 30–45 min |
| 2 | Coordinator prepares customer handover package: system documentation (as-built diagram, equipment manuals, warranty certificates), maintenance guide (panel cleaning schedule, inverter monitoring, annual inspection), emergency contacts, ERP portal login for warranty claim submission | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 30 min |
| 3 | Coordinator schedules 6-month follow-up call to verify system performance; enters customer into annual maintenance reminder program; flags for upsell opportunities (battery storage, EV charger, system expansion) at 12-month mark | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Warranty registration module with multi-vendor warranty tracking
- Customer portal for warranty claim submission
- CRM with maintenance reminder and upsell scheduling

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Multi-vendor warranty complexity**: Panels, inverter, and installation each have different warranty terms and claim processes
- **Customer neglect**: Customers may not perform required maintenance, voiding performance warranties

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence
