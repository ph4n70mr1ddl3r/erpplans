## W4537. Captive Underwriting Policy, Guidelines & Risk Acceptance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Structure (W4530); annual review; new risk line |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Captive underwriting policy across in-scope risk lines |
| **Owner** | Risk (VS-21.2) / Captive Board Underwriting Committee |
| **Participants** | Insurance (VS-26), Actuarial, Legal (VS-100.3), Compliance (VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk/Captive Board set the underwriting policy (which risks/lines the captive writes, limits, retentions, exclusions, territories/entities covered), per the W4530 scope & risk appetite (VS-21.2) | Risk / Captive Board UW Committee | Captive Board | Annual |
| 2 | Risk/Actuarial set risk-acceptance criteria & underwriting guidelines (eligibility, limits, deductibles, pricing parameters), and the underwriting authority/delegation | Risk / Actuarial | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Captive Board | Annual |
| 3 | Compliance/Legal ensure the policy aligns to domicile/regulatory rules and risk-transfer requirements, and report to the captive board | Compliance / Legal | VP Legal / Captive Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ERM/appetite (VS-21.2); insurance (VS-26); legal (VS-100.3); compliance (VS-22); captive governance (W4533)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scope-creep risk**: Captive writes risks outside appetite/expertise; mitigated by policy scope-limit & board control
- **Concentration risk**: Over-exposure to one peril/entity; mitigated by limit/concentration caps

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual policy

### Automation Opportunity
- Underwriting-guideline rules engine; limit/concentration monitoring; appetite-alignment check

### Controls
- CTL-112 (ensure captive risk-transfer adequacy.)
- CTL: underwriting policy board-approved & scope-limited (VS-21.2); risk-transfer compliant; concentration capped

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2 (ERM), VS-26 (insurance), VS-100.3 (legal), W4530 (scope), W4533 (governance), W4538 (pricing)

## W4538. Program Rating, Premium Pricing & Actuarial Valuation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Policy cycle (W4537); renewal; actuarial review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Premium pricing/rating across in-scope risk lines |
| **Owner** | Actuarial / Risk (VS-21.2) / Treasury (VS-18) |
| **Participants** | Insurance (VS-26), Tax (VS-79/W4535), Captive Board |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Actuarial develop the rating/pricing for each captive program (loss-cost + expense + risk-load + capital-load), based on loss history & exposure data (VS-21.2/VS-26) | Actuarial / Risk | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Captive Board UW Committee | Annual |
| 2 | Actuarial/Tax set arm's-length premium (transfer-pricing-compliant W4535), benchmark vs commercial market, and document the actuarial indication | Actuarial / Tax | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Captive Board/Actuarial approve rates, document the valuation, and file where required by domicile | Captive Board / Actuarial / Legal | Captive Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ERM/loss data (VS-21.2); insurance (VS-26); tax/TP (VS-79/W4535); captive governance (W4533)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-pricing risk**: Premium below actuarial cost → captive erodes capital; mitigated by risk-load & board rate-approval
- **Non-arm's-length risk**: Pricing not defensible → tax (W4535); mitigated by actuarial documentation & benchmarking

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual pricing cycle

### Automation Opportunity
- Rating/pricing model; loss-cost analytics; benchmark-vs-commercial comparison

### Controls
- CTL-690 (ensure controlled execution — Captive Underwriting, Risk Transfer & Reinsurance Program (PA-153.2).)
- CTL: rates actuarially indicated & board-approved; arm's-length (W4535); documented & filed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2 (loss data), VS-26 (insurance), VS-79/W4535 (tax/TP), W4533 (governance), W4537 (policy)

## W4539. Policy Issuance, Fronting & Ceding to Captive

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Renewal (VS-26); new entity/risk; go-live (W4536) |
| **Frequency** | Per policy/renewal (annual lines) |
| **Volume** | Policies across 5 entities, in-scope risk lines |
| **Owner** | Insurance (VS-26) / Risk / Fronting carrier (W4534) |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17), Legal (VS-100.3), Treasury (VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Insurance/Fronting carrier issue the master/local policies to BuildRight entities (admitted paper), per the W4537 underwriting policy & W4538 rates | Insurance / Fronting Carrier | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Insurance Manager | Per policy |
| 2 | Fronting carrier/Risk cede the in-scope risk to the captive (quota-share/excess, per W4540 reinsurance), and record the cession/premium transfer | Fronting Carrier / Risk / Treasury | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / CFO | Per policy |
| 3 | Finance/Treasury account the premium/cession (premium flow, commission to fronting, captive premium income), per VS-17/W4535 intercompany | Finance / Treasury | CFO | Per policy |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance (VS-26); fronting/cession (W4534); finance/GL (VS-17); treasury (VS-18); intercompany (W4535)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Issuance-error risk**: Wrong limits/entities/coverages; mitigated by policy-issuance QC & review
- **Cession-mismatch risk**: Ceded ≠ written; mitigated by reconciliation of fronting-vs-captive records

### Time Estimate
- Per renewal cycle weeks

### Automation Opportunity
- Policy/cession administration system; fronting-vs-captive reconciliation; premium-flow automation

### Controls
- CTL-690 (ensure controlled execution — Captive Underwriting, Risk Transfer & Reinsurance Program (PA-153.2).)
- CTL: policies issued per UW policy (W4537); cessions reconciled; premium accounted (VS-17/W4535)

### Cross-references

## W4540. Reinsurance Program Design & Treaty Placement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Structure (W4530); renewal; capacity need |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Captive reinsurance program (treaty/facultative) |
| **Owner** | Risk / Treasury (VS-18) / Captive Board / Broker |
| **Participants** | Actuarial, Insurance (VS-26), Reinsurers, Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk/Actuarial/Broker design the reinsurance program (excess-of-loss cat/property treaty, quota-share, facultative for large risks) protecting the captive above its retention (W4530) | Risk / Actuarial / Broker | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Captive Board | Annual |
| 2 | Risk/Broker place the treaty with reinsurers (terms, limits, attachments, premium), negotiate capacity & security (rated reinsurers), and document contracts | Risk / Broker / Legal | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Treasury/Captive Board monitor reinsurer security/concentration, manage treaty renewals, and ensure adequate catastrophic capacity for PH typhoon/quake exposure | Treasury / Captive Board / Risk | CFO / Captive Board | Annual + ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- ERM/cat exposure (VS-21.2/VS-69); treasury (VS-18); insurance (VS-26); legal (VS-100.3); captive governance (W4533)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Capacity-shortfall risk**: Insufficient cat reinsurance for PH catastrophe; mitigated by adequate treaty limits & multi-reinsurer placement
- **Reinsurer-security risk**: Reinsurer default on a cat loss; mitigated by rated/security-approved reinsurers

### Time Estimate
- ~6–8 weeks placement cycle

### Automation Opportunity
- Cat-modeling/loss-exceedance analytics; reinsurer-security monitoring; treaty-management system

### Controls
- CTL-690 (ensure controlled execution — Captive Underwriting, Risk Transfer & Reinsurance Program (PA-153.2).)
- CTL: reinsurance program adequately protects captive retention; reinsurers security-approved; treaty documented

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2/VS-69 (cat risk), VS-18 (treasury), VS-26 (insurance), W4530 (retention), W4533 (governance), W4543 (aggregate)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly close (VS-17); claims development; actuarial cycle |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + annual actuarial |
| **Volume** | Loss reserves across captive programs |
| **Owner** | Actuarial / Finance (VS-17) / Captive Board |
| **Participants** | Claims (W4545), Insurance (VS-26), Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Actuarial/Claims track loss emergence (IBNR, case reserves, development) per program using claims data (W4545) and loss triangles | Actuarial / Claims | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / CFO | Quarterly |
| 2 | Actuarial set the loss-reserve indication (IBNR + case), Captive Board/Finance book reserves (statutory + PFRS VS-17), and reconcile to fronting/carrier records | Actuarial / Finance / Captive Board | CFO / Captive Board | Quarterly/Annual |
| 3 | Audit/Actuarial perform independent reserve review (adequacy, redundancy), report to the captive board, and adjust on new data | Audit / Actuarial | Captive Board Audit Committee | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance/reserves (VS-17); claims (W4545); insurance (VS-26); audit (VS-21); captive governance (W4533)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-reserving risk**: Reserves too low → later earnings hit/capital strain; mitigated by actuarial rigor & redundancy
- **Volatility risk**: Reserve swings destabilize captive results; mitigated by disciplined methodology

### Time Estimate
- ~quarterly cycle + annual actuarial review

### Automation Opportunity
- Loss-triangle/reserve modeling; IBNR automation; reserve-adequacy dashboards

### Controls
- CTL-690 (ensure controlled execution — Captive Underwriting, Risk Transfer & Reinsurance Program (PA-153.2).)
- CTL: reserves actuarially set & board-booked (VS-17); independently reviewed (VS-21); reconciled to carrier

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk/Actuarial monitor aggregate loss vs retention/reinsurance aggregates (per-occurrence & annual aggregate), and cat exposure to PH typhoon/quake (VS-69) | Risk / Actuarial | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Captive Board | Continuous |

- Continuous monitoring + on-event response

### Automation Opportunity

| **Owner** | Risk / Captive Board / Actuarial |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17), Insurance (VS-26), Treasury (VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Time Estimate
- ~quarterly + annual cycle

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-690 (ensure controlled execution — Captive Underwriting, Risk Transfer & Reinsurance Program (PA-153.2).)
- CTL: underwriting result monitored (loss/combined ratio); cycle managed; distributions capital-adequacy-gated (W4533)
