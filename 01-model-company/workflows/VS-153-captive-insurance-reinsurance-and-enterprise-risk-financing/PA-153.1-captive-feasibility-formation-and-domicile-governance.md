## W4529. Total-Cost-of-Risk Baseline & Captive Feasibility/Business Case

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Risk-financing review (VS-21.2); premium spike; board mandate |
| **Frequency** | Feasibility cycle + periodic refresh |
| **Volume** | One enterprise total-cost-of-risk (TCOR) baseline |
| **Owner** | Risk (VS-21.2) / Treasury (VS-18) / CFO |
| **Participants** | Insurance (VS-26), Finance (VS-17.4), Actuarial advisor, Brokers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk/Insurance quantify the TCOR baseline (commercial premium + retained losses + risk-mgmt cost + admin) across property/cat, liability, fleet, crime, cyber, D&O, employee — per VS-26/VS-21.2 | Risk / Insurance / Finance | CRO / CFO | Feasibility cycle |
| 2 | Risk/Treasury/Actuarial model the captive business case (loss-history analysis, predicted retained-layer loss, reinsurance cost, capital required, savings vs commercial) with sensitivity | Risk / Treasury / Actuarial | CRO / CFO | Feasibility cycle |
| 3 | CFO/CRO present the business case to the board (VS-36), obtain go/no-go, and define success metrics vs the do-nothing baseline | CFO / CRO | Board | Per decision |

### System Touchpoints
- ERM/risk (VS-21.2); insurance (VS-26); treasury (VS-18); finance/FP&A (VS-17.4); board (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Bad-data risk**: Poor loss history → flawed captive economics; mitigated by actuarial rigor & data quality
- **Over-optimism risk**: Understated retained losses → captive under-capitalized; mitigated by conservative sensitivity

### Time Estimate
- ~8–12 weeks feasibility cycle

### Automation Opportunity
- Loss-data analytics platform; captive-economics modeling tool; TCOR dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-112 (ensure captive risk-transfer adequacy.)
- CTL: TCOR actuarially baselined (VS-21.2); business case conservative/board-approved (VS-36); success metrics defined

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2 (ERM), VS-26 (insurance), VS-18 (treasury), VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-36 (board), W4530 (structure)

## W4530. Captive Strategy, Structure & Risk-Scope Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Feasibility go (W4529); risk-scope decision |
| **Frequency** | Per formation + periodic review |
| **Volume** | One captive strategy/structure/risk-scope |
| **Owner** | Risk (VS-21.2) / Treasury (VS-18) / CFO |
| **Participants** | Insurance (VS-26), Legal (VS-100.3), Tax (VS-79), Actuarial |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk/Treasury design the captive structure (pure captive vs group/sister-captive vs protected-cell), ownership (held by BuildRight Holdings), and the risk scope it writes (e.g., property/cat retained layer, liability, fleet, cyber) | Risk / Treasury / Legal | CRO / CFO / Board | Per formation |
| 2 | Risk/Actuarial set the retention layers (self-insured retention, captive layer, commercial/reinsurance above), aligned to risk appetite (VS-21.2) and commercial-market gaps | Risk / Actuarial / Insurance | CRO / CFO | Per formation |
| 3 | Risk/Legal/Tax document the strategy/structure, validate risk-transfer (real insurance, not financing-sham), and obtain board approval (VS-36) | Risk / Legal / Tax | Board | Per formation |

### System Touchpoints
- ERM/appetite (VS-21.2); insurance (VS-26); legal (VS-100.3); tax (VS-79); board (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Risk-transfer-failure risk**: Structure lacks genuine risk transfer → regulatory/tax disallowance; mitigated by bona-fide insurance design (risk-shifting/distribution)
- **Scope-overreach risk**: Captive writes risks it can't price; mitigated by actuarial scope-gating

### Time Estimate
- ~6–10 weeks structure design

### Automation Opportunity
- Retention-layer modeling; risk-appetite/structure alignment; risk-transfer validation

### Controls
- CTL-112 (ensure captive risk-transfer adequacy.)
- CTL: captive structure bona-fide insurance (risk transfer); risk-scope actuarially gated; board-approved (VS-36)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2 (ERM), VS-26 (insurance), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-79 (tax), VS-36 (board), W4529 (feasibility), W4531 (domicile)

| **Participants** | Tax (VS-79), Finance (VS-17), Board (VS-36), Auditors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal/Corp Secretary form the captive legal entity (incorporation, articles, bylaws, shareholder = BuildRight Holdings), register, and establish governance docs | Legal / Corp Secretary | VP Legal / Board | Per formation |

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 months formation/capitalization

### Automation Opportunity
- Capital-adequacy modeling; entity/governance document management; consolidation integration (VS-17)

### Controls
- CTL-689 (ensure controlled execution — Captive Feasibility, Formation & Domicile/Governance (PA-153.1).)
- CTL: captive actuarially capitalized (≥ domiciliary min); separately accounted (VS-17); board-formed (VS-36)
