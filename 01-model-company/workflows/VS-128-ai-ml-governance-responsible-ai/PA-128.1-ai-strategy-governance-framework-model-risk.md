## W3929. AI/ML Strategy, Operating Model & Governance Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Digital/data strategy (links to VS-28/VS-113); AI-program review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | 1 enterprise AI-governance program |
| **Owner** | AI Governance / Chief Data & AI Officer |
| **Participants** | AI Governance, Data & Analytics (links to VS-28/VS-30.2), Enterprise Architecture (links to VS-113), IT/Security (links to VS-27/VS-27.3), Privacy/DPO (links to VS-91), Legal (links to VS-100), Risk/Internal Audit (links to VS-21), HR (links to VS-19/VS-103), business AI owners (Fraud links to VS-125, CDP links to VS-126, S&OP links to VS-127, Marketing links to VS-14, LP links to VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI Governance/CDAIO define the enterprise AI strategy and governance framework: the AI-use-case portfolio and roadmap, the governance operating model (AI steering, AI ethics review board, model-risk function), alignment to enterprise architecture (links to VS-113) and data strategy (links to VS-28), and the framework baseline (ISO 42001 AI Management System, NIST AI RMF) | AI Governance / CDAIO / EA | CDO / CIO | Annual |
| 2 | AI Governance establish the governance scope and boundaries: which AI/ML is in scope (incl. vendor and GenAI), the risk-tiering approach, the interaction with privacy (links to VS-91), security (links to VS-27.3), audit (links to VS-21), and legal (links to VS-100), and the AI value/KPIs (model count, value, incidents, fairness pass-rate) | AI Governance / Risk / Privacy / Legal | CDO | Annual |
| 3 | CDO/Executive ratify the strategy and governance, approve the AI budget (links to W3935), and cascade accountability to business AI owners | CDO / CIO | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Data/analytics (links to VS-28); AI/ML platform (links to VS-30.2); EA (links to VS-113); security (links to VS-27.3); privacy (links to VS-91); risk/audit (links to VS-21); legal (links to VS-100)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-owner risk**: AI/ML proliferates with no governance owner; mitigated by central AI-governance framework
- **Framework-shopping risk**: Adopts no standard; mitigated by ISO 42001/NIST AI RMF baseline


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-capture (define the enterprise ai strategy and)
- auto-count (value/kpis (model count, value, incidents, fairness)
- rule-based auto-approval (and governance, approve the ai budget)

### Controls
operational: central AI-governance framework; operational: ISO 42001/NIST AI RMF baseline
### Time Estimate
- 3–4 weeks annual strategy + ongoing governance

### Controls
operational: independent validation (three lines of defense links to W3933); operational: closure tracking
