## W3393. Working-Capital KPI Definition, Baseline & Target Setting (DPO/DIO/DSO/DWC)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual planning (links to VS-33.1); treasury/merchandise strategy refresh |
| **Frequency** | Annual + quarterly refresh |
| **Volume** | Group + 5-entity + 200-store view |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury Manager, FP&A (links to VS-17.4), Merchandising (links to VS-101), Supply Chain (links to VS-02), AR/Credit (links to VS-16) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/FP&A define the working-capital KPI set: DPO (accounts payable days), DIO (inventory days), DSO (receivable days), DWC (days working capital = DIO + DSO − DPO), and CCC; establish data sources (AP links to VS-15, inventory links to VS-05, AR links to VS-16) and calculation governance | Treasury Manager / FP&A | CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Treasury/FP&A baseline current DWC by entity/category/channel using actuals, benchmark against retail/home-improvement norms and the 6–8x inventory-turn target (per profile §12.3), and set improvement targets aligned to the cash-flow/EBITDA plan (links to VS-33.1/VS-18.1) | Treasury Manager / FP&A | CFO | Annual |
| 3 | CFO/Executive committee approve the DWC targets, decompose into DPO/DIO/DSO sub-targets by owner (Treasury-AP for DPO, Merchandising-Supply for DIO, AR-Credit for DSO), and establish the monthly review cadence (W3399) and initiative portfolio (W3398) | CFO / Exec Committee | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- BI/FP&A planning (links to VS-28.1/VS-17.4); AP/inventory/AR data (links to VS-15/VS-05/VS-16); cash forecast (links to VS-18.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Definition-inconsistency risk**: Different DPO/DIO definitions across owners distort the target; mitigated by single KPI definition and governance
- **Unrealistic-target risk**: Targets unmoored from operational reality cause dysfunction; mitigated by bottom-up validation with Merchandising/Supply/AR


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "counts payable days" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "file §12.3" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review cadence" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: single KPI definition and governance; operational: bottom-up validation with Merchandising/Supply/AR

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual

## W3394. Days-Payable-Outstanding (DPO) Improvement Initiative Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DWC target (W3393); SCF/term program (W3377/W3381) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + quarterly review |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 vendors; ~PHP 37B procurement spend |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury Manager, Procurement Director, AP Manager, Category Managers (links to VS-01) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/Procurement analyze DPO by vendor/category/segment: actual vs contracted terms, on-time-payment rate, term-negotiation opportunity, and SCF-eligibility; identify the DPO-improvement backlog (extended terms per W3381, SCF enrollment per W3379, payment-run timing per VS-15.2) | Treasury Manager / Procurement | Treasury Manager | Quarterly |
| 2 | Procurement/Category Managers execute the DPO initiatives: renegotiate terms (W3381), enroll vendors in SCF (W3379), tighten payment-run timing (links to VS-15.2), and resolve term-master data issues (links to VS-295); track initiative-by-initiative DPO gain | Procurement / Category Managers / AP | Procurement Director | Continuous |
| 3 | Treasury measure DPO movement vs target, isolate drivers (term change vs payment timing vs SCF), and report to the working-capital review (W3399); re-prioritize the backlog and flag adverse vendor reactions | Treasury Manager / FP&A | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- AP/payment-terms master (links to VS-15/VS-295); SCF platform (links to W3377); spend analytics (links to VS-67/VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-offset risk**: Term extension extracted via price concession erodes margin (links to VS-101.3); mitigated by price-for-terms analytics (W3381)
- **Vendor-harm risk**: Aggressive DPO pushback harms fragile vendors and supply continuity; mitigated by SCF access and health screening


### Automation Opportunity

- System auto-track of "track initiative-by-initiative DPO gain" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: price-for-terms analytics (W3381); operational: SCF access and health screening

### Time Estimate
- Continuous + quarterly

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly forecast; strategic decision (season, capex, acquisition) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + scenario-driven |
| **Volume** | Group + 5-entity |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury Manager, FP&A (links to VS-17.4), Merchandising (links to VS-101), Supply (links to VS-02), AR (links to VS-16) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/FP&A build/maintain the CCC forecast model: project DPO/DIO/DSO from plan + initiatives (W3394–W3396), compute forward DWC and the resulting cash generation/consumption, and integrate to the rolling cash forecast (links to VS-18.1/W233) | Treasury Manager / FP&A | Treasury Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | Treasury model scenarios: seasonal peak (ber-months inventory build per profile §13.2), import-pipeline surge (links to VS-02.2), capex acceleration (links to VS-40), supplier-distress (links to VS-68/VS-02.3), and macro/FX shock (links to VS-18.3); quantify CCC and liquidity impact | Treasury Manager / FP&A | CFO | Scenario-driven |
| 3 | Treasury/CFO use the CCC forecast to inform SCF facility sizing (W3377/W3382), dynamic-discounting throttle (W3389), debt/facility decisions (links to VS-18/W319), and the board cash outlook (links to VS-36); communicate material CCC shifts to executives | Treasury Manager / CFO | CFO / CEO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A/treasury modeling (links to VS-17.4/VS-18.1); BI (links to VS-28.1); debt facility (links to VS-18 W319)
