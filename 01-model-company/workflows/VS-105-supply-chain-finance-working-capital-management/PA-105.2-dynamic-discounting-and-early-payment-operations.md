## W3385. Dynamic-Discounting Platform Setup, Discount-Curve Design & Vendor Enrollment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | SCF program launch (links to W3377); annual curve refresh |
| **Frequency** | Annual + periodic |
| **Volume** | ~200–500 vendors; ~8,500–9,500 monthly AP invoices (per profile §10.2) |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury Manager, AP Manager, Procurement, Vendor (links to VS-03.4), IT (links to VS-27.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury Manager design the dynamic-discounting curve: discount % as a function of days-early (e.g., 2%/10, 1%/20, 0.5%/30 vs Net 45 standard), calibrated to BuildRight's cost of funds (links to VS-18.3/W319), vendor margin tolerance (links to VS-39), and the risk-free alternative (links to VS-18 short-term investment yield W318) | Treasury Manager / FP&A | CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Treasury/IT configure the dynamic-discounting module on the AP/SCF platform: approved-payable feed (links to VS-15.1), curve engine, vendor enrollment flag, payment-method/routing, and GL mapping for discount income (links to VS-17.1); integrate and test end-to-end | Treasury Manager / IT | Treasury Manager | Per setup |
| 3 | Procurement enroll vendors into dynamic discounting via the supplier portal (links to VS-03.4): explain the sliding-scale offer, opt-in, set floor/ceiling, and confirm bank account; segment vendors by discount sensitivity and prioritize high-yield targets | Procurement / Vendor | Procurement Director | Wave-based |

### System Touchpoints
- Dynamic-discounting / SCF platform; AP (links to VS-15.1); supplier portal (links to VS-03.4); GL (links to VS-17.1); treasury yield reference (links to VS-18.3/W318)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Curve-miscalibration risk**: Over-generous curve erodes yield; too-tight curve starves adoption; mitigated by annual calibration to cost-of-funds and analytics (W3388)
- **Integration-break risk**: AP feed/platform disconnect halts offers; mitigated by IT testing and reconciliation (W3392)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "counting curve" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approved-payable feed" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-545 (ensure controlled execution — Dynamic Discounting and Early Payment Operations (PA-105.2).)
operational: annual calibration to cost-of-funds and analytics (W3388); operational: IT testing and reconciliation (W3392)

### Time Estimate
- 4–6 weeks setup + ongoing enrollment

## W3386. Sliding-Scale Early-Payment Offer Generation & Vendor Acceptance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Invoice approval (links to VS-15.1) |
| **Frequency** | Daily / transaction-level |
| **Volume** | ~200–500 invoices/month (peak construction season) |
| **Owner** | AP Manager |
| **Participants** | AP Manager, Vendor, Treasury Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AP/Platform auto-generate the sliding-scale early-payment offer on each approved, enrolled invoice: discount % for each early-payment date derived from the curve (W3385); present to vendor via the supplier portal (links to VS-03.4) with net amount, discount, and effective date | AP Manager / Platform | AP Manager | Per invoice |
| 2 | Vendor reviews and accepts an offer (selects payment date) or declines (pays at standard term); system captures acceptance, locks the discount and payment date, and removes the payable from the standard payment run (links to VS-15.2) | Vendor / AP Manager | AP Manager | Per invoice |
| 3 | AP/Treasury monitor offer-to-acceptance conversion, yield capture, and vendor participation; flag low-adoption vendors for Procurement outreach (W3385) and tune the curve based on analytics (W3388) | AP Manager / Treasury Manager | Treasury Manager | Weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- Dynamic-discounting platform; supplier portal (links to VS-03.4); AP payment run (links to VS-15.2); analytics (links to VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low-acceptance risk**: Vendors ignore offers, leaving yield uncaptured; mitigated by vendor outreach and curve tuning (W3388)
- **Offer-leakage risk**: Offers generated on invoices already near-due yield negligible discount; mitigated by offer-threshold and throttling (W3389)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count % for each early-payment date derived from the curve" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews and accepts an offer" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "monitor offer-to-acceptance conversion, yield capture, and vendor participation" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-545 (ensure controlled execution — Dynamic Discounting and Early Payment Operations (PA-105.2).)
operational: vendor outreach and curve tuning (W3388); operational: offer-threshold and throttling (W3389)

### Time Estimate
- Daily

## W3387. Self-Funded Early-Payment Execution, Funding & Bank Settlement

| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | ~150–400 early payments/month (peak) |
| **Owner** | AP Manager |
| **Participants** | AP Manager, Treasury Manager, Bank (links to VS-18.2), Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AP/Platform compile the daily early-payment batch: accepted offers due today, net amount (face − discount), vendor bank routing, and entity/bank account (multi-entity per VS-72); validate cash availability with Treasury (W3389) | AP Manager / Treasury Manager | AP Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Treasury confirm funding source (operating account vs sweep per VS-18.2/W323) and release the payment file; bank executes the early payment to the vendor; AP/Platform records the discount-captured income and clears the payable | Treasury Manager / AP Manager | Treasury Manager | Daily |
| 3 | AP/Treasury reconcile the early-payment settlement to the bank statement (links to VS-18.2), confirm discount-income posting (links to VS-17.1), and resolve exceptions (W3383); report daily discount capture to Treasury | AP Manager / Treasury Manager | Treasury Manager | Daily |

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cash-shortfall risk**: Funding early payments without cash check drains operating accounts; mitigated by cash-availability validation (W3389)
- **Duplicate-payment risk**: Paying early AND in the standard run; mitigated by payable-flag removal and reconciliation (W3392)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "validate cash availability with Treasury" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "records the discount-captured income and clears the payable" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "count-income posting" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-106 (ensure SCF settlement & covenant compliance.)
operational: cash-availability validation (W3389); operational: payable-flag removal and reconciliation (W3392)

### Time Estimate

## W3388. Annual-Percentage-Rate (APR) / Discount-Yield Monitoring & Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly/monthly yield review; annual curve refresh (W3385) |
| **Frequency** | Weekly + monthly |
| **Volume** | Full dynamic-discounting portfolio |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury Manager, FP&A (links to VS-17.4), Procurement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/Analytics compute the effective APR/yield of captured discounts (discount $ ÷ face ÷ days-early × 365) by vendor, segment, and curve-band; benchmark against BuildRight's cost of funds (links to VS-18.3/W319) and short-term investment yield (links to W318) to confirm discounting beats the alternative | Treasury Manager / FP&A | Treasury Manager | Weekly |
| 2 | Treasury/Procurement identify yield leakage (low-yield acceptances, curve over-payment, concentration) and high-yield opportunities (untapped vendors, curve tightening); recommend curve/vendor changes and rebalance enrollment | Treasury Manager / Procurement | Treasury Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | Treasury Manager present yield analytics and recommendations to the SCF governance review (W3384) and feed outcomes to the working-capital dashboard (W3399); update the curve per W3385 as needed | Treasury Manager | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Dynamic-discounting analytics; BI/treasury dashboards (links to VS-28.1); FP&A (links to VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Negative-yield risk**: Paying early at a discount below the cost of funds destroys value; mitigated by APR monitoring and curve calibration
- **Analytics-blindspot risk**: Aggregate yield masks per-vendor leakage; mitigated by granular analytics


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "counts" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-identify of "identify yield leakage" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-106 (ensure SCF settlement & covenant compliance.)
operational: APR monitoring and curve calibration; operational: granular analytics

### Time Estimate
- Weekly + monthly

## W3389. Cash-Availability-Aware Discount Throttling & Treasury Authorization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily cash-position review (links to VS-18.1); liquidity event |
| **Frequency** | Daily + event-driven |
| **Volume** | Full dynamic-discounting portfolio |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury Manager, AP Manager, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury review the daily cash position and short-term forecast (links to VS-18.1/W233): operating balance, sweep availability (W323), capex draw needs (links to VS-40), tax/statutory outflows (links to VS-79), and import/LC settlements (links to VS-02.2); compute the early-payment funding envelope | Treasury Manager | Treasury Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Treasury set/adjust the daily early-payment throttle (cap, vendor-tier, curve-band) based on cash availability; throttle down in tight-liquidity periods (e.g., pre-VAT/statutory remittance, import-LC week) and up in surplus periods | Treasury Manager | Treasury Manager | Daily |
| 3 | AP execute within the throttle, escalate over-throttle high-value acceptances to Treasury/CFO for ad-hoc authorization (links to W3390), and report throttling impact on yield capture to the weekly review (W3388) | AP Manager / Treasury Manager | Treasury Manager | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Cash positioning/forecast (links to VS-18.1); dynamic-discounting platform throttle; AP (links to VS-15); treasury sweeps (links to VS-18.2/W323)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Liquidity-drain risk**: Unthrottled early payment during a cash squeeze triggers overdraft/borrowing; mitigated by daily throttle tied to forecast
- **Yield-starvation risk**: Over-throttling in normal periods leaves yield uncaptured; mitigated by dynamic, forecast-aware throttling


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "review the daily cash position and short-term forecast" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-import of "import-LC week" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-escalate of "escalate over-throttle high-value acceptances to Treasury/CFO for ad-hoc…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-545 (ensure controlled execution — Dynamic Discounting and Early Payment Operations (PA-105.2).)
operational: daily throttle tied to forecast; operational: dynamic, forecast-aware throttling

### Time Estimate
- Daily

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vendor raises an ad-hoc early-payment request via the supplier portal outside the auto-offer curve (e.g., distress, working-capital need); AP/Treasury receive the request with invoice, amount, requested date, and proposed discount | Vendor / AP Manager | AP Manager | Per request |
