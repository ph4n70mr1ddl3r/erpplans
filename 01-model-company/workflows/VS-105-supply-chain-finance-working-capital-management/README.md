# VS-105: Supply Chain Finance & Working Capital Management

> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Supply Chain Finance (SCF) & Working Capital Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **treasury-procurement discipline** that monetizes BuildRight's payer position in the
~PHP 42–45B annual COGS supply chain (~PHP 37B annual procurement spend per profile §6.5/§7.1,
~800–1,000 active vendors, 30–60-day standard payment terms per profile §10.2) to free cash,
support vendor liquidity, and protect the 12–14% EBITDA target. This value stream owns the
end-to-end program: multi-funder supplier-finance / reverse-factoring facility design and bank
partner management, vendor SCF enrollment and eligibility, dynamic-discounting / early-payment
platform operations and sliding-scale discount curves, approved-payable monetization and bank
settlement, working-capital analytics (DPO, DIO, DSO, days-working-capital / cash-conversion
cycle), inventory and AR cash-release initiatives, and the working-capital governance that links
payment-term policy to the cash forecast (links to VS-18.1) and the merchandise plan
(links to VS-101).

This is distinct from **VS-18 (Treasury & Cash)** which owns *cash positioning, banking, FX and
investments* — and where a single workflow W324 ("Supply Chain Finance & Dynamic Discounting
Program") today introduces SCF at a program-summary level; this value stream decomposes that
program into its full operational lifecycle (facility negotiation, vendor enrollment, transaction
execution, analytics, governance) that W324 only sketches. It is also distinct from
**VS-15 (Procure-to-Pay)** which executes *invoice matching and vendor payment* (the payable that
SCF monetizes, but not the financing decision), from **VS-39 (Vendor Rebate & Incentive)** which
governs *vendor-funded price/allowance programs* (a margin event, not a financing event), from
**VS-03.3 (Vendor Performance & Contracts)** which negotiates *payment terms* as a contract clause
(the upstream input this value stream optimizes against), and from **VS-68 (Trade Credit Risk) /
VS-16 (Order-to-Cash)** which are the *customer-side* (receivable) credit disciplines. SCF & WC is
the *supplier-side financing and cash-conversion* discipline that sits at the procurement-treasury
intersection.

BuildRight's exposure is large: a PHP 62.3B revenue retailer with ~PHP 37B annual procurement
spend, ~40% import component (links to VS-02.2/VS-87), top-20 vendors representing ~45% of COGS
(per profile §6.5), and a growth capex program (10–15 new stores/year per profile §3.1) that
competes for the same cash. A 5–10-day improvement in days-working-capital at this scale releases
~PHP 0.5–1.0B of cash; conversely, supplier-distress during import/currency volatility
(links to VS-18.3) or a typhoon-supply-shock (links to VS-69) directly threatens COGS continuity.
No prior value stream owned the comprehensive SCF & working-capital program.
