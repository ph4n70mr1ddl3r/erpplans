## W3377. SCF Strategy, Program Charter & Multi-Funder Facility Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual treasury/procurement strategy review; working-capital target reset (links to VS-33.1) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + as needed |
| **Volume** | 1 program covering ~50–100 key vendors (~60% of ~PHP 37B procurement spend) |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury Manager, CFO, Procurement Director, FP&A (links to VS-17.4), Bank SCF Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury Manager develop the SCF strategy and program charter: target DPO/working-capital outcome (links to W3393), eligible-spend scope (merchandise vs non-merchandise, import vs local), funder mix (single bank vs multi-funder marketplace), and the build-vs-buy platform decision; align to cash forecast (links to VS-18.1) and capex funding plan (links to VS-40) | Treasury Manager / Procurement Director | CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Treasury Manager design the facility structure: reverse-factoring (BuildRight-payable-backed) vs payables-receivables financing, facility limit, term, early-payment discount/yield curve, FX treatment for import payables (links to VS-18.3), and the true-sale vs assignment legal structure; secure tax/VAT and PFRS off-balance-sheet treatment review with Tax (links to VS-79) and External Audit (links to VS-21) | Treasury Manager / Finance / Tax | CFO | Annual |
| 3 | CFO/Board approve the SCF program, facility envelope, and risk appetite; authorize partner selection (W3378) and set the governance cadence (W3384) and KPI reporting (links to W3399) | CFO | Board / CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury management system / SCF platform; AP/payable master (links to VS-15.1); cash forecasting (links to VS-18.1); GL (links to VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Off-balance-sheet misclassification risk**: Wrong structuring brings the payable back on-balance-sheet and breaches covenants; mitigated by tax/audit sign-off (links to VS-79/VS-21)
- **Over-reliance risk**: Single-funder concentration creates refinancing risk; mitigated by multi-funder design (W3378/W3384)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-import of "import vs local" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review with Tax" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the SCF program, facility envelope, and risk appetite" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-544 (ensure controlled execution — Supplier Finance & Reverse-Factoring Program Management (PA-105.1).)
operational: tax/audit sign-off (links to VS-79/VS-21); operational: multi-funder design (W3378/W3384)

### Time Estimate
- 6–8 weeks annual cycle

## W3378. Bank / Fintech SCF Partner Selection, Onboarding & Agreement Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program approval (W3377); funder performance review; facility renewal |
| **Frequency** | At program launch + periodic renewal (1–3 yr) |
| **Volume** | 1–3 funder partners |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury Manager, CFO, Procurement, Legal (links to VS-100.3), Bank/Fintech Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury Manager issue RFP to SCF-capable banks (BDO, BPI, Metrobank, Chinabank per profile §10.4) and fintech platforms; evaluate on funding cost/yield, platform usability, vendor onboarding friction, FX capability, reporting, and integration to ERP/AP (links to VS-15) | Treasury Manager / Procurement | CFO | Per selection |
| 2 | Legal/Treasury negotiate the master SCF agreement: assignment/true-sale terms, recourse, repurchase events, pricing mechanics, data-sharing and privacy (links to VS-91), termination, and bank covenants; execute and onboard the funder to the platform | Legal / Treasury Manager | CFO | Per selection |
| 3 | Treasury Manager configure the funder on the SCF platform and ERP integration (approved-payable feed, settlement file, GL posting of finance charges), test end-to-end with a pilot vendor cohort, and document the operating runbook | Treasury Manager / IT (links to VS-27.2) | Treasury Manager | Per onboarding |

### System Touchpoints
- SCF platform; ERP/AP integration (links to VS-15); bank integration (links to VS-18.2); GL (links to VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Integration-failure risk**: AP-to-platform feed breaks stall vendor payment; mitigated by IT integration testing and reconciliation (W3380)
- **Data-privacy risk**: Sharing payable/vendor data with funders triggers DPA obligations; mitigated by privacy review (links to VS-91)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-file of "file §10.4" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-assign of "assignment/true-sale terms, recourse, repurchase events, pricing mechanics,…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document the operating runbook" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-544 (ensure controlled execution — Supplier Finance & Reverse-Factoring Program Management (PA-105.1).)
operational: IT integration testing and reconciliation (W3380); operational: privacy review (links to VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- 8–12 weeks per funder onboarding

## W3379. Vendor SCF Eligibility, Enrollment & KYC Onboarding

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor becomes top-spend / strategic; periodic enrollment waves |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + wave-based |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 eligible vendors (~60% of spend); ~10–20 new enrollments/quarter |
| **Owner** | Procurement Director |
| **Participants** | Procurement, Treasury Manager, Vendor (links to VS-03.4 portal), Funder |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement/Treasury identify eligible vendors from spend analytics (links to VS-67/VS-03.3): spend threshold, payment-term stability, vendor health, strategic importance, and import/local mix; segment into priority enrollment waves | Procurement / Treasury Manager | Procurement Director | Quarterly |
| 2 | Procurement invite the vendor to enroll via the supplier portal (links to VS-03.4); funder completes vendor KYC/AML (links to VS-86.1), credit assessment, and master SCF agreement (vendor-side discount/fee acceptance); vendor configures bank account and notification preferences | Procurement / Funder / Vendor | Procurement Director | Per vendor |
| 3 | Treasury Manager/Procurement activate the vendor on the SCF platform, confirm the payable feed and discount curve, run a test early-payment transaction, and add the vendor to the active SCF roster and governance reporting (W3384) | Treasury Manager / Procurement | Treasury Manager | Per vendor |

### System Touchpoints
- Supplier portal (links to VS-03.4); SCF platform; vendor master (links to VS-29/W287); KYC/AML (links to VS-86.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low-adoption risk**: Vendors decline enrollment due to fee/discount; mitigated by clear value proposition and tiered pricing
- **Vendor-distress risk**: Enrolling a distressed vendor masks underlying insolvency; mitigated by funder credit assessment and monitoring (W3384)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-import of "importance, and import/local mix" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "count/fee acceptance" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "reporting" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-544 (ensure controlled execution — Supplier Finance & Reverse-Factoring Program Management (PA-105.1).)
operational: clear value proposition and tiered pricing; operational: funder credit assessment and monitoring (W3384)

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 weeks per vendor onboarding

## W3380. Approved-Payable Monetization, Invoice Upload & Funder Settlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Invoice 3-way match approval (links to VS-15.1); vendor early-payment request |
| **Frequency** | Daily / transaction-level |
| **Volume** | ~200–500 invoices/month during peak construction season; ~50–150 typical month |
| **Owner** | AP Manager |
| **Participants** | AP Manager, Treasury Manager, Funder, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AP Manager process invoices through normal 3-way match (links to VS-15.1 W7); approved, SCF-eligible payables are automatically flagged and uploaded to the SCF platform with vendor, amount, due-date, and PO reference; data reconciled to AP sub-ledger | AP Manager | AP Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Vendor requests early payment on the platform (or auto-offer per enrollment); funder funds the vendor early at the agreed discount; BuildRight's obligation is re-owed to the funder at the original invoice due date; AP/SCF platform records the assignment and updates the payable beneficiary | AP Manager / Funder / Vendor | Treasury Manager | Per transaction |
| 3 | Treasury/AP reconcile the SCF settlement: vendor paid, finance charge accrued (links to VS-17.1), payable reclassified to funder, and bank settlement confirmed on due date (links to VS-18.2); resolve exceptions via W3383 | Treasury Manager / AP Manager | Treasury Manager | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- SCF platform; AP/3-way match (links to VS-15.1); GL finance-charge posting (links to VS-17.1); bank settlement (links to VS-18.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reconciliation-break risk**: SCF-platform vs AP-sub-ledger mismatch causes duplicate/missed payment; mitigated by daily reconciliation and exception workflow (W3383)
- **Discount-yield risk**: Inadvertent early-payment on near-due invoices incurs avoidable finance cost; mitigated by auto-offer thresholds and analytics (W3399)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-reconcile of "reconciled to AP sub-ledger" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "records the assignment and updates the payable beneficiary" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-106 (ensure SCF settlement & covenant compliance.)
operational: daily reconciliation and exception workflow (W3383); operational: auto-offer thresholds and analytics (W3399)

### Time Estimate
- Daily

## W3381. Extended-Terms / Payable-Restructuring Negotiation & Vendor Agreement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Working-capital initiative; vendor contract renewal; strategic vendor review |
| **Frequency** | Periodic |
| **Volume** | Top ~100–200 vendors; ~20–40 term changes/year |
| **Owner** | Procurement Director |
| **Participants** | Procurement, Treasury Manager, Category Managers (links to VS-01), Vendor (links to VS-03.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/Procurement identify term-extension candidates from working-capital analytics (links to W3393): high-spend, stable, low-risk vendors where extended terms (e.g., Net 30→60) plus SCF access improves BuildRight DPO without harming the vendor | Treasury Manager / Procurement | Procurement Director | Quarterly |
| 2 | Category Managers/Procurement negotiate extended terms with the vendor, bundled with SCF enrollment (W3379) and/or volume commitment (links to VS-39 rebates); price-for-terms analysis ensures the term gain is not offset by a price increase; update the contract (links to VS-03.3) and payment-terms master (links to W295) | Category Managers / Procurement | Procurement Director | Per vendor |
| 3 | Treasury/AP update system payment terms, communicate to AP (links to VS-15) and the SCF platform, and re-baseline the cash forecast (links to VS-18.1) and DPO target (W3393); monitor vendor behavior for adverse reaction | Treasury Manager / AP Manager | Treasury Manager | Per change |

### System Touchpoints
- Payment-terms master (links to W295); vendor contract (links to VS-03.3); AP (links to VS-15); cash forecast (links to VS-18.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Price-offset risk**: Vendors raise price to compensate for longer terms, eroding margin (links to VS-101.3); mitigated by price-for-terms analytics
- **Vendor-liquidity-harm risk**: Forcing terms on a fragile vendor causes distress/stockout; mitigated by SCF access (W3379) and vendor-health screening


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-identify of "identify term-extension candidates from working-capital analytics" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-update of "update the contract" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "monitor vendor behavior for adverse reaction" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-544 (ensure controlled execution — Supplier Finance & Reverse-Factoring Program Management (PA-105.1).)
operational: price-for-terms analytics; operational: SCF access (W3379) and vendor-health screening

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 weeks per negotiation wave

## W3382. SCF Facility Limit, Utilization & Covenant Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily utilization; monthly covenant test; facility renewal |
| **Frequency** | Daily utilization + monthly covenant |
| **Volume** | 1–3 facilities; total envelope ~PHP 2–5B |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury Manager, CFO, Funder, FP&A (links to VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury Manager monitor daily facility utilization vs limit: funded payables outstanding, available headroom, vendor enrollment velocity, and concentration by vendor/funder/segment; alert on threshold breach | Treasury Manager | Treasury Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Treasury Manager perform monthly covenant test: eligible-payable coverage, days-payable-outstanding, financial covenants (leverage, liquidity), and reporting certifications to the funder; reconcile to GL and AP (links to VS-17.1/VS-15) | Treasury Manager / Finance | CFO | Monthly |
| 3 | Treasury Manager/CFO review utilization trends, forecast facility needs vs cash position (links to VS-18.1), and decide on limit increase, funder addition, or term change; document for audit (links to VS-21) and board reporting (links to VS-36) | Treasury Manager / CFO | CFO / Board | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury management / SCF platform; AP sub-ledger (links to VS-15); GL (links to VS-17.1); cash forecast (links to VS-18.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Covenant-breach risk**: Breach triggers acceleration/default; mitigated by monthly testing and headroom buffer
- **Capacity-exhaustion risk**: Facility fully utilized blocks vendor early-payment and erodes program value; mitigated by utilization monitoring and proactive limit management


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitor daily facility utilization vs limit" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile to GL and AP" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document for audit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-106 (ensure SCF settlement & covenant compliance.)
operational: monthly testing and headroom buffer; operational: utilization monitoring and proactive limit management

### Time Estimate
- Daily + monthly

## W3383. SCF Vendor Dispute, Pricing Discrepancy & Off-Platform Exception Handling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor/funder dispute; pricing discrepancy; off-platform invoice; reconciliation break |
| **Frequency** | Exception-driven |
| **Volume** | ~20–50 exceptions/month |
| **Owner** | AP Manager |
| **Participants** | AP Manager, Treasury Manager, Vendor, Funder, Procurement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AP Manager receive and log the exception (discount/dispute, mis-routed payment, duplicate funding, off-platform invoice, reconciliation break); classify root cause and assign owner with SLA | AP Manager | AP Manager | Per exception |
| 2 | AP/Treasury/Procurement investigate: validate payable approval (links to VS-15.1), discount curve application, vendor enrollment status, and bank settlement (links to VS-18.2); engage funder/vendor as needed; propose resolution (re-process, credit, reversal) | AP Manager / Treasury Manager | Treasury Manager | Per exception |
| 3 | AP Manager execute the resolution, reconcile AP/SCF platform/GL, communicate outcome to vendor/funder, and feed systemic issues to program governance (W3384) and continuous improvement | AP Manager | AP Manager | Per exception |

### System Touchpoints
- SCF platform; AP (links to VS-15.1); GL (links to VS-17.1); supplier portal (links to VS-03.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Repeat-exception risk**: Systemic integration/config defects cause recurring breaks; mitigated by root-cause feed to governance (W3384)
- **Vendor-trust risk**: Unresolved disputes erode vendor confidence in SCF; mitigated by SLA and clear escalation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "log the exception" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validate payable approval" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile AP/SCF platform/GL, communicate outcome to vendor/funder, and feed…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-544 (ensure controlled execution — Supplier Finance & Reverse-Factoring Program Management (PA-105.1).)
operational: root-cause feed to governance (W3384); operational: SLA and clear escalation

### Time Estimate
- Per exception; 1–5 days typical

## W3384. SCF Program Risk, Concentration & Counterparty Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly program governance review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Full SCF program |
| **Owner** | CFO |
| **Participants** | CFO, Treasury Manager, Procurement Director, Risk (links to VS-21.2), Internal Audit (links to VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/Risk compile the SCF program risk view: funder concentration, vendor concentration (top-10 funded), segment exposure (import/local), recourse/true-sale status, covenant headroom (W3382), and vendor-credit/delinquency trends (links to VS-68 methodology) | Treasury Manager / Risk | CFO | Monthly |
| 2 | Treasury/Procurement review program performance vs targets (DPO gain, finance-cost yield, vendor adoption, exception rate), assess vendor-health signals and macro/import risk (links to VS-02.3/VS-87), and decide program adjustments (pricing, eligibility, limit) | Treasury Manager / Procurement | CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | CFO report SCF program status, risk, and working-capital outcome to executive committee (links to VS-33.2) and Internal Audit (links to VS-21); ensure SOX-equivalent controls, segregation of duties, and audit-trail integrity | CFO / Treasury Manager | CEO / Board | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- SCF platform analytics; treasury/risk dashboards (links to VS-28.1); audit (links to VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Counterparty-failure risk**: Funder withdrawal or vendor-cluster default disrupts the program; mitigated by multi-funder design and concentration limits
- **Control-weakness risk**: Weak SoD/audit trail enables fraud; mitigated by internal-audit review (links to VS-21) and continuous control monitoring


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logy" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review program performance vs targets" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "report SCF program status, risk, and working-capital outcome to executive…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-106 (ensure SCF settlement & covenant compliance.)
operational: multi-funder design and concentration limits; operational: internal-audit review (links to VS-21) and continuous control monitoring

### Time Estimate
- Monthly + quarterly
