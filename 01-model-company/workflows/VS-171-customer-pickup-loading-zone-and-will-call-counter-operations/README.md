# VS-171: Customer Pickup, Loading Zone & Will-Call Counter Operations

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Customer pickup, loading-zone and will-call counter operations workflows for BuildRight Depot
Corp. — owning the **bulky-goods customer self-pickup and loading experience** across 200 big-box
stores: the will-call counter and order-staging program, the loading bay/zone and appointment
scheduling, the **forklift/loading of lumber, tile, cement and appliances into the customer's own
or rented vehicle** (links VS-162 self-haul rental), carry-out and curbside/drive-up, vehicle
coordination and load securement, pickup exceptions and no-shows, and the throughput/safety/CX
analytics.

This matters at BuildRight's scale and risk profile. ~55% of revenue is B2C walk-in and the
big-box format sells bulky, jobsite-bound merchandise (lumber 14%, tiles 12%, appliances 5%,
furniture 5%, cement/bulk) that the customer takes home the same day. Customer self-pickup of
bulky goods is therefore a primary fulfillment channel — but it places **forklifts and reach
equipment in shared customer-vehicle areas**, requires staging space and appointment throughput
at the loading bay, and is one of BuildRight's highest **customer-premises-liability** exposures
(load shift, forklift contact, falling cargo, vehicle/pedestrian conflict — links VS-147). It is
also a throughput and CX bottleneck: a queue at the loading bay or a missing order at the
will-call counter converts a completed sale into an abandoned basket, a complaint (VS-13), or a
return (VS-32). At ~2.8M monthly POS transactions with a meaningful bulky-pickup share across 200
stores, even small friction compounds.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a unified program**. The capability exists only as scattered
single workflows: **W1193** (Heavy & Bulky Material Customer Pickup Scheduling & Loading Bay
Priority Management) buried in **VS-07.3 Store Receiving & Replenishment** (a *replenishment*
PA, not a customer-experience one); **W773** (Store-Level Customer Hold & Will-Call Order
Management) in **VS-09.3 Customer Amenities**; and **W4805** (Customer Pickup Journey,
Notifications & Support Escalation) in **VS-164.2 Smart Locker**. No value stream owns the
**end-to-end customer bulky-pickup, loading-bay, and will-call counter operating model**. It is
**not** covered by **VS-10 (Ecommerce/BOPIS)**, which owns the small-parcel *BOPIS counter
transaction* — this owns the *bulky-goods loading-bay experience* that often follows or stands
apart from BOPIS. It is **not** covered by **VS-07 (Store Operations)**, which runs daily store
management and absorbed W1193 into replenishment — this owns the *customer-pickup program* as a
first-class discipline. It is **not** covered by **VS-162 (Self-Haul Vehicle Rental)**, which
*rents the vehicle* — this owns the *loading of goods into whatever vehicle the customer brings
(owned, rented via VS-162, or hired)*. It is **not** covered by **VS-06/VS-74/VS-143 (Delivery)**,
which delivers *to* the customer — this owns the customer *coming to collect*. And it is **not**
covered by **VS-147 (Customer Safety/Premises Liability)**, which owns the *liability program* —
this owns the *loading-zone operations* that must be safe.

This value stream owns the end-to-end program: program design & operating model; will-call counter
operations & order staging; pickup appointment/scheduling and loading-zone capacity; bulky-goods
forklift loading into customer vehicles, spotters and load securement; coordination with
self-haul rentals (VS-162) and customer-hired vehicles; carry-out and curbside/drive-up;
exceptions (no-show/expiry, partial/wrong load, vehicle-too-small); integration with BOPIS
(VS-10), special orders (VS-09/VS-11), ecommerce (VS-10) and the order-management system;
safety/DOLE-OSH forklift-in-customer-area and premises-liability controls (links VS-24/VS-147);
CX and the pickup SLA; throughput/analytics; and continuous improvement. It integrates tightly
with VS-07 (store ops), VS-10 (BOPIS/ecommerce), VS-162 (self-haul rental), VS-143 (bulky
delivery), VS-147 (premises safety), VS-24 (HSE/forklift), VS-09/VS-11 (special/B2B orders),
VS-13 (CX/complaints), VS-32 (returns), VS-115 (calibration of scales), and VS-23 (LP).
