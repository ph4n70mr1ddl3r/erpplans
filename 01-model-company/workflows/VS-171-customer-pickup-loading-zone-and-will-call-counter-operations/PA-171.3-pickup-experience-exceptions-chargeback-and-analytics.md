## W4977. Pickup SLA, Wait-Time & Customer Experience Measurement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous CX monitoring; periodic review |
| **Frequency** | Real-time + monthly review |
| **Volume** | ~pickup transactions across 200 stores measured continuously |
| **Owner** | CX (VS-13) / Customer Pickup Program Manager / Store Ops |
| **Participants** | CX (VS-13), Store Ops (VS-07), Ecommerce (VS-10), Data (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define & measure the pickup SLA: order-ready time (from W4965), wait time at counter/bay (W4962/W4966), load time (W4970), and the end-to-end pickup duration; measure against the target and by store/region | CX / Analytics / Store Ops | Program Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Capture customer experience: post-pickup feedback (CSAT/NPS links VS-13 W65), friction signals (queue, not-ready, damage), and the pickup-journey VOC; integrate with VS-13 CX analytics | CX / Analytics | Program Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Review & act: monthly SLA/CX review by store/region, root-cause breaches (staffing, capacity, process), and feed continuous improvement (W4981) and the store-comms/task system (VS-63) | CX / Store Ops / Program Manager | VP Store Ops | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- CX/VOC (VS-13 W65); order mgmt timestamps (VS-10/VS-16); analytics (VS-28); store-comms (VS-63)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **SLA-breach risk**: Slow ready/wait/load → CX failure/abandonment; mitigated by SLA measurement and root-cause action
- **Feedback-blindness risk**: No CX capture → unmeasured friction; mitigated by post-pickup VOC
- **Variance risk**: Poor-performing stores unidentified; mitigated by store/region breakdown

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous (automated); ~monthly review 1–2 days

### Automation Opportunity
- SLA auto-measure from timestamps; CX feedback prompt; friction-signal alerts; store/region dashboards

### Controls
- CTL-745 (ensure controlled execution — Pickup Experience, Exceptions, Chargeback & Analytics (PA-171.3).)
- CTL: SLA defined & measured; CX captured; breaches root-caused; store/region tracked

### Cross-references
- links to VS-13 (CX/W65), VS-10/VS-16 (order timestamps), VS-28 (analytics), VS-63 (store comms), W4962/W4965/W4966/W4970 (SLA inputs), W4981 (improvement)

## W4978. No-Show, Hold-Expiry, Restocking & Cancellation Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer no-show; hold period expiry; cancellation |
| **Frequency** | Continuous × 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~no-show/expiry events per store per week × 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Will-Call/Loading-Zone Supervisor / Store Ops |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), CX (VS-13), Inventory (VS-05), Ecommerce (VS-10), Finance (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Track holds & expiries: monitor the hold period per policy (e.g., BOPIS 5-day links VS-10), remind approaching-expiry customers (W4966), and identify no-shows | Supervisor / CX / Store Ops | Supervisor | Continuous |
| 2 | Manage expiry/cancellation: on expiry or cancellation, restock the staged goods (links VS-05, VS-32), reverse/release the reservation, process the refund/charge per policy (restocking fee where applicable), and update order status | Store Ops / Inventory / Finance / CX | Supervisor | Per event |
| 3 | Analyze & reduce no-shows: root-cause no-shows (notification, friction, customer), feed CX (VS-13), adjust reminder policy (W4966), and manage restocking-fee policy/fairness | CX / Analytics / Store Ops | Program Manager | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Order mgmt (VS-10/VS-16); inventory/restock (VS-05); returns (VS-32); finance/refund (VS-17/VS-16 W101); CX (VS-13); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stale-hold risk**: Goods held indefinitely → lost sale/staging congestion; mitigated by hold-expiry policy
- **Refund-error risk**: Refund/charge mis-processed (restocking fee, deposit); mitigated by the policy and finance reconciliation
- **Customer-dissatisfaction risk**: Restocking fee/expiry perceived as unfair → complaint (VS-13); mitigated by clear policy and reminders

### Time Estimate
- ~per-event 10–20 min (restock + process)

### Automation Opportunity
- Hold-expiry auto-flag; reminder automation; auto-restock/refund workflow; no-show analytics

### Controls
- CTL-97 (ensure will-call release integrity & loading safety.)
- CTL: hold period enforced; expiry processed; refund/charge per policy; restocking-fee documented

### Cross-references
- links to VS-10 (BOPIS hold), VS-05/VS-32 (restock), VS-16 (W101 refund), VS-17 (finance), VS-13 (CX), W4965 (staging), W4966 (reminders), W4977 (SLA)

| **Frequency** | Event-driven × 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~loading exceptions per store per week × 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Loading-Zone Supervisor / Store Ops / CX (VS-13) |
| **Participants** | Loaders, Store Ops (VS-07), CX (VS-13), Returns (VS-32), Claims (VS-90), Inventory (VS-05) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture & assess the exception: at the bay or on customer report, document damage/shortage/wrong-load (photo, order, items), assess cause (loading error, staging error W4965, goods defect), and decide resolution | Loaders / Supervisor / CX | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per event |
| 2 | Resolve: replace/refund the damaged/short/wrong item (links VS-32 returns, VS-90 damage claims), re-load the correct/complete order (W4970), issue service recovery per CX policy (VS-13), and reconcile inventory | Loaders / Returns / CX / Inventory | Supervisor | Per event |

### Time Estimate
- ~per-event 15–40 min

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls

### Cross-references

## W4980. Pickup-Channel Fraud, Identity & Release-Integrity Controls

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Suspicious pickup; identity/ownership mismatch; fraud alert |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven × 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~pickup-fraud attempts/at-risk events across 200 stores |
| **Owner** | LP (VS-23) / Loading-Zone Supervisor / Fraud (VS-125) |
| **Participants** | LP (VS-23), Counter Staff (W4962), Fraud (VS-125), CX (VS-13), Finance (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify identity & ownership at release: ID/order verification (W4962) to prevent wrong-party release, flagged-orders review (paid status, chargeback risk, fraud-flag links VS-125), and high-value release controls (VS-23) | Counter Staff / LP | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per pickup |
| 2 | Detect pickup-channel fraud: patterns (duplicate-pickup, identity fraud, collusion, refund-then-pickup, BOPIS-fraud), monitor via the fraud program (VS-125), and flag risky pickups for hold/verification | LP / Fraud / Analytics | LP Lead | Continuous |
| 3 | Investigate & recover: investigate confirmed fraud, recover goods/loss, escalate to law-enforcement/Legal (VS-100) where material, and feed the fraud program (VS-125) and analytics | LP / Fraud / Legal / Finance | LP Lead | Event-driven |

### Pain Points / Risks

- **BOPIS/pickup-fraud risk**: Fraudulent pickups/returns (VS-125); mitigated by fraud detection and holds
- **High-value-theft risk**: High-value goods pilfered at pickup (VS-23); mitigated by release controls

### Time Estimate
- ~per-event verification 2–5 min; ~investigation per event

### Automation Opportunity
- ID/order auto-verify; fraud-pattern detection; risk-flag hold; high-value release-auth gate

### Controls
- CTL-97 (ensure will-call release integrity & loading safety.)
- CTL: ID/order verified; flagged orders reviewed; fraud monitored (VS-125); high-value LP-controlled

### Cross-references
