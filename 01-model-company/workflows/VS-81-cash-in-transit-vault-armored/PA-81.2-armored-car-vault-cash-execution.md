## W2809. Armored Car Partner Onboarding, Vetting & Contract Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | RFP, contract renewal, or new region expansion |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~3–5 year contracts |
| **Volume** | 2–3 CIT partners (e.g., ARMSCOR, LBC, private armored fleets) |
| **Owner** | CIT Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | CIT Ops Mgr, Procurement, Security/LP, Legal, Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Security/LP vets CIT partner: PNP SOSIA license, BSP registration, insurance coverage (cash-in-transit), employee background checks, armored vehicle specifications | Security / LP | CIT Operations Manager | 2–4 weeks |
| 2 | Procurement leads RFP and commercial negotiation (per-pickup fee, route pricing, change-order fee, SLA); Legal reviews liability/indemnity clauses | Procurement / Legal | CIT Operations Manager | 4–8 weeks |
| 3 | Treasury/Payment Ops executes contract; configures partner in CIT operations system; pilots one region before nationwide rollout | Treasury / CIT Operations Manager | CFO | 4–6 weeks pilot |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor master; CIT operations system; contract repository

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Security risk**: Under-vetted partner creates robbery/fraud exposure; mitigated by SOSIA/BSP license validation and reference checks


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "checks, armored vehicle specifications" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews liability/indemnity clauses" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-473 (ensure controlled execution — Armored Car, Vault & Cash Logistics Execution (PA-81.2).)
- operational: SOSIA/BSP license validation and reference checks

### Time Estimate
- 8–12 weeks per partner onboarding

## W2810. Pickup Execution, Guard Verification & Handover Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Scheduled or ad-hoc pickup |
| **Frequency** | ~600 pickups/week |
| **Volume** | 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, CIT Guard, CIT Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store Manager verifies armored guard identity (ID, route manifest, photo match, pre-shared code) before opening; denies access on mismatch | Store Manager | Store Manager | 5–10 min |
| 2 | Store Manager and guard execute two-man handover; count/verify sealed-bag IDs against manifest; both sign custody transfer receipt | Store Manager / CIT Guard | Store Manager | 10–15 min |
| 3 | CIT Operations confirms pickup completion in system; store cash-office system updates custody status; LP flags any unconfirmed handover for investigation | CIT Operations / LP | CIT Operations Manager | Per pickup |

### System Touchpoints
- Guard verification app; custody-transfer manifest; LP exception monitor

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Security risk**: Impersonation robbery; mitigated by multi-factor guard verification (ID + photo + code)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-route of "route manifest, photo match, pre-shared" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "verify sealed-bag IDs against manifest" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-update of "CIT Operations confirms pickup completion in system" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-183 (ensure CIT custody-chain & cash-in-transit integrity.)
- operational: multi-factor guard verification (ID + photo + code)

### Time Estimate
- 15–25 min per pickup

## W2812. DC/Regional Vault Operations, Counting & Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Cash received at regional/DC vault |
| **Frequency** | Daily per vault |
| **Volume** | ~4 regional vaults |
| **Owner** | Vault Operations Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Vault Ops, CIT Operations, Finance, LP |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vault Ops receives sealed deposit bags under dual-control; logs custody transfer; schedules counting by denomination | Vault Ops | Vault Operations Supervisor | Daily |
| 2 | Vault Ops counts via currency counter with counterfeit detection; reconciles to store-reported amount; investigates variances > PHP 1,000 | Vault Ops | Vault Operations Supervisor | 2–4 hours/day |
| 3 | Finance posts vault cash to GL; prepares bank deposit; LP reviews variance trend by store for shrink indicators | Finance / LP | Finance Controller | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Vault management module; currency counters; GL posting; variance analytics

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Loss risk**: Counting errors and shrink; mitigated by dual-control and currency counters


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs custody transfer" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "counts via currency counter with counterfeit detection" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "reviews variance trend by store for shrink indicators" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-183 (ensure CIT custody-chain & cash-in-transit integrity.)
- operational: dual-control and currency counters

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 hours per vault per day

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Weekly review |
| **Volume** | 200 stores; PHP 20K float each |
| **Owner** | CIT Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | CIT Operations, Store Manager, Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CIT Operations monitors cash surplus/deficit by store; identifies stores with chronic coin shortage vs. note surplus | CIT Operations | CIT Operations Manager | Weekly |
| 2 | CIT Operations redistributes cash between stores via armored car (cross-store transfer) instead of bank withdrawal/deposit; reduces cost | CIT Operations | CIT Operations Manager | Weekly |

### Controls
