## Overview

Cash-in-Transit (CIT), Vault & Armored Car Operations workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
covering the secure movement, counting, and banking of cash across 200 stores and 4 DCs. With
cash representing ~42% of ~2.8M monthly POS transactions (~1.2M cash transactions/month) and
~PHP 20K petty-cash floats per location, this is a high-volume, high-risk logistics stream.

While treasury (VS-18) owns cash positioning and bank-account management, and POS (VS-08)
handles tender capture and the cash office (W541), this value stream owns the **physical cash
logistics program**: smart-safe and pickup planning, armored car partner management, DC/region
vault operations, CIT insurance and risk, and cash analytics.
