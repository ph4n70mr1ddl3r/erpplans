## W2817. CIT Insurance Program, Coverage Limits & Claims Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual renewal; loss event |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | Network-wide cash-in-transit + cash-on-premises coverage |
| **Owner** | Risk & Insurance Manager |
| **Participants** | Risk Mgr, Treasury, LP, Insurance Broker, CIT Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk Manager structures CIT and cash-on-premises insurance: per-occurrence limits, aggregate, deductible; coordinates with CIT vendor's own coverage to avoid gaps/overlap | Risk Manager | CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Treasury/Risk validates store vault holding limits align with insurance sub-limits; flags over-limit stores for pickup frequency adjustment | Treasury / Risk Manager | Risk Manager | Quarterly |
| 3 | On loss event, LP/Risk files claim with evidence; pursues CIT vendor recovery where liable; tracks claim outcome and recovered amount | LP / Risk Manager | CFO | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance policy repository; holding-limit master; claims tracker

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Coverage risk**: Under-insured vault or uninsured transit gap; mitigated by annual coverage review and sub-limit alignment


### Automation Opportunity
- System workflow orchestration of "coordinates with CIT vendor's own coverage to avoid gaps/overlap" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validates store vault holding limits align with insurance sub-limits" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-file of "files claim with evidence" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-474 (ensure controlled execution — CIT Risk, Insurance & Cash Analytics (PA-81.3).)
- operational: annual coverage review and sub-limit alignment

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks per annual renewal; per-event variable
