## W142. Social Media & Influencer Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly content calendar; or influencer partnership agreement |
| **Frequency** | Daily posts; monthly influencer activations |
| **Volume** | Managing ~5–7 social channels (Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Viber) |
| **Owner** | Social Media Manager |
| **Participants** | Content Specialists, Influencers, Customer Service (for moderation), CMO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Content Calendar**: Develop 4-week rolling calendar aligned with W83 campaigns and W13 promos | Social Media Mgr | CMO | 4 hours/month |
| 2 | **Content Creation**: Produce short-form videos (TikTok/Reels), product feature posts, and DIY tips | Content Specialist | Social Media Mgr | Daily |
| 3 | **Influencer Sourcing**: Identify DIY/Home-Improvement influencers; vet for audience fit and engagement rate | Social Media Mgr | CMO | 4 hours/month |
| 4 | **Contracting**: Sign partnership agreements (W62); define deliverables (e.g., 2 videos, 3 stories) | Legal / Social Mgr | CMO | 2 hours/influencer |
| 5 | **Campaign Execution**: Influencer posts content; system tracks referral traffic and promo code usage | Influencer / Social Mgr | — | Per campaign |
| 6 | **Community Management**: Respond to comments/DMs; escalate complaints to W41; moderate "trolls" | CS Team / Social Mgr | — | Ongoing |
| 7 | **Performance Review**: Monthly report on reach, engagement, sentiment, and conversion ROI | Social Media Mgr | CMO | 2 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Social media management platform (e.g., Meta Business Suite, Hootsuite) for scheduling, publishing, and analytics across Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Viber
- Influencer relationship management (IRM) module for sourcing, contracting, and performance tracking
- Content creation tools (Canva, Adobe Creative Suite) for short-form video and graphic production
- Social listening and sentiment analysis tool for brand monitoring and crisis early warning
- Referral and promo code tracking system linked to POS and ecommerce for conversion attribution
- Integration with W83 (campaign planning — social media content calendar alignment), W134 (crisis communication — social media response), W41 (complaints — social media complaint escalation), W62 (vendor contracts — influencer agreements)

### Time Estimate
- Monthly content calendar development: 4 hours/month (step 1)
- Daily content creation: 1–2 hours/day for Content Specialist (step 2)
- Influencer sourcing and vetting: 4 hours/month (step 3)
- Influencer contracting: 2 hours/influencer (step 4)
- Campaign execution coordination: varies per campaign (step 5)
- Community management and moderation: ongoing, ~1–2 hours/day (step 6)
- Monthly performance review: 2 hours/month (step 7)
- Total: Social Media Manager ~15–20 hours/week; Content Specialists contribute daily

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Influencer brand safety risk**: An influencer's personal conduct or off-brand content (outside the BuildRight partnership) can reflect negatively on the brand; the Philippines' DIY/home improvement influencer space is small, limiting alternative options if a partnership goes wrong
- **Content velocity pressure**: Managing 5–7 social channels with daily posting requires a continuous content pipeline; creative fatigue and content quality erosion are risks, especially during peak campaign periods when the same team supports W83 campaign execution
- **Negative sentiment amplification**: A single viral complaint (e.g., pricing error, safety issue) on Facebook or TikTok can generate thousands of negative comments within hours; the community management team (step 6) must respond quickly while coordinating with Legal and W134 crisis protocols
- **Conversion attribution difficulty**: Tracking social media engagement (likes, shares, views) to actual in-store or online purchases is technically challenging; without reliable conversion data, the CMO cannot justify social media spend to the CFO with concrete ROI

### Staffing Implication
- **Social Media Manager**: ~15–20 hours/week for content calendar, influencer management, community management, and performance reporting. This is a dedicated role. Absorbed.
- **Content Specialists**: ~1–2 hours/day for daily content creation. Absorbed within existing W50 content team.
- **CS Team**: ~1–2 hours/day for social media comment moderation and complaint escalation. Absorbed within W41 customer service duties.
- **No incremental headcount.**

## W676. Digital Marketing Campaign Operations & Cross-Channel Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Campaign launch date; ongoing campaign optimization cycle |
| **Frequency** | Daily monitoring during active campaigns; weekly performance review; monthly cross-channel analysis |
| **Volume** | 6 major campaigns/year + 12 monthly hot deals + always-on digital marketing across Facebook, Google, YouTube, TikTok, email, SMS, app push |
| **Owner** | Digital Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Social Media Specialist, Content Creator, Media Buyer, CX Analytics Lead, Ecommerce Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Digital Marketing Manager activates campaign per approved plan from W83: configures campaign in marketing automation platform with audience segment per W673, creative assets from W190, channel mix (social media, email, SMS, app push, Google Ads), budget allocation, and flight dates | Digital Marketing Manager | Marketing Manager | 1 day |
| 2 | Media Buyer launches paid media: Facebook/Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads (Search + Display + Shopping), YouTube pre-roll, TikTok ads; sets targeting parameters, bid strategy, and daily budget caps; implements UTM tracking for attribution per W565 | Media Buyer | Digital Marketing Manager | 1 day |
| 3 | Social Media Specialist publishes organic content: Facebook posts, Instagram feed/stories/reels, TikTok content per W142; coordinates with influencer content calendar | Social Media Specialist | Digital Marketing Manager | 3 hours |
| 4 | Daily during campaign: Digital Marketing Manager monitors real-time performance dashboard: spend pacing, impression delivery, click-through rate, conversion rate, ROAS (return on ad spend), cost per acquisition; flags underperforming channels for optimization | Digital Marketing Manager | Marketing Manager | 1 hour/day |
| 5 | Social Media Specialist monitors brand mentions, responds to comments/messages, escalates negative sentiment per W142 crisis protocol | Social Media Specialist | Digital Marketing Manager | 2 hours/day |
| 6 | Digital Marketing Manager performs mid-campaign optimization: reallocates budget from underperforming to overperforming channels, adjusts creative rotation, tests new audience segments, modifies bid strategies | Digital Marketing Manager | Marketing Manager | 2 hours |
| 7 | CX Analytics Lead provides segment performance data: which customer segments are responding, attribution across channels, incremental revenue per W565 | CX Analytics Lead | Digital Marketing Manager | 3 hours |
| 8 | Weekly: Digital Marketing Manager produces campaign performance report for Marketing Manager: channel-level metrics, creative performance, audience insights, spend vs. budget, and optimization recommendations | Digital Marketing Manager | Marketing Manager | 3 hours |
| 9 | Post-campaign: Digital Marketing Manager compiles comprehensive performance report feeding into W565 ROI analysis; documents learnings for future campaign planning | Digital Marketing Manager | Marketing Manager | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Marketing automation platform (campaign setup, audience segmentation, email/SMS/push orchestration)
- Social media management tool (content scheduling, community management, social listening)
- Ad platforms: Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads (Search/Display/Shopping/YouTube), TikTok Ads Manager
- UTM/attribution module (cross-channel attribution per W565)
- Customer Data Platform (W673 audience segments)
- BI dashboard (real-time campaign performance, ROAS, CPA)

### Time Estimate
- Campaign setup: 2-3 days
- Daily monitoring: 1 hour/day during active campaigns
- Weekly reporting: 3 hours
- Post-campaign analysis: 1 day

### Pain Points / Risks
- Philippine digital advertising market fragmentation: Facebook dominates (~85% of social media ad spend) but TikTok growing rapidly, requiring split expertise and budget
- Ad fatigue from competing retailers during major sale events (3.3, 6.6, 9.9, 11.11, 12.12) driving up CPMs by 30-50%
- Attribution complexity across offline POS purchases and online touchpoints; last-click model undervalues upper-funnel channels
- Creative production bottleneck: multi-channel campaigns require 20-30 creative variants per campaign (different formats, sizes, languages)
- Philippine consumer preference for vernacular (Tagalog/Taglish) content adding creative complexity

### Staffing Implication
- Digital Marketing Manager: ~50% of time during active campaigns; manages 2-3 concurrent campaigns
- Social Media Specialist: full-time during campaigns; community management and content publishing
- Media Buyer: ~30% of time split across active campaigns; remainder on always-on optimization
- CX Analytics Lead: ~10% of time on campaign analytics support
- All roles absorbed within existing headcount; no incremental staffing required

## W677. Marketing Budget Management & Spend Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly budget review cycle; campaign spend reporting |
| **Frequency** | Monthly budget review; weekly spend tracking during campaigns; annual budget planning |
| **Volume** | Annual marketing budget ~PHP 300-500M (~0.5-0.8% of revenue); 15-20 active budget line items |
| **Owner** | Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Digital Marketing Manager, Finance Analyst, VP Marketing |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Analyst allocates annual marketing budget per W26 into monthly and campaign-level envelopes: media spend, creative production, agency fees, events, sponsorships, loyalty program costs, research | Finance Analyst | Marketing Manager | 2 days |
| 2 | Marketing Manager distributes budget to campaign owners per approved annual plan; sets spend thresholds requiring VP Marketing approval (agency POs > PHP 500K, media buys > PHP 1M) | Marketing Manager | VP Marketing | 1 day |
| 3 | System tracks actual spend against budget in real-time: media platform invoices, agency fees, production costs, event expenses, sponsorship payments; auto-flags line items exceeding 80% of monthly allocation | System (automated) | Marketing Manager | Ongoing |
| 4 | Monthly: Marketing Manager reviews budget utilization report with Finance Analyst: total spend vs. budget by line item, spend velocity (projected month-end over/under), variance explanation for material deviations | Marketing Manager | VP Marketing | 3 hours |
| 5 | Finance Analyst reconciles marketing spend to GL accounts: verifies agency invoices against deliverables, matches media invoices to platform reports, allocates shared costs (creative, research) across campaigns | Finance Analyst | Marketing Manager | 1 day |
| 6 | Marketing Manager produces monthly marketing spend dashboard for VP Marketing: spend by channel, cost per acquisition, cost per reach, budget utilization rate, and projected quarter-end spend | Marketing Manager | VP Marketing | 4 hours |
| 7 | Quarterly: VP Marketing reviews marketing ROI per W565 and budget utilization; approves budget reallocation between channels based on performance data; adjusts Q-spend plans | VP Marketing | CFO | 2 hours |
| 8 | Annual: Marketing Manager develops next-year budget proposal per W26 informed by current-year ROI data and strategic priorities per W127 | Marketing Manager | VP Marketing | 5 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Marketing budget module (budget allocation, spend tracking, threshold alerts)
- GL accounts (marketing expense accounts, cost center allocations)
- AP invoice matching (agency invoices, media invoices, production POs)
- Media platform cost reports (Meta, Google, TikTok spend exports)
- BI spend analytics dashboard (channel-level cost efficiency, budget utilization)

### Time Estimate
- Monthly budget review and reconciliation: 1 day
- Weekly spend tracking during campaigns: 1 hour
- Annual budget planning: 5 days
- Quarterly review: 2 hours

### Pain Points / Risks
- Agency and media invoice processing lag (30-60 days) distorting real-time spend tracking and causing month-end accrual estimation challenges
- Campaign cost allocation subjectivity: shared creative assets, research studies, and agency retainers difficult to attribute to individual campaigns
- Philippine media market requiring upfront deposits for print, OOH, and broadcast commitments (PHP 5-20M per commitment), locking budget flexibility
- Budget rigidity preventing reallocation during viral opportunities or competitive response situations; approval latency of 2-3 days for reallocation requests
- Foreign currency exposure on digital platform spend (USD-denominated ad accounts)

### Staffing Implication
- Marketing Manager: ~1 day/month on budget management; owns allocation decisions and VP reporting
- Finance Analyst: ~1 day/month on marketing GL reconciliation and accruals
- Digital Marketing Manager: ~2 hours/month providing spend data and variance explanations
- All absorbed within existing roles; no incremental staffing required

## W736. Marketing Data Platform Daily Operations & Campaign Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Start of marketing operations day; or campaign performance alert |
| **Frequency** | Daily review; real-time campaign monitoring |
| **Volume** | ~5-10 active marketing campaigns at any time; ~1M+ customer touchpoints/day (email, SMS, push, social) |
| **Owner** | Marketing Analytics Manager |
| **Participants** | Campaign Manager, Digital Marketing Specialist, Customer Experience Manager, CMO |

### Background

W83 covers campaign planning and execution. W565 covers campaign ROI and attribution analysis. This workflow addresses the daily operational management of the marketing data platform (MDP) — the analytics infrastructure that powers campaign measurement, audience segmentation, and channel optimization. For a company with 600,000+ loyalty members processing 2.8M monthly POS transactions, daily marketing analytics operations ensure that campaigns are performing as expected and data-driven optimizations are applied in real-time.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Morning campaign performance dashboard review**: Marketing Analytics Manager reviews: (a) active campaign performance: (i) email campaigns: open rate, click-through rate, conversion rate, unsubscribe rate; (ii) SMS campaigns: delivery rate, click rate, opt-out rate; (iii) push notifications: delivery rate, open rate, app engagement; (iv) social media ads per W142: impressions, CTR, cost-per-click, ROAS; (v) in-store promotional lift per W13: promoted SKU sales vs. baseline; (b) campaign vs. target: each campaign's KPI vs. its planned target per W83; (c) alert on underperforming campaigns: any campaign below 70% of target KPI flagged for review | Marketing Analytics Mgr | CMO | 20-30 min/day |
| 2 | **Real-time optimization**: for underperforming campaigns: (a) Marketing Analytics Manager diagnoses: (i) creative issue: low engagement despite good reach → test new creative per W190; (ii) audience issue: good engagement but low conversion → refine targeting per W673 segmentation; (iii) offer issue: good engagement but no purchase → adjust offer value or terms; (iv) channel issue: campaign performing well on one channel but not others → reallocate budget; (b) implement optimizations: (i) A/B test new creative or copy; (ii) adjust audience targeting rules; (iii) modify offer parameters; (iv) reallocate budget from underperforming to overperforming channels | Marketing Analytics Mgr | Campaign Manager | 30-60 min/day |
| 3 | **Data pipeline health check**: verify marketing data platform pipelines: (a) POS transaction data feed: last successful sync, data freshness (<24 hours); (b) ecommerce behavioral data: website clicks, cart activity, search queries flowing into MDP; (c) loyalty transaction feed: points earning/redemption events updating customer profiles; (d) third-party data feeds: social media platform metrics, email platform metrics, ad platform metrics; (e) any pipeline failures: escalate to IT per W733 API monitoring | Marketing Analytics Mgr / IT | IT Manager | 10-15 min/day |
| 4 | **Weekly campaign analytics report**: Marketing Analytics Manager compiles weekly report: (a) campaign-level performance summary with KPI tracking; (b) channel performance comparison: email vs. SMS vs. push vs. social vs. in-store; (c) audience segment performance: which segments responded best; (d) attribution analysis: last-click, first-click, and multi-touch attribution for major campaigns; (e) cost analysis: cost per acquisition, cost per conversion, ROAS by campaign and channel; (f) recommendations for next week's campaign adjustments | Marketing Analytics Mgr | CMO | 2-3 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints

- Marketing data platform (CDP/DMP) for audience data and segmentation per W156
- Campaign management platform for multi-channel execution per W676
- POS transaction data per W533 for promotional lift measurement
- Ecommerce analytics for online campaign attribution
- Loyalty engine per CRM-001 for customer engagement tracking
- Social media management per W142 for social campaign metrics
- W677 marketing budget management for spend tracking
- W565 ROI and attribution analysis for cross-campaign comparison

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Attribution complexity**: customers interact with multiple campaigns and channels before purchasing; accurately attributing the sale to the most influential touchpoint is analytically challenging
- **Data pipeline latency**: marketing analytics depend on timely data from POS, ecommerce, and loyalty systems; pipeline delays reduce the ability to optimize campaigns in real-time
- **Privacy constraints**: RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act) limits the types of customer data that can be used for marketing targeting, requiring careful consent management per W405
- **Channel fragmentation**: tracking customer journeys across POS, ecommerce, mobile app, email, SMS, and social media requires a unified customer identifier that is not always available

### Staffing Implication

Marketing Analytics Manager: ~1-2 hours/day on monitoring and optimization + 2-3 hours/week on reporting. Campaign Manager: collaborates on optimization. No incremental headcount.

### Time Estimate

**Daily operations**: 60-90 min. **Weekly reporting**: 2-3 hours.
