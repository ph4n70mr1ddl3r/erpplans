# VS-14: Marketing & Communications

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Marketing & communications: campaign planning & execution, digital marketing & social, and brand/PR/corporate comms.

## Why it matters

Seasonal campaigns (ber-months, payday, construction season), digital acquisition, and brand/PR drive footfall and ecommerce conversion; DTI sales-promotion permits and ad-spend governance apply.
