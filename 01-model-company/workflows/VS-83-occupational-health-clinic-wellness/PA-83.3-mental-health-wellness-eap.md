## W2865. Employee Assistance Program (EAP) Provision & Confidential Counseling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee self-referral or manager referral |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~2–5% utilization/year |
| **Volume** | ~6,757 employees + dependents |
| **Owner** | EAP Manager |
| **Participants** | EAP Mgr, EAP Provider, HR, OH Nurse |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EAP Manager contracts accredited EAP provider (counseling hotline, in-person/tele-counseling sessions, financial/legal advisory); structures confidentiality | EAP Manager | CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | Employee self-refers (or manager refers with consent) for personal/work stress, family, financial, grief, conflict; accesses sessions confidentially | Employee / Manager | EAP Manager | Per case |
| 3 | EAP Provider reports aggregate utilization and trend themes (no individual detail) to EAP Manager; recommends workplace interventions | EAP Provider / EAP Manager | CHRO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- EAP provider portal; confidential case tracking (anonymized); utilization analytics

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stigma risk**: Low utilization due to fear; mitigated by confidentiality assurance and awareness campaigns

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; per-session variable

## W2867. Critical Incident Stress Debriefing & Crisis Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Robbery, fatality, disaster, violence at workplace |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; rare |
| **Volume** | ~5–15 critical incidents/year |
| **Owner** | EAP Manager |
| **Participants** | EAP Mgr, EAP Provider, OH Nurse, HR, Store/DC Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store/DC Manager triggers CISD request following critical incident; EAP Manager mobilizes accredited counselor within 24–72 hours | Store/DC Manager / EAP Manager | EAP Manager | Immediate |
| 2 | EAP Provider conducts group debrief and individual follow-up for affected employees; assesses PTSD risk; refers for ongoing care | EAP Provider | EAP Manager | Per event |
| 3 | OH Nurse/HR monitors affected employees' recovery; arranges work accommodation; tracks return-to-stability | OH Nurse / HR | EAP Manager | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- CISD activation protocol; counselor network; recovery monitoring

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Trauma risk**: Untreated trauma causes long-term harm and attrition; mitigated by rapid CISD activation

### Time Estimate
- 4–8 hours per event + follow-up

## W2868. Substance Abuse Policy, Testing & Rehabilitation Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Pre-employment, random, or for-cause testing |
| **Frequency** | Annual random + event-driven |
| **Volume** | Safety-sensitive roles (drivers, equipment operators) |
| **Owner** | OH Nurse Manager |
| **Participants** | OH Nurse, HR, Legal, DOH-accredited Lab |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR/OH Nurse administers drug testing per company policy (pre-employment, random for safety-sensitive, for-cause); uses DOH-accredited lab | HR / OH Nurse | OH Nurse Manager | Per test |
| 2 | OH Nurse/HR handles positive result per due process: confirmatory test, hearing, disciplinary or rehabilitation pathway | OH Nurse / HR / Legal | OH Nurse Manager | Per case |
| 3 | EAP Manager offers rehabilitation support for confirmed cases; manages return-to-work after treatment | EAP Manager | CHRO | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Drug-test workflow; case management; rehabilitation tracker

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Legal risk**: Mishandled positive result invites DOLE/labor case; mitigated by due process and legal review

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 hours per case

## W2869. Lifestyle Disease (Diabetes/Hypertension) Management & Screening

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual APE finding or wellness campaign |
| **Frequency** | Annual screening + ongoing management |
| **Volume** | ~6,757 employees |
| **Owner** | OH Nurse |
| **Participants** | OH Nurse, Company Physician, HMO, Employee |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OH Nurse screens for lifestyle risks at APE (BP, blood sugar, BMI); identifies at-risk employees; offers confidential counseling | OH Nurse | OH Nurse Manager | Annual |
| 2 | Company Physician refers confirmed cases to disease management program (HMO or partner); coordinates medication and lifestyle plan | Company Physician | OH Nurse Manager | Per case |
| 3 | OH Nurse tracks aggregate prevalence and program participation (no individual disclosure); recommends workplace interventions | OH Nurse | OH Nurse Manager | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- APE results; disease-management program referral; aggregate analytics

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Health risk**: Untreated lifestyle disease causes absenteeism and crisis; mitigated by screening and management program

### Time Estimate
- 30–60 min per case

## W2871. Maternal & Reproductive Health Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee pregnancy notification |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~150–250 pregnancies/year |
| **Volume** | All female employees of reproductive age |
| **Owner** | OH Nurse |
| **Participants** | OH Nurse, HR, Company Physician, SSS |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employee notifies pregnancy; OH Nurse conducts occupational risk assessment; adjusts duties to protect maternal health (no heavy lifting, hazmat) | OH Nurse | OH Nurse Manager | Per case |
| 2 | HR administers maternity benefit (105-day Expanded Maternity Leave per RA 11210); coordinates SSS maternity reimbursement | HR | HR Manager | Per case |
| 3 | OH Nurse supports return-to-work post-maternity; provides lactation room and breaks per law; monitors maternal health | OH Nurse | OH Nurse Manager | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Maternal risk assessment; maternity leave workflow; lactation accommodation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Inadequate accommodation violates Expanded Maternity Leave/Lactation law; mitigated by structured program

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 hours per case

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly/annual program review |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | Program-wide |
| **Owner** | HR Analytics Lead |
| **Participants** | HR Analytics, OH Nurse Manager, Finance, EAP Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 days/quarter
