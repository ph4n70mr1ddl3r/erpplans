## Overview

Corporate Project & Program Management Office (PMO) workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — governing
the **enterprise project-portfolio and delivery discipline** across the capital and transformation
program: new-store construction (~10–15/year, links to VS-37/VS-20), store remodels (~30–40/year,
links to VS-109), DC expansion/automation (links to VS-04), major IT and ERP/digital projects
(links to VS-27/VS-30/VS-99), renewable-energy builds (links to VS-108), fleet additions (links to
VS-06), regulatory/compliance projects (links to VS-22), and enterprise transformation/change
initiatives. With annual capex of PHP 800M–1.2B plus substantial transformation spend, BuildRight
runs dozens of concurrent projects and several multi-project programs at any time, yet no value
stream owns the project-portfolio governance, stage-gate methodology, program management, resource
& capacity planning, project delivery standards, or benefits-realization discipline. This value
stream owns the PMO lifecycle: portfolio strategy and intake/classification, prioritization and
resource allocation, stage-gate and project-lifecycle standards, program management, portfolio
risk/dependency/conflict management, charter/business-case governance, and portfolio reporting; and
project delivery management (resource/capacity, planning/WBS, budget/cost control, scope/change,
quality/acceptance, risk/issue/decision, communications/stakeholder, closure/handover/lessons);
plus benefits realization, the PMIS/tooling, portfolio performance analytics, program financial
performance vs business case, PMO maturity/continuous improvement, project-management
standards/templates/methodology, vendor/contractor project performance, and the enterprise
transformation/change portfolio.

This is distinct from **VS-40 (Capex & Project Accounting)** which performs the *financial
accounting* of capital projects (request W1811, approval W1813, commitment tracking W1814, cost
tracking W1819–W1826, CIP W1827, asset turnover W1828–W1829, variance W1825, ROI review W2752) —
this value stream performs the *operational project & program management* discipline (the PMO),
while VS-40 capitalizes and accounts for its spend. It is distinct from **VS-33 (Strategic Planning
& Corporate Performance Management)** which owns *corporate strategy, annual planning/budgeting,
and corporate KPIs* (the PMO delivers the project portfolio that executes the strategy; VS-33.1
sets the budget envelope, VS-33.2 tracks corporate performance). It is distinct from the
*delivery-domain* value streams (VS-20 construction, VS-37 store opening, VS-109 remodel, VS-108
renewable, VS-27/VS-30 IT, VS-06 fleet) which *execute* their respective project types — the PMO
*governs and standardizes* delivery across all of them. It is distinct from **VS-21 (Internal Audit
& Risk)** which *audits* projects/programs (the PMO manages delivery risk within projects) and from
**VS-36 (Corporate Governance)** which governs the *corporation*. The Corporate PMO is the
*enterprise project-portfolio and delivery-management* discipline — distinct from capex accounting,
strategic planning, delivery-domain execution, audit, and corporate governance.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: PHP 800M–1.2B annual capex plus major transformation programs
(the ERP platform itself, net-zero/renewable, omnichannel, store-format evolution) require a
disciplined PMO to prioritize against strategy, allocate scarce project resources, enforce
stage-gate discipline, manage cross-project dependencies and risk, and ensure benefits are
realized. Without it, projects overrun, compete destructively for resources, deliver late/over
budget, and under-deliver on their business cases — risks that no existing value stream owns at the
portfolio level.
