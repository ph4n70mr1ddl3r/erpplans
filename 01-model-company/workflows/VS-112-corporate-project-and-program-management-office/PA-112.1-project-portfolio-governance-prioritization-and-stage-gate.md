## W3545. Enterprise Project Portfolio Strategy & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual strategy/planning (links to VS-33/VS-36); board cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Enterprise project portfolio |
| **Owner** | PMO Director / COO |
| **Participants** | PMO Director, COO, CFO, CIO, Strategy (links to VS-33), Finance (links to VS-40/VS-17.4), Executive Committee |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PMO/Strategy define the enterprise project-portfolio strategy and governance framework: portfolio categories (capital store/DC/renewable/fleet links to VS-20/VS-37/VS-109/VS-108/VS-06; IT/digital links to VS-27/VS-30/VS-99; transformation/regulatory), strategic alignment to corporate strategy (links to VS-33), governance bodies and authorities (links to VS-36), and the PMO operating model/charter | PMO / Strategy | COO | Annual |
| 2 | PMO/Finance set the portfolio investment envelope against the capex/OpEx plan (links to VS-40/VS-33.1/VS-17.4) and the resource/capacity constraints (W3553); define portfolio KPIs (on-time, on-budget, benefits realization, stage-gate compliance) and the review cadence | PMO / Finance | CFO / COO | Annual |
| 3 | COO/CFO approve the portfolio strategy and PMO charter, ratify governance bodies and authorities, and integrate to the corporate plan (links to VS-33) and board reporting (links to VS-36); communicate to the organization | COO / CFO | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy (links to VS-33); capex/finance (links to VS-40/VS-17.4); governance (links to VS-36); PMIS (links to W3562)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Strategy-disconnect risk**: Projects not aligned to strategy; mitigated by strategic-alignment scoring (W3547)
- **Governance-ambiguity risk**: Unclear authorities stall decisions; mitigated by defined governance and authorities


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "enterprise project-portfolio strategy and governance framework" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review cadence" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the portfolio strategy and PMO charter, ratify governance bodies and…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-137 (ensure project portfolio approval & cost control.)
- operational: strategic-alignment scoring (W3547); operational: defined governance and authorities

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 weeks annual

## W3546. Project Intake, Classification & Portfolio Registration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project idea/request (from any business unit) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + intake cycle |
| **Volume** | All project requests |
| **Owner** | PMO |
| **Participants** | PMO, Project Sponsor, Strategy (links to VS-33), Finance (links to VS-40), IT (links to VS-27), Real Estate (links to VS-20/VS-97) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PMO receive and log the project request/intake: idea, sponsor, problem/opportunity statement, rough scope, estimated cost/benefit, category (capital/IT/transformation/regulatory), and urgency; perform initial triage (in-scope, duplicate, defer, reject) | PMO / Sponsor | PMO Director | Per request |
| 2 | PMO classify the project: type (capital store/DC/renewable/fleet/IT/transformation/regulatory), size/tier (major/minor/small-works), delivery approach (waterfall/agile/hybrid), and the applicable stage-gate (W3548) and authority (links to VS-36/VS-40); route to the relevant delivery-domain VS (VS-20/VS-37/VS-109/VS-108/VS-27/etc.) | PMO | PMO Director | Per request |
| 3 | PMO register the project in the portfolio/PMIS (links to W3562), assign a project ID/charter-draft, and queue for prioritization (W3547) and approval (W3551); maintain the portfolio register and pipeline view | PMO | PMO Director | Per request |

### System Touchpoints
- PMIS/portfolio register (links to W3562); capex (links to VS-40/W1811); governance/authority (links to VS-36); delivery-domain (links to VS-20/VS-37/VS-109/VS-108/VS-27)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Shadow-project risk**: Projects run outside the PMO/portfolio; mitigated by intake discipline and visibility
- **Mis-classification risk**: Wrong tier/approach; mitigated by classification standard (W3548)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "log the project request/intake" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-route of "route to the relevant delivery-domain VS" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-assign of "assign a project ID/charter-draft, and queue for prioritization" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-137 (ensure project portfolio approval & cost control.)
- operational: intake discipline and visibility; operational: classification standard (W3548)

### Time Estimate
- Per request; weekly intake

## W3547. Project Prioritization, Scoring & Resource Allocation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Intake (W3546); portfolio cycle; resource constraint |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + on constraint |
| **Volume** | Project pipeline |
| **Owner** | PMO / Strategy |
| **Participants** | PMO, Strategy (links to VS-33), Finance (links to VS-40/VS-17.4), Sponsors, Resource Owners (links to VS-19), Executive Committee |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PMO/Strategy score and prioritize projects against criteria: strategic alignment (links to VS-33), financial return (NPV/IRR/payback links to VS-40), risk, urgency/regulatory (links to VS-22), resource demand (W3553), and dependencies (W3550); produce the ranked portfolio view | PMO / Strategy / Finance | PMO Director | Quarterly |
| 2 | PMO/Finance allocate the portfolio to the investment envelope (links to VS-33.1/VS-40/VS-17.4) and resource/capacity (W3553): approve/defer/reject per ranking, balance across categories and the capital/OpEx split, and manage trade-offs; sequence against the multi-year plan | PMO / Finance | COO / CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | PMO publish the prioritized portfolio and resource allocation, communicate approve/defer/reject decisions to sponsors, and update the portfolio register (W3546) and dashboards (W3552); re-prioritize on change (new request, constraint, strategic shift) | PMO | COO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy (links to VS-33); capex/finance (links to VS-40/VS-17.4); resource/capacity (links to VS-19/W3553); PMIS (links to W3562); dashboards (links to W3552)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Politics-over-merit risk**: Prioritization driven by influence not merit; mitigated by transparent scoring
- **Over-commitment risk**: Approving more than capacity/resources allow; mitigated by capacity-aware allocation (W3553)


### Automation Opportunity
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve/defer/reject per ranking, balance across categories and the…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-update of "update the portfolio register" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-565 (ensure controlled execution — Project Portfolio Governance, Prioritization & Stage-Gate (PA-112.1).)
- operational: transparent scoring; operational: capacity-aware allocation (W3553)

### Time Estimate
- Quarterly + on constraint

## W3548. Stage-Gate Methodology & Project Lifecycle Standards

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Portfolio strategy (W3545); methodology review |
| **Frequency** | Maintained + per-project application |
| **Volume** | All projects |
| **Owner** | PMO |
| **Participants** | PMO, Delivery Leads (links to VS-20/VS-37/VS-109/VS-108/VS-27), Quality (links to VS-31), Finance (links to VS-40), Sponsors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PMO maintain the stage-gate methodology and project-lifecycle standards: stages (initiate → plan → execute → close, with domain-specific gates for capital/IT/transformation), gate criteria/deliverables, go/no-go/kill decisions, and the documentation/templates per stage (charter, plan, business case, risk register, closure); align to PMBOK/PRINCE2/Agile as appropriate | PMO | PMO Director | Maintained |
| 2 | PMO apply the stage-gate to each project: review gate readiness (deliverables, budget/schedule/benefit status, risk), conduct the gate review with the sponsor/governance, and make the go/no-go/kill/hold decision; capture the decision and conditions | PMO / Sponsor / Governance | PMO Director | Per gate |
| 3 | PMO track stage-gate compliance across the portfolio, enforce discipline (no gate-skip without waiver), and feed compliance to dashboards (W3552) and maturity/CI (W3565); evolve the methodology from lessons (W3560) | PMO | PMO Director | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- PMIS/gate model (links to W3562); templates/standards (links to W3566); governance (links to VS-36); quality (links to VS-31); capex (links to VS-40)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Gate-bypass risk**: Projects skip gates, eroding control; mitigated by enforcement and waivers
- **Methodology-mismatch risk**: Heavy waterfall applied to agile IT work; mitigated by tiered/appropriate methodology


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "technology and project-lifecycle standards" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review gate readiness" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track stage-gate compliance across the portfolio, enforce discipline" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-565 (ensure controlled execution — Project Portfolio Governance, Prioritization & Stage-Gate (PA-112.1).)
- operational: enforcement and waivers; operational: tiered/appropriate methodology

### Time Estimate
- Maintained + per-gate
