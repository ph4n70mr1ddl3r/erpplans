## Overview

Calibration, Metrology & Measurement Traceability Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **measurement-system integrity discipline** that underpins transaction accuracy,
inventory accuracy, quality, compliance, and fairness across 200 stores, 4 DCs, and the supply
chain. BuildRight's operations are measurement-intensive in ways unique to big-box hardware/DIY
retail: **catch-weight and cut-to-length selling** (lumber per board foot, wire/cable per meter,
rope/hose per meter, nails and fasteners in bulk by weight, tiles by the square-meter)
transacted at 600 POS terminals across 2.8M monthly transactions; **custom fabrication and
processing** (pipe cutting, lumber cutting, sheet cutting, wire cutting, paint mixing and tinting)
at every store's services counter (VS-09.1); **bulk material handling** (cement, sand, gravel,
lumber) received and inventoried by weight/count; **DC weighbridges / truck scales** for inbound
and outbound cargo at 4 DCs; **fuel & logistics measurement** (fuel dispensing, tank gauging,
telematics) for the mixed owned/3PL fleet (links to VS-61/VS-06); **environmental and process
instrumentation** (temperature/humidity for paint/chemical storage, scales, gas detectors); and
**test & measurement tools** used in quality (VS-31), tool rental (VS-12), and field services
(VS-12/VS-70/VS-66). Across all of these, the accuracy of the measuring instrument directly
determines revenue accuracy (under- or over-charging on catch-weight), inventory accuracy (the
≥97% target), quality acceptance, regulatory compliance (DTI-BPS weights and measures, Fair Trade
Law, Consumer Act RA 7394), and customer fairness/trust. Yet no value stream owns the
calibration/metrology discipline — it is referenced incidentally across 50+ PA files (VS-08 POS
scales, VS-09 cutting and paint mixing, VS-04 DC scales and weighbridges, VS-07 store equipment,
VS-31 quality instruments, VS-12 rental tools, VS-61 fuel, VS-23 CCTV/system calibration) with no
single owner for the program, standards, traceability, scheduling, records, or compliance. This
value stream consolidates the discipline: calibration program strategy and governance,
measurement-standards and traceability (ISO 17025 / national-standard chain), calibration intervals
and tolerance methodology, calibration master data and device registry, external-lab qualification
and in-house standards-lab operations, technician training/competency, and audit/records; the
device-calibration operations for POS/catch-weight scales, cutting equipment, paint dispensers, DC
weighbridges, fuel/logistics measurement, environmental/process instruments, and test & measurement
tools; and weights & measures regulatory compliance (DTI), out-of-tolerance investigation and
measurement correction, measurement-variance/revenue-impact analytics, device lifecycle/repair,
calibration cost/outsourcing analytics, measurement-system analysis (MSA/Gage R&R), customer
measurement dispute resolution, and metrology maturity/continuous improvement.

This is distinct from **VS-08 (POS & Checkout)** which *transacts* catch-weight sales (this value
stream calibrates the scales that the POS depends on for accurate charging). It is distinct from
**VS-09 (In-Store Services)** which *performs* cutting/fabrication/paint-mixing (this value stream
calibrates the cutters, meters, and dispensers that determine yield and charge accuracy). It is
distinct from **VS-04 (DC & Warehouse)** which *operates* the DC including weighbridges (this value
stream calibrates the weighbridges that determine received/shipped weight accuracy and
reconciliation). It is distinct from **VS-31 (Quality Management)** which *tests* product quality
(this value stream calibrates the test & measurement instruments that make quality results
defensible). It is distinct from **VS-29 (Master Data)** which *maintains* the item/device master
(this value stream owns the measurement device registry and the calibration-cycle attributes). It
is distinct from **VS-07.2 / VS-20.3 (Facility Maintenance)** which *maintains* equipment (this
value stream calibrates the measurement function of equipment and instruments). Calibration &
metrology is the *measurement-system integrity* discipline — distinct from POS operations, in-store
services, DC operations, quality testing, master data, and facility maintenance.

BuildRight's exposure is pervasive and unmanaged: at scale (2.8M monthly transactions, ~134M line
items/year, ~72K receipts/year, 600 POS + hundreds of cutting/paint/fuel/weighbridge/environmental
instruments), measurement drift silently leaks revenue (under-charge on catch-weight), inflates
cost (over-issue on cutting), distorts inventory accuracy, exposes the company to DTI weights &
measures enforcement and consumer-complaint liability, and undermines quality/acceptance decisions.
No existing value stream owns the calibration standards, traceability, scheduling, records, or
analytics discipline for these measurement systems.
