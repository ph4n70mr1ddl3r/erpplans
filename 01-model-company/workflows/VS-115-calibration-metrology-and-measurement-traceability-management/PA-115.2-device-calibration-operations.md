## W3625. POS Scale & Weight-Based Item Calibration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | POS scale calibration cycle (W3619); new terminal/scale (links to VS-08); OOT trigger (W3634) |
| **Frequency** | Statutory cycle (typically annual DTI verification) + on install/OOT |
| **Volume** | 600 POS terminals with integrated/peripheral scales across 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Metrology Manager / Store Operations |
| **Participants** | Metrology Manager, Store Operations (links to VS-07), POS/IT (links to VS-08/VS-27), Calibration Technicians (W3622), Quality (links to VS-31), Compliance (links to VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Metrology Manager/Calibration Technicians calibrate each POS scale (integrated or peripheral) used for weight-based items: as-found verification at multiple test points across the range using certified test weights (traceability W3618), linearity/accuracy/eccentricity checks, adjustment as needed, as-left verification, and pass/fail/OOT determination (W3634) | Metrology Manager / Calibration Technicians | Quality Manager | Per scale |
| 2 | Metrology Manager/Compliance secure DTI-BPS verification/seal for legal-for-trade POS scales: statutory verification by DTI or authorized entity, the verification seal/sticker (links to VS-22/W3633), and the legal-for-trade certificate; manage the seal integrity (tamper-evident) and prohibit unseal by non-authorized staff | Metrology Manager / Compliance | Quality Manager / VP Compliance | Per statutory cycle |
| 3 | Metrology Manager/Store Ops manage the POS-scale calibration cycle, return-to-service or quarantine if OOT (links to W3634/W3636), capture the record (W3624), and report POS-scale accuracy and DTI-compliance to governance (W3640); coordinate emergency swap for failed scales (links to VS-08/VS-27) | Metrology Manager / Store Ops / POS-IT | Quality Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- POS/scale (links to VS-08); device registry (links to W3620); standards/test weights (links to W3618); DTI/seal (links to W3633); records (links to W3624); OOT (links to W3634)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-charge risk**: POS scale reading low under-charges customers on catch-weight (revenue leakage) and breaches fair-trade; mitigated by statutory verification and OOT response
- **Seal-tamper risk**: Broken DTI seal invalidates legal-for-trade status; mitigated by tamper-evident seals and access control


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Metrology Manager/Calibration Technicians calibrate each POS scale" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based authorization of "authorized entity, the verification seal/sticker" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "Metrology Manager/Store Ops manage the POS-scale calibration cycle, return-to-service" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-575 (ensure controlled execution — Device Calibration Operations (PA-115.2).)
- operational: statutory verification and OOT response; operational: tamper-evident seals and access control

### Time Estimate
- Per scale + statutory cycle
