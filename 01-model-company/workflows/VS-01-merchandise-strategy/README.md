# VS-01: Merchandise Strategy

> **Plan & Source** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Merchandise strategy: assortment planning & product lifecycle, pricing & promotions, and product information/content for the 35,000-SKU range.

## Why it matters

35,000 active SKUs across 200 stores; assortment, pricing, and rich product content directly drive the 28–32% gross-margin target and the ecommerce/catalog experience. Decisions here propagate to every selling and replenishment workflow.

## Owner & participants

- **Owner**: VP Merchandising / Category Managers
