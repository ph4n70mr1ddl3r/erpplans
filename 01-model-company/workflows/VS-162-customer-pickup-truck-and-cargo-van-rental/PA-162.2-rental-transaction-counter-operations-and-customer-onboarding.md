## W4753. Rental Inquiry, Reservation & Availability Promise

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer inquires at rental desk / Pro Desk (VS-43) / app (VS-75) / phone after a bulky purchase or in advance |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (per inquiry) |
| **Volume** | ~inquiries across 200 stores; converts to ~rentals/day/store sized to bulky-attach (W4745) |
| **Owner** | Rental Counter Associate / Customer Service (VS-07) |
| **Participants** | Pro Desk (VS-43), Merchandising (basket), App/Digital (VS-75) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture the rental inquiry: desired vehicle type (pickup vs van — match to the purchased basket), requested window, pickup/return store, intended load (so the right vehicle & tie-downs are assigned per W4746 spec), and customer identification (loyalty/trade account VS-13/VS-43 where available) | Rental Counter Associate | Rental Counter Supervisor | 3–5 min |
| 2 | Check real-time availability against the rental availability calendar (telematics W4749 + reservations + in-service status W4750); confirm the vehicle is registered/insured/safety-checked (W4747/W4748/W4750) before promising it; offer alternatives or the next available slot | Rental Counter Associate / Fleet | Rental Counter Supervisor | 2–4 min |
| 3 | Create the reservation with a hold-to time, send confirmation (app/SMS per VS-75), and brief the customer on license/age requirements (W4754), deposit (W4756), and what to bring at pickup | Rental Counter Associate | Rental Counter Supervisor | 2–3 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental platform/availability calendar (new — links telematics W4749); loyalty/trade CRM (VS-13/VS-43); app/notifications (VS-75); fleet in-service status (W4750)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-promise risk**: Reserving a vehicle that is in-service/safety-failed/unregistered → customer stranded at pickup; mitigated by real-time availability + pre-release checks (W4759)
- **Wrong-vehicle risk**: Assigning a pickup when the basket needs a van → overloading or refused return; mitigated by load-type capture at inquiry
- **No-show risk**: Held vehicles sitting idle; mitigated by hold-to time and overbooking discipline

### Time Estimate
- ~7–12 min per inquiry; ~across hundreds of inquiries/day group-wide

### Automation Opportunity
- App/self-service reservation with live availability; basket-aware vehicle recommendation (links POS bulky lines); automated confirmation & reminder

### Controls
- CTL: reservation only against verified-available, in-service, road-legal vehicles; hold-to time enforced

### Cross-references
- links to VS-13/VS-43 (customer), VS-75 (app), W4749 (availability), W4750 (in-service), W4754–W4758 (pickup flow)

## W4754. Driver-License Verification, Age/Eligibility Check & Driving-Record Screen

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer presents to take a vehicle (reservation W4753 or walk-up) |
| **Frequency** | Per rental |
| **Volume** | ~every rental across 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Rental Counter Associate / Rental Compliance |
| **Participants** | LTO (license validation), Rental Desk, LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inspect the customer's valid Philippine driver's license (or recognized foreign/international license): photograph + signature match, not expired, correct restriction code for the vehicle class; verify age meets minimum (typically 21+, with a young-driver surcharge below 25); record license number & expiry | Rental Counter Associate | Rental Counter Supervisor | 3–5 min |
| 2 | Screen against a watch/do-not-rent list (prior non-return W4763, unpaid damage W4762, fraud VS-125, license revocation); validate the license is not flagged using LTO online lookup where available; refuse rental with documented reason if flagged | Rental Counter Associate / LP | Rental Counter Supervisor / LP | 2–4 min |
| 3 | Record the eligibility decision with the license image + ID selfie (RA 10173-consistent, W4755 consent) into the rental record; this is the gate before agreement execution (W4755) — no rental proceeds without a passed eligibility check | Rental Counter Associate | Rental Counter Supervisor | 1–2 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental platform (driver master — new, links loyalty VS-13); LTO license validation (integration); watch/do-not-rent list (VS-23/LP); privacy (VS-91)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Invalid-license risk**: Renting to an unlicensed/disqualified driver → voided insurance + criminal liability for BuildRight (Land Transportation & Traffic Code) + the customer; mitigated by the verification gate
- **Do-not-rent risk**: Re-renting to a prior bad actor → repeat theft/damage; mitigated by the watch-list screen
- **Age-restriction risk**: Under-age rental → insurance voidance; mitigated by age check + young-driver surcharge policy

### Time Estimate
- ~6–11 min per rental; blocks W4755 if not passed

### Automation Opportunity
- LTO license barcode/QR scan & online validation; watch-list auto-screen; biometric/selfie match; eligibility auto-decision

### Controls
- CTL: valid license + age + not-on-watch-list required before agreement (gate to W4755); license image + selfie captured with consent

### Cross-references
- links to VS-23 (do-not-rent/LP), VS-125 (fraud), VS-91 (license-image privacy), W4755 (agreement gate), W4763 (non-return history)

## W4755. Rental Agreement Execution, Liability Waiver & Telematics Consent

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Eligibility passed (W4754); vehicle about to be released |
| **Frequency** | Per rental |
| **Volume** | ~every rental |
| **Owner** | Rental Counter Associate / Legal (VS-100.3) |
| **Participants** | Customer, Legal (VS-100), Privacy (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generate the rental agreement: vehicle (plate/VIN), customer (license W4754), checkout date/time & odometer, scheduled return, rate structure (W4745), deposit/hold (W4756), LDW election (W4757), permitted-use terms (no sub-rental, no off-road unless specified, no ferry/cross-island outside geofence W4749, no illegal use), and the customer's liability for damage/theft/traffic violations | Rental Counter Associate | Rental Counter Supervisor / Legal | 4–6 min |
| 2 | Capture the signed liability acceptance and the **telematics/privacy consent** (RA 10173) for GPS/speed/harsh-event data (W4749), the personal-data processing for the rental record, and the do-not-rent consequence of breach; make consent granular and recorded | Rental Counter Associate / Privacy | Rental Counter Supervisor / DPO | 2–3 min |
| 3 | Execute (e-signature preferred per VS-88/electronic records), store the agreement in the records system (VS-88 retention 10-yr per BIR / TRAIN NIRC Sec. 235), and link it to the vehicle telematics (W4749) and the billing record (W4760); the signed agreement is the legal basis for all downstream recovery (W4761/W4762/W4763) | Rental Counter Associate / Legal | Rental Counter Supervisor | 1–2 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental platform/agreement engine (new); e-signature & records (VS-88); privacy/consent registry (VS-91.2); telematics link (W4749); legal/contract (VS-100.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unenforceable-contract risk**: A poorly-drafted or unsigned agreement → no recovery basis for damage/theft (W4761/W4762/W4763); mitigated by a Legal-approved standard agreement and e-signature capture
- **Privacy-breach risk**: Telematics/ID data processed without valid consent → RA 10173 breach (VS-91); mitigated by granular consent at execution
- **Term-dispute risk**: Customer disputing charges they weren't told about (geofence breach, fuel, late fee); mitigated by clear terms + signed acceptance

### Time Estimate
- ~7–11 min per rental; standard Legal-approved template keeps it fast

### Automation Opportunity
- Pre-filled agreement from reservation + license; e-signature with read-confirmation; consent registry auto-update (VS-91.2); records auto-file (VS-88)

### Controls
- CTL: signed agreement with liability + telematics consent required before release (gate to W4758); Legal-approved template; consent recorded; retention per VS-88

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100.3 (contract), VS-91.2 (consent), VS-88 (records), W4749 (telematics), W4754 (eligibility), W4758 (release gate), W4761/W4762/W4763 (recovery basis)

## W4756. Security Deposit Authorization, Card Hold & Pre-Authorization Capture

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Agreement execution (W4755); vehicle release |
| **Frequency** | Per rental |
| **Volume** | ~every rental |
| **Owner** | Rental Counter Associate / Finance-Treasury (VS-18/VS-80) |
| **Participants** | Customer, Payment/Acquirer (VS-80), Finance (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Authorize the security deposit/pre-authorization at the deductible level set in W4748 (covering the customer's damage/theft exposure without LDW, plus fuel + potential late/overtime + cleaning): place a card hold (VS-80 payment gateway) or accept a cash/security deposit per policy; record the authorization reference | Rental Counter Associate / Finance | Rental Counter Supervisor / Finance | 2–4 min |
| 2 | Confirm the hold succeeded before vehicle release (W4758); explain to the customer how/when the hold is released (W4760) and what charges may be applied (damage, fuel short, late return, cleaning); this is the financial guarantee backing the agreement (W4755) | Rental Counter Associate / Finance | Rental Counter Supervisor | 1–2 min |
| 3 | Manage exceptions: insufficient-hold (downgrade vehicle or refuse), cash-deposit handling (VS-08.2 cash controls / VS-81 CIT risk), and the hold-release timing compliance (card holds expire; don't release a vehicle without a live hold) | Finance / Rental Counter | Rental Counter Supervisor / Finance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Payment gateway/acquirer (VS-80); cash office (VS-08.2); treasury (VS-18); rental billing (W4760); finance/GL (VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-hold release risk**: Releasing a vehicle without a valid deposit hold → no financial recovery for damage (W4762); mitigated by the hold-confirmed-before-release gate
- **Hold-lapse risk**: A card hold expiring during a long rental → exposure; mitigated by re-authorization logic for extended rentals
- **Cash-deposit risk**: Cash deposits create custody/loss exposure (VS-08.2/VS-81); mitigated by preferring card holds and Smart Safe (VS-08.2 W212)

### Time Estimate
- ~3–6 min per rental

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-deposit-hold at agreement execution; hold-status gate on release; auto re-auth for long rentals; cash-deposit Smart Safe integration

### Controls
- CTL: confirmed deposit hold required before release (gate to W4758); hold level = deductible; cash deposits via VS-08.2 controls

### Cross-references
- links to VS-80 (payment/acquirer), VS-08.2 (cash/Smart Safe), VS-18 (treasury), W4748 (deductible level), W4758 (release gate), W4760 (release/settlement)

## W4757. Loss Damage Waiver (LDW) Sale, Insurance Upsell & Coverage Explanation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Deposit placed (W4756); standard counter upsell step |
| **Frequency** | Per rental |
| **Volume** | ~every rental (offer); ~40–70% take-rate benchmark |
| **Owner** | Rental Counter Associate / Rental Program (pricing W4748) |
| **Participants** | Customer, Risk & Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Offer the Loss Damage Waiver (LDW) and any supplementary covers (e.g., cargo-cover for the customer's load, tire-and-glass, personal-accident cover for the driver/passengers) per the W4748 product design; explain what each covers and the per-day fee | Rental Counter Associate | Rental Counter Supervisor | 2–3 min |
| 2 | Clearly disclose what LDW is **not** (it is a waiver of the customer's financial responsibility for damage/theft subject to terms — **not** insurance for the customer, and not cover for gross negligence, DUI (VS-150), unauthorized use, or geofence breach (W4749)); capture the customer's accept/decline decision in the agreement (W4755) | Rental Counter Associate / Risk | Rental Counter Supervisor / Risk | 2–3 min |
| 3 | Record the LDW election into the agreement & billing (W4760); if declined, confirm the customer understands they remain liable up to the deductible via the deposit (W4756); the LDW take-rate & loss-ratio feed the annual re-pricing (W4748) | Rental Counter Associate | Rental Counter Supervisor | 1 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental platform (LDW product/pricing — new, links W4748); billing (W4760); risk/insurance (VS-26); analytics (W4768)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-selling risk**: Overselling LDW as "full insurance" → customer disputes/reputational risk + regulatory (Insurance Code); mitigated by the clear what-it-is-not disclosure
- **Under-take risk**: Low take-rate → fleet absorbs damage; mitigated by disciplined offer + re-pricing
- **Adverse-selection risk**: Only high-risk customers take LDW → loss-ratio imbalance; mitigated by bundling & pricing review

### Time Estimate
- ~5–7 min per rental

### Automation Opportunity
- Decision-tree upsell prompt; LDW-take-rate/loss-ratio dashboard feeding W4748 re-pricing; auto-recording into agreement & billing

### Controls
- CTL: LDW offer with mandatory what-it-is-not disclosure; accept/decline recorded; take-rate/loss-ratio monitored (W4768)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-26 (insurance W862/W1565), W4748 (LDW design/pricing), W4755 (agreement), W4756 (deposit), W4760 (billing), W4768 (analytics)

## W4758. Vehicle Handover, Time-and-Mileage Capture & Yard Loading Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Agreement signed (W4755), deposit held (W4756), LDW elected (W4757) |
| **Frequency** | Per rental |
| **Volume** | ~every rental |
| **Owner** | Rental Counter Associate / Yard (VS-07.1) |
| **Participants** | Customer, Yard Dispatch (VS-07.1 W438), LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture the checkout baseline: odometer reading, fuel level, date/time, and a pre-rental condition photoset (exterior + interior + cargo area + fuel gauge) timestamped to telematics (W4749); confirm CR/plate/CTPL valid (release gate from W4747) and between-rental safety check passed (W4750) | Rental Counter Associate / Yard | Rental Counter Supervisor | 5–8 min |
| 2 | Walk the customer around the vehicle to confirm condition matches the photoset, demonstrate controls/tie-downs/spare/jack/fire-extinguisher/first-aid, brief on geofence/curfew (W4749) and fuel-return policy; this is the condition baseline for the return inspection (W4759) | Rental Counter Associate / Yard | Rental Counter Supervisor | 5–8 min |
| 3 | Coordinate yard loading (VS-07.1 W438 / W1281) of the customer's purchased merchandise into the rented vehicle — forklift/load-assist for lumber/cement/tile/appliances, proper tie-down per W4746 spec, and payload check (no overloading); release the vehicle on telematics to start time-and-mileage billing | Yard / Rental Counter | Rental Counter Supervisor / Yard Lead | 5–15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Telematics checkout (W4749); rental platform condition-photoset (new); yard dispatch/loading (VS-07.1 W438/W1281); LP (VS-23); release gate (W4747/W4750)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pre-existing-damage dispute risk**: Damage not photographed at checkout → customer blamed at return (W4759); mitigated by the timestamped photoset
- **Overloading risk**: Loading beyond payload → LTO/CTPL voidance + safety; mitigated by payload check at yard loading
- **Improper-tie-down risk**: Unsecured load → road incident/liability; mitigated by tie-down demonstration & yard assist

### Time Estimate
- ~15–31 min per rental (handover + loading)

### Automation Opportunity
- Telematics auto-checkout (odometer/fuel/time); condition-photoset app with AI damage-diff at return (W4759); payload check; auto start billing

### Controls
- CTL: checkout photoset + CR/CTPL/safety-check gate before release; payload & tie-down verified at yard loading

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07.1 (W438/W1281 yard loading), W4747 (release gate), W4749 (telematics), W4750 (safety check), W4759 (return baseline)

## W4759. Return Inspection, Damage Assessment, Fuel/Cleaning Check & Re-Release Gate

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vehicle returned to the lot |
| **Frequency** | Per rental |
| **Volume** | ~every rental |
| **Owner** | Rental Counter Associate / Yard |
| **Participants** | Customer, Maintenance (W4750), LP (VS-23), Finance (W4760) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture the return baseline: odometer, fuel level, return date/time, telematics (W4749) stop; take a return condition photoset and diff against the checkout photoset (W4758); check for geofence/curfew breaches, harsh events, and unauthorized use (telematics) | Rental Counter Associate / Yard | Rental Counter Supervisor | 5–10 min |
| 2 | Assess damage (if any): triage new damage vs pre-existing, estimate repair cost (links W4750/W4761), apply the LDW/deductible decision (W4757) — if LDW taken and terms met, waive customer cost; if declined, charge the deductible via the deposit (W4756); assess fuel-short (charge refuel fee), cleaning (charge cleaning fee if interior/soiled), late return (charge overtime), and excess mileage (per rate) | Rental Counter Associate / Maintenance / Finance | Rental Counter Supervisor / Finance | 5–15 min |
| 3 | Ground the vehicle for the between-rental safety check & cleaning/refuel (W4750) before re-release; only re-release once safety-checked, refueled, cleaned, and CR/CTPL/insurance re-verified; this gate prevents releasing an unsafe/un-roadworthy vehicle to the next customer | Yard / Maintenance | Rental Fleet Maintenance Lead | Per rental |

### System Touchpoints
- Telematics return capture (W4749); condition-photoset diff (W4758); rental billing/charges (W4760); maintenance/PM (W4750); LP/exception (VS-23)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missed-damage risk**: Damage not caught at return → unrecoverable (next customer blamed or fleet absorbs); mitigated by the photoset diff and immediate assessment
- **Unsafe re-release risk**: Re-releasing without safety check/refuel/clean → next-customer incident; mitigated by the re-release gate (W4750)
- **Disputed-charge risk**: Customer disputing fuel/damage/cleaning charges; mitigated by the photoset + telematics evidence and clear terms (W4755)

### Time Estimate
- ~10–25 min per return; re-release prep absorbed in W4750

### Automation Opportunity
- AI photoset damage-diff; telematics auto-capture return metrics; auto-charge computation (fuel/late/mileage); re-release gate automation

### Controls
- CTL: return photoset + telematics diff; damage/fuel/cleaning assessed and charged per terms; re-release only after safety check/refuel/clean

### Cross-references
- links to W4749 (telematics), W4750 (re-release gate/safety), W4757 (LDW/deductible), W4758 (checkout baseline), W4760 (billing), W4761 (damage repair)

## W4760. Rental Billing, Settlement, Deposit Release & POS/Account Posting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Return inspection complete (W4759) |
| **Frequency** | Per rental |
| **Volume** | ~every rental |
| **Owner** | Finance / Rental Counter (VS-17/VS-80) |
| **Participants** | Payment/Acquirer (VS-80), Finance (VS-17), Customer, BIR (e-invoicing VS-79) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compute the final invoice from the rental record: base rate × time (W4758 checkout → W4759 return), mileage (telematics W4749), LDW fee (W4757), fuel-short (W4759), cleaning/late/excess-mileage charges (W4759), and any damage/deductible (W4759); apply trade/loyalty discount (VS-13/VS-43) if eligible | Finance / Rental Counter | Rental Counter Supervisor / Finance | 3–6 min |
| 2 | Settle against the deposit hold (W4756): capture the final charge to the card and release the unused hold remainder, or collect/refund the cash difference (VS-08.2 controls); issue the BIR-compliant receipt / e-invoice (VS-79 W473 BIR EIS) for the rental revenue; post to GL (VS-17) as rental income + fuel-recovery + LDW income | Finance / Rental Counter | Finance | 3–6 min |
| 3 | Recon the daily rental batch to telematics records, deposit holds, and GL postings; investigate variances (under-billed rentals, unreleased holds, unmatched cash); feed revenue/AR data to analytics (W4768) and finance close (VS-17.4) | Finance | Finance Controller | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental billing platform (new); payment/acquirer (VS-80); BIR e-invoicing (VS-79 W473); GL/AR (VS-17); cash office (VS-08.2); analytics (W4768)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Revenue-leakage risk**: Under-billed time/mileage/fuel/damage (links VS-118 revenue assurance); mitigated by telematics-driven billing and daily recon
- **Hold-release-compliance risk**: Failing to release holds timely → customer complaint + card-scheme issues; mitigated by the settlement SLA
- **Tax-compliance risk**: Rental revenue not BIR-invoiced (VAT 12%); mitigated by e-invoicing integration (VS-79)

### Time Estimate
- ~6–12 min per settlement; ~daily recon absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- Telematics-to-invoice auto-compute; auto capture-and-release on card; BIR e-invoice auto-issue; GL auto-post; daily recon exception dashboard (links VS-118)

### Controls
- CTL: final invoice from telematics; deposit capture/release per settlement; BIR e-invoice issued; daily batch recon

### Cross-references
- links to VS-80 (payment), VS-79 (BIR e-invoicing), VS-17 (GL/AR), VS-08.2 (cash), VS-118 (revenue assurance), W4756 (deposit), W4759 (charges), W4768 (analytics)
