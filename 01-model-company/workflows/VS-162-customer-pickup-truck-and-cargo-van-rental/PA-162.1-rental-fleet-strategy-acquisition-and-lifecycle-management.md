## W4745. Self-Haul Rental Fleet Strategy, Sizing & Business Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch; annual fleet-capex & pricing review; new-store opening (VS-37); format evolution |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on launch/new-store cohort |
| **Volume** | 1 group strategy; ~2–3 pickup trucks + 1 cargo van per store → ~600–800 vehicles across 200 stores |
| **Owner** | VP Services / Self-Haul Rental Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), Merchandising (large-item categories), Finance (VS-17.4 FP&A), Fleet (VS-06), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the fleet strategy & business model: vehicle mix (single-cab & crew-cab pickups for lumber/yard loads; high-roof cargo van/LCV for appliances/furniture/boxes), fleet size per store sized to bulky-merch attach rate & transaction volume, pricing structure (flat first-75-min fee + hourly + per-km + insurance waiver + fuel), deposit/hold policy, and the sale-conversion KPI the fleet exists to lift (large-item basket completion) | Rental Program Manager / FP&A | VP Services / COO | Annual |
| 2 | Build the unit-economics & capex case: vehicle depreciation over ~3–5 yr / ~80–120k km, CTPL + comprehensive premium, maintenance, fuel, telematics, staffing, insurance-claim reserve vs. rental revenue + attach-uplift; define the hurdle rate and the fleet right-size per store tier (Metro vs provincial; high lumber-yard stores get more pickups) | Rental Program Manager / FP&A | VP Services / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Approve the annual fleet-capex envelope, integrate into the corporate capex plan (VS-40/VS-112), and define the rollout sequence for new stores (fleet commissioned at store opening per VS-37) | Rental Program Manager / Finance / PMO (VS-112) | COO / CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Fleet/asset register (VS-06/VS-35); FP&A (VS-17.4); capex (VS-40); rental-pricing master (new — feeds POS W139-pattern & rental desk); store-tier master (VS-29)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-sizing risk**: Too few vehicles at high-attach stores → lost sales & customer churn (the exact basket-abandonment the fleet exists to prevent); mitigated by per-store-tier sizing and the rebalancing workflow (W4751)
- **Mis-priced risk**: Flat fee too low vs. depreciation/insurance → fleet subsidizes customers and erodes EBITDA; mitigated by the unit-economics model reviewed annually
- **Mis-model risk**: Choosing pickups where the basket is appliance/van-shaped (or vice-versa) → low utilization; mitigated by basket-mix analysis

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual strategy + sizing; per-store-tier sizing absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- Basket-attach-driven fleet-sizing model (links POS bulky-line data → fleet count); dynamic pricing by store/demand; capex-case auto-build from depreciation/insurance feeds

### Controls
- CTL-716 (ensure controlled execution — Rental Fleet Strategy, Acquisition & Lifecycle Management (PA-162.1).)
- CTL: annual business case & pricing reviewed by CFO; fleet count tied to bulky-attach KPI; capex approved via VS-40 gate

### Cross-references
- links to VS-40/VS-112 (capex/PMO), VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-06/VS-35 (fleet/asset), VS-37 (new-store commissioning); sets inputs for W4746–W4752

## W4746. Rental Vehicle Procurement, Specification & Fleet Acquisition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Fleet-capex approval (W4745); replacement cycle (W4752); new-store rollout |
| **Frequency** | Per acquisition batch + annual |
| **Volume** | ~150–250 vehicles/yr (fleet build + rolling replacement across 200 stores) |
| **Owner** | Rental Program Manager / Procurement (VS-03/VS-34) |
| **Participants** | OEM/dealer partners, Finance (VS-17), Fleet (VS-06), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Specify the vehicle requirements: payload (≥1,000 kg for cement/tile pallets & lumber), cargo length (≥2.4 m pickup bed or ≥3 m van), tie-down points, payload-rated tires, fuel efficiency, spare parts availability across all Philippine regions, warranty, and livery/branding (VS-14) | Rental Program Manager / Fleet | VP Services | Per batch |
| 2 | Negotiate fleet purchase with OEM/dealer (volume discount, corporate-fleet program, trade-in of retired fleet W4752, after-sales/warranty, parts lead time); structure as capex purchase or operating lease per the W4745 capex case | Procurement / Rental Program Manager / Finance | VP Services / CFO | Per batch |
| 3 | Receive vehicles, install livery + telematics (W4749) + compulsory equipment (early-warning device, fire extinguisher, first-aid, spare tire, jack, tie-down straps), register (W4747), insure (W4748), and commission into the rental fleet at the assigned store | Fleet / Rental Program Manager | VP Services | Per batch |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/PO (VS-03/VS-34/VS-15); asset register & fixed assets (VS-35); fleet master (VS-06); livery/brand asset (VS-14); insurance master (VS-26)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Spec-mismatch risk**: A vehicle that can't legally/physically carry the basket it's rented for (overloading = LTO/CTPL voidance); mitigated by payload/tie-down spec tied to basket analysis
- **Parts-availability risk**: A model with poor provincial parts support → long downtime; mitigated by parts-availability scoring in selection (links VS-31 quality)
- **Total-cost risk**: Low purchase price offset by high depreciation/maintenance → TCO worse than lease; mitigated by the TCO comparison in selection

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks per acquisition batch (spec → negotiate → deliver → commission)

### Automation Opportunity
- OEM fleet-configurator integration; TCO model auto-comparison (purchase vs lease); auto-creation of asset-register & fixed-asset records on delivery (links VS-35)

### Controls
- CTL-716 (ensure controlled execution — Rental Fleet Strategy, Acquisition & Lifecycle Management (PA-162.1).)
- CTL: vehicle spec tied to basket payload; TCO comparison documented; PO via VS-03 gate with VS-26 insurance verified before commissioning

### Cross-references
- links to VS-03/VS-34/VS-15 (procurement), VS-35 (asset), VS-06 (fleet), VS-14 (livery), W4747–W4749 (registration/insurance/telematics), W4752 (trade-in)

## W4747. Rental Vehicle LTO Registration, Conduction Sticker & Motor-Vehicle Titling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New vehicle commissioning (W4746); annual registration renewal; plate loss/transfer |
| **Frequency** | Per vehicle + annual renewal |
| **Volume** | ~600–800 vehicles initially; ~600–800 annual renewals + ~150–250 new/retired per year |
| **Owner** | Rental Fleet Compliance Officer / Legal (VS-100.3) |
| **Participants** | LTO, Fleet (VS-06), Procurement (dealer handover), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each new vehicle: process Sales Report / Deed of Sale, LTO Initial Registration, MV File Number, official receipt, plates, conduction sticker (for the pre-plate window), and the official CR (Certificate of Registration); record in the fleet/asset register (VS-35/VS-06) | Fleet Compliance / Fleet | Rental Program Manager / Legal | Per vehicle |
| 2 | Maintain the annual LTO registration renewal calendar for the entire fleet; coordinate the MVIS / PMVIC inspection, emission test, and Compulsory Third-Party Liability (CTPL) proof; renew before expiry to avoid driving an unregistered vehicle (a criminal/insurance-void offense) | Fleet Compliance / Fleet | Rental Program Manager | Annual + continuous |
| 3 | Manage plate/CRCR transfers on retirement (W4752), lost-plate replacement, and the motor-vehicle title lifecycle; ensure every vehicle on the rental lot has valid, unexpired CR + plate + CTPL before it is ever released to a customer (W4759 release gate) | Fleet Compliance / Legal | Rental Program Manager / Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Fleet/asset register & vehicle master (VS-06/VS-35); registration-expiry calendar (new — links VS-06 W1401 pattern); insurance (VS-26 CTPL); legal/records (VS-100/VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unregistered-release risk**: A vehicle released to a customer with an expired LTO registration → voided CTPL, criminal liability for BuildRight officers, and personal liability for the customer in an accident; mitigated by the release gate (W4759) blocking release on expired CR/CTPL
- **Renewal-lapse risk**: A lapsed renewal taking a vehicle off-rent → lost revenue; mitigated by the renewal calendar and PMVIC scheduling
- **Title-defect risk**: A vehicle BuildRight can't prove it owns → can't legally rent/sell; mitigated by clean CR/Deed-of-Sale capture at acquisition

### Time Estimate
- ~per vehicle 1–3 days initial registration; ~annual renewal 0.5–1 day/vehicle batched

### Automation Opportunity
- Registration-expiry calendar with 30/60/90-day alerts; auto-block vehicle release when CR/CTPL expired (links rental-desk gate); LTO renewal batch scheduling

### Controls
- CTL-94 (ensure rental fleet safety & settlement integrity.)
- CTL: valid CR + plate + unexpired CTPL required before any customer release (release gate W4759); annual renewal calendar maintained; title chain clean

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (LTO/PMVIC pattern W1401/W1536), VS-35 (asset), VS-26 (CTPL), VS-100/VS-88 (title/records); gates W4759 (release)

## W4748. Rental Fleet Insurance Program — CTPL, Comprehensive & Self-Insurance Layer

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Fleet acquisition (W4746); annual policy renewal; claim experience review |
| **Frequency** | Per vehicle + annual renewal |
| **Volume** | ~600–800 insured vehicles; 1 master fleet policy + per-vehicle CTPL |
| **Owner** | Rental Program Manager / Risk & Insurance (VS-26) |
| **Participants** | Insurance broker/underwriter, Treasury (VS-18), Legal (VS-100), Finance (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Structure the fleet motor-vehicle insurance program: Compulsory Third-Party Liability (CTPL, mandatory under RA 4136 — the Land Transportation and Traffic Code — for every registered vehicle) for every vehicle; comprehensive own-damage & theft cover (or a documented self-insured retention layer for a 600–800 fleet where it is economic); and a motor fleet excess/umbrella for catastrophic third-party bodily injury, given customers drive into public roads | Risk & Insurance / Rental Program Manager / Treasury | Rental Program Manager / CRO | Annual + per vehicle |
| 2 | Define and price the customer-facing **Loss Damage Waiver (LDW)** — the optional add-on (sold at the rental desk W4757) that waives the customer's financial responsibility for damage/theft in exchange for a fee — and the per-incident deductible/excess the customer remains liable for without LDW; set the security deposit/hold (W4756) at the deductible level | Risk & Insurance / Rental Program Manager | Rental Program Manager / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Manage the claim interface between customer incidents (W4761/W4762), the deductible recovery from the customer deposit/hold, the LDW waiver decision, and the insurer settlement/subrogation; review loss experience annually and re-price LDW/deposit/renewal | Risk & Insurance / Legal / Claims | Rental Program Manager / CRO | Continuous + annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance policy & claims (VS-26 PA-26.3); treasury/risk (VS-18/VS-21.2); rental-LDW-pricing master (new); finance/GL (VS-17); legal (VS-100)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Uninsured-loss risk**: An accident exceeding CTPL limits without comprehensive/umbrella → catastrophic BuildRight liability; mitigated by the layered program and umbrella
- **Mis-priced LDW risk**: LDW priced too low → fleet absorbs damage; too high → low take-rate and customers decline (then default to deposit at risk); mitigated by annual loss-experience re-pricing
- **Subrogation-gap risk**: Failure to recover from an at-fault third party; mitigated by the claim workflow (W4762) and legal subrogation (VS-100)

### Time Estimate
- ~annual program design 2–3 weeks; ~claim handling per event absorbed in W4761/W4762

### Automation Opportunity
- Fleet policy auto-renewal with VIN-level bind; LDW take-rate & loss-ratio dashboards; claim-to-deposit-recovery auto-link

### Controls
- CTL-716 (ensure controlled execution — Rental Fleet Strategy, Acquisition & Lifecycle Management (PA-162.1).)
- CTL: every registered vehicle carries CTPL (release gate W4759); LDW/deductible/deposit policy board-approved (CRO/CFO); annual loss review

### Cross-references
- links to VS-26 (insurance/claims W862/W1565 pattern), VS-18/VS-21.2 (risk), W4756 (deposit), W4757 (LDW sale), W4761/W4762 (claims)

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Perform the **between-rental safety check** on every vehicle before re-release: tires/brakes/lights/steering, fire extinguisher/first-aid/jack/spare, cargo tie-downs, no fluid leaks, no dashboard warning; record the checklist and block re-release on any fail | Store Maintenance / Rental Counter | Rental Fleet Maintenance Lead | Per rental |

- **Downtime-availability risk**: Long service turnaround → lost rental revenue; mitigated by PM scheduling and parts availability (VS-31)

### Time Estimate

### Automation Opportunity

## W4752. Rental Vehicle End-of-Life Disposition, Remarketing & Replacement Cycle

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vehicle reaching age/mileage/km cap (~3–5 yr / ~80–120k km); uneconomic repair; total-loss claim (W4762) |
| **Frequency** | Per vehicle + rolling ~150–250/yr |
| **Volume** | ~150–250 retirements/yr across 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Rental Program Manager / Fleet & Asset (VS-06/VS-35) |
| **Participants** | Finance/Asset (VS-35), Procurement (VS-03), Insurance (VS-26), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify vehicles reaching the replacement cap (age/km) or uneconomic-to-repair threshold from telematics (W4749) & maintenance records (W4750); validate against depreciation schedule & book value (VS-35); trigger replacement ordering (W4746) | Fleet / Asset Mgmt | Rental Program Manager / Finance | Continuous |
| 2 | Decommission the vehicle: remove telematics (W4749) & livery, recover plates/CR for LTO transfer/cancellation (W4747), wipe any on-board data (privacy VS-91), and determine disposition — trade-in to OEM dealer (W4746), wholesale remarketing, internal reassignment, or scrap (DENR-compliant end-of-life vehicle disposal VS-73/VS-114) | Fleet / Asset / LP | Rental Program Manager / Finance | Per vehicle |
| 3 | Realize residual value, post fixed-asset disposal & gain/loss (VS-35 W39 pattern), and reconcile to the depreciation schedule; feed actual residuals into the next unit-economics case (W4745) | Finance / Asset Mgmt | Rental Program Manager / CFO | Per vehicle |

### System Touchpoints
- Fixed-asset/disposal (VS-35 W39/W184); fleet master (VS-06); LTO transfer (W4747); records/privacy wipe (VS-91/VS-88); waste/scrap (VS-73/VS-114)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hold-too-long risk**: Running a vehicle past its economic life → rising maintenance + safety risk + depressed residual; mitigated by the age/km cap and TCO monitoring
- **Residual-leakage risk**: Poor remarketing → low residual recovery; mitigated by trade-in (W4746) + wholesale benchmarking
- **Data-remnant risk**: On-board customer/telematics data not wiped at sale → privacy breach (VS-91); mitigated by the wipe step

### Time Estimate
- ~per vehicle 1–3 days disposition; ~continuous monitoring absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- Age/km cap auto-flag from telematics; trade-in-vs-wholesale decision support; auto fixed-asset disposal posting; residual-actuals feed to W4745 model

### Controls
- CTL-716 (ensure controlled execution — Rental Fleet Strategy, Acquisition & Lifecycle Management (PA-162.1).)
- CTL: vehicles retired at age/km cap; plates/CR transferred/cancelled; on-board data wiped; disposal posted (VS-35)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-35 (W39/W184 disposal), VS-06 (fleet), W4746 (trade-in), W4747 (title transfer), VS-91/VS-88 (data wipe), VS-73/VS-114 (scrap)
