## W4761. Rental Vehicle Accident, Damage & Roadside Event Response

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Telematics crash/harsh-event alert (W4749); customer call-in of accident/damage/breakdown; police/third-party report |
| **Frequency** | Per event |
| **Volume** | ~accident/damage events benchmarked ~1–3% of rentals |
| **Owner** | Rental Program Manager / LP & Safety (VS-23/VS-24) |
| **Participants** | Customer, Police, Insurance (VS-26), Maintenance (W4750), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Triage the event on telematics + customer call: severity, location, injuries, third-party involvement, vehicle drivability; if injury/serious → emergency services + notify Safety/LP (VS-24) + Legal (VS-100) + Insurance (VS-26) immediately; capture the customer's statement and photos at scene | Rental Program / LP / Safety | Rental Program Manager / Safety Officer | Per event (urgent) |
| 2 | Coordinate roadside response: recovery/tow to nearest approved workshop (VS-31/W4750), substitute vehicle for the stranded customer (W4750) if the rental continues, and liaison with police for the incident report (required for insurance W4762 and any third-party claim) | Rental Program / Fleet | Rental Program Manager | Per event |
| 3 | Document the event end-to-end (telematics snapshot, photos, police report, statements, workshop damage estimate) into the claim file (W4762); preserve evidence per Legal/records (VS-88/VS-100); if the customer was at-fault and LDW declined, trigger deductible recovery (W4756/W4762); if a third party was at-fault, prepare subrogation (W4762) | Rental Program / LP / Legal | Rental Program Manager / Legal | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Telematics event alert (W4749); insurance claim (VS-26); maintenance/workshop (W4750/VS-31); safety incident (VS-24); legal/records (VS-100/VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Injury-liability risk**: An accident causing bodily injury → catastrophic liability; mitigated by immediate emergency/Safety/Legal/Insurance escalation and the umbrella cover (W4748)
- **Evidence-loss risk**: Missing scene evidence → failed insurance/subrogation recovery (W4762); mitigated by the telematics snapshot + photo protocol
- **Customer-stranding risk**: A stranded customer with no substitute vehicle → service failure; mitigated by substitute-vehicle provisioning

### Time Estimate
- ~per event 2–8 hrs active response + documentation; ~recovery absorbed in W4762

### Automation Opportunity
- Telematics auto-crash-alert with location to dispatcher + Insurance; mobile damage-estimate capture; substitute-vehicle auto-assignment

### Controls
- CTL: serious events escalated to Safety/Legal/Insurance immediately; scene evidence (telematics + photos + police report) captured before vehicle moves

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (safety), VS-26 (insurance), VS-23 (LP), VS-100/VS-88 (legal/records), W4749 (telematics), W4750 (substitute vehicle), W4762 (claim)

| **Volume** | ~claims as per W4761 event rate |

| **Participants** | Insurer/broker, Finance (VS-17), Legal (VS-100), Customer, Workshop (VS-31) |
| **Steps** |

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the claim with the insurer (CTPL for third-party bodily/property; comprehensive for own-damage/theft per W4748); submit the evidence file (W4761), workshop estimate (VS-31), and the agreement/LDW election (W4755/W4757); track to settlement | Risk & Insurance / Workshop | Risk & Insurance Lead | Per claim |
| 2 | Settle the customer-deductible: if LDW taken and terms met → no customer charge; if LDW declined/at-fault → capture the deductible from the deposit (W4756/W4759) or invoice the customer; reconcile deductible vs repair cost vs insurer settlement; if total-loss, retire the vehicle (W4752) and settle actual-cash-value | Risk & Insurance / Finance | Risk & Insurance Lead / Finance | Per claim |
| 3 | Where a third party is at-fault, pursue subrogation/recovery via Legal (VS-100) against the third party/their insurer; manage unrecovered balances; feed claim outcomes to the loss-experience re-pricing (W4748) and analytics (W4768) | Risk & Insurance / Legal | Risk & Insurance Lead / Legal | Per claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance claims (VS-26 PA-26.3 W862/W1565); rental billing/deposit (W4756/W4760); GL (VS-17); legal (VS-100); workshop (VS-31)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unrecovered-deductible risk**: Customer disputes or insufficient deposit → fleet absorbs; mitigated by the deposit level = deductible (W4748/W4756) and clear terms
- **Subrogation-failure risk**: At-fault third party not pursued → loss; mitigated by the Legal subrogation step
- **Claim-leakage risk**: Overstated estimates or duplicate recovery; mitigated by workshop estimate validation and finance recon

### Time Estimate
- ~per claim 1–6 weeks to settle depending on complexity

### Automation Opportunity
- Claim file auto-assembly from telematics/agreement/workshop; deductible auto-capture-from-deposit; loss-ratio dashboard feeding W4748 re-pricing

### Controls
- CTL: deductible collected per LDW election; insurer claim filed with full evidence; subrogation pursued for at-fault third parties; finance-recon

### Cross-references

## W4763. Vehicle Non-Return, Theft & Fraud Recovery (Geofence-Breach Response)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vehicle past scheduled return with no check-in; geofence breach alert (W4749); do-not-rent/fraud signal (VS-125) |
| **Frequency** | Per event (rare) |
| **Volume** | ~non-return/theft events benchmarked <0.5% of rentals |
| **Owner** | LP Director (VS-23) / Rental Program |
| **Participants** | LP (VS-23), Legal (VS-100), Police, Insurance (VS-26), Telematics (W4749) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Detect non-return/geofence-breach from telematics (W4749) at the grace-period expiry; attempt customer contact (per agreement W4755); if no response or confirmed unauthorized use → escalate to LP (VS-23), begin recovery, and notify Insurance (theft claim W4748) and Legal (VS-100) | LP / Rental Program | LP Director | Per event (urgent) |
| 2 | Use telematics GPS to locate and recover the vehicle with police assistance (VS-23 coordinates law-enforcement per VS-159 W4694 pattern); file a police report (theft/estafa under the Revised Penal Code / RA 10951); recover via the deposit (W4756) and pursue the customer legally for unpaid rental, damage, and costs | LP / Legal / Police | LP Director / Legal | Per event |
| 3 | Add the offender to the do-not-rent watch-list (W4754); recover via insurance if unrecovered (theft claim W4748/W4762); retire the vehicle from the register if not recovered (W4752); feed the event to fraud analytics (VS-125) and the do-not-rent screen | LP / Legal / Insurance | LP Director / Legal | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Telematics GPS recovery (W4749); LP/investigation (VS-23); police coordination (VS-159 pattern); insurance theft claim (VS-26/W4748); legal (VS-100); do-not-rent list (W4754)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Asset-loss risk**: A vehicle not recovered → ~PHP 1.5–3M asset write-off per pickup/van; mitigated by telematics + police recovery + insurance theft cover
- **Fraud-pattern risk**: Organized rental-fraud rings; mitigated by do-not-rent screen (W4754) + VS-125 fraud intelligence sharing
- **Recovery-safety risk**: Confronting a thief; mitigated by police-led recovery (never self-help)

### Time Estimate
- ~per event hours-to-days for recovery; ~legal recovery weeks-months

### Automation Opportunity
- Telematics auto-non-return-alert + GPS recovery workflow; do-not-rent auto-screen (W4754); fraud-pattern detection (VS-125)

### Controls
- CTL: non-return escalated within grace period; police-led recovery; offender watch-listed; insurance/legal recovery pursued

### Cross-references
- links to VS-23 (LP), VS-125 (fraud), VS-159 (security/law-enforcement pattern), VS-26 (theft insurance), VS-100 (legal), W4749 (telematics/GPS), W4754 (do-not-rent), W4756 (deposit), W4752 (write-off)

## W4764. Traffic Violation, Citation & Toll Violation Handling (Customer Recharge)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Citation/toll notice received by BuildRight (registered owner) for a vehicle during a customer's rental |
| **Frequency** | Per notice |
| **Volume** | ~notices benchmarked ~1–5% of rentals (speeding, no-contact-apprehension, parking, toll) |
| **Owner** | Rental Program / Legal-Compliance (VS-100/VS-22) |
| **Participants** | LTO/MMDA/toll operator, Customer, Finance (W4760), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive the citation/toll notice addressed to BuildRight (registered owner); match the notice date/time + plate to the rental record (telematics W4749 + agreement W4755) to identify the customer who was driving; verify via telematics that the vehicle was on-rent to that customer at the time | Rental Program / Compliance | Rental Program Manager / Legal | Per notice |
| 2 | Recharge the penalty + an administrative fee to the customer per the agreement (W4755): capture from the deposit (W4756) or invoice/bill the customer (W4760); contest the notice if telematics proves the vehicle was elsewhere (false-apprehension defense); pay the uncontested penalty timely to avoid BuildRight liability (registered-owner default) | Rental Program / Finance / Legal | Rental Program Manager / Legal | Per notice |
| 3 | Manage the no-contact-apprehension program (MMDA/LTO NCAP) and toll (RFID) reconciliation; report repeat-offender customers to the do-not-rent list (W4754); track notice volume as a driver-behavior KPI (links W4768) | Rental Program / Compliance | Rental Program Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental/telematics matching (W4749/W4755); billing/deposit (W4756/W4760); toll/NCAP operator integration; legal/compliance (VS-100/VS-22)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Owner-liability risk**: As registered owner, BuildRight is liable for unpaid citations → default judgment/registration hold (W4747); mitigated by timely payment + customer recharge
- **Unrecovered-recharge risk**: Customer disputes/absconds → BuildRight absorbs the fine; mitigated by deposit-capture and agreement terms
- **False-apprehension risk**: Paying a notice the customer didn't incur, or recharging the wrong customer; mitigated by telematics verification before recharge

### Time Estimate
- ~per notice 15–45 min; ~uncontested penalties paid within the LGU/LTO SLA

### Automation Opportunity
- Notice→rental-record auto-match (telematics date/time + plate + agreement); auto-recharge to deposit/billing; toll/NCAP batch reconciliation; repeat-offender auto-watchlist

### Controls
- CTL: notices matched to rental within SLA; uncontested paid timely (owner-liability); customer recharged per agreement; repeat offenders watchlisted

### Cross-references
- links to VS-22 (compliance), VS-100 (legal), VS-79 (BIR rental revenue), W4747 (owner-liability), W4749 (telematics matching), W4754 (do-not-rent), W4755/W4756/W4760 (recharge)

## W4766. Rental Regulatory Compliance — LTO/LTFRB, DOLE Driver Fitness, Consumer Act

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual compliance review; regulatory change; audit (VS-21/VS-22); incident (W4761) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | 1 compliance program across the rental fleet/operations |
| **Owner** | Rental Program / Compliance (VS-22) / Legal (VS-100) |
| **Participants** | Compliance (VS-22), Legal (VS-100), LTO/LTFRB, Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the regulatory map for self-haul vehicle rental: LTO motor-vehicle registration (W4747) + CTPL mandate (RA 10006, W4748); the BuildRight-vs-customer liability structure under the Civil Code + Land Transportation rules; Consumer Act (RA 7394) on rental-disclosure, pricing transparency, and unfair-contract terms (links VS-469); BIR VAT/e-invoicing on rental revenue (VS-79 W4760); and the distinction from LTFRB cargo-freight licensing (VS-06) — a customer self-drive rental is not a for-hire/freight operation | Compliance / Legal | Rental Program Manager / Legal | Annual + on change |
| 2 | Ensure the rental agreement (W4755), pricing disclosure (W4745/W4760), LDW language (W4757), and telematics consent (W4755/W4767) comply with consumer-protection and contract standards; review annually with Legal; assure via Internal Audit (VS-21 W344 trade-promo/spend or W339 regulatory audit pattern) | Compliance / Legal / Internal Audit | Rental Program Manager / Legal | Annual |
| 3 | Manage regulator interactions (LTO registration audits, DTI consumer complaints VS-469, BIR rental-revenue scrutiny VS-79), incident regulatory reporting (W4761), and officer personal-liability awareness (registered-owner liability); maintain the records (VS-88) | Compliance / Legal | Rental Program Manager / Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/legal (VS-22/VS-100); records (VS-88); audit (VS-21); BIR (VS-79); DTI escalation (VS-469)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Liability-structure risk**: Misclassifying the rental (e.g., as for-hire freight) → wrong regulatory regime + LTFRB exposure; mitigated by the legal classification
- **Consumer-Act risk**: Unclear pricing/terms → DTI complaint (VS-469) + penalty; mitigated by transparent disclosure
- **Officer-liability risk**: Registered-owner defaults (unpaid citations/unregistered release) → officer personal liability; mitigated by the release gate (W4758/W4759) and timely citation handling (W4764)

### Time Estimate
- ~annual review 1–2 weeks; ~continuous absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- Compliance-obligation calendar; regulator-interaction log; agreement/pricing transparency audit checklist

### Controls
- CTL: rental classified correctly (self-drive, not for-hire freight); agreement/pricing/LDW/consent Legally reviewed annually; assured by Internal Audit

### Cross-references
- links to VS-22 (compliance), VS-100 (legal), VS-21 (audit), VS-79 (BIR), VS-469 (DTI), VS-88 (records), W4747/W4748/W4755/W4757 (regulated artifacts)

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Handle Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR) per RA 10173 (VS-91.2 W271): access, correction, erasure (where legally permissible — note BIR retention conflicts), and objection; manage telematics-consent withdrawal and the do-not-rent list data minimization; coordinate with the DPO | Privacy / Rental Program | DPO | Per DSAR |

### Time Estimate

### Automation Opportunity
