## W3177. Lease Portfolio Composition & Concentration Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly portfolio review; ALM cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly/quarterly |
| **Volume** | Whole portfolio |
| **Owner** | Lease Portfolio Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Portfolio Mgr, Finance, Risk, BI (links to VS-28), Treasury (links to VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BI (links to VS-28) compute portfolio composition: outstanding balance, # of leases, average ticket, by segment (trade/corporate/government), asset category, geography, tenor, lessor partner (W3165), lease type | BI Analyst | Lease Portfolio Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | Lease Portfolio Mgr/Risk analyze concentration vs. limits: top-customer concentration, single-segment, single-asset-category, single-region; flag breaches and recommend rebalancing (origination tilt, partner diversification) | Lease Portfolio Mgr / Risk | VP Finance | Monthly |
| 3 | Finance/Treasury (links to VS-18) report portfolio metrics to ALCO/Finance Committee (links to VS-36); mature/amortize portfolio projections; align funding to asset profile (W3181) | Finance Analyst / Treasury | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Portfolio/BI dashboards (links to VS-28); treasury/ALM (links to VS-18); governance (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Concentration risk**: Adverse event in single segment/region impairs portfolio; mitigated by limits and diversification


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-analyze of "analyze concentration vs." (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-file of "file" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: limits and diversification

### Time Estimate
- 1 week per monthly cycle

## W3183. Lease Tax, VAT & Withholding Treatment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Billing cycle (W3170); tax filing; BIR inquiry |
| **Frequency** | Per transaction + monthly/quarterly filing |
| **Volume** | All lease transactions |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Mgr, Finance, Lease Operations Mgr, External Tax Counsel |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Mgr structure lease taxation per lease type and BIR rules: operating lease rentals = VATable lease income; finance lease = separate treatment of principal/interest; creditable withholding tax (EWT/CWT) on rental payments by customer; document tax positions (links to VS-79) | Tax Manager | Tax Manager | Setup + ongoing |
| 2 | System compute per-invoice: VAT (12%) on lease rental, EWT (1–10% per BIR table based on nature), any percentage tax; generate BIR-compliant invoice and Form 2307 for customer withholding (links to VS-79) | System / Finance | Tax Manager | Per transaction |
| 3 | Tax Mgr remit VAT/EWT to BIR (links to VS-79); reconcile creditable withholding credits; file income tax on lease profit; defend on BIR audit (links to VS-21); issue annual tax documents to customers | Tax Manager / Finance | Tax Manager | Monthly/quarterly/annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Tax engine (links to VS-79); e-invoicing (links to VS-79); GL (links to VS-17); audit (links to VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Tax-mis-classification risk**: Wrong treatment triggers BIR assessment and customer dispute; mitigated by structured tax design and external counsel review


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "document tax positions" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "generate BIR-compliant invoice and Form 2307 for customer withholding" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-file of "file income tax on lease profit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: structured tax design and external counsel review

### Time Estimate
- Setup 1–2 weeks; ongoing monthly
