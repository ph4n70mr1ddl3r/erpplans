## Overview

Equipment Leasing & Capital Equipment Finance workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — governing
the B2B **lease / lease-to-own / capital-equipment finance** products offered to BuildRight's
trade, project, corporate, and government customers (~5,200 trade + ~200 corporate accounts)
seeking to acquire expensive equipment (generators, compressors, scaffolding systems, large
power tools, material handling equipment, HVAC, solar/storage systems — links to VS-70) over
multi-year terms rather than purchasing outright.

This is distinct from **VS-12 PA-12.2 (Tool Rental)** which handles short-term rental
(days/weeks, operational expense), from **VS-38 (Consumer Credit & Financing)** which handles
consumer POS installment financing, and from **VS-18 (Treasury)** which handles BuildRight's own
financing. This value stream owns the **B2B lease/finance operating model**: lease product design
and pricing, customer credit underwriting and origination, lessor-partner / funding-source
management (BuildRight as originator; bank/leasing-company as lessor), lease booking, billing,
and asset lifecycle (insurance, maintenance, modification, maturity, disposition), lease
portfolio risk and residual-value management, regulatory compliance (BSP-leasing rules, Truth in
Lending Act, BIR VAT/withholding on lease rentals), and lease-portfolio yield analytics.
