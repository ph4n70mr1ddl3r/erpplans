## W5073. LPG Daily Safety Inspection, Leak Detection & BFP Fire-Code Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily cage cycle (W5067); incident |
| **Frequency** | Daily + continuous |
| **Volume** | ~200 stores × daily inspection |
| **Owner** | Cage Associate / HSE (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | Cage Associate, HSE (VS-24), Store Manager, Facilities (VS-138), BFP/fire consultant |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daily leak & condition inspection: sniff/soap-test/detector check on full cylinders & valves, cage condition (ventilation, signage, max-cylinder limit W5061), and detection-alarm function | Cage Associate / HSE | Store Manager / HSE Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Verify BFP fire-code compliance: extinguisher/suppression readiness, egress, ignition-control (no-smoking signage), FSIC currency (VS-166), and cage/yard integrity | HSE / Facilities | HSE Manager | Daily + periodic |
| 3 | Log & escalate: inspection log, flag any defect for condemnation (W5069) or maintenance (VS-138), and escalate leaks/incidents to emergency response (W5075) | Cage Associate / HSE | Store Manager | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE/inspection log (VS-24); gas-leak sensor IoT (W5061); facility/CMMS (VS-138); BFP FSIC register (VS-166); cage inventory (W5067)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Undetected-leak risk**: Slow leak → ignition/fire; mitigated by daily detection + IoT sensors (W5061)
- **Compliance-lapse risk**: Expired FSIC/fire-system deficiency → BFP violation/closure; mitigated by daily/periodic checks (VS-166)
- **Inspection-failure risk**: Inspections skipped/log-falsified; mitigated by HSE audit (VS-21) + task mgmt (VS-63)

### Time Estimate
- ~20–40 min/store/day inspection

### Automation Opportunity
- Gas-leak IoT → auto-alarm/shutdown; inspection mobile-app checklist; FSIC-renewal auto-trigger (VS-166)

### Controls
- CTL: daily inspection logged (VS-24); FSIC current (VS-166); max-cylinder limit enforced; detection-alarm functional

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-138 (IFM), VS-166 (permits), VS-21 (audit), VS-63 (task mgmt); W5061 (cage/infrastructure), W5067 (cage ops), W5069 (condemnation), W5075 (emergency)

## W5074. Cylinder Inventory Reconciliation, Shrink & Deposit/Float Accounting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily/weekly/monthly reconciliation cycle |
| **Frequency** | Daily/weekly/monthly |
| **Volume** | ~all stores × cylinder/deposit reconciliation |
| **Owner** | Inventory/O2C (VS-05/VS-16) / Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Inventory (VS-05), O2C (VS-16), Cage Associate, FP&A (VS-17.4), LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reconcile cylinder inventory: scan-count full/empty/quarantined cylinders vs system (VS-151), investigate variances, and flag shrink (theft/loss) to LP (VS-23) | Cage Associate / Inventory / LP | Store Manager / Inventory Mgr | Weekly/Monthly |
| 2 | Reconcile cash & deposit float: exchange/refill cash vs POS (VS-08/VS-16), deposit-collected vs -refunded, and the cash-chain integrity (links VS-142) | O2C / Cashier / LP | O2C Manager | Daily/Monthly |
| 3 | Report & reserve: shrink/variance reporting, deposit-float GL reconciliation (VS-17), bad-debt/shrink provision, and FP&A analytics (VS-17.4) | O2C / FP&A / Inventory | O2C Manager / FP&A | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Cylinder-asset/inventory (VS-151/VS-05); POS/cash (VS-08); O2C/AR (VS-16); LP case (VS-23); GL/R2R (VS-17); FP&A (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cylinder-shrink risk**: Pool cylinders stolen/lost → asset loss; mitigated by scan-reconciliation + LP (VS-23)
- **Cash/deposit-leakage risk**: Mis-handled exchange cash/deposit differential; mitigated by POS automation + cash controls (VS-142)

### Time Estimate
- ~weekly cycle; ~monthly deep reconciliation

### Automation Opportunity
- Scan-count vs system auto-reconciliation; deposit-float GL automation; variance/LP auto-alert

### Controls
- CTL: cylinder inventory reconciled (VS-151/VS-05); cash-chain controls (VS-142); deposit-float GL-reconciled (VS-17); shrink provisioned

### Cross-references
- links to VS-05 (inventory), VS-08 (POS), VS-16 (O2C), VS-17/VS-17.4 (R2R/FP&A), VS-23 (LP), VS-142 (cash); W5060 (asset), W5065 (exchange), W5076 (asset reconciliation)

## W5075. LPG Incident, Leak, Fire & Emergency Response & Reporting (DOLE/BFP/DOE)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Event-driven (rare but high-severity) |
| **Volume** | ~few incidents/year target (zero tolerance) |
| **Owner** | HSE Manager (VS-24) / Store Manager |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), Security/LP (VS-23/VS-159), Store Manager, BC/DR (VS-26), Legal (VS-100), Facilities (VS-138), authorities (BFP/DOLE/DOE) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Report to authorities: BFP (fire), DOLE/OSH (workplace injury), DOE (LPG incident), LGU, and insurer (VS-26), within statutory deadlines (links VS-22/VS-166) | HSE / Legal / Compliance | HSE Manager / Compliance Officer | Per regulation |
| 3 | Investigate, remedy & learn: root-cause (links VS-24/VS-133), corrective action, insurance claim (VS-26), record retention (VS-88), and distribution of lessons (W5064) | HSE / Internal Audit / Legal | HSE Manager | Post-incident |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE/incident management (VS-24); BC/DR (VS-26); LP/corporate security (VS-23/VS-159); compliance/reporting (VS-22/VS-166); insurance/claims (VS-26); records (VS-88); root-cause/CI (VS-133)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Catastrophic-incident risk**: Leak/fire/explosion causing injury/property loss; mitigated by prevention (W5064/W5073) + response plan
- **Reporting-breach risk**: Late/incomplete authority notification → penalty/officer liability; mitigated by statutory-deadline reporting (VS-22)
- **Repetition risk**: Root cause not remedied; mitigated by investigation + corrective action (VS-133)

### Time Estimate

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL: emergency plan executed & tested (W5064); authority reporting within deadlines (VS-22); incident investigated & remedied (VS-133/VS-24); records retained (VS-88)

### Cross-references

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### System Touchpoints

- **Asset/float-mismatch risk**: Cylinder pool vs deposit-float GL out of balance → mis-statement; mitigated by monthly reconciliation + audit (VS-21)
