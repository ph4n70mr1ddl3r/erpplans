# VS-175: Propane, LPG Cylinder Exchange & Gas Refill Operations

> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Propane, liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) cylinder-exchange and gas-refill operations for BuildRight
Depot Corp. — owning the **customer-facing flammable-gas cylinder program** that spans the
merchandise BuildRight sells (BBQ/patio grills, patio heaters, camping stoves, plumbing torches,
soldering/brazing, forklifts) and one of the Philippines' largest consumer-fuel markets: LPG
program & business-model strategy; bulk LPG supply, vendor and pricing; the **returnable cylinder
fleet, standardization and RA 10862 (LPG Industry Regulatory Act) compliance**; cylinder cage/yard
design and BFP Fire Code storage infrastructure; LPG retail pricing, exchange fee and refill
tariff; **DOE retailer accreditation, DTI-BPS cylinder product standards and metrology
compliance**; the HAZMAT/safety program; the **customer cylinder-exchange (full-for-empty)
transaction at POS**; the customer-owned-cylinder refill service; refill/re-valve/hydrostatic-test
vendor coordination; in-store cage daily operations; cylinder leak/damage inspection and
condemnation; fleet replenishment and inter-store rebalancing; delivery/exchange-at-home and
forklift/bulk-account service; **daily BFP/OSH safety inspection and emergency response**;
cylinder deposit/float and asset reconciliation; price/promotion and margin analytics; cylinder
turn, refill yield and utilization analytics; and demand seasonality and program ROI analytics.

This matters at BuildRight's scale and product mix. BuildRight sells the appliances and equipment
that *consume* propane/LPG — BBQ grills and patio heaters (Seasonal/Outdoor), camping stoves
(Outdoor), plumbing/brazing torches (Plumbing/Tools), and forklifts that run on LPG (Material
Handling) — and the Philippines is one of the world's largest LPG markets (cooking fuel for the
majority of households), so cylinder exchange (the "Blue Rhino" full-for-empty model) and
customer-owned-cylinder refill are recognized, high-velocity, recurring big-box services. At even
~50–100 exchange transactions per store per week across 200 stores plus a forklift/bulk-account
base, the program moves hundreds of thousands of cylinders per year with material deposit-float
and a returnable-cylinder asset pool worth tens of millions of pesos. Critically, LPG is a
**flammable gas stored under pressure** — the program carries acute fire/explosion, OSH (DOLE),
BFP Fire Code, DOE retailer-accreditation, DTI-BPS cylinder-standard, DENR empty-cylinder, and
LGU-permit exposure, and a single mishandled cylinder is a catastrophic safety incident (links
VS-24 HSE, VS-114 DG/Hazmat, VS-147 customer premises safety).

Today this discipline is **unowned as a unified program** — the defining terms "propane", "LPG",
"cylinder exchange", "gas refill", "LP gas", "cylinder fleet", and "cylinder deposit" each appear
in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers and in **zero** VS directory names; the only
adjacent reference is the single **W1063** (Store-Level Customer Welding Gas Cylinder Exchange &
Refill Service) buried in **VS-09.1 (Custom Fabrication & Processing)**, which covers *industrial
welding gas* for the fabrication service, not the consumer/retail LPG program. No value stream
owns the **LPG cylinder-exchange & refill operating model** — the bulk supply, the returnable
cylinder fleet, the BFP-rated cage/yard, the exchange/refill transaction, the deposit/float
accounting, and the safety program. It is **not** covered by **VS-09 (In-Store Services)**, which
covers *welding-gas refill for the fabrication counter* (W1063) — this owns the *retail LPG
program* sold to consumers and trade. It is **not** covered by **VS-114 (DG/Hazmat)**, which owns
*transport/regulatory compliance* for dangerous goods — this *operates the in-store cylinder
exchange/refill service* (and links to VS-114 for the DG dimension). It is **not** covered by
**VS-111 (Packaging/Pallet/RTI)**, which tracks *pallets* — this tracks the *returnable LPG
cylinder pool* (a deposit-bearing, safety-regulated, RA 10862-standardized asset). It is **not**
covered by **VS-24 (HSE)** or **VS-147 (Customer Safety)**, which own *safety policy/premises
liability* — this operates the *gas service* that must comply with them. And it is **not** covered
by **VS-12 (Installation)** or **VS-38 (Consumer Credit)** — this is a *physical-goods exchange
service with a deposit float*, not an installation or a financing product.

This value stream owns the end-to-end program: LPG strategy & business model; bulk supply, vendor
and pricing; cylinder fleet strategy, standardization, branding and **RA 10862 compliance**;
cylinder procurement, ownership/pool, asset tracking and lifecycle; cage/yard design and **BFP
Fire Code / safety infrastructure**; retail pricing, exchange fee and refill tariff; **DOE retailer
accreditation, DTI-BPS product standards and metrology (weights & measures) compliance**; HAZMAT
handling, PPE and emergency-response program design; the **full-for-empty exchange transaction at
POS**; refill/re-valve/hydrostatic-test vendor coordination; in-store cage daily operations;
customer-owned-cylinder refill (weigh/fill/seal/charge); cylinder leak/damage inspection,
condemnation and quarantine; fleet replenishment, inter-store transfer and rebalancing;
exchange-at-home, will-call and forklift/bulk-account delivery; **daily BFP/OSH safety inspection
and emergency response**; cylinder deposit, float and asset reconciliation; price/promotion and
margin analytics; cylinder turn, refill yield and utilization analytics; demand seasonality; and
program performance, compliance-audit and ROI analytics. It integrates tightly with VS-09 (in-store
service counter), VS-114 (DG/Hazmat), VS-24 (HSE), VS-147 (customer/premises safety), VS-111
(returnable-asset pattern), VS-06/VS-56 (cylinder logistics), VS-08 (POS exchange), VS-04 (DC
hazmat storage), VS-23 (LP), VS-79 (DOE/BIR excise), VS-115 (scale/metrology calibration), VS-166
(permits), and VS-26 (BC/DR).
