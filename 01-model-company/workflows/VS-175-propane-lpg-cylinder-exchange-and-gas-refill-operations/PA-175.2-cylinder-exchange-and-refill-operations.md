## W5065. Customer Cylinder Exchange Transaction & POS Workflow (Full-for-Empty)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer exchange request at store/counter |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~5,000–20,000 exchanges/week at maturity |
| **Volume** | 1 exchange transaction per customer |
| **Owner** | Store Services Lead / Cashier (VS-08) |
| **Participants** | Cashier (VS-08), Store Associate, Cage/Stock Associate (VS-07), LP (VS-23 — deposit integrity) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake the empty & validate: inspect the customer's empty cylinder (size/condition/brand per interchange policy W5059), scan the cylinder ID (VS-151), and confirm exchange eligibility (not condemned — W5069) | Store Associate / Cage Associate | Store Services Lead | Per transaction |
| 2 | Exchange & charge: swap empty-for-full at the cage, process the exchange fee at POS (price W5062), handle the deposit differential, and capture the full-cylinder ID out / empty-cylinder ID in | Cashier / Cage Associate | Store Services Lead | Per transaction |
| 3 | Customer handoff & safety: brief the customer on safe transport/storage (no enclosed vehicle cabin, upright, away from heat), issue receipt, and update cage inventory (W5067) | Store Associate | Store Services Lead | Per transaction |

### System Touchpoints
- POS/exchange module (VS-08); cylinder-asset scan (VS-151); pricing (W5062); deposit/float (W5076); cage inventory (W5067); O2C (VS-16)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Condemned-cylinder risk**: Accepting an unsafe cylinder into the pool; mitigated by intake inspection (W5069)
- **Deposit-leakage risk**: Mis-handled deposit differential/cash; mitigated by POS automation + LP controls (VS-23)
- **Transport-safety risk**: Customer transporting unsafely (enclosed cabin); mitigated by safety briefing

### Time Estimate
- ~5–8 min per exchange; ~5,000–20,000/week

### Automation Opportunity
- Barcode scan-in/out (VS-151); exchange-fee auto-calc; deposit-differential automation

### Controls
- CTL-756 (ensure controlled execution — Cylinder Exchange & Refill Operations (PA-175.2).)
- CTL: empty-cylinder intake-inspected (W5069); deposit differential POS-controlled; cylinder IDs scanned (VS-151)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08 (POS), VS-151 (auto-ID), VS-23 (LP), VS-16 (O2C); W5059 (interchange), W5062 (price), W5067 (cage), W5069 (condemnation), W5076 (deposit)

| **Frequency** | Continuous (replenishment cycle) |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of cylinders per refill cycle |
| **Owner** | Logistics/Fleet (VS-06) / Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Coordinate refill/re-valve/hydrostatic-test: vendor fills to spec, re-vals/re-quals per schedule (W5060), certifies (DTI-BPS/RA 10617 — VS-117/W5059), and returns full/certified cylinders | Refill Vendor / Logistics / HSE | Logistics Manager | Per cycle |

### Time Estimate

## W5068. Customer-Owned-Cylinder Refill Service (Weigh, Fill, Seal, Charge)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer brings own cylinder for refill (not exchange) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; demand-driven |
| **Volume** | ~1,000–5,000 refills/week at maturity |
| **Owner** | Store Services Lead / Cage Associate |
| **Participants** | Cage Associate, Cashier (VS-08), Metrology/Calibration (VS-115), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake & inspect the customer cylinder: confirm ownership/condition, check re-qual/expiry & valve safety (condemn if unsafe — W5069), and weigh tare/empty | Cage Associate / HSE | Store Services Lead | Per refill |
| 2 | Fill to spec & certify: fill by calibrated weight (VS-115) to the cylinder's rated capacity (no overfill), seal, and apply fill/date tag | Cage Associate / Metrology | Store Services Lead | Per refill |
| 3 | Charge & handoff: charge per-kg (tariff W5062), process at POS (VS-08), brief customer on safe transport, and record the refill (analytics W5078) | Cashier / Cage Associate | Store Services Lead | Per refill |

### System Touchpoints
- Refill scale (calibrated — VS-115); POS (VS-08); pricing/tariff (W5062); HSE (VS-24); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Overfill risk**: Filling beyond rated capacity → rupture/explosion; mitigated by calibrated weight fill + rated-capacity control (VS-115)
- **Unsafe-cylinder risk**: Refilling an expired/damaged cylinder; mitigated by intake inspection/condemnation (W5069)
- **Metrology risk**: Scale inaccuracy → under/over-charge, consumer-protection violation; mitigated by calibration (VS-115)

### Time Estimate
- ~10–15 min per refill

### Automation Opportunity
- Scale-fill automation with rated-capacity cutoff; per-kg POS auto-charge; calibration scheduling (VS-115)

### Controls
- CTL-756 (ensure controlled execution — Cylinder Exchange & Refill Operations (PA-175.2).)
- CTL: fill by calibrated weight to rated capacity (VS-115); unsafe cylinders condemned (W5069); tariff RA 7394-compliant (VS-118)

### Cross-references

## W5069. Cylinder Leak/Damage Inspection, Condemnation & Out-of-Service Quarantine

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Intake (W5065/W5068); periodic inspection; suspected leak/damage |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (per intake) + periodic |
| **Volume** | ~small % of pool cylinders condemned per year |
| **Owner** | Cage Associate / HSE (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | Cage Associate, HSE (VS-24), Logistics (VS-06), Supplier (disposal — VS-73) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inspect for leak/damage: visual (dents/rust/valve/base), leak-test (soap/water or detector), and re-qual/expiry check (W5060) at intake and periodically | Cage Associate / HSE | Store Services Lead / HSE | Per intake + periodic |
| 2 | Condemn & quarantine: tag out-of-service, move to a designated quarantine area (venting safely), and remove from the active pool (asset record update) | Cage Associate / HSE | HSE Manager | Per case |
| 3 | Dispose/recycle: route condemned cylinders to certified scrap/recycling (VS-73) or vendor destruction per RA 10617, and reconcile the asset record (W5060) | HSE / Logistics / Supplier | HSE Manager | Per batch |

### System Touchpoints
- Cylinder-asset record (VS-151/VS-05); HSE (VS-24); waste/disposal (VS-73); DG (VS-114); logistics (VS-06)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unsafe-cylinder-in-service risk**: Condemned cylinder re-entering the pool → safety hazard; mitigated by quarantine + asset-record lock
- **Venting risk**: Improper handling of leaking cylinder → ignition; mitigated by venting SOP + quarantine
- **Disposal-compliance risk**: Improper scrap of pressure vessels (DENR); mitigated by certified disposal (VS-73)

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 min inspection/intake; per-case condemnation

### Automation Opportunity
- Condemnation status → asset-record lock; quarantine-area inventory tracking; disposal-batch documentation

### Controls
- CTL-100 (ensure LPG cylinder safety & deposit accounting.)
- CTL: condemned cylinders quarantined & asset-locked; disposal per VS-73/RA 10617; intake inspection enforced

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-73 (disposal), VS-114 (DG), VS-151 (asset); W5060 (lifecycle), W5065 (exchange intake), W5068 (refill intake), W5076 (reconciliation)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forecast & trigger replenishment: from cage stock levels (W5067) and demand forecast (W5079), trigger full-cylinder replenishment to low-stock stores | Inventory / Logistics / Analytics | Logistics Manager | Per cycle |
| 2 | Replenish via DC/refill loop: route through the refill vendor loop (W5066) and DC (VS-04), dispatch full cylinders to stores, and rebalance empties back | Logistics / DC Ops | Logistics Manager | Per cycle |
