## W3129. Social Impact Measurement & Beneficiary Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly impact cycle; coop onboarding; program milestone |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + event-driven |
| **Volume** | All active coop suppliers and beneficiaries |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Lead |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Lead, Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, Coop Representatives, Impact Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Lead defines impact KPIs aligned to recognized frameworks (GRI, IRIS+, SDGs): livelihoods supported, direct beneficiaries (coop members + dependents), fair-wage premium paid vs. prevailing, training hours delivered, community projects co-funded | Sustainability Lead / Impact Analyst | VP Sustainability | Annual design |
| 2 | Impact Analyst collects verified data per coop: member roster, wage/earnings uplift (baseline vs. current), training completion, infrastructure/equipment invested; reconcile to BuildRight payments (W3126) and capacity programs (W3117) | Impact Analyst / Coop | Sustainability Lead | Quarterly |
| 3 | Sustainability Lead publishes impact scorecard into BuildRight ESG system (links to VS-25); auditable evidence retained (records — links to VS-88); feeds external reporting (W3130, W3132) | Sustainability Lead | VP Sustainability | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Impact register; ESG scorecard (links to VS-25); records retention (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-credibility risk**: Self-reported coop data may be inflated; mitigated by triangulation, sampling audit, and third-party verification (W3130)


### Automation Opportunity
- auto-reconcile (infrastructure/equipment invested; reconcile to buildright payments)
- auto-record (evidence retained (records — links to)

### Controls
operational: triangulation, sampling audit, and third-party verification (W3130)
### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per quarterly cycle

## W3130. Fair-Trade Certification & Third-Party Audit

| **Frequency** | Annual + per-cycle |
| **Volume** | Per coop / BuildRight program-level |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Lead |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Lead, Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, Third-Party Auditor, Coop Representatives, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Lead selects appropriate certification (WFTO, local fair-trade body, Go Lokal!, social-enterprise cert, ethical-sourcing audit); engage accredited third-party auditor | Sustainability Lead | VP Sustainability | Per cycle |
| 2 | Auditor conducts on-site audit: fair-wage compliance, no child/forced labor (links to VS-86), safe working conditions (links to VS-24), environmental compliance (links to VS-25/VS-73), traceability and records (links to VS-88), governance | Third-Party Auditor | Sustainability Lead | Per audit |

### Automation Opportunity
- auto-capture (go lokal!, social-enterprise cert, ethical-sourcing audit);)
- auto-record (traceability and records (links to vs-88),)

### Controls
operational: certification calendar and renewal alerts
### Time Estimate
- 2–4 weeks per audit cycle

## W3134. Indigenous Communities & Ancestral Domain (IPRA) Engagement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Indigenous-community sourcing opportunity; ancestral-domain product (handicraft, forest materials, traditional knowledge) |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Limited; high-sensitivity |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Lead |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Lead, Legal, NCIP Liaison, Indigenous Community Representatives, Sustainability Sourcing Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Lead/NCIP Liaison engage indigenous community per Indigenous Peoples Rights Act (IPRA / RA 8371): Free, Prior and Informed Consent (FPIC) through NCIP process; respect ancestral-domain sovereignty and community decision structures | Sustainability Lead / NCIP Liaison | VP Sustainability | Per engagement (3–9 months) |
| 2 | Legal structures benefit-sharing, intellectual property (traditional designs, cultural heritage), and Cultural Heritage / Traditional Knowledge agreements; ensure fair share of proceeds flows to community per NCIP-approved plan | Legal Counsel / NCIP | General Counsel | Per engagement |
| 3 | Sustainability Lead implements ongoing engagement: respect community protocols, no extraction without consent, benefit-share audit, and storytelling only with FPIC (links to W3132); report outcomes (links to W3129) | Sustainability Lead | VP Sustainability | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- FPIC/NCIP record; IP/benefit-share register (links to VS-36); impact tracker (links to W3129)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Biopiracy / cultural-appropriation risk**: Unauthorized use of traditional knowledge/design; mitigated by FPIC and IP/benefit-share agreements


### Automation Opportunity
- rule-based auto-approval (community per ncip-approved plan)
- auto-extract (protocols, no extraction without consent, benefit-share)

### Controls
operational: FPIC and IP/benefit-share agreements
### Time Estimate
- Per engagement; FPIC process 3–9 months

## W3135. Cooperative Risk, Governance & Anti-Exploitation Safeguard

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Periodic risk review; whistleblower/alert; audit finding |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + event-driven |
| **Volume** | All coop suppliers |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Lead |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Lead, Internal Audit (links to VS-21), Legal, Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, Coop Representatives |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Lead/Internal Audit (links to VS-21) screen coops for exploitation risk: coercion, middleman skim of fair-trade price, child/forced labor (links to VS-86), unsafe conditions (links to VS-24), environmental harm (links to VS-25/VS-73); whistleblower channel available | Sustainability Lead / Internal Audit | VP Sustainability | Quarterly |
| 2 | For flagged risk: investigate; engage coop leadership and members separately to detect middleman/coercion; remediate via governance support, capacity-building (W3117), or sanctions/offboarding (W3120) | Sustainability Lead / Legal | VP Sustainability | Per incident |
| 3 | Sustainability Lead reports anti-exploitation outcomes to Board/ESG committee (links to VS-36/VS-25); ensures safeguards auditable by third parties (W3130) and regulators | Sustainability Lead | VP Sustainability | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Whistleblower (links to VS-21/VS-86); audit findings; ESG governance (links to VS-36/VS-25)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden-exploitation risk**: BuildRight's fair-trade price skimmed by middleman before reaching artisans; mitigated by direct-to-beneficiary settlement (W3126) and member-level verification


### Automation Opportunity
- continuous audit (sustainability lead/internal audit (links to vs-21))
- auto-detect (separately to detect middleman/coercion; remediate via)
- auto-report (sustainability lead reports anti-exploitation outcomes to)

### Controls
operational: direct-to-beneficiary settlement (W3126) and member-level verification
### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per quarterly cycle

- auto-review (steering committee reviews roi and impact;)

### Controls
operational: balanced scorecard and willingness-to-pay research (links to VS-44)
