## W3113. Cooperative Sourcing Strategy & Category Coverage Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual category strategy; ESG/social-impact target setting (links to VS-25); new community-partnership opportunity |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 candidate categories; ~30–80 potential coop suppliers |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Sourcing Manager |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, Category Mgmt (links to VS-01), Sustainability Lead (links to VS-25), Legal, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Sourcing Manager + Category (links to VS-01) scan assortment for categories where coops/social-enterprises are competitive and impactful: garden/plants, home décor/furniture, handcrafted hardware, baskets/packaging, indigenous materials; size addressable spend and impact | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | VP Merchandising | 2–4 weeks |
| 2 | Set category-by-category coop-sourcing targets (% of category buy), fair-trade posture, and impact KPIs (livelihoods supported, beneficiaries, fair-wage premium); align to ESG roadmap (links to VS-25) | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr / Sustainability Lead | VP Sustainability | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Steering committee approves strategy and budget (capacity-building fund, advance-financing facility); publish to Category plans and integrate to S&OP demand signal (links to VS-02) | VP Merchandising / CFO | CEO | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Assortment/sourcing master (links to VS-01/VS-03); ESG scorecard (links to VS-25); budget (links to VS-33)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Greenwashing risk**: Coop-sourcing claims without substance damage brand; mitigated by traceable impact KPIs and third-party certification (W3130)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "enterprises are competitive and impactful" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-post of "posture, and impact KPIs" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves strategy and budget" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: traceable impact KPIs and third-party certification (W3130)

### Time Estimate
- 4–7 weeks/annual cycle

## W3114. Cooperative & Social Enterprise Identification & Due Diligence

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy defines a category gap; coop fair/trade mission; government livelihood program referral |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~30–80 candidates screened/year; ~10–25 onboarded |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Sourcing Manager |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, CDA-accredited Coop Dev't Specialist, Quality, Legal, Finance, Sustainability Lead |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sources of candidates: CDA registry, DTI-BMED, DSWD-SLP/DOLE livelihood graduates, WFTO/local fair-trade rosters, social-enterprise incubators, indigenous peoples' councils (NCIP), trade missions; referral from VS-25 partners | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr performs due diligence: legal status (CDA coop registration / SEC social-enterprise / DTI business name), governance, financial viability, production capacity, labor standards (no child/forced labor — links to VS-86), environmental practices, references; documents as vendor master record distinct from VS-03 mainstream | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr / Legal / Quality | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Per candidate (3–5 days) |
| 3 | Quality/Sustainability validate product fit: sample evaluation, raw-material provenance (links to VS-71 anti-counterfeit where relevant), compliance with product safety (links to VS-31/VS-89), and social impact baseline (members, wages, communities); recommend proceed/defer/decline | Quality / Sustainability Lead | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Per candidate |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor master with coop/social-enterprise flag (links to VS-29); supplier risk (links to VS-68); quality sample (links to VS-31)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-representation risk**: Coop overstates capacity/impact; mitigated by on-site visit, member rosters, and CDA/NCIP verification


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "enterprise incubators, indigenous peoples' councils" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "record distinct from VS-03 mainstream" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "validate product fit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: on-site visit, member rosters, and CDA/NCIP verification

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 days/candidate

## W3115. Cooperative Onboarding, Capability Assessment & Certification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Candidate passes due diligence |
| **Frequency** | Per onboarding (~10–25/year) |
| **Volume** | Per coop |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Sourcing Manager |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, Quality, Capacity-Building Partner, Finance, IT, Coop Representatives |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr onboards coop into vendor master with extended attributes: coop name, CDA registration #, member count, beneficiary community, governance, bank account for direct-to-beneficiary settlement, certification status; integrates to VS-03 vendor portal if applicable | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr / IT | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Per onboarding (2–3 days) |
| 2 | Quality/ Capacity-Building Partner conduct capability assessment against BuildRight product specification: production capacity (units/week), lead time, quality consistency, packaging/labeling ability, traceability, and gap list | Quality / Capacity-Building Partner | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Per onboarding (1–2 weeks) |
| 3 | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr plans certification path: target fair-trade/social-enterprise certifications (WFTO, local fair-trade, Go Lokal!, DTI-BMED), product-safety marks (PS/ICC, FDA-LTO where applicable — links to VS-22/VS-31); set timeline | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr / Regulatory | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Per onboarding |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor master with extended coop attributes (links to VS-29); certification register; quality (links to VS-31)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Capability gap risk**: Coop cannot meet spec/quality at volume; mitigated by capacity-building (W3117) and pilot-first sourcing


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count, beneficiary community, governance, bank account for…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-capture of "enterprise certifications" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: capacity-building (W3117) and pilot-first sourcing

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks per onboarding

## W3116. Cooperative Pricing, Fair-Trade Terms & Contract Negotiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Pre-contract; renewal; cost-change |
| **Frequency** | Per contract + event-driven |
| **Volume** | Per coop-contract |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Sourcing Manager |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, Category (links to VS-01), Legal, Finance, Coop Representatives |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr/Category determine fair-trade price: cost-of-sustainable-production + living-wage labor cost + fair margin (not least-cost); benchmark vs. mainstream (links to VS-01) and fair-trade floors; document fair-wage premium vs. prevailing | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr / Category | VP Merchandising | Per contract |
| 2 | Legal/Sustainability negotiate contract: price, volume commitment (minimum offtake to support coop planning), payment terms (favorable — e.g., 50% advance, balance on delivery), exclusivity/IP (indigenous designs — links to IPRA W3134), tenure, impact-reporting obligations, audit rights | Legal Counsel / Sustainability Sourcing Mgr | General Counsel | Per contract |
| 3 | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr/Finance set up direct-to-coop-account / direct-to-beneficiary settlement (links to W3126) and advance-financing facility (links to W3127); record contract and pricing in master data (links to VS-29) | Finance Analyst / IT | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Per contract |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract repository (links to VS-22/VS-36); pricing master (links to VS-01/VS-29); AP direct-pay (links to VS-15)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Price-asymmetry risk**: BuildRight imposing mainstream price discipline on coops undermines fair-trade; mitigated by living-wage pricing methodology and documented premium


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "document fair-wage premium vs." (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-report of "reporting obligations, audit rights" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record contract and pricing in master data" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: living-wage pricing methodology and documented premium

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 weeks per contract

| **Volume** | ~10–25 active coop capacity programs |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Sourcing Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capacity-Building Partner (NGO, government livelihood officer, technical trainer) delivers targeted programs: production technique, quality/standardization, packaging/labeling, business/financial literacy, digital tools; co-funded by BuildRight capacity fund or government livelihood grant | Capacity-Building Partner | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Per program (4–12 weeks) |
| 2 | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr aligns BuildRight demand plan (links to VS-02) to coop production capacity and harvest/seasonal cycle (W3123); plans production in batches that fit coop labor and lead time | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr | VP Merchandising | Per cycle |

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-report of "report progress" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: enterprise-graduation path (W3133) and multi-buyer diversification

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; 4–12 weeks per program

## W3118. Cooperative Quality Standards & Inspection Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New coop onboarding; new SKU; quality drift |
| **Frequency** | Per onboarding + periodic re-baseline |
| **Volume** | Per coop/SKU |
| **Owner** | Quality Manager |
| **Participants** | Quality, Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, Coop Representatives |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quality defines product specification: dimensions, materials, finish, packaging/labeling, lot/traceability codes, product-safety compliance (links to VS-31/VS-89); translates to visual standard and acceptance criteria understandable to coop artisans | Quality Inspector | Quality Manager | Per SKU |
| 2 | Quality delivers on-site training to coop: spec walkthrough, defect examples, self-inspection checklist, sampling plan (AQL); certifies coop's designated quality lead | Quality Inspector / Coop Quality Lead | Quality Manager | Per onboarding |
| 3 | Quality implements incoming inspection at BuildRight DC (links to VS-04 W3 inbound): AQL sampling, defect classification (critical/major/minor), disposition (accept/reject/rework), and feedback to coop for continuous improvement | Quality Inspector | Quality Manager | Per receipt |

### System Touchpoints
- Quality/spec master (links to VS-31); receiving inspection (links to VS-04); defect feedback

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Variable-quality risk**: Handcrafted output varies; mitigated by training, AQL sampling, and design-for-manufacturability input


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "checklist, sampling plan" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-inspect of "inspection at BuildRight DC" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: training, AQL sampling, and design-for-manufacturability input

### Time Estimate
- Per onboarding + per receipt

## W3120. Cooperative Offboarding, Exit & Transition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Coop non-performance; capacity exhaustion; category exit; coop graduates to mainstream vendor |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Per coop |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Sourcing Manager |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, Category, Finance, Legal, Quality, Coop Representatives |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr documents exit rationale (performance, capacity, strategic); for under-performance, provides cure period and improvement plan before termination (fair-trade duty of care) | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr | VP Merchandising | Per exit |
| 2 | Category/Planning identify replacement source (another coop, mainstream vendor — links to VS-03, or private label — links to VS-41); transition inventory and PO commitments without stranding coop (honour open POs) | Category / Planning | VP Merchandising | Per exit |
| 3 | Legal terminates contract per terms; Finance settles final dues; Sustainability Lead supports transition (graduation to mainstream, alternative buyers, or wind-down with dignity); record impact outcomes for posterity | Legal / Finance / Sustainability Lead | General Counsel | Per exit |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract/termination (links to VS-22); final settlement (links to VS-15); impact record

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Harmful-exit risk**: Sudden termination harms dependent community; mitigated by cure period, transition support, and responsible-exit policy


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "documents exit rationale" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-identify of "identify replacement source" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record impact outcomes for posterity" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
operational: cure period, transition support, and responsible-exit policy

### Time Estimate
- 4–12 weeks per exit
