## Overview

Cooperative & Community Enterprise Procurement workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — governing
the deliberate sourcing of merchandise, raw materials, and artisan goods from Philippine
**cooperatives (coops)**, **social enterprises**, **community/ livelihood enterprises**, and
**indigenous cultural communities** — across categories where these sources are competitive and
strategically valuable (garden & outdoor 3% of SKUs, home décor & furniture 5%, select
hardware/handicraft and packaging), in service of BuildRight's ESG/social-impact mandate (links
to VS-25), fair-trade positioning, and inclusive growth commitments.

This is the buy-side counterpart to VS-82 (Sari-Sari/MSME Micro-Wholesale) on the sell side.
While **VS-03** owns vendor management & procurement for mainstream vendors and **VS-41** owns
private-label development, this value stream owns the **cooperative/social-enterprise sourcing
model**: identification and due diligence of coop/social-enterprise suppliers, capacity building
and livelihood program partnerships (DSWD-SLP, DOLE livelihood, DTI-BMED, Cooperative
Development Authority registration), fair-trade pricing and direct-to-beneficiary settlement,
cooperative production-cycle and harvest calendar management, social-impact measurement, and
fair-trade/ethical-sourcing certification (e.g., WFTO, local fair-trade bodies) — distinct from
mainstream vendor onboarding (VS-03) by virtue of small-batch economics, capacity-building
obligation, advance-financing need, and impact-reporting duty.
