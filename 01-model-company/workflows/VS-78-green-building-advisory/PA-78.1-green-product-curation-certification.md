## W2718. Green Product Identification & Certification Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual assortment review or new green product launch |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly; 50–100 new products/year |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–3,000 green products |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Category Mgr, Procurement, Quality, Sustainability Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager identifies candidates: energy-efficient appliances (DOE label), water-saving fixtures, low-VOC paints, FSC lumber, recycled materials, LED, solar; develops criteria per category | Category Manager / Sustainability Coordinator | VP Merchandising | 1–2 weeks/quarter |
| 2 | Procurement verifies certifications: DOE Energy Label, FSC/PEFC, Green Seal/EcoLogo, LEED-contributing, BERDE-compliant; confirms validity with issuing body | Procurement / Quality | VP Merchandising | 1 week |
| 3 | Category Manager creates 'BuildRight Green Choice' designation in ERP: certification type, environmental benefit, savings data, BERDE/LEED contribution; enables filtering on ecommerce; targets 8–10% of assortment | Category Manager / IT | VP Merchandising | 1 week |

| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–3,000 green products |
| **Owner** | Pricing Analyst |
| **Participants** | Pricing Analyst, Category Manager, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing Analyst reviews pricing: green products carry 5–15% premium; validates premium vs. certification cost and customer value (energy savings); checks competitiveness | Pricing Analyst | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |

| 3 | Finance tracks green revenue: sales as % of total (target: 5–8% within 3 years), margin contribution, customer segment analysis; reports quarterly to CFO | Finance | CFO | Quarterly |
