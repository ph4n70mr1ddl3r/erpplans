## W5441. Site Cleanup Service Product Design, Pricing & Catalog

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Service launch; annual pricing review; new install/build channel introduction |
| **Frequency** | Annual review; new product/SKU as needed |
| **Volume** | ~8–15 service products (post-install cleanup, post-demolition cleanup, bulk-debris haul, skip rental, final handover clean) |
| **Owner** | Services Category Manager (VS-12/VS-09) |
| **Participants** | Pricing (VS-01.2/VS-101), Legal/Compliance (VS-100/VS-22), ESG (VS-25), Hauling vendors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the site-cleanup service catalog tied to BuildRight's install/build channels (post-appliance-install, post-tile/flooring, post-projects/VS-66, post-solar/VS-70, post-disaster-repair/VS-184, final handover clean). | Services Category Manager | VP Services | per review |
| 2 | Price each service on container-cube + crew-hours + hauling/disposal cost + margin, accounting for scrap-metal recovery and diversion-rebate offsets. | Pricing Analyst | VP Services | per product |
| 3 | Configure service items/SKUs and attach-to logic at POS/order entry so cleanup is offered with every qualifying install/build sale (links VS-08/VS-10/VS-11). | Merch Ops / IT | VP Services | per product |
| 4 | Legal review of service terms (LGU/DENR liability boundary, hazardous exclusion, indemnity) and Consumer Act (RA 7394) disclosure. | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | per product |
| 5 | Publish to the website/app/Pro-Desk catalog and train sales associates (VS-124) on attach. | Marketing / L&D | VP Services | per launch |

### System Touchpoints
- Service-item master (VS-29); pricing (VS-01.2/VS-101); POS/order attach (VS-08/VS-10/VS-11); catalog/PIM (VS-137); compliance records (VS-22/VS-100).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mispriced-haul risk**: under-pricing hauling/disposal (long haul distance, landfill tip fees, demurrage) erodes margin or loses money on every job; mitigated by cost-plus pricing with distance/zone and periodic cost re-forecast.
- **Scope-creep risk**: customers expect "cleanup" to include hazardous/regulated removal (asbestos/lead/paint) the service excludes; mitigated by explicit service terms and the W5443 pre-job assessment.

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks/annual pricing review; ~3–5 days/new product launch.

### Automation Opportunity
- Attach-rule automation at order entry; distance/zone-based hauling-cost calculator; periodic cost-re-forecast feed from disposal-vendor invoices.

### Controls
- CTL-02 (service/pricing approval); CTL-34 (Consumer Act disclosure); CTL-21 (pricing accuracy); operational: hazardous/regulated materials explicitly excluded in service terms.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-12 (installation), VS-66 (design-build), VS-143 (appliance install), VS-70 (solar), VS-184 (disaster repair), VS-29 (service items), VS-08/VS-10/VS-11 (attach)

## W5442. Customer Debris Volume & Container Size Estimation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer inquiry at POS/Pro-Desk/online; install/build sale; site survey |
| **Frequency** | Per cleanup engagement — ~thousands/yr across install base |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of estimates/yr (post-install, project, demolition) |
| **Owner** | Site Cleanup Coordinator (Services/VS-12) |
| **Participants** | Sales Associate (VS-124), Installation Supervisor (VS-12), customer, Estimation (VS-09.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gather job parameters: scope (e.g., post-kitchen-reno, post-tile job, full demolition), material mix (wood/drywall/masonry/metal/packaging), site access (stairs, elevator, parking, hauling distance). | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per job |
| 2 | Estimate debris volume (cubic meters) and weight using standard conversion factors per material class; select container/skip size (2/5/10/20 cu.m.) or truck trips. | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per job |
| 3 | For larger/project jobs, schedule a site survey (VS-09.2 takeoff) to validate; for routine post-install, use channel-standard tables. | Estimator / Coordinator | Services Manager | per large job |
| 4 | Quote price (W5441 catalog + distance/zone) and present options (one-off haul, scheduled multi-haul, skip rental period). | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per job |
| 5 | Document the estimate and assumptions (photos/site sketch for larger jobs) to support W5448 booking and any post-job dispute. | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per job |

### System Touchpoints
- Service-item/quote module (VS-11/VS-10); takeoff tools (VS-09.2); volume/weight conversion library; photo/attachment store; distance/zone rate engine (W5441).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-estimated-volume risk**: under-sized container/truck → overflow, extra trips, margin loss, and customer dissatisfaction; mitigated by conservative sizing tables and site-survey-for-large-jobs policy.
- **Access-misjudgment risk**: a container that can't physically fit the site (narrow lane, no parking, condo rules) strands the job; mitigated by mandatory access capture in the estimate and skip-placement permit (W5445).

### Time Estimate
- ~10–20 min/routine post-install estimate; ~1–2 hours + site visit for large/project jobs.

### Automation Opportunity
- Channel-standard volume tables auto-suggested from the originating sale's SKUs (e.g., a tile-job's expected rubble); photo-assisted volume estimation; distance/zone auto-rate.

### Controls
- CTL-21 (estimate accuracy/evidence); CTL-02 (quote approval threshold); operational: site survey required above a volume/value threshold.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-09.2 (takeoff/estimation), VS-12 (install scope), VS-11/VS-10 (quote/booking), VS-143 (appliance-install attach)

## W5443. Job Site Cleanup Pre-Job Hazard & Regulated-Material Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Booking confirmed (W5448); any demolition/suspect-older-building job; post-disaster (VS-184) |
| **Frequency** | Per engagement; mandatory for demolition/older-stock/post-disaster |
| **Volume** | Per cleanup job; ~100% of demolition/large jobs screened |
| **Owner** | Site Cleanup Supervisor |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), customer, Estimator (VS-09.2), VS-187 hazmat, VS-73 asbestos (W2613) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Screen the job for regulated/hazardous materials: asbestos/lead suspect (pre-1990s buildings), paint/solvent residues, refrigerants, treated wood, chemical containers, e-waste. | Site Cleanup Supervisor / HSE | HSE Lead | per job |
| 2 | For suspect asbestos/lead, route to the accredited abatement path (VS-73 W2613) — do NOT include in standard C&D hauling; pause the cleanup until resolved. | HSE | HSE Lead | per suspect |
| 3 | For household hazardous residues, route to VS-187 take-back or accredited TSD; exclude from the mixed C&D load. | Site Cleanup Supervisor | HSE Lead | per job |
| 4 | Capture site hazards (live utilities, structural instability, confined space, working at height) and brief the crew with a job-hazard-analysis (JHA) and required PPE. | Site Cleanup Supervisor / HSE | HSE Lead | per job |
| 5 | Document the assessment and customer acknowledgment of scope/exclusions; update the work order (W5448). | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per job |

### System Touchpoints
- JHA/hazard-assessment module (VS-24); asbestos/lead abatement records (VS-73 W2613); hazmat TSD records (VS-187); work-order (W5448); photo/evidence store.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Asbestos-mishandling risk**: drywall/ceiling/insulation debris containing asbestos, swept into a standard C&D load, creates a serious health and DENR (RA 6969) liability; mitigated by mandatory suspect-screening and abatement routing.
- **Crew-injury risk**: an unrehearsed demolition/structural hazard injures cleanup crew; mitigated by JHA + PPE + HSE sign-off before crew dispatch.

### Time Estimate
- ~20–40 min/routine screening; ~2–4 hours + specialist for demolition/suspect jobs.

### Automation Opportunity
- Suspect-material decision tree in the work-order; auto-routing to abatement/TSD vendors; JHA template auto-populated from job type.

### Controls
- CTL-34 (DENR RA 6969 regulated-material routing); CTL-09 (abatement specialist segregation); operational: no demolition job proceeds without a documented hazard assessment.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE/JHA), VS-73 W2613 (asbestos abatement), VS-187 (hazmat TSD), VS-184 (post-disaster hazards), VS-114 (DG transport if any)

## W5444. Crew, Container & Truck Dispatch Scheduling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Booking confirmed (W5448); multi-haul project schedule |
| **Frequency** | Daily dispatch; ~thousands of cleanup jobs/yr |
| **Volume** | ~5–20 crews + ~container/skip fleet + ~third-party hauling (VS-110) per region |
| **Owner** | Dispatch Supervisor (Services/VS-12) |
| **Participants** | Crew leads, Fleet (VS-06), hauling 3PL (VS-56/VS-110), Store/DC, customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Schedule the crew, container delivery, and haul truck against the booking window and site access constraints (W5442). | Dispatch Supervisor | Services Manager | daily |
| 2 | Optimize routing across multiple jobs/region (cluster nearby jobs, minimize empty haul miles) — coordinate with fleet/3PL capacity (VS-06/VS-56). | Dispatch Supervisor | Services Manager | daily |
| 3 | Confirm container/skip placement and LGU permit (W5445), crew certification/PPE (W5443), and customer site readiness. | Dispatch Supervisor | Services Manager | per job |
| 4 | Dispatch with mobile-app work order, route, JHA, and regulated-material flags; track arrival/time-on-site. | Dispatch Supervisor | Services Manager | per job |
| 5 | Handle exceptions (access denial, volume overrun, weather, hazardous discovery) via the dispatch escalation path. | Dispatch Supervisor | Services Manager | per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Dispatch/routing system (VS-06/VS-56/VS-74); fleet/3PL capacity (VS-06/VS-56); mobile work-order app; telematics (VS-06 W199); container fleet register (W5445).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Subutilization risk**: poorly-routed crews/trucks run empty or sit idle, eroding the service margin; mitigated by clustering and capacity-aware scheduling.
- **Permit-blocked-placement risk**: a container delivered before its LGU placement permit (W5445) is issued is refused or towed; mitigated by permit-before-placement sequencing.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 hours/day dispatch planning per region; ~5–10 min/job dispatch confirmation.

### Automation Opportunity
- Route optimization across jobs; capacity-aware auto-assignment; real-time ETA and exception alerts.

### Controls
- CTL-21 (scheduling/dispatch accuracy); CTL-25 (change management on exceptions); operational: permit-before-placement sequencing.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (fleet), VS-56/VS-110 (3PL hauling), VS-74 (jobsite delivery), VS-12 (install crew coordination)

## W5445. Skip/Dumpster & Container Fleet Sourcing, Placement & LGU Permit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Service setup; per skip-rental job; container-fleet refresh |
| **Frequency** | Per job placement; ongoing fleet sourcing |
| **Volume** | ~owned/leased container fleet + per-job LGU placement permits |
| **Owner** | Services Asset Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement (VS-34), Fleet (VS-06), LGU/Barangay (VS-76), customer, Hauling 3PL |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Source/maintain the skip/dumpster/container fleet (own + leased + 3PL) sized to service demand across regions (2/5/10/20 cu.m. sizes). | Services Asset Manager / Procurement | VP Services | ongoing |
| 2 | For each placement, secure the LGU/Barangay permit to occupy public road/right-of-way (where the container sits on-street) per VS-76. | Compliance Liaison | VP Legal & Compliance | per job |
| 3 | Confirm placement plan with the customer (driveway vs street vs private property, surface protection, accessibility). | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per job |
| 4 | Coordinate container drop with the hauler/fleet (VS-06/VS-56) and the crew schedule (W5444). | Dispatch Supervisor | Services Manager | per job |
| 5 | Track container location/dwell; manage demurrage billing for over-dwell beyond the rental period. | Services Asset Manager | Services Manager | per job |

### System Touchpoints
- Asset/container-fleet register (VS-35 extended); LGU permit records (VS-76/VS-166); dispatch (W5444); demurrage billing (VS-11/VS-15); fleet (VS-06).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unpermitted-occupancy risk**: an on-street container without LGU/Barangay permit is towed/fined (LGU ordinance); mitigated by permit-before-placement and VS-76 LGU coordination.
- **Container-loss/theft risk**: containers left on customer/street sites are stolen or abandoned; mitigated by GPS/telematics on the fleet and dwell-tracking.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 days lead for LGU permit (varies by LGU); ~10–20 min/placement planning.

### Automation Opportunity
- Permit-status tracking integrated to dispatch (block drop until permit issued); container telematics/dwell auto-billing.

### Controls
- CTL-34 (LGU permit compliance); CTL-46 (container-fleet register); CTL-21 (demurrage accuracy); operational: permit-before-placement for any public-right-of-way occupancy.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-76 (LGU permits), VS-166 (license/permit portfolio), VS-34 (procurement), VS-06 (fleet), VS-35 (asset register)

## W5446. B2B Project & Bulk Debris Engagement, SLA & Quotation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | B2B project award (VS-11/VS-66); developer/contractor/LGU bulk-debris engagement |
| **Frequency** | Per B2B project; ~hundreds of project engagements/yr |
| **Volume** | B2B project debris from developers/contractors/government — large multi-haul |
| **Owner** | Trade/Project Account Manager (VS-11/VS-107) |
| **Participants** | Services Manager, Estimator (VS-09.2), Pricing, Legal (VS-100), Customer PM |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engage with the project owner/contractor to scope debris over the project lifecycle (demolition phase, construction phase, handover) — link to VS-77 material staging and VS-11 project plan. | Trade/Project Account Mgr | Services Manager | per project |
| 2 | Estimate total debris volume/stream over project phases and design the hauling schedule (recurring multi-haul vs. on-call). | Estimator / Coordinator | Services Manager | per project |
| 3 | Quote with B2B project pricing (volume tier, dedicated container, SLA, diversion targets) and contract terms (LGU/DENR liability, indemnity, demurrage). | Pricing / Legal | VP Services | per project |
| 4 | Define SLAs (response time, hauling cycle, diversion rate, reporting) and KPIs for the project term. | Trade/Project Account Mgr | Services Manager | per project |
| 5 | Book as a project work order with milestone billing (VS-11 W165/W1288 progress billing) and integrate into the project site plan. | Trade/Project Account Mgr | Services Manager | per project |

### System Touchpoints
- B2B project module (VS-11); quotation/contract (VS-100); progress billing (VS-11); SLA/KPI tracking; VS-77 staging plan.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-scoped-project-volume risk**: a project's actual debris volume far exceeds estimate (hidden demolition, change orders), eroding the contract margin; mitigated by phase-gated re-estimation and change-order pricing.
- **Diversion-target-breach risk**: a B2B customer's sustainability KPI requires a diversion rate BuildRight can't meet on a particular site; mitigated by realistic diversion commitments upfront and W5457 diversion planning.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 weeks per B2B project engagement/scoping; ongoing through the project term.

### Automation Opportunity
- Project-phase debris forecasting from the project plan; SLA dashboard; change-order volume alerts.

### Controls
- CTL-02 (project pricing/contract approval); CTL-21 (estimate accuracy); CTL-34 (LGU/DENR liability terms); operational: phase-gated re-estimation on B2B projects.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-11 (B2B project), VS-107 (key account), VS-66 (design-build), VS-77 (material staging), VS-09.2 (takeoff), VS-100 (contracts)

## W5447. Subcontractor Hauler & Accredited-Disposer Vendor Onboarding

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Service launch; capacity expansion to a new region; vendor renewal/exit |
| **Frequency** | Per vendor onboarding; periodic recertification |
| **Volume** | ~10–40 hauling/TSD/landfill/ recycler vendors across regions |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager (VS-34/VS-110) |
| **Participants** | TPRM (VS-161), Compliance (VS-22), Legal (VS-100), ESG (VS-25), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Qualify hauling subcontractors on LGU hauling permit, DENR transporter registration, vehicle/insurance, and capacity; integrate with VS-110 freight and VS-56 3PL. | Procurement Manager | VP Procurement | per vendor |
| 2 | Qualify accredited disposers/recyclers/landfills: DENR-EMB-accredited TSD/MDRF/sanitary landfill, recycler reprocessing accreditation; verify permits (VS-22) and TPRM tier (VS-161). | Compliance / TPRM | VP Legal & Compliance | per vendor |
| 3 | Contract with waste-stream acceptance terms (clean wood, masonry, metals, mixed C&D), manifest/digital-record obligations, chain-of-custody, and audit rights. | Legal / Procurement | VP Legal & Compliance | per vendor |
| 4 | Onboard into the dispatch system (W5444) with capacity, region, and acceptance-stream data; define fallback vendors for continuity. | Dispatch Supervisor | Services Manager | per vendor |
| 5 | Periodically recertify permits/accreditation and audit a sample of manifests; manage exit/replacement on lapse or performance failure. | Compliance / TPRM | VP Legal & Compliance | annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor master (VS-03/VS-29); TPRM (VS-161); DENR permit records (VS-22); contract management (VS-100/VS-34); dispatch (W5444).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unaccredited-disposer risk**: a hauler taking C&D to an unpermitted dumpsite creates illegal-dumping liability for BuildRight (RA 9003/RA 6969) even if the hauler acted independently; mitigated by DENR accreditation verification and chain-of-custody manifests.
- **Vendor-concentration risk**: a region with a single hauler/disposer fails if that vendor lapses; mitigated by fallback vendors and capacity monitoring.

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 weeks/vendor onboarding; ~1–2 weeks/annual recert cycle.

### Automation Opportunity
- Permit/accreditation expiry monitoring (links VS-166 license-portfolio); manifest receipt and chain-of-custody digital record; capacity/cost vendor scorecard.

### Controls
- CTL-34 (DENR RA 9003/6969 accredited-disposer verification); CTL-09 (TPRM/vendor risk); CTL-44 (duplicate-invoice/manifest guard); operational: chain-of-custody manifest required for every C&D load.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-110 (freight procurement), VS-56 (3PL), VS-34 (procurement), VS-161 (TPRM), VS-22 (DENR permits), VS-166 (license portfolio)

## W5448. Site Cleanup Service Work Order, Pricing & Booking Confirmation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer accepts estimate (W5442/W5446) |
| **Frequency** | Per booking — ~thousands/yr |
| **Volume** | Per cleanup engagement |
| **Owner** | Site Cleanup Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Sales Associate (VS-124), customer, Dispatch (W5444), Finance (VS-15) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Convert the accepted estimate into a service work order: scope, container/truck, crew, schedule, price, terms, exclusions. | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per booking |
| 2 | Apply Customer Act (RA 7394) disclosures (service description, price, cancellation/refund policy, hazardous exclusion) and capture customer acknowledgment. | Site Cleanup Coordinator / Legal | Services Manager | per booking |
| 3 | Confirm booking with the customer (date/window, access requirements, prep, what to expect) via app/SMS/email. | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per booking |
| 4 | Set up revenue recognition per PFRS 15 (performance obligation satisfied at service completion; or over a multi-haul contract period) — link VS-157. | Finance | Financial Controller | per booking |
| 5 | Trigger dispatch scheduling (W5444) and record the booking in the work-order system for execution (W5449) and settlement (W5457). | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per booking |

### System Touchpoints
- Work-order/service module; CRM/customer (VS-13/VS-29); notification (app/SMS/VS-75); revenue recognition (VS-157); dispatch handoff (W5444).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-show/late-cancellation risk**: customer cancels after container/crew are committed, stranding cost; mitigated by cancellation policy with retention/fee and confirmation-touch before dispatch.
- **Revenue-recognition-error risk**: recognizing cleanup revenue at booking rather than completion distorts PFRS 15; mitigated by the service-completion trigger (VS-157).

### Time Estimate
- ~10–15 min/booking work-order + confirmation.

### Automation Opportunity
- Estimate-to-work-order conversion; automated confirmation touches; PFRS 15 performance-obligation tagging; no-show-prediction alerts.

### Controls
- CTL-21 (work-order/pricing accuracy); CTL-34 (Consumer Act disclosure); CTL-31 (revenue recognition — PFRS 15); operational: documented cancellation/refund policy.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-157 (revenue recognition), VS-15 (billing/AP for vendors), VS-13 (customer), VS-29 (master data), VS-75 (app notification)
