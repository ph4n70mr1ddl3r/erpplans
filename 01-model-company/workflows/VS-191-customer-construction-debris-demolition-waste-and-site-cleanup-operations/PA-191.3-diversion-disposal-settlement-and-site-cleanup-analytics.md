## W5457. Job Settlement, Container Demurrage & Scrap/Rebate Recovery Billing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Job completion + customer sign-off (W5455); monthly billing cycle |
| **Frequency** | Per job settlement; monthly batch |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of settlements/yr |
| **Owner** | Services Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-15/VS-17), Site Cleanup Coordinator, Hauler/Disposer (W5447), Revenue Assurance (VS-118) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reconcile the actual job: volume/weight hauled (W5451), streams routed (W5453), crew-hours, container dwell, and any change orders (W5454) vs the booked estimate (W5448). | Services Finance Analyst | Finance Manager | per job |
| 2 | Compute the final price (base service + per-cu.m/per-tonne hauling + container demurrage if over-dwell − scrap/rebate recovery credits). | Services Finance Analyst | Finance Manager | per job |
| 3 | Recognize revenue per PFRS 15 at completion (VS-157/W5448); book recovery/scrap credits against cost of service. | Finance | Financial Controller | per job |
| 4 | Settle vendor invoices (hauler/disposer) with 3-way match against manifests/weight tickets (VS-15 W7); capture demurrage/rebates accurately. | AP | Finance Manager | per invoice |
| 5 | Resolve exceptions (overruns, disputed weights, vendor overcharge) and feed into revenue assurance (VS-118) and pricing health (W5464). | Services Finance Analyst | Finance Manager | per exception |

### System Touchpoints
- Work-order/billing (VS-15/VS-11); AP 3-way match (VS-15 W7); revenue recognition (VS-157); weight-ticket/manifest (W5451/W5452); revenue assurance (VS-118).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-leak risk**: under-billed demurrage/overruns or un-captured scrap/rebate credits leak margin; mitigated by reconciliation-against-actuals and revenue-assurance monitoring (VS-118).
- **Mis-recognition risk**: recognizing recovery credits as revenue rather than cost-offset distorts PFRS 15; mitigated by the accounting policy (VS-157).

### Time Estimate
- ~10–20 min/job settlement; ~1–2 days/month batch close.

### Automation Opportunity
- Estimate-vs-actuals auto-reconciliation; demurrage auto-billing from container telematics; scrap/rebate auto-credit capture.

### Controls
- CTL-21 (settlement accuracy); CTL-31 (revenue recognition — PFRS 15); CTL-44 (3-way match on vendor invoices); operational: scrap/rebate credits captured against cost of service.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-15 (AP/billing), VS-17 (GL), VS-157 (revenue recognition), VS-118 (revenue assurance), VS-11 (project billing)

## W5458. Customer Invoicing, Dispute Resolution & Collections

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per job invoice; weekly aging review |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of invoices/yr (B2C + B2B) |
| **Owner** | AR Analyst (VS-16) |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-15/VS-16), Customer Service (VS-13), Site Cleanup Coordinator, customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generate and deliver the customer invoice with the manifest/weight evidence and before/after photos attached for transparency. | AR Analyst | Finance Manager | per invoice |
| 2 | For B2B projects, integrate with progress billing (VS-11 W165/W1288) and project terms (W5446). | AR Analyst / Project Mgr | Finance Manager | per project |

### System Touchpoints
- AR/invoicing (VS-16); billing evidence store (manifests/photos); dispute/credit-note (VS-16); collections/aging (VS-16); CRM (VS-13).

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 min/invoice generation; dispute resolution varies; ~1–2 hours/week aging review.

### Automation Opportunity
- Evidence-auto-attach to invoices; dispute workflow with evidence; automated dunning; project-billing integration.

### Controls
- CTL-21 (invoicing accuracy/evidence); CTL-31 (credit-note control); CTL-23 (dispute/fraud guard); operational: every cleanup invoice carries manifest + sign-off evidence.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (AR/collections/disputes), VS-15 (billing), VS-68 (credit risk), VS-13 (customer service), VS-118 (revenue assurance)

## W5459. Diversion Rate, Stream Composition & Circular-Economy Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly analytics cycle; ESG reporting (VS-25); B2B customer KPI (W5446) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly diversion analytics; quarterly review |
| **Volume** | 1 diversion/composition analytics view across the program |
| **Owner** | Services Analytics Lead |
| **Participants** | ESG (VS-25), Sustainability (VS-78/VS-179), Compliance (VS-22), Services Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compute diversion rate (diverted-to-recovery ÷ total C&D generated) by job, region, channel, and customer; track against target. | Services Analytics Lead | VP Services | monthly |
| 2 | Analyze stream composition (wood/drywall/masonry/metals/packaging/residuals) to identify high-volume streams for new diversion partnerships. | Services Analytics Lead | VP Services | quarterly |
| 3 | Quantify circular-economy value: scrap-metal recovery, reprocessor credits, biomass/aggregate reuse — vs landfill tip-fee avoided. | Services Analytics Lead | VP Sustainability | quarterly |
| 4 | Benchmark diversion rate vs. industry/ESG expectations and B2B customer commitments (W5446). | Services Analytics Lead | VP Sustainability | quarterly |
| 5 | Recommend improvements (new recycler partnership, on-site crushing, source-reduction) feeding W5453 routing and W5464 strategy. | Services Analytics Lead | VP Services | quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (VS-28); weight/manifest data (W5451/W5452); routing/destination data (W5453); ESG platform (VS-25); settlement/recovery data (W5457).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Diversion-underperformance risk**: actual diversion falls short of customer/ESG commitments, damaging B2B relationships and ESG ratings; mitigated by active routing optimization and new partnerships.
- **Data-quality risk**: incomplete manifests/weights distort diversion reporting; mitigated by the no-manifest-no-dispatch gate (W5452) and certified weights (W5451).

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 days/month analytics; ~1 week/quarterly review.

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-diversion-rate computation from manifest/weight data; stream-composition visualization; circular-value modeling.

### Controls
- CTL-37 (ESG/diversion evidence); CTL-21 (data accuracy); CTL-46 (manifest completeness); operational: diversion rate reported monthly with manifest-backed evidence.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-25 (ESG), VS-179 (EPR/circular), VS-73 (circular economy), VS-28 (analytics), W5453 (routing), W5460 (ESG reporting)

### Cross-references

## W5462. Vendor Scorecard, Cost-to-Serve & Hauling Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly vendor review; capacity expansion; cost-reduction initiative |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly scorecard; periodic optimization |
| **Volume** | ~10–40 haulers/disposers/recyclers (W5447) |
| **Owner** | Procurement/Services Manager (VS-110) |
| **Participants** | VS-67 vendor scorecard, TPRM (VS-161), Dispatch (W5444), Finance (VS-15) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain a vendor scorecard (haulers/disposers/recyclers): on-time pickup, manifest accuracy, accreditation currency, contamination-rejection rate, cost/cu.m., recovery-credit delivery. | Procurement Manager | VP Procurement | quarterly |
| 2 | Compute cost-to-serve by region/channel/stream (crew + container + haul + disposal − recovery) and identify cost-reduction levers. | Services Finance Analyst | Finance Manager | quarterly |
| 3 | Optimize hauling: route clustering (W5444), backhaul opportunities, container-fleet utilization, and capacity-balancing across vendors. | Services Asset Manager / Dispatch | VP Services | periodic |
| 4 | Manage vendor performance/issues/exit via VS-110 freight and VS-161 TPRM; consolidate spend for better rates where scale allows. | Procurement Manager | VP Procurement | periodic |
| 5 | Feed cost-to-serve into pricing health (W5464) and the vendor-strategy/TPRM governance. | Procurement Manager | VP Procurement | quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor scorecard (VS-67); freight procurement (VS-110); TPRM (VS-161); dispatch/telematics (W5444/VS-06); cost analytics (VS-28/VS-61).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vendor-lock-in risk**: a region dependent on a single hauler/disposer faces price escalation or service failure; mitigated by multi-vendor strategy and capacity monitoring.
- **Hidden-cost risk**: low headline haul rate offset by demurrage, rejection, and long-haul surcharges erodes the deal; mitigated by total-cost-to-serve scorecarding.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 weeks/quarter scorecard + cost-to-serve; optimization continuous.

### Automation Opportunity
- Vendor-scorecard auto-computation; total-cost-to-serve modeling; backhaul/clustering opportunity detection.

### Controls
- CTL-21 (scorecard/cost accuracy); CTL-09 (vendor concentration monitoring); CTL-37 (TPRM); operational: total-cost-to-serve (not headline rate) drives vendor decisions.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-110 (freight procurement), VS-67 (vendor scorecard), VS-161 (TPRM), VS-06/VS-61 (fleet/cost), W5444 (dispatch)
