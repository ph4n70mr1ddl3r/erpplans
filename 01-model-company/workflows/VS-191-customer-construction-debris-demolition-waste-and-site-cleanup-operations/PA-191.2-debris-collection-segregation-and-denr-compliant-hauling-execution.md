## W5449. On-Site Debris Collection, Segregation & Load-Out Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Crew/ container on site per W5444 dispatch |
| **Frequency** | Per cleanup job; daily across crews |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of jobs/yr; ~multiple streams per job |
| **Owner** | Site Cleanup Crew Lead |
| **Participants** | Crew, customer, HSE (VS-24), Dispatch (W5444) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On arrival, verify site access, JHA/PPE (W5443), container placement permit (W5445), and the work-order scope/exclusions. | Crew Lead | Site Cleanup Supervisor | per job |
| 2 | Segregate debris at load-out into streams: clean wood, drywall, concrete/masonry/tile rubble, metals, packaging/plastic, cardboard, and a separate hazardous-discovery pile. | Crew | Crew Lead | per job |
| 3 | Load container/truck by stream (single-stream mixed if disposal route accepts; multi-stream if diversion targets require separation). | Crew | Crew Lead | per job |
| 4 | Minimize contamination (no food/general waste, no hazardous in mixed C&D) — contaminated loads are rejected by recyclers/landfills and incur surcharges. | Crew Lead | Site Cleanup Supervisor | per job |
| 5 | Capture load photos, stream composition estimate, and time-on-site for the work order (W5457). | Crew Lead | Site Cleanup Supervisor | per job |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile work-order/stream-capture app; container/load photo store; dispatch (W5444); VS-24 JHA.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Contamination-rejection risk**: a mixed C&D load with food/hazardous is rejected or surcharged at the disposer, eroding margin and delaying the job; mitigated by at-load segregation and crew training.
- **Segregation-labor-cost risk**: over-segregating beyond what the disposer pays for wastes labor; mitigated by stream-segregation rules tied to the destination's acceptance/rebate terms (W5453).

### Time Estimate
- ~1–4 hours/job on-site depending on volume/access; crew of 2–4.

### Automation Opportunity
- Stream-composition photo-estimation; mobile segregation-checklist; contamination-flag prompts at load.

### Controls
- CTL-21: stream/load accuracy; CTL-34 (no hazardous in mixed C&D); operational: load photos required for every job.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE/PPE), W5443 (JHA), W5445 (permit), W5453 (routing/acceptance)

## W5450. Demolition Debris Load-Out, Dust/Noise & Neighbor Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Demolition-phase job (interior demo, full structure, post-disaster VS-184) |
| **Frequency** | Per demolition job; ~hundreds/yr |
| **Volume** | Larger/heavier debris loads; longer on-site duration |
| **Owner** | Demolition/ Site Cleanup Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Crew, HSE (VS-24), customer, neighbors/HOA, LGU (VS-76), PCAB (VS-165) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm demolition scope vs. PCAB/permit coverage (VS-165) and the LGU demolition/building permit; do not exceed the permitted scope. | Site Cleanup Supervisor / Compliance | VP Legal & Compliance | per job |
| 2 | Deploy dust/noise/vibration controls (water suppression, tarps, scheduling noisy work within allowed hours, debris chute). | Crew Lead | Site Cleanup Supervisor | per job |
| 3 | Notify neighbors/HOA per LGU/condominium requirements and manage complaints through the escalation path. | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per job |
| 4 | Sequence demolition to preserve structural elements per plan (load-bearing, shared walls) and protect utilities. | Crew Lead / Customer PM | Site Cleanup Supervisor | per job |
| 5 | Load demolition debris (heavier rubble, mixed) — possibly requiring heavy haul (VS-186 equipment, VS-74 heavy delivery) — and route per W5453. | Crew Lead | Site Cleanup Supervisor | per job |

### System Touchpoints
- LGU/PCAB permit records (VS-165/VS-76/VS-166); JHA (VS-24); heavy-haul dispatch (VS-186/VS-74); work-order (W5448).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-demolition risk**: crew removes a load-bearing or out-of-scope element causing structural/utility damage; mitigated by scope-locked execution and customer-PM sign-off.
- **Neighbor-complaint/LGU-fine risk**: dust/noise outside permitted hours triggers complaints, LGU fines, or work stoppage; mitigated by hour-limited execution and active neighbor management.

### Time Estimate
- ~0.5–3 days/demo job depending on scale; heavy-haul coordination adds lead time.

### Automation Opportunity
- Permit-scope lock-in the work order; hour-restricted-work alerts; complaint-tracking integration.

### Controls
- CTL-34: LGU/PCAB demolition-permit scope; CTL-09 (structural-scope segregation); CTL-804 (ensure controlled execution — Debris Collection, Segregation & DENR-Compliant Hauling Execution (PA-191.2).) — customer-PM structural sign-off; operational: noisy work only within LGU-permitted hours.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-165 (PCAB/RA 4566), VS-76 (LGU), VS-24 (HSE), VS-186 (heavy equipment), VS-74 (heavy delivery), VS-184 (post-disaster)

## W5451. C&D Debris Weight/Volume Verification & Weighbridge Ticket Capture

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Load departs site; arrival at disposer/transfer; billing |
| **Frequency** | Per haul/load |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of loads/yr |
| **Owner** | Site Cleanup Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Hauler (VS-56/VS-110), Disposer (W5447), Finance (VS-15), Calibration (VS-115) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture the load's volume estimate at load-out (W5449) and verify against container/truck capacity. | Crew Lead | Site Cleanup Supervisor | per load |
| 2 | At the weighbridge/disposer, capture the certified weight ticket (VS-115 W3626 scale calibration ensures accuracy); reconcile to volume estimate. | Hauler / Disposer | Site Cleanup Coordinator | per load |
| 3 | Record weight/volume, stream composition, and disposal/processing destination against the manifest (W5452). | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per load |
| 4 | Flag discrepancies (significant overweight/underweight vs. estimate, unexpected stream) for investigation. | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per load |
| 5 | Feed verified weights into billing/settlement (W5457) and the diversion analytics (W5459). | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Finance | per load |

### System Touchpoints
- Weighbridge/weight-ticket ingest (VS-115 W3626 calibrated); manifest system (W5452); work-order/billing (VS-15/VS-11); diversion analytics (W5459).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Dispute-on-weight risk**: customer disputes the hauling fee because the load weight differs from the estimate; mitigated by weighbridge ticket evidence and conservative estimation (W5442).
- **Mismeasurement risk**: an uncalibrated or non-certified weighbridge produces inaccurate weights that distort billing and diversion reporting; mitigated by certified-scale verification (VS-115).

### Time Estimate
- ~5–15 min/load verification and recording.

### Automation Opportunity
- Weight-ticket OCR/digital ingest; auto-reconciliation vs estimate; diversion-stream auto-classification.

### Controls
- CTL-21: weight/volume accuracy; CTL-46 (manifest/weight-ticket evidence); operational: certified-scale requirement (VS-115).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-115 W3626 (scale calibration), VS-15 (billing), VS-11 (project billing), W5447 (disposer), W5459 (diversion analytics)

## W5452. DENR-Compliant C&D Transporter Permitting & Hauling Manifest

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Load departs site; manifest system |
| **Frequency** | Per load |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of manifests/yr |
| **Owner** | Compliance Specialist (VS-22) |
| **Participants** | Hauler (W5447), Disposer, DENR/LGU, Finance (VS-15), Records (VS-88) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm the transporter is DENR-registered / LGU-permitted for C&D hauling (W5447 onboarding; VS-166 permit portfolio). | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | per hauler |
| 2 | Generate a hauling manifest per load: origin, stream composition, volume/weight, transporter, destination (recycler/MDRF/landfill), and generator (BuildRight on behalf of customer). | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Compliance Specialist | per load |
| 3 | Capture chain-of-custody signatures/stamps (origin → transporter → disposer) — digital where the disposer supports it. | Hauler / Disposer | Compliance Specialist | per load |
| 4 | Retain manifests per DENR (RA 6969/RA 9003) and BIR (W590 fuel/expense) record-retention (VS-88; 10-yr per BIR). | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | per load |
| 5 | Aggregate manifests for DENR/LGU periodic reporting and ESG disclosure (VS-25). | Compliance Specialist | VP Sustainability | periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Manifest system (DENR-aligned); transporter/disposer accreditation (VS-166/VS-22); records retention (VS-88); ESG reporting (VS-25); billing (VS-15).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missing-manifest risk**: a load hauled without a manifest breaks the chain-of-custody and exposes BuildRight to illegal-dumping liability if the hauler mis-disposes; mitigated by no-manifest-no-dispatch gating.
- **Mis-classified-stream risk**: labeling a regulated/hazardous stream as "C&D" to avoid TSD routing breaches RA 6969; mitigated by the W5443 pre-job screen and W5454 discovery rerouting.

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 min/load manifest generation; periodic aggregation ~1 day/month.

### Automation Opportunity
- Digital manifest generation; chain-of-custody e-signature; auto-retention and ESG/ regulatory aggregation.

### Controls
- CTL-34: DENR RA 6969/RA 9003 manifest; CTL-46 (manifest evidence/retention); CTL-09 (regulated-stream segregation); operational: no C&D load dispatched without a manifest.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-22 (DENR compliance), VS-166 (permit portfolio), VS-88 (records), VS-25 (ESG reporting), VS-187 (hazardous TSD), W5447 (vendor accreditation)

## W5453. Multi-Stream Routing to Recycler / Reprocessor / MDRF / Landfill

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Load segregation complete (W5449); hauler departs |
| **Frequency** | Per load; periodic routing optimization |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of loads/yr to ~multiple destinations |
| **Owner** | Services Asset Manager |
| **Participants** | Hauler (W5447), Recyclers/TSD/landfill, ESG (VS-25), Compliance (VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Route each segregated stream to its highest-value/accredited destination: clean wood → biomass/repurposer; metals → scrap recycler (recovery value); concrete/masonry → crusher/reprocessor; drywall/cardboard → recycler; residuals → accredited sanitary landfill/MDRF. | Services Asset Manager | VP Services | per load |
| 2 | Match destination to the customer's/project's diversion target (W5446) and BuildRight's ESG goal (VS-25). | Services Asset Manager | VP Sustainability | per load |
| 3 | Capture destination acceptance/rejection and any rebates (scrap-metal recovery, reprocessor credits) for settlement (W5457). | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per load |
| 4 | Optimize routing over time for cost/diversion/distance (route clean wood to the nearest biomass plant, etc.). | Services Asset Manager | VP Services | periodic |
| 5 | Report diversion by stream and destination into ESG (VS-25) and the analytics (W5459). | Services Asset Manager | VP Sustainability | periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Routing/destination rules engine; disposer accreditation (W5447); ESG reporting (VS-25); settlement (W5457); analytics (W5459).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Landfill-avoidable risk**: routing divertible streams to landfill forfeits recovery value and ESG credit; mitigated by stream-to-destination rules and diversion targets.
- **Destination-capacity risk**: a recycler/MDRF closes or hits capacity, stranding a stream; mitigated by fallback destinations (W5447) and capacity monitoring.

### Time Estimate
- ~10 min/load routing decision; periodic optimization ~1 week/quarter.

### Automation Opportunity
- Stream-to-destination auto-routing rules; rebate/recovery auto-capture; diversion-impact modeling.

### Controls
- CTL-34: accredited-destination compliance; CTL-21 (routing/rebate accuracy); CTL-37 (ESG/diversion evidence); operational: divertible streams routed to recovery, not landfill, where accredited capacity exists.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-25 (ESG), VS-179 (EPR/circular), VS-73 (circular economy), W5447 (accredited disposer), W5459 (analytics)

## W5454. Hazardous / Regulated Debris Discovery & Rerouting During Cleanup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Crew discovers suspect/regulated material during the job (not caught in W5443 screen) |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc / event-driven — non-trivial frequency in older-stock/post-disaster jobs |
| **Volume** | ~10–20% of demolition/older-stock jobs discover some regulated material |
| **Owner** | Site Cleanup Supervisor |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), VS-187 hazmat TSD, VS-73 abatement (W2613), Compliance (VS-22), customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crew stops work on the suspect material and isolates it; contacts the supervisor/HSE for identification (asbestos/lead suspect, paint/solvent, chemical container, refrigerant, treated/e-waste). | Crew Lead | Site Cleanup Supervisor | on discovery |
| 2 | HSE/Compliance identify the material and route to the correct handling path: asbestos/lead abatement (VS-73 W2613); household hazardous TSD (VS-187); refrigerant recovery (VS-143); e-waste (VS-187/VS-99). | HSE / Compliance | HSE Lead | per discovery |
| 3 | Exclude the material from the mixed C&D load and manifest it under the appropriate regulated-waste manifest (DENR RA 6969 / VS-114 DG transport if applicable). | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | per load |
| 4 | Inform the customer, adjust the work order/scope/price (W5448 change order), and document the discovery and resolution. | Site Cleanup Coordinator / Legal | Services Manager | per discovery |
| 5 | Feed recurring discoveries into the W5443 pre-job screen to improve future hazard detection. | Site Cleanup Supervisor | HSE Lead | periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- JHA/hazard module (VS-24); regulated-waste manifest (VS-187/VS-114); abatement records (VS-73 W2613); work-order change (W5448); compliance records (VS-22).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Co-mingling-contamination risk**: regulated material swept into a standard C&D load contaminates the whole load (rejected, surcharged, or a DENR violation); mitigated by stop-isolate-route discipline.
- **Customer-dispute-on-scope risk**: discovered hazardous material expands scope/cost mid-job; mitigated by clear change-order terms (W5448) and transparent customer communication.

### Time Estimate
- ~immediate stop + ~2–8 hours/ discovery to identify, isolate, and reroute.

### Automation Opportunity
- Suspect-material identification app (photo → likely classification → routing); auto-change-order generation; recurring-discovery feed to the pre-job screen.

### Controls
- CTL-34: regulated-stream exclusion/manifest; CTL-09 (specialist-vendor segregation); CTL-02 (change-order approval); operational: crew trained to stop-isolate-escalate on any suspect material.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-73 W2613 (asbestos/lead), VS-187 (hazmat TSD), VS-143 (refrigerant), VS-114 (DG transport), W5443 (pre-job screen)

## W5455. Site Final Cleanup, Handover & Customer Sign-Off

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Debris hauled; site ready for handover |
| **Frequency** | Per job |
| **Volume** | Per cleanup engagement |
| **Owner** | Site Cleanup Crew Lead |
| **Participants** | Customer, Services Manager, Quality (VS-31), VS-146 mystery-shop (service quality) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Perform the final site sweep/broom-clean, remove residual debris/dust, and restore the work area (surface protection removal, furniture repositioning). | Crew | Crew Lead | per job |
| 2 | Inspect against the work-order scope and a quality checklist; capture before/after photos for handover and dispute defense. | Crew Lead | Site Cleanup Supervisor | per job |
| 3 | Walk the site with the customer, confirm scope completion, and obtain signed sign-off (digital via app). | Crew Lead / Customer | Site Cleanup Supervisor | per job |
| 4 | Log any punch-list items with owner and timeline; close the punch-list before final settlement (W5457). | Site Cleanup Coordinator | Services Manager | per job |
| 5 | Trigger post-service survey (VS-146/VS-13) and revenue recognition at completion (VS-157/W5448). | Services Manager | Financial Controller | per job |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile work-order/quality-checklist app; photo store; customer sign-off capture; survey (VS-13/VS-146); revenue recognition (VS-157).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Incomplete-handover risk**: customer later disputes that cleanup was incomplete, withholding payment; mitigated by signed sign-off + before/after photo evidence.
- **Punch-list-creep risk**: open punch-list items never close, delaying settlement; mitigated by defined punch-list owner/timeline and settlement-on-closure.

### Time Estimate
- ~30–90 min/job final cleanup + handover.

### Automation Opportunity
- Before/after photo auto-capture; digital sign-off; punch-list auto-tracking to settlement; automated survey trigger.

### Controls
- CTL-21: handover/sign-off evidence; CTL-31 (revenue recognition at completion); operational: no settlement without signed customer sign-off.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-31 (quality), VS-146 (service quality/mystery shop), VS-13 (customer survey), VS-157 (revenue recognition), W5457 (settlement)

## W5456. Hauling Safety, Spill Response & Road/Route Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Hauling operations; spill/accident event; route/permit change |
| **Frequency** | Continuous safety management; per event |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of hauling trips/yr |
| **Owner** | Fleet/Hauling Safety Manager (VS-06) |
| **Participants** | Hauler (VS-56/VS-110), HSE (VS-24), Compliance (VS-22), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enforce load securement (tarped/covered C&D to prevent road litter), vehicle roadworthiness, and driver qualification/hours (VS-06 W197/W1400 driver compliance). | Fleet Safety Manager | VP Logistics | per trip |
| 2 | Plan routes to comply with LGU truck bans / weight-restricted roads / time-of-day restrictions (links VS-06 W1369/W1228). | Dispatch Supervisor | Fleet Safety Manager | per trip |
| 3 | Maintain a spill-response plan for C&D (dust/debris release, minor chemical if co-mingled) integrated with VS-24 spill and VS-114 DG where applicable. | HSE | HSE Lead | setup + drills |
| 4 | Respond to spills/accidents: contain, document, notify LGU/DENR per severity (VS-22), and coordinate insurance (VS-26). | Fleet Safety Manager / HSE | VP Logistics | per event |
| 5 | Report hauling-safety incidents/metrics into HSE (VS-24) and fleet analytics (VS-06/VS-61). | Fleet Safety Manager | VP Logistics | periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Fleet/telematics (VS-06 W199); route planning (VS-06 W1369); spill-response plan (VS-24); DG transport (VS-114); insurance (VS-26); HSE incident system (VS-24).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Road-litter/falling-debris risk**: an un-tarped C&D load sheds debris on public roads, causing accidents and LGU fines; mitigated by mandatory tarping/securement and telematics monitoring.
- **Truck-ban-violation risk**: hauling during LGU truck-ban hours results in fines/impoundment; mitigated by route/time planning and dispatch enforcement.

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 min/trip route/securement check; spill response per severity (minutes–hours).

### Automation Opportunity
- Telematics-based securement/cover monitoring; truck-ban-aware routing; auto-incident notification.

### Controls
- CTL-34: LGU road/truck-ban compliance; CTL-09 (driver/vehicle qualification); CTL-37 (insurance/road-risk); operational: tarped/secured load required for every C&D trip.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (fleet/driver safety), VS-24 (spill/HSE), VS-114 (DG transport), VS-22 (LGU/DENR), VS-26 (insurance), VS-110 (freight)
