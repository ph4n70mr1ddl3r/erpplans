## Overview

Customer construction debris, demolition waste & site-cleanup operations for BuildRight Depot
Corp. — owning the **jobsite debris-hauling and post-installation / post-build site-cleanup
service** that BuildRight performs as part of its installation (VS-12), customer design-build
(VS-66), bulky white-goods delivery & haul-away (VS-143), solar installation (VS-70), equipment
rental return (VS-186/VS-12.2), and disaster-recovery repair work (VS-184). The program covers
site-cleanup service product design, pricing, and quotation (the C&D debris volume estimate,
container/skip sizing, labor); crew and container/skip/dumpster dispatch and scheduling; the
on-jobsite debris collection, segregation (clean wood, drywall, concrete/masonry, metals, packaging,
hazardous-separated), and load-out; DENR-compliant hauling and transporter permitting
(LGU-issued hauling permits, DENR-EMB commercial/industrial waste vs. household thresholds
under RA 6969/RA 9003); diversion to accredited recyclers/reprocessors and accredited
sanitary-landfill/MDRF disposal with manifest chain-of-custody; settlement (hauling fees,
container demurrage, diversion rebates, scrap-metal recovery value); and site-cleanup service
performance, diversion-rate, and ESG-decarbonization/circular-economy analytics.

BuildRight's "Your Home Building Partner" positioning and ~45% B2B + B2C project revenue mean the
company routinely performs work that *generates jobsite debris* — drywall, tile/masonry rubble,
lumber offcuts, concrete and cement debris, metal/pipe scraps, roofing, packaging and pallets,
and occasionally regulated materials (asbestos/lead suspect per VS-73 W2613, paint/solvent
residues per VS-187, refrigerants per VS-143). At ~10–15 new stores/yr and a 5–7-year remodel
cycle plus tens of thousands of customer installations/yr, the C&D debris stream is material —
ungoverned it creates illegal-dumping liability under RA 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management
Act) and RA 6969, raises the ESG/circular-economy footprint (VS-25/VS-73/VS-179), and forfeits
scrap/reprocessing recovery value. This is distinct from VS-73 (the store's *own* operational
waste), VS-187 (household hazardous waste *take-back at the store counter*), VS-143.3 (old
*appliance* haul-away/recycling), VS-111 (BuildRight's *own* inbound packaging/RTI), VS-179
(EPR *plastic* packaging compliance), VS-90 (transit *damage* claims), and VS-109/VS-20 (debris
from BuildRight's *own* store construction, handled in-store-project waste). It elevates the single
scattered workflow **W1086** (Customer Construction Waste Disposal & Skip/Dumpster Rental
Coordination in VS-09.1) to the end-to-end C&D debris hauling + site-cleanup operating model,
following the Pass-1/5/7/8/10/11/22/23/25 single-workflow-elevation pattern. No value stream owned
the **customer jobsite debris hauling & site cleanup service** — 'construction debris hauling',
'demolition waste', 'site cleanup', 'skip/dumpster rental', and 'C&D diversion' each appeared in
zero PA files as dedicated workflow headers except the single W1086 coordination workflow.
