## W2374. Communication Read Rate Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly analysis |
| **Frequency** | Weekly; all communications sent |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 broadcasts + ~400–800 tasks/week |
| **Owner** | Store Operations Director |
| **Participants** | Store Ops Director, COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System calculates: read rate per communication (target ≥95% within 24 hours for urgent, ≥90% within 48 hours for standard); identifies stores/regions with chronically low read rates | System / Store Ops Dir | COO | 30 min/week |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113, VS-63 / W571 platform) — the read-rate dataset
- Store-comms platform (links to VS-63 / W571) — the read-receipt source
- Store-performance (links to VS-07 / W67) — the chronic-low-read-rate as a Store Manager KPI

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Read-without-action risk**: a Store Manager marks "read" without actually acting (check-box compliance); mitigated by the action-evidence requirement on action-priority communications (not just read-receipt)
- **Notification-desensitization risk**: too many notifications reduce the read-rate on all of them; mitigated by the W2378 overload assessment and the priority-discipline

### Automation Opportunity
- Read-rate auto-computed; chronic-low-read stores auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-420 (ensure controlled execution — Communication Effectiveness Analytics (PA-63.3).)
- Operational: Store Ops Director weekly review; COO escalation on urgent-unread

### Time Estimate
~30 min/week = **~25 hours/year** Store Ops Director.

## W2375. Task Completion Rate Trending

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly analysis |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; all tasks |
| **Volume** | ~400–800 tasks/month |
| **Owner** | Analytics Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Ops Director |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tracks: overall completion rate, on-time rate, quality score; trends over 6 months; identifies improving/declining functions and regions; root cause analysis for declining areas | Analytics Manager | Store Ops Director | 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113, VS-63 / W2373 task analytics) — the completion-rate trend
- Task platform (links to VS-63 / W571) — the source data
- Process improvement (links to VS-133 / OpEx) — the root-cause analysis

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rate-without-context risk**: a declining completion rate may reflect unrealistic task volume, not store failure; mitigated by the volume-contextualized view (completion vs. task-load)
- **Quality-vs-rate-trade-off blindness**: pushing the completion rate up may drive quality down (rushed execution); mitigated by the quality-score viewed alongside the rate

### Automation Opportunity
- Trend auto-refreshed; declining-area auto-flagged with root-cause-suggestion

### Controls
- CTL-420 (ensure controlled execution — Communication Effectiveness Analytics (PA-63.3).)
- Operational: Analytics Manager methodology sign-off; Store Ops Director monthly review

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month = **~60 hours/year** Analytics Manager.

## W2377. Communication Channel Effectiveness

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Semi-annual channel review |
| **Frequency** | Semi-annual |
| **Volume** | 3 channels: app, email, SMS |
| **Owner** | Digital Product Manager |
| **Participants** | Digital PM, CIO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analyzes: delivery success rate, open rate, response time by channel; identifies optimal channel per communication type; recommends channel strategy adjustments | Digital PM | CIO | 2–3 days/semi-annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Channel platforms (links to VS-75 app, VS-10 email, SMS gateway) — the 3 channels
- Analytics (links to VS-28 / W113) — the channel-effectiveness dataset
- Store-comms platform (links to VS-63 / W571) — the multi-channel orchestration

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Channel-fragmentation risk**: using all 3 channels for the same message creates noise and inconsistency; mitigated by the channel-selection-by-priority discipline (urgent = app + SMS, standard = app, info = app/email)
- **Channel-obsolescence risk**: a channel (SMS) becomes less effective as Store Managers mute it; mitigated by the channel-effectiveness review and the channel-strategy adjustment

### Automation Opportunity
- Channel-effectiveness auto-computed; optimal-channel auto-suggested per priority

### Controls
- CTL-420 (ensure controlled execution — Communication Effectiveness Analytics (PA-63.3).)
- Operational: Digital PM methodology sign-off; CIO channel-strategy review

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/semi-annual = **~40–50 hours/year** Digital PM.

## W2378. Information Overload Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly review |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | 200 stores receiving communications |
| **Owner** | Store Operations Director |
| **Participants** | Store Ops Director, COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reviews volume of communications per store per week; identifies periods of excessive volume (>15 communications/week); recommends consolidation and prioritization to prevent overload | Store Ops Dir | COO | 2–3 hours/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-28 / W113, VS-63 / W571) — the per-store communication volume
- Task/comms platform (links to VS-63 / W571) — the volume source
- Coordination (links to VS-63 / W2366 task creation) — the cross-HQ-function volume coordination

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Overload-desensitization risk**: >15 communications/week causes Store Managers to tune out, missing the urgent ones; mitigated by the overload-threshold alert and the consolidation/prioritization
- **HQ-silo-volume risk**: each HQ function sends its own communications without awareness of the cumulative volume; mitigated by the Store Ops Director cross-function volume coordination

### Automation Opportunity
- Per-store volume auto-tracked; overload-threshold auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-420 (ensure controlled execution — Communication Effectiveness Analytics (PA-63.3).)
- Operational: Store Ops Director quarterly overload-review sign-off; COO escalation on chronic overload

### Time Estimate
~2.5 hours/quarter = **~10 hours/year** Store Ops Director.

- **Strategy-without-execution risk**: the strategy is updated but not operationalized (the priority-framework isn't enforced); mitigated by the strategy translated into the platform configuration (priority-levels, volume-targets) and the quarterly effectiveness review

- Strategy auto-informed by the year's analytics; roadmap auto-aligned with the platform-usage telemetry

### Controls
- CTL-420 (ensure controlled execution — Communication Effectiveness Analytics (PA-63.3).)
