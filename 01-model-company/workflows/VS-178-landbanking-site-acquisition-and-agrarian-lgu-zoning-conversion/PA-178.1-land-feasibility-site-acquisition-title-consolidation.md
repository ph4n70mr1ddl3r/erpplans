## W5129. Store Footprint Expansion Site Suitability & Strategic Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual expansion plan release (VS-33); site lead generation |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc per candidate site |
| **Volume** | ~30–40 site candidates evaluated annually for 10–15 selection slots |
| **Owner** | Director, Facilities & Real Estate & Land Acquisition |
| **Participants** | Strategy (VS-33), Finance (VS-17.4), Construction PMO (VS-112), Legal Operations (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Perform physical site evaluation: check plot area (8,000-15,000 sqm), frontage width, traffic access, utility availability, and terrain stability. | Director, Facilities & Real Estate & Land Acquisition | Director, Facilities & Real Estate & Land Acquisition | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Conduct market mapping: analyze regional trade area, competitor density (VS-57), household income, and trade-pro concentration. | Director, Facilities & Real Estate & Land Acquisition | Director, Facilities & Real Estate & Land Acquisition | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Prepare preliminary financial model: estimate acquisition cost, site-prep Capex, target payback period, and net present value (NPV). | Director, Facilities & Real Estate & Land Acquisition | Director, Facilities & Real Estate & Land Acquisition | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Draft site suitability report and route to Real Estate Committee for stage-gate approval (VS-112). | Director, Facilities & Real Estate & Land Acquisition | Director, Facilities & Real Estate & Land Acquisition | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy/FP&A (VS-33/VS-17.4), location master (VS-29), real estate GIS tool.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Zoning misalignment**: Site fails locational clearance later; mitigated by early CLUP review in step 1.

### Automation Opportunity
- GIS-based automatic trade-area analysis and demographic scorecards.

### Controls
- CTL-764 (ensure controlled execution — Land Feasibility, Site Acquisition & Title Consolidation (PA-178.1).)
- CTL-764: Real Estate Committee formal approval required before title search; board authorization limits adhered to.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-3 weeks per candidate site evaluation

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-112 (PMO), VS-57 (competitive intelligence)

## W5130. Land Ownership Search, Title Verification & Due Diligence

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved suitability report (W5129) |
| **Frequency** | Per approved site candidate |
| **Volume** | ~20–25 due diligence reviews annually |
| **Owner** | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager |
| **Participants** | Legal Operations (VS-100), Registry of Deeds (RD) liaison, Geodetic Engineer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Request Certified True Copies of Transfer Certificates of Title (TCT) from the Registry of Deeds. | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Trace title history (root search) to identify potential overlapping claims, land patents, or free patents. | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Verify liens, mortgages, encumbrances, and pending litigations (lis pendens) annotated on the title. | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Confirm land ownership matching with tax declarations and secure Certified True Copy of Tax Declarations from City/Municipal Assessor. | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Registry of Deeds database, Municipal/City Assessor database, Legal matter system (VS-100).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fake Title / Fraud**: Overlapping titles or fraudulent TCTs; mitigated by physical root search at Registry of Deeds and local assessor verification.

### Automation Opportunity
- Title document OCR analysis for detecting standard encumbrance clauses.

### Controls
- CTL-141 (ensure land title & acquisition due diligence.)
- CTL-764: Certified True Copies of TCT signed by RD Registrar; legal due diligence sign-off mandated before purchase offer.

### Time Estimate
- ~10-15 working days per title verification

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal operations), VS-161 (third-party risk)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Request Certified Statement of Real Property Tax Liabilities from the City or Municipal Treasurer's Office. | Land Acquisition Specialist / Tax Accountant | Land Acquisition Specialist / Tax Accountant | 1-2 hours |

| 3 | Draft tax settlement agreement: compute total taxes due, negotiate deduction of arrears from the final purchase price. | Land Acquisition Specialist / Tax Accountant | Land Acquisition Specialist / Tax Accountant | 1-2 hours |

### Pain Points / Risks

### Automation Opportunity
- LGU electronic tax payment portals integration.

### Controls
- CTL-141 (ensure land title & acquisition due diligence.)
- CTL-764: Tax Clearance Certificate secured from LGU Treasurer; payment matched to escrow deduction rules.

### Time Estimate
- ~5-7 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-79 (tax management), VS-15 (P2P), VS-97 (real estate portfolio)

## W5132. Extrajudicial Settlement (EJS) of Estate Negotiation & Heir Consent

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Title search (W5130) showing deceased owner on TCT |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per site transaction |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 multi-heir negotiations annually |
| **Owner** | Land Acquisition Manager / Legal Specialist |
| **Participants** | Heirs of the deceased owner, Legal Operations (VS-100), Notary Public |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify and locate all legal heirs of the deceased registered owner; compile family tree and birth/death certificates. | Land Acquisition Manager / Legal Specialist | Land Acquisition Manager / Legal Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Negotiate settlement terms: distribute proceeds or arrange buy-out of minor/dissenting heirs' shares. | Land Acquisition Manager / Legal Specialist | Land Acquisition Manager / Legal Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Draft the Deed of Extrajudicial Settlement of Estate with Absolute Sale (EJS-with-Sale). | Land Acquisition Manager / Legal Specialist | Land Acquisition Manager / Legal Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Publish EJS in a newspaper of general circulation for three consecutive weeks as legally required under Rules of Court. | Land Acquisition Manager / Legal Specialist | Land Acquisition Manager / Legal Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 5 | File EJS and proof of publication with the BIR for estate tax clearance and local Registry of Deeds for transfer. | Land Acquisition Manager / Legal Specialist | Land Acquisition Manager / Legal Specialist | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Civil Registry records, newspaper publication tracking, Legal contract library (VS-100).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Dissenting heir claim**: Omitted heir contesting the sale; mitigated by thorough EJS publication and securing notarized waivers from all heirs.

### Automation Opportunity
- None (requires physical verification and signature gathering).

### Controls
- CTL-141 (ensure land title & acquisition due diligence.)
- CTL-764: EJS signed by 100% of heirs; publication affidavit verified by Legal; estate tax paid and BIR CAR (Certificate Authorizing Registration) secured.

### Time Estimate
- ~60-90 days (due to publication requirements)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal), VS-79 (tax)

### Controls
- CTL-141 (ensure land title & acquisition due diligence.)

## W5134. Deed of Absolute Sale (DOAS) Negotiation, Drafting & Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Clear title due diligence (W5130) and survey confirmation (W5133) |
| **Frequency** | Per site transaction |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 transactions annually |
| **Owner** | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager |
| **Participants** | Seller, Escrow Agent (Bank), CFO, CEO, Legal (VS-100), Treasury (VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Negotiate final purchase price, payment terms (escrow/milestones), and responsibility for transfer taxes (CGT, DST, Transfer Tax, Registration Fees). | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Draft Deed of Absolute Sale (DOAS) incorporating technical descriptions, warranties of title, and zoning conversion clauses. | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Execute DOAS: arrange simultaneous signing by parties, notarize the document, and request Treasury to release escrow/downpayment per contract terms. | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Prepare tax file for filing Capital Gains Tax (CGT) and Documentary Stamp Tax (DST) with the BIR. | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | Legal & Compliance Counsel / Land Acquisition Manager | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP accounts payable (VS-15), treasury payment systems (VS-18), Legal contract database (VS-100).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Tax Valuation Dispute**: BIR assessing zonal value higher than contract price, delaying CAR; mitigated by pre-calculation of zonal value in step 1.

### Automation Opportunity
- Legal contract templating engine.

### Controls
- CTL-764 (ensure controlled execution — Land Feasibility, Site Acquisition & Title Consolidation (PA-178.1).)
- CTL-764: Dual officer signatures (COO/CFO/CEO) per corporate table of authority; notary verification; escrow release tied to notarized DOAS.

### Time Estimate
- ~5-10 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-15 (P2P), VS-18 (treasury), VS-100 (legal), VS-79 (tax)
