# VS-178: Landbanking, Site Acquisition & Agrarian/LGU Zoning Conversion Operations

> **Asset & Infrastructure** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Landbanking, site acquisition, title consolidation, and agricultural-to-commercial zoning conversions for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the operational lifecycle that secures prime property locations to support the 10–15 new store openings per year: strategic feasibility and land footprint assessment; registry searches and title verification due diligence; EXTRAJUDICIAL SETTLEMENT (EJS) of estate negotiations with multi-heir properties; real property tax (RPT) arrears settlement; boundary disputes, technical descriptions, and geodetic surveys; deed of absolute sale (DOAS) negotiation and execution; title transfer and Registry of Deeds (RD) registration; land lot consolidation and subdivision; Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR) clearances and agrarian reform exemptions; National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) ancestral domain check and Certificates of Non-Overlap (CNO); Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC) assessments and tribal community agreements; agricultural land reclassification with municipal/city councils; DAR land use conversion orders; tenant farmer compensation, relocation, and livelihood settlement governance; DENR Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) applications; joint-venture (JV) landowner development agreements; intercompany leases with BuildRight Property Management, Inc.; Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) variance applications; barangay clearances and road right-of-way (RROW) easements; locational clearances; site groundbreaking permitting; and site handover to construction PMO.

This matters at BuildRight's scale. BuildRight expands by **10–15 big-box stores per year** (~8,000–15,000 sqm each) across provincial Philippines. Acquiring and converting such large plots involves complex regulatory regimes (DAR, NCIP, LGU zoning, BIR tax clearance) that represent significant capital commitments and site-delivery timeline risks. Currently, VS-20 covers construction and leasing as tenant, and VS-97 covers existing asset property management, leaving the land banking and conversion operations unowned. A dedicated land banking program resolves site delivery bottlenecks and ensures clean titles.

This value stream owns the end-to-end program: mapping workflows, configuring system parameters, executing compliance actions, coordinating with external partners, and managing performance dashboards.
