## W5145. Joint-Venture (JV) Landowner Development Agreement Negotiation & Structuring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategic target site owned by prominent local family refusing outright sale but open to JV development |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per JV transaction |
| **Volume** | ~2–3 joint ventures annually |
| **Owner** | VP of Store Operations / Director of Real Estate |
| **Participants** | Landowner, Legal (VS-100), Finance (VS-17.4), BuildRight Property Management, Inc. |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct commercial modeling: analyze land appraisal value vs. BuildRight's store-construction Capex. | VP of Store Operations / Director of Real Estate | VP of Store Operations / Director of Real Estate | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Structure JV terms: land contribution as landowner equity; cash contribution and store building as BuildRight equity; negotiate lease rentals, revenue sharing, or store-in-store concession rights. | VP of Store Operations / Director of Real Estate | VP of Store Operations / Director of Real Estate | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Draft JV Development Agreement defining governance, board seats, profit distribution, and exit strategies. | VP of Store Operations / Director of Real Estate | VP of Store Operations / Director of Real Estate | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Incorporate a site-specific joint venture entity or register JV agreement under `BuildRight Property Management, Inc.` | VP of Store Operations / Director of Real Estate | VP of Store Operations / Director of Real Estate | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal contract database (VS-100), intercompany ledger (VS-72), ERP asset capitalization (VS-40).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Landowner default / Title liens**: Landowner encumbers land after JV execution; mitigated by annotating JV agreement on land title TCT.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-766 (ensure controlled execution — Property Joint-Ventures, LGU Zoning Compliance & Site Development Governance (PA-178.3).)
- CTL-766: JV agreements reviewed by External Counsel, approved by Board of Directors (VS-36); corporate bank accounts mapped.

### Time Estimate
- ~3–6 months

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal), VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-72 (shared services), VS-40 (capex), VS-36 (governance)

## W5147. LGU Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) Conformity & Zoning Variance Application

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Locational clearance review indicating site zoning is borderline or conflicts with new CLUP maps |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per site variance necessity |
| **Volume** | ~3–5 variance applications annually |
| **Owner** | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer |
| **Participants** | LGU Zoning Administrator, Local Zoning Board of Appeals (LZBA) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | File formal Application for Zoning Variance / Exception with the LGU Zoning Administrator. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Provide variance justification: job creation (~29 employees per store directly, plus construction labor), local business growth, and tax contributions. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Present case before the Local Zoning Board of Appeals; address environmental, traffic, and noise concerns. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Secure LZBA Board Resolution granting the zoning variance or exception clearance. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- LGU zoning map database, Legal case system (VS-100).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Locational clearance denial**: Store building permit blocked; mitigated by proactive LZBA presentation and local community support letters.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-766 (ensure controlled execution — Property Joint-Ventures, LGU Zoning Compliance & Site Development Governance (PA-178.3).)
- CTL-766: LZBA Board Resolution certified and filed with LGU City Engineer; signed copy attached to building permit application.

### Time Estimate
- ~45-90 days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal), VS-76 (LGU compliance)

## W5148. Barangay Clearance, Road Right-of-Way (RROW) & Easement Agreement Negotiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Zoning ordinance/variance approved; site access requires traversing private plots or LGU easements |
| **Frequency** | Per site construction preparation |
| **Volume** | 10–15 negotiations annually |
| **Owner** | Land Liaison Officer / CSR Manager |
| **Participants** | Barangay Chairman, Adjacent landowners, City Engineer, Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Apply for Barangay Clearance for the construction project at the local Barangay Hall. | Land Liaison Officer / CSR Manager | VP Legal & Compliance | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Negotiate Road Right-of-Way (RROW) and easement agreements with adjacent private landowners (multi-party negotiation over weeks) to secure highway and utility connectivity. | Land Liaison Officer / CSR Manager | VP Legal & Compliance | 2–4 weeks elapsed (per-session effort 1–2 hours) |
| 3 | Draft easement agreements specifying compensation, restoration terms, and structural rules. | Land Liaison Officer / CSR Manager | VP Legal & Compliance | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Notarize and annotate RROW/easement agreements on adjacent property titles at the Registry of Deeds to guarantee permanent access (2–4 weeks elapsed incl. RoD queue). | Land Liaison Officer / CSR Manager | VP Legal & Compliance | 2–4 weeks elapsed (per-session effort 1–2 hours) |

### System Touchpoints
- Barangay clearance logs, Legal contract repository (VS-88).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Access blockade**: Adjacent owner blocks utility/road access during construction; mitigated by early notarized RROW agreements annotated on titles.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-766 (ensure controlled execution — Property Joint-Ventures, LGU Zoning Compliance & Site Development Governance (PA-178.3).)
- CTL-766: Notarized RROW agreements checked by Legal; Barangay Clearance secured before mobilization.

### Time Estimate
- ~15-30 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-88 (document control), VS-100 (legal), VS-76 (LGU compliance)
