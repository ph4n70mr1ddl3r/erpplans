## W4401. Customer Incident & Accident Response (First Aid, Stabilize, Secure)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer injury/near-miss/property-damage on premises |
| **Frequency** | Per incident (~daily across 200 stores for minor; severe less frequent) |
| **Volume** | Minor incidents ~ hundreds/yr; recordable/severe fewer; near-misses (if captured) thousands |
| **Owner** | Store Manager / First Aider (VS-83) / Customer Safety |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), First Aider/Clinic (VS-83), HSE (VS-24), Security (VS-23), Customer Safety, Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store/First Aider secure the scene (stop hazard, cone-off, evacuate if needed), provide first aid/clinic referral, call emergency services for serious injury, and care for the customer | First Aider / Store Ops / Security | Store Manager | Immediate (minutes) |
| 2 | Store/Customer Safety capture the incident in the system (W4392) — what/when/where, customer details (PII per VS-91), witnesses, photos/CCTV, equipment/merchandise involved | Store Manager / Customer Safety | Store Manager | Within hours |
| 3 | Store/Customer Safety preserve evidence (CCTV clip, damaged item, spill sample), notify insurance (W4387/W4405), and avoid admission of liability (per legal W4387) | Store Manager / Customer Safety / Insurance | Store Manager / Head of Customer Safety | Within 24 hrs |

### System Touchpoints
- Incident system (W4392); first-aid/clinic (VS-83); CCTV/evidence (VS-23.2/W4392); insurance (VS-26); legal (VS-100); privacy (VS-91)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Response-delay risk**: slow first aid worsens injury + liability; mitigated by trained first-aiders on every shift (VS-83) & emergency protocol
- **Evidence-loss risk**: scene cleaned before documentation → claim defenseless; mitigated by mandatory photo/CCTV/witness capture before clean-up
- **Admission-of-liability risk**: store staff apologizes/admits → prejudices defense; mitigated by no-admission protocol (W4387)

### Time Estimate
- Immediate response minutes; ~30–60 min documentation per incident

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile incident app with CCTV auto-clip; witness/statement template; auto-notify insurance/legal; no-admission prompt

### Controls
- CTL: trained first-aider every shift; scene secured; evidence captured (photo/CCTV/witness); no admission of liability; insurance notified within 24 hrs

### Cross-references
- links to VS-83 (clinic), VS-23.2 (CCTV), VS-26 (insurance), VS-100 (legal), VS-91 (privacy), W4387 (framework), W4392 (system), W4405 (notification)

## W4402. Customer Incident Investigation, Documentation & Root Cause

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Recordable/severe incident (W4401); repeat near-miss |
| **Frequency** | Per recordable incident |
| **Volume** | ~tens–hundreds of investigations/yr (severity-driven) |
| **Owner** | Customer Safety / HSE (VS-24) / Store Manager |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), Store Ops (VS-07), Risk (VS-21), Legal (VS-100), Insurance (VS-26), IFM (VS-138) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Safety/HSE investigate — scene/witness/equipment/merchandise/CCTV review, sequence-of-events, contributing factors (design, maintenance, training, staffing, procedure) | Customer Safety / HSE / Store | Head of Customer Safety | Days–weeks |
| 2 | Customer Safety/HSE determine root cause (5-Whys/fishbone), assign causal factors to standards/design/maintenance/training, and document findings & evidence | Customer Safety / HSE / Risk | Head of Customer Safety | Per investigation |
| 3 | Customer Safety/Risk feed findings into CAPA (W4404), standards change (W4386), training (W4406), and the loss-data loop (W4385); retain records (VS-88) | Customer Safety / Risk / Legal | Head of Customer Safety / CRO | Per investigation |

### System Touchpoints
- Incident/evidence system (W4392); HSE (VS-24); risk (VS-21); legal matter (VS-100); records (VS-88); IFM (VS-138); BI (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Shallow-investigation risk**: blame-the-customer conclusion → root cause missed → repeat; mitigated by structured root-cause method & no-blame culture
- **Evidence-spoliation risk**: evidence not preserved/retained → claim/litigation disadvantage; mitigated by evidence-preservation protocol & retention (VS-88)

### Time Estimate
- ~4–20 hrs per investigation (severity-dependent)

### Automation Opportunity
- Investigation template with root-cause tool; CCTV/evidence link; finding→CAPA auto-flow; trend analytics

### Controls
- CTL: structured root-cause investigation; no-blame culture; evidence preserved/retained (VS-88); findings feed CAPA (W4404)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-21 (risk), VS-100 (legal), VS-88 (records), W4386 (standards), W4404 (CAPA), W4406 (training)

## W4403. Customer Injury/Premises-Liability Claim Handling & TPA Liaison

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer/claimant demand; lawyer letter; TPA referral |
| **Frequency** | Per claim (~tens–hundreds/yr) |
| **Volume** | Claims across ~205 sites; aggregate claim-cost exposure |
| **Owner** | Insurance (VS-26) / Legal (VS-100) / Customer Safety |
| **Participants** | Insurance (VS-26), Legal (VS-100), TPA, Treasury (VS-18), Risk (VS-21), Store (VS-07), Customer Safety |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Insurance/Legal open the claim in the system, acknowledge to claimant (no admission), assign TPA/adjuster, and set reserve per framework (W4387) | Insurance / Legal / TPA | CFO / CRO / VP Legal | Per claim |
| 2 | Insurance/Legal/TPA investigate liability & damages (gather evidence W4402, medical/damage proof, comparative-negligence), negotiate, and recommend settle/defend within authority | Insurance / Legal / TPA | CFO / VP Legal (settlement authority) | Weeks–months |
| 3 | Legal manage litigation if unreached (VS-100), Treasury fund settlement (VS-18), Insurance recover from insurer/TPA close & feed loss-data to prevention (W4385) | Legal / Treasury / Insurance | VP Legal / CFO | Per claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance/claims (VS-26); legal matter (VS-100); treasury (VS-18); risk (VS-21); incident/evidence (W4392); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reserve-understatement risk**: low reserve → earnings surprise; mitigated by actuarial/TPA reserving & periodic review
- **Settlement-authority-breach risk**: unauthorized settlement; mitigated by authority matrix (W4387) & legal sign-off

### Time Estimate
- ~hours–tens of hours per claim; litigation extends months–years

### Automation Opportunity
- Claim workflow with reserve/authority matrix; TPA portal; medical/damage document capture; loss-run & loss-triangle analytics

### Controls
- CTL: no-admission protocol; TPA/adjuster assigned; reserve per framework; settlement within authority matrix; loss-data fed to prevention

### Cross-references
- links to VS-26 (insurance), VS-100 (legal), VS-18 (treasury), VS-21 (risk), W4387 (framework), W4402 (investigation), W4408 (analytics)

## W4404. Customer-Safety Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Incident investigation (W4402); audit finding (W4407); repeat hazard |
| **Frequency** | Per finding |
| **Volume** | Hundreds–thousands of corrective actions/yr across ~205 sites |
| **Owner** | Customer Safety / HSE (VS-24) / Store Manager |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), Store Ops (VS-07), IFM (VS-138), Merchandising (VS-55), Real Estate (VS-20/VS-97), Training (VS-19.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Safety/HSE open CAPA per finding — corrective (fix this instance) + preventive (fix the system), assign owner/due-date, and link to root cause (W4402) | Customer Safety / HSE | Head of Customer Safety | Per finding |
| 2 | Assigned owner (Store/IFM/Merch/RE) execute CAPA — repair, redesign, standard change (W4386), retrain (W4406), rezone (W4389) — and evidence completion | Store Ops / IFM / Merch / RE / Training | Respective VP | Per CAPA |
| 3 | Customer Safety/HSE verify effectiveness, close CAPA on evidence, and trend repeat-findings for systemic action (W4408) | Customer Safety / HSE | Head of Customer Safety | Per CAPA |

### System Touchpoints
- CAPA module in incident system (W4392); store task (VS-63); IFM CMMS (VS-138); standards (W4386); training (VS-19.4); BI (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **CAPA-not-executed risk**: action opened but never closed → repeat incident; mitigated by due-date tracking & effectiveness verification
- **Symptom-fix risk**: corrective without preventive → recurrence; mitigated by mandatory preventive (system) action

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min per CAPA to manage; execution varies

### Automation Opportunity
- CAPA workflow with auto-routing/due-date/escalation; effectiveness-verification checklist; repeat-finding analytics

### Controls
- CTL: every finding has corrective + preventive action; owner/due-date assigned; effectiveness verified before close; repeats trended

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-138 (IFM), VS-55 (merch), VS-63 (task), W4386 (standards), W4402 (investigation), W4406 (training), W4408 (analytics)

## W4405. Regulatory (BFP/DOLE/LGU/BIR) & Insurance Incident Notification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Notifiable incident (W4401); fatality; major injury; fire |
| **Frequency** | Per notifiable incident |
| **Volume** | Few–tens of notifiable events/yr |
| **Owner** | Compliance (VS-22) / Customer Safety / Legal (VS-100) |
| **Participants** | Compliance (VS-22), Legal (VS-100), HSE (VS-24), Insurance (VS-26), LGU (VS-76), Customer Safety |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliance/Legal determine notifiability & recipient per the matrix — BFP (fire), DOLE (if worker involved / OSH-reportable), LGU (local incident/permit), DTI/Consumer (product-related), insurer (per policy 24-hr) | Compliance / Legal / Insurance | VP Legal / Head of Customer Safety | Immediate |
| 2 | Compliance/Legal file the notification within statutory window (often 24–72 hrs), preserve evidence (VS-88), and manage regulator follow-up/inspection (W4391) | Compliance / Legal / HSE | VP Legal | Per window |
| 3 | Insurance notify insurer/TPA within policy window, cooperate with investigation, and manage any breach-of-notice coverage issue (W4387/W4403) | Insurance / Legal | CFO / CRO | Per window |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance (VS-22); legal (VS-100); LGU (VS-76); HSE (VS-24); insurance (VS-26); records (VS-88); incident (W4392); notification matrix (W4387)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Late-notification risk**: missed statutory window → penalty/coverage denial; mitigated by 24–72-hr notification matrix & auto-trigger
- **Mis-notification risk**: wrong recipient/content → regulator friction; mitigated by matrix & legal review

### Time Estimate
- ~hours per notifiable event

### Automation Opportunity
- Notifiability decision-tree; auto-notification within window; regulator-response workflow; evidence-preservation

### Controls
- CTL: notification matrix maintained; statutory window met; insurer notified within policy window; evidence preserved

### Cross-references
- links to VS-22 (compliance), VS-100 (legal), VS-76 (LGU), VS-26 (insurance), W4387 (framework), W4391 (regulatory), W4401 (response)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Onboarding (VS-19); annual cycle; CAPA (W4404); new hazard/equipment |
| **Frequency** | Onboarding + annual + on change |
| **Volume** | ~5,800 store + ~600 DC staff + contingent (VS-98) trained annually |
| **Owner** | Customer Safety / L&D (VS-19.4) / HSE (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | L&D (VS-19.4), HSE (VS-24), Store Ops (VS-07), HR (VS-103), Contingent (VS-98), Sales Enablement (VS-124) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Safety/L&D/HSE build the customer-safety curriculum — hazard awareness, daily inspection (W4393), spill/chemical response (W4397), equipment rules (W4395), incident response (W4401), first aid, crowd (W4399), vulnerable-customer (W4400) | Customer Safety / L&D / HSE | Head of Customer Safety / CHRO | Annual + on change |
| 2 | L&D/HSE deliver (onboarding + annual refresh + drills — fire/evacuation, spill, first-aid, crowd), track competency/certification, and target contingent workforce too | L&D / HSE / Store Ops / HR | CHRO / COO | Per cycle |
| 3 | Customer Safety/HSE verify competency in practice (W4407), retrain on CAPA (W4404), and refresh on regulation/hazard change | Customer Safety / HSE / L&D | Head of Customer Safety | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- L&D (VS-19.4); HSE (VS-24); HR/competency (VS-103); contingent (VS-98); enablement (VS-124); incident/CAPA (W4392/W4404)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Untrained-staff risk**: staff unable to respond → worsened injury + liability; mitigated by onboarding + annual + certification gate
- **Drill-complacency risk**: drills not taken seriously → poor real response; mitigated by realistic drills & effectiveness check

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 hrs onboarding + ~2 hrs annual refresh per head; drills periodic

### Automation Opportunity
- e-learning + competency tracking; certification gate; drill scheduling & after-action; micro-learning on CAPA

### Controls
- CTL: curriculum covers all hazards/zones; onboarding + annual + certification; contingent included; drills realistic; competency verified (W4407)

### Cross-references

## W4408. Customer-Safety Performance Analytics, Benchmarking & Continuous Improvement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Reporting cycle; strategy review (W4385); continuous improvement |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + annual strategy |
| **Volume** | Enterprise customer-safety analytics across ~205 sites |
| **Owner** | Customer Safety / BI (VS-28) / Risk (VS-21) / OpEx (VS-133) |
| **Participants** | BI (VS-28), Risk (VS-21), Customer Safety, HSE (VS-24), Insurance (VS-26), OpEx (VS-133), Store Ops (VS-07) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Safety/BI build the analytics — injury/near-miss rate, frequency/severity, by zone/cause/site, claim cost/frequency/severity, CAPA closure/aging, audit score, leading indicators (inspection %, hazard density) | Customer Safety / BI / Risk | Head of Customer Safety / CRO | Monthly |
| 2 | Customer Safety/Risk benchmark vs retail/home-improvement industry and internal prior-period, identify hotspots/trends, and feed strategy (W4385)/CAPA (W4404)/standards (W4386) | Customer Safety / Risk / BI | Head of Customer Safety | Monthly/annual |
| 3 | Customer Safety/OpEx drive continuous improvement — eliminate repeat root causes, adopt best-practice from best-performing sites, and measure ROI of prevention investment | Customer Safety / OpEx / Store Ops | Head of Customer Safety / COO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- BI (VS-28); incident/claims (W4392/VS-26); risk (VS-21); OpEx/process-mining (VS-133); store-ops (VS-07); finance (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lagging-only risk**: tracking only injuries (lagging) → misses leading signals; mitigated by leading-indicator set (inspection %, near-miss, hazard density)
- **Analytics-not-actioned risk**: insights not converted to CAPA/standards; mitigated by mandatory feed to W4404/W4386

### Time Estimate
- Monthly analytics; ~annual benchmarking

### Automation Opportunity
- KPI dashboard with leading+lagging; loss-triangle/frequency-severity analytics; hotspot heat-map; benchmark import; ROI model

### Controls
- CTL: leading + lagging indicators tracked; benchmarked; insights actioned via CAPA (W4404)/standards (W4386); ROI measured

### Cross-references
- links to VS-28 (BI), VS-21 (risk), VS-133 (OpEx), VS-26 (insurance/loss-run), W4385 (strategy), W4404 (CAPA), W4407 (reporting)
