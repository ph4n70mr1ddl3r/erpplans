## W4385. Customer Safety & Premises-Liability Strategy, Scope & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Enterprise risk review; major incident; new format/zone |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on strategic change |
| **Volume** | One enterprise customer-safety & premises-liability program across ~205 public sites |
| **Owner** | Head of Customer Safety / Risk (VS-21) / COO |
| **Participants** | Risk (VS-21), Store Ops (VS-07), HSE (VS-24), Legal (VS-100), Insurance/BCP (VS-26), IFM (VS-138), Real Estate (VS-97) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk/Customer Safety set the program strategy — scope (in-store, yard, parking, will-call, doorstep), objectives (zero fatalities, injury-rate target, claim-cost cap), and the prevention-by-design operating model | Head of Customer Safety / Risk | COO / CRO | Annual |
| 2 | Risk map the exposure landscape — premises-liability bodily injury, property damage, falling/shifted stock, powered-equipment contact, chemical exposure, crowd crush, parking — and the loss-history baseline | Risk / Insurance / Legal | Head of Customer Safety / CRO | Annual |
| 3 | Risk document & approve the strategy, then cascade to standards (W4386), liability/insurance framework (W4387), governance (W4388), zone design (W4389), and the incident system (W4392) | Head of Customer Safety / Risk | COO / Audit Committee | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Risk register (VS-21.2); incident-management system (W4392); insurance/claims (VS-26); legal-matter (VS-100); IFM CMMS (VS-138); BI (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scope-blindness risk**: program covers only the sales floor while the lumber yard and parking lot (where the highest-severity incidents occur) drift; mitigated by full-zone scope and loss-history-driven prioritization
- **Strategy-execution gap**: strategy approved but never operationalized into standards; mitigated by mandatory cascade to W4386/W4389 and annual audit (W4407)

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual strategy + on major-incident trigger

### Automation Opportunity
- Loss-history heat-mapping to priority sites/zones; auto-cascade of strategy to standards/zone design; KPI dashboarding

### Controls
- CTL-671 (ensure controlled execution — Customer Safety Risk Strategy, Standards & Premises-Liability Framework (PA-147.1).)
- CTL: strategy approved by COO/Audit Committee; scope covers all customer zones; loss-history baseline established; reviewed annually (W4407)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2 (risk), VS-26 (insurance), VS-100 (legal), VS-138 (IFM), W4386 (standards), W4387 (liability), W4407 (audit)

## W4386. Customer Safety Standards, Hazard Register & Risk Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4385); new hazard; incident learning |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | One customer-safety standards & hazard register; ~205 site risk assessments |
| **Owner** | Customer Safety / HSE (VS-24) / Risk (VS-21) |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), Store Ops (VS-07), IFM (VS-138), Risk (VS-21.2), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Safety/HSE build the customer hazard register — slip/trip/fall, falling stock, shifted pallets, powered-equipment contact, chemical exposure, sharp/cut, parking, crowd, child — per zone and per merchandise type | Customer Safety / HSE | Head of Customer Safety | Annual |
| 2 | Customer Safety conduct site risk assessments (likelihood × severity × exposure), define the mandatory control set per hazard (engineering/administrative/PPE/signage), and set inspection frequency | Customer Safety / HSE / Store Ops | Head of Customer Safety / COO | Annual + per site |
| 3 | HSE/Store Ops publish the standards, train store teams (W4406), and review on incident learning (W4402) or change | HSE / Store Ops | COO / Head of Customer Safety | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Hazard register & risk-assessment module (W4392); HSE (VS-24); IFM PM (VS-138); store-ops task (VS-63); BI (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hazard-blindness risk**: undocumented hazard (e.g. unstable tile-stack height) → uncontrolled exposure; mitigated by mandatory register + per-merchandise-type hazard mapping
- **Control-effectiveness risk**: control specified but not field-verified → drift; mitigated by inspection (W4393) and audit (W4407)

### Time Estimate
- ~6–8 weeks annual; ~1 day per new-site assessment × ~205 sites

### Automation Opportunity
- Hazard-library templates by zone/merchandise; risk-scoring engine; control-vs-hazard auto-link; mobile risk-assessment app

### Controls
- CTL-164 (ensure customer safety & premises-liability control.)
- CTL: hazard register maintained; risk-assessed per site; controls field-verified (W4393/W4407); reviewed on incident

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-138 (IFM), VS-63 (store task), W4385 (strategy), W4393 (inspection), W4406 (training), W4407 (audit)

## W4387. Premises-Liability, Insurance & Legal Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4385); insurance renewal; claim; legal review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on renewal/claim |
| **Volume** | One premises-liability/insurance framework; ~205 covered locations |
| **Owner** | Insurance/Risk Transfer (VS-26) / Legal (VS-100) / Customer Safety |
| **Participants** | Insurance/BCP (VS-26), Legal (VS-100), Treasury (VS-18), Risk (VS-21), Customer Safety |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Insurance/Legal define the premises-liability coverage framework — CGL/public liability limits, deductibles, TPA arrangement, named-peril vs all-risk, and the additional-insured requirement for landlord/lessor sites | Insurance / Legal / Treasury | CFO / CRO / VP Legal | Annual + renewal |
| 2 | Customer Safety/Insurance set the claims-handling protocol (notice, TPA liaison, reserve, settlement authority) and the loss-data feedback loop into prevention (W4385/W4386) | Insurance / Customer Safety / Legal | CFO / CRO | Annual |
| 3 | Legal set the litigation/defense framework (counsel panel, defense strategy, settlement authority, NLRC/court process) and the statutory-notification matrix (W4405) | Legal / Insurance | VP Legal / CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance policy/claims (VS-26); legal matter (VS-100); treasury (VS-18); risk register (VS-21.2); incident system (W4392)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-insurance risk**: inadequate limits/deductibles → uninsured loss; mitigated by annual exposure-based limit review
- **Late-notice risk**: claim reported late to insurer → coverage denied; mitigated by mandatory 24-hr notification protocol (W4405)

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual framework + per-renewal/claim

### Automation Opportunity
- Coverage-vs-exposure modeling; auto-notification on incident; TPA-claim workflow; loss-run analytics

### Controls
- CTL-671 (ensure controlled execution — Customer Safety Risk Strategy, Standards & Premises-Liability Framework (PA-147.1).)
- CTL: limits exposure-based & renewed; TPA-governed; 24-hr notification (W4405); loss-data fed to prevention

### Cross-references

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Safety/Risk establish the governance structure — program owner, site accountability (Store Manager as first-line), second-line HSE/Risk assurance, third-line Audit, and Board/Audit-Committee oversight | Customer Safety / Risk / Governance | COO / Audit Committee | Annual |
| 2 | Customer Safety publish the RACI — who inspects (store/HSE), who approves corrective action, who manages claims, who notifies regulators/insurers — across all sites and zones | Customer Safety / Risk | COO | Annual |

- **Three-lines-blur risk**: second-line also performs first-line → assurance not independent; mitigated by clear three-lines separation & Audit oversight

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks annual framework

### Automation Opportunity
- RACI workflow with role-based task routing; accountability dashboard; exception/consequence tracking

### Controls
- CTL-164 (ensure customer safety & premises-liability control.)
- CTL: single program owner; first-line Store-Manager accountability; three-lines separation; Audit oversight (W4407)

## W4389. Customer Safety by Zone — Sales Floor, Yard, Lumber/Tile & Parking Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4385); new store (VS-37); remodel (VS-109); hazard learning |
| **Frequency** | Per design + on change |
| **Volume** | Zone safety designs for 4–5 zones × ~205 sites |
| **Owner** | Customer Safety / Real Estate (VS-20/VS-97) / Store Ops (VS-07) |
| **Participants** | Real Estate (VS-20/VS-97), Store Ops (VS-07), HSE (VS-24), IFM (VS-138), Merchandising (VS-01/VS-55) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Safety/Real Estate design zone-specific safety: sales-floor (aisle width, clear-height, endcap stability, spill protocol); lumber/tile yard (separated pedestrian/forklift lanes, stacked-load height limits, banding); parking (lighting, crosswalks, cart corrals, speed) | Customer Safety / Real Estate / HSE | Head of Customer Safety / VP Real Estate | Per design |
| 2 | Merchandising/IFM specify safe stacking/pallet/racking & display standards (load, height, stability, restraint) per merchandise type (cement, tile, lumber, appliances) and embed in planogram (VS-55) | Merchandising / IFM / Customer Safety | Head of Customer Safety / VP Merchandising | Per change |
| 3 | Store Ops/IFM implement the design (barriers, signage, line-marking, lighting), inspect on change, and feed learning into W4386/W4402 | Store Ops / IFM | COO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Real estate/design (VS-20/VS-97); planogram/space (VS-55); IFM CMMS (VS-138); store-ops (VS-07); remodel (VS-109); hazard register (W4386)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pedestrian-equipment-conflict risk**: customers and forklifts sharing lumber/tile yard aisles → high-severity contact; mitigated by separated lanes, banking-hours-only equipment rules, and spotters (W4395)
- **Stack-collapse risk**: unstable cement/tile/lumber stacks → falling-stock injury; mitigated by height/restraint standards & inspection (W4394)

### Time Estimate
- Per-design weeks; ~1–2 days/site zone setup

### Automation Opportunity
- Zone-design templates; safe-stacking standards library linked to planogram; mobile zone-setup checklist

### Controls
- CTL-671 (ensure controlled execution — Customer Safety Risk Strategy, Standards & Premises-Liability Framework (PA-147.1).)
- CTL: zone designs approved; stacking standards enforced (W4394); pedestrian-equipment separation (W4395)

### Cross-references

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Third party working on-site; vendor delivery; remodel |
| **Frequency** | Per engagement + continuous adherence |
| **Volume** | ~220 DC + DSD deliveries/day (W7: ~200/day DC + ~20/day DSD), remodel crews, service vendors, promodizers, concessionaires |
| **Owner** | Customer Safety / Vendor Mgmt (VS-03) / Store Ops (VS-07) |
| **Participants** | Vendor Mgmt (VS-03), Store Ops (VS-07), HSE (VS-24), Contingent Workforce (VS-98), Legal (VS-100), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vendor Mgmt/Legal set third-party on-premises safety terms — contractor safety induction, insurance/indemnity (additional-insured), equipment rules during trading hours, incident-reporting duty — in contracts | Vendor Mgmt / Legal / Customer Safety | VP Legal / Head of Customer Safety | Per contract |
| 2 | Store Ops/Customer Safety induct & badge third parties (DSD drivers, remodel crews, promodizers, service vendors) on customer-safety rules, equipment-exclusion zones, and reporting | Store Ops / Customer Safety / HSE | Store Manager / Head of Customer Safety | Per visit/engagement |
| 3 | Customer Safety/HSE monitor third-party adherence, stop unsafe work, and enforce consequence (contractual/insurance) | Customer Safety / HSE / Store Ops | Head of Customer Safety / VP Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor/contract (VS-03); contingent workforce (VS-98); store task (VS-63); HSE (VS-24); insurance (VS-26); legal (VS-100); incident (W4392)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Third-party-caused incident risk**: contractor/forklift injures customer → BuildRight still liable as premises occupier; mitigated by induction, indemnity, additional-insured, and stop-work authority
- **Untracked-presence risk**: unbilled/unbadged third party on floor → no accountability; mitigated by induction/badge gating (links VS-98)

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min induction per third party; ongoing monitoring

### Automation Opportunity
- Third-party induction/badge kiosk; insurance/indemnity verification gate; equipment-zone geofencing; stop-work workflow

### Controls
- CTL-671 (ensure controlled execution — Customer Safety Risk Strategy, Standards & Premises-Liability Framework (PA-147.1).)
- CTL: third-party safety terms in contract; induction mandatory; additional-insured/indemnity; stop-work authority

### Cross-references
