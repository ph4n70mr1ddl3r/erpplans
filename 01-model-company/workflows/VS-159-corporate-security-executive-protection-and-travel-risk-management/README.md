# VS-159: Corporate Security, Executive Protection & Travel Risk Management

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Corporate Security, Executive Protection & Travel Risk Management workflows for BuildRight Depot
Corp. — owning the enterprise **corporate-security discipline** that protects BuildRight's people,
principals (the family/CEO/board of a privately-held Filipino corporation), assets, and reputation
from **intentional human threats** at the corporate (not store-merchandise) level: executive
protection and close protection, protective intelligence and threat assessment, the global security
operations center (GSOC), executive and employee travel risk / duty of care, corporate
investigations (fraud, embezzlement, IP theft, insider threat), workplace-violence prevention,
kidnap & ransom / extortion response, civil-unrest and active-assailant response, and coordination
with law enforcement (PNP/NBI).

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. "Executive protection", "protective
intelligence", "corporate security", "travel security", and "kidnap & ransom" each appear in
**zero** PA files; "threat intelligence" appears once (and is cyber, VS-27.3 W397). Site-level
physical security is covered (store W71/W171, DC W797) and audited (VS-21.3 W358), retail
shrinkage/theft LP is owned by VS-23, customer premises safety by VS-147, cyber by VS-27.3, and
crisis comms by VS-14.3 — but the **dedicated corporate-security & executive-protection program**
(the function that runs protective intelligence, principal protection, travel-risk/duty-of-care,
corporate investigations, and coordinated crisis response to intentional threats) is genuinely
unowned.

This is distinct from **VS-23 (Loss Prevention)** which protects **merchandise** from shrink/theft
at store/DC — this value stream protects **corporate people, principals, and the enterprise** from
intentional threats. It is distinct from **VS-147 (Customer Safety/Premises Liability)** which
protects **customers on premises** from accidental harm — this value stream protects **executives,
travelers, and the corporation** from targeted/deliberate threats. It is distinct from **VS-27.3
(Cybersecurity)** which protects **information/digital assets** — this value stream protects
**physical people and operations** (and coordinates with cyber on converged threats). It is distinct
from **VS-21.3 W358 (Physical Security Audit)** which **assures** security controls — this value
stream **operates** the corporate-security program. It is distinct from **VS-26 (BCP/Crisis)** which
restores operations after disruption — this value stream **prevents and responds to** targeted
human threats (and feeds VS-26 where an incident escalates).

BuildRight's exposure is structural: a high-profile PHP 62.3B family corporation with a named
CEO/board, 200 stores across Philippine regions (some with elevated kidnap/extortion/civil-unrest
exposure), executive and buyer travel (domestic inter-island and international sourcing trips to
China/Taiwan/ASEAN per VS-122), high-value cash and inventory, and an increasingly digital and
public-facing brand — the absence of a structured corporate-security & executive-protection
program is a recognized gap in Philippine large-enterprise risk management.
