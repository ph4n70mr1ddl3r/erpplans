## W4673. Corporate Security Strategy, Charter & Risk-Appetite Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Enterprise risk strategy (VS-33/VS-21.2); board direction; annual review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | 1 corporate-security strategy & charter |
| **Owner** | Chief Security Officer (CSO) / Head of Corporate Security |
| **Participants** | CEO, CFO, Head of Internal Audit & Risk (VS-21.2), VP Legal (VS-100.3), Board Risk Committee (VS-36), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the corporate-security strategy and charter: mission, scope (people, principals, assets, reputation), governance (CSO reporting line — typically to CEO/Head of Internal Audit & Risk/VP Legal & Compliance), and the risk-appetite for intentional-human-threat exposure (executive protection, travel, investigations) | CSO / Head of Internal Audit & Risk / VP Legal | CEO / Board Risk Committee | Annual |
| 2 | Align the strategy to enterprise risk management (VS-21.2 W122) and BCP (VS-26); define the corporate-security risk taxonomy (protective, travel, investigative, workplace-violence, civil-unrest, kidnap/extortion, convergence with cyber VS-27.3) and KRIs | CSO / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | CEO | Annual |
| 3 | Obtain board/risk-committee endorsement (VS-36); fund the program (CSO headcount, GSOC, vendors, insurance — links VS-26 K&R cover); publish the policy enterprise-wide | CSO / CFO / Board | CEO / Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ERM/risk register (VS-21.2); board governance (VS-36); insurance/K&R (VS-26); policy management (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Charter-ambiguity risk**: Unclear CSO authority/scope vs. LP (VS-23), HSE (VS-24), cyber (VS-27.3) → gaps/overlaps in a crisis; mitigated by an explicit charter and RACI with adjacent functions
- **Under-resourcing risk**: Program funded only after an incident; mitigated by board-endorsed risk-appetite and quantified exposure

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual strategy + charter (CSO + leadership)

### Automation Opportunity
- Risk-appetite dashboard; charter/RACI workflow linking adjacent security/risk functions

### Controls
- CTL-166 (ensure executive-protection & travel-risk control.)
- CTL-707: charter board-endorsed (VS-36); explicit RACI vs. LP/HSE/cyber; risk-appetite documented (VS-21.2)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33/VS-21.2 (strategy/ERM), VS-36 (board), VS-26 (BCP/insurance), VS-23/VS-24/VS-27.3 (adjacent security functions)

## W4674. Protective Intelligence & Threat Assessment Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Threat indicator; pre-event/pre-travel; periodic principal review; tip from HR/LP/ethics |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + per case |
| **Volume** | ~dozens of assessments/yr (events, travel, principals, disgruntled-employee flags) |
| **Owner** | CSO / Protective Intelligence Lead |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19/VS-84), LP (VS-23), Ethics (VS-119), Legal (VS-100.3), law-enforcement liaison |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Operate the protective-intelligence function: intake threat indicators (from OSINT/HUMINT W4675, HR disgruntled-employee flags VS-84/VS-150, LP VS-23, ethics hotline VS-119, social-media monitoring); triage and assess credibility, capability, and proximity | Protective Intelligence Lead / CSO | CSO | Per case |
| 2 | Conduct threat assessments for principals (W4678), high-risk events (W4684), and pre-travel (W4687); assign a threat level and recommend protective posture (enhanced EP, route change, event cancellation, law-enforcement referral) | Protective Intelligence Lead | CSO / VP Legal | Per case |
| 3 | Document each assessment and decision; coordinate with Legal (defamation/privacy limits VS-91/VS-100.3) and law enforcement (PNP/NBI) where a credible threat warrants; close and review the case | CSO / Legal | CSO | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Case/threat-assessment system (security ops); HR/LP/ethics feeds (VS-19/VS-23/VS-119); OSINT/HUMINT tools (W4675); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Late-intelligence risk**: A credible threat detected only after it materializes; mitigated by continuous monitoring + cross-function intake
- **Privacy/defamation risk**: Threat assessment on an individual breaching privacy (VS-91) or defaming; mitigated by Legal review and documented, evidence-based process

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous intake; ~1–3 days per significant assessment

### Automation Opportunity
- Cross-function threat-indicator intake; OSINT/social-media alerting; case-management workflow

### Controls
- CTL-707 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Security Strategy, Threat Intelligence & Risk Framework (PA-159.1).)
- CTL-707: assessments documented and evidence-based; Legal/privacy-compliant (VS-91/VS-100.3); law-enforcement referral where warranted

### Cross-references
- links to VS-19/VS-84/VS-150 (HR/labor/drug-free), VS-23 (LP), VS-119 (ethics), VS-91/VS-100.3 (privacy/legal), W4675 (OSINT), W4678 (principal profile)

## W4675. Open-Source & Human Intelligence (OSINT/HUMINT) Monitoring & Alerting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous monitoring; alert threshold |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (24/7) |
| **Volume** | ~25–40 monitored entities (10–20 principal names per W4678, brand/trademark terms, key executives, regions, sourcing destinations) |
| **Owner** | Protective Intelligence Lead |
| **Participants** | CSO, Legal (VS-100.3), Privacy (VS-91), external intelligence provider |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Operate OSINT monitoring (news, social media, dark-web mentions of principals, brand, threats, protests near sites/HQ, geopolitical events in sourcing destinations VS-122); HUMINT via vetted sources and the guard force (W4677) | Protective Intelligence Lead / Provider | CSO | Continuous |
| 2 | Triage alerts: filter noise, escalate credible indicators to protective intelligence (W4674); correlate with internal indicators (HR, LP, cyber VS-27.3 W397) for converged threats | Protective Intelligence Lead | CSO | Per alert |
| 3 | Govern monitoring within privacy/legal limits (VS-91/VS-100.3 — monitoring public OSINT, not unlawful surveillance); retain intelligence per policy (VS-88) | CSO / Legal / Privacy | CSO / VP Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- OSINT/social-media monitoring platform; cyber threat intel (VS-27.3 W397); security ops case system; records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Alert-fatigue risk**: High OSINT volume → real threats missed in noise; mitigated by tuned thresholds and correlation
- **Over-collection/legal risk**: Monitoring exceeding lawful/privacy bounds → liability (VS-91); mitigated by Legal/Privacy governance and public-source scoping

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous (GSOC analyst capacity); provider-led

### Automation Opportunity
- OSINT/dark-web monitoring platform; alert-correlation engine; privacy-scoping rules

### Controls
- CTL-166 (ensure executive-protection & travel-risk control.)
- CTL-707: monitoring lawful and privacy-compliant (VS-91/VS-100.3); alerts triaged and escalated; intelligence retained per policy

### Cross-references
- links to W4674 (protective intel), VS-27.3 W397 (cyber intel), VS-122 (sourcing destinations), VS-91/VS-100.3 (privacy/legal)

## W4676. Corporate Security Operations Center (GSOC) — 24/7 Monitoring & Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | 24/7 operations; incident alert |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (24/7) |
| **Volume** | 1 GSOC; ~ monitoring of sites, travel, intelligence, alarms across 200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ + travelers |
| **Owner** | GSOC Manager / CSO |
| **Participants** | LP (VS-23), Facilities (VS-138), IT/cyber (VS-27.3), BCP (VS-26), guard force (W4677) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Operate the GSOC 24/7: monitor site alarms/CCTV (VS-23/VS-71), traveler location (W4687/W4688), intelligence feeds (W4675), and incident channels; triage and dispatch response (guard, LP, law enforcement, medical) | GSOC Analysts / GSOC Manager | CSO | Continuous |
| 2 | Coordinate response across functions: corporate-security incidents, site incidents (VS-23/VS-147), cyber incidents (VS-27.3 W383), BCP events (VS-26); maintain the incident command role until handoff | GSOC Manager / CSO | CSO | Per incident |
| 3 | Log every incident, response, and outcome in the case system; produce daily/weekly intelligence & incident summaries for CSO/leadership; feed trend analysis (W4696) | GSOC Manager | CSO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- GSOC platform (alarm/CCTV/traveler/integration); LP systems (VS-23); cyber SOC (VS-27.3); BCP (VS-26); case management (W4696)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Coverage-gap risk**: A GSOC that doesn't run 24/7 or doesn't integrate traveler/intel feeds misses the window to intervene; mitigated by 24/7 staffing and integrated monitoring
- **Handoff-confusion risk**: Unclear incident-command handoff across corporate-security/LP/cyber/BCP; mitigated by the charter RACI (W4673)

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous; 24/7 staffing (shift model)

### Automation Opportunity
- Integrated GSOC platform (alarm/CCTV/traveler/intel); automated dispatch; incident-command workflow

### Controls
- CTL-166 (ensure executive-protection & travel-risk control.)
- CTL-707: 24/7 staffing; integrated monitoring; incident-command RACI; every incident logged

### Cross-references
- links to W4673 (charter), W4675 (intel), W4687/W4688 (travel), VS-23/VS-27.3/VS-26 (adjacent incident functions), W4696 (analytics)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set enterprise physical-security standards (access-control system, CCTV coverage and retention, perimeter, intrusion detection, visitor management) for HQ/executive sites above the store/DC baseline (VS-07 W71/VS-04 W797); govern the enterprise access-control/CCTV platform | Corporate Physical Security Manager / CSO | CSO | Per standard refresh |
| 2 | Set guard-force standards (posting, training, arming posture, command-and-control, vetting); define the guard agency SLA and oversight (W4680); coordinate guard response with the GSOC (W4676) | CSO / Guard-Agency Mgr | CSO | Per standard |

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per principal + quarterly review |
| **Volume** | ~ board + C-suite + family principals (~10–20 profiles) |
| **Owner** | CSO / EP Lead |
| **Participants** | Protective Intelligence (W4674), Legal (VS-100.3), Insurance (VS-26 K&R), principals' offices |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain a threat profile per principal (exposure from role, wealth, public profile, travel, family, residence, recent events/decisions that could attract threat); set the baseline protective posture | EP Lead / Protective Intelligence | CSO | Per principal + quarterly |
| 2 | Maintain the principal-risk register entry in the ERM (VS-21.2); review quarterly with the Head of Internal Audit & Risk and principals' offices; adjust posture on threat change | CSO / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | CEO / Board Risk Committee | Quarterly |
| 3 | Coordinate K&R insurance coverage adequacy (VS-26), privacy of principal data (VS-91), and Legal liaison (VS-100.3) for any threat-related matter | CSO / Insurance / Legal | CSO / VP Legal | Quarterly + on change |

### System Touchpoints
- Principal-profile system (security ops); ERM (VS-21.2); K&R insurance (VS-26); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Static-profile risk**: A profile not updated after a threat-attracting event (e.g., a controversial business decision) → under-protection; mitigated by quarterly + event-triggered review
- **Privacy risk**: Principal profile data mishandled; mitigated by tight access and VS-91 governance

### Time Estimate
- ~per principal profile 1–2 days; ~quarterly review absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- Profile-change/event-trigger review; principal-risk-register integration with ERM; access-controlled profile repository

### Controls
- CTL-166 (ensure executive-protection & travel-risk control.)
- CTL-707: profiles reviewed quarterly + on event; K&R coverage adequacy assessed; principal data privacy-governed (VS-91)

## W4679. Travel Risk Intelligence, Geopolitical & Kidnap/Extortion Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Executive/employee travel (W4687); geopolitical event; destination risk change |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + per trip |
| **Volume** | ~80–150 monitored executive and buyer trips/year (domestic inter-island + international sourcing VS-122) |
| **Owner** | Protective Intelligence Lead / Travel Risk Manager |
| **Participants** | CSO, Travel/Mobility (VS-160), Treasury (VS-18), Insurance (VS-26 K&R), sourcing (VS-122) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain destination-risk intelligence (crime, kidnap, civil-unrest, terrorism, health, natural-disaster ratings) for domestic Philippine regions and international sourcing destinations; refresh continuously | Travel Risk Manager / Provider | CSO | Continuous |
| 2 | For each high-risk trip (executive travel, cash-carrying VS-81, sourcing to elevated-risk destinations), produce a travel-risk assessment and recommend posture (route/lodging, EP support, tracking, avoidance); brief the traveler (W4687) | Travel Risk Manager / EP | CSO | Per trip |
| 3 | Monitor active-trip travelers for emerging threats (W4676 GSOC); maintain K&R/extortion response readiness (W4686) and feed travel-risk into mobility policy (VS-160) | Travel Risk Manager / GSOC | CSO | Per active trip |

### System Touchpoints
- Destination-risk intelligence platform; traveler-tracking (W4687); GSOC (W4676); K&R insurance (VS-26); mobility (VS-160)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stale-intelligence risk**: Destination risk not updated (e.g., a sudden civil-unrest event) → traveler exposed; mitigated by continuous monitoring + active-trip alerting
- **Kidnap-response-unreadiness risk**: A kidnap/extortion incident with no rehearsed response → catastrophic outcome; mitigated by W4686 protocol and K&R cover (VS-26)

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous intelligence; ~per high-risk trip 1–2 days assessment

### Automation Opportunity
- Destination-risk feed into travel-booking; active-trip geo-alerting; K&R-readiness checklist

### Controls
- CTL-166 (ensure executive-protection & travel-risk control.)
- CTL-707: destination intelligence continuously refreshed; high-risk trips assessed and briefed; K&R response readiness maintained

|---|---|

| **Owner** | CSO / Corporate Security Vendor Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement (VS-34), TPRM (VS-161), Legal (VS-100.3), Finance (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select and contract corporate-security vendors (guard agencies, EP providers, intelligence/investigation firms) via procurement (VS-34) and TPRM due diligence (VS-161): licensure (PNP-SOSIA for security agencies), vetting, insurance, confidentiality | CSO / Procurement / TPRM | CSO / CFO | Per vendor |

### Time Estimate
- ~per vendor selection 1–3 weeks; ~ongoing oversight absorbed

### Automation Opportunity
- Vendor-licensure/insurance tracking; SLA dashboards; TPRM integration (VS-161)

### Controls
- CTL-707 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Security Strategy, Threat Intelligence & Risk Framework (PA-159.1).)
