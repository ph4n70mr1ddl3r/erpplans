## W4681. Executive Protection Program Design & Close-Protection Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Principal daily operations; threat-level change; event |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (daily EP detail) |
| **Volume** | ~ principals under routine EP (~C-suite + family); details per principal |
| **Owner** | EP Lead / CSO |
| **Participants** | EP agents, principals' offices, drivers (W4683), GSOC (W4676) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design and operate the EP program: standing close-protection details scaled to each principal's threat profile (W4678), protective logistics (vehicles, comms, medical), and the low-profile/high-profile posture toggle | EP Lead | CSO | Continuous |
| 2 | Execute daily EP operations: principal movements, venue/vehicle advance, protective surveillance and counter-surveillance, access control at principal engagements; coordinate with GSOC (W4676) and route security (W4683) | EP Agents / EP Lead | CSO | Daily |
| 3 | Review EP posture after each significant movement/event; capture lessons and adjust; maintain EP readiness drills (medical, evacuation, attack response) | EP Lead / CSO | CSO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- EP scheduling/operations system; comms; GSOC (W4676); principal profile (W4678); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Complacency risk**: Routine EP that drills down to ceremony fails in a real incident; mitigated by realistic drills and posture review
- **Profile-too-high risk**: Overt EP attracting attention/creating target; mitigated by low-profile posture where appropriate

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous (EP detail staffing model per principal)

### Automation Opportunity
- EP scheduling/route-planning tools; protective-surveillance tech; drill-readiness tracker

### Controls
- CTL-708 (ensure controlled execution — Executive Protection Principal Travel and Event Security (PA.)
- CTL: EP posture matches threat profile (W4678); drills maintained; GSOC coordination

### Cross-references
- links to W4676 (GSOC), W4678 (profile), W4683 (transport), W4686 (K&R response), VS-26 (K&R insurance)

## W4682. Principal (Board/Family/CEO) Travel Security & Advance Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Principal domestic/international trip |
| **Frequency** | Per trip |
| **Volume** | ~ principal trips/yr (domestic inter-island + international) |
| **Owner** | EP Lead / Travel Risk Manager |
| **Participants** | EP agents (advance + traveling), Travel/Mobility (VS-160), intelligence (W4679), local fixers/security |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each principal trip, conduct the protective advance: destination threat assessment (W4679), lodging/venue/transport vetting, route planning, medical/hospital identification, local security/law-enforcement liaison, and comms plan | EP Advance / Travel Risk | CSO | Per trip |
| 2 | Deploy traveling EP and coordinate with the principal's itinerary; maintain GSOC check-in cadence (W4676) and traveler-tracking (W4687); manage deviations and emerging threats | Traveling EP / GSOC | CSO | Per trip |

- EP/travel ops; destination-risk (W4679); GSOC/traveler-tracking (W4676/W4687); mobility (VS-160)

### Time Estimate
- ~per trip 2–5 days advance + trip-duration coverage

### Automation Opportunity
- Advance-checklist workflow; destination-risk feed; check-in/tracking automation

### Controls
- CTL-708 (ensure controlled execution — Executive Protection Principal Travel and Event Security (PA.)
- CTL: protective advance completed per trip; GSOC check-in maintained; post-trip debrief

### Cross-references
- links to W4679 (destination risk), W4687/W4688 (travel tracking/lost-contact), VS-160 (mobility), W4686 (K&R)

## W4686. Kidnap & Ransom (K&R), Extortion & Blackmail Response Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Kidnap/extortion/blackmail incident or credible threat |
| **Frequency** | Rare (rehearsal continuous) |
| **Volume** | ~ hopefully zero incidents; 1 rehearsed protocol |
| **Owner** | CSO / Crisis Response Lead |
| **Participants** | CEO, VP Legal (VS-100.3), CFO, K&R insurer/consultant (VS-26), law enforcement (PNP/PNP-ACG/NBI), comms (VS-14.3), GSOC (W4676) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain and rehearse the K&R/extortion response protocol: incident activation, crisis-response team, law-enforcement notification, K&R insurer/consultant engagement (VS-26), negotiator coordination, and family/principal liaison — with strict information control | CSO / Crisis Response Lead / K&R Consultant | CEO / VP Legal | Rehearsal continuous |
| 2 | On incident: activate the protocol, engage law enforcement and K&R consultant, manage communications/information (avoid leakage that endangers the victim), and coordinate ransom/payment decisions per policy and law | CSO / VP Legal / CFO / K&R Consultant | CEO / Board | Per incident |
| 3 | Conduct post-incident review (with the K&R consultant and law enforcement); capture lessons, support the victim/family recovery (VS-83), and adjust protocols and insurance (VS-26) | CSO / VP Legal / K&R Consultant | CEO | Post-incident |

### System Touchpoints
- Crisis-response protocol/case system; K&R insurance (VS-26); law-enforcement liaison; comms (VS-14.3); GSOC (W4676)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unrehearsed-response risk**: A kidnap with no rehearsed protocol → catastrophic delay/error; mitigated by continuous rehearsal and K&R consultant pre-engagement (VS-26)
- **Information-leakage risk**: Leakage during an incident endangers the victim; mitigated by strict information control and limited need-to-know
- **Payment/legal risk**: Improper ransom/payment creating legal/ABC exposure; mitigated by Legal (VS-100.3) and insurer guidance

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous rehearsal (tabletop ~2–4×/yr); per-incident all-consuming

### Automation Opportunity
- Crisis-response activation workflow; secure comms; tabletop-scheduling tracker

### Controls
- CTL-708 (ensure controlled execution — Executive Protection Principal Travel and Event Security (PA.)
- CTL: K&R protocol rehearsed; K&R insurer/consultant engaged (VS-26); information control; Legal-guided payment decisions

### Cross-references
- links to VS-26 (K&R insurance/BCP), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-14.3 (crisis comms), VS-83 (victim/family support), W4676 (GSOC), W4694 (incident response)

## W4687. Employee Travel Risk, Pre-Travel Briefing & Itinerary Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee/business trip (domestic inter-island or international) |
| **Frequency** | Per trip |
| **Volume** | ~ buyer/sourcing (VS-122), executive, field-sales (VS-140), audit (VS-21), training trips |
| **Owner** | Travel Risk Manager / CSO (joint with Travel/Mobility VS-160) |
| **Participants** | Travel booking (VS-160), HR (VS-19), managers, GSOC (W4676) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each business trip, screen the destination against travel-risk intelligence (W4679); require pre-travel briefing for elevated-risk destinations (threats, health, local laws, emergency contacts, check-in cadence) | Travel Risk Manager / Mobility | CSO / HR | Per trip |
| 2 | Enroll the traveler in itinerary tracking (linked to GSOC W4676) for the trip duration; confirm travel/medical insurance and K&R coverage applicability (VS-26); brief duty-of-care obligations to the manager | Travel Risk / Mobility / GSOC | CSO | Per trip |
| 3 | Monitor active travelers for emerging destination threats (W4676); on threat, alert and advise (shelter, relocate, evacuate — W4688); debrief high-risk trips | GSOC / Travel Risk | CSO | Per active trip |

### System Touchpoints
- Travel booking (VS-160); itinerary-tracking platform; destination-risk (W4679); GSOC (W4676); insurance (VS-26)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Duty-of-care risk**: An employee harmed on a business trip to a known-risk destination without briefing/tracking → employer liability; mitigated by mandatory pre-travel briefing + tracking
- **Blind-traveler risk**: A traveler the GSOC cannot locate in a crisis; mitigated by itinerary-tracking and check-in cadence

### Time Estimate
- ~per elevated-risk trip 0.5–1 day briefing + trip monitoring

### Automation Opportunity
- Destination-risk screen at booking; itinerary-tracking platform; automated check-in and geo-alerting

### Controls
- CTL-708 (ensure controlled execution — Executive Protection Principal Travel and Event Security (PA.)
- CTL: elevated-risk trips briefed + tracked; duty-of-care documented; insurance confirmed

### Cross-references
- distinct from VS-141 (daily commute); links to VS-160 (mobility), W4679 (destination risk), W4688 (lost-contact/medevac), VS-26 (insurance)

| **Frequency** | Rare (preparedness continuous) |

| **Owner** | GSOC Manager / CSO (joint with HR/OH VS-83) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19), Occupational Health (VS-83), medical-evac provider, insurance (VS-26), family, law enforcement/embassy |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the lost-contact/medical-evacuation protocol: trigger thresholds (missed check-in, destination crisis, medical event), escalation, medical-evac provider engagement, and family liaison | GSOC Manager / CSO / OH | CSO / CHRO | Continuous readiness |

### Controls
