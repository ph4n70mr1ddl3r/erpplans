## 4. GRC usage notes

- The function pairs in §2 are access-control rule seeds: for each, configure mutually exclusive permission sets so one user cannot hold both functions; the CTL's workflows column scopes where the conflict can arise.
- Dual-authorization entries are workflow-routing rules, not permission conflicts: model them as two-step approvals with distinct approvers enforced at submit time.
- Self-approval blocks are same-user constraints on a specific object (e.g., CTL-43: the account's Sales Rep cannot be its credit approver) — enforce as initiator-vs-approver inequality checks.
- Detective controls in the C26–C33 operating family monitor these segregations after the fact; their absence from this register is by design, not a gap.
